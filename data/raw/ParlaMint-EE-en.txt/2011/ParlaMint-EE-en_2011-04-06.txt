ParlaMint-EE_2011-04-06_U25	Ladies and gentlemen, I have a draft to hand over on behalf of the Centre Party Group. This bill, the draft law amending the State Property Act, is already the third time in the proceedings of the Riigikogu. The idea and purpose of this draft is that the salary of the President of the Republic of Estonia should be the limit of which the members of the board of directors of the company and foundation established by the state would not earn more. The current situation, where the salary of the Estonian Head of State is less than that of many other officials who are paid from another state, does not, in our opinion, correspond to the size of the responsibility of these people or to the sense of justice of the people. Thank you.
