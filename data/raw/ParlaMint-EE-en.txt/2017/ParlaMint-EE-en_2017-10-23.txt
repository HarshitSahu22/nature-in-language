In organising elections, the Election Committee of the Republic follows the Riigikogu's Home and Rules of Procedure Act. In short. Before going to the election procedures, I would remind you that the Vice-Chairmen of the Riigikogu are elected at the same time. A member of the Riigikogu may appoint a candidate. The proposed person must consent to the application. The vote shall be secret. Voting time shall be filled in on ballot papers. Every member of the Riigikogu has one vote. The votes shall be taken publicly as soon as the vote is closed. Objections to the organisation of the vote shall be submitted to the selection panel immediately after the end of the vote and on the outcome of the vote, without delay after the results of the vote have been announced. The result of the election shall be announced immediately after the possible protests have been examined. The two candidates who have received the most votes will be vice-chairmen of the Riigikogu. The first vice-chairman shall be the candidate with the highest number of votes and the second candidate with the highest number of votes received as vice-chairman. An additional round of elections will be held among the equal votes gathered. I will present the voting procedure before the vote is announced. Any questions? No, it's not. Members of the Commission will check the ballot box and seal it with a security seal right here in front of you. Honourable Members of the Riigikogu! Although I believe that you have achieved professional skill over the years, nothing has to be done with my duty to introduce you to voting procedures. Just in case, suddenly no one remembers. First of all, the Electoral Commission will sign a ballot paper against you. Please submit your identity document to the electoral committee for voting slip, it is a certificate, passport, ID card or driving licence of a member of the Riigikogu. The ballot papers shall be completed in the booth. A member of the Riigikogu shall tick a cell with a cross at the name of the candidate he votes for. A note marked with more than one cell or not marked with any cell shall be deleted. Voting is organised in the lobby next to the Chamber of the Riigikogu. You go out the back door to the voting room and there are tables in the lobby where you get the ballot paper. After receiving the note, you will make your decision in the voting booth, break the note and go to the voting box where, after the note has been sealed, you will put your note in the box. If you have accidentally erred or violated the note and notice it before you are put in the ballot box, you have the right to receive a new ballot paper when the corrupt note is returned. Any questions? There are no questions. According to § 87 (5) of the Home and Working Procedures Act, members of the Riigikogu have 30 minutes to vote from the beginning of the voting.
