ParlaMint-EE_2017-03-21_U4	Mr President, I would like to thank you very much. Dear Riigikogu! The Economic Commission has initiated a draft amendment to the Railway Act and the Public Transport Act. The reasons are quite technical. The amendment of the Railway Act is due to the need to clarify the provisions to be transposed in Directive 2007/59/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council on the certification of train drivers operating locomotives and trains on the rail system in the Community and Directive 2012/34/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a single European railway area. The inappropriate transposition of the provisions has been highlighted in the European Commission's EU Pilot enquiry No 7110/14 and EU Pilot inquiry No EUP (2016) 9115. As a matter of fact, the amendments have a major impact and do not require the addressee to change its behaviour. The Public Transport Act implements Regulation (EU) No 181/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the rights of passengers travelling by bus and amending Regulation (EC) No 2006/2004 as regards the derogation granted in Article 2 (4) and (5). The text of the draft and the explanatory memorandum to the draft has been prepared by the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications and the Committee on Economic Affairs has been initiated by agreement. In short, the draft aims to remedy the shortcomings found in our laws. Thank you.
