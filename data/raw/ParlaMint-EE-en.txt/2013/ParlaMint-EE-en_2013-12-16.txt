The requested persons shall be identified by the Member States using the database established by Interpol for the international search of persons. In order to declare a person as a fugitive, an arrest warrant must be selected in the country concerned. After an arrest warrant has been issued by a Member State's court, that State shall be able to enter the person in the Interpol database. Interpol shall monitor any search ads entered into the database by the Member States. If there is any doubt that there is a conflict with Article 3 of the Interpol Statute, the notice of request shall be transmitted to the supervisory body, the CCF, and the requested persons shall have access to the CCF by means of applications. Question 3: 'Do you think that Estonia is a member of Interpol? ' I consider Interpol membership important because it is the only organisation through which the competent law enforcement authorities of most countries in the world can be cooperated. ' Estonia has been a member of Interpol for over 20 years. During this time, hundreds of wanted persons have been apprehended during police cooperation.
