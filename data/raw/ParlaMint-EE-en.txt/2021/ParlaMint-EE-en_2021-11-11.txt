ParlaMint-EE_2021-11-11_U2	Good morning. Mr President-in-Office of the Council. Ladies and gentlemen! Good hearing viewers! On behalf of the Conservative People's Party of Estonia, I hand over the draft decision of the Riigikogu to the Executive Board of the Riigikogu. The content of the draft is to propose that the Government of the Republic accept a negative corona test as evidence of infection safety. It is our will to propose to the Government of the Republic by the Riigikogu that the negative COVID-19 result be quickly accepted as evidence of infection safety in public places and events. In a situation where schools recognise a negative test result as an infection control measure, there is no justification for not recognising the same measure in the rest of society. The acceptance of the negative COVID-19 test result as evidence of infection safety will significantly reduce the current unequal treatment and alleviate tensions in society. The draft has been submitted to the sitting department electronically. Thank you!
