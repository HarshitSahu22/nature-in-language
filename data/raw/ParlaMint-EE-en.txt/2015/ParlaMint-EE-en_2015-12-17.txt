In the 1990s, the burden on national officials and judges of the Election Commission of the Republic was sufficient to organise elections alongside their main work, with the assistance of the Secretariat of the Election Commission. To date, the organisation of elections has become significantly more labour-intensive and it is appropriate to entrust this task to the separate electoral unit. For example, the organisation of elections is more labour-intensive in connection with the introduction of electronic voting, the increase in the European Parliament elections and the increase in voting time. The amendments are due to enter into force on 1 January 2017. Thank you.
