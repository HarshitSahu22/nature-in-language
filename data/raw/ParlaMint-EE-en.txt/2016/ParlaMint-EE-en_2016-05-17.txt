Ladies and gentlemen, On behalf of the Group of the Estonian Centre Party, I am handing over a draft decision to initiate a legislative amendment which would also allow self-employed persons to have paid paternity leave at national level. This would lead to an end to the unequal treatment of self-employed workers compared to other working fathers. Paternity leave is a ten-day leave which the father can take during a period immediately preceding or following the birth of the child. We asked the Minister for Health and Labour on 23 March and the Minister for Justice Urmas Reinsalu on 19 April. The Minister of Justice replied and found that this was not a legally correct situation. We therefore want to remedy this situation. We want all fathers treated equally. Thank you.

ParlaMint-EE_2016-05-17_U4	Honourable President of the sitting! Ladies and gentlemen, Good guests! The Economic Commission will initiate a draft law amending the Product Compliance Act. At the end of last year, the Chancellor of Justice asked the Riigikogu for a proposal to bring the Product Compliance Act into line with the Constitution. This was about setting accreditation fees. Accreditation is known to be a public service for which the imposition of a fee must be provided either by law or by a mandate rule. However, this mandate is not granted and accreditation fees have been set up by an accreditation centre. As a result, this draft is a correction of errors. There is a proposal that some specific fees be set by the Riigikogu itself, but at the same time a mandate has been given for some components to impose these fees on the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications. I very much hope for your support so that we can get this bill passed as a law and corrected by mistakes before the summer vacation. Thank you.
