ParlaMint-EE_2016-08-29_U8	Hello, honourable Members of the Riigikogu. Last night's decisions of the Election Commission of the Republic were registered as candidates for the President of the Republic in the first round of voting (read in advance order) Mailis Reps, Allar Jõks and Eiki Nestor. Thus, according to the law, allar Jõks, Eiki Nestor and Mailis Reps are listed in alphabetical order. We're about to go to the election procedures. Before that, let me introduce the voting procedure. We have one hour to vote. The election committee gives the member of the Riigikogu a voting slip together with an envelope. The note shall be issued on presentation of the identity document and against signature. An identity document is, for example, a certificate issued by a member of the Riigikogu, an identity card, a passport or a driving licence. The vote shall be secret. The ballot papers shall be filled in in the voting booth. Every member of the Riigikogu has one vote. The member of the Riigikogu shall tick the box at the name of the candidate he votes for. After filling out the note, the voter puts the note in the envelope and gives it to a member of the selection board who puts a seal stamp on the envelope and then puts the envelope in the ballot box. In the event of an infringement of a ballot paper before that ballot box is placed in the ballot box, the voters shall have the right to receive a new note in the event of the return of the corrupt note. Before counting the votes, a member of the Riigikogu has the right to make a written protest against the violation of voting procedures. It shall be reviewed by the Election Commission of the Republic and shall make its own motivated decision. After the vote has been completed and any protests have been resolved, the electoral committee will read the votes publicly in this very Chamber. While I am here, I will also take this opportunity to explain to you the procedure for determining the result of the vote. First of all, the Commission will determine the number of ballot boxes, i.e. the number of members of the Riigikogu who participated in the vote. The selection panel will then determine the number of invalid notes. It shall be void where the cross is marked in more than one box or in which or in which there is no seal imprint on the envelope. Separate identification shall be made of the notes not marked in any box of the cross. The Commission shall then count the votes cast on each candidate. We will announce the result of the vote by reading out the minutes of the Republic Election Commission on the outcome of the vote. But before you determine the result of the election, you have the right to make a written protest against the infringement of the voting procedure. I will announce the result of the election by reading out the decision of the Republic Election Commission on the result of the election. A candidate who is voted for by a two-thirds majority of the Riigikogu, i.e. at least 68 members of the Riigikogu, is declared elected. The vote will take place in the lobby next to the Chamber of the Riigikogu. Only members of the electoral commission and persons serving the electoral commission may be present in the voting area other than members of the Riigikogu. Thank you!
