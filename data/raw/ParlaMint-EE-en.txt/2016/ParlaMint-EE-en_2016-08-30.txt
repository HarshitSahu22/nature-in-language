ParlaMint-EE_2016-08-30_U8	Thank you very much for your question. The electoral committee of the Republic received a similar address this morning and this morning, at its meeting, the electoral committee of the Republic formed a position on this issue. I read to you this statement: 'Article 79 (4) of the Constitution states that the President of the Republic shall be elected by secret ballot. It is the duty of the Election Commission of the Republic to ensure the conditions for secret voting, as the organiser of the vote. Voting procedures in the election of the President of the Republic are laid down in § 9 of the Presidential Election Act. The conditions laid down by law for secret voting have been set up by the Election Commission of the Republic in the current elections: the voter votes with anonymous ballot papers and votes are filled in the voting booth. The Election Commission of the Republic of Estonia checks the voting environment in the election of the President of the Republic, except for the moment of filling in the ballot paper in the booth. Every voter will vote in the voting booth himself. The electoral commission of the Republic does not control the behaviour of the electorate at the moment of the vote, as the monitoring of the electoral activity in the voting booth would, in turn, run counter to the principle of the secrecy of the election. Implementation of the principle of secrecy is also the responsibility of the voter. A member of the Riigikogu shall vote on the principle of a free mandate and on his conscience. The electoral committee of the Republic considers that any agreement to prove its possible choice after the vote cannot be binding on the voters. Conflict with the principle of secrecy may arise by publicising its choice to third parties, as it may put pressure on other voters who may be expected to do the same. The Act on the election of the President of the Republic allows protests if voting procedures have been violated. In the examination of the protest, the Election Commission of the Republic shall take into account the specific facts. The Election Committee of the Republic calls on voters to respect the principle of secrecy of voting laid down in the Constitution and the Presidential Election Act. ' Thank you!
