ParlaMint-EE_2016-10-03_U9	Mr. Chairman! Good afternoon, honourable Riigikogu. Let me remind you of the voting arrangements. Kersti Kaljulaid has been registered in the first round of voting as a candidate for President of the Republic by decision of the Election Commission yesterday. The name of Kersti Kaljulaid is therefore entered on the ballot paper. The election committee gives voting ballot papers to the members of the Riigikogu together with the envelope on presentation of the identity document and against signature. The vote shall be secret. The ballot papers shall be filled in in the voting booth. Every member of the Riigikogu has one vote. As only one candidate has the name on the ballot paper, the member of the Riigikogu will tick the box of the note if he or she votes in favour of the candidate. After filling out the note, the voter puts the note in the envelope and gives it to a member of the selection board who puts a seal stamp on the envelope and then puts the envelope in the ballot box. In the event of a breach of the ballot paper before the ballot box is placed in the ballot box, the voters shall have the right to receive a new note against the corrupt note. Before counting the votes, a member of the Riigikogu has the right to make a written protest against the violation of voting procedures. The Commission shall examine and decide on the protest. After the end of the vote and possible protests have been resolved, the electoral committee will read the votes publicly in this Chamber. First, the Commission will determine the number of notes in the ballot box, then the number of invalid notes. Voting papers which do not bear the impression of the seal of the electoral commission shall be invalid. The number of notes not marked with a cross shall be identified separately and the Commission shall then count the votes cast on the candidate. On the outcome of the vote, the Electoral Commission will draw up a record which I will read to you. After that, you have the right to protest against a violation of the voting procedure. The election results will be decided by the electoral committee, which I will read to you as well. The candidate is declared elected if he is voted for by a two-thirds majority of the Riigikogu, which is at least 68 members of the Riigikogu. The vote will take place in the lobby next to the Chamber of the Riigikogu. Only members of the electoral commission and persons serving the electoral commission may be present in the voting area other than members of the Riigikogu. Thank you for your attention.
