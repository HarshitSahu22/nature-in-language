According to § 69 of the Constitution of the Republic of Estonia, the Riigikogu elects the President of the Riigikogu and two vice-chairmen from among its members. Due to the Riigikogu's Law on the Rules of Procedure and Homes, the Riigikogu organises voting in the election of the President and Vice-Chairs, explains the results of the elections and solves protests by the Riigikogu Election Committee. In organising the election, the Riigikogu Election Committee is governed by § 7, § 81 (2) and § 86.89 of the Riigikogu Rules of Procedure Act. Before going to the election procedures, let me briefly introduce the election of the Riigikogu Board. First, the President of the Riigikogu is elected. The Vice-Chairmen of the Riigikogu are elected at the same time after the election of the Chairman of the Riigikogu. A member of the Riigikogu may appoint a candidate. After the election of the President of the Riigikogu, candidates for the Vice-Chairs of the Riigikogu shall be appointed. The proposed person must give his or her consent to the application. Voting shall be by secret procedure and shall be carried out by means of a ballot paper. Each member of the Riigikogu has one vote in the election of both the Chair and Vice-Chairs of the Riigikogu. The votes shall be taken publicly as soon as the vote is closed. Objections to the organisation of the vote shall be submitted to the selection panel immediately after the end of the vote and the protest on the outcome of the vote shall be made immediately after the results of the vote have been announced. The results of the election shall be announced immediately after the possible protests have been examined. A candidate who receives more than half of the valid votes shall be elected as chairman of the Riigikogu. If no candidate receives more than half of the valid votes, an additional round shall be held between the two candidates with the highest votes. In the case of equal votes, lots shall be cast. If there is only one candidate on the list of candidates for the Chairman of the Riigikogu, he will be elected if he receives more votes in favour than against.
