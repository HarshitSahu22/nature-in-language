The Commission considers that the Ministry of Health should have put in place a procedure that is capable of taking decisions on the prevalence of co-infection on the basis of a planned vaccination process. With regard to the assessment of the communication of the decisions taken by the government. The Commission considers that the Covid-19 crisis has shown the vital importance of effective strategic communication. The central strategic communication tool in the Republic of Latvia is the State Chancellery and related ministries communicators. At the beginning of 2020, within the framework of the State Chancellery, a Strategic Communication Coordination Department was established. During the crisis, a special communication coordination group has also been set up, which includes all ministries, the State Chancellery, a representative from the Office of the Prime Minister, as well as separate subordinate institutions. According to the Commission, the main task of strategic communication during the Covid-19 crisis has been and remains to provide Latvian society with quality and perceptible information. Information must be uniform and reach as many different groups of society as possible, regardless of their differences, education, social security, age and other characteristics. The analysis of target audiences has been and remains a major challenge in the implementation of the crisis communication. A serious problem in the field of communication relates to disinformation, its scale and its presence in modern information sources. According to the Commission, greater attention should be paid to explaining the structural framework in society.
