Draft law is prepared for adoption and approval of the eleventh Additional Protocol to the Constitution of the World Postal Union. The Constitution is a basic instrument which contains the basic provisions of the Union, setting out the organisation's objective, scope, accession procedures, principles for determining annual expenditure, as well as the minimum applicable documents and procedures for their adoption, amendment and approval. The Constitution may be amended only at an assembly of the Union. The amendments adopted are included in the Additional Protocol. The 11th Additional Protocol to the Constitution was signed at the 27th convention of the Union in Abidjan on 26 August last. The amendments to the Constitution improve the means of compulsory approval of documents at national level, providing that union documents may not only be ratified but also be approved, adopted or acceded to by the Member States in accordance with their national regulations. The Constitution has been supplemented by the rule that documents accepted at the convention can be signed after the convention in Berne’s headquarters. The Constitution strengthened the permanent status of the agreements and regulations of the World Postal Convention by providing for amendments to these instruments to be further drawn up by means of additional protocols rather than the re-issue of extensive consolidated versions of these texts. This will significantly facilitate the publication and validation of documents. Taking into account the provisions of the Constitution and the Latvian national regulation on the approval of international treaties, the Additional Protocol should be ratified and the declaration of ratification should be submitted to the Depositary of the World Postal Union – the Director General of the International Bureau.
