In September 1998, the Energy Law was adopted, which was amended on 15 June 2015, supplementing it with content that is in line with EU legislation on the development of the internal energy market of the European Union. Section 42 of the Energy Act stipulated that all natural gas users had the right to freely choose their natural gas trader. The transitional provisions of the law stipulated, inter alia, that the entry into force of Section 42 of the law was determined by a separate law.
