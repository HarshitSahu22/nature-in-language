ParlaMint-LV_2016-10-20-PT12-380-U10	Dear President, Dear colleagues, The Budget and Finance (tax) Commission of the Saeima has examined draft law No.658/Lp12 - Amendments to the Credit Register Law. The draft law was drawn up at the initiative of the Bank of Latvia, and its aim is to promote the use of the data included in the Credit Register for the assessment of the person’s creditworthiness by providing members of the Credit Register with the right to receive the information included in the Credit Register about the person who submitted the application to the member of the Credit Register, as well as to promote the additional possibility for participants of the Credit Register to assess the possible creditworthiness of the recipient of the credit letter and the possible recipient of the guarantee service, as well as to supplement the law with a reference to Directive 2014/17/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 February 2014 on consumer credit agreements relating to residential immovable property. One of the objectives of the Credit Register Law is to contribute to the promotion of financial stability of Latvia, to provide, among other things, the participants of the Credit Register with additional possibilities to assess the creditworthiness of their potential client. The law provides for the right of a member of a credit register to receive the information included in the credit register about a person specified in the law, not only on the basis of its written application, but also on the person’s application submitted in the form previously agreed in writing between that person and the member of the credit register. The institutions involved in project development are the Ministry of Finance, the Bank of Latvia, the Financial and Capital Market Commission. The fourth subparagraph of the second paragraph was drawn up by the Ministry of Economic Affairs. When assessing the impact of a legal act on the economy, it should be stated that the administrative burden will be reduced for members of the Credit Registry and for persons who apply to them for a financial service with credit risk, credit letter or guarantee service or for the conclusion of a customer's principal agreement, as it will be possible to obtain more effectively the information included in the Credit Register for the assessment of the creditworthiness of individuals. The legal act does not have an impact on the state budget, nor does it change the regulation established by the Law on the Credit Register, which ensures non-discriminatory access of creditors of the European Union and European Economic Area Member States to the Credit Register. The Association of Commercial Banks of Latvia, the public organisation “Association of Leasing Employers of Latvia”, the Association of Krajaizdējumi and the Latvian Insurers’ Association have expressed their support for the draft law. The Commission calls for support to be given to the draft law at first reading.
