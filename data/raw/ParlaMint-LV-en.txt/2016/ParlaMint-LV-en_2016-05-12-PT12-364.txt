According to Section 6(1) of the Law currently in force herein, special economic zone or licensed capital companies are entitled to apply a reduction of 80 per cent of the calculated amount of the tax on immovable property tax on the real estate property owned, held or used in the territory of the zone or free port. The application of the abovementioned tax credit to a special economic zone or a licensed capital company shall require the authorisation of the zone administration or the free port administration, which may be granted to it only if the specific conditions laid down in Paragraph Two of Section 5 of this Law are fulfilled. In those specific cases, municipalities in whose territories are located free port or special economic zones are not entitled to apply a tax credit different from that (it is 80 per cent) and to lay down other additional conditions for the application of the tax credit as well as for the reduction of the tax credit. On the other hand, according to another law – the Law on Real Estate Tax – municipalities are entitled to apply tax relief in the amount of 90, 70, 50 or 25 per cent of the amount of real estate tax to certain categories of real estate taxpayers. Moreover, Article 3.1 of the Law on Real Estate Tax establishes the real estate tax rate and the principles of tax relief that municipalities must observe in cases where municipalities exercise the right delegated to them by law to decide to issue binding provisions in certain cases. For example, one of the principles is the principle of territorial development and territorial adjustment, according to which a municipality uses a tax rate or tax incentives to promote and regulate the development of its territory. In order to avoid a described situation in which municipalities cannot fully apply the tax policy instruments provided for in the Law “On Real Estate Tax” and to promote further independence of municipalities in applying the real estate tax relief, in line with the principles of tax relief laid down in the Law “On Real Estate Tax”, I submitted this proposal.
