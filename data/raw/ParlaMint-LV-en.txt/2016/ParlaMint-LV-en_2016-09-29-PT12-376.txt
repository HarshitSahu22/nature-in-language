In particular, we want to change this system, and there will be no further obstacle to the provision of maintenance for such a long period of time. The parent will be able to apply to the administration of the Maintenance Guarantee Fund by himself in an out-of-court procedure with an application and to apply for the payment of maintenance from the Maintenance Guarantee Fund. The Fund will then recover from the other parent the maintenance paid by the State in a regressive manner. Thus, in the Law on Civil Procedure, these amendments concern issues regarding initiation of claim for maintenance, recovery of maintenance, as well as the record of execution of maintenance cases. I therefore call on the Legal Commission to support this draft law at first reading.
