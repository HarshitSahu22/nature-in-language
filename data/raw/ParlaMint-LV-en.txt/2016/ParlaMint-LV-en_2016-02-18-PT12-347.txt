It is also foreseen that foreign residents who are detained in accordance with the procedure laid down by the Immigration Law, except for vulnerable persons - minor foreigners, disabled persons, foreigners of age, who are awarded an old-age pension in the Republic of Latvia, pregnant women, single families with minor children and persons who have suffered serious psychological, physical and sexual violence, may be placed in place of temporary detention. In addition, the draft law stipulates that temporary detention facilities shall have a fenced area of at least 15 square metres, for walking in fresh air. Currently, the average area of walking areas of temporary detention places established by the State Police is 18.7 square meters. Against this background, the existing walking areas of temporary detention facilities will not need to be rebuilt in order to adapt them to the minimum area provided for in the draft law. The draft law provides that the place of short-term detention shall provide a space for the collection and inclusion of biometric data in the biometric data processing system, as well as for photography of the detained and its special features and preparation of the criminalistic characteristics. In order to ensure the right of a person to clean and favourable, smoke-free tobacco and herbal smoke products, as well as to the vapour of electronic smoking devices, established by legislation, the aim is to impose a ban on persons detained from smoking in short-term detention facilities. The draft law provides for supplementing the law with a norm, which states that video surveillance is carried out instead of short-term detention. This is the only effective means to fully ensure the security of persons in temporary detention, maximum monitoring of their behaviour and safeguarding internal order. The aim of video surveillance is not only to protect the interests of ensuring order in the place of temporary detention, but also the rights of persons who have been placed in the place of temporary detention. The draft law provides that representatives of a diplomatic or consular mission may visit the place of temporary detention to meet a foreigner inserted in it without restriction. Taking into account the number of complaints received by the State Police from persons placed and held in place of temporary detention and the content of such complaints, as well as the content of the requests submitted to courts and assessment of judgments, the draft law provides to instruct the Cabinet of Ministers to set uniform hygiene requirements in places of temporary detention.
