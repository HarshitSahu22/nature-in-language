ParlaMint-LV_2021-10-21-PT13-2305-U2	Hello, dear colleagues! Honorary Head of the sitting! We work with the draft law “Amendments to the Covid-19 Law on Disease Management” (No. 1204/Lp13). Let me tell you about the substance of this draft law. The aim of the draft law is to promote the vaccination process against Covid-19. In particular, during the period from 1 November 2021 to 31 March 2022, it is planned to pay a allowance of EUR 20 per month to persons living in Latvia who have reached the age of 60 and have completed a complete vaccination course against Covid-19 infection by 31 December 2021 or have received the opinion of the Consilium of the Clinical University Hospital on the need to postpone vaccination against Covid-19 until 31 March 2022 due to health condition. They will thus be supported at a time when the country is experiencing a general price increase, including an increase in energy prices. In the Commission's examination of this draft law, we discussed with the Parliament's Legal Office, and the question was raised as to why the 60-year age limit for this support has been set. In this context, it is important to note that the spread of the virus is particularly significant for the elderly, who, due to Covid-19, are characterised by a severe course of disease and are also likely to die. Therefore – for persons over 60 years of age and older. This is one of the priority groups and has already had a previous chance of vaccination. Unfortunately, statistics show that in this age group... So it's over 60 years... 60-year-olds and parents... only 57.45 percent have been vaccinated. But absolutely... So, there's the first potties they've got... The total vaccination rate is 53.06 per cent of these populations. Overall, there are about 527 220 such citizens in Latvia, so – quite a large group. And – with a really high risk of getting into a difficult hospital. And that can even lead to worse consequences... It is therefore this group that is defined as the beneficiaries of this aid. The Budget and Financial (Tax) Commission examined this draft law and supported it for its first reading. The budget and financial (tax) commission calls for the urgent nature of this draft law.
