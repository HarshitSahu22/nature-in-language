If the income is not sufficient to discharge the liabilities in any of those amounts, one third of the debtor's income shall be transferred to redemption, but not less than one third of the minimum monthly salary. However, the statutory procedure for the qualification of liabilities allows for the creation of a paradoxical situation where the deletion of major liabilities results in more favourable conditions for the discharge of liabilities than for the cancellation of minor liabilities. In particular, with a relatively small proportion of the liabilities paid, higher liabilities can be extinguished in a shorter time, but with a relatively larger proportion of the liabilities paid the lower liabilities can be extinguished in the longer term.
