The next item is the bill “Amendment to the Code of Conduct for Penalties of Latvia”, first reading. Member of the Commission for Defence, Home Affairs and Prevention of Corruption Juris Rancāns. Please!
ParlaMint-LV_2021-04-22-PT13-2239-U10	Dear colleagues, The aim of the draft law prepared by the Cabinet of Ministers in this case is to ensure continuity of execution of the custodial sentence after 1 May 2021 due to the judgement of the Constitutional Court of 7 November 2019 in the case on the inconsistency of Article 50.4 of the Latvian Penalty Code. In this judgment, the Constitutional Court recognised that the aforementioned norm, which establishes a more severe penalty regime for persons depending on the sole criterion, namely gender membership, violates the prohibition of discrimination laid down in the Constitution. In its judgment, the Constitutional Tribunal provided for a transitional period until 1 May 2021. That provision shall cease to apply from that date. The responsible - the Ministry of Justice - has made it clear that this judgment has been partially enforced, but it cannot be fully enforced due to inadequate prison infrastructure. Thus, the draft law provides that men convicted of committing a serious and particularly serious crime, as well as men displaced from a partially closed prison for serious or systematic violations of the regime, shall be punished in accordance with the procedure laid down in the Latvian Penal Code or the procedure in force until 30 April 2021. In the Commission, when hearing representatives of the Ministry of Justice, the draft law was supported. The Commission also calls for support for this draft law at first reading.
