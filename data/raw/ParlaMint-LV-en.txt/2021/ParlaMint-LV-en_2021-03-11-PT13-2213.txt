Draft Law “Amendments to the Covid-19 Law on the Management of Infectious Effects” (No. 973/Lp13), first reading. Member of the Commission for Budget and Finance (Tax) Mārtiņš Steins.
ParlaMint-LV_2021-03-11-PT13-2213-U8	Hello, colleagues, again! This draft law is “Amendments to the Covid-19 law on the control of the spread of infection” (No. 973/Lp13). The purpose of this draft law is also to clarify the legal framework for certain measures to prevent and manage threats and consequences of Covid-19 infection, to supplement the law with new rules in order to improve the public and economic situation and to contribute to the stability of the national economy. There are a number of specific proposals. One is to set a longer deadline for the submission of the annual income statement for 2020 and payment of income taxes for other persons; to remove the limit for personal income tax in 2020 and 2021; to waive the requirement for the members of the board of a capital company to pay the minimum income tax and state social insurance contributions from the minimum wage if the capital company has not had any employee who receives remuneration of at least the minimum wage. The amendments also provide for changes in the calculation of gambling taxes during an emergency period, which prohibits gambling in gambling venues, gaming halls and casinos, by providing that gambling tax is paid only for the days of the month during which gambling operators were allowed to organise the gambling in question. The amendments are also aimed at setting a longer deadline for the submission of annual reports and the performance reports of public benefit organisations for the year 2020. Provision is also made for the publication of information about those taxpayers who have received state aid in accordance with the law and its total amount. It also provides for a framework to support the taxi industry in managing the effects of the Covid-19 pandemic and ensuring continuity of operations. The amendments were designed to create a green corridor for operators in the catering sector, accelerated and facilitated coordination of the activities of outdoor terraces, mobile catering units, tasting stands and other outdoor catering facilities, including by reducing potential costs for a faster return to business. An extension of the insolvency moratorium is encouraged if there are certain features. A longer deadline for submission of the annual report of the social enterprise has been set; a longer deadline for the capital companies to convene a meeting of the members (shareholders) for approval of the annual report and consolidated annual report of the capital company; as well as a longer period for submission of the report of the public person on the use of the gift has been set. The Commission supported this draft law at first reading and supported the urgency of the draft law. On behalf of the Commission, I ask the Saeima to support the urgency of this draft law.
