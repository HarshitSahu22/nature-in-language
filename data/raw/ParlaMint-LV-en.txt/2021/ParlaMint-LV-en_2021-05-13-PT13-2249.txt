This requirement is necessary as social enterprise status grants access to support for social enterprises, including financial support. It will therefore be possible to prevent undue financial support from being granted to companies that are not doing business well and to ensure better management of European Union funds. In practice, it has been established that the applicants formally qualify for the status of social enterprises have little social impact, but when assessing their activities in general, it can be concluded that the objective of the company’s activities is to generate profits that are contrary to the criteria for granting the status of social enterprises. Therefore, the social enterprise feature is specified, which will allow the Social Enterprise Commission to carry out a more qualitative assessment of the impact of the company's planned activities on the achievement of the statutory objectives when assessing the applicants' compliance with the characteristics of social enterprises.
