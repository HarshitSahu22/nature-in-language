ParlaMint-LV_2021-12-15-PT13-2342-U2	Good evening, colleagues! This draft law (No) 895/Lp13) The Saeima has been on the agenda for almost a year – since January 2020. At first reading, we examined this draft law on 18 February. And then, in preparation for the second reading, a working group of the Committee on Education, Culture and Science was set up, which worked on all the issues that needed to be clarified for the second reading. I will briefly remind you of what this bill is about. The draft law intends to continue and improve the reform of the content of vocational education and training, therefore also in relation to professional qualifications in the Latvian Qualifications Framework. The draft law develops regulation of modular vocational training programmes, principles of accumulation, transfer and recognition of learning outcomes achieved, as well as how part of the professional qualification can be certified by means of a set of model documents. The draft law also provides for the introduction of the processing of data on the monitoring of graduates of vocational education programmes and the insurance of students against accidents during the entire period of acquisition of vocational education programmes. The draft law clarifies the norms which extend the possibilities for the acquisition of further vocational education and training programmes, and therefore also for the methods of testing these qualifications.
