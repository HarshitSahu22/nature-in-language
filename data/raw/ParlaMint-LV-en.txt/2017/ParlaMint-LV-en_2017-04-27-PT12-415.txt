In its meeting on 25 April, the Commission for Defence, Home Affairs and the Prevention of Corruption examined and supported the draft law “Amendment to the Law on National Security”. In view of the current security situation, several countries in Europe, such as Great Britain, Denmark, Germany and the Netherlands, have already made changes to their legislation to prevent the spread of radicalisation and extremism. Countries that respect their commitment not to use war as a means of resolving conflicts have an interest in preventing their nationals from being involved in armed conflicts abroad, justifying the need for control on the basis of significant threats to national and public security arising from the return of such persons to their country. Analysing the current situation in Europe in relation to radicalisation trends and their consequences, it has been established that it is also necessary to introduce a legal mechanism for establishing an exit ban in Latvia, thus protecting public security as defined in Article 116 of the Satversme of the Republic of Latvia. Thus, the draft law provides for a supplement to the law with an opportunity to impose a ban on an individual's exit from the Republic of Latvia.
