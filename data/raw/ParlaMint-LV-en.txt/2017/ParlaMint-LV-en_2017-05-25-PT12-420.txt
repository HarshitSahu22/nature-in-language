The purpose of the proposals is to align the tax administration's liability for money withheld and not repaid in good time with that of the taxpayer when the tax has not been paid in good time. In my view, there must be a complete symmetry here. In relations between tax administration and taxpayers, the logic is as follows: the fact that tax revenue is not received in time in the budget means that the budget starts to credit the taxpayer with the amount of tax after the tax due or late payment of the tax, and therefore the legislator requests interest from the taxpayer on the use of the credit, i.e. late payment. According to the law, the unpaid basic debt requires 0.05 per cent for each day of delay. I would point out that, in the annual calculation, this is 18 per cent, which is actually paid by card users for cards issued abroad by highly developed economies. When, in turn, the taxpayer credits the budget, similar situations arise between the taxpayer and the tax administration, and the law describes two situations: one in which the tax administration incorrectly recovers payment from the taxpayer, and another in which the amount of tax has been overpaid. In both cases, the money withheld is reimbursed by the administration, but it never happens immediately, but is always delayed. It would be natural for the tax administration to pay the same late payment as the taxpayer when the tax is late, but this is not the case. The first paragraph of Article 28 currently in force provides that the amount to be repaid is increased by half of the penalty payment if repaid within 15 days after the payment has been wrongly recovered, and only after the 15th day the amount to be repaid is increased by the full penalty payment. However, overpaid tax amounts shall be refunded within 15 days without payment of any late payment. However, if the amount is not repaid within the legal deadline, it shall be increased by three fifths of the penalty payment. I propose: in the event of incorrect recovery by the tax administration of the payment or undue retention of the amount overpaid, to require that the amount to be repaid be increased by the full amount of the penalty payment, not by its part. By aligning the financial responsibility of the tax administration with the financial responsibility of the taxpayer for the undue withholding of money, we increase the responsibility of the tax administration and hence the efficiency of tax administration. Also from a legal point of view, the responsibility of the taxpayer and the tax administration before the law must be balanced, balanced and, in order to ensure this balance, I would ask for support for these my proposals - proposals 7 and 8 too.
