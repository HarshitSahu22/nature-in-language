This law also contains a provision requiring that the annual budget for public media should not be less than that of the previous year, thereby ensuring a degree of financial independence. This issue has become very acute in recent times in connection with sponsorship agreements concluded by Latvijas Radio on certain sections of the programme, so it is very important to foresee in this draft law the moment when our public media - Latvian Radio and Latvian Television - will no longer operate in the advertising market, thus ensuring independent content creation and giving a much clearer playing field for commercial media so that they can create diverse and necessary content for society.
