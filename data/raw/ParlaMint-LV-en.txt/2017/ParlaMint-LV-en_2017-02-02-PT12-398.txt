In order to prevent such cases and to make it easier for the Road Police to work and make things clearer, amendments have been made: one relates to the cases where documents need to be presented and whether they need to be physically delivered; the other relates to the use of various intoxicating substances, and these cases have been clarified. The first paragraph of Section 25 of the Road Traffic Law determines which documents must be present at all to the driver and which must be presented at the request of police officers, border guards or customs officers. In practice, there are now cases where many drivers interpret this provision in such a way that the driver, for example at the request of the police officer, only has to present these documents visually and not be given them for inspection. In view of this, the draft law sets out what documents should be handed over to these officials for physical checks at the request of police officers, border guards or customs officials.
