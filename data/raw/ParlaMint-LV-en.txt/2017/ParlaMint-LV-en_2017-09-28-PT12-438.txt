The draft law complements the delegation referred to in the law by enabling water management service users to agree with the service provider on the amount of compensation and the procedure for payment thereof, so that the users of the service who carry out or intend to carry out the construction of pre-cleaning equipment can invest in the construction of pre-cleaning equipment. This does not mean that during the lifetime of the arrangement the user of the service would be fully exempted from payment of the waste water from production, nor does it provide for the total abolition of compensation charges for increased discharge of contaminated waste water. In the Commission, we have examined this draft law and we ask for it to be declared urgent.
