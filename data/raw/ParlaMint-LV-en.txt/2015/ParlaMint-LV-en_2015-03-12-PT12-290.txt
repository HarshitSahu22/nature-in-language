The spouses of ambassadors and other diplomats in most cases go with them to their place of employment abroad, thus leaving their jobs in Latvia for a long period of time. While working on this draft law for second reading, the Budget and Finance (Tax) Commission has received 388 proposals, many of which are from the applicants themselves, the Ministry of Finance. And the Commission concluded that the work had not been completed to the end, because several proposals from the Legal Bureau were received, which is conceptual. So, basically, much more rational work would go ahead if this draft law were discussed in the working group. That is why the Commission was in favour of asking for an extension of the deadline for the submission of proposals so that a large part of the proposals could be consolidated and simplified. And then the draft law could be passed on to the commission in a much more advanced version. The Commission's request is therefore to extend the deadline for submitting proposals until 15 April this year so that the working group set up can successfully complete its work within about one month, and then we can move forward more quickly in the work of the Saeima. I therefore call for an extension of the deadline for the submission of proposals.
