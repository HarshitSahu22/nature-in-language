ParlaMint-LV_2015-06-11-PT12-309-U104	Good morning, colleagues! We work with law No.256/Lp12 - Amendments to the Law on Support for Unemployed and Job Seekers. A little bit about the content of the bill. At the moment, a situation has arisen that unemployed persons apply to obtain the status of an unemployed person, who, although they comply with the conditions of the Law on Supporting Unemployment and Labour Seekers (have obtained the necessary authorisation to reside legally in the territory of the Republic of Latvia), have not declared their place of residence here, thus creating problems for the State Employment Agency in cases when it has to contact the unemployed person in order to inform about a question that is not favourable to a person. In order to solve this problem, the draft law provides that the support established by the law shall be entitled to receive only those persons who have fulfilled the obligation to declare their place of residence, if it is covered by the first paragraph of Section 6 of the Law on Declaration of the Residence.
