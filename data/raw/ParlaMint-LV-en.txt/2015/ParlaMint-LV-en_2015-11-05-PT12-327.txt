Although the Commission recognises that the changed certification regime for construction specialists is significantly better than the previous one due to the tragedy, it still needs a number of further improvements. The certification function of construction specialists should be delegated by the Construction Law, not by delegation agreements, as there is currently an opportunity to set up a certification body that is not interested in the competence and responsibility of specialists, but in applying favourable conditions to certified persons. It is also necessary to provide for certification bodies to have the right to revoke certificates in cases where they find serious professional misconduct. It should be stipulated in the regulatory acts that the experts in the design of a construction project must have experience in the design of the construction works for which they are mandatory and must include the requirement that professional activities and ethical violations are unacceptable. The functions of the State Construction Control Bureau, created by the tragedy, should be expanded by establishing this office as a pre-trial dispute resolution institution and imposing on it an obligation to organise regular training of municipal construction inspectors. In addition, it should be noted that there is insufficient funding for the office at the level of existing functions.
