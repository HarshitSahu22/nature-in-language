ParlaMint-LV_2015-11-26-PT12-332-U100	Colleagues! This draft law, as well as the next two Saeima, has been submitted by our President of Latvia to improve the regulation of the actions of the state's senior officials and institutions in the event of the external threat of the state, when timely and coordinated action is crucial. A clear and unambiguous decision-making mechanism is needed to avoid divergent interpretations of the powers of national authorities. Currently, the Institute of Chief of State Security included in the National Security Law duplicates the powers of other state defence and executive institutions, as well as during the war could complicate the management of state defence. The draft law provides that the principal would take over the management of state defence during the war only if the Cabinet of Ministers, for the reasons caused by the threat to the state, is prevented from performing its functions and there is an urgent need to take appropriate state defence decisions. If the Cabinet renews its activities, then it takes over its competences. The draft law provides that the principal or a person authorised by him shall participate in the meetings of the Cabinet of Ministers with advisory rights. The draft law defines the time of war on the basis of national threats formulated in the Charter of the United Nations and the North Atlantic Treaty. Provision is also made for the obligation of the Minister of Defence, Commander of the National Armed Forces and units of the National Armed Forces to react immediately to military aggression, as well as the right to apply armed forces. NBS entities are to take military protection measures without awaiting a separate decision or order and to act in accordance with the national defence operational plan.
