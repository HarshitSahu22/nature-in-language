ParlaMint-LV_2015-10-22-PT12-324-U90	Dear colleagues, This proposal provides for a very simple case: the salary of any worker who receives income from the budget is made public. Since 13 May 2010 amendments to Section 92(2) of the Law on State Administration have been adopted, which provides that remuneration paid to officials of the institution is published on the institution's website, indicating the name, title, position and amount of remuneration paid, there is a rather strange situation that one part of the state employees' salaries are published, for example, salaries of civil servants, police officers, firemen, members of procurement commissions, who are state officials, but are not publicly disclosed, for example, salaries of scientists, municipal employees or teachers.
