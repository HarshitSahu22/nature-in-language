One of the arguments is the possibility to establish a fictitious public organization, claiming to be an heir of pre-war organization, and to acquire property belonging to other persons. In the process of denationalisation, no ethnic group or nationality was given any advantage or exception. The draft law changes the current policy of Latvia in the area of denationalisation of property and intends to grant a different approach on an ethnic basis. It is a renewal of the process of denationalisation, at least for public organisations. In addition, judges have not been given the opportunity to exercise the right to effective judicial protection in accordance with Article 92 of the Satversme, namely to appeal against a negative decision against them. Having regard to these considerations, the Legal Commission considers that the right of the respective judges to continue their work in the position of a judge of the Department of Criminal Cases of the Supreme Court is not adequately assessed in accordance with the principle of transparency and procedural justice.
