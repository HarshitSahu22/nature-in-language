ParlaMint-LV_2020-10-22-PT13-2127-U2	Hello, dear colleagues! In the Saeima Commission for Education, Culture and Science, we received the draft law “Amendment to the Law on Song and Dance Festival” (No. 743/Lp13), which is related to the establishment of XII Latvian School Youth Song and Dance Festival in 2021. However, taking into account the unknown nature of the epidemiological situation caused by Covid-19, and the possibilities for further action of the Cabinet of Ministers, which has not been discussed to a sufficient extent in the draft law... The Commission decided to draw up an alternative draft law, namely the ‘Amendments to the Song and Dance Festival Act’ (No. 793/Lp13), which in turn provides that “The Cabinet of Ministers is entitled to take a decision on the events of XII Latvian School Youth Songs and Dance Festivals not held in 2020 until the end of 2021, ensuring compliance of their developments with the restrictions, precautionary measures imposed by laws and regulations on the spread of Covid-19 [...]”. Thus, if due to the above mentioned circumstances the School Youth Song and Dance Festival cannot be held in 2021 (we very much hope that this will not be the case), the Saeima will be able to decide on further actions and tasks to the Cabinet of Ministers. On behalf of the Commission, I call on the draft law tabled by the Cabinet of Ministers, Amendment to the Law on Song and Dance Celebration, to be rejected by voting against.
