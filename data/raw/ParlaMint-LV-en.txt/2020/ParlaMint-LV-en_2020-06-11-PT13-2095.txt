In addition, information on formal procedures for dealing with a person's financial difficulties will therefore be available in one place, thus reducing the administrative burden on creditors and cooperation partners as a whole. However, in the current version, the objective of the insolvency register, enshrined in Article 12 of the insolvency law... and the particulars to be recorded on it... it is not sufficient to ensure, without amendment, the publicity of the procedure contained in the related draft law ‘Law on the Exemption of Natural Persons from Debt’. This Article is therefore intended to be extended in accordance with the provisions of the draft law on the exemption of natural persons from debt. Article 7 of the related draft law entitled ‘Law on the Exemption of a Natural Person from Debt Liabilities’ contains a limitation on the application of the mechanism where the natural person is subject to the restrictions laid down in Article 130 of the Solvency Act. The restriction is set to deter... to prevent natural persons from using the use of a natural person's debt discharge in order to avoid the execution of an obligation in good faith. It should also be necessary to impose a similar restriction on the application of the insolvency proceedings of a natural person.
