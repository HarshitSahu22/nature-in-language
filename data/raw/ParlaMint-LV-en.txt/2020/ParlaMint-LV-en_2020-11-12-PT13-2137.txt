The aim of the draft law is to extend the payment of the sickness benefit from the state budget in cases where Covid-19 is found to be ill or quarantine is established. The draft law provides for a fixed-term procedure for paying sickness benefit and issuing a sick leave in the event of a person suffering from respiratory disease. This will motivate self-insulation of the person. These amendments are largely aimed at limiting the further spread of Covid-19. The Social and Labour Commission decided on the urgency of the draft law, so I ask you to first support the urgency of the draft law.
