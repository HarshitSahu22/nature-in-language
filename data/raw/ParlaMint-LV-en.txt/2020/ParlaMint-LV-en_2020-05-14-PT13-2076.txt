This new alternative version of the law defines more specifically the composition of the administrative offences. The wording of the alternative law significantly narrows the scope of administrative responsibility with regard to possible infringement of the property rights of holders of copyright or related rights. The alternative draft law also provides for the addition of Chapter XII ‘Administrative infringements in the field of copyright and related rights and competence in administrative infringement proceedings’, which would include two new articles containing editorially specified compositions of the administrative infringement, penalties and a reference to the competent authorities in the application of penalties. In particular, penalties are provided for the use of copyright or related rights in public execution without the consent or consideration of the subject of copyright or related rights; reproduction of copyright or related rights; non-payment of carrier's fees or charges for the reproduction of works; making the subject of copyright or related rights available to the public by wire or in any other way that it can be accessed in a place and at a time individually selected; retransmission of the subject of copyright or related rights without the consent or payment of the subject of copyright or related rights. The draft law provides that in the future (and it is a new norm in comparison with previous proposals), in the future administrative infringement proceedings on violations of copyright and related rights referred to in Article 72 of the law are carried out by the State Police, whose resources have learned how to do so, but have not so far performed this function directly. In view of this, we first call for the rejection of the draft law submitted by the Cabinet of Ministers (No. 591/Lp13) and support...
