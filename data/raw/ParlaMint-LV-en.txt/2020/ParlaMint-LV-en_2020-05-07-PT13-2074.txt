In its meeting today, the Commission for Social Affairs has examined and supported the first reading of the draft law on "Amendments to the Law on Social Services and Social Assistance". As stated in the explanatory memorandum, the draft law provides that: ‘(1) in a crisis situation no benefit shall be granted to an individual resident or for a person in a family who has been granted a severance grant or a severance grant in accordance with statutory provisions; (2) the municipality shall not increase the severance grant in a crisis situation or reduce it by the part concerned for the period during which a person who receives a severance grant or a severance grant is granted a severance grant in accordance with statutory provisions. At the same time, for a clear understanding of the Law on Social Services and Social Assistance, the draft law provides for clarification of the wording of the second sentence of the first paragraph of paragraph 37 of the transitional provisions of the law. The rules contained in the draft law will apply from the date of its entry into force.’ In essence, the draft law therefore provides that a person may not at the same time qualify for two benefits (i.e. benefits in the event of a crisis and a severance grant); it excludes this double funding and allows local authorities not to increase the amount of benefit in the event of a crisis by EUR 50 per month for each child if the person has already been granted it in the form of a supplement with a severance grant. In conclusion. The Commission on Social and Labour Affairs calls for urgency to be given to the draft law.

Draft law “Amendments to law on the functioning of state institutions during an emergency in relation to the spread of Covid-19”, first reading. Member of the Commission for Defence, Home Affairs and Prevention of Corruption Juris Rancāns.
ParlaMint-LV_2020-05-07-PT13-2074-U16	Good evening, colleagues! The draft law “Amendments to the Law “On Activities of State Institutions in the Period of Emergency Related to the Distribution of Covid-19”” provides that the implementation of administrative acts is not suspended with respect to the decision of the Administration of the Maintenance Guarantee Fund on forced enforcement. The draft law clarifies the civil procedural time limits and related issues during an emergency. The draft law also provides for an extension of the period for the application of educational measures to children and the limitation periods for their execution. In addition, it is stipulated that sworn lawyers may be granted leave for a period of 12 months on the basis of an emergency. It is also provided that if the nationals of a Member State of the European Union, of the European Economic Area or of the Swiss Confederation or of persons residing in those States have expired their validity, they may enter and leave Latvia using this document to return to their country of residence. It is foreseen that the Ministry of Defence has the right to dispose of personal protective equipment without an auction to the armed forces of the Allied States of Covid-19 to limit the spread of the Covid-19. It is also foreseen that the State Defence Military Objects and Procurement Centre has the right to purchase personal protective equipment and disinfectants to provide support to the countries most affected by Covid-19 without the application of the procurement procedure. The transfer of purchased funds to foreign countries and their transfer to institutions is organised by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The draft law also provides that those employees of the Ministry of the Interior and Prison Administration who had been affected by accidents and who had established a rehabilitation course that ends during an emergency are entitled to receive this course in full within three months of the end of the emergency. The draft law also specifies the conditions for remote discussion of the sustainable development strategy and development programmes by local authorities. Finally, the draft law also provides that the Administration of Legal Aid organises consultations to solve the problems caused by the emergency. The draft law sets out more detailed conditions for doing so. Within the Commission, the draft law has been endorsed conceptually. The Commission is asking for urgency to be established for this draft law.
