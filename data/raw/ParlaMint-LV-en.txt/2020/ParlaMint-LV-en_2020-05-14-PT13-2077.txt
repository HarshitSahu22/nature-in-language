On the agenda – draft law “Amendments to the Law “On Maternity and Sickness Insurance”, first reading. Member of the Commission for Social and Labour Affairs Andris Skride.
ParlaMint-LV_2020-05-14-PT13-2077-U14	Good evening, dear colleagues! The Committee on Social and Labour Affairs has today examined and unanimously supported the first reading draft law “Amendments to the Law on Maternity and Sickness Insurance”. The draft law presupposes... The amendments provide that the person to whom a sick leave has been issued in connection with a Covid-19 illness or quarantine disease will be granted and paid the sickness benefit from 22 March 2020 until 31 December 2020 for the period from the second day of the sick leave. To date, the statutory deadline was from 22 March to 30 June of this year. So it's extended. The amendment of the law also provides for the provision of assistance to those who have been granted a sick leave in connection with the Covid-19 or in connection with quarantine, and who have thus now exceeded the total amount of the sickness benefit prescribed by the law. The Act provides that until 31 December of this year, sickness benefit payments will not include those days of incapacity for work for which a person has been given a sick leave in connection with Covid-19 or in quarantine. As I said, the Commission unanimously supported and first called for urgency to be given to the draft law.
