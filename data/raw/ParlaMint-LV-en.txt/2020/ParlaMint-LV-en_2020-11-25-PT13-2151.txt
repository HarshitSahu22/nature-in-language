ParlaMint-LV_2020-11-25-PT13-2151-U2	Good evening, colleagues! The Social and Labour Commission has therefore examined and supported at first reading the draft law “Amendment to the law on maternity and sickness insurance”. And, as we know, as the community continues to spread, certain pre-school and general education institutions are also being shut down more and more, and as a result, many families with children under the age of 10 (the Cabinet of Ministers has established that one cannot leave a child by this age alone) or a child with disabilities under the age of 18, and in which it is impossible for parents to work remotely due to the specific nature of their work... This requires support for such families. Support is also needed for persons who are supporting a person with disabilities from the age of 18, who have been provided by the municipality with a day care centre or a day care centre service and who, due to conditions related to Covid-19 infection, cannot use a day care centre. The aim of the draft law is to introduce for a limited period of time a new form of support – a one-off sickness benefit – for 14 calendar days, which the socially insured person will be entitled to claim once during the period from 2 December 2020 to 31 December 2020 and once again during the period from 1 January 2021 to 30 June 2021 under certain conditions: one of the parents or one of the adoptives of the child, or a member of the foster family, or a guardian or another person who, in accordance with a decision of the orphan court, actually takes care of the child, if the child cannot work remotely and the child is under 10 years of age (including) or a child with a disability under 18 years of age, and one person who is a person with a disability under 18 years of age to whom the municipality has granted a daily care centre or day centre service. And this one-off sickness benefit will be paid in the amount of 60 per cent of the average salary of the claimant for the previous 12 calendar months. The allowance shall not be paid if the person is employed, pursues an economic activity and receives income or a sickness benefit, parental benefit, maternity benefit, paternity benefit or a severance grant. The benefit can be claimed no later than within 10 days from the date when the pre-school group or educational institution is closed or the training process is started remotely. The sickness benefit will be financed from the general budget of the State. The Commission for Social and Labour Affairs has therefore examined this draft law and called for its urgency to be determined first.
