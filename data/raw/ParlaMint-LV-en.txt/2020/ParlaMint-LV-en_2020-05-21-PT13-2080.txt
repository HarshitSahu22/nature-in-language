The members of the Commission have unanimously supported the draft decision and the draft decision states that the decision will enter into force on 16 June this year. The mandate of the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of the Republic of Latvia Ivars Bickovics will end on June 15, this year. According to the law “On Judicial Power”, the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court from among judges of the Supreme Court is confirmed by the Saeima for five years following the recommendation of the Plenary Session of the Supreme Court. The Plenary Session of the Supreme Court on 20 April of this year decided to propose that the Saeima approve Aigars Strupiša as the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court. Since 2014, Aigars Strupišs has been a judge of the Supreme Court. He was a member of the Disciplinary Court from 2014 to 2018, including its President from 2015 to 2018. Since 2018 is a member of the Council for the Judiciary, as well as the Chairman of the Department of Civil Cases of the Supreme Court. Until the position of the Supreme Court judge Aigars Strupišs has had more than 20 years of experience in academic work, as well as for more than 25 years he has worked in private practice in the field of commercial law. He has participated in drafting several laws, advised international organisations, and participated in important international projects. Aigars Strupišs is also the author of many legal publications. On Tuesday, the members of the Legal Commission and the Subcommittee on Judicial Policy heard Mr Strupišs about the intended vision of development of the Supreme Court, as well as discussed issues related to strengthening public trust in the judiciary. And therefore, since the unanimous candidate was approved in the sitting of the Commission, I call on the Legal Commission to approve Aigars Strupitis as the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court.
