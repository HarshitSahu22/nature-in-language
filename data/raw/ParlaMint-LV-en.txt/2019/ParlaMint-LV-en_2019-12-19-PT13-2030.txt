ParlaMint-LV_2019-12-19-PT13-2030-U134	We work with the draft law “State Border Guard Law”. A new law is needed because the existing one is outdated. It has been in force for more than 20 years, amended 18 times and lost six chapters from its original volume. Thus, it is necessary to update the law by improving the legal framework of the border guard in accordance with modern needs and consistency with the requirements of other regulatory enactments. The terminology used in the Border Guard Law has become obsolete and differs from the terminology used in European Union legislation, for example the Schengen Borders Code, and this creates inconsistencies in the application of the provisions of the Border Guard Law. The differences in terminology also exist between the Border Guard Law and the State Border Law of the Republic of Latvia. The implementation of the European Union framework and the implementation of the Frontex Regulation are also foreseen in the context of the activities of the European Border and Coast Guard Agency. The new draft law establishes that there should be compatibility of the border guard with the National Armed Forces in the field of equipment, training and procedures. It also introduces the possibility for the border guard to be appointed as a professional service soldier. A precise definition of the border guard, the introduction of minimum requirements for professional training appropriate to the border guard and the position to be filled, as well as the establishment of military training in border guard. Also, the Institute of the oath of the border guard has been restored, bringing the oath to the oath of the soldier, the ban on border guards to unite in trade unions and participate in strikes has been lifted. The draft law provides for changes in the competence of the border guard. It is established that the border guard will have the right to verify the identity of the person and whether the person has the right to stay in Latvia, as well as to remove invalid travel documents in the whole territory of Latvia. The right of access to ownership of the specified border guard has been established, as well as the right of the border guard to persecute, suspend, inspect and detain vessels in the waters of the Republic of Latvia in accordance with Article 111 of the UN Convention on the Law of the Sea, also outside the waters of the Republic of Latvia. The conditions for the use of firearms and special means as laid down in the Border Guard Law are specified, allowing the possibility to respond also in the case of unmanned aircraft obstructing the performance of the functions of border guards. The fact that the border guard is and will remain the subject of the operational action and the investigative authority shall not be altered. The Commission has examined and supported the draft law at its meeting on 3 December this year. On behalf of the Commission, I call for support for the draft law at first reading.
