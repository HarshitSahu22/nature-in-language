The first chapter includes general rules defining the meaning and purpose of the law, as well as introducing new terms for Latvian legislation – “diaspora”, “remigration”, “remigrant”, “diaspora policy” and “diaspora organisation”. These new terms have been introduced both in order to promote a better understanding of the legal provisions and to harmonise the terminology used, and in order to introduce terminology that is consistent with practice and actual application. Scope of the law. ( The head of the sitting was knocked down with a hammer.) The general provisions also include a detailed explanation of the scope of the law. The law defines the role of the Diaspora in the state and legal system of Latvia, the basic principles of cooperation, the tasks and directions of the policy of the Diaspora, the ways of participation of the Diaspora in decision-making and public life, as well as the persons involved in the implementation of the policy of the Diaspora and their competence in the policy sector of the Diaspora. And there is a clear purpose of the law. In this section we also define the nature of the law, the aim - to create a system and unified framework for the implementation of the policy of the Diaspora in order to provide opportunities for people living abroad to build, maintain and expand ties with Latvia, as well as to promote the preservation of the Latvian language, culture and sense of belonging to Latvia in the Diaspora while ensuring favourable conditions for cooperation and re-migration. It has been defined that the Diaspora is an integral part of Latvian society, and that the purpose of the Diaspora Law is to ensure that the Diaspora can freely build, maintain and expand links with Latvia, as well as that the State has a duty to develop and implement a consistent policy aimed at the Diaspora. The second chapter of the draft law deals with the policy and governance rules of the Diaspora, defining the main tasks of the policy of the Diaspora and the responsibilities of the state institutions and local authorities in the implementation of the policy of the Diaspora. Within the framework of this draft law, the policy tasks of the Diaspora cover aspects such as support of the Diaspora initiatives, access to and acquisition of Latvian language and culture, strengthening of the Latvian identity, support of the Diaspora participation, protection of the rights of the Diaspora, as well as access to Latvian art, culture and music and other aspects. The draft law also defines those fundamental principles which must be respected in the design and implementation of the policy of the Diaspora. Principles such as the principle of participation, the principle of access to information, the principle of equal opportunities and others are mentioned here. In the field of Diaspora policy, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs has established in the draft law the leading state administration institution, the tasks of which will include drafting planning documents, preparation of requests for the implementation of the policy of the State budget financing for the Diaspora policy, coordination of the activities of institutions involved in the implementation of the policy of the Diaspora. According to the competence established in the draft law, a number of state administrations – Ministry of Economics, Ministry of Education and Science, Ministry of Culture, Ministry of Environmental Protection and Regional Development, Ministry of Welfare, Ministry of the Interior and Ministry of Health – are involved in planning and implementing policy. The Cabinet of Ministers will also have a role to play in order to perform, for example, the integration of professionals with international experience into the Latvian state service, as well as opportunities to join the old-age pension insurance. The draft law lays down an obligation for all public administrations to assess the impact of relevant sectoral policies on the Diaspora and to ensure timely involvement of the Diaspora organisations in the discussion of decisions affecting the Diaspora policy. The second chapter of the draft law provides for the establishment of a Diaspora Advisory Council aimed at promoting a coherent development of the policy of the Diaspora and its annual priorities, and the follow-up and evaluation of the policy of the Diaspora. The third chapter deals with measures to support the Diaspora. Serious work has been invested to ensure that the law includes a set of specific activities that will be able to help address issues relevant to the Diaspora in areas such as the recognition of educational documents, qualifications and regulated professions, residence registration or child registration in a kindergarten or school, the maintenance of an unlimited minimum, and others. There are also specific measures to promote and support re-migration for those who wish to return to Latvia. In the field of education and culture, support is provided for the operation of the Diaspora weekend schools and professional development of the Diaspora teachers. The inclusion of support measures in the law will ensure sustainability and funding for essential educational activities - summer camps, schools. An exchange programme will be created that will allow children and young people of the Diaspora to study in schools in Latvia for a certain period of time or to study at higher education institutions in Latvia. The involvement of entrepreneurs and professionals of Latvian origin in the development of Latvia's economy and economy will be encouraged by setting a framework for working with dividends in the field of export, innovation, investment and tourism promotion. In the area of promoting the civil and political participation of Diaspora, there is state support for strengthening it, including the necessary legislative changes... the availability of national information and e-services. In the field of re-migration support, the state and local authorities have a duty to support the reunification of the Latvian people by taking measures that support re-migration and facilitate the return or transfer of representatives of the Diaspora to permanent life in Latvia. Support measures have been established that facilitate the movement of representatives of the Diaspora to Latvia, as an opportunity to register children at pre-school and general education institutions in a timely manner even before returning to Latvia. There is also an obligation to provide support for the development of Latvian language skills of the Diaspora representatives who have moved to Latvia in order to improve their ability to integrate into the labour market. The draft law also includes activities in the field of recognition of regulated professions, establishing that it is necessary to review and improve simplified procedures for education, academic degrees and professional qualifications acquired abroad, including regulated professions, simplified alignment of documents and recognition of professional qualifications, practice in the relevant sector or work in higher education institutions in Latvia.
