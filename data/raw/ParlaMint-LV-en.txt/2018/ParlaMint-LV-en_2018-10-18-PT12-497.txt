It is also foreseen that the same person will be able to hold no more than two consecutive terms of office. Currently, the Chief of Service is appointed by the Prosecutor General for four years, but this situation will, of course, change as a result of changes. In addition, the procedure for the Advisory Council of the Control Service has been changed - it will no longer be called and will not be led by the Prosecutor General, but by the Chief of the Control Service. The draft law also clarifies the responsibilities and rights of the service in order to better identify, evaluate and mitigate the risks of money laundering and terrorist financing at our own national and European Union level.
