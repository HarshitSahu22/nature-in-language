Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, thank you very much for the floor, and I would like to reiterate very briefly what proposal we will now discuss and vote on. The proposal seeks a comprehensive system of legal anchoring of new components and technologies in the field of electronicisation in the health sector, as legislation is currently very fragmented, there are only some elements misleading electronicisation in the health sector. The proposed bill brings a minimalized but robust base of e-health, including its basic infrastructure, but does not in any way limit the future development of e-health services. They will ensure that there is so much real-time existence available and correct data on health service providers, health workers and patients, enabling them to be identified. The draft law is intended to contribute in many ways to improving the functioning of the health services system, especially the currently affected pandemic disease COVID-19, which has shown us how necessary it is to share data within the Czech health care system, and since we actually have experience here, I think that electronization is the only right way. I would now like to comment on the amendments that have been applied. I would like to begin by saying that the registration proposal contained in the resolution of the Committee on Health No. 371/2021, it is a registration, marked A, I fully support. As regards Mr Třešňák's amendment, which is marked by letter B, I would like to mention the following. This amendment reduces the period for the entry into force of the obligation of health service providers and social services providers providing health services under the Health Services Act without authorisation to comply with eHealth standards, from 10 years to five years, that is to say, it reduces the transitional period for adaptation to new standards under the Act on Electronicisation of Health Services, so that they should take effect on 1 January 2026. In principle, in the original government proposal, we proposed those 10 years on the basis of a debate with various stakeholders in health care affected, i.e. entities, representatives of providers who sought this legisvacation period of 10 years. On the other hand, I understand that there is an effort for some shortening, so I will have a neutral position on this amendment, and I will leave it to the House's decision to deal with this amendment. Thank you for your attention.
