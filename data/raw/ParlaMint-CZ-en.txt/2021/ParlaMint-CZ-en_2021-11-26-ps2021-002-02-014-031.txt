ParlaMint-CZ_2021-11-26-ps2021-002-02-014-031.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Dear Members of the Government, ladies and gentlemen, allow me, on behalf of the President, to make this point. We are to discuss the proposal, which follows mainly from Section 85 of Act No. 247/2000 Sb. , on the elections to the Parliament of the Czech Republic. [[Correct No. 247/1995 Coll.]] Under this provision, the Chamber of Deputies is obliged to communicate to the Ministry of Finance the number of valid votes cast for political parties, political movements or coalitions. The report shall be sent for the purpose of paying a contribution to the reimbursement of electoral costs. As has already been said, you have put a resolution of the mandate and immunity committee on the table, where it is proposed to adopt the House resolution plus the numbers of votes and the number of mandates. So I would like to make a motion for a resolution from the House as proposed by the mandate and immune committee at its third meeting on 23 November: "To a proposal to cover electoral costs to political parties, political movements or coalitions for elections to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic in 2021 Chamber of Deputies I. informs the Ministry of Finance pursuant to § 85 of Act No. 247/1995 Coll., on elections to the Parliament of the Czech Republic, as amended by the amendments, the number of valid votes cast for individual political parties, political movements and coalitions held in elections to the Chamber of Deputies in 2021 and the number of seats of Members in individual political parties and movements; II. Recommends that the Ministry of Finance should pay the electoral contribution to political parties, movements and coalitions under the provisions of Section 85 of the Act cited.' Thank you.
