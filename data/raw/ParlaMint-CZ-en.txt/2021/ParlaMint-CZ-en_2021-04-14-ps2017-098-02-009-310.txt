ParlaMint-CZ_2021-04-14-ps2017-098-02-009-310.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me present to you a proposal to amend Act No. 438/2020 Coll., on the adjustments to the provision of the infirmary in connection with emergency measures during the epidemic and on the amendment of Act No. 187/2006 Coll., on sickness insurance, as amended, which I submit together with my colleagues Kateřina Valachová, Hana Aulicka Jírovcová, Věra Kovářová, Pavla Golasowská, Olga Richterová and others. The purpose of our proposal is to allow entitlement to the infirmary at the time of the effectiveness of emergency measures in the Coronavirus, including the closure of certain educational establishments, in particular parts of primary and kindergarten schools, in the care of children under 10 years of age, and their relatives in the direct or secondary line, under Section 772 of the Civil Code, even if they do not live together in one common household, provided that these relatives are properly insured employees. Technically, these are changes and adjustments in the three provisions of this Act, namely in its paragraphs 3 and 5, and proof of compliance with the condition of a relative relationship with a child for the creation of a claim to the infirmary will be demonstrated by the applicant against the Czech Social Security Administration by an honest statement. At the same time, we propose that for those to whom the submitted legislation allows this treatment to be re-used, the date on which the need for child care arises should be considered as the date on which the amendment of the law becomes effective, if the need for this care continues at the same time on that day, and given the necessity and urgency of the measure presented, we also propose the effectiveness of the law in the proposed version on the date of its publication. That is why we also propose that the Chamber of Deputies agree with the bill in the first reading under Section 90 para. 2 of Act No. 90/95 Coll., on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, due to the urgent need to urgently address the issues of increasing financial security for low-income parents, especially for single parents, who are now forced to receive only low medical care in the long term, and also, for example, due to the highly social need to strengthen their health care status, which is now insufficient even as a result of the fact that many health professionals are now forced to take care of their young children in the long term. This is what we want to change with our proposal and enable parents to choose who will take care of their children at the time of the closure of school facilities, even with regard to the amount of income of parents and relatives of the child and other criteria that are important for decision-making of this type. The proposed law is in line with the constitutional order of the Czech Republic, it also fully respects the Charter of Fundamental Rights and Freedoms and also complies with the legislation of the European Union, as evidenced by the opinion of the Parliamentary Institute on our proposal. We reasonably assume that the proposed legislation will act neutrally in relation to the state budget, or that it will not significantly burden it against the current state, and it can also be assumed that this infirmary will often be drawn by the family member whose employment income and income decline is lower, and that therefore there can also be a reduction in the entitlement to state budget expenditure. The proposal will also have significant positive social impacts, as it will help prevent many people from falling into social welfare systems, debt traps or executions and insolvencys. Ladies and gentlemen, I beg you to support this proposal. Thank you.
