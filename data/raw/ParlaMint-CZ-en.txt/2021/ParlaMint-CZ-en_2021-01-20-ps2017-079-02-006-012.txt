ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-20-ps2017-079-02-006-012.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, before second reading, I propose a proposal for a law on the implementation of special regimes in the field of foreign trade, which adapts two directly applicable Union regulations, in its up-to-date version concerning the protection against the effects of legislation adopted by certain third countries, or the so-called blocking regulation. The bill was discussed last year by the Guarantee Economic Committee, also by the Foreign Committee, and no amendments were tabled which would have an impact on the substance of the whole proposal. All the questions raised during the committee's deliberations were answered and both committees recommended that they give their consent to this government proposal. First, the indisputable benefit of the European regulation adapting the bill is the limitation of the possibility of financing armed groups of security forces involved in trade in selected raw materials in areas destabilized by local conflicts or fulfilling the characteristics of so-called collapsed states, which are often at the heart of various security threats, including in the international field, also for our country. Secondly, the law will ensure that the effects of US sanctions on the interest of EU economic operators legally engaged with third countries are mitigated. I therefore believe that the law, which has the said regulations in itself and which follows them, will contribute a great deal to strengthening the security of our country. Ladies and gentlemen, I would therefore like to ask you to support this proposal. Thank you very much.
