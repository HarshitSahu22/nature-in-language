ParlaMint-CZ_2021-05-05-ps2017-101-01-003-003.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the Government's bill amending the law on recovery procedures and resolution of the crisis in the financial market. As has already been said at the first reading of the proposal, the reason for the proposal is the implementation of the amendment to the directive establishing a framework for the recovery and resolution of credit institutions and investment firms known under the abbreviation BRRD. The aim of the amendment to this Directive, and thus the proposed amendments to the law, is to further strengthen the effectiveness of the regulatory framework for crisis management and the resilience of regulated entities to possible economic shocks. In fact, the proposal brings changes mainly in capital requirements and eligible liabilities, restrictions on the sale of subordinated eligible liabilities to retail customers or the extension of the powers of the Czech National Bank to declare a moratorium on liabilities already in the previous crisis management phase. [[15.10 hours]] Finally, let me point out that the date from which the effective national legislation implementing the Directive was to be implemented, which was set at 28. 12. 2020 has passed. Thank you for shifting the proposal in the legislative process, because otherwise the Czech Republic is in danger of being punished for failing to comply with the obligations arising from membership of the European Union. Thank you for your attention.
