ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-27-ps2017-079-05-017-328.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, thank you. I'm submitting a contract paper today. This is historically the first double taxation treaty with Senegal, as the President has already said, signed on 22 January 2020 in Dakar. The conclusion of this international tax treaty follows the intention to limit the tax burden on taxpayers when excluding undesirable international double taxation, which normally occurs without the existence of a tax contract in international economic relations. The Double Taxation Treaty, which is currently being proposed in relations between the Czech Republic and Senegal, was prepared on the basis of OECD and UN model models, and, of course, also responds to the internal tax rules of both countries. It can be concluded that the text of the contract fully reflects developments and trends in the international tax field. This tax agreement will enable the coordination of the activities of the tax authorities of both States to limit possible tax evasion and, last but not least, will also enable the relevant exchange of information, in accordance with all existing international standards. The negotiation of this tax agreement will increase the legal certainty of potential investors in both states. Its closure and implementation will not have a direct impact on the state budget. The overall contribution resulting from the application of this Treaty will depend in practice on the future development of the various forms of economic cooperation. In this context, it can be noted that the balance of mutual trade is significantly in favour of our country due to relatively low imports from Senegal. The turnover of trade between the Czech Republic and Senegal amounted to less than $18 million in 2019. The double taxation treaty between the Czech Republic and Senegal will certainly open a space for Czech business entities to help develop mutual economic relations, including investment activities. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask for consent to its ratification. Thank you.
