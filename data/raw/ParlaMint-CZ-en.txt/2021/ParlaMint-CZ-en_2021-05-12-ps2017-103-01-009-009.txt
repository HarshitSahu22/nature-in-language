ParlaMint-CZ_2021-05-12-ps2017-103-01-009-009.u4	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Yeah, third time's the charm. Dear colleagues and colleagues, I may be a little bit longer, but in the same sense. The government is presented with a bill amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the law on the protection of notifiers, which is the previous press number 1150. This law makes amendments to Act No. 99/1963, Civil Code of Procedure, as amended, in particular Act No. 2/1969 Coll., on the establishment of ministries and other central bodies of the state administration of the Czech Republic, as amended, Act No. 221/1999 Coll., on Occupational Soldiers, as amended, Act No. 361/2003 Coll., on the Service of Security Corps Members, as amended, Act No. 251/2005 Coll., on Labour Inspection, as amended, Act No. 253/2008 Coll., on certain measures against the legalization of proceeds from crime and terrorist financing, as amended, and Act No. 234/2014 Coll., on Civil Service, as amended. At the same time, Government Regulation No 1 is repealed. 145/2015 Coll., on measures relating to the notification of suspected infringements in the Service Office. The proposed proposal is a response to the directive of the European Parliament and of the Council of the EU, which has already been mentioned here, the 2019/1937 of 23 October 2019, on the protection of persons who report infringements of Union law and aim to add missing elements of the protection of notifiers to the Czech rule of law and transform the directive accordingly. The proposed legislation aims to ensure the effective functioning and enforcement of the regulation provided by the draft law on the protection of notifiers, thus guaranteeing the notifiers, within the meaning of the Act on the protection of notifiers, certain protection against retaliatory measures and to offset their employment status in possible litigations. That's why it's reflected in so many laws. The aforementioned directive obliges Member States to bring it into their legal systems, and since the proposal for implementation of the aforementioned directive is tabled in our legal order, it is one of the first ones and this implementation is an obligation for Member States, I also recommend that the proposal submitted be supported in the discussion.
