ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-27-ps2017-079-05-031-063.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, back to the MIT. And let me introduce you to the government's proposal for an amendment to the Act concerning state-supported insurance and export financing. Before I present the Government's proposal, I would like to point out that the current situation brought about by a covid with the expected enormous impact on the economy led to the lightning amendment of this law in April and enabled the export insurance company, i.e. EGAP, to provide guarantees. This was a fundamental change, i.e. not only insurance, but also guarantees for repayment of loans from producers, manufacturing, business companies, etc., which is a success. This product is used, it is used by larger companies over 250 employees, while the smaller ones have guarantees of ČMZRB. The amendment also included a long-term upcoming step, namely the transfer of most ČEB and EGAP funds to the central liquidity management system of the general Treasury accounts, including the need to amend budgetary rules. The amendment was discussed in a state of legislative emergency. So we are now going back to the proposal, which is the result of really long-term negotiations on the form of a system of state-supported insurance and export financing. The aim is to improve the performance, competitiveness of our exporters. to enable investment in innovation and, at the same time, to stand up to foreign markets. So what is the content? The list of insurance and reinsurance instruments provided by the Export Insurance Corporation as well as the instruments supported by the financing of the Export Bank is transferred from law to the implementing legislation and consequently the approval procedures for all newly introduced export bank products and export insurance companies are regulated in the law. The existing condition of ownership of at least two thirds of the shares of the export bank directly by the State is hereby repealed, which will enable the export bank to consolidate as a 100% subsidiary of the export company, EGAP export insurance company. After experience - for others - with the effects of unsuccessful trades from past years, which is simply a fact, especially from the turn of 2010, to public finances, the implementation of individual business cases is subject to the Government's consent depending on the amount of export credit risk insurance rather than the existing limits linked to the percentage of EGAP's insurance capacity, or export insurance companies. The same process of approval of business cases is introduced for the Export Bank. The control powers of the Ministry of Finance in relation to the management of the Export Insurance Corporation are specified by analogy to the existing definition of the control power of the Ministry in relation to the management of the Export Bank. The possibility of exporting insurance companies and export banks to set up legal entities or to acquire shares in legal entities for the purpose of recovering claims or preventing damage - an important thing - and it must be the same after the prior approval of the Ministry of Finance. The annual binding of 250 million in the State budget on the levelling of interest differences is removed and replaced by more general provision of advances on the mechanism for matching those differences. And then the procedure for granting subsidies from the state budget to the Export Bank to cover losses from the operation of the supported financing is further clarified. So, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to support this material both at today's meeting and during the next legislative hearing. Thank you very much.
