ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-018-146.u1	146. 1. supplement to the participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and the participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the territory of the Czech Republic in 2021 /German press 1208/ I ask the Minister for an opening word.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-018-146.u2	Thank you for the floor. The material contains information on the completion of two exercises of the armed forces of the Czech Republic outside the Czech Republic and specifying information about 43 exercises already approved by the government, of which 33 exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and 10 exercises in the Czech Republic, where some attributes exceeding the approved framework were changed during their preparation. These are in particular terms, numbers of assigned forces and resources, places and number of days. The above changes in the training plan with foreign partners reflect particularly the emergency measures taken in relation to the occurrence of pandemics, the requirements of foreign partners in the field of preparation for foreign operations, including the construction of the ability to achieve alliance standards and the current needs of the Czech Army. Once again, a detailed list of changes is set out in the Annex to this material. The exercises referred to in the 1st Supplement will be financed under the approved budget of the Ministry of Defence chapter for 2021. Additional requirements for funds in the field of foreign training are not created. Thank you for your attention.
