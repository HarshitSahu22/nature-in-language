ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-012-118.u1	118. Information on participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the territory of the Czech Republic during the period January to June 2020 /German press 996/ I ask the Minister for an opening word.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-012-118.u2	Thank you for the floor. During that period, military exercises were carried out to the extent approved by the Government of the Czech Republic. Units of the armed forces of the Czech Republic took part in 11 exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and three exercises with foreign partners in the territory of the Czech Republic. The main tasks of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in training with foreign partners in the first half of 2020 were the preparation of deployment units in operations and into standby forces, as well as the construction and development of capabilities to meet NATO and EU collective defence commitments. Due to exceptional measures in relation to COVID-19 disease, most of the originally planned exercises were cancelled. The military exercises, which will eventually take place with changed deadlines, are set out in the 1st and 2nd addition to the 2020 exercise plan, which has already been, or will be, submitted to the Government for approval. The total amount needed to carry out these exercises was CZK 7.2 million. A detailed overview of the exercises for the period from January to June 2020 is given in the Annex to this material. Thank you for your attention.
