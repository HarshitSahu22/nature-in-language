ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-019-152.u2	Thank you for the floor. The main content of the material is information on the political, military and financial aspects of the activities of Czech troops and soldiers in 2020 in Afghanistan, Iraq, Mali, Baltic, Sinai Peninsula, Golan Heights, Kosovo, Bosnia and Herzegovina and the headquarters of European Union maritime operations in Spain and Italy. The material also includes information on rapid impact projects that were implemented only in Mali in 2020, and information on the involvement of the Czech Republic in UN missions in 2020. Compared to previous years, the activities of our soldiers in foreign operations and their preparation affected three basic factors. Reduction of the Resolute Support mission in Afghanistan, command of the EU Training Mission forces in Mali, and impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on all foreign operations involving Czech participation. Finally, I would say that 1 032 members of the Ministry of Defence took turns in foreign operations. We spent CZK 972 million on their work from the budget chapter. I cannot remember when I regret to say and to say that the death of a member of the Army of the Czech Republic in the mission of multinational forces and observers in Sinai took place in 2020. Thank you for your attention.
