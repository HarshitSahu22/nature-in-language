ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-15-ps2017-118-02-003-022.u4	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I consider this bill to be very smooth, but I would like to draw attention to one thing that has taken place on the constitutionally-legal committee. The biggest discussion was on one provision, which was then recommended by the Constitutional and Legal Committee to withdraw from the bill given by the Ministry of the Interior. This is the case where the Ministry of Interior proposed that, at the request of the police authority, the municipal police or other administrative body which made the notification of the offence pursuant to § 73, the competent administrative authority inform it without undue delay of the way the case was handled. The Constitutional Legal Committee recommended that this provision be deleted because it was - I would say simply - toothless. The administrative authority is bound by secrecy and could only tell the police or the municipal police that the offence has been committed, it has been decided, but not against which person. Then the information value of the information thus submitted would be zero. In addition, it said that any administrative authority declaring an offence could request a communication as decided in the offence. You know, police and military violations are also being reported, and that would mean that anyone who reported an officer or a soldier's offence could then ask for a statement as to whether the offence has been resolved or how. Therefore, the constitutionally-legal committee recommended in its resolution that this provision of the law be deleted. That's all I wanted to inform you about. Thank you.
