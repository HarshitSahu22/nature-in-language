ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-26-ps2017-079-04-003-042.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the reason for the proposed amendment to the Act on Metrology - I believe that the non-conflict law - is the transposition of the Commission directive adapting to so-called technical progress, the Annex to the Council Directive. The existing definitions of basic units no longer correspond to technical progress in the field of measurement and therefore the new definitions reflecting the latest developments in measurement science were adopted at the 26th session of the General Conference on Weights and Measures in 2018 and subsequently the said Directive was adopted. In the legal order of our country, the definitions of basic units of SI are contained in the law on metrology, and it is therefore necessary to bring these definitions into line with the new definitions. Since this draft law is a transposition regulation with a short deadline for transposition and in fact only parametric changes without the possibility to deviate, I therefore propose that this material be approved at first reading under Section 90 of Act No. 90/1995 Coll., on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, as amended. Finally, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to give your consent to this proposal. Thank you.
