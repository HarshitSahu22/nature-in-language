Government bill amending Act No. 312/2002 Coll., on officials of local authorities and on the amendment of certain laws, as amended, and other related laws /house printing 641/ - first reading On behalf of the Government, the proposal submitted by the Prime Minister and the Minister of the Interior will be given by Jan Hamáček, to whom I give the floor. Please.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-27-ps2017-079-05-025-057.u2	Thank you. Mr Vice-President, fellow Members, I would like to present, very briefly, the Government's proposal for an amendment to the Act on Officials of Territorial Self-government and other related laws. The main objective is the education of civil servants of municipalities and regions. We are trying to respond to developments that have taken place in this area since 2003 and to knowledge from application practice. This means: in particular, we are dealing with a greater focus of ongoing training on the expertise of officials, to reduce the scope of compulsory education of an official from 18 working days within three years to 9, to introduce the possibility of obtaining specific competence also to persons who are not civil servants, to make the system of accreditation of educational institutions and educational programmes more effective. The proposal also includes amendments to the laws on social services and on the social and legal protection of children. Thank you.

ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-27-ps2017-079-05-025-057.u4	Thank you for the floor. Dear citizens, the quality of the services provided by the state depends on two areas: what the organizational systems to provide the service look like, that is, standards, roles, processes, finance, etc., and the competences of the workers who keep the system running. This amendment is, of course, focused on the quality of the education of officials, so the second issue. They are primarily officials of the local authorities. The aim is therefore to ensure that officials have quality education, one that enables them to have competence for providing the best possible services. However, this amendment is rather a marginal modification. In general, it deals with the refinement of certain parts of the law governing, for example, the form of initial education, also opening up the possibility of obtaining special professional qualifications for non-officials. It monitors the efficiency of the work of the Ministry of Interior, especially in the area of accreditation. On the contrary, what is not addressed is the scope of the training courses themselves, diversification or individualisation in the education of officials of self-government. However, this is a positive shift. Thank you.
