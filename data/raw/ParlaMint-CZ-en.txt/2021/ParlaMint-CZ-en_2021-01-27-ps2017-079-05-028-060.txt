Government bill amending Act No. 250/2016 Coll., on liability for and management of offences, as amended, and some other laws /house print 670/ - first reading On behalf of the Government, the proposal submitted by the Prime Minister and the Minister of the Interior will be given by Jan Hamáček, to whom I give the floor. ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-27-ps2017-079-05-028-060.u2	Thank you. This time it is a government bill amending the law on liability for offences and their proceedings and some other laws. In this proposal, we take into account our experience and respond to the needs of application practice, and we also mitigate the qualification requirements for authorised officials who conduct infringement proceedings. Furthermore, we propose to abolish the obligation to process the review of offences, anchoring the protection of privacy and the security of persons acting in infringement proceedings. We are now introducing the substance of the offence, which affects the unauthorized handling of pyrotechnics in connection with the event of sporting events. We are introducing the possibility of imposing restrictive measures in relation to drug offences. There is also a new factual nature of the offence aimed at non-compliance with the restrictive measures imposed in the infringement proceedings. This, in particular, is in relation to the punishment of so-called spectator violence and drug offences. So much for me. Thank you.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-27-ps2017-079-05-028-060.u3	Thank you and I would like the rapporteur for first reading, Mrs Zuzana Ožanová, to take the floor. Please, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-27-ps2017-079-05-028-060.u4	Hello, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen. The Minister of the Interior has already said most of the things. Anyway, I'd like to recapitulate a little bit. The law has been valid for two years and its effectiveness has been evaluated. It has been found that some of the problems that have arisen need to be addressed. In particular, it is a reduction of the qualification requirements for authorised officials, a repeal of the obligation to process the review of offences, protection of privacy and security of persons acting in the infringement proceedings and appropriate procedural protection of vulnerable victims in this proceeding, and an increase in the effectiveness of restrictive measures, removal of barriers to mutual communication between administrations. There has also been an addition of legislation in relation to the use of pyrotechnics. In addition to these amendments, the jurisdiction of the municipal authority of the municipality of the basic type to deal with offences against order in the state administration committed by breach of the obligation laid down in the municipal or regional regulation is then enshrined. Thank you for your attention.
