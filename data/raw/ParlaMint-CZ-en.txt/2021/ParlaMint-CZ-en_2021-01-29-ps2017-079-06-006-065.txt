ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-29-ps2017-079-06-006-065.u2	It is a government bill amending the law on international judicial cooperation in criminal matters. Its main objective is to submit a proposal responding to the adoption of the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council of 2018/1727 of 14 November 2018 on the European Union Agency for Judicial Cooperation in Criminal Matters Eurojust and replacing and repealing Council Decision 2002/187/JHA establishing the European Union Agency for Judicial Cooperation in Criminal Matters as the legal successor of the European Judicial Cooperation Unit. Since the Eurojust Regulation brings a completely new comprehensive regulation, which is directly applicable to the Czech Republic, it takes precedence over national legislation, it is necessary to adapt the law on international judicial cooperation in criminal matters, in particular the provisions which are already contained in the regulation, from the law. The proposal is further aimed at unifying the procedures in international judicial cooperation in criminal matters and eliminating the shortcomings identified in the evaluation of the current application of this legislation. A number of measures are therefore proposed to ensure that the procedures in international judicial cooperation fully correspond to the procedures used in practice and to clarify certain provisions so that their interpretation is unambiguous and does not cause application difficulties. In this regard, it is proposed, in particular, to provide for the jurisdiction of the court to perform international cooperation in the preparatory procedure, to adapt the procedure for simplified transmission and extradition, to provide for the reimbursement of costs of international judicial cooperation, to specify the obligation to obtain or submit a translation of a document, including exceptions to this obligation and to carry out the takeover of persons from a foreign State or another Member State, or to transfer such person to another Member State. I guess that's it for the introduction. I thank you for your attention and please support this law.
