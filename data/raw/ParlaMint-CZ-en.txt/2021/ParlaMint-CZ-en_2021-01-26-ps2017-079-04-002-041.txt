ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-26-ps2017-079-04-002-041.u4	Thank you for the floor, Mr President, dear colleagues. The aim of the proposal, as the Minister has already mentioned, is to align national arrangements with European Regulation No 1143/2014 on prevention and regulation of the introduction or introduction and spread of invasive non-originating species. The proposal simultaneously corrects the adaptation deficit of Council Regulation No 708/2007 on the use of foreign, locally absent species in aquaculture. The proposed proposal should contribute to preventing the introduction and introduction into nature and landscape of invasive non-originating species, as well as to ensuring regulation and mitigation of the adverse effects of invasive and non-originating plant and animal species on biodiversity and related ecosystem services. There are currently around 12 000 non-originating plants and animals in the territory of the European Union, of which approximately 10 to 15 percent are associated with adverse effects on nature. In the territory of the Czech Republic it is one of the most famous, as has already been mentioned here, for example Bolshevník the magnificent or rak red. In accordance with the Regulation, non-originating species are defined as organisms which have been introduced or planted outside their natural area. An invasive non-originating species shall be regarded as being of a non-originating species for which it has been found that its introduction or introduction or introduction or spread endangers biodiversity and associated ecosystem services or has an adverse impact on them. The regulation will bring a new agenda, which will require communication with the European Commission at the central level in relation to mandatory reporting, cross-border impact cases, ensuring the processing of expert analyses and supporting documents. The proposed procedures and measures will make it possible to reduce future costs related to further expansion of invasive non-originating species, including impacts on biodiversity and ecosystem links, which, in my view, are often difficult to quantify. The proposed proposal also includes the substantive amendment of the Act on the Protection of Animals against Abuse, Forest Law, Water Act, Hunting Act, Fishing Act and finally the Act on Plant Health Care. For this reason, I also recommend discussing this press in the Committee on Agriculture. Finally, allow me to propose to release the Government's bill to second reading and, at the same time, in view of the transposition deadlines, I would like to propose a reduction in the deadline for the negotiation of this Act by 30 days. Thank you for your attention.
