ParlaMint-CZ_2021-03-09-ps2017-087-05-003-039.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce to you the bill amending Act No. 235/2004 Coll., on Value Added Tax, as amended, and Act No. 242/2016 Coll., Customs Act, as amended. Within the EU, the rules of the single digital market are gradually being modernised and barriers to distance selling and cross-border provision of services are being removed. In addition to strengthening consumer rights, new rules are also being introduced to ensure a more transparent, fairer and predictable online environment for businesses. There are also adjustments in the area of value added tax aimed at facilitating cross-border trade, ensuring fair competition between EU businesses and combating tax evasion. The draft law is submitted to ensure the implementation of new European legislation on cross-border e-commerce, which includes the following fundamental changes: The extension of the scope of transactions to which a special scheme of one administrative place may be applied, i.e. the extension of the possibility to fulfil VAT obligations only to the tax authorities in one Member State instead of the need to register and fulfil those obligations vis-à-vis the tax authorities of all the Member States in which the performance is carried out. Cancellation of the exemption of imports of goods up to EUR 22 so that goods from third countries are not favoured by the fact that their imports are not subject to VAT. Goods which the customer can buy, for example, in the Czech Republic, are subject to taxation, so that this exemption, which is linked to the import of goods, does not apply and VAT on these goods is paid. The introduction of new import arrangements for goods of up to EUR 150 to simplify the fulfilment of import obligations. The introduction of special rules on the supply of goods sold via electronic platforms. The proposal also contains partial amendments to the regulation in relation to EU law, which take place on the basis of an evaluation of the needs of application practice, the development of other legislation and, in particular, the case law of the EU Court of Justice. The proposed extension of the existing system will be launched in accordance with the timing at EU level next year, i.e. 2021. I would like to ask you to discuss the bill in committee and to approve it. We are under pressure from the implementation deadline, the law has been here since about July last year. This is not a regret, I know that there have been many priorities and we are in the covido period, but implementation is important because all 27 EU countries will be involved in this system. Thank you.
