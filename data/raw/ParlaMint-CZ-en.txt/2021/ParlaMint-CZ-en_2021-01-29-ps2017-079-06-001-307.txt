ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-29-ps2017-079-06-001-307.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, on 18 January 2021 it was exactly a year since the third most tragic fire in the House of Social Services for the Mentally Affected in Vejprt. Just a reminder of the fire of the Olympik Hotel in May 1995, when the same number of victims was held, the fire in Prague in 2010, when nine people died, and the last time the fire of the panel house in Bohumín on August 8, 2020, where eleven people were killed. Fires of social services facilities where clients are bedridden or with a serious mental disorder, as in Vejprty, are one of the most difficult interventions for firefighters due to non-cooperation of these persons during evacuation from the affected object by fire due to misunderstanding the situation and non-cooperation with firefighters during evacuation. Just the behavior of these adults is like the behavior of children at the age of eight. After this fire in Vejprty, I immediately sought a solution to prevent these tragic events in social services facilities with the General Directorate of the Fire Rescue Department of the Czech Republic and professional organisations dealing with fire protection. One of the most important things in the event of fire is early detection of fire. The sooner the fire is detected or observed and reported on the emergency line, the greater success is in saving people and property at the site of the fire. If the unit arrives within 10 minutes of the fire, the fire is in the first stage of its development and the damage to life and property are mostly minimal. It was a fire in Vejprty, which was observed after 45 minutes. This is the fourth phase of the fire and there is nothing to burn in the fire section. Very little was missing in Vejprty, so that the victims would not be even more, and also that the fire would not spread to the roof structure and other fire sections. This did not happen thanks to the professional intervention of JSDH Vejprta and the troops of the Fire Rescue Corps of the Ústí Region. 31 Members signed the parliamentary proposal. This Act is intended to amend the Fire Protection Act and the Integrated Rescue System and pursues three basic objectives. Firstly, establishing clear conditions for early warning of exceptional events for social services facilities providing residential services and for conservation buildings and at the same time the classification of buildings per category according to fire hazard assessment. This is to determine whether specific buildings will be subject to fire safety and population protection requirements, which will subsequently be assessed by the state fire surveillance authority in fire safety solutions or similar documentation. On the one hand, it is about tightening the conditions of fire safety of certain buildings, on the other hand, defining whether specific buildings will be subject to the performance of state fire surveillance and the authority concerned in the territorial and construction proceedings and in the common territorial and construction proceedings in terms of the protection of the population by the fire rescue corps. The aim of the proposal is, therefore, to rationalise the increase in safety in installations where there is an increased risk of evacuation in the light of the issue of evacuation. Last but not least, it is a proposal that aims to improve the fire safety of specific buildings following practice, rationalize and optimize the category of buildings from the point of view of fire safety. The proposal was prepared in cooperation with the Ministry of the Interior, the Fire Rescue Corps of the Czech Republic, i.e. the Gestor of the Fire Protection Act. The proposal was sent to the Government for its opinion, which, by its resolution of 26 October 2020, number 1087, agreed with the proposal. It also submitted a number of comments for further consideration. It is therefore envisaged, in the context of the second reading, to discuss the Committee on Security and the subcommittee on Fire Rescue, that these comments will be examined and, where appropriate, brought to the amendments. Thank you for your attention.
