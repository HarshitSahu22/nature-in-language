ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-27-ps2017-079-05-006-049.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce a government bill amending the Bank Act, the Savings and Loan Cooperative Act, the Capital Market Business Act and some other laws. The reason for submitting this draft law is the implementation of the amendment to the European Capital Requirements Directive and amendments to the Capital Requirements Directive. It is, in substance, the introduction of the global standard for banking regulation, the so-called Basel III, into the law of the European Union. The aim of this revised adjustment is to strengthen the regulatory framework and to create conditions for ensuring stability, security and sufficient resilience of the banking sector and the entire financial system. These are the introduction or further specification of several independent institutes. The proposed bill does not go beyond the changes necessary in connection with the implementation of European Union law and was prepared in close cooperation with the Czech National Bank. As far as material content is concerned, the draft law introduces changes mainly in the field of supervision on a consolidated basis, the determination of capital requirements or the remuneration of employees. Finally, let me point out that the date from which the effective national legislation implementing the Capital Requirements Directive is to be implemented was set at 29 December 2020. Therefore, the proposed law needs to be discussed as quickly as possible. Otherwise, the Czech Republic is threatened with a penalty for failure to fulfil the obligations arising from membership of the European Union, but you know that. Thank you for your attention.
