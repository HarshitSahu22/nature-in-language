Mr President, honourable Members, in connection with the natural disaster that struck the municipalities in South Moravia on 24 June 2021 and the municipality of Stebno in Lounsko on 30 June 2021, Government Decree No. 257/2021 Coll., on the use of State Investment Support Fund funds in the form of a subsidy and a loan for the renewal of dwellings affected by the natural disaster on 24 June 2021. On the basis of a qualified estimate, the owners of the affected objects can benefit from a subsidy of up to CZK 2 million for the renewal of the dwelling; in the event of major damage, they can finance the renewal by means of a low-rated loan of up to CZK 3 million. Support may be requested under the terms of the Government's regulation within two years of the natural disaster. According to preliminary information is damaged property for living about 1,600. The amount of available funds of the Fund is already so low that the recovery programme's claims cannot be covered by resources other than state budget resources. The Government, by its resolution No. This appropriation is intended to cover expenditure on studies, meetings of experts, meetings of experts, information and publications directly linked to the achievement of the objectives of the Programme. 257. The volume of funds released, that is CZK 1.4 billion, takes into account the actual amount of aid requirements applied to support the affected dwellings on 13 August 2021, fully in the area of aid in the form of a subsidy. This volume of applied requirements is increased daily by newly accepted applications, currently registering 867 applications. The claims for renewal of the affected dwelling above the released amount of CZK 1.4 billion will subsequently be addressed and financed from a source determined by a government decision. In view of the calculation of costs according to the applied requirements of the owners of the affected dwellings only until date 13. 8. 2021 and, in particular, the administrative, time-consuming change in income and expenditure limits, the Fund, submitted by the draft amendment to the budget for 2021, is therefore asking for funding to be provided for the implementation of the government regulation adopted by increasing the expenditure limit in the area of programme expenditure up to CZK 1.8 billion, which will be fully covered on the revenue side up to CZK 1.8 billion from the state budget through Chapter 317, Ministry of Regional Development.
