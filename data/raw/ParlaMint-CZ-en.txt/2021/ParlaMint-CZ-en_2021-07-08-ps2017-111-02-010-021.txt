ParlaMint-CZ_2021-07-08-ps2017-111-02-010-021.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, ladies and gentlemen, let me present my amendment, which I will then formally sign up to in the context of a detailed debate. At the same time, the Constitutional Legal Committee supported and approved certain amendments in the so-called lobbyist footsteps of the legislation, and I will allow myself, following further amendments which have subsequently been approved, to propose a minor amendment which aims to make everything work here in practice. The point is that the constitutional-legal committee has anchored the process of objecting lobbyists against lobbyists' content, thereby enabling lobbyists to object to information about themselves listed in the lobbyist trail, as well as to lobbyists in the event of a declaration. At the same time, however, the draft CIP did not determine that this objection should also appear in the lobbyist trail against which the content is contested. This proposal therefore complements the amendment that I propose here and please support it proposed by the Institute for Printing 565, while maintaining the changes proposed by the CIP. In fact, this is merely to add point (e) to paragraph 2, and that is to say, if the amendment, which I therefore justify, is approved, "the lobbyist footstep of the legislation declared in the Collection of Laws and International Treaties contains (e) an objection to the data contained in the lobbyist footstep of the legislation under the Lobby Act'. That's all, and thank you, colleagues, for your attention.
