ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-020-162.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, this is the last material that goes to the Ministry of Defence. The subject of the material is basic information about the standard approval process during flights and transits of armed forces of other states through the territory of the Czech Republic according to the Constitution of the Czech Republic. By its resolution, the Government authorised the crossings and transits of the armed forces of the European Union States which ratified the Treaty of European Defence Agency. Furthermore, it has authorised flights and transits to the armed forces of the Member States of the North Atlantic Alliance and States participating in the Partnership for Peace programme, not least the countries with which the Czech Republic maintains a partnership. Allowing the crossing or transit will also be possible for the armed forces of other states, but only provided consent from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. By approving this procedure, the Czech Republic will be seen as a reliable and trustworthy partner in this area with our allies. The Government's decision is also entirely in line with the Ministry's analysis of the existing legislation on the current practice of declaring consent to overflights and transits of armed forces through the territory of the Czech Republic, which the Government of the Czech Republic took note of by its resolution number 580 of 22 June 2015. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, in conclusion, I would like to thank mostly for the constructive debate and support received by the Ministry of Defence during this parliamentary term on the floor of the Chamber of Deputies. Thank you again.
