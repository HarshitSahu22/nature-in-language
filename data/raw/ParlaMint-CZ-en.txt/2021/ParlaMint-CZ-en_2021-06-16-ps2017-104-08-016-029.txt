The proposal responds to the needs of application practice, alleviates qualification requirements for authorised officials who conduct infringement proceedings, abolishes the age limit of 50 years as a condition for exemption from qualification requirements, abolishes the obligation to process the review of offences, anchors the protection of privacy and security of persons acting in criminal proceedings and the proper procedural protection of vulnerable victims in such proceedings. The new factual nature of the offence, which affects the unauthorised handling of pyrotechnics in connection with the conduct of sporting events, allows for the imposition of restrictive measures in relation to so-called drug offences and the new factual nature of the offence is aimed at non-compliance with the restrictive measures imposed in the infringement proceedings. This is all aimed at things related to so-called spectator violence at sporting events, or drug offences.
