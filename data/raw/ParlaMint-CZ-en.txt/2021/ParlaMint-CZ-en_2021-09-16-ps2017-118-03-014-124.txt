ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-014-124.u1	124. Information on flights and transits of armed forces of other states carried out through the territory of the Czech Republic in the 1st half of 2020 /German press 1030/ I ask the Minister for an opening word.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-014-124.u2	Thank you for the floor. The information provides a comprehensive overview of landings, flights and transits within 48 hours in the Czech Republic of other armed forces through the territory of the Czech Republic, in the first half of 2020. The material also includes a comparison of individual numbers of flights of foreign military aircraft and land transports of foreign armed forces through the territory of the Czech Republic over the last five years. Land and air transport of other armed forces was carried out during that period, mainly for reasons of participation in military operations, such as Kosovo, Force Resolute Support Mission Afghanistan, further participation in military training, actions and negotiations to secure them. For the purpose of crossing and landing in the territory of the Czech Republic, a total of 3 272 air transports including 775 charter flights and 120 landing of other armed forces, 9 landing in favour of the Army of the Czech Republic and 3 landing in order to participate in events, transport of medical aids in favour of the Ministry of Interior and so on. [[12.00 hours]] Land transport in order to take part in foreign service and work travels, foreign visits, courses and transit through the territory of the Czech Republic was carried out in a given period a total of 48, separate movements without the escort of the Military Police, two road currents, no railway transport. The reduction in land transport was caused by the announcement of an emergency situation due to COVID-19 disease. Thank you for your attention.
