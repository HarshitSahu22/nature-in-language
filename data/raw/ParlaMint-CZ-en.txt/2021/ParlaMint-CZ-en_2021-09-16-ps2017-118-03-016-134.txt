Information on participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the Czech Republic and participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the Czech Republic during the period July to December 2020 /German press 1175/ And I'm asking the Minister for an opening word.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-016-134.u2	Thanks again for the word. Units of the armed forces of the Czech Republic participated in 14 exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and 11 exercises with foreign partners in the territory of the Czech Republic. The total financial amount needed to carry out these exercises was CZK 244 million. The main tasks of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in training with foreign partners in the second half of 2020 were: preparation of units for deployment in operations and into standby forces, construction and development of capabilities to meet NATO and EU collective defence commitments. Due to exceptional measures in relation to COVID-19 disease, most of the planned exercises with foreign partners were cancelled. A detailed overview of the exercises for the period July to December 2020 is given in the Annex to the presented material. Thank you for your attention.
