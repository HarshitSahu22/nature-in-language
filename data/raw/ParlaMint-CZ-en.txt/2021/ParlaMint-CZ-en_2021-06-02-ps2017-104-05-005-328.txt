ParlaMint-CZ_2021-06-02-ps2017-104-05-005-328.u2	Hello. Mr President, Madam Members, allow me to reiterate the Government's amendment to Act No. 155/1998 Coll., on fertilizers. The draft law implements the adaptation of the Czech rule of law to Regulation 2019/1009 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down rules for the making available on the market of European Union fertilizer products. The draft law also contains an accompanying amendment to the Act on Conformity Assessment of specified products when making them available on the market. Furthermore, the law provides for the obligation to keep records of the quantities of the main and by-products harvested, except permanent grassland. This provision shall have suspensive effect from 1 January 2022. Agricultural entrepreneurs operating on an area of more than 200 hectares according to the farm land use register, according to user relations, will be required to keep the register in electronic form under the law. This provision shall also have suspensive effect from 1 January 2022. On the basis of the knowledge of application practice, the new mandatory annex to the application and registration of fertiliser is the operating order of the waste recovery facility under Law No. 185/2001 Coll., on Waste. The Government's draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 35th meeting on 13 May 2020 and recommended by its resolution to approve the draft law in the submitted version. [[11.30 hours]] The Agricultural Committee, as a guarantee, also discussed the Government's bill on 11 May 2021 and adopted a resolution recommending the Chamber of Deputies to vote in third reading on the amendments tabled to the bill in the proposed order. Thank you for your attention.
