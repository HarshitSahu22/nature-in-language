ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-013-123.u1	123. 2. supplement to the participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and the participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the territory of the Czech Republic in 2020 /German press 1024/ I ask the Minister to take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-013-123.u2	Thank you for giving me the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, these changes in the exercise plan with foreign partners reflect, in particular, the emergency measures taken in relation to the occurrence of COVID-19 disease, and the requirements of foreign partners in the field of preparation for foreign operations, including the construction of the ability to achieve alliance standards and the current need of the Czech Army. The material contains information about the newly planned five exercises of the armed forces of the Czech Republic, three exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and two exercises in the Czech Republic and specifying information about the ten exercises already approved by the government, nine exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and one exercise within the Czech Republic, which during their preparation changed some attributes beyond the approved framework. These are in particular terms, numbers of assigned forces and resources, places and number of days. The exercises referred to in the 2nd supplement will be financed under the approved budget from the chapter of the Ministry of Defence and additional requirements for funding in the field of foreign exercises are not created. A detailed list of the amendments is given in the Annex to this material. Thank you for your attention.
