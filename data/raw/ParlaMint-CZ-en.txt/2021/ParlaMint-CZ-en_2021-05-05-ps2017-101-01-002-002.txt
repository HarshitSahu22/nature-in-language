ParlaMint-CZ_2021-05-05-ps2017-101-01-002-002.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce a government bill amending the Bank Act, the Savings and Loan Cooperative Act, the Capital Market Business Act and some other laws. As has already been said in the first reading of the draft law, the reason for presenting the draft law is the implementation of the amendment to the European Capital Requirements Directive and the amendments to the Capital Requirements Regulation aimed at strengthening the regulatory framework and creating conditions for ensuring the stability, security and sufficient resilience of the banking sector and of the entire financial system. In substance, the proposal introduces changes mainly in the field of supervision on a consolidated basis of capital requirements or remuneration of employees. Please allow me to state that the date from which the effective national legislation implementing the Capital Requirements Directive was to be implemented, which was set at 29. 12. 2020 has passed. As we are in danger of being punished for failing to comply with the obligations arising from membership of the European Union, I thank you very much for discussing this law and moving forward in the legislative process.
