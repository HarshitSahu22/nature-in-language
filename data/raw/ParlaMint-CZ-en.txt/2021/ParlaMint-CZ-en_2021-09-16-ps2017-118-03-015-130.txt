Participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the territory of the Czech Republic in 2021 /German press 1120/ And I ask the Minister for an opening word.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-015-130.u2	Thank you for giving me the floor. The military training plan of the Army of the Czech Republic with foreign participation in 2021 includes activities with the armed forces of both NATO Member States and the European Union, as well as non-Member States and partner countries of NATO and the European Union. The plan is based on political-military recommendations and on the priorities and objectives of foreign cooperation of the Ministry of Defence Ministry in 2021, the tasks set out in the Ministry of Defence Ministry's activity plan and NATO exercises programmes. The material contains an overview of 138 planned exercises of the armed forces of the Czech Republic outside the Czech Republic and an overview of 71 exercises of the armed forces of other states in the Czech Republic. On the basis of political-military priorities, the training plan is effectively set up again in four areas: preparation for deployment in emergency operations, bilateral and regional cooperation and capacity building. The priority goal of the exercises with foreign partners in 2021 is to examine and increase the level of readiness of the armed forces of the Czech Republic to protect it in cooperation with allies, as well as to prepare for the joint operation of troops in operations and emergency forces of international organizations. The material includes four exercises of the armed forces of other states in the territory of the Czech Republic, which will be carried out without direct cooperation with our army. To ensure the activities of the armed forces of the Czech Republic within the framework of the planned international exercises in 2021, an amount of CZK 150.8 million is earmarked in the Ministry of Defence's budget. Thank you for your attention.
