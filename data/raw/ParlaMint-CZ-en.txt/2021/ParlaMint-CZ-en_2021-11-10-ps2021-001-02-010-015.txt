Proposal to determine the number of members of the Committees of the Chamber of Deputies In the introduction, let me remind you that the Committees of the Chamber of Deputies are set up according to the principle of proportional representation in accordance with Section 115 para. Council Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 1605/2002 of 25 June 2002 on the Financial Regulation applicable to the general budget of the Union and repealing Council Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 1605/2002 (OJ L 298, 26.10.2012, p. 1). On this point, I will open a debate in which I see the first President of the KDU-ČSL of Marek Výborný, who I hereby give the floor to.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-11-10-ps2021-001-02-010-015.u2	Madam Vice-President, Prime Minister, Government, colleagues, by agreement of all Members of the Clubs, I would like to make the following proposal on the number of members of the various committees: 16 members for the Committee on Petitions, 25 members for the Committee on Social Policy, 16 members for Review, 16 members for Organisational Committee, 16 members for Election Committee, 20 members for European Affairs, 25 members for European Affairs, 25 members for Economic Committee, 20 members for Health, 20 members for Safety, 16 members for Defence, 20 members for Social Policy, 20 members for Science, Education, Culture, Youth and Sports, 25 members for Public Administration and Regional Development, 20 members for Health, 25 members for Environment Committee, 20 members for Committee, 20 members for Foreign Committee and 16 members for Agricultural Committee. Madam President, I ask the House to vote on this proposal. Thank you.
