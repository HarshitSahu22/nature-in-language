Promise by Members The actual act of the promise will take place as follows: Mr Petr Fiala as the oldest Vice-President of the Chamber of Deputies VIII. The term of office shall be given by the Constitution the prescribed promise and shall be the first to put it in my hands. After that, Mr Petr Fiala will read your names in alphabetical order and you will gradually approach me. You will make a promise by saying the word "I promise" and by hand. You'll confirm your promise by signing it. Signing sheets are placed on the newsstand table. I would like to inform you that, in accordance with Section 6 (a) of our Rules of Procedure, by refusing a Member's promise or by making a promise, subject to the mandate of the Member, it shall cease to exist. I will ask Mr Petr Fiala to step up to the speaker's desk and take the floor. I'll ask the rest of you to stand up.
