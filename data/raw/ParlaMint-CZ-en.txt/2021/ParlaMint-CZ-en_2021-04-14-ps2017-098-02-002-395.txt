ParlaMint-CZ_2021-04-14-ps2017-098-02-002-395.u1	395. Proposal for the election of members of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund Here too we occupied two seats, it was the first round. Published 185 ballots, kvorum 93. 184 tickets were submitted, 1 ticket was not surrendered. Jindřich Kadrnka won 59 votes, Pavel Kováčik 93, Jaroslav Lempera 11 and Zdeněk Tréšek 85. Pavel Kováčik was elected to the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund in the first round. One seat remains vacant and two candidates with the highest number of votes will proceed to it, Jindřich Kadrnka with 59 and Zdeněk Tréšek with 85 votes.
