Government bill amending Act No. 374/2015 Coll., on recovery procedures and resolution of the crisis in the financial market, as amended, and other related laws /house print 904/ - first reading I will ask the Vice-President of the Government and the Minister of Finance to present this press under the authority of the Government. Please, Madam Vice Prime Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-27-ps2017-079-05-007-050.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the Government's bill amending the law on recovery procedures and resolution of the crisis in the financial market. The proposal was prepared by the Ministry of Finance in close cooperation with the Czech National Bank. The reason for submitting the draft law is once again the implementation of the amendment to the directive establishing a framework for the recovery and resolution of credit institutions and investment firms known under the abbreviation BRRD. The aim of the amendment to the BRRD directive, including the proposed amendments to the law, is to further strengthen the effectiveness of the regulatory framework for crisis management and the resilience of regulated entities to possible economic shocks. Among the main changes brought by the law are the unification of the European framework for crisis management with the international standard to the requirement for the overall capacity to absorb losses, when the so-called internal and external capital requirement and eligible liabilities are being differentiated and thus the associated definition of persons subject to resolution and groups subject to resolution. The purpose of this change is that the institution's possible default is primarily addressed at the level of the designated resolution entity and not at the level of subsidiaries. The draft law also introduces restrictions on the sale of subordinated eligible liabilities to retail customers, such as citizens, as they are relatively complex instruments. Furthermore, the Czech National Bank's power to declare a moratorium on liabilities already in the previous crisis management phase is extended in order to prevent further deterioration of the institution's financial position. The draft law also includes the related sub-regulations of the Capital Market Business Act and the Payment Act. Amendments to the Financial Market Recovery and Resolution Act are also proposed in response to existing experience and in response to an assessment of the transposition of the original version of the Directive by the European Commission. Finally, let me point out that the date from which the effective national legislation implementing the directive is to be implemented was set at 28 December 2020, so the proposed law needs to be discussed as quickly as possible, otherwise the Czech Republic is threatened with penalties for failure to fulfil its obligations in the same way as in the previous case. Thank you very much.
