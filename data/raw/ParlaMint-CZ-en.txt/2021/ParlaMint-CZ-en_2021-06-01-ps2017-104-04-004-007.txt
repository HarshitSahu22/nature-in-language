Now I am just stressing - I repeat, we do not have an emergency situation, the pandemic is developing very well and the government can well prepare for a possible next wave of standard procedures. These standard procedures, as set out in the Public Procurement Act, are Section 29 points. (a) a law which allows the contracting authority a general exception when it is not obliged to act under the law. The contracting authority shall not be obliged to award a contract in a procurement procedure if the execution of the procurement procedure would jeopardise the protection of the basic security interests of the Czech Republic and at the same time cannot be taken to enable the execution of the procurement procedure to be carried out. This is one of the means that can be used without adopting an amendment to the Public Procurement Act. In addition, it is another general exception to the Public Procurement Act § 29 letter. (c) which covers cases where the award or performance of a contract is carried out in the context of specific security measures provided for by other legislation and at the same time no action may be taken to enable the execution of a procurement procedure to be carried out. Another means in the Law on Public Procurement is § 63 para. The Commission shall be empowered to adopt delegated acts in accordance with the procedure referred to in Article 5 to make use of the negotiated procedure without publication. The next paragraph is Section 198, when, in view of the need for quick purchase in a crisis situation, and I believe that this is the case that the Member has referred to, it is stated in the law directly in paragraph 1. Where it is possible to use the negotiated procedure without publication, where it is not possible to award a contract in another type of procurement procedure for time-related reasons caused by the crisis. Crisis means an emergency situation, including armed or war conflicts in the Czech Republic, which has already arisen or is imminently threatened by its emergence and which seriously threatens or limits the lives, health of people or property or requires measures to ensure essential supplies for the population. So here we have a specific provision which, if necessary, could be used for the purchases mentioned by the Member. The fundamental reason why we rejected this amendment in the Senate is, in particular, that it opens the door to possible corruption, and contracts could thus be commissioned economically, inefficiently, inefficiently, and in particular without control. In addition, I would like to point out that a certain time has elapsed since the proposal was tabled, and there are now even fewer reasons for adopting this amendment. Another reason for rejection, rejection is also a problematic relation to European legislation, and in particular the inadequacy of the proposed legislation. Less restrictive measures in the context of public procurement in the context of threats to the population as recommended by the Ministry of Regional Development, the ÚOHS and the European Commission can be achieved in particular by shortening the time limits for speeding up open or restricted procedures or organisation of negotiated procedures without publication. These are therefore further grounds for rejecting this law. The proposed modification is a non-systemic solution. The amendment to the Public Procurement Act is made for half a year, i.e. now for a shorter period with an explicit indication of one type of virus, and therefore lacks the necessary generality.
