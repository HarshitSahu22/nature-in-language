ParlaMint-CZ_2021-05-12-ps2017-103-01-001-001.u2	Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly present House Press No. 793, which is an amendment to the notary order. This print here has been discussed quite in detail at first reading and therefore allow me a relatively simplified form of presentation of this printing. I will mention mostly the basic points, the fundamental changes this amendment brings. The most important point is the possibility of introducing the possibility of an online establishment of a business company, of any kind. It is not only the establishment of s. r. o. but really all kinds of companies. At the same time, it allows the distance processing of the operations required by each business company and sometimes necessary during its operation. It is a very important point that allows digitalisation of the founding of the company, and I believe it will find support for you. The next item, which is also very important, is the change in the apostolic agenda, which was currently only in the Gesture of the Ministry of Justice, but this amendment brings it closer to citizens, brings it into the regions, and that is certainly also a very positive change. The third crucial point it brings is to improve safety, protect business owners, because it introduces the possibility of verifying signatures and the possibility of a public register of such signatures, so our citizens will certainly appreciate that. That's about all for starters. I would also like to mention that, at first reading, I promised, more or less, on behalf of all the petitioners, that we would prepare an amendment which would take account of the comments made in the commentary procedure.
