ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-26-ps2017-079-04-008-047.u4	Thank you for the floor. I don't want to delay, it's an advanced time, and I think you're all tired. This is a government bill, the main objective of which is to adapt, to the extent necessary, Regulation 2018/1805 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 November 2018 on mutual recognition of freezing orders and confiscation orders. That Regulation provides for judicial cooperation procedures between EU Member States with the exception of Denmark and Ireland in respect of cross-border recognition of freezing orders, confiscation of property in criminal proceedings, and repeals previous framework decisions which have regulated similar issues at EU level. This is not, therefore, a new type of judicial cooperation; on the contrary, this kind of judicial cooperation is now normally provided both on the basis of the implementation regulation to EU Member States and on the basis of legal assistance. Please note that the adaptation period has already expired on 19. 12. 2020 and the public prosecutors and courts are thus faced with a situation where the regulation in question must apply without the corresponding national implementing provisions being adopted, and therefore some issues need to be bridged by interpretation. I am asking for the support of this proposal and, at the same time, I am in favour of § 91 para. 2 of the Rules of Procedure ask and ask for a shortening of the time limit for the discussion of this Government Bill from 60 days to 30 days, because I am appealing that the adaptation period has already expired 19. 12. 2020 and, therefore, it is essential that we adopt this adjustment.
