ParlaMint-CZ_2021-12-14-ps2021-004-01-011-007.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present a government proposal by which the Chamber of Deputies is presented to agree to ratify the amendment to the International Financial Corporation Agreement, also referred to as the IFC. The submitted material results from last year's resolution by the Governing Council of the IFC to amend the IFC Agreement, which constitutes the basic document of this institution, belonging to the World Bank Group, and sets out the principles of its functioning. The IFC agreement increased the threshold needed to approve a possible capital increase from 80 per cent to 85 per cent of the total voting power of all Member States. The adoption of that amendment to the Agreement is in the nature of a change in the presidential category's international treaty, requiring the approval of the two chambers of Parliament. The amendment of the Agreement does not require an amendment to the national legislation of the Czech Republic, does not affect the obligations contained in other international treaties by which the Czech Republic is bound and complies with the law of the European Union. The adoption of the amendment has no impact on the state budget. The Czech Republic supported the amendment of the IFC Agreement on 19 September 2018 by voting in the Governing Council, on the basis of Government Resolution No. 578 of 12 September 2018. Within the framework of the IFC, the amendment to the Agreement entered into force on 16 April 2020 by approving the relevant resolution. I therefore recommend that the Chamber of Deputies agree to ratify this agreement. Thank you.

ParlaMint-CZ_2021-12-14-ps2021-004-01-011-007.u6	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, good evening. Allow me to present to you the opinion of the SPD MEP on House press number 45, which is a government proposal by which the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to agree to ratify the amendment of the Agreement on the International Financial Corporation resulting from the Governing Council Resolution No. 273 of 16 April 2020. International financial corporations, as has already been said, are members of the World Bank Group and are essentially the largest globally active development institution of its kind. Its activities are aimed at supporting the private sector in so-called developing and transforming economies, for example in the form of long-term loans or capital injections. The envisaged amendment to the Agreement concerns an increase in the minimum voting power that will be needed when approving the capital increase, from current 80 per cent to 85 per cent. Aid for capital increases by the United States of America was conditional on the maintenance of their veto rights in the event of future decisions of this type. The amendment of the agreement thus preserves the right of veto for the United States of America, even if they do not participate in the expansion of the corporation's capital and their voting power. [[19.40 hours]] In the case of the International Financial Corporation it is generally a project with many dubious features, whose connection to the national interests of the Czech Republic is only very free, if any. Moreover, we invest about a billion a year from our own state budget in development aid as the Czech Republic. The International Financial Corporation is an entity which currently has 180 members of each Member State. Its co-owners are the governments of these Member States, but the organization itself has company status, which means that it can deal with individual governments and private institutions separately. We consider the right of veto in this situation only to one of the Member States, including in matters which are not directly related to it, to be indefensible, like membership itself under these conditions, but not only for these reasons in such an organisation. We therefore propose to reject the government's proposal at first reading. Thank you.
