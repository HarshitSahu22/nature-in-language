ParlaMint-CZ_2021-03-03-ps2017-087-02-006-005.u2	Mr President, thank you for the floor. Concerning the amendment law: a comprehensive amendment was tabled to it by Mr Kolovratník. This amendment law amends a total of 58 laws and, as I have already said, a comprehensive amendment was therefore tabled by Mr Kolovratník and a number of separate proposals for individual laws in the amendment law contained in the Economic Committee. The amendments concerned, for example, the integration of water management decisions into the construction process, the extension of the possibility for advertising equipment to be placed, the abolition of health limits or the introduction of building charges. Mr Kolovratník's summary amendment, today actually from the Economic Committee, was recommended for discussion by the Economic Committee and for approval and the position of the Ministry of Regional Development is in favour of this proposal. Most of the individual amendments were not approved by the Economic Committee, and the Ministry's position on most of them was disagreeable. The most discussed amendment was the proposal by Members - Mr Kupka, Mr Bartoš, Výborný, Mrs Kovářová and Mr Feri - to introduce a construction fee. The fee was proposed as an alternative to conditional upon the intention to conclude a planning contract. The municipality should be able to introduce a building fee by decree. The fee would be collected from completed buildings in the territory of the municipality or its selected part according to their floor area and could be used from the municipality's budget for the construction, maintenance and operation of public infrastructure. If the construction were subject to a fee, its payment would be a condition of approval. I note that this amendment was not adopted by the Committee on Economic Affairs or by the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development. I am glad because I have seen a number of negative responses from the builders and representatives of the public administration against this fee. As regards the discussion of the material in the other committees, the Constitutional and Legal Committee at its meeting on 3 February interrupted the discussion of the amendment law and recommended the adoption of the amendment law in the submitted version by the Public Administration and Regional Development Committee. So much for discussing the amendment law in committees, and once again I thank all those who have participated in the preparation of all the proposals, and I am ready to lead to a substantive discussion on individual proposals. I would merely ask anyone who has ambition for amendments to consult with the Ministry of Regional Development. It is a very complex law, which has many ropes, and certainly our aim is to find the right path, just as we have been looking for it with Mr Jurečka or Mr Dolínek, and then these proposals have a chance of success. Thank you.
