ParlaMint-CZ_2021-03-09-ps2017-087-05-007-999.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the current legislation on electronisation in health care is considerably fragmented. The draft law on the electronisation of health care, the House's press 1163, and the subsequent House's press 1164, therefore ensures the necessary compliance with requirements under legislation and laws on the protection of personal data and cyber security. Since electronicisation in health care directly and indirectly affects the processing of personal data, the current situation calls for rules on the protection of personal data and cyber security requirements, which is addressed by the proposed legislation. The electronicisation of health services must comply with all applicable standards and laws and respect the right to the protection of personal data, while ensuring the use of eHealth services on a purely voluntary basis and setting standards for electronic health services is necessary. We will therefore support both the release of this press and the ensuing debate. We will support both of these prints for committee discussions. Thank you for your attention.
