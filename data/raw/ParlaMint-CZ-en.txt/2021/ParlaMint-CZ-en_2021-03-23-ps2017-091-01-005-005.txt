ParlaMint-CZ_2021-03-23-ps2017-091-01-005-005.u4	Thank you, Mr President. Very briefly. The aim of the proposal is to anchor the area of safety of the dedicated technical equipment in a modern way. Today, this area is governed in many ways by the legislation that has been passed. The proposed draft law is based on an assessment of the current legal situation and practical knowledge in the work with technical equipment. The proposed substantive amendments concern, in particular, the definition of the term "reserved technical equipment', including the breakdown of the different types of "lifting, electrical, gas and pressure', the safety requirements for the operation of such equipment and the health protection at work during their use, the setting of the conditions for the professional competence of persons, the method of verification of professional competence for activities on such facilities, the determination of the performance of the state administration in the area of the safety of the operation of such equipment, the definition of the obligations of legal entities undertaking natural and natural persons concerned in the substance of the draft law, the formal incorporation of the register of qualified persons and the authorisation as well as the setting of a fee for the professional activity of the entrusted organisation. Ladies and gentlemen, the adoption of this law is necessary to set up more effective security of operation of these facilities, but also to protect the lives, health and safety of persons, property or the environment. That's why I'm asking for his support. Thank you.
