ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-017-137.u1	137. Information on flights and transits of armed forces of other states carried out through the territory of the Czech Republic in the second half of 2020 /German press 1186/ This material will be presented again by the Minister of Defence Lubomír Metnar. Minister, please, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2021-09-16-ps2017-118-03-017-137.u2	Mr President, thank you for the floor. Ladies and gentlemen, the information provides a comprehensive overview of landings, flights and transits within 48 hours in the Czech Republic of other armed forces through the territory of the Czech Republic, for the second half of 2020. The material also includes a comparison of individual numbers of flights of foreign military aircraft and land transports of foreign armed forces through the territory of the Czech Republic over the last five years. The ground and air transport of other armed forces was carried out in that period due in particular to participation in military operations, to participation in military trainings, actions, negotiations and to their security. For the purpose of crossing and landing in the Czech Republic, a total of 3 557 air transports including 652 charter flights, 202 landing of other armed forces and 12 landing in favour of the Army of the Czech Republic were carried out. Land transport in order to participate in foreign service and work travels, foreign visits, courses and transit through the territory of the Czech Republic was carried out in a given period a total of 177 separate movements without military police escorts, 26 road currents and two rail transports. The reduction in land transport was caused by the announcement of an emergency due to COVID-19 disease. Thank you.
