Mr President, fellow Members, let me briefly remind you of the bill which amends certain laws in connection with the further electronisation of public authorities' procedures. It was prepared in response to the government's commitment made in its programming declaration, in particular in the Digital Czech Republic chapter. The primary objective is to change the concept of legislative incorporation of a legal title for the use of data from public information systems. This will significantly accelerate the process of determining usable data and will make data sharing more effective, which is based on the principle "to circulate data, not people'. The Senate discussed the bill on April 29th. He returned it to the House with amendments. These are legislative technical changes, partial clarification of the law adopted and also an adjustment of effectiveness. For the Ministry of the Interior, I would like to say that we support the approval of the bill as approved by the Senate. Thank you.
