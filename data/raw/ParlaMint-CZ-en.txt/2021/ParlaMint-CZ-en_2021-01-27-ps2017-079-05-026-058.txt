ParlaMint-CZ_2021-01-27-ps2017-079-05-026-058.u2	Thank you. Dear Vice-President, colleagues, this is one of the two bills amending the law on the Police of the Czech Republic and some other laws. This proposal aims to address both the non-current legislation of certain provisions of amended laws, as well as shortcomings in application practices and practices of police, law enforcement authorities and intelligence services in the field of police and criminal law. The common denominator of all the proposed changes is the effort to improve and streamline instruments and procedures so as to strengthen the protection of internal order and the security of the population. In the area of police regulations, the proposal responds in particular to the fact that the Police Act has not undergone any major amendment in ten years of its effectiveness, and logically contains obsolete or in practice unusable provisions, especially in procedures and authorisations to respond to new or very rapidly developing threats such as cybercrime, threats to exposed persons, terrorism, etc. Some of the police's authorisations are no longer consistent with its current capabilities, making it difficult to respond effectively to new trends and ways of endangering public order and security, such as unmanned devices or jamming of electronic communications. The proposal continues to address certain criminal institutes when it significantly makes the use of the institute more effective and also introduces the possibility of using intelligence information as evidence in criminal proceedings. I know that the issue of intelligence is perceived very sensitively, and I would like to say here that it was the aim of both the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Justice to find a solution that would ensure the full preservation of the procedural rights of all parties to the criminal proceedings. To this end, it is part of the proposal to set significant limits for the use of this procedure. I'm just pointing out that I'm going to override any wide-ranging debate that I know that this law, although we've tried to explain everything, has raised quite a broad debate.
