ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-24-ps2013-019-04-009-010.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly state the draft law amending Act No 1 on the subject. 484/1991 Coll., on Czech Radio, as amended, and Act No. 213/2001 Coll., on the operation of radio and television broadcasting. The main purpose of the proposed regulation is to create conditions for extending the possibility of receiving a station spoken by the Czech Radio Plus, which also brings to the audience, in addition to the news, publicity and educational programmes, especially in the field of science, in the band of very short waves. Currently, the Czech Radio Plus station already broadcasts on digital networks, on the Internet and on most smart phones on medium wave throughout the Czech Republic from 16 to 24 hours and in Prague and the surrounding area on very short waves, but only in certain hours. The proposed legislation will extend the possibilities of the Czech Radio to broadcast this station on very short waves and will enable it to significantly improve the performance of the public service in the field of radio broadcasting. The proposed amendment to Act No. 231/2001 Coll., on the operation of radio and television broadcasting, correct the shortcomings of its amendment made by Act No. 496/2012 Coll., the deadline for submitting the annual report of the Radio and Television Broadcasting Council to the Chamber of Deputies and for the Czech Radio Plus station is amended by the ban on advertising and teleshopping. I would like to stress that the proposed legislation does not give rise to any claims on the state budget or on other public budgets. All costs related to the extension of the possibility to broadcast Český rozhlas Plus on very short waves will be covered by the Czech Radio's own funds. I believe, colleagues, that you will support this amendment, and thank you for your attention.
