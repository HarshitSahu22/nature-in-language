ParlaMint-CZ_2014-07-15-ps2013-012-01-000-000.u1	Ladies and gentlemen, honourable Members of the Government, I am opening the 12th session of the Chamber of Deputies. Please settle in your benches and pay attention to me. I called the meeting pursuant to § 51 para. 4 of our Rules of Procedure, at the request of 67 Members, and the invitation was sent to you on Tuesday, 8 July. Before we proceed to the necessary steps to begin the meeting, I would like to tell you what you all know. On the eighth of July, four members of the 4th Brigade of the rapid deployment of the Army of the Czech Republic died in Afghanistan as a result of the suicide bomber attack. They were Sergeant David Beneš, Sergeant Ivo klusák, Corporal Libor Ligač and Corporal Jan Šenkýř. Sergeant Jaroslav Lieskovan suffered severe injuries, which he succumbed to on 14 July. I'd like to ask that we all honor their memory with a minute's silence. [[The noise in the hall was quieted and all present rose.]] Thank you. I'd like you all to sign up with your ID cards and, if necessary, tell me who's asking for a replacement card. In the meantime, I will inform you that Mr Hamáček votes with card number 1, Mr Fiala - card 9, Mr Adam - card 12 and Mr Velebný - card number 13. Before we proceed to identify the verifiers of this meeting, allow us to allow new colleagues to make a parliamentary pledge.
