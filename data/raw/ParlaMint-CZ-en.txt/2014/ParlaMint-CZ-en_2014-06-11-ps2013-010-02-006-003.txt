ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-11-ps2013-010-02-006-003.u2	Good morning, thank you, Mr President. Dear Members, allow me to present the Government's bill amending Act No. 252/1997 Coll., on Agriculture, and Act No. 256/2000 Coll., on the State Agricultural Intervention Fund, as amended. The bill is presented as the initiative material of the Ministry of Agriculture. The original bill, House press 1063, was not discussed by the Chamber of Deputies because of its dissolution. The main reasons put forward by the proposed legislation as regards Law No 252/1997 Coll., on Agriculture, are in particular: refinement of general provisions on common market organisations, direct payments, rural development programme and operational programme Fishing. Simplifying the register of agricultural entrepreneurs with a view to simplifying trade law and with changes brought about by the Act on Fundamental Registers. Furthermore, a change in the operation of land use records according to user relations, so called. LIPPIS, with a view to unifying the method of administration of subsidies in the Department of Agriculture following the reduction of agencies for agriculture and rural areas by the Ministry of Agriculture, whose major activity was taken over from 1. 1. 2013 State Agricultural Intervention Fund. A refinement of the provisions on land registering, land elements registering and the registration of objects and their procedural means of updating. The main amendments to Act No. 256/2000 Coll., on the State Agricultural Intervention Fund, in particular the wording of the provisions on the financing of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund, supplementing the provisions on the provision of national subsidies on the basis of the Ministry's mandate under Section 2 (b). (d) the Act on Agriculture by means of an agreement to grant subsidies and to supplement the provisions and grant subsidies and the possibility of electronic submissions. The positive impact of the bill on the business environment of the Czech Republic can be considered, in particular, to simplify the register of agricultural entrepreneurs by abolishing the institute of local jurisdiction and abolishing the register of agricultural production. As regards the explanatory memorandum, I would like to clarify the information on the new basic regulations of the Council and Parliament of the EU which were adopted at the end of 2013 and subsequently published in the Official Journal of the EU in the meantime. These are the new basic regulations of the Council and Parliament of the EU concerning amendments to the common agricultural and fisheries policy of the European Union for the period 2014 to 2020 in the field of direct payments, the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development, the common organisation of the markets in agricultural products. These already published European Union regulations will be followed by the still unaccepted implementing regulations of the Commission of the European Union, which will be adopted by the institutions of the European Union during 2014. As regards the amendments of the Agricultural Committee, press No. 98/3 I declare that I agree with them because they contain the necessary adjustments to the material submitted in connection with the current amendments to the European Union rules and the requirements of application practice. In the case of amendments to Act No. 252/1997 Coll., on Agriculture, this is mainly about supplementing the definition of the role of the Ministry of Agriculture in relation to the common agricultural policy of the EU and the content of the management and control systems of the Ministry of Agriculture. Supplement to the framework focus of the Rural Development Programme of the Czech Republic for the period 2014 to 2020. Expansion of forms of aid granted by the Support and Guarantee Fund for farmers and forestry. Supplementary technical assistance to applicants for applications. Modifications of competence and scope in the field of registration of use according to the user relations of LIPPIS between the Ministry of Agriculture and the State Agricultural Intervention Fund. Modifications to land use records according to the user relations of the LPIS, the replacement of the soil block and the parts of the soil block only by the soil block and the replacement of the landmark records of ecologically important elements. Extension of the deadline for users to report changes in the land use register according to the user relations of LIPPS and non-disruption of § 6 by leaving a report on the state of agriculture. In the case of amendments to Act No. 256/2000 Coll., on the State Agricultural Intervention Fund, this is in particular an emphasis on the role of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund as an administrative authority and the addition of the possibility of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund to obtain data from forest economic plans, in particular for the purposes of controls on subsidies to the Czech Republic's Rural Development Programme for the period 2014 to 2020. Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for your attention.
