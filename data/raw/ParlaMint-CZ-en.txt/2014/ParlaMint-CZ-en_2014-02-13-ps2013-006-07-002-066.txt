ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-13-ps2013-006-07-002-066.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the report on the development of small and medium-sized enterprises and its support in 2012 was drawn up on the basis of Law No. 47/2002 Coll., on the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises. The report describes the development of selected economic indicators of small and medium-sized enterprises in 2012 compared to the previous period and also contains summary information on financial support granted to small and medium-sized enterprises from national and European Union funds by individual ministries last year. To begin with, I would like to say that the business activity carried out a total of 1.12 million in 2012. legal and natural persons falling within the category of SMEs, the proportion of SMEs in the total number of enterprises being 99,86 %. I would also like to point out that small and medium-sized enterprises employed 1.79 million. employees from about five million-dollar workforce. In 2012, there was an annual increase in the exports of small and medium-sized enterprises by 98 billion, i.e. by 6.6 percent. The share of SMEs in total exports thus increased by two percentage points to 53,5 %. I would like to mention that support programmes aimed at SMEs are analysed in the report. For example, within the OPI it was at 31. 12. 2012 decided on aid amounting to 50 billion. Kč. We supported small and medium-sized enterprises in the activities of science and research to a total of 1 billion. Kč. Of course, SMEs also support other ministries. All data can be found in the material. It is therefore clear that we focus mainly on promoting innovative activities, exports and energy, as well as making development and operational funding available. The report contains information on the preparation of the next programming period and the new Operational Programme Enterprise and Innovation for Competitiveness and also describes the implementation of the SME programme both within the MIT and within other departments. In conclusion, I would like to make an apology, even if from my point of view it's vis maior. The discussion of the progress report in 2012 to 2014 is not very up-to-date, but that is how it was composed and the last House did not have time to discuss it. Thank you for your attention.
