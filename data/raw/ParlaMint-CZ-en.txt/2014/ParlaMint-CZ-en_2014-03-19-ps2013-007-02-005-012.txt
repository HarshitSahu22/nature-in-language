On 27 November 2013, the Chamber of Deputies approved the Senate's legal measures - perhaps you all remember that we were here for a few days and we had to discuss the legal measures that came from the Senate - it was very quick - which, among other things, introduces an obligation to pay value added tax and land under construction. At the time, if you remember, it was full of newspapers that only Members sat in benches and already increased the tax burden for both entrepreneurs and individuals. By the end of 2013, such land under construction was exempt from value added tax. Newly, the land is exempt from value added tax only after the expiry of five years from the issue of the first authorisation or from the date on which the first use of the building was started, on the date earlier. As everyone here can imagine, the price of the property under the apartment is between 15% and 30% of the price of the building. The main consequence of the change is and will be the increase in the prices of new buildings, because their price has to rise by tax. Only in the smallest apartments could it mean an increase in the order of tens of thousands of crowns. For larger buildings, there will be an increase in hundreds of thousands. The changes described above have a significant negative impact on the market for new buildings and certainly on the development and construction sector as a whole. The timing of the change and the very short adaptation period are also negative when the changes effective from January 2014 were approved at the end of 2013. If you remember, it was at the end of the year, then maybe the developers used the fact that they still have a month left to buy land without tax, so it may have partly moved the real estate market, but only for a month, and already during January it was clearly seen a decline in the purchase of land or in the movement of land, flats and other properties. For the reasons set out above, we are submitting a proposal which once again exempts land under new constructions from value added tax by abolishing the five-year period at which the exemption can be used. The aim is to reduce the tax burden on the purchase of new flats and buildings and support for property housing. The positive impact of our proposal on the construction sector as an important part of the Czech economy is not negligible. If any industry in the Czech economy is failing, it is mainly construction. According to our deep conviction, the Czech economy will no longer bear further tax increases. The state should realize that if it wants a bigger piece of pie, it must first let people make a bigger piece of pie. You can't keep taking new and new money from the economy and making holes in the state budget with their help. If we have a deficit budget here today, this is primarily because politicians and officials have failed to manage effectively the money they have so far collected from citizens. The solution to this situation is systemic changes towards the purpose of spending each koruna out of the state register, not new and new tax increases at the expense of ordinary citizens of our country and selected funds into the Treasury, which at its bottom has completely obvious holes. Thank you.
