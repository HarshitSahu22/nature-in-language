Let me briefly introduce you to the proposal for ratification of the bilateral social security agreement with the United States of America, House Press 136. The content of a very short document is to supplement the already existing Czech health insurance contract to ensure the same rights and obligations for American citizens sent to the Czech Republic as they are to be broadcast to the United States by Czech citizens. They do not pay health insurance contributions in the United States if they remain insured in the Czech system under the existing social security agreement, while otherwise they do not. Citizens of the United States are forced to make contributions to health insurance in the Czech Republic even when they remain covered by the American system, including health care insurance. This certain disproportion is the result of a collision of two different systems and, in addition to ensuring equal rights on both sides, the purpose of the proposed proposal and the fulfilment of one of the main objectives of this type of contract, so that double insurance is not paid out of the same gainful activity. This is, therefore, an equivalent of what, for example, double taxation agreements pursue in the tax field, one of which was discussed in the previous paragraph.
