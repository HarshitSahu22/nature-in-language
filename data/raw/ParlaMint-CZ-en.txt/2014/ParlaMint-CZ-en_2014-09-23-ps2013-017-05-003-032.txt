ParlaMint-CZ_2014-09-23-ps2013-017-05-003-032.u2	Good evening, Mr President, honourable Members. Allow me to introduce a government bill amending Act No. 156/1998 Coll., on fertilisers, soil aids, auxiliary plant preparations and substrates, and on agrochemical testing of agricultural soils, as amended. The submitted government proposal contains only one amendment to the Fertilizers Act, but it is necessary for the practical application of this Act. It is proposed to repeal the provision according to which the central control and testing institute of the agricultural holding repeals the authorisation for the registration of fertilizer, respectively. an auxiliary soil substance or an auxiliary plant preparation, provided that the soil aid or an auxiliary plant product contains the active substance of the plant protection product. The intention is to remove the legal uncertainty of the holders of registrations of the products concerned, which this provision, removed from the context of the whole law, causes. The Government Bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 10th meeting on 24 June 2014. No amendment has been adopted in the context of its discussion. I therefore recommend the approval of this government bill in the submitted text. Thank you for your attention.
