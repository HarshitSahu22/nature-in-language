Government bill amending Act No. 114/1995 Coll., on Inland Navigation, as amended, Act No. 254/2001 Coll., on waters and amending certain laws (water law), as amended, and Act No. 634/2004 Sb., on administrative fees, as amended /German press 177/ - second reading We are at second reading and I will ask that the Minister of Transport Antonín Prachař take the floor under the authority of the Government. Before there is any justification for my opinion, I will ask Mr Stanislav Pflégr to take his place at the rapporteur's table. Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-10-ps2013-010-01-010-007.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the amendment tabled by the Act on Inland Navigation brings about changes in the part governing the operation of ports. The conditions for the authorisation to operate the land part of ports and for the revocation of such authorisation shall be newly laid down. The list of public ports is now being revised. For the use of a public port it will be possible to negotiate a price according to price rules. The area of operation of other important places on the water path, such as the marina, the port, the platform, and the berth, is newly introduced into the law. Due to the need to respond in national law to the updated version of United Nations Economic Commission for Europe's Resolution No. 24 on navigation rules on inland waterways in Europe of 1986 and to the needs of inland navigation practice, the proposed draft law introduces a new regulation of the rules on navigation. This area is currently included in Decree No. 344/1991 Coll. and according to the proposed legislation, the rules of navigation will continue to be contained in two legislations. The law on inland navigation will lay down the primary rights and obligations of participants in navigation, define the powers of the navigation authority to issue individual administrative acts and measures of a general nature and lay down penalties for non-compliance with legal obligations. The implementing legislation provides for details on how to ensure the safety and smoothness of the voyage within the limits laid down by law, the obligations of navigation operators, the types of navigation on the waterway and its importance. In view of the development of the use of vessels for sports purposes, the requirements of practice, public response and the need to ensure the safety of persons wishing to engage in such activities, and of other operators, the bill brings a conceptual change. The use of certain types of vessels will no longer be referred to reserved water areas, in the case of current legislation it is water scooters, but the use of vessels in a specific way, i.e. for sports purposes. All duties of the master and crew member of the vessel will not need to be respected on these areas. At the same time, the Law lays down which specific provisions do not apply to the operation of vessels on defined water areas. The aim of this legislation is to enable the implementation of European Union law, i.e. measures in relation to the transposition of Directive No 13 of the European Parliament and of the Council. Directive 2008/68/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 September 2008 on the inland transport of dangerous goods and the implementation of Regulation (EC) No 2008/68/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council Regulation (EC) No 1177/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010 on the rights of passengers travelling by sea and on inland waterways. Furthermore, the amendment of Regulation No 1 of the European Community is hereby amended as follows: 2006/2004. The European Commission has initiated infringement proceedings against the Czech Republic for the non-application of that regulation. The Economic Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic, by its resolution 177/1 of 22 May, recommended that the draft law be discussed and approved as amended by amendments and legislative technical comments with which the Ministry of Transport agrees. Thank you.
