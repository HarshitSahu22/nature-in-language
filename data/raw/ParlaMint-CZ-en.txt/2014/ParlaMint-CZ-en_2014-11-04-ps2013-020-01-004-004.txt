ParlaMint-CZ_2014-11-04-ps2013-020-01-004-004.u10	Hello, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen. I would briefly justify the amendment that is inserted into the system. The main objective of the amendment is to strengthen the transparency of the process of granting subsidies and return financial assistance from territorial budgets. The submitted government bill only implies the publication of the subsidy provider's intention and the contracts concluded with the beneficiaries. These published actions therefore do not provide the public with a more detailed overview of the whole process of granting subsidies and return financial assistance, but only of its beginning and end. The information on the summary of all applications submitted will not be available to the public for such published acts, nor will there be publicly available information on the reasons why some applications have been rejected, while other applications have been accepted. Therefore, in order to increase transparency in the entire grant and return financial assistance process and to strengthen public control over this process, it is proposed that providers also be required to publish a list of all applications received and, following the adoption of the request decision, a list of applications that have been approved and a list of applications that have been rejected. In the case of rejected applications, providers will also be obliged to provide a brief justification for their refusal on individual applications. The proposed compulsory publication of the list of applicants and beneficiaries is the usual institute to be filled in for different categories of subsidies and return assistance. I will sign up for this proposal in a detailed debate.
