The proposed bill therefore takes over European legislation also for a national area which would not otherwise have been affected. Thus, the customs authorities will follow similar principles to those applied to goods under customs control in the domestic market, in particular with the principle of cooperation with the holders of intellectual property rights. Similar legislation already works in Slovakia, for example. The proposed legislation will, first of all, make the fight against counterfeits more effective. The current effect will also be the protection of consumers who will be better protected against counterfeits by the proposed treatment.
