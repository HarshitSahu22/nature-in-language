ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-06-ps2013-006-03-009-045.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Government is proposing to you to agree with the Czech Republic's approach to the Convention establishing the European Communication Office. The European Communication Office (ECO) is an expert body and also a secretariat for the European Conference of Postal and Telecommunications Reports, or CEPT, of which the Czech Republic has been a member since 1993. ECO was created by the gradual reorganisation of the European Radiocommunication Office and the European Telecommunications Office, with the ECO Convention being created in 2002 from the text of the Convention adopted in The Hague in 1993 for the European Radiocommunication Office. CEPT in ECO forms the basis for the joint action of European countries in global matters, in particular in the bodies of the World Postal Union and the International Telecommunications Union, in harmonising the technical and regulatory conditions of electronic communications in Europe. On the basis of the mandate of the European Commission, studies and proposals for European Union harmonisation documents are also processed in ECO. The effect of the 28 EU Member States' joint action, of which only Lithuania and the Czech Republic are currently not members of ECO, is significantly increasing due to the effect on 48 countries involved in the CEPT. For these reasons, the Government proposes to accede to the ECO Convention. The ECO Convention is acceptable to the Czech Republic, it is not contrary to the international obligations of the Czech Republic or the obligations arising from membership of the EU. It is in accordance with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic. Finally, let me stress that the Czech Republic's approach to the Convention establishing the European Communication Office is necessary due to the importance of this organisation for the full operation of the Czech Republic in the European Union and other international organisations concerned. Thank you for your attention.
