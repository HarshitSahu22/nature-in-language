ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-14-ps2013-006-08-016-065.u1	65. Annual Report on Economics of Czech Television in 2012 /house print 22/ The report submitted was discussed by the Election Committee. The committee resolution was delivered to you as press 22/1. I ask the rapporteur, who is Mr Martin Komárek, to inform us of the committee's deliberations and to present a motion for a resolution to the Chamber of Deputies. Please, Mr. Ambassador.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-14-ps2013-006-08-016-065.u2	Madam, honourable Members and, of course, Mr President, I, as rapporteur, would like first of all to emphasise that this is the 2012 report on the economics of Czech Television, which is a long time ago, and it is not our fault, it is not the fault of this House or the electoral committee that we discussed it this late. I don't have to remind you of the political events that put her on the agenda today. As we all know, Czech Television is an organization that is supposed to manage a balanced budget, and that is what Czech Television has succeeded. In truth, however, who would not have managed with a balanced budget when he receives about six billion from the concessionaires, i.e. from us - the owners of television receivers. So what I'm saying is, without defying the report, she's, to some extent, formal. That she's basically saying that TV ran a balanced budget, which isn't so hard. It doesn't say anything about things like tense relationships inside the TV news department. Nothing is said about whether television spends more than two billion CZK effectively, for which it buys external services and external programmes. However, we have heard both the Vice-President of the Council of Czech Television Mr Dědič and the Director of Czech Television Mr Dvořák, who answered our questions, and there was no significant contradiction. We all know that television is a prime policy and that there were various lobbyists or hired conspirators who probably wanted to convince you that the report had to be approved. But I think that the Director has spent money for nothing, because I, as rapporteur of the electoral committee, want to say to you here that we have also proposed to approve the report in our resolution, unanimously, after a detailed debate. But we all said that we would have stricter criteria for a report that is no longer a prehistoric report, but a report from the modern era. That we think that the Czech Television Council should be more committed to what the Election Committee wants to do, which means that the really good money that Czech Television has is its product, i.e. in particular news, publicity, but also live shows, as high as possible, absolutely top-notch, to serve dealers and to make funds spent as cost-effectively as possible, which we are not 100% convinced of in the 2012 report. But not because we have any suspicions, but because the report, which is verified by a good audit firm, does not account for that. So I repeat: The Election Committee and I, as rapporteur, recommend to the House that the 2012 report by the Czech Television Council on the management of Czech Television be approved.
