Government bill amending Act No. 191/2012 Coll., on the European citizens' initiative /German press 28/ - second reading The submitted proposal under the authority of the Government will be presented by the Minister of the Interior Milan Chovanec. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-07-ps2013-006-04-002-004.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce the Government's bill amending Act No. 191/2012 Coll., on the European citizens' initiative. First of all, the bill aims to adapt its terminology and control procedures to Act No. 255/2012 Coll., on control, which became effective on 1 January 2014. The supervisory competence of the Ministry of the Interior vis-à-vis persons entrusted with the assessment of electronic systems for the collection of declarations of support for the European citizens' initiative is thus terminally changed to the competence of control. However, there are no changes in content. [[10.20 hours]] The second objective is the partial modification of the verification procedure of the declaration of support for the European citizens' initiative. The Ministry of Interior, as the body responsible for verifying the declaration of support for the citizens' initiative in the Czech Republic, allows access to other data from the basic population register, from the population register information system, from the civil card registration information system and from the travel document registration information system, which are absolutely necessary for the verification. In assessing the declaration of support for the citizens' initiative, it is necessary to verify whether the supporter of the European citizens' initiative has reached at least 18 years of age and is a citizen of the State of the European Union and is therefore eligible to support the citizens' initiative. For this reason, it is proposed to supplement the authorisation of the Ministry of Interior to use the data on the date of birth from the basic population register and the information system of the population register and the national citizenship statement. In order to avoid abuse of data on deceased persons, it is also necessary to verify whether the person died before the relevant citizens' initiative was registered with the European Commission and whether the data of persons declared dead were not misused. For this reason, it is proposed to supplement the authorisation of the Ministry of Interior to use data on the date of death or the date which was stated as the date of death in the decision of the court on the declaration of death, or as the date which the person declared dead did not survive. Since the organisers have a period of 12 months to collect a declaration of support for the citizens' initiative during which the data of the signed persons may be changed, it is proposed to further complement the Ministry's access to data history. However, the history of data from these population information systems will only be used if the supporter of the European citizens' initiative is not identified on the basis of the data contained in the statement of support or in the basic population register. Thank you very much.
