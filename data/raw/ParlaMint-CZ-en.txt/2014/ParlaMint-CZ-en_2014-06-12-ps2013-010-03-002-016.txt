ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-12-ps2013-010-03-002-016.u2	Madam President, honourable Members, let me make a proposal for Law No. 266/1994 Coll., on Railways, as amended. The Government submits to the Parliament of the Czech Republic a draft law amending Act No. 266/1994 Coll., on Railways, as amended. This is a brief partial amendment to the Railway Act aimed exclusively at the implementation of European Union law. The proposed bill constitutes the last corrective measure to respond to the judgment of the Court of Justice of the European Union in Case C555/10. By this judgment, the Court held that the Czech Republic did not properly implement Directive 2001/14/EC on the allocation of railway infrastructure capacity, the charging of railway infrastructure and the issuing of safety certificates in four areas. Corrective measures responding to this judgment were not legislative in three areas and have already been adopted and discussed with the European Commission. In relation to the last area, the Court held that the decision of the Railway Authority as a regulatory body under Article 30 of Directive 2001/14, in particular the area of the allocation of transport capacity, its use and negotiation of the price for its use, can only be reviewed by the Court and this review must not be preceded by an administrative review, that is, under the Act on Railways, an appeal to the Ministry of Transport. The submitted draft amendment to the Railway Act responds to this requirement and in the case of selected procedures conducted The Railway Authority excludes the administrative review carried out by the Ministry of Transport. The decisions of the Railway Authority in these cases will therefore only be contested by a legal action. From the point of view of the substance of the mechanism described above, the procedure concerning the content of the runway declaration, the allocation of transport capacity, the disputes in the conclusion of contracts between the railway operator and the carrier on the operation of rail transport and the procedure for temporary limitation and stopping rail transport is included. Furthermore, the selected offences are included in the vast majority of the owner or railway operator involved in the breach of capacity allocation obligations and the limitation or stopping of rail traffic. In the decision imposing a penalty, the action against this decision was granted a suspensive effect directly by law, given that the penalty procedure always has only one participant and the imposition of a penalty may potentially have a major impact. For the other proceedings, the suspensive effect of the action was kept as a standard at the request of one of the parties and the decision of the court which would consider the impact of such a step on all interested parties. Furthermore, the proposal contains a transitional provision according to which the procedures already initiated are to be completed in accordance with the existing legislation, due to the legal certainty of the participants. As regards the practical aspects of the impact of the proposal on administrative proceedings issued by the Railway Authority, the proposal will have a real impact, particularly in the area of disputes between carriers and infrastructure managers in the allocation and use of railway infrastructure. In 2013, eight appeals were made to the Ministry of Transport in this area. In the light of past years' experience, even the decisions of the Ministry of Transport on appeal are regularly contested by the action and therefore the parties to the proceedings are already using all possibilities of review, including the courts, as a standard. Therefore, the proposed model for releasing the administrative review of the Railway Authority's first-instance decision through appeal should not result in a significant increase in the actions and possible overload of the courts. In other cases of selected procedures, either the decisions of the Railway Authority are not contested by appeal, restriction and stopping rail transport, for example, or are issued completely sporadically. In most cases, these are sanctions. It should also be said that the proposed solution represents, in particular, a rapid response, if possible, to the Court's judgment and takes into account the existing organisation of state administration in the field of rail transport, the material possibilities of individual offices. However, this is only a temporary solution, pending the transposition of the European Directive 2012 next year. This transposition will be carried out by an extensive amendment to the Railways Act, in which, inter alia, the position, activities and powers of the regulatory body will be conceptually newly defined following the new regulation of this Directive No 2012. The proposal was also discussed and agreed by representatives of the European Commission in addition to the inter-ministerial commentary procedure. If the proposal is not accepted in the near future, or if it is adopted in the form that does not meet the requirements of the European Commission, there is a very high risk that the European Commission will bring an action against the Czech Republic for the imposition of financial sanctions. In view of this threat, I would like to propose that the Chamber of Deputies give its consent at first reading. Thank you.
