ParlaMint-CZ_2014-04-30-ps2013-008-02-009-011.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, at the 6th meeting of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic on 11 February of this year, I have introduced you to the proposal for an amendment to the trade law, which you will now discuss. Let me briefly remind you of the content of the amendment aimed at improving the business environment in the Czech Republic. The amendment removes the entrepreneur's obligation to repeatedly document documents to the municipal trade office, which have already been documented to some trade office. The Municipal Trade Office will store their electronic copies in the trade register. The documents will thus be accessible to all trade offices in the Czech Republic. [[10.30 hours]] In order to improve the protection of privacy and personal data of persons listed in the trade register, some publicly available data will be transferred by the amendment to the public. The non-public information should therefore become the address of the place of residence and the address of the place of residence of entrepreneurs and other natural persons who are entered in the trade register. Also, data on an entrepreneur should be transferred to non-public data after four years have elapsed since the date of expiry of its last trade licence. This information shall be communicated by the Trader's Office on the basis of evidence of legal interest. The bill submitted was discussed in the Economic Committee, which adopted a resolution in which the Chamber of Deputies recommends that the bill be discussed and approved as amended by the three amendments adopted. The Ministry of Industry and Trade agreed with these proposals. Thank you for your attention.
