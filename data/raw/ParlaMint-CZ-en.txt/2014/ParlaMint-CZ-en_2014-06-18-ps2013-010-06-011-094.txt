Please, Minister, take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-18-ps2013-010-06-011-094.u2	Thank you, Madam President. Ladies and gentlemen, the material presented includes an overview of the participation in military exercises of the Czech Army forces and crews with foreign partners in and outside the Czech Republic during the period July to December 2013 and informs about the participation of the Czech Republic's commanders and crews in these exercises. During that period, military exercises were carried out according to a plan approved by Government Resolution No 909 of 5 December 2012 and the first supplement to a plan approved by Government Resolution No 421 of 5 June 2013. Units and crews of the Czech Republic participated in 34 military exercises during this period. Of this number, 9 military exercises took place on the territory of the Czech Republic, including one commercial use of military training areas without the participation of the Czech Republic. The total financial amount needed to carry out these exercises was CZK 41 375 000. During the reference period, the following main military exercises were carried out: KANDAK 2, SABER JUNCTION, STEADFAST JAZZ 2013, RAMSTEIN ROVER 2013. The list of other exercises for the period July to December 2013 is set out in the Annex to the document presented. The Government took note of this material by its resolution 205 of 2 April 2014. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, I would like you to take note of this information and thank you.
