Motion for a resolution adjusting more detailed rules for the deliberations of the Chamber of Deputies and its institutions On this point, on Tuesday, 21 October, the motion for a resolution was distributed to you in the bench, and I ask the President of the Chamber of Deputies, Jan Hamáček, to present the proposal. Please, Mr. Chairman, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-22-ps2013-019-02-005-106.u2	Thank you. Mr Vice-President, honourable Members of the Government, fellow Members, I will briefly make a very simple amendment to the resolution of the Chamber of Deputies. The reason why we are approaching this amendment is that, on Thursday, when there are written interpelations on the agenda, there is a conflict of written interpelations and the possibility of Members to modify or propose modifications to the agenda and, in my opinion, a legitimate criticism was expressed that the debate on the agenda is shortening the period during which they can exercise their right to written interpellation. That is why, as the House's leadership, we have asked the legislative department to find a solution, and the proposed solution is precisely a motion for a resolution that will allow proposals to be presented to amend the agenda after 30 minutes after the completion of written consultations on Thursday. This means either the period from the end of written interventions, or from 11 o'clock, 30 minutes thereafter will be intended for proposals for changes to the programme. So much justification, and then I will read in a detailed debate the motion for a resolution, which, after all, you all have at your disposal. Thank you.

ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-22-ps2013-019-02-005-106.u4	Yes, thank you. So I will make a motion for a resolution: Chamber of Deputies in accordance with § 1 para. 2 of Act No. 90/1995 Coll., on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, amends the resolution of the Chamber of Deputies number 755 of the 23rd meeting of 25 November 2003 of IV. the parliamentary term as follows: The following shall be added at the end of the text of point III: "If the date of the sitting of the Chamber of Deputies is Thursday, proposals may be made only after the end of the item Response to written interpelations, no later than 30 minutes after the end of this item or, where appropriate, at the latest. Since the opening of the next meeting of the Chamber of Deputies; the period allocated for oral intervention shall be without prejudice to this.'; So much for a motion for a resolution.
