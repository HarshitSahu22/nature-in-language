Government bill amending Act No. 191/2002 Coll., on Voluntary Service and on Amendments to Certain Acts (the Voluntary Service Act), as amended (the House Press 76/ - Second Reading) As the Minister is excused for health reasons, the Prime Minister Bělobrádek will take the floor. ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-19-ps2013-007-02-001-005.u2	Mr President, honourable Members of the Government, colleagues, allow me to make a proposal for this law on voluntary service. Allow me to state the Government's bill by being processed for the purpose of adapting the Voluntary Service Act to a re-codified private law. In particular, with regard to the new Civil Code and the Commercial Corporation Act, the forms of legal persons to which the Ministry of the Interior may grant accreditation will be revised. Foundations, foundation funds, constitutions and social cooperatives shall be completed. The draft law also takes into account the requirements laid down in the Government's resolutions, and, on the one hand, that the Voluntary Service Act should also apply to traditional voluntary work in non-state non-profit organisations on a member basis, in schools and educational establishments, as well as the requirement to establish the acquisition of certified work experience certificates acquired during voluntary activities. Therefore, the volunteer will be able to continue volunteering in the organization of which he is a member. The condition will be that he performs this service outside his obligations arising from his or her Membership in relation to that organisation and will have no other legal relationship with it. The sending organisations will have a new obligation to issue to the volunteer, at his request, a certificate of long-term volunteering, the model of which is set out in the Annex to the draft law. That's the introduction. Thank you for your attention.
