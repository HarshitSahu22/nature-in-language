ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-07-ps2013-006-04-001-003.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly sum up the draft law at your disposal as House Press 29 by the Chamber of Deputies. As mentioned at the first reading, this government bill is a set of several government bills that were prepared at the beginning of last year on the basis of a government-approved timetable for the submission of draft amendments in connection with the adoption of a new regulation. Individual government bills were submitted to the Chamber of Deputies, which, however, failed to discuss them as a result of its dissolution. Now these previously separate proposals are merged into one law, which includes a total of 69 amendments to the laws. The main purpose of this proposal is, above all, the removal of special procedural provisions governing control procedures in specific laws. These provisions will be replaced by the application of the relevant general provisions of the new control order, which took effect on 1 January 2014. The draft law was discussed by the Audit Committee of the Chamber of Deputies, which adopted a resolution on it containing amendments of a legislative technical nature which were consulted with the Ministry of the Interior. I agree with these amendments adopted by the Supervisory Committee. Thank you for your attention.
