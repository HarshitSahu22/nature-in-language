ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-20-ps2013-007-03-006-034.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, one more time in a row. Like my previous speech, it concerns the draft air transport contract between the European Community and its Member States, of the one part, and Canada, of the other part. The Air Transport Agreement presented between the European Community, its Member States and Canada is one of the concrete results of the implementation of the EU's external aviation policy. This Agreement was signed in Brussels on 17 December 2009. The objective of the Agreement is to establish uniform and liberal rules for the operation of air services between the EU and Canada. The agreement represents a significant step towards opening up mutual markets and investment opportunities in air transport. In addition, other important areas such as aviation safety, air traffic control or environmental protection are addressed in the agreement. The agreement can be expected to contribute to the significant development of air transport between the two parties. Due to these attributes, the agreement is considered beneficial from the point of view of the Czech Republic. The agreement is being submitted to the Czech Republic for the second time since its discussion was suspended in 2010 due to the visa policy of Canada against the Czech Republic. The agreement does not require changes in our legal order and its implementation will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Czech Republic for the referral of material for discussion in the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
