ParlaMint-CZ_2014-12-10-ps2013-023-06-008-064.u2	Allow me to present you with more material. It is therefore, as has already been said, a United Nations Convention on the jurisdictional immunities of States and their property. So I'll try to explain it in a clear way. It is an important provision that would grant our cultural or scientific property, which we lend somewhere, immunity, which would mean that this property or some kind of archive object or art object that we lend somewhere, for example for an exhibition and the like, if it has this immunity and is temporarily lent abroad, for example for an exhibition, it will not be possible to confiscate the property. That's what they mean by protection, the immunity that such state property would have provided. So far, there has been no such thing, so maybe you can remember that in such a particular case we have been threatened with seizure of valuable art objects recently, because someone has filed a lawsuit against the state, the Czech Republic, and on this basis demanded the seizure of our property and it has been brought to trial. If we were to approve of this, this situation should not happen again, because our property of art, cultural, would be protected by this immunity. That is the essence of this proposal. So far, the Convention has been ratified by 16 states, as well as nine states of the European Union. I believe that this proposal is in our interest, because, as I have already said, we have faced similar situations recently and it would be quite advantageous for us to avoid such a risk measure. So perhaps it is clear, please support the proposal.
