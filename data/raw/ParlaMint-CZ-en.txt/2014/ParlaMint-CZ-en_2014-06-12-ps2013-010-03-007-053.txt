Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China Hong Kong on the transfer of sentenced persons (Hong Kong, 4. 3. 2013) /house print 104/ - second reading Madam Secretary of Justice will take the floor on behalf of the government. Please, Madam Secretary.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-12-ps2013-010-03-007-053.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, while in the first two cases these were agreements on legal aid in criminal matters and the extradition of wanted persons for the purposes of criminal proceedings, in this third case they are already convicted persons, or the Czech Republic should be most interested in issuing these persons when a final judgment on guilt and punishment has already been made. Therefore, the exact same reasoning applies as in the previous two agreements, with the difference that in this case, the persons convicted of criminal offences are already a final judgment. Of course, it is not possible to issue Czech citizens, as in previous cases. What makes this agreement different from the two previous agreements is that we do not need to understand the extradition of sentenced persons only by those who have been charged with unconditional imprisonment, that is, essentially, the execution of prison sentences, prison sentences, but also those who have been sentenced to constitutional forms of protective treatment or detention, that is, perpetrators who are not sane but are dangerous, their stay at liberty is dangerous, and they need to be closed in detention centres. Such persons may also be issued under this Agreement for the implementation of such measures in Hong Kong. Of course, there is a standard - a ban on the threat of the death penalty. Then, in addition to another agreement, there is a provision on juveniles where the person transferred can be viewed as a minor, regardless of whether he was sentenced as a minor in the transferring country. This is also governed by the regime of the beneficiary country. The implementation of the agreement here already requires assistance in communication through Interpol's national central office, in addition to the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of the Interior, and will be linked to financial requirements for the transport of persons transferred from Hong Kong to the Czech Republic. This will be provided by the Prison Service of the Czech Republic. Nevertheless, it will not be associated with increased financial requirements from the point of view of the budget of the participating institutions, the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of the Interior, as this is already envisaged in the budgets of the respective departments. In accordance with Article 132 (2) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, The Czech Republic can and will certainly, therefore, require the sentenced to be compensated for the costs incurred in the context of their taking over for the execution of the sentence to their homeland. Thank you for your attention and please give a favourable assessment of this government proposal, which is such an imaginary dot for three agreements, which should create a good legal framework for legal aid and other related actions between the Czech Republic and Hong Kong in the area of criminal law. Thank you.
