ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-20-ps2013-007-03-003-031.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, this should be less linguistically varied. Let me therefore present the Government's proposal to agree to the already mentioned ratification of the Basel Convention on the control of the movement of hazardous waste across borders and their disposal and amendments to Annex IX to this Convention. The Czech Republic took over the obligations of the Parties to the Basel Convention as the successor state of the Czech and Slovak Federal Republic with effect from 1 January 1993. The 11th meeting of the Conference of the Parties to the Basel Convention adopted a decision on the inclusion of two new entries in Annex IX to the Convention, namely fractions after the processing of beverage packaging and waste resulting from the production of labels. New items are already part of the existing legislation of the European Union, and since this regulation is directly applicable in the Czech Republic, amendments to Annex IX to the Basel Convention will not have effects on the law of the Czech Republic or on the state budget, public administration or the business sector. The Government's proposal to adopt amendments to Annex IX to the Basel Convention also includes a proposal for additional ratification of the Basel Convention, originally approved as government contracts. The reason for this is only formal reconciliation of the process of negotiating this Convention with the existing provisions of the Constitution of the Czech Republic, which currently requires the approval of the Parliament of the Czech Republic and subsequent ratification by the President of the Republic for such an international treaty. Thank you for your attention.
