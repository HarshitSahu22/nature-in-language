Proposal by the Chamber of Deputies for the appointment of a member of the Radio and Television Council Here too, 174 ballots were issued. 75 votes were cast for Jana Kasalová, 45 votes for Václav Mencl, 30 votes for Daniel Novák, and one vote for Marek Poledníček. So no one was elected in the first round. I will remind you that following the revision of the electoral commission's resolution, it was agreed that the election was a two-way option, which means that Jana Kasalová and Václav Mencl are moving forward to the second round of the election for the Grand Council, the Radio and Television Broadcasting Council. At this point, I'll take a personal footnote. Thank you to colleagues who have activated this review. I am glad that this item is not over for the President of the Election Commission and that the House can continue to do so. Today's second-to-last choice was a point.
