Proposal by the Chamber of Deputies for the appointment of a member of the Radio and Television Council As regards point 86, the appointment of one member of the Radio and Television Council, issued by ballots, was 163, delivered 162, one ticket was not surrendered. 73 votes were cast for Jana Kasalová, 55 votes were cast for Václav Mencl and 14 votes for Václav Žák. I'll remind you that under the rules of the secret election, the person who reaches an absolute number of votes in the first round, that is to say, kvorum 82, can be elected. In this case, it was no one. Jana Kasalová and Václav Mencl proceed to the second round of the Rules of Procedure.
