ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-24-ps2013-019-04-014-017.u2	Good afternoon. Thank you, Madam President. Dear colleagues, allow me to present and introduce a bill amending Act No. 61/2000 Coll., on Maritime Navigation, as amended, and Act No. 634/2004 Coll., on Administrative Fees, as amended. The aim of the proposed amendment to the Maritime Navigation Act is to implement the requirements of international maritime conventions into the legal order of the Czech Republic to create conditions for their ratification and transposition of secondary legal acts of the European Union in the field of training and certification of seafarers, conditions for work at sea and protection of the marine environment. In the context of international maritime conventions, it is necessary to ensure, in particular, the implementation of amendments to the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping, adopted in 2010 by the Conference of the Parties to this Convention in Manila, Philippines, and the adaptation of the Czech rule of law to the planned ratification of the 2006 International Labour Organisation Convention on Work at Sea, so-called. an agreement on work at sea. These international conventions will become part of our rule of law after ratification, and it is therefore necessary to ensure the compliance of the Maritime Payment Act with them. The Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping is the most important international treaty which lays down minimum requirements for the competence of seafarers, their training and certification. Their main objective is to ensure increased protection of life and property at sea and protection of the marine environment. The draft law waives the stricter conditions for the certification of seafarers not required by the Convention to the extent that such legislation appears to be an unjustified burden on applicants. The Convention on Work at Sea was adopted at the 94th session of the International Labour Conference on 23 February 2006 and its ratification is carried out by the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs. It is an international instrument aimed at ensuring the right of all seafarers to decent employment. The aim of the bill is also to ensure the compatibility of the legal order of the Czech Republic with secondary acts of European Union law, which follow the international legislation mentioned. It is Directive 2012/35/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Directive 2008/106/EC on the minimum level of training of seafarers and the directive following the Convention on Work at Sea. Some of the provisions of the Maritime Labour Convention were reflected at Union level in Council Directive 2009/13/EC implementing the Agreement to the Maritime Labour Convention of 2006 concluded by the Union of operators of European Community and European Transport Workers' Federations and amending Directive 1999/63/EC. The second Directive implementing the Convention on Work at Sea, Directive 2013/54/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on certain obligations of the flag State with regard to compliance with and enforcement of the Convention on Maritime Labour. Furthermore, the draft law removes the provisions relating to the administrative punishment of the illegal discharge of pollutants from the ship of the Maritime Payment Act, following the fact that the transposition of Directive 2009/123/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Directive 2005/35/EC on ship pollution and the introduction of penalties for infringements is punishable by the provisions of the Criminal Code and Act No. 418/2011 Coll., on the criminal liability of legal persons and proceedings against them. Thank you for your attention.
