ParlaMint-CZ_2014-11-04-ps2013-020-01-001-001.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, let me put this briefly, and this reminds us of the so-called proper amendment to the Consumer Tax Act. Two main areas of the amendments relating to the prevention of abuse and circumvention of tax regulations are proposed in the Consumer Tax Act and lead to an improvement in the effectiveness of tax collection. The first heading is to tighten the adjustment of the authorisation procedure process so that the maximum reduction is the number of risky entities that are allowed to dispose of selected products subject to excise duty. The second main change is the introduction of a register of persons loading special mineral oils. The purpose of this measure is to prevent tax fraud related to the illegal incorporation into fuel, in particular diesel, of non-taxed special mineral oils. It is also proposed to change the maximum amount of excise duty for the tax warehouses of mineral oils from the current CZK 100 million to CZK 1.5 billion, in the case of the provision of funds to the tax administrator's deposit account. The same maximum amount of tax collateral is also proposed to be introduced where the tax guarantee is provided through a financial guarantee. This proper amendment also includes the amendment of the Act on mandatory marking of alcohol. It is proposed that the partial adjustment of the bail settings of persons obliged to label alcohol in such a way that it is not discriminatory to those whose activity is directed rather at alcohol in smaller consumer packages. Another bail zone for very small entities is also introduced. The effectiveness of a proper amendment to the Act on excise duties is proposed as of 1 January 2015, except for part six, for which for legislative technical reasons it is necessary to take effect at 31. 12. 2014. This proper amendment was discussed at first reading on 23 September 2014 at the 17th meeting of the Chamber of Deputies, ordered for discussion by the Committee on Budgets. It was discussed at the 13th meeting of 2 October 2014 and recommended that the Chamber of Deputies approve it without any comments. Finally, I would like to ask you to discuss a proper amendment to the Consumer Tax Act, and I would like to inform you that, in the context of a detailed debate, I will propose a reduction of the deadline between second and third reading to 48 hours. ParlaMint-CZ_2014-11-04-ps2013-020-01-001-001.u6	Good afternoon. I will present to you in a simple way my amendment, which may benefit and may find support at third reading. The existing text of the Act requires that the relationship between persons who purchase gas for heating their buildings in excess of 10 tonnes per year meets the same requirements that are usual for large commercial gas customers. Often they are natural persons heating only a larger family building, a small guest house, or persons who cross the limit limit of ten tonnes only during very cold winters. The issue of a compulsory special authorisation is administratively very demanding and burdens both the applicant and the state administration. By adopting the proposal, the threshold for compulsory ownership of the special permit shall be shifted from 10 to 20 tonnes. This step does not create a risk of tax evasion. However, the administration will decrease and the competitive conditions between gas suppliers will improve. I am still going to sign up for this amendment in a detailed debate. Thank you.

ParlaMint-CZ_2014-11-04-ps2013-020-01-001-001.u8	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Good afternoon, ladies and gentlemen. Allow me to introduce to you our new amendment, which basically brings further transparency and anti-corruption measures. In short, the tax administrator should disclose, in a way that allows remote access to tax warehouse operators, information about the fact that there has been a reduction in tax collateral or a waiver. [[15.10 hours]] The reasoning is simple. Tax relief in the form of a reduction in tax collateral or a waiver is essentially public aid, which the public should be informed of. The proposed text is not a breakthrough in tax secrecy because it does not foresee the disclosure of a specific amount of collateral or the amount by which the collateral was reduced. Only the fact that a specific tax warehouse operator has been reduced or waived will be published. Any non-standard pricing policy of the tax warehouse operator or other non-standard behaviour will be able to compare other market participants with information on tax relief. The provision will thus contribute to the mutual control of individual operators. The publication should also reduce the corrupt potential and, in the case of individual decisions of the customs authorities concerning the authorisation of a reduction in the standard legal amount of the tax collateral or the authorisation of a complete waiver of such collateral. The proposed measure will thus contribute overall to greater transparency and fairness of the market for selected products. The measure should not give rise to more substantial additional costs on the part of the tax administrator, so I assume that it could find support for you, too, and I will be signing up for this amendment in a minute in a detailed debate. Thank you.
