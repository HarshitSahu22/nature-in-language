There is also a certain optimism that leads me to this, given that it is the last point, and you will certainly have more attractions than emission allowances. However, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly reiterate what the substance of the proposal is. The aim of the proposed bill is, in particular, adaptation to the Commission regulation on the basis of which the rules in the greenhouse gas emission allowance trading process have changed since 2013. The regulation newly obliges Member States to ensure that operators who are issued more free allowances than they are entitled to under allocation rules return unduly received free allowances to the European Union allocation account. In the proposed amendment to the Act, the operator of the installation is obliged to return the unduly issued allowances to the EU allocation account and, at the same time, there is a penalty for breaching this obligation. The proposal continues to abolish the obligation for emission verifiers to be authorised to verify the amount of greenhouse gas emissions. Verifiers will therefore continue to have to have only accreditation from the Czech Accreditation Institute for the performance of their activities, which is awarded on the basis of evidence of professional competence. It is also proposed that the administrative fee of CZK 3 000 be abolished for changing the greenhouse gas permit. Finally, as part of the adaptation to the Commission's regulation, Act No. 253/2008 Coll., on certain measures against the legalization of proceeds from crime and terrorist financing, as amended. The national administrator of the allowance trading registry is required to fulfil a number of new obligations, in particular the obligation to cooperate with competent authorities, which are, in addition to the Financial Analytical Service of the Ministry of Finance, in particular the law enforcement authorities, the obligation to detect and report suspicious transactions, the obligation to remain silent, the training of employees in the fight against money laundering and terrorist financing and other obligations. That is all for starters and thank you for your attention.
