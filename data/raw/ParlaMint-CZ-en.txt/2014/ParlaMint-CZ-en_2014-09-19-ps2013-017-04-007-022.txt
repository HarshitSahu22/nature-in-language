ParlaMint-CZ_2014-09-19-ps2013-017-04-007-022.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, may I present to you the bill tabled. You have before you a bill that amends four laws on the capital market. The main purpose of the proposal is to clarify certain provisions of the Investment Companies and Investment Funds Act in response to knowledge from application practice and to the necessary implementation of European legislation. Following these changes, there was also a need to adapt the provisions of the Capital Market Business Act. Our aim is to regulate the Act on Financial Arbitrator in terms of unification of terminology used by this Act and the Act on Investment Companies and Investment Funds. Furthermore, the proposed draft law contains a minor amendment to the Act on the Activities of Occupational Pensions Institutions for the purpose of the transposition of the European Directive in order to avoid over-reliance on credit ratings when investing in debt instruments. The bill was consulted and created in cooperation with the professional public from the private and academic sphere, the Czech National Bank and the Association for Capital Market of the Czech Republic. It is therefore non-conflict and clearly supports the interpretation of the regulation in question and the legal certainty of the addressees of the standard. An amendment was tabled to the draft law, the contents of which are mainly amendments aimed at specifying the normative text in terms of its clarity and terminology used. The Ministry of Finance considers these changes beneficial and therefore agrees with the amendment. The Committee on Budgets discussed the draft law and recommended its approval as amended by the amendment. In view of this, I would like to ask for support from the proposed law, as amended by the amendment, and for its smooth approval. Thank you for your attention.
