ParlaMint-CZ_2014-04-29-ps2013-008-01-004-012.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce the Government's bill amending Act No. 185/2001 Coll., on Waste and amending some other laws, as amended. This draft law aims to ensure the mere transposition of Directive 2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2012 on waste electrical and electronic equipment. The deadline for transposition of this directive has already expired on 14 February this year. The draft law is therefore submitted with a delay, which was caused by its complicated preparation and the necessity of its major revision during the autumn of last year. The government approved draft law is limited to the transposition of the directive, with further necessary changes in the legislation on the treatment of electrical equipment and electrical waste, which are complex and systemic in nature, to be dealt with only within the framework of the forthcoming new legislation on the recovery of end-of-life products. Following the requirements of Directive 2012/19/EU, the following questions are subject to the regulation of the Government's draft law: extending the scope of the substantive scope of the legislation on the treatment of electrical equipment and electrical waste, the new regulation of certain definitions, the introduction of a new institute of authorised producer representative, the extension of the registration obligations of electrical equipment manufacturers, the simplification of the administrative form of the list of electrical equipment manufacturers and a number of other points. As the deadline for transposition of the directive has already expired on 14 February, on 31 March 2014 the European Commission initiated infringement proceedings against the Czech Republic in respect of the timely implementation of this Directive. Given that the bill brings relatively fundamental changes in the legislation on the treatment of electrical equipment and electrical waste, the Government does not propose that the Chamber of Deputies give its consent at first reading. However, given the above, i.e. the initiation of infringement proceedings, I would like to ask you to shorten the time limit for the committee to discuss this proposal by 30 days, so that the draft law can be discussed and approved at the next meeting of the Chamber of Deputies in June at the latest. Thank you for your attention.
