Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Kosovo on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income taxes signed in Pristina on 26 November 2013 (the first reading) I ask the Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister Andrej Babiš to take the floor. Please.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-20-ps2013-007-03-016-032.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, without tax agreements there is an unnecessary burden on taxpayers and on international double taxation in international economic, commercial and cultural relations. The cause is a conflict of national tax laws. It is therefore common between countries to negotiate bilateral double taxation agreements. No such international comprehensive tax treaty is currently applicable in the mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Kosovo. Neither the tax treaty with former Yugoslavia nor the tax treaty with Serbia and Montenegro apply. This is a comprehensive tax contract which thus governs taxation and the exclusion of international double taxation of all possible types of income. This important contract will also contribute to an objective distribution of the right to tax on individual types of income between the two States, i.e. between the State of the source of income and the State of its beneficiary. The contract was negotiated on the basis of OECD and UN model models and is based on the tax laws of both states as a standard and does not impose any other obligations on tax entities than those contained in those laws. As all contracts of this type, this also provides for basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of both Contracting Parties. It allows the settlement of disputes arising from the interpretation and implementation of the contract, the exchange of information and coordination of the tax authorities of both states, which are aimed at limiting possible tax evasion and fraud. At the same time, the treaty ensures the non-discrimination of Czech entities in Kosovo and will increase the legal certainty of investors and other tax entities, in particular as regards the existence and level of their tax liability. On the basis of the above, I therefore propose that the Chamber of Deputies should take account of this fact when discussing this Treaty and that this international treaty should be directed to the relevant House Committees at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
