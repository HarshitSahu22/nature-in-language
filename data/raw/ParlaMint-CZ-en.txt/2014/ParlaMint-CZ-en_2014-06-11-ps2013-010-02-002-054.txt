Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the amendment of Annex A to the Stockholm Convention on persistent organic pollutants/German press 109/ - second reading Again, I ask the Minister of the Environment to take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-11-ps2013-010-02-002-054.u2	Hello again. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I recognise that for the ninth hour of the morning, the mention of persistent organic pollutants is a little unnerving, but I hope you will pay attention to it for a moment. So let me present the Government's proposal to agree to ratify the amendment to Annex A to the Stockholm Convention on persistent organic pollutants. The Czech Republic has been a Contracting Party to this Convention since 2004. Last year, a decision was taken at the Conference of the Parties to include a new substance, hexabromocyclododecane, and its isomers in Annex A to this Convention. Therefore, after the entry into force of the amendment to Annex A, the Convention will newly contain a total of 23 chemicals. This substance - I prefer to say the shortcut of HBCDD instead of hexabromocyclododecane to not scare you completely - is therefore used as an additive flame retardant, but is easily released into the environment and has proven negative effects on the correct development and hormonal balance of the organism according to laboratory animal tests. In 2011, this substance was also included in the Annex to the REACH European Regulation, so it is also monitored under this Regulation. In the Czech Republic, this substance is produced and used as an additive flame retardant in insulation materials in the construction industry. The Czech Republic will therefore register a special exception in accordance with the decision of the conference on its production. In view of the above, the adoption of an amendment to Annex A to the Stockholm Convention will not require an amendment to the legislation in force for the areas concerned, nor will it have any impact either on the company or the public administration or on the state budget. This Convention has the character of a presidential treaty from the point of view of national law, the adoption of an amendment to the Convention, including the annexes which are included, then subject to ratification by the President of the Republic, and therefore the consent of the Parliament of the Czech Republic is required. Thank you.
