ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-29-ps2013-019-05-004-020.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the proposal for this law is intended primarily to regulate the increase in energy efficiency in the Czech Republic and to promote energy savings. The main reason for amending the law is Directive 212/27/EU on energy efficiency, which the Czech Republic is obliged to transpose. Another reason is an additional partial adjustment to the implementation of the already transposed directives, namely the Energy Performance Directives and the Energy Efficiency Directive for the end-user and energy services. The last reason for the amendment is the experience from the application practice of the current text, when in the last amendment of the law there were major adjustments to obligations for citizens of the Czech Republic and after more than an annual practical application some obligations need to be adjusted. The part of the Energy Efficiency Directive implemented in this amendment mainly regulates the fields of energy audit, energy assessment, economical use of energy by central institutions and energy services. The proposed changes include an obligation for large entrepreneurs to undertake an energy audit every four years. The law also introduces an area of energy services, with the Ministry publishing, alongside the list of energy specialists, a list of so-called energy service providers from which citizens can obtain information about available companies on the market. Energy service providers have so far been regulated under the Energy Act, but due to the need and continuity of new requirements by the EU, the whole area of energy services is addressed in this amendment. A fundamental novelty regarding the energy performance certificate of buildings is the introduction of a relief from the obligation to have a processed licence in the case of the sale or lease of a building built before 1947. This option shall be subject to a written agreement between the Parties. The relief is set mainly for family houses, where the processing of the licence is too financial and also formal for these old buildings. Another significant change is in the field of state energy concept, which is standardized by its processing, including the definition of state energy planning objectives in the energy sector. Certain changes are also made in the provisions relating to municipalities and regions in the context of the processing of territorial energy concepts. The state energy inspection will significantly strengthen the control activity towards the quality of the documents processed, such as the energy performance certificates of the building or energy audits. dáma and gentlemen, in conclusion, I would like to point out that most of the proposed changes to the law reflect one of the European directives, and a minor modification of the proposed changes can cause problems in reporting implementation of these directives to the European Commission. Thank you for your attention.
