Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Special Administrative Area of the People's Republic of China Hong Kong on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters (Hong Kong, 4. 3. 2013) / House print 103/ - first reading Please, Madam Secretary, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-20-ps2013-007-03-010-024.u2	Thank you. Mr President, fellow Members, I do not wish to delay you, but this is an important agreement that will affect our relations with Hong Kong for a long time. So, like I said, it's the second of three draft agreements. This is about mutual legal assistance in criminal matters. It was also signed in Hong Kong 4. 3. It also covers only this special administrative area of the People's Republic of China. We have also not yet had a bilateral agreement on mutual assistance. It is also governed by the new Act No. 104/2013 Coll. It regulates in detail the various types of legal assistance, the acquisition of documents, evidence, the provision of persons, identity, service of documents, the temporary transfer of persons restricted to liberty, inspections and so on. For us, it is probably the most interesting provision that regulates the proceeds of crime and the ability to secure and confiscate them. We know that Hong Kong is one of the world's financial centres, so without this provision, it would not make sense to recommend the agreement. Similarly to the previous agreement and the following agreement, the possibility of refusing to process a request should there be justified concern about the imposition or execution of the death penalty, so this agreement also reflects the obligations arising for the Czech Republic from the human rights treaties. The implementation of the Agreement is, as in the previous case, within the scope of two ministries, justice and the interior. This agreement will not be linked to increased financial requirements for the state budget, with a maximum of some of those costs being payable from the chapters of those ministries. Even here, however, we do not expect that these will be high amounts of money. Thank you and I would also like to make a favourable assessment of this proposal in this case.
