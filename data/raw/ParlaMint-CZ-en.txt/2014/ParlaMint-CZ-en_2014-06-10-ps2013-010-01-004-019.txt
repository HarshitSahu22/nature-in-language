Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, at today's meeting of the Chamber of Deputies, the government is presenting a government proposal for an amendment to the VAT Act concerning the membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. It is an amendment to the VAT Act submitted in accordance with the Government's legislative work plan for 2014 and with the expected effectiveness from 1 January 2015, with the exception of certain points for which it is proposed to be effective from 1 October 2014. The reason for the presentation of the draft law is the obligation to implement changes in the value added tax area approved in the European Union's legislation to reflect changes in the determination of the place of performance in the supply of services resulting from Council Directive 2008/8/EC amending Council Directive 2006/112/EC. Further, the introduction of a special scheme for one administrative place resulting from Council Directive 2008/8/EC amending Council Directive 2006/112/EC as regards the place of supply of services in connection with changes in the rules for the destination of the place of supply and the provision of telecommunications services, radio and television broadcasting services and electronically supplied services to a non-taxable person. For a further projection of the changes in territorial scope resulting from Council Directive 2013/61/EU amending Directives 2006/112/EC and 2008/118/EC as regards the French outermost regions, in particular Mayotte. [[16.30 hours]] As already mentioned, the effectiveness of the Act is proposed as of 1 January 2015, with the exception of certain points for which it is effective from 1 October 2014, namely the regulation of territorial scope and the possibility of registration for a special scheme of one administrative place from 1 October 2014 for persons wishing to apply this special scheme from 1 January 2015. The Government discussed and approved this proposal at its meeting on 28 May 2014. It also accepted the proposal that the Chamber of Deputies should be asked, in the light of the nature of the changes contained in this proposal, to give its consent to the draft law at first reading pursuant to § 90 para. 2 of the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, I ask you to approve the proposed bill amending Act No. 235/2004 Coll., on Value Added Tax, as amended, at first reading. Thank you.

First, it is a change in the rules for determining the place of performance for telecommunications services, radio and television broadcasting services and electronically supplied services to a non-taxed person. All these services are taxed in the State of the recipient of the service. Next, it is the extension of special regimes of one administrative place for so-called. unestablished persons. The special scheme is extended by a range of telecommunications and broadcasting services following a change in the rules for determining the place of performance. And in the third time, it is a new definition of the territory of an EU Member State as regards the French outermost regions. These regions shall be considered as a third country for VAT purposes. The amendment also introduces some changes in the structure of the VAT Act. The proposed legislation is fully in line with the constitutional order of the Czech Republic.
