ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-14-ps2013-006-08-011-029.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me present to you the bill tabled. You have a law before you, which is proposed only to amend the Bond Act. The Bonds Act was last substantially amended in 2012 when this change brought the bond world significantly closer to private law and had clearly positive effects. There has also been a significant increase in the number of primary bond issues due to the amendment. Bonds are thus becoming an important and welcome source of finance for companies, at the same time representing another opportunity for investors. The proposal contains, in substance, technical changes. In particular, it deals with the legal status of the so-called hybrid debt securities, specifies the requirements of the bonds and allows greater flexibility in the escalation of the emission conditions of the bonds. The proposal was consulted and created in cooperation with the professional public from the private and academic sphere, the Czech National Bank, the Czech Banking Association and the Association for Capital Market of the Czech Republic. It is therefore non-conflict and its characteristics are pro-growth. In view of this, I would like to ask you to support the proposed law and its approval. Thank you.
