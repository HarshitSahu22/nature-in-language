ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-11-ps2013-010-02-004-059.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen. In the third order, and I promise that today the last treaty I propose to you is ratification, virtually. consent to the ratification of the Basel Convention on the Control of Transboundary Movements of Dangerous Wastes. Again, at the meeting last year, the Conference of the Parties to the Basel Convention adopted a decision on the inclusion of two new items in Annex IX to the Convention, namely fractions after the processing of beverage packaging and waste resulting from the production of labels. New items are already part of the current legislation of the European Union. Since this Regulation is directly applicable in the Czech Republic, the amendments to the Annex will not have an impact on the law of the Czech Republic or on the state budget, public administration or the business sector. By adopting amendments to Annexes IX, this Annex will be brought into line with the said European regulation, so it is proposed that the Parliament of the Czech Republic give its consent to the amendments to Annex IX. Thank you for your attention.
