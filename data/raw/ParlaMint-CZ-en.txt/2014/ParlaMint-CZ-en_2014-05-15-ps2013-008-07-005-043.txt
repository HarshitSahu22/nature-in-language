Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Protocol on the Prevention, Repression and Punishment of Human trafficking, in particular with women and children, supplementing the United Nations Convention against Transnational Organised Crime of 15 November 2000, signed for the Czech Republic on 10 December 2002 /German press 139/ - First reading I ask the Minister of the Environment Brabec to give the opening statement, and I also ask Mr Šarapatka to take the floor at the rapporteur's table. Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-05-15-ps2013-008-07-005-043.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, first of all, I would like to thank you for the helpfulness you have with me so far, and bear with me for a moment. It is possible that I will perform for some other ministers. The aim of the present Protocol is to combat trafficking in human beings as a serious form of crime and human rights violations, which, moreover, is generally implemented at international level. The Protocol is the first binding international document which contains both a clear definition of trafficking in human beings and a list of the specific measures to be taken by the Parties to combat this crime. At the same time, the Protocol contains measures to ensure assistance to victims of trading and their legal protection. The Czech Republic signed this protocol in 2002. However, it could not ratify it before the national legislation on liability of legal persons for acts which the Protocol provides as criminal. The implementation of the Protocol in national law is, in principle, ensured today with one exception: there is no clear criminalisation in national law of the conduct of accepting victims of trafficking in human beings for the purpose of exploitation. The necessary amendment to the Criminal Code is now being discussed by the Chamber of Deputies as part of the package of amendments to criminal law, which is the House press 45. To be completed, I state that the protocol was submitted to Parliament of the Czech Republic in May 2013, but due to the dissolution of the Chamber of Deputies, the approval process in the Czech Parliament was not completed last year. Thank you for your attention.
