The primary objective is to ensure as broad a level playing field as possible for all European Union air carriers and partner countries on the transport market. The agreement will provide more opportunities especially for air carriers and passengers, which will contribute to the further development of relations between the Member States of the European Union and the State of Israel, not only in the field of civil aviation.
