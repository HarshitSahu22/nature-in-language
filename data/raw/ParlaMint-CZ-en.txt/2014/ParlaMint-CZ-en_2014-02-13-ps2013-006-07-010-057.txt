Proposal by the Chamber of Deputies for the election of candidates for members of the Council of the Institute for the Study of Totalitarian Regimes (2) The choice took place in a secret way. The number of tickets issued was 155, so the commission was 78 to reach the nomination. I note the following: the number of ballots issued was 155, the number of outstanding and invalid ones was 155. 78 votes were cast for Zdeněk Bárta, 71 votes for Milena Bartlová, 13 votes for Iveta Hlásecká, 22 votes for Jaroslava Janderová, 16 votes for Ivana Koutská and 33 votes for Eduard Stehlík. Based on these results, he was elected in the first round and Mr Zdeněk Bárta will be nominated from the House to the Senate. And now organizational information. Since the Chamber of Deputies is to propose at least two candidates to the Senate and only one has emerged from this election, this choice will be repeated, virtually. will be announced once again to the vacant one, and will take place from the very beginning, that is, at the March meeting of the Chamber of Deputies. At this moment, I am declaring a new deadline for submitting candidates, nominees to the Council of the Institute for the Study of Totalitarian Regimes. The deadline for submitting to the Election Commission is Friday 7 March at 14 o'clock. The proposals will then be discussed by the Commission and the vote will be repeated at the March meeting. Thank you. That's point 57.
