ParlaMint-CZ_2014-05-13-ps2013-008-05-003-035.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I will also refer to the detailed presentation of the Government's proposal and the recommendation of its approval in the first reading, given the advanced time. I would just like to point out that the Convention itself has, of course, already been ratified and entered into force on 1 December 2013. This is an additional protocol, which mainly concerns the punishment of the spread of racist and xenophobic materials, threats and insults with racist or xenophobic motivation and the punishment of gross easing or denying genocide or crimes against humanity by means of computer systems, or modern media. So while the purpose of the Framework Convention was to create a legal framework for the effective fight against computer crime, this is already a specific specification of the instruments in the way in which, in the particular area I was talking about, the Convention is still being pre-conditioned. All legislative obstacles that prevented the Czech Republic from being able to accede to the Protocol are removed. Therefore, I would like to recommend to you, like all the committees designated to do so, to approve the Additional Protocol and thank you for its possible favourable assessment.
