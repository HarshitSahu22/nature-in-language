Proposal for amendments to the composition of the institutions Chambers of Deputies The Election Commission received the following proposals for amendments to the Chamber of Deputies' bodies from the Members' Clubs within the prescribed deadline - yesterday, 17 June - and adopted its resolution number 67, which was sent to the Presidents of the Members' Clubs yesterday following the committee meeting. As regards resignations, the resignation of Mr Martin Novotný from the ODS Club was proposed by the House Investigation Committee to investigate the way in which the Opencard case was investigated. As far as nominations are concerned, there are three of them on the "covenger" against the aforementioned resignation. It's the new nomination of Mr. Marek Benda, ODS, to the House investigation committee to investigate the Opencard case. We also register two new nominations. Firstly, it is for the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development. The Social Democracy Embassy Club is nominated by Petr Kořenko and the Social Democracy Committee is nominated by Mr Pavel Ploc. In both cases, it is a nomination for former Member Jiří Zimola, who, as you know, is no longer a Member. Next, I'll quickly remind you that Law No. 90/1995 Coll., on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, as amended, provides that amendments to the established bodies and commissions can only be made with the consent of the Chamber of Deputies. The election shall be held in a majority manner. The Rules of Procedure shall not lay down how to vote. Surely you all know that this election or election on changes in the institutions has always taken place by public vote. I therefore propose, as a representative of the electoral committee, a public vote by means of a voting device. Mr President, I recommend that, after the debate on the proposed candidates, the House, first of all, with one vote, state the resignation of Members and then make the choice of new Members of the Chamber of Deputies, as proposed by the Members' Clubs. Here, too, I propose a public choice by means of an en bloc voting device, unless another proposal has been made in the debate. Please open the debate.
