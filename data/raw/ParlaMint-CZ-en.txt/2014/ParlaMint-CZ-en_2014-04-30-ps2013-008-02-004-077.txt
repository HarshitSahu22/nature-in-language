As with the previous material, I would like to submit an annual report to the Chamber of Deputies on the activities and accounts of the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure for 2013. On the basis of Law No. 104/2000 Coll., as amended, on the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure, has been submitted to you for approval a draft Annual Report on the Activities and Accounts of the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure for 2013. This material was discussed and agreed by the Government of the Czech Republic on 24 March 2014. The Annual Report of the State Fund, which includes the financial statements, provides comprehensive information on the activities and management of the State Fund in 2013. In accordance with the law, the accounts were verified by an independent auditor. The auditor's statement is part of the annual report. Basic data on the management of the State Fund. For 2013, the State Fund's revenue budget of 58,6 billion was approved. The approved budget, including expenditure on claims under the Operational Programme Transport and the European Investment Bank, amounted to 64.5 billion. Real income amounted to 51.9 billion. the expenditure, i.e. the appropriations made available to the beneficiaries and the expenditure on the apparatus' activities, amounted to a total of 49,2 billion in 2013. Undrawn funds have been transferred to 2014 and are planned for the preparation and approval of this year's budget. The main problem of the lack of funding for transport infrastructure was not the lack of funds in the past year, but in particular the problem of land purchases, long approval processes and delays related to the Authority's decision-making practice to protect competition. Despite the lower drawdown, however, it should be noted that the Operational Programme Transport was the best pumping sector operational programme of the Czech Republic on 31 December 2013, where the set draw limits for 2013 were met. This year, unlike previous years, a large number of procurement procedures are launched, which gives hope for a significant higher drawdown even in the future. However, it will all depend on the results of the procurement procedures and the speed of resolving any appeals to the Office for the Protection of Competition. Measures that could help in the future to properly draw resources include preparation of the Line Buildings Act, amendment of the Public Procurement Act and, last but not least, amendment of Act 416 on land purchase for transport infrastructure construction purposes. I would like to propose to the Chamber of Deputies that the material submitted be approved. Thank you.
