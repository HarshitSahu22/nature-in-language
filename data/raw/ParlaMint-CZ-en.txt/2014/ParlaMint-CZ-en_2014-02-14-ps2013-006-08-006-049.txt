Government bill amending Act No. 191/2012 Coll., on the European citizens' initiative /German press 28/ - 3rd reading I ask that Mr. Milan Chovanec and Mr. Vladimír Koníček, Commissioner of the Supervisory Committee take the position at the rapporteur's table. The motion to reject and the amendments to this bill have not been tabled. I am therefore opening up a general debate in which I have no application at the moment. If no one signs up for a general debate, I am ending the general debate. I ask if there is an interest in the final words of the appellant or rapporteur. It's not. We will now proceed to the vote on the whole bill. I will make a motion for a resolution which we will subsequently vote on: ' The Chamber of Deputies agrees with the Government's bill amending Act No. 191/2012 Coll., on the European citizens' initiative, according to the House's press 28." I am opening the vote on this resolution. All in favor, raise your hand and press the button. Who's against it? In the vote number 150, 162 Members registered, for 123, against 10. I am of the opinion that the bill has been approved. Do I have Mr Radim Fiala's factual comment? No. This concludes the discussion of this bill, which has been approved. I thank the Minister and the rapporteur. Next, we will discuss the point.
