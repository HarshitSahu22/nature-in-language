ParlaMint-CZ_2014-09-25-ps2013-017-07-006-047.u6	Have a nice evening. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present my report on the House press No. 297, the Government Bill amending Act No. 329/2011 Coll., on the provision of benefits to disabled persons and on the amendment of related laws. The purpose of the proposed legislation, as stated in the report, is to simplify the procedure for issuing the PZP licences to persons who have previously issued a valid certificate of exceptional advantages or who have issued a DZP licence in 2012 and 2013 in accordance with the regulations in force at the time of validity longer than 31. 12. 2015 and for which, under the applicable legislation, it would now be necessary to reopen administrative proceedings and to assess the state of health in the administrative procedure. The current conditions of entitlement to POZP are close to the conditions of the certificate of exceptional advantages under the original regulations and the re-assessment would result in significant complications of the Czech Social Security Administration in terms of the capacity for assessment and, of course, the increase in costs already mentioned here by the Minister. In this situation, the best solution appears to be essentially the automatic rollover of existing valid certificates to the new POZ without administrative proceedings, which is also the basic content of this amendment. So it's basically a technical amendment that has support and associations of people it touches and part of which is modified on their initiative. In addition to this basic purpose, the amendment also provides for a slight link between the mobility allowance proposed by the new Member State and the licence holder, which will allow greater flexibility for granting it and is also more legally pure. At the same time, for reasons of data protection, it is proposed that no new birth number should be entered on the POZP and that symbols identifying certain types of disability are marked on those cards only at the request of the person. So I consider these modifications to be conflict-free and appropriate. The amendment is well developed and corresponds to the need to resolve the current situation. I have no significant comments on the amendment. I agree with the appellants to agree with the proposed legislation at first reading in accordance with the provisions of Section 90. However, given that the veto of the two MPs has been raised here for discussion under this paragraph, I suggest that we shorten the deadline for discussion by 30 days, and I also propose that we discuss this law in the social committee.
