ParlaMint-CZ_2014-12-02-ps2013-023-01-005-011.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Dear colleagues, dear colleagues, have a nice evening. Allow me to introduce a government bill amending Act No. 449/2001 Coll., on hunting, as amended. The draft law ensures the administration of financial contributions to selected hunting activities after 1 January 2015, as the branch network of the Ministry of Agriculture will be reduced by the end of 2014. regional agencies for agriculture and rural areas, which are currently responsible for the administration of these contributions. Until 31 December 2013, the regional authorities managed these financial contributions on the basis of the State Budget Act. As a result of the entry into force of the amendment to the Budgetary Rules Act No. 501/2012 Sb. However, as of 1 January 2014, the rules on granting financial contributions are not issued in the form of an annex to the State Budget Act but in the form of a government regulation. As of 1 January 2014, the Ministry of Agriculture has managed the provision of financial contributions through the already mentioned regional agencies for agriculture and rural areas, which will, however, be abolished at the end of this year. The proposed amendment therefore establishes the competence of the regional authorities to decide on the granting of financial contributions to selected hunting activities and, in connection with this, to check compliance with the conditions for granting financial contributions. Following this adjustment, there is also an addition, respectively. a specification of the provisions relating to the provision of financial contributions. For the sake of completeness, I add that this is a competence which the regional authorities have already exercised in the past, from 1 January 2001 to 31 December 2013. However, as a result of the amendment of the legislation, there has been a change in competences, but the contribution to the exercise of the delegated powers has not been reduced by the regional authorities. [[16.20 hours]] So this government amendment to the law is really purely technical in nature, and therefore, as I have already announced at first reading, I would ask us not to read any amendments that would alter the other character of the hunting law, because we are preparing the amendment to the Hunting Act for discussion in the Chamber of Deputies during the next year. Thank you for your understanding and attention.
