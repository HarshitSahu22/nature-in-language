Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic on cooperation on the register of residents and birth numbers signed on 20 January 2014 in Prague /German press 138/ - second reading Please let the Home Secretary Milan Chovanec take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-30-ps2013-019-06-003-073.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Dear colleagues, the purpose of the contract is to remove the inaccuracies of the data on citizenship held in the information systems of the population of both states, i.e. the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic, which arose as a result of the division of the Czechoslovak Federal Republic as at 1. 1. 1993. To this end, there will be a one-off comparison of data on persons who are given an indication of citizenship of the Czech and Slovak Federal Republic with the population of the second state. Furthermore, the contract is intended to remove incorrect data in the register of birth numbers and to ensure reciprocal recognition of the birth numbers allocated to those citizens before date 1. 1. 1993 on the territory of the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic. The Czech Republic shall recognise the birth numbers allocated before that date in the Slovak Republic by this Act. However, the Treaty does not only solve problems arising from the division of the Czech and Slovak Federal Republics, but also allows for the exchange of information with the registration of residents and registers of birth numbers and the aim is to ensure the topicality and accuracy of the data in these registers registered. Furthermore, the contract will allow the provision of data from population records to public authorities and self-government authorities for the purposes of searching for property owners. The contract contains an amendment to the protection of personal data. The details of the implementation of the cooperation under the contract shall be set out in the implementing protocol annexed to your information. Thank you for your attention.
