Information on flights and transits of the armed forces of other states carried out through the territory of the Czech Republic in the second half of 2013 was submitted by the Prime Minister to the Chamber of Deputies and the Senate of the Czech Republic on the basis of Government Resolution No. 122 of 26 February 2014 This material provides an overview of the transits and flights of the armed forces of other states through the territory of the Czech Republic carried out on the basis of Government Resolution No. 906 of 5 December 2012. Land and air transport of the armed forces of other states was carried out during that period mainly due to participation in military exercises, trainings and meetings and to the security of operations ENDURING FREDOM, PEACOCK RELOADED, NEW DAWN, ISAF, AFRICOM, KFOR and EUFOR. Part 2 of the presentation report contains summary information on land and air transport carried out for the purpose of stay in the Czech Republic, the detailed overview of which is given in Annex No. 1. Annex No 2 then provides a detailed overview of transit procedures and crossings. In Annex No 3 a comparison is made between the number of flights carried out between 2008 and 2013 for each half-year.
