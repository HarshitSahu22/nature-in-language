Mr President, honourable Members, with this proposal, partial amendments to the Act on the organisation and implementation of social security are being made and this is a solution to the issue of the preservation of documents which are of public interest if they are in the so-called 'public interest'. private writers. These are the documents necessary for the proper implementation of pension insurance. In the context of the safekeeping of such documents, private writers are subject to certain obligations relating to cooperation with social security authorities, so that they are required to provide copies of these documents for decisions on pension claims and, at the same time, if they do not fulfil or violate the obligations laid down, the law imposes sanctions and also addresses situations in which this private file room is terminated without a legal successor. I will add that, as has been said here, this proposal has been discussed by the Committee on Social Policy and it recommends that it be approved. Thank you.
