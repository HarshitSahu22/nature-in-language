ParlaMint-CZ_2014-04-30-ps2013-008-02-012-016.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to inform you of the reasons for the amendment. 361/2000 Coll., on Traffic on the Roads, and Act No. 247/2000 Coll., on the acquisition and improvement of competence to drive motor vehicles. Government proposal for amendment of Act No. 361/2000 Coll., on road traffic and amending certain laws, the Road Traffic Act, as amended, and Act No. 247/2000 Coll., on obtaining and improving competence to drive motor vehicles and amending certain laws, as amended, ensures, in particular, proper implementation of EU legislation into the Czech legal system. This is a partially revised and supplemented bill, which was already submitted by the Government to the Chamber of Deputies in April last year, but in view of its dissolution, the hearing was not completed. In particular, the Government proposal completes the transposition of Directive 2011/82/EU on facilitating the border exchange of information on road safety traffic offences and Directive 2006/126/EC on driving licences and their two amendments. From a material point of view, the proposed proposal contains, in particular - and this is important - the provision of basic information on traffic offences in the official language of another Member State of the European Union, unless the offence is a citizen of the Czech Republic. Furthermore, exchange of information on issued driving licences and driving licences between EU Member States. Sub-adjustments in the definition of categories of vehicles for which driving authorisations are granted and sub-adjustments in the definition of training vehicles. I'd touch individual points. The provision of basic information on traffic offences in the official language of another EU Member State, unless it is a citizen of the Czech Republic, is the completion of the transposition of Directive 2011/82, the exchange of information on a vehicle and its operator from the vehicle register between EU Member States under this Directive, which has already been incorporated into Act No.56/2011 Coll. with effect from 1. 1. 2015. The Government's proposal in accordance with the directive ensures that the person suspected of committing a traffic offence is provided with basic information on the infringement proceedings in a language he understands. The obligation will be implemented through standardised forms which the municipal authorities will send together with the notification of the opening of the transfer procedure. Amendments to Act No 361 relating to the exchange of information on the granting of driving licences and driving licences between EU Member States follow up on the launch of the electronic system for the exchange of such information under the RESPER project already last year. The operation of the system shall be ensured by the Ministry of Transport through the Central Vehicle Register and its involvement in the RESPER system. The information exchanged will serve for the purposes of administrative management and control related to the granting, recognition and withdrawal of driving licences and licences. With regard to the sub-regulation in the definition of vehicle groups, these amendments to the Driving Licence Directive follow up on negotiations with the European Commission, in which it was agreed to eliminate certain terminology deficiencies. These modifications do not have a more significant impact on holders of existing driving licences and licences, except for changes in the category of vehicles D1, these are drivers authorised to drive minibuses. A transitional provision is included in the proposal for this group. Sub-treatments in the definition of training and test vehicles shall implement amendments to the Driving Licence Directive. They change the technical parameters of the vehicles and allow a wider use of automated and semi-automated control systems. The current regulation allows vehicles to be used only with a manual control system. Government proposal is related to membership of the Czech Republic in the EU. The Czech Republic has already launched two proceedings for the unsettling transposition of directives 2011/82, border exchange of information, and Directive 2006/126, the creation of legal conditions for the use of RESPER. These proceedings are already under formal notice. Therefore, I would like to propose that the Chamber of Deputies should agree with the Government's proposal at first reading, so that these proceedings are no longer applied by the European Commission. Thank you for understanding.
