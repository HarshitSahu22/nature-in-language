ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-10-ps2013-010-01-003-023.u4	The Auditor Act is a legal standard regulating the profession of statutory auditor, the performance of audit activities by auditors and audit firms, the activity of professional self-government and the exercise of public oversight over such activities. It shall include a comprehensive set of rights and obligations of auditors in the conduct of audits and shall also include a system to check the proper performance of audit activities. In this system, each auditor and audit firm shall be subject to periodic checks and shall in particular be intended to verify that they carry out audits in accordance with legal requirements. The initial objective of the proposed proposal is to ensure the quality and effectiveness of the system for checking the proper performance of audit activities by adjusting some of its parameters. The source for these proposed adjustments is additional Union legislation based on Directive 2006/43/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council on statutory audits of annual and consolidated accounts, which elaborates how quality of statutory auditors and audit firms should be ensured. Public oversight also plays an irreplaceable role in this area. The Public Audit Supervisory Board, which was established in the Czech Republic in 2009, performs this supervisory function. However, it has proved necessary to strengthen its position so that its legal competence in the supervision of the performance of audit activities can be effectively implemented. The draft law, which is now being presented to you, equips the Audit Public Supervision Council with certain other powers in the area of the quality control system and codifies its scope for administrative offences, the substance of which is defined in Title X of the draft law. International cooperation of supervisory authorities is another area in which an adjustment was needed. This consists in allowing the joint exercise of audit control and access to the editor's file and other documentation held by the auditor to the supervisory authorities of third States, which the European Commission shall declare appropriate on the basis of its evaluation. These are key areas of adjustment. Furthermore, the draft law removes the interpretational uncertainties and some other shortcomings of the Act on auditors identified in its application in practice, while at the same time projecting changes to the legislation on the performance of the audit activity caused by the adoption of certain other laws, in particular in connection with the recodification of private substantive law. Around the European Union, the function of statutory auditor is associated with the public interest. Therefore, auditors are subject to very strict requirements for expertise, independence and ethical behaviour. The quality and objective verification of company financial statements strengthens the confidence of all users in the integrity of this reported information and contributes to economic stability. A sound audit in the public administration during the review of the management of local authorities and voluntary associations of municipalities is then positive in society. With this statement, I would conclude my introductory words and ask you to release this law for second reading. Thank you for your attention.
