One of them is in particular the absence of their registry, which would contain the most important information about what medical devices are on the Czech market. Furthermore, a number of sub-areas have no binding and clear rules. An example of these areas can be referred to, inter alia, the development of clinical trials or the implementation of the service of medical devices, which is also addressed by this proposal, as well as the still legally uninhibited possibility of decision-making by the official authority on what is or cannot be regarded as a medical device. Medical devices should continue to be kept in a established register, and their inclusion in that register should be a simple process based on the principle of notification. It is not so true that the conditions for the entry into the market of a medical device are limited in any way, and the market regulator does not in any way decide whether or not to launch a medical device on the market. The new system introduces an electronic management of the agenda, thus reducing the amount of administrative burdens in transparent administrative procedures with remedies. In the meantime, following the discussion in the Chamber of Deputies, this proposal was discussed in the Health Committee, in which a comprehensive amendment was also discussed, which amended some of the not-technically successful provisions of this law, which clearly helps its suitability to address the issue of medical devices.
