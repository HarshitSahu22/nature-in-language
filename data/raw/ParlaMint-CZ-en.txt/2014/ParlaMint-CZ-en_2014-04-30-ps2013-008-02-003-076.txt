ParlaMint-CZ_2014-04-30-ps2013-008-02-003-076.u2	I'm here all the time to start today. Mr President, honourable Members, in accordance with Act No 104/2000 Coll., on the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure, as amended, you have been submitted for the approval of material 76 - Annual Activity Report and Financial Statements of the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure 2012. This material was discussed and agreed by the Czech Government in 2013. In the same year it was also properly submitted to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic for approval, but due to the end of the parliamentary term of the Chamber of Deputies, the material was not discussed, so I submit it this year. The submitted material provides comprehensive information on the activities, financing and management of the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure in 2012. The accounts of the State Fund, which is part of the annual report, have been verified by an independent auditor in accordance with the law. In the 2012 State Fund's activities, it can be briefly stated that the revenue budget was approved in the amount of 58,3 billion. Real income reached 57.8 billion. A large decline compared to the budget was mainly due to myth revenue, amounting to more than 1.5 billion. Budget of expenditure including unused appropriations, so-called the claims of the 2011 Transport Operational Programme were approved at the rate of 66.1 billion. Real expenses amounted to 52 billion. The lower drawdown by the beneficiaries in 2012 affected, in particular, the delay in the tendering procedures resulting from the amendment to the Public Procurement Act, the absence of implementing rules until June 2012, and the adjustment of the general commercial conditions responding to the conclusions of EU and European Court of Auditors' controls, the delay in constructions due to the challenge of a large number of competition competition authorities and the complex negotiation of building permits, including the purchase of land and territorial decisions. I think that we have had several discussions about this here and in the context of the interpelling of Members and Members to the Minister of Transport. Since this material has already undergone all possible audits, it has been approved, it has not been submitted to the Chamber of Deputies, as I said in my speech, so I recommend that the Chamber of Deputies approve this additionally. Thank you.
