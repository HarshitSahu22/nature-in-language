Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Beijing Treaty on the Protection of Artistic Performances in Audiovision signed in Geneva on 29 April 2013 /German press 74/ - Second reading I ask that the Minister of Culture Daniel Herman take the floor. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-12-ps2013-010-03-004-050.u2	Madam President, colleagues, as I have already informed you here at my first reading speech, in June 2012, an international treaty by the World Intellectual Property Organisation on the protection of artistic performances in audiovision was adopted at the Beijing diplomatic conference. The adoption of the Beijing Treaty represents an important milestone to bridge the gap in the international system of intellectual property rights. It will allow actors and other performers to participate internationally with producers of sound image recordings, especially with film producers, on income generated from the use of films in which they work. They will also guarantee protection of their personal rights. The contract is fully in line with our legal order and the obligations arising from it do not require any changes or additions to our existing copyright regulation. The Government has agreed to this international treaty and has recommended the President of the Republic to ratify the Treaty following its signature and the approval of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. The Treaty was signed for the Czech Republic subject to ratification on 29 April 2013. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to propose that the Chamber of Deputies agree to ratify the Beijing Treaty. Thank you for your attention.
