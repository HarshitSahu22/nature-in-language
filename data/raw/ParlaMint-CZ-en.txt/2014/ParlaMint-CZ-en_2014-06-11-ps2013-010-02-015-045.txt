ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-11-ps2013-010-02-015-045.u2	Mr Vice-President, Dear Government, honourable Members, allow me, on behalf of the Senate, to present a Senate proposal to amend Act No. 185/2001 Coll., on Waste and amending certain other laws, as amended. Years of experience has shown that waste purchase is closely linked to the criminal activity of a number of persons involved in the purchase. Those who transmit metal waste often commit many crimes in the acquisition of metal objects, such as theft, damage to foreign property, threats to the operation of a generally beneficial facility, and so on. In consistent application of the criminal law, law enforcement authorities should prosecute persons who buy metal waste prohibited by decree. However, it is a fact that there is no consistent application of criminal liability and the scope of crime in the area does not fall. Statistics of the Police of the Czech Republic show that after a short-term decrease in the number of offences committed in connection with the treatment of colored metals, there has been more than a double increase in this crime in 2010 and 2011. The failure of existing bans and restrictions, which have not been able to prevent criminality, which lies mainly in the theft of metal objects, in view of the relatively high purchase price of iron and non-ferrous metals, triggers the need to amend the current legislation. The Senate proposal therefore contains a tightening of the penalty for collection operators in the event of a breach of the ban on the purchase of waste provided for by implementing legislation by natural persons in its establishment. The withdrawal of the authorisation to operate shall be carried out. This means that only one-off violation of such an obligation will be sufficient, and not as it has been repeated, and the regional authority will be entitled to revoke or amend its consent to operate the collection, respectively. equipment for the recovery and disposal of waste collection or purchase. Currently, the prohibition on the purchase of waste within the meaning of the current version of Decree No. 383/2001 Coll., on details in waste management, applies mainly to wastes having the character of an artwork, a religious or religious object and generally beneficial equipment or parts thereof. Dear colleagues, colleagues, every proposal that leads to a reduction in crime is always an asset. That is why I would like to request the release of the proposal in the legislative process. Thank you for your attention.
