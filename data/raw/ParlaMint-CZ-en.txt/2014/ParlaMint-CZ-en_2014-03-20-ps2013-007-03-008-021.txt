Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Principality of Andorra on the exchange of information in tax matters signed in Madrid on 11 June 2013 /German press 15/ - Second reading I ask the Prime Minister and Finance Minister Andrej Babiš to take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-20-ps2013-007-03-008-021.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to justify the Government's proposal to the Chamber of Deputies for the ratification of the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Principality of Andorra on the exchange of information on tax matters. The Agreement was signed in Madrid on 11 June 2013. The agreement for the Czech Republic was signed by Ambassador Karel Beran and the Principality of Andorra by his ambassador in Madrid Jaume Gaytan Sansa. The Ministry of Finance, in cooperation with the General Financial Directorate, is negotiating with the States with preferential tax regimes in order to obtain another instrument in the fight against tax evasion. So far, the Ministry has managed to negotiate 13 final draft agreements on the exchange of information, seven of which are already in force and announced in the Collection of International Treaties, remaining at different stages of the legislative process. The agreement with Andorra was negotiated on the basis of Government Resolution 227 of 22. 3. 2010 and fully complies with OECD standards for international exchange of information in the tax field. The Agreement with Andorra shall apply to all taxes laid down by the laws of the Contracting Parties. The Czech Republic will thus be able to request the competent authority of the Principality of Andorra for information that supports the correct finding, determination and payment of taxes, including investigation or prosecution of tax offences. We expect from these measures not only the possibility of Czech administrators adopting a tax which would not be paid by Czech tax residents, but also an increase in the tax discipline of domestic tax residents for the future. Under the agreement, we can also request information from banks and other financial institutions. [[10.30 hours]] The agreement with Andorra can be seen as a success of the Czech Republic in negotiating international tax agreements, which will not only have a favourable fiscal impact on the income of the Czech Republic, but also contribute to increased prevention of financial crime in general. I therefore recommend that the Chamber of Deputies agree to ratify this agreement. Thank you for your attention.
