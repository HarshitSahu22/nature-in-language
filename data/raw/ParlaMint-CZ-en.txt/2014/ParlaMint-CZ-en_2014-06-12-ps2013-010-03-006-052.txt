ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-12-ps2013-010-03-006-052.u1	52. Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Special Administrative Area of the People's Republic of China Hong Kong on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters (Hong Kong, 4. 3. 2013) /house print 103/ - second reading I ask the Minister of Justice, Mrs Helena Válková, to take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-12-ps2013-010-03-006-052.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, this is another of the three agreements between the Czech Republic and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China, now on mutual legal assistance in criminal matters, signed on 4 March 2013, the Foreign Committee discussed 15 April 2014. Ratification recommended. Similarly to the previous agreement, it contains a fairly extensive list of all options and reasons for refusing to process such a request. There is also a paragraph that regulates the threat of the imposition or execution of the death penalty. Even in this area, no agreement has yet been concluded - a bilateral agreement. So the negotiation of this agreement will significantly extend the contractual basis for both countries, and there will also be better mutual relations in criminal matters in this regard. It is, of course, conformable to our new law on international judicial cooperation. The implementation of this agreement will be in the gesture of the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of the Interior as well as the previous contract. It will be covered by the budgets planned. It will not mean any increased entitlement to the budgets of both ministries, interiors and justice. I therefore ask you to consider this agreement favorably, as did the previous one. Thank you.
