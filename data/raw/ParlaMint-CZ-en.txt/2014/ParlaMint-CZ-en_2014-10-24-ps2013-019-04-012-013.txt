ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-24-ps2013-019-04-012-013.u2	Thank you again. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, I am encouraged by the success of presenting these standards so far, I dare to address you this time with a request for approval in the first reading of the bill amending the Real Estate Tax Act. The aim of the bill is to implement substantive clarifications, the need for which arose from application practice. The draft law also seeks to eliminate the uncertainties arising from the recodification of private law and related changes in the legislation on real estate tax. Some terms are further clarified, their harmonisation with other legislation and the implementation of legislative technical adaptations. An example of the proposed changes can be given by specifying the boundary between the subject of the land tax and the subject of the construction tax and the units tax, where the time at which the construction or unit becomes the subject of the construction and unit tax is clearly defined. In the area of real estate tax management, e.g. proposes to adjust that a penalty for a late claim of the tax will not arise if the taxpayer lodges a late tax return or additional tax return on a lower tax without being called upon by the tax administrator to do so. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed on 1 January 2015. Finally, once again, I would like to ask you for the release of this bill to second reading and for the shortening that will be read in the detailed debate, the reduction of the deadline for discussing this amendment to 30 days. Thank you.
