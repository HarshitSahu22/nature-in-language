ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-01-ps2013-017-09-007-043.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen. The submitted amendment to the Price Act pursues two objectives. Firstly, it is a cost saving for publishing the Price List, which has a long-term falling cost, and customers clearly prefer its electronic form published on the Ministry of Finance's website. It is also proposed that the Price List be published on the public administration portal in order to ensure verification of the digital form of the price decision in a documented form. The same way of publishing price decisions on price regulation was already adopted in the Energy Act for the Energy Regulatory Authority's price decision. Secondly, following the new control rules, the amendment is intended to allow price controls to be carried out by auditors on the basis of the presentation of certificates and not to have a written mandate for each individual case, for example in retail or services. The law contains the requirements to be contained in the licence. The new solution will make it possible to carry out price controls more flexible, especially in smaller stores and premises. In view of the technical nature of the amendment points and the briefness of the amendment, the Government has asked the Chamber of Deputies to give their consent to the bill at first reading. The accelerated discussion will allow the existing contracts for printing and distribution of the Price List to be terminated on 1 January 2015 and to be translated into a more cost-effective way of publishing it. Thank you for your attention.
