Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Additional Protocol to the Convention on Computer Crime on Criminalisation of Crimes of a Racial and Xenophobic nature committed through Computer Systems (Strasburk, 28 January 2003) /German press 75/ - first reading I ask the Minister of Justice to give us her opening words.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-06-ps2013-006-03-007-043.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to say at last - we finally have a proposal from the Government to approve the Additional Protocol to the Convention on Computer Crime, which we have long waited for, because the Additional Protocol builds on the Convention on Computer Crime, the ratification of which was approved by the previous Chamber of Deputies and which entered into force on 1 December 2013. The Additional Protocol was negotiated in the Council of Europe and opened for signature in January, 28 January, 2003. It entered into force after obtaining a sufficient number of signatures on 1 March 2006. To date, this Protocol has been signed by 38 States, with 20 States depositing the instrument of ratification. [[9.40 hours]] Why it's so important and why we haven't signed the protocol yet. It's always such a concern that we don't sign something that our criminal code still doesn't fit, as well as the penal code. At present, we are already meeting all relatively strict requirements, both in terms of - and in particular - the criminal code and in terms of the criminal code. Thus, as regards the sanctions for the spread of racist and xenophobic materials, threats and insults with racist or xenophobic motivations that are more widely defined, the punishment of gross easing or denying genocide or crimes against humanity committed with the help of computer systems. While the aim of the Convention is always to create a framework, a legal framework, to combat which phenomenon, here computer crime, by harmonising the elements of the facts in the field of computer crime, in order to ensure the penalty of perpetrators, to establish the necessary national investigative powers in the field of, in particular, ensuring evidence in electronic form and in the investigation of computer crime, the introduction of a prompt and effective regime of international cooperation in relation to criminal offences, which relate, in particular, to those modern information technologies. The Additional Protocol aims to harmonise criminal law material for the purpose of combating the spread of racism and xenophobia on the Internet and to improve international cooperation in this area. So, basically, it's about the exchange of experience in general, and specifically about cooperation on individual cases. As I said at the outset, all possible legislative, potential, real obstacles have passed away, we can sign it with a calm conscience - finally. So I hereby allow you to ask for this consent, and I think that if we take a favourable view of this proposal, we will only supplement the existing national regime in the area of criminal protection against computer crime. Thank you.
