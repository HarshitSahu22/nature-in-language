ParlaMint-CZ_2014-12-02-ps2013-023-01-004-010.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce the bill amending the Real Estate Tax Act. The bill can be described as so-called. a technical amendment. Its main objective is to implement substantive clarifications, the need for which arose from application practice, as well as to remove certain uncertainties in the legislation on real estate tax resulting from the recodification of private law and related changes. The draft law was discussed at first reading on 24 October 2014 at the 19th meeting of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic and ordered to discuss the Committee on Budgets. At the same time, the deadline for its committee hearing was reduced to 30 days for this proposal. The material was discussed at the 12th meeting of the Committee on Budgets on 13 November 2014 and the Committee on Budgets recommended that the Chamber of Deputies approve the bill. The proposed effectiveness of the Act is on 1 January 2015. In view of the time remaining until the end of the year and the expected length of the legislative process, I would like to ask you to approve an amendment which will shift the effectiveness of the bill to 1 January 2016. Finally, I would like to ask you to discuss and support this bill. Thank you for your attention.
