ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-10-ps2013-010-01-005-025.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present to you the bill tabled. You have before you a bill proposing the amendment of four laws on the capital market. The main objective of the proposal is to clarify certain provisions of the Investment Companies and Investment Funds Act in response to knowledge from application practice and to the necessary implementation of European legislation. Following these changes, there was also a need to adapt the provisions of the Capital Market Business Act. Another objective is to regulate the Act on Financial Arbitrator as regards terminology used by this Act and the Act on Investment Companies and Investment Funds. Furthermore, the proposed draft law contains a minor amendment to the Act on the Activities of Occupational Pensions Institutions for the purpose of the transposition of the European Directive in order to avoid over-reliance on credit ratings when investing in debt instruments. The bill was consulted and created in cooperation with the professional public from the private and academic sphere, with the Czech National Bank and the Association for Capital Market of the Czech Republic. It is therefore non-conflict and supports the clear interpretation of the regulation in question and the legal certainty of the addressees of the standard. Thank you for your attention.
