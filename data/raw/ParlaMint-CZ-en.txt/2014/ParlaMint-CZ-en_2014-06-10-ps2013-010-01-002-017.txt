ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-10-ps2013-010-01-002-017.u4	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce a government bill amending Act No.353/2003 Coll., on excise duties, as amended, concerning membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. The proposal is presented on the basis of the deterioration of the Czech koruna's directional exchange rate vis-à-vis the euro, as this development significantly affects the fulfilment of the requirement for minimum taxation of cigarettes laid down in Council Directive 2011/64/EU on the structure and rates of excise duty on tobacco products. According to Article 10 of the Directive, the Czech Republic is obliged, with effect from 1 January 2014, to meet a specific minimum requirement for the amount of excise duty of EUR 90 per thousand of all cigarettes and a percentage requirement of 60 per cent of the weighted retail selling price. The specific requirement for 2014 must be met by conversion according to the koruna-euro direction exchange rate, which was valid on the first working day of October 2013. The rates of the tax are designed to allow the application of Article 18 (2) of the basic Regulation to be applied to the possible adjustment of the rates for 2015. In order to avoid further adjustment of excise duty rates on tobacco products within a short period of time. I would also like to stress the fact that if the law had not come into force by 30 September 2014, new higher excise duty rates would have to be proposed based on the indicative rate applicable on the first working day of October 2014. This course will most likely still be marked by the intervention of the Czech National Bank of November 2013. In this case, the excise duty rates for cigarettes would have to be increased by an additional 5,5 per cent compared to the proposed proposal. New higher rates would have a significant impact on the domestic market. Against the current prices of cigarettes, the price of the box would increase by six and seven crowns, which would mean approximately three crowns more than the impact on the prices presented. The jumping increase in cigarette prices is associated with a high risk of switching consumers to the black market, the increase of which has also been detected at present. In the context of a change in the rates of excise duty on tobacco products, cigarettes are further proposed to introduce a transitional period for the sale of cigarettes taxed at the old rate of excise duty of three months from the date of application of the new rate of tobacco duty. The measure applies to unit packs of cigarettes intended for direct consumption with a tobacco sticker containing a tax rate other than a new one. After the transitional period, cigarettes in free tax circulation with a tobacco sticker containing a different from the new excise duty rate shall be considered as unmarked under the Law on excise duty. Effectiveness is proposed on the first day of the third calendar month following the date of publication of the law. Only in this way can the statutory time limits for ordering and collecting tobacco stickers with a new excise duty rate be maintained. If the draft law is not approved at first reading, the continuity of the placing of tobacco products at a new rate of excise duty in free tax circulation will be jeopardised. The Government has discussed and approved this proposal at its meeting on 21 May 2014 and has also adopted the proposal that the Chamber of Deputies should be asked, in view of the nature of the changes contained in the proposal submitted, to give its consent to the draft law at first reading pursuant to Section 90 (1) of the Rules of Procedure. 2 of the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies. Madam President, honourable Members, ladies and gentlemen, I ask you to approve the proposed bill amending Act No.33/2003 Coll., on excise duties, as amended, at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
