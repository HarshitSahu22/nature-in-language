ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-10-ps2013-010-01-009-004.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I present to you at second reading a draft law amending the law on electronic communications and the Postal Services Act, which aims in particular to bring those laws into line with the new rules of control and to supplement the lack of sanctions for non-compliance with the obligations laid down in the European roaming regulation on public mobile communications networks in the European Union. As you know, the Economic Committee presented the bill discussed at its meeting on 22 May and recommended to the Chamber of Deputies its approval as amended by the amendment tabled by Mr Urban and Mr Birke. I consider it necessary to draw your attention to the fact that, in the event of the adoption of Mr Urban's amendment concerning information on amendments to the Treaties and the possibility of withdrawing from the Treaty, we would be in conflict with the European Universal Service Directive, which provides for an obligation to inform the participant of any amendment to the Treaty and of the possibility to terminate the Treaty in the event of any changes, not only in certain formalities. As regards Mr Birke's amendment, inserting a new amendment point No. 3, we agree that the limitation of the level of settlement of liabilities at the early termination of the contract should be based only on consumers. On the other proposals made by the Member, i.e. replacing the term 'participant' by the word 'consumer', I must state that, although we understand the Member's position in relation to corporate customers, it must also be borne in mind that the concept of consumer does not include natural persons engaged, i.e. not even small businessmen, and I would like to stress that the regulation of the Electronic Communications Act was aimed primarily at protecting individuals who are always weaker than the position of entrepreneurs. It is irrelevant to us whether it is a natural person who is not in business or a natural person who is in business. Thank you for your attention.
