ParlaMint-CZ_2014-09-18-ps2013-017-03-003-026.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the aim of the proposed legislation on explosives precursors and the amendment of Act No. 634/2004 Coll., on administrative fees, is to ensure national regulation within the Czech Republic for the implementation of the regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council of 98/2013 on the marketing and use of explosives precursors. This Regulation is intended to regulate, in the framework of the fight against the threat of terrorism, access by the general public to selected, commonly available chemicals, e.g. certain products in the field of swimming pool chemistry, cleaning products, modeling supplies, etc. [[11.10 hours]] Although it is a directly applicable regulation of the European Union, it is necessary to adopt national legislation which, in particular, regulates the activities of the authorities which will implement, control and, where appropriate, impose sanctions. In the absence of the law, the regulation would impose an absolute ban on the sale of substances identified by the regulation as explosives precursors to persons from the general public in the Czech Republic, which would have adverse effects on both the national economy and the consumer public. The Czech Republic has chosen a regime that will allow citizens to access explosives precursors either on the basis of a permit for their acquisition, which will be issued by the district mining authorities, or by registering the sale of selected commodities. The Act, like the Regulation, concerns the relationship between manufacturers or vendors of explosives precursors on one side and persons from the general public on the other. It does not concern the making available of explosives precursors between businessmen. The Act therefore lays down the conditions for the granting of authorisations, conditions and arrangements for the registration of sales, obligations of sellers, manufacturers and distributors of explosives precursors, and further determines the contact point to carry out the tasks of the European Commission and receives information on suspicious transactions. The proposed law requires that products containing explosives precursors are clearly marked on their packaging in such a way as to make it clear that their acquisition is possible only if the conditions are met. The authorities of the State Mining Administration, the Czech Mining Authority and the district mining authorities and the authorities of the Czech Trade Inspection Authority will be responsible for carrying out the control activities. The exercise of the control activity will consist, on the one hand, in the continuous control of the fulfilment of the obligations and, on the other hand, in the subsequent investigation of incentives, shortcomings and checks. The law was ordered at first reading to discuss the Economic Committee of the Chamber of Deputies. The Committee discussed on 19 June, and the Economic Committee recommends that the Chamber of Deputies discuss and approve the House press 213 as amended by the Government Bill. Thank you for your attention.
