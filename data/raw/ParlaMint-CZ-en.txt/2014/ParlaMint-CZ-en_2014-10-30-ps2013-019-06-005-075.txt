ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-30-ps2013-019-06-005-075.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the material submitted to you contains a proposal to ratify the so-called Treaty of Police Cooperation between the Czech Republic and Armenia. The purpose of this Agreement is to provide the necessary legal framework for effective cooperation between the police authorities of both States in combating all forms of crime. The agreement allows, for example, the exchange of operational and strategic information, whether transmitted on request or spontaneously, the creation of working teams, the broadcasting of consultants and liaison officers, cooperation in the search for persons and matters or cooperation in the field of armed security escorts, for example in aircraft. The Agreement also provides for the requirements of the request for cooperation, the possibility of refusal of cooperation, reimbursement of costs, protection of personal data or classified information. It's a standard contract. The text is based on the Czech draft treaty and contains provisions that enable and facilitate the sharing of information transmitted by the Armenian authorities with other states of the European Union, respectively Schengen States, for border control purposes and the issuance of visas. Thank you for your attention.
