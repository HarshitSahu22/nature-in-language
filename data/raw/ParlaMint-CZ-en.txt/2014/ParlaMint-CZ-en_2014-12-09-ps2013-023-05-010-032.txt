ParlaMint-CZ_2014-12-09-ps2013-023-05-010-032.u8	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present a report for the first reading of press no. 219 on local fees, which is a proposal by Members of Parliament and Members of Zelienková, Pastuchová and others for the issue of the Act amending Act No. 565/1990 Coll., on local fees. It is true that, on the basis of the legislation in force in the Local Charges Act, municipalities can charge local charges for children and subsequently executable them. In the event of late payment and payment of fees, according to § 11 para. 3 The local tax law may increase the fee to three times. This is quite common in practice, particularly in the case of charges for the operation of collection, collection, transport, sorting, recovery and disposal of municipal waste. Unpaid waste charges have been measured directly in the past to children, and the child usually learns about the existence of its finally measured debt only after reaching the age, when he starts to enforce it, including an increase in the costs of execution. The aim of the bill is to exempt selected categories of minors from the fees for the operation of the system of collection, collection, preparation, sorting, recovery and disposal of municipal waste and to transfer the fee obligation from minors to their legal representative. Furthermore, this regulation is intended to allow municipalities to mitigate the excessive hardness of the law by fully or partially condoning this fee. It is true that if we move the law from first reading to second reading, then some system solutions, including retroactivity, may need to be addressed. Thank you.
