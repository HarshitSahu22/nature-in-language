Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the amendment of the Kyoto Protocol to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change, the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change and the Kyoto Protocol to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (the House Print 79/ - first reading) I ask the Minister of the Environment, Richard Brabec, to take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-06-ps2013-006-03-008-044.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, good morning. Let me briefly comment on the Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic will submit its consent to ratify the amendment of the Kyoto Protocol to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change, the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change and the Kyoto Protocol. The Czech Republic has been a Contracting Party to the Convention since 21 March 1994 and a Contracting Party to the Protocol since 16 February 2005. The amendment to the Protocol was adopted at the 8th meeting of its Contracting Parties, which took place from 26 November to 7 December 2012 in Doha, Qatar. The amendment to the Kyoto Protocol consists primarily in defining new commitments to reduce greenhouse gas emissions for economically advanced countries to be met by 2020. The purpose of the measure is to contribute to the stabilisation of greenhouse gas concentrations in the atmosphere at a level that would prevent significant negative impacts on the environment and the population and thus reduce the expected public and private expenditure. The EU and its 27 Member States then committed themselves together with Croatia and Iceland to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 20 per cent by 2020 compared to 1990. I would like to stress that the reduction target adopted is fully consistent with the objective set by EU legislation within the framework of the so-called 'EU regulation'. the climate-energy package of 2009. The amendment of the Kyoto Protocol to this effect will therefore not require an amendment to the legislation in force in that field, nor will it have an impact on the state budget, the public administration or the corporate sphere. The Government's proposal to adopt an amendment to the Protocol also includes a proposal for additional ratification of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change and the Kyoto Protocol adopted on this Convention, originally approved as Government Treaties. The reason for this step is only a formal reconciliation of the process of negotiating these contracts with the existing provisions of the Constitution of the Czech Republic, which requires the approval of the Parliament of the Czech Republic and subsequent ratification by the President of the Republic for such contracts. Thank you for your attention.
