ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-06-ps2013-006-03-003-039.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the purpose of the contract is to provide the legal framework necessary for effective cooperation between police and customs authorities, respectively. other authorities of the two States responsible for combating crime. Cooperation under the Treaty will cover all forms of crime. Cooperation will include the exchange of operational strategic information, whether transmitted on request or spontaneously, the creation of working teams, the broadcasting of consultants and liaison officers, cooperation in the search for persons and matters and in the protection of witnesses. The contract shall lay down the requirements of the request for cooperation, the possibility of non-cooperation, the reimbursement of the costs associated with this and the procedures for the transfer of personal data and classified information. The Treaty reflects the fact that the Czech Republic is part of the Schengen area, therefore contains important provisions enabling the Czech Party to share information transmitted by the Israeli Party with other Member States of the European Union, respectively. the Schengen area within the Schengen Information System and other databases under European law, without the need to seek the consent of the Israeli Party in any particular case. From the point of view of national law, this is the so-called Treaty of the Presidential category, which requires the approval of both chambers of the Parliament of the Czech Republic and ratification by the President of the Czech Republic for its validity. Thank you for your attention.
