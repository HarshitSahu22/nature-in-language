ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-01-ps2013-017-09-002-129.u2	Hello, Mr President, honourable colleagues from the government, Members, Members. Submitted draft amendment of Act No. 239/2013 Coll. was discussed in detail at the second reading last week. Surely, his detailed introduction is no longer necessary. Let me just sum up the main points briefly. Act No. 239/2013 Coll., which substantially amends Act No. 56/2001 Coll., on the conditions of operation of vehicles on roads, came into force in August last year. Its effectiveness, with the exception of one provision, was established on 1 January 2015. This law introduces, among other things, some entirely new institutes and procedures in the field of registration marks. These are especially wish signs, the possibility to exchange damaged plate with the designated registration mark without the need to assign a new and the possibility to issue a third plate with the assigned registration mark, for example, on bicycle carriers. The Act also includes a new modification of the information system of technical inspection stations, in which it is a new element of the insertion of data showing the presence of the vehicle at the station, which implies technical equipment on the part of the technical inspection station operator to enable image recordings to be taken, i.e. video and photos. The application of the abovementioned institutes, which form only part of Act 239 and are therefore not key provisions, would have caused difficulties from the point of view of organisational and technical security since January 2015, as has been shown in the preparation of the implementation of the new legislation and the processing of implementing decrees. Therefore, the proposed bill shifts the entry into force of these selected provisions by 12 months, which is on 1 January 2016. It is clear from the previous discussion as well as from Mr Stanjura's applied amendment that only delaying the inclusion of data at the technical inspection station in the STK CIS information system can be seen as problematic. Let me just reiterate that Law 239 as a whole will clearly contribute to improving the quality of the operation of technical inspection stations and enable effective control. The delay in the effectiveness of this partial requirement on the contents of the STK information system will not change anything. At the same time, the shift of efficiency will create a sufficient transitional period which was not in the law, not only for the State, but also for operators of technical control stations for gradual connection to the central system of STK CIS, so as to ensure full functionality of the system and at the same time the technical equipment of the STK stations can be used as far as possible and thus there is no increase in the additional costs of operators. In view of the above, I cannot therefore agree with this amendment and I would like to ask you to approve the bill in the version submitted. Thank you.
