ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-26-ps2013-007-06-005-050.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the material shall be presented in accordance with the provisions of Article 43 (2). Annex V.Part 2.116 (d) (a) Constitutional Act No. 1/1993 Coll., Constitution of the Czech Republic, as amended by the Constitutional Act No. 300/200 Coll., according to which the government decides to transit the armed forces of other states through the territory of the Czech Republic or to fly over the territory of the Czech Republic. The Government's decision allows for the implementation, without undue delay, of the requirements for the crossings and transits of the armed forces of other states referred to in Government Resolution No. 988 of 18 December 2013. At the same time, it allows the armed forces of the Czech Republic to apply on the principle of reciprocity for permission to fly through the territories of the states mentioned in the said Government resolution. Part of the material, the reference report for the Parliament of the Czech Republic, contains an evaluation of existing practice in the approval of overflights and transits of armed forces of other states through the territory of the Czech Republic and a detailed justification for the solution proposed for 2014, in particular with regard to the obligations arising for the Czech Republic from NATO membership. In a similar way, the issue of flights and transits of armed forces of other states through the territory of the Czech Republic was also solved in previous years. Mr President, colleagues, thank you for your attention.
