Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Protocol governing the Treaty between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Singapore on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income taxes and signed in Singapore on 26 June 2013 /German press 111/ - first reading Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-20-ps2013-007-03-013-028.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Protocol governing the Treaty between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Singapore on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income tax was submitted to the Chamber of Deputies and the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic in August last year. In view of the political situation, the debate of the Parliamentary press number 1133 and Senate press 178 was suspended with the end of the parliamentary term of the Chamber of Deputies. This contractual document is therefore submitted repeatedly to the Parliament of the Czech Republic. The Treaty between the Czech Republic and Singapore is currently in force between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Singapore on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income tax signed in Singapore on 21 November 1997. This Treaty entered into force on 21 August 1998 and was published in the Czech Republic in the Collection of Laws under No. 224/1998. The protocol signed at the same time is an integral part of this Treaty. The initiative to negotiate another protocol to the contract came from the Ministry of Finance of the Czech Republic due to the need to ensure the currently applied standards in the field of tax exchange and to update certain provisions of the contract. The negotiation of a protocol in the field of international tax relations is nothing special. The Czech Republic has negotiated a similar protocol e.g. in the tax agreement with the Netherlands, Denmark, Moldova, Serbia, Russia, Belarus, etc. The Protocol, which will form an integral part of the Treaty, will contribute to greater legal certainty and to better coordination of the activities of the tax authorities of both states which aim to limit possible tax evasion and fraud. In this context, it can be noted that a completely new text of the article Exchange of Information has been negotiated, which will ensure its extended form in relation to taxes of all kinds and naming. This protocol will also ensure the collection of banking information from Singapore and break the concept of domestic interests into information in Singapore, which is currently preventing the exchange of information effectively. [[11.00 hours]] On the basis of the above, I propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take into account those facts when discussing the Protocol to the Treaty on the Prevention of Double Taxation with Singapore, and I propose that the Protocol to this international tax treaty be directed to the relevant committees at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
