Government bill amending Act No. 505/1990 Coll., on Metrology, as amended / House print 303/ - first reading The Minister of Industry and Trade Jan Mládek will present the proposal on behalf of the Government. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-29-ps2013-019-05-006-053.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the fundamental objective of the proposal for an amendment to the Act on Metrology is to meet the needs of practice and to eliminate the developmentally overcome requirements. The proposal specifies the rights and obligations of individual authorities that ensure implementation of the law, the Office for Technical Standardization, Metrology and State Testing and the Czech Metrology Institute. It also increases consumer protection when checking measurements set by gauges, as their interests may be significantly damaged by incorrect measurement. According to the proposal, they will be able to request testing of the gauges and the documents obtained will then be used in the solution of the complaint. These may be cases of municipal gauges such as water meters, electricity meters, gas meters and heat meters. The draft amendment also allows design changes to defined gauges, which are no longer being placed on the market, but are subject to metrology control during operation. Furthermore, the proposal provides for the possibility of extending the validity of the measuring instruments. However, the authorisation of this extension depends on the result of the so-called statistical selection tests. A test to determine whether a given set of gauges meets the requirements on the basis of the selection test. This measure will make a significant contribution to reducing the burden on users of defined gauges, as the period of validity of the verification can be extended for the sets of gauges, not individual pieces. Thank you for your attention.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-29-ps2013-019-05-006-053.u3	I thank the Minister and please now the rapporteur for the first reading, Mr Jaroslav Klaška, for the floor. Please, rapporteur, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-29-ps2013-019-05-006-053.u4	Mr Vice-President, Dear Government, fellow Members, allow me to briefly comment on this amendment. In the course of the effectiveness of the Act on Metrology, the development of technologies in this field has moved forward, and, as practice shows, some of the provisions of the Act have already been overcome in the light of this technological development. The aim of the amendment is therefore to respond to the needs of practice, to eliminate developmentally overcome requirements, to adapt certain provisions with regard to application practice, to implement changes in technical nature and to reduce administrative burdens for small and medium-sized entrepreneurs. There are no major changes in the legislation in the field of metrology or European Union law is being implemented by the proposal. That's all from my brief intelligence report. Thank you for your attention.
