The Treaty provides for the conditions for requiring and providing certain specific types of legal assistance, including cooperation in identifying, securing and confiscating the proceeds of crimes, which I think is very important. It should be pointed out that the area of extradition is excluded, i.e. the extradition and surrender of convicted persons, because there is no guarantee that all our already signed standards in the field of human rights have been complied with, so this area is excluded from the contract.
