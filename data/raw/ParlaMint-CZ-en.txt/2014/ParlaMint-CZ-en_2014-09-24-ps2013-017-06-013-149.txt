Proposal for the election of the Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the Interinstitutional Union As far as Parliament's permanent delegation to the Interinstitutional Union is concerned, 144 ballots have been issued, the same number, i.e. 144 tickets, has been surrendered. Only one candidate was voted out, and this was Mr. Milan Šarapatka. From that number it follows that thevorum was 73. 41 Members voted for Milan Šarapatka. So no one was elected in this election.
