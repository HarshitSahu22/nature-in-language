ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-19-ps2013-010-07-009-097.u2	Thanks for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, my dear colleagues, before I make the resolution, I would like to introduce you briefly to the activities of the financial arbiter. There are some interesting things there, especially for those who want or deal with consumer credit and the problem that follows us. The financial arbiter is an out-of-court authority to resolve certain private disputes in the financial market. Arbitr was established on 1 January 2003, so it has been active for 11 years. Since the establishment, the financial arbiter has been part of the Czech National Bank. In the last five years, the adoption of several amendments to the laws has gradually expanded significantly the scope of the financial arbitrary, which is felt by the financial arbitrary until today. At present, the financial arbiter thus resolves disputes between consumers and financial service providers, the provision of payment services, the mediation or provision of consumer credit, the collective investment through a standard fund, the offer, provision or mediation of life insurance or the exchange trade. For ten years after the creation of the financial arbiter, only tens of disputes were dealt with annually, up to 200 disputes in 2012. The year 2013 was marked by a leap increase in the number of disputes pending, up to 700. Also, the number of questions completed almost doubled to 2,300. For the most part, the disputes raised and the questions received concerned the area of consumer and mortgage loans, namely the dispute over the credit management fee. In the last quarter of last year, the financial arbiter had to deal with a joint motion to initiate the procedure, which reached more than 93 thousand proposals. The financial arbiter managed to process the proposals adopted to initiate the procedure while reducing the total number of 2 339 procedures covered by this year's solution. The activities of the financial arbitrary are also activities in the field of education and in increasing the financial literacy of the population by means of websites, articles, interviews published in various media, participation in conferences and seminars. Total expenditure on financial arbitrary activity for 2013 amounted to 16 017 thousand. Crowns. During the year 19 permanent employees and irregularly 20 external collaborators provided the activities of the arbitrary. It is worth mentioning, although it does not concern the 2013 report, how the Institute of Financial Arbitrary has dealt with the enormous increase in the agenda. From 1. 1. 2014 increased staff status by a total of 21 functional posts. In budgetary expenditure, this means an increase to more than twice as much as in 2013. Thank you for your attention.
