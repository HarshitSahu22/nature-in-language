ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-12-ps2013-010-03-014-067.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the main objective of this treaty is the regulation of arms exports, and what I think is very important is that the Czech Republic, together with the other Member States, belongs from a very advanced system of control of international arms trade from a global point of view to a small group of countries where these transactions are already subject to authorisation, or, if you wish, licensing, in order to prevent the risks associated with the international movement of these goods. This contract is therefore fully in line with the relevant standards of the Czech rule of law, in particular the Foreign Trade Act on Military Materials and other generally recognised principles of international law and obligations already taken over by the Czech Republic under other international treaties. So no provision of the Czech rule of law for ratification of this Treaty needs to be amended. Another important information is that this agreement will have no negative impact on the business environment, because the Czech Republic is already implementing everything that is contained in it and, on the contrary, it can be said that it will be for us, virtually. for our business entities, in a way favourable, because it lays down stricter conditions for other states, so it can be said that they will not be disadvantaged.
