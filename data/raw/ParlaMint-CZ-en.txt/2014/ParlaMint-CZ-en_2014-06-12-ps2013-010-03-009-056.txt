ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-12-ps2013-010-03-009-056.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, a new protocol to the international treaty relating to international taxation is tabled at the meeting. In this particular case, it is a protocol to an existing double taxation treaty with Singapore. The Treaty between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Singapore on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income tax signed in Singapore on 21 November 1997 is currently being applied in mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and Singapore. This Treaty entered into force on 21 August 1998 and was published in the Czech Republic in the Collection of Laws under No. 224/1998 Coll. The protocol signed at the same time is an integral part of this Treaty. The Czech Republic has agreed protocols on tax agreements with several states and negotiations are ongoing on the negotiation of protocols with other states in order to comply with international standards, particularly in the field of information exchange. The protocol, which will form an integral part of the treaty, will certainly contribute to greater legal certainty and, above all, to better coordination of the activities of the tax authorities of both states in order to limit possible tax evasion and fraud. In this context, it can be noted that a completely new text of the article Exchange of Information has been negotiated, which will ensure its extended form in relation to taxes of all kinds and naming. This protocol will also ensure the collection of banking information from Singapore and break the concept of domestic interest into information in Singapore, which is currently preventing effective exchange of information. I therefore recommend that the Chamber of Deputies of the Czech Republic take into account those facts when discussing the protocol on the double taxation treaty and agree to its ratification. Thank you for your attention.
