ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-11-ps2013-006-05-002-075.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the bill amending the Act on elections to the European Parliament and certain other laws has already been introduced here at its first reading. So let me briefly remind you of it and remind you of its contents. In particular, the draft law responds to the adoption of a directive of the European Union on the rules governing the exercise of the right to be elected in elections to the European Parliament. Better conditions are created for candidacy in elections to the European Parliament for citizens of the Union who decide to run in a non-home Member State. The applicant's obligation to submit a certificate from the home Member State that he has not been deprived of the right to be elected or that such electoral incapacity is not known to the competent authority shall be removed. This obligation is now replaced by an honest statement by the candidate that he has not been deprived of the right to be elected. The Member State of residence for which the candidacy is envisaged shall then refer to the Member State of origin of the candidate with a request for verification of such a declaration. [[Submitted?]] In the event that the information from the Member State of origin denies the candidate's declaration of honour, the Member State of residence shall take all steps necessary to establish the residence in order to prevent him from running for office (?), where appropriate, in the election or exercise of the mandate. The provisions governing the census of votes shall be adapted so that, as soon as the vote is completed in the elections to the European Parliament in the territory of the Czech Republic, that is to say Saturday at 14 o'clock, the district electoral committee will begin the vote counting and the results of the vote for the electoral districts will be forwarded to the Czech Statistical Office, which will process them and will wait until the vote in the elections to the European Parliament in all the Member States of the Union with overall electoral results. On the basis of the European Commission's notice, the bill also regulates the conditions for the registration of citizens of other Member States in the electoral list for elections to the European Parliament and the exchange of information between Member States in order to avoid any duplication of votes. Last but not least, the bill projectes a change in the electoral laws introduced by the new Civil Code, which took effect on 1 January 2014. Given that the directive should have been transposed into the legal systems of the Member States by 28 January 2014, and in view of the fact that, under the proposed legislation, elections to the European Parliament should already take place in May of this year, the proposed law should become effective as soon as possible. I would therefore like to make a proposal later in the context of a similar debate. The Chamber of Deputies to shorten the deadline for initiating the third reading of the proposal within 48 hours. Thank you for your attention.
