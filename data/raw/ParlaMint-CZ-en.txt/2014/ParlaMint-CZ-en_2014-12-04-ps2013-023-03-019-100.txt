Proposal for the election of members of the Council of the State Fund of Culture of the Czech Republic The fourth choice, it was the difficult one, it was the 25 names to the Council of the State Fund of Culture of the Czech Republic. Although there were many names and candidates, we had to maintain the parameters of the secret choice, i.e. we can only choose who gets an absolute number of votes. Only one candidate became one, and it was Mr. Jiří Dědeček. Once again - Jiří Dědeček was elected, he won 78 votes. According to the provisions of the secret election to the second round, the number of candidates is twice as many as the number of vacant posts, so it happens to be based on the 24th. So all 24 names in the quotes failed in the first round proceed to the second round.
