ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-11-ps2013-006-05-005-035.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, the proposed amendment to the Trade Act has been prepared in order to continue to improve the business environment in the Czech Republic by further reducing the administrative burden on entrepreneurs. The administrative burden is reduced by abolishing the obligation of entrepreneurs to repeatedly document to the municipal trade office the documents that have already been documented to a trade office and the facts which certify these documents have remained unchanged. Trade offices will create electronic copies of the documents submitted by entrepreneurs when entering or during the trade business, which will be stored in the trade register's data repository. These documents will then be accessible to all trade offices in the Czech Republic. Upon request of the Office for the Protection of Personal Data and the Public, an amendment to the Trade Register shall be carried out which shall contribute to the improvement of the protection of private personal data kept in this Register. There will be no disclosure of previously accessible data on the address of the place of residence, the address of the place of residence of entrepreneurs and other natural persons registered in that register, and then to disclose all data on the entrepreneur registered in the trade register after the expiry of four years from the date of expiry of his last trade licence. I would like to ask, ladies and gentlemen, for the support of this proposal and its release for second reading. I would also like to stress that this proposal was submitted by the previous government, but I am fully adopting it. Thank you for your attention.
