ParlaMint-CZ_2014-09-19-ps2013-017-04-006-021.u2	Thank you. Mr President, fellow Members, the bill amending Act No. 93/2009 Coll., on auditors and on the amendment of certain laws, I mentioned and presented the main headings of proposed amendments at the 10th meeting of the Chamber of Deputies of the Czech Republic on 10 June 2014. So let me briefly repeat the substance. In our legal order, the Auditor Act lays down the requirements of the European Union in the field of regulatory of statutory audit and auditing professions. Directive No 1 of the European Parliament and of the Council provides the basis for this legislation. 2006/43/EC on statutory audits of annual and consolidated accounts. The proposal amending the Auditor Act, which we are discussing here, primarily responds to the adoption of additional EU legislation, in particular the European Commission Recommendation No. 2008/326/EC laying down the provisions of that Directive relating to the quality control system of statutory auditors and audit firms. The recommendation is a guide on how the quality of the work of the statutory auditor and audit firm should be ensured. Public oversight plays an important role in the quality control system of auditors. The Audit Public Supervision Board, established by the Audit Act in 2009, is hereby entrusted with the oversight function of the quality control system. However, it has proved necessary to strengthen its position so that its competence in the supervision of the quality of audit activities can be effectively exercised. The draft law, which is now submitted to you, therefore equips the Audit Public Supervision Council with certain other powers in the field of quality control and control systems, as well as with the application of administrative offences that are newly defined in the Act. The main areas of change are the adjustments to the quality control system and to the public oversight and scope of the Audit Board. As regards further adjustments made, these are changes responding to experience based on the almost five-year application of the Auditor Act, when the need arose to remedy identified uncertainties or shortcomings, as well as legislative technical adjustments related, for example, to the recodification of private law or to the adoption of other legislation to which it was necessary to respond. Thank you for your attention. And now I dare ask you to release the bill amending Act No. 93/2009 Coll., on auditors and on the amendment of certain laws, until the third reading. Thank you.
