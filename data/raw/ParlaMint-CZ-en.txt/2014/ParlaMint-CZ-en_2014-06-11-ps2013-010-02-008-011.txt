ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-11-ps2013-010-02-008-011.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly state the Government's bill amending Act 266/2006 Coll., on employee accident insurance, as amended, and other related laws. The proposed bill is of a technical nature when the provisions on the entry into force of the Staff Accident Insurance Act are shifted by one year, i.e. with effect from 1 January 2016. Similar adjustments are also made in laws which refer to and follow the Staff Accident Insurance Act. The reason for this delay of effectiveness is the need to obtain the time necessary in particular for the overall assessment of all possibilities of employee safety in an accident at work and, where appropriate, in an occupational disease. If the most appropriate way of securing employees in these cases is decided, the chosen solution will be translated into the legal order of the Czech Republic. I can inform you that the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs is currently discussing material of a non-legal nature with an objective description of the current state of security during work accidents and occupational diseases and with an assessment of the real variants of the future material solution of this security. This material should be submitted to the Government of the Czech Republic for a decision, not later than the third quarter of this year. The draft law was discussed by the Committee on Social Policy, which recommends that the draft law be approved by proposing the effectiveness of the Staff Accident Insurance Act to be postponed by two years. I can say that I agree with this amendment and consider it rational, because it will create sufficient time for future legal solutions, whatever option is chosen, because there will always be a need for extensive legislative intervention. Thank you for your attention.
