ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-06-ps2013-006-03-020-056.u2	Thank you. Let me remind you that now we have two ballot points, number 56 and 59, where the election will be made in a secret way, using tickets and urns in the State Acts. Now to paragraph 56, the motion to elect a member of the Supreme Audit Office. To date, one seat of the Supreme Audit Office is still vacant and the President of the SAO has submitted a proposal for the election within the statutory deadline. Until the dissolution of the Chamber of Deputies last year, however, the election of a member of the Supreme Audit Office did not take place. On 20 December 2013, the President of the Supreme Audit Office submitted a new proposal to the Chamber of Deputies to elect a member of the SAO and it is a proposal to Mrs . Hana Hykšová. On this point, the Election Commission has adopted its resolution number 31, which I will now read to you. That wasn't dealt to you on your desks. It is resolution number 31 of the Election Commission of the 6th meeting on 23 January, where the Election Commission instructs President Martin Kolovratník to introduce you to the following points: firstly, the proposal of a candidate for a member of the Supreme Audit Office, as submitted by the President of the SAO, Mrs . Hana Hykšová; and secondly, with the fact that the special law does not provide for a method of choice and the electoral commission proposes a secret choice; Thirdly, the newly elected member shall take up office by making a pledge in the hands of the President of the Chamber of Deputies of Parliament. This motion for a resolution has been referred in writing to all Members' clubs. As I have already quoted from the resolution, the special law does not provide for a choice and we in the Commission propose to make a secret choice. Now, Mr President, I ask you to open the debate, both on the proposed candidate and on the way in which to vote. I may add that the proposed candidate has fulfilled and prescribed all the documented documents deposited in the Secretariat of the Election Commission. Please.
