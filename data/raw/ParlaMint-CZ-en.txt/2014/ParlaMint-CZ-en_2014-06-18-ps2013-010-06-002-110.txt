ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-18-ps2013-010-06-002-110.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, allow me, on behalf of the Government, to present to you the draft law No. 266/1994 Coll., on Railways, as amended. The Government submits to the Parliament of the Czech Republic a draft law amending Act No. 266/1994 Coll., on Railways, as amended. This is a brief partial amendment to the Railway Act aimed exclusively at the implementation of EU law. The proposed bill constitutes the last corrective measure to respond to the judgment of the Court of Justice of the European Union in Case C-555/10. In that decision, the Court held, inter alia, that the decision of the Railway Authority as a regulatory body pursuant to Article 4 (1) (a) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union was taken by the Court of Justice in accordance with Article 4 (1) (b) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. 30 Directive 2001/14/EC, in particular the area of the allocation of transport capacity, its use and negotiation of the price for its use, can only be reviewed by the court and this review must not be preceded by an administrative review, i.e. under the Act on Railways of Appeal to the Ministry of Transport. The submitted draft amendment to the Railway Act responds to this requirement and in the case of selected procedures conducted The Railway Authority excludes the administrative review carried out by the Ministry of Transport. The decisions of the Railway Authority in these cases will therefore only be contested by a legal action. From the point of view of the substance of the mechanism described above, the procedure concerning the content of the runway declaration, the allocation of transport capacity, the disputes in the conclusion of contracts between the railway operator and the carrier on the operation of rail transport and the procedure for temporary limitation and stopping rail transport is included. Also included are selected offences, in the vast majority of the owner or railway operator, which are related to the breach of capacity allocation obligations and the limitation or stopping of rail transport. According to statistics, as decided by the Railway Authority and the Ministry of Transport in recent years, it is clear that the proposed model will fall to a relatively small number of decisions by the Railway Authority. The release by appeal of an administrative review of the Railway Authority's first-instance decision should therefore not result in a significant increase in the actions and possible congestion of the courts. It should also be said that the proposed solution represents, in particular, a rapid response, as far as possible, to the judgment of the Court of Justice and takes into account the existing organisation of state administration in the field of rail transport and the material possibilities of individual offices. However, this is only a temporary solution, pending the transposition of the European Directive 2012/34/EU next year. This transposition will be carried out by an extensive amendment to the Railway Act, in which, inter alia, the position, activity and powers of the regulatory body will be conceptually newly defined following the new regulation of this area in Directive 2012/34. The proposal was discussed by the Economic Committee and recommended it for approval. Thank you.
