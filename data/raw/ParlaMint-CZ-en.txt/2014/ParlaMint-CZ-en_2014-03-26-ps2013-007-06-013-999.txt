ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-26-ps2013-007-06-013-999.u1	Point No 43 The first electoral point, or the first election, was point No. 43, Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the OSCE Parliamentary Assembly. We voted, or we commented, on the proposal of a candidate - Ivana Dobešová from the ANO movement, and I note that 176 ballots were issued in this election, the same number of tickets were submitted and 110 Members voted for Ivana Dobešová. Mrs. Dobešová has been elected. I congratulate her with this. [[Let's hear it.]] I look forward to seeing her as our dignified representative in this honored plenary.
