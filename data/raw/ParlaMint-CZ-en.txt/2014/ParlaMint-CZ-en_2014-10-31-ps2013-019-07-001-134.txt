ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-31-ps2013-019-07-001-134.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to follow up the process of discussing this bill, which, in accordance with the Government's programming declaration, improves patients' access to spa care while at the same time adjusting the conditions for reimbursement of spa care from the public health insurance system, so that the legislation in this area is in line with the March decision of the Constitutional Court, which at the end of this year repeals the current 2012 decree on the so-called indicative list for spa rehabilitation care. [[10.10 hours]] As a basic problem from the point of view of medicine, the length of stay for individual diagnoses is currently seen. The 2012 Decree consolidated the duration of the stay to 21 days with the possibility of extension. Practice has shown that this possibility of extension is not used as it would be desirable, and often spa patients are released without sufficient improvement in their health status. The design therefore determines the length of stay of the spa treatment again for 28 days for selected indications. This is mainly the treatment of diseases of the circulatory, respiratory and musculoskeletal tract, mental disorders, skin or gynaecological diseases. Furthermore, the possibility of extending repeated treatment stays is extended, in particular in children and adolescents, for whom this possibility is limited by the current decree. At the same time, the specific needs of individual groups of patients were taken into account, and it is therefore proposed, for example, that people suffering from interstitial pulmonary fibrosis as an occupational disease who were entitled only to a comprehensive stay and repeated stays could only benefit from this care during repeated stays. Mr President, honourable Members, the proposal tabled improves patients' access to spa rehabilitation care while at the same time complying with the conclusions of the Constitutional Court. I therefore believe that you will also support it in the subsequent legislative process. Thank you for your attention and support.
