ParlaMint-CZ_2014-04-30-ps2013-008-02-008-010.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to mention the Government's bill amending Act No. 435/2004 Coll., on Employment, as amended, and Act No. 582/1991 Coll., on the organisation and implementation of social security, as amended, and Act No. 251/2005 Coll., on Labour Inspection, as amended. The reason for submitting this government bill is, in particular, further reduction of the administrative burden on employers, support for employment of people with disabilities and regulation of control activities in the employment sector. The Government's bill is a comprehensive amendment to several legislations. The fundamental change in this amendment is the introduction of a category of persons with disabilities as a sub-category of natural persons with disabilities and the related powers of district social security administrations, which will assess the health status of natural persons and decide on their inclusion in the category of persons with disabilities. This is related to the introduction of a contribution to the employment of disabled people at a protected post. An important part of the bill is the incorporation of the possibility of the Office of Labour of the Czech Republic to find the necessary data directly from the competent authorities in case of their waiver of confidentiality by the employer to the Office of Labour of the Czech Republic, as well as the new facts of offences and administrative offences related to the control of illegal work and the management of records in the field of the provision of products or services in the performance of the so-called compulsory share and, last but not least, a reduction in the minimum amount of fines for administrative offenses allowing the execution of illegal work from 2 500 (?) CZK 50,000. Finally, the Government's proposal contains legislative technical adjustments resulting from the fact that the Employment Act was amended several times in 2011 and 2012. The Government's proposal was ordered to discuss the Committee on Social Policy, which recommended it to be approved in the version of the parliamentary amendment prepared by the MPSV. The amendment to the Government Bill, in addition to some technical specifications and minor modifications, above all increases the maximum amount of the contribution to the employment of disabled people at a protected post of CZK 5,000, with the possibility of increasing the contribution in the case of a disabled person by a maximum of CZK 1000 - variant 5+1. It addresses the conflict with the draft law amending Act No. 326/1999 Coll., on the residence of foreigners in the territory of the Czech Republic and on the amendment of certain laws, as amended, Act No. 435/2004 Coll., on Employment, and other related laws, House printing 30, repealing the Green Card Institute, and introducing an employee card as a new type of residence permit. I have no objection to the above amendment contained in the resolution of the Social Policy Committee.
