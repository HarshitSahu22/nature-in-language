Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic on cooperation on the register of residents and birth numbers signed on 20 January 2014 in Prague /German press 138/ - first reading Again, I ask Minister Brabec to give the opening word for the government. ParlaMint-CZ_2014-05-15-ps2013-008-07-004-042.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the purpose of the contract is, in particular, to remove the inaccuracies of the data on citizenship held in the information systems of the population of the two states that were created as a result of the division of the CSFR. To this end, there will be a one-off comparison of data on persons with an indication of citizenship of the CSFR with the population of the other state. The contract is also intended to remove incorrect data in the register of birth numbers and to ensure reciprocal recognition of the birth numbers allocated before 1. 1 1993 in the Czech Republic in the Slovak Republic. The Czech Republic already recognises the birth numbers allocated before that date in the Slovak Republic. However, the Treaty does not only solve problems arising from the division of the CSFR, but also allows for the exchange of information with the registration of residents and the register of birth numbers in order to ensure the up-to-dateness and accuracy of the data kept in them. The contract will also allow the provision of data from population records for the purposes of searching property owners to government and self-government authorities. The contract also contains a personal data protection regime. The details of the implementation of the cooperation under the contract shall be laid down in the Implementing Protocol. Thank you for your attention.
