Allow me to present to you the Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Framework Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Federal Republic of Germany on cross-border cooperation in the field of medical rescue services signed in Pilsen on 4 April 2013 - hereinafter referred to as the Framework Agreement. This government proposal was already discussed by some of the institutions of the Parliament of the Czech Republic in 2013, but due to the dissolution of the Chamber of Deputies, the ratification process was not completed. For this reason, the Government's proposal to Parliament of the Czech Republic was tabled again for discussion this year. So let me briefly remind you of this international treaty. The Framework Agreement is the first international treaty that allows the intervention of one state's exit group of medical rescue services in the territory of the other state, which the Czech Republic has negotiated. So far, cross-border cooperation, which also includes medical rescue services as part of an integrated rescue system, is regulated only in case of emergency and disaster assistance. The main motive for establishing cross-border cooperation in this area is in particular the temporal aspect of the availability of emergency pre-hospital care in the border area. With regard to the national division of competences in the field of medical rescue services in both states - in the Czech Republic they have legal responsibility for its provision and organisation of the region within their separate competence, in the Federal Republic the responsible state ministries of the interior of individual free states - are presented an international contract designed as a framework which defines the basic legal framework for cross-border cooperation, allows for certain exceptions to existing national legislation and leaves room for the specification of the rules and conditions of such cooperation in the individual border areas on the agreement of authorised bodies responsible for the provision of medical rescue services, that is in the Czech Republic of Liberec, Ústecký, Karlovarský, Pilsen and South Bohemia Region, in the Federal Republic of State Ministries of the Interior of the Federal States of Saxony and Bavaria. The use and effective fulfilment of cross-border cooperation in this area will thus be decided by the regions concerned and requires separate arrangements for cross-border cooperation to be concluded. The regions will be able to use cross-border cooperation to ensure the continuous availability of emergency pre-hospital care, especially in the sparsely populated and difficult to reach border areas on the Czech side. [[9.30 hours]] In the first phase cross-border cooperation is expected mainly on an ad hoc basis according to the current need. In the longer term, the region could use this cooperation more systematically. Both Contracting Parties presume reciprocal reciprocity of cross-border cooperation and its use in those cases where this is appropriate from a time point of view with regard to the deployment of exit bases for medical rescue services and adequate emergency care providers. For this reason, it is assumed that the implementation of the framework agreement will not bring increased claims on the state budget or on public health insurance funds. The framework agreement falls within the category of so-called presidential treaties, which is why it is submitted before ratification by the President of the Republic to express its consent to both chambers of Parliament of the Czech Republic. The negotiation of this international treaty is a foreign policy priority for both countries, this topic is regularly the subject of negotiations by the top representatives of both states and at regional level this form of cooperation has support. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, thank you for your attention.
