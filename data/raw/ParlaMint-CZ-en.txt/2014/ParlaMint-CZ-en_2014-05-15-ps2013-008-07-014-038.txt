ParlaMint-CZ_2014-05-15-ps2013-008-07-014-038.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to present to you a contractual document relating to international taxation. This is a completely new treaty to prevent double taxation with the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg. This Treaty will replace in mutual tax relations between the two countries a double taxation treaty signed in 1991, which does not sufficiently reflect current relations between the Czech Republic and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg. It can be recalled that this new treaty was already discussed in the Czech Parliament during the last parliamentary term, but the second reading in the Chamber of Deputies was no longer held, and therefore it needed to be renegotiated, even in the Senate, which even approved its ratification in June 2013 by its resolution number 242. The Treaty has now been renegotiated in the relevant Houses of Committees, always with the recommendation of consent to its ratification. For information it can be said that in April this year, the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic again agreed to ratify it. In principle, this is a comprehensive bilateral agreement which regulates taxation and, at the same time, the exclusion of international double taxation of different types of income and which will certainly help to develop mutual economic activities. This Treaty provides for the basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of the Czech Republic and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, laying down the principle of equal treatment of entities of both Contracting Parties, allowing informal settlement of disputes arising in its interpretation and implementation. It also allows for the exchange of information in the context of taxes of all kinds and the designation between the competent authorities of the Contracting Parties. The Treaty does not impose any other obligations on taxpayers beyond the tax laws in force in the Contracting States. This international tax agreement will not have a direct impact on the state budget of the Czech Republic. The overall contribution resulting from the conclusion of the contract will depend on the pace of development of the various forms of economic cooperation between the two countries. So I would like to propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take into account the above-mentioned facts in the discussion of the double taxation treaty between the Czech Republic and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg and agree to its ratification. Thank you for your attention.
