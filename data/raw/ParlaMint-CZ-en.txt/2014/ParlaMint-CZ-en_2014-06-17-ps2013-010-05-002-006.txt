The Government proposal completes the transposition of European Directive 2011/82/EU on facilitating the cross-border exchange of information on road safety traffic offences and Directive 2006/126/EC on driving licences and their two amendments. From a factual point of view, the proposed proposal contains, in particular, the provision of basic information on traffic offences in the official language of another Member State of the European Union, unless the offence is a citizen of the Czech Republic. This is the completion of the transposition of Directive 2011/82/EU. The exchange of information on driving licences granted between Member States of the European Union is linked to the start of the electronic system for the exchange of such information within the European Union. It's called. the RESPER system. Here I would like to point out that the Czech Republic has already been criticised several times by the Federal Republic of Germany for not providing such information, virtually. the impossibility of providing such information. In addition, the sub-regulations in the definition of the categories of vehicles for which driving licences are granted follow up the negotiations with the European Commission and eliminate certain minor technological deficiencies. Here is an example of a category D1 microbus driving licence where correction is needed. Furthermore, they are sub-adjustments to the definition of training and test vehicles in relation to the amendments to Directive 2006/126/EC. They change the technical parameters of vehicles and allow for wider use of automated and semi-automated control systems. The Government's proposal was discussed by the Economic Committee, which finally recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to adopt the Government's draft without amendments. The adoption of a government proposal is necessary in order to fulfil the obligations arising from its membership of the European Union in the Czech Republic. Thank you.
