Proposal for the election of the Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the Central European Initiative The next choice concerned the Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the Central European Initiative. Only Mr Stanislav Grospič was proposed. He won 69 votes, so even in this case he was not reached a kvora and was not elected. This election will be repeated, i.e. the second round to which Stanislav Grospič is proceeding will take place.
