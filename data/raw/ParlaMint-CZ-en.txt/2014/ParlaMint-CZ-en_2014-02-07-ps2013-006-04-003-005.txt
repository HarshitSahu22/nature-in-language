Government bill amending Act No. 499/2004 Coll., on Archiving and File Service and on Amendments to Certain Acts, as amended /German Press 31/ - Second Reading The proposed proposal will also be presented here by the Interior Minister Milan Chovanec. Please, Minister, take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-07-ps2013-006-04-003-005.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Dear colleagues, dear colleagues. Let me briefly state the Government's bill amending Act No.499/2004 Coll., on Archiving and File Service and amending certain acts as amended. The draft law is submitted following the adoption of the new Act No.255/2012 Coll., on control, which took effect on 1 January 2014, as it is necessary to harmonise procedures in the field of control, as well as in the field of archiving and filing services. At the same time, the issue of so-called commercial writings is addressed and the provision of expert administration of documents located in these writing rooms, for whose storage is of public interest. This problem was referred to in the 2010 Ombudsman's summary report on the Ombudsman's activities. In particular, it is the establishment of the necessary framework for the obligations of commercial files in the field of professional administration of documents and the obligations of both public and private agents, the establishment of the facts of administrative offences in the event of failure to comply with the obligations laid down and the determination of the competence of the State's regional archives in relation to commercial registers in the field of control. The draft law in question has already been discussed in the first reading of the Chamber of Deputies during the previous parliamentary term, as a press of 1061, within the framework of the Government Bill amending certain laws under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of the Interior in connection with the adoption of the Rules of Auditors. Due to the dissolution of the House no further discussion has taken place. The draft law was discussed by the Supervisory Committee of the Chamber of Deputies, which adopted it with an amendment which deletes certain documents arising from the activities of commercial companies and cooperatives, which will thus no longer be necessary to retain and allow for the selection of archival sites. I agree with this proposal. Thank you for your attention.

ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-07-ps2013-006-04-003-005.u6	Thank you for the floor. Mr Vice-President, ladies and gentlemen, I would just briefly mention the amendment I made to the Audit Committee. I have noticed that the Minister has agreed with him, which I am very pleased about. However, since I had some reactions to him about archivists, I would say a few words. This amendment concerned commercial writings, related to Annex 1, which are documents arising from the activities of cooperative companies, with the exception of housing and notary cooperatives. The purpose of the amendment was to remove some documents so that they were not considered archivals within the meaning of the said Act on Archiving. These were, of course, documents of no value to future generations. Of course, important documents remain in Annex 1, such as the minutes of statutory bodies, supervisory bodies, but not such as, for example, the minutes of operational meetings. That's what we think is completely useless. It is also, of course, a reduction in the administrative burden on business entities and, often, business secrets, which may include these entries from management meetings. So I would like to stress that the essential documents that are of value for the future, for future generations, will certainly be preserved in that annex number one. Thank you.
