ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-01-ps2013-017-09-001-146.u2	Mr President, colleagues. Allow me to introduce a government bill amending Act No. 156/1998 Coll. on fertilisers, soil aids, auxiliary plant preparations and substrates and agrochemical testing of agricultural soils, i.e. the Fertilizers Act, as amended. The submitted government proposal contains only one amendment to the Fertilizers Act, but it is necessary for the practical application of this Act. It is proposed to repeal the provision whereby the central control and testing institute of the agricultural holding repeals the authorisation for the registration of fertilizer or, as the case may be, an auxiliary soil substance or an auxiliary plant product, provided that the soil aid or plant auxiliary product contains the active substance of plant protection products. The intention is to remove the legal uncertainty of the holders of registrations of the products concerned, which this provision, removed from the context of the whole law, causes. The Government's bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies at its 10th meeting on 24 June this year. In its second reading, no amendment was applied. I therefore recommend the approval of this government bill in the submitted text. Thank you for your attention.
