ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-11-ps2013-010-02-014-002.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, Mr Senator, I would briefly like to comment on the returned bill from the Senate. The bill submitted was returned to the Chamber of Deputies by the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. It contains, in particular, adaptation measures in a number of major European Union legislation, particularly in the field of civil aviation security, which have already entered into force and must therefore be applied in full by national authorities. In addition to the necessary and extensive implementation of European Union law, the proposal also responds to problems identified in administrative practice. For example, it introduces simplified procedures in the field of international air transport, lays down clear conditions for border cooperation in the provision of air navigation services and facilitates the provision of claims by providers of such services or removes unnecessary administrative requirements for air personnel. The Chamber of Deputies approved this bill by resolution No. 193 of 26 March 2014 subsequently forwarded to the Senate on 7 April. On the basis of the opinion of the Legislative Department of the Senate Office and its subsequent consultation with representatives of the Ministry of Transport, several errors were identified in the proposal, which however are limited in number and do not affect substantial provisions and are essentially of a technical nature. However, the petitioner considered it appropriate for these defects to be eliminated by means of the Senate's amendments. The bill was subsequently discussed at the 20th meeting of the Senate on 23 April, with the Senate's resolution No. 472 proposed the law to return to the Chamber of Deputies with amendments. The relevant amendments were prepared in cooperation with the petitioner and legislative department of the Senate Office and the Ministry of Transport agrees with them. In the light of the above, the Ministry of Transport asks the Chamber of Deputies to approve the bill returned as amended by the amendments to the Senate. Thank you.
