At present, the birth fee is paid only to the first living child, amounting to CZK 13,000, and is subject to a family income survey. If another child or children born live, twins, multiples, is born with the first live child, the amount of the birth rate is 19,500 crowns. The person entitled to the maternity allowance shall be entitled only if the relevant income does not exceed 2,4 times the family's living minimum. The proposal is presented with the aim of adjusting the birth rate so that the entitlement to this benefit is created by a wider circle of families with children, so that the birth rate of the second child can be paid to these families in the order of the second child, amounting to CZK 10,000. The second child is understood as a second live child, regardless of how many births the second live child is born.
