Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Air Transport Agreement between the European Community and its Member States, of the one part, and Canada, of the other part /German press 133/ - Second reading Once again, instead of the Minister of Transport, Minister Helena Válková justifies this government proposal. Please, Madam Secretary, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-12-ps2013-010-03-012-061.u2	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, the Air Transport Agreement between the European Community and its Member States and Canada is one of the concrete results of the implementation of the European Union's external aviation policy towards key partners in the European Union. It aims to establish uniform and liberal rules for the implementation of air transport between the European Union and Canada. In addition to opening up mutual markets and investment opportunities in air transport, other important areas such as aviation safety, air traffic control and environmental protection are addressed in the agreement. The agreement should contribute to the further development of air transport between the two parties and its negotiation can therefore be considered beneficial from the point of view of the Czech Republic. I would therefore like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to give my consent to ratify this agreement. Thank you.
