ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-13-ps2013-010-04-006-031.u2	Thank you for the floor. Madam President, Members, allow me to present this bill at first reading. The aim is to make partial amendments to the Act on the Organisation and Implementation of Social Security, which will enable problems related to the storage of documents for which the public interest is stored, namely documents found in so-called private writers, from the point of view of social security. In particular, these are the documents needed for the proper implementation of pension insurance. It is a problem that the Ombudsman has also pointed out. There is an obligation on private writers to provide social security authorities with copies of these documents in connection with the safekeeping of the documents necessary for deciding on the claims of insured persons of pension insurance, and if they fail to do so, penalties shall be imposed. At the same time, the procedure for the disappearance of a private file room without a legal successor is regulated. Basically, I don't think it's very useful to read the details here, but it's a technical modification. So, honourable Members, I would like to ask you to release this government proposal for second reading.
