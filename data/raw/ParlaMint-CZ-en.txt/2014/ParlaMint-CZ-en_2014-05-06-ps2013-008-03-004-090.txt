ParlaMint-CZ_2014-05-06-ps2013-008-03-004-090.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, may I present to you the Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the Framework Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Federal Republic of Germany on cross-border cooperation in the field of medical rescue services signed on 4 April 2013, hereinafter referred to as the Framework Agreement. This framework contract is the first international treaty allowing cross-border interventions of the health rescue services exit groups that the Czech Republic has negotiated. So far, cross-border cooperation, which also includes medical rescue services as part of the integrated rescue system of the Czech Republic, is regulated only in case of disaster and emergency assistance. [[18.10 hours]] The main motive for establishing cross-border cooperation in this area is the time-frame for the availability of medical rescue services in border areas. Cross-border cooperation on the Czech side will touch Liberec, Ústecký, Karlovy Vary, Pilsen and South Bohemian Region and on the German side of the federal states of Saxony and Bavaria. The use and effective fulfilment of cross-border cooperation will thus be at the decision of the regions and envisages the conclusion of separate arrangements for cross-border cooperation. The regions will be able to use cross-border cooperation to ensure the continuous availability of medical rescue services, especially in sparsely populated and difficult to reach border areas from the Czech side. Cross-border cooperation thus represents an alternative to the need to build a new exit base for medical rescue services in order to comply with the legal driving time. In the first phase, the use of cross-border cooperation is expected mainly on an ad hoc basis according to the current need. In the longer term, the region could use this cooperation more systematically. Both Parties presume reciprocal reciprocity and use of this form of cooperation in those cases where this is appropriate from a time point of view with regard to the deployment of exit bases for medical rescue services. The regions concerned enter into negotiations with their German partners with a proposal to start cooperation on a free basis and to evaluate their scope, efficiency and financial intensity after a certain period of time. In the light of the above, I therefore believe that cross-border cooperation may be in addition to its primary objective, i.e. saving life and health, bringing a positive financial effect. I believe that the ratification process and the entry into force of this international treaty will be completed as soon as possible. The regions concerned, in cooperation with their German partners, are already preparing individual arrangements for cross-border cooperation and planning details of synergies. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, I am asking you to give your consent to the ratification of this international treaty and thank you for your attention.
