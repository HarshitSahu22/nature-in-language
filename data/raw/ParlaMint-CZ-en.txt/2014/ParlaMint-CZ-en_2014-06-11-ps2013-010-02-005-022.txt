ParlaMint-CZ_2014-06-11-ps2013-010-02-005-022.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I promise, last time on this block, I will allow myself to draw your attention, this time on a subject other than the treaties. The proposed bill aims to adapt to Commission Regulation No 389/2013 on the basis of which the rules in the greenhouse gas emission allowance trading process have changed since 2013. The regulation newly obliges Member States to ensure that operators who are issued more free allowances than they are entitled to under allocation rules return unduly received free allowances to the EU allocation account. Larger quantities may be issued if the operator does not notify a substantial change in the operation of the installation in due time and does not provide appropriate information to the Ministry of Environment. In the proposed amendment, the operator of the installation is obliged to return the unduly issued allowances to the EU allocation account and, at the same time, penalties are laid down for breach of that obligation. The proposal also repeals the obligation for emission verifiers to be authorised to verify greenhouse gas emissions, which is, say, a reduction in red tape, an administrative complexity, where verifiers will continue to be obliged only to have accreditation from the Czech Accreditation Institute, which is granted on the basis of evidence of competence under the relevant Commission regulation. By granting authorization, it currently constitutes an unnecessary administration and puts domestic entities at a disadvantage because, according to the directly applicable Commission regulation, both verifiers and foreign entities can operate in the Czech Republic only on the basis of accreditation obtained in their Member State. By abolishing the obligation to carry out emissions verification on the basis of authorisation and maintaining accreditation, sufficient conditions will be ensured for the performance of the emissions verification activity, but the disadvantage of domestic entities will be eliminated and therefore the excess administration will be removed. It is also proposed that the administrative fee of CZK 3,000 be abolished for the change of the greenhouse gas permit. This fee means inadequate costs for operators, because the change in such authorisation occurs quite often due to the amendment of emission detection regulations and in most cases it is not a demanding administration, so it is once again a reduction in unnecessary administration for entrepreneurs. Act No 389/2013 is also amended as part of the adaptation to the Commission Regulation No 389/2013. 253/2008 Coll., on certain measures against the legalization of proceeds from crime and terrorist financing, as amended. The national administrator of the allowance trading register, which in the case of the Czech Republic STE, a. s., has a new obligation to fulfil a number of obligations, in particular the obligation to cooperate with competent authorities, which are, in addition to the Financial Analytical Service of the Ministry of Finance, in particular the authorities active in criminal proceedings, the obligation to detect and report suspicious transactions, the obligation of confidentiality and training of employees in the fight against money laundering and terrorist financing. Thank you for your attention.
