ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-19-ps2013-007-02-004-020.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I present to you a small amendment to the Act on Air, namely the proposal which amends Act No. 201/2012 Coll., on Air Protection. It complements the possibility of exchanging emission ceilings between operators and eliminates other shortcomings in the current form of the law resulting from the experience of its application. Operators of smaller stationary resources included in the transitional national plan are significantly disadvantaged by the existing wording of the law. While large operators with a large amount of resources can optimally disintegrate the planned investments in order to reduce environmental impact and increase energy production efficiency, smaller operators, often with only one source, do not have this option, which leads to unnecessary cost increases without an environmental effect. [[Strong noise in the hall.]] The amendment point aims to allow the exchange of part of the emission ceilings between operators, excluding the possibility of increasing emissions for the Czech Republic and within the most loaded areas. The rapid adoption of this change is essential for the preparation of investments and eliminates the uncertainties associated with the operation of stationary resources by smaller operators. The amendment point concerning compensatory measures removes the difference between their application between stationary resources and land roads, which saves the cost of their implementation on the side of the Road and Motorway Directorate or cities. The remaining amendments will reduce the administrative burden on businesses and state institutions and clarify certain obligations in time. Further proposals to regulate the law reduce the administrative burden on operators, thereby reducing costs when fulfilling the same environmental requirements.
