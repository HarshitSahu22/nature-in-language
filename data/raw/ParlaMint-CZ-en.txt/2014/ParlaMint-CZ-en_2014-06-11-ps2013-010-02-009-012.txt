So let me briefly describe the government bill amending Act No. 6/2002 Sb., on courts, judges,judges and state administration of courts and on the amendment of some other laws. The proposed legislation assumes that the term of office of the Presidents of the Colleges of the Supreme Court and the Supreme Administrative Court will be re-established for a period of five years. The application is based on the fact that most of the other officials of the court, that is to say the Presidents and Vice-Presidents of the courts, already have a term of office. The President and Vice-President of the Supreme Court and the President and Vice-President of the Supreme Administrative Court shall be appointed for a period of ten years, the Presidents and Vice-Presidents of the Supreme, Regional and District Courts for a period of seven years. The expiry of the term of office shall be the reason for the resignation of the chair of the college of the competent court. The establishment of the term of office of the College Chairs should contribute to staff renewal in these functions and to the selection of the optimum persons who should be responsible for those functions. Thank you for your attention.
