The Protocol has already been discussed and agreed to ratify the Budget and Foreign Committee of the Chamber of Deputies as well as the Senate, including both committees, the Committee on Foreign Affairs, Defence and Security and the Committee on Economic, Agriculture and Transport. The Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of Ukraine on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income and property taxes signed in Kiev on 30 June 1997 applies to mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and Ukraine. The Treaty entered into force on 20 April 1999 and is published in the Czech Republic in the Collection of Laws under No. 103/1999 Coll. I would like to say that the protocol, which arose from the initiative of the Czech Party, fully reflects international developments and trends in the international tax field, particularly in the context of the activities of the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development. The protocol, which will form an integral part of the double taxation treaty, will certainly contribute both through the provision of an extended exchange of tax information and other amendments to the text to greater legal certainty and to better coordination of the activities of the tax authorities of both states aimed at limiting possible tax evasion and fraud, while at the same time ensuring that Czech tax residents, in compliance with all conditions, ensure, for example, a smooth application of the exemption method in the context of income collected from resources in the territory of Ukraine, from the exercise of dependent activities. I believe that the conclusion of this Protocol to the Treaty will further facilitate business for Czech entities on the prospects of the Ukrainian market and will be an important opportunity to establish further trade relations with Ukraine. The needs of the emerging Ukrainian economy have great potential. For example, energy, infrastructure, agri-food complex, ecology, engineering, metallurgy, chemical industry, medical technology, drug production, etc. can be named as a promising sector.
