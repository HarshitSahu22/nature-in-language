Allow me to state the Government's bill amending Act No. 156/1998 Coll., on fertilisers, soil aids, auxiliary plant preparations and substrates and agrochemical testing of agricultural soils, as amended. [[Act on Fertilizers]] The submitted government proposal contains only one amendment to the Fertilizers Act, but it is necessary for the practical application of this Act. It is proposed to repeal the provision according to which the central control and testing institute of the agricultural holding repeals the authorisation for the registration of fertilizer, respectively. an auxiliary soil substance or an auxiliary plant preparation, provided that the soil aid or an auxiliary plant product contains the active substance of the plant protection product. The intention is to remove the legal uncertainty of the holders of registrations of the products concerned, which this provision, removed from the context of the whole law, causes. Ladies and gentlemen, in view of the minimum scale and technical nature of the material, I suggest that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic give its consent to the draft law at first reading in accordance with Section 90 paragraph. 2 of the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies.
