ParlaMint-CZ_2014-04-30-ps2013-008-02-002-046.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, thank you again today for the floor. I would like to present to you a proposal to give my consent to the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and Bosnia and Herzegovina on international road transport. These agreements shall be negotiated with countries which are not part of the European Union separately and among others. addresses the issues of contingents for road freight transport, i.e. individual bilateral permits, which are based on discussions between the professional departments of the Ministry of Transport of the Czech Republic and the countries mentioned. Among other things, personal transport matters are also being dealt with and charging matters are being dealt with. The submitted international treaty on road transport replaces the original Agreement between the Government of the Czechoslovak Socialist Republic and the Government of the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia on international road transport, which was concluded in 1963. Since this original agreement no longer meets the current conditions, the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia no longer exists, it was necessary to negotiate a new bilateral agreement on international road transport. After the negotiations, the contract was signed on 22 May 2013 in Leipzig. The aim of the proposed adjustment is to create a comprehensive framework for the operation of freight, passenger road transport between the two States. The text of the contract is in accordance with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, including obligations arising from membership of the Czech Republic in the EU. The contract does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of Parliament of the Czech Republic to give my consent to ratify this contractual document. Thank you.
