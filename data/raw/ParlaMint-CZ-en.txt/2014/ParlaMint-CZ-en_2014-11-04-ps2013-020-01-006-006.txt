ParlaMint-CZ_2014-11-04-ps2013-020-01-006-006.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce a government bill amending Act No. 526/1990 Coll., on Prices, as amended. The submitted government proposal contains only two changes of a rather technical nature. One is a proposal to terminate the publication of the Price List with paper-based regulatory decisions, thereby saving the costs of printing and distribution. The Price List will continue to be accessible digitally on the public administration portal. The second amendment is to adapt the requirement of the new law on control and to allow the mandate to check prices to be in the form of either a written mandate or a licence. This is a practice that has also been used in the past. The Government Bill was discussed by the Budget Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 14th meeting on 23 October 2014. Two amendments have been adopted in the context of its discussion, which I agree with. I therefore recommend the approval of the Government's bill, as amended by these proposals. In a detailed debate, I will propose a reduction of the deadline between second and third reading to 48 hours. Thank you for your attention.
