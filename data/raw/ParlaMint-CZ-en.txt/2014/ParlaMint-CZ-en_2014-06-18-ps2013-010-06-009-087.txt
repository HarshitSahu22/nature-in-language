Proposal for the election of members of the Council of the Czech Press Office /2/ As far as point 87 is concerned, the choice of two members of the ČTK Board, the number of ballots issued and surrendered is identical, 163 and, in this case, also, the rating was 82. This means that no one has been elected here in the first round, and since, according to the Rules of Procedure, twice the number of candidates in the second round is the number of places occupied, so logically, in this case, all four candidates proceed to the second round. According to the number of votes it would be in the order of Jakub Heikenwälder, Miroslav Augustin, Kristián Chalupa and Václav Legner. So much for the results.
