ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-06-ps2013-006-03-001-037.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the prior agreement of the two chambers of Parliament with the proposals of certain decisions of the institutions of the European Union is a necessary condition for a representative of the Czech Republic to agree to such proposals when voting in the Council. The proposal submitted by the Council for a Council decision is one of those rules under Parliament's Rules of Procedure, due to the European legal basis chosen. For these reasons, the Government shall submit to the two chambers of Parliament for prior approval of this proposal for a Council decision. The purpose of the proposal for a Council Decision is to repeal Council Decision 2007/124/EC establishing a specific programme for the prevention, preparedness and management of the consequences of terrorist attacks and other risks related to security, which was designed for the period from 1 January 2007 to 31 December 2013. For the years 2014 to 2020, the financing of the project in this area will be regulated by a new regulation establishing the Internal Security Fund with two components, firstly for police cooperation and border control. The Czech Government has no reservations about the proposal for a Council decision, as it is a purely legislative technical response to the adoption of new regulations for a further financial perspective and financial period. The adoption of a proposal for a Council decision will have no impact on the rule of law and will not be linked to any direct implications for the state budget. Old projects will be completed on the basis of transitional provisions, new funds will no longer go into old funds and new projects will be announced according to a new regulation that will be discussed by the European Parliament. Thank you for your attention.
