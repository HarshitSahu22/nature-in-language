ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-24-ps2013-019-04-013-014.u2	Thank you. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, every year money is needed to cover the budget deficit of the state budget, and so far it is necessary that the possibility of issuing government bonds be laid down by law for this purpose. Similarly, it is necessary to provide funds on an ongoing basis for the repayment of the sovereign debt, which means, in particular, the instalments of the nominal values of the previously issued government bonds. These funds are also obtained by selling government bonds, and the issue of these bonds also requires the issue of a law. It's published every year or every two years. The proposed bill is untypical in the fact that, according to him, bonds are to be issued not only to cover the budget deficit of the state budget for this year, but also the actual deficit for last year. Last year, the Act on the issue of bonds for the budget deficit of the State budget for 2013 was prepared, but before the Chamber of Deputies approved it, it was dissolved and the law could not continue in the legislative process. The budgeted deficit then changed to real and its coverage was taken into the proposed draft law. [[13.00 hours]] In addition to the authorisation to issue bonds to cover the two mentioned deficits, which are contained in Section 1, the draft law provides for the authorisation to issue bonds to cover the repayment of the sovereign debt that will take place next and next year. It is the content of § 2, which is formulated in the same way as Act No.89/2014 Coll., which allows the issue of bonds to cover the repayment of state debt accrued this year. The sale of government bonds increases the government debt, but in the case of bonds whose yield is set to repay the government debt, the government debt after payment of the instalments will fall again. However, the Ministry of Finance decided not to increase state debt at all until the end of 2015 and to leave it at the end of 2013. Therefore, until then, it will only sell government bonds with a cumulative nominal value not exceeding the sum of the amounts used at the same time for repayment of the sovereign debt. The Chamber of Deputies is discussing under press number 295 of the amendment to the Budgetary Rules Act, according to which the authorisation to issue government bonds to cover the budget deficit of the State budget will no longer be necessary, nor will it be necessary to establish a special law to issue government bonds to cover the repayment of the sovereign debt. A special law will only be necessary in the event that the actual deficit is higher than the budgeted one and therefore the money borrowed through the sale of government bonds will have to cover the amount that the State Budget Act did not count on. Thank you for your attention.
