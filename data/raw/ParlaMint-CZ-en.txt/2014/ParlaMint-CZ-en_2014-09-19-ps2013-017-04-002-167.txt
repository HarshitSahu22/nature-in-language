Mr President, honourable Members, ladies and gentlemen, I would just like to briefly summarize what happened in view in 2013. First of all, we were influenced by two significant legal changes. An amendment to the CNB Act was approved, which first defines our macro-prudential policy tasks, and another substantial change was the adoption of the Investment Companies and Funds Act, which transposed the EU directive. The Exchange Act was also approved. In the area of European regulation, an agreement has been reached on the creation of a single GSM system, first supervision, first pillar of the banking union. Proposals were submitted by the European Commission to create further pillars of the single restructuring mechanism and, of course, the directive on the recovery and restructuring of credit institutions was being prepared. As part of the licensing activity, no new bank licence was granted, but was withdrawn from the major members of the Metropolitan Savings Association permission to act as a cooperative reserve. As part of the supervision of banks, great attention has been paid to the development of the quality of credit portfolios, the creation of adjustments, the adequacy of capital, liquidity and overall management. The results of the stress tests and the entire results of our supervisory activity confirmed the good resilience of the banking sector. On the contrary, in the cooperative-based sector, we have focused on more basic issues, the classification of the credit portfolio, the adequacy of the creation of adjustments, the financing of economically linked groups, compliance with regulatory limits and the origin of cooperative-based capital. In the field of the provision of capital market services, the main problem was the involvement of investment intermediaries in the provision of investment services to shares and investment certificates associated with the acquisition of inadmissible incentives from a securities trader. In the insurance sector, it has been confirmed that the insurance sector as a whole is adequately capitalised. I further note that the Central Credit Register, which has been operating here since 2002, was still in operation. Since 2013, we have also made available data from international exchange in that year. The CNB's scope of supervision of compliance with consumer protection rules focused mainly on the segment of insurance and credit institutions. [[9.30 hours]] The financial market of the Czech Republic as a whole was stable in 2013, achieving good economic results. The crisis in international financial markets did not jeopardise the stability of the domestic financial market. The banking sector is healthy, sufficiently capital-equipped, profitable, has sufficient resources and does not have a liquidity problem. On the contrary, the management of the cooperative base sector as a whole has deteriorated in a given year. The insurance sector is profitable, stable, and has achieved moderate growth in the prescribed premiums in 2013. I'd like to put an end to that in a nutshell.
