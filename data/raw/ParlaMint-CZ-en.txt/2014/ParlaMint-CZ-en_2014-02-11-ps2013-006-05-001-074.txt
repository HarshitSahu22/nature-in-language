Mr President, colleagues, let me introduce you to House Document 296 on the proposal for the Rules on the Management of Members' Clubs for 2014. Briefly. The amount of the allowance for the costs of Members' clubs shall be set at a fixed amount of CZK 24,500 and at a variable amount of CZK 3 550 per club member. The funds shall be made available to the MPs on a monthly basis. It must be said that the Chamber of Deputies' Office, which manages the budget of the Chamber of Deputies, also includes in its budget chapter all revenue and expenditure relating to contributions to the costs of individual Members' clubs. On this proposal, the Organisational Committee also adopted a resolution at its last meeting on 5 February 2014. I will introduce you to this resolution. The Organizing Committee proposes to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the Rules for the Management of Members' Clubs for 2014 with a monthly contribution, as I have stated. The rules governing the management of membership clubs for 2014 shall be annexed to this resolution. At the 6th meeting of the Chamber of Deputies, the Organizing Committee gives reasons for this proposal by Mr Václav Votava. I have thus also fulfilled my obligation under the Organising Committee.
