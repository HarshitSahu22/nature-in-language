Proposal to shorten the legal deadline for the discussion of the House of Press 101 in the Committees of the Chamber of Deputies I ask that this proposal be made by Mr Roman Sklenák, the President of the Social Democratic Parliamentary Club. Please, Mr. Chairman, take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2014-04-29-ps2013-008-01-002-001.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, honourable members of the Government, colleagues and colleagues, on 4 April 2013, the Czech and German Health Ministers signed the rámcový Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Federal Republic of Germany on cross-border cooperation in the field of medical rescue services. In May 2013, this Treaty was submitted to the Parliament of the Czech Republic to express its consent to ratification. Unfortunately, due to the dissolution of the Chamber of Deputies, this ratification process has not been completed and the treaty had to be submitted to Parliament again. Since the border regions concerned have been planning together for a long time details of synergies, it is very desirable that this Treaty should enter into force as soon as possible and, in fact, cross-border cooperation in the field of medical rescue services can begin. The only committee to which the treaty was commanded, namely the Foreign Committee, has already discussed the treaty and has recommended that the Chamber of Deputies agree to its ratification. Therefore, after consulting the Minister for Health, I propose a reduction in the deadline for discussing this press in committees by 30 days.
