ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-06-ps2013-006-03-004-040.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, this is essentially an appeal to the reservations relating to international cooperation in connection with the adoption of a new law on international judicial cooperation in criminal matters, which has become effective on 1. 1. 2014 and which no longer requires us to leave the reservations that we are now calling off. So the law on international judicial cooperation brought many institutes of international judicial cooperation, which are modern, which our practice called for. The preparation of the law lasted a long time, so it can be greatly acknowledged that it occurred, eventually it was approved and became effective on 1 January this year. However, in order to be able to follow this more flexible adjustment, changes are needed compared to the previous legislation contained in Title XXV. criminal order, to reflect on the reservations that the Czech Republic has entered into on certain multilateral international agreements, which, of course, are bound in criminal matters in the field of international judicial cooperation. This Decision is addressed to the Member States. 21. The European Convention on the Extradition of Parts 3 and 4 of the European Convention on the Supervision of Conditionally Released and Condemned Perpetrators and the Inter-American Convention on the Enforcement of Criminal Judgments abroad. From all the reservations it follows in essence that in the context of the authorisation of transit, respectively. the transit for the purposes of the addition is subject to assessment of all conditions for the admissibility of the issue as laid down in Title XXV. Criminal code. The law on international judicial cooperation, or our newly approved law on criminal matters, simplifies the conditions for transit clearance, so we do not need this reservation. This is a reservation on Article 4 (1) (a) of the CRR. 21 The European Convention. Reservations to Parts 3 and 4 of the European Convention on the Supervision of Convicted and Conditionally Released Perpetrators and the reservation to the Inter-American Convention on the Enforcement of Criminal Judgments abroad limit the transmission and acceptance of enforcement of decisions on conditional convictions or conditional release, as the national legislation of Title XXV. the criminal order allowed only the taking over of supervision and control of the sentenced person. It therefore did not allow the execution of the rest of the sentence or the entire sentence. The withdrawal of these reservations therefore allows for the fact that we have a new law that comprehensively regulates this, and it is no longer necessary to have those reservations. It also makes it possible to decide whether the conditionally sentenced or conditionally released in the probationary period has proved to be successful. The proposed appeals are in line with the foreign policy interests of the Czech Republic, constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic and obligations arising for the Czech Republic from international law and membership of the European Union. Of course, they will not be associated with any demands to increase the state budget. For this reason too, but above all, for that reason, I naturally propose that the government proposal be approved. Thank you.
