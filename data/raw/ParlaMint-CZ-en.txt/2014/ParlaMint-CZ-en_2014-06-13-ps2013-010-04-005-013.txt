The proposal responds to the situation arising as a result of the temporary measure on the basis of Act No. 314/2012 Sb., which provided that only one third of price growth would be used instead of full price growth for the increase in the percentage of pensions paid for 2012, 2013, 2014 and 2015. The purpose of the measure was to achieve savings in the cost of pension benefits. In practice, these measures showed that the real value of paid pensions was reduced and thus the purchasing power of pensioners was reduced, since the average old-age pension was only CZK 137 and only CZK 45 per month in January last year. Therefore, the government has committed itself to a return from next year to the original rules on raising pensions, and this will be implemented by this proposal. If this proposal had not been implemented, it can be assumed that the increase in pensions would have been similar to this year, i.e. approximately CZK 50 per month. Following an increase in pensions in 2015, according to the proposed text, a return to the adjustment will take place before last year, which means that prices will be fully taken into account and real wage growth will be taken into account by one third. It should be noted that the renewal of this rule may not mean that valorisation will be appropriate in 2015. Wage growth will not show at all in valorisation, because real wages have fallen in 2013 and the current forecast for price increases is around 1.3 percent, which means increasing the average old-age pension by only approximately 150 crowns. Therefore, as an additional measure in the transitional period, an exceptional valuation scheme is proposed to increase pensions in January 2015 which would guarantee that the average old-age pension would have a total increase of at least 1,8 per cent in order to avoid a real reduction in the value of pensions. This reduction represents a monthly increase of around CZK 205. For these reasons, please accept this proposal. Thank you.
