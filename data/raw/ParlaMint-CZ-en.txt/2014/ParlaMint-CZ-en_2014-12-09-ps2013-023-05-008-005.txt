ParlaMint-CZ_2014-12-09-ps2013-023-05-008-005.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, the Senate has given you back a bill proposing an amendment to the Act on Investment Companies and Investment Funds and some other laws on the capital market. The main objective of the proposal is to clarify certain provisions of the Investment Companies and Investment Funds Act in response to knowledge from application practice and to the necessary implementation of European legislation. Following these changes, there was also a need to adapt the provisions of the Capital Market Business Act. Another objective is to regulate the Act on Financial Arbitrator as regards terminology used by this Act and the Act on Investment Companies and Investment Funds. Furthermore, the proposed draft law contains a minor amendment to the Act on the Activities of Occupational Pensions Institutions, the purpose of which is to transpose the European Directive in order to avoid over-reliance on credit ratings when investing in debt instruments. The bill was consulted and created in cooperation with the professional public from the private and academic sphere, with the Czech National Bank and the Association for Capital Market of the Czech Republic. It is therefore non-conflict and supports the clear interpretation of the regulation in question and the legal certainty of the addressees of the standard. The bill was returned to the Senate with amendments to clarify the normative text in terms of its clarity. The Ministry of Finance agrees with these amendments. In view of this, I would like to ask you to approve the proposed law, as amended by these amendments. Thank you for your attention.
