ParlaMint-CZ_2014-02-07-ps2013-006-04-004-006.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the draft law amending the law on elections to the Parliament of the Czech Republic. This is a partial technical amendment which is only implemented in the years when the Senate elections are held. These will take place in the second half of 2014. The need for amendment is triggered by a change in the population in the electoral districts of the Senate, where the population has increased or fallen below the threshold laid down by the Law on elections to Parliament, which amounts to plus minus 15 per cent compared to the average population attributable to one mandate in the Czech Republic. The purpose of this legislation in the Act on elections to Parliament is to fulfil the principle of equality of electoral law, showing that electoral districts account for approximately the same number of voters. In view of the term of the Senate elections, the proposed law should take effect on 1 June 2014, before the likely date of the election announcement. For this reason, I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies to agree with the proposal at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
