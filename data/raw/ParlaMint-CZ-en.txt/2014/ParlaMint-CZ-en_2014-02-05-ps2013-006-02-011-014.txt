The draft law amending the chemical law and certain other laws is re-sent to the Chamber of Deputies because the identical proposal was submitted to the House in July 2013, but as a result of the dissolution of the House, it was no longer discussed on Parliament's premises. The actual content of the bill presented. It is, as I said, the adaptation of the chemical law to a directly applicable European Union regulation. Consequently, I would like to conclude by justifying the proposal that the draft law amending the chemical law and certain other laws is presented in order to ensure an appropriate transposition, whereby the deadline set for carrying out this transposition expires on 14 February 2014 and, above all, it is necessary to ensure the transposition of Article 30 of Directive 2012/18/EU as soon as possible on the control of major accident hazards with the presence of dangerous substances. In case of non-compliance with this deadline, the Czech Republic is exposed to the risk of initiating proceedings for non-compliance with its obligations arising from its membership of the European Union. For this reason, through the Government, I would like to ask you that the House approve the proposal at first reading pursuant to § 90 para. 2. Thank you for your attention.
