ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-20-ps2013-007-03-005-033.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, hello. I would like to submit a draft air agreement agreement between the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and the Government of the State of Israel on the other. The Euro-Mediterranean Air Agreement between the European Union, its Member States and the Government of the State of Israel is another real step after Morocco in the process of creating a common Euro-Mediterranean aviation area, which has been implemented since 2005 in accordance with the external policy of the European Union. The primary objective is to ensure as broad a level playing field as possible for all European Union air carriers and partner countries on the transport market. The Agreement regulates all major areas in civil aviation, such as the determination of the scope of traffic rights, the conditions for operating air transport between Contracting Parties and cooperation in the field of safety and, in particular, aviation security. The text of the agreement is in accordance with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic. The agreement does not require a change in the Czech legal order and its implementation will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to give my consent to ratify this agreement. Thank you.
