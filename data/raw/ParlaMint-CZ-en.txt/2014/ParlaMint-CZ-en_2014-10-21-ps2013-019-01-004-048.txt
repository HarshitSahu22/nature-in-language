ParlaMint-CZ_2014-10-21-ps2013-019-01-004-048.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce you to the proposed bill amending Act No. 378/2007 Coll., on Medicines and amending certain related laws, as amended. The purpose of the draft amendment to the Act on Medicines is to allow the use of funds in a special account of the State Institute for Drug Control by way of derogation from the purpose of their application provided for in Section 112 of this Act. According to the provisions of the SÚKL, these funds are to be reimbursed for the costs of carrying out professional activities by the entities at the request of the SÚKL. This means can then be used by the SÚKL only to ensure its activities carried out under the Act on Medicines or by special administrative regulations, e.g. Public health insurance law, if this activity cannot be provided to the extent necessary from budgetary resources. The proposed draft law proposes extending the possibility of their use by establishing the possibility of one-off transfer of these funds from its special account to the income account of the state budget of the Czech Republic established for the Ministry of Health. The reason for the transfer of these funds for the needs of the Health Department is in particular the entry into force of Act No. 109/2014 Coll. amending the Act on Insurance against Public Health Insurance and the Act on Public Health Insurance, which increases monthly payments for state insured persons by CZK 58 per month from 1 July 2014. The purpose of this proposal was to increase public health insurance funds in order to compensate for the loss of income of health service providers as a result of the abolition of most regulatory charges. This measure represents an impact on the state budget in 2015 of around 4.2 billion. Kč. In order to cover the above state budget entitlements, the Ministry of Health can participate only by outside budget resources of the State Institute for Drug Control. Therefore, the government decided to propose an amendment to the Act on Medicines, which will ensure a one-off transfer of funds of 1,7 billion in 2015. to the state budget of the Czech Republic. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, so much for the introduction to the government bill. I believe that, on the basis of these facts, you will support the proposal. Thank you for your attention.
