ParlaMint-CZ_2014-04-30-ps2013-008-02-007-009.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, you have a law before you, which is proposed only to amend the Bond Act. The Bonds Act was last substantially amended in 2012, when this change brought the bond world significantly closer to private law and had clearly positive effects. This amendment also led to a significant increase in the number of primary bond issues. Bonds are thus becoming an important and welcome source of finance for companies. At the same time, they represent other opportunities for investors. The proposal contains, in substance, technical changes. In particular, it deals with the legal status of so-called hybrid debt certificates, specifies the requirements of the bonds and allows greater flexibility in the making available of the issue terms of the bonds. The Czech National Bank, the Czech Banking Association and the Association for the Capital Market of the Czech Republic were regularly consulted in its preparation. The proposal is therefore non-conflict and its characteristics are pro-growth. The Committee on Budgets has discussed the proposal and unanimously recommended its approval. There was no discussion of this proposal on the committee. In view of this, I would like to ask you for the support of the proposed law and for its smooth approval. Thank you for your attention.
