The reason for submitting a draft law is the obligation to implement changes in the field of value added tax in approved European Union legislation. This is a reflection of changes in the determination of the place of performance in the provision of the service, the introduction of a special scheme of one administrative place and the presentation of changes in territorial scope with regard to the French outermost regions, in particular Mayotte. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed on 1 January 2015, with the exception of the regulation of territorial scope and registration for a special scheme of one administrative place for which it is proposed to be effective from 1 October 2014. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, I therefore ask you to discuss the draft amendment to the Value Added Tax Act. Thank you for your attention.
