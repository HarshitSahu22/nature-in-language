The aim of the proposed modification is to allow the treatment of wild game only on the basis of an evaluation of the disease situation of parasites carried out by the veterinary administration to the extent and within the time limits laid down by the Animal Health Programme published by the Ministry of Agriculture. This is therefore the treatment of wild game based on monitoring coordinated by the State Veterinary Administration.
