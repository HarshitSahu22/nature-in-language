ParlaMint-CZ_2014-03-18-ps2013-007-01-002-003.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Hello, ladies and gentlemen. I would just like to start by summing up some of the things that the draft amendment touches on. Firstly, this law directly introduces the applicable European Union rules and new contractual instruments of international law. It introduces adaptation measures in the field of legislation, first of all, air personnel on air traffic controllers, provision of air navigation services in so-called "air navigation services'. a single European sky, but a professional survey of the causes of accidents and civil aviation security, in particular by terrorism. Two. The proposed bill specifies and complements the rules governing the operation of commercial air transport, in particular the foreign air carrier. Thirdly, it ensures the implementation of the concept of future legislation, which will result in the harmonisation of the rules of the procedure for the performance of individual administrative agendas with a minimum of derogations and exceptions, in particular the removal of specific legal standards excluding the application of the administrative order without determining any other special procedure. Fourthly, it provides for the updating of administrative charges in the civil aviation sector by amending the Act on administrative charges. The proposal tabled, as I have already said, contains, in particular, adaptation measures for a number of major provisions of European Union law, particularly in the field of civil aviation security, which have already entered into force, and must therefore be applied to the full extent of the national authorities. Its preparation was preceded by a long and careful preparation involving consultation with the professional public and the legislation contained therein has been long awaited. By its resolutions of 26 February 2014, the Economic Committee and the Security Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic recommended that the draft law be discussed and approved. The Economic Committee also adopted amendments to the legislative draft, with which the Ministry of Transport agrees. Thank you for your attention.
