The report on the state of agriculture of the Czech Republic, which has been published since 1994, is a statistical-information material representing the activities in the agriculture department in a given year. Although agriculture in the Czech Republic showed positive results in 2012, it does not show good condition in terms of structure and trends. Trends of development show a strengthening orientation on the production of commodities with little added value, which is certainly not a characteristic that a country with such a deep agricultural tradition could pride itself on. The development of some livestock sectors, which are gradually losing their positions - pigs, poultry, eggs - is particularly worrying, although the prerequisites for competitive production in the Czech Republic certainly exist. It should also be pointed out that the data in the report on the state of agriculture point to an increasing dependence of our agriculture on subsidies, which is certainly not a positive phenomenon in terms of strengthening competitiveness, but it is a reflection of the functioning of the Czech Republic within the framework of the common agricultural policy. The problem is also still a high percentage of leased land, resulting in adverse impacts on the quality of the agricultural soil fund and a high percentage of erosion-threatening land.
