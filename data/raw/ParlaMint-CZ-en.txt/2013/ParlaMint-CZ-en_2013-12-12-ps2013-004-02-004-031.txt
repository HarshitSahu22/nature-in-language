Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2013-12-12-ps2013-004-02-004-031.u2	Hello, Mr President, Members of Parliament, fellow Members of the Government. Let me present the justification for the proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic submits a proposal for consent to the Agreement establishing a European Molecular Biological Laboratory - EMBL. The European Molecular Biological Laboratory is an international research organisation based in Heidelberg, Germany and brings together 20 European states. During its 40 years of existence, it has been able to build the most important and technologically advanced European research infrastructure dealing with molecular biology and genetics. Its activities take place in five research institutes - Heidelberg, Cambridge, Hamburg, Grenoble and Monte Rotondo. EMBL has technologies that are at the absolute world top and are not available to Czech scientists elsewhere. Here I would like to say that Czech scientists have been involved in the activities of the European Molecular Biological Laboratory all along, more or less since 1989. We even have seven permanent workers in Heidelberg, Vladimír Beneš, one of the research infrastructure managers. [[11.10 hours]] The main purpose of the Czech Republic's accession to the EMBL is the involvement of growing Czech research centres financed from the Structural Funds in wider international cooperation that enables them to develop. Among the research institutions that showed intense interest in membership in EMBL are, for example, Charles University in Prague, Masaryk University in Brno, Institute of Molecular Genetics of the Academy of Sciences in Krč, major infrastructure projects CEITEC and BIOCEV and many others. Membership in the EMBL will provide these institutions, scientists and students of our universities with a unique opportunity to use state-of-the-art technologies and capacities to benefit their research and participate in wider international projects. The high contribution of potential membership in EMBL for Czech research was confirmed by a detailed analysis prepared by the Technology Centre of the Academy of Sciences at the Ministry of Education. In connection with the approach, the Czech Republic will pay an access fee of EUR 990 575 payable during the first three years of membership and, on the one hand, an annual member contribution, which would amount to about €650 thousand for the first three years of membership according to the preliminary calculation. Both amounts are derived from the net national income criterion according to OECD statistical data. In the medium-term budgetary perspective for 2013 and 2014, the EMBL membership appropriations have already been included, amounting to EUR 1 million for each of the two years. For this investment, the Czech Republic will receive a unique opportunity to expand its research opportunities in the field of genetics, plant and animal biology, medicine, pharmacy and other related fields. The government approved the intention to join the EMBL in December last year. On this basis, an official request was sent to the EMBL Council for approval of the adoption of the Czech Republic. The EMBL Council complied with this request at its meeting on 25 June 2013. At this point, it is only for the Parliament of the Czech Republic to agree to ratify the accession and subsequently the President of the Republic to sign the instrument of ratification. The entry of the Czech Republic is expected at the beginning of 2014. Please note that we originally assumed this during 2013. Thank you for your attention and I beg you to give your consent to this proposal.
