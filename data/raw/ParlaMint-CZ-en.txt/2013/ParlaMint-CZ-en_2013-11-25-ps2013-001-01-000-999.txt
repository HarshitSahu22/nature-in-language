ParlaMint-CZ_2013-11-25-ps2013-001-01-000-999.u1	Dear Members, honourable Members, honourable Members of the Government, dear guests. The President of the Czech Republic convened a meeting of the Chamber of Deputies in its seventh term on 25 November 2013. Initiating meeting I have called the Chamber of Deputies in accordance with Section 22 of the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies. I am opening this inaugural meeting, and I warmly welcome you all to it. The agenda of the constituent meeting shall be determined by the relevant provisions of our Rules of Procedure and shall be circulated to you on 18 November 2013. First of all, it is my job to introduce you to the apologies of today's day. No one apologizes from among the Members. So let me introduce you to the excuses put forward by the members of the government. Apologies to Prime Minister Jiří Rusnok for foreign travel, Minister Jiří Cienciala for health reasons, Minister Jan Fischer gives no reason and Minister Dalibor Štys apologizes for foreign travel. At the same time, I announce that the replacement card - the first replacement card in this parliamentary term - was issued to Mr Jiří Pospíšil and has card number 1. Ladies and gentlemen, we will proceed to item one of this setting-up meeting.
