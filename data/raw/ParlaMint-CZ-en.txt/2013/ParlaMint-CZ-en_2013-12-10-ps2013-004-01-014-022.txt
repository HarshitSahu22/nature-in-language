ParlaMint-CZ_2013-12-10-ps2013-004-01-014-022.u2	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the bill amending the Act on elections to the European Parliament and certain other laws. In particular, the proposal responds to the adoption of a European Union directive on rules for the exercise of the right to be elected in elections to the European Parliament. Better conditions are created for candidacy in elections to the European Parliament for citizens of the Union who decide to run in a non-home Member State. The applicant's obligation to submit a certificate from the home Member State that he has not been deprived of the right to be elected or that such electoral incapacity is not known to the competent authority shall be removed. This obligation is now replaced by an honest statement by the candidate that he has not been deprived of the right to be elected. The Member State of residence of which the candidacy is submitted shall then refer to the Member State of origin of the candidate requesting verification of such a declaration. Where the information from the Member State of origin denies the candidate's declaration of honour, the Member State of residence shall take all steps necessary to prevent him from running for office or, where appropriate, in the election or in the exercise of his mandate. The provisions authorising the addition of votes shall be adjusted so that, as soon as the vote is concluded in the elections to the European Parliament in the Czech Republic, that is Saturday at 14 o'clock, the district electoral committee will begin the vote counting and the results of the vote for the electoral districts will be transmitted to the Czech Statistical Office, which will process the overall results of the elections, but will wait until the vote in the elections to the European Parliament is closed in all the Member States of the European Union. On the basis of the European Commission's notice, the bill also regulates the conditions for the registration of citizens of other Member States in the electoral list for elections to the European Parliament and the exchange of information between Member States in order to avoid any duplication of votes. [[16.30 hours]] Last but not least, the bill projectes a change in the electoral laws introduced by the new Civil Code, which will take effect on 1 January 2014. Ladies and gentlemen, since the directive must be transposed into the laws of the Member States by 28 January 2014, the proposed law should take effect as soon as possible. For this reason, I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies to give their consent to the bill at first reading. Thank you for your attention.

It is, in general, technical matters related to the technique of exercising the right to vote. I think that, in essence, the proposed amendment, although quite extensive, generalizes the technical matters in question, when it first deals with the provisions of the formal declaration of non-freedom of law, second responds to Act 90/1976, which regulates the basic rules governing elections to the European Parliament in terms of its refinement, concerning the launch of the census of district electoral committees and the completion of this census, only after publication of the results of the elections by the Czech Statistical Office. The third heading concerns the terminology and its alignment with Act No.62/2003 Coll., on Elections to the European Parliament, with Act No.89/2012 Coll., i.e. with the Civil Code. Furthermore, these are certain implementations from the point of view of the European Union pilot project, the transposition of a possible duplication of the exercise of electoral rights in elections to the European Parliament.
