The submitted material contains subsidy programmes for 2014, the purpose of which includes the extent of the funds referred to in § 2d para. The Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic approves the Act on Agriculture at the same time as the State Budget for each year. The grant programmes for 2014 are linked to already proven programmes established by previous governments. These programmes were analysed and found to be effective. The subsidy programmes are in accordance with § 2d para. 2 of the above Act implemented by the Minister of Agriculture and administered on the basis of the principles which it has issued. The programmes are specifically intended to support restructuring and increase the competitiveness of the Czech agrarian sector. Animal production is preferred in the submitted proposal for the amount of allocated resources. This is because of the efforts to prevent adverse developments in certain livestock sectors, which are necessary to maintain employment in rural areas. In accordance with § 2d para. The proposal for grant programmes in agriculture for 2014 was approved by the Government of the Czech Republic on 13 November 2013.
