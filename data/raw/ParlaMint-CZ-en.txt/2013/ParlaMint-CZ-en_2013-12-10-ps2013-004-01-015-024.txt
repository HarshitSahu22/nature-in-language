ParlaMint-CZ_2013-12-10-ps2013-004-01-015-024.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the Government's bill amending Act No. 191/2012 Coll. on the European citizens' initiative. First of all, the bill aims to adapt its terminology and control procedures to Act No.255/2012 Coll., on control, which takes effect on 1 January 2014. The second objective is the partial modification of the verification procedure of the declaration of support for the European citizens' initiative. The Ministry of Interior, as the body responsible for verifying the declaration of support for the citizens' initiative in the Czech Republic, allows access to other data from the basic population register from the population information system, the civil identification system and the travel document registration information system to be specified on the interpretation of Regulation No. 211/2011 on the citizens' initiative at European Union level. These are data which are strictly necessary to verify the declaration of support for the citizens' initiative. The draft law in question has already been discussed in the first reading of the Chamber of Deputies during the previous parliamentary term. Due to the dissolution of the House no further discussion has taken place. In view of the objectives of the draft law, namely the harmonisation of its terminology and procedure with the control order, which, as I have already said, takes effect on 1 January 2014, and the provision of the necessary means to assess the declaration of support for the citizens' initiative, with citizens' initiatives already organised, the proposed law should become effective as soon as possible. For this reason, the government is proposing to the Chamber of Deputies to agree with this bill at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
