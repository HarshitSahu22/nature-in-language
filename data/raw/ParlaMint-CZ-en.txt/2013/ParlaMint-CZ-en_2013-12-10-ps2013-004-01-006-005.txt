Proposal for the establishment of a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the activities of the National Security Office and the manner in which it is established or elected I state that pursuant to Article 145 of Law No. 412/2005 Coll., on the protection of classified information and on security competence, the Chamber of Deputies sets up an audit body for the activities of the NSA consisting of seven Members. Once again, I propose that this supervisory body should have the status of a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies, and I propose that the Commission be set up according to the principle of proportional representation. I shall therefore make a motion for a resolution: Chamber of Deputies I. establishes a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the activities of the NBU, consisting of seven Members; II. provides that the Commission shall establish itself on the basis of the principle of proportional representation.
