As regards the issue of income, as I have already said, the total amount is CZK 780 million. If we look at the SFRB budget's spending page, we are not considering direct subsidies from tenderers, but only and only return forms of credit, or liability for the repayment of commercial loans, since 2011 and even in the following years. Therefore, the expenditure side of the SFRB's budget for 2014 envisages the following items: loans to natural and legal persons of 1 billion 320 million, loans to help restore damaged property in floods of 20 million, reductions in loans to young people of 80 million, payment of a contribution to interest on Panel and New Panel, expenses to provide support and administrative direction of the fund of CZK 73,2 million. Expenditure for 2014 is planned to amount to CZK 2 billion 534 million. For 2015, in terms of the long-term outlook, revenues are then set at 845.5 million, a little higher than the figure in 2014, then 20 million more for 2016, amounting to CZK 886,1 million. The expenditure amounts to the planned, forecasted for 2015 of 2.2 billion and, mutatis mutandis, for 2016. Originally, the draft spending part of the SFRB budget was under low-interest loans for young people and families amounting to 1 billion 20 million. We took the liberty, and this is how we presented this proposal also in the Public Administration Committee, to increase by 300 million. Why do we have this proposal? In the past, the free funds of SFRB were deposited in fixed-term accounts and SFRB had an annual amount of around 150 to 180 million in the revenue part of this budget. The amendment of the law led to the fact that all funds of the State and, ultimately, local authorities were transferred to the CNB and, of course, these funds are not in any way interest. Therefore, we see as a good achievement not only in relation to the applicants to increase this volume by 300 million, because the applications are there, but, of course, by providing only interest-bearing loans, these 300 million will not only be used to help those applicants, but will be charged at least 2 to 3 percent of CZK as such. On the basis of the above, it is therefore proposed to increase the volume of credit funds by 300 million in order to expand the activities of credit programmes by a programme to support the modernisation of flats by natural persons. I will also mention here in this context, it appears in the budget, under the financial instrument JESSICA the financial volume of CZK 288 million. It is - information for you - a project that is co-financed by European sources, fully managed by Komerční banka, which means, it also carries risks for the repayment of these funds. And the magical thing is that these funds flow to the Czech Republic as European resources. And, of course, once they are returned to the applicant due precisely to credit possibilities, they will continue to be used in this way by the State Housing Development Fund as national financial resources. The total volume is 688 million and it is planned that CZK 288 million could be released through JESSICA next year. This is basic information on the draft budget of the SFRB for 2014 and the medium term perspective. Of course, I'll be ready to answer your questions. Thank you for your attention at the moment.
