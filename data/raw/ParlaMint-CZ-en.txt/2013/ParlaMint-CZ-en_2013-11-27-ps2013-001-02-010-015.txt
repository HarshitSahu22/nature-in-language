Proposal to determine the number of members of the Committees of the Chamber of Deputies Let me remind you in the introduction that the Committees of the Chamber of Deputies are set up according to the principle of proportional representation, which is Section 115 para. Council Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 1605/2002 of 25 June 2002 on the Financial Regulation applicable to the general budget of the Union and repealing Council Regulation (EC, Euratom) No 1605/2002 (OJ L 298, 26.10.2012, p. 1). The meetings of the Members' Clubs to determine the number of members of the various committees gave rise to the following proposals, and if you don't mind, I would read them: Committee on Petitions 15 members Budget Committee of 25 Members Supervisory Committee of 15 members Organisational Committee of 18 Members Election Committee 15 members European Affairs Committee 19 members Economic Committee of 25 Members Constitutional Committee 19 members Committee on Defence 15 Members Security Committee 15 members Social Policy Committee 21 members Health Committee 25 members Committee on Science, Education, Culture, Youth and Sports 21 members Public administration and regional development committee 21 members Committee on the Environment 15 members Foreign Committee 15 members the agricultural committee of 19 members. As far as the Committee is concerned, I would like to point out that the 18 members already include the President of the Chamber of Deputies and the Vice-President of the House, who are members of this committee by law. So I would like to open the debate and ask if anyone is applying to it. I see no one, and therefore I am ending this debate. I will ask whether we can vote on this proposal as a whole, or whether there is a requirement that individual committees be voted on separately. I see that this is not the case and that there is a great will to vote at once. So I've read the numbers, everyone knows what we're voting on. If so, please, who agrees with me or the proposed number of committees? Initiating vote, who is in favour, press the button and please raise your hand. Who opposes this proposal? The vote is number 13, 182, for 179, against no one, so I note that we have approved the motion for a resolution and have set the number of members of the various committees of the Chamber of Deputies as proposed.
