Proposal for the establishment of a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the activities of Military Intelligence and the manner in which it is established or elected According to Article 21 of Law No. 289/2005 Coll., on Military Intelligence, the Chamber of Deputies sets up a monitoring body for the control of the activities of Military Intelligence consisting of seven Members. Pursuant to Article 47 of the Rules of Procedure, I propose that this supervisory body be appointed as a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies. In accordance with Article 115 (2) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Furthermore, I propose that this Commission be set up in accordance with the principle of proportional representation and, where appropriate, that the members be elected in a different way.
