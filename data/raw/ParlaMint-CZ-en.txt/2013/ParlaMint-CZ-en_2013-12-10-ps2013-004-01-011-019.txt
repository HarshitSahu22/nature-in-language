ParlaMint-CZ_2013-12-10-ps2013-004-01-011-019.u4	Mr President, honourable Members, ladies and gentlemen, let me put forward a draft law amending the Act on Public Health Insurance as part of the transposition of Directive 2011/24/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on the application of patients' rights in cross-border healthcare. The submitted amendment to the Act, in accordance with the said directive, confers the right of Czech insured persons to reimbursement of the costs of health services which they have decided to receive in another Member State of the European Union. The basic requirement therefore laid down in the directive in question is the right to reimbursement of the costs of health services incurred in the territories of the Member States of the European Union outside the competent Member State. These costs should be borne by insured persons up to the amount of the costs that a health insurance undertaking would incur if those services were to be used in the territory of the Czech Republic, but always up to the maximum of the actual costs. In order to calculate the reimbursement of costs, objective, transparent and non-discriminatory criteria must be established, which the proposed amendment addresses by reference to the legislation under which the amount of reimbursement can be calculated. Another requirement of the directive is the incorporation of the so-called national contact point, whose task is to provide information on the use of health services within the European Union. By carrying out this activity, it is proposed to entrust an entity that already performs the liaison position under the European Union's rules on the coordination of social security systems, which is the Centre for Interstate Payments. At the same time, the transposition Directive gives the Member State the possibility to limit the use of certain categories of health services under that scheme by using the Institute of Transitional Consent. The Institute also regulates the proposed amendment, providing that the categories of health services whose compensation is subject to prior consent may be defined by a government regulation. Therefore, if, in applying the amended law, it would be found that financial stability of the public health insurance system could be jeopardised, the Government's regulation constitutes a relatively rapid and effective brake on the avoidance of possible threats to the system. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen. Finally, it should be noted that the deadline laid down for the transposition of the directive in question into the national laws of the Member States expired on 25 October 2013. It is therefore absolutely necessary, taking into account the obligations of the Czech Republic arising from membership of the European Union, that the draft law be discussed and approved without undue delay. It is therefore also proposed that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic should give its consent to the draft law at first reading, as otherwise there is a danger of initiating proceedings and violating the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, which could have very adverse consequences.
