ParlaMint-CZ_2013-12-19-ps2013-004-04-013-999.u1	Point 50 With regard to resolution number 25, i.e. proposals for the Heads of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to Inter-European Organisations, Kateřina Konečná has been nominated as Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the Inter-European Union. She received 125 votes and was elected. Parliament's permanent delegation to the NATO Parliamentary Assembly - Antonín Seďa received 163 votes and was elected. The Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the OSCE Parliamentary Assembly - Martin Stropnický received 138 votes and was elected. The Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe - proposed by Dana Váhalová. She received 156 votes, she was also elected. Finally, the Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the Central European Initiative - Kateřina Konečná received 121 votes for the KSČM and the Head of the delegation was elected. That is all for the committees and delegations from me. Thank you for your attention and I give the floor to the President.
