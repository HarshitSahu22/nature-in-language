ParlaMint-CZ_2013-11-25-ps2013-001-01-002-002.u1	2. Promise by Members Let me introduce you to this point. The actual act of the promise will therefore take place as follows: I will first ask Mr Lubomír Zaorálek, who was elected as the second oldest Vice-President of the Chamber of Deputies of the previous, i.e. the sixth term, and he will present the Constitution with the prescribed promise. He'll be the first to put it in my hands. Then Mr Lubomír Zaorálek will read your names in alphabetical order, and you will approach it and make a promise by saying the word "I promise" and by handing it. You'll confirm your promise by signing it. Signing sheets are placed here in front of you at the newsstand table. I'm warning you, ladies and gentlemen, according to Section 6 of the letter. (a) our Rules of Procedure shall cease to exist by refusing a Member's promise or by making a pledge, subject to the mandate of the Member. I therefore ask first Mr Lubomír Zaorálek to come to the speaker's desk and take the floor. I beg all of you to rise.
