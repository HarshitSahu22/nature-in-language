ParlaMint-CZ_2013-12-06-ps2013-002-02-003-009.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the only member of the government in resignation. At its third meeting on 3 December 2013, the mandate and immunity committee discussed the request of the Ministry of Finance to communicate the number of valid votes cast for each political party, political movement or coalition in the elections to the Chamber of Deputies held on 25 and 26 October 2013 and adopted its resolution number 12, which was distributed to you. I therefore propose to adopt the following resolution: Chamber of Deputies 1. informs the Ministry of Finance pursuant to § 85 of Act No. 247/1995 Coll., on elections to the Parliament of the Czech Republic, as amended, the numbers of valid votes cast for individual political parties, political movements and coalitions in elections to the Chamber of Deputies held in 2013; the results are set out in the Annex to this resolution; 2. Recommends that the Ministry of Finance should pay the electoral contribution to political parties, political movements and coalitions under Section 85 of the Act cited. Thank you for your attention.
