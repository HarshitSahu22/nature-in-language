This bill is a set of several government bills that were prepared at the beginning of this year on the basis of a government-approved timetable to submit draft amendments to the laws in connection with the adoption of a new regulation. Individual government bills were submitted to the Chamber of Deputies, which, as a result of its dissolution, failed to discuss. Now these previously separate proposals are merged into one law, which includes a total of 69 amendments to the laws. The main purpose of this proposal is, above all, the removal of special procedural provisions governing control procedures in specific laws. These provisions will be replaced by the application of the relevant general provisions of the new control order, which will take effect on 1 January 2014. In view of the impending date of entry into force of the Regulations, the draft law should be adopted as soon as possible. For this reason, I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies to give their consent at first reading. Thank you.
