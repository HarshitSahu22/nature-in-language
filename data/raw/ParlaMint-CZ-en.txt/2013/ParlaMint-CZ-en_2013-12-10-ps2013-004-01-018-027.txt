Dear Members, honourable Members, let me briefly mention the Government's bill amending Act No. 499/2004 Coll., on Archiving and File Service and amending certain laws, as amended. The draft law is submitted following the adoption of the new Act No.255/2012 Coll., on control, which takes effect on 1 January 2014, as it is necessary to harmonise the procedure in the field of control, as well as in the field of archiving and filing services. At the same time, the issue of so-called commercial writings is addressed and the provision of expert administration of documents located in these writing rooms, for whose storage is of public interest. This problem is referred to in the overall report on the Ombudsman's activities for 2010. In particular, it is the establishment of the necessary framework for the obligations of commercial files in the field of professional administration of documents and the obligations of public and private bodies, the establishment of the facts of administrative offences in cases of failure to comply with the obligations laid down and the determination of the scope of the State's regional archives in relation to commercial files in the field of control and the replacement storage of documents at the end of the file room. The draft law in question has already been discussed by the Chamber of Deputies during the previous parliamentary term as House press number 1061 in the context of the adoption of the regulations by the Ministry of the Interior in the context of the Government's draft law amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the rules of procedure. Due to the dissolution of the House no further discussion has taken place. I am terribly sorry, because I am likely to touch someone again, but since I am authorized here by the Government to submit this proposal and the Government has approved a request for the amendment of this law to be renegotiated pursuant to § 90 para. In view of the need to ensure that amendments to the legislation which respond to the adoption of Act No. 255/2012 Coll. take effect as soon as possible after this Act takes effect, and the need to remove as soon as possible the problems of the retention of documents in commercial writers, in particular with regard to their link to the application of the right to retirement or other social security, the Government proposes that the draft law of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic should be approved at first reading. Thank you.
