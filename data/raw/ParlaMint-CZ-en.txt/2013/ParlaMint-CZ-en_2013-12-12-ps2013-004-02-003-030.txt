Madam Members, allow me to present the proposal tabled by the Government of the Czech Republic to discuss the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. It is a draft financing agreement 11. EDF, or Internal agreements between representatives of the governments of the Member States of the European Union meeting within the Council on the financing of Community assistance under the multiannual financial framework for the period 2014 to 2020 under the Partnership Agreement and on the allocation of financial assistance to the overseas countries and territories covered by Part Four of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. The European Development Fund, which is the abbreviation EDF, remains outside the budget of the European Union and is intended to finance the Union's development cooperation with African, Caribbean and Pacific countries, which are signatories to the Partnership Agreement between these countries and the European Union, the so-called Coton Agreement. Each EDF is concluded for a five-year period on the basis of the so-called internal agreement between the Member States. The tenth European Development Fund is currently being implemented, which is valid - for your information - until 2013 and started in 2008. Internal financing agreement 11. The European Development Fund was negotiated within the European Union and signed by the Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Czech Republic at the Council of the European Union in Luxembourg on 24 June, following the approval of the Government and the authorisation of the President of the Republic. The Internal Agreement sets out the objectives and principles of Union development cooperation with the ACP countries, or countries I have mentioned. The main objective that the government of the Czech Republic fully supports is the eradication of poverty, the promotion of sustainable development and the involvement of these countries in the world economy. Assistance is implemented on the basis of country-specific and regional strategic programmes, which the Czech Republic actively participates in within the European Union. The 11th European Development Fund is to be made up of a total of 30 billion. 506 miles. EUR. The Czech Republic accounts for a share of about 0,8 percent, or a contribution of 243 million. 270 thousand. EUR 97, which is fully counted as official development aid. [[11.00 hours]] The contribution shall be paid in instalments on the basis of a call from the European Commission. Part of the Fund is invested through the European Investment Bank's specific investment facility, the EIB, and part is implemented in the form of direct budgetary support. Development projects, particularly infrastructure, are also underway. The Czech entities have so far been mainly used under this fund as providers of consulting services or subcontractors of multinational companies. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, in cooperation with the Foreign Cooperation Council, is systematically working to raise awareness of the opportunities under this Fund among potential realizers. Internal financing agreement 11. The European Development Fund requires national approval in all EU Member States. From the point of view of the legal order of the Czech Republic, this is a treaty of the presidential category, which requires the approval of the Parliament of the Czech Republic, namely both chambers. The government was submitted to express its consent to ratification in August 2013, the House's press then no. 1132, Senate press No. 177. However, because of the dissolution of the Chamber of Deputies at the time, the agreement was not discussed. The Government recommends that the ratification of the Internal Agreement be carried out in accordance with the EU Recommendation by 31 December 2013 in order to implement this Fund from January 2014. Therefore, the internal financing agreement is 11. The European Development Programme is now being re-submitted by the Government with a request for discussion by Parliament in a shortened procedure. The internal financing agreement for this fund is, according to Government Resolution No. 483 of 19 June 2013 in accordance with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic as well as international legal obligations of the Czech Republic. The Ministry of Finance will ensure the implementation of the agreement and will ensure the provision of an overall financial contribution of the Czech Republic amounting to EUR 243 million of EUR 270 thousand 97 in crown equivalent for the period 2014 to 2020. The payment will be paid in the same way as in the case of a compulsory contribution to the EU budget from budget chapter No. 398 - General Treasury Administration in accordance with Council decisions on financial contributions from Member States to finance 11. The European Development Fund. I therefore recommend to the Chamber of Deputies to give their consent to the ratification of this Treaty. Ladies and gentlemen, ladies and gentlemen, thank you for your attention.
