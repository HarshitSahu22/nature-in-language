ParlaMint-CZ_2013-12-19-ps2013-004-04-012-999.u2	Thank you, Mr President. I have one of my last major performances in this opening season. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to introduce you to the results of the secret elections to the permanent committees and delegations of the Chamber of Deputies. I will report the results to you in accordance with resolutions 24 and 25, as you have received them, in the same order. To speed up and shorten this information, I will just say that in all cases, i.e. for all names, 176 ballots were submitted and 176 valid and invalid ballots were submitted. So the number of non-denials was zero. And now I'm just going to introduce you to every committee and every chairman or chairman with a number of positive votes, i.e. votes in favour, and a statement as to whether or not he was elected. Radka Maxová was proposed to the chair of the Permanent Commission of the Chamber of Deputies for Family, Equal Opportunities and National Minorities. She got 157 votes and was elected. Chairman of the Permanent Commission of the Chamber of Deputies for BIS Control - proposed by Marek Benda. He received 138 votes and was elected. Chairman of the Permanent Commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the Control of the Activities of the General Inspection of Security Forces - designed by Václav Klučka. He received 157 votes and was elected. Chairman of the Permanent Commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the activities of the NBU, the National Security Office - proposed by Adam Rykala. He received 144 votes, he was also elected. Chairman of the Permanent Commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the Control of the Operation of Military Intelligence - designed by Bohuslav Chalupa. He received 149 votes and was elected. So in all of them, it was in the first round, logically. Finally, the chair of the Permanent Commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the use of wiretapping and telecommunications traffic records, the use of surveillance of persons and things and the interference of electronic communications - proposed by Mr Daniel Korte. He received 126 votes and was elected chairman of this commission.
