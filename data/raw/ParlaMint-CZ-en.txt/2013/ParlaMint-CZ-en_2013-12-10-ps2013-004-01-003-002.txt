The KSČM club presents a proposal for the establishment of a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the Constitution of the Czech Republic and the Constitutional System. In the Senate, the Committee on the Constitution and Parliamentary Procedures has long been working. There is therefore no opponent in the Chamber of Deputies and there is no law on the intercourse of both chambers in the Czech constitutional system. This sometimes causes difficulties, because according to Article 41 of the Constitution, there are things that require the agreement of both chambers, without the possibility of overvoted and deviated from the order of one chamber from the other. In this respect, the lack that we have had here for virtually 17 years since the establishment of the Senate has never been eliminated. At least part of it was always removed. For example, the establishment of the Supreme Administrative Court lasted less than five years after the Senate was completed. And then we didn't make much progress except in 2004, when Article 10 was changing due to our commitments in the European Union. At this point, I consider the absence of the Constitutional Commission of the Chamber of Deputies to be a grave mistake. I considered it a mistake in 2010, when it was not established from so-called. economical reasons. I do not understand the austerity reasons, because, of course, any of the Members can direct the Commission, whether it will be the President of the Constitutional Committee, another Member or Vice-President of the House, it does not matter, and that is not really a matter that would in any way burden the Chamber of Deputies economically. It is more about whether, after 20 years of the existence of the Constitution of the Czech Republic, it is not necessary for us to deal with the functioning of individual constitutional articles rather than on an ad hoc occasion, e.g. when changing the President's election from indirect to direct or using those powers of the President of the Republic and attempting to shift between Article 62, 63, i.e. by co-signing the Prime Minister or not, but by the specific things that we then evaluate in some context of the long-term activity, not within a single term.
