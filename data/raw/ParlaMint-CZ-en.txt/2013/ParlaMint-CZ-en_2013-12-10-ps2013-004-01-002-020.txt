Let me briefly introduce you to the content and purpose of the bill presented, which was discussed by the Government 14. 8th this year. Let me briefly put forward the proposal for a government amendment to the Consumer Protection Act, which aims to prevent the abuse of consumer confidence by non-serious entrepreneurs who organise so-called demonstration actions. These actions are mainly aimed at seniors, who are often offered and sold poor quality and overpriced products. In doing so, various unchoosy and thoroughly sophisticated aggressive practices are used, ranging from manipulation leading to a sense of commitment or gratitude of the participant through actions of false or distorted information to threats or psychological pressure and physical attacks. It is still very difficult to carry out checks on such actions. In order to ensure effective control by the Czech Trade Inspection Authority, it is therefore proposed to impose on entrepreneurs an obligation to inform the supervisory authority 10 days in advance of the event and to determine the minimum extent of the information to be provided by the entrepreneur in the notification of the event to the supervisory authority. There is also an obligation to provide complete and accurate information on their minimum extent and in invitations to consumers. Because of the seriousness and urgency of the solution to this problem, I welcome you in accordance with the provisions of § 90 para. 2 of the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies to ask for consent to the draft law at first reading. Summary. There are aggressive trade practices, bordering on blackmail and restricting personal freedom, during the transfer operations. In order to ensure effective control by the COI, a 10 working days notice is proposed before the event. Third: Due to the urgency of the problem, a short-term discussion and approval is proposed in the first reading. Thank you for your understanding and your approval and your attention.
