ParlaMint-CZ_2022-11-29-ps2021-045-04-005-004.u2	Thank you. Dear Mr Vice-President, colleagues, good evening. I would therefore like to briefly state the law already mentioned, which is an amendment to Act No. 73/2012 Coll., on substances that damage the ozone layer and on fluorinated greenhouse gases, as amended, i.e. House printing 75. It is a bill that has already been discussed by the Government in the last parliamentary term of 25 November 2021, when this bill was approved. This means that we have had this bill for some time in this parliamentary term in the Chamber of Deputies. The main objective of the bill is to make the system for the management of controlled substances and fluorinated greenhouse gases, i.e. F-gases, more efficient. So I will only allow myself because we are at second reading and at first reading there has already been a more detailed presentation of this bill, to say the essential things which are the purpose of this legislation. This means that the deadline for the transfer of controlled substances is extended for disposal. The operation of the disposal and regeneration of F-gases is modified. It is also subject to a new permit instead of the original certificate. The ban on the handling of disposable containers for F-gases appearing on the single European market from illegal imports is now amended. The requirement for the form of a customs declaration is further amended. The method of issuing certificates is changing. Newly, it is also distinguished whether the performance of regulated activities will be subject to a certificate issued by an evaluation and certification body or a permit by the Ministry of Environment. It is also limited to the content of the information system of certified persons and certain penalty provisions are regulated. It responds, of course, to the practice. It is also intended to simplify the demands on the business environment and also to monitor developments, such as, for example, packaging for these gases, so that we can make it more economical for those who are dealing with them. Thank you for your attention.
