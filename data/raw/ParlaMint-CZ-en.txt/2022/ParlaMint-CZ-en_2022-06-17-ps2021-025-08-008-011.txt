ParlaMint-CZ_2022-06-17-ps2021-025-08-008-011.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, honourable Members, let me once again introduce the Government's amendment to Act No. 326/2004 Coll., on Plant Health Care. The aim of the proposal is, in particular, the introduction of an obligation to identify plant protection products for professional users with unique identifiers, in order to prevent the falsification of these products, to electronize records of the use of preparations and ancillary devices, in addition to improving the availability of plant protection products, by simplifying the processes of mutual recognition of authorisations for the use of products for own use. In addition, the implementation in response to the catastrophic overgrowth of field voles should be adapted to the procedural and professional procedures of the central control and testing institute of the agricultural holding in the catastrophic state of harmful organisms and, in this context, the definitions and procedures for determining the population density of harmful organisms are revised in particular. Further facilitate the application of plant protection products in the form of capsules or granules by drones, unless a height of 5 metres above the ground is exceeded, and make partial changes in the area of treatment with products with a view to simplifying and clarifying legislation, for example in the area of competence for the handling of preparations. The draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee at its 8th meeting on 5 May this year. By his resolution, he recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the Government's bill. Thank you for your attention.
