Proposal for the election of members of the Czech Television Council In the second election to the Czech Television Council it was the choice of four members for the full term, the results are as follows: Petr Brozda 93, Czendlik Zbigniew 92, Ivana Denčevová 91, Monika Pajerová 89, Jan Novák 92, Jiří Padevět 89, Michal Schuster 88 and Radek Žádník 88. None of these candidates have been elected either. Here I can add to the footnote that, although with a close result of sometimes two or three votes, but was not elected, no one has achieved the kvora. This also means that the election has failed and no one has been elected to the Czech Television Council. For these two media councils, the procedure is as follows: The Chamber of Deputies must first announce a new public challenge through Madam President to organisations that can nominate proposed candidates. They will then be discussed by the Election Committee, reducing the number proposed to three times, and only then will the Chamber of Deputies proceed to a new election of these vacant posts.
