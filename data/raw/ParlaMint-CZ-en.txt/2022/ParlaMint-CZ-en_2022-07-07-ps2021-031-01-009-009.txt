ParlaMint-CZ_2022-07-07-ps2021-031-01-009-009.u2	Thank you, Madam President. Good evening, ladies and gentlemen. Let me briefly state the draft law amending Act No. 67/2013 Coll., which regulates certain issues related to the provision of performance related to the use of apartments and non-residential premises in the house with apartments, as amended. [[18.50 hours]] The bill is presented on the basis of a positive Government resolution of 30 March 2022 registered under number 240. Translated draft law prepared by the Ministry of Regional Development on the basis of the need to transpose the relevant provisions of Directive 2018/2002 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2018 - the Energy Efficiency Directive, hereinafter referred to as the Directive - and the Commission Recommendation 2019/1660 into the Czech legal order. These provisions strengthen the rights of beneficiaries of centrally prepared heat and hot water for accurate, reliable, clear and in particular timely, information on their consumption so as to have sufficient frequent feedback on what their real consumption looks like. During the preparation of the draft law, consultations were held with the Ministry of Industry and Trade, which coordinates the transposition of these and other provisions of the directive. In order to be informed, the obligation of the service provider to inform the recipient of the service of its observed heat consumption and consumption of the jointly prepared hot water at regular, the directive envisaged at least monthly intervals shall be transferred, provided that such consumption can be collected using remotely applied readings. In fact, there is an ongoing obligation in case individual reading devices change. So they're already assembling those that are remotely deductible, that's the five-year cycle. However, this directive no longer regulates this, this transposition only regulates the deductions. Another transposition clause is proposed to provide for the obligation of the service provider to inform the energy service provider, at the request of the service recipient, under the Energy Management Act, of the billing and consumption of heat and consumption of the jointly prepared hot water and the service recipient for the last three years. The proposed amendments do not go beyond the requirements of the directive. In any event, I believe that, given the current situation where there will undoubtedly be great emphasis on heat consumption in the coming period, and many of our citizens will think about how to reduce this consumption, it is indeed desirable to increase the awareness of consumption at regular intervals. In addition to the amendments to the Act relating to the transposition of the Directive, it is proposed to clarify the provisions on determining the level of the consumer component of the recipient of services, which will thwart the detection of its consumption of heat or hot water by the service provider in some of the precisely defined ways. This explicit clarification is based on interpretation practice and therefore does not constitute any significant change in the content of the legislation. In the context of the already adopted transposition amendment to the implementing legislation of Decree No. 269/2015 Coll., again as amended, which extends the list of information that service providers must provide in the billing for the billing period, it is also proposed explicitly to provide that the maturity of the default on advances for services does not affect such billing defects which do not lead to the error of the determination of the amount of the default. On 10 December 2020, the bill was sent to an inter-ministerial commentary procedure. In total, 75 comments were made, 52 of which were identified as essential, and all comments were settled. Since the draft amendment concerns a very sensitive area at present, as I have already mentioned, while bringing new obligations to service providers - this is regular monthly information, I do not oppose the debate on the context of this proposal, but, in view of the implementation need, we have decided to submit this proposal for approval at first reading, because Decree No. 376/2021 Coll., amending the Decree on the settlement of heating costs and the joint preparation of hot water for the house. In the context of the prerequisite for more significant efforts to reduce heat consumption for individual service recipients, we are considering its redesigned amendment. I think this is the moment when we should open up a debate as soon as this decree is amended. It is necessary to hold this expert discussion with both service providers, with whom we are constantly in contact, and with experts in the field of construction. I therefore consider it most reasonable that we should actually approve this implementation proposal at first reading, then take the regulation of the decree and discuss it with the partners that I mentioned, as well as on the ground of the proper parliamentary committees. Members of the Chamber of Deputies will not lose the opportunity to enter even if we approve the implementation proposal today. At the same time, I must point out that the Gesture of transposition of the relevant directive is the Ministry of Industry and Trade. At the time of preparation of the draft amendment to the Act implementing this part of the text from the EU Recommendation 2019/1660 was required. Thank you for your attention. I would like to request the approval of this bill at first reading with the reference that the implementation matters will then be further resolved in the amendment of the appropriate decree, which I would like to discuss with my colleagues and colleagues here in the House, for example on the relevant committees. Thank you.
