ParlaMint-CZ_2022-06-17-ps2021-025-08-010-012.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, honourable Members, allow me to reiterate the Government's bill amending Act No. 97/1996 Coll., on the protection of hops. The aim of the draft law is to modernise the register of hops and its link to the control processes and to rephrase the requirements for the application for the inclusion of hops in the register of hops. The proposal will allow the Central Inspection and Testing Institute of the Agricultural Institute to carry out checks on hops to verify the information contained in the hop register, to initiate the procedure for updating the official records if it finds non-compliance between the data contained in the register and the facts, and to further facilitate the updating of the model documents needed to comply with the provisions of the Act by publishing them on the website of the ÚKZÚZu. The proposal also continues to adapt to the relevant Commission regulation laying down detailed rules for the examination of hops and hop products, in particular with a view to anchoring the area for the verification of hops and hop products directly in the law, not just in the decree. The draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee at its 8th meeting on 5 May this year and by its resolution, it recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the Government's bill. Thank you for your attention.
