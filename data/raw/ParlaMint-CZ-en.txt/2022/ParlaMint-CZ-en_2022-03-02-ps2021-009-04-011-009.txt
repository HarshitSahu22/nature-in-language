ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-02-ps2021-009-04-011-009.u2	Thank you, Mr President, honourable Members, allow me to present the Government's amendment to Act No. 242/2000 Coll., on organic farming. The aim of the amendment is, in particular, adaptation to Regulation 2018/848 of the European Parliament and of the Council on organic production and labelling of organic products. Further adaptation to Regulation 217/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls is proposed. The draft amendment to the Act on organic farming as part of the adaptation to the abovementioned European Union legislation is: it introduces an electronic database of organically reared animals; specify the scope of the bodies having an obligation to register in the organic farming system; also extends the duration of the bio-products certificate in the context of reducing the administrative burden following the frequency of checks to two years; replaces the term of the certificate of origin of organic products in practice by the term already used, the certificate of organic products; it replaces seed and other propagating material of plants in the category of bioproduct, while under the legislation in force, it falls within the category of other bioproduct; it regulates the substance of the offences of natural persons, legal persons, business individuals and persons entrusted with them. Since the last major amendment to the Act on organic farming took place already in 2011, it is necessary to respond to knowledge from application practice. In addition to adaptation, the proposal replaces, for example, the term "ecological entrepreneur" in practice already used by the term "ecological farmer." [[12.40 hours]] It also specifies how organic products certified in the territory of the Czech Republic can be labelled by a national logo for organic production and allows for the publication of models of applications for registration in the organic farming system on the Ministry's website. Thank you for your attention.

So for me, I would just like to say very briefly that the main objective of this proposed legislation is to adapt the obligations and requirements laid down for the Member State and the competent authorities in the new regulation to the national legislation. In particular, it is an adaptation of the Act to the requirement for the creation of an electronic database of animals, the modification of the duration of certificates for bio-products following the frequency of checks and the modification of the circle of operators with an obligation to register in the organic farming system. The new regulation regulates both plant and animal production rules, including algae production, aquaculture animals, and processed food or feed production rules, as well as the conditions for certification, labelling and control of bio-production. The Government has already submitted a proposal to the House in the previous parliamentary term, namely 21. 5. 2020 as printing 871. However, the discussion of this House press was concluded at the end of the parliamentary term. For myself, I recommend letting this bill into second reading. Thank you.
