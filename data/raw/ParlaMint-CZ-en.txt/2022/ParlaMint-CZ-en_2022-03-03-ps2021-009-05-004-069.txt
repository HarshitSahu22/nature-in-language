ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-03-ps2021-009-05-004-069.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, as has already been said by the President, this is an agreement on the phase of the use of carrier rockets Ariane, Vega and Soyuz, but at the moment there is a change in the real world and life, because Russia has stepped out of this cooperation on the basis of sanctions by the European Union. However, it doesn't change the deal because we were tied to the use of Ariane and Vega launchers from the Guyana base. [[12.20 hours]] It is a matter that is important for Czech companies, because as this agreement establishes the involvement of Czech companies, according to one of the most important paragraphs it will correspond to the involvement of the Czech Republic in the whole project. The direct impact of the agreement means that the Czech companies, which participated in the whole ESA project, will also have a share in subsequent use. It is an area in which the Czech Republic is one of the important partners, one of the things that turns the view of the Czech Republic from a cheap assembly room towards countries where innovation is created, where high added value is created. That is why I am very attached to this and I am asking for the support of this proposal. If a new declaration were not accepted, the Czech companies in the supply chain could be replaced by foreign companies with traditionally strong space industries in the phase of the use of these missiles, i.e. in their normal operation. The new statement ensures that the Czech companies will be able to supply components for Ariane and Vega launchers on a commercial basis throughout their use or during the period of validity of the declaration. Thank you for your attention and any support.
