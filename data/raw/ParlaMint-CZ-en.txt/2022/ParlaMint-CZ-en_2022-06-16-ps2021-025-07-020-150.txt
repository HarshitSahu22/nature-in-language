ParlaMint-CZ_2022-06-16-ps2021-025-07-020-150.u2	Thank you very much, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, this law has already been mentioned yesterday by Minister Jurečka, but at this point, let me just briefly remind you what the main objectives are. The first objective is to simplify the administration of the housing allowance. According to the proposal, an application for a housing allowance would be submitted only once and income and the cost of housing would be newly documented only twice, not four times a year. A new party to the proceedings for all benefits of state social support and assistance in material distress will be able to submit to the Labour Office all documents in the form of a simple copy, with the possibility of maintaining the possibility of requiring the submission of the original or certified copy of the instrument in case of doubt. Furthermore, the maximum monthly amount of parental allowance from the current 10 000 to 13 000 crowns is increased for parents without a daily basis or with a lower daily basis. The authorisation provisions for adjusting the amounts of normative housing costs are also laid down, while the possibility of increasing the amount of normative housing costs during the calendar year is newly enshrined. In the field of aid in material distress, the basic adjustment of the levy of emergency immediate assistance is essential. This is an extension of another type of emergency that, when it occurs, a person may also be considered by the Labour Office taking into account income and social and property conditions as a person in material distress. Another emergency situation is a situation where a person may be threatened by housing emergency or other housing hazards, by the absence of basic living conditions, this situation is difficult to prevent and cannot be handled by his own forces. I consider it very important to increase the bonus for the pension for the purposes of entitlement and the amount of benefits of assistance in material distress, to simplify the procedure by providing further derogations from the administrative order and to reduce the quantities of notifications delivered. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed on 1 July 2022, in particular with regard to changes in the legislation of the housing allowance, for which the period for which an application is submitted from July to June of the following year is set. Finally, the bill was discussed by the Committee on Social Policy, which recommends that it be approved in the text presented, i.e. without amendments. In accordance with the committee resolution, I therefore ask you to support the proposed bill. Thank you for your attention.
