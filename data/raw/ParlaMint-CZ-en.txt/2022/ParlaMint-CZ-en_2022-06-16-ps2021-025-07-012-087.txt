The submitted material shall contain the text of the Air Agreement, jointly negotiated and signed by representatives of the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and Armenia, on the other. The Agreement aims to adapt the area of civil aviation between the European Union, its Member States and Armenia in a uniform manner. At the same time, the agreement replaces the relevant provisions of existing bilateral air agreements between individual Member States and Armenia, including the one concluded by the Czech Republic with Armenia in 2010. The agreement creates a common transport market with new opportunities for air carriers, at the same time actually creating a fair and competitive environment. In exchange for the removal of operational restrictions, Armenia gradually harmonises its regulatory framework with European Union legislation in key areas such as operational security, security, air traffic control and passenger rights. The agreement is in accordance with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, its negotiation does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not affect the expenditure of the state budget. So - in my opinion - it will rather have, but positive, at a time when more cooperation between airlines can be developed.
