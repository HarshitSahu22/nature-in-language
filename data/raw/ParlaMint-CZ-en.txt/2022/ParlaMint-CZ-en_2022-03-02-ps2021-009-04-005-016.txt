First of all, I will remind you that this proposal has already been here in the last parliamentary term as the Parliamentary Press 1190. At this point, it is the implementation of three new EU legislation, firstly the transposition of the so-called alcohol directive, the transposition of the directive on common defence efforts and the transposition of a directive responding to the covid-19 pandemic in the field of VAT. As far as the alcohol directive is concerned, it's mostly the calculation of Plato degrees for beer. Here Member States can make use of the exception that it will apply until the end of 2030. This proposal envisages the application of this exception, that is, the calculation would be applied until 2031. The second part, the essential part of the alcohol directive, concerns the application of a reduced excise duty rate in another Member State. This applies to small independent distillers. As far as the directive on common defence efforts is concerned, there is an extension of the existing provisions where NATO's armed forces have a special regime. This extends to the armed forces of a Member State involved in the defence efforts of the common security and defence policy within the EU. And the third is VAT exemptions due to the covid-19 pandemic. On behalf of the government, I ask you to approve this press in Section 90, because last time it was this way from the point of view of time distress, and, of course, when we are in March 2022, the time distress is greater than it was at the end of the last parliamentary term. Thank you.
