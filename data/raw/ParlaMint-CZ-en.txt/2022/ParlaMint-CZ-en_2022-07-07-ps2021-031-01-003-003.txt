ParlaMint-CZ_2022-07-07-ps2021-031-01-003-003.u4	Thank you for the floor, Madam President. Dear members of the government, esteemed colleagues, submitted an international treaty between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the State of Qatar on the prevention of double taxation in the field of income taxes and on the prevention of tax evasion and avoidance of tax obligations was signed recently on 21 June 2022. Minister of Finance Zbyněk Stanjura, as he mentioned earlier, signed a double taxation treaty with his ministerial counterpart Ali bin Ahmed Al Kuwari on his visit to Doha, Qatar. There will now be a standard legislative process in both countries leading to the entry into force of the Treaty and its subsequent practical implementation. Both ministers also agreed on the need for an early conclusion of an investment protection agreement, which could be signed between the two countries by the end of 2022. According to the reference report, it was prepared on the basis of OECD and UN model models, reflecting the national legislation of both countries. In addition to the unambiguous determination that income is subject to taxation in which country, the contract will also increase the legal status of Czech business entities operating economic activities in Qatar and vice versa. The conclusion of the contract will increase the legal certainty of investors and other tax entities in both countries and will support the development of economic relations with Qatar, which is among the richest countries in the world. According to International Monetary Fund statistics, nominal per capita GDP was nearly $63,000. The negotiation of double taxation agreements is one of the important agenda of the tax section of the Ministry of Finance. Thanks to this, the Czech Republic currently has 93 such contracts, which benefit not only Czech entrepreneurs but also the Czech Republic itself. The possible double taxation is undesirable as it reduces the income that residents - natural and legal persons of one State - derive from another State. These may include, in particular, revenue from the implementation of construction, assembly, research and other activities, the use of patents and other industrial rights, technical knowledge and experience, manufacturing-technical knowledge, interest, equity, production and technical cooperation and other sources. Double taxation also affects, for example, the income from the use of copyright for works of literary, artistic and scientific, pensions, income from employment and the income of performers and athletes. The international treaty is intended to remove one of the obstacles to the development of mutual economic relations between the two States and to increase the legal certainty of investors. Therefore, I recommend releasing the second reading and ordering the Foreign Committee. Thank you for your attention.
