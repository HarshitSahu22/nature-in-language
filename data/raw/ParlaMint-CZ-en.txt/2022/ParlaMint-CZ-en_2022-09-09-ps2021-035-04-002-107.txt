Madam President, honourable Members, allow me to present the Government's amendment to Act No. 378/2007 Sb., on pharmaceuticals. The main objective of the bill is to adapt two directly applicable EU rules, namely the regulation on veterinary medicinal products and the regulation on the production, placing on the market and use of medicated feedingstuffs. Both Regulations shall take effect on 28 January of this year. In order to ensure proper adaptation, it is therefore necessary for national legislation to take effect as soon as possible. The regulation on veterinary medicinal products has taken over the structure and a large part of the content of the regulation of veterinary medicinal products and the existing legislation contained in Directive 2001/82/EC. Therefore, much of the legislation does not change in terms of content. Only the form is changing, this is the place in the directive where the legislation is now contained in a directly applicable regulation. The draft law therefore repeals certain provisions concerning veterinary medicinal products and medicated feedingstuffs which have been duplicated. It was also necessary in a number of places to make a consistent distribution of legislation on medicinal products for human and veterinary use. In addition to the Act on Medicines, the Act on Advertising Regulation, the Law on Feed and the Act on Administrative Fees are further amended to ensure proper adaptation. The Government's draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies at its 8th meeting on 5 May this year, and by its resolution, it recommended that the Chamber of Deputies approve the Government's draft law. At the second reading, which took place on 17 June, several amendments were tabled: Mr Pavel Bělobrádek's amendments A1 to A3 which specify the legislation on veterinary medicinal products and respond to the development of relevant European Union legislation. I agree with these amendments. Furthermore, Amendments B by Mrs Martina Lisová and others and Tom Philipp and others, C1 and C2. These amendments extend exclusively to the field of medicinal products for human use. In agreement with the Minister of Health, I agree with these amendments, in addition to Amendment C2 in the section on access to electronic recipes and vaccination records by the Institute of Health Information and Statistics. The debate in the Chamber of Deputies has shown that there is no broad consensus over this amendment, which could jeopardise the rapid approval of the bill as a whole.
