ParlaMint-CZ_2022-06-16-ps2021-025-07-017-092.u2	Hello, Madam President, honourable Members, I would like to present to you the Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty on Social Security between the Czech Republic and Mongolia. This is a standard contract of this type which should follow up on contracts already negotiated, in particular to avoid double taxation or protection of investments. Its task is primarily to define in which state the migrant worker will pay the insurance and, at the same time, to establish the resulting pension rights. It is important for employers sending their employees to the other state to be sure of where they are to take insurance premiums for them and for workers themselves, in particular, that the insurance period in the second Contracting State will not be lost to them, but will be taken into account in the pension granted in the future. The Treaty is re-sent to the Chamber of Deputies because it was no longer discussed during the previous term of office. Thank you for your attention, please agree to the ratification.
