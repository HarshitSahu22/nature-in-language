ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-10-ps2021-014-01-005-005.u2	Thank you for the floor. Madam President, honourable Members, on behalf of Minister Bartoš, I present to you the draft budget as well as the medium-term perspective on the State Investment Support Fund. This draft budget considers granting aid in the form of subsidies and low-interest loans. For 2022, the planned volume of subsidies granted amounts to CZK 3,078 billion and loans granted to CZK 2,393 billion. In total, CZK 5.471 billion is budgeted for the State Investment Support Fund's programming expenditure. In addition, the State Fund will support the acquisition of social housing under the Programme Construction for Municipalities, the regeneration of public spaces in housing estates, the part of the project to revitalize the territory with the old building burden from the Brownfields programme, and, above all, the Živel programme, under which subsidies are granted for the restoration of dwellings destroyed by a tornado in South Moravia in June 2021. Discounted loans will be supported by the acquisition of available housing under the construction programme for municipalities, the construction of rental apartments for a defined group of residents, which are seniors 65+, health or income defined persons, elderly persons under 30 years of age, the acquisition or modernisation of dwellings for young people under 40 years of age, the repair and modernisation of residential buildings and part of projects from the Brownfield programme. In addition to expenditure on active programmes, expenditure on the payment of interest subsidies and subsidies on the reduction of principal at the birth of a child of a total amount of CZK 521 million, as well as expenditure on the provision of aid and administrative expenditure of CZK 185 million, is also budgeted. In total, together with programme expenditures, the Fund's budget is estimated at CZK 6,177 billion. On the revenue side there are claims on the state budget of CZK 909 million, of which CZK 800 million is intended for the Brownfield programme, which will be financed from the National Recovery Plan, and CZK 109 million will be used to cover expenditure related to the reduction of the principal of the loan granted to young people at the birth of the child and to cover part of the interest subsidies of the Panel programme. These grants will be provided through the MMR chapter. The programme expenditure of the Fund will be covered by the use of entitlement from unused expenditure of Chapter 317 MMR, amounting to CZK 3,128 000 000. Other expenditure on programmes, administration and interest subsidies of CZK 2,140 billion will cover the Fund from its own resources, from the fund's income of CZK 781 million. These are mainly loan instalments and the remaining part of CZK 1,359 billion will be covered by available funds from the Fund. As far as the budgetary perspective is concerned, the amounts are already significantly lower here, due to the reduction of the Fund's available funds. The fund plans CZK 2,13 billion for programme expenditure in 2023 and CZK 1,14 billion for 2024. In the field of income, the fund provides for subsidies from the state budget for the payment of interest subsidies and for the reduction of CZK 109 million for 2023 and CZK 106 million for 2024. The draft budget was approved by the Government on 23 February 2022 by Resolution 122. The Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development of the Chamber of Deputies on 2 March 2022 discussed the draft budget and the medium-term outlook at the 4th meeting and recommended its approval by resolution number 20. Madam President, honourable Members, I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies to approve the draft budget and the medium-term outlook of the State Investment Support Fund. Thank you.
