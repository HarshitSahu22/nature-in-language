ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-10-ps2021-014-01-006-006.u2	Good afternoon. Thank you for the floor, Madam President, honourable Members. The submitted material, the draft budget of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund for 2022, was prepared on the basis of Section 6c of Act No. 256/2000 Coll., on the State Agricultural Intervention Fund and amending certain other laws, as amended. The draft budget of the SZIF for 2022 respects the total subsidy from the chapter of the Ministry of Agriculture for the State Agricultural Intervention Fund for 2022 amounting to almost CZK 42 billion, of which CZK 35 billion for the common agricultural policy, CZK 5 billion for the agricultural national subsidy for the circus, CZK 200 million for marketing support for quality brands and CZK 1.85 billion for administrative expenditure. The draft budget of the SZIF for 2022 is based on the allocated subsidies from the chapter of the Ministry of Agriculture for the State Agricultural Intervention Fund notified by the administrator of the chapter. The basic revenues in the draft budget of the SZIF for 2022 are subsidies from the chapter of the Ministry of Agriculture for 2022 amounting to CZK 42 billion, of which a subsidy from the state budget of more than CZK 10.6 billion and a subsidy from the European Union budget of CZK 31.2 billion. The other revenue used to draw up the draft budget of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund for 2022 amounts to a total of CZK 545 million, of which sales proceeds from intervention purchases of less than CZK 227 million, revenues from technical assistance to the rural development programme of CZK 318 million and transfers from the EU to the TAIEX programme of CZK 221 thousand. The expected intervention purchases in 2022 of less than CZK 227 million will be financed by the Ministry of Finance's loan. To secure the common agricultural policy, funds of CZK 35 billion, including direct payments of CZK 22.6 billion, the Rural Development Programme, including the Horizontal Rural Development Plan, amount to less than CZK 12 billion and measures of the common organisation of the market, amount to CZK 781 million. The agricultural national subsidies amount to less than CZK 5 billion and administrative expenditure of CZK 2 168 000 000 will be determined in 2022. The Ministry of Agriculture allocated CZK 1.85 billion to these expenditures. The marketing support for quality marks amounts to CZK 200 million. The total resources and their use in 2022 represent funds of more than CZK 42,5 billion and fully respect the expenditure framework established by the methodology for drawing up the state budget for 2022, including the balance. The draft budget submitted was drawn up in accordance with the relevant regulations of the European Commission issued for the common agricultural policy and, at the same time, respects the conditions laid down for its implementation in the Czech Republic, issued by the relevant regulations of the Government of the Czech Republic. The draft budget of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund for 2022 was discussed and approved by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic on 22 February of this year. Thank you for your attention.
