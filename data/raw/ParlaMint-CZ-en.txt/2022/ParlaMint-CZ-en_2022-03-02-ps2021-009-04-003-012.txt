ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-02-ps2021-009-04-003-012.u4	Thank you for the floor. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to make a proposal for an amendment to Act No. 166/1999 Coll., on Veterinary Care. The main objective of the draft act is to respond to Regulation 216/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2016 on animal diseases and to amend and repeal certain animal health acts, which is the legal framework for animal health, which is effective from 21 April 2021. This Regulation builds on the existing European legislation on animal health and constitutes a general legal framework which will be progressively completed by the adoption of implementing regulations. Another aim of the bill is to complete the implementation of the regulation on official controls, which was the subject of the last amendment to the veterinary law. The draft law also complements the legislation of the central registration of dogs in addition to the adaptation of those regulations and lays down the related obligations of dog breeders and private veterinarians. Furthermore, it repeals the obligation of the breeder keeping the hives without delay after the detection of the mortality of the hives at the sites of the hives to report mortality above the established limit of the regional veterinary administration and the obligation to require a veterinary certificate to move the hives outside the territory of the region, which will contribute to the reduction of administrative burden. Furthermore, the draft law includes, for example, an amendment to the breeding law, which takes into account the requirements of Union regulations. Thank you for your attention.
