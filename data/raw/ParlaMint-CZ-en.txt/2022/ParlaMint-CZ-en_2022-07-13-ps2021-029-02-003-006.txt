The aim of the amendment is, in particular, adaptation to Regulation 2018/848 of the European Parliament and of the Council on organic production and labelling of organic products. Further adaptation to Regulation 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council on official controls is proposed. The proposal for an amendment to the Act on organic farming in the framework of adaptation to the abovementioned European Union legislation introduces an electronic database of organically reared animals, specifies a range of bodies having an obligation to register in the organic farming system in order to reduce the administrative burden, further extends the validity of bio-products certificates following the frequency of checks for two years. It replaces the term of the certificate of origin of organic products in practice by the term of the organic product certificate. It includes seed and other propagating material of plants into the category of bioproduct, while under the legislation still in force it fell into the category of other bioproduct. It regulates the facts of the offences of natural persons, legal persons engaged in business, natural persons and persons entrusted with them. Since the last major amendment to the Act on organic farming took place already in 2011, it is necessary to respond to knowledge from application practice. In addition to the adaptation, the proposal replaces, for example, the term ecological entrepreneur in practice by the term already used by the ecological farmer. It specifies the method of labelling organic products certified in the Czech Republic by the national logo for organic production. It will allow the publication of a model for registration in the organic farming system on the Ministry's website. The draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies at its 8th meeting on 5 May 2022 and its resolution recommended that the Chamber of Deputies approve the Government's draft law as amended by the amendments adopted. The amendments adopted, which have been drawn up in cooperation with the Ministry of Agriculture, are in line with the concept of the petitioner, will contribute to the improvement of the Government's bill, and I therefore agree with them.
