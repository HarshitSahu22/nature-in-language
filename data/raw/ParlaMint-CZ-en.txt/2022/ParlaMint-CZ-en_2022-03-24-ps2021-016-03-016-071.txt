Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-24-ps2021-016-03-016-071.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka on the transfer of sentenced persons signed in Colombia on 14 October 2021. There is currently no bilateral or multilateral contract between the Czech Republic and Sri Lanka concerning the transfer of sentenced persons and the transfer of sentenced persons to Sri Lanka cannot be implemented on a non-contractual basis, for example on the principle of so-called reciprocity assurance, as Sri Lanka's legislation requires the existence of a separate international treaty in force for this type of international judicial cooperation. In view of these facts, the transfer of convicted persons between the Czech Republic and Sri Lanka is not possible at all at the moment. For this reason, the agreement submitted is considered to be very effective, since the agreement will allow cooperation between the Czech Republic and Sri Lanka in the transfer of sentenced persons. The convicts will thus be given the opportunity to execute the sentence of imprisonment in the state of which they are citizens and in an environment that will facilitate their return to society. The agreement is in line with the foreign political interests of the Czech Republic and extends the possibilities of international judicial cooperation in criminal matters between the Contracting Parties. The implementation of the contract will fall within the competence of the Ministry of Justice, the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The costs of implementing the contract will be covered by the budget chapters of these departments, with the impact on the state budget being insignificant.
