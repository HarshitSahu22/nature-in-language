ParlaMint-CZ_2022-11-29-ps2021-045-04-002-007.u8	Thank you for the floor, Madam President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly comment on this Parliamentary press 163 for the SPD. Although the Law on Surveying and amending and supplementing certain laws related to its implementation since 1994 has been amended many times, the amendments were mostly partial and did not meet what they were supposed to do. It has not been revised in any way as a whole. The time has changed over the years and some of the provisions of this law are really obsolete, do not correspond to technical progress and the development of information technology in this area. The authorisation for the Czech office of survey and cadastral for the implementation of legislation on the provision of survey expenditure to the public by decree is not sufficiently regulated in the empowering provisions. The Czech office of surveying and cadastral cannot thus act in an operational way by a generally binding law on the new possibilities of providing these data, which evolve relatively quickly with the development of technology. The proposed legislation is based on the revision made of the various provisions of the present Law on Land surveying. This means, for example, that the selected data managed by this Office will be provided free of charge as open data. In the law it is necessary to make individual partial adjustments which will contribute to ensuring terminology and substantive consent to the new legislative development as well as to the development of new technologies and products. This amendment will also remove the financial and administrative burden on entrepreneurs in obtaining geospace information. Our SPD movement therefore agrees with this proposal, including all the amendments that have been tabled. Thank you for your attention.
