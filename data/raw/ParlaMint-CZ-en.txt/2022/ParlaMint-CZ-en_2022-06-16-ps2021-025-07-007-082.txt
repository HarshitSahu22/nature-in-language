I will stress that 80 to 90 per cent of all goods transported in the world are currently moving on the seas, so this agreement is also a necessary condition to avoid unnecessary damage. At the moment, the Czech Republic has no naval merchant ship that sails under its flag, but a number of smaller vessels are sailing under its flag, but they are also governed by these conditions. The involvement of the Czech Republic in an international maritime organisation is a long-term essential part of our transport strategies. Similarly, joining the COLREG Convention also means our Czech contribution to avoiding unnecessary collisions on the seas. Thank you for supporting this convention.
