ParlaMint-CZ_2022-09-08-ps2021-035-03-002-099.u2	Thank you. Madam President, colleagues, guests, I wish you all a good morning, and I would like to briefly mention this government proposal, which is the ratification of the social security agreement between the Czech Republic and the Federal Republic of Brazil, that is to say the House press No 157. It is in principle a contract that the Czech Republic has concluded with many other countries in the world so that we can prove with those countries where there is, say, greater economic activity, or that this economic activity will develop, so that we can also contribute to a better business environment and to a better environment for the citizens of the Czech Republic, or the citizens of the country, in this case the Federal Republic of Brazil, who are engaged in business or activity in the territory of both states as employees of companies and are sent to work either from the Czech Republic to Brazil or from Brazil to the Czech Republic. The purpose of the contract is, in particular, to define clear rules for companies and workers to participate in social insurance during employment in the second state while, at the same time, laying down clear detailed principles for determining the future retirement pensions and their entitlements for those persons. If these contracts are not concluded, then when these people work in these countries, then they are not entitled to any form of support under the pension scheme. That's why this contractual relationship here. This treaty is really the key to creating a clear assumption, a clear social background for these people, so that when they work, they, too, take their social insurance into the systems of those states, so that in the future they have some predictable entitlement to pay their future pension. In this respect, the Treaty corresponds to all internationally recognised requirements for the coordination of social security applied in bilateral and multilateral treaties, as well as European Union rules. All the details of this contract are part of this press and part of the detailed justification in the explanatory memorandum. So I would briefly end my opening appearance on this material at second reading, because I think I've got it all figured out, and all the other details are the contents of the material. Thank you for your attention.
