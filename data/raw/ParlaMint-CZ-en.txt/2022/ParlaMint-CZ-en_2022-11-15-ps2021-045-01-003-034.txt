Government bill on special voting methods in the election of the President of the Republic in 2023 /German press 334/ - first reading pursuant to § 90 para. 2 Please note that it is proposed that we give our consent to the bill at first reading. On behalf of the Government, the proposal submitted will be presented by the Minister of Education Vladimír Balaš instead of Minister Rakušan, who is duly excused. Please. Minister of Education, Youth and Sports of the Czech Republic Vladimír Balaš: Thank you, Madam President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce the Government's bill on special voting methods in the election of the President of the Republic in 2023. Based on previous years' experience in holding elections at the time of the covid-19 epidemic, the merits of the obstacle to the exercise of active electoral rights in the case of restrictions on voters to personal freedom for reasons of public health protection have been discussed. For this reason, the Ministry of the Interior is working intensively on comprehensive legislation on the exercise of electoral rights by voters limited to personal freedom in order to protect against the spread of all infectious diseases. The proposed proposal should therefore be discussed thoroughly with all the bodies and authorities concerned, as the characteristics of certain infectious diseases require greater safety measures than sufficient for covid-19. For this reason, too, the key role of the Fire Rescue Corps is envisaged. The thorough preparation of this comprehensive regulation is time-consuming, so the law will not be available for the January election of the President of the Republic. At the same time, however, we believe that it is necessary for the election of the President of the Republic to allow, at least, voters restricted to personal freedom in order to protect public health against covid-19 disease. These voters can be up to a hundred times as many as those with other infectious diseases, and the voting methods that allow their safe participation in the elections have proved successful in previous years. Unlike the September municipal elections, the election of the President of the Republic takes place in one constituency, which is the whole of the Czech Republic, so it is easier to organise the vote in special ways. The current proposal should be the last one-off law adopted for election purposes in response to the covid-19 epidemic. A general regulation should already be available for further proper choices. This bill envisages the same special voting methods as in 2020 and 2021, that is voting in the electoral post, the so-called drive-in, the vote in the closed residence facility and the vote in the special portable ballot box. The use of special voting methods other than previous years shall not be conditional on the duration of an emergency or similar situation. It is sufficient for the application of the law to take effect no later than the 16th day before the first day of the election of the President of the Republic, i.e. on 27 December 2022. In view of the urgency of this matter, I therefore allow myself to ask the Chamber of Deputies to approve the bill at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
