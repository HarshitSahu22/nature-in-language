Proposal for the election of members of the Supervisory Board of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund In addition, we voted for three members of the Supervisory Board of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund, those I will say, by ordinary members, with the following result: Oldřich Černý 26 votes, Jakub Hruška 98 votes, was elected, Pavel Moulis 97, was elected, and Josef Stehlík 96, also by one vote, and was therefore elected. We have occupied three seats, three seats are occupied, and this is the end of the election, and the Supervisory Board of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund is fully occupied, at least on the side of the House. There I may add that, under the law of the Vice-President of this Council, the Senate elects from among its members, so it is a choice that takes place in another Chamber of Parliament.
