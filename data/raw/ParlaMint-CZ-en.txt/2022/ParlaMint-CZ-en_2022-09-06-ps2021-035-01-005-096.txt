The main aim of the proposed legislation is to adapt the legal order of the Czech Republic, in particular the Act on Agriculture and the Act on the State Agricultural Intervention Fund, to the following adopted or new, directly applicable and immediately applicable regulations of the European Union. The proposed bill thus adapts to our legal order a number of European regulations or regulations of the European Parliament, which you have mentioned in the explanatory memorandum, and I will not quote them here. However, I would like to mention that under these European Union regulations, a proposal for a strategic plan of the common agricultural policy in the Czech Republic for the years 2023 to 2027, hereinafter referred to as the strategic plan of the CAP, which was officially submitted to the European Commission for comments on 28 January 2022, has been prepared for the Czech Republic. The European Commission sent its comments on 26 April 2022 and it was precisely on the basis of these comments that it was necessary to respond and adapt the non-legislative document presented in some aspects. There are several reasons for submitting the proposed legislation. As regards the Act on Agriculture, this is in particular a clarification of the provisions and wordings following the preparation of the draft strategic plan, a clarification of the modification of the agricultural business register, a wording of the issue of granting subsidies, a clarification of the regulation of land use records according to user relations, a formulation of the registration of fruit sets, an addition of the records of units of spatial distribution of forest, a modification of the provisions and a wording in the field of cross-compliance checks. As regards the Act on the State Agricultural Intervention Fund, this is in particular a clarification and addition of the provisions on the competence and operation of the Fund, an adjustment of the provisions on the Fund's financial resources, a clarification of the provisions on the method of granting subsidies to the Fund, new procedural provisions and a single application for agricultural subsidies. Furthermore, the issue still covered by European Union law. I would like to say that this proposed bill is absolutely necessary to ensure that the strategic plan's subsidies are drawn up by 2027.
