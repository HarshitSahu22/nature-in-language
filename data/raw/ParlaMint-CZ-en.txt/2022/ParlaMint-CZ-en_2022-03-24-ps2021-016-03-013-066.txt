ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-24-ps2021-016-03-013-066.u2	Thank you for the floor. This is an amendment to the International Financial Corporation Agreement, which belongs to the World Bank Group. To put it simply, it is a change in order to require a premium of 85 per cent instead of 80 after a possible capital increase. The Czech Republic voted in favour of this change in 2018, the change has already been applied. Finally, the largest shareholder agreed with it, which means the United States of America, and this act will complete the internal approval process within the Czech Republic. The Senate of the Czech Republic agreed to the ratification on 3 February this year.
