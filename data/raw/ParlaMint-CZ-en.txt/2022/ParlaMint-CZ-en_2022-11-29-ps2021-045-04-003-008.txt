ParlaMint-CZ_2022-11-29-ps2021-045-04-003-008.u2	Thank you for the floor. The draft law is an adaptation proposal and is a response to the amendment regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council of 2020 of December 2020 amending the regulation on cooperation with the Office of the European Public Prosecutor and the effectiveness of the investigation of the European Anti-Fraud Office. At the same time, the draft law ensures the application of the relevant provisions of the original regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council number 883 of 2013, which has not yet been implemented in the Czech legal order. The adoption of the proposed law is necessary in order to fulfil the newly introduced obligation of a Member State to provide the European Anti-Fraud Office, I think that the brand, or the abbreviation OLAF, is well known in the Czech Republic as well, as part of its investigation, data from the Central Accounting and Security Boxes and cash transaction records. In the Czech Republic, the Ministry of Finance will provide synergies in this matter. The draft law determines precisely the Ministry of Finance as the authority ensuring coordination with OLAF, determines the competence of the Ministry of Finance in this area, describes in detail the manner in which records of transactions are to be provided, lays down the obligation of confidentiality of all data and penalties for infringements, regulates the processing of personal data, lays down sanctions for requested persons for not providing requested information. At the same time, the accompanying bill is presented, which will be the next point. So today we will be discussing a law amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of this bill. The effectiveness of the law is proposed on the first day of the second calendar month following the date of its publication in the Collection of Laws, as the regulation adapted by the Czech law has already become effective on 17 January 2021. We discussed this at first reading on June 3, and the law was directed to the Committee on Budgets as a guarantee committee. Thank you.

ParlaMint-CZ_2022-11-29-ps2021-045-04-003-008.u6	Thank you for the floor. Shortly to this House press number 218. The proposed legislation is presented on the grounds of the need to allow the fulfilment of the obligations referred to in Article 2 (1) (a) (ii) and (iii) of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 to be carried out. Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, 3a of Regulation 883/2013, and then from the need to implement these aspects of cooperation with OLAF in the area proposed by law which require implementation. A certain section of cooperation between the authorities of the Czech Republic and OLAF is thus regulated. The draft law does not have an ambition to regulate cooperation with OLAF in criminal matters or in the right legal area under other directly applicable European Union rules. The results of OLAF's control activities with the beneficiaries of the subsidies are merely of a recommendation nature. Therefore, there is no legal act directly affecting the rights and obligations of a controlled person or any measure which another competent authority would have to adopt and start to comply with. The draft law primarily defines a new obligation, in so far as it provides information available from the Central Account Register and cash transaction records. In addition to providing this information, the role of the Ministry of Finance in the so-called anti-fraud coordination service is added to the proposed text in order to ensure effective cooperation with OLAF. Thank you for your attention.
