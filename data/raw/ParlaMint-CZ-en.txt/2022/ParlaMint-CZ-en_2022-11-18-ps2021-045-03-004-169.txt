ParlaMint-CZ_2022-11-18-ps2021-045-03-004-169.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the Government's bill amending the law on electronic operations and the authorised conversion of documents has been discussed live at first reading. Therefore, allow me only a brief reminder. The aim of the bill is to abolish the rule of compulsory establishment of a data box for natural persons who use one of the qualified means of electronic identification after January 2023. The Ministry of the Interior supports the establishment of data boxes, but does not want to go through a way where the data box would be established by a non-business natural person as a result of another activity of this person, for example after the person logged in via a bank ID to the Portal of a citizen. The establishment of a data box of a natural person should be left to the decision of a non-business natural person. In this respect, the proposal is based on the law on the right to digital services, according to which non-business individuals cannot be forced to use digital services or to do digital acts. I consider it a more friendly approach to users if they are offered the possibility of establishing a data box, leaving it to their will to make use of the offers. In case of a lack of interest in this service, they will not be forced to actively do any work. Finally, in response to some discussion posts from the first reading, I would add that there are no obstacles on the part of the Ministry of the Interior to the establishment of data boxes. On the contrary, the Ministry is fully prepared to set up data boxes. Thank you for your attention.
