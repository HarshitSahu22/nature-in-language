ParlaMint-CZ_2022-04-06-ps2021-016-06-001-079.u2	Hello, Madam President, honourable Members, the Government is proposing an amendment to the Act on the treatment of genetically modified organisms and genetic products, as amended. The proposal was prepared to ensure the transposition of the European Parliament Directive on the deliberate release of genetically modified organisms into the environment, which was implemented in the framework of a wider amendment to selected EU food law legislation in June 2019 by Regulation 2019/13/81 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the transparency and sustainability of the EU food chain risk assessment. The implemented implementation should have been implemented by 27 March 2021. The amendment to that amending regulation aims to increase the transparency of the risk assessment carried out in the framework of food and feed safety. To this end, so-called standard data formats are introduced to contribute to the effective processing of applications assessed by the European Food Safety Authority. The form of standard data formats has also been introduced in this context into the Directive on the deliberate release of GMOs into the environment so that the relevant documents processed for the purposes of assessing GMOs used as food and feed, or as raw materials or preparations for their production, are presented in a uniform form. Another area that has been changed in the Directive on the deliberate release of GMOs into the environment is legislation, protection of information that the GMO operator is obliged to submit in the context of the respective authorisation processes and whose publication could harm them in the context of competitive relations. The amendment responds to the said wording of the Directive. The possibility of submitting relevant applications in the form of standard data formats is added, and the provisions relating to the protection of selected data contained in the relevant applications for authorisation for the handling of genetically modified organisms, in terms of their definition, related procedural issues as well as their own protection regime, are adapted. Since this submitted material is identical to the material which was already approved in 2020. The proposed amendment, in the same wording, went through a proper inter-ministerial reminder procedure in the same year, which ended without any conflict. The draft amendment was discussed by the Chamber of Deputies at first reading on 26 January 2022, and was directed to the Committee on the Environment. The proposal on 23. 2. 2022 discussed and issued a resolution by which the Chamber of Deputies recommended that the proposal be approved in the draft text. [[9.30 hours]] On 23 March 2022, the proposal was discussed by the Chamber of Deputies at second reading. The Committee on the Environment, as a guarantee committee, renegotiated the bill on 30 March 2022, stating that no amendments had been tabled on the proposal, and maintained its recommendation to adopt the bill in the proposed version. I note that no amendment has been tabled on the proposal. Thank you for your attention.
