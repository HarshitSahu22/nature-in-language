I will briefly comment on this Government proposal for a law on coordination of cooperation with the European Anti-Fraud Office. The proposed legislation is presented on the grounds of the need to allow the fulfilment of the obligation under Article 4 (1) (a) of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 to be carried out. Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, 3a of Regulation 883/2013 and the need to implement these aspects of cooperation with OLAF in the area of the draft law which require implementation. A certain section of cooperation between the authorities of the Czech Republic and OLAF is thus regulated. The draft law does not have an ambition to regulate cooperation with OLAF in criminal matters or in the correct legal field under other, directly applicable European Union rules. The results of OLAF's controls on the beneficiaries of the subsidy are merely of a recommendation nature. Therefore, there is no legal act directly affecting the rights and obligations of a controlled person or any measure which he would have to take without a further competent authority and begin to comply with it. The draft law primarily defines a new obligation, in so far as it provides information available in the central accounts and cash transaction records. In addition to providing this information, the role of the Ministry of Finance in the so-called anti-fraud coordination service is added to the proposed text in order to ensure effective cooperation with OLAF. Thank you.
