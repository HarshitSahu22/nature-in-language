ParlaMint-CZ_2022-06-17-ps2021-025-08-005-013.u2	Thank you again, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present the Government's amendment to Act No. 378/2007 Sb. The main objective of the bill is to adapt two directly applicable EU rules, namely the regulation on veterinary medicinal products and the regulation on the production, placing on the market and use of medicated feedingstuffs. Both Regulations shall take effect on 28 January 2022. In order to ensure proper adaptation, it is therefore necessary for national legislation to take effect as soon as possible. The regulation on veterinary medicinal products has taken over the structure and a large part of the content of the regulation of veterinary medicinal products and the existing legislation contained in Directive 2001/82/EC. Therefore, much of the legislation does not change in terms of content. It's only a form that's changing, that's the place in the directive. The legislation is now contained in a directly applicable regulation. The draft law therefore repeals certain provisions concerning veterinary medicinal products and medicated feedingstuffs which have been duplicated. It was also necessary to adapt to a consistent distribution of legislation on medicinal products for human and veterinary use in many places. In addition to the Act on Medicines, the Act on Advertising Regulation, the Law on Feed and the Act on Administrative Fees are further amended to ensure proper adaptation. The Government's draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 8th meeting on 5 May 2022 and recommended by its resolution to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the Government's draft law. Thank you for your attention.
