ParlaMint-CZ_2022-09-08-ps2021-035-03-012-004.u4	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, no amendments have been tabled to the Government Bill on the entry and import of certain cultural goods into the customs territory of the European Union and on the amendment of certain related laws, namely the Parliamentary press number 150. The House press 150 was discussed by the Guarantee Committee on Science, Education, Culture, Youth and Sports on 22. 6. 2022 and Resolution 70 of the Guarantee Committee recommended that the House Press 150 be approved in the submitted text. At the meeting of the Guarantee Committee, there was broad agreement on this matter. Just to remind you, I would briefly sum up what is being done in the Houses of the Press 150. It is a government bill on the entry and import of certain cultural goods into the customs territory of the European Union and on the amendment of certain related laws. It is presented on account of the adaptation of domestic law to Regulation 2019/880 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2019 on the entry and import of cultural goods. The implementation period for the part of the regulation to which this law applies has already expired on 28 December 2020. The need for adoption of the proposed legal standard results from the commitments of the Czech Republic given by its membership in the European Union and from an intense interest in protecting world cultural heritage from illegal export and criminal activity. Thank you.
