The aim of this bill is to amend the relevant provisions of the Act on Administrative Fees and the Act on Advertising Regulation in connection with the present draft Act on Medical Devices. By amending the Act on Advertising Regulation, the Czech rule of law is adapted to the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council of Europe 2017/746 of 7 April 2017. This is a repeal of Directive 98/79/EC and Commission Decision 2010/227/EU. So we should just look at some details here. We have, of course, a rather large discussion on the Health Committee. In heading 97, the word "medical' is deleted after the word "medics' and specified information is inserted there.
