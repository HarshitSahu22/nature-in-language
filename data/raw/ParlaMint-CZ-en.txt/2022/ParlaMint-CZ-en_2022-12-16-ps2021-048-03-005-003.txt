Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, as has already been said, I present to you the House Press No 106, the bill amending Act No 106. 480/2004 Coll., on certain services of the information society and on the amendment of certain laws, as amended. [[Act on certain information society services]] [[12.20 hours]] The draft amendment is submitted because of the adaptation of Regulation 2019/1150 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019 on the promotion of justice and transparency for business users of online brokering services, which provides for the definition of trade relations between Internet intermediary service providers and business users of their services. Specifically, it is to ensure adequate and effective enforcement of the regulation in question and the designation of the supervisory authority of the Czech Republic. We have received an amendment on the proposal from Mr . Vojtěch Munzar. This proposal aims to clarify the text from the point of view of the procedure of the Czech Telecommunications Authority in the event of a minor breach of the obligation provided for by the European Regulation in question. We can agree with this amendment. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to support the proposed bill for two reasons. First, because of the urgency of time. The deadline for adaptation to the legal order expired in July 2020 and the current situation could therefore have a negative impact on the Czech Republic. The second reason is that it is only a technical amendment intended to ensure the adequate and effective enforcement of the regulation in question. Thank you.
