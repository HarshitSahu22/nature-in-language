ParlaMint-CZ_2022-06-16-ps2021-025-07-011-086.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the Government proposal by which Parliament is to present its agreement to ratify the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Armenia on facilitating the application of the European Convention on Extradition. The purpose of this Agreement is to resolve cases where the citizens of one Contracting Party are in the territory of the other Contracting Party in absentia, but after a proper summons sentenced to commit an offence, but before the entry and execution of the sentence imposed, they will return to the territory of their home State. There is currently no contractual basis for the transfer of enforcement, since the Republic of Armenia has not ratified the Additional Protocol to the Convention on the Transfer of Convicted Persons, which allows the execution of the sentence imposed to the State of nationality of the person concerned in the event that the person was sentenced to prison and that person fled to the State of its nationality before the sentence was imposed. Moreover, neither the Czech Republic nor the Republic of Armenia normally issue their own nationals. Any request for extradition could therefore not be accepted in such cases. The implementation of the agreement will be under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Justice, the Ministry of the Interior, which provides communication through the Interpol National Central Office and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which ensures communication through diplomatic channels. The implementation of the Agreement will not be linked to increased financial requirements for the State budget and will be covered by the chapters of those ministries. Thank you for your attention and please give a favourable assessment of the government proposal.
