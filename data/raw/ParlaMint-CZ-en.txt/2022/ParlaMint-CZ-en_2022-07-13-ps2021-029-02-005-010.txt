ParlaMint-CZ_2022-07-13-ps2021-029-02-005-010.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to rephrase the Government's bill amending Law No 1 97/1996 Coll., on the protection of hops. The aim of the draft law is to modernise the register of hops and their link to the control processes and to rephrase the requirements for the application for the inclusion of hops in the register of hops. The proposal will allow the Central Inspection and Testing Institute of Agricultural Dogs to carry out checks to verify the information contained in the hop register, to initiate the procedure for updating the official record if it finds non-compliance between the data contained in the register and the facts, and to further facilitate the updating of the models of documents needed to comply with the provisions of the law by publishing them on the website of the Central Inspection and Testing Institute of Agriculture. The proposal shall also continue to adapt to the relevant Commission Regulation laying down detailed rules for the verification of hops and hop products, in particular with a view to anchoring the arrangements for the verification of hops and hop products directly in the law, not only in the decree. The draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies at its 8th meeting on 5 May this year, and it recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the Government's draft law. No amendments were made at the second reading on 17 June this year. Thank you for your attention.
