ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-10-ps2021-014-01-004-004.u2	Good afternoon. Madam President, honourable Members, I am presenting to the Parliament of the Czech Republic a draft budget of the State Environmental Fund for 2022 and the medium-term outlook of the State Environmental Fund for 2023 and 2022. The draft budget of the State Fund is presented in accordance with the Act on the State Environmental Fund of the Czech Republic and the Act on budgetary rules. The Government approved the draft budget on 16 February 2022. The draft budget was further discussed by the Committee on the Environment and approved on 23 February 2022. Budget revenues and expenditure of the State Environmental Fund of the Czech Republic are not a separate chapter of the state budget, but are aggregated into the category of public finances. The revenues of the State Environmental Fund for 2022 are budgeted at CZK 33.39 billion. A significant increase compared to revenues in previous years is due mainly to the budgeted revenue from the state budget of Chapter 315, namely the National Recovery Plan of CZK 9 billion and revenue from the expenditure of the Modernisation Fund from the European Investment Bank of CZK 20 billion. The basic revenue of the State Fund results from charges for environmental pollution or from the drawing of natural resources, including income from the Waste Act, estimated income of CZK 850 million, income for the drawing of groundwater, estimated income of CZK 312 million, revenues for discharges of contaminated waste water, estimated income of CZK 180 million. Another source of revenue is the reimbursement of the State Fund's costs already incurred, including, for 2022, transfers from Chapter 315 of the Ministry of Finance to the programme of financing water management projects of municipalities through the State Environmental Fund amounting to CZK 1.2 billion, transfers from the State Budget to the reimbursement of the State Fund, expenses paid by the European Union Technical Assistance Programme OPŽP 2014+, and costs of the State for the administration of the New Green Savings Programme and from the National Fund for financing EEA Financial Mechanisms projects by Norway. The estimated amount of refund for 2022 is CZK 641 million. The expenditure of the State Environmental Fund in 2022 in the budgeted amount of CZK 27.55 billion consists primarily of the following items: 12.8 billion Modernisation Fund, 9.01 billion National Recovery Plan, 4.2 billion National Environmental Programme, 280 million EEA financial mechanisms Norway, 250 million umbrella schemes OPZP 2021+, 240 million loans for financing projects OPZP 2014+. [[16.20 hours]] The total planned amount of all expenditure, including expenditure of the State Fund of the RU administered in 2022, will reach CZK 41.55 billion in the following structure: CZK 27.55 billion in individual calls for programmes financed from the budget of the State Fund of the CR and expenditure on the operation of the State Fund of the RU in the total budgeted amount, CZK 11 billion in 2014+, CZK 2 billion in the OPŽP 2021+ and CZK 1 billion in the Operational Programme Fair Transformation. [[RU?]] Expenditure for the OPZP 2014+, 2021+ and Fair Transformations are declared in the expenditure of Chapter 315 of the Ministry of Finance. The budgeted operating expenditure of the State Fund of the RU for the year 2022 amounts to CZK 716 million, i.e. 1.72% of the total planned amount of expenditure administered by the State Fund of the RU in 2022. The financing of most of the programmes financed from the state fund budget has no impact on the state budget except 9 billion on the pre-financing of the National Recovery Plan, then will be refunded from the EU, 1.2 billion subsidies for water management projects, 641 million technical assistance of the operational programme of the RU New Green Savings and Refund of the Norwegian Funds, 250 million CZK transfer from the state budget to the umbrella schemes of OPZP 2021+ and 100 million for the New Green Savings programme. The budget of the State Fund for 2022 also includes a proposal for a medium-term perspective that predicts revenue and expenditure between 2023 and 2024. Thank you for your attention and for your support and approval. Thank you.
