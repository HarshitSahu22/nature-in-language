ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-02-ps2021-009-04-012-013.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present the Government's amendment to Act No. 378/2007 Sb., on pharmaceuticals. The main objective of the bill is to adapt two directly applicable EU rules, namely the regulation on veterinary medicinal products and the regulation on the production, placing on the market and use of medicated feedingstuffs. Both Regulations shall take effect on 28 January 2022. In order to ensure proper adaptation, it is therefore necessary for national legislation to take effect as soon as possible. The regulation on veterinary medicinal products has taken over the structure and a large part of the content of the regulation of veterinary medicinal products and the existing legislation contained in Directive 2001/82/EC. So much of the legislation does not change in terms of content, only the form is changing, this is the place in the directive, the legislation is now contained directly in the regulation applied. The draft law therefore repeals certain provisions concerning veterinary medicinal products and medicated feedingstuffs which have been duplicated. It was also necessary in a number of places to make a consistent distribution of legislation on medicinal products for human and veterinary use. In addition to the Act on Medicines, the Act on Advertising Regulation, the Law on Feed and the Act on Administrative Fees are further amended to ensure proper adaptation. Thank you for your attention.

[[12.50 hours]] The main objective of this proposal is to adapt Regulation 2019/6 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2018 on veterinary medicinal products and repealing Directive 2001/82/EC as well as Regulation 2019/4 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the production, placing on the market and use of medicated feedingstuffs, amending Regulation 183/2005 of the European Parliament and of the Council and simplifying Directive 90/167/EEC. The declared objectives of this Regulation are to reduce the administrative burden on the veterinary pharmaceutical industry, strengthen the internal market, increase the availability of veterinary medicinal products while ensuring the highest level of public health, including addressing the issue of antimicrobial resistance of animals and the protection of the environment. As the Minister said here, the regulation will enter into force on 28 January 2022. The draft law therefore repeals the provisions relating to veterinary medicinal products which are directly regulated in the regulation on veterinary medicinal products and re-establishes the powers of the legal authorities in the performance of the state administration in the field of veterinary medicinal products. Furthermore, the rules laid down in the Regulation on veterinary medicinal products shall be added where the Regulation so requires or permits, or where this is strictly necessary for the completeness of the legislation taking into account the needs of veterinary practice. The facts of infringements of the obligations laid down in the Regulation on veterinary medicinal products and the obligations laid down in this Act on medicinal products are also laid down. The adaptation of the legal order of the Czech Republic in relation to the two regulations requires changes in other legislations, therefore the draft act contains a further amendment to Act No. 40/1995 Coll., on Advertising Regulation, Amendment to Act No. 91/1996 Coll., on Feeding, and Amendment to Act No. 634/2004 Coll., on State Charges. I would like to propose an agricultural committee as a guarantee committee, and I would like to ask you to be released for second reading. Thank you for your attention.
