Mr Vice-President, fellow Members, allow me to present this House's printing number 318 and therefore the draft budget of the State Environmental Fund for 2023 and the medium-term outlook for 2024 to 2025. The essential content of this proposal, when we look at 2023, is the revenues of the State Environmental Fund of CZK 32.2 billion. The largest volume of these revenues is revenue from the transfer of the European Investment Bank to finance the Modernisation Fund, which is CZK 28 billion. It is also revenue from the state budget from Chapter 315 of the Ministry of the Environment for the implementation of the National Recovery Plan amounting to CZK 720 million. From Chapter 315 of the Ministry of Environment, I also expect additional income, namely the reimbursement of the costs already incurred by the State Environmental Fund in the amount of CZK 1.3 billion. In particular, it covers operational expenditure from the technical assistance of operational programmes. Last but not least, the income of the State Environmental Fund is income resulting from legislation, i.e. environmental pollution charges or natural resources that are budgeted at CZK 2 billion. As regards the expenditure of the State Environmental Fund for 2023, these are budgeted at CZK 34.5 billion and are broken down into individual subsidy programmes as follows: Expenditure on the implementation of projects in the Modernisation Fund is expected at CZK 23 billion. This is mainly about investment support for modernization of heat plants and industrial plants, construction of photovoltaic power plants and last but not least the financing of the New Green Savings Programme, including New Green Savings Light for Low Income Households. CZK 6.5 billion are planned for the National Recovery Plan, i.e. funding of the New Green Savings Programme, energy savings of public buildings, and CZK 3.4 billion will be paid under the National Environment Programme. Mostly support for municipalities for the construction of sewers and taps. CZK 361 million will be provided through the financial mechanism of the European Economic Area - The adaptation projects, the loans from the State Environmental Fund are planned in the amount of CZK 342,2 million. This is co-financing of the project of the Operational Programme Environment. The expenditure on the roofing scheme amounts to CZK 50 million. Support for domestic waste water treatment plants is planned to pay CZK 44.6 million for innovative financial instruments in the form of revolving loans from the sources of European funds. The State Environmental Fund also administers projects that are audited in chapter 315 of the Ministry of Environment. For 2023, expenditure on the financing of the Operational Programme Environment 2014+ and 2021+ is planned totalling CZK 18 billion, i.e. sewerage, plumbing, energy savings, water retention projects in the countryside and so on. Expenditure of the Operational Programme Fair Transformation of CZK 7 billion, in particular the implementation of strategic projects, and expenditure on financing of the Programme New Green Savings from Chapter 315, amounting to CZK 2 billion. The budgeted operating expenditure of the State Environmental Fund for 2023 amounts to CZK 750 million, i.e. 1.2% of the total planned amount of expenditure administered in 2023. The total planned amount of all expenditure, including expenditure by the State Environmental Fund administered, is therefore predicted to amount to approximately CZK 60.7 billion in 2023. The budget of the State Environmental Fund for 2023 also includes a proposal for a medium-term perspective, which includes revenue and expenditure between 2024 and 2025. The main direct expenditures will be under the Modernisation Fund, where the annual financial flow of around 35 billion crowns is expected. Thank you for your attention.
