ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-02-ps2021-009-04-010-010.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, we are putting forward a draft law amending Act No. 111/1994 Coll., on Road Transport, as amended, and other related laws. The main objective of the proposed bill is to ensure the implementation of a number of EU regulations at legal level. Above all, these are regulations which form part of the so-called mobility package. The bill addresses several areas of legislation. Firstly, it is the operation of international road transport and cabotage by vehicles or combinations of vehicles of the largest permissible weight of 2,5 to 3.5 tonnes, intended for the transport of animals or goods, where the bill changes in particular the definition of a large vehicle and the subject matter of the licensed trade linked to road freight motor transport. It is also the posting of drivers by carriers established in other countries, where the bill defines cases where domestic labour rules do not apply to drivers sent to the Czech Republic. It also lays down the administrative obligations of foreign carriers in connection with sending a driver to our territory and, at the same time, provides for the competence of the state administration authorities to exercise control and enforcement of compliance with those rules. The draft law also provides for the exercise of control and state professional supervision of compliance with the requirements for weekly working hours, the length of shifts at night and breaks on food and driver's rest, including the necessary exchange of data. The draft law also provides for the possibility of temporary derogations from the requirements for driving time, safety breaks and rest periods and for the requirements for keeping records of these facts, which will be determined by time and territorially limited measures of a general nature issued by the Ministry of Transport. The draft law also deals with cross-border cooperation in the field of control of the establishment of carriers and compliance with vehicle driving time requirements, safety breaks and rest periods and record-keeping requirements, while the Ministry of Transport will receive and handle requests from other Member States and the transport authorities will carry out the required checks. A new penalty will be imposed on the customers of the transport in case they knew or could have known that the carriage of the carrier would have infringed any of the selected requirements. The draft law also reflects the implementation of other partial requirements arising from EU regulations, such as the demonstration of financial capacity and the extension of the list of data held in the register of road transport entrepreneurs. The Ministry of Transport will also carry out the performance of state professional supervision in road transport to a greater extent, at the same time completely exempting the competence of customs authorities from the Road Transport Act. The draft law also aims to reduce the administrative burden on recipients of legislation by allowing entrepreneurs, for example, not to have to report permanent changes to the data on vehicles operated by them. Finally, additional requirements from application practice are incorporated into the bill. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to support the proposed bill amending the Road Transport Act and other related regulations. Thank you very much for your attention.
