ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-03-ps2021-009-05-009-074.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, this is a government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Armenia on facilitating the application of the European Convention on Extradition of 13 December 1957. The purpose of the Agreement negotiated between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Armenia on facilitating the application of the European Convention on Extradition is to resolve cases where citizens of one Contracting Party are sentenced in the territory of the other Contracting Party in absentia for the commission of a criminal offence, but before the entry and execution of the imposed penalty, they will return to the territory of their home state. Since neither the Czech Republic nor the Republic of Armenia issue its own nationals as a standard, any extradition requests could not be granted in such cases. [[12.50 hours]] In order to fulfil the purpose of criminal proceedings, it is not appropriate to transmit criminal proceedings under the European Convention on the Transfer of Criminal Procedure. Since Armenian law for the transfer of prison sentences requires a contractual basis, the Czech and Armenian parties have already started negotiations on this agreement in 2016, on the basis of which it would be possible to carry out the transfer of prison sentences between these States. Thank you for your attention and any support.
