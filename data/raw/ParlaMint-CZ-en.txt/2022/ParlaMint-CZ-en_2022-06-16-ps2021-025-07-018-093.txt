ParlaMint-CZ_2022-06-16-ps2021-025-07-018-093.u2	Thank you, Madam President. Ladies and gentlemen, may I briefly present to you the Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty on Social Security between the Czech Republic and Bosnia and Herzegovina, signed in Sarajevo on 18 May 2021. So far, social security relations are governed by a contract with former Yugoslavia, written in 1957. The contract is very outdated and does not meet current needs. The new Treaty corresponds to internationally recognised requirements for the coordination of social security, applied in bilateral and multilateral treaties, and also to EU rules. It defines participation in social and health insurance in the Contracting States, regulates sickness and maternity benefits, cash benefits and health care, pensions, occupational injury benefits, unemployment benefits and child allowance. The change from previous treatment is in the field of provision and payment of health care - the cost is no longer borne by the state budget, but by the health insurance company for which the person is insured. Thank you for your attention and please agree to ratify this agreement.
