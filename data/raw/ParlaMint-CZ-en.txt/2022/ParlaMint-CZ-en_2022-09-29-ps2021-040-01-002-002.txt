As has already been said here by the Minister, the reason for submitting the bill requires an accelerated response to the economic consequences of Russian aggression in Ukraine. In this context, and in order to enable Member States to implement appropriate measures to support the economy, the European Commission adopted the Temporary Crisis Framework for State aid measures to support the economy after Russia's aggression against Ukraine. The temporary framework allows, inter alia, the introduction of financial instruments to support liquidity in the form of subsidised State guarantees for investment loans or working capital loans to ensure that banks continue to grant loans to all companies affected by the current crisis. The proposed legislation aims to enable the Export Insurance Company to provide a guarantee in line with the Temporary Framework for new individual loans granted to business entities. The Export Insurance Corporation is already in 2020 and 2021 under the amendment of Act No. 58/1995 Coll. implemented by Law No 214/2020 Coll. it provided guarantees for loans in response to a crisis caused by the pandemic of covid-19 diseases. The proposal provides for the possibility for guarantees to be provided in other emergency situations, with the continued purpose and scope of the guarantees, the conditions for granting them, the amount of coverage of the outstanding credit principal and the procedure for the payment of all funds from the State budget to the Export Insurance Corporation under the guarantee scheme laid down by the Government Regulation. Given that the Temporary Framework allows the provision of guarantees only until the end of 2022, it is necessary to discuss this draft law promptly and to approve it as soon as possible. For that reason, it is proposed that the Chamber of Deputies comply with § 90 para. 2 of Act No. 90/1995 Coll., on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, as amended, agreed with the bill at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
