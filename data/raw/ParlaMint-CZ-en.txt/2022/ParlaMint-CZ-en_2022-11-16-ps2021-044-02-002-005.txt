ParlaMint-CZ_2022-11-16-ps2021-044-02-002-005.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to briefly comment on the proposal tabled, the material submitted, the bill amending Act No. 458/2000 Coll., on business conditions and on the performance of state administration in the energy sectors and on the amendment of certain laws, the Energy Act, as amended, and Act No. 183/2006 Coll., on Landscape Planning and Construction Regulations, Construction Act, as amended. In view of the unprecedented situation in the energy market and the need to address energy security and reduce dependence on fuel imports from the Russian Federation, the Ministry of Industry and Trade is proposing an amendment to the Energy Act, which significantly facilitates the authorisation processes for renewable energy sources. The Government approved the draft amendment to the Energy Act at its meeting on 26 September 2022 and decided to supplement paragraphs 7 to 9 of Section 19d, according to which participants in the electricity and gas market can obtain an exceptional market situation under the conditions laid down in the Government's regulation on lending, credit or state guarantee to avoid damage in the national economy or to address an exceptional market situation. The draft law is presented in order to promote the optimal development of the use of renewable resources and thus to contribute to the fulfilment of the European Union's commitments in the field of energy and climate while ensuring the energy self-sufficiency of the Czech Republic. The current energy law is inadequately responding to the potential for the use of energy resources to ensure the coverage of its own electricity consumption. According to the valid version, entities must become licensed entities, that is, energy market operators, even though their main motivation is self-production, not energy business. The proposed amendment to the Energy Act increases the limit of the power plant's power generation from which the electricity licence granted by the Energy Regulatory Authority is required, in connection with the definition of the business in the energy sectors, from 10 kW to 50 kW. The proposed amendment to the Energy Act also includes an amendment to the Construction Act, which aims to facilitate decision-making by the construction authorities and to simplify the approval processes on the location of the construction for renewable power plants with installed power, again up to 50 kW. For installation of a renewable energy source with a total installed power of up to 50 kW, which is part of the construction, for example photovoltaics on the roof, if they meet the conditions of installation safety, no building permit or notification to the building office will be necessary. At the same time, resources for the production of electricity from renewable sources with installed power up to 50 kW, with the exception of the construction of a waterworks, if they are located in a built-up area or in a pawnable area, will not require a decision on the location of the building or territorial approval. The draft law was discussed on 25 October 2022 by the Economic Committee and approved the following amendments: the acceleration of the authorisation processes for connections up to 25 metres and their connection to the distribution system, the amendment of the construction law on spatial planning in order to facilitate the production of energy from renewable sources and the adaptation of the Act on supported energy sources, which responds to the ongoing notification procedure with the European Commission. The Economic Committee approved yet another amendment, namely the technical modification of the supplier of the last instance, according to which the supplier of the last instance may be more traders in the scope of electricity or gas supply by mutual agreement. The Ministry of Industry and Trade agrees with all of the above amendments. The proposed modifications to the Energy Construction Act and the Act on Supported Energy Resources will contribute significantly to speeding up the construction of renewable energy production plants, while also bringing about a reduction in administrative requirements and efficiency in the processes necessary for the construction and operation of renewable energy resources at the level of state and public administration. Thank you for your attention.
