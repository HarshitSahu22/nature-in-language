ParlaMint-CZ_2022-05-13-ps2021-019-08-001-075.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, just a very brief introduction to the material with which you had the opportunity to get acquainted both at first and second reading. We submit a draft law amending Act No. 111/1994, on road transport, as amended, and other related laws. The main objective of the bill is to ensure the implementation of a number of European Union regulations at the legal level. Above all, these are regulations which form part of the so-called mobility package. The bill addresses several areas of legislation. Firstly, it is the operation of international road transport and cabotage by vehicles or combinations of vehicles of the largest permissible weight of 2,5 to 3.5 tonnes intended for the transport of animals or goods, where the draft law changes in particular the definition of a large vehicle and the object of the licensed trade linked to road freight motor transport. It is also the transmission of drivers to carriers established in other states. Furthermore, the draft law regulates the exercise of control and state professional supervision of compliance with the requirements for weekly working hours. Furthermore, it is to establish the possibility of temporary derogations from vehicle driving requirements and safety breaks and rest periods. It also deals with cross-border cooperation in the field of control of the establishment of carriers. It also provides for a new penalty for transport agents in case they knew or could have known that they would have violated any of the selected requirements by carrying out the transport of the carrier. The draft law also aims to reduce the administrative burden on recipients of legislation by allowing entrepreneurs, for example, not to have to report permanent changes to the data on vehicles operated by them. Finally, additional requirements from application practice are incorporated into the bill. The Economic Committee adopted six amendments to the draft law with which the Ministry of Transport agrees. Other amendments have not been applied. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to approve the Government's bill, as amended by the amendments.
