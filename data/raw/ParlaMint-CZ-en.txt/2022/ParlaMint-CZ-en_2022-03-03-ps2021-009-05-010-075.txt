Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Agreement between the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and the Republic of Armenia, on the other hand, on air transport /German press 133/ - first reading Please have Minister of Transport Martin Kupka present the opening words.
ParlaMint-CZ_2022-03-03-ps2021-009-05-010-075.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the material presented contains the text of the air agreement jointly negotiated and signed by representatives of the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and Armenia, on the other. This agreement aims to adapt the area of civil aviation between the European Union and Armenia in a uniform manner by replacing the relevant provisions of the existing bilateral air agreements between the Member States of the European Union and Armenia, including the one between the Czech Republic and Armenia of 2010. The agreement creates a common liberalised transport market with new opportunities for air carriers, which naturally contributes to improving the services offered and creating a fair and competitive environment. The agreement is in accordance with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic. Its negotiation does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not affect state budget expenditure. I hereby request the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to refer the material to the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
