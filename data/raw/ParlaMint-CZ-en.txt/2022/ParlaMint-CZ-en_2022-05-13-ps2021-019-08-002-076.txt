[[10.10 hours]] The bill can be divided into three basic areas. Firstly, the safety management of selected roads during their design, construction and operation. Another area is the provision of a European electronic toll service and a third area of information on the absence of payment of a time fee or toll in cross-border situations. The only change that does not come from Union law is the abolition of the obligation to equip a vehicle with an electronic device in the electronic toll system, i.e. the so-called OBU unit, if it is already apparent from its registration mark that it is a vehicle exempted from charging, that is, if it has a military registration mark, an electric vehicle registration mark or a special registration mark of a historic vehicle. The Economic Committee adopted an amendment to the draft law concerning the extension of the possibility of general use of roads by oversized vehicles, as well as the domestic transport of working machinery used for the repair or maintenance of roads. This is a significant material relief for those who have to take care of repairs and reconstruction of communications. In this respect, no one uses the possibility of moving on the roads of the first, second and third classes to travel just for fun with the finisher or with other oversized cargo in the territory of the Czech Republic.
