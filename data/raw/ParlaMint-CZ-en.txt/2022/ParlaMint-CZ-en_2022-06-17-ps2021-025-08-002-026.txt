The proposed approval of the law at first reading in accordance with the procedure laid down in § 90 para. The Rules of Procedure are justified by the expiry of the deadline for the adaptation of the Czech rule of law pursuant to the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2019 entitled Act on Cyber Security. As a Member State of the European Union, the Czech Republic has an obligation to adapt its legal order under a regulation of the European Union called the Cyber Security Act. The objective of the Cyber Security Act is to identify the objectives, tasks and organisational aspects of the European Union Agency for Cyber Security and to establish a Union system for product certification, processes and services of ICT. [[ENISA]] The aim of the proposed adaptation government bill is to establish, as required by the regulation, the so-called national cybersecurity certification body. That authority shall have a number of tasks set out in the Regulation, in particular the task of overseeing the rules included in European cybersecurity certification systems and enforce those rules, where appropriate, to carry out the authorisation of bodies, conformity assessment and so on. Furthermore, the draft law contains an adjustment to sanctions for infringements of the Cyber Security Act and the follow-up European cybersecurity certification systems and certain procedural aspects, the authorisation procedure resulting from the European Regulation. As has already been mentioned by the Prime Minister, given that the deadline for adaptation laid down by the European regulation has already expired, the current bill is confined solely to the adaptation issue. In this context, the Government is proposing to the Chamber of Deputies that the Government's bill should be approved at first reading. I support this proposal.
