ParlaMint-CZ_2022-09-30-ps2021-035-06-006-113.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly remind you of what has already been said here at first and second reading. The reason for submitting the bill is the obligation to adapt our rules of law to the regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April on the entry and import of cultural goods intended to protect the integrity of cultural heritage in the countries of its origin. The draft law sets out the procedure of the Ministry of Culture and Customs if the entry of a cultural property from a third country into the customs territory of the European Union is contrary to the legislation of that third country and to its capture by the Czech customs office. At the same time, it lays down rules for the safe preservation and protection of such an article, its examination by a professional contribution organisation only after any decision on its seizure and subsequent return to the country of origin, which is the main objective. The deadline for the adaptation of the said European Directive and the regulation thus expired on 28 December 2020. The delay was mainly due to the failure to complete the legislative process by the last Chamber of Deputies. I welcome the recommendation from the Committee on Science, Education, Culture, Youth and Sports to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the draft law in the submitted version.
