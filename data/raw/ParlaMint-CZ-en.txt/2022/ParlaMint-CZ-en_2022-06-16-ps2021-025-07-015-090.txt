ParlaMint-CZ_2022-06-16-ps2021-025-07-015-090.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, the reason for submitting this proposal is to agree with the additional ratification of the International Convention on Mutual Administrative Assistance in the Prevention, Investigation and Repression of Customs Delicacies. The additional ratification of the Convention, which has so far been undertaken by 54 States, will extend the number of States with which preliminary and necessary cooperation can take place. The customs authorities of the Czech Republic will be able to properly meet the objectives of the Convention in the fight against illicit trade in goods, from narcotic psychotropic substances to, for example, cultural goods, medicines or endangered species of wildlife and plants. Of course, this will also succeed in suppressing fraud in joint customs operations. This is the aim of this ratification and our agreement with it. Thank you so much for any support.
