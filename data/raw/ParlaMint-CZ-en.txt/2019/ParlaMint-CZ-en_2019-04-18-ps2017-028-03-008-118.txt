ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-18-ps2017-028-03-008-118.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Inter-American Convention, respectively. The Czech Republic's accession to it will expand our contractual base by a total of 25 countries with which we do not have a treaty on cooperation in criminal matters today, namely Latin America and the Caribbean. This is therefore a rather significant shift, and thanks to the fact that we will have concluded this cooperation agreement with them in criminal matters, our law enforcement authorities will be much easier to carry out such acts in the form of requests in the territory of those foreign states, such as service of documents, inspection of the place, interrogation of persons and so on. This means that this should make it very easy and, in particular, speed up cooperation where there will therefore be an international element in the direction of the countries with which we do not yet have the agreement. Example - I can mention several states, there are Colombia, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina etc., etc. This means that our contractual base will really expand in a fundamental way. At the same time, two reservations from the Czech Republic are proposed on the contract, both a reservation of mutual criminality for the execution of acts and a reservation according to which we will not be obliged to allow the transit of a Czech citizen if it is transported under the regime of this Convention. As far as the option protocol is concerned, it then extends the contractual base to tax offences which are otherwise excluded by the basic convention, so that, thanks to the option protocol, cooperation in criminal matters will also be possible here. It is therefore, as I have already said, a very beneficial thing in terms of the effectiveness of cooperation in the area of criminal law, and I ask the power to agree to ratify both the Convention itself and the Optional Protocol. Thank you for your attention.
