ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-019-136.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, at the meeting, I am now presenting a double taxation treaty with the Republic of Korea, signed on 12 January 2018 in Seoul. The foreign policy of the Czech Republic is certainly aimed at developing mutually beneficial economic relations with other countries of the world. The conclusion of this international tax contract follows the intention to limit the tax burden on business entities when excluding unintended international double taxation. At present, while an international double taxation treaty is being applied in the mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Korea, it is nevertheless a contract that has been in force since 1995 and which no longer complies with the current conditions. For these reasons, the competent authorities of both countries have agreed to renegotiate it. The Double Taxation Treaty, which is currently being proposed in the relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Korea, was prepared on the basis of OECD and UN model models, which, of course, responds to the internal tax rules of both countries. This tax agreement is very important for our business entities. The Republic of Korea is an advanced country and, at the same time, our large non-European trading partner. The long-term positive trend in the growth of mutual trade can be demonstrated by the turnover of trade between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Korea, which, for example, amounted to almost 5 billion in 2017. USD, which shows an increase of around 20 per cent compared to the previous annual period. There are already a number of major Korean investments in the Czech economy. For example, investments by Hyundai can be mentioned. At the same time, the Korean economy represents a significant part of the Far East's economic potential and it can be assumed that the country's position among our important economic partners will be strengthened further. I can conclude by saying that the text of the contract fully reflects developments and trends in the international tax area. This tax agreement will allow better coordination of the activities of the tax authorities of both states to limit possible tax evasion and, last but not least, will enable the relevant exchange of information. I therefore ask you, ladies and gentlemen, to take into account my facts and support the commandments of this treaty to the relevant House Committees. Thank you for your attention.
