ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-021-138.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. After the Bonn Convention, we'll try the Berne Convention. Let me briefly present the Government's proposal to approve the ratification of the Convention on the Protection of European Plane Growing Plants, Wild Animals and Natural Sites. The basis of this Convention is to protect, in particular, species and habitats whose protection requires the cooperation of more than one State. The proposal for additional ratification of the Convention is submitted to Parliament because the accession of the Czech Republic to the Berne Convention in 1998, which was then understood as a government treaty, took place before the amendment of the 2001 Constitution, which clearly amended the criteria defining the Treaty of the presidential category. And now for change. The 37th meeting of the Standing Committee of the Berne Convention, held in Strasbourg in December 2017, adopted an amendment to Annex II containing a list of strictly protected animal species. The amendment to this Annex consists in the inclusion of a new species, in this case a feature of the Balkan one. But in the Czech Republic this subspecies is not a feature, and therefore this change has no impact on the commitments of the Czech Republic towards the Berne Convention and does not require any change to national legislation. Thank you for your attention and thank you for bringing this Treaty forward together for further negotiations in the Foreign Committee. Thank you.
