ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-25-ps2017-026-04-009-030.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Dear colleagues, allow me to briefly revisit the Government's bill, which amends certain laws on the internal administration section, as part of the continuation of the interrupted first reading. The main reason for its submission is the need to adapt certain regulations within the competence of the Ministry of the Interior to the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on the promotion of the free movement of citizens by simplifying the requirements for the submission of certain authentic instruments in the European Union. This directly applicable European Union Regulation is intended to make it possible to replace the requirement for the official translation of certain documents, such as matriculation documents, with a multilingual standard form issued by the competent authority together with its own document. Following this, it is necessary in Czech law to introduce an obligation to issue these forms and also to accept them for documents originating from other Member States of the European Union. This change should bring administrative simplification to Czech citizens if they submit documents to the Czech authorities in a foreign language, as well as when they submit Czech documents abroad. Furthermore, the proposal includes, in particular, modifications aimed at specifying the conditions for the verification of copies and signatures, as well as related changes in the authorised conversion of documents. I would also like to draw attention to further measures to reduce the administrative burden, since it is proposed to establish and manage data boxes exclusively electronically, such as using an electronic ID card, without the need for a visit by the competent authority. Within the framework of the Basic Registers Act, the possibility is introduced to entrust the administration of basic registers with certain activities in issuing data extracts, data records and data from basic registers. In the Act on Administrative Fees, in particular following the terminology of the new Civil Code, the circle of persons exempt from certain administrative charges is updated, e.g. the fee for the verification of copies or signatures. The application of the Act is proposed for 16 February 2019, which corresponds to the date of entry into force of that European Regulation. In view of the need for an accelerated discussion of the adaptation legislation, they allow themselves to propose a reduction of the time limit for the examination of the draft law in the Committees of the Chamber of Deputies to 30 days. Thank you for your attention.
