Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Belarus on pension insurance signed in Minsk on 14 March 2018 /German press 165/ - first reading I ask that the opening words be given by the Minister of Labour and Social Affairs Jana Maláčová. Madam Secretary, please have your word.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-31-ps2017-026-07-002-124.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the main purpose of this Treaty is to define in which state a migrant worker will pay social security. It can be regarded as equivalent to a double taxation treaty in the social field. In addition to the adjustment of the contribution of insurance premiums, the adjustment of pension rights is a key part of the contract. It is based on the principle that the State provides a pension corresponding to the period of insurance completed under its legislation. If the periods of insurance completed in one of the Contracting States are insufficient for entitlement, but the sum of the periods in both countries is sufficient, the contract contains the principle of insurance addition and the possibility of determining the so-called partial pensions. In both content and formal terms, the contract is a standard international document. More detailed information on the contract shall be provided in the submission of the proposal. In the light of the above, I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies to release the proposal for second reading. Thank you.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-31-ps2017-026-07-002-124.u3	Thank you, Madam Secretary. The rapporteur for the first reading is Mr Michal Ratiborský. Mr. Rapporteur, please have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-31-ps2017-026-07-002-124.u4	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, honourable Members of the Government, dear colleagues, as the Minister has already said, the translated international pension agreement is a standard document that respects the basic recognised principles, that is to say equality of dealing with citizens, the nationality of insurance in the state of employment with precisely defined exceptions, the addition to insurance in both states for entitlement to benefits and, finally, export of pension benefits, that is to say, their pay abroad. The contract shall apply to old-age, invalidity and survivors' pensions, each of which shall provide a partial pension corresponding to the periods of insurance completed under its legislation. The contract will represent certain financial impacts on the state budget due to the number of Belarusian citizens who will be entitled to a Czech partial pension during the Czech period. But this is a relatively small number. For the sake of clarity, some 500 Belarusian citizens have a job permit and some 300 are doing business here. Although the number of Czech citizens employed and insured in Belarus is so far minimal, the contract will allow our citizens to take into account the time spent in Belarus in assessing pension rights in the Czech Republic even without parallel payment of insurance premiums in the Czech Republic. The Government of the Czech Republic expressed its resolution of 24 August 2016, number 756 and the Treaty was signed by the Czech Party on 14 March 2018 in Minsk. I recommend releasing the contract for second reading and ordering the Foreign Committee as a guarantee committee. Thank you for your attention.
