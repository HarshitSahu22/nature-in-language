ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-18-ps2017-028-03-011-121.u2	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, in the case of the Convention on Computer Crime, the Czech Party is already a Contracting Party. The Czech Republic is a Contracting Party to this Convention, however, in 2013, when it approached the Convention, it made a reservation which we are now proposing to withdraw, namely that, in the case of processing data retention requests, mutual criminality would no longer be investigated in the future. I note that this is a request where data must be quickly secured, there is no physical access to these data, respectively. these data are not currently made available into their content or to Czech law enforcement authorities, let alone foreign. In order to be able to switch to handover in the future, nothing changes there. There will continue to have to come another application and there will continue to be investigation of double criminality. In other words, the point now is, in principle, that at the request of the police authority, the police authority, makes a backup of data in order to prevent their loss or change in the future, and that data is not yet made available to anyone, let alone to the other Contracting Party. This is because it is necessary to examine double criminality at this time is really inefficient and it creates the risk that the data will be lost in the meantime, and it is basically some immediate unrepeatable act that should be done quickly from the nature of the matter. And that reservation prevents it. That's why the government is proposing to call it off. And I beg very much to support this government proposal.
