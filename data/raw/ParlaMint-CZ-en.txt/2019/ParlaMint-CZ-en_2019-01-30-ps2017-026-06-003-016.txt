ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-30-ps2017-026-06-003-016.u8	Good morning, Mr President, honourable members of the government, dear colleagues, dear colleagues. I would like to sign up for my amendment under the number of House document 2120. I'll just add a brief explanation. As has already been said here, the aim of the amendment to Act No. 187/2006 Coll., on sickness insurance, is to unify the percentage of all reduction limits for the calculation of the benefits of cash assistance in maternity and compensation in pregnancy and maternity at 100%. If we take the budgetary implications, they are quite significant in my opinion. From this point of view, I would like to propose a compromise proposal, where the first reduction limit is 100 per cent, from the amount above the first reduction limit to the second reduction limit would be 90 per cent and from the amount above the second reduction limit to the third reduction limit would be 60 per cent. The amount above the third reduction limit would no longer be taken into account. I am convinced that this compromise will find your support, and at the same time I dare ask you for it. Thank you for your attention.
