Information on flights and transits of armed forces of other states carried out through the territory of the Czech Republic in the second half of 2018 /German press 426/ This material will once again be presented by the Minister of Defence Lubomír Metnar. Minister, please take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-07-ps2017-035-10-004-273.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. This information provides a comprehensive overview of landings, flights and transits within 48 hours in the Czech Republic of other armed forces through the territory of the Czech Republic, for the second half of 2018. The material also includes a comparison of individual numbers of flights of foreign military aircraft and land transports of foreign armed forces over and over the territory of the Czech Republic over the last five years. Land and air transport of other armed forces was carried out during that period, mainly because of participation in military operations, participation in military exercises, trainings and demonstrations of military technology. For the purpose of crossing and landing in the territory of the Czech Republic, a total of 3 618 air transports including 727 charter flights and 262 landing of other armed forces were carried out. Land transport for the purpose of exercise and transit through the territory of the Czech Republic was carried out in a given period a total of 336 separate movements without the escort of the Military Police, 30 road currents and 4 railway transports. Thank you for your attention.
