ParlaMint-CZ_2019-12-03-ps2017-039-05-003-280.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Audit Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic carried out a check on the legitimacy and regularity of the distribution of funds and the management of state property in accordance with the relevant law, discussing complaints about the provider's progress in evaluating grant projects and submitting opinions to the Bureau of the Grant Agency. The main activities of the Grant Agency were to provide special-purpose support for the solution of grant projects in the reference period. In the report submitted, the Audit Board notes that the selection process and evaluation of grants were carried out in a standard manner and that the recommendations of expert panels and expert commissions were always respected over the past period. The members of the Board of Auditors physically carried out 51 inspections at the meetings of expert panels and expert committees during the reference period, i.e. October 2018 to September 2019, were also checked by 142 grant projects at 12 institutions at the location of the beneficiary's registered office. During the reference period, 23 complaints were received by the Supervisory Board concerning the failure to grant aid or the outcome of the opposition procedure. They were all settled by the Audit Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic. The draft annual remuneration for the activities of the members of the Supervisory Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic, which you all have at your disposal, is annexed to the report.
