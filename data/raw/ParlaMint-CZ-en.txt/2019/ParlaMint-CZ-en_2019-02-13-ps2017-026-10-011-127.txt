ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-011-127.u2	Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, the two protocols were adopted unanimously at the 39th session of the ICAO International Civil Aviation Organisation Assembly held in Montreal on 27 September to 7 October 2016. The Protocol amending Article 50 (a) of the Convention amends the number of members of the ICAO Council from current 36 to 40. This change is appropriate due to the increasing number of ICAO Member States. At the same time, it should increase the chances of the Czech Republic being elected to this important body and thus enable it to defend Czech and European interests more actively here in the future. Similarly, the Protocol amending Article 56 of the Convention introduces only one amendment consisting in increasing the number of members of the Air Commission from 19 to 21. The increase in the number of members is appropriate not only because of the increasing number of ICAO members, but also because more experts with diverse experience from different working environments, from different parts of the world can bring a new view of certain problems, significantly enrich the debate within the Air Commission and thus positively influence its outputs. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic for the referral of material for discussion in the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
