Proposal for a member of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund At the next election point, the election of the Supervisory Board member of the Wine Fund, unfortunately, we were not successful either. 161 ballots were issued here, so the quorum required for the election was 81. The valid and invalid ballots were delivered to 159 and two outstanding ballots were registered by the Election Commission. Jiří Dušek won 74 votes and Miroslav Volařík won 63 votes. Like I said, the deal was 81, so no one was elected. Unfortunately, the choice of this second round ends again and we will declare a new deadline.
