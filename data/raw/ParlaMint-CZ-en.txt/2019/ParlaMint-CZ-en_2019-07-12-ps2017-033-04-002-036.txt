To date, according to the applicable law, only persons with severe visual or auditory disabilities, persons with combined hearing and vision defects, persons with severe carrier and musculoskeletal disease and persons with severe mental retardation can benefit from a contribution to compensatory aids to varying extent. Our parliamentary amendment comes with a proposal to extend the circle of people who will be able to draw a contribution for some compensatory aids.
