ParlaMint-CZ_2019-03-13-ps2017-027-06-012-121.u2	Deputy Ombudsman, Ombudsman, we vote under Act No. 349/1999. In this case, the 60-day term for the election of the new representative of the Ombudsman will expire on 5 April, because it is at that date that the mandate of the current representative, Dr Stanislav Křečka. Under the quoted law, the Ombudsman's representative elects The Chamber of Deputies for a term of six years from candidates, two of which are proposed by the President of the Republic and the Senate. We addressed both the Office of the President of the Republic and the Office of the Senate. On 30 January, the electoral commission received the President's proposals, which are proposed by Professor Zdeněk Koudelka and Dr. Stanislav Křeček. On February 4, the Senate nomination, proposed by Professor Markéta Selucká and Dr. Eliška Wagnerová, arrived. The Election Commission sent both CVs and materials, letters from candidates, to the chairmen of the MPs. It adopted Resolution No 110 of 13 February, which states that all four proposed candidates meet the requirements which are necessary for our choice and assumption of this function. In resolution No 110 we make four proposals. Zdeněk Koudelka, designed by the President, Stanislav Křeček, designed by the President, Markéta Selucká, designed by the Senate, and Eliška Wagnerová, also proposed by the Senate. Once again, a special law does not provide for a choice. In the resolution, the motion for a choice is secret. Here I must say that the newly elected representative of the Ombudsman takes up his duties by making a pledge in the hands of the President of the Chamber of Deputies, who is legally obliged to do so within ten days of the day of the election, and his term of office is also for six years.
