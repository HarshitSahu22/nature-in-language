The remuneration of the time fee will be recorded in a newly established information system operated by the State Transport Infrastructure Fund, where appropriate exemptions from charging will also be registered. The check on the payment of the time fee will be carried out in particular by means of a video-detector where, using the camera system, the vehicle's license plate will be verified whether the vehicle has a paid time fee.
