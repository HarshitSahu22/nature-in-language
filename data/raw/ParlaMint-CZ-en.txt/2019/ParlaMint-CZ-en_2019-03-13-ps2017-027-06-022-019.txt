ParlaMint-CZ_2019-03-13-ps2017-027-06-022-019.u2	So hello, Mr President, honourable Members. Allow me to present the Government's draft amendment to Act No. 503/2012 Coll., on the State Land Office and on the amendment of certain related laws. The proposed amendment aims to make the activities of the State Land Office more effective and to address some of the current problems arising from its practice. There are three laws being amended: the Act on the State Land Office, the Act on the Treatment of Ownership Relations to the Land and the Law on Fines and Bail for Non-compliance with laws governing the transformation of agricultural cooperatives, etc. In particular, the aim of the draft amendment on the SAO is to anchor the state's priority in the transfer of the land from the reserve intended for State development programmes; specify the method of valuation of the assets; introduce a new type of claim transfer of the construction and related property placed on the land to unify ownership of the land and buildings on them. Proposal for a technical amendment to Act No. 229 optimises the procedure for the transfer of agricultural parcels to eligible persons by public offer and introduces the possibility of repeatedly offering the land in the public offer if the previous offer was in vain. Draft amendment of Act No. 39/1993 Coll., solves cases of repayment of bail made pursuant to this law by an entity that has disappeared in the meantime, with bail becoming the income of the state budget. Thank you for your attention.

The aim of the proposed amendment is to make the activities of the State Land Office more efficient and cost-effective. The State Land Office, as the manager of the State Land Reserve, will ensure that the Land Exchange does not fall under the law of 50,000 hectares, thereby ensuring proper and economical management of this property. By amending Act No. 229/1991 Coll. there is an adjustment of the procedure for issuing replacement land, which aims to reduce administrative complexity for both the State Land Office and the municipalities. It shall align the rights of persons who have received sufficient entitlement by transferring from relatives in a series of direct. It specifies in which circumstances the application is invalid and enables the land to be included repeatedly in public tenders. Thank you for your attention.
