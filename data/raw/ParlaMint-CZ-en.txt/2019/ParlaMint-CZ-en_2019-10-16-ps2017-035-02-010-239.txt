Proposal for the election of members of the Supervisory Board of the State Housing Development Fund In the next election we occupied two seats of members of the Supervisory Board of the State Housing Development Fund. This choice was only successful in part, in half. 174 tickets were issued and handed in. Jan Hrnčíř won 36 votes, Jan Kubík 80, Michal Kučera 37 and Jaroslava Nestěrová 94 votes. It means that Ms. Jaroslava Nestěrová was elected in the second round. And one place in this supervisory board remains vacant. It was the second choice. This means that it is over and the electoral commission will issue a new deadline for the proposal. And finally, the final election point.
