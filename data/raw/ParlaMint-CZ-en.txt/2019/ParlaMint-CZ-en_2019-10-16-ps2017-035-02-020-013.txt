Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Ministry of Transport is submitting a bill amending Act No. 111/1994., on road transport, as amended, and other related laws. The main objective of this proposal is, above all, the revision of the legislation on taxi services and the implementation of certain partial changes in other areas covered by the Road Transport Act. In the area of taxi services, the rules for the operation of taxi services are clearly regulated in connection with its liberalisation by using popular mobile applications and other electronic means of communication consisting in the waiver of the requirement to fulfil certain obligations, namely the mandatory use of a taxi meter and the marking of vehicles by a roof lamp. At the same time, it is proposed to reduce the obligations of taxi carriers and drivers in such a way as to avoid an unjustified advantage for the provision of taxi services. In alternative ways. At the same time, however, the taxi-service regulation is complemented by certain institutes that will lead to better protection and enforcement of customers' rights. That's what we're after. One of them is a new institute of taxi service intermediary, which sets out certain obligations for the performance of its activities, which aim in particular to ensure that the object of mediation is only legal services, i.e. services provided by authorised entities, carriers and drivers holding the necessary permits, the concession of the carrier and the driver's authorisation of taxi services. Furthermore, a new way of marking taxi services vehicles is introduced so that vehicles which are not equipped with a roof lamp are clearly identifiable for both customers and control authorities. This new identifier will be the registration sticker of the taxi service vehicle, which will be issued by the transport authorities and which will have to be marked by each vehicle operated by the taxi service. The proposal also limits the scope of the municipality's authorisation to regulate taxi services in its territory. The possibility for municipalities to provide taxi drivers with the obligation to demonstrate the test knowledge of the place of registration, the operator of the taxi meter and the relevant legislation is completely deleted, and the possibility for municipalities to determine the requirement for the color of the bodywork and the minimum dimensions of the taxi service vehicle is deleted. Outside the area of taxi services, in favour of the addressees of legal regulation, the compulsory labelling of large vehicles by commercial name of road transport operator is waived and there are partial changes in the regulation of obligations in the transport of dangerous goods. [[17.20 hours]] The Road Transport Act further includes a special regulation against the Employment Act, which follows EU regulations on posting workers in the framework of the provision of services, and it is proposed that copies of documents proving employment relationship with the driver be made available in the vehicle for foreign carriers. The incorporation of the obligation in question into the Road Transport Act will enable the relevant offence to be dealt with by the transport authorities, which will make the administrative penalty more effective, which is currently not implemented to a sufficient extent by labour inspectors. The changes contained in the proposal also affect the licensing procedure in which the procedures for issuing binding opinions by the authorities concerned are partially clarified so as to avoid excessive extension of the licensing procedure as a result of the inactivity of these authorities. Following knowledge from application practice, other sub-regulations in the Road Transport Act are also carried out. Thank you for your attention.
