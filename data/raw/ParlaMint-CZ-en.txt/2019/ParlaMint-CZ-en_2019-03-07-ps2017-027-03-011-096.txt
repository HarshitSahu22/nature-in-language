ParlaMint-CZ_2019-03-07-ps2017-027-03-011-096.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me present to you for discussion the Partnership and Cooperation Agreement between the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and the Republic of Singapore, on the other. This agreement is intended to create the prerequisites for strengthening relations between the EU and Singapore in the political area and for intensifying cooperation in a number of other areas. It is also intended to replace in relations between the EU and Singapore the existing contractual framework set out in the 1980 Cooperation Agreement concluded between the European Economic Community and the Association of South-East Asian Nations. Several similar partnership and cooperation agreements have already been concluded in the South East Asian region, and some others are being discussed. The Partnership and Cooperation Agreement can be welcomed from the point of view of the Czech Republic. Bilateral relations between the Czech Republic and Singapore have been very good for a long time, which is also seen from top-level contacts, see the Prime Minister's journey to Singapore in January 2019 or the visit of President Singapore to the Czech Republic in May 2017. Trade exchange with Singapore is comparable to larger countries in the region. According to preliminary data of the ČSÚ for 2018 the export of the Czech Republic to Singapore reached 7,5 billion. Crowns. Singapore plays the role of a regional trading and financial centre. This agreement is, from the point of view of the legal nature, a so-called mixed agreement, which means that some parts of it fall within the competence of the Member States and some parts fall within the competence of the EU. The Government of the Czech Republic agreed to negotiate this agreement on 13 June last year by Resolution No. 374. The agreement was subsequently signed at the edge of the ASEM Summit in Brussels on 19 October last year. [[12.50 hours]] The agreement contains all the usual political clauses on human rights, the International Criminal Court, weapons of mass destruction, small arms and light weapons, and the fight against terrorism. It covers cooperation in a number of areas such as the environment, climate issues, energy, health, education and culture, science, transport and so on. It also concerns legal cooperation, anti-money laundering measures and action against organised crime and corruption. It also contains provisions on cooperation in the field of taxation. The Agreement is in accordance with constitutional order, with obligations arising from EU membership, with commitments entered into under other applicable treaties and with the generally recognised principles of international law. There are no direct effects on the state budget from the agreement. That's all. Thank you for your attention.
