ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-28-ps2017-039-03-007-171.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, ladies and gentlemen, a double taxation treaty is now tabled at the meeting with the Republic of Korea, signed on 12 January 2018 in Seoul. The conclusion of this international tax contract follows the intention to limit the tax burden on business entities when excluding unintended international double taxation. The international double taxation treaty, which has been in force since 1995, is, however, a treaty which no longer meets the current conditions, is currently being applied in the mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Korea. For these reasons, the competent authorities of both countries have agreed to renegotiate it. A comprehensive bilateral agreement has been negotiated, which regulates taxation and, at the same time, the exclusion of international double taxation of all kinds of income and which will certainly help further development of mutual relations. The Treaty does not replace the national tax rules of the Contracting States and cannot therefore create a new tax obligation but only regulates the obligation arising from national rules. It contributes to the objective distribution of the right to tax on individual types of income between the two States, that is between the State of the source of income and the State of its beneficiary. The contract in question also provides for the basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of both countries, as well as the principle of non-discrimination, informal resolution of disputes arising from its implementation, and also for the relevant exchange of information between the competent authorities of the two States. The contract will increase legal certainty of potential investors of both countries and create suitable space for other Czech business entities. The conclusion of the contract will not have a direct impact on the state budget of the Czech Republic. The overall contribution resulting from its closure will depend on the pace of development of the various forms of economic cooperation. The Double Taxation Treaty, which is now being proposed in the relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Korea, was prepared on the basis of OECD and UN model models, and, of course, also responds to the internal tax rules of both countries. For information, the Senate of Parliament of the Czech Republic agreed to ratify this Treaty in October last year. honourable Members, on the basis of the above, I propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take into account these facts when discussing this international treaty and agree to its ratification. Thank you all for your attention.
