And then point number 2 in Section 125c para. Annex V.Part 2.116 (d) (d) the following sentence shall be added at the end of the text after the comma: Article 4 (1) (a) (e) point 4, which is committed by a natural person after the age of 68 years, shall be fined up to a thousand crowns. Article 2 The effectiveness is replaced by the following: This Act shall take effect on 1 March 2020. Now let me justify it. To point 4. The current legal situation does not allow the competent authority of the state administration to increase the speed of the tunnels on motorways. The maximum allowed speed in tunnels, which is 80 km per hour, is based on technical standard ČSN 737507 Design of road tunnels issued by the Office for Technical Standardization, Metrology and State Testing. However, the quoted standard is only a technical regulation and does not take into account other aspects of road transport. The main principle of the first part of the proposed adjustment is that the competent authority can increase the maximum speed in motorway tunnels up to 110 km per hour as part of local traffic adjustment. The possibility of assessing the situation and authorising to set maximum speed directly by law is more systematic than the limit based on the technical standard. When determining the maximum speed, in each individual case it will be based on the technical state of the tunnel, the assessment of the safety of operation, the density of operation as well as the installed safety elements in the tunnel. The increase in speed has support on the side of the Road and Motorway Directorate, which in the past has said that the technical state and security of the Czech tunnels allows the increase in speed, for example the tunnel Valík u Plzně on the motorway direction Rozvadov. The bill is based on the state of tunnels in the Czech Republic, which is one of the safest in the European Union, and with regard to their security and technical status, both the control and camera system, lighting, fire detection and traffic signs usually vary. Therefore, these tunnels are prepared for higher speed. The proposal is based on a similar adjustment in neighbouring countries, such as Austria and Germany, which are always given to us as an example where it is possible to drive in tunnels at a 100-mile speed. The provision allows the competent authority to increase the maximum speed in motorway tunnels up to 110 km per hour under local regulation. The new legislation is based on smooth traffic while maintaining its safety. High funds have been invested in building a network of motorway tunnels to place a strong emphasis on traffic safety and technical parameters. It is therefore unnecessary to restrict traffic in places where there is no reason to do so. Increasing the speed in tunnels with adequate safety elements will contribute to the smoothness of traffic and strengthen the business environment, as it will contribute to improving logistics. Further to point 5. The current legislation states that a fine ranging from CZK 5 to CZK 10 000 and a ban on proceedings for 6 to 12 months will be imposed on a person driving a motor vehicle who is not in possession of a valid medical assessment. Retirement persons must have a medical examination before 65th and 68th birthdays, at the latest on the given day, every two years thereafter. A doctor's certificate must always be carried with the seniors. In the case of senior drivers who only fail a medical opinion, the limits of the possible fine are too high and should be based on the fact that the pensioner is among the low-income population. For comparison purposes, this offence is based on the equivalent of driving under the influence of an addictive substance, i.e. alcohol or drugs, which certainly does not make a pensioner. The submitted legislation provides that persons over 68 years of age who fail a medical certificate should be fined only up to a thousand crowns. In practice, it is common for seniors to be unaware that they must undergo a medical check every two years if they are over 68 years of age. This is not a serious infringement of the traffic rules and therefore it is not necessary to require a high amount for their infringement as it is a formal deficiency. It may therefore be that a pensioner who has two days forfeited this inspection will pay such an outrageous amount and gets caught in a debt trap. It shall be authorised by the administrative authority to impose a ban on driving, i.e. the driver may, of course, be prohibited from driving. But the fine is for seniors to be completely liquidated. The adjustment allows the administrative authority, on the basis of an assessment of all the circumstances of the offence, to impose a fine of CZK 1000 less than that which may be imposed on other persons, on a driver older than 68 years of age and which does not have a valid medical assessment. The legislation in question allows for a reduction of the fine to be imposed on pensioners who forfeit their medical certificate. Nowadays, when there is an excess of information that is often different, there are common cases where obliged persons do not realize that every two years after 68 years of age, they have to undergo a medical examination for the purpose of issuing an aptitude assessment. High fines represent a significant impact on the pensions budget of these people, and for some of them they can be completely liquidated and lead them into a debt trap. The assessment of the amount of the fine shall not preclude the application of administrative discretion, so that it will always be individual, taking into account all the circumstances of the case and will depend on the decision of the competent authority, which may take into account, for example, the length of the lapse in the validity of the health assessment or the repetition of the same offence. Efficacy. Since the effectiveness mentioned in the draft law is already in the past, the unified effectiveness of all the amendments' provisions on 1 March 2020 is proposed.
