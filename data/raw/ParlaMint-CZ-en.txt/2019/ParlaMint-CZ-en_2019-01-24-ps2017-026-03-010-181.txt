Participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the territory of the Czech Republic in 2019 /German press 358/ This material is once again given by the Minister of Defence, Metnar, who now has the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-24-ps2017-026-03-010-181.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, this is the last report in this package that I am presenting to you. This House press contains a plan for military exercises of the Czech Army forces and crews with foreign participation in 2019. It includes activities with the armed forces of both NATO Member States and the European Union, as well as non-Member States and partner countries of NATO and the European Union. The plan is based on policy-military recommendations, on the priorities and objectives of foreign cooperation of the Ministry of Defence in 2019, on the tasks set out in the Ministry of Defence Ministry's activity plan and NATO exercise programme. The material contains an overview of planned military exercises with foreign partners in 2019, when 145 exercises of armed forces outside the Czech Republic and 62 exercises of armed forces of other states in the Czech Republic are planned. On the basis of political-military priorities, the training plan is effectively composed of four areas, including preparation for deployment into operations, emergency forces, bilateral and regional cooperation and capacity building. The priority goal of the exercise with foreign partners in 2019 will be to examine and increase the level of readiness of the armed forces of the Czech Republic to defend it in cooperation with allies. The exercise also serves as a preparation for the joint operation of troops in operations and emergency forces of international organizations. The material also includes five exercises of the armed forces of other states in the territory of the Czech Republic, which will be carried out without direct cooperation with our army. In order to ensure the activities of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in the framework of the planned international exercises in 2019, the amount of these exercises worth CZK 137.7 million is earmarked in the budget of the Ministry of Defence chapter. This material has also been discussed in the Defence Committee and thank you for your support.
