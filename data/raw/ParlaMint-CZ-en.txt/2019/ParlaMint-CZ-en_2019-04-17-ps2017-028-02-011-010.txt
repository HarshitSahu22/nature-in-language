Let me briefly remind the Government's bill, which amends some of the laws in the internal administration section. I presented this in detail during the first reading which took place in January this year at the 26th meeting of the House. The main reason for submitting this proposal is the need to adapt certain regulations within the competence of the Ministry of the Interior to the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on the promotion of the free movement of citizens by simplifying the requirements for the submission of certain authentic instruments in the European Union. This directly applicable European Union Regulation allows for the replacement of a requirement for the official translation of certain documents, such as matriculation documents, by a multilingual standard form issued by the competent authority together with its own document. Following this, the Czech law requires the proper powers of the authorities to issue these forms. This change brings simplification of administration to Czech citizens if they submit documents in a foreign language to the Czech authorities, as well as when they submit Czech documents abroad. Furthermore, the proposal includes changes responding to practical knowledge, in particular in relation to the verification of copies and signatures, in relation to the management of basic registers or changes concerning the possibility of establishing and managing data boxes exclusively electronically, for example by using an electronic ID card, i.e. without the need for a visit by the competent authority. The draft law was discussed by the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development as a guarantee which applied the amendments in its resolution. These are of a legislative technical nature, and it is proposed, inter alia, to postpone the date of entry into force of the law following the current course of the legislative process. I agree with these proposals for the Ministry of the Interior and thank you for your attention.
