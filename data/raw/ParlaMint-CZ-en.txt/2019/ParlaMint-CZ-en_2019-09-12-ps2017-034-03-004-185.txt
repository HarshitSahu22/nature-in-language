Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia on Air Transport Services /Departmental Press 197/ - Second Reading The government is once again entrusted to Minister Brabec to introduce us to this government proposal. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-09-12-ps2017-034-03-004-185.u2	Thank you again for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, the Air Agreement with Saudi Arabia was signed on 7 December 2016 and was subsequently submitted for the first time to the Parliament of the Czech Republic for its ratification. In July 2017, further ratification of the Agreement was not supported in the Senate plenary, but it was not a disagreement with the substance of the Agreement. [[11.50 hours]] The Ministry of Transport decided to re-submit this material to the Parliament of the Czech Republic, especially in order to ensure legal certainty for the current and future operation of air carriers between the Czech Republic and Saudi Arabia. Czech Airlines operates a regular seasonal line between Prague and Riyadh for the third year and before this summer season they experienced problems in approving their flights to Saudi Arabia. This newly requires an approved air agreement for the regular operation of the Czech Airlines in the summer period 2019. The agreement regulates purely operational economic aspects of air transport and without it there are unexpected problems in operation with negative economic impacts, in particular on air carriers and passengers. In the framework of the new ratification process, the Senate already agreed to ratify this Agreement on 15 August 2018 and the Foreign Committee recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to grant its ratification approval at its meeting on 21 February 2019. I would therefore like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to give my consent to the ratification of the agreement. Thank you.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-09-12-ps2017-034-03-004-185.u3	Thank you, Minister. In this case too, Mr Navrkal took up the role of rapporteur of the foreign committee. Please state the reasons for the committee's resolution. Please, you have the floor.

I would add that the Senate has already agreed to the ratification at its 17th meeting on 15 August 2018. Thank you for your attention.
