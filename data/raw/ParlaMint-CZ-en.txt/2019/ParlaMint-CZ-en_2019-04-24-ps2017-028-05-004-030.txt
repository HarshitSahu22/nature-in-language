ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-24-ps2017-028-05-004-030.u2	Thank you, Mr Vice-President, for the floor. Hello, ladies and gentlemen. I propose to you to discuss a draft amendment to the law amending certain laws on business regulation in the financial market. The reason for submitting this amendment must be adapted to the European Union Regulation on the prospectus, which will take effect on 21 July 2019. In this context, in particular in the Capital Market Business Act, the existing transposition of the prospectus directive, which is replaced by this Regulation, is abolished and further necessary amendments are made. Furthermore, the adaptation of the European Union regulation on money market funds, on transactions providing financing in the Investment Companies and Investment Funds Act is being completed. [[13.00 hours]] This amendment also changes the distribution of supplementary pension savings in such a way that it is no longer linked to the distribution of investment instruments under the Capital Market Business Act. This will allow a separate distribution of supplementary pension savings without the current need to be authorised by an investment intermediary. It is therefore necessary to adjust both the Capital Market Business Act and the Supplementary Pension Savings Act at the same time, and these adjustments must take effect at the same time until the end of 2019 when the transitional period for professional examinations under the amendment to the Capital Market Business Act, which took effect on 3 January 2018. This amendment also addresses the long-disputed issue of insolvency of entities without legal personality, namely sub-funds of investment funds and mutual funds, so that their bankruptcy does not threaten their managers or other funds managed by them. It is also proposed to make a number of other minor changes to the Bonds Act, the Investment Companies Act and the Investment Funds Act. For example, the adjustment of the investment fund promoter is generalised, ex ante supervision by the depositary for qualifying investor funds is cancelled and tolerance of the most significant valuation errors is adjusted. As part of the preparation of the draft law, fundamental questions were consulted with a wide range of stakeholders, in particular the Association for the Capital Market, the Ministry of Justice and the Czech National Bank. Thank you for your attention.
