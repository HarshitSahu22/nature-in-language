Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Kingdom of Cambodia on Air Services /German press 252/ - first reading The proposed proposal will be presented by the Minister of Transport Dan Ťok. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-017-134.u2	Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, the relations between the Czech Republic and Cambodia in the field of civil aviation are currently regulated by the 1964 Air Agreement. Given its age, this agreement does not correspond to the current situation in the field of air transport and in addition some of its provisions do not comply with Union law. In view of these reasons, an entirely new aviation agreement has been negotiated aimed at establishing a liberal framework for air carriers of both parties, corresponding to current trends in civil aviation for the possible future implementation of air transport between the two States and for the cooperation of air carriers. The new agreement was approved by the Government of the Czech Republic in January 2015 and its signature took place on 5 June 2018 in Phnom Penh. Although Cambodia is not from an economic point of view among important trading partners of the Czech Republic, which corresponds to the size of the transport market, the negotiation of a new contractual framework can generally be beneficial in terms of further development of mutual relations, especially in connection with the gradual growth of the Cambodian economy, including the development of tourism. The agreement does not require adjustments to the Czech legal order and its implementation will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic for the referral of material for discussion in the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you for your attention.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-017-134.u3	Thank you, Minister. The rapporteur for first reading is Mrs Karla Šlechtová. Please, rapporteur, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-017-134.u4	Mr President, colleagues, this agreement has the character of an international presidential treaty in the Czech Republic, as it regulates matters under Article 49 (a) and (e) of our Constitution. The agreement contains, among other things, what the Minister of Transport has mentioned here, also the regulation of the rights and obligations of persons as well as of matters whose regulation is reserved for the law. This agreement is submitted before ratification by the President of the Czech Parliament, both to the House and to the Senate of the Czech Parliament, to express its consent to ratification. It will not require increased requirements for the state budget and will comply with both national law and international law. The Organizing Committee has proposed to order a contract for discussion to the Foreign Committee. Thank you.
