Mr President, Mr Government, Members, as a representative of the petitioner, I will allow you to briefly sum up the content of the material as part of the second reading. In 2016, the Ministry of the Interior prepared an amendment to the law on citizenship, which allows Čech who lost Czechoslovak citizenship before 1989 because they went into exile so that they could regain this citizenship of the Czech Republic with a declaration. In Article 31, there was an opportunity for children and grandchildren to gain citizenship by this declaration. The then Chamber of Deputies deleted this part of this law, which was negatively reflected in relations between Czech society and Čech living abroad. Several petitions from abroad and complaints came to the commission, which I am the chairman of at the moment, when it often happened that one family had children with Czech citizenship and children without Czech citizenship, because this law made a certain time dividing line there. The material presented thus corrects this error. I am pleased that the members of both committees, i.e. constitutionally and security, supported the amendment without reservation. Thank you again, and I would like to ask the rest of you for your support. Thank you.
