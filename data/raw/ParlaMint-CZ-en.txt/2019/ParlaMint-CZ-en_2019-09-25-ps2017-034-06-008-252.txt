Proposal for appointment of members of the Supervisory Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic The House was successful at the appointment of a member of the Supervisory Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic. 180 ballots were issued, 180 also submitted, so none were surrendered. Kvorum is 91. This quorum applies to all four electoral points, with which I will introduce you to the results. At the Audit Board of the Grant Agency, Stanislav Labík received 102 votes and Mr Václav Rejholec received 30 votes. Stanislav Labík was therefore elected a member of the Audit Board of the Grant Agency and that is the end of this election.
