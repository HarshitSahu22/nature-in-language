Before I make these changes, I still have to say that this government proposal proposes a fundamental change in the evaluation of domestic research that has already been carried out in the years past, in the last two years, is being implemented at the moment, and this change in principle means a shift from so-called quantity to quality. In other words, domestic research and scientific output are not only assessed on the basis of quantitative data, i.e. bibliometric data, but is assessed on the basis of a request from the professional public, based on world trends, on qualitative indicators associated with the quality of the entire research organisation. [[Noise in the hall.]] For this reason, a new principle of evaluation was introduced, a new methodology of evaluation. The methodology is called Methodology 2017+. In view of the gradual introduction of this methodology both at the level of the Academy of Sciences and at the level of research organisations and at the level of universities, it was necessary to amend the law precisely with regard to universities so that the evaluation corresponds to the possibilities of current universities. In other words, the proposed legislation consists in changing the way in which research, development and innovation are assessed, in the sense that the emphasis is placed on the evaluation of the entire research organisation as a whole while maintaining a bibliometric analysis of individual results. The aim is therefore to establish basic evaluation procedures for research organisations. The details are then given in the methodology.
