Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly sum up the proposed amendment to Act No. 187/2006 Coll., on sickness insurance, which, among other things, regulates the payment of cash assistance in maternity and compensation in pregnancy and maternity. These doses are calculated according to current rules from the so-called reduced assessment base. The reduction is currently taking place by calculating the amount to the first reduction limit in 100 per cent, only 60 per cent is calculated from the amount above the first to the second reduction limit and 30 per cent is calculated from the amount above the second reduction limit to the third reduction limit. The third reduction limit will no longer be taken into account. There is some disadvantage to those who have a slightly higher income, so that they are medium or higher income. The proposed adjustment alleviates this disadvantage by effectively abolishing the reduction of the assessment base, by uniting the percentage adjustment for all reduction boundaries for the calculation of the adjusted assessment base, for the calculation of the cash-based maternity assistance and compensation allowance in pregnancy, to 100 per cent. So much for the not-so-simple mattress. However, I would just like to say that, in the course of the debate in the Chamber of Deputies, of course, I have noted comments which were about certain shortcomings and, above all, have been brought to the attention of the absence of transitional provisions. It is also the fact that, in the proposal, we have neglected the creation of a social benefit, which was thus enacted in the previous parliamentary term, the so-called paternal one. We therefore want to remedy both by the amendments that I am proposing here today. One supplement is a transitional provision that would only apply to new cases or new recipients of maternity assistance if my proposal was adopted.
