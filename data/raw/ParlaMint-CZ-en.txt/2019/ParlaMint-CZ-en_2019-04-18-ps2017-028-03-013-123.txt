ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-18-ps2017-028-03-013-123.u2	Thank you. Ladies and gentlemen, the material presented was prepared on the basis of Article 19 of the Constitution of the International Labour Organisation. It provides for the Government to submit to Parliament every new convention and recommendation of this international organisation, including its opinion on them. The purpose of this commitment is, above all, to inform the legislator about the current development of international law in the field of labour and social affairs. Two documents were submitted to both the Senate and the Chamber of Deputies in 2016 concerning two different conventions governing the work of seafarers. The first document amends the 2006 Maritime Labour Convention. Parliament agreed to ratify this Convention in 2017. The submitted amendments shall contain procedural rules concerning the extension of the relevant maritime certificate issued under the Convention for individual ships. In fact, it is about aligning this procedure with the modification applied to other certificates that are in the sense of the International Maritime Organisation. Since the existing national regulation in the Maritime Navigation Act fully complies with these rules, it is proposed to ratify these amendments in order to maintain the commitments of the Czech Republic in this area in line with international developments. The second document deals with the technical specifications for the issue of Navy books. The relevant Convention of the International Labour Organisation was not ratified by the Czech Republic and dealt with in a different way. Therefore, the government only took note of this second document without the need for any changes. Thank you.
