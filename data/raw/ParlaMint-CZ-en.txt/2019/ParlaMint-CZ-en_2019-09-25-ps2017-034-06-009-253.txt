Proposal for the appointment of a member of the Supervisory Board of the Czech Technical Agency The House was also successful at the Monitoring Council of the Technology Agency. Here 180 ballots were issued, 179 ballots were submitted, one was not surrendered. Robert Čep won 93 votes and Vlasta Radová won 50. Robert Čep was elected a member of the Monitoring Board of the Technological Agency and that is the end of this election.
