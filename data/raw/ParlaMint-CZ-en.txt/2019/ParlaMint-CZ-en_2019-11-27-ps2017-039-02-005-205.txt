ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-27-ps2017-039-02-005-205.u2	Thank you. Ladies and gentlemen, I would just like to remind you of the draft law on the territorial administrative breakdown of the state. He was put here to somehow update the structure and replace the obsolete law, which is from 1960. So the 1960 bill still mentions the seven old counties. So, formally, we have laws that, as you know, still mention the old regions, even though they are no longer functional, and they are, of course, different from those thirteen self-governing regions today. So the old regulation is no longer being used at the moment, and it is, of course, good to deal with it and to remove certain adjustments. According to the new legislation, this means, of course, other changes, not only that the seven old regions are being abolished, but the administrative districts will now be defined by a list of municipalities and military retreats, namely the delimitation of districts is done by deriving from the administrative districts of municipalities with extended scope. For the capital city of Prague, a special division is maintained on ten districts at district level. The proposed solution, as far as I am aware, has been consulted with the municipalities concerned as well as with the competent authorities of the state administration. The important thing here is to say that the new legislation does not concern the courts, it does not concern the prosecutors, they have their districts defined by a special law. And I'll just add one piece of information on how to deal with the specific situation in the Turnov region. The Ministry of the Interior has dealt fairly intensively with all the entities concerned and agreement has been reached on the amendment of the text of the draft law. This regulation should allow the current situation in Turnov region to be maintained in the form of a functioning administrative district of the municipality with extended scope and at the same time not to change the jurisdiction of individual municipalities into districts. The amendment to the amendments that I am describing here has been adopted in its resolution by the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development, and I can say to the appellant that he does not have a problem with this and that he agrees to it. That's all there is to say.
