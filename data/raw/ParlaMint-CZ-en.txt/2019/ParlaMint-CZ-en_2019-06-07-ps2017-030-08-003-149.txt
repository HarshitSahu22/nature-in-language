ParlaMint-CZ_2019-06-07-ps2017-030-08-003-149.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, let me briefly mention in the third reading the Government's proposal for a law amending the law on intelligence services, the Military Intelligence Act and the Security Information Service Act. The proposed proposal responds to the development of public administration, the development of eGovernment and society in general in relation to the needs of effective performance of intelligence tasks. The essence of this is the modification of existing rights of intelligence services in the context of changing conditions so that intelligence services can effectively carry out their activities necessary to ensure the security of the Czech Republic in the following years. The draft law contains a specific procedure for obtaining information from the Directorate-General for Customs and extending the possibility to request information from banks and the possibility to request such information through another competent authority. Following the development of eGovernment and public administration information systems, the proposal further regulates the conditions for the application of registration data protection in other public administration information systems. The development of eGovernment and information society services in general are then linked to the proposed modifications to the cover and cover documents. For the use of means and documents, it is necessary to ensure that reliable records are also available on the cover or cover document, both in the information systems of the public administration themselves and in the information systems accompanying them. The proposal introduces the possibility of reporting services to create a regularly updated digital image reference database. In the field of intelligence, the deadline for the use of intelligence technology by the Security Information Service and Military Intelligence is unified with a deadline for the duration of wiretapping and recording of telecommunications traffic under the Criminal Code. In conclusion, I would like to mention how the discussion of the draft law in the committees to which the new draft law was directed for discussion has turned out. Both the Security Committee and the Constitutional Committee recommended that the Government's proposal be approved, stating that the Constitutional Committee recommended that the draft law be approved, as amended by its proposed amendments. The first of these amendments means limiting the originally proposed reformation of § 11 para. This Decision shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. All other amendments, adopted by the Constitutional Committee and recommended to approve them, are directly linked and are linked to the unification of the rules on cover documents and covering instruments. I agree with all these amendments made in the resolution of the Constitutional Legal Committee for the Government as a petitioner. On 17 April this year, the draft law passed both a general and a detailed debate in the second reading, and no further amendments were tabled for this. The Guarantee Committee, which is the Security Committee, then discussed all the amendments mentioned at the second reading at its meeting on 2 May 2019 and recommended that they be adopted. Thank you for your attention.
