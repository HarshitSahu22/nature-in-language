ParlaMint-CZ_2019-09-25-ps2017-034-06-019-180.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. It's another digi-law by the third time, so hopefully we can rest for a while. This proposed legislation creates the conditions for the creation of a so-called banking identity. This represents a simple and free form of access to eGovernment services, but also to online private sector services. The creation of a banking identity would mean that 5.5 million Internet banking users in our country will be able, in addition to performing normal banking operations, to gain free access to electronically provided services from the state and local authorities. The establishment of bank identity for bank clients will also not be charged. As a result, the online services of the state, but also of the private sector, are expected to be much more attractive to users. However, the SONIA project will not only bring benefits to citizens; this will allow limited access of banks to the basic registers as well. This approach will enable banks to better prevent the legalisation of proceeds from crime and terrorist financing, as well as preventing the misuse of identity. All these benefits of the SONIA project, as it is called, make it a great example of how cooperation between the private and public sectors can work together. In order to implement it, I am asking you to support this amendment of the law. Thank you.
