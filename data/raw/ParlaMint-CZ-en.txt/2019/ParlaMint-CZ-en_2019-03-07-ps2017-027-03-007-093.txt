ParlaMint-CZ_2019-03-07-ps2017-027-03-007-093.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Let me introduce you to the proposal for ratification of the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Latvia on the amendment of the Agreement between the two Governments on the promotion and mutual protection of investments signed in Riga on 25 October 1994 and the expiry of the Agreement which was negotiated in the form of an exchange of notes. This is presented in accordance with Government Order No 853 of 9 July 2008 and No 1529 of 1 December 2008. With these resolutions, the Government of the Czech Republic approved the method and procedure for the termination of certain bilateral investment agreements concluded by the Czech Republic with EU Member States. The list of termination agreements is set out in the Annex to resolution 853 of 9 July 2008. The investment agreement with Latvia is then set out in point 10 of this Annex. The resolution was adopted in view of the fact that, following the accession of the Czech Republic to the European Union, agreements on the protection and promotion of investments concluded between EU Member States became incompatible with EU law, which was also confirmed by the recent decision of the Court of Justice of the European Union in Case C284/14 Achmea. In accordance with the Government's approved procedure, the Czech Republic submitted in two waves a proposal to terminate bilateral investment agreements concluded between the Member States of the European Union. The first wave of termination notes was distributed in the first half of 2009, the second in the first half of 2016. Latvia responded positively to the second of these notes and agreed to terminate the existing investment agreement in the note of 28 December 2017, including the exclusion of the application of the so-called withdrawal periods. It follows from the agreement of the Contracting Party that investments existing on the date of expiry of the Czech-Latvia Investment Agreement will no longer be subject to protection under this Agreement for a further period of 10 years. The notes thus exchanged together form an agreement on the amendment and termination, which is now submitted to the Parliament of the Czech Republic to express its consent to ratification. The Agreement shall enter into force on the first day of the month following receipt of a later note confirming compliance with the requirements of national law necessary for the entry into force of this Agreement. The Ministry of Finance has been informed that all national requirements necessary for the entry into force of the Agreement have already been met on the Latvian side. In view of this, it is recommended that the ratification process be terminated as soon as possible in order to exclude the possibility of possible initiation of arbitration proceedings against the Czech Republic under an investment agreement still in force. The termination agreement is in line with the constitutional order, other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, with the obligations arising from the membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union, with the obligations assumed under other applicable treaties and with the generally accepted principles of international law. Thank you for your attention.
