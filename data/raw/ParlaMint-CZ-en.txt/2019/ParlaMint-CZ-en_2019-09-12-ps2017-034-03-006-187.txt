ParlaMint-CZ_2019-09-12-ps2017-034-03-006-187.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, as has already been mentioned, at second reading, the proposal to ratify the amendment to Article 124 of the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court. I would like to remind myself very briefly that the purpose of this transitional provision was to allow future parties to postpone the jurisdiction of the International Criminal Court for a period of seven years from the entry into force of the Rome Statute for the Contracting Party concerned against war crimes in cases where the crime was allegedly committed by the citizens of the future Contracting Party or on its territory. In the background of the adoption of this transitional provision, efforts were made to quickly reach as many Contracting Parties as possible. In our opinion, Article 124 is no longer being used. 14. the Assembly of the Parties to the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court in 2015 adopted a resolution which decided to delete Article 124. The reasons rested in his lack of update and in the pursuit of the purest possible text of this Statute. The purpose of the national discussion leading to the deletion of Article 124 is therefore to complete the process of expressing the Czech Republic's consent to the deletion of Article 124, which is no longer being used. I therefore believe that, like the Senate, the Chamber of Deputies will agree to ratify this change. Thank you for your attention.
