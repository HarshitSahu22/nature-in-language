ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-24-ps2017-026-03-005-176.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Let me introduce you to this House press number 187. The main content of the material is information on the political, military and financial aspects of the activities of Czech troops and soldiers in 2017 in Afghanistan, Kosovo, Bosnia and Herzegovina, the Sinai Peninsula, the Golan Heights, the United Kingdom, the Malian Republic, Iraq and Italy. The material also includes information on the implementation of the rapid impact projects implemented in 2017, but only in Afghanistan, and information on the state of the Czech Republic's involvement in UN missions in 2017 and on the opportunities and opportunities for the Czech Republic to operate in these missions on the basis of the Security Council resolution of 9 June 2015. As in previous years, last year also remained a priority mission for the Czech Republic called Resolute Support in Afghanistan. Thank you for your support.
