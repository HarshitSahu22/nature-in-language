ParlaMint-CZ_2019-10-16-ps2017-035-02-019-012.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Allow me, colleagues, to briefly remind you of the content of the draft amendment to the Act amending the Act on the State Housing Development Fund. In particular, the aim of the draft law is to extend the scope of the existing State Housing Development Fund to other activities related to regional development support, which are within the competence of the Ministry of Regional Development. It is therefore in no way a proposal to abolish this fund or to change its focus, but rather to extend its existing activities. By no means will housing be deprived, on the contrary, for the next period we expect annual subsidies from the state budget and increased activities in particular, e.g. about cooperative housing and other activities related to housing, of course. We will gradually transfer selected support to this state fund and to the Ministry of Regional Development, which is aimed at municipalities and regions, so that we can have all the subsidy titles and instruments, as they say, under one roof. Therefore, we are also proposing to change the name of the State Housing Development Fund to the State Investment Support Fund. The name has been the subject of deep debates and best reflects our intention more comprehensively to support the development of cities and municipalities in various aspects of the quality of life of the local population. We will continue to support investment not only in housing, but also in sustainable development of municipalities, cities and regions and in territorial planning, but also for example. tourism. However, the intentions must be consistent with strategic documents and concepts approved by the government of the Czech Republic, e.g. the concept of housing policy or regional development strategy and, of course, in line with the public interest. However, the Fund's budget will remain approved by the Chamber of Deputies, so you will see for yourself that we will not reduce housing expenditure from this fund. In the draft law, the basic legal framework of the Fund's method and procedure for granting aid, which has been laid down so far in the various implementing rules, has not always been uniform. The details will then continue to be specified in the implementing rules. From a factual point of view, I would also like to underline the proposed extension of the possibility of granting subsidies to cooperative shares. At present, the State Fund does not grant loans to young people for cooperative housing because the law does not allow subsidies for cooperative shares, i.e. even for deduction of a child born, which is CZK 30,000. Since we intend to support cooperatives more significantly, this legislation is essential. In addition, it is proposed to supplement the provisions on the use of information from public administration information systems needed by the Fund for its proper operation. In the context of the State Investment Support Fund's control activities, the application of the Financial Control Act to the Fund's procedure should be explicitly anchored in order to check the accuracy of the data contained in the requests and to comply with the conditions for the provision and drawing of funds. In conclusion, I would like to reiterate once again that, by renaming the State Housing Development Fund, housing support does not disappear, only the scope for implementing other housing support measures directly or indirectly related. Thank you for your attention.
