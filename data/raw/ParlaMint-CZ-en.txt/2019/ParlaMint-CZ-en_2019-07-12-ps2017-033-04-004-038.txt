ParlaMint-CZ_2019-07-12-ps2017-033-04-004-038.u2	Hello, Mr President, honourable Members. Allow me to present the Government's draft amendment to Act No. 503/2012 Coll., on the State Land Office and on the amendment of certain related laws. The proposed amendment aims to make the activities of the State Land Office more effective and solves some of the current problems arising from its practice. The Government's draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 23rd meeting on 15 May 2019 and recommended by its resolution The Chamber of Deputies shall approve the Government's bill as amended by the amendments adopted. I must say that the amendments adopted are in line with the concept of the petitioner. They will contribute to improving the government bill, and I therefore agree with them. Mr Jan Pošvář's amendment concerning the increase of the minimum reserve of state land from 50 to 100,000 hectares was applied in the second reading of the Government Bill, which took place on 6 June 2019. In general, the reasoning of the proposed amendment can be accepted, however, the proposal to increase the minimum margin of state-owned enterprises cannot be accepted for objective reasons, since the amount of available land, i.e. those which are not affected by any claims - i.e. restitution applications, actions, claim transfers - or not included in a government-approved development programme reserve - are below the proposed amount. So we have to disagree with him for objective reasons. The Agricultural Committee, as a guarantee committee, adopted its resolution of the 24th meeting of 12 June 2019 in agreement on the amendments applied after the first reading and the opposition to the amendment applied in the second reading of the Government's draft law. Thank you.
