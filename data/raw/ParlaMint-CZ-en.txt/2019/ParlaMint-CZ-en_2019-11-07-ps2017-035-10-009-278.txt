ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-07-ps2017-035-10-009-278.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Government, by its resolution, has authorised the crossings and transits of the armed forces of the European Union States. Furthermore, it has authorised flights and transits to the armed forces of the Member States of the North Atlantic Alliance and States participating in the Partnership for Peace programme, not least the countries with which the Czech Republic maintains a partnership. Allowing the crossing or transit will also be possible for the armed forces of other states, but only provided consent from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The Czech Republic will be seen as a reliable and trustworthy partner in this area with our allies by the approved procedure.
