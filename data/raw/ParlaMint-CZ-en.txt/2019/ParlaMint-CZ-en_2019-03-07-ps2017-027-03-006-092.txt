ParlaMint-CZ_2019-03-07-ps2017-027-03-006-092.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, I propose to give my consent to the ratification of the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Poland on the amendment and termination of the Agreement between these countries on the promotion and mutual protection of investments signed on 16 July 1993 in Budapest and negotiated in the form of an exchange of notes. It is presented in accordance with Government Resolutions No 853 of 9 July 2008 and No 1529 of 1 December 2008. With these resolutions, the Government of the Czech Republic approved the method and procedure for the termination of certain bilateral investment agreements concluded by the Czech Republic with EU Member States. The list is then set out in the annex to Government Order 853, and the investment agreement with Poland is set out in point 14 of that Annex. The resolution was adopted in view of the fact that, following the accession of the Czech Republic to the European Union, agreements on the protection and promotion of investments concluded between EU Member States have become incompatible, which has also been confirmed by recent decisions of the EU Court of Justice in Case Achmea C-284/14. In accordance with the Government's approved procedure, the Czech Republic submitted in two waves a proposal to terminate bilateral investment agreements concluded between the Member States of the European Union by agreement. The first wave of termination notes was distributed in the first half of 2009, the second in the first half of 2016. The Republic of Poland responded only to the other of these notes and on 10 January 2018 sent a note which it also proposed to terminate the existing investment agreement by means of an agreement by exchanging notes. The Czech party adopted the proposal by a note from 11 April 2018 with the expiry of the existing investment agreement, including the exclusion of the application of the so-called withdrawal period. It follows from the agreement of the Contracting Parties that investments existing on the date of expiry of the existing Czech-Polish Investment Agreement will no longer be subject to protection under this Investment Agreement for a further period of 10 years. The notes thus exchanged together form an agreement on the amendment and termination, which is now submitted to the Parliament of the Czech Republic to express its consent to ratification. The Agreement shall enter into force three months after the date of receipt of the later note confirming compliance with the requirements of national law necessary for the entry into force of this Agreement. As of this date, the existing agreement between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Poland on the promotion and mutual protection of investments signed in Budapest on 16 July 1993 will expire. The agreement is in accordance with constitutional order, other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, with obligations arising from the Czech Republic in the European Union, with commitments entered into under other applicable treaties and with generally recognised principles of international law. Thank you for your attention.
