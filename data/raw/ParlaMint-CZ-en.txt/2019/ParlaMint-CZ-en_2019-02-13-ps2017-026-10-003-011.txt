And you introduced me as a translator, which is true. However, I am now using my right as a Member and I want to comment on my amendment. I had already announced some time ago that I would come up with proposals for changes that would lead to a faster termination of a foreigner's stay in our territory if the foreigner was a security threat or his stay in the territory had to be terminated for other reasons. In the first place, I focus on foreigners who commit repeated crime in the Czech Republic. In my opinion, a repeated conviction for a deliberate offence constitutes a perfectly legitimate reason for the abolition of a foreigner's residence. In the case of this relapsed person, it is clear that it is not aimed at redress and its stay in our country threatens the security of the state and its inhabitants. [[10.50 hours]] The actual cancellation of the residence permit lasts more than two years today with follow-up remedies, even if these foreigners pose a security threat to the Czech Republic. My aim is not to deprive these foreigners of procedural rights that guarantee them our constitutional order, namely the right to turn to an independent court, but my aim is to set the whole process so that the time is reduced to less than six months. In these cases, therefore, we must act quickly and uncompromisingly. Therefore, I propose that in cases where a foreigner's stay is interrupted because of the commission of intentional crime in the Czech Republic, or if a foreigner poses a threat to public order, the matter should not be discussed in appeal proceedings and a foreigner might challenge the decision of the Ministry of the Interior by an administrative action, such as in asylum matters. The administrative courts themselves would then be given time limits within which they must decide on the action. This would significantly reduce the duration of the proceedings. I think it is entirely legitimate for such cases to be dealt with as a priority by the courts. My amendment also aims to accelerate and streamline the procedure for the administrative expulsion of a foreigner. According to my proposal, the risk of serious injury associated with the return of a foreigner to his country of origin will be assessed only in the case of those countries which the Czech Republic does not consider to be a safe country of origin. Of course, the list of these countries is set out in a special decree. Thank you.
