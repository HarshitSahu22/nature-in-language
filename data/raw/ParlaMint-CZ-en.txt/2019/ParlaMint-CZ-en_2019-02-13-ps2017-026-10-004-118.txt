ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-004-118.u2	Thank you. Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, the relations between the Czech Republic and Mongolia in the field of air transport are not yet regulated by any international agreement. Specific steps to prepare the agreement began in 2013, the expert meeting then took place in 2015. The agreement was approved by the Government of the Czech Republic in April 2016, which was subsequently signed on 25 May 2017 in Ulanbatar. The agreement is of a liberal nature and brings a modern contractual framework creating suitable conditions for possible future implementation of air traffic between the two States and for cooperation between air carriers. Although regular or irregular air transport between the Czech Republic and Mongolia is currently not provided by any air carrier, the said territory can be considered interesting in terms of Czech civil aviation for the future. The agreement does not require adjustments to the Czech legal order and its implementation will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic for the referral of material for discussion in the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
