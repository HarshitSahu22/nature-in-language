During this period, troops of the armed forces of the Czech Republic participated 52 military exercises with foreign partners outside the Czech Republic and 23 exercises with foreign partners in the Czech Republic. The total financial amount needed to carry out these exercises was 53,8 million. Crowns. The main tasks of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in training with foreign partners in the first half of this year were: preparation of units for deployment in operations and into emergency forces, development of interoperability within bilateral regional cooperation, construction and development of capabilities to meet NATO and EU collective and defence commitments.
