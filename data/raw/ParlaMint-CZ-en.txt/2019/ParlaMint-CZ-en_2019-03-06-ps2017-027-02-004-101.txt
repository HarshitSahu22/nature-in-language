ParlaMint-CZ_2019-03-06-ps2017-027-02-004-101.u2	Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, honourable Members, the Ministry of Transport is submitting a draft law amending Act No. 114/1995 Coll., on Inland Navigation, as amended, and Act No. 634/2004 Coll., on Administrative Fees, as amended. The main objective of the draft law is to ensure the transposition into the national administrative order of Directive 2009/100/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 2016/1629 of 14 September 2016 laying down technical requirements for inland waterway vessels, amending Directive 2009/100/EC and repealing Directive 2006/87/EC. The complete transposition of this Directive will be achieved through the adoption of implementing legislation, which is also remembered by the empowering provisions contained in the draft law. [[11.10 hours]] In particular, in accordance with the requirements of the Directive, the basic conditions for the operation of vessels on inland waterways, the procedure for approving the technical competence of vessels, the issue of certificates of vessels, the conditions for shortening and extending the period of validity of certificates of vessels and the establishment of a new registration of certificates of vessels demonstrating compliance with harmonised technical requirements, including ensuring the transmission of the selected data to the European database of hulls and their deletion. The draft law also brings further changes beyond the framework of that directive. The most significant of these changes is the introduction of an obligation on operators of certain vessels operating on specified transport-related waterways to equip the vessel with a vessel tracking and tracing equipment and navigation chart display equipment and information on the position and movement of vessels. The legal anchoring of this obligation represents another step in the gradual implementation of harmonised river information services of RIS in the Czech Republic. A number of amendments have been applied to the bill. It can be summarised that The Ministry of Transport agrees with all the amendments recommended in the resolution of the Economic Committee. These amendments include the modification of the drawing of digitised photographs and signatures from existing information systems in the issuing of certificates; the modification of the sacrifice of a case in a common danger; the extension of the scope of the Ministry of Transport and the State Navigation Administration on navigation safety prevention activities; updating the Union legislation reference; Legislative technical adaptations, including a change in the date of entry into force - all referred to as Amendment A - and an adjustment to the removal of ferry ships from the obligation to equip them with a vessel tracking and movement device with an electronic chart display device and information on the position and movement of vessels - that is Amendment C, Mr Bauer's amendment. On the other amendments, which is specifically Mrs Matušovská's amendment under letter B, namely to delete the amendment clause governing the amendment of the conditions for implementing technical inspections of vessels laid down in the decision authorising a legal person, and to Mr Kolovratník's amendment, which is Amendment D, which proposes to supplement the condition for the decision authorising a legal person to carry out technical inspections of vessels consisting in having his own regulation specifying the technical requirements for the construction and eligibility of the rules, the Ministry of Transport takes a negative view. The Committee on Economic Affairs also adopted a non-advising opinion on these amendments. Thank you for your attention.
