Mr President, honourable Members, the draft budget of the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure for 2020 and the medium-term outlook for 2021 and 2022 is presented in the submitted material. The budget and medium-term outlook of the SFDI between 2020 and 2022 is drawn up in accordance with the Act on the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure in its current version, broken down by area of financing and based on financial frameworks established by the Government of the Czech Republic. The budget is prepared as a Bilanc with the involvement of claims. Currently, the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure is not the beneficiary of any loan, but it plays the role of the financial manager of the loan to finance transport infrastructure from the European Investment Bank. The budget and medium-term outlook of the State Fund were approved by the Committee of the State Fund, discussed by the Supervisory Board, agreed by the Government of the Czech Republic on 14 October 2019 and submitted to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic for approval in accordance with Resolution No 731. In view of the legislatively demanding, lengthy and complexly predictable process of preparing transport buildings, the budget for 2020 is designed as well as for 2019, so as to allow flexible financing of investment actions to the maximum extent possible following the successful completion of their preparation, competition and conclusion of the contract for their implementation. For this reason, a common investment global item has been established in the budget for state investors, the Road and Motorway Directorate and the Railway Infrastructure Administration, in which funds are allocated to ensure the gradual financing of newly launched investment actions. If there is a higher need for funding given to the actions during 2020, e.g. by speeding up the preparation of certain actions than the allocation provided for in the SFDI budget, negotiations with the Ministry of Finance will be conducted following the development of the state budget in order to increase the budget of the SFDI to cover these actions. This procedure has already been agreed when discussing the amount of expenditure frameworks for the State budget for 2019. The budget of 2020 is based on the Ministry of Finance's indicative figures for the total amount of CZK 70.2 billion of Czech national resources. With the involvement of the European Union funds of CZK 17.1 billion, including the unused claims of the Operational Programme Transport 2014 to 2020 and the facility for linking Europe to CEF, the total budget for 2020 is CZK 87.3 billion.
