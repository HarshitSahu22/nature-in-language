The main impetus for the preparation of this amendment was the obligation to transpose a directive of the European Parliament and of the Council on the conditions for entry and residence of foreign nationals for research and study purposes. Our legal order is already in line with most of the requirements of this directive. Even so, the proposal will facilitate the situation of foreigners who reside here for the purpose of scientific research or study. With this proposal, the government is also responding to the current situation in the area of migration for the purpose of carrying out economic activity. The government's aim is to direct and, in particular, to manage migration responsibly for the purpose of carrying out economic activity, even depending on the expected development of the economic cycle in specific forms and needs of the labour market, as well as on the country of origin of foreigners. The proposal contains tools for how the government can achieve this goal. At the same time, the government is aware of the need to integrate newcomers into Czech society. It therefore proposes a new obligation for third-country citizens who want to stay in the Czech Republic for more than a year to undertake an adaptation integration course. The Ministry of the Interior analysed the amendments applied in detail.
