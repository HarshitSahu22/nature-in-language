ParlaMint-CZ_2019-06-06-ps2017-030-07-004-013.u2	Hello, Mr President, honourable Members. Allow me to introduce the Government's Bill No. 503/2012 Coll., on the State Land Office and on the amendment of certain related laws. The proposed amendment aims to make the activities of the State Land Office more effective and solves some of the current problems arising from its practice. There are three laws, the Act on the State Land Office, the Law on the Treatment of Property Relationships to Land and Other Agricultural Property and the Law on Fines and Bail for non-compliance with laws governing the transformation of agricultural cooperatives and the correction of property injustices in the field of property relations to land and other agricultural property. The aim of the draft amendment to the SÚ Act is to establish, in particular, the state's priority for the transfer of land from the reserve intended for development programmes of the State in the course of the merger of claims under other laws, to specify the method of valuation of assets and to abolish the possibility of a repayment scheme, to introduce a new type of claim transfer of the construction and related property placed on the land. Proposal for a technical amendment to Act No. 229/1991 Coll. optimises the procedure for the transfer of agricultural parcels to beneficiaries by public offer and introduces the possibility of repeatedly offering the land in the public offer if the previous offer was in vain. Draft amendment of Act No. 39/1993 Coll. solves the cases of repayment of bail made pursuant to this law by an entity that has ceased to exist in the meantime, with bail becoming the income of the state budget. The Government's draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee in the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 23rd meeting on 15 May 2019 and recommended by its resolution to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to approve the Government's draft law as amended by the amendments adopted. The amendments adopted are in line with the concept of the petitioner, they will contribute to improving the government's bill, and I therefore agree with them. Thank you.
