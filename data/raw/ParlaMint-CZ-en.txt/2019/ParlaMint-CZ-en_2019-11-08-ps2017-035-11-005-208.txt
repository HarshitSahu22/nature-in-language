The aim of this bill is to address some of the shortcomings that have so far arisen in practice in the application of the Commercial Corporations Act. She participated in the creation of this law expert commission consisting of leading Czech academics, representatives of practice and representatives of the Supreme Court of the Czech Republic dealing with this issue. The proposed changes affect various aspects. Although it is at first glance a comprehensive amendment, it contains only a few conceptual changes. In many places, changes made in other provisions are merely reflected in order to ensure consistency of the law. According to the targeted changes, they can be broken down into the following categories: on the one hand, it is a reduction in the regulatory burden for entrepreneurs and a strengthening of autonomy. It is also about ensuring greater transparency in the organisational structures of capital companies and cooperatives. Furthermore, it is a modification of the monistic system of the internal management of a public limited company which meets the requirements of transparency. In addition, the solution to the problem of so-called inactive companies that pose a risk of being used for fraud. In addition, the elimination of shortcomings in the transposition of certain provisions of the EU Directives and the implementation of the interconnection of the Commercial Register. Furthermore, ensuring greater protection of the rights of members and strengthening their legal certainty. And after all, the removal of inaccuracies or uncertainties, e.g. modification of the protest at the general meeting, etc. Finally, the elimination of legislative technical or terminology errors and duplications. The effectiveness is generally proposed from 1 January 2020. The effectiveness of the amendment of the Basic Registers Act to 1 July 2020 in order to set up processes for the processing of new reference data. I will then comment on the various amendments. Thank you for your attention.
