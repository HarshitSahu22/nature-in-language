ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-17-ps2017-028-02-015-015.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce the Government's amendment to Act No. 226/2013 Coll., on the marketing of wood and timber products, in short the law on wood. The measures against the placing on the European Union market of illegally harvested timber and timber products are currently based on two complementary EU regulations. One is the regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council of the European Union laying down the obligation on operators to place timber and timber products on the market, the so-called Wood Regulation, which was adapted by means of the Wood Act. Regulation of the Council on the import of timber into the European Community, or regulation on licences, which was adapted through the Customs Administration Act of the Czech Republic. The proposed amendment to the Act is submitted because of the creation and functioning of a comprehensive system for monitoring the marketing of timber and timber products on the territory of the Czech Republic in one law. The application has been implemented under two laws. Specification of the scope of customs offices, the Directorate-General for Customs, the Ministry of Agriculture and the authorised persons in connection with the licensing regulation, as well as specification of administrative procedures. Furthermore, it is the elimination of duplication of controls by economic operators under the Wood Regulation by the regional authorities and the Institute for Forest Treatment. This legislation protects the European market from timber and timber products from illegal sources and thus has a positive impact on the business environment. The draft law also simplifies the facts of the offences, including the incorporation of the principle and professional rules of the Act on liability for and proceedings concerning offences. The Government's draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 20th meeting on 20 March this year and recommended by its resolution to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to approve the Government's draft law as amended by the amendments adopted. The amendments adopted are in line with the concept of the petitioner, thereby contributing to the improvement of the Government's bill, and I therefore agree with them. Thank you.
