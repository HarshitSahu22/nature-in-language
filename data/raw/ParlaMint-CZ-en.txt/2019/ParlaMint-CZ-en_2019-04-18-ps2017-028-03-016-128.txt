Madam Members, at second reading you already have a proposal to give your consent to ratifying the amendments to Article 8 of the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court. Let me reiterate that the amendments in question complement the three new facts of war crimes, namely: 1. the use of weapons that use microbiotic or other biological agents or toxins, whatever their origin or method of production, 2. the use of weapons the primary effect of which is an injury to shrapnels which are undetectable by X-rays in the human body; and 3. the use of laser weapons specifically designed to cause permanent blinding of unprotected vision by their sole combat action or one of their fighting effects, i.e. unprotected eyes or eyes equipped with aids that correct vision. These facts will be applicable both at the time of the international armed conflict and at the time of the armed conflict of no international nature, and the definitions in question copy the facts of these crimes under international law as set out in the relevant international treaties. The Czech Republic is a party to all these international treaties. The purpose of extending the category of war crimes prosecuted by the International Criminal Court is to increase the protection of victims and to achieve the punishment of the perpetrators of these crimes. I therefore believe that, like the Senate, the Chamber of Deputies will also agree to ratify the amendments in question. Thank you for your attention.
