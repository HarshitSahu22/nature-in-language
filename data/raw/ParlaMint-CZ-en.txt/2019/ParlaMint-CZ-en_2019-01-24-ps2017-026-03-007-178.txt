ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-24-ps2017-026-03-007-178.u1	178. Government decision on flights and transits of armed forces of other states through the territory of the Czech Republic in 2019 /German press 272/ Mr. Defense Minister Metnar is ready to give us this material. Please, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-24-ps2017-026-03-007-178.u2	Thank you for the floor. By its resolution, the Government authorised the crossings and transits of the armed forces of the EU States which ratified the Treaty of the European Defence Agency. Furthermore, it has authorised flights and transits to the armed forces of the Member States of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and States participating in the Partnership for Peace programme. Last but not least, the countries with which the Czech Republic maintains a partnership. Allowing the crossing will also be possible for the armed forces of other states, but only provided consent from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The Czech Republic will be perceived by our allies as a reliable and trustworthy partner in this area. The Government's decision is also entirely in line with the Ministry's analysis of existing legislation on the current practice of declaring consent to overflights and transits of armed forces through the Czech Republic, which the Czech Government took note of by its resolution of 580 of 22 July 2015. This material has also been discussed by the Defence Committee, and I am asking you to support it. Thank you.
