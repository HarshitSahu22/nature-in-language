ParlaMint-CZ_2019-03-26-ps2017-027-09-002-080.u2	Hello, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, dear government. Let me present to you the parliamentary proposal, which is an amendment to Act No. 252/1997 Coll., on Agriculture, and Act No. 256/2000 Coll., on the State Agricultural Intervention Fund. The amendment of both laws aims primarily to transfer administration and provide agricultural national subsidies from the Ministry of Agriculture to the State Agricultural Intervention Fund. The current method of administration of agricultural national subsidy programmes is divided between the Ministry of Agriculture and the State Agricultural Intervention Fund, which results in a lower efficiency of this administration associated with an increased administrative burden on both the Ministry of Agriculture and the Fund, as well as on the grant applicants. On the basis of the provisions of Section 2d of the Agriculture Act, the State Agricultural Intervention Fund is currently entrusted with the administration for the Ministry of Agriculture of applications for subsidies which are exclusively financed from national sources. The proposed proposal will be that these aids will be newly implemented exclusively by the State Agricultural Intervention Fund, on the basis of the principles on the grant of subsidies to be issued by the Ministry in the current way, including the fact that the purpose of the programme to support these activities, which are financed exclusively from national resources, including the extent of the funds allocated to these programmes, will continue to be approved for each year by the Chamber of Deputies at the same time as the state budget. The only exception to this proposed system will be to provide support for genetic resources under Act No. 154/2000 Coll., on breeding, breeding and registration of livestock, and Act No. 148/2003 Coll., on genetic resources of plants and micro-organisms, which will continue to be carried out by the Ministry or by the person responsible for their highly professional specifics. By transferring the administration of agricultural national subsidies to the State Agricultural Intervention Fund, it will be possible to use the current administrative environment and information system of the Fund, which is connected to registers, electronic submission and more information for clients. The Ministry will also be able to focus more on setting agricultural policy within individual commodities in the area of national subsidies, which is the role of policy maker. The amendment also creates the prerequisites for strengthening electronic communication between the applicant and the State Agricultural Intervention Fund. Thank you for your attention.
