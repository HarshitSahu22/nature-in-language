ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-18-ps2017-028-03-015-127.u2	Hello, Mr President, honourable Members. Through the Prime Minister, you have been presented with the agreement to ratify the Protocol on Sustainable Development of Agriculture and Rural Development to the Framework Convention on the Protection and Sustainable Development of the Carpathians, hereinafter referred to as the Protocol on Agriculture. These protocols are instruments for the implementation of the Carpathian Convention which enable the objectives of the Carpathian Convention to be pursued and cooperation to be developed in key areas of the Carpathian region. The adoption of the Protocol will not entail the obligation for the Czech Republic to pay any obligatory contributions beyond the contributions already made to the proper budget of the Carpathian Convention. This means that no additional separate fund will be created, only for the agricultural protocol. The current contribution of the Czech Republic under the Carpathian Convention amounts to EUR 33,000 per year and is covered by the chapter of the State Budget 315, which is the Ministry of the Environment. In addition to the Czech Republic, the Parties to the Carpathian Convention and the Protocol are also Hungary, Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Serbia and Ukraine. In the Czech Republic, parts of the South Moravian, Zlínský, Olomoucký and Moravian-Silesian Region fall under the scope of the Carpathian Convention. The main reasons why we propose ratification of the Protocol on Agriculture are solidarity with the countries of the Visegrad Four and the Balkans, promoting the sustainable development of mountain border regions in Beskydy and other parts of the Carpathian region and strengthening cooperation in agriculture with Ukraine and Serbia, which is not yet covered by the common agricultural policy of the European Union. In accordance with the provisions of the legislation in force, the implementation of the Protocol will be ensured by the Ministry of Agriculture in cooperation with its departmental organisations, the Ministry of Environment and local partners in the Karpat region. The Government agreed by its resolution number 800 of 6 November 2017 and signed it under the authority of the President of the Republic for the Czech Republic by an extraordinary and authorised ambassador in Ukraine. Ukraine is both the depositary of the Carpathian Convention and the Protocol on Agriculture. The Government's proposal to approve the ratification of the Agriculture Protocol has already been discussed by the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic, which, by its resolution number 525 of the 18th meeting held on 17 October 2018, agreed to the ratification of the Agriculture Protocol. The Protocol on Agriculture has so far been signed by five Contracting Parties - the Czech Republic, Hungary, Romania and Poland, as well as Slovakia, which ratified the Protocol on Agriculture. Thank you for your attention.
