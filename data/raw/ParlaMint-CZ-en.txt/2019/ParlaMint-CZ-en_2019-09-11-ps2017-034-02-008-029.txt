ParlaMint-CZ_2019-09-11-ps2017-034-02-008-029.u2	Once again a good day, Mr President, Madam Members. Let me briefly state the Government's proposal for an amendment to Act No. 326/2004 Coll., on Plant Health Care. The proposed bill is an adaptation to the regulation of the European Union on protective measures against harmful plant organisms and to the European Union regulation on official controls. The aim of the adaptation is to delete the duplicate provisions newly contained in the directly applicable regulations, to specify the competences of the competent authorities, to lay down detailed procedural rules and to lay down penalties for infringements of the obligations laid down in the Regulations. In addition to the adaptation to the abovementioned Regulations, the aim of the proposal is in particular to make changes in the field of labelling, repacking, movement and storage of plant protection products in order to improve their traceability; to make changes in the field of the placing of plant protection products on the market where they have undergone a period of application ensuring that such products are safer. The draft law also contains a technical accompanying amendment to the Act on the Central, Audit and Testing Institute of Agriculture, which also adapts the two mentioned regulations. The draft law also includes an amendment to the Act on Administrative Fees, which allows the collection of administrative fees for acts carried out under the Regulation. The Government's draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its meeting on 4 September 2019 and recommended by its resolution to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to approve the Government's draft law as amended by the amendments adopted. I will just add that the amendments adopted are in line with the concept of the Ministry of Agriculture and will contribute to the improvement of the Government's bill, and we therefore agree with them. Thank you.
