ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-07-ps2017-035-10-010-279.u2	Thank you again for giving me the floor. Mr President, this information provides a comprehensive overview of landings, flights and transits within 48 hours in the Czech Republic of other armed forces through the territory of the Czech Republic, in the first half of this year. The material also includes a comparison of individual numbers of flights of foreign military aircraft and land transports of foreign armed forces over and over the territory of the Czech Republic over the last five years. Land and air transport of other armed forces was carried out during that period due mainly to participation in military operations such as KFOR, further participation in military exercises, trainings and demonstrations of military technology, such as Dark Blade, and negotiations to secure them. For the purpose of crossing and landing in the Czech Republic, a total of 3 279 air transports were carried out, including 768 charter flights and 204 landing of other armed forces. Land transport for the purpose of exercise and transit through the territory of the Czech Republic was carried out in a given period a total of 457 separate movements without military police escort and 48 road currents and 1 rail transport. Thank you.
