ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-27-ps2017-039-02-001-201.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Hello, ladies and gentlemen. Let me briefly remind you of the main points of the proposal for an amendment to certain laws in the field of business regulation in the financial market. The reason for submitting this amendment is in particular the need to adapt the legal order to the European Union Regulation on the prospectus. The prospectus is an information document which is required to be processed in the public offering of securities and the purpose of which is that potential investors have all the necessary information to decide whether to purchase the security. The legislation of the prospectus is still contained in the Capital Market Business Act. The prospectus will be amended by a directly applicable European regulation. This adjustment should therefore be deleted from the Capital Market Business Act and some other necessary changes should be made to it. [[9.10 hours]] This amendment also amends the distribution of supplementary pension savings in such a way that it is no longer linked to the distribution of investment instruments under the Capital Market Business Act. This will allow a separate distribution of supplementary pension savings without the current need to be authorised by an investment intermediary. It is therefore necessary to adjust both the Capital Market Business Act and the Supplementary Pension Savings Act at the same time, while these adjustments must take effect at the same time. This amendment also deals with the long-disputed issue of insolvency of entities without legal personality, namely sub-funds of investment funds and mutual funds. It will be possible for insolvency proceedings to be conducted directly against a specific sub-fund or mutual fund so that the bankruptcy of such entities does not endanger their management or other funds managed by it. It is also proposed to make a number of minor changes to the Bond Act and to the Investment Companies and Investment Funds Act. For example, the investment fund promoter's adjustment is generalised, the ex ante supervision by the depositary for qualified investor funds is cancelled and the tolerance of insignificant valuation errors is adjusted. As part of the preparation of the draft law, fundamental questions were consulted with a wide range of stakeholders, in particular the Association for the Capital Market, the Ministry of Justice and the Czech National Bank. Thank you.
