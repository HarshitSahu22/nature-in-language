Proposal for the election of a representative of the Ombudsman I can reveal that the House was also successful in the election of the Ombudsman's representative. Here, too, was kvorum 87. Ms. Lenka Marečková received 29 votes and Mrs. Monika Šimůnková received 89 votes. It is therefore by two votes, but Mrs Šimůnková is becoming a representative of the Ombudsman. On behalf of the committee, I can guarantee that there have been several recounts, and the numbers are fine.
