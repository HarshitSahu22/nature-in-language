ParlaMint-CZ_2019-06-06-ps2017-030-07-005-014.u2	So hello again. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce the Government's amendment to Act No. 91/1996 Coll., on feed. The main objective of the bill is to adapt the Law on Feed to Regulation 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 March 2017 on official controls. In particular, in order to simplify official control against controlled and more efficient implementation, the draft act provides for the obligation for operators to notify and continue the interruption of approved or registered activities, refine and supplement the requirements required for the operator's registration or approval of operations, re-establish the time limit for the preservation of samples and allow the person to apply for compensation for the samples taken, provide for authorisation for the establishment of storage conditions for bulk feed on agricultural land in the implementing decree, update references to the directly applicable provisions of the European Union, refine certain provisions of the basic terms, follow-up to those amendments to regulate offences. The Government's bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 23rd meeting on 15 May 2019 and recommended by its resolution to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to approve the Government's bill as presented to the Chamber of Deputies. Thank you for your attention.
