ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-28-ps2017-039-03-004-169.u2	Mr President, thank you for the floor. Mr President, honourable Members, the relations between the Czech Republic and Cambodia in the field of aviation are currently regulated by the 1964 Agreement. As you can see, this deal is a long time ago. It does not correspond to the current situation in the field of air transport and in addition some of its provisions do not comply with Union law. On these grounds, an entirely new aviation agreement was negotiated with a view to creating a liberal and modern framework consistent with current trends in civil aviation for possible future implementation of air transport between the two States and for the cooperation of air carriers. The signing of this Agreement took place on 5 June 2018 in the capital of Cambodia Phnom Penh. Although Cambodia is not from an economic point of view among important trading partners of the Czech Republic, which corresponds to the size of the transport market, the negotiation of a new contractual framework can generally be beneficial in terms of further development of mutual relations, especially in connection with the gradual increase in Cambodian economy, including tourism development. The agreement does not require adjustments to the Czech legal order and its implementation will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to give my consent to ratify this agreement. Thank you for your attention.
