Proposal by the Chamber of Deputies to elect candidates for members of the Council of the Institute for the Study of Totalitarian Regimes This election was successful from the perspective of the electoral commission, 181 ballots were issued, 179 tickets were handed in, so two tickets remained outstanding. Anyway, thevorum, which is calculated from the number of tickets issued, is constant, i.e. 91. František Bublan won 96 votes and was elected, Jiří Padevět 49 votes, he was not elected. Mikuláš Pešta 23 votes and has not been elected. Eduard Stehlík 95 votes and was elected. This choice therefore ends successfully.
