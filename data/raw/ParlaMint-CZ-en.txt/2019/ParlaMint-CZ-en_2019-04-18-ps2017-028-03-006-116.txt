ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-18-ps2017-028-03-006-116.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, fellow Members, relations between the Czech Republic and Mongolia in the field of air transport are not yet regulated by any international treaty. Specific steps towards the preparation of the Air Agreement began in 2013, the expert meeting then took place in 2015. The signing of the agreement subsequently took place on 25 May 2017 in Ulanbatar. The agreement is of a liberal nature and brings a modern contractual framework creating suitable conditions for possible future implementation of air traffic between the two States and for cooperation between air carriers. Although regular or irregular air transport between the Czech Republic and Mongolia is currently not provided by any air carrier, the said territory can be considered interesting in the future from the perspective of Czech civil aviation. The agreement does not require adjustments to the Czech legal order and its implementation will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to give my consent to ratify this agreement. Thank you for your attention.
