Proposal for the election of members of the Council of the Czech Press Office At the election of members of the Czech Press Office we selected from three candidates for two vacant seats. Again 180 ballots were handed over, the kvorum was 91. Angelika Bazalová won 75 votes, Michal Semín 31 and David Soukup 93. So David Soukup was elected in the first round. One seat has been filled and one seat remains vacant. The other two members of the election, Mrs. Bazalová and Mr. Semín, are moving on to him.
