ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-18-ps2017-028-03-004-189.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Audit Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic under Act No. 130/2002 Coll. carried out a check on the legitimacy and regularity of the distribution of funds by the Grant Agency and the management of state property in the reference period, discussed complaints about the provider's progress in assessing the design of grant projects and submitted opinions to the Bureau of the Grant Agency. In the report submitted, the Audit Board notes that the selection process and evaluation of grants were carried out in a standard manner and that the recommendations of expert panels and expert commissions were always respected over the past period. The members of the Supervisory Board physically carried out 74 inspections at meetings of expert panels and expert commissions. During the reference period, this is October 2017 to September 2018, 186 grant projects were audited by nine institutions. During the reference period, 27 complaints were received by the Supervisory Board concerning the failure to grant aid or the outcome of the opposition procedure. They've all been taken care of. The report shall be accompanied by a draft annual remuneration for the activities of the members of the Board of Auditors of the Grant Agency. And I can now just say that, as far as the general debate is concerned, that is all, on my part, the rest only in the debate in detail, which I am signing up for.
