ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-016-133.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, dear colleagues, now a few words about animals. The 12th Meeting of the Conference of the Parties to the Convention on the Protection of Migratory Species of Wild Animals, commonly referred to as the Bonn Convention, which took place in October 2017, adopted amendments to Annexes I and II to this Convention. The meeting decided in total to include 34 new species in Annexes I and II to the Convention containing lists of species covered by this Convention. However, there are only two of the newly classified species in the Czech Republic. It is a small grey and a small one whose protection is already covered at national level by the existing applicable legal order of the Czech Republic and does not require the adoption of new specific safeguard measures. The adoption of amendments to the Annexes will also have no impact on the state budget, public administration or the corporate sphere. Thank you for your attention.
