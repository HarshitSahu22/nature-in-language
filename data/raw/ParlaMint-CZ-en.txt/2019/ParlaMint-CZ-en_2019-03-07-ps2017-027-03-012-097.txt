ParlaMint-CZ_2019-03-07-ps2017-027-03-012-097.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the agreement between the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic on updating the border documentary work is a technical document intended to ensure the amendment of Annex No 1 to the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic on common state borders in order to correspond to the current state of the marking of national borders in the field. There is no change in the course of national borders under the contract. On the basis of the Treaty on Common State Borders, there is a regular examination of national borders. The result of the examination is so-called border documents in which changes are recorded compared to the valid border documentary work. These include, for example, the new location of the border signs, the replacement of the direct indication of the course of the border by indirect or vice versa and so on. These border documents are subsequently nationally approved on both sides. On the Czech side, border documents were approved by the Minister of Interior on April 7, 2017. The approved border documents become part of the border documentary work and therefore amend Annex No. 1 to the Convention on Common National Borders on the basis of a special international treaty which is just presented by the Treaty. Thank you for your attention.
