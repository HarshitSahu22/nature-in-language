ParlaMint-CZ_2019-06-21-ps2017-030-12-002-156.u2	Hello, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, very briefly. Measures against the placing on the European Union market of illegally harvested timber and timber products are based on two complementary regulations. The proposed amendment to the Act is submitted for reasons of the creation and operation of a comprehensive system of control of the marketing of timber and timber products on the territory of the Czech Republic in one law. This is then to clarify the scope of the customs offices. What is important is that this legislation protects the European market from timber and timber products from illegal sources, i.e. has a positive impact on the business environment. The amendments adopted are in line with the concept of the petitioner and will contribute to improving the government's bill, and I therefore agree with them. The Government's proposal was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies at its meeting on 20 March 2019 and adopted Resolution 85 in which it recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the Government's bill as amended by the amendments adopted. Thank you.
