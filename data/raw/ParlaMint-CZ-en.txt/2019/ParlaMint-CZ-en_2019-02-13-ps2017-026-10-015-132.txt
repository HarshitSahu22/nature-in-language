ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-015-132.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, the purpose of the transitional provision of Article 124 of the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court was to allow future parties to postpone the jurisdiction of the International Criminal Court for a period of seven years from the entry into force of the Rome Statute for the Contracting Party concerned against war crimes in cases where the crime was allegedly committed by the citizens of the future Contracting Party or within its territory. In the background of the adoption of this transitional provision, efforts were made to rapidly reach as many Contracting Parties to the Rome Statute as possible at the time of its adoption and, as a result, the widest possible jurisdiction of the International Criminal Court. Today, Article 124 is no longer being used. 14. the Assembly of the Parties to the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court in 2015 adopted a resolution which decided to delete Article 124. The reasons rested in its lack of update and in the pursuit of as broad a text as possible of the Rome Statute, which would not contain the provisions no longer being used. The purpose of the national discussion leading to the launch of Article 124 is therefore the completion of the process of expressing the Czech Republic's consent to delete Article 124 from this international treaty. From a substance point of view, this is a technical matter. I therefore believe that you will support the Government's proposal to agree to the amendment to Article 124 of the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court. Thank you for your attention.
