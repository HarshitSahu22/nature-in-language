Acts are multilateral international agreements governing the functioning of this international organisation included in the UN system, which has 192 Member States. In particular, it lays down rules on postal services between Member States' postal administrations, regulates the conditions for the exchange of letters, parcels and postal vouchers and liability in the event of loss and damage to consignments. The Act is regularly revised by the highest body, which is the Congress of the World Postal Union, which usually meets every four years. The output is changes and additions to individual contracts and they are presented to the Parliament of the Czech Republic now. The most important changes that we are discussing, and are approved by virtually every Congress, can be described as changes to the fee paid by the postal administration to ensure the mutual exchange of consignments. Their fair setting is very complicated and can lead to steps that are currently being announced by the United States of America when they are considering leaving the World Postal Union. The World Postal Union Act was on 26. Congress signed by the Czech Republic subject to their ratification. The Czech Government has already agreed to these acts by its resolution of 3 October 2018. The Czech Post is entrusted with the practical fulfilment of the content of the Act in the Czech Republic on the basis of the Postal Services Act. The texts of the World Postal Union Act, which are annexed to the House press today, bring no complications for the Czech Republic and do not call for any claims on the state budget.
