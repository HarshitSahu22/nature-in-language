Proposal for the election of members of the Council of the State Cinematography Fund It was our job to fill three seats. 181 ballots were issued and handed over, the quorum required for the election is 91. Tereza Dvořáková won 109 votes and was elected, Lubor Dohnal won 87 votes, was not elected. Jiří králík 18, was not elected, Marcel Řimák one vote, Vít Schmarc zero votes, Daniel Tuček 10 votes, Petr Vítek nine, Jiří Voráč 38, Antonín Weiser one vote. These candidates have not been elected. And Ondřej Zach 103 votes and was elected. The result of the election is that in the first round Tereza Dvořáková was elected with 109 votes and Ondřej Zach with 103 votes and moving to the second round to one unreleased place two candidates with the highest number, which are Lubor Dohnal with 80 votes and Jiří Voráč with 38 votes.
