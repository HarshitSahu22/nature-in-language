ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-26-ps2017-028-06-013-088.u2	Hello. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, this bill is entirely and exclusively based on practice. The problem of shutting down long-term vehicles is not only about Prague, Ostrava, but also about other cities. You encounter this problem whenever you see a car parked in a parking lot that doesn't meet the definition of a wreck. This bill seeks to impose a little obligation on vehicle operators. This means that a vehicle which does not have a valid technical inspection for more than six months shall not be placed on the road and the owner of the road may take steps to remove the vehicle. As far as cooperation with the authorities is concerned, the government will intervene to the minimum possible here, and only always at the request of the owner of the infrastructure. For me, that's all.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-26-ps2017-028-06-013-088.u3	Thank you. I now ask that the rapporteur take the floor for the first reading by Mr Jiří Kohoutek. Please, Mr. Ambassador.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-26-ps2017-028-06-013-088.u4	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. The amendment responds to the current problem where vehicles left behind by their owners on the roads on a monthly basis, because they are not formally or technically fit to operate. This means that they have, for example, a technical inspection that has been passed, or a technical certificate has been withdrawn from them for some fault by police control. The amendment proposes to provide for an explicit ban on motorways, roads, local roads and publicly accessible vehicle special purpose roads which, for more than 6 months, must not be operated on roads under the Road Traffic Conditions Act due to the futile expiry of the time limit for carrying out a regular technical inspection or technical incapacity of the vehicle identified by a technical inspection or by a technical road inspection. According to the law in force, these vehicles cannot be cleaned because they do not comply with the definition of the wreck referred to in Section 19 of the Road Act, according to which the wreckage is a vehicle which is clearly technically unfit for failure to operate on roads and the restoration of eligibility would require replacement, addition or repair of essential parts of the mechanism or construction of a road vehicle. The problem with out-of-control cars, which their owners have left on the road and do not respond to the call for withdrawal, is a problem that needs to be addressed. That's all, thank you for your attention.
