ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-24-ps2017-026-03-013-186.u2	Have a good day. As far as the financial arbitrary activity is concerned, 1,352 proceedings were initiated in 2017. The most frequently discussed disputes and settled questions were life insurance and consumer credit of 82 percent, and the procedure started in previous periods. A total of 3,220 proceedings took place. The length of the proceedings shall be influenced by the arbitrary party to bring the parties to the dispute to a settlement or rejoinder of the proposal. The results for 2017 were thus in line with this effort, when in the case of decisive disputes just 60 per cent was concluded by an approved reconciliation. As regards the management of the institution, the Financial Arbitrary Office is an organisational component of the State and a separate entity whose income is part of the budget chapter of the Ministry of Finance. The approved budget for 2017 was 56 690 thousand, the actual expenditure was 53 521 thousand, the budget for expenditure was drawn from 79 %.

ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-24-ps2017-026-03-013-186.u4	Thank you. Ladies and gentlemen, regarding the activities of the financial arbiter, I would like to mention that it does not work optimally from the point of view of many financial services consumers. The financial arbiter records a large number of undecided cases. Originally, the financial arbitrary was set up to speed up decision-making. Instead, the decision takes more than a year. Clearly, such a financial arbiter cannot solve many problems in the financial market. For this reason, I would like to appeal to the government to ensure sufficient funds for the financial arbiter to improve its activities so that all clients are protected against the unfair practices of certain persons on the financial market. Thank you for your attention.
