Mr Vice-President, ladies and gentlemen, this point is likely to be a little less significant with all due respect to him, but, of course, what we voted for a moment ago is historic. So let me briefly introduce to you the protocols that were unanimously adopted at the 39th session of the Assembly of the International Civil Aviation Organisation, and I will also use the ICAO abbreviation, which took place from 27 September to 7 October 2016 in Montreal. First Protocol on the amendment of Article 50, point (b). (a) the Convention on International Civil Aviation amends the number of members of the ICAO Council from 36 to 40 today. This change is appropriate due to the increasing number of Member States, at the same time it should increase the chances of the future election of the Czech Republic to this important body. This would enable the Council to actively defend Czech and European interests. The second Protocol on the amendment of Article 56 of the Convention on International Civil Aviation introduces only one amendment consisting in increasing the number of members of the Air Commission from 19 to 21. The increase in the number of members is appropriate not only because of the increasing number of ICAO Member States, but also because more experts from different parts of the world can bring a new perspective on certain problems. In this way, they can significantly enrich the debate within the Air Commission and thus positively influence its outputs. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to give my consent to ratify this agreement. Thank you.
