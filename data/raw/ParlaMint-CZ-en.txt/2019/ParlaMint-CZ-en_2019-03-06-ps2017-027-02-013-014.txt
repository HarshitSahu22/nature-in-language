Another problem of the current system is the low level of electronically submitted tax returns. A high number of paper-based tax returns are unnecessarily burdened by the tax administrator's employees, thereby increasing the demands on the state budget. The main advantages of electronic tax return are minimizing errors associated with manual filling and checking, allowing better communication with authorities or easier monitoring of processing status. Electronic submission of tax returns allows the whole process to be accelerated and administrative costs reduced and archiving simplified. This proposal extends the current three-month period for the submission of a tax return by one month in cases where the tax entity submits a tax return electronically. This provides a positive incentive for citizens to make more use of electronic tax returns, while it does not provide any negatives for existing taxpayers who do not want to make use of the electronic submission option. There is also a minor adjustment in the case of filing by tax advisors, where new power of attorney will no longer be required, but only the actual act of filing by this way will suffice. While this change brings some minor negatives, the Financial Administration can only deal with delayed tax returns after the longest of the deadlines, but at the same time it is a considerable simplification and simplification of the process for both entrepreneurs and the Financial Administration itself. In the event that the tax entity does not submit the tax return electronically within the extended period and neither does the tax advisor, this extension will not be taken into account and any penalties will be calculated in the same way as if it had not submitted the tax return within a three-month period. The proposal thus provides positive incentives for persons submitting tax returns for the use of electronic forms of filing, while allowing persons who can fill out a tax return to file a return on time without the need to use a tax advisor. At the same time, the proposal should reduce the cost of the State of tax collection and help to reduce the error rate of tax returns made. The employees of the financial authorities will thus be able to carry out the tasks they are used to during April, including checks on paper returns. The increased number of electronic submissions should be reduced by the labour demands of the staff of the Financial Office. The proposal does not bring any fundamental changes.
