Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Ministry of Transport is submitting a bill amending Act No. 111/1994 Coll., on Road Transport, and other related laws. The main objective of the bill is, above all, the revision of the legislation on taxi services and the implementation of certain partial changes in other areas covered by the Road Transport Act. In the area of taxi services, the rules for the operation of taxi services are clearly regulated in connection with its liberalisation by using popular mobile applications and other electronic means of communication consisting in the waiver of the requirement to fulfil certain obligations, namely the mandatory use of a taxi meter and the marking of vehicles by a roof lamp. At the same time, it is proposed to reduce the obligations of taxi carriers and drivers in such a way as to avoid an unjustified advantage for the provision of taxi services in so-called alternative ways. At the same time, however, the taxi service regulation is complemented by certain institutes that will lead to better protection and enforcement of the rights of taxis customers. One of them is a new institute of taxi service intermediary, which sets out certain obligations for the performance of its activities, which aim in particular to ensure that only legal services are the subject of mediation, that is to say services provided by authorised entities - the carrier and the driver who hold the necessary authorisation: the concession of the carrier and the authorisation of the driver of the taxi service. [[Very bad hearing because of the noise in the room.]] Furthermore, a new way of marking taxi services vehicles is introduced so that vehicles which are not equipped with a roof lamp are clearly identifiable for both customers and control authorities themselves. This new identifier will be the registration sticker of the taxi service vehicle, which will be issued by the transport authorities and which will have to be marked by each vehicle operated by the taxi service. The proposal also reduces -
