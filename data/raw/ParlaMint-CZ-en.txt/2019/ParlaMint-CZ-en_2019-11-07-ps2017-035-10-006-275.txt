ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-07-ps2017-035-10-006-275.u1	275. 1. supplement to the participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and the participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the territory of the Czech Republic in 2019 /German press 480/ I ask the Minister of Defence to once again present this House press.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-07-ps2017-035-10-006-275.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. This material contains information about 25 newly planned exercises of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in 2019 and specifying information about 38 exercises that have already been approved and during which preparations some attributes have changed beyond the approved framework. These are, for example, the number of assigned forces and means, dates or venue. These changes in the training plan with foreign partners reflect in particular the requirements of foreign partners in the field of preparation for foreign operations, including the construction of the ability to achieve alliance standards and the current needs of the Czech Army. A detailed list of the amendments is also given in the Annex to this material. These exercises will be financed under the approved budget of the Ministry of Defence chapter for 2019. Therefore, additional requirements for funds in the area of foreign exercises and increases in the Ministry of Defence's budget do not arise. Thank you for your attention.
