ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-30-ps2017-026-06-027-042.u2	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. Dear Members, the proposed amendment to several criminal law laws responds immediately to Council Regulation 2017/1939 on the establishment of the Office of the European Public Prosecutor. It is a regulation that has been adopted under the enhanced cooperation regime involving a total of 22 Member States of the European Union, including the Czech Republic, while the following Member States remain outside: Denmark, Ireland, Hungary, Poland, Sweden and Great Britain. The Czech Republic has therefore become involved with most of the other Member States in this cooperation. The aim of this European regulation, which in itself is a directly applicable regulation and is applicable without amending the law, is to strengthen or change the role of law enforcement authorities in the prosecution of criminal offences committed against the financial interests of the European Union. It is to this end that a special European office, the Office of the European Public Prosecutor, is hereby established, which will play the role of senior law enforcement authority in the prosecution of these offences. Although the regulation is a directly applicable regulation, on the basis of its evaluation, it was concluded that, after all, adaptation to this regulation requires the amendment of certain national regulations, and that is the change that is now being brought forward by the Government's draft law. The system will, in principle, work in such a way that, in addition to the highest European prosecutor, there will be an institute of European prosecutors who will be assigned directly to the newly established office and will sit in Luxembourg, along with European prosecutors who, on the contrary, will be active in the Czech Republic and will be selected basically from Czech prosecutors. It is they who will oversee the prosecution of crimes committed against the financial interests of the European Union. This should strengthen to some extent independence from national authorities, as they will be excluded from the classic system of prosecutors in this area.
