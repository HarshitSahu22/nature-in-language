ParlaMint-CZ_2019-03-07-ps2017-027-03-009-132.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, the material presented was prepared on the basis of Article 19 of the Constitution of the International Labour Organisation. It provides for the Government to submit to Parliament every new convention and recommendation of the International Labour Organisation, including its opinion on them. The purpose of this commitment is, above all, to inform the legislator about the current development of international law in the field of labour and social affairs. In order to fulfil this obligation, the Chamber of Deputies and the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic submitted Recommendation 205 adopted in 2017, which deals with the issue of the necessary responses to the successful management of situations following serious conflicts or natural disasters in terms of social and employment. The recommendation contains basic guidance on both prevention and follow-up in the post-crisis reconstruction period. It highlights the importance of functional labour markets, sustainable businesses and complex social systems, crisis management and planning, as well as the role played by social dialogue in promoting and implementing sustainable solutions. It is also based on the fact that a well-functioning society enabling a decent life for the population is more coherent and resistant to potential conflicts, whether cross-border or national. [[12.10 hours]] The analysis of the individual measures recommended showed that the guidelines on prevention already belong to standard and traditional parts of Czech law. As well as the requirements for crisis management are sufficiently reflected in existing legislation. Any responses to disasters will thus be conducted within the meaning of the recommendation and for this reason the government does not consider it necessary to take any new measures on the basis of this document. Thank you for your attention.
