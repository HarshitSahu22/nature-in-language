ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-18-ps2017-028-03-007-117.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, in the 26th session of the Chamber of Deputies, my esteemed colleague, the Minister of Culture, presented to you the Agreement on a comprehensive and enhanced partnership between the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community and their Member States, of the one part, and the Republic of Armenia, of the other part. Let me just remind you that this agreement, which was provisionally implemented on 1 June last year, provides a broad framework for political, economic and sectoral cooperation between the EU and Armenia and will replace the 1999 agreement that is currently more closely targeted. Among the main objectives of the Agreement are strengthening a comprehensive political and economic partnership, contributing to strengthening democracy and the political, economic and institutional stability of Armenia, promoting, protecting and strengthening peace and stability at regional and international level, and strengthening the rule of law and respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms in Armenia, strengthening mobility and interpersonal relations, supporting Armenia in developing its economic potential and bringing its legislation into line with the acquis of the European Union and strengthening trade cooperation in accordance with the rights and obligations arising from WTO membership of the Parties. From the perspective of the Czech Republic, the agreement has the potential to contribute to the further development of mutual relations, including trade. Economic cooperation between the Czech Republic and Armenia is still not at the level at which it could be given the economic structure and supply of both countries. Czech companies show interest in the Armenian market, therefore the Czech Republic has an interest in cultivating the business environment, transparency, eliminating non-tariff barriers to trade and promoting shared values and democratic establishment of Armenia. This Agreement between the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community and their Member States, of the one part, and the Republic of Armenia, of the other part, has been discussed on 21 February by the Foreign Committee of the Chamber of Deputies, which, by its Resolution No. 56 recommends that the Chamber of Deputies agree with the ratification. Ladies and gentlemen, let me ask you the same thing. Thank you.
