ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-07-ps2017-035-10-007-276.u1	276. Information on deployment of forces and resources of the Ministry of Defence in foreign operations in 2018 /German press 495/ Once again, I ask the Minister to present this material.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-07-ps2017-035-10-007-276.u2	Thank you for giving me the floor, Mr President. The main content of the material is information on the political, military and financial aspects of the activities of Czech troops and soldiers in 2018 in Afghanistan, Kosovo, Bosnia and Herzegovina, the Baltics, the Sinai Peninsula, the Golan Heights, the United Kingdom, the Malian Republic, Iraq and Italy. The material also includes information on rapid impact projects - these are small projects implemented in order to obtain support for the local population or the cooperating armed forces of the host country, these projects were implemented only in Afghanistan in 2018 - and information on the state of involvement of the Czech Republic in UN missions in 2018 and on the opportunities and opportunities for the Czech Republic to operate in these missions, which is annexed to the material under the Security Council resolution of the State of 9 June 2015. [[11.50 hours]] At the end of 2017, there was a need to increase the number of soldiers in some foreign operations already during 2018. Specifically, it was a mission in Afghanistan, Mali and Iraq. In accordance with this, a new mandate was approved in mid-2018 for the operation of forces and resources of the Ministry of Defence's Ministry of Foreign Operations, which takes account of the increase already in 2018 and is valid until the end of 2020. Like in previous years, last year, Resolute Support remained the most demanding mission for the Czech Republic in Afghanistan. In September, our military contingent was strengthened by two teams of instructors for training special forces of the Afghan army and police and in the province of Logar and Herat by a total of 90 soldiers. In addition to the tasks carried out under the Alliance Mission, members of the Military Police provided the protection of the Ambassador and the Representation Office of the Czech Republic in Kabul. Unfortunately, in the second half of 2018, in August and October, four deaths of Czech soldiers and seven serious injuries occurred in connection with the mission of Resolute Support in Afghanistan. Another important area of our foreign presence was supporting the fight against Daeš in Iraq as part of the global coalition. Throughout the year 2018, the Air Advisory Team, the Military Police Training Unit and the Chemical Army Training Unit, worked in Iraq. In October and November 2018, our military presence in the European Union training mission in Mali was significantly strengthened by 70 soldiers. In addition to fulfilling the previous tasks, the protection of forces has been provided in the area of the training centre Koulikoro. The whole new foreign activity of our army in 2018 was involvement in an alliance initiative, a so-called enhanced forward-looking presence in Lithuania and Latvia. Since mid 2018, the Army of the Czech Republic has worked in two fighting groups, under the leadership of Germany in Lithuania and under the direction of Canada in Latvia. Our first troops have already completed their missions, and in addition to the main task, that is to strengthen the security of allies on the eastern wing of the Alliance, the mission for our soldiers was important thanks to intensive training and long-term stay in the international environment. Nine military observers of the Army of the Czech Republic worked in UN missions in Kosovo, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, the Malian Republic and the Central African Republic. The Czech Republic has prepared forces and means for the possible activation of NATO emergency forces in its territory. 191 soldiers were earmarked for deployment in NATO's rapid response operations, including very high alert forces. In 2018 we spent a total of 1.29 billion on the involvement of our forces and resources in foreign operations. CZK and these funds were financed from the Ministry of Defence's budget. A total of 1 297 members of the Defence Department took turns in 2018 in foreign operations. In conclusion, working in foreign operations alongside the defence interests of the Czech Republic and their allies has also made a significant contribution to the fight against terrorism. Thank you for your attention.
