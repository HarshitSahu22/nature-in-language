ParlaMint-CZ_2019-01-24-ps2017-026-03-008-179.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. This material contains information about the newly planned nine exercises of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in 2018 and specifying information about 17 exercises already approved by the government. For these exercises, some attributes exceeding the approved framework have changed during their preparation. These changes in the training plan with foreign partners reflect in particular the requirements of foreign partners in the field of preparation for foreign operations, including construction, ability to achieve alliance standards and the current needs of the army of the Czech Republic. A detailed list of the amendments is given in the Annex to this material. The exercises referred to in the second supplement will be financed under the approved budget of Chapter 307, which is the Ministry of Defence for 2018. Therefore, additional demands for an increase in the Ministry of Defence budget do not arise. Again, this material has been discussed in the Defence Committee and I thank you for its support.
