ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-27-ps2017-039-02-002-202.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly remind you of the draft law on double taxation in relation to Taiwan. One of the objectives of Czech foreign policy is the development of versatile and mutually beneficial economic relations with other states and jurisdictions, including Taiwan. Taiwan ranks among industrialised countries and so-called Asian tigers. Main industries - information technology, metal processing, electrical and chemical industries. The economic relations of Taiwan and the Czech Republic have a total turnover of almost 30 billion for 2017. Crowns. Trade balance significantly in favour of Taiwan, a large volume of exports to the Czech Republic, Taiwan invests in the electrical industry in the Czech Republic and exports these goods to the Czech Republic. Czech Republic exports cars, microscopes, tyres. The areas of energy, bio- and nanotechnologies, aviation industry and green technologies are prospective for the application of Czech companies on the Taiwan market. Some forms of economic relations and income resulting therefrom may give rise to the tax liability of residents of the Czech Republic in Taiwan and vice versa. In these cases, international double taxation of income is currently taking place, thereby reducing the income generated by residents of one jurisdiction from another's jurisdiction. Given the fact that the Czech Republic does not recognise Taiwan as a state, it is not possible to conclude an international double taxation treaty with it and thus eliminate one of the obstacles to economic cooperation with it. The draft law unilaterally replaces the double taxation treaty, with Taiwan being expected to adopt similar national measures. The double taxation rules contained in the Act correspond to the text of the standard international double taxation conventions, the OECD model contract, for the sake of the maintenance of reciprocity in relation to Taiwan, which will take the same measures nationally, and to ensure the same interpretation of the various provisions as in the interpretation of international agreements. In view of the fact that amendments to the bill have not been tabled, I therefore ask you to approve the bill as amended by the Government. Thank you for your attention.
