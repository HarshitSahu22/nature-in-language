Ladies and gentlemen, I present to you for the second reading a bill amending the law on financial control in public administration. The reason for submitting the bill is the fulfilment of the legislative obligations arising from the membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. This bill partly implements the European Directive on requirements for Member States' budgetary frameworks. The adoption of the bill will end its transposition into the Czech legal order. In the Czech Republic, a notice was sent in 2014 about non-compliance with the deadline for this transposition. This is a technical amendment. Its aim is merely to incorporate the minimum requirements laid down by the European Directive and, in accordance with the requirements of the Directive, to extend the scope of the public administration bodies affected by the Act on Financial Control. According to the draft law, the public administration is also a new voluntary union of municipalities, a voluntary organisation of the voluntary union of municipalities and the state organization of the Railway Infrastructure Administration. The bill was ordered to the inspection committee. On 9 November 2018, he issued a resolution recommending the approval of the bill with an amendment. The amendment facilitates the setting up of the internal control system for municipalities and voluntary associations of municipalities. The Ministry of Finance agreed to the amendment.
