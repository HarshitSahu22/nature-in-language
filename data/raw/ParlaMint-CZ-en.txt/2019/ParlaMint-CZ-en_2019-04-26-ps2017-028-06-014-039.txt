ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-26-ps2017-028-06-014-039.u2	Hello. Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to state the Government's bill amending Act No. 424/2010 Coll., the purpose of which is to postpone the termination of registration of birth numbers in civil documents for three years, i.e. on 31 December 2022. The reason for delaying the termination of the registration of birth numbers in civil certificates for this period is to avoid problems that would arise in application practice. At present, a number of public authorities' agendas are not adapted to the fact that the birth number is not mentioned in the ID card. A number of private information systems, such as banks, insurance companies or telephone operators, also operate on the basis of the birth number as a unique identifier of a natural person. Although the birth number for the identification of natural persons should not be used in the future, this will continue to be an important figure, which the majority of the Czech authorities should not be able to demonstrate if they were required by public authorities or private entities. Since it has been shown that the particular solution for the replacement of the native numbers in individual agendas is not effective, an additional coordinated approach is needed. Things will therefore be taken up by the Ministry of the Interior, which will prepare a comprehensive solution in cooperation with other ministries to minimise the use of the birth number when verifying the identity of natural persons. In view of the fact that the registration of birth numbers in civil certificates will be completed on 31 December 2019, it is necessary that the draft act submitted becomes effective as soon as possible and no later than 31 December 2019. For this reason, I would like to ask you to approve the proposal at first reading pursuant to § 90 para. 2 of the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies. Thank you for your attention.
