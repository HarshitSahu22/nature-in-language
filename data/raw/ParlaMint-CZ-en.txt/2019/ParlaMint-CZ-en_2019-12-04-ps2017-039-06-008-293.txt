The draft budget of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund is based on the grants granted from the chapter of the Ministry of Agriculture for SZIF notified by the administrator of Chapters 26 July 2019 and 29 August 2019. The basic revenue in the draft budget of SZIF for 2020 is CZK 41 954 177 000, of which CZK 11 190 439 000 from the state budget and CZK 30 763 738 000 from the budget of the European Union. The planned intervention purchases in 2020 of CZK 226 800 000 will be financed by the Ministry of Finance's loan. The draft budget submitted was drawn up in accordance with the relevant regulations of the European Commission issued for the common agricultural policy and, at the same time, respects the conditions laid down for its implementation in the Czech Republic by the relevant regulations of the Government of the Czech Republic. The draft budget of SZIF for 2020 was discussed and approved by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at the 29th meeting of 28 November 2019.
