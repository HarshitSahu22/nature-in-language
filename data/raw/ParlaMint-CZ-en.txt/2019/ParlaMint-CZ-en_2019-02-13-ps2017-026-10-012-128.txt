Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia on Air Transport Services /Departmental Press 197/ - First Reading Under the authority of the Government, this press will be presented by the Minister of Transport Daň Ťok. Please, you have the floor, Minister.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-012-128.u2	Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, honourable Members, the Czech Republic and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia do not yet have an international agreement in the field of civil aviation. This agreement was signed on 7 December 2016 and was subsequently submitted for the first time to the Parliament of the Czech Republic last year to grant its ratification consent. The agreement went through the first reading and the foreign committee in the Chamber of Deputies, but was subsequently withdrawn from the second reading programme due to the rejected proposal in the Senate plenary in July 2017. The reason for rejecting the motion in the Senate was not opposition to the substance of the agreement. This was the attitude of some senators towards the existing regime in Saudi Arabia, foreign policy of the country, the state of human rights, etc. [[12.00 hours]] The Ministry of Transport decided to re-submit this proposal to the Parliament of the Czech Republic, especially in order to ensure legal certainty for the current and future operation of air carriers between the Czech Republic and Saudi Arabia. Czech Airlines operates a regular seasonal line between Prague and Riyadh for the third year and before this summer season they experienced problems in approving their flights to Saudi Arabia. This newly requires a ratified and valid air agreement between the two states for the regular operation of the Czech Airlines in the next summer period of this year. The agreement regulates purely operational economic aspects of air transport and without it there are unexpected problems in operation with a negative economic impact in particular on air carriers and passengers. As part of the ratification process, the Senate has already agreed to ratify this Agreement on 15 August 2018. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic for the referral of material for discussion in the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you for your attention.
