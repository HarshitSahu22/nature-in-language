The report summarises information on the state and development of crime in the Czech Republic each year and on measures aimed at reducing it. The situation in the field of internal security and public order remained stable from the perspective of the police in 2017. As in previous years, there was no serious breach of public order in 2017. There was no open expression of international terrorism in the Czech Republic. The first level of threat to terrorism still applies, which was actually introduced after the attack on Brussels airport in March 2016. The police monitor the situation and, in cooperation with other security forces, take security measures. - I stress that we are talking about the report in 2017. From the perspective of crime development, the decline in registered crimes is characteristic for 2017. This is the lowest number of registered offences in at least 15 years. The crime idea decreased by 7.3 % year on year and the direct explanation slightly increased to 46,9 %. Despite the overall decline in crime, however, there are areas where development is the opposite. The increase in crime has been recorded in recent years mainly in the field of cybercrime - an increase of 5.5 % in 2017 - and a rise of 5.4 percent in vice in relation to cybercrime in the environment of social networks. In 2017, the security situation in industrial zones in the Hradec Králové and Plzeň Region was stabilized in connection with the high concentration of foreigners, employees. The Ministry of the Interior and the Police of the Czech Republic continue to monitor the situation carefully, as problems not only in the area of public order and security may also occur in new locations with an increasing number of foreign workers. The material was discussed by the Internal Security Committee on 19 April 2018 and also by the Security Council of the State on 27 April 2018.
