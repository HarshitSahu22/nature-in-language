ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-12-ps2017-026-09-001-219.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, honourable Members of the Government, allow me to inform you that on 10 December 2018, the President of the Chamber of Deputies received a request from the Regional Directorate of the Police of the Czech Republic, the Services of the Criminal Police and the investigation of the capital city of Prague, the Department of General Crime of the Ninth Department, for the consent of the Chamber of Deputies to prosecute a Member of Miloslav Rozner. In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, this request by the Police Authority was included on the agenda of this 26th meeting of the Chamber of Deputies and is currently being held as item 219. The mandate and immunity committee began to discuss the police authority's request at its 20th meeting on 18 December last year, and, by resolution 86, suspended the case. At the subsequent 21st meeting of the mandate and immunity committee, which took place on 5 February 2019, the consultation of the police authority's request was also suspended, by Resolution 89, because of hearing the observations of the supervisory prosecutor on the procedure for requesting a report on the supervisory clearance carried out, which apologized before the meeting of the mandate and immunity committee. Therefore, the consultation of the police authority's request has not yet been completed and the mandate and immunity committee has maintained its request to hear the supervisory prosecutor on the matter in question. I therefore translate to the Chamber of Deputies the proposal that item 219 on the agenda of the 26th meeting of the Chamber of Deputies should be interrupted by the next meeting of the Chamber of Deputies. Thank you.
