ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-18-ps2017-028-03-003-204.u1	204. Draft amendment of the budget of the State Housing Development Fund for 2019 /German press 427/ This material will be provided by the Minister for Regional Development, Klára Dostálová. Madam Secretary, please take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2019-04-18-ps2017-028-03-003-204.u2	Thank you so much for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the proposed amendment to the budget of the State Housing Development Fund addresses the need to cover the financial requirements of the prepared government regulation for the final-credit programme Construction. This government regulation was approved on Monday 15. 4. and we are really ready to announce this programme at the end of April. In 2019, the total expenditure under this programme is expected to be 1 billion, with 650 million non-refundable subsidies and 350 million low-interest loans. The loan part will be covered by the current total approved volume for loans granted, the subsidy part needs to be strengthened within the framework of the budget change. An increase of CZK 650 million is therefore proposed for investment transfers to municipalities under the Construction Programme. The second item is the coverage of the financial claims of the Regeneration of Public Area programme in the housing estates. This programme is the so-called second round of the JESSICA programme. Last year the program was launched, however, the contract can be concluded only after proof of the contract for the work with the contractor. That is why we are demanding the transfer of money from year to year and the strengthening of the budget by another CZK 100 million that we would involve in those contracts this year. An increase in the item for investment and non-investment transfers to municipalities under the Housing Regeneration Programme is proposed by 100 million. The total proposed increase in the expenditure limit of the Funds for 2019 amounts to CZK 750 million and will be covered by the Fund's free resources without entitlement to the State budget. I take the liberty of asking for your approval. Thank you.
