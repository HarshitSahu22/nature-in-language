The Czech Republic is a Contracting Party to the 2007 Convention which was concluded with the United States of America and which regulates cooperation in the field of science. The contract was concluded for a fixed period. Some other contractual instruments, such as the agreement between the Ministry of Industry and Trade and the Ministry of Energy of the Czech Republic, follow up on it cooperation in the field of science, research and innovation and many other positive activities. The purpose of the protocol now submitted, which is to be agreed with it in order to be ratified subsequently, is to extend the validity of the Convention with the United States of America by 10 years, so that cooperation in the field of science can continue during the next period. Given the positive contribution of the scientific cooperation itself with the United States of America, please support this proposal. Thank you for your attention.
