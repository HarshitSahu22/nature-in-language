ParlaMint-CZ_2019-03-13-ps2017-027-06-004-006.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me briefly to say a few more words. As mentioned at first reading, the Government's proposal for this Act amending the Act on the Activities of Occupational Pensions Institutions is processed on the basis of the obligation to transpose and supervise the European Directive on the Activities of Occupational Pensions Institutions. It is a revised directive that regulates these institutions, extending the requirements for their activities. In particular, it is an adjustment of the institution's governance system, remuneration policy, extended information obligations requirements for participants and the possibility of cross-border transfers of pension plans. I also said at first reading that the employment pension system is not in place in the Czech Republic, yet we are obliged to implement the provisions of this new directive into the rule of law. Therefore, the existing provisions of the Employment Insurance Institution Act, which has already been implemented in the previous directive in this field, are supplemented. The obligation to transpose all the provisions was already mentioned before the European Court of Justice, which decided that although the employment pension system in the Czech Republic is not in place, the Czech Republic is obliged to transpose all the provisions of the directive. Thank you for your attention.
