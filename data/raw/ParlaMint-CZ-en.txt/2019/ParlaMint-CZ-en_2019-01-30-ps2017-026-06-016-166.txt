Proposal for the election of the Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the Central European Initiative The first and actually the only point was successful. It was the choice of the Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the Central European Initiative. It was therefore a substitute for Mr Petr Dolínek for Bohuslav Sobotka. In this election, 172 ballots were issued and 87 were awarded, and Petr Dolínek received 107 votes, so I note that he was elected as the Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the Central European Initiative. And unfortunately, as I announced, the other elections were unsuccessful.
