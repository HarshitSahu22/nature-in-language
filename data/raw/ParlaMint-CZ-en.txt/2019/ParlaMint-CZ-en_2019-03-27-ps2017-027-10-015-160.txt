Proposal for appointment of members of the Supervisory Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic Published and delivered 162 ballots, kvorum 82. František Hrdlička won 100 votes, Petr Konvalinka 84 votes and Hana Vykopalová 90 votes. This choice was therefore successful and in the first round all three candidates were elected to the Audit Board of the Grant Agency.
