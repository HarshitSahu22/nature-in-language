Ladies and gentlemen, this material contains information about the military exercises carried out to the extent approved by the Government of the Czech Republic. Units of the armed forces of the Czech Republic participated in 62 military exercises with foreign partners outside the Czech Republic, 30 exercises with foreign partners in the Czech Republic. The total financial amount needed to carry out these exercises was CZK 60,2 million. The main tasks of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in the field of exercises with foreign partners in the first half of 2018 were the preparation of units for deployment in operations, the development of interoperability within bilateral regional cooperation and the construction of capabilities. Detailed overview of exercises for the period January to June 2018 is given in the Annex to this material. This material has also been discussed in the Defence Committee, and here I am asking for your support. Thank you.
