ParlaMint-CZ_2019-09-11-ps2017-034-02-010-015.u2	Thank you for the floor. Ladies and gentlemen, this is a government bill on the selection of persons in the management and supervisory bodies of legal entities with the state's ownership participation, the so-called nomination law. This proposal follows, in substance, a similar bill, which was submitted in the previous term by the Ministry of Finance, to which the Government of Bohuslav Sobotka ordered it to be processed, but eventually discussed it at its meeting on 5 April 2017, but did not approve it. The current government therefore re-worked the draft nomination law in a similar version, approved it and subsequently submitted it to the Chamber of Deputies. The content of this government proposal is the obligation of individual ministries to carry out an open, transparent tender for the position in question in the selection of persons to the managing authority of a legal person with the ownership of a State falling within the competence of that Ministry. According to the draft law, the ministries will also have to submit all personnel nominations not only to the management and supervisory bodies of legal entities with the ownership participation of the State which fall within their competence, to the committee for personnel nominations, which will then discuss them and take an opinion on them. The proposed law also regulates the position, composition and competence of this committee, which will be an advisory body of the Government. After all, this is how the Committee on Personnel Nominations works now, but so far only on the basis of the Government's resolution, with the proposed amendment having its existence and scope to stabilise by adapting it at the level of the generally binding legislation. The proposed legislative nomination process will then apply to all commercial corporations regardless of the amount of the State's equity in them, as well as to state enterprises, national enterprise and state organisation Railway Infrastructure Administration. The opinion of the committee on the nomination will be of a recommendable nature. However, if the Ministry deviates from the committee's recommendation, it will be obliged to justify its departure and publish it immediately via the website. Finally, I would like to briefly mention the discussion of the bill by the Economic Committee of the Chamber of Deputies, which recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the Government's nomination bill as amended by two partial amendments reducing the required age limit for participation in the selection procedure from 28 to 25 years, and the effectiveness of the law is further regulated. Both of these changes can be fully agreed for the government as a petitioner. Thank you for your attention.
