Proposal for the appointment of members of the Radio and Television Broadcasting Council Here too, 175 tickets were issued, even here the kvorum necessary for the election was 88. Milan Bouška won 61 votes, Ladislav Jakl 85 votes, Vadim Petrov 93 votes, Milada Richterová 41 votes and Olga Ulrichová 27 votes. As I said, the quorum required for the election is 88, which means one candidate in two has been elected, it is Mr. Vadim Petrov with 93 votes. For the second round, two candidates will compete in quotation marks for one vacancy. According to the highest number of votes, it is Milan Bouška with 61 votes and Ladislav Jakl with 85 votes.
