ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-28-ps2017-039-03-010-174.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, may I present to you the Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Additional Protocol to the Convention on Human Rights and Biomedicine relating to Biomedical Research, signed on 11 May 2018 in Strasbourg. As a member of the Council of Europe, the Czech Republic, an international organisation working on the principle of discussion and search for common solutions, participates, inter alia, in solving economic, scientific, legal issues, as well as the maintenance and development of fundamental human rights and freedoms. It is with this aim that the Czech Republic signed the Convention on Human Rights and Biomedicine in 1998. In the light of developments in the various fields of medicine, four additional protocols have been developed extending the existing principles and the legal protection of the persons concerned, which are governed by that Convention. One of these documents is the Additional Protocol related to biomedical research. Ratification of this protocol by the Czech Republic will ensure the highest legal standard of protection of dignity, identity and integrity of all who become participants in the research process. The Additional Protocol provides a clear guarantee of their fundamental rights. The Additional Protocol was adopted in 2005 and entered into force in 2007, signed in 22 countries of the Council of Europe and ratified in 10 of them, including Hungary, Slovakia, Slovenia or Norway. The rules and conditions for conducting biomedical research, which the Additional Protocol specifies in order to ensure the highest possible level of participants in research, are also respected in the Czech Republic. From the point of view of the Czech Republic, ratification of this Additional Protocol is a confirmation of the high ethical standard of research projects implemented here. Ladies and gentlemen, ladies and gentlemen, ladies and gentlemen, I am therefore asking you to give your consent to ratify this international treaty and thank you for your attention.
