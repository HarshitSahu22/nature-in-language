ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-08-ps2017-035-11-004-235.u4	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I will just repeat very quickly what the proposal for an amendment to the Employment Act is about. The aim of this proposed amendment is to ensure at least partial compensation for the effects of the increase in minimum wage, which entered into force 1. 1. 2019, in the form of an increase in the allowance for employment of people with disabilities by CZK 800 per person per month. The estimated cost of this proposal, this measure, can be calculated at approximately CZK 300 million per year. I would like to point out that, without this compensation, there is a risk of reducing the posts of disabled persons. According to its internal survey, the association of employers with disabilities states that if this compensation had not occurred, up to 51 percent of employers would have had to terminate their employment with the least executive employees. If a further increase in the minimum wage is expected, up to 14 percent of the employers of people with disabilities say that they would have to cease their activities, and 17 percent of employers would not be able to employ 50 percent of people with disabilities, that is, they would not be able to keep their companies in a so-called protected labour market. Here it should be pointed out that the role of the so-called protected labour market is absolutely irreplaceable when employing people with disabilities. It should also be known that from annual expenditures to support 56 thousand jobs for disabled people, up to two thirds of the costs are returned to public finances, to public budgets, in the form of contributions, but also to social spending savings. According to the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs, one unemployed person in the social system costs approximately CZK 207 000, while the proposed increase means CZK 9,600 per person per year. The proposed measure will therefore certainly pay off. However, I still have to comment on the amendment that is part of the proposal. Following the experience of three quarters of a year, which we are seeking to adopt this proposal, so that companies employing disabled people receive this increase of aid, the proposed amendment contains an authorisation for the government to increase the contribution to the employment of disabled people in an adequate way in the event of further increases in minimum wages, so that the government can immediately fulfil what is and was the result of tripartite negotiations this year. Therefore, please support this amendment as well. Finally, I would like to thank all those who are co-chairs of this bill, my colleagues and colleagues from social democracy, but I would also particularly like to thank Mrs Lenka Dražilová, the Chair of the Sub-Committee for Persons with Disabilities, and all the members and members of this subcommittee, as well as all the members and members of the Social Committee, where we agreed that this step must be taken. On behalf of everyone else, I would like to thank Mr Kaňkovský, who also supported the inclusion as quickly as possible, so that we can make it. And, of course, great thanks are due to the rapporteur, who knows well in her own experience what she is talking about. I therefore ask all of you, fellow Members, to support this proposal. It will certainly be a good deed to people who have managed to overcome their disability and try to work as hard as they can. It will be help and support to those who provide them with this work, in particular to production cooperatives and others who create jobs for disabled people. Both belong to respect and respect.
