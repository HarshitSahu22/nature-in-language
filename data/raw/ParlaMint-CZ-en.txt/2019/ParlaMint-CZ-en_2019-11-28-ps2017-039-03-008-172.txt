ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-28-ps2017-039-03-008-172.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, ladies and gentlemen, at today's meeting, the Multilateral Convention on the implementation of measures to combat the reduction of the tax base and the relocation of profits in relation to tax agreements signed in Paris on 7 June 2017 is now being presented. The Convention is one of the tangible results of the BEPS project - Base erosion and profit shifting - Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development and has been developed as an innovative approach which takes the form of an international multilateral convention and which allows the collective and relatively short period of time to implement the conclusions of this project into the content of existing international double taxation treaties. The Czech Republic has decided to use this Convention in a way that will implement all so-called double taxation agreements in its bilateral agreements. the minimum standards resulting from the BEPS project in the context of these contracts. The agreed minimum standards concern, in particular, the introduction of a rule against the abuse of these bilateral agreements, the explicit mention of the objective and meaning of the contracts, and then the amendments to the Articles of the Double Taxation Treaties, which affect the area of case management by agreement with a view to effective and timely resolution of any disputes arising in the area of application and interpretation of these major international tax agreements. Out of the number of 88 contracts in force, the Czech Republic will now cover the Convention 52 contracts, where the effects of the Convention on their texts can be predicted at the moment. Contracts for which the effects are now unpredictable, for example because the other Party does not communicate and solve problems, have not yet signed the Convention, has not covered the Treaty with the Czech Republic by the Convention and so on, the Czech Republic will not now be included under the regime of the Convention. As regards the currently uncovered double taxation agreements in force, nothing precludes their possible inclusion under the Convention regime in the future. This applies both to contracts already in force and, of course, to contracts which are not yet in force at the moment. For information, he agreed to ratify this Convention in December last year by the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. Ladies and gentlemen, on the basis of the above, I propose that: The Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic took the above into account when discussing this international multilateral convention and agreed to its ratification. Thank you all for your attention.
