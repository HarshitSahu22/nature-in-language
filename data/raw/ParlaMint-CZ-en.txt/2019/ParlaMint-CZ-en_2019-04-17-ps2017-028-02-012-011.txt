Ladies and gentlemen, let me put forward a bill amending Law No. 565/1990 Coll., on Local Charges, and some other related tax laws. I would like to sum up only the most important changes contained in the proposal because it was presented in detail at first reading. This Act introduces a new residence fee which will replace the existing fees for a spa or recreation stay and accommodation capacity. It will therefore be possible to introduce a fee for any municipality without any link to the presence of a spa or increased tourism. The fee will be charged for any short-term paid stay regardless of the type and destination of the place where it is provided, i.e. next to hotels, motels and guesthouses, also in private apartments, studios and the like. The maximum limit for the amount of the fee is proposed at CZK 21 for 2020, which corresponds to the sum of the rates of the previous two fees, and subsequently CZK 50 for each day of stay except for the date of beginning of stay. This is a reaction to the fact that the amount of the spa or recreational stay fee has not changed for more than 25 years. In so doing, the specific amount of the fee up to this maximum limit shall always be determined by the individual municipality in a generally binding decree. Furthermore, the exemption of persons and the relief of fees from dogs, when all persons who run dog shelters, regardless of their founder, and the existing relief for persons whose only source of income was old-age, widow's and widow's or disability's pensions, will be replaced by a relief for all persons over 65 years of age and the exemption of persons holding a ZTP licence. Only holders of the ZTP/P licence have been exempted so far. Furthermore, an anti-abuse clause is introduced for the fee for the use of public space and the fee for admission fees. A new condition for exemption from this fee will be the fact that the proceeds from the event were actually paid for charity purposes. The effectiveness of this law is proposed for 1 January 2020. Thank you for your attention.
