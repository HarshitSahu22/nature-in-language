It concerns liability for damage caused to pupils from both primary and kindergarten schools. [[Constant noise in the hall.]] The current regulation contained in the Labour Code contains only the adjustment of liability for damage to primary school pupils, not children in kindergartens. They are liable for damage under another legislation. The current situation can cause problems if one legal person operates both primary school and kindergarten. In such a case, the head of these schools would have to conclude two completely different insurance contracts, which is generally not desirable. This problem is not very well known. In addition, insurance companies have passed this different legal situation in recent years and the school directors have failed to draw attention to existing differences in legislation. It was during the non-negotiation of a special insurance contract for a kindergarten that the performance could have been reduced, or zero, by the insurance company when the child was injured at a kindergarten. For these reasons, we have decided to submit this amendment, which will unite the current legal situation. That's the point, the more that she was--
