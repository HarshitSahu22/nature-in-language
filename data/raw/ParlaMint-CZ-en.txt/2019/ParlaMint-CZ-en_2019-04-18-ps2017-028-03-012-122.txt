Ladies and gentlemen, the pension agreement submitted with Belarus, House press number 165, is a standard contract of this type. Its main objective is to define in which state a migrant worker will pay social security. It can be regarded as equivalent to a double taxation treaty in the social field. It is based on the legal principles of equal treatment of citizens, jurisdiction to insurance in the state of employment, the addition of periods of insurance completed in both states and the payment of benefits abroad. From a material point of view, it provides for pension benefits, old-age, invalidity and survivors' pensions. The Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic has already made a positive statement on the ratification of the Treaty. In accordance with the recommendation of the Foreign Committee, I would also like to request such consent from your House. Thank you for your attention.
