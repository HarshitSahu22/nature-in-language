Mr President, honourable Members, through the Prime Minister, the Protocol on Sustainable Agriculture and Rural Development to the Framework Convention on the Protection and Sustainable Development of the Carpathians has been submitted to you for your approval. This Protocol on Agriculture is one of the protocols agreed in the Framework Convention on the Protection and Sustainable Development of the Carpathians. In addition to the Protocol on Agriculture, the Czech Republic is a signatory of other protocols to the Carpathian Convention, namely the Protocol on the Protection and Sustainable Use of Biological and Landscape Diversity, the Protocol on Sustainable Tourism, the Protocol on Sustainable Forest Management and the Protocol on Sustainable Transport. Protocols are instruments for the implementation of the Carpathian Convention which enable the objectives of the Carpathian Convention to be pursued and cooperation to be developed in key areas of the Carpathian region. The Carpathian Convention itself was adopted at the 5th Ministerial Conference Environment for Europe in Kiev in May 2003 and entered into force on 4 January 2006. The Czech Republic is one of the founding parties to this Convention. In the Czech Republic, parts of the South Moravian, Zlínský, Olomoucký and Moravian-Silesian Region fall under the scope of the Carpathian Convention. When negotiating the protocol, the Czech Republic promoted not to duplicate activities with existing international contractual obligations. The aspects contained in the draft protocol are largely addressed in the framework of the common agricultural policy of the European Union and the relevant documents adopted at European Union level from the perspective of the Czech Republic. The main reasons why we propose ratification of the Protocol on Agriculture are solidarity with the Visegrad Four countries. In accordance with the provisions of the legislation in force, the implementation of the Agriculture Protocol will be ensured by the Ministry of Agriculture in cooperation with the Departmental Organisations and the Ministry of Environment, while the Ministry of Agriculture will play the role of coordinator. Cooperation with partners in the Carpathian region is also envisaged in the active implementation of the Protocol on Agriculture. On 6 November 2017, the Government of the Czech Republic gave its approval by its resolution of 800 and, under the authority of the President of the Republic, by an extraordinary and authorised ambassador to Ukraine, Radek Matula signed it for the Czech Republic on 16 March 2018, as Ukraine is both the depositary of the Carpathian Convention and the Protocol on Agriculture. The adoption of the Protocol will not mean the obligation for the Czech Republic to pay any mandatory contributions beyond the contributions already provided by the Parties to the Carpathian Convention to the proper budget of the Carpathian Convention, which means that no additional separate fund will be created only for the Protocol on Agriculture. The current contribution of the Czech Republic under the Carpathian Convention amounts to EUR 33 000 per year and is covered by the chapter of the State Budget 315 of the Ministry of the Environment. The Protocol on Agriculture has so far been signed by four Contracting Parties, in addition to the Czech Republic also Hungary, Romania and Poland. Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for your support for this proposal and thank you for your attention.
