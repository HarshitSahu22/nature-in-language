Ladies and gentlemen, members of the Government, Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic is presented material Activity and Management Report of the Office for Access to Transport Infrastructure 2017. Act No. 322/2016 Sb. was at 1. 4. 2017 Office for Access to Transport Infrastructure based in Prague established. ÚPDI is the central body of the state administration. Its main activity is the regulation of access to rail transport, regulation of access to services including dispute resolution and price control in the field of railway infrastructure. ÚPDI is also in charge of supervising the negotiation of airport charges and addressing the interoperability problems of the European electronic myth. The report submitted was drawn up on the basis of Article 9 of Law No. 322/2016 Sb. providing a basic overview of the activities of the Office for Access to Transport Infrastructure in 2017. The report was submitted to the Government of the Czech Republic and to the Chamber of Deputies. The Government took note of the report on 27 March 2018. In addition, the Annual Report of the CUPDI for 2017 was discussed at the Economic Committee on 5 June 2018. The opening words were given by Pavel Kodym, President of the Office. The report stated in detail how the Authority was created, sought premises and recruited employees in 2017. As I said before, the Economic Committee of the Chamber of Deputies became familiar with the report on 5 June 2018 and adopted the following resolution: Economic Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic after hearing the interpretation of the President of the Office for Access to Transport Infrastructure Pavel Kodym, reports by Mr Ondřej Polanský and after a general and detailed debate, first recommends: The Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to approve the Activity and Management Report of the Office for Access to Transport Infrastructure for 2017, the House Press 149, second, instructs the Committee's rapporteur to present at the meeting of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic a report on the outcome of the discussion of this report in the Economic Committee, which has happened, thirdly instructs the Chair of the Committee to submit this resolution to the President of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic.
