ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-28-ps2017-039-03-009-173.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, honourable Members, as mentioned at first reading, the Agreement on the Protection of Investment between the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and the Republic of Singapore on the other, were signed in the autumn of last year. The Senate has already expressed its consent to the ratification, and the Committee on Budgets and Foreign Affairs in the Chamber of Deputies has made a positive statement on the ratification of the agreement. The negotiated agreement should ensure a high level of protection of investments and does not interfere with state law on regulation in the public interest. It contains all the innovations of the European Union's approach to investment protection, including enforcement mechanisms, which are not included in our valid bilateral investment agreement with Singapore of 1995, which will be replaced by this agreement. The implementation of the Agreement will ensure that investors from the European Union and their investments in Singapore are granted fair and equal treatment and will not be discriminated against in comparison with Singaporean investments. At the same time, the Agreement protects investors from expropriation in the European Union and their investments in Singapore, except in the case of expropriation for public purposes, in accordance with proper procedures, on a non-discriminatory basis, while paying compensation equal to the fair market value of the expropriated investment. The Agreement contains a modern and reformed dispute resolution mechanism consisting of a permanent first-degree tribunal and a permanent appeal tribunal which should conduct dispute settlement procedures in a transparent and impartial manner. From the perspective of European Union law, the agreement constitutes an international treaty of the so-called mixed type, which is negotiated jointly by the European Union and its Member States, as it also covers areas which go beyond the competences of the Union. The Agreement shall enter into force after it has been approved under the relevant national procedures by all Member States, it shall be agreed by the European Parliament and the relevant Council decision shall be taken concluding the Agreement. The agreement is fully in line with the constitutional order of the Czech Republic and other parts of the rule of law, with commitments taken over under other applicable international treaties and with the generally recognised principles of international law, as well as obligations arising from membership of the European Union. Thank you for your attention.
