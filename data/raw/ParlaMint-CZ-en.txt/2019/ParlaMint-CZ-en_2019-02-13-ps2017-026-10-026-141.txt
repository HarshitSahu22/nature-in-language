ParlaMint-CZ_2019-02-13-ps2017-026-10-026-141.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, may I present to you the Agreement on the Establishment of the International Foundation of the EU - LAC, which the Government of the Czech Republic is submitting to Parliament of the Czech Republic for its approval of ratification. The idea of establishing the Foundation was established at the 6th summit of the EU countries and the Latin American and Caribbean Community in Madrid in May 2010, with the main objective of the Foundation's existence to contribute to strengthening partnership and mutual knowledge and understanding between the two regions. At first, the Foundation existed as an entity established in accordance with German civil law, so that it would then, at the request of the Member States, turn into an international organisation. Its founding agreement, which the Government is now submitting to Parliament, was opened for signature on 26 October 2016. [[15.00 hours]] The Agreement establishing an international foundation consists of a total of 30 articles setting out the objectives of the Foundation, general rules and guidelines as regards its activities, structure and functioning. The Agreement defines all members of the Foundation, which are Latin American and Caribbean States and the European Union and its Member States. The Foundation is also open to the participation of the Community (?) as such. The Agreement does not contain any obligations arising from membership of the Foundation. This is not linked to any one-off or regular financial commitment having an impact on the state budget. Deposits are voluntary from the text of the agreement, and the Foundation may also use resources obtained through external financing from public and private institutions. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Czech Republic is not planning to contribute financially to the operation of the Foundation at this time. The activities of the Foundation consist in the implementation of projects aimed at deepening the links between the two regions, i.e. the European Union and Latin America and the Caribbean. Primarily, it helps to establish contacts between organisations from the business, cultural and social fields. Supports contacts between civil society, e.g. in the field of higher education or youth. It creates a database of contacts between universities, scientific centres and small and medium-sized enterprises. The Foundation also supports research into relations between the European Union and the Community of Latin American and Caribbean States, including financial support for selected research projects. Following the approval of the College of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, the agreement was properly passed through inter-ministerial commentary proceedings and was approved by the Government of the Czech Republic, by its resolution number 454 of 10 July 2018. In accordance with this resolution, the agreement was subsequently signed in Brussels on 7 November 2018 by the Ambassador and Permanent Representative of the Czech Republic to the EU, Jakub Dürr. Mr President, honourable Member, I believe that on the basis of these facts you will support the development of the Czech Republic's links with such a promising region as Latin America, and you will agree to ratify the agreement in question. Thank you.
