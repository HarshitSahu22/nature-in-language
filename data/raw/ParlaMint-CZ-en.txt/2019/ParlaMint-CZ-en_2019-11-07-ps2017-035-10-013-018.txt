ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-07-ps2017-035-10-013-018.u2	Hello. Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, our proposal for a law on the property of the Czech Republic, House Press 69, introduces the so-called care of a proper economy. Under the current legal situation, the State is not allowed to recover some of the damage that has been caused repeatedly to its assets. In particular, it is damage to the State's assets which are incurred by individual organisational units of the State, or the State as a whole, by paying the fine for the administrative offence. Typically it is, for example, the payment of a fine for a breach of the Public Procurement Act according to the decision of the Office for the Protection of Competition, but it may also be other administrative offences, where one organizational component of the State is granted to another organizational component within the State. This is always the case where the State, or the organisational component of the State, breaks the law through one of its employees and pays the State, or one organizational component of the State, for such negotiations, a fine which immediately becomes the income of the State, or another organizational component of the State. The Committee on Budgets was adopted at its 22nd meeting on 3. 4. 2019 an amendment that addresses this issue comprehensively, the so-called fiction of a legal person. We support this comprehensive proposal as a SPD. And I would just like to remind you that this comprehensive proposal already takes into account Mr Michálek's amendment and depends on whether it is sufficient for him to do so. So that would be all for the introduction. Thank you.
