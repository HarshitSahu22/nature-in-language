ParlaMint-CZ_2019-11-28-ps2017-039-03-005-170.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic on the updating of the border documentary is a purely technical document intended to ensure the amendment of Annex No. 1 The Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic on common national borders in order to correspond to the current state of the marking of national borders in the field. There is no change in the course of national borders under the contract. In accordance with the Treaty on Common National Borders, national borders are regularly tested. The result of the examination is so-called border documents in which changes are recorded compared to the valid border documentary work. These are, for example, the new location of the border signs, the replacement of the direct indication of the course of the border by indirect or vice versa, and so on. These border documents are subsequently nationally approved on both sides. On the Czech side, border documents were approved by the Minister of Interior on 7 April 2017. The approved border documents become part of the border documentary work and therefore amend Annex No. 1 a treaty on common national borders under a special international treaty which is currently being presented by the Treaty. Thank you for your attention.
