ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-016-202.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to briefly mention the government proposal by which Parliament shall submit to express its agreement to ratify the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Argentine Republic on extradition signed in Prague on 25 November last year. I must say that there are currently no bilateral agreements in force between the Czech Republic and the Argentine Republic in the field of international judicial cooperation in criminal matters, and neither is the Argentine Republic a Contracting Party to the Council of Europe Conventions concerning the extradition of persons and other forms of international judicial cooperation. Judicial cooperation shall take place on a non-contractual basis. [[12.30 hours]] The aim of the extradition agreement between the Czech Republic and the Argentine Republic is to increase efficiency, increase speed and simplify extradition procedures, which will contribute to more effective enforcement of law and more effective criminal sanctions. The agreement is in line with the foreign political interests of the Czech Republic and will help extend the possibilities of international judicial cooperation in criminal matters between the Contracting Parties. [[The noise in the hall lasts.]] As regards the costs associated with the implementation of the contract, they will be covered by the budget chapters of the ministries involved.
