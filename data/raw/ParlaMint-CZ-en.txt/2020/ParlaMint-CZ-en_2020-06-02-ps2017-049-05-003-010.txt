ParlaMint-CZ_2020-06-02-ps2017-049-05-003-010.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me put forward a proposal for a law on international cooperation in the resolution of tax disputes in the European Union, which I presented at first reading. The main objective of this bill is to provide another procedural instrument for taxpayers, to possibly resolve disputes in the interpretation and implementation of double taxation agreements between the competent authorities of the EU Member States in the form of arbitration procedures, as enshrined in the EU Directive. The bill transposes the European directive and does not depart from it or go beyond it in a fundamental way. There has been a delay in preparing the final proposal, but it is a matter which must be addressed as uniformly as possible in all Member States, so priority has been given to negotiations on how the single procedure under the directive is to be applied and to await clarification of the position of the other Member States. The transposition period expired on 30 June 2019 and therefore the law is proposed to be effective on the 15th day of the first calendar month following the declaration of the law. Thank you for your attention.
