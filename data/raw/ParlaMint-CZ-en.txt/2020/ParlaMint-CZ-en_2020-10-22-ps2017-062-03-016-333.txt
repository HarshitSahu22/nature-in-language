ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-22-ps2017-062-03-016-333.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, this material is now being presented to give its consent to the ratification of the agreement signed in Luxembourg by all EU Member States on 25 June 2019. The agreement was signed by the Minister for Regional Development Klára Dostálová for the Czech Republic and was signed in Hanoi for the EU and Vietnam on 30 June 2019. The Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic has already expressed its approval for ratification by its resolution of 30 January this year. The Foreign Committee of the Chamber of Deputies agreed to ratify the agreement on 3 April this year, and now the ratification of the agreement is presented for approval of the plenary of the Chamber of Deputies. The negotiated agreement brings legal certainty and predictability that should help attract and sustain investments supporting their economy in the EU and Vietnam. As one of the largest foreign investors in the country, the EU will benefit from the better investment climate that will be created by implementing the agreement. The agreement ensures a high level of protection of investment, but it does not interfere with EU law, its Member States and Vietnam to regulate and pursue legitimate public policy objectives such as the protection of public health, safety and the environment. The Agreement contains a modern and reformed dispute resolution mechanism consisting of a permanent first-degree tribunal and a permanent appeal tribunal which will conduct dispute settlement proceedings in a transparent and impartial manner. As from its entry into force, the Agreement will fully replace existing bilateral agreements on the promotion and protection of investments concluded between Vietnam and EU Member States. This will also put an end to the Czech-Vietnamese investment agreement of 1997, including provisions on the application of the withdrawal period. In practice, this means that investments existing on the date of expiry of this investment agreement will no longer be subject to protection from it for a further period of ten years. From the perspective of Czech constitutional law, this is a Treaty of the presidential category. The Agreement shall enter into force after it has been approved under the relevant national processes by all Member States. The European Parliament has given its consent to the agreement on 12 February this year. Thank you for your attention.
