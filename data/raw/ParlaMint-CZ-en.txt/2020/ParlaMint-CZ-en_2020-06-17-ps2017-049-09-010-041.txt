[[18.20 hours]] The second amendment concerns the municipality as a taxable person. The scope of the municipalities as a public authority in the field of waste management is a very complex activity of an economic nature. The municipality's waste management system may be operated separately by the municipality, through a waste company which may be owned by municipalities or associations of municipalities, or through a private waste company where municipalities purchase a service to ensure the operation of the waste management system, including sorted collection. Similar services and activities such as municipalities may also be provided by authorised persons for at least part of the waste. In addition, the municipality may, under the waste management system for citizens, either levy a municipal waste charge on the basis of a generally binding decree issued in the separate jurisdiction of the municipality, a local waste management fee under a special law, or a compensation for the management of waste by natural persons under a contract. In some of the above cases, the VAT scheme for municipalities is ambiguous and leads to various application of VAT in practice. In addition, all entities involved in waste management other than municipalities are considered taxable persons for VAT purposes. This leads to significant distortions of competition in the waste management market. Classification of collection, transport, sorting or supply of municipal waste including related services in the Annex number Even the activities in which municipalities are taxable persons unite the tax regime for all operators on the market. I have already submitted the same proposal on the Guarantee Committee and unfortunately I have received a negative opinion from the Ministry of Environment, stating that it is a wild rider, so-called. unacceptable legislative sticker. I am not a constitutional lawyer, but, as far as I understand it, the Constitutional Court has devoted itself to its findings and has set criteria by which such a wild rider is recognized. The amendment must relate to the subject matter of the Act under consideration, its fundamental purpose must have a close relationship with the fundamental purpose of the bill under consideration. The adaptation of a specific subject must not be amended by provisions of a general nature; a general subject may be amended by a specific proposal and an amendment containing a permanent amendment to a draft law foretelling a transitional change in law cannot be regarded as having a narrow relationship. If I evaluate these criteria, I believe that this is not a wild rider in my case, because the subject matter of the law is to be the subject of my proposal. The government itself considers the amendment to be an effort to achieve recycling commitments, and this is what my proposal allows. That is why I am re-submitting this amendment, which is being conducted in the system, as I have already mentioned, under number 5752, and I will sign up to it in a detailed debate. Thank you.
