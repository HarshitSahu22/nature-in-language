ParlaMint-CZ_2020-03-06-ps2017-041-04-001-175.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, very briefly. On the fourth of March 2020, you were presented with the bill before second reading. In view of his quick discussion, which took place without comment, I would like to present it to you by the third reading. The main objectives of the draft law are the introduction of market value as an alternative in cases where the normal price cannot be determined; anchoring the concept of rapidly growing trees and their method of valuation prepared in cooperation with the Ministry of Agriculture, this is a reaction to modern trends in tree growing into a chipping plant, which is a renewable fuel source; a new approach to the valuation of the right of building and material burdens and the related valuation of immovable property burdened by material burdens and similar rights. The proposed bill is of a general nature. It is therefore usable for a wide range of entities and for different valuation purposes. It is supplemented by a proposal for the provisions in question in other related laws. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed on 1 January 2021. Thank you for your attention.
