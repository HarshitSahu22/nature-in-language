ParlaMint-CZ_2020-09-16-ps2017-058-02-002-335.u18	So, once again, hello, Mr President, Madame Members. Allow me to present the Government's draft amendment to Act No. 254/2001 Coll., on Waters, and other related laws. This material, this proposal was drafted jointly by the Ministry of Agriculture and the Ministry of the Environment. It is based on the concept of protection against drought consequences for the territory of the Czech Republic approved by Government Resolution 24 July 2017 No. 528. As far as changes are concerned, it introduces new concepts of drought and water shortages. An obligation shall be introduced in the processing of the dry management plan. Priority shall be given to the ways in which water is used for the purposes of drawing up dry plans and to the authorities for the management of droughts and water shortages, etc. Their competencies are being corrected. Another major change in the draft water law is, for example, the introduction of an obligation to measure the amount of surface and groundwater collected. Furthermore, the draft law contains an amendment to the Act on the jurisdiction of the administration of state tangible reserves, which regulates the conditions for the provision and use of state tangible reserves for the resolution of emergencies. The draft law also includes an amendment to the Crisis Management Act, which relates to the need for a clear division of competences of the authorities for drought and crisis authorities in cases where a state of emergency occurs. The proposal also contains an amendment to the Waterwater and Sewer Act, which aims in particular to enable water operators to respond by limiting the supply of drinking water to declare a water shortage in accordance with the Water Act. This Government proposal was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies at its 34th meeting on 15 April and recommended that the Chamber of Deputies approve the Government's bill as amended by the amendments adopted. The draft law was also discussed in the meantime by the Committee on the Environment and the Economic Committee. As part of the second reading, which took place on 18 June, a number of amendments were tabled which fall into different areas not only of water law. The Agricultural Committee, as a guarantee, discussed this government bill at the 37th meeting of 24 June and adopted a resolution which further recommends: The Chamber of Deputies shall vote in the third reading on the draft bill in the proposed order. Thank you.

ParlaMint-CZ_2020-09-16-ps2017-058-02-002-335.u20	Thank you for the floor. Ladies and gentlemen, the purpose of this amendment to the Water Act, which we are discussing in the third reading, is to protect surface and groundwater, to lay down conditions for the economical use of water resources and to improve the quality of surface and groundwater. The main principle of this amendment is the preparation of plans for drought and water shortages. The state should be more aware of how water is managed in our country. The law envisages drawing up both drought and crisis dry plans, and it also includes greater control of water management. According to the officials, the amendments should relate mainly to undertakings. The law also defines what is dry and what is a state of water shortage. Drying will be understood to be a hydrological drought due to a lack of precipitation and is manifested by a drop in flow in water flows and a decrease in groundwater levels. The state of water shortage will be understood as a temporary state with a possible impact on basic human needs, economic activity and the environment. The Czech Republic has been suffering from an unprecedented wave of dry years since 2015, even though it has been raining quite well this year. Water shortages are signed not only in agriculture, but more and more often in the common needs of the public. Therefore, in cases of drought and the consequent state of water shortages, the situation needs to be dealt with by more extreme means than the existing water law provides. The amendment to the Water Act, which is intended to allow stricter measures to be taken against drought, will support our SPD movement. Thank you.
