ParlaMint-CZ_2020-03-06-ps2017-041-04-002-014.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Ministry of Transport is submitting a bill amending Act No. 61/2000 Coll., on Maritime Navigation, as amended. As I have already had the opportunity to present here at first reading, the main objective of this bill is to transpose the Council Directive of the European Union of 23 January 2018. The aim is to improve and optimize the existing maritime protection system in the event of their abandonment. There is an addition to the existing legal obligation of the maritime operator to have insurance for the coverage of the outstanding salary and, where appropriate, other financial performance resulting from the basic employment relationship entered into throughout its operation. Following this regulation, it is in particular the designation of the Maritime Authority, which is the Ministry of Transport under the Maritime Navigation Act. In addition to the implementation of the above-mentioned Union legislation, the proposed draft law contains, to a large extent to a limited extent, changes to unifyingly irrelevant, requested by the needs of application practice. Let me give you an example: it is proposed for example. explicitly exclude the possibility of operating nuclear-powered ships under the national flag of the Czech Republic, possibly specifying the requirement for the establishment of a maritime vessel operator and expressly anchoring this requirement as a condition for the registration of a maritime vessel in the Maritime Register of the Czech Republic, etc. No amendments have yet been tabled to the bill, so please, if it is possible to continue reading this bill and thank you for your attention.
