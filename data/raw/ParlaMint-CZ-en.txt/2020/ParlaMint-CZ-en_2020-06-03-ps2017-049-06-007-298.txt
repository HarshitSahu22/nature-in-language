Proposal for the election of members of the Council of the State Cinematography Fund In the election of the members of the Council of the State Cinema Fund we occupied two seats, the House managed to occupy one seat and one will therefore remain vacant. This election was attended by 182 Members and Members of Parliament, so the quorum required for the election was 92 and Mr Čestmír Kopeckýý received 116 votes, that is the one who was elected. Other unsuccessful candidates - Richard Němec 58, Natasha Slavíková 81 and Jiří Voráč 49. This was a two-way choice for three places, and we took one place a week ago, so today it's the other. And once again, one place remains vacant, a new proposal from the House will have to be sent back to the Minister of Culture.
