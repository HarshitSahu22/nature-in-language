The new requirements of European law largely change the overall context of the actual owner's institute and its registration and increase its importance. It is crucial that the concept of a beneficial owner be defined in the law precisely in order to reflect the requirements of European law. The record of real owners is one of the tools for combating money laundering and terrorist financing. In particular, the subject matter of the new law will be the modification of the detailed definition of the beneficial owner, the method of access to the data in the register, the process of recording data on the beneficial owner, the automatic transmission of certain data from the public register, the solution of incorrect or missing data in the register, the penalties for infringements of the obligations relating to the registration of beneficial owners. Further requirements and adjustments relating to the proposed proposal are the subject of a bill amending Act No. 253/2008 Coll., on certain measures against the legalization of proceeds from crime and the financing of terrorism, the promoter of which is the Ministry of Finance.
