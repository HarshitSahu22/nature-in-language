ParlaMint-CZ_2020-03-11-ps2017-041-06-004-044.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce the Government's proposal for an amendment to Act No. 139/2002 Coll., on land adjustments and land offices and amending Act No. 229/1991 Coll., on the modification of property relations in land and other agricultural assets. The aim of the proposed bill is to contribute to an effective solution to the consequences of climate change and to the restoration of ecological stability of the landscape through land treatment. For this reason, the proposal for an amendment to the Land Adjustment Act introduces the possibility of implementing land adjustments, if necessary, to address water management measures in more downstream cadastral territories, and thus better respond to drought-induced changes. Furthermore, it specifies the content of complex and simple land adjustments, with the possibility that simple land adjustments can be made newly without exchange or transfer of property rights. Furthermore, it unites the adjustment of the price for the purchase of land for land treatment with the amendment of the Act on the State Land Office, at the usual price. It lays down the conditions for the transfer of ownership to the property of the municipality or other persons, as appropriate, in order to ensure the supervision of the State of the further management of the land. It shall make partial changes to the process in the land adjustment procedure aimed at making them more effective. The proposal also includes an amendment to the Cadastral Act, which only makes partial adjustments directly following the amendment to the Land Treatment Act, which also aims to increase the effectiveness of land treatment proceedings. Thank you for your attention.
