The proposal of the Liberec Region's representative to issue a law amending Act No. 243/2000 Coll., on budgetary determination of taxes, as amended / House print 753/ - first reading We received the Government's position as House Press 753/1. The submitted proposal under the authority of the Liberec Region's representative is justified by the Governor Martin Půta. Please take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-06-02-ps2017-049-05-008-207.u2	Good evening, ladies and gentlemen, Madam Secretary, Ministers, honourable Members, Members. I'll be brief. I think my two previous speakers have already described it quite in detail. I think the main problem we're talking about here is the long-term non-financing of road repair II. and III. classes from the state budget. When the regions took this property from the state in 2003, political promises were made that there would be some sort of system of financing for road repairs II. and III. classes. I note that these are less than 90 percent of all roads, and yet no amount is used to finance repairs because they all end up in the state budget. Of course, I see the government's negative position, I understand, I think that every finance minister, every finance minister, has to see it the same way. On the other hand, what has been going on in the last five years, what the Governor of Netolický has been talking about, is that every year the Regional Association negotiates with the government some share of the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure. Usually the meeting ends sometime in the summer months. From the perspective of the regions of repair and modernisation, it is difficult to plan. And I think that this proposal would allow the regions to know what funds they will have in their budgets every year to repair roads II. and III. classes designated, and the state would not have to look for any funds in its reserves every year during the year. The second problem that we have tried to solve in this proposal of the Liberec Region representative is that since 2000 the same coefficients have been set for the budgetary determination of individual regions. In the meantime, of course, there has been a development, for example, in the population, and I will be talking about Central Bohemia, which has 250,000 inhabitants more than they had in 2000. In the Association of Regions of the Czech Republic, the problem has long been dealt with by various working groups. It must be said that no compromise proposal, on which all the regions would agree, has logically occurred. The proposal put forward before the first reading tries to address this in some way. And I think you have a great opportunity to use him, by releasing him into the next round of negotiations, in some way to deal with the situation that has arisen in the last week. I don't want to get too deep in here, but it has to be said that funding for self-government is not entirely ideal after the Corona crisis. It's not just about investment. I think that a number of regions, a whole number of municipalities will have a big problem in the coming months with financing their normal operation. So, ladies and gentlemen, I politely ask you to consider not allowing this proposal for second reading. Thank you for your attention.
