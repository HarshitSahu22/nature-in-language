ParlaMint-CZ_2020-04-08-ps2017-044-02-010-011.u2	Mr President, colleagues, I will try to be very brief. I'd like to introduce you to the draft amendment to the Real Estate Intermediation Act. In fact, the profession of real estate brokers has come into such a double complex situation. Of course, the movement of persons is limited, etc. On the other hand, it is a very new law, which actually took effect in March of this year and actually under this law it is an obligation to switch from a free trade to a trade bound by it and with it are of course related things, that is to prove qualifications. This can be demonstrated by real estate intermediaries either by vocational higher education or by three years of practice and high school education with a high degree or by a certificate of qualification. Many real estate brokers want to take advantage of the third option, that is to say a certificate on the composition of a professional qualification test, but unfortunately it is not possible to actually provide them with education during the time of the coronavirus crisis. Therefore, the regulation of the law is very simple: in Section 25 on real estate mediation, the period for proving compliance with the conditions will be extended. I would like to thank the Economic Committee, Mr Munzar, very much, because we have agreed that this is a really comfortable time, that we are extending the time and, of course, we are still allowing free trade for six months, so that no one is really limited. I would therefore like to ask for your support as amended by the amendment tabled by Mr Munzar, who approved the Economic Committee. Thank you.
