Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce the Government's amendment to Act No. 139/2002 Coll., on land adjustments and land offices and on amendment of Act No. 229/1991 Coll., on the modification of property relations to land and other agricultural property. The aim of the proposed bill is to contribute to an effective solution to the consequences of climate change and to the restoration of ecological stability of the landscape through land treatment. For this reason, the proposal for an amendment to the Land Regulations Act introduces the possibility of implementing land adjustments, if necessary, to address water management measures even in more downstream cadastral territories. Furthermore, it specifies the content of complex and simple land adjustments, unifies the price adjustments for the purchase of land for land adjustments with the modification of the Act on the State Land Office, at the usual price. It lays down the conditions for the transfer of ownership to the property of the municipality or other persons, as appropriate, in order to ensure the supervision of the State of the further management of the land. It shall make partial changes to the process in the land adjustment procedure aimed at making them more effective. Part of this proposal is also an amendment to the cadastral law, which only makes partial adjustments directly to the amendment to the Land Treatment Act, which also aims to increase the effectiveness of land treatment. The Government's bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of Parliament at its 35th meeting on 13 May 2020 and recommended by its resolution to approve the Government's bill as amended by the amendments adopted. The amendments adopted are in line with the Ministry's concept and will contribute to improving the government's bill, and we therefore agree and support them. Thank you.
