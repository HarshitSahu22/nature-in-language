ParlaMint-CZ_2020-01-29-ps2017-040-06-001-033.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the bill is presented as a transposition amendment to national legislation, the aim of the implementing directive being to ensure that the time limit for calculating the amount of oil stocks is changed from 31 March to 30 June each year, and to change the method for calculating the oil equivalent of imports of petroleum products. The proposed regulation is necessary to ensure proper implementation of the implementing directive into the legal order of the Czech Republic. The preparation of the amendment was carried out in cooperation with the Ministry of Industry and Trade, which is in this case the promoter of the amendment of the Act representing the management of material reserves. The proposal went through a full interdepartmental reminder procedure and was submitted to the government without conflict.
