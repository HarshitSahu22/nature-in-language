ParlaMint-CZ_2020-01-21-ps2017-040-01-011-012.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the draft law amending Act No. 280/2009 Coll., Tax Code, as amended, and related other laws. The proposed law is actually a legislative basis for the implementation of the MY Tax project, or Moder and Jednochné daňa, whose implementation is, together with the support of simplification and electronisation of the tax system and general public administration, one of the points of the government's programming declaration. Thus, the law's gravity is a large amendment to the tax code, which is a general tax regulation, which deals with four major topics. The first is the promotion of electronicisation, i.e. the simplification of administration in communication with the tax administrator, to positively motivate the use of electronic means, and therefore sets out legislative prerequisites for modernising the portal Financial and Customs Administration, in particular in the new concept of tax information box, which will enable tax entities, among other things. obtain more information via the Internet and allow for pre-fulfilment of electronic submissions, in particular tax returns. In the field of electronization the design brings e.g. the extension of the deadline for submitting a tax return on income taxes by one month in the case of submission electronically, the shorter time limit for returning the overpayment in the case of electronically submitted applications, the waiver of the obligation to submit forms electronically for those who voluntarily set up their data boxes, or the reduction of the penalty for non-compliance with the electronic form of submission by half. The second issue is the simplification of control procedures - we have already talked about controls here - where a correspondence form of communication under tax control will also be admitted, which will give rise to greater flexibility, and it will always depend on the tax entity, what his will will will be, whether he wants to shorten this control. The third issue is the revision of the penalty system, which should include: to mitigate the effects of certain sanctions, in particular interest, to eliminate non-systemic exemptions in this area, and thus, in particular, to reduce interest to the level of standard interest on late payment applied in the private sector. There will therefore be a decrease in interest penalties from 16 to 10 per cent per year. The fourth key issue is the introduction by both the professional public and the caselaw of the long-term possibility of paying part of the excess deductions claimed on value added tax in the event of excessive deductions being held as a result of an ongoing control procedure. This change will reduce the negative financial impact of this detention, but will also require an extension of 15 days for the tax administrator's search activity, which will target the control activity, continue the trend of unnecessary checks and detention of funds and greater scope for checking and informal communication. From a number of other partial amendments contained in the draft law, it is possible to indicate, in particular, the extension of the circle of less invasive ways in which reinsurance orders are executed or to allow natural persons to request a change in the tax identification number so that it does not contain a birth number. Thank you for your attention.

Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to support the amendment which I propose to the press, 580, i.e. to the Government Bill amending Act No. 280/2009 Sb. The Government's draft law contains the abolition of the tolerance of five working days, for which there is no penalty for the late claim of tax under Section 250 of the Tax Code in the event of late submission of the tax claim. However, such cancellation shall be replaced by a more targeted solution. Instead of the current flat model, where all tax entities are allowed five working days late when making a tax claim, the government's bill introduces an escalated tolerance according to the amount of hardened tax, in the form of an increase in the minimum limit for the existence of a fine on late submission of a tax claim from 200 to 500 Czech crowns. The consequence of the graduation is therefore the fact that the smaller the tax, the longer the delay in filing the tax claim can be. This will not result in a fine being imposed where the obligation to submit a tax claim is met with a delay and the basis for calculating the fine is relatively low. The amendment I propose respects the government's proposed design, but at the same time it considers it appropriate to extend the range of tax entities to which the solution will be positively targeted, respectively. further strengthen the positive effect of government-designed structures on small and medium-sized entities. It is therefore proposed to increase the minimum limit of the fine from CZK 500 to CZK 1,000. The result will be both an extension of tolerance, twice the state proposed by the government, and a higher number of subjects for which the change will be positive compared to the current state. Moreover, an increase to this value will mean unification with the minimum limit proposed by the government for the creation of interest on late payments, which also amounts to CZK 1,000, which, in my view, will contribute to greater clarity and clarity. I reiterate that this amendment is a proposal to support small and medium-sized enterprises. I hope to receive your support for this amendment, which I am now asking for.
