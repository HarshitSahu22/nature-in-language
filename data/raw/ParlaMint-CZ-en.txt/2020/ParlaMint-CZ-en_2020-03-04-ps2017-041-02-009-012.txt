ParlaMint-CZ_2020-03-04-ps2017-041-02-009-012.u2	Once again, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen. Allow me to present the Government's proposal for an amendment to Act No. 219/2003 Coll., on the circulation of seeds and seeds. The main aim of the proposed adaptation is to re-regulate the area of circulation of hop propagating material. Requirements are laid down for hop propagating material and obligations to be fulfilled by the supplier of such propagating material. Further, the proposals for amendments are based on the experience of the Agricultural Control and Testing Institute with this ever-expanding method of growing hops, as the legislation in force is still set up for an increasingly less-used system of multiplication from hop hops. The current legislation has been revised and a number of provisions have been clarified to reflect current practice. As an accompanying amendment, it is also proposed to amend the Act on the Protection of Plant Variety Rights, where legislative technical adjustments are made, in particular, resulting from the knowledge associated with the application of the Act. In the Act on Administrative Fees, it is proposed to withdraw from item 90 the fee for the grant of a licence to the register, as this registration is simultaneously cancelled in the Act on the protection of rights to varieties. It is also proposed to delete item 91 which provides for a fee for the acceptance of the application for revocation of rights because it is not used and thus brings both items into line with the wording of the Act in force on the protection of plant varieties rights. This Government Bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies at its 32nd meeting on 19 February 2002 and by its resolution it recommended that the Chamber of Deputies approve the Government Bill as amended by the amendments adopted. [[correct 2020]] These adopted amendments are in line with the concept of the petitioner, thereby contributing to the improvement of this government bill, and I therefore agree with them. Thank you.

ParlaMint-CZ_2020-03-04-ps2017-041-02-009-012.u6	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Dear colleagues, dear colleagues, Minister, I, together with the Czech Moravian Breeding and Seed Association and the Central Inspection and Testing Institute, have allowed the farm to prepare a simple amendment aimed at aligning the terminology of the law on seed circulation and planting with the European Union Directives, in particular Council Directive 66/401/EEC on the marketing of fodder plant seed. The purpose of the use of the mixture for agricultural or non-agricultural production does not exactly describe its practical use. From the point of view of European Union legislation and agricultural practice, this is mainly about whether mixtures are used as fodder plants or are used in agricultural production in a different way, in the context of greening, intermediate crops or other environmental measures. It is necessary to say here that Czech breeding companies have developed new species in an effort to respond to social demand and market new species that significantly contribute to the diversification of grown species and are suitable intermediate crops, components into mixtures and are not listed in the species list. The proposed adjustment thus removes the current confusion in terms of agricultural and non-agricultural mixtures and allows the portfolio of the mixtures offered to be expanded. Even species not included in the species list are clearly beneficial in mixtures for agricultural use. This change has no negative impact on the already written grant titles that are related to the issue. In a detailed debate, I will sign up for this. Thank you for your attention.
