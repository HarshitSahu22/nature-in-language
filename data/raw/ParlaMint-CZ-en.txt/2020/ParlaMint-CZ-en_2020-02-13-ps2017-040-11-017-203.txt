ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-017-203.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present the International Convention on the Measurement of Ships TONNAGE, 1969, which is an international convention adopted by the IMO International Maritime Organisation and is one of the technical conventions of the International Maritime Organisation, which sets out the requirements for the accounting of the marine vessel's internal space, the so-called TONNAGE. TONNAGE is the basic figure for calculating registration and port charges. The uniform determination of TONNAGE ensures equal access for vessels in the field of charges. The TONNAGE Convention was signed in London on 23 June 1969. After the division of Czechoslovakia, the Czech Republic acceded to the Convention of Suctions. The Convention for the Czech Republic therefore entered into force on 1 January 1993. Upon completion of the process of proper implementation pursuant to Article 10 of the Constitution of the Czech Republic, the Convention shall be submitted to the Parliament of the Czech Republic for approval, including partial amendments to the Annex adopted since its establishment. The text TONNAGE is in line with the constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, including obligations arising from the membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union and does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not affect the expenditure of the state budget. Thank you for your attention.
