Government bill amending Act No. 156/1998 Coll., on fertilisers, soil aids, auxiliary plant preparations and substrates and on agrochemical testing of agricultural soils (Fertilizer Act), as amended, and Act No. 90/2016 Coll., on the assessment of conformity of specified products when making them available on the market, as amended /German press 755/ - first reading Minister of Agriculture, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-03-11-ps2017-041-06-005-045.u2	Thank you. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce a government bill amending Act No. 156/1998 Coll. The draft law implements a partial adaptation of the Czech rule of law to Regulation 2019/1009 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down rules for the supply of EU fertilizer products to the market. This Regulation amends Article 3, the number of the 1107/2009, which defines the term plant biostimulant. The draft law also contains an accompanying amendment to the Act on Conformity Assessment of specified products when making them available on the market. In view of the effectiveness of the other provisions of this Regulation, the full adaptation of the Fertilizers Regulation will be carried out by another amendment to the Fertilizers Act planned in two years. According to the Act on Fertilizers in force, records are carried out on the quantity, type and time of application of fertilisers, soil aids, plant biostimulants, etc. The obligation to keep records and the quantities of the main and by-product harvested, with the exception of permanent grassland, is now proposed. Agricultural entrepreneurs operating on an area of more than 200 hectares according to the farm land use register, according to user relations, will be required to keep records in electronic form according to the law. This provision shall have suspensive effect from 1 January 2022. On the basis of the knowledge of application practice, the new mandatory annex to the application for the registration of fertilisers is the operating order of the waste recovery facility, pursuant to Act No. 185/2001 Coll., on Waste. Thank you for your attention.
