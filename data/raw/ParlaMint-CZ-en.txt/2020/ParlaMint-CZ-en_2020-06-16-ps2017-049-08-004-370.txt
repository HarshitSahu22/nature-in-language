ParlaMint-CZ_2020-06-16-ps2017-049-08-004-370.u2	Good afternoon. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the current power international policy uses a number of open and hidden activities of the entire spectrum of instruments and operations known as hybrid threats to achieve its objectives. These conflicts take place in the area of diplomatic, information, economic, financial and legislative. Risks come in the form of acts of foreign power, cyber attacks, energy and raw material threats, industrial security, terrorism and illegal migration. The cores of this action are targeted disinformation aimed at influencing, disorientation and in targeted segments of society to influence, change, create opinions, attitudes, and even activate targeted groups of people. Disinformation is not a lie. It is enough to work selectively with information, to set up a public agenda, to work creatively with distribution channels, to shift contexts and the like, to significantly influence public opinion in the consequences of these activities, to avoid democratic debate, democratic processes and, last but not least, to jeopardise the security and sovereignty of the Czech Republic. Our security system reflects these threats, however, with turbulent technological development they accelerate the ways of modification of known ways so far and this is happening much faster than any state's ability to respond adequately to this process. A standing committee on hybrid threats may be political parliamentary support which, if necessary, can immediately adopt and initiate legislative decisions so as to reduce the risk of delays and reactive times to the least possible extent. In a detailed debate, I will therefore propose a resolution on the establishment of a permanent committee on hybrid threats with a parity representation of the political parties elected to the Chamber of Deputies. Thank you.
