ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-22-ps2017-062-03-014-331.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, this time from the interior department, I present to you the material presented as a material which is a proposal for ratification. Readmission agreements with Mongolia. The contractual documents of this type are one of the important instruments for combating illegal migration. The Agreement contains a basic obligation of the Contracting Parties to take back a citizen who does not fulfil or no longer fulfils the applicable conditions for entry or residence in the territory of the other State. It also contains the main principles relating to the procedure for implementing this commitment, understand the submission of applications, the deadlines for their processing, the deadlines for the implementation of the takeover, the provisions relating to the protection of personal data, etc. The purpose of the agreement is to speed up the transfer of illegally staying persons, to shorten their stay in reinsurance facilities, while reducing the costs associated with the detention and subsequent transfer of such persons. At the explicit request of the Mongolian party, the agreement is negotiated on the outside as an intergovernmental treaty, but it requires national approval, namely by Parliament of the Czech Republic, and subsequently ratification by the President of the Republic, as it is an international treaty governing the rights and obligations of persons and an anchoring regulation reserved by the law. [[12.20 hours]] A protocol was agreed to implement the agreement, which was signed at the same time as the agreement, on 20 May last year in Prague. The Protocol is a contractual document of the government category and is therefore attached to the material for information. Its validity shall be subject to the validity of the Agreement. Thank you for your support and your attention.
