The proposal is very simple. It relates to the amendment of the Labour Code, where simple adaptations are proposed, in the paragraph of the valid version of the Labour Code in Section 213 para. 1 of Law No. 262/2006 Sb. replaced the words "four weeks " with the words "five weeks. " The proposal concerns the basic terms of the calendar leave. I would just briefly like to remind myself that it is clear that current legislation, as stated in our legal order as well as in the Labour Code, harms employers' employees operating business and non-business activities in a way compared to employees employed under § 109 para. 3 The Labour Code and the length of leave shall be reduced by one week. The proposed proposal removes the difference in the entitlement length of the basic period of leave when it sets out the basic period of leave for at least five weeks. At the same time, the possibility of deferring leave by the employer, or individual or collective agreement, or by internal regulation or decision of the employer is maintained. The proposal referred to above does not affect the length of the prescribed leave of teaching staff, academic staff and higher education staff. Here I would just like to point out that it compares the current asymmetry, where we have several categories of employees in the Czech Republic, workers who are entitled to different lengths of the basic period of the calendar leave, and we consider this change to be essential, especially at a time when it is constantly said that some options should be reduced for example by working time.
