ParlaMint-CZ_2020-12-10-ps2017-075-01-006-006.u4	Mr President, Ministers, ladies and gentlemen, I would briefly like to state the reasons for the main principles of the proposed legislation. The proposed amendment primarily responds to the Constitutional Court's decision of 11. 2. 2020. The main proposed amendment is to establish an explicit requirement for prior requests for access to the central register of notifications for all categories of public officials, i.e. not only for public officials pursuant to § 2 para. 2 of the Act on Conflict of Interest, as is the case under current legislation. According to the Constitutional Court, access to the information from the notification should be conditional on requests for access to the central register for all public officials. However, it is clear from the Constitutional Court's argument that this is unconstitutional in Section 13 (1). 3, which is presented by an amendment and modified to correspond with the law. More or less, you will be able to access the register by means of an operator of postal services on a document with an officially certified signature or electronically via the mailroom, where it is accompanied by a guaranteed electronic signature of the applicant. They are removed from the law, or in this amendment they are excluded from the duties of mayor and vice-mayor of 1st and 2nd municipalities. Which is probably just a short summary of this proposal. Thank you.
