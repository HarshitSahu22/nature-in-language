The principle was that there was some concern that financial flows would not be monitored. Here I can only declare that the Czech Republic has approached this competence fairly conservatively, by limiting the supervisory authorities' powers to the possibility of simply asking banks or other financial institutions, and only the owners. So, in case I say this a little bit more by buying it, that there would be some problem in cross-border contact, some unfair sale, some really serious error, where the person who sells, for example, through electronic stores will be invisible at the moment, so the supervisory authority will only be able to find out by means of the account number what the company is, not that it is investigating specific flows or what is taking place in his accounts.
