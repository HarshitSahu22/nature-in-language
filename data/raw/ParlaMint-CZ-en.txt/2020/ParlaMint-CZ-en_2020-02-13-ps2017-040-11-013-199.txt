ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-013-199.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, this material is presented for the approval of the ratification of the Agreement signed in Luxembourg by all EU Member States on 25 June 2019. Ms Klára Dostálová signed the agreement for the Czech Republic and was signed for the EU and Vietnam in Hanoi on 30 June 2019. In the Senate, the Committee on Economic, Agriculture and Transport and the Committee on Foreign Affairs, Defence and Security have already expressed its agreement on ratification. The negotiated agreement brings legal certainty and predictability that the EU and Vietnam should help attract and sustain investments supporting their economy. As one of the largest foreign investors in the country, the EU will benefit from the better investment climate that will be created by implementing the agreement. The agreement ensures a high level of protection of investment, but it does not interfere with EU law, its Member States and Vietnam to regulate and pursue legitimate public policy objectives such as the protection of public health, safety and the environment. The Agreement contains a modern and reformed dispute resolution mechanism consisting of a permanent first-degree tribunal and a permanent appeal tribunal which will conduct dispute settlement proceedings in a transparent and impartial manner. As from its entry into force, the Agreement will fully replace existing bilateral agreements on the promotion and protection of investments concluded between Vietnam and EU Member States. This will also put an end to the Czech-Vietnamese investment agreement of 1997, including provisions on the application of the withdrawal period. In practice, this means that investments existing on the date of expiry of this investment agreement will no longer be subject to protection from it for a further period of 10 years. From the perspective of Czech constitutional law, this is a Treaty of the presidential category. The Agreement shall enter into force after it has been approved under the relevant national procedures by all Member States, it shall be agreed by the European Parliament and the relevant Council decision shall be taken concluding the Agreement. Thank you.
