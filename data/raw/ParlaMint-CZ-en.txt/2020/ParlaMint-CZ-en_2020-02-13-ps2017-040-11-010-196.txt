ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-010-196.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, the relations between the Czech Republic and the Sultanate of Oman in the field of civil aviation are currently not regulated. Based on the interest of both parties, the meeting of expert delegations on the text of the international agreement took place in Prague in 2011. Subsequently, the Government of the Czech Republic agreed to the agreement by its resolution in December 2011. The appropriate date and place of signature was not found until 2019 when the agreement was signed on 25 March 2019 on the occasion of a visit to the Senate delegation of the Czech Republic in Oman. The text of the agreement is liberal, is consistent with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, including obligations arising from membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. The agreement does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not affect state budget expenditure. The agreement constitutes one of the options for further developing relations between the two countries provides a legal basis for the possible operation of air services. It offers operators of both sides a wide range of possibilities for commercial use. I would therefore like to ask you to refer this agreement to the Committees of the Chamber of Deputies. Thank you.
