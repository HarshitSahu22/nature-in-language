ParlaMint-CZ_2020-06-02-ps2017-049-05-005-012.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, the government has submitted a bill amending Act No. 218/2000 Coll., on budgetary rules and on the amendment of certain related laws. Furthermore, I will only apply a shortcut to the budgetary rules. The first reading of the proposal took place on 24 January this year. In addition to the amendment to the budgetary rules, the draft contains amendments to the Act on budgetary rules for territorial budgets, the Act on Agriculture, the Act on the Promotion of Research, Experimental Development and Innovation from Public Means, the Act on Foreign Development Cooperation and Humanitarian Aid, the Service Act and the Act on the Register of Contracts. In particular, the law proposes: 1. Specify the authorisation to issue the Order on budgetary composition. 2. Expand the budgetary system of programming funding to register non-investment subsidies and return financial assistance provided from the state budget. Thus, it is the unification of the registration of all subsidies in a single place within one system managed by the Ministry of Finance. 3. To lay down the obligation to bind the salaries in the organisational units of the State for vacant posts. The basic idea of the proposed measure is that the vacancy does not require the need for the same budgetary security as the seat occupied. As part of the effort to save the expenditure of the State budget, it is proposed to commit appropriations to salaries and related expenditure in the event that the post is free all calendar days in the calendar month. However, the first month when the seat is vacant will not be binding. 4. Announcement of the use of the State budget surplus to reduce state debt in the event that the State budget has ended up in surplus. It is proposed to make it clear that this surplus is to be used to reduce sovereign debt, since State debt must be kept within reasonable limits. Of course, this was true before the pandemic when we passed this law. 5. The penalty for late payment for breach of budgetary discipline shall be reduced from one perm daily to 0.4 perm daily. To date, the penalty is set at one per cent of the amount of the contribution for each day of delay in the payment of the contribution, which in some cases is too heavy a burden for those who have violated budgetary discipline. It is therefore now proposed to reduce the periodic penalty payment to 0,4 per mile of the amount of the levy for each day of delay. The periodic penalty payment thus provided will fulfil its penalty function without being overly burdensome or, where appropriate, winding-up against the infringer. In addition, a large proportion of the periodic penalty payments are currently being waived. 6. Specify the adjustment for withdrawal of the subsidy or refundable financial assistance. It's more of a procedural aspect. 7. The regulation of the provisions relating to the management of sovereign debt is specified. It is added in paragraph 36, which clearly defines the methodology for reporting in the koruna amount of the sovereign debt denominated in foreign currencies, with the emphasis on the fact that the koruna value of the sovereign debt with which the remuneration of the nominal value or principal of the financial derivatives secured through financial derivatives is related must take into account the contractual exchange rate. 8. The adaptation of the National Fund is added. In addition to the funds already listed in the law, the national funds are the funds of the European Union Solidarity Fund. The European Union Solidarity Fund is amended by Council Regulation No 2012/2002 of 11 November 2002 establishing the European Union Solidarity Fund. As regards the grant of subsidies from the national fund, it is proposed to apply to this provision as well as to all provisions relating to the grant of subsidies from the State budget. It is also proposed to anchor the correct designation of the European Maritime and Fisheries Fund. As regards the regulation of the Act on budgetary rules for territorial budgets, this regulation follows up on the refined authorisation of the decree on budgetary composition and also reduces the penalty for late payment for breach of budgetary discipline as well as in Act No. 218/2000 Coll. Furthermore, legislative technical changes are made in this law. The amendments to the Act on Agriculture, the Act on the Promotion of Research, Experimental Development and Innovation from Public Means and the Foreign Development Cooperation and Humanitarian Aid Act are submitted to ensure that subsidies and return financial assistance from the State budget provided under them are managed in a cost-effective way in the budgetary system of programming financing. The proposed amendment to the Act on the Register of Contracts is of a technical nature only. Its aim is to refer to the correct provisions of the budgetary rules. Thank you for your attention.
