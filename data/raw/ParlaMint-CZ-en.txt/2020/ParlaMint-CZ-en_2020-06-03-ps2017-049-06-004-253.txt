ParlaMint-CZ_2020-06-03-ps2017-049-06-004-253.u2	Once again a good day, Mr President, Madam Members. In short, I will make a proposal for a government amendment to Act No. 219/2003 Coll., on seed and seed circulation. The main aim of the proposed adaptation is to re-regulate the area of circulation of hop propagating material. The requirements for propagating material to be fulfilled by the contracting authority are set out there. These proposals are based on the experience of the Institute of Agriculture, which means the Central Audit and Testing Institute. As a follow-up amendment, it is also proposed to amend the Act on the Protection of Plant Variety Rights, where legislative technical adaptations are carried out. In the Act on Administrative Fees, it is proposed to withdraw from item 90 the fee for the grant of a licence to the register. It is proposed to repeal item 91 which provides for a fee for the acceptance of the application for revocation of trade marks. The Government's bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies. By his resolution, he recommended to Parliament's Chamber of Deputies to approve the Government's bill as amended by the amendments adopted. The amendments adopted are in line with the Ministry's concept and I would like to say that they will contribute to improving the government's bill, and I therefore agree with them. Thank you for your attention. Thank you.
