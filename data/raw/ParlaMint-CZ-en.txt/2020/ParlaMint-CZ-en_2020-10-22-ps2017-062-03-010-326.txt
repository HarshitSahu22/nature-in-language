ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-22-ps2017-062-03-010-326.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly inform you of the agreement submitted on the transfer and sharing of contributions to the Single Crisis Management Fund, referred to as the IGA. I have already presented this intergovernmental agreement, which complements the text of the Single Resolution Mechanism Regulation, in the previous negotiations. I will therefore just repeat that the IGA will apply to the Czech Republic from the date of its possible entry into the banking union. For the Czech Republic as a Member State outside the banking union, there are currently no other obligations arising from the closure of the IGA. However, IGA is also relevant for countries which do not yet participate in the banking union. Through the provisions on so-called non-contractual liability ensures that states outside the banking union through the budget of the European Union do not cover any costs of decisions under a single crisis management mechanism in which they will not participate. The IGA also regulates the procedure for the future accession of a new member to the banking union or the possible withdrawal of a member from the banking union. The Czech Republic is therefore actively involved in the negotiation process of the text of the agreement and has also become a signatory to it. Ratification process in IGA has been in practice since 2014, when it was first submitted to the Parliament of the Czech Republic. The Senate then agreed to ratify, but the ratification process in the Chamber of Deputies did not finish before the end of the parliamentary term. IGA is therefore now being presented to Parliament again. The Senate has already given its consent, and I would be very happy to have the Chamber of Deputies give her their consent. I remind you that all signatory states have already completed ratification with the exception of the Czech Republic, Denmark and Poland. Thank you for your attention.
