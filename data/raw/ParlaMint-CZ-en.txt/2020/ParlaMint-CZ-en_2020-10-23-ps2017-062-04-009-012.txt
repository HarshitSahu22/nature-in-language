Next thing I'm gonna do, I'm just gonna put in the replacement maintenance bill. The draft Act on supplementary maintenance for the careless child and on the amendment of certain related laws, which is submitted on the basis of the 2019 legislative work plan and the Government's programming declaration, address, in particular, the situation of families with children, who may be in an adverse social and financial situation due to the failure to replace maintenance by the compulsory parents. The purpose of the replacement maintenance is to finance the loss of income from the family in which the dependent child lives, beyond the current social benefits. At the same time, these families will be motivated to deal with the situation, as the application for execution or judicial enforcement is a condition for entitlement to replacement maintenance. Replacement maintenance will be paid to regional branches and branches for the capital city of Prague Office of Labour of the Czech Republic. The replacement maintenance will be the amount of the difference established and paid maintenance obligation, but not more than CZK 3,000 per month. The amount of the replacement maintenance shall be based on the monthly average imposed and the maintenance obligation paid for the four calendar months preceding the four calendar months for which the claim is proved and applied. The replacement maintenance will be provided for a limited period of time. The claim shall thus expire no later than after payment of 24 instalments of the child's replacement maintenance or termination of the child's carelessness. After termination of entitlement to replacement maintenance, i.e. after payment of 24 instalments or termination of non-refundability, or earlier if it can be assumed that the replacement maintenance will no longer be paid, the maintenance claim shall be transferred to the State by decision of the Labour Office. The decision will specify the extent to which the maintenance claim will be passed on to the State. The Labour Office shall then file a motion for the execution of a judgment or execution, where appropriate in certain cases inviting the authorised person to submit the application himself. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed for 1 July 2021. Thank you for your attention.
