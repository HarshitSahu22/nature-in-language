ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-22-ps2017-062-03-002-316.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me present the Council of Europe Convention on Film Co-production, which revises the wording of the original 1992 Treaty. Its aim is to facilitate the closure of international film co-productions, to enable the creation of European films, which are produced by several co-production countries in view of their financial complexity, and at the same time not to lose the opportunity to receive support from other, in particular, national support schemes. The revised text responds to the development of new technologies that have transformed the way films are produced, distributed and made available, and in particular to the development of public funding, both at national and regional level. We are also creating a legal framework for full participation in future film works and for smaller co-producers. The revised Convention only brings changes in the procedural nature. The Czech Republic has been a member of the original Convention in 1997 and there will be no commitments with the negotiations of this revised Convention requiring legislative amendments. I would therefore like to thank the honourable Members for their attention.
