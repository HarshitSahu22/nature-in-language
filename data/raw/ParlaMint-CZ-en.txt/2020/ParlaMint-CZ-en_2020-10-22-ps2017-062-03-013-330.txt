ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-22-ps2017-062-03-013-330.u2	Let me continue with the other documents submitted, this time again from the Ministry of Transport. The relations between our country and the Sultanate of Oman in the field of civil aviation are not regulated in any way, and therefore, on the basis of the interest of both parties, an expert discussion on the text of the international agreement took place in Prague in 2011. Its agreement was subsequently agreed by the Czech Government, with the appropriate deadline and place of signature not until 2019, when the agreement was signed on the occasion of a visit to the Senate delegation of the Czech Republic in Oman, on 25 March. The text of the agreement is liberal, is consistent with constitutional order, with other parts of the legal order of our country, including obligations arising from Czech membership in the EU. The agreement does not require any changes in the Czech legal order. It won't affect the state budget. The agreement thus represents one of the options for further developing relations, providing a legal basis for the operation of air services and for carriers of both parties offering very good opportunities for commercial use. The agreement has already been discussed in the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic, it has been recommended to give consent to the ratification of the agreement, and I would therefore also like to ask the Chamber of Deputies to make a positive statement. Thank you.
