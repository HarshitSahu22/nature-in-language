ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-22-ps2017-062-03-011-327.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, the Chamber of Deputies is tabled a proposal to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Argentine Republic on mutual administrative assistance in customs matters signed in Buenos Aires on 6 March 2019. In this respect, I would like to mention the following. In view of the growing need for international cooperation between customs authorities in the fight against breaches of customs legislation and also the fact that the customs authorities of the Czech Republic and the Argentine Republic currently have no legal instrument to enable them to cooperate effectively in pre-empting and investigating breaches of customs legislation, the customs authorities of the Contracting Parties have agreed to submit a proposal to their governments for the conclusion of this Treaty. In particular, the purpose of the contract is to prevent damage to the financial interests of both Contracting Parties by effective cooperation in preventing and investigating breaches of customs legislation. The Treaty will also facilitate the exchange of information and experience of customs administrations in the area of the fraudulent import of goods and illicit trafficking in narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. The General Directorate of Customs will be responsible for the implementation of the contract in the Czech Republic. The implementation of the contract will not have an impact on the state budget and will not require changes in the personnel, material or financial security of the customs administration's activities, on the contrary, a reduction in the number and extent of customs fraud can be expected and thus a higher revenue for the state budget. The Government of the Czech Republic approved the proposal to negotiate the contract on 31 October 2018 by order number 705. Thank you for your attention.
