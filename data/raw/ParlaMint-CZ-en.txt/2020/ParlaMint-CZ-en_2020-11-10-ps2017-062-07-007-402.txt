Proposal for the appointment of a member of the Radio and Television Broadcasting Council At the first ballot point, the appointment of a member of the Radio and Television Council, 169 ballots were issued, the same number of valid and invalid ballots were submitted. The ballot required for the election was 85. Hana Dohnálková, the only candidate, won 138 votes and was elected a member of the Radio and Television Broadcasting Council. I congratulate you from a distance, and its mandate will begin on the date of appointment as Prime Minister.
