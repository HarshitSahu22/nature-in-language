ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-23-ps2017-062-04-001-354.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, press 669, the draft Act on Arms Management in some cases affecting internal order or security of the Czech Republic has been presented as a parliamentary initiative. As a basis we took the Ministry of the Interior drawn up by the same name bill, which went through inter-ministerial commentary proceedings and was designed to be compatible with the new Act on Weapons, and we redesigned it to be compatible with the current Act No. 119/2002 Coll. The reason for our step was uncertainty as to whether for time reasons the entire package of new weapons laws could be adopted by the end of the parliamentary term, with the fact that this law and its adoption are very important for the security of the Czech Republic. After the loss of the obligation to complete the military service in the company, the percentage of residents who are able to safely and effectively control the weapon if necessary fell, and this fact is a risk to the Czech Republic in the current unstable and rapidly evolving world. The proposal envisages the creation of a system exclusively voluntary shooting preparation of citizens of the Czech Republic, who, after completing the programme, will form a specified advance of the state. The condition for participation in this programme is, inter alia, that the citizen will be the holder of an arms licence. The proposal for a government regulation by which this law should be implemented and which has been sent to us by the Ministry of the Interior must be considered appropriate. Members have worked a lot of work on this bill across the political spectrum and there has been agreement. I therefore recommend to the Chamber of Deputies the adoption of this law.
