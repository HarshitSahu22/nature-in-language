ParlaMint-CZ_2020-11-10-ps2017-062-07-011-017.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, once again we meet in the context of the discussion of the draft amendment to the Mining Act, an important law, and of all the related laws that must be said, the Czech Mining Authority has drawn up together with the Ministry of Industry and Trade. The reason for all the proposed changes is to minimise possible future expenditure from the state budget for removing the effects of mining activity, mine disposal, quarrying in the event of irresponsible management of mining companies, while at the same time aligning the existing state restrictions as the owner of mineral wealth, within the framework of the adjustment of the remuneration rates from the minerals recovered. The Guarantee Committee has agreed to several amendments. Let me comment on some of them at this point. Mr Dolínek's amendment extends to mining organisations the deadline for the transfer of funds - reserves - to the remediation and reclamation of land affected by mining and the settlement of mining damages, up to 30. 6. 2030. A number of negotiations took place with regard to the determination of a specific date, and we finally agreed that, in view of the current situation of mining companies, those smaller companies with cash problems, we would support this proposal. In addition, Mr Adamec's amendment to the Economic Committee has been agreed, adjusting the mechanism for changing the rates of payments from extracted minerals. This amendment automatically introduces the government's obligation to change the rates of payments from minerals obtained every two years by means of a fixed mechanism. This proposal does not, in principle, contradict the government's objectives to tie changes to the rates of remuneration with changes in reference prices, which is why we are adopting a neutral position on it. I also agree with the slight amendment to prolong the entry into force of the amendment to the Mining Act, as this material is already under discussion at a time when the current date of application would be unrealistic. Of course, I am ready to negotiate and discuss all the other amendments that can be read. Thank you.
