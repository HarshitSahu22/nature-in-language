The revenue from this sale will be received from the state budget. It is also proposed that the draft law be discussed in accordance with the procedure laid down in § 90 para. 2 to 6 of the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, so that the Chamber of Deputies may give its consent at first reading.
