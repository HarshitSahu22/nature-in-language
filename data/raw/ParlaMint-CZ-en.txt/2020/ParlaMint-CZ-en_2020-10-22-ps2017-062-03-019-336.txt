Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty on Extradition between the Czech Republic and the Argentine Republic (Prague, 25. 11. 2019) /house print 691/ - second reading I beg the Minister of Justice Marie Benešová to take the floor. Madam Secretary, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-22-ps2017-062-03-019-336.u2	Thank you for your word. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to briefly remind the Government's proposal by which the Chamber of Deputies in the second reading submits to the ratification agreement between the Czech Republic and Argentina on extradition signed in Prague on 25 November last year. The first reading took place at the 40th meeting of the Chamber of Deputies and the Foreign Committee discussed the proposal on 30 April this year and recommended to give consent to ratification. The Senate agreed to the ratification at its 21st meeting on 30 April this year. There are currently no bilateral agreements in force between the Czech Republic and the Argentine Republic in the field of international judicial cooperation in criminal matters, and the Argentine Republic is neither a Contracting Party to the Council of Europe Conventions concerning the extradition of persons and other forms of international judicial cooperation. International judicial cooperation in criminal matters, or the extradition of persons for criminal prosecution or the execution of prison sentences, is currently taking place between the two States on a non-contractual basis. The system of extradition agreements between the Czech Republic and Argentina will therefore deepen efficiency, increase speed and simplify extradition procedures and will certainly contribute to more effective enforcement of law. The agreement is therefore in line with foreign policy interests, and I therefore ask for a favourable assessment of this government proposal. Thank you.
