ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-003-189.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Allow me to mention this point this time on behalf of the Minister of Transport, and I will be very brief. The relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Chile in the field of civil aviation have not been regulated until now by any bilateral international treaty. There is no direct air link between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Chile, but airlines from Chile and Spain have already expressed an interest in normal commercial cooperation between air carriers in the form of joint line labelling arrangements, the so-called code-sharing, between Santiage de Chile and Prague via Madrid. This was also an impulse for the adjustment of contractual relations in the field of commercial air transport. In 2017, an expert meeting took place which resulted in a draft text of the Air Agreement approved subsequently at the Government meeting signed on 11 December 2018. The agreement is a prerequisite for the bilateral development of both direct and indirect international air services between the Czech Republic and Chile. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to refer the material to the Committees of the Chamber of Deputies. Thank you for your attention.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-003-189.u3	Thank you, Minister. The rapporteur for first reading is Mrs Karla Šlechtová. Please, rapporteur, you have the floor.
