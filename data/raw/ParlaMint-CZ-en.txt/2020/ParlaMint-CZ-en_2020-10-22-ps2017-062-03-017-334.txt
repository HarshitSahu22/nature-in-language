Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Botswana on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income taxes signed in Pretoria on 29 October 2019 /German press 671/ - second reading I ask the Vice-President of the Government and Secretary of Finance Alena Schillerová to take the floor. Madam Secretary, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-22-ps2017-062-03-017-334.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, at the meeting, I present a double taxation treaty with Botswana signed on 29 October 2019 in Pretoria. This is historically the first double taxation treaty with Botswana. The conclusion of this international tax treaty follows the intention to limit taxpayers' tax burden in the absence of undesirable international double taxation, which normally occurs without the existence of a tax treaty in international relations. There is therefore currently a non-contractual relationship between the Czech Republic and Botswana in the mutual tax relations, and the existence of a double taxation treaty is certainly necessary for the function of tax relations and the development of relations between the two states. Botswana is a small but prosperous and very open economy, which offers Czech investors many opportunities for investment, especially in the fields of mining, energy, information technology and health. As far as mutual trade is concerned, trade turnover amounted to less than $2 million in 2018, with exports of the Czech Republic significantly prevailing. The Double Taxation Treaty, which is currently being proposed in relations between the Czech Republic and Botswana, was prepared on the basis of OECD and UN model models, and, of course, also responds to the internal tax rules of both countries. The Treaty will enable the coordination of the activities of the tax authorities of both States to limit possible tax evasion and will, last but not least, enable the relevant exchange of information, in accordance with all existing international standards. The negotiation of this double taxation treaty will increase the legal certainty of potential investors in both states. Its closure and implementation will not have a direct impact on the state budget. The overall contribution resulting from the application of this Treaty will in practice depend on the future development of the various forms of economic cooperation, where this Treaty will certainly open up space for Czech business entities, thereby helping to develop mutual economic relations, including investment activities. So let me ask you to ratify this treaty. Thank you for your attention.
