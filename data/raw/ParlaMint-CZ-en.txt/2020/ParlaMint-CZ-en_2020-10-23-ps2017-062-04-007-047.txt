ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-23-ps2017-062-04-007-047.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me present, as part of my second reading, the Government's proposal for a law on the Collection of Laws of Territorial Autonomous Communities and certain administrative offices. It is proposed to set up a Collection of laws and regulations of local authorities and certain administrative authorities, the administrator of which will be the Ministry of the Interior. The collection will be publicly accessible electronic information system, anyone will be able to use it. A comprehensive modern database will be created in which it will be possible to look up and learn about the content of generally binding decrees and regulations of all local authorities in the Czech Republic. The collection will also serve to declare the laws of the administrative authorities which are not published in the Collection of Laws, as well as to publish some other documents relevant to the performance of the public administration, which are so far published fragmentedly, and which need to be used by several means to find them. The establishment of this collection is to be achieved in a state where it is guaranteed that this collection contains, in fact, all the applicable and effective legislation of the local authorities and the legislation laid down by law of the administrative authorities with limited territorial scope, for which the proposed condition of the entry into force and effectiveness of this legislation is intended by publication in the central collection of legislation and, at the same time, the imposition of an obligation on the legislators to insert all existing legislation within a transitional period of three years into the collection of legislation under sanctions for the cessation of their validity. This substantive solution was subject to a thorough analysis during the preparation of the draft law and was assessed as the only really effective way of achieving the objective of the proposal, which is primarily to ensure legal certainty of addressees and legal standards and the knowledgeability of the law. For example, the Constitutional Court has repeatedly drawn attention to the fundamental shortcomings of the current fragmented legislation in this regard. The draft Act on the Collection of Laws of Territorial Self-governing Bodies and Certain Administrative Offices is accompanied by a draft of several laws which currently contain rules on the publication of various laws which are not published in the Collection of Laws. This draft amendment law has the number of the House press 576 and is on the agenda of the Chamber of Deputies meeting after the present draft law. The bill has already been discussed by the Guarantee Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development and I agree with the amendments adopted by this discharge. Thank you for your attention.

ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-23-ps2017-062-04-007-047.u6	Thank you for the floor. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to read here the amendment of my absent colleague Věra Kovářová, who is under the number of House document 5413. The aim of the amendment is to maintain the current way of declaring the legislation of local authorities, through their official plates. The change in the way in which this legislation is published is a clear intervention in the constitutionally guaranteed right to self-government, especially if the municipalities and the regions will have to re-published all the already effective generally binding decrees in the established Collection of laws of local authorities. If the municipalities and the regions had not done so, the re-published legislation would have ceased to apply. It is possible to agree with the intention of the petitioner of the bill that it is necessary to ensure sufficient public access to legal acts of municipalities, towns and regions, but it is not possible to agree with the condition of validity through the publication in the Collection of Laws of Territorial Autonomous Bodies. The fulfilment of the intention of the petitioner can also be achieved in another constitutionally consistent manner, which is also permitted by the attached RIA, through the obligation of the authorities to publish their legislation on the established collection within 15 days of its publication on the official board of the local authority. I will sign up for the amendment in a detailed debate. Thank you for your attention.
