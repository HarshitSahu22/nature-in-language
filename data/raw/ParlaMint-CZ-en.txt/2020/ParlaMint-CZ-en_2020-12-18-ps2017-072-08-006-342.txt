The funding allocated to the subsidy programmes shall be at the same level in the draft budget for 2021 as they were in the proposal for this year. The distribution of funds into individual programmes is mainly directed to the livestock sector, where it is 2.5 billion. CZK 578 000 000 000 CZK are allocated to the plant production sector and to the food industry. A total of CZK 193,000,000 is provided for other activities. A new framework programme for the management of risks and crises in agriculture is included in the material so that it can be launched and administered if necessary, in accordance with Law No. 252/1997 Coll., on Agriculture. The programme is presented without funds. In the event of damage caused, for example, by natural disasters, exceptional events, by adverse weather conditions, it will be launched and financed on the basis of a relevant government resolution.
