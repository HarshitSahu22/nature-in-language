So allow me to state here the content of the bill on medical devices. First of all, I would like to stress that the main part of the proposal is the adaptation of the Czech rule of law to certain regulations of the European Parliament and of the Council. This Regulation was adopted at European level with its twin in vitro diagnostic medical devices in order to ensure a higher level of safety of medical devices used, and thus patient safety throughout the European Union. [[13.00 hours]] The draft law therefore, following this regulation, has the task of adapting the Czech rule of law to EU rules. In particular, duplicate national arrangements should be abolished. Furthermore, in the light of the regulation, the proposal addresses in particular issues of competence, where it designates individual national authorities, in particular the State Institute for Drug Control, as supervisory authorities, or entrusts them with the exercise of the powers conferred on each Member State by the regulation. The proposal regulates in detail the obligations of individual types of operators in the field of medical devices as well as the issue of the labelling and identification of medical devices. Furthermore, a new adjustment is provided for in the course of clinical trials, market surveillance and vigilance systems. I don't know if that's how you read it, I'm sorry. Last but not least, in order to meet the obligations of the Czech Republic as a Member State of the European Union, it is necessary to ensure the enforcement of the rules laid down by the regulation, by defining the various factual elements of the offences. The proposed proposal thus ensures that the EU-wide rules laid down in particular in order to ensure patient safety are enforceable on the territory of the Czech Republic. Within the framework of national legislation, issues of prescription, dispensing, use or servicing of medical devices need to be comprehensively adapted beyond that Union regulation. The proposal was discussed by the Health Committee, which supported it at its meeting on 9 September of this year and recommended it for approval. Thank you for your attention.
