Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty on Social Security between the Czech Republic and Mongolia, signed in Prague on 20 May 2019 /German press 511/ - first reading On behalf of the Government, the proposal submitted will be presented by the Minister of Labour and Social Affairs Jana Maláčová. Please, Madam Secretary, take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-008-194.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the submitted social security agreement with Mongolia is a standard contract of this type, which should follow up on the agreements already negotiated, in particular, on the prevention of double taxation or on the protection of investments. Its task is primarily to define in which state the migrant worker will pay the insurance and, at the same time, to establish the resulting pension rights. For employers sending their employees to Mongolia, it is important to have confidence in where they are to take insurance premiums for them and, in particular, for workers themselves, that the insurance period in the second Contracting State will not be lost to them, but will be taken into account in the future in the allocated pensions. More detailed information is provided in the presentation report. I beg your release for second reading. Thank you.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-008-194.u3	Thank you. The rapporteur for the first reading is Mr Jiří Mihola. Please, rapporteur, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-008-194.u4	Thank you. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly summarize that there is a relatively high-quality contractual base between the Czech Republic and Mongolia. These documents should also be supplemented by the social security agreement contained in this material, which we are discussing right now. Like documents in the social field negotiated with more distant countries, overseas countries and the like, the submitted social security contract between the Czech Republic and Mongolia is regulated in addition to participation in insurance only for the provision of pension benefits. The Treaty with Mongolia is a standard document that fully respects the fundamental internationally recognised principles. I recommend that she be accepted and discussed in the Foreign Committee. Thank you.
