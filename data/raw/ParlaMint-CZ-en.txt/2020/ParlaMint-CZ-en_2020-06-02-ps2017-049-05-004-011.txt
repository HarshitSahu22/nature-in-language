ParlaMint-CZ_2020-06-02-ps2017-049-05-004-011.u6	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, since there is a law on banks and related laws, I would like to make an amendment to help people dealing with the distribution of insurance and reinsurance, which is in Act No.170/2018 Coll. This is about helping those who were to pass their tests this year and could not do so because of viral infection. Since this proposal was very difficult to get into the system under the number 5509, I will then apply to it, so I will then allow this amendment to be accurately read in the detailed debate, but now within the explanatory memorandum, I would like to say that the law is based on other current government measures for viral infection, where it is desirable that there should be no concentration of persons in the restricted area, and as part of the insurance distribution to insurance intermediaries, the obligation to carry out an expert test is imposed. By category, there may be up to 2,000 questions. Only their description covers 876 pages of text. The tests must be carried out by approximately 40,000 insurance intermediaries plus their employees. Due to the complexity of the test, courses are to be organised for insurance intermediaries to prepare them for the test. Both the preparation courses and the examinations themselves necessarily relate to the repeated assembly of persons in schools and classrooms. A demonstration of general knowledge shall be imposed on intermediaries carrying out their activities for a period of less than three years. General knowledge is demonstrated by a certificate of graduation or proof of higher education. Even obtaining a graduation certificate at this time or a similar certificate depended on the renewal of teaching in schools and this was only partially successful. The purpose of the Act is to provide time for completion of preparatory courses, postpone deadlines for completion of examinations and obtain proof of general knowledge. In the case of tests, a delay of one year is proposed; in the case of obtaining evidence of general knowledge, a delay of 13 months is proposed. The reason for this is that a document proving general knowledge will be obtained by graduating from one of the secondary schools. The date for obtaining the document is up to the end of May 2022. However, since the school year ends in June, it is proposed to postpone by 13 and not by 12 months, that is until the end of June 2023. I have here an evaluation of the compliance of the proposed amendment with constitutional order. The proposed bill is in accordance with the constitutional order of the Czech Republic, the Charter of Fundamental Rights and Freedoms, the Constitution of the Czech Republic and other constitutional laws. The assessment of the compliance of proposed legislation with European Union legislation and with international treaties to which the Czech Republic is bound is assessed in this way. The proposed bill is in accordance with the legislation of the European Union and with the international treaties to which the Czech Republic is bound. Expected economic and financial impact of proposed legislation on the state budget, as must always be stated, also other public budgets and the business environment of the Czech Republic, social impacts and environmental impacts. I, as the appellant, do not foresee any economic and financial scope of the proposed legislation for the state budget or for other public budgets. The proposal will have no impact on the environment. The proposal will have a favourable impact on the business environment of the Czech Republic and will also have a favourable social impact on employees of insurance intermediaries who, for time reasons, would not fulfil their obligations under the existing wording of the Act. Then there is also an assessment of the impact of the proposed solution in relation to privacy and personal data protection.
