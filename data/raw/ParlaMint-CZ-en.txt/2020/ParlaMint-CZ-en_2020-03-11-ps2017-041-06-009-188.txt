Proposal for the election of members of the Czech Radio Council In the event of an election to the Czech Radio Council, where we occupied two seats, the councilman Mr.Marek Pokorný was elected, who won 135 votes. Hana Dohnálková with 77 votes and Jaroslav Šebek with 25 votes will proceed to the second round. One seat is occupied, Mr. Marek Pokorný will take office from 27 March. One place remains vacant and will be the second round. Results of other candidates: Michal Lukeš 17 votes, Markéta Pravdová 1 vote and Zoša Vyoralová 2 votes.
