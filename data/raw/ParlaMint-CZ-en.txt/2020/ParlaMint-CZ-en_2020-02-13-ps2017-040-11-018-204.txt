Ladies and gentlemen, the Convention on International Rules for Preventing Collisions at Sea of 1972, the COLREG Convention, the International Convention is adopted by the International Maritime Organisation and is one of the most important international maritime conventions concerning the operation of vessels on the open seas and any downstream waterways on which sea vessels can sail. The main objective of the COLREG Convention is to ensure uniform operating rules for maritime vessels. The COLREG Convention was signed in London on 20 October 1972 and entered into force for Czechoslovakia on 15 July 1977. After the division of Czechoslovakia, the Czech Republic acceded to the COLREG Convention. The COLREG Convention for the Czech Republic therefore entered into force on 1 January 1993. In order to complete the process of proper implementation pursuant to Article 10 of the Constitution, the Convention shall be submitted to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to give its consent to the COLREG Convention, including partial amendments adopted gradually from its inception to date. The text of the COLREG Convention is in line with the constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, including obligations arising from the membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union and does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not affect the expenditure of the state budget.
