ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-011-197.u2	Thank you. Mr Vice-President, colleagues, this material contains a proposal to ratify the so-called readmission agreement with Mongolia. The contractual documents of this type are one of the important instruments for combating illegal migration. This Agreement shall, in particular, contain the basic obligation of the Contracting Parties to take back a citizen who does not fulfil or no longer fulfils the applicable conditions for entry or residence in the territory of the other State. It also contains the main principles relating to the procedure for implementing this commitment, i.e. submission of an application, deadlines for processing, deadlines for implementation, provisions relating to the protection of personal data and so on. The purpose of the agreement is to speed up the transfer of illegally staying persons, to shorten their stay in reinsurance facilities, thereby reducing the costs associated with their detention and subsequent transfer. At the explicit request of the Mongolian party, the agreement is negotiated on the outside as an intergovernmental treaty, but it requires national approval by the Parliament of the Czech Republic and ratification by the President of the Republic, as it is an international treaty that regulates the rights and obligations of persons and provides for an adjustment reserved in the law. Thank you for your attention.

ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-011-197.u4	Mr President, Minister, Members of the Government, Members of Parliament, as the Minister has said, the treaties on the transfer and readmission of persons residing illegally in the territory of the State are the so-called readmission agreements, which form the legal basis for the return of illegally staying persons to their countries of origin. In this case, it is a treaty between the Czech Republic and Mongolia. In relation to Mongolia, the area of return of illegally staying persons is not yet regulated by contract, and therefore this agreement, which I support, is submitted to Parliament for approval and is recommended for further discussion of the Chamber of Deputies.
