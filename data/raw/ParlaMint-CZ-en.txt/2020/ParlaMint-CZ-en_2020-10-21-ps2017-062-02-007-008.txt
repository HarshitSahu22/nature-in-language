ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-21-ps2017-062-02-007-008.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce House Press No. 1061, the draft Act on certain adjustments in the area of state social aid benefits and the contribution to care in connection with the emergency situation of the epidemic, which was drawn up by the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs and which, at the time of the declared emergency situation in the territory of the Czech Republic, ensures the continuity of the payment of benefits for which the income to which he insisted on entitlement on 30 September 2020 is tested. These are welfare benefits, child allowance, housing allowance and an increase in the care allowance under the Social Services Act. It is proposed that the Labour Office should base its entitlement, amount and payment of these benefits for the fourth calendar quarter of 2020 on data on income, housing costs and other facts relevant to the entitlement to those benefits, which have been substantiated for entitlement to payment and the amount of those benefits for the third quarter of 2020, i.e. income, housing costs and other relevant facts for the second calendar quarter of 2020. The benefits for which the beneficiaries have already submitted supporting documents shall be assessed on the basis of these supporting documents. These are approximately 35 percent of the beneficiaries. For others, benefits of the same amount as in the third quarter of 2020 will be automatically paid. This will be a net prolongation of entitlement to benefits, for which income from the third quarter is tested for the fourth quarter as well. It is reasonable to assume that this adjustment will bring the intended effect of eliminating citizens' contacts at a time of restriction of movement and reducing the administrative burden of the Office of Labour of the Czech Republic. New submission of an application for a levy shall be without prejudice to this adjustment. In addition, the administration of benefits will be significantly reduced at a time when the employment office will be burdened with the increased needs of its clients in the field of employment and other non-insurable social benefits. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed on the date of its publication. Thank you for your attention.
