Government bill amending Act No. 151/1997 Coll., on property valuation and amending certain laws (Act on property valuation), as amended, and other related laws /house printing 501/ - second reading Under the authority of the Government, the draft bill will be presented by Vice-President of the Government and Secretary of Finance Alena Schillerová. Madam Secretary, please take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-03-04-ps2017-041-02-013-008.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce to you the bill amending the property valuation law, which was prepared in accordance with the legislative work plan of the government. The main objective of the draft law is to introduce market value as an alternative in cases where the usual price cannot be determined, to establish the concept of a rapidly growing tree and their method of valuation prepared in cooperation with the Ministry of Agriculture - this is a response to modern trends in tree growing to a chipping plant, which is a renewable fuel source - and a new approach to the valuation of the right of construction and material burdens and the related valuation of immovable property burdened with material burdens and similar rights. The proposed bill is of a general nature. It is usable for a wide range of entities and for various valuation purposes. It is supplemented by a proposal for the provisions in question in other related laws. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed on 1 January 2021. Thank you for your attention.
