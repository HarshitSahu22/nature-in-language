ParlaMint-CZ_2020-04-09-ps2017-044-03-002-017.u2	Thank you. Allow me to mention the bill on the extension of time limits for the Senate's supplementary elections. This Act is intended, within the meaning of the Constitutional Act on Security of the Czech Republic, to establish a clear legal basis for the postponement of additional elections to the Senate in the constituency No. 32. It is a constituency based in Teplice, i.e. a mandate emptyed by the death of Jaroslav Kubera. These additional elections were originally scheduled to take place on 27 and 28 March 2020. As a result of the announcement of the emergency situation and the related crisis measures restricting freedom of movement, the vote was postponed by the Government resolution of 15 March 2020. 218. This step was based on the legal view that the Constitutional Security Act of the Czech Republic only regulates the extension of the term of office, which does not occur in the case of additional elections to the Senate. On 1 April 2020, the Supreme Administrative Court expressed a different legal opinion on the grounds of its decision in another, not directly related case, and questioned the Government's resolution on the delay of voting in supplementary elections. This legal opinion of the Supreme Administrative Court has no immediate legal effect with regard to supplementary elections in District No. 32, but it is likely that the Supreme Administrative Court would have followed it even in future insolvency proceedings. Therefore, for the sake of legal certainty, the government proposes to address the situation by adopting a law corresponding to the requirements of the constitutional law on security of the Czech Republic. Under this proposed law, the additional elections should take place by the end of June 2020, with a specific deadline to be set by the President of the Republic. The draft law is being discussed in a shortened legislative emergency, as it is in the interests of legal certainty that the situation in the case of supplementary elections should be clarified as soon as possible. The draft law has already been discussed by the Constitutional and Legal Committee, which proposed a legislative technical amendment in the text of the Act. With this amendment, the Ministry of the Interior and Minister Hamáček identify themselves. Thank you for your attention.
