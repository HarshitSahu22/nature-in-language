ParlaMint-CZ_2020-11-12-ps2017-062-09-006-515.u6	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Colleagues, ladies and gentlemen, the intention of my proposal is to effectively eliminate the fundamental difficulties mentioned in the explanatory report of the law on applicants with foreign previous education, the complications in the process of formal recognition of previous education and the threat of access to the study by these applicants due to procedural delays. At the same time, the proposal shall ensure equal rights in the admission procedure to all tenderers. The idea of the proposal is based on the joint opinion of the Czech Conference of Rectors and the Council of Higher Education Institutions, which recommended at the beginning of this calendar year to the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports, that the procedure for verifying the condition of obtaining a previous education under Section 48 of the Higher Education Act be accessible to all applicants for higher education in the Czech Republic. The explanatory memorandum refers to the fundamental difficulties faced by foreign applicants in particular in demonstrating their education. It deals with the introduction of a 90-day deadline for documenting previous education. However, this deadline is insufficient. At some regional offices, the overpressure of requests for nostrification is so great that it takes two months for applications to be dealt with at all. Then they will begin to assess the applications and prescribe nostrification tests if necessary. However, they are not published at the time of the conclusion of secondary schools. It is easy for a student to fail the prescribed period of 90 days. Moreover, the proposed amendment does not address the fundamental problem of the unequal position of different candidates in the recognition of previous education. The unequal position lies in verifying the fulfilment of secondary education under two different laws and two fundamentally different sets of criteria. While some applicants examine whether their foreign graduation is sufficient to receive a university in a given foreign state, other applicants require their foreign graduation to be equivalent to Czech. This leads to absurd situations where two applicants have a graduation certificate from the same school, for example from the USA, but while one is recognised under § 48 para. Article 4 (1) (a) and (b) (d) the higher education law, and is accepted for university, the second regional authority refuses to grant a nostrification certificate pursuant to § 108 para. 5 school law and is rejected in admission proceedings. However, equal rights and equal access to education under the Charter of Fundamental Rights and Freedoms require the same criteria to be applied to all university students. Achieving a previous education is a general condition for admission to college, which applies to all types of study programmes and to all types of higher education institutions. The aim of the newly proposed text is therefore to ensure that the achievement of the previous education is assessed by the same criteria for all applicants, in such a way that if a candidate is applying for a university course without institutional accreditation, he will also be able to verify the condition for obtaining a previous education under paragraph 2. Article 4 (1) (a) and (b) (d) and as referred to in point (d) of Article 4 (1) of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013; Annex V.Part 2.116 (d) (c). Specifically, it will be able to demonstrate compliance with this condition at a university which has an institutional accreditation for at least one field of education and which it will choose. The current text of the Higher Education Act allows this university to charge an appropriate fee for the tasks associated with the assessment of the fulfilment of the conditions for admission to study. In this way, two major difficulties, mentioned in the explanatory report of the law, namely complications in the process of formal recognition of previous education and the threat of access to the study by those candidates, will be effectively resolved due to procedural delays. Thank you.
