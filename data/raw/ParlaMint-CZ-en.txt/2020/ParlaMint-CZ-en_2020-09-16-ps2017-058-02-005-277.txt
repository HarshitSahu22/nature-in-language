The aim of this brief amendment is to help and support hundreds of publicly beneficial organisations providing the necessary social, health and other services, whose need has become apparent during the ongoing pandemic. Further efforts to help sports clubs and teams as well as efforts to help cultural organisations and facilities. What is the essence of this brief amendment to the Income Tax Act? We, together with the petitioners, propose temporarily, it is a covido thing, for the period 2020 and 2021 to increase the possibility of deduction of donations from the tax base from the current 10 percent respectively. 15 % - depends on whether it is a legal person or a natural person, on the proposed 30 per cent. Why are we proposing such a short amendment? It is evident that in the coming days, weeks and months there will be a decrease in the financing of publicly beneficial activities, sports clubs, cultural organizations by state administration, self-government, but also by the private corporate sector, I suppose. These subjects, as I've named them, are actually dependent on help from private donors and donors. It is also likely that the current economic situation will lead to a reduction in the incentive to donate even to donors who support these sectors in the long and stable manner and would continue to donate in a normal economic situation. Our aim is not to increase donations, I would like to point out, but rather to maintain the willingness of socially responsible companies to help co-finance these projects and entities that have always supported them in the long term. Let me also mention that this is indirect aid, which should have a minimal impact on the state budget. The benefits of this support will be exclusively given to organisations, in my opinion, which do not rely on state subsidies for the long term, but are actively seeking their own efforts by their private donors. In our opinion, such an organization should be supported by the state. I would like to inform you, because the proposal was not made a few days ago, and we have discussed it with many organizations in the long term, and I would like to inform you that this proposal has the support of the Czech Olympic Committee and many entities that bring together hundreds of publicly beneficial organizations. I have also consulted this proposal and asked for support from my colleagues, who I know have long been engaged in this topic, such as my colleague Helena Válková, Mr Jan Chvojka, Chairman of the Czech Social Democratic Parliamentary Club, or I have tried to discuss this sport with my colleague Milan Hnilička.
