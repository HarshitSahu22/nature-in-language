ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-23-ps2017-062-04-008-048.u2	Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to follow up my opening words on the draft law on the Collection of Laws of Territorial Self-governing Bodies and certain Administrative Offices and briefly introduce the Government's draft law amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the Act on the Collection of Laws of Territorial Self-governing Bodies and certain Administrative Offices. The draft amendment law includes a total of 14 laws, which currently contain the rules of publication of various legislations, which are not published in the Collection of Laws. The general rules for the publication of legislation proposed in the new central collection will be contained in the Act on the Collection of Laws of Territorial Self-governing Bodies and Certain Administrative Offices. Therefore, partial publication rules are deleted from individual laws without, however, interfering with the scope of the regulatory activities of local authorities and individual legal authorities. The draft law has already been discussed by the Guarantee Committee on Public Administration and the Regional Proposal. I agree with the amendments adopted by this committee. Thank you for your attention.
