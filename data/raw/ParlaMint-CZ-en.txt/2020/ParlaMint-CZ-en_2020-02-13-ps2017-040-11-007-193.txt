Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Argentine Republic on mutual administrative assistance in customs matters /German press 507/ - first reading The proposal submitted shall be presented by the Vice-President of the Government and Secretary of Finance Alena Schillerová. Please, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-007-193.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. I now submit to the Chamber of Deputies a proposal to approve the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Argentine Republic on mutual administrative assistance in customs matters signed in Buenos Aires on 6 March 2019. In this context, I would like to state the following: in view of the growing need for international cooperation between customs authorities in the fight against breaches of customs legislation, as well as the fact that the customs authorities of the Czech Republic and the Argentine Republic currently have no legal instrument to enable them to cooperate effectively in preventing and investigating breaches of customs legislation, the customs authorities of the Contracting Parties have agreed to submit a proposal to their governments for the conclusion of this Treaty. In particular, the purpose of the contract is to damage the financial interests of both Contracting Parties in the form of effective cooperation in preventing and investigating breaches of customs legislation. The Treaty will also facilitate the exchange of information and experience of customs administrations in the area of the fraudulent import of goods and illicit trafficking in narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. The General Directorate of Customs will be responsible for the implementation of the contract in the Czech Republic. The implementation of the contract will not have an impact on the state budget, will not require changes in personnel, material or financial security of the customs administration's activities, on the contrary, a reduction in the number and extent of customs fraud can be expected, thereby ensuring higher revenue in the state budget. The Government agreed on a proposal to negotiate a contract on 31 October 2018 by resolution No. 705. I'm sure you'll object to where the delay is. It was clearly caused on the Argentine side. Thank you.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-007-193.u3	Thank you. The rapporteur for the first reading is Mr Petr Beitl. The rapporteur, please, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-007-193.u4	Hello, ladies and gentlemen. Argentina is the third largest trading partner in Latin America for the Czech Republic, and there is indeed a deliberate violation between the two countries in order to circumvent the provisions relating to the reimbursement of customs duties, taxes and other similar cash rules levied in connection with the import or export of goods or provisions governing the prohibitions and restrictions on imports or exports of specific goods, including narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. The conclusion of the contract is therefore in the interests of the Czech Republic, given that the contract is aimed at preventing and investigating breaches of customs legislation, which is particularly detrimental to its financial interests. Thank you for your attention.
