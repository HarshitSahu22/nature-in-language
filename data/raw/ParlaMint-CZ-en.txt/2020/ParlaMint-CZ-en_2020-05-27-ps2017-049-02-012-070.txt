This is therefore largely the same proposal, with only partial corrections. The proposed bill is, among other things, the implementation of the My Tax project - modern and simple - which, together with the support of simplification and electronisation of the tax system and general public administration, is one of the points of the government's programming declaration. In addition to a number of partial changes in the issue of the tax process, which are a response to the development of application practice and case-law, four basic topics are at the heart of the bill's proposal, namely the promotion of electronicisation, simplification of control procedures, revision of the penalty system and refund of tax deduction. Compared to the House of Press 580, amendments have been made to the bill, which compromise the subjects that were accused here on the floor of the Chamber of Deputies by the opposition, and which incorporate some of the changes required by the Senate. The following adjustments are in particular: A factual extension of the deadline for repayment of tax deductions from 30 to 45 days has been removed. In the event of a simultaneous determination of the tax deduction, the current situation will be maintained, which, given the current economic and social situation, does not so much interfere with cash flow payers of value added tax. This will remove the main reason for the opposition's dissenting position, i.e. it will not be done for the so-called return of the undisputed part of the excess deduction of the repayment of the part before the measurement, but only within the scope of the assessment, or during the course of tax control or the procedure to remove doubts. Following this, the Institute, i.e. the advance on tax deduction, is adjusted to be assessed as I have already said during the tax control or the procedure for eliminating doubts. Newly, there will be the possibility of individual remission of the fine for the late claim of the tax, which is not possible today. It is the work of Mr Skopeček's theme, which came up with this idea in the context of the discussion of Parliament's press 580, and the fulfillment of my promise to include this issue in the nearest amendment to the tax system. The flat-rate tolerance for which no interest on late payments shall be recovered shall not arise. At present, this tolerance is four working days and should be deleted and replaced by a stepwise tolerance in the original proposal. Now it returns to the bill in a shortened compromise period of three calendar days while maintaining the said tiered tolerance. There will be an increase in the interest on the wrongly fixed tax to twice as long as the wrongly fixed tax is recovered under the execution procedure. This change shall be found to apply in the event of execution on the basis of an unlawful freezing order. The format of the so-called form submission will be newly included in the facts set out in the Decree of the Ministry of Finance and will be explicitly expressed that, in the case of details of the facts proving the claims of the tax entity and other information necessary for the administration of taxes, the issue of the decree will be mandatory. A change is to be deleted which should have allowed the tax administrator, who receives a visual or audible record of this fact, not to have to notify in advance the persons involved in the act unless the nature and circumstances of the act permit it. In view of the time frame of the next legislative process, it is proposed to establish the effectiveness of the Act from 1 January 2021, which will ensure sufficient legisvacation for the implementation of the proposed amendments. This is adapted to the transitional provision concerning the extension of the deadline for the submission of electronic tax returns so that this benefit can be used already for the submission of tax returns for the tax period of 2020.
