ParlaMint-CZ_2020-10-22-ps2017-062-03-020-337.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the international Convention on the Measurement of Ships called TONNAGE is one of the international conventions adopted by the International Maritime Organisation for International Maritime Transport. Since the original Convention has been accepted only as a government contract without being ratified later, this lack needs to be amended, the material submitted for additional ratification. It shall be submitted including one amending resolution. Although no naval merchant ship currently makes use of the possibility of registration under the flag of the Czech Republic, the law resulting from equal access to maritime navigation for inland and sea states guaranteed by the international conventions has been effectively used by the Czech Republic in the past and nothing prevents its use in the future. Ratification of this Convention, including its annexes and amendments, is also necessary in order to fulfil the Czech Republic's commitment to membership of the European Union. Thank you for your attention.
