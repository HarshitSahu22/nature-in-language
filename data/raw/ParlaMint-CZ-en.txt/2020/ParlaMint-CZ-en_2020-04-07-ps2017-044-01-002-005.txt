Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to briefly mention the Government's proposal for a law on some credit redemptions in connection with the COVID-19 pandemic. The draft law responds to the expected negative consequences of the pandemic in the area of repayment of loans. Due to the crisis, existing debtors will become insolvent and will not be able to repay their loans either because of illness, quarantine, the need for child care, the decline in contracts, or directly as a result of government emergency measures. We proceeded to prepare the proposal after repeated failure to resolve the situation by self-regulation. It is true that some banks have approached the issue actively and proclientically, but unfortunately, even after repeated calls, the sector has failed to agree on a single solution with uniform parameters. Moreover, not all credit providers have started to offer their debtors the possibility of interruption or other modification of instalments. The proposal introduces the so-called the protection period during which, at the choice of the debtor, the repayment of the loan may be suspended for three or six months at the choice of the debtor. During this election, the consumer and the CFI do not pay the credit payments, all future instalments will shift for an appropriate period to the future, the principal will continue to be remunerated during the protection period, but interest will be paid in the case of consumers in the amount of the reprisal plus eight percentage points, which today corresponds to nine percentage points, i.e. interest on late payment under the Civil Code. Legal persons will also be able to use the protection period, but it is proposed that only principal payments be deferred after the protection period, but that interest will continue to be paid on an ongoing basis. In order for the debtor to be able to use the protection period, it will be sufficient to notify the creditor of his intention and to state that he is doing so as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic. Creditors do not examine the notification and will not be able to charge any fee for the delay in maturity. The introduction of this option significantly eliminates the risk of a massive increase in outstanding loans in the economy and the resulting consequences in the form of executions and insolvencys. Finally, I would like to say that I am ready to discuss all the constructive comments on the proposal. Thank you for the opportunity to discuss this proposal in a short procedure, because believe that many citizens, but especially small entrepreneurs, are waiting for this law. Thank you.
