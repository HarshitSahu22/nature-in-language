ParlaMint-CZ_2020-01-21-ps2017-040-01-003-003.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I present this law at second reading amending the Postal Services Act. This is an amendment which is an adaptation regulation to the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on cross-border parcel delivery services. This is a certain increase in price transparency, which obliges parcel delivery services operators, among others. the obligation to provide national regulatory authorities with information on their services, the number of consignments, their prices, etc. In other words, these data are provided to the Czech Telecommunications Authority within the Czech Republic and it is an indication of prices which are subsequently published and compared on the European Commission's website, with the fact that the content of the regulation is already being implemented in practice. The draft law has an adaptation character, it represents a minimalist implementation of the regulation. The proposal, on the basis of your decision, was addressed by the Economic Committee which recommended that the proposal be discussed, approved in the version of the approved amendment by the rapporteur, Mr Luzar. Its proposal concerns the modification of the originally proposed effective date. We agree, of course, with the proposed change. I would therefore like to thank the Economic Committee and the rapporteur and ask you to support the proposal. Thank you.
