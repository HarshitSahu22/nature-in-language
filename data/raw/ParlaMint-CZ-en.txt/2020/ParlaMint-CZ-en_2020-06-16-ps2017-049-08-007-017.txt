ParlaMint-CZ_2020-06-16-ps2017-049-08-007-017.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, at second reading, I present to you the law on certain measures relating to the ban on chemical weapons. The law is proposed by the State Office for Nuclear Safety and the Ministry of Industry and Trade is a representative of the applicant. The proposal was approved by the Government on 12 November last year, with the proposed law based on experience gained in the exercise of control by the State Office for Nuclear Safety, on all international activities based on the so-called "Nuclear Safety Authority'. Convention on the Prohibition of the Development, Production, Stockpiling and Use of Chemical Weapons and on their Destruction, on requirements for the protection of public interests in the field of chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear. In particular, it is necessary to bring the wording of the law into full compliance with the Convention. What exactly is happening? There is a relatively fundamental conceptual change in the area of banning the use of certain substances. Unlike the current wording of the Act, the Convention and the Amendment to the Law prohibit the use of all substances which are capable of causing death, or temporary incapacity or permanent injury to human or animal health, or the destruction of plants, and are therefore potentially exploitable. Some information obligations are also being clarified. The amendment reformes the criminal part of the law and specifies some information obligations. The amendment should contribute to strengthening the Czech Republic's ability to meet its international commitments to increase the efficiency of the administration. We believe that this is a problem-free law, and thank you for its support.
