ParlaMint-CZ_2020-01-21-ps2017-040-01-005-005.u8	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Colleagues, ladies and gentlemen, I would also like to bring you closer to the content of my amendment, to which I will still sign up in a detailed debate. This amendment concerns the community of owners of units, i.e. all citizens who own an apartment. According to the existing legislation and the proposed legislation, different rules are laid down for the adoption of the statutes of the associations of the owners of the units, where only some of them require the form of a authentic instrument, namely a notary registration, depending on who they are established by. This also depends on the requirements for the amendment of these statutes, where the amendment of the statutes of the SVJ, which have been adopted in the form of a notary registration, must continue to be carried out forever in the form of a notary registration, whereas, in the case of amendments to the statutes of the other SVJ, this is not the case, or it has not yet been established that this should be the case. In this context, I will note that the question of the form of the statutes of the owners of the units established before 1. 1. 2014 was already the subject of a judicial decision whereby each of the two Supreme Courts in the Czech Republic assessed the case differently, until it finally came to the conclusion that the Supreme Courts agreed not to require a notarial registration in the case of these associations of owners of units for the amendment of the statutes. However different ways of establishing a community of owners of units in the new Civil Code may justify different requirements for the form of statutes, in the case of their changes these different conditions are no longer justified in my opinion, because there is nothing in these functioning communities that makes them different. For this reason, I therefore propose an amendment which clearly states that the form of the authentic instrument is not required to amend the statutes of the members' communities. This will strengthen legal certainty in this matter and, at the same time, avoid further possible litigation on this issue, which could still arise. Finally, I note that this amendment has the support of the Association of Czech and Moravian Housing Cooperatives as an interest association of housing associations and associations of owners of units throughout the Czech Republic. Thank you for your attention.
