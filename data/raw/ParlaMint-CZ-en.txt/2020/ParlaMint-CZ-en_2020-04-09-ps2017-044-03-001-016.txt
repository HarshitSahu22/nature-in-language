Mr Vice-President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to briefly state the Government's proposal for a law on special rules for the exercise of the profession of a doctor and non-medical health profession by persons who have received vocational training in a Member State other than the European Union, in connection with emergency measures in the outbreak in 2020. This proposal responds to the current situation caused by the disease pandemic COVID 19 and aims to ensure sufficient health care workers at a time when part of their profession cannot perform because of quarantine and when health service providers are overwhelmed by a large number of acute patients. Therefore, it is primarily based on the initiative of hospitals themselves in case there would actually be some massive increase in patients and the risk of a loss of care in acute bed care providers. In order to achieve this objective, the proposed law will allow people who have foreign health education to be able to pursue a medical profession in the Czech Republic for the duration of the emergency period and in the next several months. They may be persons who have never applied for or for various reasons have not obtained the qualification of a medical profession, e.g. because they have not yet passed an aprobation test. Since it is a matter of ensuring the provision of health services during the duration of the emergency and for the necessary period after its termination, i.e. in a crisis situation, it is proposed that those persons may be employed directly by individual bed care providers or social care providers who have an urgent need to provide for the provision of health care by staff under the conditions laid down in this Act. However, they have an obligation to inform the Ministry of Health and the local regional authority, and also the Czech Medical Chamber in the case of doctors. In order to ensure the quality of health services, these persons will work under the direct professional guidance of a doctor with specialised competence in the relevant field, or in the case of non-medical professionals under the guidance of a non-medical healthcare professional who is competent for the sole pursuit of the profession. Thus, it will not be a separate exercise of the health profession as defined. Therefore, in a situation where students of medical faculties and students preparing for the exercise of non-medical health professions are also used to ensure the functioning of health services providers, it seems appropriate that graduates of foreign health schools or medical faculties who have not yet been recognised as qualified to pursue a medical profession in the territory of the Czech Republic may also be involved. Again, I'm saying that this motion is based on the initiative of hospitals in case there's any threat of failure of care within the given hospitals. So I'm asking for his support. Thank you.
