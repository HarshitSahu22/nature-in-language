ParlaMint-CZ_2020-09-29-ps2017-058-05-005-049.u4	Dear Vice-President, honourable Members and Colleagues, Act No. 117/2020 Sb., which was published in the Collection of Laws on 24 March this year, lays down the conditions for the provision of guide services in tourism in the Czech Republic. The activities of the guide outside the buildings and territories defined in the law of nature and landscape protection will be authorised according to the accepted regulation to be carried out only by the person who issued the Czech national guide card. The issued card will be required to use the guide in the conduct of the guide's work and will take effect on 1 November 2020, with the provision of the obligation to report the guide's activities only with the licence to take effect on 1 January 2021. The issue of the licence is subject to a fee of CZK 1,000. Given the current situation and impact of the disease pandemic COVID-19 on the provider of guide services, I submit an amendment proposing the postponement of the effectiveness of Act No. 117/2020 Coll. On 1 January 2021, except for Article 12 (2) (a) of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013, the following provisions shall apply: This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. That measure is intended to ensure that travel guides have sufficient time to adapt to the new legislation in the law. In order to mitigate the financial impact of the pandemic on the severely affected segment of the guide services, the proposal further envisages a temporary waiver of the fee obligation when issuing the Czech national guide card from 1 January 2021 to 28 February 2021. This estimated blackout of approximately CZK 2 million will be covered by the Ministry of Regional Development's budget. The draft law was approved by the Government of the Czech Republic at its meeting on 1. 6. The Government's bill aims to mitigate the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on guide providers. In view of this fact and because there is only a small change in scope, which consists in delaying the effectiveness of the law by two months and temporary waiver from the fee requirement for the issue of the Czech national card guide, the government proposes that the Chamber of Deputies of Parliament of the Czech Republic approve the draft law at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
