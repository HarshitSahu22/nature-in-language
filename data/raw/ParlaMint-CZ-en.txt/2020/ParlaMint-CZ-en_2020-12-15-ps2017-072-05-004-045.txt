The proposed legislation will apply only to the national rail, regional railways, railway vehicles operated on these routes. What is the aim of the directive on this interoperability? To define more comprehensively and clearly the conditions for the overall compatibility of the rail system in the European Union, to support the development of rail transport services in the European Union and to complete the creation of a single European railway area. It can be summed up that the bill includes extensive conceptual changes to the existing legislation for which the railway sector is impatiently waiting. The address of this regulation is a number of different entities from railway operators and carriers through railway vehicle holders, railway vehicle maintenance bodies, contracting entities, interoperability constituents manufacturers and various other infrastructure subsystems, or railway vehicles. The draft law also significantly regulates the competence of the railway office. It shall also include the transfer of selected competences to the Union institutions. In view of the specified transposition period of the two Directives and the expected entry into force of a number of related regulations, the adoption of an appropriate implementation adjustment should be considered key in the foreseeable future.
