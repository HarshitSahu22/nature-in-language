Proposal for the election of members of the Czech Radio Council In the election of members of the Council of the Czech Radio - there are two seats - the results were as follows. René Levínský 22, Ondřej Matouš 83, Josef Nerušil 28, Nina Nováková 7, Jaroslav Šebek 63 and Ivan Vodochodský 72 votes. Therefore, no one has reached the Kvora, no one has been elected and twice the number of vacant seats, i.e. 4 candidates, is proceeding to the second round. They are Ondřej Matouš with 83 votes, Ivan Vodochodský with 72, Jaroslav Šebek with 63 votes and Josef Nerušil with 28 votes.
