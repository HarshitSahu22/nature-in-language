ParlaMint-CZ_2020-02-13-ps2017-040-11-009-195.u2	Thank you for the floor. I present this contract as Vice Prime Minister Havlíček. Mr Vice-President, ladies and gentlemen, Curaçao is one of the four autonomous countries of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, Aruba, Saint Martin and the Netherlands themselves, located in the South Caribbean. The relations between the two countries in the field of civil aviation have not yet been modified in any way. In August 2015, the Czech Republic was approached with a proposal for a contractual adjustment of these relations and in 2016 agreement was reached on the text of the Air Agreement at expert level. The Agreement contains the usual provisions on the conditions for the performance of commercial air transport between the Contracting Parties. The text of the agreement is liberal and corresponds to current developments in civil aviation. This agreement can in future contribute to the development of air transport from the Czech Republic to the Caribbean region, where Czech air carriers are already operating their flights. The agreement provides them with a legal basis for the possible expansion of their business activities in this area. The strategic location of Curaçao in the middle of the American continent, along with liberal politics, is a prerequisite for further strengthening tourism and financial and logistics areas on the island. I would therefore like to ask you to refer the matter to the committees. Thank you.
