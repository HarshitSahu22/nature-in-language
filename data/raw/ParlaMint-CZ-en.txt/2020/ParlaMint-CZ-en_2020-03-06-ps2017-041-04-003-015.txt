ParlaMint-CZ_2020-03-06-ps2017-041-04-003-015.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the bill tabled is aimed at selected topics which must be subject to specific national regulation in order to fulfil the obligations arising from the membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. The proposal for a law can be divided into four main areas, which I have already represented here at first reading, but I will nevertheless allow the four main areas to be briefly re-interpreted. The first of the modified areas is the issue of management time requirements. large vehicles. These are safety breaks, they are rest periods, they are periods of keeping and keeping records of all these facts, and the draft law provides for the obligation of carriers to ensure these requirements, in accordance with the Union regulation. The second of these revised areas is the issue of control, administrative punishment for possible non-compliance with these requirements, which I said in that point number one, or again the driving time, it is a safety break, it is a rest period, etc. It should be said that this right, which is extended - the right to collect bail under the Road Transport Act - will be newly available in particular to transport authorities within the meaning of this Act. Thus, the draft of this law also imposes an explicit ban on the production, offer, or promotion, sale or assembly of equipment which is intended for the unauthorised change of data kept by the tachograph. The third of these revised areas is the issue of issuing and handing over tachograph cards, the management of the relevant information system, whereby the purpose or design of the law is to make a number of relatively partial changes in terms of issuing and handing over driver cards, business cards, workshop cards, checking cards, etc. [[9.20 hours]] The fourth of these revised areas is the issue of the conditions of professional competence of drivers selected by large vehicles, while the draft law extends the exemption from the requirement of professional competence of drivers. In addition to teaching, training will also become a mandatory part of the regular training of drivers and, at the same time, the partial provision of training through eLearning or simulator, so we are also trying to use new technologies. Following the adoption of the draft law, drivers from third countries will be allowed to demonstrate professional competence by means of a driver certificate issued by the transport authority in accordance with a specific regulation. Three amendments to the draft law were adopted by the Economic Committee. This is a disbandment of the local jurisdiction of the transport authorities in relation to the driver's authorisation of taxi service and, in the case of applicants for a driver's authorisation of taxi service from third countries, the requirement to prove the right to stay in the Czech Republic. The second amendment states that the obligation to equip a vehicle with a proof of cargo and to retain it relates only to large vehicles. Finally, the third amendment was, in order to extend some exceptions to the scope of this Regulation, to remove the shortening of the heading from which transport must be carried out over 50 km from the carrier's establishment, in simple terms, we are trying to make it easier for the carriers to have a simpler regime within 50 km of the registered office. The Ministry of Transport has expressed a positive opinion on all these amendments, we agree with them and we recommend that this be respected. Thank you all for your attention.
