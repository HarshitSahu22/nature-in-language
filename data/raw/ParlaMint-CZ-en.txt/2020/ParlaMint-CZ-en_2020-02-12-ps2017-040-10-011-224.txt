Proposal for appointment of members of the Supervisory Board of the Technology Agency of the Czech Republic The Chamber of Deputies has been successful. 179 ballots were issued, 178 were submitted and one was not surrendered. Kvorum necessary for the election - and this will be the same for all three elections - is 90 and Martin Kobert won 102 votes in this election, Mirka Wildmannová 25. Martin Kobert was therefore elected a member of the Monitoring Board of the Technology Agency and this option ends. Another choice was similarly successful.
