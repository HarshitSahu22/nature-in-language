ParlaMint-CZ_2020-01-31-ps2017-040-08-003-060.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr Vice-President, colleagues, I will be very brief here. It's called. the amendment law, which we present as standard in these situations in the interests of clarity, so that accompanying amendments to the Waste Act and to the End-of-life Act are independent into the proposed amendment law. The proposed bill includes a total of nine laws. Perhaps the most important is the Local Charges Act and the most important amendment proposed by the Amendment Act is actually a new amendment to the municipal waste charge. Newly, this issue will only be regulated in the Local Charges Act, thus eliminating the existing two-track regulation. The amendment to this law was processed in close cooperation with the Ministry of Finance as his gesturer. The municipality will be able to choose one of the two types of fees with respect to its specifics and needs. The first option is a flat-rate fee based on the registration of a taxpayer in a given municipality, or on the ownership of an immovable property used for housing or recreation in which no person is registered. The second option is the charge which is derived from the actual amount of waste produced. The municipalities are therefore given flexibility in the choice of one of the types of charge, which was one of the requirements of the Union of Towns and Municipalities in the context of this law. Municipalities can work with different types of fees even now and no application problems are foreseen. Thank you.
