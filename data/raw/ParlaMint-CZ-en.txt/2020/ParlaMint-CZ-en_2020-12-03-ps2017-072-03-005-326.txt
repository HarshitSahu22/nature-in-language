ParlaMint-CZ_2020-12-03-ps2017-072-03-005-326.u2	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, I present a contractual document relating to international taxation. This is historically the first double taxation treaty with Senegal signed in Dakar on 22 January 2020. The foreign policy of the Czech Republic is certainly aimed at developing mutually beneficial economic relations with other countries of the world. The conclusion of this international tax treaty follows the intention to limit taxpayers' tax burden in the absence of an unintended international double taxation, which is normally due to the extension of the tax treaty in international economic relations. The Double Taxation Treaty, which is currently being proposed in relations between the Czech Republic and Senegal, was prepared on the basis of OECD and UN model models, and, of course, also responds to the internal tax rules of both countries. It can be concluded that the text of the contract fully reflects developments and trends in the international tax field. This tax agreement will enable the coordination of the activities of the tax authorities of both States to limit possible tax evasion and, last but not least, will also enable the relevant exchange of information, in accordance with all existing international standards. The negotiation of this double taxation treaty will increase the legal certainty of potential investors in both states. Its closure and implementation will not have a direct impact on the state budget. The overall contribution resulting from the application of this Treaty will depend in practice on the future development of the various forms of economic cooperation. In this context, it can be noted that the balance of mutual trade is significantly in favour of our country due to relatively low imports from Senegal, with Senegal joining the largest trading partners of the Czech Republic in sub-Saharan Africa. The double taxation treaty between the Czech Republic and Senegal will certainly open up space for other Czech business entities, thus helping to develop mutual economic relations, including investment activities. Therefore, I dare ask you to support this treaty and the commandments to the House Committees. Thank you.
