The purpose of the contract presented is to provide the security authorities with the necessary legal framework for effective cooperation in the fight against all forms of crime, to lay down the basic rules for their contacts and the principles for the management of the information transmitted. Cooperation will include the exchange of operational and strategic information, the creation of working teams, the broadcasting of consultants and liaison officers, as well as cooperation in the search for persons in the provision of aircraft security escorts, etc. The Treaty also provides for the requirements of the request for cooperation, the possibility of non-cooperation, the payment of costs, the protection of personal data and the transmission of classified information. Last but not least, the Treaty gives the Czech Party the opportunity to share information provided by the Georgian Party with other EU Member States or the Schengen area.
