ParlaMint-CZ_2020-09-29-ps2017-058-05-006-050.u4	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. The Government submitted this draft law to the Chamber of Deputies on 2 June this year. The proposer, who is the Ministry of Industry and Trade, proposed to discuss this law so that the House could agree with it at first reading according to the relevant provisions of the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies. The subject matter of the amendment to the draft law, as already stated by the appellant, is certain rights and obligations in the area of demonstrating the qualification conditions laid down for the performance of certain works. Their list contains an annex to the law, the purpose of which is in particular to reduce some negative effects caused by emergency measures in the Coronavirus epidemic. It is a draft law of limited duration, intended to cover employment relationships lasting between 12 March 2020 and 31 December 2020. To put it simply, this is a fiction of fulfilling the qualifying assumptions, where it is considered that an employee who has fulfilled the qualifying conditions laid down for the performance of a particular work on 11 March 2020 meets these assumptions even during the period lasting until the end of the calendar year 2020. For that period, the employer should not be obliged to carry out periodic training of employees for the purpose of demonstrating compliance with qualification conditions except in cases where such training is carried out by the employer by electronic means. It is a fact that some of the restrictive measures caused by the emergency situation of standard compliance with the legal requirements for the qualification of employees made it very difficult, if not impossible. And it is also an undoubted fact that demanding employers now, at the time of the emerging economic crisis, to meet certain regulatory conditions backwards or additionally, but for a limited period of time also newly, would mean further negative interference in rebooting their activities particularly financially. For these reasons, the proposed adjustment is a necessary and positive step that will temporarily give our employers and employees the proverbial helping hand. In view of the latest regulation in the Act on the Collection of Laws, the effectiveness of the Act is exceptionally proposed on the first day following the publication of this Act, given that this Act applies only to employment relationships lasting between 12 March 2020 and 31 December 2020. Thank you.
