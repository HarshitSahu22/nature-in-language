Colleagues, colleagues, whales I had to take personally, so don't let Karel Havlíček take it personally, but I just had to comment on this myself. But I'll be brief. Allow me to say something very briefly about a government proposal to approve the ratification of amendments to the International Whale Regulation Convention schedule. The delegation of the Czech Republic at the meetings in 2016 and 2018 performed in accordance with the position of government and also in agreement with the coordinated position of the European Union. The amendments to this timetable relate in particular to the extension of zero quotas for commercial whaling and the establishment of new quotas for so-called indigenous whale fishing for livelihoods. I would just like to be here because sometimes there are questions about why the Czech Republic is a member of the Whaling Convention, if only the whales in our rivers do not sail too much, so it is simply because we are a member of the European Union and that the membership of the Czech Republic has been a party to the Convention since 2005, as well as other members of the European Union contribute to defending and promoting the interests of the European Union in the area of marine biodiversity protection, and I dare say that the Czech Republic also contributes to balancing the voice of those, say, votes for whale hunting, such as Japan and some other states, and thanks to this, it has managed to improve certain populations or stocks of whales in the world seas in recent years. So it's also our contribution to something that may not be directly related to us, but I think it's the right thing to do. So on my behalf and on behalf of the whales, I would like to ask you to agree to ratify this convention. Thank you.
