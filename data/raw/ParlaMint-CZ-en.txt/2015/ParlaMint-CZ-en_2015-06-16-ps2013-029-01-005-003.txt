ParlaMint-CZ_2015-06-16-ps2013-029-01-005-003.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce the amendment to Act No. 563/1991 Coll., on Accounting, and some other laws, discussed at first reading on 11 March 2015. I would like to remind you that the amendments to the Act are related to the implementation of a fundamental act of the European Union in the field of accounting, i.e. Directive 2013/34/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on annual accounts, consolidated accounts and related reports of certain types of undertakings. The proposed bill contains a new adjustment of the simple accounting regime and the possibility of its application by very small accounting units, subject to the conditions laid down. This adjustment also brought about minor technical changes to the Income Tax Act and the Reserves Act for the determination of the income tax base. The adoption of the amendment to the Accounting Act will be followed up by changes in the relevant sub-legal standards, decrees and standards of the Ministry of Finance. The proposal was discussed in the Committee on Budgets, which recommended Parliament to give its approval to the Government proposal, as amended by the amendments adopted. I agree with the amendments adopted by the Committee on Budgets. I would therefore like to ask you to release the motion submitted by the Chamber of Deputies until the third reading. Thank you for your attention.
