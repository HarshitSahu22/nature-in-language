ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-07-ps2013-029-05-001-173.u2	Ladies and gentlemen, good afternoon. The Organizing Committee shall submit to the Chamber of Deputies a proposal to lend or award state honours which, in accordance with the wording of Section 8 of Act No. 157/1994 Coll., on state honours of the Czech Republic, presents to the President of the Republic. As of 11 February, the Sub-Committee for the preparation of a proposal for loan or award addressed the initiatives of citizens, organisations and MPs and 59 proposals were submitted on 20 May, of which 26 were in memoriam. Members adopted 42 proposals, of which 14 in memoriam. On the basis of the defence of the adopted proposals, the subcommittee selected and submitted 33 proposals to the Organising Committee by its fourth resolution of 21 May 2015, including ten in memoriam, in the following composition: Order of the White Lion one, proposal in memoriam, řád of G Masaryk 5 proposals, of which 3 in memoriam, Medal of Heroism 2 proposals, Medal of Merit 25 proposals, of which 6 in memoriam. The Organising Committee has accepted and presented them to the Chamber of Deputies for discussion and for adoption of the resolution by the Fourth Resolution of the Sub-Committee for the preparation of a proposal for granting or granting state honours, and I would therefore like to present the motion for a resolution: The Chamber of Deputies submits to the President of the Republic pursuant to Section 8 of Act No. 157/1994 Coll., on state honours of the Czech Republic, proposals I. on the loan of the Order of the White Lion of the military group referred to under No. 1, II. to lend the order of Tomáš Garrigu Masaryk listed under numbers 1 to 5, III. to award the Medal of Heroism listed under Nos 1 and 2, IV. to award the Medal of Merit listed under numbers 1 to 25. So much for a motion for a resolution.
