ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-003-209.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the proposed subsidy programme has been drawn up and prepared in accordance with the accompanying resolution of the Agricultural Committee from January this year on the Ministry of Agriculture subsidy programme for 2015. The subsidy programme is intended for fishing areas in which the exercise of fishing law forms an integral part of recreational fishing. The area of exercise of fishing law exercised in inland waters is not integrated into the framework of the common agricultural policy of the European Union and therefore there is no reason for the proposed subsidy title to be notified by the European Union. The financial support from national resources through the programme presented does not overlap with the measures of the operational programme Fisheries and other subsidy programmes. The draft funding from the State budget of the chapter Agriculture for the K2015 subsidy programme is set at 15 million. CZK for the economic result subsidy, i.e. non-investment grant. The subsidy will be implemented by the calculated amount per hectare of the area of the fishing area received by the users of the fishing area. The amount of the subsidy for the beneficiary will be determined on the basis of the CZK coefficient per hectare calculated from the total hectares of the fishing areas of all applicants and the funds available. Each applicant shall receive a subsidy of the amount calculated from the total area of the fishing areas for which it proves to be a user, multiplied by the coefficient. The maximum limit is CZK 500 per hectare. The reason for submitting this new subsidy title intended only for 2015 is the fact that there are a number of different negative factors for fish stocks in fishing areas in the Czech Republic. These are systemic and long-term effects that are manifested gradually on the fish population, their species composition, number and quality, including stability, sustainability and resilience, one of these important factors being the cormoran of great importance. The purpose of the subsidy programme is therefore to support the biological diversity of fish stocks in surface waters designated by the user of fishing areas. The K2015 subsidy programme - Support for non-production functions of fishing areas was Resolution No. 866 approved by the Government of 26. 10.; 92. by order of the Committee on Agriculture, 24th Meeting of 11. 11. The Agricultural Committee recommends: The Chamber of Deputies will approve this subsidy programme. The subsidy programme in question is prepared for administration on the basis of the Ministry of Principles issued. So important, I guess. Thank you for your attention. I beg you to approve this grant title, which has been discussed with the fishing public and which is very much needed for this year. Thank you.
