ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-019-136.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, at the meeting of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic, a double taxation treaty is being submitted with the Government of the Islamic Republic of Iran, signed in Prague on 30 April 2015. This treaty certainly responds to the internal tax rules of both countries, and has been agreed on the basis of OECD and UN model models at expert level. Thus, no such treaty is currently applied to the mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and Iran, so undesirable international double taxation may arise. As a standard, the Treaty provides for basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of both countries, as well as the principle of non-discrimination, informal resolution of disputes arising from its implementation, and ensures the relevant exchange of information between the competent authorities of the two States. An integral part of the contract is also a protocol which guarantees the right to apply their national legislation to both States in order to prevent tax evasion and fraud and also responds to the situation where Iran is not able to fully exchange bank information at the moment and obliges it to inform the Czech Party immediately about the changes made in this area. The negotiated contract does not replace national tax rules and cannot therefore create a new tax obligation, but only regulates the obligation arising from national rules. They address cases where the right to levy tax is enshrined in their national law by both signatory States. In these situations, the treaty is certainly superior to the national regulation. Over time, some restrictive measures against Iran have taken effect, implying certain barriers to trade in various types of goods, equipment, technology, etc. Despite these measures, the double taxation treaty is very important for our business entities. The Double Taxation Treaty will certainly increase the legal certainty of potential investors in both states. On the basis of the above, I propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take into account those facts and order this treaty and the protocol for it to the relevant House Committees at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
