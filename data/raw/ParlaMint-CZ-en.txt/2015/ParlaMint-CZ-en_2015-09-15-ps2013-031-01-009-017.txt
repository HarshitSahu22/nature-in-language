The aim is to adopt common European Union rules on the granting of international protection and to prevent abuse of this system. For example, measures such as new arrangements for the treatment of repeated applications for international protection are proposed. This should prevent abuse of international protection proceedings for the purpose of the unjustified extension of these applications and of the time limits associated with them. Furthermore, the automatic suspensory effect of the appeal should be abolished. Furthermore, we are dealing with the assessment of specific needs in vulnerable applicants for international protection.
