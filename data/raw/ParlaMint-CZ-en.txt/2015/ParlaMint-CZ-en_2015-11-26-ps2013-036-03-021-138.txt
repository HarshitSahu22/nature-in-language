ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-021-138.u2	Hello, Mr Vice-President, Members. I here submit a treaty because the Institute of the International Labour Organisation requires the Government to refer to Parliament every convention, protocol and recommendation of this organisation within one year of their adoption, including an opinion on them. The aim of this obligation is, above all, to inform the legislator about the current development of international law in the field of labour and social affairs. This is where the material is presented, where the three standards are adopted at last year's conference. It is the Protocol to Convention No 29 on forced labour, then on accompanying recommendations for this protocol and amendments to the Convention on Maritime Labour, which the Czech Republic has not yet ratified. The first protocol focuses primarily on preventing forced labour and protecting victims in terms of their rights, particularly in criminal proceedings, rights to social rehabilitation and assistance, and stresses the need to improve international cooperation in the field - as I see it, there are heated debates taking place outside the International Labour Organisation. [[A Member of the Negro House discussing in the left side of the hall.]] In the fight against trafficking in human beings. This material therefore includes a proposal for ratification of this Protocol. As far as the Sea Labour Convention is concerned, even though we have not ratified it, we need to present it. Thank you.

ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-021-138.u4	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I think the Minister has said everything that this protocol is about. I think we all agree that the ban on forced or compulsory labour is one of the fundamental human rights and that forced and compulsory labour violates human rights and the dignity of millions of men and women and, unfortunately, children around the world. Without repeating this, I will say that the protocol, including other proposed recommendations and amendments, corresponds to the current Czech national legislation and is in practice in accordance with constitutional order and other international treaties, the Czech Republic is a signatory of. Therefore, in order to comply with our obligations arising from membership of the International Labour Organisation, I recommend that the Government of the Czech Republic agree to ratify the Protocol on the International Labour Organisation Convention No. 29 on forced labour and take note of Recommendation No 29 of the International Labour Organisation. 203 on additional measures for forced labour and the 2004 amendment to the Convention on Work at Sea. Thank you.
