ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-23-ps2013-033-08-003-091.u4	Hello, Mr President, Dear Government, fellow Members. The bill on compensation for loss of employment for political reasons between 1948 and 1989 to former professional soldiers and employees of the Ministry of Defence and amending certain laws was served to Members 22. 12. 2014. The main facts on this have already been told by Mr. Deputy Speaker Antonín Seďa. Let me reiterate that the main aim of this proposal is to pay a regular monthly compensation allowance, namely (a) to people who have been persecuted by the previous regime by the loss of employment in the armed forces, namely CZK 1,500 a month, and (b) to widowers and widowers of persons who have been persecuted by the previous regime by the loss of employment in the armed forces, namely CZK 750. This has not been the case for the citizens of the Czech Republic, former soldiers and employees of the Department of Defence whose service or employment is terminated for political reasons and for which the declared participation in judicial rehabilitation has already been properly compensated, but for the remaining groups of injured persons who, for political reasons of employment, have not been deprived of their personal freedoms. [[Did they come?]] These are the main reasons for submitting this proposal. Let me also reiterate the information that the Government expressed its opposition to this proposal on 19 January, mainly because it does not count on an increase of that amount in the State budget for the next year, and also on account of other possible undefined costs associated with the possible payment of compensation. This means, above all, the costs that administration, registration and payment would bear. Let me conclude by saying that, of course, with a full understanding of this intention, it would be good to consider not only the real financial possibilities of the Czech Republic, but also that there would be many similar groups affected by the former regime in our society. Thank you.
