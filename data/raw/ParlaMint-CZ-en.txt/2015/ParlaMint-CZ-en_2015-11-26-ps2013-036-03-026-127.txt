ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-026-127.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to justify the Government's proposal to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to express its agreement to ratify the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Principality of Monaco on the exchange of information on tax matters. The Agreement was signed in Monaco on 31 July 2014. It was signed for the Czech Republic by Ambassador Marie Chatardová, Minister of Foreign Affairs José Badia for the Government of the Principality of Monaco. The negotiations with jurisdiction with the preferential tax regime have resulted in 14 final draft texts of this type of international treaty, which are subsequently undergoing a legislative process leading to ratification by the President. Nine agreements have already entered into force, which are announced in the Collection of International Treaties. The object of the agreement is the taxes levied on which the parties agreed in the course of expert negotiations. These are income taxes and property transfer taxes or real estate taxes. VAT is deducted from the agreements, as Monaco is in the customs union with France and European Union rules govern VAT in the exchange of information. Information provided under this Agreement shall be subject to tax confidentiality. By concluding the agreement, the Czech Republic will be able to request the competent authority of the Contracting State for information that is relevant to the finding, determination and security of payment of taxes, or to investigate or prosecute tax offences. However, due to the legal constraints laid down by the domestic law, cooperation in obtaining evidence reserved for judicial authorities in criminal matters does not include cooperation. The Contracting Parties shall also undertake to provide, upon request, information held by banks or other financial institutions. The application of the agreement can be expected to give Czech tax administrators a tax which Czech tax residents would not have paid, as well as an increase in the tax discipline of domestic residents for the future. The negotiation of this agreement should contribute to a favourable fiscal impact on the income of the Czech Republic and to increased prevention of financial crime in general. I therefore recommend agreeing to ratify this agreement. Thank you for your attention.
