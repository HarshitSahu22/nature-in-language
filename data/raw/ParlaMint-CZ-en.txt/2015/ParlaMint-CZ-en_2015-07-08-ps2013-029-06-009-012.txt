I'll justify the motion. The basic obligation of banks and consumer and credit cooperatives and other creditors offering or lending to consumers as creditors is to assess with professional care the ability of the consumer to repay the loan provided. It is from the diction of Act No 145, on consumer credit and amending certain laws, as amended, which states that the creditor is not only obliged to take account of information submitted to him by the consumer but, if necessary, may consult databases that offer relevant information for the purpose of assessing consumer credit. This is the link to the Czech Social Security Administration. The provisions of Annex 3 to Decree-Law No 163, on the performance of the activities of banks, consumer and credit cooperatives and securities traders, directly obliges creditors to ensure - I quote - that they have information available that will enable them to assess the financial and economic credit of the counterparty prior to trade. The debtor shall prohibit the negotiation of the transaction without the assessment of the counterparty's credit. This follows, in particular, from the Bank Act and the Implementing Decree, in relation to consumer loans, from the Consumer Credit Act. This is such basic information on the amendment. The proposal has no other negative effects. It is basically the use of the ČSSZ database for assessing the bonita of individual applicants for consumer credit. In a detailed debate, I will formally apply to this amendment, which is in the database under number 2614.
