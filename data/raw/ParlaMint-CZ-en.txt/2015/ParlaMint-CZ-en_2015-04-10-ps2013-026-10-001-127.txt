ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-10-ps2013-026-10-001-127.u4	On 19 March 2015, the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic discussed the amendment to the Energy Management Act, which implements the Energy Efficiency Directive and subsequently implements the already transposed Energy Performance Directive of buildings and the Energy Efficiency Directive of the end-user and energy services. I have already introduced you to the substance of the proposed amendment and the main reasons for its submission in its discussion by the Chamber of Deputies. For this reason, I would now like to focus on the provisions which are the main topic of the ongoing discussions. The reason for returning the amendment of the Act to the Chamber of Deputies is the lack of consensus in the Senate in the discussion on the proposed wording of Section 7 para. Article 4 (1) (a) and (b) (f) and (g) a law relating to the obligation to install heat energy equipment. Similar discussions have already been held during the vote on amendments here in the Chamber of Deputies. In particular, it was discussed whether the proposed text goes beyond the requirements of the directive. The response to this discussion was the award of a study which is already being carried out by ČVUT, which will address the cost efficiency of the installation of heat meters. Amendments to the Senate are governed by the provisions of Section 7 (1) of the Rules of Procedure. Article 4 (1) (a) and (b) (g) in particular so that, after completion of the study, its conclusions can be easily implemented into the legal order of the Czech Republic at the level of the implementing legislation. The Senate amendment is an acceptable solution for the formulation of the abovementioned obligation. It does not interfere with the installation obligation, which results from the requirements of the directive, for which insufficient implementation is conducted with the Czech Republic the infringement procedure for the EU Treaty on the Functioning of the Energy sector. At the same time, however, it gives room for rapid legislative adjustment according to the study's conclusion, i.e. allows for exemptions from the obligation in cases where the installation of equipment registering the supply of heat has been cost-inefficient. The second two amendments were the proposals made by Senator Seitlová, who even more clearly explain the rights and obligations of homeowners, so as not to endanger privacy. We thought that this was protected by the new Civil Code, but I do not have a problem with this once more being highlighted in this law. End of závěr. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I agree with the amendments of the Senate to the Parliament of the Czech Republic and recommend that we adopt the bill as approved by the Senate. Thank you for your attention.
