Dear members of the government, esteemed colleagues, submitted an amendment to the Armed Forces Act, as the Minister of Defence said, responds to the amendment of the defence legislation and defines the concepts that are already being used in our army. The basic change is the interference of military rescue units and their replacement by a new institute assigned by the military service to carry out the tasks of civil defence under the Geneva Convention. The amendment specifies operational deployment and task force, the novelty is the re-inclusion of the definition of military oath. The composition of a military oath is a necessary condition for the performance of a military active service. A new ban on the organisation of political assemblies and electoral campaigns in military facilities and training units is being introduced. Pursuant to Section 7, the Government, on a proposal from the Minister of Defence, is to determine the systemization of the General Staff of the Army of the Czech Republic for the relevant calendar year to the extent specified by the law. Also, Section 44b introduces a new risk premium for soldiers operating in foreign operations.
