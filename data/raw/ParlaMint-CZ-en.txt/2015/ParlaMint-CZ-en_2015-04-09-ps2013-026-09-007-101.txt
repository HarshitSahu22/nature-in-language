ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-09-ps2013-026-09-007-101.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, in the first half of last year The Chamber of Deputies received a total of fifteen petitions, but the number of signatures has already reached 280,383 signatures, and there are also cases where individual petitions are supported by citizens on the Internet. Thematically, these petitions were focused on a wide range of topics. Of the most numerous references to the petition organised by the People's Rights Party, the Zemanovci demand the adoption of a law on proof of origin of income and property, including the possibility of seizure of illegally held property. A petition by the citizens of Havířov also addressed a similar topic. A more significant petition was a petition signed by more than 110,000 petents demanding the rescue of the rural post office, and a petition of more than 10,000 signatures involved disagreement with the implementation of the plan of extraction and treatment of gravel sandstone in Uherské Ostroh no. 2. In detail, I refer to the press release 312.
