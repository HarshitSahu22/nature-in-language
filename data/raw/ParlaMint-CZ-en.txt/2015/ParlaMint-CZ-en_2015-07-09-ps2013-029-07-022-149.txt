Dear colleagues, on the submitted material Government proposal to extend the duration of the British Military Advisory and Training Team in the Czech Republic in the Czech Republic, which was approved by the Government of the Czech Republic by a resolution of 27 April this year, I would like to say a few syllables more than the previous materials. I hope you'll forgive me. The British team has been active in the Czech Republic since 2000 on the basis of Parliament's approval, which has agreed to stay for a period of five years. In 2005 and then in 2010, Parliament agreed to extend its stay for a further period of five years. This last five-year deadline will expire on 9 October this year. The activities of the British team, consisting of a maximum of 30 persons, consist in organizing courses for Petty Officers of the Czech Army and the armed forces of NATO Member States, the EU and also States participating in the Partnership for Peace programme. There is still a great deal of interest in these courses. The British Party has requested an extension of its team's duration in our territory. Therefore, I believe that continuing the activities of the British team is in the interests of the Czech Republic. The government therefore proposes further extension of the duration of its operation on our territory for another five years and further proposes to agree to the stay of foreign participants in the courses organised by this British team in the number of up to 100 people in the individual course. Thank you for your attention and I would like to ask you to agree to the proposal. Thank you.
