ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-007-184.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the material presented informs on the political, military and financial aspects of the activities of Czech troops in Afghanistan, Kosovo, Bosnia and Herzegovina, the Sinai Peninsula, the UK, Iceland and the Republic of Mali. Last year, the Czech Republic was particularly involved in the AISAF operation in Afghanistan, our activities were focused on three areas: 1. construction of Afghan security forces; 2. protection of allied forces; 3. support for deployed forces. The material also includes information on the implementation of rapid impact projects in Afghanistan, which are intended to support the construction of civil infrastructure in the country. In 2014, a total of 29 of these projects were carried out with a total value of CZK 1.89 million, focusing mainly on the field of education. In 2014, we spent CZK 1.43 billion on the involvement of our forces and resources in foreign operations, which meant 3,4 percent of the defence budget for the year in question. This amount was fully paid from the budget chapter of the Ministry of Defence. [[12.30 hours]] The work in foreign operations has a significant contribution to maintaining and restoring international peace and security, and the experience gained in the operations is then used to further improve the quality of training of our forces and significantly translate into the preparation of new units, broadcast into foreign operations. Thank you for your attention.
