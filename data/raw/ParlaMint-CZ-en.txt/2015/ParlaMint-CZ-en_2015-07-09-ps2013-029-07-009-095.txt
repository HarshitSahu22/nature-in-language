ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-009-095.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, civil aviation relations between the Czech Republic and Hong Kong are regulated by the Air Services Agreement signed in 2002. In order to bring this agreement into line with Union law, its amendment has been negotiated with the Hong Kong Party. The main modifications take place in the articles relating to the designation of air carriers and the withdrawal or suspension of their operating authorisations. The amendment to the Agreement was signed in Hong Kong on 14 August 2014. The negotiation of an amendment to the agreement does not require adjustments to our legal order and its implementation will not have an impact on the expenditure of the state budget. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic for the referral of material for discussion in the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
