ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-008-050.u1	50. Government bill amending Act No. 20/1987 Coll., on State Monument Care, as amended /house print 473/ - first reading Once again, I beg the Minister of Culture.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-008-050.u2	Thank you. Mr President, colleagues, let me briefly justify the Government's proposal for a technical amendment to the State Heritage Act. Sightseeing zones as well as sightseeing reservations are instruments of the area protection of the monument fund with a long tradition. The values of the historical cores of cities are currently perceived by local authorities as carriers of their identity and their pride. Most of the items listed in the UNESCO World Heritage List in the Czech Republic are monument reserves or landmark zones. The main reason for the bill is the establishment of an explicit legal authorisation for the form of the declaration of landmark zones pursuant to Section 6 of the Act. Another important reason is to allow changes in the declarations for existing landmark zones by introducing this uniform form as well as for any changes to existing acts, whether the decrees or regulations of regional national committees, or the decrees of the Ministry of Culture, which have been declared in the past. To this end, it is proposed in Section 6 of the Act on State Monument Care to provide for the form of measures of a general nature for the declaration of conservation zones by the Ministry of Culture and for possible changes to already declared conservation zones to be allowed under the transitional provision. The amendment responds to the development of the rule of law, when the scope for declaring landmark zones from regional national committees to the Ministry of Culture was transferred and a new administrative order was adopted, which offers the possibility of using a new institute of measures of a general nature. Although this institute can be used for newly declared landmark zones without amendment of the law, it cannot be used for changes to existing, already declared landmark zones by existing legal acts. Therefore, the proposed adjustment does not constitute the introduction of a new authorisation. The substantive competence established in Section 6 of the State Heritage Act is maintained. The proposal merely ensures continuity in declaring other territories as landmark zones if they fulfil the legal characteristics and, above all, will enable them to respond to these changes following any changes in the state of knowledge of cultural values of the declared landmark zones and, at the same time, avoid any doubt as to the further progress of the Ministry of Culture in this matter. This is therefore a technical amendment. I therefore propose that the Chamber of Deputies release this bill to second reading. Thank you for your attention.
