ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-07-ps2013-033-02-004-036.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present to you the bill tabled. You have before you a bill amending the Capital Market Business Act and other related laws. In particular, the draft law aims at the implementation of European legislation, namely the revised transparent directive with a transposition deadline of 26 November 2015, and the Omnibus II Directive with a transposition period of 31 March 2015 and with effect from 1 January 2016, a directly enforceable regulation on the improvement of securities trading in the European Union and CSDs, and the UTCS Directive governing the activities of depositories, in principle remuneration and penalties, with a transposition period of 18 March 2016. [[10.00 hours]] Further amendments to the laws concerned are caused by interpretational uncertainties and the need to respond to market developments and reflect experience. The proposed changes follow the attractiveness of the fund's business, the simplification of process issues in the capital market, as well as the harmonisation of administrative charges in the financial market segment. The draft law was consulted and created in cooperation with the CNB and the professional public from the private academic sphere. Thank you for your attention.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-07-ps2013-033-02-004-036.u3	I thank the Minister for his opening words. I now ask that the rapporteur take the floor for first reading by Mr Roman Kubíček.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-07-ps2013-033-02-004-036.u4	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, we have a government bill amending Act No. 256/2004 Coll., on Capital Market Business. The aim of the regulation is to comply with the legislation of the Czech Republic and the European Union, to implement European regulations into the Czech legal order and to harmonise the regulation of administrative charges. The amendment will contribute to the higher attractiveness of the fund business and the Czech capital market and ensure its better functioning while increasing consumer protection. The proposed amendment addresses the harmonisation of administrative charges in Act No. 634/2004 Coll., on Administrative Fees, as amended. As follows, for example, from the administrative practice of the Czech National Bank, it is necessary, above all, to align the amount of administrative fees relating to the application procedure, to re-charge certain proceedings and to abolish certain charges due to their irrelevance by the actual wording of the laws. The result will be to eliminate confusion, increase legal certainty and ensure compliance with legal standards with the needs of practice. The main benefits of the proposed adjustment, as I said, are better functioning of the capital market. The draft law was consulted with the Czech National Bank and market representatives, especially the Association for Capital Market. I recommend you proceed to the next reading. Thank you.
