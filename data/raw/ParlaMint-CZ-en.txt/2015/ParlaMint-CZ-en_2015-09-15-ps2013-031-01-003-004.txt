ParlaMint-CZ_2015-09-15-ps2013-031-01-003-004.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the bill tabled by the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to the Chamber of Deputies concerns several areas of legislation. First of all, it is the protection of publicly accessible special purpose roads against pollution, destruction, damage and regulation of the powers of municipal authorities in the field of public administration. Furthermore, there is a change in the concept and definition of categories and classes of motorways and roads, in the follow-up to the highest speed permitted in the Road Traffic Act. The proposal also contains more detailed arrangements for the handling of towed motor vehicles or, where applicable, for the handling of towed vehicles. their wreckage, introduces the possibility of excluding transit freight on Road II. and III. classes and last but not least, there are adjustments aimed at facilitating the legal and property preparation of road construction. In addition to these more complex changes, the proposal also includes partial adjustments, for example, in the field of road crossings with runways, control weighing, infrastructure management, the forfeiture of prepaid tolls and others. The proposed proposal also includes the abolition of a valid but still ineffective amendment to the Road Act, which was to change the way in which the payment of the time fee for the use of selected roads was to be paid, from a paper roadmark to an electronic device. This Government proposal was approved by the Chamber of Deputies on 29 April 2015 and was also discussed by the Senate on 17 June 2015, which returned it to the Chamber of Deputies with amendments. The amendments concern the resignation of the police's position in establishing local and transitional arrangements for road traffic, the explicit determination of the possibility of entrusting sub-activities to other persons by the infrastructure manager, including the earlier entry into force of the provisions relating to the infrastructure manager, the abolition of the possibility of increasing the maximum holiday speed on the motorway to 150 kilometres, the road vegetation problem at the motorway and motorway transit section, the possibility of placing the maintenance of technical infrastructure in local roads and minor legislative technical adaptations. The Ministry of Transport agrees with these amendments, so allow me to ask you to approve the bill returned, as amended by the Senate's amendments. Thank you.
