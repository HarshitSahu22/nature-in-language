ParlaMint-CZ_2015-01-22-ps2013-025-03-003-065.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the National Security Office is based on the practical need to exchange this information with specific states in line with the foreign policy interests of the Czech Republic in order to prioritize the negotiation of contracts for the exchange and mutual protection of classified information. It should be stressed that contracts for the exchange and mutual protection of classified information are negotiated only with those States which demonstrate the ability to ensure reciprocal provision of information with the Czech Republic and the security of such transmissions. Current Czech-Serbian relations are traditionally at a very good level. The Czech Republic supports the integration of Serbia into European structures. The versatile development of political, economic and cultural relations is one of the important objectives of Czech foreign policy. Currently, the Office provides assistance to its Serbian counterpart in the area of the legal framework for the protection of classified information, as well as in individual areas of security, be it personnel, industrial, physical, administrative and other. The Authority also provides assistance in cyber security between the two states. [[11.20 hours]] Given that Serbia is a signatory to the NATO Security Agreement within the framework of the Partnership for Peace, the candidate country for access to the European Union, and in many recent years there has been a renewal of intensive cooperation between the Czech Republic and Serbia, the agreement submitted is fully in line with our foreign policy. The submitted contract is a standard contract for the protection of classified information, which regulates in particular the following areas - equivalence of classified classification levels, conditions of access to and handling classified information, transmission of classified information, recognition of security authorisations of both countries, cooperation of the security authorities of both countries, regime of classified contracts, regime of visits requiring access to classified information. So these are the basic attributes of this agreement. The contract was signed in Belgrade on 13 May 2013 and submitted to the Parliament of the Czech Republic on 29 June 2013. Given the dissolution of the Chamber of Deputies, it was not possible to discuss this Treaty on the ground of the previous Chamber of Deputies. The Committee on Foreign Affairs, Defence and Security of the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic recommended agreeing to the ratification by its resolution No. 195 of 17 September 2014 The Senate subsequently agreed to ratify its resolution No. 30 December 3, 2014. The Foreign Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic recommended that the ratification be approved by its resolution No. 61 of 13 November 2014 Thank you for your attention.
