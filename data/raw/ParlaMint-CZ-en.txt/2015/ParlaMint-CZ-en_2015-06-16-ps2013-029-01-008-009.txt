ParlaMint-CZ_2015-06-16-ps2013-029-01-008-009.u2	Ladies and gentlemen, the subject of today's discussion is the Government's proposal for an amendment to the Act on budgetary determination of taxes, the Chamber's Press 416. It is proposed to increase the percentage by which the regions participate in the return on value added tax from 7,86 per cent to 8,92 per cent, with effect from 1 January 2016. From a legislative point of view, this will be a change to the provisions of Section 3 para. Article 4 (1) (a) (b) Annex No shall remain unchanged. 1 to the Act on budgetary determination of taxes, according to which individual regions of the Czech Republic participate in the defined part of the proceeds. The share of the regions in the tax return was constructed on the basis of the amount of funds for the powers transferred from the busy district authorities or ministries within the framework of the reform of the public administration. This increased the share of the regions gradually to 8,92 % by the end of 2011. Since 2012, the shares thus defined have been adjusted in relation to the expected effects of the tax measures taken. The regions have long been demanding the abolition of these corrections, such as an increase in VAT to 8,92 per cent. In response to these requirements, an amendment to the Act on Budgetary Determination of Taxes is proposed, which will increase the proportion of regions in VAT revenue to 8,92 % with effect from 1 January 2016. The regions of the proposal will receive funds within the framework of the budgetary determination of taxes, and they will be able to decide on their use separately. There will be a substantial increase in the tax revenue of the regions from VAT by less than 8% since 2016. In absolute terms, this is an increase in the tax revenues of the regions by CZK 3.5 billion. Financial effects in the following years will depend on the amount of value added tax collection. The proposal was discussed by the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development, which adopted an amendment to it concerning the municipalities, but not the regions covered by the proposed amendment. The Ministry of Finance does not agree with this proposal. It should be noted that this amendment, which proposes to increase the proportion of municipalities in VAT from 20.83 % to 21.4%, means further entitlement to the state budget. Specifically in 2016 it is about CZK 2 billion. At the same time, it should be said that tax revenues have been increased by approximately CZK 16.6 billion in the context of two amendments since 2008. This is a permanent increase with an annual increase in line with the increase in tax collection. Of this CZK 15.1 billion was at the expense of the state budget. At the same time, the last amendment concerning the regions which increased their tax revenues was adopted in 2005. The proposal was also discussed by the Budget Committee of the Chamber of Deputies, which found no grounds for change, and therefore did not accept any amendments to the proposal and recommended that Parliament's Chamber of Deputies approve the Government's bill without comment.
