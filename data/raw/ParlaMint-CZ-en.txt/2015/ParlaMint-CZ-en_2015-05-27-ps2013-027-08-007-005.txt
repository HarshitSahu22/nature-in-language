ParlaMint-CZ_2015-05-27-ps2013-027-08-007-005.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the bill on pyrotechnic articles and their handling is tabled on account of the transposition of the directive on the supply of pyrotechnic articles on the market and the implementing directive establishing a system for the traceability of pyrotechnic articles. The proposed law thus constitutes a comprehensive regulation in the field of pyrotechnic articles, which is currently regulated by various legislations. In addition to amendments related to pyrotechnic articles, the pyrotechnics Act also contains a technical amendment to Act No. 156/2000 Coll., on the verification of firearms and ammunition. This amendment adapts the law to the requirements of the Brussels Convention on the Mutual Recognition of Small Arms Test Marks. I agree with the amendments applied. The proposed law was discussed in the Economic Committee, which recommended that the Chamber of Deputies discuss and approve this House's press, as amended by the approved amendments. Thank you for your attention.
