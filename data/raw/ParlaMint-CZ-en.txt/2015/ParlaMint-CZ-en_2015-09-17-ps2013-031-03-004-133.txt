ParlaMint-CZ_2015-09-17-ps2013-031-03-004-133.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Government of the Czech Republic approved on 27 October 2014 the Government's proposal for amendments to articles and ratification of the UN's Statutes of the World Tourism Organisation. On 25 February 2015, the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic agreed to this ratification. In May 2015, the government material was discussed in the first reading of the Chamber of Deputies. The date of 9 May 2011 was published in the Official Journal of the European Union and published in the Official Journal of the European Union. 6. the Foreign Committee agreed to the Government's proposal and recommended the granting of approval for ratification. [[11.40 hours]] The amendments to the articles of the Statutes are a response to changes which the organisation itself undergoes and reflect the real needs of the organisation. Their purpose is to ensure the effective functioning of the World Tourism Organisation and to expand the language diversity of the organisation. The amendments concern the name of the organisation which became a specialised organisation of the United Nations in 2003, the modification of membership and the competences of associated members of the participation of associated members at the General Assembly and Executive Board meetings, the introduction of the Chinese language as one of the official languages of the organisation and the establishment of the organisation's budget in the single currency of the euro. It is a material of a non-legal nature. Thank you for your attention.
