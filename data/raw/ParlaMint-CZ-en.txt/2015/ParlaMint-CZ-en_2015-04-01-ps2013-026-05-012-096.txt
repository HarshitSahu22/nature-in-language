Proposal for a member of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund The first choice was the choice of one member of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund. We issued 164 ballots, 163 submitted, one not submitted. The quorum required for success, for achievement or for election was in this case also in all other 83 votes. The results of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund are as follows: Miloslav Janulík received 44 votes and Josef Uhlík received 115 votes, so Josef Uhlík was elected and this option ends.
