ParlaMint-CZ_2015-09-15-ps2013-031-01-007-064.u9	Thank you, Mr President. Good evening, dear colleagues, dear colleagues, dear guests at the gallery. After all, because it's already been a month since I had the opening word for this House press, I will allow myself to recapitulate some of the essentials of this material. The aim of this amendment and this material is to implement a multiannual programme of support for the continued application of sustainable biofuels in transport for the period 2015 to 2020 which was 12. 8. 2015 approved by the European Commission. The multiannual support programme was drawn up by the Ministry of Agriculture and approved by the Government on 6. 8. 2014 by resolution 655. The submitted material shall include all necessary texts of the Law on excise duties and the Act on Air Protection, in accordance with the obligations arising from the decisions of the European Commission to which the multiannual support programme has been successfully notified. Support for clean and high-percent biofuels in transport of the Czech Republic will be maintained by the approval of the submitted material, but the following fundamental changes will occur: Firstly, the reduction of aid for individual types of biofuels, i.e. an increase in excise duty rates. Secondly, abolishing the possibility of accounting for supported net and high-percent biofuels into the obligation of distributors to place on the market a minimum share of biofuels. The aid currently granted is based on a previous multiannual support programme which expired on 30. 6. 2015. From then on, until the amendment to the Law on excise duties comes into effect, there will be unlicensed support provided in the Czech Republic as a result of a significant extension of the discussion of the proposal in the Chamber of Deputies. It is now disproportionately high, but it is also drawn in favour of biofuels that are used to fulfil the above-mentioned obligation. [[19.40 hours]] The European Commission has ordered the Czech Republic to take corrective measures by 1 at the latest. 1. 2016 and, at the same time, ensure that the Czech Republic provides for increased rates of excise duty for a transitional period in order to compensate for undue aid. In the absence of appropriate corrective measures, i.e. acceptance of printing 418 by 1. 1. 2016, the Czech Republic is exposed to the risk of sanctions by the European Commission. In accordance with the decision of the European Commission, the period from 1. 1. 2016 to 30. 6. 2017 the excise duty rates should be as follows: for FAME B100 4 crowns 59 cents per litre and for mixed diesel 9,263 kroner per litre. The amount of excise duty refund on alcohol used for the production of ethanol E85 should be CZK 10,229 per litre. After the end of this transitional period, the excise duty rates should be as follows: for FAME B100 2,19 kroner per litre, for mixed diesel 8,515 kroner per litre, and the return on alcohol in E85 should be 10,97 kroner per litre. The level of proportionality of the aid will be assessed each year and the excise duty rates will be adjusted accordingly if further overcompensation is found. The total cost of support for biofuels for the entire period 2015 to 2020 is now estimated at a maximum amount of 8.7 billion. Crowns. However, given market developments, it is very likely that they will be lower. By implementing the aid, the European Union's binding targets for reducing greenhouse gas emissions and using renewable energy sources in transport will be met. To this end, it is necessary to maintain a steady increase in the consumption of renewable energy in transport, i.e. mainly through sustainable biofuels. The existence and development of the biofuels market, which will grow, process, deliver and consume in our country, stabilise these sectors and allow people to maintain jobs and create new jobs where appropriate. At the same time, there will be no increase in imports and therefore also in dependence on fossil fuels, which is an important factor in terms of energy security in line with the updated state energy concept. In addition to the tax advantage of biofuels, the proposed bill also includes the introduction of continuous monitoring of the performance of the fuel supplier's obligation to mix biofuels into the Czech market. If the fuel supplier fails to comply with the prescribed obligation during the calendar year, he will be obliged to provide a security from the volume of biofuels not delivered. In the event of failure to fulfil that supplier's annual obligation, this security shall be counted against the fine of the volume of biofuels not delivered. The reduction of the fixed period will contribute significantly to the reduction of the use of biofuels contrary to the Air Protection Act. At the same time, there will be a reduction in the amount of any default on the fine from the outstanding volume of biofuels in cases where the fuel supplier has put fuel on the Czech fuel market in the long term without fulfilling the statutory obligation in the reference period. Thank you for your attention.
