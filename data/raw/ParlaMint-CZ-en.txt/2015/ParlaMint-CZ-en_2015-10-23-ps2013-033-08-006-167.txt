ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-23-ps2013-033-08-006-167.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Colleagues, colleagues, just briefly to remind you what the amendment to the law is about. The fundamental changes include, in particular, a new, broader definition of the cultural property, which will be part of the national cultural treasure of a Member State without other restrictive criteria to date. In the future, it is sufficient that the subject matter, the cultural property, is identified or defined by the Member States as a national cultural treasure. It will no longer be a condition that it must be part of a public or other collection. Further amendments consist in extending certain deadlines and also specifying the concept of necessary care. Thank you for your attention.
