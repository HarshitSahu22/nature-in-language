ParlaMint-CZ_2015-05-21-ps2013-027-06-011-075.u4	Thank you. I would like to briefly present to you an intergovernmental agreement, referred to as the IGA, which complements the Single Resolution Mechanism Regulation, referred to as the SRM. It is one of the key components of the emerging banking union. This Agreement complements the text of the Regulation and addresses certain issues relating in particular to the establishment of a single crisis management fund. As far as the content of this agreement is concerned, first and foremost, how national crisis management funds in the states belonging to the banking union will gradually merge. Furthermore, it is ensured that states outside the banking union do not have to bear the costs of decisions that they do not participate in through the European Union budget. On the basis of the delegation of the Prime Minister and the President of the Republic, this agreement was signed for the Czech Republic by the Ambassador and Permanent Representative of the Czech Republic to the EU, Mr Martin Povejšil, subject to procedural ratification. The Agreement was signed by all EU Member States except the United Kingdom and Sweden. The Czech Republic participated in the Intergovernmental Conference on this Agreement on the basis of the resolution of the Committee on the European Union of 2 January 2014 as a negotiating party, i.e. an EU Member State that considers becoming a Contracting Party. The Czech delegation negotiated equivalent conditions for the Czech Republic at the conference and recommended accession to the treaty. For the Czech Republic as a Member State outside the euro area from the signature, respectively. conclusion of this agreement, there are no obligations at this time, as its effectiveness is postponed until the Czech Republic becomes a member of the banking union. In their declaration, the signatories to this Agreement have committed themselves to seeking the timely completion of the ratification process of this Agreement in accordance with the requirements of their respective national legislation in order to allow full operation by 1 January 2016. This agreement was already discussed in November of last year by the Budget Committee of the Chamber of Deputies, and in December of last year it was granted consent to the ratification of the Senate. In view of the progress of the negotiations, we ask for a reduction in the time limit for the committees to 30 days. Ladies and gentlemen, I ask you, on behalf of the Government, to support this agreement so that ratification can be completed after its approval by the Chamber of Deputies of the Czech Parliament. Thank you for your attention.
