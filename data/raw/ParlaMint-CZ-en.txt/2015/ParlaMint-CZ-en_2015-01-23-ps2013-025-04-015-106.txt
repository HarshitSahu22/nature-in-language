Proposal for the election of members of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund In the case of the election of two members of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund, the issued tickets were also 172, thevorum was the same, 87. Petr Adam received 57 votes, Petr Bendl 34 votes, Miloslav Janulík 55, Miroslav Volařík 91 votes and Pavel Pastorek 36. In this case, the House was partly successful, in the first round Mr. Miroslav Volařík was elected and became a member of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund. There is one more seat left, and two Members will compete for this post, because, in accordance with the electoral order, double the vacant seats with the highest number of votes. Petr Adam and Miloslav Janulík progress to the second round in this case. We're going to pick that place out of them.
