ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-07-ps2013-033-02-006-064.u2	Thank you for the floor, Madam Vice-President. I will be very brief not to suppress the purpose of the proceedings under Section 90. Thank you very much for the willingness of the House to discuss this law in this regime. The amendment is the first step towards the solution of the long-term uncomfortable position of the Czech Switzerland National Park Administration compared to the remaining national parks, i.e. the Krkonoše National Praku, the Šumava National Park and the Podyjí National Park. The Czech Switzerland National Park Administration was established by law in 1999 as a budgetary organisation and administrative office. All other national park administrations have a different form, they are contribution organisations established by the Ministry of Environment. The administration of the Czech Switzerland National Park is now subject to the regime of the new Civil Service Act and the proposed amendment aims to ensure that the form of administrative office is lost. Until now, the head of administration and seven executives have been transferred to the state service regime. If there were a continuation of the transfer of employees of the Czech Switzerland National Park Administration to the state service, there would be 12 additional posts. This would be related to the unnecessary administration and the financial costs, as detailed in the explanatory memorandum. Since our amendment was on the agenda of the Chamber of Deputies only now, there was the opening of the transfer of executives to the service law regime, as I said earlier, in the meantime between the filing of the bill and today's discussion. Nevertheless, the adoption of this proposal is still important as quickly as possible and mitigates the financial effects of the process initiated. Dear ladies and gentlemen. Therefore, I thank you for your willingness and readiness to discuss the draft in section 90, and thank you for your attention.
