ParlaMint-CZ_2015-12-10-ps2013-036-07-003-216.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I present to you the proposal for an amendment to the Small and Medium Business Support Act, on the basis of which a new entity called the Entrepreneurship and Innovation Agency will be created. This agency will be subject to Act No. 234/2014 Sb., on Civil Service, and will play the role of intermediate entity from the point of view of European Structural and Investment Funds. The amendment is prepared on the basis of the request made by the European Commission at the meetings of the delegation of the Czech Republic with the European Commission at the January and February meeting of this year at the Ministry of Interior for implementation of the Service Act. The European Commission asks that all intermediate bodies for each operational programme be subject to a service law or similar legislation. In this request, the European Commission relied on the commitments made by the Czech Republic in the Partnership Agreement, which is the basic document for the application of cohesion policy in the Czech Republic for the programme period 2014 to 2020. We have therefore chosen an option where the current agency for business and investment support CzechInvest will be separated from the relevant department dealing with European Structural and Investment Funds into the new Agency for Enterprise and Innovation. ParlaMint-CZ_2015-12-10-ps2013-036-07-003-216.u4	The split will take place through the submitted amendment to Act No. 47/2002 Coll., on the promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises by which the CzechInvest Agency was established. Without the binding promise of such a process from the Czech Republic, the European Commission did not intend to approve the Operational Programme Enterprise and Innovation for Competitiveness and intended to block the drawdown of EUR 4.3 billion, which was allocated to the OP PIK from the European Regional Development Fund. Any delay in the approval of this Regulation and therefore in the fulfilment of the agreements with the European Commission poses a risk of halting the drawing of funds from the European Structural and Investment Funds. In view of this, we have recommended to the Economic Committee as a guarantee committee not to accept Mr Urban's amendment in the part where it proposes to sub-class CzechInvest under the Civil Service Act. On the contrary, I agree with the Member's proposal to supplement the forms of support for preferential services. At its meeting on 2 December, the Economic Committee adopted Mr Urban's amendment. I'd like to explain why we did it. The purpose of the agency, such as CzechInvest and CzechTrade, was originally to create an institution that is halfway between the business sector and the state administration. By sub-classing CzechInvest, whose main task is to acquire foreign investment, this agency would lose its sense, because already those people might be part of the Ministry of Industry and Trade straight away. Thus, we would violate the very meaning of CzechInvest. The solution that we originally assumed and which was not finally accepted, that part of CzechInvest will be under the state service and part will be under the labour code, unfortunately it did not pass, and therefore I am forced to submit this law. In view of the fact that there will be a significant delay in the subsequent implementation of the Act as amended by the amendment in the implementation of the agreements with the European Commission, and therefore to increase the risk of halting the use of EU funds for the Czech Republic, I ask you to approve the Government Bill. In conclusion, honourable Members, I would like to ask you once again to support the Government's bill and at the same time to shorten the deadline between second and third reading to seven days.
