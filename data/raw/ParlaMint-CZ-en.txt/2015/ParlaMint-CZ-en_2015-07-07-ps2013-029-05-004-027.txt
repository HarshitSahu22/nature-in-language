ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-07-ps2013-029-05-004-027.u2	Thank you, Madam President. Ladies and gentlemen, the Government has submitted to you a draft law on the termination of pension savings and the related proposal called. an accompanying law, which I will talk about later. The proposals were prepared on the basis of recommendations from the pension reform expert committee, which was tasked with preparing a substantive proposal for the termination of the scheme in accordance with the Government's intention to end pension savings. The main objective is to stop reducing the amount of funds collected under pension insurance premiums for pension payments. The termination of pension savings is proposed to be implemented in such a way that the ownership rights of the participants are fully respected and the effects of both the state administration of the pension company and, in particular, the participants are minimised. For these reasons, a participant may decide to use either the possibility of transferring all his funds to his designated account or postal order or the transfer of funds as a contribution to the third pillar. The termination of pension savings is further designed to respect the annual cycle for the payment of pension insurance premiums. As of 1 January 2016, pension funds will be cancelled on 1 July 2016, the funds will be paid to participants in the last quarter of 2016 and the winding-up of pension funds will be terminated by transferring funds which will not be paid to the participants into an account designated by the insurance manager, i.e. not later than the first days of January 2017. In view of the private nature of the contracts on which the scheme is based and the annual basis for payment of premiums, the proposals are designed in such a way that the individual steps are always set at a specific date. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to approve the proposed bill at first reading because of the timetable for setting the system to end. From the point of view of the purpose and set-up of the settlement process of participants' resources and the preparation of the entities concerned, the temporal aspect plays an essential role. Thank you for your attention.

The draft law addresses the procedure for the termination of the pension savings scheme, the participant's claims, the death of the participant in the savings period, the prohibition of the savings strategy from the date of entry into liquidation of pension funds, the obligation to notify pension companies on the procedure for the termination of pension savings, the transmission of a statement of pension savings, information on investment in pension funds, the remuneration of the pension company, the liquidation in pension funds and the payment of the participant's funds. The Act also provides for the treatment of accounts of outstanding funds, provides for a depositary of the pension fund, which checks whether the pension company disposes of the pension fund's assets in accordance with this Act. It deals with the merger of pension funds and the submission of the final annual report for 2016 on the winding-up of funds. Supervision of compliance with the obligations of pension companies and the depositary of the pension fund under this law is exercised by the Czech National Bank. The Pension Savings Act shall apply mutatis mutandis to the exercise of supervision under this Act. It also deals with the cancellation of fixed premiums and the disappearance of outstanding payments. In the next section, it deals with the payment of pension insurance premiums, where it provides that the participant may, during the period of his participation in pension savings in the periods prior to 2016, pay the pension insurance premiums provided for by the Social Security Insurance Act and the contribution to the state employment policy provided that he has fulfilled the condition set out in Section 23 (2) of the Act.
