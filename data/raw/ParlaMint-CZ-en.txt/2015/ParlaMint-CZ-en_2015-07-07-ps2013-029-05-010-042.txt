ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-07-ps2013-029-05-010-042.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, I will try to be brief, I believe you will appreciate this at this hour. The proposed proposal for amendments to the rules of procedure in the field of civil law brings three innovations. The first is to simplify or reduce the registration of a limited liability company, if it is a company, I would say, in the simplest form possible, i.e. which contains only the required institutes, when such registration is exempt from the legal fee, and at the same time the notary registration fee is reduced, so the whole thing will take place for 100 euros. It is something that should support the business initiative, and it is something that I would like to emphasise what the European Commission requires as so-called. a prior condition which is a condition for us to benefit from European subsidies in the next programming period. For this reason in particular, this is an extremely serious proposal. The second amendment concerns European succession certificates. This is, in principle, a technical institute, or it is the transposition of a regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council which establishes a pan-European system of inheritance certificates that allows better discussion of the heritage with a cross-border element. Finally, the third amendment concerns, in turn, the implementation of the regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council, this time in the field of safeguard measures, and it concerns the possibility for safeguard measures introduced in one Member State to be translated into a situation in another Member State, i.e. adjusted by the court to correspond to what is in another Member State, if necessary. These are three elements which contain a relatively simple proposal. I stress in particular the first, i.e. the limited liability company, its entry for EUR 100. I stress once again, it is something that the European Commission requires, which makes our use of European subsidies conditional upon, and I therefore ask for the support of this proposal. Thank you.
