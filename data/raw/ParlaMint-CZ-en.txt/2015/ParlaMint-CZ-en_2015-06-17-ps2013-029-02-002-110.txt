ParlaMint-CZ_2015-06-17-ps2013-029-02-002-110.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me a brief introduction to the amendment to the Act on Advertising Regulation. The fact that this amendment responds primarily to the European Commission's criticisms of excessive and duplicate regulation of advertising has been repeatedly mentioned on my part, both at first and second readings in the Chamber of Deputies and during discussions in the Guarantee Committee. Specifically, it is an explicit ban on hidden and subliminal advertising and advertising aimed at children and young people because these forms of advertising are unfair commercial practices and are therefore already included in an explicit ban on advertising, which is an unfair commercial practice. The European Commission calls for these duplicate provisions to be deleted. It was also always pointed out by the petitioner that infringement proceedings were initiated with the Czech Republic in January last year, so-called infringement, and if this excessive regulation is not removed from the advertising regulation law, the proceedings will continue, which may result in the imposition of sanctions. The proposed proposal outside the area following the need to align Czech legislation with Union law, for example, newly defines the provisions on electoral advertising in order to allow for infringement of the obligation in this area. Furthermore, it adapts the law of the European Parliament and of the Council on the provision of information on food to consumers and transfers supervisory powers to advertising on foodstuffs containing health or nutrition claims from the trade authorities to the State Agricultural and Food Inspection. Furthermore, the proposal responds to problems encountered by supervisory authorities in combating the advertising of lotteries operated without proper authorisation. Let me briefly comment on the amendments applied. As a guarantee, the Election Committee applied the amendments in the resolution of 17 March as press 363/2 to the proposal. He agrees with these MPO and supports them. At second reading, other amendments were applied, namely Mr Chvojka, Mr Korte, Mr Hovorka, Mr Stanjura and Mrs Vyzula and Mrs Kovářová. I consider it necessary to emphasise once again that the adoption of Mr Korte's or Mr Hovorka's amendments, which return to the Act on the regulation of advertising provisions on the explicit ban on subliminal and hidden advertising, on advertising for children, would have consequences for the Czech Republic, which I have already mentioned, namely another procedure for infringement and the risk of sanctions.
