ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-08-ps2013-033-03-005-136.u2	Good still in the morning. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, let me present a contractual document relating to international taxation for discussion. In this particular case, it is a protocol to an existing double taxation treaty with Kazakhstan signed in Almaty on 9 April 1998 and entered into force on 29 October 1999. This contract was announced in the Czech Republic in the Collection of International Treaties under number 3/2000. The protocol, which was created on the initiative of the Czech Party, ensures in particular the extension of the possibility of exchange of information in the scope of all taxes, i.e. for example. also value added tax, which under contract is currently not covered by the exchange of information. It should be stressed that the text of the protocol, including the so-called The extended exchange of information fully reflects international developments and trends in the international tax field, particularly in the work of the European Union and the OECD. The protocol, which will form an integral part of the existing treaty, will certainly contribute to better coordination of the activities of the tax authorities of both states in order to limit possible tax evasion. It will certainly also contribute to the smooth implementation of the contract by further adaptations to its text, e.g. the issue of permanent establishment, dividends, interest and more. On the basis of what has already been said, I therefore propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take into account these facts when discussing this protocol to the international treaty and that this contractual document be ordered at first reading to the relevant House Committees, which will examine it in detail. Thank you for your attention.
