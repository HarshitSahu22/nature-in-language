ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-07-ps2013-026-07-005-028.u2	Good afternoon. Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the proposal for this law has been drawn up in order to ensure the transposition of Directive 2012/18/EU on the control of major-accident hazards with the presence of dangerous substances, known rather as the Seveso Directive, in this case Seveso III, which was published in July 2012 in the Official Journal of the European Union and whose transposition should take place on 1 June 2015. This Directive lays down rules for objects in connection with which the use of such hazardous chemicals and mixtures presents an increased risk of emergencies takes place. The regulation in question, adopted in order to prevent such incidents, lays down a system of measures which individual operators of such objects are obliged to introduce in order to prevent major accidents. Let me just briefly comment on the main changes introduced by the new legislation compared to the existing legal situation. It is above all that there is an extension of the scope of the new Act on the Prevention of Major Accidents, in relation to the buildings of underground gas reservoirs. Furthermore, the draft new law defines more precisely the obligations of operators concerning the categorization of the objects used by them. There is harmonisation of the requirements for the content of the safety documentation and significant changes, as well as the process of approval of the security documentation by the regional authorities. Among other things, there is an explicit anchoring of the role of the fire rescue corps of the region in the processing of external emergency plans. There is also a system of legislation to control compliance with the obligations imposed by the Act on the Prevention of Major Accidents. That is all for the introduction and thank you for your attention.
