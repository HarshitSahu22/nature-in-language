ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-07-ps2013-033-02-003-006.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, since this is a second reading, allow me to briefly state on the proposal that this is a very simple amendment aimed at supplementing the legitimately defined purposes of the use of funds acquired in the process of small privatisation by the possibility of using these resources to reduce the negative balance of pension insurance. This is a similar arrangement which is already contained in the Act on the abolition of the National Property Fund. Following the discussion of the proposal at first reading on 19 May 2015, the draft law was directed to the Committee on Budgets, which discussed it on 10 June 2015 and recommended for approval with two minor legislative technical comments, which I agree with as a representative of the appellant. Given that more funds are currently concentrated in a special account of the proceeds from the so-called small privatisation than is needed to fulfil the purpose defined by the law, I consider it most appropriate that some of these funds be used to reduce the negative pension insurance balance as soon as possible. I would therefore like to ask for the support of another rapid and smooth procedure in this proposal by a further legislative process. Thank you for your attention.
