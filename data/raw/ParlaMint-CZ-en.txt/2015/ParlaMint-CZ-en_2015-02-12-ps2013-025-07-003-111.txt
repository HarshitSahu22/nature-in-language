Proposal for the establishment of a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the work of the Chamber of Deputies Office / House document 1676/ The House document 1676, which has been delivered to you, contains the resolution of the Organising Committee number 113 of 20 November 2014. Under the authority of the Organising Committee, the proposal is justified by the Vice-President of the Chamber of Deputies Vojtěch Filip. You have the floor, Mr. Vice-President.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-02-12-ps2013-025-07-003-111.u2	Thank you. Mr President, Members of the Government, Madames and gentlemen, I will be brief. At the beginning of our term of office in the negotiations on the establishment of the institutions of the Chamber of Deputies, unlike in the past, there was no institution which was part of the institutions of the Chamber of Deputies all the time, namely a permanent commission for the work of the Chamber of Deputies Office. The Office was responsible for the division of the work of Mrs. First Vice-President Jaroslava Jermanová and authorized members of clubs who were to ensure that individual requirements were implemented. After a year of evaluation, we find that the institution is missing and therefore, on a proposal made by the Organising Committee, you have submitted this document, which establishes a permanent committee of the Chamber of Deputies for the work of the Chamber of Deputies Office. We recommend, on the basis of the resolution of the Organising Committee of the Chamber of Deputies, that the Permanent Commission have 15 members. It is established that the Commission will be established in accordance with the principles of proportional representation. In this respect, you have a proposal, and if we vote it, we can set up a committee. The presidents of the MPs will nominate its individual members, and the commission would start working after it is established and populated, so that I can express it in a simpler way. Thank you.
