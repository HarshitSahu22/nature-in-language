ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-02-ps2013-026-06-006-078.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, the Treaty presented amends the Treaty in force on police cooperation, which has entered into force since 1 July 2006. The reasons for amending and supplementing the contract in force are, in particular, greater demands on cooperation in the field of security and in the field of all security forces involved in this cooperation. Newly introduced, for example, is the possibility of placing police officers in the territory of the other State under the command of a territorial policeman in the context of cross-border interventions in the territory of the other State in the event, and I would like to point out when it is a threat to the life or health of persons, the transfer of persons between Contracting States or the transfer of persons in custody or persons in the execution of punishment through the territory of the other State. Cooperation under the contract is newly extended to the area of offenses of natural persons. The application of the contract will also bring new requirements for clarification or change in practice, particularly where the existing text is broken down with the current requirements of practice shared by both States. The need for partial changes was also triggered by the reorganisation of police forces on both sides, i.e. in the territory of the Czech Republic and Austria. Thank you for your attention.
