ParlaMint-CZ_2015-09-30-ps2013-031-06-003-043.u4	- a completely new solution to the operation of gambling in the territory of the Czech Republic, namely the bill on gambling, which, together with the bill on gambling tax and amending law, forms one functional unit, which represents a completely new regulatory framework for the gambling market. The proposed regulation reflects the objectives of the Government of the Czech Republic in the field of gambling and, in view of the ongoing infringement proceedings on the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, the result of harmonisation with European Union law. The proposed bill replaces the completely inadequate and obsolete Act No. 202/1990 Coll., on lotteries and other similar games, as amended, which causes a number of problems. On the other hand, the bill on gambling provides a comprehensive regulation of the operation of gambling, in particular in the form of consistent protection of betting and their surroundings, provision for measures to prevent and combat sociopathological phenomena associated with the operation of gambling, the opening of the Czech gambling market for operators established in a Member State of the European Union, the regulation of the operation of gambling via the Internet, the new authorisation procedure and, last but not least, an effective control mechanism. The draft law also significantly strengthens the position of individual administrative units in the relevant areas of gambling. With the Ministry of Finance as a central regulator, it removes excessive administrative burdens when issuing and canceling permits, which are up to tens of thousands a year. At the same time, thanks to the emerging monitoring system, as well as the blocking measures for online gambling operations, new regulation will enable public authorities to effectively prevent illegal activities. A significant strengthening of powers is also taking place with the control authority, which will be the new customs authority. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed from 1 January 2017. Thank you for your attention.
