ParlaMint-CZ_2015-05-27-ps2013-027-08-006-004.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me present to the plenary of the Chamber of Deputies for the second reading a government bill amending Act No. 158/2000 Coll., on the search, exploration and extraction of mineral resources from the seabed beyond the limits of the powers of the States and on the amendment of certain laws, as amended. The reason for this submission is that in 2010, the British Petroleum oil rig in the Gulf of Mexico crashed. As a response, the European Commission has created a directive uniting security conditions within the European Union for offshore oil and gas production activities. All Member States have an obligation to implement this Directive into their national legislation. For the Czech Republic, as an inland state, there is only one obligation under the directive. If a company registered in the Czech Republic undergoes a major accident outside the territory of the EU Member States in activities related to the exploration and extraction of oil and gas in the sea, the company will be obliged to send information through the MIT on this major accident at the request of the European Commission. These will then be sent to Brussels by the Ministry. In the framework of the amendment of the original law of 2000, some technical amendments were made to its wording in order to bring it into line with the current legislation, in particular with the new administrative order and the Civil Code. Thank you for your attention and please support and release the bill to third reading.
