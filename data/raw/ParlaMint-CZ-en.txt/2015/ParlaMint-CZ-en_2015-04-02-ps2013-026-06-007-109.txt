ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-02-ps2013-026-06-007-109.u1	109. 1. supplement to the plan of military exercises units and staffs of the Czech Army with foreign partners in and outside the Czech Republic in 2014 /public press 240/ This material will be presented by Minister of Defence Martin Stropnický. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-02-ps2013-026-06-007-109.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, I present to you the material 1. supplement to the plan of military exercises units and staffs of the Army of the Czech Republic with foreign partners in and outside the Czech Republic in 2014, which was approved by Government Resolution No. 399 of 28 May 2014. The material contains an overview of the planned participation in military exercises of the Czech Army with foreign partners in and outside the Czech Republic in 2014, which was not included in the material Information on military exercises of units and crews of the Czech Army with foreign partners in and outside the Czech Republic planned in 2014, approved by the Government on 8 January 2014 by Resolution No. 32. Military exercises planned by the Ministry of Defence for the period 2014 were based on membership of the Czech Republic in NATO and the EU. The participation of the units and crews of the Czech Republic in the planned exercises is the fulfilment of the essential requirements of NATO and EU governing bodies in achieving the necessary level of their interoperability with the units of other Member States and NATO and EU headquarters in carrying out joint tasks. The participation of the Czech Republic in exercises with foreign participation was financed from the budget of the Ministry of Defence. Priority attention has been paid to units and units destined for operations in the NRF foreign missions, which are the forces of NATO's rapid response, and to BG EU - Battle Groups EU. The submitted material includes the completed exercises in 2014. This is 11 exercises planned outside the Czech Republic and five exercises in the Czech Republic. The exercises listed under headings 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 9 outside the territory of the Czech Republic and the exercises listed under headings 1 and 3 in our territory have already been approved by the Government and have only been specified in the 1st supplement. The costs of carrying out these exercises were incurred from the current budget for this area and the Department of Defence did not require any increase in the budget approved for 2014. Part of this part of the material is also an annex containing exercises completed for 2014. Mr President, fellow Members, I would like to ask you to take note of this information and thank you for your attention.
