Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Mauritius on Air Transport /German press 641/ - first reading I ask that the opening words be given by the Minister of Transport Dan Ťok. Please, Minister.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-024-141.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Mauritius in the field of civil aviation are currently not regulated. On the basis of the interest of the Mauritius Party, an expert delegation meeting was held in November 2014, resulting in a mutually acceptable text of the Agreement. The Government of the Czech Republic agreed to the negotiations by its resolution in June 2015. The Agreement was subsequently signed in August 2015. The text of the agreement corresponds to current developments in the field of civil aviation, is consistent with the constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, including obligations arising from membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. The agreement does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not affect state budget expenditure. I would like to ask you to refer the material to the Foreign Committee. Thank you.
