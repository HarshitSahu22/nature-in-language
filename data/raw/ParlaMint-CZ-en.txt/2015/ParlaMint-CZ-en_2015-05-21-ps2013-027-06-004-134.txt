However, while studying the annual reports, and I would like to say to the parliamentary parties, I have encountered a novelty that has not yet been here. The YES movement identified in its revenue when I put it this way, expert work for members of the MP's in total of 4 million and some change. It's new. I think it's possible, even if it's unusual. However, if a political party is doing something beyond its core activity, it is called. secondary activity and taxes are paid from this. For example, when a political party owns a house, rents it, the rent pays a corporation tax of 19 percent. I am not sure that billing members of the expert service is the main activity of the political party. But I'm not the one who should be making the decisions. If it were a commercial activity, it would have to be taxed as a secondary activity and in that case the ANO would not have paid a 19-percent corporation tax. Many members of the ANO movement are angry when we say YES and Agrofert, sometimes I understand it, but according to the annual report, the ANO accounts are kept by Agrofert and all accounting documents of the ANO 2011 political movement are stored at the headquarters of the Agrofert movement. In fact, if it were true and the procedure was consistent with tax laws, it could also be aid to other political parties who have not yet chosen such a procedure because they rightly believed that this could be a commercial activity unrelated to the main activity of the political party and would have to pay income tax. So the motion for a resolution is that the Chamber of Deputies asks the Prime Minister to ask the Financial Administration to assess whether payment to expert members of the Parliament of the Czech Republic, whether Members or Senators, is part of the main activity of political parties and is not subject to the Corporate Tax Act. I believe you will all support this, because it is the innovatorial process of those who have said how they will save public funds. It's a pretty good billing process. I think it's possible, really, even if it's unusual. But I think it should have been taxed and that the ANO movement did not pay CZK 600,000 corporation tax. Thank you.
