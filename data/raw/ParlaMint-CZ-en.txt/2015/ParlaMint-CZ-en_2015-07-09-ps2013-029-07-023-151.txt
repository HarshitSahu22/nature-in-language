I consider the abolition of most regulatory fees with effect from 1 to the most important parameters affecting the management of health insurance undertakings. 1. 2015, increase in salaries of workers in bed care segments as one of government priorities, increase in minimum wage by CZK 700 to CZK 9,200 from 1. 1. As a result of the increase in insurance premiums for persons without taxable income, the abolition of the maximum assessment base for self-employed persons and employees, as well as the reduction of the value added tax rate for medicinal products from 15 to 10 per cent, which also took place at the beginning of 2015. The total revenue of the public health insurance scheme, including revenue from the taxed activities, is planned to amount to 247,5 billion. CZK, this represents a year-on-year increase of 3.1 %. Total expenditure of the public health insurance scheme, including taxes, is planned at 247,9 billion. Crowns. Compared to the previous year, total expenditure is increased by 2.8 %. The sum of estimates of the planned balance of revenue and expenditure for the public health insurance scheme is negative at 0.4 billion. Employee health insurance companies plan a negative balance for 2015, general health insurance company intends to achieve a positive result of nearly 1.5 billion. However, according to the latest models, the management of health insurance companies should develop in a more positive direction and the resulting balance should therefore achieve a slightly positive value. The estimated average cost of health services per insured person in 2015 is higher than the expected average cost in 2014. The average costs are to reach CZK 22 985, which represents an annual increase of CZK 669, and by 3% in relative terms. None of the health insurance companies envisages the existence of liabilities after the due date vis-à-vis health service providers, financial stocks in reserve bank accounts for all occupational health insurance companies are planned in accordance with the legislation. The general health insurance company plans to increase the balance of the reserve fund to CZK almost one billion by the end of 2015, but its reserve fund will not be filled in the prescribed amount. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, at its meeting on 23 February 2015, the Government discussed the joint opinions of the Health and Finance Ministries on health insurance plans for the year 2015. By resolution 125, the Government recommended that the health insurance plans of health insurance companies be submitted to the Chamber of Deputies to discuss the proposal for their approval. Subsequently, on 6 May, this material was discussed in the Health Committee which approved it. The Committee recommended that the Chamber of Deputies agree to the material. Thank you for your attention.
