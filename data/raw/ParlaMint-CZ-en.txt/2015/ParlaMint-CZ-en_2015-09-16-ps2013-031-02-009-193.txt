Proposal for appointment of members of the Supervisory Board of the Grant Agency In the case of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic there are three seats vacant, we received only two names from the committee and the selection took place in the following way. 155 ballots were issued and handed over, so the number of non-denials is zero. 110 Members voted for Stanislav Labík, and 97 of our votes were cast for Věra Kalvodová. Kvorum in this election was 78, so I can say that both candidates, Stanislav Labík and Věra Kalvodová, were elected in the first round. That concludes this choice. Perhaps I will remind you that one seat in this agency remains vacant, but that is at this point in time in the gesture of the Committee on Science, Education and Youth, which must deliver another nomination to the House through the Commission.
