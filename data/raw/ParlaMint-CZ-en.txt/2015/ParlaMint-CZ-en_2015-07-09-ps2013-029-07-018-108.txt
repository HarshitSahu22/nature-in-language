ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-018-108.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I would now like to present to you the Optional Protocol to the Convention on the Rights of the Child establishing a procedure for submitting a notification which was approved by the UN General Assembly in December 2011 and entered into force in April last year. The Protocol will allow individual notifications to be sent, which will draw attention to specific cases of breaches of the Convention or any of the first two option protocols, thereby contributing to increasing the effectiveness of the protection of the rights of the child enshrined in these internationally binding documents. This is therefore the procedure in which the notification can be made. That's the content of this option protocol. Of course, this is a whole series of quite fundamental things, such as - children who should have been allowed into armed forces 18 years ago to participate in combat actions, and a number of other things that concern inhumane degrading treatment and punishing children. So I think our position here is clear. And the third option protocol concerns precisely the procedure whereby notifications concerning these matters can be made. I think there should be nothing to prevent the Chamber of Deputies from ratifying this protocol.
