I therefore ask the Minister of Industry and Trade Jan Mládek, on behalf of the Government, to present this press. Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-01-23-ps2013-025-04-011-026.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the reason for the presentation and handling of the draft Act on pyrotechnic articles is the transposition of the directive on the supply of pyrotechnic articles on the market and the implementing directive establishing a system for the traceability of pyrotechnic articles. The newly proposed law constitutes a comprehensive regulation in the field of pyrotechnic articles, which is currently regulated by various legislations. The proposed law establishes a framework for the verification and labelling of pyrotechnic articles, including rules on CE marking. Furthermore, the Act lays down the obligations of individual operators, the conditions and competence for the handling of pyrotechnic articles as well as the essential requirements for the storage of pyrotechnic articles. The aim of the new legislation is to increase the protection of the life and health of persons, property, safety and the environment. In addition to amendments related to pyrotechnic articles, the pyrotechnics Act contains a technical amendment to Act No. 156/2000 Coll., on the verification of firearms and ammunition. This amendment adapts to the requirements of the Brussels Convention on the Mutual Recognition of Small Arms Test Marks. Thank you for your attention.
