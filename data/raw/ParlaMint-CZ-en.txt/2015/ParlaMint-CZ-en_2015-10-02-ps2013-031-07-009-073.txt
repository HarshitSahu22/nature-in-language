ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-02-ps2013-031-07-009-073.u2	Madam President, colleagues, the bill you have before you is being proposed by the Government to strengthen and streamline the support system provided by the State Cinematography Fund. Let me briefly comment on how this purpose should be achieved. The State Cinematography Fund provides funds in two schemes in the form of support for cinema, respectively. cultural cinema projects, and in the form of support for the film industry through film incentives. Currently, the fund's activities and support for cinema are financed mainly from its own activities and so-called. parafiscal charges from private entities which have their business activity based on the use of audiovisual works. Support through film incentives is financed from the state budget. A fundamental change in the proposed regulation is the creation of a new stable source of support for cinema, namely the participation of the state budget. It is proposed that an annual subsidy be granted from the State budget for the Fund's activities, the amount of which shall correspond to the amount of revenue realised by the audiovisual fee fund for the calendar year preceding the calendar year in which the draft budget of the Fund is drawn up. The support will be intended mainly for the development and production of Czech cinematographic works, as well as support for cinemas, promotion of Czech cinema in the Czech Republic and abroad, or the training of a film viewer. Other fundamental changes concern the actual process of granting support from the Fund, as well as the scheme for providing film incentives. The submitted amendment makes the present film incentive system more effective in the sense that the film incentive applicant can apply at any time during the fiscal year, not only once a year, as has been the case so far. These changes will allow better use of the capacity of the Czech film industry and avoid possible exits of potential investors, let us understand foreign production. Thank you for your attention.
