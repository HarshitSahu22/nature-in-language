Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Nagoya Protocol on access to genetic resources and fair and equal sharing of benefits arising from their use, signed in New York on 23 June 2011 /German press 345/ - first reading I ask the Minister of the Environment to take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-02-ps2013-026-06-002-073.u2	Good morning or good morning, ladies and gentlemen, Mr President, colleagues. I'm glad to be back to my point after a long time. So let me briefly present the Government's proposal to give consent to the ratification of the so-called The Nagoya Protocol on access to genetic resources and fair and equal sharing of benefits arising from their use. The Nagoya Protocol was adopted on 29 October 2010 at the 10th meeting of the Conference of the Parties to the Convention on Biological Diversity. The Czech Republic has been a member of this Convention since 1994. The Protocol regulates the conditions for access to genetic resources and related traditional knowledge, fair and equivalent sharing of benefits arising from the use of these resources, also the principles for compliance by users of genetic resources and related knowledge and, last but not least, the principles for measures such as monitoring, collection and transmission of information to the international information system. The international treaty also deals with the dissemination of general awareness of the importance of biodiversity of genetic resources and related traditional knowledge by capacity building and technology transfer in the area. Effective implementation of the Protocol will create the prerequisites for increasing legal certainty and trust of the entities concerned at both international and national level. Ratification of this international treaty is also in the interests of business entities, as well as scientific and research entities, as they will have a specific legal framework for their activities for the acquisition of genetic resources and related traditional knowledge in this matter. Let me just remind you that the Government has already agreed to the Nagoya Protocol on 15 September last year and the Senate has discussed the proposal on 14 January and has given consent to its ratification. Thank you for your attention.
