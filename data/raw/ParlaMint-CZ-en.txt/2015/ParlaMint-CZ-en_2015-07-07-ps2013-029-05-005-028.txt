Government draft law amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the Act on the End of Pension Savings /The House Print 494/ - First Reading in accordance with § 90 para. 2 Now I ask that the Government's bill amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the Act on the End of Pension Savings, House Press 494, state the Prime Minister and Finance Minister Andrej Babiš. Please, Minister, take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-07-ps2013-029-05-005-028.u2	Thank you, Madam President. Ladies and gentlemen, as I have already mentioned in Parliament's press 493, the bill amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the Act on the termination of pension savings by the so-called accompanying law contains the necessary amendments to the other regulations concerned. The proposals submitted for amendments to the legislation in connection with the termination of the second pillar include proposals for partial amendments in the third pillar. These relate in particular to allowing entry into the system and for children and releasing certain investment limits in order to allow a higher evaluation of participants' resources. Further changes in the third pillar will be made on the basis of the ongoing work of the Expert Committee on Pension Reform. Thank you for your attention.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-07-ps2013-029-05-005-028.u3	I thank you and I ask that the rapporteur take the floor for first reading, Mr Roman Kubíček.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-07-ps2013-029-05-005-028.u4	Thank you for the floor, Madam Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, the main objective of the proposed law is to ensure compliance with the termination of pension savings and the associated possibility of the participant to pay pension insurance premiums to the entire legal order of the Czech Republic. It is necessary to take account of the disappearance of this fund system in other legislation. Furthermore, in view of the state's interest in creating favourable conditions for the creation of long-term sustainable and socially adequate pensions and the sustainability of public finances, the proposal regulates the area of the so-called third pillar to motivate participants to long-term individual savings on old age. It is necessary to strengthen the attractiveness of supplementary pension savings and its capacity to evaluate participants' resources. Thank you for your attention.
