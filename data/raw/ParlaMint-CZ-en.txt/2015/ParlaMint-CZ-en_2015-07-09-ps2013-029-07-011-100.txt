ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-011-100.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the relations between the Czech Republic and the Ethiopian Federal Democratic Republic in the field of civil aviation are not currently regulated. On the basis of the interest of both parties in the negotiation of the Agreement, an expert delegation was held in 2011, resulting in a mutually acceptable text of the Agreement. The Czech Government agreed to the agreement by its resolution in June 2012 and subsequently the agreement was signed in November 2014. The agreement does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not affect state budget expenditure. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic for the referral of material for discussion in the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
