Tax evasion has a negative impact not only on honest entrepreneurs, but on all citizens of the Czech Republic who carry tax burdens on their shoulders. Taxes are paid to all state services, i.e. to operate police, firefighters, army, education, health, social area, transport, repairs of state property, such as roads and highways, which we all use.
