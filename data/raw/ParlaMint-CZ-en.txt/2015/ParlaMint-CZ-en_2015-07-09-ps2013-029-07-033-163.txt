Report on petitions received by the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic, their content and manner of processing for the period from 1. 7. to 31. 12. 2014. The Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic received a total of 18 petitions during this period. The largest number of signatures, 42 554, was collected for the draft law on the National Park of Šumava. Petenti demanded that the Senate bill be rejected. A large number of citizens, 24 520, disagreed with the granting of special rights to the religious organization of the headquarters of Muslim communities. A serious petition for the removal of all ammunition and dangerous materials from the premises of former military warehouses in the Cadastral of the villages of Vlachovice and Vrbětice was also a serious petition during this period. When dealing with petitions, the procedure is governed by Act No. 85/1990 Coll., on the Law of Petition, and Act 90/1995 Coll., on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, as amended. 85. resolution of the Committee on Petitions from the 15th meeting of 3 March 2015 on the report on petitions adopted by the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic, their content and manner of processing for the period from 1. 7. to 31. 12. 2014: Committee on Petitions I. Approves the submitted report on petitions received by the Chamber of Deputies, their content and how they are processed for the period from 1. 7. to 31. 12. 2014; II. Recommends to Parliament's Chamber of Deputies to adopt the following resolution: Parliament's Chamber of Deputies takes note of the Report on petitions adopted by the Chamber of Deputies, their content and the way they are handled during the period from 1. 7. to 31. 12. 2014; III. empowers the Chair of the Committee to inform the Chamber of Deputies of this resolution and to justify the report submitted.
