Government draft law on conformity assessment of specified products when making them available on the market /German press 565/ - first reading pursuant to § 90 para. 2 The Minister of Industry and Trade Jan Mládek will present the proposal on behalf of the Government. Please take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-20-ps2013-033-05-010-052.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, the reason for submitting a draft law on the assessment of the conformity of specified products when they are placed on the market is to ensure proper implementation of the European Union legislation to be applied within the single European market. The law partially transposes 12 directives and one regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council. These are sectors such as explosives, simple pressure vessels, electromagnetic compatibility of weights with non-automatic activity, gauges, lifts, equipment for use in explosion environments, electrical equipment, radio equipment, pressure equipment, marine equipment, recreational craft and personal protective equipment. The act referred to regulates the general requirements for specified products placed on the market, the rights and obligations of the persons who place them on the market or on the market supply the specified products, the conditions and methods of defining the specified products and setting out the technical requirements which those products must comply with before they are placed on the market. The law also regulates the exercise of administrative activities for products which are regulated by the directly applicable EU rules on the marketing of products. Finally, I would like to ask you for support for the adoption of the law at first reading in accordance with § 90 para. 2 of the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, on the ground that this is a technical regulation on the basis of which government regulations will be adopted into the Czech legal order of the EU directive. Another reason for the nearest entry into force of the Act is the possibility of early initiation of a procedure allowing notified bodies to carry out the activities required for conformity assessment. Thank you for your attention.

ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-20-ps2013-033-05-010-052.u6	Thank you for the floor, Madam Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, the aim of the regulation is solely to fulfil the legislative obligations arising from membership of the European Union for the Czech Republic. Compliance with harmonised technical requirements is a condition for the free movement of products in the single European market. Therefore, ensuring a high quality regulatory framework at national level is essential for the competitiveness of domestic producers within this market. This also applies mutatis mutandis to importers from third countries and to limited extent to distributors of products from other Member States. By not adopting this legislation, there would be a risk that the Czech Republic may be exposed to the possibility of initiating a non-compliance procedure under Article 260 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. At the same time, there would be a risk that other Member States of the European Union will legitimately prevent them from moving in the internal market of the Union for specified products placed on the market under the EU directives that are not properly implemented. Because the veto was expressed on the recommendation of consent at first reading... The reason for trying to accelerate this process is that this directive should apply from April next year. If this law or bill is not being processed, there is a risk that our testing institutes will not be able to certify these products for Czech producers and Czech manufacturers will have to carry out this certification in certified facilities abroad. I therefore recommend that the law be reduced by at least 30 days. That's my suggestion. Thank you.
