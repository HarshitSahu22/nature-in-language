Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Ethiopian Federal Democratic Republic on Air Transport /German Press 431/ - Second I ask that the Minister of Transport Dan Ťok take the floor here as well. Please, Minister.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-030-131.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the relations between the Czech Republic and the Ethiopian Federal Democratic Republic in the field of civil aviation are not currently regulated. On the basis of the interest of both parties in the negotiation of the Agreement, an expert delegation meeting was held in 2011, resulting in the text of the Agreement. The Government of the Czech Republic agreed to the agreement by its resolution in June 2012. The agreement was subsequently signed in November 2014. The text of the agreement is very liberal, consistent with current developments in civil aviation. It is consistent with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, including obligations arising from membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. The agreement does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not affect state budget expenditure. The agreement represents one way to further develop relations between the two countries. The air carriers of both parties offer a wide range of possibilities for commercial use. I would like to ask you in this way to agree to ratify this contractual document. Thank you.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-030-131.u3	Thank you, Minister. I will also ask the rapporteur of Mr Pavel Plzák's foreign committee here to justify the resolution of the committee that has been delivered to you as House Press 431/1. Please, Mr. Ambassador.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-030-131.u4	Thank you, Mr President, once again for the floor. Ladies and gentlemen, the Foreign Committee discussed this Treaty at its 20th meeting on 3 September 2015. As has already been said, this treaty was created on the initiative of the Ethiopian party, which had an interest in flying in the pool with Lufthansa to Prague. Since this initiative was launched five years ago, we were wondering if these Ethiopian Airlines still existed. They exist, even fly with modern Dreamliner Boeings. After the reasons of Deputy Minister of Transport Mgr Rudolecký Rudolecký, Mr Pavel Plzák's intelligence report and, following the debate, the Foreign Committee recommends in its resolution: The Chamber of Deputies adopted the following resolution: "The Chamber of Deputies agrees to ratify the agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Ethiopian Federal Democratic Republic on air transport." Thank you.
