This is the government proposal to avoid a negative practice, which is that it is terminated by teachers after ten months and subsequently a new employment relationship has been concluded with them since September of the following school year. During the discussion in the Chamber of Deputies, there was also a vote in favour of and support for amendments concerning the professional qualifications of selected categories of teaching staff in order to anchor the qualifications of graduates of higher education arts courses for the teaching of artistically focused subjects. The Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports agreed, and consequently the Chamber of Deputies supported this amendment. Unfortunately, this legislative amendment made in the Chamber of Deputies was not reflected in the provisions of Section 22 of the Law on teaching workers, and therefore the Senate responded with its amendment. Therefore, if the Senate version is supported, there is a correction of an inconsistent adjustment that could otherwise cause theoretically so-called performers to teach other subjects than art. Therefore, the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports proposes to approve the Act as approved by the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. Thank you for your attention.
