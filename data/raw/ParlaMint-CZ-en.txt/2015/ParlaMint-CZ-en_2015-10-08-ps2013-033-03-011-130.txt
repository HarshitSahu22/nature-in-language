ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-08-ps2013-033-03-011-130.u2	Thank you, Mr President, on behalf of myself and on behalf of Iceland, for getting to it today. So let me briefly introduce you to the content of the material presented. At the 8th meeting of the Parties to the Kyoto Protocol, held at the turn of November and December 2012 in Doha, Qatar, the Parties adopted an amendment to the Kyoto Protocol, on the basis of which the European Union and its Member States, together with Iceland, undertook to reduce their greenhouse gas emissions by 20 per cent compared to the 1990 reference year. In accordance with Article four paragraph 1 of the Kyoto Protocol and on the basis of a declaration made in connection with the adoption of the amendment to the Kyoto Protocol at the Doha Climate Conference, the European Union, its Member States and Iceland will fulfil that reduction commitment jointly. Iceland is not a member of the European Union and therefore the European Union and its Member States conclude with it a separate international agreement on Iceland's participation in the joint fulfilment of the commitments of the European Union, its Member States and Iceland in the second Kyoto Protocol control period, defining the conditions for the fulfilment of the reduction commitment in 2013 to 2020. In the proposed agreement, Iceland undertakes, inter alia, to ensure that aggregate anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions expressed in carbon dioxide equivalent as defined in the Kyoto Protocol amendment do not exceed the assigned amount of greenhouse gas emissions set out in the Agreement during the second control period. It also has an obligation to comply with the European Union legislation relating to the implementation of the Kyoto Protocol amendment and to fulfil other technical conditions. The implementation of the Agreement and the monitoring of the fulfilment of the agreed commitments will be dealt with so-called a joint implementation committee. Its members will be representatives of the European Union, the Member States of the European Union and Iceland. Thank you for your attention.
