Colleagues, colleagues, this proposal is submitted following a proposal for a law on the assessment of the conformity of specified products when they are placed on the market under a new legislative framework. At the same time as the Act on Conformity Assessment is adopted, Act No. 22 adapt in such a way as not to duplicate the provisions on notified bodies, but referred to the Conformity Assessment Act, which comprehensively addresses the issue of bodies entrusted with the performance of conformity assessment activities, the so-called notification of bodies. Implementation of EU rules thriving with the Institute of Notified Bodies will not be ensured by means of Act No. 22/1997 Coll. and a government order issued for its implementation, but a conformity assessment act. Given that it is necessary to ensure that the notification of the bodies of the European Commission pursuant to the Conformity Assessment Act is carried out as soon as possible and therefore it is not possible to agree on the veto basis in the first reading, I propose a reduction of 30 days again.
