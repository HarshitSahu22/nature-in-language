Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to make a brief statement and thus remind you of what is called. a rate amendment to the Consumer Tax Act. This rate amendment shall be submitted in order to comply with the minimum requirements for the taxation of cigarettes laid down in Council Directive No 1/2004/EC. This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. One requirement is to ensure a minimum specific taxation of cigarettes corresponding to EUR 90 per thousand. In 2014, this minimum requirement was met; for 2015, the temporary derogation provided for in the abovementioned Directive applies. However, for 2016, the rates of excise duty need to be adjusted so that minimum taxation is restored. The result will be an increase in excise duty on cigarettes by about three crowns per box of 20 pieces. The tax increase was discussed as part of expert negotiations, so that it would fall evenly on all price segments, thus there was no distortion of the market. At the same time, an increase in the excise duty rate for other tobacco commodities, i.e. cigars, cigarillos and smoking tobacco, is proposed. Smoking tobacco has so far been taxed equivalently -

In order to ensure the stability of the tobacco industry and the predictability of tax revenues, it is further proposed to set excise duty rates for all tobacco products over the medium term. The years 2017 and 2018 proposed a gradual increase in excise duty on cigarettes by between CZK 0,90 and 1 per box in 2017 respectively. by 1,20 in 2018. A slight and regular increase in excise duty on tobacco products is more acceptable for all concerned than a jumping increase. Regular and moderate increases in excise duty will thus help prevent the unwanted growth of the black market in tobacco products, which is at risk of a significant one-off increase in their price. Minor amendments to the provisions relating in particular to the regulation of authorisation procedures and procedures for the provision of selected products or means of transport, the need for which arose from practice, are also part of the tariff amendment of the Act on excise duties. Since the rate amendment to the Consumer Tax Act contains a three-year plan to increase the taxation of tobacco products, the effectiveness of the individual changes is proposed on a regular basis with a distance of the whole calendar year, i.e. always from 1 January 2016, 2017 and 2018.
