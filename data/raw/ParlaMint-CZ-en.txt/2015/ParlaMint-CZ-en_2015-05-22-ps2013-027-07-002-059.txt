Allow me to present my news report to the House of Press 404 on the amendment to the Act on Public Auctions. The purpose of the proposed legislation, as the applicant has already mentioned, is that on 1. 12. 2014 came into force by a law which amends certain laws in the field of the financial market, and in the second part of this law there was an amendment to the Act on limitation of payments in cash and on the administration of taxes and charges. From this date, payments of more than CZK 270 000 must not be made without cash, causing a problem with the Act on public auctions. As the petitioner has already mentioned, the law on public auctions says that receiving cash cannot be restricted. The amendment to the Act on public auctions simply unifies the amount of the cash limit in a simple way and sets it in such a way that it will always be flexiblely subject to the Act on limitation of cash payments. As Mr President has already mentioned, the Government has discussed the amendment proposal and has given its consent to the bill. From the rapporteur's point of view, I do not have any fundamental comments on the proposed amendment, and I agree with the appellants to agree with the proposed legislation at first reading in accordance with the provisions of Section 90 (2) of the Rules of Procedure. This Decision shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. Thank you.
