ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-02-ps2013-026-06-010-112.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce this material. It is a 2nd addition to the plan of military exercises units and staffs of the Czech Army with foreign partners, as was said, in the territory of the Czech Republic and outside them. He informs about planned exercises with foreign partners in 2014, which was not included in the Information on military exercises of units and crews of the Czech Republic with foreign partners in and outside the Czech Republic in 2014, as approved by the Government Resolution of 8 January 2014 No. No 32 or 1st supplement to this material approved by the Government order of 28 May 2014 399. The exercises referred to in the second supplement were financed under the approved budget of the Ministry of Defence chapter for 2014 within the meaning of Section 22 of Act No. 218/2000 Coll., on budgetary rules and on the amendment of certain related laws. There were therefore no additional requirements for funding in the area of foreign exercises. Part of this part of the materials is an annex containing completed exercises in 2014. These are four exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and three exercises in our territory, one of which has already been approved and is only specified in the supplement. The training of GROUND PEPPER on the territory of Slovakia and the IRON SWORD exercises on the territory of Lithuania were further planned in the framework of measures to assure allies about alliance security and the validity of NATO collective defence commitments in the context of the crisis in Ukraine. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to take this information into account. Thank you.
