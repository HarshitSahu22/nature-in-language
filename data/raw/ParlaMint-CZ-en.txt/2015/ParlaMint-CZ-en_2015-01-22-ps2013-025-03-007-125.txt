ParlaMint-CZ_2015-01-22-ps2013-025-03-007-125.u2	Mr President, thank you for the floor. Dear colleagues, the Czech Government resolution number 548 of 9 July 2014 approving the Report on the situation in the area of migration and integration of foreigners in the Czech Republic for 2013 was imposed on the Minister of the Interior to justify this report in the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. The report I submit is informative. It describes the situation in the area of legal and illegal migration, international protection and integration of foreigners in the Czech Republic and provides a basic analysis of migration trends. At the same time, it provides information on legislative activities relating to the Act on the residence of foreigners, i.e. Act No. 326/1999 Coll., and Act on Asylum, i.e. Act 325/1999 Coll., and Act on Employment, i.e. Act No. 435/2004 Coll. The report also pays attention to the evaluation of Schengen cooperation within the European Union and within the framework of the Schengen evaluation of the Czech Republic, the main part of which took place in 2012. The report also deals with the area of visa policy, the issue of implementation of readmission agreements and voluntary returns, foreign development cooperation of the Czech Republic, projects by the Ministry of Interior, which focus on specific categories of foreigners. Furthermore, humanitarian projects and migration projects are described here. The report also characterizes the criminal activity of foreigners committed in the Czech Republic. The fact in the report shows that the situation in the area of migration and integration of foreigners in the territory of the Czech Republic in 2013 did not make significant changes against developments in recent years and the migration and security situation in the Czech Republic can be seen as stable in this respect. Allow me now a few up-to-date data for 2014. On 31 December 2014, 451 923 foreigners were registered with authorised temporary or permanent residence in the Czech Republic, which is 10 387 more than at the end of 2013. Strangers thus account for around 4 percent of the current population in the Czech Republic. The following trends arise from this report and from these analyses: In the Czech Republic there are already more foreigners with permanent residence status, namely 251 thousand, than with temporary residence status, where this status currently has about 200 thousand foreigners. This is the result of the economic crisis, when the arrival of foreigners has been more or less stable since 2008. The number of foreigners from countries outside the European Union is 266 thousand, of which almost two thirds, 67 percent, reside in a permanent territory. The number of foreigners from European Union countries is 185 thousand, but citizens of this area are not obliged to register and therefore the real number is probably higher. [[12.40 hours]] Of these citizens of the European Union, there are most Slovák, about 90 thousand, then there are Germans and Poland, around 19,000. Newcomers are therefore approximately 15,000 a year, with more than half coming to educational activities in the Czech Republic, around 61 percent, in the last year the purpose of the stay has increased again, and this is the purpose of employment, about 15 percent. From the point of view of transit illegal migration, the Czech Republic was not part of the main migration routes. Foreigners were mainly provided for illegal stay in the Czech Republic, about 95,7 percent of the total number of foreigners secured in connection with illegal migration in the Czech Republic, so this number is almost absolute. In the 12 months of 2014, 4,822 people were provided in connection with illegal migration. Compared to the same period of the previous year, it is an increase of around 670 people. On 4 September 2014, the Security Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic discussed the report and took note of it by its resolution number 43. I believe that the material evaluated the issue in 2013 in accordance with the Government resolution, and I would therefore ask you to take note of the report submitted. Thank you for your attention, ladies and gentlemen.
