ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-017-104.u2	Yes. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me present to you one of the Economic Partnership Agreements. This is the type of agreements that are concluded with countries such as Africa, the Caribbean or the Pacific. Here, in this case, what I'm presenting to you is an agreement with the Caribbean states. This is an agreement, a so-called mixed treaty, to be ratified and ratified by all Member States of the European Union. Its purpose, of course, is to encourage integration, in this case the Caribbean countries, into the world economic system and increase their benefits from international trade. As from 29 December 2008, the Agreement shall be provisionally applied by the Union and the CARIFORA States within the limits of Union competence. This agreement, which is presented to you, is in line with the constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, the obligations arising from EU membership, as well as the obligations assumed under other applicable treaties and generally recognised principles of international law. It will have no direct impact on the state budget.
