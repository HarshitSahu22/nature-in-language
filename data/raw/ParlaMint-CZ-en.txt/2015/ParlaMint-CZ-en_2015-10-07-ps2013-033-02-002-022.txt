ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-07-ps2013-033-02-002-022.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Colleagues, let me briefly remind you of what has already been said here in the first reading of the Government amendment to the Act on the return of illegally exported cultural goods. The main reason for the proposed legislation is the adoption of a new Directive of the European Parliament and of the Council on the return of cultural goods unlawfully exported from the territory of a Member State which will replace the existing Directive with effect from 19 December 2015. The obligation to implement the new directive must be fulfilled by individual Member States, including the Czech Republic, by 18 December 2015. The directive does not contain fundamental changes compared to the existing regulation, the existing procedures remain maintained and the proposed amendment does not therefore constitute a major amendment to that law, but only the necessary interventions. Among the changes proposed by the proposed legislation is incorporated into Act No. 101/2001 Coll., includes, in particular, a new, broader definition of cultural goods, which will be part of the national cultural treasure of a Member State without other restrictive criteria to date. [[9.50 hours]] In the future, it is sufficient that the subject matter, the cultural property, is identified or defined by the Member States as a national cultural treasure of artistic, historical or archaeological value in accordance with Article 36 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. At the same time, it will no longer be a condition that it must be part of a public or other collection of museums, galleries, archives or libraries, or belong to specified categories or meet the criteria of age or financial value as set out in the Annex, which is simultaneously deleted in this context. The national cultural treasure already defines the existing wording of § 2 para. 3 of the Law. Further amendments consist in the extension of certain time limits, for example for examining cultural goods from two to six months, for bringing an action before a competent court in a Member State from one year to three. At the same time, the possibility of communication with the use of an internal market information exchange system is introduced for mutual cooperation between Member States in returning illegally exported cultural goods, the so-called IMI system. The draft law was discussed on 3 September 2015 in the Committee on Science, Education, Culture, Youth and Sports, which did not comment on it. I therefore propose that the Chamber of Deputies approve this bill. Thank you for your attention.
