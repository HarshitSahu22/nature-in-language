In addition to amendments related to pyrotechnic articles, the pyrotechnics Act also contains a technical amendment to Act No. 156/2000 Coll., on the verification of firearms and ammunition which the law adapts to the requirements of the Brussels Convention on the Mutual Recognition of Test Marks for Small Arms.
