Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to mention the parliamentary proposal for an amendment to the law which is being conducted in the system as House Press No. 366. Last year, Parliament of the Czech Republic adopted a law on providing care in a children's group. The aim of this law was to extend the possibility of caring for pre-school children, as well as tax support for both operators of facilities providing such care and parents placing their children in such facilities. The desired effect was to support parents in aligning work and family life and their earlier return to the labour market. However, the preparation of the law did not take into account equipment and services before the adoption of the law for a long time well functioning, but which, by their very nature or for financial or other reasons, cannot fulfil the criteria of this law. These are, for example, forest kindergartens, low-floor facilities, children's clubs and other facilities. [[12.40 hours]] Since the effects on such already existing facilities and services could not be taken into account during the legislative process itself, or was the effort, but did not, it is appropriate to take these effects into account by amending the law. If the amendment to this law had not been adopted, these devices would have had to cease their activities, which would have significantly negative effects both in moral and economic terms. This amendment changes the provisions of the Child Care Act in a children's group so that - and this is the key moment - the registration of service providers becomes voluntary. The conditions of the law will therefore have to be met only by those providers who register with the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs. At the same time, only these providers will be able to benefit from the tax relief resulting from the Income Tax Act. Thus, the motivation for setting up children's groups remains for employers, universities and other entities, which is actually the main purpose of this child group law. At the same time, the existing wide range of pre-school childcare facilities that have been operating successfully for many years and have a positive impact on employment and employment and non-working lives of persons whose application would be excluded due to child care. The most important points dealt with in the amendment to this law can be summarised in four points. It comes first, and it is a merit of the whole amendment, an optional registration of the provider, and there is a specification of the benefit user, a terminology modification and a transitional annual deadline for registration is set out. Finally, I would like to add that the proposed amendment is the fulfillment of the promise made by the presidents of coalition clubs, and was discussed with representatives of all member clubs except the KSČM club, which was invited but did not accept the invitation, or has a different, completely different view on this matter. It was also discussed with representatives of those pre-school alternatives, which it most affects, especially with representatives of the Czech Council of Children and Youth, with the Association of Forest Kindergarten Schools, with Man in Need and others. I would like to ask you to support this amendment. Thank you.
