ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-05-ps2013-035-02-005-007.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce the bill amending Act No. 586/1992 Coll., on Income Tax, as amended. The draft law is a response in particular to the requirements arising from the need to fulfil the content of the relevant items of the Government's declaration and the coalition contract and the mandatory implementation of the new directive of the Council of the European Union in the field of income tax by 31 December 2015 at the latest. In accordance with the Government's programming declaration, a further increase in the tax advantage on dependent children is proposed for 2016. The second child from the current CZK 15 804 per year at CZK 17 004 per year and the third and each other from the existing CZK 17 004 per year at CZK 20 604 per year. This is already the second parametric increase of parametric advantage on the child. In the context of the implementation of the amendment to Council Directive 2011/96/EU on a common system of taxation for parent and subsidiary companies from different Member States, it is explicitly established that the parent company cannot apply the exemption of the shares received in profit if the subsidiary can reduce its taxable amount. The reason for this adjustment is that some Member States allow, within the framework of their national tax legislation, to apply the profit shares paid as an item that reduces the tax base, which would lead to the so-called application of the directive. double non-taxation. Other partial proposed changes aim to address shortcomings in the legislation on income taxes, which have been reflected in application practice or in the adoption of another legislation. [[18.00 hours]] These are, for example, changes in the taxation of income from electricity generation for small sources which are not subject to business activity and whose performance is limited in terms of impact on the distribution system and serves mainly for own consumption, and the taxation of the share of profits paid to persons who are not members of a trading corporation. The Act is proposed as of 1 January 2016 with regard to the specified transposition periods. Finally, I ask you to approve the Government's bill amending Act No. 586/1992 Coll., on Income Tax, as amended. I am of the opinion that, in the context of a detailed debate, it will be proposed to shorten the time limit for the discussion of the draft law in committees to 30 days. Thank you for your attention.
