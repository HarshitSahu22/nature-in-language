ParlaMint-CZ_2015-05-21-ps2013-027-06-008-071.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen. Training of marine crew members plays an important role in maritime safety. For this reason, the Member States of the International Maritime Organisation (IMO) adopted the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers in 1978. The Convention entered into force on 28 April 1984 and entered into force on that date for the former Czechoslovak Socialist Republic. The Convention is now made up of its own text, Annex, Regulation and STCW. In view of developments in technology in particular, a comprehensive revision of this Convention has been decided. The Conference of the Parties adopted extensive amendments to the Annex and Regulation in Manila, Filipino. Manila changes, it was June 25, 2010. The adoption of Manila changes is necessary in order to strengthen safety, life and property at sea, to protect the marine environment and to ensure the possibility of 1200 Czech sailors, crew members of naval ships to apply to the labour market. In order to formalise the process of negotiating the Convention with current constitutional requirements, I also submit the original text of the Convention together with Manila amendments for ratification. Manila changes are in line with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, with obligations arising from membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union and obligations arising from international law. The implementation of Manila changes will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. I would like to ask you in this way to give my consent to ratify this agreement. Thank you.
