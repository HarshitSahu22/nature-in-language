Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, with the material presented, we are carrying out a task which results from Section 14, paragraph 6 of Law No. 526/1990 Coll., on Prices, as amended by Act No. 403/2009 Coll., amending Act No. 526/1990 Coll., on prices, where it is imposed that the government presents an overview of the activities of price control bodies for the previous calendar year to the Chamber of Deputies by 30 April each year. The revised summary is a summary of the results of the price controls carried out by all the existing price control authorities during the year considered, i.e. Specialized Financial Office, Customs Office for Central Bohemia Region, State Energy Inspection, Energy Regulatory Office, State Office for Drug Control, Czech Telecommunications Office, City Hall of Prague, regional authorities and municipalities. In the case of price controls carried out by the Specialised Financial Authority in 2013, this audit focused in particular on prices for which factually regulated prices are applied, such as water prices, drinking water, transferred and drained, collection prices, transport, disposal of municipal waste, as well as prices of crematorium services and cemetery services provided in connection with the hire and use of grave sites and prices of health performances. The control activity of the regions and municipalities focuses mainly on the control and labelling of goods prices and, in the case of the City Council of Prague, also on checking whether maximum prices in taxi services are exceeded. The Energy Regulatory Authority is also involved in price controls, which focused on compliance with the rules on price regulation in kind, in particular in the heat and electricity sectors. The centre of price controls has moved to this office from the State Energy Inspection. The Czech Telecommunications Office carries out price control in the field of telecommunications. Respect for prices, regulation of prices of medicines and medical devices is controlled by the State Institute for Drug Control. The inspection report submitted shows that price rules are still being violated, with the scope of price control dependent on relatively limited capacity options. In this context, I would like to point out that I consider it wrong that the number of price control workers does not meet the needs. The negative phenomenon is definitely the fact that their number in the Ministry of Finance has been steadily declining in recent years. This personnel situation was addressed in the context of this review by the Supervisory Committee of the Chamber of Deputies at its meeting on 4 September and stated in the resolution that the number of staff of all inspection bodies was not sufficient. By strengthening price control, we adopted measures consisting of increasing the number of price control workers in 2015, in connection with the intention to transfer the price control of the Specialised Financial Office to the Ministry of Finance in the interests of operational management of these controls. This will happen immediately after minor changes to the law on the competence of the Czech authorities in the field of prices. Thank you for your attention.
