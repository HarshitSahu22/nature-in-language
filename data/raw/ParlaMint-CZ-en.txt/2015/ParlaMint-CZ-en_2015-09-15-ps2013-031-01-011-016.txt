ParlaMint-CZ_2015-09-15-ps2013-031-01-011-016.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the Government's proposal for a law on the Czech Fire Rescue Corps and on the amendment of certain follow-up laws. The existing legal regulation of the position and organisation of the Fire Rescue Corps is contained primarily in the current Act No. 238/2000 Coll., on the Fire Rescue Corps of the Czech Republic and on the amendment of certain laws, and in implementing legislation. There have been various changes since its effectiveness affecting the functioning of the Fire Rescue Corps, both in legal and factual matters. For example, this is the case. on developments in technology, communications, information systems and others. A proposal for a new law has therefore been drawn up which takes account of all the changes that have taken place in the past period. We are seeking to establish the legal anchoring and legal status and competence of the Fire Rescue Corps in order to meet the practice of today and the needs of today. The aim is to achieve a state where the position of the Fire Rescue Corps is regulated in a clear and systematic manner, including institutes which are not currently regulated. Following the current legislative process at the level of the Chamber of Deputies, I consider it necessary to thank you for discussing the bill in particular in the Committee on Security, which adopted an amendment which contributes to improving this standard. In particular, it is a refinement in the field of flue gas routes or the possibility of grant titles for all fire protection units. I am ready to assess and at the third reading to comment on all the amendments that have been applied at second reading, as always, of course. Thank you for your attention.
