Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the rámcový Agreement on Partnership and Cooperation between the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and Mongolia, on the other, signed in Ulánbatar on 30 April 2013 /German press 227/ - Second reading I now ask the Minister of Foreign Affairs Lubomír Zaorálek to present the press. ParlaMint-CZ_2015-01-23-ps2013-025-04-020-073.u2	Thank you. Dear colleagues, perhaps I can say that the Czech Republic has a very friendly relationship with Mongolia. In recent days, we have even been visited by the Mongolian President, Mr Elbegdordž, as you may have also noticed, which is, of course, just a demonstration of how friendly and quite developed relations are. It is no wonder that I will recommend this agreement between the European Union and Mongolia, which actually replaces the existing contractual base, which is unfortunately quite outdated and does not reflect the reality of today. Mongolia is regarded by the European Union as an important partner, which is also due to large stocks of mineral resources. Let me just remind you that as far as Czech companies are concerned, many companies are active in the Czech Republic in the field of mining industry, health care, transport and energy. We, too, would want to get the level of trade relations to a higher level, because when I was talking about relatively good friendly relationships here at the beginning, I still think the economy isn't responding very well. I think we almost have a duty to support the modernisation of the contractual base and it would be something that would definitely benefit Czech-Mongolian relations.

I would like to introduce you to the committee's resolution. Following the reasoning of the first Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs, Petr Drulák and Mr Robin Böhnisch's report, and after the debate, the Foreign Committee recommends: The Chamber of Deputies to adopt the following resolution: The Chamber of Deputies agrees to ratify the Framework Agreement on Partnership and Cooperation between the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and Mongolia, on the other, signed in Ulanbatar on 30 April 2013. Furthermore, it instructs the President of the Committee to present this resolution to the President of the Chamber of Deputies and empowers the rapporteur to report to the meeting of the Chamber of Deputies on the results of this government proposal at a meeting of the Foreign Committee. Thank you for your attention.
