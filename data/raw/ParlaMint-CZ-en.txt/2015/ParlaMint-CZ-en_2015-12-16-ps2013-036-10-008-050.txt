ParlaMint-CZ_2015-12-16-ps2013-036-10-008-050.u2	Mr President, colleagues, let me, under the authority of the Government, introduce a bill amending Act No. 111/2009 Coll., on basic registers, as amended, and some other laws. The aim of the proposal is to simplify processes in the management of basic registers and their associated agenda information systems. The main principles of the proposed regulation are to take into account the practical experience gained during the three-year operation of the system of basic registers, to enable the public authorities to use direct access to the agenda information systems in specified cases without the obligation to use such access through the information system of basic registers and to allow the use of data from basic registers and to bodies other than public authorities, provided that other legislation so provides. The system of basic registers has been in operation for more than three years. During this period, the Ministry of the Interior has gathered a number of knowledge and experience justifying the need to amend certain provisions of the Basic Registers Act. It is therefore proposed to clarify some existing provisions and to remove the interpretational uncertainties of the law, thereby reducing the administrative burden on entities and improving the data content of the basic registers. Thank you for your attention.
