Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Visegrad Patent Institute Agreement signed in Bratislava on 26 February 2015 /German press 447/ - first reading I now give the floor to the Minister of Industry and Trade, Jan Mládek, who will give us his opening words. Please, Minister.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-05-27-ps2013-027-08-004-085.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the Visegrad Patent Institute Agreement is a measure to promote innovation and to strengthen the competitiveness of companies in the region. Czech industry, research and development urgently need help protecting technical innovation abroad. By creating the Visegrad Patent Institute, the Visegrad Group States seek to facilitate foreign protection to their applicants by reducing the costs of research while maintaining the necessary quality. The advantage will undoubtedly also be communication in the Czech language. The approval of the International Union for Patent Cooperation, which will meet in October 2015, is required for the creation of the VPI. However, it is necessary for the agreement to enter into force before this meeting. Therefore, the Czech Republic must ratify the agreement by June 2015 at the latest in order to subsequently deposit the instrument of ratification with the depositary by the end of July and it will become valid by the end of September 2015. Due to the changes considered in the identification of international research bodies, the Czech Republic would be at risk of never having to create the Visegrad Patent Institute and of all the efforts that the Czech Republic has put into the negotiation of the agreement so far. The establishment of the VPI will not require any investment or new employees to use existing professional research and research capacities of the national patent offices of the Contracting Parties. As Minister of Industry and Trade, I welcome the approval of the ratification of the agreement by the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. Thank you for your attention.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-05-27-ps2013-027-08-004-085.u3	I thank the Minister and now I ask the rapporteur for the first reading, Mr Pavel Šrámek. Please, rapporteur, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-05-27-ps2013-027-08-004-085.u4	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, I believe that the Minister has said all the essential things here, including the fact that one of the specific benefits of the agreement will also be a significant reduction in the cost of fees associated with international applications under the patent cooperation agreement. For each application, in addition to the registration fee, it is necessary to apply for the search that the Minister has referred to and to pay the administrative fee, which is currently EUR 1 875 for electronically submitted applications. [[9.40 hours]] The provisions of the Visegrad Institute will reduce the financial burden on applicants, i.e. entrepreneurs, to about half. The expected administrative fee for the search will therefore be between 800 and 900 euros. I must also remind you that these are groups of only four states that decide together. It can therefore be assumed that most requests for research will remain in the applicant's country, so income will also go into the country's state budget. Another advantage is that the opinion will be delivered in the language of the applicant's country, and this is very important for the text of patents. In the case of a patent dispute, language differences are sometimes crucial in decision making. I therefore recommend ordering this press to the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
