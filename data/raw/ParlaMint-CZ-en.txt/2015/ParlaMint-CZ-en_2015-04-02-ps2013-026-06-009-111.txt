Information about military exercises units and staffs of the Czech Army with foreign partners in and outside the Czech Republic for the period January - June 2014 /German press 350/ This material will also be presented by Minister of Defence Martin Stropnický. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-02-ps2013-026-06-009-111.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me give you this information. The material contains an overview of the participation in military exercises of the Czech Army with foreign partners in and outside our territory during January - June 2014 and informs about the participation of commanders and crews of the Czech Republic in these exercises. In that period, military exercises were carried out in accordance with a plan approved by the Government Resolution of 8 January 2014 number 32 and the first supplement to the plan approved by the Government Resolution of 28 May 2014 number 399. Units and crews of the Czech Republic participated in 40 military exercises with foreign partners during this period. From this number 14 exercises took place in our territory. The total financial amount needed to carry out these exercises was 23 955 thousand. Crowns. During the reference period, these main military exercises were carried out. I will limit myself with your permission only to their names: BALT 2014, Composite Challenge, European Air Transport Training, further - and this is quite exotic shortcut - EQHTIC, further MOUNTAIN FLIGHT 2014, RELIBLE SWORD 2014, TOXIC LEAK 1, YELLOW CROSS. The list of other exercises for the period from January to June 2014 is set out in the Annex to the presented material. The Government took note of this material by its resolution of 1 October 2014 number 795. So, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I thank you and I ask that you take this information into account.
