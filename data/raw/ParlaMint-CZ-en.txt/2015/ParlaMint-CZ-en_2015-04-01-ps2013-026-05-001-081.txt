ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-01-ps2013-026-05-001-081.u2	Mr President, Prime Minister, honourable Ministers, honourable Members, I just want to explain very briefly what will be voted on. The most important thing in this proposal is the adjustment in the direction of cultivation of the execution of the execution procedure relating to spouses. This means that it should finally be ensured that if the spouses split up the joint assets, the executioner will also proceed precisely according to how the joint assets split up. So far, somewhat absurdly, the executor has prosecuted a joint fortune, as if the spouses hadn't split up at all, and then it was up to the other damaged husband to sue back, which, of course, has weakened all confidence in the rule of law. The second significant change in this respect is that it will be possible to achieve only half of the balance on account of the debtor. These are, from my point of view, the main elements of this proposal. At the same time, I would like to stress that an important complement to this proposal is the Member's proposal Tejc and other Members, in which a number of other measures appear, in which the Ministry of Justice participated. I believe that you will support the proposal in the third reading, because it is a proposal for which many people injured by unfair executions are impatiently waiting. Thank you.
