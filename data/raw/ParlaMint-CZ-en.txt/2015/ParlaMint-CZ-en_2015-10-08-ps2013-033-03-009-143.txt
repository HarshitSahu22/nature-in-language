ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-08-ps2013-033-03-009-143.u2	Thank you, Mr President. I feel a pleasant shaking that I finally got to the point that belongs to me. I'll also try to host him this way. Let me, then, Mr President, fellow Members, present to you the Government's proposal to ratify the amendment to Annex I to the Convention on the Effects of Industrial Accidents Transboundary States, adopted in Geneva on 5 December last year, i.e. 2014. The Czech Republic has taken over the obligations of the Contracting Party to the Convention on the Effects of Industrial Accidents Transboundary States with effect from 10 September 2000. The Convention currently has 41 Contracting Parties, including the European Union. The Convention supports the cooperation of the State in the preparation and implementation of national legislation on the prevention of major industrial accidents and the exchange of information on the experience gained in assessing the risks of accidents and their consequences. [[12.10 hours]] Amendment of the Annex I shall be carried out in order to harmonise the classification of dangerous substances in the Annex I referred to in the United Nations Globally Harmonised System for the Classification and Labelling of Chemicals and Directive 2012/18/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4. 7. 2012 on the control of major accident hazards with the presence of dangerous substances, i.e. so-called Directive SEVESO III. New version of the Annex I will support the harmonisation of the conventions and legislation of the European Union. Amendment of the Annex I the Convention is in accordance with EU law and the new Act No. 224/2015 Coll., on the prevention of major accidents, and its adoption will not have an impact on public administration, the business sector or the state budget of the Czech Republic. The proposal was initiated by the EU and its Member States, which, by adopting it, pushed their standards to the level of the European Economic Commission. Under the conditions of the Czech Republic it is a change to the Treaty of the Presidential category, therefore the approval of the Czech Republic and ratification by the President of the Republic is necessary to approve this amendment. Thank you for your attention.
