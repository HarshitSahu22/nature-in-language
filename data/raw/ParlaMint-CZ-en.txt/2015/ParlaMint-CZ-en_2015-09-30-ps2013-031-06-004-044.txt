ParlaMint-CZ_2015-09-30-ps2013-031-06-004-044.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce the bill on gambling tax. The bill on gambling tax follows up on the proposal for the Act on Gambling and together with the so-called "Gambling Tax Act'. By amending law, they form one whole which constitutes a new comprehensive regulation of the gambling market. The main objective of the new regulation is to ensure measures to prevent and combat sociopathological phenomena associated with gambling and the protection of players. As a result of the proposed legislation, there is an increase in tax revenues. The changes contained in the draft law are mainly aimed at increasing the taxation of gambling, to which the government has committed itself in the coalition contract and the government's programming declaration. [[12.30 hours]] The proposed law responds to the opening up of the gambling market to entities established abroad and to the increasing use of the Internet in this area. These entities, unlike the current legislation, will be newly taxed. There will also be taxation of illegal gambling operations, which are not subject to taxation today. The proposed gambling tax rates for individual gambling are higher than those existing for lottery levies and other similar games. The highest rate of gambling is 35 percent and will be applied to technical games, such as winning instruments or video lottery terminals. A rate of 30 per cent is proposed for live games, meaning card or roulette, lottery and bingo. The lowest rate of gambling tax of 25 percent is burdened with exchange rate bets, totalizer games, small-scale card tournaments and large raffles. For the technical game, the fixed part of the levy is replaced by the minimum tax, with the amount of CZK 9,200 per quarter and the instrument. The minimum tax on the technical game is thus almost twice as much as the current fixed part of the levy. As a result of the above changes, an additional return on gambling tax of approximately 1.9 to 2.3 billion is expected. CZK compared to the proceeds of the current contribution from lotteries and other similar games. This additional revenue should primarily serve to increase the financing of sport. In order to achieve the above objectives, it is proposed to continue to divide the revenue from technical games between the State and the municipality, but in a new ratio of 65:35 in favour of municipal budgets. This ensures that the additional contribution is received by the State budget. The calculation is made in such a way that the income of municipal budgets is essentially maintained. The proceeds of the tax on other games are proposed to continue to divide between the State and the municipality, at the current rate of 70:30 in favour of the State. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed in the same way as the Act on Gambling, i.e. on 1 January 2017. Thank you for your attention.
