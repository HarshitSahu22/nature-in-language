ParlaMint-CZ_2015-12-09-ps2013-036-06-009-146.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the budget and the medium-term outlook of the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure between 2016 and 2018 are based on the financial frameworks set by the Government of the Czech Republic for 2016 and the medium-term outlook for 2017 and 2018, the budget being prepared as being balanced with the involvement of SFDI's estimated balances on 31. 12. 2015. The budget of 2016 is based on the increased indicative figures of the Ministry of Finance of the Czech Republic amounting to CZK 47,1 billion of national resources and is working with the total national resources of CZK 51.3 billion at the end of 2015 with the planned balance of SFDI funds. With the involvement of the EU funds of CZK 14,3 billion, the total budget proposed for 2016 amounts to almost CZK 65,7 billion. The crucial factor for establishing a budget is the need to respect the following requirements as far as possible. To repair and maintain the network and prepare actions according to the government approved document of the transport sector strategy, the second phase, to ensure the financing of projects financed from the OPD 2007 to 2013 to ensure sufficient drawdown of the OPD 2014 to 2020 to meet specific milestones, i.e. pre-defined and monitored targets in the OPD 2014 to 2020 so that the Czech Republic will not be reduced funds from the OPD to ensure financing of already-built-up events and to start up newly prepared events. The financial framework from national sources of CZK 51.3 billion allows the financing of repairs and maintenance, the preparation of actions, the completion of national and co-financed actions from the EU to cover part of the newly launched actions according to their state of readiness. Following the successful completion of the preparation, individual actions will be included in the budget of the SFDI during 2016 subject to the existence of available funds. These resources can be generated, for example, by lower implementation prices for individual competing events or by reaching higher than expected tax revenues. In case of further need, negotiations will be conducted with the Ministry of Finance following the development of the state budget in order to increase the budget of the SFDI to cover actions that do not have budget coverage today. In particular, the budgetary perspective is complicated when, at the end of 2016, we expect the launch of large-scale constructions D11, R35, R48, which will require significantly more funding than is budgeted in the proposal. Their implementation is necessary in order to ensure that the allocation of OPD 2014 to 2020 is used in compliance with the N+3 rule. For the Ministry of Transport, the optimal guarantee of national resources would be about CZK 52 billion per year. Prerequisite of so-called draft financing options contained in approved transport sector strategies. Based on the above, I propose to the Chamber of Deputies of the Czech Republic to approve the budget of the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure for 2016 and the period of the medium term, for 2017 and 2018. Thank you for your attention.
