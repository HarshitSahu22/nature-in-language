Proposal for appointment of members of the Supervisory Board of the Technology Agency of the Czech Republic In the case of the election of two members of the Audit Board of the Technology Agency of the Czech Republic, we chose - it was the second round of the election - two places from two candidates. A total of 141 ballots were submitted and issued, and the quorum required for the election was 71 votes. Václav Havlíček received 71 votes, i.e. exactly one, and was elected. Likewise, Ivan Ohlídal was elected. He won 81 of our votes. I note that both candidates, Václav Havlíček and Ivan Ohlídal, were elected members of the Audit Board of the Technology Agency of the Czech Republic. Their four-year mandate shall begin on the date of adoption of the resolution of the Chamber of Deputies. This audit board is now complete and fully booked.
