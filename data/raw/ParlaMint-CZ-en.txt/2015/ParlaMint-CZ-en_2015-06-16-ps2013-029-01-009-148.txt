ParlaMint-CZ_2015-06-16-ps2013-029-01-009-148.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the proposal for force and resources of the Ministry of Defence in UNDOF and MINUSMA missions. The Czech Republic's long-term intention is a gradual return to UN missions. Just a reminder, we closed our major operations in UN missions in 1998 by shutting down the field hospital in East Slavonia. Since then, we've been sending only individuals to UN missions to serve as observers. The government approved the operation of MO forces and resources in UNDOF missions at Golan Heights and MINUSMA in Mali by its resolution of 22 April this year. We came from the following criteria: UN requirements in our defined interest regions, i.e. Middle East, North Africa and Sahel. Furthermore, we were based on the existence of our other commitments, particularly within NATO and the EU, as well as on the availability of our human, material and financial capacities. In my opinion, the proposed scope of involvement in both missions is adequate and creates room for the gradual greater involvement of our country in UN missions. As far as UNDOF is concerned, the UN has asked us for three officers to take positions in the command structure of the mission, and in the submitted material we are asking for the mandate of five persons, in case the UN asks us later to fill additional positions. As far as MINUSMA is concerned in Mali, it is the type of UN mission whose task is not only to monitor compliance with the ceasefire between the enemy parties, but also to actively contribute to the stabilisation of the security situation in its area of responsibility. I consider the participation of the Czech Special Forces Unit as part of the Dutch contingent, which, by the way, is the largest of European countries, to be an appropriate way to make a visible contribution to the UN mission's objectives and to gain a number of invaluable experiences. Since April 2013, 38 of our soldiers have been active in the EU training mission in Mali. Our contribution to stabilizing the situation in Mali will thus be more visible and complex. At the same time, from a logistical point of view, it is better to keep soldiers of two missions in one territory than in more operational areas. In terms of financial complexity, the proposed action in both missions should not exceed 182 million. CZK and will be financed from the Ministry of Defence budget. The part, about 30 percent of the funds, will be refunded to us under the UN Financial Mechanism. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, thank you for your attention and I ask for the approval of the material presented. Thank you.
