Government bill amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the Public Procurement Act /Household print 638/ - first reading Please, Minister Karla Šlechtová, who will make a proposal under the authority of the Government. Please, Madam Secretary, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-25-ps2013-036-02-008-067.u2	Mr President, thank you for the floor. There are several laws that are changing in connection with the Government's draft law on public procurement. This is a modification of the Act on the Property of the Czech Republic and its representation in legal relations, the amendment of the Act on the Protection of Competition, the amendment of the Act on the Promotion of Research, Experimental Development and Innovation, the amendment of the Act on the Protection of Classified Information and Security Eligibility, the amendment of the Act on public passenger transport services and the amendment of other laws, administrative offences of the client, the amendment of the Act on criminal liability of legal persons and proceedings against them. These are key laws that are presented in connection with the proposal for a new law on public procurement. Thank you.
