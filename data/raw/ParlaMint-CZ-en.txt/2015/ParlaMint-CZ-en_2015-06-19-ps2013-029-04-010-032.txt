ParlaMint-CZ_2015-06-19-ps2013-029-04-010-032.u7	The Act amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the Sales Recording Act. It is a proposal that is an integral part of the bill on the record of sales, which the government has set itself as one of its priorities. The aim of the bill is to implement changes to the laws that are necessary to establish a system of record-keeping of sales or which are compensation for the impact of the introduction of electronic records in certain segments of the economy. The proposal contains an amendment to the Income Tax Act, an amendment to the Value Added Tax Act and an amendment to the Administrative Charges Act. In the field of income taxes, the bill introduces a one-off discount for taxpayers of income tax on natural persons who are obliged to register sales under the Sales Recording Act. The tax discount is proposed at a maximum of CZK 5,000. In the area of value added tax, the bill converts catering services from the basic 21 percent tax rate to the first reduced 15 percent tax rate. The reason for this is an effort to mitigate the impact of the introduction of sales records, taking into account the risk of price increases in the food service facilities segment of ordinary citizens, for which the available analyses show a high extent of sales reduction and non-compliance with tax obligations. The amendments to the Value Added Tax Act also include an amendment to the electronic submission of forms in order to achieve greater efficiency in the processing of the data transmitted. In the area of administrative fees, in connection with the introduction of a binding assessment in the field of sales records, it is then proposed that this binding assessment be charged only CZK 1000 instead of standard CZK 10,000, in order to ensure legal certainty of a wider range of entrepreneurs for the newly introduced obligation. The effectiveness of the bill is directly linked to the effectiveness of the bill on record of sales. In conclusion, I am going to propose a reduction in the deadline for discussing this bill in committees for 30 days. Thank you for your attention.
