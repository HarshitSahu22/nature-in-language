ParlaMint-CZ_2015-01-21-ps2013-025-02-007-017.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Colleagues, colleagues, the Ministry of Culture submitted the bill to the Government in accordance with the legislative work plan of the Government. The main purpose of the proposed regulation is to create conditions for extending the possibilities of receiving the station spoken by the Czech Radio Plus in the band of very short waves. It is a station which Czech Radio already broadcasts to a limited extent and which also brings publicism and educational programmes especially in the fields of science, technology and history to listeners in addition to news coverage. The proposal also contains a partial amendment to Act No.231/2001 Coll., on the operation of radio and television broadcasting and on the amendment of other laws that are corrected by legislative technical deficiencies, the deadline for the submission of the annual report of the Radio and Television Broadcasting Council to the Chamber of Deputies and the Czech Radio plus station is amended by the prohibition on advertising and teleshopping. The proposed legislation does not give rise to any claims on the state budget or other public budgets. All costs related to the extension of the possibility to broadcast Český rozhlas Plus on very short waves will be covered by the Czech Radio's own funds. The Government discussed this bill on 9 July 2014 and approved it by resolution 526. The first reading of the bill in the Chamber of Deputies took place on 24 October last year. On 12 November 2014, this draft law was discussed in the Election Committee. However, the discussion was suspended for the purpose of processing the compromise amendment. In the subsequent discussion of the bill on 7 January 2015, the electoral committee approved the amendments tabled and by resolution number 70 recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to agree to this government bill, as amended by the amendments adopted. Thank you for your attention.
