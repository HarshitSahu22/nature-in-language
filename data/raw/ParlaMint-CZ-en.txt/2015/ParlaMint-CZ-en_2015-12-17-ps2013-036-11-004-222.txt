ParlaMint-CZ_2015-12-17-ps2013-036-11-004-222.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Colleagues and colleagues, on behalf of the Minister of Finance, I present to you for discussion a contractual document in the field of international tax relations, namely, historically the first double taxation treaty with the Principality of Liechtenstein. This contract was signed on 25 September 2014 in Prague. This is a standard comprehensive bilateral agreement that regulates taxation and, at the same time, the exclusion of international double taxation of all possible types of income. This Treaty will certainly help to develop mutual economic activities. There is currently no agreement in the mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and Liechtenstein, and therefore there may be undesirable international double taxation. In the case of Liechtenstein, this is the last Member State of the European Economic Area with which a double taxation treaty has not yet been concluded. The reason for this is the fact that the Czech Republic did not establish diplomatic relations with this country until September 2009. The Treaty governs the basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of the Czech Republic and Liechtenstein. It lays down the principle of equal treatment of the entities of both Contracting Parties. It shall allow for the informal settlement of disputes arising from its interpretation and implementation and shall also allow for the exchange of information in the context of taxes of all kinds and the designation between the competent authorities of the Contracting Parties, in accordance with the existing international standard, including the exchange of bank information. The contract applies as the main method of excluding double taxation of income of Czech tax residents, a simple method of crediting foreign tax. This international tax agreement will not have a direct impact on the state budget of the Czech Republic. The overall contribution resulting from the conclusion of the contract will depend on the pace of development of the various forms of economic cooperation. This Treaty certainly does not replace national tax rules and cannot thus create a new tax obligation. For your information, the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic has already agreed to ratify the contract. I propose that the Chamber of Deputies take into account the facts mentioned in the discussion of this Treaty and agree to its ratification. Thank you for your attention.
