In 2010, British Petrol's oil rig in the Gulf of Mexico crashed. In response to this, the European Commission has created a directive uniting the security conditions for offshore oil and gas production activities within the EU. All Member States have an obligation to implement this Directive into their national legislation. For the Czech Republic as an inland state, the only obligation arises from this directive.
