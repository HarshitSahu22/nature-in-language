ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-016-097.u2	Yes, the World Postal Union, one of the UN's professional organizations. [[Noise in the hall.]] Allow me, ladies and gentlemen, to present to you the proposal by which Parliament will be submitted for the approval of the World Postal Union Act. Its main mission is to ensure international postal contact between 192 Member States. The rules of contact are governed by multilateral contractual documents, which together are called the Act of the Union. It sets out the terms and conditions for the exchange of letters, packages, postal orders. This practical delegation and fulfilment of the content of the Act is entrusted to us by the Czech Post on the basis of the Postal Services Act, which obliges the holder of the postal licence to provide international postal services in accordance with the Acts of the World Postal Union. The concluded contracts have the character of presidential treaties and have been signed by the authorised representative of the Czech Republic, subject to its ratification. This is the material you have at your disposal. I would like to ask you to release these contracts for further consultation and then at second reading to give your consent to the ratification. That's all.
