ParlaMint-CZ_2015-06-16-ps2013-029-01-001-001.u10	Thank you for the floor, Madam President. Ladies and gentlemen, I will read you the current wording of Section 5 para. 3 which defines the scope of the activities of Military Intelligence. Military Intelligence Provides Information (a) originating abroad, important for the defence and security of the Czech Republic, (b) intelligence services of foreign power in the field of defence; (c) the intentions and activities directed against defence of the Czech Republic, (d) the intentions and activities threatening classified information in the field of defence of the Czech Republic. A thoughtful listener, who may be few, when I listen to the chatter from the left side, would surely notice that there is something extra. What's an extra something? Well, this is originating abroad, important for the defence and security of the Czech Republic. By this word, this wording, by the way, found itself in the law on the basis of the 2004 amendment, and it was the parliamentary initiative of then Nečas, Vidím and Kladívek, and it is a question of what this two words "and security" could have caused. In any case, these two words open up the possibility that Military Intelligence operates in a field where it is not supposed to operate because it has to operate in the field of defence. This may bring about an undesirable duplication of intelligence activities, a waste of forces and resources, and even a very unhealthy competition between intelligence services - the intelligence services of military and civilian services. I think it's not only wrong, it's basically illegal. And now, in the language of law, iron logic, I will explain why. Military intelligence is according to § 3 letter. (c) Law No 153/1994 Coll., on Intelligence Services, or the Act which we are currently dealing with, as amended, part of the Ministry of Defence. The Ministry of Defence does not have, pursuant to Section 16 of Act No. 2/1969 Coll., on the establishment of ministries and other central bodies of the state administration of the Czech Republic, i.e. according to the so-called law of competence, no powers in the field of security, eo ipso or organizational component of the Ministry of Defence Military Intelligence has and cannot have any powers in the field of security. It follows that the provisions of Article 5 (1) (a) and (b) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union are to be applied. 3 The current wording of Act 153, as I quoted it, point (a), is contrary to the above-mentioned competence law, violates the division of the performance of the state administration between the various departments and is contrary to the principles on which the structure of intelligence services in the Czech Republic is built, respectively. the division of competence between the various intelligence services. I said there might be an undesirable duplication, and that's the least of the damage. For this reason, I propose in Section 5 of Law No. 153/1994 Coll. delete from the taxative list the scope of Military Intelligence in the field of security of the Czech Republic. [[15.20 hours]] Since there is virtually no legisvakance in this bill, i.e. 15 days after the publication, I also propose divided effectiveness in my amendment, so that the change in the scope of the Military Intelligence I proposed would take place until date 1. 1. 2016. So much basically to justify my proposal, to which I will sign up in detail in the debate. Thank you for your attention.
