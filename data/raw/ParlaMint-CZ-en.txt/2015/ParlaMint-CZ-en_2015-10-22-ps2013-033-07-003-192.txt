The report on the situation in the field of internal security and public order in the Czech Republic provides comprehensive information on the state and development of crime and measures aimed at reducing it. A total of 288 660 offences were detected in the Czech Republic in 2014. There was a decrease of 11.3 % year on year and a return to the trend of crime after year. 114 611 people were prosecuted and investigated for criminal activity, which represents a year-on-year decline of 2.6 %. Although the number of prosecuted and investigated recidivists fell year on year, their share in the total number of offenders, that is to say, 114 611 increased to the historically highest 53.2 percent, i.e. the number of recidivists is more than half the total number of acts. The proportion of foreigners in the total number of perpetrators of this crime is stable around 6,5 percent. However, there is still a sharp increase in the number of Vietnamese nationals committing drug crimes. This is a trend that unfortunately continues. The overall level of clarity increased to 49 percent, the damage found dropped by 1.2% to 28 billion, while police secured a total of CZK 7.78 billion in criminal proceedings. In 2014, 629 people were killed on roads in the Czech Republic, the second lowest since 1961. The lowest number of persons killed was recorded in 2013, it was 583 persons, just to remind you. At regional and local level, the implementation of the crime prevention programme continued. In total, 265 projects were supported in 2014 in the field of social prevention, situational prevention and information of citizens, which are implemented in 146 municipalities within the regions of the Czech Republic.
