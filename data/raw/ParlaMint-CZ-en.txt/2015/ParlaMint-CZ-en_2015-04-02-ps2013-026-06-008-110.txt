ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-02-ps2013-026-06-008-110.u2	Thank you, Mr President. In the first half of 2014, the Prime Minister submitted to the Chamber of Deputies and the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic on the basis of Government Resolution No. 684 of 27 August 2014. This material provides an overview of the transits and crossings of the armed forces of other States through territories carried out under Government Order No 988 of 18 December 2013. The ground and air transport of the armed forces of other states during that period was carried out mainly for reasons of participation in military exercises, trainings and negotiations and to secure Enduring Freedom operations, meetings of the Dutch army in the military training area of Boletice, Grifon Spring, ISAF, AFRICOM, KFOR and EUFOR. Part two of the presentation reports provide summary information on land and air transports carried out for the purpose of stay in the territory of the Czech Republic, the detailed overview of which is given in Annex 1, and transit and transit procedures, the detailed overview of which is given in Annex 2. Annex 3 gives an overview of the flights for which authorisation has been granted by applying the special procedure and Annex 4 shows a comparison of the number of flights carried out from 2009 to the first half of 2014 for each semester. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, thank you for your attention.
