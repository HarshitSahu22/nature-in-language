Proposal for the election of the President of the Permanent Commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the work of the Chamber of Deputies Office On the next election point, we decided to elect the chair of the Permanent Commission of the House for the work of the Office of PS. Even here, 164 tickets were issued, 163 were submitted, one was not submitted. Jan Bartošek won 59 votes, Petr Kořenek 93, so that Mr Petr Kořenek becomes chairman of this permanent commission and the election is over. Congratulations to my colleague Kořenek and good luck to his work. I hope he doesn't get away from the election committee for that.
