ParlaMint-CZ_2015-02-12-ps2013-025-07-004-068.u2	Good morning again. Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, this time as Minister of Transport, I will try to be as brief as possible. The International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships is the most important international treaty dealing with the issue of the protection of the marine environment against pollution from ships. It was adopted at an international conference in 1973 and its annexes lay down technical requirements for the construction of maritime ships and their equipment in such a way as to minimise to a maximum the risk of leakage of pollutants into the marine environment. For reasons of worldwide increasing air protection care, the Parties adopted the Convention on 26 September 1997 amending the Protocol, and an additional Annex was added to the original Annexes laying down rules for the limitation of gaseous exhalation from ships. In terms of the protection of the marine environment and the global importance of the Convention, it is desirable for the Czech Republic to express its consent to be bound by all its parts. Access to the Protocol is necessary in order to fulfil the obligations of the Czech Republic arising from membership of the European Union. The materials contained in the Convention are closely linked to Union legislation aimed at the area of technical competence of vessels and the sanctioning of pollution of the marine environment. The practical impact of the proposed MARPOL contract is evident in the case of sea yachts flying the flag of the Czech Republic, as they also fall within its scope. Their numbers are not insignificant in the Czech Republic, they reach hundreds of these ships. The aim of the 1997 Protocol and of the entire Convention is, inter alia, to: to regulate the rights and obligations of persons and the regulation of matters the regulation of which is reserved in the law. For the same reason, the 1997 Protocol has the character of an international presidential treaty. I would like to ask you in this way to agree to ratify this contractual document. Thank you for your attention.
