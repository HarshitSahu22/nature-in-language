ParlaMint-CZ_2015-12-11-ps2013-036-08-006-052.u1	52. Government bill amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of atomic law /German press 561/ - first reading This proposal is also made by the Minister of Industry and Trade Jan Mládek on behalf of the Government. Please, Minister.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-12-11-ps2013-036-08-006-052.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Government is submitting to you a draft law amending certain laws in connection with the draft new atomic law, which was submitted by the House press number 560. The proposed regulation is based primarily on the draft new atomic law and adapts the related laws to the new legislation contained therein. Once the atomic law had ceased to apply, the related legislation would not have been in line with the new legislation, which, of course, is undesirable. The proposed amendments to the laws are mainly legislatively technical in nature, consisting of a direct adaptation to the new legal text. Outdated concepts are being removed, institutes missing as well as presently or changes to institutes which will no longer have relevance in the legal environment of new atomic legislation. The Government has discussed a bill amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the atomic law, on 13 July and, as I propose to you, approved it. Thank you for your attention and for your approval of this law.
