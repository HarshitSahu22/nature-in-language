ParlaMint-CZ_2015-12-10-ps2013-036-07-005-015.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the proposal for a law on the assessment of the conformity of specified products when they are placed on the market is presented to you in order to ensure proper implementation of twelve directives and one regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council. It is an area of explosives, simple pressure vessels, electromagnetic compatibility, non-automatic weighing instruments, lift gauges, equipment for use in explosion environments, electrical radio and pressure equipment, ship's equipment for recreational craft and personal protective equipment. The proposal was discussed in the Economic Committee where the amendments were applied. I agree with these amendments for the MIT. Dear Members, honourable Members, I would like to ask for your support for the proposal tabled at second reading.
