Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Islamic Republic of Pakistan on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income taxes signed in Prague on 2 May 2014 /German press 228/ - first reading I ask that Minister Brabec also give the opening word here.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-02-12-ps2013-025-07-007-077.u2	Thank you, Mr President, for the floor. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to present to you a contractual document in the field of international tax relations, namely the Double Taxation Treaty with the Islamic Republic of Pakistan. This contract was signed on 2 May last year in Prague. This is a standard complete bilateral agreement that regulates taxation and, at the same time, the exclusion of international double taxation of different types of income. There is currently no treaty in the mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and Pakistan, and therefore there may be undesirable international double taxation. This agreement, which was agreed on the basis of OECD and UN model models at expert level, standardized the basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of the Czech Republic and Pakistan. It lays down the principle of equal treatment of the entities of both Contracting Parties, allows for informal settlement of disputes arising in the interpretation and implementation of the tax contract, as well as for the exchange of information in the context of taxes of all kinds. The Treaty deals with taxation of all types of income and generally applies as a method of exclusion of double taxation a method of simple credit for foreign taxes. This international tax agreement will not have a direct impact on the state budget of the Czech Republic. The overall contribution resulting from the conclusion of the contract will depend on the pace of development of the various forms of economic cooperation. This Treaty certainly does not replace national tax rules and cannot thus create a new tax obligation. I propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take into account those facts when discussing the Double Taxation Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, and I would like to propose that this international treaty be ordered at first reading by the relevant House Committees. Thank you for your attention.
