Government bill amending Act No. 61/2000 Sb., on maritime navigation, as amended, and other related laws /house print 271/ - second reading I ask the Minister of Transport Dan Ťok to present this press on behalf of the government. Minister, please, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-01-21-ps2013-025-02-002-012.u2	Good morning, Mr President, honourable Members. The aim of the proposed amendment to the Maritime Navigation Act is to implement the requirements of international legal agreements into the legal order of the Czech Republic, to create conditions for their ratification and transposition of secondary legal acts of the European Union in the field of training and certification of seafarers, conditions for work at sea and protection of the marine environment. In particular, in the context of international maritime conventions, it is necessary to ensure the implementation of amendments to the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping, the STCW Convention, adopted in 2010 by the Conference of the Parties to this Convention in Manila, Philippines, and to adapt the Czech rule of law to the planned ratification of the 2006 International Labour Organisation Convention on Work at Sea. These international conventions will become part of our rule of law after ratification, and it is therefore necessary to ensure the compliance of the Maritime Navigation Act with them. The STCW Convention is the most important international treaty which lays down minimum requirements for the competence of seafarers, their training and certification. Its main objective is to ensure increased protection of life and property at sea and protection of the marine environment. The draft law waives the stricter conditions for the certificates of seafarers not required by the Convention to the extent that such legislation appears to be an unjustified burden on tenderers. The Convention on Work at Sea was adopted at the 94th session of the International Labour Conference on 23 February 2006 and its ratification is carried out by the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs. It is an international instrument aimed at ensuring the right of all seafarers to decent employment. [[9.30 hours]] The aim of the bill is also to ensure the compatibility of the legal order of the Czech Republic with secondary acts of European Union law, which follow the international legislation mentioned. Certain provisions of the Convention on Labour at Sea were reflected at Union level in Council Directive 2009/13/EC implementing the Agreement to the Convention on Maritime Labour of 2006 concluded by the Union of operators of maritime vessels of the European Community and the European Transport Workers' Federation and amending Directive 1999/63/EC. The second Directive implementing the Convention on Work at Sea is Directive 2013/54/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on certain obligations of the flag State with regard to the Convention on Work at Sea and its enforcement. The draft law continues to remove the provisions relating to the administrative punishment of illegal discharges of pollutants on ships under the Maritime Navigation Act, following the fact that the transposition of the Directive of the European Parliament and of the Council amending Directive 2005/35/EC on ship pollution and the introduction of penalties for infringements is punishable by the provisions of the Criminal Code and Act No. 418/2011 Coll., on the criminal liability of legal persons and proceedings against them. The Economic Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic, by its resolution on 14 January 2015, recommended that the draft law be discussed and approved as amended by the amendments agreed by the Ministry of Transport. I hereby ask you to approve the second reading. Thank you.
