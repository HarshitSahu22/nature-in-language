ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-014-093.u1	93. Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratify the amendment of the International Convention on Whaling Regulation adopted in Jersey on 14 July 2011, in Panama on 6 July 2012 and in Portorože on 18 September 2014 /German press 410/ - second reading Mr. Environment Minister, please.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-014-093.u2	Thank you. I am glad that the position of the Czech Republic as a whaling power has aroused such interest, even in opposition clubs. [[Laughing in the hall]] I will refer again, Mr President, colleagues, to the longer opening words from the first reading, where I have just justified why the Czech Republic has entered this Convention. At this point, this proposal concerns the approval of the ratification of the amendments that took place gradually in 2011, 2012 and 2014 at the individual meetings of the Convention, and the Commission adopted amendments to the schedule of the Convention, consisting primarily of shifting the data defining the fishing season periods and for those fishing seasons following the moratorium on commercial whale fishing which entered into force in 1986, fixed a zero quota. Under the conditions of the Czech Republic, this is a change to the Presidential Treaty and therefore the approval of Parliament and ratification by the President of the Republic is necessary to approve this amendment. Thank you for your attention.
