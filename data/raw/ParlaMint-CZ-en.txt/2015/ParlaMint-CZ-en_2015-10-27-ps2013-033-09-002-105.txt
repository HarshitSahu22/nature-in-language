The bill responds to a significant deterioration in the security situation in Europe, and because the risk of spreading ideas and currents that are not compatible with the Western civilization circuit to which the Czech Republic belongs is greatly increased, so ask you to release this law into second reading. In Europe, the Czech Republic, except the Czech Republic, strengthens ideas that deny the personal, political and other rights of citizens for their nationality, gender or religion. It is quite clear that such currents can pose a significant security risk for the Czech Republic. Churches and religious societies are one of the ways through which these thought streams can spread. The Czech laws do not take into account the aspect of security in the recognition of special rights for churches in particular. There are two levels of registration in the Czech Republic. The first stage is in principle only registration. At the recognition of the second degree, the churches may teach religion at state schools, instruct those performing clergy activities to perform the clergy service in the armed forces of the Czech Republic or in places where detention is carried out, the penalty of imprisonment, to perform ceremonies in which church marriages are concluded or to establish church schools. There is no doubt that such special rights give churches considerable opportunities to spread the ideas they proclaim. In the light of the deterioration of the security situation, we therefore propose that the acquisition of such special rights be conditional on the fact that the church does not give rise to reasonable concern that it threatens the Czech Republic's foreign policy interest, nor does it give rise to reasonable suspicion that it threatens public policy or national security. Therefore, the new Section 14a introduces the so-called the specific procedure under which the risks referred to above will be excluded - the procedure for granting authorisation to exercise special rights. The Ministry of Interior will request comments from the Office for Foreign Relations and Information, the Security Information Service, Military Intelligence and the Police of the Czech Republic. The institution concerned shall be expressed within 30 days of the date of receipt of the request from the Ministry. A statement containing consent or opposition to the granting of a right to exercise special rights shall be justified. This procedure is intended to ensure that the granting of special rights to the Church does not pose any security risk to the Czech Republic. This proposal is not discriminatory, it is not xenophobic or racist, as I have heard here, it is only a guarantee against the fact that if any religion is a threat to the Czech Republic, so that security services can stop it at a time when it is time. I therefore beg you and ask for your support so that there is no rejection, and this proposal has been released for second reading on the committees. Thank you.
