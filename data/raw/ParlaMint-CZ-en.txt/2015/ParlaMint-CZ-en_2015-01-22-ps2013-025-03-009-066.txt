[[Found the right base.]] The submitted international treaty on road transport replaces the original agreement between the Government of the Czechoslovak Socialist Republic and the Government of the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia on international road transport concluded in 1963. Since this original agreement no longer meets the current conditions, it was necessary to negotiate a new bilateral agreement on international road transport. After the negotiations, the contract was signed on 22 May 2013 in Leipzig. The proposed adjustment aims to create a comprehensive framework for the operation of road freight and passenger transport between the two States. The contract regulates the area of road freight and passenger transport, in particular the authorisation system, the types of transport not subject to authorisation procedures. The obligations of carriers and penalties resulting from breach of the contract are also already regulated in the contract. The text of the contract is in accordance with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, including obligations arising from membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. The contract does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to give my consent to ratify this contractual document. Thank you.
