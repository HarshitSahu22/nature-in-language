Proposal for appointment of members of the Supervisory Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic At the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic - we therefore voted in the second round of six members - before that we were successful, that we managed to choose three of them. In this election we issued 164 ballots and so 164 were submitted, valid and invalid, and the results are as follows: Lubomír Grmela won 32 votes, Ivan Hubač 4, Libor Jan 88 and was elected, Zdeněk Jirásek 29 votes, Tomáš Krejčík 75, Stanislav Labík 27, Peter Lichard 54, Irena Šlamborová Šlamborová 31 votes, Jiří Masopust 8, Jan Picek 20, Mária Režňáková 103 votes and was elected, Mirka Wildmannová 98 votes and was elected. The quota needed to reach was 83. This means that there are still three remaining members to be elected in the Grant Agency Board at the moment. This was the second round of the first election, so the election is completely over. I submit that the Election Commission will announce a new deadline and await new proposals in cooperation with the Committee on Culture, Youth and Sports. I presume we will announce this election at the next, April or May meeting of the Chamber of Deputies. The second choice concerned three members of the Monitoring Board of the Technological Agency.
