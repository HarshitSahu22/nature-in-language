ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-02-ps2013-031-07-002-171.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce the Government's proposal for a law on the Czech Fire Rescue Corps and on the amendment of certain laws. As has already been said several times, we are seeking to establish the legal anchoring and legal status of the Fire Rescue Corps in such a way that it corresponds to the practice in today's day. The aim is to achieve a situation where its status will be adjusted in a clear and systematic manner, including institutes which are not currently regulated. The Government's proposal is therefore adapted to the scope of the Fire Rescue Corps, including its organisational components, its status, the duties of its members and employees, as well as cooperation with other authorities and persons. Finally, I would like to point out that I agree with all the amendments contained in the Security Committee's motion for a resolution, and I have no objection to them. Thank you for your attention.
