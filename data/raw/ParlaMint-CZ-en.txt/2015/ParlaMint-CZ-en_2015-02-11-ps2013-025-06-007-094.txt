ParlaMint-CZ_2015-02-11-ps2013-025-06-007-094.u2	Mr President, my dear colleagues, before the final vote on this press, I would like to turn to you with a request to support my amendment, which, in my view, deletes an unconstitutional provision from the Criminal Code that, on the basis of the decision of the State Attorney, the property of the suspect could be seized before the prosecution itself began. That is, at the time of the preparatory procedure, when all the documents are still being collected, the prosecutor could decide to seize the property of the suspect. In our Charter of Fundamental Rights and Freedoms, there is a provision that the fundamental human rights and freedoms of our citizens can only be interfered with within the limits of the law, based on the decision of the court. I believe that this is not a political issue, but a question of constitutional certainty and legal certainty, so that we do not increase the competence here, for example, of prosecutors at the expense of the courts. I therefore appeal to you and ask you to support my amendment, which has been tabled and loaded. Thank you.
