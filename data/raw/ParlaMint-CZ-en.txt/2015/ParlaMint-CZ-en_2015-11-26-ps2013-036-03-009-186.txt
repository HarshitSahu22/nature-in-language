In August 2015, the armed forces of the United States turned to the Ministry of Defence of the Czech Republic with a request for permission to transit United States armed forces, which - at that time - will move through the territory of the Czech Republic to NATO exercises in Hungary. The transits were then carried out on roads in three two-day transfers between 9 and 14 September. The individual columns were accompanied by the military police of the participating states. A technical break was planned in the Czech Republic. In the framework of the transit was carried oversized cargo and the consent of the Ministry of Transport was requested for the transport of this cargo. All the costs of supporting this passage have been borne by the United States of America. Thank you.
