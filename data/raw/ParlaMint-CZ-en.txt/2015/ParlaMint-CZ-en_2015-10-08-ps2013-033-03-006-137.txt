ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-08-ps2013-033-03-006-137.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, this time I would like to justify the Government's proposal to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to express its agreement to ratify the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Cook Islands on the exchange of information on tax matters. Since 2010, the Ministry of Finance has negotiated, in cooperation with the General Financial Directorates with jurisdictions with a preferential tax regime, an agreement on the exchange of information for tax purposes. For the time being, these negotiations have resulted in fourteen final draft texts which are subsequently undergoing a legislative process leading to ratification by the President of the Republic. Eight agreements have already entered into force, which are announced in the Collection of International Treaties, and an agreement with the Bahamas Community will be announced in the near future. The agreement was negotiated on the basis of a model proposal for a directive on expert negotiations on agreements on the exchange of information on tax matters and a draft agreement on the exchange of information on tax matters approved by Government Resolution No. 227 of 22 March 2010. The proposed agreement fully complies with OECD standards for the international exchange of tax information. The objective of the agreement is to collect taxes agreed upon by the Contracting Parties in the course of expert negotiations, i.e. in the Czech Republic taxes of all kinds and names and in the Cook Islands taxes on income of natural and legal persons, withholding tax and value added tax. Information provided under this Agreement shall be subject to tax confidentiality. By concluding the agreement, the Czech Republic will be able to request the competent authority of the Contracting State for information that is relevant to the determination, determination and security of the payment of taxes or, where applicable, the payment of taxes. to investigate or prosecute tax offences. However, given the legal constraints imposed by the domestic law, the information provided may be used in criminal proceedings only as operational information. The approval of an agreement on the basis of which an international exchange of tax information with jurisdictions so far considered tax havens will take place is highly desirable. A positive impact can therefore be expected mainly on the tax revenues of the state budget of the Czech Republic, due to an effective reduction in tax evasion. The agreement also acts as a preventive measure to strengthen the payment morale of Czech taxpayers. I therefore recommend agreeing to ratify this agreement. Thank you for your attention.
