Proposal for the election of the Head of Parliament's Permanent Delegation to the Interinstitutional Union - Round 2 Members of Stanislav Grospič, Jaroslav Lobkowicz, applied for this position. Stanislav Grospič received 91 votes, Jaroslav Lobkowicz received 50 votes, so in the second round of this election he was elected Stanislav Grospič and became Head of Permanent Delegation. [[Let's hear it.]] I think that applause belongs to all of us because we voted several times since the fall. For myself and for the electoral committee, I am glad that one of the electoral points has succeeded in reaching the end. Unfortunately, in the case of the election of the chairman of the committee of inquiry for serious misconduct on D47, we were not so successful.
