ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-024-152.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the Act on the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure requires the Government to submit an annual accounts and an annual report on the activities of the SFDI and subsequently to submit it for approval to the Chamber of Deputies by 31 March at the latest. The annual report on the activities of the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure for 2014, which includes the annual accounts, provides comprehensive information on the activities, financing and management of the Fund in 2014. The State Fund for Transport Infrastructure had an approved budget of CZK 71 billion for 2014. The approved budget consisted of 5 billion OPD2 funds which, due to the fact that the operational programme had not been approved, could not be drawn. The real-time budget for 2014 was therefore 66 billion. A total of 52 billion were released in 2014, representing 73 percent of the budget approved and 79 percent of the budget actually used. In view of the fact that underdrawn national funds can be transferred to 2015, these were used in 2015 to co-finance EU funds and finance repairs of the second and third classes in the regions. It is also partly calculated to pay for the transfer of railway buildings from České dráhy to SŽDC. Thank you for your attention.
