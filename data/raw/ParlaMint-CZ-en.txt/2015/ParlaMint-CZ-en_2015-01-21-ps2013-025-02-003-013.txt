ParlaMint-CZ_2015-01-21-ps2013-025-02-003-013.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me remind you of the content of the bill tabled, which amends Act No. 378/2007 Coll., on Medicines and on Amendments to Some Related Acts, as amended. The purpose of the draft amendment to the Act on Medicines is to allow the use of funds in a special account of the State Institute for Drug Control by way of derogation from the purpose of their application provided for in Section 112 of this Act. According to that provision, the State Institute for Drug Control is responsible for these funds as compensation for the costs of carrying out professional activities by the entities at whose request these acts are carried out, provided that such activity cannot be provided to the extent necessary from budgetary resources. The proposed bill proposes extending the purpose of using these extra-budgetary funds of the State Institute for Drug Control by establishing the possibility of their one-off transfer from its special account to the income account of the state budget of the Czech Republic established for the Ministry of Health. [[There's a lot of noise in the room.]] The reason for the transfer of these funds for the needs of the resort is, in particular, the entry into force of Act No. 109/2014 Coll., amending the Act on Public Health Insurance Insurance and the Act on Public Health Insurance, which increased monthly payments for state insured persons by CZK 58 per month from 1 July 2014, i.e. to 845 kroner. The purpose of this proposal was to increase public health insurance funds in order to compensate for the loss of income of health service providers as a result of the abolition of regulatory fees. This measure represents an impact on the state budget of around 4.2 billion this year. Crowns. The Ministry of Health can only participate in the above-mentioned state budget entitlements. Therefore, the government has decided to propose an amendment to the Act on Medicines, which will ensure a one-off transfer of these funds of 1.7 billion this year. CZK for the state budget. This amount has been fixed in such a way that it does not impose additional requirements of the SÚKL on expenditure from the state budget. This appropriation is intended to cover expenditure on studies, meetings of experts, information and publications directly linked to the achievement of the objectives of the programme.
