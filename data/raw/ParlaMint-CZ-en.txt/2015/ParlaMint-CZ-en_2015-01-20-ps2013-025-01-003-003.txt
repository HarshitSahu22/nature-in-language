Amendments to the Senate Nos 2 and 3 seek to clarify and consolidate the right of the beneficiary of the subsidy to a fair and non-discriminatory position in the procedure for granting the subsidy. Amendments to Senate Nos 4 and 5 aim to abandon the proposal that debts from state notes be listed as state financial liabilities. Amendment to Senate 6 proposes to abolish together three points, which change all of them together in the current state of repayment of subsidies. The Senate recommends maintaining the current situation in which the refunded subsidy is again a means of the state fund that provided it. The amendment to Senate No 7 is a correction of the legislative technical error, namely the repeal of the footnote with the citation of the decree on the powers and the issue of this decree. [[17,00 hours]] Amendment to the Senate 8 responds to the provisions for the entry into force of the proposed amendment to the Budgetary Rules Act on 1 January 2015, which can no longer be complied with, and such firm effectiveness would mean genuine retroactivity that is not acceptable. This defect in the motion would have healed the Senate version of the proposal. Finally, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to express my conviction that the Chamber of Deputies will consider the reasons for the Senate's adoption of the amendments as beneficial changes and approve the draft law tabled as amended by the Senate's amendments.
