I would like to present to you a draft law amending Act No. 123/1998 Coll., on the right to environmental information, as amended. The aim of the bill is to ensure full compatibility of Czech legislation with the INSPIRE directive. This is Directive 2007/2/EC of 14 March 2007 establishing an infrastructure for spatial information in the European Community in such a way as to avoid the initiation of infringement proceedings by the European Commission on the Functioning of the European Union. The INSPIRE Directive lays down rules for establishing a European spatial data infrastructure. The basic principles of the directive are: spatial data are to be collected and created only once, meaning one entity, ensuring the possibility of combining spatial data from different sources and sharing it between many users and applications, ensuring easier search and publication of spatial data. [[It's noisy in the hall.]] In January 2013, the European Commission pointed out the Czech Republic within the framework of the so-called a pilot project for certain deficiencies in the transposition of this Directive. The issue of criticism was, in particular, the lack of or incorrect transposition of certain definitions and concepts with which the directive works and the incorrect transposition of the requirements of the directive, so that the reasons for denying access to spatial data are interpreted in a restrictive manner. The proposed proposal constitutes an amendment of a technical nature which specifies the existing legislation -
