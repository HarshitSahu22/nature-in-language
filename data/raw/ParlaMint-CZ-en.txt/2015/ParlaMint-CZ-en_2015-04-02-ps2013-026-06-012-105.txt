ParlaMint-CZ_2015-04-02-ps2013-026-06-012-105.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, on the basis of Act No. 250/1997 Coll., on Agriculture, the report on the state of agriculture of the Czech Republic for 2013 is presented. The report on the state of agriculture, which has been published since 12994, is a statistical-information material whose main mission is to inform the public about the year-on-year comprehensive development of Czech agriculture and food industry. [[12.30 hours]] From the overall point of view of the economy of the Czech Republic, there was a slight annual increase in the nominal share of agriculture in gross value added to 1.57 % in 2013. The share of food industry has not changed year on year and is 2.44 %. The total employment in the national economy slightly increased by 0,02 percentage points, mainly as a result of a more pronounced decline in the number of employees in other sectors of the national economy than that observed by agriculture. Due to an increase in consumer food prices by 4.9 percent, the proportion of household food spending and soft drinks increased to 20,2%, an increase of approximately 0,2 % of the percentage point, which was significantly higher than the average inflation rate, which was 1.4 percent. The weather conditions in the Czech Republic were marked by the June floods, which mainly affected vegetable growers and fish production, but otherwise the weather was relatively favourable for agriculture. The year 2013 shows, in terms of the economic results of the agricultural sector, a second preliminary estimate of the general agricultural account, a continuation of positive trends in 2012 and a stabilisation of the agricultural sector with a slight increase of 4 per cent on average in all indicators of the general agricultural account. The overall economic result of the sector slightly increased to 16,7 billion. CZK and approached the peak achieved in 2011, which was then 17.4 billion. Kč. Favourable trends include growth in agricultural production by 4,7 %, further growth in gross and net value added by 2,4 %. From the point of view of investments in agriculture, it can be concluded that, following the decline in the investment activity of agricultural holdings in 2012, its recovery occurred as a result of improving the economic situation of enterprises. The long-term trend of reducing agricultural employment has slowed down its pace, but even with a slighter annual decline of 1.8 percent, the number of workers in agricultural holdings is only slightly above the threshold of 100 000 and in 2013 it was 103,5 thousand. The disparities in agricultural wages decreased to 80.3% compared to the average of the national economy, where wages were virtually stagnating in 2013. The balance sheet deficit of Czech agrarian foreign trade decreased by 1 billion year on year in 2013 due to a higher increase in value on the export side than imports. CZK 23.7 billion. CZK and the degree of cover for exports increased by 1.3 percentage points to 87 percent, which is the most favourable value since the Czech Republic joined the European Union. The year 2013 was the final year of the financial year 2007 to 2013 in which the direct payments of the European Union reached a 100% level compared to the entry year 2004. In line with the multiannual financial framework of the European Union and the application of the so-called financial discipline in the European Union for 2013, direct payments to enterprises that exceeded these payments by EUR 2000 were reduced by 2.45 %. Total aid increased year-on-year from 41.5 billion in 2013. CZK 42.7 billion. CZK, that's almost 3%. The aid to agricultural holdings alone without expenditure on so-called general services has increased from 33,8 billion. CZK 35.1 billion. CZK, that's 3,9 percent. The most important contribution to this year-on-year change was a year-on-year increase in expenditure from the European Union's resources by 7.2 % and a national subsidy from the Ministry of Agriculture by 13,8%. From the figures on the structure of the aid paid, as set out in the report, it is clear that the agricultural policy of the Czech Republic after accession to the European Union is more focused on support for farm incomes than on aid for the necessary restructuring and improvement of the competitiveness of agriculture and food industry. The aid is increasingly involved in the business income of the agricultural sector. It is clear from the economic results of agricultural holdings that virtually no agricultural category would have been viable without the aid amount in question. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to take note of the report on the state of agriculture of the Czech Republic in 2013. Thank you.
