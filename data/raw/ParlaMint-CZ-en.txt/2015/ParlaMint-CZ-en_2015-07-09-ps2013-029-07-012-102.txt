ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-012-102.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the Czech Republic has so far no bilateral international treaty with Jamaica. The Air Transport Agreement should thus become the first such document regulating these relations at bilateral level. The agreement was approved by the Czech Government in October and subsequently signed in November 2014. The impulse for the agreement was the introduction of irregular flights between the Czech Republic and Jamaica in 2012. I would like to ask you to refer the material to the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
