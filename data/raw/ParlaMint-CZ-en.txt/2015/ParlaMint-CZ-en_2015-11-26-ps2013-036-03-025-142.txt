Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic, the Republic of Austria and the Slovak Republic on the Trostate Border Point Dyje - Moravia, signed on 29 September 2015 in Vienna /Household Print 645/ - first reading I now ask that the opening word be given by Mr. Richard Brabec, Minister of the Environment, on behalf of the Government, as Minister of the Interior, Milan Chovanec.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-025-142.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the purpose of the Treaty between the Czech Republic, the Republic of Austria and the Slovak Republic on a three-state border point Dyje - Moravia is a firm anchoring point of contact between the national borders of these three states. The three-state border point is located in the bed of the Moravia River in the area surrounding the Dyje River. It is currently moving, as well as the national border with the Republic of Austria on the river Dyji. In the section concerned, the centre line of the Dyje stream is underway. The national borders with the Slovak Republic are therefore non-viable throughout the whole length from 1 April 2011, except for the section adjacent to the three-state border point. It is about 60 meters, where they run the centerpiece of the Moravia stream. The concept of moving state borders at the border of water flows is currently outdone. The Czech Party is interested in gradually adapting common state border agreements with neighbouring countries so that the national borders are not moving over the whole length. The anchoring of the point of contact of national borders with Austria and Slovakia is a condition for the implementation of this intention. The contract is based on the current position of the three-state border point, so there will be no change in the course of the national borders. Thank you for your attention.
