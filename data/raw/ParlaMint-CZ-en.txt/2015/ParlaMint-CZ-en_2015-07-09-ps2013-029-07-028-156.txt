ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-028-156.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Good evening, dear colleagues. On the basis of Government Resolution No 496 of 10 May 2006, the Minister of Agriculture was ordered to submit to the Minister of Agriculture on a regular basis after consultation with the Minister of Finance by 30 April of each calendar year starting from 2008 The Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic information on the implementation of flood control measures in the Czech Republic for the previous calendar year in the Gesture of the Ministry of Agriculture. Flood situations represent the biggest threats of natural disasters in the Czech Republic. This fact is given by the area of the Czech Republic on a continental and global scale. The emergence of floods cannot be prevented, but only mitigate their effects on the lives and property of the population. This fact significantly affects the position of the Ministry of Agriculture and the watercourse administrator on this issue. The presented material contains basic information on the drawing of funds and the achieved parameters for the two most important, financially demanding programmes of the Ministry of Agriculture in the area of flood prevention and support for the debauchery and reconstruction of ponds and the construction of water reservoirs and information on the completion of the programme for the implementation of land modifications aimed precisely at flood prevention measures. The implemented flood prevention measures significantly limit the risks of flood damage, thus fulfilling the objectives and measures set out in the main water catchment area plan of the Czech Republic approved by the Government Resolution of 23 May 2007. The material was discussed by the Government and approved by Resolution 267 of 15 April 2015. The task of submitting the material in question to the Government's resolution of 10 May 2006 was imposed in connection with the European Investment Bank loan received to finance the programme Support for flood prevention II, which is now ending this year and with the implementation of which more than CZK 11 billion was invested in the implementation of more than 400 flood prevention measures in the Czech Republic. This material has also been discussed by the Committee on Agriculture and the information provided, which you have here today in this form. I beg her approval. Thank you for your attention.
