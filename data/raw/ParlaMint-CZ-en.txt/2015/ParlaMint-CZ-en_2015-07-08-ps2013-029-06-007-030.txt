ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-08-ps2013-029-06-007-030.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly say so. a rate amendment to the Consumer Tax Act. This rate amendment is submitted in order to comply with the minimum requirements for the taxation of cigarettes laid down in Council Directive 2011/64/EU. One requirement is to ensure a minimum specific taxation of cigarettes corresponding to EUR 90 per thousand. In 2014, this minimum requirement was met; for 2015, the temporary derogation provided for in the abovementioned Directive applies. However, for 2016, the rates of excise duty need to be adjusted so that minimum taxation is restored. This will result in an increase in excise duty on cigarettes by about three crowns per box of 20 pieces. The tax increase was discussed as part of expert negotiations so that all price segments would fall evenly and therefore there was no distortion of the market. At the same time, an increase in the excise duty rate for other tobacco commodities, i.e. cigars, cigarillos and smoking tobacco, is proposed. Smoking tobacco has so far been taxed equivalently by 80 per cent to the minimum taxation of cigarettes. It is proposed to increase this ratio to 85 per cent from 1 January 2016. The rate of taxation was also increased for cigars and cigarillos. In order to ensure the stability of the tobacco industry and the predictability of tax revenues, it is further proposed to set excise duty rates for all tobacco products over the medium term. For 2017 and 2018, a gradual increase in excise duty on cigarettes is proposed by up to 90 cents up to one crown per box in 2017, respectively. 1.20 kroner in 2018. A slight and regular increase in excise duty on tobacco products is more acceptable for all concerned than a jumping increase. Regular and moderate increases in excise duty will also help prevent the unwanted growth of the black market in tobacco products, which threaten to increase their prices at a significant one-off rate. Minor changes in the provisions relating, in particular, to the regulation of authorisation procedures and procedures for the provision of selected products or means of transport, the need for which arose from practice, are also part of the tariff amendment of the Act on excise duties. Since the rate amendment to the Consumer Tax Act contains a three-year plan to increase the taxation of tobacco products, the effectiveness of the individual amendments is proposed on a regular basis with a distance of the whole calendar year, always from 1 January 2016, 2017 and 2018. Thank you for your attention.
