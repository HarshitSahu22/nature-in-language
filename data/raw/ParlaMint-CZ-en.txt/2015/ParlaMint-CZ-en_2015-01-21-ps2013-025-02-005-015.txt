ParlaMint-CZ_2015-01-21-ps2013-025-02-005-015.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, let me briefly mention this point. The reason for the processing of the amendment is, in particular, an adjustment to the authorisation to issue the implementing regulation, namely a decree laying down the details for the settlement of the costs of the supply of heat and centralised provision of hot water between the recipients of the services. In this context, the amendment amends the repeal clause abolishing the authorisation to issue the existing decree, which is still contained in the Energy Act. The amendment to the Services Act will eliminate the problems raised by the current regulation of the empowering provision, which, in the opinion of government legislation, is probably contrary to the constitutional principles of the Czech Republic on the requirements of the provisions of the limits of the law in the issuing of sub-laws. The amendment also responds to the amendment of Act No.406/2000 Coll., on Energy Management, as amended, where, among other things, the obligation to install in the apartment of a device registering the supply of heat energy is stated by the modification contained in Section 6 para. 1 where it is possible to settle the costs of heat and hot water according to rules other than those laid down in the Decree, but always derived from heat and thermal energy meters. Other reasons for proposing an amendment to the Services Act are the elimination of unnecessary hardness of the Act in the event of the application of a fine in the event of failure to fulfil the obligation of the service provider or the recipient of the services, where the amount of the fine laid down by law appears to be significantly high - CZK 100 for each starting day of delay. Furthermore, the repeal of the provision on the possibility of applying late payment fees, since the Civil Code no longer provides for the Institute for late payment fees and only provides for the possibility of charging late payment interest for late payment cases. In practice, this two-track adjustment is causing great problems. Another reason is the unification of the nomenclature. The Government Bill on Services, in brief, was discussed at first reading on 29 October 2014 and was ordered for discussion and discussed in the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development and the Sub-Committee on Housing Policy. The amendments contained in resolution 78 of the Public Administration and Regional Development Committee of 12 November are agreed by the Ministry of Regional Development. Thank you for your attention.
