Amendments to Act No. 67/2013 Coll., which regulates certain issues related to the provision of performance related to the use of apartments and non-residential spaces in the house with apartments, and Act No. 458/2000 Coll., on business conditions and on the performance of state administration in energy sectors and on the amendment of certain laws, e.g. The Energy Act, hereinafter referred to as the Services Act, is, in particular, an amendment to the authorisation to issue the implementing act. In particular, it is a decree laying down the details for the settlement of the costs of the supply of heat and centralised provision of hot water between the recipients of the services. The amendment also deals with the regulation of penalty measures, where the amount of the fine laid down by law appears to be very high, i.e. CZK 100 for each starting day of delay, and therefore the amount of the fine of CZK 50 for each starting day of delay is proposed. Furthermore, the provision on the possibility of applying the late payment fee is hereby repealed, since the Civil Code no longer provides for the Institute for the late payment fee. When discussing a proposal in the Senate, the amendments proposed in the amendment to Act 406/2000 on Energy Management were addressed, because these two laws are very closely linked to each other, where the issue of the obligation to install devices registering the supply of heat energy to apartments is addressed. The text of Section 6 of the draft amendment to the Services Act was therefore modified to prepare a solution in the event of a situation where the installation of the instruments would not be necessary and, at the same time, the procedure for clearing the costs, when the legal regulation of the Ministry of Industry and Trade laid down the obligation to install the meters. The Ministry of Regional Development clearly agrees with these modifications from the Senate. I recommend that you accept the amendment approved by the Senate. Thank you.

Mr President, honourable Members of the Government, honourable Members, Madame Members, I have been tasked with the Senate to justify the amendments adopted by the Senate to press 308. I therefore come before you and repeat what has been said, but I will make it a little easier in the sense that Act 301, which is an amendment to the Act on Energy Management, is what follows the Act 308, which we now approve. Already in 2012, the Energy Management Act established a global obligation for the owners of buildings and the community of unit owners to equip end-user apartments with devices regulating and registering energy consumption. The new obligation stemmed from the European directive. In practice, however, it has been shown that installation and measurement are not always technically feasible or cost-effective. The Senate has verified that the European directive for such cases allows for an exemption from this obligation to install meters in all housing units of residential and residential buildings. It therefore amended the wording of the present amendment to the Energy Management Act, which you approved in the preceding paragraph. In fact, this press, print 308, the Services Act we are discussing, in the amended version, brings the text that you have approved into line with what was in the original draft Services Act. The amendments to the Senate govern the Services Act in accordance with the European Union Directive so that, if owners are not obliged to install heat meters or indicators in individual apartments or other parts of the house, there may be an agreement between the homeowner and the tenants of those housing units on how to settle the payment for heat, hot water or settlement under other legislation. This would not have been possible according to the wording initially adopted. Thank you for your attention and, in accordance with the opinion of the petitioner, I would like to recommend the approval of this Senate amended version.
