If Mr. Governor of the Ústí Region Oldřich Bubeníček is not present, I will ask the appointed member of the council of the Ústí Region, Mrs. Gabriela Hubáčková, to take the floor. Please, madam, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-05-19-ps2013-027-04-004-066.u2	Mr Vice-President, honourable Member, honourable Members, the proposal of the Ústí Region seeks to contribute to the reduction of the number of exemptions granted from the ban on the carriage of goods on the first-class road in working order by introducing an administrative fee which has not yet been introduced. It would be introduced for trucks and special vehicles of a maximum permissible weight exceeding 7 500 kg and trucks and special vehicles and special vehicles of a maximum permissible weight exceeding 3 500 kg with attached trailers, on Sundays and on other working quiet days from 1 to 22 hours, on Saturdays between 1 July and 31 August at 7 to 13 o'clock, on Fridays from 1 July to 31 August at 17 to 21 o'clock. [[16.20 hours]] The current regulation of the Road Traffic Act and amendments to certain laws allows vehicle operators to apply for an exemption from the driving ban on working quiet days, due to a worthy special consideration. It must be admitted, as you yourselves know, that on the days of the truck's working rest, it cannot be overlooked on our roads and constitutes a substantial part of the traffic. However, the regional authorities granting exemptions have a limited option not to grant an exemption. If the operator of the vehicle gives reasons which are generally recognised as worthy of consideration, the exemption shall be granted. The reverse procedure would be illegal and discriminatory in relation to operators. In particular, as stated in the explanatory memorandum, the proposal by the Ústí Region to introduce an administrative fee for the authorisation of the exemption should contribute to the demoing of vehicle operators in requests for exemption where the transport of cargo is not necessarily necessary on working quiet days, and seek to organise the transport of cargo more effectively, i.e. the operation of vehicles. Failure to grant an exemption is not subject to a fee. The administrative fee proposed by the Ústí Region therefore fails to comply with the regulatory function, as stated in the Government's opinion, since in our view any vehicle operator can afford to pay it. Moreover, in our view, there is no reason not to charge an exemption from the ban. The amount of the proposed administrative fee alone corresponds to the amount of the other administrative charges under heading 26 of the Administrative Fee Tariff and, therefore, to the management of the procedure, its costs. In our view, a financial analysis whether the amount of the fee represents the actual amount of administrative costs related to the issuance of the permit, which the government is criticising, is therefore not necessary in the present case. The draft of the Ústí Region is a proposal designed to contribute in its expected result to reducing the number of trucks in transport on working quiet days. It is a proposal based on the possibilities of the region itself. If it is therefore argued by the government that the introduction of a new administrative fee and, if necessary, new substantive regulation of restrictions on the driving of certain vehicles on working quiet days should be properly discussed in the standard legislative process, then the government will certainly welcome such steps by the Ústecký Region, but it has not yet done so. The Ústí Region thus considers this proposal to be debatable and voteable and is ready to recommend legislative technical amendments responding to the Government's position in committee. Thank you.

The proposed bill introduces an administrative fee for authorising an exemption from the ban on heavy goods transport on weekends and working quiet days. It introduces a fee of CZK 500 for a period of up to six months and CZK 1,000 from six to twelve months. The fee is not yet subject to authorisation. The petitioner justifies the amendment submitted by a large number of permits issued. The exemption in the Ústí Region for 2012 lists 234 permits. Furthermore, the justification for introducing an administrative fee - the petitioner wants to limit the number of applications submitted for exemption, which I do not think should be a financial statement to reduce the number of such authorisations. It also wants to obtain the resources to cover administrative expenditure through the introduction of a fee. The government's negative statement has already been mentioned here. I am joining this negative statement by the Government and will propose a rejection in the debate. Thank you for your attention.
