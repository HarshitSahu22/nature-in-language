ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-06-ps2013-033-01-003-056.u2	Madam Vice-President, honourable Members, the main objective of the amendment is to extend the range of activities which the State Fund of Transport Infrastructure can finance from its budget, in particular in order to make the multi-source financing process of transport infrastructure more efficient, thereby extending the range of quality projects to which it will be possible to draw funds from the European Union Fund, such as the possibility of financing road and motorway management, the possibility of financing repairs, maintenance and administration for major inland waterways, including the possibility of financing the construction, repair or management of port infrastructure in Hamburg, or changing the financing of maintenance routes. The proposed amendment to the legislation will not affect the total amount of funds managed by the Fund. The proposal also contains a number of partial procedural and organisational problems related to the internal activities of the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure, such as the modification of terminology or publication of the Committee's decisions on the Fund's website instead of in the Transport Bulletin. It also includes the partial implementation of the European Union Directive on the creation of a single European railway area. I would like to ask you to support the proposed bill, as it contains an adjustment for the fund's activities. Thank you for your attention.
