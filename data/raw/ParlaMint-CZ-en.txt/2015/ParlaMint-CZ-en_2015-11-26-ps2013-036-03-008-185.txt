ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-008-185.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the material presented informs on the operation of the Deployable Communication Module, DCM, in NATO Operation "Active Fence" in the Republic of Turkey. The operation of the Czech Army in the mission was approved by the Government and both Chambers of Parliament in October 2014 within the framework of the mandate for foreign operations in 2015-2016, with the fact that they would be informed of it in the case of a specific deployment, paragraph 1/9 of the resolution of the Senate of 23 October 2014 of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. 635. The operation is aimed at strengthening the air and missile defence of Turkish territory and population in the context of the conflict in Syria and was launched in January 2013. Since the beginning of the operation this is the third activity of the Czech DCM in Turkey. The first two took place in 2013 and 2014. The Czech Republic was involved in a group of seven DCM members from 28 April to 7 September this year, with the fact that it completed the operational task from 1 May to 31 August 2015. Their task was to ensure, among other things, the integration of units in both the area of operation and between the operations headquarters and the superiors of the alliance headquarters. The funding of DCM members in the Republic of Turkey is covered by the budget chapter of the Ministry of Defence. Total costs are estimated at 5,663 million. Crowns. On 24 June 2015, the Government took note of the material by its resolution number 500. Thank you for your attention.
