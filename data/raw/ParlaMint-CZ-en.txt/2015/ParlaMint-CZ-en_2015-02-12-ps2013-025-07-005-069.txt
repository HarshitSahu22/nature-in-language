ParlaMint-CZ_2015-02-12-ps2013-025-07-005-069.u2	Thank you again. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, today you will have to bear with me a little longer. I have several roles here, and this is one of them. Let me therefore justify the Government's proposal by which the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic is presented to express its agreement to ratify the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Bahamas on the exchange of information on tax matters. This agreement was signed on 6 March last year in Nassau. Since 2009, the Ministry of Finance has launched a number of negotiations with countries with preferential tax regimes, the so-called tax havens, and so far has negotiated fourteen draft agreements on the exchange of information with relevant jurisdictions. This proposal for an agreement is another one that continues in the legislative process leading to ratification by the President. The negotiation of this agreement also took place on the basis of a model draft directive for expert negotiations on agreements and a draft agreement on the exchange of information on tax matters approved by the Government resolution on 22 March 2010. This Agreement fully complies with OECD standards for the international exchange of tax information. By concluding this agreement, the Czech Republic will be given the opportunity to request the competent authority of the Contracting State for information that is relevant to the determination, determination and security of payment of taxes, or to investigate or prosecute tax offences. The application of the agreement can be expected to give Czech tax administrators a tax which Czech tax residents would not have paid, as well as an increase in the tax discipline of domestic residents for the future. The negotiation of this agreement should contribute to a favourable fiscal impact on the income of the Czech Republic and to increased prevention of financial crime in general. I would therefore like to ask you to give your consent to ratify this agreement. Thank you for your attention.
