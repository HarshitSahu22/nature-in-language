Therefore, I propose to the transitional provisions of the draft amendment to the Act in Art. In addition to points 9 and 10, the aim is to ensure that, in the light of the new textation and definition, the existing mandate of persons to authorise the carrying out of the assessment of hazardous properties of waste does not expire, as well as the evaluation already carried out. There is no reason for such impacts of the amendment from a factual point of view, because despite the different definition of certain hazardous properties, the implementation of the new assessment would not lead to a different outcome. The issue of new mandates to assess hazardous properties of waste and the certificate of exclusion of hazardous properties would therefore be only a formal and very costly process. Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 February 2011 laying down the rules and general principles concerning mechanisms for control by Member States of the Commission's exercise of implementing powers (OJ L 55, 28.2.2011, p. 13). The proposed amendment to Section 7 (1) applies to 1357/2014. 6 which only alters the codename of one of the hazardous properties of waste in such a way as to correspond to the new codename referred to in this Annex. The second amendment concerns the refinement of the definition of sludge and treated sludge and their use on agricultural land. In the submitted government draft amendment to the Waste Act, the definition of sludge from sewage treatment plants is changed. In addition to this change, I consider it appropriate to further adapt the definition of sludge from septic sludges and other similar equipment so that it applies only to sludges which come from water cannons used for the treatment of household waste water before discharge. Another part of my proposal is the reaction to the development of recent years, during which waste water treatment plants have been created in the Czech Republic, which do not have sufficient capacity to adjust or. the storage of all sludges that they produce. At the same time, new processing plants have been created which can carry out sludge treatment so that they can be used on agricultural land. However, according to the findings of the Czech Environmental Inspection Authority, a number of cases of circumvention of the Waste Act have also led to a breakdown of the conditions that can be adapted. Entities that were intended to modify sludges only took advantage of the possibility of re-entering the sludge and exhibiting the application program without actually disposing of hazardous properties. Therefore, my amendment retains the possibility for the sludge treatment to be carried out by an entity different from the waste water treatment plant operator, but at the same time adds to the law clear conditions and rules for their transmission and storage. [[16.10 hours]] The proposal also empowers the Ministry of the Environment to establish technical requirements and adaptation technologies and storage requirements for treated sludges, thereby ensuring adequate health and environmental protection conditions. In view of the fact that the Ministry of the Environment will have to prepare such implementing legislation and operators of equipment for treatment or storage of sludge will have to ensure compliance with its requirements, it is proposed to shift efficiency to 1 March 2016. I believe that these proposed changes, which are consulted with the Ministry of the Environment, will benefit the cause and will find your support at the third reading.
