ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-027-128.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, civil aviation relations between the Czech Republic and Hong Kong are regulated by the Air Services Agreement signed in 2002. In order to bring this agreement into line with Union law, its amendment has been negotiated with the Hong Kong Party. The main modifications take place in the articles relating to the designation of air carriers and the withdrawal or suspension of their operating authorisations. The amendment to the Agreement was signed in Hong Kong on 14 August 2014. The negotiation of an amendment to the agreement does not require adjustments to our legal order, its implementation will not have an impact on the expenditure of the state budget. At the same time as the amendment of the agreement to the Parliament of the Czech Republic, the existing agreement, which was approved under the legislation in force at the time, is submitted to the Parliament of the Czech Republic for approval of ratification as an international treaty of the government category. In order for the entire existing agreement, as amended, to be immediately binding, it also needs to be ratified and promoted to the international Treaty of the presidential category. I would like to ask you in this way to give your consent to ratify this contractual document. Thank you.
