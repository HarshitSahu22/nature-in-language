ParlaMint-CZ_2015-01-22-ps2013-025-03-004-075.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Convention on the participation of foreigners in public life was adopted in 1992 and should have been a means of promoting the integration of foreigners in the field of political and social affairs at local level. The obligations arising from the Convention are divided into three chapters, with States obliged to assume the obligations under Chapter A. This provides for the right of foreigners to freedom of expression, the reception and dissemination of information, freedom of assembly, freedom of association, including the right to establish trade unions to protect their interests. Chapter B concerns advisory bodies representing foreigners at local level and Chapter C of the electoral right of foreigners at local level. Accession States may exclude the emergence of commitments under these chapters by their declaration. The Czech Republic had already signed the Convention in 2000, so it could not be ratified because the necessary legislation was lacking to ensure compliance with the obligations under Chapter A. In the following years, the extent of the commitments to be accepted by the Czech Republic has been repeatedly discussed. On the basis of up-to-date analyses taken into account, inter alia, security aspects, the Government shall submit for approval a proposal for ratification of the Convention to the extent of Chapter A and propose the application of Chapter B and C to exclude the declaration in accordance with Article 4 (1) (a) of the Convention. This Convention shall apply from 1 January 2016. This means reminding once again that it is the ratification of the mandatory Part A of this Convention. Thank you for your attention.
