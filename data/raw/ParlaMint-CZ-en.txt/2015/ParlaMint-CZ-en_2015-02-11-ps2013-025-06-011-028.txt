ParlaMint-CZ_2015-02-11-ps2013-025-06-011-028.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, honourable Members, I mention here the Government's bill amending the Employment Act. First of all, this proposal unites the approach to granting unemployment aid to those who are granted by the law and are not allowed to carry out so-called non-complicating employment while receiving or not receiving unemployment aid simultaneously. Today there are categories of people who can receive support and at the same time carry out so-called non-cooling jobs, they are directors of companies or members of administrative and supervisory boards. This proposal unites it. And there are a few other modifications that you all have in the background. Thank you for your attention.
