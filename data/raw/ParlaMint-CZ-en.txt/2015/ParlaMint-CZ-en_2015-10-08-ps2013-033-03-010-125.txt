ParlaMint-CZ_2015-10-08-ps2013-033-03-010-125.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I must say that the Postal Union has been lucky today, because otherwise there was a risk that ratification will perhaps take place throughout the parliamentary term, so I am very pleased that we have the opportunity to discuss it. Allow me, after the first reading and after the meeting in the Foreign Committee, to briefly present the Government proposal by which Parliament will be given approval to ratify the World Postal Union Act, signed at the 25th Congress of this international government organization. The main mission of the World Postal Union is to ensure international postal contact between 192 Member States. The rules of such contact are governed by multilateral contractual documents, which are collectively referred to as the Union Act. The Act lays down the conditions for the exchange of letters, parcels and postal vouchers and liability in the event of loss and damage to consignments. The Czech Post, s. p., is entrusted with the practical fulfilment of the content of the act in the Czech Republic on the basis of the Postal Services Act, which imposes on the holder of the postal licence among others. also provide international postal services in accordance with the World Postal Union Acts as one of the basic postal services. The concluded contracts have the character of international presidential treaties and therefore have been signed by the authorised representative of the Czech Republic subject to their ratification. The content of the amendments discussed and agreed at the 25th Congress was presented in detail at the meeting of the Foreign Committee. The Foreign Committee has recommended that the Chamber of Deputies agree to ratify the World Postal Union Act. That is why I believe that you, ladies and gentlemen, will also support this resolution of the guarantee committee today. Thank you for your attention.
