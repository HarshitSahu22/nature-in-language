Proposal for a member of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund With regard to the election of one member of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund, 44 Members voted for Miloslav Janulík. Pavel Pastorek received 24 votes and Josef Uhlík received 79 votes. Unfortunately, we weren't successful here either. Thus, in the case of Mr Josef Uhlík, he was not elected by three votes. In accordance with the electoral order, twice the candidates for the number of vacant posts, i.e. two, will proceed to the second round. Josef Uhlík and Miloslav Janulík progress. We will also organise this second round in the first week of April.
