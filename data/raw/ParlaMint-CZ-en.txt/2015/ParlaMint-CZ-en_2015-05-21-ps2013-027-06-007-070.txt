Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Islamic Republic of Pakistan on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income taxes signed in Prague on 2 May 2014 /German press 228/ - second reading On behalf of the Government, the Minister of Finance and Deputy Prime Minister Andrej Babiš will present Karla Šlechtová to the Minister of Regional Development. ParlaMint-CZ_2015-05-21-ps2013-027-06-007-070.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, a contractual document from international tax relations is presented to you for discussion, namely historically the first double taxation treaty with the Islamic Republic of Pakistan. This contract was signed on 2 May 2014 in Prague. This is a standard comprehensive bilateral agreement that regulates taxation and, at the same time, the exclusion of international double taxation of all possible types of income. This Treaty will certainly help to develop mutual economic activities. There is currently no treaty in the mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and Pakistan, and therefore there may be undesirable international double taxation. This Treaty governs the basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of the Czech Republic and Pakistan, lays down the principle of equal treatment of entities of both Contracting Parties, allows for informal settlement of disputes arising from its interpretation and implementation, as well as the exchange of information in the context of taxes of all kinds and names between the competent authorities of the Contracting Parties, in accordance with the existing international standard, including the exchange of bank information. The contract applies as the main method of excluding double taxation of income of Czech tax residents the method of simple credit of foreign tax. This international tax agreement will not have a direct impact on the state budget of the Czech Republic. The overall contribution resulting from the conclusion of the contract will depend on the pace of development of the various forms of economic cooperation. This Treaty certainly does not replace national tax rules and cannot thus create a new tax obligation. We propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take into account those facts when discussing this Treaty and agree to its ratification. Thank you.
