ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-011-188.u2	Thank you for the floor. Ladies and gentlemen, briefly about the content of this information. Units of our armed forces participated in 68 military exercises with foreign partners during this period. 28 exercises were carried out from this in our territory. The total financial amount needed to carry out these exercises was CZK 54.5 million. Among the most important exercises carried out in the reference period were exercises for the preparation of units in a fighting group of the European Union built on the basis of the V4 states. It was a MEDICAL MAN exercise in our territory. Next, it was a CAPABLE LOGISTICIAN exercise in Hungary to align and certification the logistics module of this group. In the third time, I would appoint a very high NATO emergency exercise called NOBLE JUMP. The objectives of these exercises were fulfilled by the units of the Czech Republic. Thank you.
