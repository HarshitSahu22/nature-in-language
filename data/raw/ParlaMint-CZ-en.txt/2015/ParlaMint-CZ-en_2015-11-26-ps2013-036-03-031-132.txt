ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-031-132.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the Czech Republic has so far no bilateral international treaty with Jamaica. The Air Transport Agreement should thus become the first such document regulating these relations at bilateral level. The agreement was approved by the Government of the Czech Republic in October and subsequently signed in November 2014. This is a liberal text based on the Czech proposal and contains an adjustment of all important areas of air transport, such as the determination of the scope of transport rights and other necessary conditions for the operation of commercial air transport between Contracting Parties, cooperation in the field of operation, security and aviation security, and others. The impulse for the agreement was the introduction of irregular flights between the Czech Republic and Jamaica in 2012. The Air Agreement will establish a clear legal framework in the field of air transport between the two States and can also contribute to the further development of relations with Jamaica. I would like to ask you in this way to agree to ratify this contractual document. Thank you.
