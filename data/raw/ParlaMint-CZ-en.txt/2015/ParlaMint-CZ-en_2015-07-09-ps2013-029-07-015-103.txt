Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Agreement between the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and Iceland, on the other hand, on Iceland's participation in the joint fulfilment of the commitments of the European Union, its Member States and Iceland in the second period of the Kyoto Protocol to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (the House Press 474/ - first reading Mr. Environment Minister, please.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-015-103.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce you to the content of the material. At the 8th meeting of the Parties to the Kyoto Protocol, held in Doha, Qatar, the Contracting Parties adopted an amendment to the Kyoto Protocol. On this basis, the European Union and its Member States, together with Iceland, have undertaken to reduce their greenhouse gas emissions by 20 per cent compared to the reference year, namely 1990. This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. The European Union, the Member States and Iceland will implement this reduction commitment together, on the basis of a declaration made in connection with the adoption of the amendment to the Kyoto Protocol. Since Iceland is not a member of the European Union, the Union and its Member States therefore conclude with it a separate international agreement on Iceland's participation in the joint fulfilment of the commitments of the European Union and its Member States and Iceland in the second Kyoto Protocol control period, defining the conditions for the fulfilment of the reduction commitment in 2013 to 2020. Thank you for your attention.
