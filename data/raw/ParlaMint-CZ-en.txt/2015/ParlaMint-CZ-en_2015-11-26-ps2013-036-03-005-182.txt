ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-005-182.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, in the context of the development of the security environment in Eastern Europe, serious colleagues are mainly carried out in Poland and the Baltic States with NATO exercises designed to demonstrate the allies' commitment to collective defence. In mid-February of this year, the Ministry of Defence was asked by the US Party for permission to transit the U.S. Armed Forces unit, which returned through the territory of the Czech Republic from NATO exercise to its base in Germany. On 16 March 2015, the Government was informed of the transit requested, pursuant to Article 43 (1) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. Annex V.Part 2.116 (d) (a) The Constitution of the Czech Republic decided by its resolution number 197 on the implementation of this transit. The actual transit took place from 29 March to 1 April in the interest of the public and the media, as planned, without emergencies. The individual columns of the passing units have always been accompanied by the Military Police and also by the Police of the Czech Republic. The passing unit consisted of a total of 120 vehicles and 530 persons. By allowing this passage, our readiness to participate in collective defence within the Alliance and to be a reliable ally was demonstrated. Mr President, honourable Members, thank you for your attention.
