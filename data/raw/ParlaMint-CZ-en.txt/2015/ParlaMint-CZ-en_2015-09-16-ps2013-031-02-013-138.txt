ParlaMint-CZ_2015-09-16-ps2013-031-02-013-138.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the agreement tabled on the Visegrad Patent Institute is a measure to promote innovation and an instrument to strengthen the competitiveness of companies in the region. The agreement will create a mechanism for reducing the costs of Czech industry, research and development in patent research, through the creation of a joint institute of four Visegrad countries. Its establishment will not require any investment or new employees, it will use the fully existing capacities of the national authorities. The agreement was discussed by the Foreign Committee of the House, which recommended in its resolution to give its approval for ratification. Thank you for your attention.
