ParlaMint-CZ_2015-06-16-ps2013-029-01-010-150.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Members of Parliament, honourable Members, the deployment of forces and means of the Ministry of Defence's Ministry of Airspace Protection in the framework of NATO operation in 2015 was approved by the Government by its resolution of 3 June this year. The government responds to NATO's urgent request for an extraordinary takeover of the Canadian air force's mission to engage in an operation by an international coalition against the so-called Islamic State and will therefore not be able to provide protection of Icelandic airspace at the same time. Specifically, it will be a posting of 70 persons with five aircraft JAS-39 Gripen, one of which will be a backup, between 20 July and 31 August of this year. From NATO's point of view, this is an important contribution to the Alliance's defence capabilities. This step will confirm our solidarity with alliance allies and our ability to respond quickly and flexibly to the needs of common defence. At the same time, this will be a concrete, albeit indirect contribution of the Czech Republic to the fight against the international coalition against the so-called Islamic State. According to the material approved by the Government, the financial costs related to our involvement in the protection of Icelandic airspace will not exceed 24 million. CZK and will be fully covered from the budget chapter of the Ministry of Defence. In fact, the costs will most likely be lower, because in the meantime we have agreed to cooperate in the transport of our aircraft and soldiers to Iceland with Sweden. While the Swedish side will have to pay part of the costs, we believe that the total costs will be close to 20 miles. In addition to saving itself, I put on the brink for completion, this cooperation is proof of the high quality of our bilateral relations with Sweden in the field of defence. I therefore ask the Chamber of Deputies, ladies and gentlemen, to take note of the material presented. Thank you.
