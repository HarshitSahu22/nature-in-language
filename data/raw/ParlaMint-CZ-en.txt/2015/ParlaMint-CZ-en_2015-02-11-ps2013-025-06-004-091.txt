ParlaMint-CZ_2015-02-11-ps2013-025-06-004-091.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me mention the material of the MIT's proposal for an amendment to the Act on Investment Incentives, which includes the related amendment to the Income Tax Act, the Employment Act and the Real Estate Tax Act. I would merely like to recapitulate that the draft amendment to the law is based on the new European rules on public support, which bring fundamental changes to the system of investment incentives. The proposed bill adapts investment incentives to these new rules while increasing their attractiveness for foreign investors, which will ensure the competitiveness of the Czech Republic in international competition. Everything has already been described in detail in the first and second readings. The Economic Committee approved the amendment to the Act as amended by the four amendments. The first two relate to the adjustment of the calculation of the tax reductions as a form of investment incentive. The resulting amount is newly based on average values from three tax periods and thus reflects the actual amount of the taxpayer's income. I agree with these amendments because this amendment will eliminate the impact of one-off fluctuations in the taxpayer's income and prevent speculative negotiations and will contribute to greater motivation for expanding companies to expand production in the Czech Republic. On the third amendment of the Economic Committee, which contains an amendment to the transitional provision to the amendment to the Income Tax Act, my position is neutral, let's say negative. But it's more forced because I respect the position of the Treasury, which is relevant in this case. I agree with the last amendment by the Economic Committee to move the amendment into effect on 1 May this year. I also agree with Mr Novotný's amendment tabled at second reading, which is a legislative technical specification. Thank you for your attention.
