ParlaMint-CZ_2015-11-26-ps2013-036-03-028-129.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, allow me to present to you a proposal to approve the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the French Republic on cooperation in the field of exploration and use of space for peaceful purposes. This is the first bilateral international contract in the field of space activities, which the Czech Republic is concluding. The Treaty establishes a framework for cooperation on the construction, operation and use of space systems and deepens the possibilities of cooperation in the field of research and space sciences. Cosmic activities are a very progressive and highly technically and scientifically demanding industry. This is confirmed by the increasing demand for space systems services and the dynamically increasing market volume. We assume that the negotiation of an international treaty with France as a European and world cosmic power will bring us new economic and scientific opportunities and will increase the reputation and position of the Czech Republic in the international cosmic community. The proposed text of the Treaty is in accordance with the constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, the obligations arising from membership of the European Union, the obligations assumed under other applicable treaties and the generally accepted principles of international law. The contract will not require changes in the Czech legal order. I would like to ask you in this way to agree to ratify this contractual document. Thank you.
