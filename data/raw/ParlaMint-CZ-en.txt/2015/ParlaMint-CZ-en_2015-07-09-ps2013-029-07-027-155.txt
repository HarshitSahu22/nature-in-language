ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-027-155.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the summary is a summary of the results of the price controls carried out by all price control bodies. The results are very diverse. It can be noted that the criticism of Parliament's Supervisory Committee on the results of controls by the Energy Regulatory Authority in recent years appears to have been reflected in an increase in the number of controls carried out by the Energy Regulatory Authority itself and by the State Energy Inspection Authority. The checks carried out by the specialised financial authority managed by the General Financial Directorate in 2014 focused in particular on the control of possible abuse of economic position on the market and on the rules governing the substantive direction of water, municipal waste and transport prices. Unfortunately, I have to say here that the price control activity provided by the General Financial Directorate has a downward trend compared to the situation a few years ago when price controls were methodically managed directly by the Ministry of Finance. In the last two years after the transfer of price controls to the Financial Administration, the annual fines imposed have decreased. The General Financial Directorate will therefore have to exercise more stringent supervision over the activities of the specialised financial office. On this point, I note that approval of the tasks of Government Resolution No. 86 of this year's water management concept will be submitted to the government in September of this year's proposal that price control in water supply be provided directly by the Ministry of Finance through a group of controllers created here. In conclusion, the price controls are carried out by the regions and municipalities, which focus in particular on the control of the labelling of goods prices, the lack of labelling is very critical of citizens and, in the case of the City Council, hl. m. Prague also to check compliance with the maximum prices of taxi service set by Prague City Hall. Thank you for your attention.
