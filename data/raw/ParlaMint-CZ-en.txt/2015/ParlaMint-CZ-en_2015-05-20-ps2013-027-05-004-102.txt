Economic stability, what is it? Based on certain parameters for assessing the economic stability of the entity under consideration and thus defining the risk that the entity will meet all its obligations to the State. Therefore, if the object is economically stable, there is a low risk that it will not pay the relevant taxes, whether consumer or VAT, and in such a case the smooth functioning of the tax warehouse by the entity is possible and the State should only exercise a control function as to whether the entity is properly fulfilling its obligations towards the State and thus pays the relevant taxes. The second method of control is the guarantee or the composition of the relevant financial amount. The law allows guarantee of all risks in the form of guarantee, whether bank guarantee or guarantee by insurance company, or by the composition of the relevant financial amount on account of the customs office at the Czech National Bank. What problems may arise from the ambiguous nature of the current version of the law?
