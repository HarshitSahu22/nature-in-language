ParlaMint-CZ_2015-07-09-ps2013-029-07-003-034.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, in connection with the modification of the draft laws of the defence legislation, which we launched at 11 o'clock, a new law is now being introduced regulating the conduct of the military reserve service called for to perform military active service. It is a law to replace the already historically surpassed Act No. 220/1999 Coll., on the course of basic or replacement service and military exercises and on some legal relations of soldiers in reserve. The proposed proposal is intended to significantly simplify the entire adjustment by bringing it closer to the terms of the service of professional soldiers. At the same time, the proposal is intended to promote interest in the voluntary performance of military active service, whether in the form of military exercises or entry into active backup. To increase the attractiveness of military active service performance, the law increases the financial evaluation of soldiers at the time of exercise or deployment, increases the remuneration paid for the period of inclusion in the active backup and also introduces support to military employers in active reserve while they have to release them due to military active service. In substance, the law is based on the Act on Occupational Soldiers and takes over most of the legislation for professional soldiers, and only in the event of the necessary differences is a special regulation for reserve soldiers. The adoption of the Act is not linked to higher costs than foreseen by the already agreed strengthening of the Ministry of Defence budget chapter. Although the effects on the business sector have increased somewhat by extending the active backup tasks, I consider them compensated in proportion. In fact, employers are granted financial compensation for the time of the employee's release. The negative effects on other public authorities and self-government, as well as on specific social groups protected by anti-discrimination rules, do not arise from the draft law. The law is fully in line with the constitutional order of the Czech Republic and assumed international obligations. Even in this law, I am prepared to discuss its content, open to any changes that will help to the fundamental objective of this legislation. Thank you for your attention.
