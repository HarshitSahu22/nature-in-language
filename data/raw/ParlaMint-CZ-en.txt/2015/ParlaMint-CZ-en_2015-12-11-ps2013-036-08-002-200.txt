ParlaMint-CZ_2015-12-11-ps2013-036-08-002-200.u4	I'll do it briefly again. This is the 11th report of the Czech National Bank on financial stability. However, we issue it for the second time as an institution which is responsible for the implementation of macro-prudential policy under Section 2 of the CNB Act. This means, among other things, that we have tools at our disposal to do so. One of the most important is the obligation to create a security capital buffer and a so-called countercyclical capital buffer over it. We have announced this at 0 percent, or we have not requested it, but at the same time we have stated and we are still saying that the probability that the zero rate will be applied in the next two years, as the economy is coming up from recession to growth. Otherwise, I would like to say that we have also introduced some preventive recommendations for credit institutions, especially for banks providing loans secured by residential real estate. We've limited security in some way. Institutions should not provide loans that exceed the value of collateral and, even between those 90, 100 per cent of the value of collateral, should move cautiously and cautiously according to this Recommendation. In addition, we have dealt quite significantly, and the report reflects this, credit risk. Here I can only say that credit risk is reflected in the share of credit in default, but, in turn, in the context of the default cycle, loans are falling for both non-financial corporations and households. On the contrary, the only areas where non-residents grow are foreign, which is related rather to several specialised state institutions on the credit market. From this point of view, we have reason to be optimistic. At the same time, the supply of credit and credit dynamics is already growing. Although the loans are growing and increasing, together we are assessing the risks to financial stability in the Czech Republic as being largely low and, at the same time, we can say that the strengthening of the capital facilities of banks and insurance companies has further strengthened the already high resilience of our financial sector to adverse shocks. With this positive statement, I would like to conclude my opening words. Once again, I am ready to answer your questions.
