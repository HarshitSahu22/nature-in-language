ParlaMint-CZ_2015-03-11-ps2013-026-02-020-017.u1	17. Government bill amending certain laws in the context of the adoption of budgetary responsibility legislation /German press 413/ - first reading Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2015-03-11-ps2013-026-02-020-017.u2	Thank you. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, the bill tabled is an accompanying law to the Act on the rules of budgetary responsibility which implements the Constitutional Act on budgetary responsibility. In addition to the efforts to consolidate the national fiscal framework, the comprehensive legislative proposal follows some of the requirements of the Stability and Growth Pact reformed in 2011. However, the preparation of comprehensive legislation is based on the assumption that the implementation of budgetary policy and its responsibility fall within the competence of the Member States of the European Union. At the same time, the proposal responds to the obligation to properly transpose Council Directive No. Directive 2011/85/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 November 2011 on requirements for Member States' budgetary frameworks in national law. The proposed bill amends 19 legislation, in particular with regard to the introduction of new numerical fiscal rules, the imposition of obligations on public institutions to draw up a draft budget and a medium-term budgetary outlook, and to publish them by setting up a National Budget Board and, in particular, by electing its chairman and members. The proposed legislation is fully compatible with the legal principles of European Union law and brings the legal order of the Czech Republic closer to the provisions of the founding treaties and to the secondary law of the European Union. In addition to the efforts to consolidate the national fiscal framework, the bill follows some of the requirements of the Stability and Growth Pact reformed in 2011 and transposes Council Directive No. 2011/85/EU on requirements for Member States' budgetary frameworks. Member States have undertaken to bring into force the legislation necessary to comply with this Directive by 31 December 2018. 12. 2013. The Czech Republic received the so-called "European Commission" in this context in January last year. formal notice on the incomplete implementation of the directive. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed from 1 January 2016, except for the amendment of the Act on budgetary determination of taxes for which it is proposed to take effect on 1 January 2018. Thank you for your attention.
