[[11.20 hours]] The Czech Republic, as a member of the European Union, has long supported, of course, the preservation of the moratorium on commercial whale hunting, and it has long been consistent in this matter, with the voice of the whole European Union, of course. Among other things, the members of the International Whaling Commission are 25 of the 28 countries of the European Union. The point I'm putting to you is to agree to ratify the amendment to the International Whaling Regulation Convention schedule. The Convention, as I said, was agreed in 1946 and the Czech Republic joined it and became a Contracting Party to the Convention in 2005. The Convention currently has a total of 88 Contracting Parties and, as I said, 25 Member States of the European Union. At its 63rd, 64th and 65th meetings in 2011 to 2014, the Commission decided to adopt certain amendments to the schedule of the Convention consisting of a shift of data defining the fishing season periods and, for these fishing seasons, a moratorium on commercial whale hunting has been followed by a so-called zero quota, which, of course, is also supported by the Czech Republic. It concerns individual species of whales, and I'm not going to name them here to save time. Let me just say that it is precisely with the knowledge that the Czech Republic is not concerned with whale hunting and is not going to do so in the near future, and in view of the small extent of the amendments adopted at these meetings, their approval has been postponed so that they can be ratified together with the modifications to the timetable adopted by the Commission at its 65th session. This procedure is also aimed at reducing the administrative burden on both governments and, of course, Parliament subsequently, with all these changes being decided at once over the past years. The changes to the timetable are in line with the law of the European Union and the Czech Republic, and their adoption will have no impact on law, public administration, business sector, or state budget. Now let me just thank you for your attention and affection for whales. Thank you.
