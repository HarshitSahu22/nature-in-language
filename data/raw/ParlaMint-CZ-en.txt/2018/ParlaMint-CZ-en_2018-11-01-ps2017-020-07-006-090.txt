ParlaMint-CZ_2018-11-01-ps2017-020-07-006-090.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, I will try to be brief. This material is presented on the basis of Government Resolution 398 of 22 May 2017. This is another international agreement that strengthens the legal status and functioning of Czech investors in Iran. In this context, I refer to an international treaty already negotiated to avoid double taxation or an international agreement on economic cooperation. By negotiating this investment agreement, the Czech Republic follows other Member States of the European Union, which in the past have already negotiated such an agreement on the promotion and protection of investment with Iran, such as Iran. France, Germany, Slovakia. The agreement follows the release of the financial regime in the Islamic Republic of Iran. The gradual removal of trade barriers makes it easier for Czech investors to operate on the Iranian market and allows, including the possibility of using the dispute resolution system through international arbitration. The purpose of the agreement is therefore to provide Czech investors with comparable investment conditions in Iran's territory, both Iranian investors and foreign investors operating in Iran. In the event of non-compliance with commitments made by Iran, e.g. in the case of illegal expropriation of Czech investment by Iranian state authorities, Czech investors are offered an option to resolve any disputes arising from the agreement either by reconciliation or through international arbitration. The agreement contains all standard guarantees and principles of investment protection. The Agreement shall be concluded for a period of 10 years and shall enter into force on the 30th day following receipt of the last notification that all the formalities necessary for the entry into force of this Agreement have been fulfilled. The representative office of the Czech Republic in Tehran informed the Ministry of Finance that the text of the agreement was approved by the Iranian Parliament. The Agreement shall be in force until one of the Parties has given written notice. The agreement is in line with the foreign policy interests of the Czech Republic. It will benefit the cooperation of the Czech Republic with the Islamic Republic of Iran in the economic field and will ensure comparable investment protection, as other EU Member States that have already concluded this agreement enjoy. Thank you for your attention.
