Allow me to give the introductory word on the Government Bill amending Act No. 269/1994 Coll., on the Criminal Register, as amended. The draft law is presented because of the need to adapt directly applicable legislation of the European Union. The main objective of this proposal is to bring Czech legislation into line with Regulation 2016/1191 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 July 2016 on the promotion of the free movement of citizens by simplifying the requirements for the submission of certain authentic instruments in the European Union and amending Regulation No 1024/2012 to ensure proper adaptation of this Regulation. The time limit for adapting the Regulation is fixed by 16 February 2019. The quoted regulation introduces multilingual forms to facilitate the free movement of persons within the European Union by removing certain formalities relating to the acquisition of translations and the most frequently used authentic instruments. In the Ministry of Justice, in specified cases, the obligation to attach a multilingual standard form to the public documents relates specifically to the extract from the criminal record. These forms are to be used as a translation aid, i.e. if the authority to which the authentic instrument is submitted considers the information contained in the multilingual standard form sufficient for its processing, it should not further require translation of the authentic instrument. Although the regulation is directly applicable by law, it places certain rights on the adaptation of national law and requires organizational technical amendments. The current regulation does not comply with the requirements of the Regulation, since it does not allow this form to be issued when applying for an extract from the criminal record to be requested, does not modify the form in which the form is to be issued, and the procedure for dealing with cases where it is not possible to issue an extract from the criminal record for waiting in the light of difficulties in producing a multilingual standard form should be adapted. For the proper application of this directly applicable regulation, Act No. 269/1994 Coll., on Criminal Register, as amended, adapt. Thank you for your attention and please support this law. Thank you.
