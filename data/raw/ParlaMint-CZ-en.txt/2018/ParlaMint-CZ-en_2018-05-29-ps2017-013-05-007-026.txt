ParlaMint-CZ_2018-05-29-ps2017-013-05-007-026.u4	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, one of the main objectives of the proposed regulation is, above all, to achieve greater complexity in relation to judicial interpreters and sworn translators. The amendment seeks, above all, to separate the legal arrangements for the status and activities of judicial interpreters and translators from the legal arrangements for the status and activities of judicial experts. To distinguish the activities of a judicial interpreter from a judicial translator, to increase the legal certainty and legitimate expectations of applicants for entry in the list of court interpreters and judicial translators. Set clear qualification requirements. There should be a clear definition of responsibilities and the introduction of related compulsory insurance. Strengthen the supervision of persons performing interpreting and translation activities related to tightening up and making administrative penalties more effective. The measure should lead to greater motivation for judicial interpreters and judicial translators. Measures can then be seen in the revision of the remuneration system and the increase in the current level of remuneration and compensation. At the same time, there is greater electronisation of the performance of this profession. The current legislation on this issue of 1967 and 1973, even after three amendments, does not sufficiently meet the current conditions arising from the development of society and technological development. The adaptation also addresses the current lack of adaptation of at least the professional competence of persons who perform interpreting and translation activities. Moreover, the existing law does not distinguish between the profession of interpreter and translator. The aim of the government bill is to stabilise the interpretation and translation sector and, above all, to improve the performance of this activity. I recommend a referral to the next reading.
