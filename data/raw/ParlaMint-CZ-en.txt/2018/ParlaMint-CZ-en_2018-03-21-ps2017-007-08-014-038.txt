Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Treaty between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan on cooperation in the fight against crime signed on 23 November 2017 in Astana /German press 53/ - first reading The proposed proposal will be presented by the Interior Minister Lubomír Metnar. Please, Minister, take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2018-03-21-ps2017-007-08-014-038.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Treaty presented is a standard regulation of the area of police cooperation with non-sequential countries. It therefore regulates cooperation in the prevention and detection of crime, in particular through the exchange of information. Furthermore, the contract allows e.g. cooperation in the search for persons, matters, cooperation in the field of stolen or lost travel documents, the posting of experts as advisors in specific cases or cooperation in the training of police officers. The Treaty in mutual relations shall replace: Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan on cooperation in the fight against organised crime, illicit trafficking in narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, terrorism and other types of dangerous crime of 9 April 1998, which no longer fully meets the needs of the security authorities. [[16.20 hours]] At the explicit request of the Kazakh Party, the contract is negotiated on the outside as intergovernmental. From the point of view of national law, this is a Treaty of the Presidential category, which is subsequently submitted to the two chambers of Parliament of the Czech Republic after the signature to give their consent to ratification and subsequently to be submitted for ratification by the President of the Republic. Thank you for your attention.
