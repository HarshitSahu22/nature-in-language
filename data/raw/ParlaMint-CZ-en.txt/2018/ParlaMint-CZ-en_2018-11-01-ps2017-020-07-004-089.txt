I submit a proposal for the approval of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to ratify the agreement between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Poland. In essence, it is essential for this Treaty to be negotiated. The resolution was adopted on the basis of the Government's resolution that, following the accession of the Czech Republic to the European Union, agreements on the protection and promotion of investments concluded between EU Member States became incompatible. In accordance with the Government's approved procedure, the Czech Republic submitted in two waves a proposal to terminate bilateral investment agreements concluded between the Member States of the European Union by agreement. The first wave was distributed in the first half of 2009, the second in the first half of 2016. The Republic of Poland responded to the second of these notes. The Czech Party adopted a note on 11 April 2018 with the expiry of the existing investment agreement, including the exclusion of the application of the so-called withdrawal periods. It follows from the agreement of the Contracting Parties that investments existing on the date of expiry of the existing Czech-Polish Investment Agreement will no longer be subject to protection under this Agreement for a further period of 10 years. The notes thus exchanged together form an agreement on the amendment and termination which is now submitted to the Parliament of the Czech Republic for approval of ratification, and the agreement will enter into force three months after the date of receipt of the later note confirming compliance with the requirements of national law necessary for the entry into force of this amendment and termination agreement. As of this date, the existing agreement between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Poland on the promotion and mutual protection of investments signed in Budapest on 16 July 1993 will expire. Thank you for your attention.
