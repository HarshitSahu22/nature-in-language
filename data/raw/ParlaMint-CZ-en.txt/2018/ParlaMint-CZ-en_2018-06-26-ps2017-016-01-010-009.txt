Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me very briefly to mention the main points of the Government's proposal for a law which, in particular, because of the transposition of two directives of the European Parliament and of the Council, amends the Act on Trade Marks, the Law on the Enforcement of Industrial Property Rights and the Act on Administrative Charges. The main changes to the existing legislation on trade marks include, in particular, allowing non-traditional trade marks to be registered, such as: the introduction of certification marks or the strengthening of the status of the proprietor of the trade mark in the enforcement of his rights to trade marks. The proposed proposal within the meaning of the European Union Directive 2015/2436 aligns the management of trademarks in the Czech Republic with their administration throughout the EU and with the EU trade mark system regulated by the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council of 2017 under number 1001, which is also used in the Czech Republic. The amendment to the Industrial Property Enforcement Act consists in the introduction of the possibility for owners of business secrets to seek cash compensation by judicial means caused to them by the unauthorised acquisition, use or disclosure of their business secrets, as required by EU Directive 2016/943. By amending the Act on Administrative Fees, only the provisions governing administrative fees under the Act on Trademarks are transferred to the Act on Administrative Charges. The draft law was discussed at a meeting of the Economic Committee at which no substantive amendments were tabled. With two legislative technical recommendations from the House administration, the petitioner agreed. The Economic Committee recommends that the Chamber of Deputies discuss the draft law and approve it. Ladies and gentlemen, I am asking you to support this proposal and to release it by the third reading. Thank you for your attention.
