ParlaMint-CZ_2018-11-15-ps2017-022-01-001-001.u2	Thank you, Mr Vice-President, dear colleagues, for the purpose of the Government's bill amending Act No. 236/1995 Coll., on salaries and other requirements relating to the exercise of the office of representatives of state authority and of certain state authorities and judges and Members of the European Parliament, as amended, is to mitigate a significant increase in the level of salary bases of representatives of state power. This is due not only to the continuous increase in the average wage in the non-business sector but also to the gradual increase in the number of times the average wage used to determine the salary base of State officials on the basis of the provisions of Section 3a of Act No. 236/1995 Coll. The draft amendment is intended to stabilise the amount of multiples for the calculation of the salary base of State officials at 2,5, which is valid for the calendar year 2018, in order to avoid an exceptional jump increase in the multiples of the salary base, to 2.75 times. It is also proposed to bring the legislation into line with the decision of the Supreme Court of the Czech Republic, according to which it has been in practice since 2015, namely to clarify that for the purposes of calculating the salary bases, an indication of the average nominal monthly wage should be used for the calculated number of employees in the non-business sector, not the average nominal monthly wage of natural persons in the non-business sector. Two amendments were applied to the Government's bill at second reading. Mr Tomio Okamura, the first Mr, who proposes de facto to freeze the level of the salary base for representatives from 1 January 2019 to 31 December 2021 at this year's level. The second amendment was applied by Mr Michálek, in which he proposes a change in the statistical quantity for the calculation of the salaries of all constitutional officials, including judges and prosecutors from the average wage in the non-business sector to the average wage in the national economy. In this context, it proposes to increase the value of multiples for representatives from 2.5 to 2.54 and for judges from 3 to 3.06 average nominal monthly wages. The Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs takes a disagreeable position on both amendments. Mr Michálek also applied the motion for an accompanying resolution of the House, which has two parts.
