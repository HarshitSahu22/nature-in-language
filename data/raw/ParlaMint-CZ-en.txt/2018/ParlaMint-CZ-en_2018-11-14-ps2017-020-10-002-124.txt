Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, at the beginning of the third reading, I shall briefly mention that the bill addresses acute personnel problems in the medical opinion service and contains two measures. It is a proposal to transfer medical examiners from the regime of the Civil Service Act to the Labour Code and also a proposal to transfer the competence of the assessment committees of the MPSV to assess the state of health for the purposes of the administrative appeal procedure for non-insurable social benefits to doctors of the Czech Social Security Administration. A detailed explanation of the two proposals has already been given in the first and second reading of the draft law. Three amendments were tabled during the second reading. As for Amendment A1, this is a proposal to complement the new competence of the MPSV review committees to assess the state of health for non-insurable social benefits for the purposes of judicial review, and to Amendment A2, this is a proposal to maintain the existing regulation, i.e. not to transfer the competence from the MPSV assessment committees to the doctor of the ČSSZ, overall from a factual point of view, that both amendments cannot be approved at the same time. There would be an illogical situation where the assessment board would continue to assess the state of health for the purposes of the administrative appeal proceedings on non-insurable social benefits, but at the same time would be given a new competence to assess the state of health still for the purposes of the judicial review procedure. The panel would therefore consider the same thing twice, i.e. once in the context of the appeal procedure administrative and second in the context of the review procedure judicial, which is not even in the case of the assessment of invalidity. From the point of view of the petitioner, the amendment referred to as A1 can be recommended for adoption. There is no comment on the amendment under letter B, which regulates the postponement of electronic disability in the sickness insurance law by one year, and I recommend that you agree with it. Thank you for your attention.
