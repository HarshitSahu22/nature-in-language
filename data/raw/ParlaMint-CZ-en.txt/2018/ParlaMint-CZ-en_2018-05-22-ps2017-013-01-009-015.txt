ParlaMint-CZ_2018-05-22-ps2017-013-01-009-015.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Hello, ladies and gentlemen. Allow me to mention the draft law on granting the state guarantee to the Czech Republic to secure the loan to the International Monetary Fund. In August last year, the Government decided that the Czech Republic would comply with the International Monetary Fund's request to provide a new bilateral lending facility to ensure sufficient liquidity for the International Monetary Fund in the event of unforeseen events. The Ministry of Finance proposed to provide a loan of 1.5 billion. EUR from the foreign reserve assets of the Czech National Bank in the same amount as the previous loan from 2013. The Czech Republic has so far provided the International Monetary Fund with two loans, respectively. loans, total of 2,53 billion. EUR, both of which were secured by a State guarantee under special law No. 216/2013 Coll. The first loan granted in 2010 was fully repaid in 2016, the second loan from 2013 was not drawn over the entire period by the International Monetary Fund and expired in August 2017. [[17.40 hours]] The Act on Budgetary Rules in Section 73 authorises the Czech Republic to grant state guarantees only on the basis of a special law with effect from 1 January 2001. The guarantee for the International Monetary Fund's lending will be 1.5 billion. EUR. The budgetary rules also provide for the debtor's obligation to pay half a percent of the guaranteed amount to the State budget. It is unrealistic for us to demand this remuneration from the International Monetary Fund, which is why this remuneration is not required in the draft special law on state guarantees. I would also like to point out that the new bilateral loan agreement between the Czech National Bank and the International Monetary Fund was already concluded in Washington in October last year within the annual meetings of the International Monetary Fund. However, the Treaty will enter into force only after the entry into force of the State Guarantee Act. Thank you for your attention.
