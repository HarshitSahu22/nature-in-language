ParlaMint-CZ_2018-11-02-ps2017-020-08-002-028.u4	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. [[Please continue.]] The reason is that today the notary order allows the profession of notary only to the citizens of the Czech Republic. The judgment of the European Court of Justice requires us to allow the citizens of other Member States of the European Union to enter this profession. And we respond to that judgment by changing the law in this regard. In other words, the amendment will allow a notary's profession to be pursued by a national of a Member State of the European Union other than the Czech Republic. On the other hand, it is necessary to disprove the concerns that this change may raise, because other conditions will still apply that one who is to be a notary must meet. Among other things, they must have a Czech Law Faculty, a Czech law degree, which is taught in principle only in the Czech Republic, they do not teach Czech law anywhere abroad, and it cannot be assumed that they would start with it. Another reason is that they must control the official language to carry out individual procedural tasks, which is therefore the Czech language. In other words, therefore, it is not in principle possible to expect any surge of foreigners. Moreover, there is still a numerus clausus in terms of the number of notaries, so you cannot expect any influx of foreigners into this profession. In principle, the fight with the European Union, which, from my point of view, was lost in advance, the judgment confirmed it, was perhaps to some extent unnecessary. And now, when the Czech Republic is threatened with financial penalties for failing to bring its right into line with the judgment, it is therefore appropriate to approve this amendment. However, as I said, other conditions apply: Czech law school, knowledge of Czech language and execution, among other things, exams which also consist in Czech language. That means we're talking about units of cases where a foreigner may start a notary profession. In practice, it's probably mostly Slovák. I'm begging you, it's a non-controversial thing, we deliberately didn't add anything else, nothing else that wouldn't respond to the verdict. So there's no gold-plating.
