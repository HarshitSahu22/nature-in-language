Proposal for the election of members of the Council of the Czech Press Office 170 ballots were issued, 169 were surrendered, 1 was not surrendered. The quorum required for the election in this election was 86. Pavel Foltán won 89 votes and was elected, became a member of the ČTK Council, Jana Gáborová won 85 votes, was not elected, Michal Pehr 47 votes and Jiří Táborský 71 votes. So only Pavel Foltán was elected in the second round. In addition to this protocol, I will remind you that there is one vacancy left, and according to the information I have from Gremia, we should vote for it at the April meeting of the week from 9 April, which means that until 9 April I can also inform you that the electoral commission will declare a deadline for the nomination, and on Wednesday 11 April we would try to fill that final seat. The second election point was the election of thirteen members of the Council of the State Fund of Culture of the Czech Republic.
