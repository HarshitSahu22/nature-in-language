ParlaMint-CZ_2018-06-28-ps2017-016-03-003-007.u2	Thank you. Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, the law on the processing of personal data repeals the existing law on the protection of personal data, implements the adaptation to the General Data Protection Regulation and the transposition of the criminal directive governing the processing of personal data by judicial and police authorities. The proposed law also introduces exceptions which, according to GDPR, Member States can use to facilitate the implementation of GDPR as much as possible. During the first reading, we cooperated with the various committees to make use of the Czech Republic's exceptions to the maximum extent wherever possible and it makes sense. The draft of this law also regulates the position of the Office for Personal Data Protection. It is based not only on the requirements of practice but also on the new requirements of European law on the full independence of supervisory authorities and on intensive cooperation and codecision of supervisory authorities, particularly in cross-border cases.
