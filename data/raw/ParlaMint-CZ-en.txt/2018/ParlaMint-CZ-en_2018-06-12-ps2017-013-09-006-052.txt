ParlaMint-CZ_2018-06-12-ps2017-013-09-006-052.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, fellow Members, dear colleagues, I will try to represent the Foreign Minister Martin Stropnický, who is ill, and I would really like to introduce to you again the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of Japan on visas for work leave signed in Tokyo last June. Minister Stropnický here spoke of her in January. The agreement will be particularly interesting for young citizens of the Czech Republic between 18 and 30 years of age, as it will extend the territory of non-EU countries to which they can travel, and not only to make an annual stay, but also to make extra money on the costs associated with it. Agreements of this kind are negotiated with New Zealand, Canada, the Republic of Korea, Israel and Chile and are very popular in practice. On a non-contractual basis, such a program works, for example, in relation to Taiwan and Australia. I therefore believe that you will support the Government's proposal to give consent to the ratification of the agreement. Thank you for your attention.

Instructs the President of the Committee to present this resolution to the President of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic; III. Instructs the Committee's rapporteur to report to the Chamber of Deputies at the meeting on the results of the discussion of this government proposal at the meeting of the Foreign Committee. So much for our committee's resolution on the material.
