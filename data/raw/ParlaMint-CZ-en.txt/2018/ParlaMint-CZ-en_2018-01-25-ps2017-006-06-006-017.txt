ParlaMint-CZ_2018-01-25-ps2017-006-06-006-017.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce a government bill amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the insurance and reinsurance distribution law. House printing No. 49 is a proposal for a set of amendments to the laws in force which result from the House press No. 48, which I presented a moment ago, means from the government's draft law on insurance and reinsurance distribution, and specifically the amendment of the following laws: trade law, insurance law and the Act on administrative fees. [[10.00 hours]] The change in the trade law consists in the fact that the category of separate insurers of insurance events, which is now regulated by a special law, is transferred to the standard trade regime as a business linked. The reason for this step is both the formality of existing legislation and the fact that the directive on the distribution of insurance and other European legislation does not count on the special regulation of such persons. Thank you for your attention.
