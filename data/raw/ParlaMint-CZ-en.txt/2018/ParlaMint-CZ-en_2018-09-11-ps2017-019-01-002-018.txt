ParlaMint-CZ_2018-09-11-ps2017-019-01-002-018.u2	Mr President, Prime Minister, honourable Members of the government, ladies and gentlemen, I will try to be quite brief, however, we ask you to approve this bill in the regime of Section 90, so I think it is fair again to justify it a little. This is an amendment that affects mainly legal professions, but not just them. The point is that by 30 June 2017, the value added tax was the actual date of the transaction. At the time of the last amendment to this Value Added Tax Act, it was said that for transactions that were longer than 12 months, it was always at the end of the year. The problem is that for those legal professions, as the text says, if it is a service provided by law or by a decision of a public authority to a third party, if the payment for the provision of that service is paid by the State, then in a number of cases it is typically an ex offo representation. But the same goes for free legal aid and some other actions. The state on the basis of the law or the decision of the public authority, most often the court, will say: You will represent someone and we will, because it is an ex offo representation, pay you back in time. But, of course, all the time the trial is under way, the person who provides the service, first of all, is not paid for, and second of all, she is not even sure what will be paid for it, because that is the decision of the public authority responsible for the action. And it can take two or three years and she should pay VAT at the end of each year, but from an amount that she does not really know and is not able to derive, she would actually loan the state budget through the VAT paid. Why it got into the VAT Act, it's pretty clear. To avoid fraud. But there, in the case where the amount is paid by the State and this is done on the basis of the decision of the public authority, such fraud clearly cannot occur, because it is the State who decides how much will be paid. And therefore, from the amount paid, it will be paid in conclusion, value added tax, only when it is paid. That is why I think it would be fair for us to make this change. Indeed, it has no dangerous or potentially dangerous influence on the state budget. On the contrary, it would lead to individual natural or legal persons not lending public finances. And as you can see, the government has also expressed a favourable opinion on this. So I believe it could pass through the House in Section 90 mode. We believe that this is such a simple amendment and that it is so dangerous to open the Value Added Tax Act that it would be better if we could do it in one reading. Thank you for your attention.
