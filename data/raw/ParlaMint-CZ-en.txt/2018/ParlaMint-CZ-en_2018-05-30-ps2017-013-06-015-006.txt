The Senate Bill amending Act No. 182/1993 Coll., on the Constitutional Court, as amended, and Act No. 250/2016 Coll., on liability for and management of offences /house print 30/ - second reading Under the authority of the Senate, Senator Michael Canov will bring the bill forward, whom I welcome here, and I ask him to take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2018-05-30-ps2017-013-06-015-006.u2	Mr President, Minister, ladies and gentlemen, the substance of the amendment is to abolish the criminal immunity of constitutional judges. So far, when a constitutional judge commits an offence, he only has to go directly to the Constitutional Court, where he can get a complaint. In the event of an amendment, it would have the same conditions as any citizen. Normally, a misdemeanor would be dealt with in criminal proceedings. Only if he had requested it would he have gone before the disciplinary senate at the Constitutional Court, but also there he would have been dealt with according to the rules for offenses. A similar amendment was once adopted for Members and Senators, it was in 2002, and it proved itself. So, of course, I will be very happy to see this amendment adopted and approved. Thank you for your attention.
ParlaMint-CZ_2018-05-30-ps2017-013-06-015-006.u3	Thank you, Senator. In the first reading, we ordered this proposal to be discussed by the Constitutional Legal Committee as a guarantee committee.
