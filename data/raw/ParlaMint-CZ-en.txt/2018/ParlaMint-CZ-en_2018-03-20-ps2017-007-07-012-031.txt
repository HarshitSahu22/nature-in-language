ParlaMint-CZ_2018-03-20-ps2017-007-07-012-031.u4	Thank you again. The submitted government bill contains a total of nine laws, mostly in the field of social insurance, and can be briefly described as amending, since it does not contain any substantive adjustments. The draft law concerns only the correction of legislative technical omissions which arose last year, i.e. in the last year of the term of office of the last Chamber of Deputies. In 2017, a number of laws were adopted to amend all basic social legislation. The legislative process was in principle parallel, it was not predictable and it was not clear what the effectiveness of individual laws would be, which was based on the date of publication in the Collection of Laws, and which law should therefore be followed by another. In addition, the laws were amended in the Chamber of Deputies by numerous parliamentary amendments, which contained some legislative technical inconsistencies. This resulted in unintended legislative consequences which, while not endangering the rights of entitled persons, make it difficult in practice to apply certain provisions and, for some provisions, doubt about the resulting adjustment. These are, in particular, erroneous references resulting from renumbering the provisions in another law, introducing duplicated provisions, temporal non-compliance with new provisions or inaccurate terminology. These deficiencies have already been brought to the attention of the Senate committees and have also been required to remedy them in the form of a corrective amendment. A draft amending law has therefore been drawn up to correct the deficiencies identified. The legislative technique of this remedy depends on the nature of individual deficiencies, e.g. in some cases, certain provisions need to be repealed and re-approved, but already with the right effect. The individual proposed adjustments are explained in detail in the specific part of the explanatory memorandum. Given the urgency of time, as many changes have already taken effect, the general effectiveness of the law is proposed on the date of its publication. This was also the reason why the government proposed a single reading procedure to discuss the bill. The draft law, since it does not contain, I repeat once again, does not contain any substantive adjustments, has no financial impact. Thank you for your attention.
