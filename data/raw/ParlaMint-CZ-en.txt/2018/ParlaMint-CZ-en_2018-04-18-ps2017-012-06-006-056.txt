Proposal for the election of members of the Council of the State Cinematography Fund First, the election of three members of the Council of the State Cinema Fund. 175 ballots were issued, 174 were submitted, one was therefore not surrendered and the quorum required for the election is 88, which was, after all, in all four points. The results are as follows: Helena Bendová won 140 votes and was elected, Ivana Hejlíčková 26 votes and was not elected, Jaromír Kallista 148 votes and was elected, Tomáš Škrdlant 3 votes and was not elected, Marta Švecová 113 votes and was elected, Daniel Tuček 12 votes and was not elected. We voted for three candidates for the State Cinema Fund. So all three seats are filled and this option ends.
