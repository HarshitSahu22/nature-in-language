ParlaMint-CZ_2018-03-21-ps2017-007-08-012-036.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Colleagues and colleagues, I will be very brief because I have made this point more extensive in the first reading. So let me just briefly remind you that the subject of the amendment to the Rotterdam Convention is the inclusion of new hazardous chemicals in the Annex to this Convention. Specifically, industrial chemicals are short-chain chlorinated paraffins and all tributyltin compounds, as well as carbofuran and trichlorfon pesticides. The inclusion of these substances in Annex III to the Rotterdam Convention will be subject to a prior consent procedure, which in practice means that the delegated authority of the importing State decides whether to authorise or prohibit the import of such substance, or lays down certain conditions for such imports. From the point of view of the Czech Republic, it must be stressed that all these substances have been banned from production and use in the Czech Republic for many years, so that we, as the Czech Republic, actually confirm the amendment of the Rotterdam Convention, namely Annex III, do not actually change any regime in the Czech Republic, but we will support that these substances can, of course, be used limitedly in other states that are members and participants of the Rotterdam Convention. All of it, I guess. Thank you.
