Let me reiterate the bill amending Act No. 159/1999 Coll., on certain conditions of business in the field of tourism. I would just like to repeat here - I will not delay - that the bill in question is a pure transposition of the EU Directive 2015/2302 on package travel services and related travel services. The national transposition regulation should take effect by 1 July 2018 at the latest. The adoption of a bill is necessary to fulfil the obligation to the European Union. On 17 May 2018, the draft law was discussed at the 15th meeting of the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. After discussion, the Senate returned the bill to the Chamber of Deputies in the text of the amendments adopted. The amendments to the Senate concern only legislative technical adaptations to the Civil Code, which change nothing about the substance and importance of the original proposal. I therefore agree with the adoption of the proposal as amended by the amendments to the Senate. Thank you for your attention.
