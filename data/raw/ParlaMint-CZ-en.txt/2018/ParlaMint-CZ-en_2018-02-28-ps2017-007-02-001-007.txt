ParlaMint-CZ_2018-02-28-ps2017-007-02-001-007.u2	Thank you for the floor. Good morning. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the proposal for an amendment to the Income Tax Act concerns so-called. funds for qualified investors. These funds benefit from tax advantages of 5%, but they are classic investment funds that invest in financial markets. In principle, these are ordinary public limited liability companies, such as: Building apartments, houses. However, instead of tax on income 19 %, they pay only 5 % tax. The funds are based mostly by developers, the shares of these funds are hardly traded on the stock exchange and their ownership structure is not very scattered. The Income Tax Act allowed these funds in § 17b para. Article 4 (1) (a) (a) an exception when they register their shares on the stock exchange. There are 34 such funds on the stock market today with profits of around 3 billion. CZK per year, instead of income tax around 600 million. They take about a quarter a year. So this is a classic example of an advantage for large investment firms. The stock exchange has already taken certain measures to ensure that the Income Tax Act is not used massively, but these measures do not have the power of legislation. The exchange may evaluate the eligibility of the so-called orderly trading, assessed by the so-called the distribution of the issue, whether the securities are held by at least five investors. On the basis of this condition, 12 funds have been excluded from registration, but the proposal is not to allow more or less classic stock companies that just put on an investment fund label and take some formal action to have tax exemptions like this. The bill in the second reading in the Senate went through 45 votes at 50 present, across the spectrum. The proposal was also discussed by the government which recommended it for approval. I note that the proposal has already been consulted by the Ministry of Finance before the Senate has been discussed and it has been stated that it is appropriate to remove this hole in the Income Tax Act. So I'm asking you to support this Senate bill for second reading. Thank you for your attention.
