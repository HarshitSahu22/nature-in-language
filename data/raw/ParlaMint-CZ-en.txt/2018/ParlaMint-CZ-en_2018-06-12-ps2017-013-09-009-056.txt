Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Council of Europe Convention on a comprehensive approach to the protection, security and provision of services in football matches and other sporting events (July 3, 2016, Saint - Denis) /German press 60/ - second reading Minister Metnar is ready, Mr Bžoch is also ready. I note that the Committee's resolution has been delivered as House Press 60/1, and I will now ask the Minister of the Interior to present an introductory report.
ParlaMint-CZ_2018-06-12-ps2017-013-09-009-056.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Dear Members, this Convention replaces the 1985 Convention on Spectator Violence, when the current Convention does not reflect changes in procedures and good practice in this area, which has taken place in recent years. The Council of Europe Convention on a comprehensive approach to the protection, security and provision of services in football matches and other sporting events does not focus solely on security measures, but recognises the need to ensure cooperation with all public and private sector actors concerned in ensuring the protection and security of football matches or, where appropriate, the need to ensure cooperation with all relevant actors in the public and private sectors. at other sports events, and a pleasant environment for viewers. It is a framework document which leaves the Member States to adapt these principles to national conditions and to their legal order. The commitments arising from the new Convention of the Czech Republic are already being fulfilled, so there is no need to adopt new legislative measures or increase the state budget. The provisions on international cooperation are an important part of the Convention. This is already ensured mainly by means of the so-called national football information centres. These centres act as central contact points for the exchange of information and facilitate international police cooperation in the context of international football matches. Their activities will continue after this adjustment. Thank you.
