ParlaMint-CZ_2018-09-19-ps2017-019-06-002-110.u2	Mr President, thank you for the floor. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce you to the content of the Government's bill which you are about to discuss and the reasons for its processing. This is a draft law aimed at the transposition of two directives of the European Parliament and of the Council amending the Act on Trade Marks, the Law on the Enforcement of Industrial Property Rights and the Act on Administrative Fees. Pursuant to Directive 2015/2436, the system of trade marks in the Czech Republic is aligned with the systems of trade marks in other EU Member States and the EU protection lock system, as amended by Regulation 2017/1001. Thus, unjustified differences in national arrangements are removed and the legal certainty of the proprietors of trade marks within the internal market is strengthened. The substantial changes to the proposed trade mark legislation include, for example, the introduction of certification marks, the waiver of the mandatory graphic representation of trade marks preventing the registration of certain non-traditional trade marks or the introduction of information to the proprietor of the trade mark by the Industrial Property Office before the expiry of the trade mark. The amendment to the Industrial Property Enforcement Act consists in the introduction of the possibility for owners of business secrets to seek appropriate compensation by judicial means, which was caused by the unlawful acquisition, use or disclosure of their business secrets, as required by the relevant directive. The amendment to the Act on Administrative Charges consists in the transfer of administrative and administrative fees in proceedings relating to trade marks so far regulated by the Act on Trademarks into the Act on Administrative Charges. The bill submitted was discussed at the meeting of the Guarantee and Economic Committee 20. 6. 2018, on which only two legislative technical amendments to the draft law were proposed, with which the petitioner agrees. At the second reading of the bill at the House meeting 26. 6. an amendment concerning the amendment to the Trade Marks Act has been tabled. The Economic Committee at its 28th meeting. 6. adopt a resolution in which it does not recommend approving this amendment. The petitioner agrees with this resolution of the Economic Committee. The main reasons for rejecting an amendment requesting the maintenance of the original § 6 are its non-systemicity with regard to the functioning of the Act on Trade Marks as a whole, as well as the need to properly implement the law of the European Union consisting of the obligation to ensure that the refusal of registration of a trade mark may occur only if the earlier mark is properly used, i.e. in objections, and not at the very beginning of the proceedings. Madam, honourable Members, I hereby ask you to approve the bill in its third reading. Thank you.
