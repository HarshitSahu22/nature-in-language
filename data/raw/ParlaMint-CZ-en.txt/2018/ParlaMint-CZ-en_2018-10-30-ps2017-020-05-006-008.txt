ParlaMint-CZ_2018-10-30-ps2017-020-05-006-008.u8	Good evening, Dear Government, honourable Members. I would like to read three amendments to the amendment to the Copyright Act, House press number 175, which were recorded as House document 1518. They are all of a nature rather technical and definitely not a revolution in the concept of copyright law. I see them as minor consensual changes that can make life a lot easier for a lot of people. My first amendment is merely an attempt to straighten out a double standard. According to the current regulation, if someone is illegally using the author's work, the collective administrator is entitled to claim compensation for unwarranted enrichment in the amount of twice the normal amount. The purpose of compensation for damage and the issue of unjust enrichment is in the Czech constitutional and legal order otherwise only compensatory, that is, one party will return what has been wrongly acquired, and doubling this amount is a special kind of penalty, for which there are no reasons. In addition, it is an amendment that is completely unique and non-systemic in the Czech legal system. The plaintiff has a double advantage. Not only can it choose between compensation according to the really lost profit or the price of the licence normal at the time of use of the work, as we know, for example, in the case of digital dissemination of multimedia content is significantly different, but also the penalty is to be doubled. I suggest we cross out a sentence that allows this duplication. The second amendment corrects the current inappropriate situation with regard to copying notaries. At the moment there is a valid exception which directly prohibits the production of a copy of musical and musical musical works. This leads to special situations where, for example, simple playing for joy is pointlessly limited when multiplying to paper. We would like to change this situation in such a way that the production of a copy of the notebook is possible for its own use. That means, if you go to the Fish Mass at the Main Station at Christmas, you can multiply the notes by which you will sing. This is the aim of this amendment. The last amendment concerns the issue of orphan works. Very simple. At this point, based on something we consider a legislative hole rather than anything else, collective administrators are entitled to sue users of orphan works. So they are such works whose author is not known or has not been able to trace it at all. We do not think that the role of collective administrators should be to prosecute users of works by authors who, for whatever reason, do not show interest in their work and do not want to be represented. It would remain possible for the users of the works to ask the collective administrator to license an orphaned piece, for example, for situations where he would like to achieve complete legal certainty even if the author appeared in the future. In this way, there will be no major harm to the rights of collective administrators and the situation will be settled. Thank you for your attention.
