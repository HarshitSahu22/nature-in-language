Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of Japan on visas for work leave signed in Tokyo on 27 June 2017 /German press 44/ - First reading Please have Foreign Minister Martin Stropnický present the opening words. Please, Minister.
ParlaMint-CZ_2018-01-25-ps2017-006-06-003-021.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, hello. The aim of the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of Japan on visas for work leave, hereinafter referred to as the Agreement signed last June in Tokyo, is to allow young people between the ages of 18 and 30 to not only make an annual stay in the territory of the other Contracting Party, but also to make money on the costs associated with it. This is therefore a treaty that is clearly and strongly oriented towards young citizens of both states, and we believe that it is a clear benefit from this point of view. Agreements of this kind are negotiated by the Czech Republic, for example, with New Zealand and Canada, with the Republic of Korea, with Israel, with Chile. In practice, they are proving to be very beneficial, especially, as I have already said, for our young citizens, whose interest in making such stays abroad prevails considerably. On a non-contractual basis, such a program works in relation to, for example, Taiwan and will soon start to work in relation to Australia. So much for a brief introduction. I believe that you will support the Government's proposal to agree to ratify this agreement, and thank you for your attention.
