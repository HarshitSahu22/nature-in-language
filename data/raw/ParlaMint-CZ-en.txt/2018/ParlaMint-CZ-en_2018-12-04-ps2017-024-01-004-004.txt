ParlaMint-CZ_2018-12-04-ps2017-024-01-004-004.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, I will be very brief. Let me briefly remind you of the draft amendment to the Bond Act and some other laws. The reason for submitting this proposal is to try to remedy the shortcomings in the existing legislation on mortgage bonds and to adjust some other shortcomings in the bond law highlighted by practice. Another reason is to remove the uncertainty of the current legal regulation of insolvency law in relation to covered bonds. The draft law therefore excludes the automatic maturity of covered bonds and excludes covered bonds from the substance of property. The Senate has returned the bill to the Chamber of Deputies with an amendment which corrects the legislative technical error that occurred by adopting another amendment in the meantime, which must be taken into account in this amendment. I therefore ask you to support this bill, as amended by the Senate amendment, and I thank the Senate for this warning.
