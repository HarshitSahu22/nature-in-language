ParlaMint-CZ_2018-11-14-ps2017-020-10-010-017.u6	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Hello, esteemed colleagues. I would like to sign up for the amendment as House Document No 1512. This is an amendment to House press No. 157, which is a proposal from a group of Members amending Act No. 114/1995 Coll. on Inland Navigation, as amended. My amendment removes the river Berounka from River Kilometer 37 to the port of Radotín from the list of transport significant waterways. [[11.40 hours]] Annex No. 2 of Act No. 114/1995 Coll. currently defines the watercourse Berounky from river kilometer 37 to the port of Radotín as a usable, transport-important waterway. As a result of the inclusion of this watercourse between the transport-relevant usable routes, the obligation of the competent authorities, in particular the Ministry of Transport and the Directorate of Waterways as an investor, is to promote the navigability of this watercourse in order to fulfil the purpose of Law No. 114/1995 Coll. Due to the hydrological conditions on the watercourse, navigation can only be considered if large-scale construction works are carried out to permit the navigability of this section of the watercourse. The municipalities and cities in the Central Bohemia Region have defined these construction buildings and have expressed a common dissenting position with the navigation of Berounka under the current legislation. The implementation of that navigability plan will result in irreversible interventions in the valuable natural nature of the whole area, a negative impact on the living conditions of the local population, a threat to the basic ecological values of the territory concerned, provided that the economic profitability of the entire project has not been reliably demonstrated in view of the estimated multi-billion investment costs. The project of navigating the watercourse Berounka must be considered to be obsolete at present and contrary to the current principles of water transport, care for nature and landscape and, above all, the principle of sustainable development. With regard to other reasons for submitting my amendment, I refer to the detailed written reasoning in the system. This is briefly on my amendment, to which I will sign up in a detailed debate. Thank you.
