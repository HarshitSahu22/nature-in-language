Proposal for the election of a member of the Council of the Czech Press Office The first ballot point is the election of a member of the Czech Press Office Council. In this election, 167 ballots were issued. The vote for the election was therefore 84 votes. The quorum is the same for all three electoral points, i.e. 84. At the Czech Press Office proposed candidate Jana Gáborová won 91 votes and was therefore elected, becoming a member of the ČTK Council. Mr. Kristián Chalupa has won 54 votes. So Mrs. Gáborová has been elected. That concludes the choice. I also note that the ČTK Council is complete with this step.
