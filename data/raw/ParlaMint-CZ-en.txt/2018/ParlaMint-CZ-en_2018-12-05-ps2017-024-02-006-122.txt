Mr Vice-President, fellow Members, I would like to present very briefly the draft amendment law on the law on the processing of personal data. This proposal was prepared by the Ministry of the Interior in cooperation with a number of ministries, in particular the Ministry of Transport, Justice and Finance, and this proposal is a task which has not been completed by the previous governments because elections have entered into the preparation of the law. The Government has taken the preparation of the law responsibly and complements it with the regulation of laws in other departments, so that the rule of law is properly and unambiguously in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation. This law provides for general exemptions from the general regulation, respectively. The previous law amended the general exemptions from the general regulation and the amendment law, on the other hand, contains a number of amendments implementing partial and targeted exemptions from the general regulation in the context of specific regulations. This achieves greater legal certainty and more balanced protection of personal data in specific areas that need it. The amendment law also contains amendments introducing certain parts of the directive on the protection of personal data in criminal matters. These are those parts which cannot be dealt with jointly for all competent authorities, because they differ between themselves in nature, tasks, structure or procedures. This applies in particular to amendments to the laws on security and criminal law. Last but not least, this law implements a European directive on the use of Air Passenger Name Record data, the so-called PNR, in order to combat terrorism, into the law on police and civil aviation. This law has been discussed by six committees, and I will, of course, give individual opinions on the relevant proposals, but it can be said in general that I am particularly prepared to accept the proposals of the Committee on Constitutional Law and the Committee on Health. Thank you.
