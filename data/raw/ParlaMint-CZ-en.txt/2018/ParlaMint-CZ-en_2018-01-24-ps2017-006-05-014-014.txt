[[There are only women present from the government.]] The Czech National Bank's monetary monopoly includes the exclusive right to issue banknotes and coins as well as commemorative coins. The proposal therefore merely complements the Czech National Bank's authorisation to issue commemorative notes and trade coins in addition to the already existing right to issue commemorative coins issued, for example, on the anniversary of major events in national history. Commemorative banknotes are issued by a number of emission banks, such as Polish and Swedish, Central Bank and Scottish emission banks in the European Union, outside Europe, for example, the Canadian, Australian, New Zealand, Singaporean, Thai and Filipino central banks. A special occasion will be, for example, the 100th anniversary of the Czechoslovak currency in 2019, which could be remembered in addition to commemorative coins by a commemorative note. Specifically, on the occasion of this anniversary, the Czech National Bank intends to issue a commemorative coin worth CZK 10,000 and a commemorative banknote whose nominal value will be CZK 100. The possibility of the Czech National Bank issuing trade coins - Czech ducats - is added. Following the regulation of the Czech National Bank Act, Act No. 136/2001 Coll., on the circulation of banknotes and coins. As rapporteur, I recommend releasing the proposal for this press to the second reading. Thank you.
