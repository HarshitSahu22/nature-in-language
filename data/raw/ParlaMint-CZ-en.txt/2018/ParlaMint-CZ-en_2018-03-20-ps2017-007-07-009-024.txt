ParlaMint-CZ_2018-03-20-ps2017-007-07-009-024.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the Ministry of Industry and Trade carries out, in cooperation with other departments under Act No. 38/2008 Sb. control of foreign trade in goods which could be used for the execution of the death penalty, torture or other cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment. This law follows up on the directly applicable European Union law governing foreign trade in those goods. Since the last amendment of the Act, a new Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 November 2016 has entered into force and it is therefore necessary to proceed to the partial amendments to the Act in question and to adapt it to that directly applicable regulation. These are in particular new provisions of the law for the transit of goods or the provision of brokering services and technical amendments. The nature of the control system does not change. In the context of changes in the law, administrative fees are revised to follow the law in a conceptual manner. The original proposal was approved by the Government in March 2017 and submitted to the Chamber of Deputies. The Chamber of Deputies failed to discuss the amendment in the previous parliamentary term. We therefore submit an identical bill for renegotiation. Since the adaptation period expired in December 2016, we propose to agree with the draft law at first reading. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to support this material and its approval at first reading so that the obligation to the European Union is fulfilled as soon as possible. Thank you for your attention.
