ParlaMint-CZ_2018-05-22-ps2017-013-01-012-019.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, I present to you the government's proposal for an amendment to the Bonds Act and some other laws. As I explained at first reading, the reason for the proposal is to address the shortcomings in the existing legislation on mortgage bonds and to adjust some other shortcomings in bond law, which have been highlighted by practice. The mortgage bonds are securities that only banks are allowed to issue. These are bonds that are covered by mortgage credit claims, so sometimes more generally, covered bonds are also mentioned. From the point of view of investors, these are above average safe securities and from the point of view of their issuer, i.e. banks, they are an easy way to raise funds for providing mortgage loans on favourable terms. Our main goal is to make the issue of mortgage mortgage bonds more attractive for investors. At present, the legislative form of mortgage mortgage bonds lacks major institutions that are usual in other legal systems of developed Western Europe, which causes negative assessment of the legal framework for mortgage bonds by credit rating agencies and consequently leads to lower attractiveness and lower competitiveness of the Czech Republic in this area. Two major shortcomings have been identified in the existing legislation, which this amendment removes. This is first of all the unclear scope and mortgage regime under the insolvency law, i.e. the extent of assets that will belong to the mortgage nature, especially in relation to mortgage loans payments after bankruptcy. Furthermore, it is a matter of automatic repayment of mortgage mortgage bonds liabilities in the event of a declaration of bankruptcy on the issuer's assets, so-called. Acceleration. In order to remedy the above shortcomings of the current legislation, the amendment builds the insolvency law regime in relation to covered bonds and explicitly excludes the automatic maturity of covered bonds in the event of insolvency proceedings and excludes covered bonds from the substance of property. Furthermore, the amendment to the Bonds Act complements the modification of the so-called Act. a collateral agent, a person representing the interests of bondholders in relation to collateral, while the bondholders themselves are not entitled to act on their own, even during insolvency proceedings and enforcement proceedings. The so-called "modification" is added. the monitors of covered blocks, i.e. persons who perform the function of a supervisor and control, in relation to covered bonds, how the issuer complies with legal requirements. Another change is the introduction of the institute called a forced management of covered blocks, another bank appointed to this function by the Czech National Bank, which is responsible for ensuring the management of assets and debts related to covered bonds, if the coverage of bonds or the proper repayment of covered bonds debts is at risk. The proposed amendment also introduces the regulation of the so-called law. negative-yield bonds or mandatory convertible bonds. The bill submitted is not a transposition. This is a national legislative regulation, but it respects European legislation and is fully in line with the commitments for the Czech Republic resulting from its membership of the European Union. In preparing the amendment, we were based on the experience of European countries with a developed capital market. Thank you for your attention.
