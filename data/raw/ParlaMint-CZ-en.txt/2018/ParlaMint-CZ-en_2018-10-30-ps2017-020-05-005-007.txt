This is due not only to the continuous increase in the average wage in the non-business sector, but also to the gradual increase in the number 236/1995 Coll., by multiplying the average wage used to determine the salary base of State officials on the basis of the provisions of Section 3a of Act No. As you know, the draft amendment is intended to stabilise the amount of the multiple for the calculation of the salary base of State officials to 2,5, which is valid for the calendar year 2018, in order to avoid a jump increase in the number of civil servants' salary bases for the planned 2.75 times the average wage in the non-business sector. Another purpose of the amendment is to bring legislation into line with the finding of the Supreme Court of the Czech Republic, according to which it has been in practice since 2015. It is therefore proposed to specify that for the purposes of calculating the salaries, an indication of the average nominal monthly wage should be used for the calculated number of employees in the non-business sector, not for the average nominal monthly wage of natural persons in the non-business sector. Both the Constitutional Committee and the Budget Committee at their meetings on 10 October of this year recommended to the Chamber of Deputies that the proposal be approved without amendment.
