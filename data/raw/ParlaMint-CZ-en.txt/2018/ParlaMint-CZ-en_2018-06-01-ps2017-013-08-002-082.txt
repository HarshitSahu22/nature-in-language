ParlaMint-CZ_2018-06-01-ps2017-013-08-002-082.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, let me just briefly remind you that this is a transposition amendment. The deadline is long over. It is the introduction of a European investigation order in criminal matters. This does not appear to be controversial even in the constitutional committee's deliberations. Amendments concerning thematically something slightly different have been read in the constitutional committee. They adjust the system of using money from cash penalties to cover the cash penalties imposed by a criminal order, which is very much needed, which was simply forgotten in the original version. I also support this amendment and, overall, I am asking for support from the proposal. Thank you.
