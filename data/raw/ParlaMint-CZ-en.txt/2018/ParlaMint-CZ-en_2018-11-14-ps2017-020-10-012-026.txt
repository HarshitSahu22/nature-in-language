This is the House press 301, whose author is the Government of the Czech Republic. In front of me, the Home Secretary described this bill in detail. I would still touch on some specific laws. So the proposed bill is an adaptation law, as has already been said here, which adapts the legal order of the Czech Republic to the regulation of the European Parliament and Council of Europe (?) - 2016 under number 1191 of 6 July 2016 on promoting the free movement of citizens, simplifying the requirements for the submission of certain authentic instruments in the European Union and amending Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013, 1024/2012. In fact, five laws change by draft law. I'm going to touch these individual laws. Firstly, the first law - it is a necessary adaptation which is contained in the amendment of Act No. 301/2000 Coll., on matrices, name and surname and on the amendment of related laws, Act No. 186/2013 Coll., citizenship of the Czech Republic, and about the amendment of certain laws. The Regulation provides for a new obligation to attach a form to the certificate issued. It is, for example, a birth certificate, marriage certificate or death certificate, a certificate of registered partnership, a certificate of legal capacity for marriage, a certificate of legal capacity for entry into a registered partnership and a certified exit from the population register, upon request. In response to this, the amendment to the Act on Matrices also amends the issue of the issuance of forms for matrices and certificates, the waiver of the requirement for verification and official translation into the Czech language of foreign public documents, which are submitted under the Act on Matrices, if these have been issued under the regulation. Furthermore, the current procedure for reporting to and searching for data in the registers, documents and copies of the journals shall be specified. The proposal also contains legislative technical adaptations. In addition, the amendment to the Act on citizenship of the Czech Republic proposes the introduction of the possibility of abandoning the requirement of higher verification and official translation into the Czech language of foreign public documents, which are submitted for the application for citizenship of the Czech Republic, if these have been issued under the regulation. The amendment to the Basic Registers Act proposes to attach a form to the verified exit from the public administration information system, which is issued from the population register and which contains an indication of the address and place of residence. It is also an amendment to Act No. 634/2004 Coll., on administrative fees, where it responds to the amendment to the Act of Matrition. Furthermore, changes are proposed in the definition of a range of entities in the non-profit sector exempted from certain administrative charges. It is proposed to increase the administrative fee for inspection of the specific registration and to the documentation for it to 50 CZK, currently it is only 20 CZK. In case of searching in matrice books or collections of documents held until 31 December 1958, which is a search, it is proposed to set an administrative fee of CZK 200 for each and every hour. Then it's - thirdly - an amendment to Act No. 21/2006 Coll., on the verification of the conformity of a copy or a copy with a document and verification of the authenticity of the signature, and on the amendment of certain laws, the Act of Verification, where it is amended and specifying certain provisions concerning in particular the vision of documents bearing so-called. visible hedging elements, legalisation of the signature on foreign-language documents, where the obligation to provide official translation of documents for the purposes of legalisation would lapse. This appears to be an inefficient and disproportionately burdensome applicant. It is also, for example, proof of the identity of the applicant for legalisation or witnesses in the case of legalisation involving two witnesses, where it is proposed to specify or to extend the list of documents by which the applicant can prove his identity. Next. It is Act 300/2008 Coll., on electronic operations and authorized conversion of documents, which regulates the issue of data boxes and authorized conversion of documents. The aim of this amendment is to create the possibility of creating a data box and managing access to it without the need for a personal visit by the Office, the administrative authority or the authorities implementing the signature legalization, in particular by means of a identification card with machine-readable data and a contact electronic chip, which will be available to most citizens of the Czech Republic within ten years. It is also a regulation of the rules concerning the authorized conversion of documents so that it does not allow to circumvent the prohibition of vision. This is in particular the introduction of a ban on the conversion of certain documents. And lastly, it's actually the fifth amendment to the law, better said, it's the amendment to Law No. 111/2009 Coll., on basic registers, which presents the possibility of the Ministry of the Interior and the Czech Statistical Office to transfer some of its responsibilities to the Basic Registers Administration, which is the operator of basic registers. The decision to use this option is left to the decision of the Ministry of the Interior and the Czech Statistical Office due to greater operational decision-making on these issues. I just want to stress that this amendment does not concern the Register of ROIAN, which is the Register of Territorial Identification of Addresses and Real Estate. So in conclusion, just a summary.
