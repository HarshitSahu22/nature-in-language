ParlaMint-CZ_2018-10-30-ps2017-020-05-002-004.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the government bill amending the Exchange Act was presented in detail at the last meeting. Allow me, then, to briefly repeat the essential points of the proposal. The submitted amendment introduces the right of the customer to withdraw from the exchange agreement within two hours of the exchange. In the case of automatic exchange machines, the withdrawal period is set at three working days, and if the customer cannot withdraw from the contract, as this prevents him from having an obstacle on the side of the exchange office, the withdrawal period is set at six months. In order to avoid abuse of the institution of withdrawal for speculation, the extent to which it is possible to withdraw is limited to a thousand euros. It will therefore be possible to withdraw from around 90 to 95 percent of the exchange shops. Pre-contractual information shall be deleted for transactions where it is possible to withdraw from the contract. The client will thus receive only one document, where all necessary information will be provided for him, including information on the right of withdrawal. The proposal also states that the more favourable terms of the shift, so called. VIP offer must not be advertised in the form of exchange rate. There will be only one exchange ticket in the premises and its surroundings to avoid confusion of the standard exchange rate and more favourable offer. The right to claim remuneration for the execution of the exchange trade shall also be limited. The remuneration will only be taken into account at the rate of the exchange rate. Thank you for your attention.
