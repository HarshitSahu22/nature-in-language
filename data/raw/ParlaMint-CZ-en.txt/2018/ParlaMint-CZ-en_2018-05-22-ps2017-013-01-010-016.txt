Ladies and gentlemen, please allow me, following the second reading of the House Press and its subsequent discussion in the Guarantee Budget Committee, to briefly sum up the main objectives and content of the bill. The Government's proposal for a law on the distribution of insurance and reinsurance is a transposition standard implemented in our legal order by the European Directive on the distribution of insurance. However, the draft law also contains elements of national regulation that respond to certain specific problems of the domestic insurance market and whose common objective is consumer protection. These problems are mainly damage to clients in the negotiation of life insurance, the problem of low professional qualifications of insurance distributors and significantly limited current supervisory instruments of the Czech National Bank as a competent supervisory authority. The core increase in consumer protection in the negotiation of insurance products is mainly to strengthen the liability of insurance undertakings and insurance intermediaries for the quality of their distribution network and, further, to ensure that the client is informed in a clear and transparent manner about the essential aspects of insurance offered to him. This applies mainly to the sale of investment life insurance, a product with which the so-called problem is most associated. Reconfidence. That is why we are introducing a standardised form that should enable clients to find out more easily in insurance offers and to evaluate the benefits of the insurance product. Furthermore, the Act introduces a new standard of professional competence for insurance vendors. While the existing system of requirement for the expertise of insurance distributors is based on formal verification of theoretical knowledge from all insurance sectors, the new law, on the other hand, stresses the need for depth rather than the breadth of the necessary knowledge and the related practical skills that are necessary for qualified negotiations with the customer. Thank you for your attention.
