This term has already been known by Roman law, when the Supreme Court often comes out of this right in its decision making, when it claims that the executive is obliged to exercise his duties with care to a proper economy, which means to behave responsibly, conscientiously, in the same way as if he were caring for his own property, that is, that he does not knowingly reduce it or threaten it in any way, but also to evaluate and reproduce the property to the maximum extent possible that is currently achievable. Czech legal science also accepts such a concept. The law of the Czech Republic has this concept already enshrined in Act No. 90/2012 Coll., on Commercial Corporations. According to this law, a person who acts carefully and with the necessary knowledge deals with the proper care of the economy, with the necessary loyalty. It is considered that he acts carelessly, who is not capable of proper care, even though he had to find out when taking up his duties or performing them, and does not result in consequences for himself. Conceptual attributes of care to a proper economy are carefulness, necessary knowledge, business decisions, good faith, reasonable assumption, informed behaviour, actions in the defendable interest of a business corporation, but do not expect it to be equipped with all the expertise, but for its responsibility is sufficient basic knowledge to identify the threat, damage and prevent it from being caused on managed property.
