ParlaMint-CZ_2018-01-25-ps2017-006-06-002-020.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, this time for myself and for the government, I am submitting an Additional Protocol to the Criminal Code on Corruption to be ratified. The protocol is quite old, it's from May 15, 2003. Of course, we didn't approach him until August 21, 2017, which is a bit of a shame, but we're correcting the shame right now. Its content is simple. In the Corruption Convention itself, Member States undertake to criminalise corruption proceedings against official persons and judges, etc. and this Protocol also extends to the arbitrator. That's all he does. The Czech rule of law is in line with the provisions of the Protocol, ratification will therefore be a political expression of support for this initiative. Thank you for your attention.

As already stated by the Minister, the additional protocol submitted complements the Criminal Code Convention on Corruption, which entered into force on 1 July 2002. This protocol extends the circle of persons for whom it is necessary to criminalise active and passive corruption of domestic and foreign judges and jurors, in our legal order of alternates. Otherwise everything, I think, has been presented to a sufficient degree by the Minister here. Perhaps just for the sake of emphasis - all these criminal acts are already being prosecuted by the criminal code in the Czech Republic today, so from this point of view the protocol submitted in our legal order is already being implemented.
