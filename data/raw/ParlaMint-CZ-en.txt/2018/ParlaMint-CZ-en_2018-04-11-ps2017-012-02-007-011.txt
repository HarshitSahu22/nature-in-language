ParlaMint-CZ_2018-04-11-ps2017-012-02-007-011.u2	Dear Government, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to briefly mention the proposal for the Act amending Act No. 141/1961 Coll., on Criminal Procedure, Criminal Code, as amended. The proposed amendment to the Criminal Code aims to increase the motivation of the entity to report the case of passive corruption. According to the current regulation, cases of passive and active corruption are criminal. In the last parliamentary term, a new amendment to the Criminal Code was adopted by Section 159c, a special provision on the temporary suspension of criminal prosecution, which is helpful to the person who has promised a bribe only because he has been requested by the other person, but not already for those who, when seeking a bribe, were provided by the other person in time and bribes, and immediately thereafter made the notification. There are such cases, too, and there are not few in everyday life. In this way, the law enforcement authorities will not know about these crimes and corruption crime remains unpunished. The proposal for this amendment responds to this. As a description of the target state, we therefore aim to ensure that the state's attorneys derive from § 2 para. 3 of the Code of Criminal Procedure shall be obliged to prosecute any criminal offences which it becomes aware of, unless otherwise provided in the law or declared international treaty. Following the adoption of Section 159c of the Criminal Code, the Attorney General has the opportunity to decide to give priority to the public interest in finding a person requiring a bribe and not to prosecute the person who has promised a bribe. He no longer has that option with the person who provided the bribe, but only because he was asked for it. It is therefore proposed to provide the State's attorney with such a possibility under the conditions met. In general, the aim of the legislation affecting corruption is to protect the purity of public life, impartial and objective procurement of matters of general interest by persons who provide matters of general interest against various forms of corruption, bribery. One way to achieve this objective is by proposing a change in the rule of law in the possibility of motivating the pledge of a briber to notify the law enforcement authorities of a bribed infringement and further working with law enforcement authorities. The proposed change will meet the requirements of practice, since with a longer period of time it turns out that by releasing the former special effective remorse for bribery, Czech criminal law has lost the provisions by which persons have been positively motivated to report corruption and, in part, only the persons who have promised the bribe, not already provided it, which is inadequate. So I guess that's how much the opening report is as a promoter.
