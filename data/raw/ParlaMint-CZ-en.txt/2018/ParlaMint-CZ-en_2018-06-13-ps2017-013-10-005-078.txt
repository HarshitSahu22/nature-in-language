ParlaMint-CZ_2018-06-13-ps2017-013-10-005-078.u2	Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly sum up what concerns our amendment to the Act on the Office of the President of the Republic. Our draft introduces a request for security clearance of the President of the Republic to a top secret level. We have long argued that the Chancellor of the President of the Republic should have security clearance because he is working with information that may pose a security risk to the state in the wrong hands. The office of the President of the Republic is an important and respected position that deserves to be occupied by a trustworthy and tested person who will defend the interests of the state, not his personal. Therefore, my fellow Members, I beg you to support our amendment. Thank you.
