ParlaMint-CZ_2018-02-27-ps2017-007-01-003-004.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce, as part of my second reading, the proposal for an amendment to the Tax Code and some other laws which is being transposed into the legal order of the Czech Republic. Directive DAC 5 or amendment to the European Directive on administrative cooperation in the field of taxation. In order to combat tax evasion more effectively, this Directive requires tax administrators to have access to the collected data collected today by banks and other financial institutions and economic operators in the fight against money laundering. According to Directive DAC 5, these data are to be used by the tax administrator in the framework of the exchange of information in international cooperation in tax administration. In addition, the draft law provides for the collection and use of these data also within the domestic tax administration. The reason for this is the attempt not to make the difference between the domestic and foreign tax authorities regarding access to the information needed to avoid tax evasion. The exception is only data obtained from lawyers and other legal professionals, which can only be obtained for the purposes of international cooperation. In view of the overrun of the transposition period of the DAC 5 Directive, particularly as a result of the delay caused by the elections to the Chamber of Deputies last year, the effectiveness of the law is proposed on the date of its publication in the Collection of Laws. Thank you for your attention.
