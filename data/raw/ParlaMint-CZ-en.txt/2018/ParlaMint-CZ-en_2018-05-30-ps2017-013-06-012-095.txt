Proposal for the election of members of the Supervisory Board of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund On the second ballot point, we supplemented the Supervisory Board of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund. Once again, 167 ballots have been submitted and issued, the quorum required for the election again 84. We selected two candidates and the results are as follows: Petr Havel 52 votes, he was not elected, Monika Oborná 94 votes and was elected, Ladislav Velebný 40 votes, he was not elected, and Jitka Věková 19 votes, nor was she elected. It was the second round of the election, so we took one seat, Mrs Monika Oborná. This means that the election is now ending, and we as the electoral commission are going to call a new deadline for a new election. We have already agreed on a census with colleagues from the electoral committee, so at the moment for club presidents information: I am announcing a new deadline for the addition of one member of the State Agricultural Intervention Fund. The deadline, colleagues and colleagues, is until Monday, June 11th, at 12 o'clock. Then the nomination would be discussed by the electoral commission, the ten-day deadline will pass, and in the last week of June, we could make that choice.
