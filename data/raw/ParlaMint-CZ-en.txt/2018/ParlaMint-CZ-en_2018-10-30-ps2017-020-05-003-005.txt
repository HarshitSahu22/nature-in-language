Government bill amending Act No. 154/2000 Coll., on breeding, breeding and registration of livestock and amending certain related laws (the Breed Act), as amended (the House Press 169/ - second reading) On behalf of the Government, the proposal submitted by the Minister of Agriculture Miroslav Toman. Please, Minister, take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2018-10-30-ps2017-020-05-003-005.u2	Hello, Mr President, honourable Members. Allow me to introduce the Government's amendment to Act No. 154/2000 Coll., on breeding, breeding and registration of livestock. The proposed proposal ensures, in particular, the adaptation of the breeding law to the EU 2016/1012 Regulation on breeding animals. The aim of the Government's draft amendment to the Act is to ensure the conditions for the breeding and breeding of livestock in the Czech Republic following the entry into force of the said Regulation of the European Union, which takes place on 1 January 2005. 11. 2018. The draft amendment to the Act divides the listed livestock into the main animal species regulated by the regulation, i.e. bovine, porcine, ovine, caprine and equine animals and other farmed animals of animal species not listed in this Regulation, i.e. poultry, fish, bees, where the current legislation does not change. Following a regulation of the European Union, provisions relating to recognised breeding associations and pig holdings have been recast. The new regulation allows more breeding books for the same breed to exist in one Member State. Furthermore, the regulation introduces the cross-border functioning of breeding books. Although the evaluation and recognition of breeding books will continue to be carried out by the Ministry of Agriculture, the regulation allows for appeals against Member States' decisions to the European Commission. Due to the introduction of a new zootechnical certificate by a regulation of the European Union, the issue of putting breeding animals into circulation has also been adapted. The draft amendment of the Act has been discussed continuously with the concerned bodies for which the method of adaptation of the EU regulation is acceptable and taking into account the current breeding practice in the field of breeding and breeding of livestock. The Government Bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 15th meeting on 10 October 2018 and recommended by its resolution to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to approve the Government Bill as amended by the amendments adopted. The amendments adopted are in line with the concept of the Ministry of Agriculture and will contribute to improving the government's bill, and I therefore agree with them. Thank you.
