ParlaMint-CZ_2018-06-12-ps2017-013-09-008-055.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, very briefly. This Treaty should allow the mutual transfer of sentenced persons in one State to another, in so far as they are citizens of the other State and if those persons agree to it. The last condition for practicality is that at least one more year of punishment remains to be carried out, so as not to be passed on to the execution of some short periods of punishment. Please support this proposal. Thank you.
