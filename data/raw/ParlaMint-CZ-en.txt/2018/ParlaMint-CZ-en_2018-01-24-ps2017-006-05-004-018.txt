Government bill amending Act No. 56/2001 Coll., on the conditions of operation of vehicles on roads and on the amendment of Act No. 168/1999 Coll., on liability insurance for damage caused by the operation of the vehicle and amending certain related laws (Act on liability insurance from the operation of the vehicle), as amended by Act No. 307/1999 Coll., as amended, and other related laws /house print 51/ - first reading As I have already announced, under the authority of the government, the press will state the Minister of Transport Dan Ťok. Minister, please take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2018-01-24-ps2017-006-05-004-018.u2	Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, the aim of the draft law presented is, in particular, the implementation of European Union law, in particular three directives governing periodic technical inspections of vehicles, road technical checks on commercial vehicles and permissible dimensions and mass of vehicles. The most extensive are the entries into Act No. 56/2001 Coll., which will be the most important change in the inclusion of vehicle emission measurement as one of the control groups in the periodic technical inspection of the vehicle. At the same time, the draft law envisages that emission measurements can also be carried out at the emission measurement station prior to carrying out other checks at the technical inspection station. It shall be up to the vehicle operator to decide whether to carry out a technical inspection only at the technical inspection station or to carry out such inspection at the emission measurement station and subsequently at the technical inspection station. The protocol on the overall result of the technical examination will always be issued by a control technician whose competence will include measurement of emissions. At the same time, however, the existence of a sub-category of control techniques whose authorisation will only be limited to the measurement of emissions will be allowed. The two-wheel system for carrying out technical inspections through technical inspection stations and emission measurement stations will therefore be maintained to some extent, but in a uniform form by the Union. Into Law No. 56/2001 Coll. a modification is added to make speedometer rewinding more difficult and the approval procedure for emission measurement stations is simplified. The adoption of the bill will also achieve the implementation of two European Union regulations governing the approval of vehicles. Into Law No. 13/1997 Coll., on Roads, the proposal comes in particular in the field of control weighing. The adjustments to the permissible masses and dimensions of vehicles also concern entry into Act No. 361/2000 Coll., on Road Traffic, where a conceptual change is proposed to move part of the regulation in this area from the implementing regulation to the legal level. In addition, the Road Traffic Act proposes partial amendments to the regulation of technical road controls. The bill was already prepared in the spring of last year, but in view of the end of the parliamentary term of the previous Chamber of Deputies, its discussions were suspended. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to support the proposed bill, as it is necessary to adopt it as soon as possible, in particular with regard to the obligations arising from the membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. Thank you for your attention.
