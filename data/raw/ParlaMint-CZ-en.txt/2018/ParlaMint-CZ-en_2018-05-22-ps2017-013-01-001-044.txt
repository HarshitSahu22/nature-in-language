ParlaMint-CZ_2018-05-22-ps2017-013-01-001-044.u4	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce a bill which has been drawn up in connection with the fulfilment of the legislative obligations of the Czech Republic arising from its membership of the European Union and amending two laws governing the protection of industrial property, namely the Act on Trade Marks and the Law on the Enforcement of Industrial Property Rights and the Act on Administrative Charges. The proposal submitted is transposed into the Act on Trademarks by Directive No 1 of the European Parliament and of the Council. With a view to simplifying the registration and management of trade marks throughout the European Union, to approximate the main procedural rules in each Member State and to ensure that registered trade marks enjoy the same protection under the laws of all Member States of the European Union. The draft amendment to the Act on Trademarks will in principle ensure the Czech Republic the same conditions for the acquisition and maintenance of the right to a trade mark as contained in the regulation of the trade mark of the whole of the European Union. This will contribute to strengthening the legal certainty of the proprietors of trademarks and other entities concerned, and thus to increasing the competitiveness and commercial growth of individual undertakings. The main amendments to the proposed amendment include, in particular, the introduction of the registration of non-traditional trademarks, such as sound, the strengthening of the status of the owner in the enforcement of the rights to its trade marks, the introduction of the certification mark protection, the specification of the requirements of the trade mark application and the determination of the obligation of the Industrial Property Office to inform its proprietor before the expiry of the trade mark. [[16.00 hours]] The proposal in question is further incorporated into the Law on the enforcement of industrial property rights in some of the provisions of Directive No. 20/943 on the protection of undisclosed know-how and commercial information against unauthorised acquisition, use and disclosure of such information. A major part of that directive has already been transposed into the Czech legal system, but, in view of the need to ensure full compliance with this Union regulation, it is necessary to include in it the provisions relating to the right of the owner of the business secret to pay compensation for the breach of his business secrets. The proposal also includes an amendment to the Act on Administrative Fees to which the regulation of administrative charges levied by the Industrial Property Office and the amount of those administrative fees remaining retained is transferred from the Act on Trademarks.
