ParlaMint-CZ_2018-06-13-ps2017-013-10-006-081.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me give you the following point, which is the draft law No. 311/2013 Coll., on the transfer of ownership to units and group family houses of some housing cooperatives and on the amendment of certain laws. I would just like to begin by knowing that we are already at the third reading, I would like to bring the overall proposal of the amendment and its original objective. Law No 311/2013 Sb. in paragraph 24, the advantage of the so-called deferred establishment of the owners' associations for certain housing cooperatives, but not for all such cooperatives, but only for those who started transferring apartments before 1. 7. 2000 and at the same time started with the administration of common parts. This advantage was part of the already repealed Housing Property Act, but in a somewhat broader form. According to the annulled regulation, the ex lege community was formed when the cooperative's share in the common parts fell below one quarter. Until then, the cooperative had been acting as administrator and decided on common house management issues, although it had already had a share in common parts of less than one half. On the basis of the amendment, the new law adopted an amendment in 2013, according to which the mandatory formation of SVJ after the new regulation was effective was linked to a reduction in the share of the housing cooperative in the common parts of the house instead of the originally established threshold of a decline below one quarter of the decline already below one half. However, this led to the fact that the legal regulation did not fall on the cooperatives where the cooperative's share fell below one half before the law was effective, but did not fall below one quarter when the community would have been formed under the regulation contained in the repealed Housing Property Act. Thus, the original aim of the proposed amendment was to eliminate the lack of which I had just mentioned and to apply the legislation under Section 24 also to those housing cooperatives where the co-ownership share of the common parts of the house decreased to less than one half before the date of application of the Civil Code and this special law. Secondly, the aim of this law is to clearly determine for what number of votes the members of the units are eligible for a meeting under this law. The proposal was discussed in the Constitutional Legal Committee, which recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the proposal in the submitted text. The proposal was also discussed in the Sub-Committee on Housing Policy and the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development. In addition to the proposal submitted on the initiative of the Association of Czech and Moravian Housing Cooperatives, the questions of repayment of loans drawn by housing cooperatives for repairs, modernisation and reconstruction of common parts were discussed here, at a time when the house was already divided into units owned by other owners. The following amendments were tabled at second reading following the examination of the bill. Amendment A1 identical to Amendment B2, according to which, on the date of the creation of the SVJ, debts from outstanding loans drawn by housing cooperatives as representatives of individual owners are transferred directly from the law to the SVJ, subject to those owners to whom the obligation to pay their debts did not pass, they are also excluded from liability for these debts. By adopting this proposal, however, there could be a situation that the owners of units who, for whatever reason, could have doubts that they were obliged to pay off the loan could have been invoking litigation and thus endangering the repayment of the loan, which would have thus remained liable only to those members to whom the apartments have not yet been transferred. For these reasons, I am actually going to ask here to disagree with proposals A1 and B2. For this reason, precisely because of the problems of repayment of loans, Amendment B4 was prepared which extends the period for the compulsory establishment of a community of owners of the units until a loan for the repair and modernisation of houses divided into units which drew on the housing cooperative was paid up, but until the share of the housing cooperative in the common parts falls below one quarter. The adoption of this proposal will leave a solution to individual cases arising from housing cooperatives' situations, as the general adjustment does not provide for a satisfactory adjustment of all the possibilities that may arise in practice when these loans are repaid. I agree with this proposal, i.e. proposal B4, for the Ministry of Regional Development. And with the third amendments, when B1 is the same as A2 which addresses the effectiveness of the law, and B3 which extends the time limit for the assembly to establish a community of owners of units, I also agree. I would therefore like to conclude by summing up that, for the reasons set out here as the petitioner, I support the amendments identified as B1, B3 and B4. Thank you for your attention.
