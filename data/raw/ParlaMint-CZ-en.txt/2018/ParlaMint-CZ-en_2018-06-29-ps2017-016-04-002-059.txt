The aim of the proposed bill is, in particular, the implementation of the law of the European Union, in particular the three directives governing periodic technical inspections of vehicles, road technical checks on commercial vehicles and permissible dimensions and masses of vehicles. The most extensive are the entries into Act No. 56/2001 Coll., which will be the most important change in the inclusion of vehicle emission measurement as one of the control groups in the periodic technical inspection of the vehicle. At the same time, the draft law envisages that emission measurements can also be carried out at the emission measurement station prior to carrying out other checks at the technical inspection station. It shall be up to the vehicle operator to decide whether to carry out a technical inspection only at the technical inspection station or to carry out such inspection at the emission measurement station and subsequently at the technical inspection station. The protocol on the overall result of the technical examination will always be issued by a control technician whose competence will include measurement of emissions. At the same time, however, the existence of a sub-category of control techniques whose authorisation will be limited to the measurement of emissions will be allowed. The two-wheel system for carrying out technical inspections through technical inspection stations and emission measurement stations will therefore be maintained to some extent, but in a comfort form by union.
