Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Protocol on amendments made to the Agreement establishing the International Investment Bank and its Statutes, agreed on 8 May 2014 in Havana and signed by the Czech Republic on 3 December 2015 in Hanoi /German press 895/ - first reading The proposed proposal will be presented by Richard Brabec, the Minister of the Environment, as Vice-President of the Government and Minister of Finance Andrej Babiš, whose mandate by the Government is present. Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-08-ps2013-049-03-011-206.u2	Thank you for the third time. Mr President, colleagues, the material presented by the Ministry of Finance today is a proposal for ratification of the Protocol on amendments made to the Agreement establishing the International Investment Bank and its Statutes, agreed in Havana on 8 May 2014 and signed by the Czech Republic on 3 December 2015 in Hanoi. The international investment bank was founded in 1970 and then Czechoslovakia was one of the founding members. The Czech Republic then succededed to the Agreement on the Establishment of the International Investment Bank. At present, the share of the Czech Republic in the paid-up capital is EUR 30.37 million, which together with the international rating makes the Czech Republic one of the important shareholders of the bank. The need for a change in the existing incorporation documents of the International Investment Bank has resulted from its dynamic development over the last three years. The aim of the amendments to the statutory documents is to complete the conversion of the International Investment Bank into a modern international financial institution. The main changes to the Protocol concern the introduction of a three-level management system, i.e. the Governing Council, the Board of Directors and the Board of Directors of the Bank, the structure of the core capital and, in particular, the introduction of a proportional vote on the basis of the shares in the bank's paid-up capital. The Protocol was signed by the Czech Republic on the basis of a Government resolution, subject to articles governing the privileges and immunities of representatives and representatives of this institution. The reservation was made because of the excessive scope of the immunity and reservations. After the signature, the reservation was confirmed by the Government's order of 2 May 2016. The agreement of the protocol will not have a direct impact on the state budget and the content of the protocol is, after reservation of the Czech Republic, in accordance with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic. As this is an amendment to the international agreement of the presidential category, I am now asking you to give your consent to the ratification of the protocol and to confirm the Czech Republic's reservations as stated in the draft resolution. Thank you.
