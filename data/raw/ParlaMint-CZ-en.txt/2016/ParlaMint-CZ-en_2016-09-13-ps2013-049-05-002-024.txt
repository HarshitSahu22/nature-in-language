The second key point of the amendment to the Act on Medicines is, as I have already said, the modification of the possibility of restrictions. reexports of medicinal products from the Czech Republic abroad. The purpose of the proposed measure is to ensure that due to economic interest on the part of distributors of medicines, there is no shortage of medicinal products for the needs of patients in the Czech Republic. A system of monitoring and evaluation of the situation on the market for medicinal products is in place in the Gesture of the State Institute for Drug Control. Those medicinal products for which it will be found that due to their volume on the Czech market, their deficiency could occur in the event of a re-export, will be listed by the Ministry of Health and will be obliged for distributors to notify their intended distribution abroad to the State Institute for Drug Control. I repeatedly stress that anchoring the system of restrictions or bans so-called. the return of medicinal products is not contrary to European law and the principle of free movement of goods.
