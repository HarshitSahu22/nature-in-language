Ladies and gentlemen, Members of the Government, allow me to inform you of the meeting of the mandate and immunity committee on the matter of the consent of the Chamber of Deputies to prosecute Mr Štěpán Stupčuk. On 29 April 2016, the President of the Chamber of Deputies received a request from the Regional Directorate of the Police of the Central Bohemia Region, the Regional Department of the Prague West, for the approval of the Chamber of Deputies to prosecute Mr Štěpán Stupčuk. On the same day, the application was forwarded to the secretariat of the mandate and the Immune Committee and all its members had the opportunity to get acquainted with the file material. Facts and expected legal qualifications were included in the police's request. The mandate and immune committee discussed the police's request at its 38th and 39th meetings which took place on 5 May this year. Mr Stupčuk, who had the opportunity to comment personally on the police's request, asked, at the end of his speech before the members of the mandate and the Immune Committee, to be prosecuted for the actions defined in the police's request. After examining whether the conditions for restricting immunity of a Member of the Chamber of Deputies were laid down, the mandate and immunity committee adopted Resolution 159 at its 39th meeting, which was distributed to you, and the opinion of the committee on the police's request, Mr Roman Procházka will also inform you of the rapporteur of the mandate and immunity committee. So much for my brief report on the discussion of the request of the Police of the Czech Republic for the consent of the House to the criminal extradition of Mr Štěpán Stupčuk by the mandate and immune committee. Thank you for your attention.
