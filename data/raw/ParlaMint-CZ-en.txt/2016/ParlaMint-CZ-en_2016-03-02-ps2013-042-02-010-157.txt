ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-02-ps2013-042-02-010-157.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to say briefly that the aim of the proposed bill is, in particular, to extend the scope of the activities which the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure can finance from its budget, particularly in order to make the process of multi-source financing of transport infrastructure more effective. This extension of the Fund's purpose will extend the range of quality projects to which EU funds can be drawn, resulting in a more efficient drawdown of these funds. The total amount of funds managed by the Fund will not be affected by the proposed amendment to the legislation. After discussing the proposal at second reading, the meeting of the Economic Committee was resumed as a committee of guarantee, which assessed the amendments applied and recommended the method of voting on the third reading amendments.
