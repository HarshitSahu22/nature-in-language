ParlaMint-CZ_2016-01-19-ps2013-039-01-004-003.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, let me present a proposal for a law amending the law on the recognition of professional qualifications and other competences of members of the Member States of the European Union and of certain members of other States and on the amendment of certain laws. On 15 December 2015, at first reading, you agreed with the Government's draft law. Since this is a transposition amendment, the deadline has been reduced to 30 days. I can only reiterate that the main objective is to ensure full harmonisation of national rules on the recognition of professional qualifications, as well as the issue of a European professional licence and a warning mechanism, which will guarantee greater flexibility and functionality of the current recognition process in individual Member States. In view of the transposition deadline, the deadline has been reduced to 30 days, which is why I would like to continue defending this government proposal. On 14 January 2016, the proposal was discussed by the Committee on Science, Education, Culture, Youth and Sports, which recommended that the Chamber of Deputies agree to this proposal. At the same time, the rapporteur, Mr František Vácha, has been read by amendments which are of a legislative technical nature and respond to the development of the legal order and the laws in question during the examination of the proposal. I give my consent to these proposals in advance. As regards the amendment concerning the amendment of the law on municipal police, the written amendment 3402, I cannot, unfortunately, give my consent to this, because the amendment of the law on municipal police is not part of the Government's draft law and, given the requirement of Czech citizenship for municipal police officers, it is in no way a regulated profession which is therefore remotely unrelated to the free movement of persons and the proposed transposition proposal. I thank you in advance for the debate which will then be held. Thank you.
