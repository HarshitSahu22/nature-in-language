ParlaMint-CZ_2016-07-13-ps2013-048-06-001-154.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the subject of the draft Railway Act is, in particular, to ensure the transposition of the European Directive on the creation of a single European railway area. This proposal has already been presented and discussed in detail both at the plenary of the Chamber of Deputies and in the Economic Committee and the subcommittee on Transport. Allow me, then, only a brief introduction. The submitted government proposal intervenes in a number of areas of the Railway Act, either in a partial or in a coherent manner. The most important areas of the amendment concerned include the categorization of runways and the definition of access to different categories of runways, in particular lifts; new arrangements for the operation of service facilities governing the rights and obligations of operators of such facilities and carriers using services therein; a new version of the amendment to the licensing of rail transport operations; a new adjustment to the allocation of railway capacity, including the possibility of assessing the risk to the economic equilibrium of rail transport operated under a public service contract; fifth adjustment of the scope and procedure of rail inspection in the emergency investigation. In addition to the above-mentioned topics, the proposal for a change also includes administrative punishment and the organisation of public administrations in the field of railways, in particular in the context of the proposed establishment of the Office for Access to Transport Infrastructure. Furthermore, some partial national adjustments, in particular in the field of felling of trees along the tracks and the construction of railways. A number of amendments have been tabled to the proposal. The opinion of the Ministry of Transport on them is in principle identical to that of the Guarantee Economic Committee, with two exceptions. Unlike the Economic Committee, the Ministry of Transport does not support the adoption of the amendment by Mrs Matušovská, referred to as C5. This amendment inserts a new § 46s into the Railway Act containing, in particular, the obligation of the railway operator and the carrier to use certain categories of staff accredited by the Ministry of Transport when training certain categories of persons. In our view, this adjustment will not lead to an increase in traffic safety on the runways and will increase the costs of railway operators and carriers. By way of derogation from the Economic Committee, the Ministry of Transport also does not support the adoption of a set of amendments by Mr Birke marked with the letter H. It is a set of entries into the Act on Railways and the Act on Nature Conservation and Landscapes, which aim to remove felling of trees in the vicinity of the railway from the scope of the Act on Nature Conservation and Landscapes, i.e. from the competence of nature and landscape conservation authorities. There is certainly no dispute here that the existing pre-approval regime for the cutting of trees around the track is not appropriate, it is time-consuming and administratively demanding and makes the operational response more difficult in the event of a threat. However, the Government's proposal in this area contains changes that change the cutting regime from the authorisation to the notification and, at the same time, significantly strengthen the competence of the railway office and the safety aspects of the railway operation. These are changes agreed and agreed by the Ministry of Transport and the Ministry of Environment. They are the result of quite extensive discussions and let me ask you to support the wording of the Government proposal in this area. In others, the opinions of the Ministry of Transport on amendments are identical to those of the Guarantee Economic Committee. The honourable Members, the proposal put forward by the Government to the Chamber of Deputies, is the result of long and very hard work, numerous negotiations with the bodies concerned and public authorities, and is necessary in terms of the implementation of European Union law, but also contains solutions to national problems in the field of rail transport. So let me ask you to accept it. Thank you.
