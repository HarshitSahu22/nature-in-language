The relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Seychelles in the field of civil aviation are not yet regulated by a valid international agreement. On the basis of the interest of both parties, an expert meeting was held in December 2012, resulting in a mutually acceptable text of the Agreement. The Government of the Czech Republic agreed to the agreement by its resolution in September 2013. The agreement was signed this June. The text of the Agreement fully corresponds to current developments in civil aviation. It is in line with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, including obligations arising from membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. The negotiation of this agreement does not require any changes in the Czech legal order and will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. The agreement represents one way to further develop relations between the two countries. The air carriers of both parties also offer a wide range of possibilities for commercial use. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic for the referral of material for discussion in the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
