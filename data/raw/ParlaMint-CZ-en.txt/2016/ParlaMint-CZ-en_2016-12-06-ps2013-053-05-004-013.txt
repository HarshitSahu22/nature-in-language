ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-06-ps2013-053-05-004-013.u2	Hello again. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly remind you of the proposal for an amendment to the Act on Public Health Insurance Insurance. The main objective of the proposed bill is to adapt the existing system of redistributing insurance premiums to public health insurance by re-taking into account the actual costs of health insurance companies for the treatment of chronically ill insured persons whose treatment is associated with higher consumption of medicinal products. The current model of redistribution of insurance premiums, taking into account, in principle, only the age and sex of insured persons, will be further enriched or, say, extended to include insured persons in so-called pharmaceutical-cost groups, or Pharmacy-based Cost Groups, in short, PCG, as you all probably call this law. Given that the launch of this new redistribution system for 2017 would no longer be possible, we are prepared to support the amendment by the Committee on Health, which postpones the effectiveness of the proposal by one calendar year while updating the coefficients contained in the transitional provisions so that they do not come from the data for 2013, but from the data for the previous year. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, in line with the Government's programming statement, which I believe will ensure a fairer distribution of insurance premiums among health insurance companies, depending on the cost of individual insured persons, and I therefore really believe that you will support it in the legislative process, and thank you very much for your attention.
