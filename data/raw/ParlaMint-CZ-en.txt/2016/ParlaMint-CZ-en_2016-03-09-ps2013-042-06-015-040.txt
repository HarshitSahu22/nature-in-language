Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-09-ps2013-042-06-015-040.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce the draft accompanying law to the customs law. The subject matter of this proposal is primarily the changes made by the Union Customs Code and by the Customs Act in other related laws. Most of these changes have a terminology and legislative technical character. As part of changes to the Act on Financial and Customs Administration of the Czech Republic, important related measures are proposed to strengthen the fight against tax evasion and to increase the efficiency of the activities of financial and customs offices. First of all, the introduction of the national territorial competence of these authorities to carry out tax control. This will allow for a more optimal use of their capacities while at the same time carrying out checks on tax entities involved in carousel fraud from one place. [[17.50 hours]] The second amendment constitutes an extension of the police competence of the customs authorities so that they can verify the commission of VAT offences. This will improve and strengthen the fight against tax evasion in the field of criminal sanctions. Part of this bill is already the amendment of the VAT Act, in which it is proposed, among other things, to introduce an institute of remission of fines, which will cover fines of CZK 10,000, CZK 30,000 and CZK 50,000, and the institute of exclusion of fines relating to fines of CZK 1,000. Thanks to the waiver, it will be possible to take into account the justified reasons for delay in submitting the inspection report, in which this adjustment will also apply to cases which occurred before the proposed amendment is effective. In the case of a fine of CZK 1000, one error for the calendar year in question will be tolerated. The tax administrator shall take this into account automatically without the need to submit an application. In addition, fines of this amount resulting from the amendment proposed will cease to be effective by law and thus a certain tolerance transitional period will be set back up. The general effectiveness of the Act is proposed in the same way as the Customs Act on 1 May 2016. In conclusion, it will be proposed to shorten the deadline for discussing the draft law in committees to 30 days. Thank you for your attention.
