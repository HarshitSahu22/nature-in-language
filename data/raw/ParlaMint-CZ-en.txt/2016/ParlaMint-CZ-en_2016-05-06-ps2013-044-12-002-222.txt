The Czech National Bank closed in 2015, with a loss of CZK 549 million, equity fell to a positive value of CZK 22 billion 850 million, the number of employees of the Czech National Bank was 1 352 at the end of last year and the total balance sheet total of the Czech National Bank was CZK 1 trillion 628 billion. ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-06-ps2013-044-12-002-222.u4	Mr President, Prime Minister, honourable Members, I would just like to point out a few things that might interest you. Firstly, the share of the increase in reserves of 16.5 billion contributed to nine billion interventions last year, but there was also an increase of EUR 4 billion due to a positive balance of external flows in connection with the drawing of funds from European Union funds, or money from the Union. On the contrary, in liabilities, this was reflected in the commitment to banks, to the State and to public institutions, mostly to the Treasury and, of course, the issue of banknotes and coins. While the world is debating whether the banknotes are leaving, last year the volume of banknotes and coins increased by 9 percent. The impact of the economic result on the Czech National Bank's balance sheet was negligible. Let me just remind you that we managed negative capital minus 140, and at this point we have positive capital. The capital of the central bank is strictly separated from public budgets and has no impact on public budgets in this respect. As far as management is concerned, it can be divided into three basic areas: monetary, issue and management of currency and traffic. I have already talked about monetary operations, perhaps I would like to pick up that the management of foreign reserve assets in the monetary field contributed net returns of CZK 17.3 billion last year, an annual increase of 18 percent. The fact that we have been holding 10 percent of our reserves in stock indices for several years helped us most last year means that stock indices have brought about 42 percent of our revenues. On the contrary, the fact that we live in a world in which central banks have zero or negative returns is starting to manifest itself, which means that it is very difficult to place them. As far as the issue and management of currency are concerned, I have already said that our banknotes have increased in circulation, but in terms of production we have reduced the number of banknotes purchased, we are buying from the State price printer, and coins by 5%, which reflected in the lower cost of its acquisition. In terms of revenues, there are the dominant items revenue from the sale of numismatic material, or gold and silver coins, 430 million. In traffic, I would like to point out that our operating costs have increased by one percent. The most significant part of operating costs is labour costs, almost 70 per cent. We've also grown one percent. The low dynamic reflects, on the one hand, the fact that we have narrowed down the labour force for a long time, but also that last year we did not have to make any major restructuring action, in other words, we did not have to pay our severance payments. On the contrary, I must say that last year our workforce did not decrease. I said here last year that we have historically the lowest number of employees. We are still increasing our employees in consumer supervision and protection, and that is the biggest reason why our employees grew 31 to 1,352. The rest of the operating costs are becoming more and more repairs to buildings that are just getting old. In conclusion, I would like to say that the negative economic result will be left in the balance sheet. In line with the long-term financial management principles, we will pay off this loss from the CNB's future profits. Of course, in view of such a negative result, neither the reserve fund nor the contribution of free profits to the state budget was made. The equity decreased by 3.5 billion to a positive value of CZK 22.85 billion. I will allow myself to end this and, of course, I will be very happy to answer all your questions.
