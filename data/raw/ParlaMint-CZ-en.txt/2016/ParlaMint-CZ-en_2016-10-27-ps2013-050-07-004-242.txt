ParlaMint-CZ_2016-10-27-ps2013-050-07-004-242.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, let me introduce you to the material entitled Report on the situation in the area of migration and integration of foreigners in the Czech Republic for 2015. The submitted report introduces us to the situation in the field of legal, illegal migration, international protection and integration of foreigners in the Czech Republic just in 2015, compared to the previous year, possibly with other flights, as the analysis is designed. Last but not least, this report also deals with the criminal activity of foreigners in the Czech Republic. This is a topic that has already been discussed at the first point. For the sake of completeness, the material is also marginally affected by the situation in the area of migration at European Union level last year. On 5 October the report was discussed and took note of its resolution by the Security Committee of the Chamber of Deputies. From the facts mentioned in the report, the situation in the area of migration and integration of foreigners in the Czech Republic in 2015 despite increasing the number of illegally transiting persons did not make significant changes compared to developments in previous and migratory security situations in the Czech Republic can be assessed as stable in 2015. As of 31. 12. 2015 had a permanent residence of 467 562 foreigners on the territory of the Czech Republic, which is an increase of 3,5 percent The most are the citizens of Ukraine, which is about 106 thousand, Slovakia, which is 101 thousand, Vietnam, which is about 56 thousand. The cizinec thus accounted for approximately 4.4 percent of the population in the Czech Republic. As of 31. 8. 2016 was allowed to stay 485 195 foreigners in the Czech Republic. During illegal migration in the Czech Republic, 8 563 persons were secured in 2015. This is a year-on-year increase of 77 percent. The most is the citizens of Syria, which was 2 016, Ukraine 1 256 and Kuwait 591. In January to September 2016, 4 146 persons, 3 966 persons for illegal residence were provided for illegal migration. Compared to the previous year, it is a decline of 3 055 people, i.e. the years 2016 and 2015 among themselves. From January to October 10, 2016, 471 cases of illegally transiting migrants were secured, including Afghánec 138, Syřan 117, Iráčan 74. Statistics of asylum seekers in the Czech Republic remained relatively low in 2015. 1 525 applications were submitted, of which Ukrajinec 694, Syřan 134, Kubánec 128. From January to 30 September 2016 1 128 people requested asylum in the Czech Republic, of which 382 Ukrajinec, 143 Iráčan, 70 Kubánec and also 70 Syřan. This is a permanent condition, virtually. This is a slight increase of 13 people compared to 2015, when it was 1 115 people. Thank you for your attention.
