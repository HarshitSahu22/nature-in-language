Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-01-ps2013-042-01-008-008.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, I will be very brief. Let me just remind you that the aim of the amendment is the regulation of the storage of CO2 in natural rock structures and that this legislation needs to be adapted to European legislation so that the objectives to which the Czech Republic has committed itself are met in the context of the management of the so-called. EU Pilot, so as not to initiate infringement. To this end, it is proposed to remedy the shortcomings of implementation, which consist in the incorrect implementation or failure to implement certain provisions of the relevant Directive of the European Union, in particular by introducing relevant definitions, by inserting a new paragraph in Section 5 so that the introductory sentence of Annex 1 to the CCS Directive is correctly implemented and to amend the clause in Section 7 below. I would therefore like to stress that this is a purely technical amendment which responds to the relevant EU Pilot management, in which it was decided to implement Article 3 (2), (4), (15) 16, 20 and 21, Article 11 (2) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. 2 and Annex 1 to the CCS Directive, so that their implementation into the Czech legal order is in accordance with European law and in the opinion of the Commission. Thank you.
