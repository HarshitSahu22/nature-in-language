ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-08-ps2013-049-03-017-186.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Colleagues, colleagues, this time a little bit of my garden, although I don't think I'd want metamidofos in my garden. So let me present the Government's proposal to agree to ratify the amendment to Annex 3 to the Rotterdam Convention on the Prior Consent Procedure for Certain Dangerous Chemicals and Pesticides. Because it's a second reading, I'm really going to limit myself to basic repetition. The amendment to Annex 3 is made for the inclusion of a new chemical, namely a pesticide metamidophos, and in accordance with the Directive concerning the Member States of the European Union. However, these countries, including the Czech Republic, have not used this substance since 30 June 2007. However, as a Contracting Party to the Convention, the Czech Republic has undertaken to fulfil its obligations for the Czech Republic and therefore to include this new chemical in Annex 3. It is such an important measure for the fulfilment of the objective of the Convention. The acceptance of this substance in Annex 3 to the Convention will have no impact on the public administration, business sector or the state budget of the Czech Republic. Thank you for your attention.
