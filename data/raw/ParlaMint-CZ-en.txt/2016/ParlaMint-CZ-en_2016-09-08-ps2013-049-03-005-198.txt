Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and Turkmenistan on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income and property taxes signed in Ashchabad on 18 March 2016 /German press 805/ - first reading I ask the Prime Minister and Finance Minister Andrej Babiš to give the opening statement. Please, Minister.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-08-ps2013-049-03-005-198.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, at the meeting of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic, a double taxation treaty is tabled with Turkmenistan signed on 18 March 2016 in Ashchabad. This treaty certainly responds to the internal tax rules of both countries, while it was agreed on the basis of OECD and UN model models at expert level. Thus, no such treaty is currently applied in the mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and Turkmenistan, and therefore undesirable international double taxation may arise. As a standard, the Treaty provides for basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of both countries, as well as the principle of non-discrimination, informal resolution of disputes arising from its implementation, and ensures the relevant exchange of information between the competent authorities of the two States. The negotiated contract merely regulates the obligations arising from national rules and, at the same time, cannot create a new tax obligation. They deal with cases where the right to levy tax is enshrined in their national law by both signatory States. In these situations, the treaty is certainly superior to the national regulation. This contract is important for our business entities. Turkmenistan is one of the important states of Central Asian space. It has considerable mineral wealth and a certain potential for its economy, and it can be assumed that the status of Turkmenistan as one of our trading partners can become significant over time. The Double Taxation Treaty will certainly increase the legal certainty of potential investors in both states. On the basis of the above, I propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take into account those facts and order this agreement at first reading to the relevant House Committees. Thank you for your attention.
