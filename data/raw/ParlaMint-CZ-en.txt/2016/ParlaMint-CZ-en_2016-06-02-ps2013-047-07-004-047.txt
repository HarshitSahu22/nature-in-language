ParlaMint-CZ_2016-06-02-ps2013-047-07-004-047.u2	Thank you. Now, allow me to introduce a government bill amending Law No. 156/1998 Coll., on fertilisers, soil aids, auxiliary plant preparations and substrates, and on agrochemical testing of agricultural soils, or Act on fertilisers, as amended. The proposed law removes the overlap between the definition of an auxiliary substance under the Fertilizer and Plant Protection Products Act under the Plant Health Care Act. The Czech Republic committed itself to this amendment in 2014 in the framework of proceedings for infringement of the TFEU. The aim is to stop the proceedings and thus avoid any action before the European Court of Justice. The process of notification of mutually recognised fertilisers is also newly regulated. prior notification. Some other EU Member States, such as Germany, France, Spain, are also in place in their legal system in the proposed version of the notice. More than five years of experience with the mutual recognition system has proved its justification in the field of fertiliser trade. However, from the point of view of the supervisory authority, there are several significant shortcomings which are to be eliminated by the proposed law. The contents of the authorisation for the registration of fertilisers have been rephrased in particular for the purpose of clearly defining the conditions under which the fertiliser will be put into circulation. It was added that even in case of a change in the label or package leaflet, an amendment to the marketing authorisation should be requested. The label or package leaflet of fertilizer will newly contain information that will provide the consumer with more detailed information on the product. A new provision is also proposed which lays down the conditions under which liquid fertilisers and auxiliary substances on agricultural parcels may be applied by air. The proposed entry into force on 31 August 2016 is subject to obligations arising from negotiations between representatives of the Czech Republic and the European Commission in the resolution of disputes concerning nitrophenolates. Thank you for your attention.
