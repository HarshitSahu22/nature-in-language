ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-14-ps2013-044-03-003-148.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr Vice-President, Members, this Government proposal regulates cross-border cooperation in the field of medical rescue services. This cross-border cooperation has so far been regulated only in the event of disaster and emergency assistance. The logical and fundamental motive for establishing this cross-border cooperation is in particular the temporal aspect of the availability of the medical rescue service in the border area. Cooperation on the Czech side will touch the region of South Bohemia, the Region of Vysočina and the region of South Moravia, in Austria the federal countries of Upper and Lower Austria. In discussing the text of this Treaty, a similar contract with the Federal Republic of Germany served as a model. The real fulfilment of this cooperation will be the decision of the regions. Separate cross-border cooperation arrangements are envisaged. I believe we all perceive that this is a very useful and practical thing and can literally save lives on both sides of the border. Thank you.
