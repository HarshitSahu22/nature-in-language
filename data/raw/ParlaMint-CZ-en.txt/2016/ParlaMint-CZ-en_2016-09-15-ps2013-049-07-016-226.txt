Dear colleagues, I present the material of the Government's decision on flights and transits of armed forces of other states through the territory of the Czech Republic in 2016, which the Government of the Czech Republic took note of by its resolution of 14 December 2015. By its resolution, the Government authorised the crossings and transits of the armed forces of the European Union States which ratified the Treaty of The European Defence Agency. Furthermore, it has authorised flights and transits to the armed forces of the Member States of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and States participating in the Partnership for Peace programme. Last but not least, the countries with which the Czech Republic maintains a partnership. The permission to fly over will also apply to the armed forces of other states, but only provided consent from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Thank you for your attention.
