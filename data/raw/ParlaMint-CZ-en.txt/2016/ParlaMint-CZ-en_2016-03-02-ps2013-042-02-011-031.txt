October - The Day of Creation of the Independent Czechoslovak State, December 25 - Christmas Day and December 26 - Christmas Day. On Christmas Eve, as I said before, it would be forbidden to sell from 12 to 24 hours. It is proposed to prohibit sales only in seven days of the year. Thus, we do not go the way of some surrounding states where sales on Sundays and on selected holidays are prohibited. This restriction would not apply to stores whose sales area does not exceed 200 m2, it would not concern service stations, pharmacies, shops at airports, train and bus stations, shops in medical facilities. The ban would not apply when a state of danger, an emergency, a state of emergency or a state of war has been declared. The restriction would also apply to pawnshops, waste collections and establishments dealing with used goods, irrespective of the size of the sales area. The economic impact that we have often dealt with on your committees is difficult to quantify. It can be assumed that sales from these seven days will shift either to smaller sellers or to other working days. Thus, there might not be a tax blackout. But the merchants themselves are unable to estimate the impact it could have on their sales. In addition, higher revenues in tourism and other leisure services can be assumed. The social impact will be very favourable. Free days will not only be for employees of the affected shops, but also for other people opportunities for more intensive family life, for joint trips, cultural events, sports and other activities. The proposed solution is in accordance with the constitutional order of the Czech Republic, it does not contradict the international agreements by which the Czech Republic is bound.
