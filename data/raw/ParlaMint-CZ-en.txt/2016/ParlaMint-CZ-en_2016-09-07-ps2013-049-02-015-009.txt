ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-07-ps2013-049-02-015-009.u6	Thank you. I would like to sign up for the Dawn Parliamentary Club under number 4837. Just a quick show. The amendment is intended to prohibit increases in income taxes in the event of the activation of the budgetary constitution. It is primarily based on the fact that the economy of the state, which is so indebted that it is necessary for it to start a fiscal brake mechanism, is in no way able to bear further tax increases. While raising tax rates in the short term could increase tax collection, in the long run it will only lead to a reduction in economic activity and thus a decrease in tax collection. By doing so, the petitioners want to achieve that, if the fiscal constitution is activated, the government will not take the easiest step, which is blunt tax increases, but that it will start to implement corrective structural steps that will lead to a long-term healthy development of public finances. At the same time, I want to declare that this amendment is without prejudice to the general competence of the Government to increase income taxes and that the restrictions resulting from this proposal apply only to the actions of the government imposed by the fiscal brake. Thank you.
