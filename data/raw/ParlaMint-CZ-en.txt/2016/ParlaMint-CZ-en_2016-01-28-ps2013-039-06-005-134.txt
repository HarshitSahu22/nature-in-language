ParlaMint-CZ_2016-01-28-ps2013-039-06-005-134.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, a contractual document relating to international taxation is presented to you for discussion. In this particular case, it is the protocol of an existing double taxation treaty with Kazakhstan signed in Almaty on 9 April 1998 and entered into force on 29 October 1999. This contract was announced in the Czech Republic in the Collection of International Treaties under number 3/2000. The protocol will be an integral part of it. For your information, this protocol was discussed by the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic last May and agreed to its ratification. This Protocol confirms that both Kazakhstan and the Czech Republic declare their readiness to exchange information in the context of taxes of all kinds and naming, even whether it is information held by banks or other financial institutions or whether it is information relating to the ownership of different entities. The protocol therefore ensures, in particular, an extension of the possibilities of exchange of information in the scope of all taxes, such as VAT, which is currently not covered under the contract. It should also be stressed that the text of the Protocol fully reflects international developments and trends in the international tax field, particularly in the framework of EU and OECD work. This contractual document will contribute to better coordination of the activities of the tax authorities of both states in order to limit possible tax evasion. It will certainly also contribute to the smooth implementation of the contract through further adaptations to its text, e.g. the issue of still establishment, dividends, interest, etc. Therefore, the text of the existing 1999 contract is updated to better reflect current relations between the Czech Republic and Kazakhstan. On the basis of what has already been said, I propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take account of these facts when discussing this protocol on the international treaty and agree to its ratification. Thank you for your attention.
