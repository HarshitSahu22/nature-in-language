ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-06-ps2013-049-01-004-004.u2	Madam President, honourable Members, it is certainly not necessary to present the draft law at this point, because the Chamber of Deputies has dealt with this proposal in detail and has been presented in many discussions. The Senate discussed the bill in August, returning it to the Chamber of Deputies with several amendments. The Senate's amendments are in part of a technical nature when gaps or inaccuracies are removed at several design points. I support the adoption of these amendments to the Senate. In particular, for the training of railway and infrastructure personnel under Section 46s, which was included in the proposal in the House, it is necessary to solve the issue of the administrative accreditation fee for the provision of education for its smooth application. Another set of amendments concerns the area of ensuring non-discriminatory access of the carrier on the train. Here the Senate proposes to add to the list of cases where the train operator is obliged to provide the carrier with non-discriminatory access to the train, even if the carrier has to use the train to access equipment that does not belong to the owners or the train operator. This proposal does not significantly alter the content of the Government's proposal or go against the principles contained therein, which is why I support this proposal. It mainly concerns the situation on the network of OKD trains. Ladies and gentlemen, as I have already said, the Senate's amendments do not significantly interfere with the content of the Government's proposal, remove some of the partial shortcomings arising from the proposal and further complement the adjustment of non-discriminatory access to trains. I support the adoption of the Senate's amendments for the Ministry of Transport. So let me ask you to approve the Government's draft law amending the Railway Act, as amended by the amendments adopted by the Senate. Thank you for your attention.
