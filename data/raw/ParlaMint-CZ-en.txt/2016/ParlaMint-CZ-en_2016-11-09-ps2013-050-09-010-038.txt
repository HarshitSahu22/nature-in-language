ParlaMint-CZ_2016-11-09-ps2013-050-09-010-038.u2	Thank you for the floor. Hello. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, may I present to you the draft law which amends the Capital Market Business Act and other related laws. The draft law implements several European capital market legislation, in particular the Financial Instrument Markets Directive, the Directive on criminal sanctions for market abuse, the regulation on markets in financial instruments and the regulation on market abuse. Since the rules under the Market Abuse Regulation are to apply before the rules under the Financial Instruments Market Directive, the draft law presented has a divided effect. This amendment continues to unify the regulation of distribution on the financial market, the proposed amendment being similar to the recently adopted Consumer Credit Act. We consider it important that similar issues should be regulated as far as possible in the laws governing the various sectors of the financial market. These are in particular questions relating to qualification, economic and other requirements for the provision of investment services. This bill concerns, in particular, the Capital Market Business Act, but it also amends the relevant regulations, in particular the Capital Market Supervisory Act, the Administrative Charges Act, the Bank Act, the Criminal Code and the Criminal Code. The draft law was continuously consulted with the professional public from the private and academic sphere and with representatives of the Czech National Bank. Thank you for your attention.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-11-09-ps2013-050-09-010-038.u3	I thank the Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister Andrea Babiš. I will ask the rapporteur Roman Kubíček to give us the news report. Mr. rapporteur, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-11-09-ps2013-050-09-010-038.u4	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the reason for presenting the bill is the need to implement new European regulations on the capital market section into the Czech legal order. Membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union implies a commitment to adequately incorporate EU legislation into Czech law. Given that these European regulations replace or modify existing Union regulations, but which have already been implemented in relevant sectoral laws, it is therefore necessary to bring these laws into line with the new Union regulation. The main objective of the regulation is to comply with the legislation of the Czech Republic and the European Union through the implementation of the newly adopted European legislation into the Czech rule of law. Another objective is to achieve an ideal legal and factual state in sensitive implementation, application and use of discretion. A higher attractiveness of the Czech capital market can be really achieved, ensuring its better functioning and at the same time tightening up the regulatory framework in order to protect consumers. The main benefits of the proposed adjustment are, above all, the better functioning of the capital market, its increased attractiveness and the strengthening of consumer protection. In the end, it will be a pro-growth measure. Thank you for the floor.
