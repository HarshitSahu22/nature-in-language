ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-25-ps2013-047-02-005-013.u2	Madam President, honourable Members, let me briefly remind you of the Government's bill on trust services for electronic transactions. The aim of the proposed bill is to adapt the legal order of the Czech Republic to the adoption of a regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market. In particular, the part of the Regulation relating to trust-creating services. The adaptation to the electronic identification section will be implemented separately, i.e. separately, because of the relative autonomy of this issue and the later adaptation term. In particular, the proposed law lays down certain procedures for trust providers and trust-creating service requirements. In addition, the supervisory authority determines the provision of trust services, which will be the Ministry of the Interior in the light of its current e-signature remit. It shall also lay down penalties for acts contrary to this Regulation. Thank you for your attention.
