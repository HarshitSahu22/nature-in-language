ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-15-ps2013-049-07-015-225.u1	225. 4. supplement to the participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and the participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the territory of the Czech Republic in 2015 /German press 665/ Once again, I'll ask Secretary of Defense Martin Stropnický to present the material. At the same time, I will ask the rapporteur, this time Karel Černý, to prepare himself. Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-15-ps2013-049-07-015-225.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Dear colleagues, the subject of the material is information on the participation of the armed forces of the Czech Army within the framework of bilateral and regional cooperation and the strengthening of the capacity of common protection of the external borders of the Schengen area in Slovenia. The primary objective of this cooperation - BLED 2015 exercise is the title - was to consolidate outside the established plan of joint activities the coherence of the allies, to face the current security threats and unforeseen situations in Europe. This particular alliance cooperation has contributed to increasing the capacity of our soldiers to participate in the possible protection of the national borders of the Czech Republic, both by self-help and in cooperation with another European Union country or the North Atlantic Treaty organisation. This exercise was organized in accordance with the concept of so-called snap exercises, i.e. immediate, rapid exercises investigating the ability of the armed forces to respond to sudden situations both in terms of planning and deployment itself. The exercise was carried out from 12 November 2015 until 15 December of the same year. The necessary funds, approximately CZK 3 million, were earmarked within the approved budget of the Ministry of Defence chapter for 2015. Mr President, colleagues, I ask that you take note of the 4th supplement to the participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the Czech Republic and the participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the Czech Republic in 2015. Thank you.
