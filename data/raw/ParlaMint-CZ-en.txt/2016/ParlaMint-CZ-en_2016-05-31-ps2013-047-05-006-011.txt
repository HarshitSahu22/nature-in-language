ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-31-ps2013-047-05-006-011.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, as I have already said in the opening words for first reading, the proposed amendment to the Auditor Act implements the measures laid down in the amendment to the Directive of the European Parliament and of the Council on statutory audits of annual and consolidated accounts and the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on specific requirements for statutory audits of public-interest entities. Both of these European legislation are, with effect from 17 June 2016, a new framework for the regulation of statutory audit in the EU. The amendment to the Audit Act is quite extensive because it significantly changes the rules of the quality assurance system for the performance of audit activities and the implementation of quality checks and extends the competence of the Audit Public Supervision Council in the context of the European Regulation, not only in the field of quality control but also in the case of penalties. Furthermore, the independence and impartiality of the auditor are strengthened by law, the requirements for carrying out statutory audits are specified. The quality of the audit and the clarity of the output of audit activities are crucial. I believe that the bill will contribute to the higher quality of audits carried out and will provide users of audit reports with a higher degree of assurance that verified data on the accounts correspond to the actual state of the audited entity's management. Thank you for your attention and I would like to request that you discuss the proposal.
