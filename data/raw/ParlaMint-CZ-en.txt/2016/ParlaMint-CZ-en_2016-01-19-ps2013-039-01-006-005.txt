ParlaMint-CZ_2016-01-19-ps2013-039-01-006-005.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present to you the bill tabled. You have before you a bill amending the Capital Market Business Act and other related laws. In particular, the draft law aims to implement several European legislation. The first is a revised transparency directive with a transposition deadline of 26 November 2015 aimed at supporting small and medium-sized securities issuers admitted to trading on the stock exchange, by reducing the administrative burden. It cancels e.g. a requirement for the publication of interim reports by the statutory body. It also strengthens the sanctioning powers of supervisory authorities. The second European regulation is the Omnibus II Directive with a transposition period of 31 March 2015 and effective from 1 January 2016, which provides for the disclosure of the final terms of the offer by the issuer of securities admitted to trading on the stock exchange. A third European regulation is a directly enforceable regulation on improving the settlement of securities trade in the European Union and CSDs with the aim of unifying the requirement for the settlement of trade in European Union financial instruments and rules for the operation of CSDs. This will in particular simplify cross-border securities transactions. The fourth European regulation is the UTCS V Directive governing the activities of investment fund depositors with a transposition period of 18 March 2016. This Directive aims to increase the protection of investors' assets in investment funds. Further amendments to the laws concerned are caused by interpretational uncertainties and the need to respond to market developments and reflect experience. The proposed changes follow the attractiveness of the fund's business, the simplification of process issues in the capital market, as well as the harmonisation of administrative charges in the financial market segment. The bill was consulted and created in cooperation with the CNB and the professional public from both the private and academic sphere. Thank you for your attention.
