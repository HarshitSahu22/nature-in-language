ParlaMint-CZ_2016-01-20-ps2013-039-02-004-018.u2	Madam President, colleagues, let me briefly remind you of what has already been said here in the first reading, on the Government's proposal for an amendment to the State Heritage Act. Sightseeing zones as well as sightseeing reservations are instruments of the area protection of the monument fund with a long tradition. The values of the historical cores of cities are currently perceived by local authorities as carriers of their identity and their pride. Most of the items listed in the UNESCO World Heritage List in the Czech Republic are monument reserves or landmark zones. The main reason for the bill is the establishment of an explicit legal authorisation for the form of the declaration of landmark zones pursuant to Section 6 of the Act. Another important reason is to allow changes in the declarations for existing conservation zones. To this end, it is proposed in Section 6 of the Act on State Monument Care to provide for the declaration of landmark zones By the Ministry of Culture, the form of measures of a general nature and any changes to the already declared conservation zones can be made on the basis of an adjustment in the transitional provision. The amendment responds to the development of the rule of law, when the scope for declaring landmark zones from regional national committees to the Ministry of Culture was transferred and a new administrative order was adopted, which offers the possibility of using a new institute of measures of a general nature. Therefore, the proposed adjustment does not constitute the introduction of a new authorisation. The substantive competence established in Section 6 of the State Heritage Act is maintained. The proposal merely ensures continuity in declaring other territories as landmark zones if they fulfil the legal characteristics and, above all, will enable them to respond to these changes following possible changes in the state of knowledge of cultural values of the declared landmark zones. The draft law was discussed on 3 September 2015 in the Committee on Science, Education, Culture, Youth and Sports, which did not comment on it. I therefore propose that the Chamber of Deputies approve this bill. I remind you that this is not a new draft of the conservation law, it is not yet under discussion at the moment, but about the amendment of the existing number 20/1987 Coll. Thank you for your attention.
