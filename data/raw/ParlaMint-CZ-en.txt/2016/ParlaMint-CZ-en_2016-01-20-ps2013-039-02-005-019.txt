ParlaMint-CZ_2016-01-20-ps2013-039-02-005-019.u2	Thank you. Madam President, colleagues, the bill before you is presented by the Government in order to strengthen and streamline the support system provided by the State Cinematography Fund. Let me briefly comment on how this purpose should be achieved. The State Cinematography Fund provides funding in two schemes, in the form of support for cinema, or cultural projects in the field of cinema, and in the form of support for the film industry through film incentives. At present, the Fund's activities and support for cinema are financed mainly from its own activities and from so-called parafiscal charges from private entities which have their business activity based on the use of audiovisual works. Support through film incentives is financed from the state budget. A fundamental change in the proposed regulation is the creation of a new stable source of support for cinema, namely the participation of the state budget. It is proposed that an annual subsidy be granted from the State budget for the Fund's activities, the amount of which will correspond to the amount of revenue realised by the audiovisual fee fund for the calendar year preceding the calendar year in which the draft budget will be drawn up by the Fund. The support will be intended mainly for the development and production of Czech cinematographic works, as well as support for cinemas, promotion of Czech cinema in the Czech Republic and abroad, or the training of a film viewer. Other fundamental changes concern the actual process of granting support from the Fund, as well as the scheme for providing film incentives. The submitted amendment makes the present system of providing film incentives more effective in the sense that the applicant for a film incentive can submit an application at any time during the fiscal year, not only once a year, as has been the case so far. These changes will allow better use of the capacity of the Czech film industry and avoid potential business of potential investors, let us understand foreign production. Both committees have made recommendations and agree with the draft. I suggest that I agree with the draft, too. Thank you for your attention.
