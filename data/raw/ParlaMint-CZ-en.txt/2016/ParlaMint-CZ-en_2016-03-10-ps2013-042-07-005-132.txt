By concluding the agreement, the Czech Republic will be able to request the competent authority of the Contracting State information that is relevant for the identification, determination and security of payment of taxes, or investigation or prosecution of tax offences. However, due to the legal constraints laid down by the domestic law, cooperation in obtaining evidence reserved for judicial authorities in criminal matters does not include cooperation. The Contracting Parties undertake, upon request, to provide information held by banks or other financial institutions. The approval of an agreement on the basis of which tax relevant information can be exchanged with jurisdictions considered until recently as tax havens should lead to an effective reduction in tax evasion. Agreements of this type act as a preventive measure to strengthen the payment morale of Czech taxpayers. I therefore recommend agreeing to ratify this agreement. Thank you for your attention.
