Mr Vice-President, Members, this initiative to ratify this Convention also arose from the Czech Moravian Confederation of Trade Unions. This Convention provides for prohibited work for pregnant and lactating women, regulates the minimum length and other requirements of maternity leave, the amount and nature of cash benefits to women on maternity leave, the protection period and the question of the return of women to employment after maternity leave. Furthermore, the Convention provides for the prohibition of discrimination on grounds of maternity and the requirement to grant breaks for breastfeeding. [[There's noise in the room.]] This Convention is in accordance with the constitutional order of the Czech Republic. In the vast majority of national law issues, these commitments are already included in our legislation. But we have the only thing where we ask for an exception. It is a question of women who carry out work on employment agreements or agreements that take place outside employment, because in this way we do not protect these women from termination or immediate termination of employment by the employer, and this category of mothers will also not be able to qualify for periods of breast-feeding. It's because it's just work that's really off the job, or outside the main job, and sometimes it doesn't even pay.
