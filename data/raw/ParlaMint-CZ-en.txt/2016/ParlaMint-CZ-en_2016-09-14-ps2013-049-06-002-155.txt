Since most municipalities are logically intersecting both state and regional roads, this is a paskvil, because sewer systems must be connected, and these communications of the higher order cannot be missed somehow. Our municipality has submitted a request for a grant for the construction of sewerage to the State Environmental Fund and we are preparing for this construction with everything. However, we have come across the negative position of our regional authority on the authorisation of the special use of the I/11 state road for the reasons stated. Please, please, please make sure that we do not have blocked the possibility of realization of this construction not only in our country, but everywhere else." And I'm joining the mayor's plea.
