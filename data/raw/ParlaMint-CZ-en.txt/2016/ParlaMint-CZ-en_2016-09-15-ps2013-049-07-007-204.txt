ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-15-ps2013-049-07-007-204.u2	Thank you. Ladies and gentlemen, you must know that the International Convention against Doping in Sport was adopted at the General Conference in Paris in 2005. It is a multilateral treaty that the Czech Republic signed in 2007 and the problem is that new circumstances, new substances and the work of anti-doping organizations must be constantly taken into account. That is why we are withdrawing attachments that must be updated and which are integral parts of the contract. So this is also the approval that is ahead of us Annex I and Annex II, which, as far as I know, are updated annually. The amendment to Annex I, which we have here, entered into force on 1 January 2016, updates and complements the Annex to the newly classified substances and methods with doping effect. For example, there is the meldonium, which has been talked about a lot, due to its increased use for performance support. Based on the finding that the doping effects of clonidine and carboanhydrase substances are not fully verifiable, the use of these substances for medical purposes is authorised. The amendment to Annex II then entered into force on 14 March 2016 and simplifies for athletes and doctors the use of some of the least serious doping substances for medical purposes. So I might still be able to inform that this year our Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports, in cooperation with us, with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, has submitted an objection to UNESCO because of the legislative approval process and has specified that amendments to the Convention will not be valid for the Czech Republic at the given date, but only after the end of the legislative process in the Czech Republic. On the basis of this objection, UNESCO expects immediate approval of the amendments and immediate notification of their adoption by the Czech Republic at the earliest possible date. And then I think I can say that the material has no impact on the state budget or on public budgets, nor does it concern gender equality.
