Government bill on biocidal products and active substances and amending certain related laws (Biocide Act) /German press 589/ - third reading At the rapporteur's table is the Minister of Health Svatopluk Němeček and I will ask for the same rapporteur of the Guarantee Committee, the Committee on Health, Mr Soňa Marková, to take the position at the rapporteur's table as well. The amendments are set out in the Houses of Press 589/2, which was delivered on 25 May this year. The resolution of the guarantee committee was delivered as House Press 589/3. I ask the Minister if he is interested in speaking before the debate is opened. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-06-29-ps2013-048-02-006-152.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the proposal has been discussed several times here in the Chamber of Deputies, so let me just reiterate that the main objective of the proposal is to implement a regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the making available on the market and the use of biocidal products, which regulates a considerable part of the obligations in the field of making biocidal products available on the market. As a result of the adoption of a directly applicable Union regulation, it is necessary to remove duplications and deviations in the Czech legal order and to establish only such legislation that will allow its smooth application and linking to national legislation. As part of the second reading, Mrs Marková and Mr Volný's amendments have been read, with which I agree. In the first case, it is a technical regulation where the language requirements of the applications submitted and the consistency with the Administrative Code are adapted in this respect, in the second amendment, the terminology relating to the customs procedure is changed following the adoption of the new Union Customs Code. Mr President, honourable Members, I believe you will support this proposal in today's discussion. Thank you for your attention.
