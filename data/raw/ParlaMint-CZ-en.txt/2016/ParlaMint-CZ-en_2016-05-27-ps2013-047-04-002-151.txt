ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-27-ps2013-047-04-002-151.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce the Government's proposal for an atomic law at third reading. The new atomic law is to replace Act No. 18/1997 Coll., on the peaceful use of nuclear energy and ionising radiation, the so-called atomic law, and on the amendment and addition of certain laws. The current Act No. 18/1997 Coll. is intended to remain effective only temporarily in the residual form as the legislation on liability for nuclear damage, pending the adoption of new legislation in this area. The new Atomic Act brings the legislation on the peaceful use of nuclear energy and ionising radiation into line with the requirements of the newly adopted Euratom regulations and recommendations of international organisations. The amendments to the bill were applied by the Economic Committee and the Committee on the Environment. The proposals of both committees are appropriate in terms of future application of the law and can be agreed with them. In general, it is possible to agree with the amendments made by Mr Jan Birk at second reading. On the contrary, I cannot agree with the other amendments made by Members at second reading. Some of these proposals are discriminatory in nature because they unjustifiedly favour certain beneficiaries of a nuclear account which is intended solely to ensure the safe management of radioactive waste or, on the contrary, the payer's contribution to that account. The proposal to completely abolish the current nuclear law is unacceptable, as the law will, in its residual form, temporarily continue to regulate the responsibility for nuclear damage. Other parliamentary proposals come across texts of the law arising from international or Union obligations of the Czech Republic. In some cases, proposals are redundant because the allegedly limited activity does not prohibit the draft law in any way, nor does it need to be authorised by the State. In accordance with the above, I ask you to give your consent to the proposed law, as amended by the amendments recommended by the Guarantee Committee. Thank you for your attention.
