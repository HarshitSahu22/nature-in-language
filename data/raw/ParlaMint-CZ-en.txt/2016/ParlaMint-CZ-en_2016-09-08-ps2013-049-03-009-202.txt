ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-08-ps2013-049-03-009-202.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, something about waterbirds from me. On 14 November last year, the Sixth Meeting of the Parties to the Agreement on the Protection of African-Eurasian Migratory Water Birds adopted amendments to Annexes 2 and 3 to this Agreement in Bonn. The amendments introduce a new taxonomic system in the Annexes to the Agreement, which results from the official expert publication published in 2014, and refines the professional names of certain types of migratory water birds. Another content of the text is also the conversion of two dozen species into a higher level of protection. The submitted Government proposal contains the current text of Annex 2 and Annex 3 and the Agreement and therefore, at the same time as the amendments adopted at the Sixth Meeting of the Parties, amendments to Annex 3 adopted at the previous, fifth meeting of the Contracting Parties in May 2012 shall also be submitted for consent. The content of these amendments was the adaptation of the Agreement Action Plan, namely the clarification of the wording of certain passages and the provision of a taxative list of unauthorised hunting methods. The adoption of amendments to Annexes 2 and 3 will not require amendment of the legislation in force in the Czech Republic and will also have no impact on the state budget, public administration or the corporate sphere beyond what is already provided for by the legislation of the European Union and the Czech Republic. Thank you for your attention.
