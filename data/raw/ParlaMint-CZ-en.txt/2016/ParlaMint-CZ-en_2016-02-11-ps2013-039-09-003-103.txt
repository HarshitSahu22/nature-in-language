ParlaMint-CZ_2016-02-11-ps2013-039-09-003-103.u2	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, I come before you with a bill amending Law No. 329/2011 Coll., on the provision of benefits to persons with disabilities and on the amendment of related regulations, as amended, and Act No. 89/2012 Coll., Civil Code. According to the applicable legal condition, that is to say, § 9 para. 1 of Law No. 329/2011 Coll., on the provision of benefits to persons with disabilities, is entitled to a contribution to a special aid by a person who has a severe defect in the carrier or locomotive system or severe auditory disability or severe visual impairment of the character of a long-term adverse health condition and its health does not preclude the grant of this allowance. The beneficiary of the allowance is therefore the person who is designated by the special aid and subsequently becomes its owner. Article 898 (1) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union provides that: Furthermore, the Civil Code provides that parents need the consent of the court to legal proceedings relating to the child's existing and future assets or to individual parts of that property. The exception to this rule is those cases where these are ordinary matters or exceptional matters, but they are of minimal property value. However, the acquisition value of most of the special equipment is mostly in tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands, and therefore cannot be regarded as normal or negligible matters. It is therefore a case and a contribution to the purchase of a motor vehicle. The amendment of the said legislation also resulted in the repeal of the implementing decree of the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs No. 182/1991 Coll., which in § 35 para. 2 provides that the allowance for the purchase of a motor vehicle is to be granted to the parent of the dependent child. Law No 329/2011 Coll., on granting benefits to persons with disabilities, not only defines the persons entitled to the allowance, as I mentioned in the introduction, but also clearly defines in the following paragraph the persons who can be entitled to a special aid allowance, provided for the purchase of a motor vehicle or a special restraint system. There is therefore a change compared to the original legislation and the owner of the motor vehicle as a special aid for a minor disabled child is no longer a parent, but the child itself, and therefore the parent must obtain the consent of the court in advance in accordance with the applicable legislation. The Labour Office has not responded to this situation as the authority responsible for deciding on the granting of the contribution to the special aid and continues to issue this decision without requiring the relevant decisions of the courts. However, this has been strictly required by motor vehicle registers and, in the event of non-submitting them, refuse to register the motor vehicle, thereby preventing the use of the vehicle for the needs of their disabled child. [[11.20 hours]] I am therefore convinced, and this was also the reason for the amendment of the law, that in the event of the acquisition of a special aid for the affected child, the legal requirement for the approval of the parent's actions by the court is not justified. The application for this contribution is decided by the relevant regional branch of the Labour Office, based on precisely defined legal conditions and the final decision depends on the professional assessment of the health status of the child by the assessor and assessment of the family property situation by the labour office, all in the best interest of the child. In conclusion, I would also like to stress that the adoption of this proposed legislation will not entail increased financial costs for the state budget or other public budgets. On the contrary, I am assuming some savings in funds from the state budget for the judicial system and for the institutions of the social law protection of children, as there will be a decrease in judicial proposals in the order of hundreds. The social impact for the child and his family can only be seen as positive because it will simplify their access to the purchase of a special aid for their disabled child. I also ask for your support because I propose that this proposal be discussed in accordance with Section 90 of the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, because the situation is truly trivial and the better and quicker we can resolve this, the sooner we can help these disabled people. Thank you.

ParlaMint-CZ_2016-02-11-ps2013-039-09-003-103.u4	Thank you for the floor. My explanatory report will be similar, as has already been said here. The reason for the submission of the parliamentary proposal is the elimination of problems caused by the combination of the amendment of laws, when the adoption of Act No. 329/2011 Coll., on the provision of benefits to persons with disabilities, was abolished by Act No. 100/1988 Coll., on social security, together with the implementing decree of the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs No. 182/1991. Judgment of the Court of First Instance in Case C-482/99 France v Commission, ECLI:EU:C:2011:556, paragraph 13. 2 provided that the allowance for the purchase of a motor vehicle would be granted to the parent of the dependent child, which means that the owner was the parent of the minor child directly. Newly pursuant to § 9 para. 1 of Act 329/2011 Coll. is the beneficiary of the allowance of the person to whom the special aid is intended, regardless of age, the legal capacity, i.e. not the legal representative, the parent. This subsequently also becomes the owner, while at the same time according to the provisions of § 898 para. 1 of the Civil Code provides that parents need the consent of the court to legal proceedings relating to the child's existing and future assets or to individual parts of such assets. Thus, the owner of the motor vehicle as a special aid for a minor is no longer a parent, but a child itself, and therefore the parent is forced to obtain the consent of the court in accordance with the provisions cited. However, the Labour Office, as the authority responsible for deciding on the granting of a contribution to a specific instrument, shall continue to issue decisions on the award of the contribution without requiring the relevant judicial decisions. However, these judicial decisions strictly require motor vehicle registers and refuse to register the motor vehicle precisely in the event that they are not submitted. Parents thus find themselves in a situation where they are unable to use the motor vehicle they have purchased for the needs of their disabled child. Unsubstantiated current legislation so that, instead of helping, parents of disabled children get into unsolvable complications. The proposed amendment is intended to remove the problems of parents with registration with the use of medical aids. At the same time, unnecessary burdens on the courts will be reduced by an unnecessary agenda. The Government in its Opinion of 15. 6. 2015 gave its consent and recommended, at the same time, to load exclusively the legislative technical regulation of Article 1 of the draft law without any substantive modification. I agree with the petitioner that consent should be given to the bill in the first reading according to § 90 para. 5.
