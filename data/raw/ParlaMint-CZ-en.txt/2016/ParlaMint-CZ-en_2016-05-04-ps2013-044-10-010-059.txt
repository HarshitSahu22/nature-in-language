ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-04-ps2013-044-10-010-059.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, the draft law on the use of funds from criminal penalties imposed in criminal proceedings is tabled following the Government's programming declaration. To put it very simply, this proposal would introduce a principle that imposed property sanctions in criminal proceedings would be imposed on a special fund and would then be paid out of that fund by those who were harmed by the particular offences. This means that it will prevent today's undesirable situation, where the state will be able to exonerate, for example, the cash penalty or the punishment of forfeiture or seizure of property, but it will no longer get on the victim with the crime. So the principle is the opposite, the confiscated will first satisfy the damage and then it is up to the state to claim damages from the perpetrator. It is a solution that also corresponds to foreign trends, and which we believe will significantly improve the lives of people who have been affected by some criminal activity. I'm not gonna tire you out any longer with an example and I'm gonna ask for your support.
