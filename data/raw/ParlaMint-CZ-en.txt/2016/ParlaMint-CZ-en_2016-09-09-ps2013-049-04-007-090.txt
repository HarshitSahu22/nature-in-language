However, in order to recall the bill, I would like to make two comments. First, a citizen of the Czech Republic is entitled to compensation for loss of employment by means of a compensatory contribution to retirement, who was dismissed from the service or employment relationship by the competent authority of the armed forces on account of political persecution between 25 February 1948 and 29 December 1989 and was issued a certificate pursuant to Article 22 of Act No. 87/1991 Coll., on Out-of-court Rehabilitation. The allowance is proposed in the amount of CZK 1 500 to the person who arose the claim, and CZK 750 to the widow or widower after that person. And the second point concerning the costs of this compensation. The original proposal was based on the number of beneficiaries for the first half of 2014, when a total of 2,627 persons were considered. After more than two years of discussion of this proposal, this figure fell to less than 1,100 people. And since the average age of entitled persons exceeds 80 years, I fear that the adoption of this bill will really be moral rather than financial satisfaction. For this reason, I thank all of you who will support the discussion of this proposal in the Defence Committee. Thank you for your attention.
