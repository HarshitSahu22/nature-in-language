ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-08-ps2013-053-07-006-217.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, I'm going to ask you to be lenient, because there's a little more of these reports, but it needs to be discussed. I therefore submit material Information on the participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the Czech Republic and the participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the Czech Republic during the period July to December 2015. During that period, military exercises were carried out to the extent approved by the Government of the Czech Republic. Armed forces participated in 91 military exercises with foreign partners. Of this number, 33 exercises took place in our territory. The total financial amount needed to implement them was 129,8 million. Crowns. Among the main tasks of the Czech Republic in the field of exercises with foreign partners in the second half of 2015 were preparation of dedicated forces and means for action in the European Union and NATO standby forces, preparation of air force units for the Resolute Support operation and operations within Air Policying, participation in exercises within the framework of measures to assure allies the validity of collective defence commitments, construction of capabilities and, last but not least, exercises to protect the external borders of the Schengen area in Hungary and Slovenia in the context of the current development of the refugee crisis. In the area of the construction of the capabilities of the Czech Republic, the main goal was to practice the ability of the ground forces to participate in a large-scale ground operation and the ability of air force units to provide support to ground forces in the conduct of ground operations. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I would like you to take note of this material and thank you for your attention.
