In 2009 and 2012, the Executive Authority of the Convention on Long-Term Transboundary Environmental Pollution adopted amendments to three protocols, namely the Protocol on persistent organic pollutants, the Protocol on Acidification, Eutrophication and Ground Ozone, called. The Gothenburg Protocol, and the Protocol on Heavy Metals. The following changes are taking place in those protocols. For all three protocols, first, the procedure for adopting amendments to the Annexes setting limits for the discharge of harmful substances is simplified. This creates conditions for faster implementation of the necessary measures to protect human health and the environment. Furthermore, a time-limited mitigation of the requirements for the application of new emission limits is introduced. The purpose is to create the prerequisites for extending the scope of the Contracting Parties to which air pollution control requirements will apply. For the Protocol on persistent organic pollutants and the Gothenburg Protocol, a range of dangerous substances will be extended which will be subject to the rules for limiting and reducing their emissions following the entry into force of the amendments. Finally, on the Gothenburg Protocol, by the way. extend its scope to health protection against suspended particulate emissions and establish new national emission ceilings applicable from 2020. What I would like to stress is that the changes to the protocols in question are based on European Union legislation and are or will be reflected in European Union or Czech legislation in the foreseeable future. Only minor changes in Decree No 1 are taken into account. 415/2012 Coll., which addresses permitted levels of pollution and implements the relevant provisions of the Air Protection Act. The Czech Republic is currently already fulfilling the national emission ceilings applicable to 2020, with the exception of sulphur dioxide, where this reduction is expected to be achieved within the prescribed period. By adopting amendments to those protocols, the Czech Republic will support the efforts of the international community to impose on a global scale stricter requirements for limiting and reducing the emissions of harmful substances, as well as pressure on those countries that have a high share in the long-range transmission of pollutants. Thank you for your attention and I would like to ask you for further consideration of this proposal. Thank you.
