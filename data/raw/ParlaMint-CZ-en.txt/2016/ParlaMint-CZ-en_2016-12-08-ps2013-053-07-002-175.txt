ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-08-ps2013-053-07-002-175.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. It's an interesting subject. I apologize to the Minister, too. I have the honor of taking over this thing for her today. The content of this press is the amendments to Annexes I and II to the International Convention against Doping in Sport. The International Convention against Doping in Sport was adopted at the 33rd session of the General Conference of UNESCO in Paris in 2005 and is a multilateral treaty which entered into force for the Czech Republic in 2007. The purpose of this Convention is to take into account new circumstances in the fight against doping, to strengthen the activities of national anti-doping organisations and to establish a global contractual framework for the joint fight against doping by governmental and non-governmental organisations. Annex I is an integral part of the Convention, a list of prohibited substances and methods, and Annex II means an international standard for therapeutic derogations. In the context of the development of doping substances and methods in the field of sport, these Annexes are updated annually. Amendment to the Annex I update and supplement the Annex on newly classified substances and methods with doping effect and amendment of Annex II simplifies the use of some of the least serious doping substances for medical purposes for athletes and doctors. The Convention belongs to the category of contracts whose acceptance or acceptance of their amendments is subject to the approval of the Parliament of the Czech Republic and the ratification of the President of the Republic. Annex I and Annex II are an integral part of this Convention and the adoption of amendments thereto requires the same constitutional procedure to be followed. The material submitted has no impact on the state budget, public budgets and does not concern gender equality. Thank you for your attention.
