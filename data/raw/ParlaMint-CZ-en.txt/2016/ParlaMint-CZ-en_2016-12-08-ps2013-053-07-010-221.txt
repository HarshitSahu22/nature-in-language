ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-08-ps2013-053-07-010-221.u2	Thank you for the floor. Dear Members, this supplement has been approved by Government Resolution No. 754 of 24 August this year. It contains information about changes to the Czech Armed Forces training plan in the period from the end of August this year to the end of the year. This is one new exercise and specifying information on five exercises that has already been approved by the government and that have changed some attributes beyond the approved framework during their preparation. These changes in the exercise plan with foreign partners take into account the current needs of our army and also reflect the needs of our army in the area of increasing capabilities. They also respond to the options and requirements or offers of our partners. The exercises referred to in the second supplement were and will be financed under the approved budget of the relevant chapter of the Ministry of Defence. Thank you for your attention.
