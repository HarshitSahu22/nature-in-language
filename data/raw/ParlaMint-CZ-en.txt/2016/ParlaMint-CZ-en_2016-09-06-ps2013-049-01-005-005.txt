ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-06-ps2013-049-01-005-005.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the current draft law on biocidal products and active substances and on the amendment of certain related laws. As part of the discussion of the draft law by the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic on 24 August 2016, an amendment containing two legislative technical amendments was approved. The bill was returned to the Chamber of Deputies for these reasons. Now, I would simply briefly recall the content of the bill. Its main objective is to implement a Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products, which regulates a significant part of the obligations in the field of making biocidal products available on the market. As a result of the adoption of a directly applicable Union regulation, it is necessary to remove duplications and deviations in the Czech legal order and to establish only such legislation that will allow its smooth application and linking to national legislation. In addition to harmonisation, the Biocide Regulation also introduces new instruments for the authorisation of preparations and, beyond the original concept of national authorisation or mutual recognition of national authorisations in another Member State, it introduces a uniform Union authorisation allowing applicants to supply the product thus authorised to all Member States of the European Union. The Ministry of Health supports the bill in the version approved by the Senate plenary, as the failure to make these changes could have a negative impact on the application of the law. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, I consider the present draft law to be important in order to ensure that our legal order is compatible with European Union law and in order to ensure the proper functioning of the biocide market. That is why I believe that you will support this government proposal, as approved by the Senate. Thank you for your attention.
