ParlaMint-CZ_2016-02-11-ps2013-039-09-004-009.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the main objective of the bill is to extend the range of activities that the State Fund for Transport Infrastructure can finance from its budget, in particular in order to make the multi-source financing process of transport infrastructure more efficient, thereby extending the range of quality projects to which funds from European Union funds can be drawn. For example, the possibility of financing road and motorway management, the possibility of financing repairs, maintenance and administration for major inland waterways, including the possibility of financing the construction, repair or administration of port infrastructure in Hamburg, or a change in the financing of cycle paths from maintenance to repair. The proposed amendment to the legislation will not affect the total amount of funds managed by the Fund. The proposal also responds to some partial procedural and organisational problems related to the internal activities of the SFDI, such as the modification of terminology or the publication of the Committee's decisions on the Fund's website instead of in the Transport Bulletin, and includes the partial implementation of the European Union Directive on the creation of a single European railway area. The draft law was discussed by the Economic Committee and the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development. The two committees recommend that the draft law be discussed in the text of the amendments adopted, the amendment adopted by the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development being identical to the amendment adopted by the Economic Committee at its meeting. The Ministry of Transport agrees with the amendments adopted. Thank you.
