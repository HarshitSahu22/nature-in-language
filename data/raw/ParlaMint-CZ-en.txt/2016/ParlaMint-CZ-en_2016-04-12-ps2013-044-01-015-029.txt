ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-12-ps2013-044-01-015-029.u1	29. Government bill amending Act No. 262/2011 Coll., on Resistance and Resistance to Communism, as amended by Act No. 250/2014 Sb. House print 570/ - second reading I ask Minister Dienstbier to take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-12-ps2013-044-01-015-029.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I will make a very brief statement on the amendment to the Act on Resistance and Resistance to Communism. I would point out that it is not whether or not the law should be in this form, but when it is, it should be functional. So we are removing certain, say, technical and legislative shortcomings that are in this law. I'd sum it up to, like, three points. The proposal addresses the competence uncertainties between the Ministry of Defence and the Ethics Commission, while, in addition to setting out the competence of the Ethics Commission for the review procedure and for the renewal of the proceedings, it clearly determines its competence to discuss remedies against all the fundamental decisions of the Ministry of Defence. The Minister for Defence should therefore decide, in particular, on measures against inactivity and degradation against procedural resolutions which do not end the proceedings in the case. The draft law is also intended to fill a gap with regard to the definition of integrity and reliability as conditions for the performance of a member of the Ethics Commission. It also complements provisions relating to the operation of members of the Ethics Commission in relation to the Office of the Government of the Czech Republic, which ensures the performance of the duties of the Ethics Commission and its other activities professionally, organizationally, financially and materially. There were no amendments to the committee. In that sense, I have nothing to say. Thank you for your attention.

The Organizing Committee recommended the discussion of the bill on 3 September 2015 and proposed the Defence Committee as a guarantee committee. The first reading took place on 9 March 2016 at the 42nd meeting and the House disagreed with the discussion so that it could agree with the draft law at first reading. The bill was ordered for discussion by the Defence Committee and the time limit for discussion by the Committee was 30 days. The Defence Guarantee Committee discussed the bill at its 29th meeting on 31 March 2016 and there was no amendment and the committee adopted a recommendation resolution delivered to Members as press 570/1.
