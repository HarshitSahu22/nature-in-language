ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-02-ps2013-042-02-006-162.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Colleagues, colleagues, the bill before you is presented by the government in order to strengthen and streamline the support system provided by the State Cinematography Fund. Let me briefly remind you of the main points of the proposal. This is mainly about financial stabilisation of the resources of the State Cinematography Fund. At present, the Fund's activities and support for cinema are financed mainly from its own activities and so-called parafiscal charges from private entities which have their business activity based on the use of audiovisual works. It is proposed that an annual subsidy be granted from the State budget for the Fund's activities, the amount of which shall correspond to the amount of revenue realised by the audiovisual fee fund for the calendar year preceding the calendar year in which the draft budget of the Fund is drawn up. Other fundamental changes contained in the draft relate to the fund support process and also to the film incentive scheme. The submitted amendment makes the present system of providing film incentives more effective in the sense that the applicant for a film incentive can submit an application at any time during the fiscal year, not only once a year, as has been the case so far. These changes will allow better use of the capacity of the Czech film industry and avoid possible exits of potential investors, let us understand foreign production. Finally, I would like to say that the bill was discussed after second reading in the Guarantee Committee on Science, Education, Culture, Youth and Sports. I recommend agreeing with the amendments. Thank you for your attention.
