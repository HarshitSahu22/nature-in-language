Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Treaty between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Islamic Republic of Iran on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income taxes and the Protocol to it signed in Prague on 30 April 2015 /German press 484/ - second reading Now I ask the Prime Minister and Finance Minister Andrej Babiš to take the floor. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-01-26-ps2013-039-04-004-204.u2	Thanks for the word. Good evening, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen. A contractual document related to international taxation is submitted to you for discussion. In this case, it is a double taxation treaty with the Government of the Islamic Republic of Iran, which was signed on 30 April 2015 in Prague. A standard comprehensive bilateral contract has been negotiated, which regulates taxation and, at the same time, the exclusion of international double taxation of different types of income and which will certainly help to further develop mutual economic activities. This Treaty certainly does not replace national tax rules and cannot thus create a new tax obligation. They deal with cases where the right to levy tax is enshrined in their national law by both signatory States. In these situations, the treaty is certainly superior to the national regulation. There is currently no treaty in the mutual tax relations between the Czech Republic and Iran, and therefore there may be undesirable international double taxation. The contract provides for an objective distribution of the right to collect income taxes between the two States in cases where income has a source in the territory of one State and flows from beneficiaries with their head office or residence in the territory of the other State. As a standard, it regulates the basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of the Czech Republic and Iran. It lays down the principle of equal treatment of the entities of both Contracting Parties, allows for informal settlement of disputes arising from the interpretation and implementation of the tax agreement and ensures a relevant exchange of information between the competent authorities of both States. The contract deals with taxation of all types of income and applies as the main method of excluding double taxation of income of Czech tax residents the method of simple credit of foreign tax. An integral part of the contract is also a protocol which guarantees the right to apply their national legislation to both States in order to prevent tax evasion and fraud and also responds to the situation where Iran is not able to fully exchange bank information at the moment and obliges it to inform the Czech Party immediately about the changes made in this area. The conclusion of the contract will not have a direct impact on the state budget of the Czech Republic. The overall contribution resulting from its closure will depend on the pace of development of the various forms of economic cooperation. I propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take into account those facts when discussing this Treaty and agree to its ratification. Thank you for your attention.
