ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-10-ps2013-042-07-006-133.u2	Madam President, honourable Members, I present to you the Treaty with the Federal Republic of Germany. This is a comprehensive document designed to adapt the cooperation of police and customs authorities to the situation following the abolition of checks at the common border. This document is intended to replace previous documents, for example, an agreement on cooperation in the fight against organised crime, signed by both parties in 1991. It also amends the Treaty and Appendices to the European Convention on Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters and facilitates its application from 2000 and the Treaty on Rail Transport through the Common State Borders and facilitates the 1997 Rail Transit Treaty. The Treaty consistently separates police and judicial forms of cooperation. It's divided into three parts. The first one regulates the area of police cooperation and includes the adaptation of all its modern institutes. The second part is an amendment to the Convention on Judicial Cooperation and the third part contains common and final provisions. In addition to standard cooperation in preventing and clarifying criminal offences and in preventing violations of public order and security, the contract also covers clarification of administrative offences. Newly, in relation to the Federal Republic of Germany, for example, the possibility of cross-border interventions in the territory of the other State in the event of imminent threat to the life or health of persons is closed. Furthermore, the transfer of persons between the Contracting States or the transit of persons in custody or in the execution of a sentence through the territory of the other State shall be regulated. Thank you for your attention.
