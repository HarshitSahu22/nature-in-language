Ladies and gentlemen, the proposed amendment to the Act on State Property has been returned to the Chamber of Deputies with amendments to the Senate. These amendments address, in particular, the necessary shift in efficiency from 1 January 2016 to 1 March 2016 due to the progress of time. They also prevent a partial collision with the already approved draft accompanying law to the law on recovery procedures and resolution of the crisis in the financial market. At the same time, however, they partly follow the amendments proposed by the Chamber of Deputies, in which some partial exclusions from the proposed regime are added to the proposal to concentrate the unnecessary immovable property of the State in the hands of the Office for State representation in property matters. Modifications concern, on the one hand, the management of the real estate of the State used by the Constitutional Court and, on the other hand, the maintenance of certain premises for the management of non-needed real estate within the resort by direct transfer of such property to another organisation of the same resort where it is needed. However, these changes proposed by the Senate do not undermine the fundamental objective of the amendment to the State Property Act, i.e. the gradual concentration of immovable state property in the hands of another organisational body of the ÚZSVM. I would therefore like to ask you to approve the bill as amended by the Senate's amendments. Thank you for your attention.
