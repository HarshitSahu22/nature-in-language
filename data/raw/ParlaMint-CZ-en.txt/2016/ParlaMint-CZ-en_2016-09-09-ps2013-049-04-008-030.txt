ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-09-ps2013-049-04-008-030.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the subject of the bill is to bring Czech legislation into line with Directive No. 2013/48 on the right of access to a lawyer in criminal proceedings and proceedings relating to a European arrest warrant and so on. Our right is essentially harmonised, it is largely in line with this directive, whose implementation deadline, perhaps the most important message I have for you, ends 27. 11th of this year, so we have time for harmonisation by 27 November. Most of our rights are in line, there are a few more or less technical details to be added, which concern, after all, as a material, the whole improvement of the rights charged in the context of European interstate cross-border criminal proceedings. This includes, for example, the right to inform third parties about restrictions on personal freedom, the right to communicate with third parties and consular authorities, the possibility of allowing the so-called double defence in the transfer proceedings, the possibility of the person whose surrender is concerned, to choose in the event of the loss of a cooperating lawyer, the purpose of which will be to assist the defender in the transmitting state, and so on. The proposal is indeed purely harmonisation and technical, improving the position of our citizens. That is why, at least in the course of the negotiations, there have been no problems or amendments. I think the rapporteur will tell you more about it. Thank you.
