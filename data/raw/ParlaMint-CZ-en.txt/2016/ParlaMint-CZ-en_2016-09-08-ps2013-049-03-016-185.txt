ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-08-ps2013-049-03-016-185.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the Czech Republic currently has international agreements to prevent double taxation with more than 80 countries in the world, but Chile is still missing. In the context of international taxation, it is crucial whether a country which has a certain role in the business and financial intentions of the Czech taxpayers has a signed tax treaty with our state, and that is certainly Chile's case. For your information, in April this year, the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic agreed to ratify this Treaty. The Treaty, which is currently proposed in mutual relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Chile, deals with taxation and exclusion of international double taxation of all types of income and property. The contract uses as the main method of excluding double taxation of income and property of Czech tax residents the method of simple credit of foreign tax. The negotiated contract does not replace national tax rules and cannot therefore create a new tax obligation, but only regulates the obligation arising from national rules. They deal with cases where the right to levy tax is enshrined in their national law by both signatory States. In these situations, the treaty is certainly superior to the national regulation. As a standard, the Treaty provides for basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of both countries, as well as the principle of non-discrimination, the informal resolution of disputes arising from its implementation, and ensures the relevant exchange of information between the competent authorities of the two States. The conclusion of the contract will not have a direct impact on the state budget of the Czech Republic. The overall contribution resulting from its closure will depend on the pace of development of the various forms of economic cooperation. On the basis of the above and mentioned above, I therefore ask you that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take these facts into account when discussing this international treaty and agree to its ratification. Thank you.
