ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-08-ps2013-053-07-011-222.u2	Thank you, Mr President, that's right, it's the last of the long series. I therefore submit the information that the Government of the Czech Republic has taken into account by its resolution No. 741 of August 24th of this year. The information provides a comprehensive overview of landings, flights and transits, within 48 hours, other armed forces through the territory of the Czech Republic, in the first half of this year. The material also includes a comparison of individual numbers of flights of foreign military aircraft over our territory over the last five years. Land and air transport of other armed forces was carried out during that period due mainly to the participation of these forces in military exercises, trainings and negotiations to secure military operations such as SABER STRIKE, MORAVIAN GRIFFON 16, ENDURING FREDOM, RSM and KFOR. In particular, in order to move exercises, participation in international exercises in the territory of the Czech Republic was carried out a total of 169 ground and 153 air transports of other armed forces. Transit transits and crossings through our territory were carried out in a given period a total of 4 104 transports, i.e., of which 149 transits and 3 955 air crossings. So much for information. Thank you for your attention.
