ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-12-ps2013-044-01-005-009.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce a government bill amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of the Act on the Collection of Laws and International Treaties. The aim of this proposal is to amend certain laws in connection with the adoption of the Act on the Collection of Laws and International Treaties. The proposed legislation brings in particular the necessary amendments to the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Senate and the Act on the Constitutional Court. The proposed law also makes terminology changes to a number of laws in connection with the unification of the Collection of Laws and Collections of International Treaties into one Collection of Laws and International Treaties. Thank you for your attention.
