ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-08-ps2013-049-03-006-199.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to justify a government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Agreement between the Czech Republic and Belize on the exchange of information in tax matters. This agreement was signed on 12 February 2016 by the Czech Ambassador of the Czech Republic in Mexico, PhDr Lubomír Hladík and Ambassador Belize to Belize, Mexico, Oliver del Cid. The Ministry of Finance, in cooperation with the General Financial Directorate, has negotiated an agreement with jurisdictions and preferential tax arrangements on the exchange of information for tax purposes since 2010. For the time being, these negotiations have resulted in 14 final draft texts which are subsequently undergoing a legislative process leading to ratification by the President. 12 of these agreements have already entered into force, which are announced in the Collection of International Treaties. The proposed agreement fully meets OECD standards for the international exchange of tax information in which the Czech Republic has long played an active role. The subject of the agreement is taxes of all kinds and names in the Czech Republic and Belize. Information provided under this Agreement shall be subject to an obligation of confidentiality. By concluding the agreement, the Czech Republic will be able to request information about Czech residents. These will be used in the correct assessment of taxes, possibly. to investigate or prosecute tax offences. However, given the legal constraints imposed by the domestic law, the information provided may be used in criminal proceedings only as operational information. The Contracting Parties undertake, upon request, to provide information held by banks or other financial institutions. The approval of an agreement on the basis of which tax relevant information can be exchanged with jurisdictions considered until recently as tax havens should lead to an effective reduction in tax evasion. Agreements of this type act as a preventive measure to strengthen the payment morale of Czech taxpayers. I therefore recommend agreeing to ratify this agreement. Thank you for your attention.
