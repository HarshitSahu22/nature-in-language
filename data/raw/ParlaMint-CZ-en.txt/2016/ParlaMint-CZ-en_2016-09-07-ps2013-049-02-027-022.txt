ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-07-ps2013-049-02-027-022.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to make a proposal for a law amending Act No 1 for the second reading. 360/1992 Coll., on the pursuit of the profession of authorized architects and on the pursuit of the profession of authorized engineers and technicians active in construction, submitted by the Ministry of Regional Development. The draft amendment to the Act of Authorization was prepared in accordance with the Government's legislative work plan for 2015, in order to make adjustments and refine certain provisions of the Act of Authorization which constitute problems in application practice and to bring the Act of Authorization into line with the Civil Code and the Trade Business Act. The proposal was submitted without contradictions. The content of this amendment is clarification of legislation, adaptation of the current market situation and changing conditions of the two chambers' member bases, and alignment of the legal situation where the literal interpretation is broken down with the purpose and purpose of the regulation. The proposed amendment has no impact on state and public budgets, on local authorities or on the environment. The proposed changes affect natural and legal persons as business entities. We assess the effects on these entities positively as the amendment of the law will allow a wider range of their activities in conjunction with the pursuit of the profession and a higher degree of legal certainty given by clarifying the existing legislation. As part of the first reading, two comments were made by Mr Petr and Mrs Fischerová, which were clarified during the first reading. At first reading, the amendment to this Act was allocated to the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development, which discussed it at its 43rd meeting on 7 June 2016. Three amendments were tabled during the committee procedure. The first amendment to which Mr Petrů was made in committee discussion contained the inclusion of the formation of the Agricultural Chamber into the authorisation law. After a detailed debate and explanation by the representatives of the petitioner, this proposal was withdrawn. The second amendment proposing authorisation for the Ministry of Regional Development to issue fees and price lists in the form of implementing legislation was presented by Mr Kořenek. This proposal, too, was withdrawn after a detailed debate and explanation by the representatives of the petitioner. The last amendment was Mr Petr's proposal. Its subject was legislative technical amendments to the amendment to the Act of Authorization, supplementing Act No. 126/2016 to the list of amendments, regulation of terminology, that is to replace the words "the person concerned" by the words "authorized person" and also the amendment to the amendment to this Act. This proposal was adopted by the Committee and contained 213 of the resolution of the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development. Thank you for your attention and also for your cooperation.
