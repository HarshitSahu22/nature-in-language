ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-12-ps2013-044-01-004-008.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, let me briefly remind you of the Government's bill on the Collection of Laws and International Treaties and on the drafting of legislation published in the Collection of Laws and International Treaties. The aim of the proposal is to make the necessary changes to the legal order related to the fundamental modernisation and electronisation of the creation and publication of legislation. The proposal belongs to a broader framework of the strategic objectives of the government in the framework of the project called eGovernment, namely increasing the competitiveness of the Czech Republic, democratization of the exercise of public power and the fight against corruption. The main principle of the proposed regulation is the introduction of a free, legally binding electronic form of the Collection of Laws and International Treaties, which would therefore be free of charge, as I said, available to a citizen in a very simple form through the Internet and the state guaranteed repository and server. Another principle is the introduction of a legally binding application of the text of the amendment to the legislation. At present, the text of the amendment does not provide a satisfactory and understandable overview of the proposed amendments to the legislation in the form of individual amendments. In this way, each amendment will also contain a legally binding full text of the legislation indicating the amendments adopted for better orientation on the substance. The proposal is further aimed at improving the quality and efficiency of the legislative process. The draft legislation will be submitted through an electronic law-making system. This system will include specialised templates for the different types of legal acts and acts which will take into account their required form and structure. This instrument will provide modern and effective options and tools that will facilitate work with the draft law and the preparation of its amendments and support their vote in the Chamber of Deputies and the Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. Thank you for your attention.
