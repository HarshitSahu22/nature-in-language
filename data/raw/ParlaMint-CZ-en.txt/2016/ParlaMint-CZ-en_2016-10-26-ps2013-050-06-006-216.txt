ParlaMint-CZ_2016-10-26-ps2013-050-06-006-216.u2	Thank you for the floor. Have a good afternoon. I will state that the House was not very successful at both electoral points, that it was not possible to choose, and now I will be specific on the announced point - the election of the Chamber of Deputies for the appointment of one member of the so-called House of Deputies. great media councils, i.e. the Radio and Television Council. 154 ballots were issued. There were also 154 tickets handed over to the valid and invalid ones. This means that the quorum required for the election in the first round was 78 votes received. The results were as follows: Milan Bouška won 14 votes, Jiří Mejstřík 26 votes, František Vostál 35 votes, Jaroslav Kravka 32 votes and Jaroslav Tajbr 30 votes. I can therefore say that no one has been elected in the first round and, in accordance with the electoral order, two candidates shall proceed to the second round with the highest number of votes achieved. The advances are proposed candidate Jaroslav Kravka, who had 32 votes, and František Vostál with 35 votes.
