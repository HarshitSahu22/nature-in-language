ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-12-ps2013-044-01-008-012.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, let me briefly remind you of the content of the Government's bill on certain offences. The reason for the submission of the Government's draft Act on certain offences is that the new Act on Liability for and Procedure for Infractions will, unlike the existing Act on Infractions, regulate individual offences. The transgressions provided for today in the special section of the Criminal Code will be transferred to the individual laws governing the performance of the public administration in which the obligations for which the infringement is punishable are laid down. Those offences for which this procedure is not possible are the subject of the present government bill on certain offences. These offences generally affect infringements of general rules, not obligations contained in specific laws. These are, for example, offences against public order, property or civil coexistence. The Constitutional Committee has tabled amendments to the bill, with which I agree. Thank you for your attention.
