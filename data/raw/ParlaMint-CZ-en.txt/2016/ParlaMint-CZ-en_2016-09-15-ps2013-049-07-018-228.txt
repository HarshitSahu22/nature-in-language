Information on flights and transits of armed forces of other states carried out through the territory of the Czech Republic in the second half of 2015 /German press 735/ Minister Stropnický is ready, so is colleague Klučka. You can justify this report, Minister.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-15-ps2013-049-07-018-228.u2	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. Dear colleagues, I present a material that provides a comprehensive overview of stays, in particular international exercises, landings, flights and transits within 48 hours in the Czech Republic of the armed forces of other states through the Czech Republic, for the second half of 2015. The material also includes a comparison of individual numbers of flights of foreign military aircraft over the territory of the Czech Republic over the last five years. Land and air transport of the armed forces of other States was carried out during that evaluation period mainly due to the participation of these forces in military exercises, trainings and negotiations to secure military operations. A total of 195 land and 211 air transports of the armed forces of other states were carried out for the purpose of staying in the Czech Republic. Transit transits and crossings through our territory were realized in a given period a total of 3 863. Of this was 93 alone, 19 road and 3 751 air. Thank you for your attention.
