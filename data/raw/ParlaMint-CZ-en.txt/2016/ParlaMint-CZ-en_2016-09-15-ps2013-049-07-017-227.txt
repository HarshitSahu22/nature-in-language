ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-15-ps2013-049-07-017-227.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the material contains an overview of planned military exercises with foreign partners in 2016. It is 115 exercises of the armed forces of the Czech Republic outside the territory of our Republic and 60 exercises of the armed forces of other states in our territory. The material also includes an overview of five exercises of the armed forces of other states in the Czech Republic, which will be carried out without the participation of the troops of our army in the military training area of Boletice. These are the armies of Belgium, Austria and the Netherlands. The priority goal of the exercise with foreign partners in 2016 is to examine and increase the level of readiness of the armed forces of the Czech Republic to defend it in cooperation with allies. The estimated costs of securing this activity and international exercises are the circa 149 miles. CZK and will be secured through the proper budget of the Ministry of Defence. This material was approved by the Government on 21 December 2015, and I would like to ask you to take note of this material. Thank you.
