ParlaMint-CZ_2016-11-29-ps2013-053-01-004-006.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, let me remind you of the draft law on European political parties and European political talents and about the amendment of certain laws available to you as the Parliamentary Press 876. The aim of the proposal is to adapt the Czech rule of law to the regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council under the number 1141 of 2014 on the status and financing of European political parties and European political foundations. The purpose of European political parties and associated foundations, as a de facto transnational grouping with European legal personality, is to link policy at national and Union level under the European Regulation. Under the regulation, European political parties should be assisted in their efforts to ensure the link between European civil society and the institutions of the European Union. The essence of the regulation is to create a legal and administrative basis for the provisions of European political parties and foundations, their registration by the EU Office for European political parties and their financing from the general budget of the European Union. The draft law also addresses, for example, the change of the legal form of the European political party or foundation to the association and the change of the legal form of the association to the European political party. Furthermore, the law regulates synergies with the Ministry of the Interior, with the Office for European Political Parties and Foundations and designates the Ministry of Finance as a national contact point. The subject matter of the law is only the necessary amendments required by the European regulation. We do not go beyond what is necessary in implementing these regulations. Thank you for your attention.
