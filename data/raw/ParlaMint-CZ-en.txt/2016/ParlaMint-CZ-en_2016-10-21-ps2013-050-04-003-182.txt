The draft law is intended to ensure that persons engaged in selected activities of animal production and animal production are allowed to claim the refund of excise duty on mineral oils which have been consumed for selected activities of animal production and animal production. The Government's bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies on 31 August 2016 as a guarantee committee and recommended by its resolution to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the Government's bill as amended by the amendments adopted. [[10.20 hours]] The Chamber of Deputies discussed the Government's bill at second reading on 16 September 2016. In the context of this discussion, a comprehensive amendment has been tabled which allows livestock undertakings to apply for a refund of excise duty on diesel consumed in primary agricultural production, taking into account the intensity of livestock farming of the main livestock species. Three amendments were then tabled on this comprehensive amendment. The first two amendments will make it possible to apply for the refund of excise duty on diesel consumed also for the implementation of forest and pond management, and the third will allow natural persons to apply for refund within six months of the date on which they were able to apply for the first time. Since a number of species or categories of livestock cannot be reliably substantiated by the stocking intensity from 1 January 2016 and it is therefore not possible to ensure the application of the correct excise duty rate according to the stocking density from that date, the comprehensive amendment provides, on a transitional basis from 1 January 2016 to 1 July 2017, for alternative legislation to enable the refund of excise duty on diesel consumed in animal production activities independently of the stocking intensity. Taking into account the development of agriculture in the Czech Republic and taking into account the impact of the proposed measures taking into account the intensity of farming on the state budget of the Czech Republic, the comprehensive amendment envisages applying an increased and highest rate of tax only until 31 December 2018. In order to reduce the administrative burden for both the State and the bodies concerned, the comprehensive amendment envisages a uniform basic rate of refund for all agricultural primary production activities from 1 January 2019. The comprehensive amendment will contribute to improving the situation in animal production and animal production, and I therefore agree with it. Three other amendments were tabled to the comprehensive amendment, which I also agree with.
