ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-02-ps2013-042-02-005-161.u2	Mr President, colleagues, allow me once again to draw the sense of the government's proposal for an amendment to the State Heritage Act. The aim of this purely technical amendment is, in response to the development of the rule of law, to adjust the explicit authorisation for the form of declaration of conservation zones by measures of a general nature. This uniform form is also introduced by means of a transitional provision, as well as any amendments to any existing regulations that have so far been declared. The proposed amendment does not constitute the introduction of a new authorisation. The competence established in Section 6 of the Monument Act is maintained, a pure form of declaration is added and, at the same time, it is possible to respond as a result of significant changes in the state of knowledge of cultural values within the existing declarations. No amendments have been applied to the bill, and I therefore propose that the Chamber of Deputies approve it. Thank you for your attention.
