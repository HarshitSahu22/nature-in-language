ParlaMint-CZ_2016-11-29-ps2013-053-01-006-008.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, let me briefly remind you of the draft law amending the law on municipalities, the Regional Law, the Capital of Prague Act and other related laws on the remuneration of members of representatives. The subject matter of the draft law is the modification of the new system of remuneration of members of the local authorities, whose aim is in particular: firstly, to harmonise the rules on remuneration of members of representatives in the municipal law, in the Regional Law and in the Prague Capital Act, secondly, to resolve application and performance problems and to simplify and make clear the entire system of remuneration of members of the council, thirdly, to set up a fair consistent system of remuneration with clear internal ties, which is predictable and understandable. The proposal maintains the existing concept based on the fact that the basic method of determining the monthly remuneration for the performance of the duties of a member of the local authority is governed by law. The Government shall be empowered to issue a government regulation fixing the amount of the monthly remuneration to members of the Boards of Directors. The proposal shall also maintain the division of members of the Boards of Directors into vacant and unreleased members. Releasing members of the councils shall continue to be entitled by law to a monthly remuneration of the amount laid down by the Government's regulation, the unpaid members of the council may be paid a monthly remuneration, but a decision on whether they will be granted and the determination of the amount thereof shall be reserved to the local authority. The Government's regulation shall determine the maximum amount of remuneration to be paid to a non-released member of the council. However, for unreleased mayors, or mayors or captains, the proposal newly fixes a minimum monthly remuneration of 0,3 times the amount of the monthly remuneration which would be due to the relaxed functionary for the performance of the function. Furthermore, the provision of extraordinary remuneration or the provision of a so-called severance grant is amended, for example, instead of the current remuneration at the end of the term of office of a member of the council. The right to a severance payment of one plus three monthly remunerations to date shall be fixed, i.e. one monthly remuneration to which the monthly remuneration is attributed by the number of years for which the official has performed his duties. I agree with the amendments of the Committee on Public Administration and Regional Development. Thank you for your attention.
