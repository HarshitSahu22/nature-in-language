Proposal for the election of a member of the Supreme Audit Office As far as the election of a member of the Supreme Audit Office is concerned, 144 ballots have also been issued, and this has been the case at all three ballot points. The call for election was therefore 73. In the case of a member of the Supreme Audit Office, the only proposed candidate Jan Málek received 85 of our votes and was therefore elected. In this election, I can remind you that this is a proposal from the Chamber of Deputies to the Prime Minister, who appoints a member of the SAO, a new member in office, by his office or by his decree.
