The Regional Conference is a platform for discussing current trends in crime and a strategy for facilitating improved cooperation between INPERPOL Member States. We consider the fact that this event is held in the Czech Republic and in Prague as a very good thing also towards the presentation of the Czech Republic. The necessary prerequisite for this conference is the provision of the privileges and immunities of Interpol and the participants of the conference. INTERPOL calls for privileges and immunities from all host countries for its conferences. In relation to INTERPOL, this question is not addressed in any bilateral or other contract or contract of another type. For this reason, this very agreement was prepared. By agreement, the INTERPOL will be granted the conditions normally granted to international organisations. They will also be provided to conference participants in connection with the fulfilment of their official duties and their tasks. Even in this case, the scope of privileges and immunities that will be granted do not deviate from the usual standards applicable to this type of action in the world. INTERPOL's officials will then use the privileges and immunities that are usually granted within diplomatic congregations. All conditions as well as other advantages regarding, for example, facilitating entry into the Czech Republic are limited only and only for the duration of this conference, respectively. for the time necessary to travel and travel from the Czech Republic.
