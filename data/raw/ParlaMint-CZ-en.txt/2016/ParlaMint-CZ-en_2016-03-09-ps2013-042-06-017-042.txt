ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-09-ps2013-042-06-017-042.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, a proposal for a related amendment law is presented under number 719 to the former Central Account Register Act. This draft law addresses the technical adjustments to the related regulations necessary for the transmission of the relevant information to its own database of central account records. Only proposed changes to the Criminal Code are dealt with on the basis of the requirements of practice, namely with the Supreme Prosecutor, the settlement of existing co-formulatory inaccuracies, the provisions relating to the case presented, as it regulates the breach of banking secrecy. I therefore ask you to support this amendment law and to refer it to the next reading. Thank you for your attention.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-09-ps2013-042-06-017-042.u3	Thank you, Minister. I now ask the rapporteur for the first reading of Mrs Kristýna Zelienková to take the floor. Please, rapporteur.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-09-ps2013-042-06-017-042.u4	Thank you again for the floor. As the Minister has already mentioned, it is necessary to make the following legislative changes in connection with the House press 718. So, in parallel with the bill on central accounting, it is proposed to correct the Act on Information Systems of Public Administration by extending the Czech National Bank, the Act on Banks and the Act on Savings and Credit Cooperatives and some related measures, both of which concern breaking the confidentiality of central accounts. Furthermore, the Act on Free Access to Information, where not only the provision of the information in question other than qualified applicants by the administrator of the central account records, is excluded from the scope of this Act, but also that the Act on Free Access to Information should not apply to information already obtained from the central account records by the authorised applicant. Furthermore, the Code of Criminal Procedure, in connection with the search and detection of crime and prosecution of perpetrators concerning funds in accounts that could be secured in criminal proceedings, for example, as a return to crime. Furthermore, the Data Protection Act, where the purpose of the Central Account Register Act should also result in the status of this Recording in relation to this Data Protection Act. The effectiveness of the proposal for this related law shall be 15 days after its publication. I recommend agreeing to the proposed amendments to the laws in relation to the proposed Central Account Register Act at first reading.
