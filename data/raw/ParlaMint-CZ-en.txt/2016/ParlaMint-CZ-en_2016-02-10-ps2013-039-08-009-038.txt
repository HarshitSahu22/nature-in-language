ParlaMint-CZ_2016-02-10-ps2013-039-08-009-038.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, may I briefly present to you the Government's bill amending certain laws in the field of money circulation and the foreign exchange economy and repealing Law No 1 on the subject. 219/1995 Coll., Foreign Exchange Act, as amended. The amendment to the Act on the circulation of banknotes and coins simplifies the exchange of non-standardly damaged banknotes and coins. The draft law precises the definition of non-standardly damaged banknotes and puts, for example, a banknote with an area of less than 50 per cent between non-standardly damaged banknotes. Another change is that deposit ATMs will have to newly reject suspicious coins, while according to the previous adjustment they had to hold these coins, which was technically difficult to do. With the process of liberalising the foreign exchange economy and capital operations for the last two decades, the foreign exchange law, which regulated the treatment of foreign currency resources, has become an excess legal standard in the Czech legal system and is therefore abolished. Inevitable limitation of payment operations during an emergency period shall be taken into its provisions by the Crisis Act. As a result of the abolition of the foreign exchange law, the criminal code newly defines the substance of the offence as a threat to the foreign exchange economy. The bill was created by the Ministry of Finance and the Czech National Bank. The material has also been consulted by the professional public and sent to the European Central Bank for comments. In view of my arguments, I would like to ask you to support this bill. Thank you for your attention.
