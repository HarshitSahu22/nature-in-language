ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-07-ps2013-049-02-006-006.u2	Thank you. Mr President, colleagues, I do not want to repeat here what has already been said about the government proposal in the first reading. As the promoter of this transposition amendment, however, I consider it most necessary to draw attention to two very relevant circumstances. In order to implement the relevant European directive into our legal order, a transposition period of 10 April t. r. The proposal approved by the Government at the beginning of February should therefore be adopted as soon as possible in the light of real possibilities, optimally so as to take effect on 1 January next year. Otherwise, as the Government has already been formally informed by the European Commission, the Czech Republic is at risk of bringing an action before the Court of Justice of the European Union. In this situation, I cannot see any of the amendments which he has recommended for the approval of the guarantee committee as a complication. In particular, it is the introduction of additional remuneration for the use of audiovisual works which would be collected in the framework of compulsory collective management and the application of so-called. extended collective management on the flat-rate licensing of all copyright works for any use on the Internet by any type of user. I presume that both proposals lack an analysis of their impact. The first of the proposals is contained in the inserted new paragraph 16, which complements the new section 25b, and in the related addition to paragraph 64 by inserting a new point (e) in § 97d para. 1. This introduces a claim to some additional remuneration for authors of audiovisual works and works of audiovisually used, who were already rewarded for the grant of the licence by the producer of this work, not only the film, but for example. even a commercial clip, but only one time. Moreover, after 10 years, users of these works should pay these authors by means of a collective administrator for each individual use. Of course, they have already paid for the licence given to the producer concerned, respectively. to the distributor. In fact, there would have been a double payment for the same, regardless of how high the original one-off licence fee was. All this in a situation where the Civil Code guarantees for these cases, but with appropriate criteria, the right to an appropriate additional reward, so-called. Best seller clause. The sequence of these claims does not solve the proposal. Although pressure is growing in other states to strengthen the negotiating positions of authors towards producers, the Ministry is not aware of any examples of such a proposed adjustment. Moreover, the proposal lacks a very important transitional provision in this case. In addition to that, I certainly cannot accept the amendment to Section 98f of the present paragraph 64, which practically eliminates the new comprehensive arrangement for the creation of tariffs, which has been particularly focused on recently. Ladies and gentlemen, I hope that I have been able to explain my concerns about the outcome of this government proposal in a clear manner. I firmly believe that you will take them into consideration in your decision-making. In the event that the bill is returned to committee, I believe that this discussion will take place as soon as possible and that a compromise solution will be found. I therefore propose a reduction of 30 days in this case. Thank you for your attention and understanding.
