ParlaMint-CZ_2016-02-10-ps2013-039-08-007-012.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, the proposal tabled concerns three areas. The first of these should contribute to facilitating entry into business by making it significantly cheaper and reducing the costs of setting up a new company. So if it is a very simple society, a completely simple esero without any peculiarities that anyone would have to judge, the cost will be below 100 euros. At the same time, we will also meet the condition of the European Union, the so-called preliminary condition to which the drawing of European funds is bound. However, I am just saying this to supplement, because I think it is primarily a good measure and good news for our business public. The second and third things are things that are harmonisational. For one thing, our right to adapt to Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council 650/2012. This concerns the so-called European succession certificate and aims to facilitate the negotiation of inheritance where the property is located in the territory of several states. Finally, the third area is in favour of amending the adaptation of our right to regulation No. 606/2013 on mutual recognition of protection measures in civil matters, where it is possible for the competent authority in the requested Member State to be able to adapt the facts of the safeguard measure where necessary for its smooth performance in that other State. In summary, three things: both a significant reduction in the company creation and then two harmonisations, that is, the introduction of inheritance certificates and the flexibility of the recognition of protective measures. Thank you.
