ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-24-ps2013-047-01-006-229.u2	Ladies and gentlemen, at the last meeting of the Chamber of Deputies, Mr Koníček spoke with the objection that he was not followed consistently under the resolution of the Chamber of Deputies No. 755 of 2003, which regulates the presentation of proposals for amendments to the approved agenda of the House meeting. At the meeting of the political Gremium, it was subsequently agreed that the amendment of this resolution would be prepared. This amendment was sent to you on 10 May, to all Members' clubs and non-attached Members, and on 19 May it was delivered to all of you as House Document No 4381. [[19.30 hours]] It is proposed that Articles 1 and 2 be deleted from the House's resolution so far, which provide for the impossibility of a fixed classification of points and the obligation to include existing or newly proposed points until the end of the relevant agenda blocks. On the other hand, the opportunity to present proposals in the course of 30 minutes should be maintained and the possibility for the House to proceed differently in duly justified cases. I present the motion for a resolution to you as House document No. 4381 for discussion and I recommend its approval in the version submitted. Thank you for your attention.
