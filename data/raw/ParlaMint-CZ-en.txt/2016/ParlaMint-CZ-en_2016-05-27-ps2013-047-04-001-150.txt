ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-27-ps2013-047-04-001-150.u2	Madam President, honourable Members, I have already introduced you to the substance of the proposed amendment to the State Enterprise Act in the previous discussion of this proposal. I would therefore just like to remind you today that the amendment sets out more precisely the rules for the management of state-owned enterprises. It further specifies the position of director of a state enterprise and strengthens the position of members of supervisory boards in the field of internal control of the undertaking and the competence of the founders towards state enterprises in terms of control of the management of funds and the management of state property. At the same time, the amendment brings the State Enterprise Act into line with the new general legislation governing the status of legal persons and their bodies, in particular the Civil Code.
