Mr President, colleagues, on the basis of Act No. 252/1997 Coll., on Agriculture and the Plan of Non-Legal Tasks of the Government for the first half of 2015, the Report on the State of Agriculture of the Czech Republic for 2014 is presented. The report on the state of agriculture of the Czech Republic, which has been published since 1994, is a statistical-information material representing events in the department in the previous year. According to national accounts statistics, the agricultural sector participated in total GDP formation at basic current prices of 1.82 %, representing a year-on-year slight increase of 0.01 percentage point. In the case of the food industry, on the other hand, the share of total GDP has fallen by 0,02 percentage points to 2,25 percent. The year 2014 represents a further strengthening of the stabilization of the agricultural sector with significant growth in all global indicators of the comprehensive agricultural account compared to the results of 2013. The economic result reached a record value of 2.9 billion in 2014. Crowns. Production of the agricultural sector increased by 6,5 % with growth in crop production and livestock production by 4.7 % and 10 percent respectively. Intermediate consumption grew slower, 4.4%, which was positively reflected in the growth of other global indicators. The evolution of agricultural producer prices in 2014 showed a year-on-year decrease of 3,7 %. Agricultural production, including fish, is a greater decline than the prices of entry into agriculture recorded, minus 1,3%. The share of agriculture in state budget expenditure decreased by 0.23 percentage points in 2014 to 3,85 percentage points. While the total expenditure of the State budget increased by 3.3 % year on year in 2014, the expenditure of the State into the agricultural sector decreased by 2.6 % compared to 2013. The reduction of employment in agriculture continued at a more moderate pace in 2014. The year-on-year decline in farm workers was 1.4 %, their number decreased to approximately 102,1 thousand. The share of agricultural workers in total employment in the national economy of the Czech Republic was 2.1 % and has not changed practically year on year. The turnover of Czech agrarian foreign trade is constantly increasing. In 2014, it increased by 32 billion yoy. CZK, that's 9.3% per 377.3 billion. Crowns. Of which, on the export side, the value increased by 18.9 billion. CZK, that's 11.8 per cent per 179.5 billion. CZK, and on the import side increased by 13.1 billion. CZK, that's 7.1 per cent per 197,7 billion. Crowns. As a result of this development, the negative balance of agrarian foreign trade in the Czech Republic decreased by 5.9 billion year on year. Kroner, that's almost a quarter to 18.2 billion. Crowns. The level of export cover has improved by 3.8 percentage points to 90.8%, which is the most favourable since the accession of the Czech Republic to the European Union. The share of the balance sheet deficit in Czech agrarian exports has decreased from one-third to one-tenth in the last five years. A summary of the 2014 Report on the state of agriculture of the Czech Republic also includes an overview of the necessary measures from the report. The report on the state of agriculture of the Czech Republic for 2014 was discussed in the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies on 23 September 2015 by resolution 81, delivered to Members as press 595/1. Thank you for your attention.
