The notification was made after the approval of the draft law by the Government of the Czech Republic, as agreed between representatives of the Ministry of Finance and the European Commission. As part of the notification, a detailed opinion was delivered by the European Commission and Malta and comments by Austria. In response to the comments made, the Czech Republic sent a report to the European Commission on the measures it intends to take, when the European Commission responded to this in February 2016 by expressing the content of accepting the arguments raised by the Czech Republic and the comments on the measures which the Czech Republic intends to adopt so that the draft law does not create obstacles to the freedom to provide services or the freedom of establishment of service providers in the internal market. The measures mentioned are reflected in the so-called by the rapporteur's notification amendment. As opposed to the current situation, the Gambling Act provides a comprehensive regulation for the operation of gambling, where, in conjunction with the aforementioned proposals of the Gambling Tax Act, it creates a modern and effective regulatory framework, the content of which is balanced regulation to protect people from the negative effects of gambling, while ensuring an appropriate business environment with adequate tax revenue for the State. The finding of this compromise was completed by the Government when, as a result of the commentary procedure on the Gambling Tax Act, the bills had to be aligned from the perspective of these government objectives. In view of the events of the last few days when the proposed blocking measures for online gambling have become subject to criticism, I consider it important to mention that in no case do these measures constitute an unconstitutional intervention in the fundamental rights and freedoms of our citizens, as is often misinterpreted, since, in view of the proposed system of administrative management, procedural instruments of protection and judicial review, these measures have been considered proportional by the Government of the Czech Republic and a number of experts. It should be noted that lower wins can also lead to lower attractiveness of playing in general.
