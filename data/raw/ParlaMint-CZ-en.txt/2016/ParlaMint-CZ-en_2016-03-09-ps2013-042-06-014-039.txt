ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-09-ps2013-042-06-014-039.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce the draft customs law. On 1. 5. The new EU-wide legislation on customs matters in the form of the European Union Customs Code will become effective in 2016. The proposed law should adapt the Czech rule of law to this code. In doing so, they will replace the current customs law. Customs issues include, in particular, the supervision of international trade in the European Union and the management of customs duties which are the income of the EU budget. A substantial majority of the customs matters are already covered by the Union Customs Code, which is directly applicable by the Regulation. National regulation is thus left rather restrictive space, especially in the process and competence area. The first of these applies general procedural rules of the tax and administrative order, the second is given the authority of the Customs Administration of the Czech Republic. The proposed customs law then constitutes a law which links all those provisions together and adapts them to the customs area. In addition to adapting to the Union Customs Code, the proposed law responds to certain practical problems in the customs area and removes certain legislative uncertainties and duplications. At the same time, in some areas it reduces the administrative burden on importers and other business entities. These include, for example, the possibility of guaranteeing a customs duty by a direct representative or the possibility of re-use of a compound amount to secure a customs duty, which will simplify the customs procedure in particular for small and medium-sized importers. The proposal also provides for a reduction of sanctions against importers who themselves report errors in customs declarations and pay the duty due. The general effectiveness of the Act is proposed on 1 May 2016. I conclude with my introductory words and note that it will be proposed to shorten the deadline for discussing the draft law in committees to 30 days. Thank you for your attention.
