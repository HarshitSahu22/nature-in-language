ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-14-ps2013-044-03-008-145.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, from 17 to 19 May this year, 44 will take place in Prague. The European Regional Conference of INTERPOL. The Regional Conference is a platform for discussing current trends in crime and a strategy for improving cooperation between the Member States included in the INTERPOL system. The necessary prerequisite for holding this conference is the granting of the privileges and immunities of Interpol and the participants of the conference. INTERPOL calls for privileges and immunities from all host countries for its conferences. This issue is not addressed in any bilateral or multilateral treaty in relation to INTERPOL, for which reason this agreement has been prepared. By agreement, interpol representatives will provide the conditions normally provided to international organisations (?), that is to say, these members will enjoy the same rights and obligations as other international organisations arriving in the Czech Republic. These will be provided to the participants of the conference in connection with the fulfilment of their official duties and their tasks. [[12.20 hours]] Even in this case, the scope of privileges and immunities does not deviate from the usual standards granted. INTERPOL's officials will then benefit from the privileges and immunities that are usually granted within diplomatic congregations. However, all conditions, as well as the advantages of facilitating entry into the territory of the Czech Republic, are limited to the duration of this conference and will expire after that date. Thank you for your attention.
