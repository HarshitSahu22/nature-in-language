Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Chile on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income and property taxes signed in Santiago de Chile on 2 December 2015 /Household printing 705/ - first reading I ask the Prime Minister and Finance Minister Andrej Babiš to give the opening statement. Please, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-10-ps2013-042-07-003-145.u2	Thank you for the floor. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, you are under a double taxation treaty with the Republic of Chile signed in Santiago de Chile on 2 December 2015. The Czech Republic currently has international agreements to prevent double taxation with more than 80 countries in the world. But Chile is still lacking among them. In international taxation, it is absolutely essential that a country which has a certain role in the business and financial intentions of Czech taxpayers has a tax treaty with our state. And I'm sure that's the case with Chile. The negotiated contract does not replace national tax rules and cannot therefore create a new tax obligation, but only regulates the obligation arising from national rules. They deal with cases where the right to levy tax is enshrined in their national law by both signatory States. In these situations, the treaty is certainly superior to the national regulation. As a standard, the Treaty provides for basic forms of cooperation between the competent authorities of both countries, as well as the principle of non-discrimination, the informal resolution of disputes arising from its implementation, and ensures the relevant exchange of information between the competent authorities of the two States. The Treaty, which is currently proposed in relations between the Czech Republic and Chile, deals with taxation and exclusion of international double taxation of all types of income and property. It was prepared on the basis of OECD and UN model models, and, of course, responds to the internal tax regulations of both countries. The contract uses as the main method of excluding double taxation of income and property of Czech tax residents the method of simple credit of foreign tax. On the basis of what has already been said, I therefore propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic take account of these facts when discussing this international treaty and that this treaty be ordered at first reading to the relevant House Committees, which will certainly examine it in detail. Thank you for your attention.
