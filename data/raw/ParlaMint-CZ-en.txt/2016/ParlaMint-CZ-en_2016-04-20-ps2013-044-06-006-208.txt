Proposal by the Chamber of Deputies for the appointment of a member of the Radio and Television Broadcasting Council As for the next item, i.e. the motion of the Chamber of Deputies to appoint a member of the great media council, 154 ballots were issued here as well. The Commission has only received back 150 and therefore four have not been surrendered. Number of votes reached: Milan Bouška 26, Jan Mrzena 53 and František Vostál 32. Therefore, no one has reached the needy kvora, no one has been elected in the first round, and since we are voting for one candidate, two nominees with the highest number of votes achieved go to the second round, so Jan Mrzena moves with 53 votes and František Vostál with 32 votes.
