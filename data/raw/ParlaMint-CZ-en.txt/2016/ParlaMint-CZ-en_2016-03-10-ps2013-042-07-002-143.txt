It also includes the area of sickness, health and pension benefits, the area of accidents at work and occupational diseases. Again, it is based on the generally accepted principles of equality of loading citizens and belonging to insurance in the state of employment and, for example, on the addition of periods of insurance completed in both states and the export of benefits abroad.
