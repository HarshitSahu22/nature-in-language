ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-08-ps2013-053-07-007-218.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, for information, I present government material for the protection of the airspace of the Republic of Iceland in the framework of NATO operation in 2016, which was approved by the Government on 16 March this year by Resolution No. 217. Our work in Iceland is based on a regular request from the High Command of the Allied Forces in Europe and has been planned for a long time, so unlike our work in Iceland last year, this was not an extraordinary deployment. Cooperation between allies in the protection of alliance airspace is one of NATO's most important contributions to defence. This year, a contingent of the Czech Republic participated in the protection of the airspace in the number of 75 persons with five aircraft JAS-39 Gripen, one backup, from 26 September to 10 November 2016. This is the third action of forces and means of the defence department within the alliance protection of Iceland's airspace. We first worked in Iceland in 2014, for the second time last year. The planned financial costs amounted to CZK 36 million 420 thousand and will be drawn, respectively. were drawn from the budget chapter of the Ministry of Defence. Thank you for your attention.
