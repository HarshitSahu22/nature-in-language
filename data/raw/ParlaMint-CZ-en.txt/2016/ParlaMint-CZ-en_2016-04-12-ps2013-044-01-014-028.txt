ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-12-ps2013-044-01-014-028.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce a government bill amending certain laws in connection with the adoption of an atomic law. This law brings a set of legislative technical changes following the new comprehensive legislation on the peaceful use of nuclear energy. The reason for adopting this legislation is to bring the laws in question into line with the proposed legal situation. These are minor changes in a very limited number of laws. Probably the most important change is the fundamental amendment of Act No. 18/1997 Coll., on the peaceful use of nuclear energy and on the amendment and addition of certain laws, which is to be abolished except for a few provisions governing the liability for nuclear damage. The legal regulation of liability for nuclear damage is thus left in the residual form of Act No. 18/1997 Coll. outside the framework of the adaptation of the peaceful use of nuclear energy and ionising radiation. Following the future development of international law in the field of compensation for damage caused by nuclear accidents, it is envisaged to replace the residual act No. 18/1997 a new regulation of nuclear liability. Thank you for your attention.
