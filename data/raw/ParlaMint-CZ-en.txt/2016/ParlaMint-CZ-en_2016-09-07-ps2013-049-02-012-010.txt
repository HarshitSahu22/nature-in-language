Ladies and gentlemen, second reading continues the discussion of the Government's bill, which is an accompanying law on the bill on budgetary liability rules, in which it implements the Constitutional Act on budgetary responsibility, which was to take place by the end of 2013. The proposal also responds to the obligation to properly transpose into national law the Council Directive on requirements for the budgetary frameworks of the Member States. The proposed bill amends 19 legislation, in particular with regard to the introduction of new numerical fiscal rules, the imposition of an obligation on public institutions to draw up a draft budget and a medium-term outlook for the budget and to publish it and by the establishment of a National Budget Board, and in particular the choice of its chairman and members. At the same time, I would like to agree with the amendments that have been approved during the discussion of this House's press in the Committee on Budgets. Thank you for your attention.
