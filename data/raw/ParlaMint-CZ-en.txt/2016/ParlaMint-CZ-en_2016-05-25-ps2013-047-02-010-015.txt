ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-25-ps2013-047-02-010-015.u2	Madam President, honourable Members, the aim of the bill is to ensure greater transparency in the financing of electoral campaigns. The bill submitted is in line with the Government's programming declaration, under which the government has committed itself to tightening up the system of financing political parties by introducing spending limits for electoral campaigns. The essence of the proposal lies in a uniform regulation of the rules on electoral campaigns and their financing in the laws governing elections to Parliament, the election of the President of the Republic, the election to regional councils and the election to the European Parliament. [[16.00 hours]] The definition of the electoral campaign and its time-limit are also introduced and the obligation of candidate bodies to set up a transparent account for the financing of the electoral campaign is laid down. Control of the financing of the electoral campaign shall be entrusted to the Office for the Management of Political Parties and Political Movements. This office should be established by an amendment to the Act on Association in Political Parties and in Political Movements, which is simultaneously discussed as the House Press 569. In addition to the proposed legislation on electoral campaigns and their financing, the contribution of political parties to elections to the Chamber of Deputies and elections to the European Parliament is increased from 15 000 for electoral costs. CZK 19,000. Kč. The increase is responsible for inflation over the period since the introduction of this contribution. Thank you for your attention.
