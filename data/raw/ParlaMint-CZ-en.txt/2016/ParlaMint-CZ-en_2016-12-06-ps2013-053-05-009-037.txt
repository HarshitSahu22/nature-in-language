ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-06-ps2013-053-05-009-037.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr Vice-President, Members, the aim of this bill is to implement the transposition of Directive 96/71/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council on the posting of workers in the framework of the provision of services, and to amend Regulation No 1024/2012 on administrative cooperation through a system for the exchange of information on the internal market, which has been adopted in particular to prevent, prevent and combat abuse and circumvent the relevant rules imposed by undertakings which make unfair or fraudulent use of the benefits of the free movement of services within the EU. In the Labour Inspection Act, the State Labour Inspection Office provides for new powers to check compliance with the legislation in the performance of the work in the context of transnational provision of services and in the field of cross-border communication. In addition, the rules for the delivery and fulfilment of information obligations in the context of cross-border cooperation, including the European Commission, are being newly anchored. In the Labour Code, the existing provision of broadcasting for the performance of work under the transnational provision of services is supplemented by an institution of guarantee for the remuneration due and other amounts not provided by the sending employer by the person to whom the employee was posted. The Employment Act proposes an amendment to the employer of mandatory registered information for certain posted employees and extends the obligation to have copies of documents demonstrating the existence of employment relationship with the sending employer in the context of transnational provision of services at the place of work. As has already been said here, in view of the length of the legislative process, the deadline for transposition of the directive of the European Parliament expired on 18 June 2016, and the Government has therefore submitted a draft law to the House with a proposal for consent at first reading as soon as possible. Thank you.
