ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-23-ps2013-042-10-010-044.u1	44. Government bill on security activities and amendment of related laws /German press 495/ - first reading On behalf of the Government, Milan Chovanec, the Interior Minister, will present this proposal. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-23-ps2013-042-10-010-044.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the content of the bill is based, in particular, on the new legislation prepared in the previous parliamentary term by the then subcommittee on the municipal police and private security services of the Defence and Security Committee of the Chamber of Deputies. It is from our point of view a balanced proposal for a new law, which regulates security activities carried out mainly by entrepreneurs for other or anyone for their own use, i.e. so-called. non-commercially. The basic objectives of the proposed new legislation are: the exclusion of private security services from the trade law regime, the establishment of clear entry conditions for the performance of security activities by entrepreneurs and their employees, the authorisation to operate in the field of commercial security on the basis of a licence issued by the Ministry of the Interior for a period of ten years after the previous expression of intelligence services and the Police of the Czech Republic, the categorization of individual security activities according to their specific content, the establishment of conditions for the performance of security activities by natural and legal persons for their own use on so-called. non-commercial basis. Another criterion governing this standard is the adaptation of the basic principles of the relations of private security services with other entities. Another criterion is the definition of offences and administrative offences in the field of security activities and penalties imposed on them, including the withdrawal of licences, and, last but not least, the establishment by the State of the control of the performance of security activities of these services. First of all, I consider the establishment of a legal framework for the performance of security activities and a clear definition of the scope of private security services as the basis of the new legislation. Another aspect that I consider important is the security clearance of commercial security entities. Next, we have an ambition to eliminate unreliable entrepreneurs operating e.g. in the field of so-called gray economies. Another is the improvement of the provision of services to private security customers in all respects, including for example. due to the significantly higher requirements imposed on the qualifying assumptions of the entities offering these services. Last but not least, it is the elimination of the lack of legislation that the Czech Republic has not yet had, and we are only one of the few countries that do not have such a regulation within the European Union. The new legislation carries with it increased costs on the part of the State, in particular on the side of the Ministry of Interior, which are related to personnel and material provision of extradition, possibly withdrawal or suspension of valid licences for the performance of private security activities, and to register persons who will carry out selected security activities for their own use, and not least to check compliance with the obligations of operators and persons who will carry out selected security activities for their own use, impose sanctions and address any complaints and objections by the entities concerned. Finally, let me express my conviction that the outcome of the next legislative process will be the adoption of a quality law that will benefit entrepreneurs in the field of private security services and, above all, their customers. This will undoubtedly contribute to the comprehensive parliamentary amendment prepared by the subcommittee on the Police of the Czech Republic, the municipal police and private security services, aimed in particular at tightening the conditions for the performance of private detectives' security activities so that such services cannot be abused in the future. Thank you for your attention.
