ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-10-ps2013-042-07-009-136.u2	Thank you for the floor. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to justify the Government's proposal to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to agree to ratify the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Kingdom of the Netherlands in relation to Aruba on the exchange of information on tax matters. On 29 June 2015, it was signed for the Czech Republic by Mrs. Jana Reinišová, an extraordinary and authorised Ambassador of the Czech Republic in the Kingdom of the Netherlands, including Curacao, Saint Martin Island and Aruba Island, and Mr. Alfonso Boekhoudt, authorised minister - representative of Aruba to the Dutch Government. The negotiations with jurisdiction with the preferential tax regime have resulted in 14 final drafts of the text of this type of international treaty, which subsequently undergoes a legislative process leading to ratification by the President. Nine agreements have already entered into force, which are announced in the Collection of International Treaties. The aim of the agreement is to collect taxes agreed upon by the Contracting Parties in expert negotiations, according to the text of the agreement in the Czech Republic tax of all kinds and naming and in Aruba tax of all kinds and naming including excise duties. Information provided under this Agreement shall be subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations. By concluding the agreement, the Czech Republic will be given the opportunity to request the competent authority of the Contracting State and information that is relevant for the determination, determination and security of payment of taxes in the event of an investigation or prosecution of tax offences. However, due to the legal constraints laid down by the domestic law, cooperation in obtaining evidence reserved for judicial authorities in criminal matters does not include cooperation. The Contracting Parties shall also undertake to provide, upon request, information held by banks or other financial institutions. The approval of an agreement on the basis of which tax relevant information can be exchanged with jurisdictions considered until recently as tax havens should lead to an effective reduction in tax evasion. Agreements of this type act as a preventive measure to strengthen the payment morale of Czech taxpayers. I therefore recommend agreeing to ratify this agreement. Thank you for your attention.
