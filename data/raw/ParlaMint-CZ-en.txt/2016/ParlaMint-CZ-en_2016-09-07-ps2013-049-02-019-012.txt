Government bill amending Act No. 96/1993 Coll., on Building Savings and State Support for Building Savings and Supplementary Act of the Czech National Council No. 586/1992 Coll., on Income Tax, as amended by the Czech National Council Act No. 35/1993 Coll., as amended /house print 782/ - second reading Even this proposal, under the authority of the Government, will be presented by the Minister of Finance Andrej Babiš. Please, Minister.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-07-ps2013-049-02-019-012.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly summarize what I have already said in the first reading of the amendment to the Act on State Savings and State Support for Building Savings. Again, I would like to stress that this is only a technical amendment which does not interfere in any way with the amount of State aid or the conditions for obtaining it. The reason for submitting this technical amendment is in particular to make the exercise of supervision of compliance with the conditions for granting state aid for building savings more effective. The Ministry of Finance will be able to respond more flexibly to individual citizens' initiatives, as it will have legal support in obtaining the necessary information from building savings banks outside the control system, i.e. faster and with less bureaucracy. The process will also be simplified for participants in building savings themselves, who will not have to supplement their individual incentives with an officially verified signature. For example, this is the case. the citizens' complaints that they did not receive State aid. Standard checks in construction savings banks will, of course, continue to run according to the control rules. The proposed changes further regulate the terminology and specification of the provisions in the field of transmission of information on State aid returned between the construction savings bank and the Ministry of Finance. The impact on the participants in the construction savings is therefore minimal and positive, as other changes resulting from this amendment include the addition of a registered partner among the loved ones. Therefore, it will be possible to provide a new loan from the construction savings also to finance the housing needs of this person. This eliminates unjustified discrimination against registered partners. Finally, I would like to point out that the amendment, which is of a purely legislative technical nature and which was approved by the Committee on Budgets on 31 August, is in agreement with the Ministry of Finance. In the light of the above, I ask you to give your consent to the proposed bill. Thank you for your attention.
