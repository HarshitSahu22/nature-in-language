Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Council of Europe Convention on Measures against Human trafficking (Warsaw, 16 May 2005) /German press 894/ - second reading I ask that the Minister of Justice Robert Pelikán take the floor. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-01-ps2013-053-03-005-176.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, very briefly, because I presented the convention in more detail at first reading. It is a Council of Europe Convention, one of those conventions which are more symbolic to us, because our legal order already meets all the requirements set out in this Convention. However, it is important for us because by introducing measures against human trafficking, it protects international values that are expensive to us. Unfortunately, we are today the only European country or the only state of the European Union that has not yet ratified this Convention, although it has already been signed on 16 May 2005 in Warsaw. Therefore, I beg to support its ratification.
