The relevant bodies of the proposed legislation are the competent administrative authorities, i.e. the Civil Aviation Authority, the Ministry of Transport as its superior appeal authority, the Institute for the Detection of Causes of Accidents. Other operators concerned are Václav Havel Airport Prague operators. It has an impact on service providers and air operators in the Czech Republic. It will not have an impact on the budget, only on the increase of certain administrative activities of the institutions concerned. So much news.
