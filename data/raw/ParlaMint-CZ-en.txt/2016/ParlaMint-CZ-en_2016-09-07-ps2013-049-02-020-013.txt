Government bill amending Act No. 563/1991 Coll., on Accounting, as amended /house print 812/ - second reading The Minister of Finance and Deputy Prime Minister Andrej Babiš will also present this proposal under the authority of the Government. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-07-ps2013-049-02-020-013.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly state that the proposal for an amendment to the Accounting Act primarily addresses the transposition of Directive 2014/95/EU as regards the presentation of non-financial information. This obligation will apply exclusively to large enterprises and large parent companies, which are also public-interest entities. In particular, non-financial information relating to corporate social responsibility, such as environmental protection, social and employment issues, respect for and respect for human rights, the fight against corruption and bribery, will be provided. The main reason for this obligation is to increase the comparability of non-financial information within the European Union and the ever-increasing requirements for business transparency. This part of the amendment to the Accounting Act translates a European Union regulation in which there is no discretion, while the draft amendment does not go beyond the requirements of the EU directive. The second issue referred to in the draft amendment to the Accounting Act concerns the extension of the possibility of converting entities which are not commercial corporations. The aim of this extension is to increase the legal certainty of accounting entities which are not commercial corporations and are undergoing a transformation process. I would like to ask you, fellow Members, to release the motion by the Chamber of Deputies until the third reading. Thank you for your attention.
