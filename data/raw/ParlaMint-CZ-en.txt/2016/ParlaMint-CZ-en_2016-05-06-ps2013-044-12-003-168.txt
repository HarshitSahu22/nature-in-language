The main principle of the proposed legislation is the introduction of a free legally binding electronic form of the Collection of Laws and International Treaties. Another of the principles is the introduction of a very binding full text of the amendment to the legislation. In this way, each amendment will also contain a legally binding full text of the legislation indicating any amendments to this proposal. The proposal is further aimed at improving the quality and efficiency of the legislative process. The draft legislation shall be submitted and discussed electronically. Thank you for your attention.
