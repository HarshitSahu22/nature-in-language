The storage of carbon dioxide into natural rock structures is part of a technology consisting of the capture of CO2 from industrial plants, its transport to storage and subsequent injection into a suitable underground geological formation. The Act on the storage of carbon dioxide into natural rock structures transposes the requirements of the European Community Directive on the geological storage of CO2 but at the same time states that it is not possible to allow the storage of CO2 into natural rock structures until 2020.
