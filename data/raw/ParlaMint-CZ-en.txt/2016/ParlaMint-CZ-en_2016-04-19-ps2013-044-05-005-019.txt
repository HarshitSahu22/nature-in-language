ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-19-ps2013-044-05-005-019.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly present to you the Government bill amending certain laws in the field of money circulation and the foreign exchange economy and repealing Act No. 219/1995 Coll., the Foreign Exchange Act, as amended. The bill is primarily a technical amendment to the Act on the circulation of banknotes and coins. The Act on the circulation of banknotes and coins has been effective for about four years and its use in practice has shown the need to make certain technical changes to it. In particular, for example, the exchange procedure for non-standardly damaged banknotes and coins is simplified, also reducing the requirements for so-called deposit ATMs so that they can only refuse and do not have to hold them back, which was technically difficult to do. The proposal also repeals the foreign exchange law. During the past 20 years, the liberalisation of the foreign exchange economy has resulted in a significant loss of the foreign exchange law's materiality. Moreover, the legislation on exchange was transferred to a separate law on exchange activities. The foreign exchange law is therefore only a torsion today, which can be abolished without any problems and at least partly contribute to the clarification of our legal order. In connection with the abolition of the foreign exchange law, there is also an amendment to the crisis law and the Criminal Code. The Committee on Budgets discussed the draft law and recommended its approval without amendments. Thank you for your attention.
