The purpose of this law is to create a new institution, the Agency for Enterprise and Innovation, which will be separated from CzechInvest. The reason for this separation is to negotiate with the European Commission that it is necessary for those CzechInvest workers currently engaged in the allocation of European money to fall under the service law.
