ParlaMint-CZ_2016-06-29-ps2013-048-02-005-151.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the House press 702 is a government proposal amending Act No. 143/2001 Coll., on the Protection of Competition. The reasons for the submission were three - compliance with the Civil Code, that is one. Secondly, increase the legal certainty of the parties to administrative proceedings and thirdly respond to the current case law of the European Court of Human Rights and the Constitutional Court of the Czech Republic. In the course of the discussion before the second reading of the Act in question in the Economic Committee, which was designated as a guarantee committee, one amendment was tabled. The proposal regulates the provisions of § 22 para. This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. The reasons for the proposal are based on the fact that the new Public Procurement Act no longer provides for the possibility of imposing a ban on transactions. Furthermore, the proposal regulates the rules of legal access of the Office to the basic registers maintained by the Ministry of the Interior. No further amendments were tabled at second reading on 24 May 2016. No further amendments have also been tabled in the negotiations before the third reading in the Guarantee Economic Committee. The motion to reject the bill was not filed either. The petitioner agrees with the proposed amendments to the text and considers that the amendment of the proposal resulting from the negotiations of the guarantee committee is appropriate and agrees with Resolution No. 286 Economic Committee of the 39th Meeting. In view of the above, it appears desirable to support the amendment to the Competition Act, as proposed by the Economic Committee. I would like to ask you for your support for the approval of the amendment submitted. Thank you for your attention.
