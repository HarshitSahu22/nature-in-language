ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-08-ps2013-053-07-008-219.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, the subject is clear from what has already been said here. The material contains information about the newly planned exercises of the armed forces this year. These are 18 exercises outside the territory of the Czech Republic and 7 exercises of the armed forces of other states in our territory. In addition, specifying information on 38 exercises already approved by the government, which changed some attributes beyond the approved framework during their preparation, these were the numbers of assigned forces, resources, deadlines, venue, etc. The changes are based on the current needs of the Czech Army for its effective preparation for foreign operations and also take into account the requirements of partners in the field of preparation for emergency operations or bilateral or regional cooperation. The exercises referred to in the 1st Supplement are financed under the approved chapter of the Ministry of Defence budget for 2016. Thank you for your attention.
