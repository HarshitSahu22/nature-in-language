ParlaMint-CZ_2016-10-18-ps2013-050-01-007-008.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Colleagues, colleagues, allow me, on behalf of the Minister of Agriculture, to present the Government's bill amending Act No. 156/1998 Coll., on fertilisers, soil aids, auxiliary plant preparations and substrates, and on agrochemical testing of agricultural soils, i.e. the Fertilizer Act, as amended. The proposed law removes overlap between the definition of an auxiliary substance under the Fertilizers Act and the definition of a plant protection product under the Plant Health Care Act. The Czech Republic committed itself to this amendment in 2014 in the framework of proceedings for infringement of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. The aim is to stop the proceedings and thus avoid any action before the European Court of Justice. The process of reporting mutually recognised fertilisers is also newly regulated. Some other Member States of the European Union, such as Germany, France or Spain, are also in place in their legal system as well as the so-called preliminary notification in the proposed version of the notification. More than five years of experience with the mutual recognition system has proved its justification in the field of fertiliser trade. The contents of the authorisation for the registration of fertilisers have been rephrased in particular for the purpose of clearly defining the conditions under which the fertiliser will be put into circulation. It was added that even in case of a change in the label or package leaflet, an amendment to the marketing authorisation should be requested. The label or package leaflet of fertilizer will newly contain information that will provide the consumer with more detailed information on the product. A new provision is also proposed which lays down the conditions under which liquid fertilisers and auxiliary substances on agricultural parcels may be applied by air. The Government Bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 33rd meeting of 23 June 2016 and recommended by its resolution The Chamber of Deputies shall approve the Government's bill as amended by the amendments adopted. The amendments adopted are in line with the concept of the petitioner, they will contribute to improving the government's bill, and I therefore agree with them. Thank you for your attention.
