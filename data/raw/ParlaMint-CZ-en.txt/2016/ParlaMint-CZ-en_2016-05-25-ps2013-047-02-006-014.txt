ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-25-ps2013-047-02-006-014.u2	Thank you for the floor. Madam President, honourable Members, allow me very briefly to comment on this proposal. This is House Press No. 764, which is an accompanying law to the Government Bill on Trust Services for Electronic Transactions. The aim of the bill is to reflect the changes introduced by the regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market. This means it is a purely accompanying law to the previous law. Everything else has been said. Thank you.
