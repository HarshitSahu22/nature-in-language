ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-03-ps2013-042-03-007-144.u1	144. Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification amendment of Appendix III to the Rotterdam Convention on the Prior Consent Procedure for Certain Dangerous Chemicals and Pesticides in International Trade adopted in Geneva on 15 May 2015 /German press 698/ - first reading I ask the Minister of the Environment to take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-03-ps2013-042-03-007-144.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. I'll be very brief. The Czech Republic has been a Contracting Party to the Rotterdam Convention since 24 February 2004. The amendment to Annex III is made to include a new chemical in this Annex, namely methamidophos pesticide. In accordance with Directive 91/414/EEC, Member States of the European Union have not applied that substance since 30 June 2007. As part of EU legislation, this substance is listed in Annex I, Part 3, to the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on the export and import of dangerous chemicals. Methamidophos is not used in the Czech Republic. However, as a Contracting Party to the Convention, the Czech Republic has undertaken to fulfil its obligations and the inclusion of a new chemical in Annex III is an important measure for the fulfilment of the Convention's objective. The admission of methamidophos to Appendix III to the Convention has no impact on public administration, business sector or state budget. The Rotterdam Convention is a Treaty of the presidential category, and the amendment of its Appendix III also applies to the Czech Republic requires the approval of the Parliament of the Czech Republic and subsequent ratification by the President of the Republic. Thank you for your attention.
