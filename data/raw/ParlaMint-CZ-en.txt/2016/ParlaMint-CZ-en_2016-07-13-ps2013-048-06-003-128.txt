ParlaMint-CZ_2016-07-13-ps2013-048-06-003-128.u2	Good morning, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen. As already mentioned here, I present to you the agreement between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Chile on the work holiday programme, which was signed last October in Prague. The essence of this employment holiday agreement is that young people between the ages of 18 and 30 would be able to make a registration stay in the territory of the other Party through this agreement. This means Čech in Chile, young Chilan in the Czech Republic, and under this agreement they could not only stay in that country, but they could work there for a maximum period of one year. You know we already have such an agreement with several countries like New Zealand, South Korea, Canada, and Israel. Now there's a similar deal being made with Chile. Just for your information, to be clear, if you want to go to Chile today, you can go without a visa within three months for a tourist stay. And the whole point of the deal is that there could be young people here from 18 to 30 years of age all year long and, of course, they could be working too. After that year, they must return, but in the case of this agreement between Chile and the Czech Republic, there is one more thing that it is possible to ask for a similar working holiday again. That means he has to come back after that year, but then he can report back to her and get her. So there's still a chance to issue a similar permit repeatedly, not just once. So I think if I were to summarize that Chile is an attractive country for the Czech Republic and it is certainly attractive for Czech students and youth. And I have no doubt that he will be interested in using this work agreement, because for the time being, wherever we have negotiated it, if we already have experience, it turns out that it is a very good user or successfully used thing. I think there is a great chance that it will be similar in this agreement between Chile and the Czech Republic. I have no idea what would be crucial to the vote yet, but I think that is in a nutshell the essence of what I am presenting to you, and I believe that we will find a clear consensus on this in the House. Thank you.
