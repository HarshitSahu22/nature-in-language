ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-16-ps2013-049-08-002-026.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present, during my second reading, a draft law amending Act No. 61/1988 Coll., on mining activities, explosives and the State Mining Administration, as amended, and Act No. 83/2013 Coll., on the labelling and traceability of explosives for civil use. The main reasons for the amendment are three. The first reason is to complete the implementation process of the European Parliament and Council. [[Directive]] The amendment on questions relating to the supply of explosives for civil use on the market and supervision of them is supplemented by Act No. 90/2016 Coll., on the assessment of conformity of specified products when they are made available on the market. The second reason lies in the fulfilment of Government resolution 33 of 14 January 2015, which was adopted as a response to events in Vrbetice. The amendment unites terminology and requirements of the Act on Explosives and the Act on Weapons. It specifies and unifies the decision-making process in doubt as to whether or not a matter is an explosive. It introduces binding opinions of district mining authorities in building procedures for the authorisation and establishment of safety zones for explosives management buildings. The last reason is the implementation of partial changes, the need for which resulted from application practice. For example, the previous regulation related to the control and maintenance of destroyed mining works is being clarified. In view of the factual nature of the House press and its comprehensive discussion by the various ministries, by the professional public as well as by colleagues of the Economic Committee, I ask you, honourable Members, to support this proposal. In this context, I also draw attention to the deadline set by the European Union for the implementation of the relevant directive. That's all. Thank you for your attention. The Act was supported by Parliament's Economic Committee on 8 June 2016 at second reading. Thank you for your attention.
