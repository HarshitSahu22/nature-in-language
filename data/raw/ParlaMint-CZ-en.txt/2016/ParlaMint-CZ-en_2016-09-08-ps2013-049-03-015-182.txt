ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-08-ps2013-049-03-015-182.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, may I present the Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the Framework Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Austria on cross-border cooperation in the field of medical rescue services signed on 21 January 2016 in Znojmo. So far, cross-border cooperation between the Czech Republic and Austria, which also includes medical rescue services as part of the IRS, has been regulated only in case of disaster assistance and emergencies. The main motive for establishing cross-border cooperation in this area is the time-frame for the availability of medical rescue services in border areas. Cross-border cooperation on the Czech side will affect the South Bohemia Region, the Vysočina Region and the South Moravian Region, and the federal states of Upper Austria and Lower Austria in Austria. With regard to the national division of competences in the field of medical rescue services in both countries, where they have legal responsibility in the Czech Republic for its provision of the region and in Austria the Land is responsible, the submitted international contract is conceived as a framework contract. In negotiating the text, the framework agreement between the Czech Republic and the Federal Republic of Germany served as a model. The framework agreement defines the basic legal framework for cross-border cooperation, allows for exceptions to existing national legislation and leaves room for the specification of the rules and conditions of such cooperation in individual border areas to the agreement of authorised entities, that is in the Czech Republic of the regions concerned and in Austria of the Länder. The use and effective fulfilment of cross-border cooperation will thus be at the decision of the regions and envisages the conclusion of separate arrangements for cross-border cooperation. The Government's proposal was discussed on 5 April 2016 in the Senate Committee on Health and Social Policy and in the Committee on Foreign Affairs, in the plenary of the Chamber of Deputies of Parliament on 12 April 2016 at 1st reading. The Committee on Health of the Chamber of Deputies of Parliament discussed the Government proposal on 28 April 2016 and the Foreign Committee of the Chamber of Deputies on 26 May 2016. On 25 May 2016, Parliament's Senate agreed to ratify. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I am asking you to give my consent to ratify this international treaty and thank you for your attention.
