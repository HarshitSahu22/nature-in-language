ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-06-ps2013-049-01-001-001.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly remind you of the draft law amending certain laws in the field of money circulation and the foreign exchange economy and repealing Act No. 219/1995 Coll., Foreign Exchange Act, as amended. The bill is primarily a technical amendment to the Act on the circulation of banknotes and coins. For example, it simplifies the exchange procedure for non-standardly damaged banknotes and coins and also reduces the requirements for so-called. deposit ATMs. The proposal also repeals the foreign exchange law, as it has become a mere torsion as a result of the liberalisation of the foreign exchange economy, which can be abolished without a problem, and at least partly contribute to clarifying our legal order. In connection with the abolition of the foreign exchange law, there is an amendment to the crisis law and the Criminal Code. The Senate returned the bill to the Chamber of Deputies as amended by the two amendments adopted. The amendments clearly define the relationship between two provisions of the crisis law and regulate the effectiveness of the law. I can already express my consent to the adoption of the law, as approved by the Senate, for the plaintiffs. Thank you for your attention.
