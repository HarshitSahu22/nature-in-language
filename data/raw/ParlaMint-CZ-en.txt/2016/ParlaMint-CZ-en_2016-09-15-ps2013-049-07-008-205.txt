ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-15-ps2013-049-07-008-205.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Members of Parliament, by submitting this Convention for ratification approval, I think we are fulfilling the long debt that we have here. I repeat again what Mr President said, it is a convention of 16 May 2005, which you have not heard, more than ten years old. It entered into force on 1 February 2008, it was also some time ago. And at this point, we are the only country - the only country in the European Union that has not yet ratified it. It is a convention that has a completely commendable purpose and content. The Convention, which aims to strengthen internationally the measures put in place to limit human trafficking as one of the most reprehensible forms of crime. Our right is in full compliance with the obligations which we may take upon ourselves by ratifying this Convention, with the only exception where we also make reservations in this respect, and that is the scope of our criminal law's personnel force. Unlike what the Convention assumes, our criminal law does not interfere in cases where the victim was a Czech national, but otherwise the matter had no relation to the Czech Republic at all, and at the same time the territory where it would have happened would not have been criminal. It is a situation, I think, rather theoretical, but we must cover it formally. So that's also the content of the document. I think that's enough to begin with, and I'm asking for your support. Thank you.
