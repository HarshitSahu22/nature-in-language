ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-01-ps2013-042-01-005-005.u2	Mr President, honourable Members. Allow me to briefly state the government's draft law on basic registers. The system of basic registers has been in operation for more than three years. During this period, the Ministry of the Interior has gathered a number of knowledge and experience justifying the need to amend certain provisions of the Basic Registers Act. It is therefore proposed to clarify some of the existing provisions and to remove the interpretational uncertainties of the law as outlined by three years of practice. Furthermore, provision is added to the Act on Basic Registers, which allows state authorities carrying out activities in the field of security and defence of the Czech Republic, public security and crime detection to use direct access to the agenda information system without the obligation to use this access through the basic registry information system. Direct access is also granted for the protection of the rights of persons in court. I note that even in the case of direct access, records are kept on the acceding person and the citizen has the right to know who has access to his data. The existing wording of the Basic Registers Act does not yet include the possibility of drawing up data contained in basic registers by entities other than public authorities. It is therefore proposed that the rule conditional upon the drawing up of data from the basic registers, which still applies only to public authorities, is applied to so-called private data users. The proposed amendment does not extend the authorisation of any private user to draw data from the basic registers. However, it aims to ensure a higher level of data protection where a special law allows non-public authorities to draw up such data. This means this is a prediction of a condition that could occur in the future. Thank you for your attention.
