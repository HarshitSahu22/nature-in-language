ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-20-ps2013-044-06-008-054.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, allow me to introduce the Government's bill on trust services for electronic transactions. The aim of the proposed bill is to adapt the legal order of the Czech Republic to the adoption of a regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on electronic identification and services that create confidence for electronic transactions in the internal market, namely that part of the regulation concerning services that create trust. The proposed law takes a minimalist approach in relation to the regulation. In this way, the law only regulates what the regulation leaves for regulation by national law. Therefore, the regulation contained in the proposed law sets out in particular certain procedures for trust providers and trust-creating service requirements. The proposed law also provides for the supervisory authority of trust-creating service providers, which will be the Ministry of the Interior in the light of its current e-signature remit, as well as sanctions for acts contrary to this Regulation. Thank you for your attention.
