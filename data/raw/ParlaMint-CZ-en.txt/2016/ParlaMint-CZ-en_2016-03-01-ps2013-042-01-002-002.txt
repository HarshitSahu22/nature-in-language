With regard to the tax return, we expect that after the introduction of a new regulation of gambling, although the market for heavy gambling will shrink, the rates will increase and the taxation of Internet operators will occur. The total revenue distributed between the State and the municipality will thus be maintained at the level of the estimate of the yield in 2016. However, it will also include the money that is still flowing to the Czech Olympic Committee in the form of a discount on the contribution. The taxation of slot machines will be similar to the amendment to the Lottery Act adopted at the end of last year, taxation of other gambling will be close to existing rates. Tax rates are designed to reflect the harmfulness of individual gambling and their profitability. This will bring us closer to the advanced European economies. In determining tax rates, we were therefore based on expert adictological studies as well as economic analysis of the market. Gambling operators have allowed us to look into their accounts and we have compiled a model to estimate how different tax rates will affect their business. In parallel with the influence of the measures contained in the Gaming Act on the gambling market, we are able to estimate the development of the market and the expected yield of the tax. With regard to the budgetary determination of taxes, we have tried to ensure that the tax return at the current level is maintained for municipalities. Of course, the real yield for a particular municipality also affects whether or not the municipality bans slot machines on its territory. The revenue estimates therefore concern all municipalities together. Unlike the lottery levy, we do not want to maintain the tax credit derived from the donation to the Czech Olympic Committee. But the reason is not that the state does not want to promote sport. On the contrary. The reason is that the state will support sport from its budget. And the same goes for self-government, whether the regions or the municipalities that are closer to the citizens. Neither the State nor the self-government should let another entity distribute the money from the collected taxes for them. A similar theme is the purposeful binding nature of the tax return. While the tax credit does not go into the public budget at all, in the case of purpose, the money flows into the budget, but can only be used in one predetermined way. Even this solution is wrong.
