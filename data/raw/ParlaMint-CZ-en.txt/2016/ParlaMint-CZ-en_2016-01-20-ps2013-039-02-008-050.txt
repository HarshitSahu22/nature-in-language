ParlaMint-CZ_2016-01-20-ps2013-039-02-008-050.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, the aim of the proposal is to change the definition of certain Senate electoral districts contained in the annex to the Act on Elections to the Parliament of the Czech Republic. These are electoral districts in which there has been a decline or, on the contrary, an increase in population, by acting either below or above the established limit and the coefficient laid down by the law. Changes in the definition of electoral circuits are due to demographic developments, but were prepared on the basis of data supplied by the Czech Statistical Office, and according to the proposal submitted (?) the regional authorities concerned, which are also based on discussions with each of the municipalities concerned. At the same time, it is necessary to redefine the boundaries of the constituency number 61 with its registered office in Olomouc, following the adoption of the Act on Frontiers of Military Departures, which changes the borders of the military retreat Libá and the emergence of new municipalities. In addition to the change in the definition of Senate electoral districts, it is also proposed to amend other electoral laws following the Act on the Borders of Military Departures. The law must take effect as soon as possible so that Senate elections can take place in autumn 2016. That is why I am asking you, and I suggest that the Chamber of Deputies resolve and give its consent pursuant to Paragraph 90 (1) of the Rules of Procedure. 2, and it has already approved this proposal at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
