Government draft law on the use of funds from property criminal penalties imposed in criminal proceedings and on the amendment of certain laws /German press 650/ - second reading Under the authority of the Government, the bill submitted will be presented by the Minister of Justice Robert Pelikán. Minister, please take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-07-ps2013-049-02-029-028.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, the aim of this proposal is to improve the status of victims of crimes, where it is rare today that victims are not compensated because there will be nothing left of the property of the perpetrator after the State applies property sanctions, i.e. a cash penalty or seizure of property or proceeds from crime. It is therefore proposed to introduce a specific system in which the funds thus obtained under criminal proceedings will be deposited into the Fund and the victims of the crime will be satisfied. I want to stress - the crime in question. That means, it's not that there's a kind of global fund that makes all the victims happy. The proposal was discussed in the committees with several amendments being applied. The first suggests that the amount remaining, if necessary, should be 2 % used for the probation and mediation service to finance assistance to victims of crime. That's something we're very supportive of. The probation and mediation service is long-term underfunded and, at the same time, important in an effort to protect and help victims. At the same time, the probation and mediation services do not intervene in the criminal proceedings themselves, so there is no danger of any threat to the fairness of the criminal proceedings. [[18.40 hours]] Another amendment concerns improving the status of dependants in order to make it possible somewhere at all; elsewhere, it has simplified the possibility for the maintenance due to be paid out of this very fund. Here too, this proposal has our full support. And the last is an adjustment to efficiency, so that, on the one hand, there will be the necessary links with the other proposals we have here and, on the other hand, the time to create the necessary information systems. Thank you for your attention.
