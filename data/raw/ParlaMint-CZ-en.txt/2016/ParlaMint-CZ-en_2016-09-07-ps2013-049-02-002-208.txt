ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-07-ps2013-049-02-002-208.u2	Hello, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen. Allow me, in my third reading, to introduce a government bill amending the Act on certain measures against the legalization of proceeds from crime and terrorist financing and other related laws. We are currently ensuring the transposition of the fourth EU directive against money laundering by adopting this bill. The deadline for its implementation is set until June 2017, however, under the influence of the current threat of terrorism, the Member States have agreed to implement it by the end of this year. This is also the reason why we want and need the speedy approval of this proposal. At the same time, we are already taking part in the preparations for the revision of the directive, which is being concluded in the institutions of the European Union, and which will also be adopted very soon, taking into account the priority of combating terrorist financing. For this reason, we do not agree with Mr Klaška's amendment, so that the record of the real owners, the legal entities introduced by this law, is publicly available. [[Noise in the hall.]] We consider it wiser to wait for the definitive text of the revised directive. Its proposal also goes in this direction, but it is still in the negotiations and the result cannot be estimated now. However, if the register of beneficial owners were to be public, this change would have to be reflected in a number of other related provisions. Adoption of only the proposed partial amendment would cause problems in the application of the Act on Public Registers. On the other hand, the current proposal also allows access to information about the beneficial owner of a person who demonstrates a legitimate interest and we have verified that it fully transposes the relevant provisions of the current Directive against money laundering. We agree with all other amendments and propose to approve the bill with these amendments. Thank you for your attention.
