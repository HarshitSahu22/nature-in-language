This year my driving licence issued at the Znojmo Municipal Authority will expire. However, its renewal cannot be carried out through another city office without a change in permanent residence. It is also not possible to have a completed driver's licence sent to another city office, unlike the ID card. Because of the change of driving licence, I have to take two days of vacation and travel twice 200 km there and back with a cost of about 900 CZK only for a fare, which is responsible for a student like me for example. the weekly food budget.' The amendment gives these people a helping hand when it allows them to apply for a driving licence, not in a community with extended activity in a place of residence, as foreseen by the current regulation, but in any municipality with extended scope. The issue of driving licences will also be facilitated. In addition, as submitters, we strive to ensure that, where the Authority already has a photograph of an applicant fulfilling certain parameters, it is not obliged to require it from the applicant again. I think that's very helpful and sensible. That's what public administration should look like in the 21st century.
