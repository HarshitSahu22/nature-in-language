ParlaMint-CZ_2016-07-13-ps2013-048-06-004-224.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly remind you here that the main objective of this proposal is the transposition of the new European Union directive against money laundering and terrorist financing, which was adopted in May 2015. The implementation period is June 2017. However, in the context of terrorist attacks, the European Commission called on States to implement it by the end of 2016 at the latest in its Action Plan to strengthen the fight against terrorist financing. The main changes in the system of anti-money laundering and terrorist financing measures are: the introduction of the principle of risk assessment into all stages of the anti-money laundering and terrorist financing system, the reduction in the amount of cash payment each entrepreneur has to identify his client, the inclusion of almost all types of gambling operators among obliged persons and, last but not least, the establishment of a register of real legal entities. I would also like to respond briefly to the first reading debate, namely the issue of politically exposed persons. A politically exposed person is a person in a significant public office of national or regional importance, which, of course, is not an ordinary official, even in the Party Secretariat or in the Ministry. In the relevant provision of the proposal, the list of functions is demonstrative and is given here for basic orientation only, taking into account the international overlap of this definition. Given the complexity of this issue, the petitioner assumes the issue of a methodological instruction based on Giddens FATF, in which it will be specified more precisely and in more detail which specific functions are covered by this provision. In this methodology, the comments made during the first reading can also be taken into account. The Committee on Budgets recommended for approval as a guarantee proposal. I would therefore like to ask you to support the proposal for further discussion. Thank you for your attention.
