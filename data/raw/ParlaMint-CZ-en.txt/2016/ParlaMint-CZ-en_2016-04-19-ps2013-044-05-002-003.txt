ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-19-ps2013-044-05-002-003.u6	Thank you, Mr President. Colleagues, let me remind you very briefly of the aim of the bill that we are now discussing. This is to strengthen and streamline the support system provided by the State Cinematography Fund. A fundamental change in the proposed regulation is the creation of a new stable source of support for cinema, through the participation of the state budget. From the State budget, an annual subsidy should be granted for the Fund's activities, the amount of which corresponds to the amount of revenue realised by the Fund in respect of the calendar year preceding the calendar year in which the draft Fund's budget is drawn up. Other changes concern the process of granting support from the Fund itself. The applicant for a film incentive will be able to submit an application at any time during the fiscal year, not only once a year, as is the case so far. These changes will allow better use of the capacity of the Czech film industry and avoid possible departures of foreign productions. The effort to resolve the problem of delaying the collection of fees for the provision of audiovisual media services on request has been found inadmissible in the deliberations of the Senate. Therefore, we are debating the bill again now, and I recommend the adoption of the Senate version in the light of the situation. Thank you for your attention.

The amendment to the Law adopted by the Chamber of Deputies contained a delay in the effectiveness of these provisions until 2019, due to the legislation of the European Union. As of 1 January 2016, the original envisaged effectiveness of the draft amendment to the Audiovision Act was not possible due to the long legislative process, and therefore the provisions on deferment of effectiveness until 2019 may appear to be retrospective, although the fees from the State Cinema Fund have not yet been levied and the delay of effectiveness is in favour of the entities concerned.
