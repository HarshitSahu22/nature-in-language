Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Auditor Act is a legal regulation regulating the conduct of audits by statutory auditors and audit firms. At the same time, the professional self-government of auditors and the exercise of public oversight are regulated. The Act contains a comprehensive set of rights and obligations of auditors in the verification of financial statements and also includes a quality assurance system. The submitted amendment to the Auditor Act implements the requirements of European Union legislation with effect from 17 June 2016, namely Directive No 56 of 2014 amending Directive No 43 of 2006 on statutory audits of annual and consolidated accounts, and Regulation No 537 of 2014 on specific requirements for statutory audits of public-interest entities. The subject matter of the amendment is the strengthening of public oversight of audit professions, which is carried out in the Czech Republic by the Board on Public Audit Supervision. The area that has undergone a significant adjustment is the quality assurance system. With regard to the European Regulation, the law now requires that the quality control of auditors verifying the accounts of public-interest entities be carried out by the supervisory authority and its quality controllers. The position of the Chamber of Auditors as a self-governing professional organisation is hereby amended by carrying out quality control only for auditors who do not audit public-interest entities. [[12.50 hours]] The role of statutory auditor is associated with the public interest throughout the European Union, so auditors are subject to very strict requirements for expertise, independence and ethical behaviour. This amendment therefore also strengthens the independence of the auditor. By this statement, I would like to conclude my introductory words and I would like to ask you to approve the amendment of the law in the first reading in accordance with § 90 para. 2 of the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies. The reason is that this standard should take effect at the same time as that Regulation, i.e. 17 June 2016. Thank you for your attention.
