ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-10-ps2013-042-07-008-135.u2	Thank you. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, you have before you the proposal to ratify the amendments to the Convention on International Rail Transport and to withdraw the reservations of the Czech Republic to this Convention. [[COTIF]] The Czech Republic has been a Member State of the Convention since its inception. In 1999, the Convention was amended, but it took another seven years to ratify a sufficient number of states to take effect. In the meantime, however, the European Union has improved its own right to regulate certain areas of rail transport. The COTIF Member States, which are also members of the EU, had to give priority to European law under the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and were therefore obliged to make a reservation on the application of certain parts of the Convention. In 2009, the Euronovela Convention was therefore adopted, which united the two legal regimes. The Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic is therefore submitted for approval a proposal to ratify this amendment of the Convention and at the same time a proposal to withdraw the reservations of the Czech Republic to the Convention. The Convention has been discussed in a foreign committee which recommends: The Chamber of Deputies agrees to ratify this document, and I therefore dare ask you to approve it. Thank you.
