ParlaMint-CZ_2016-06-02-ps2013-047-07-003-046.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Dear colleagues, let me introduce a government bill amending Act No. 154/2000 Coll., on breeding, breeding and registration of livestock and amending certain related laws, i.e. breeding law, as amended. The aim of the proposed bill is to adjust the conditions for guaranteeing the Ministry of Agriculture for the conservation of biodiversity of agriculture in selected breeds and species of animals. The preservation and sustainable use of animal genetic resources is closely linked to the preservation of biodiversity and genetic diversity of indigenous species and breeds of animals important for nutrition and agriculture, which were historically located in the Czech Republic. The proposed legislation specifies the conditions of operation and the overall administration of the Ministry of Agriculture programme, which is called the National Conservation and Use Programme for Plant Genetic Resources, Animals and Micro-organisms Important for Nutrition and Agriculture. This national programme contains, in one part, conditions and procedures to ensure the protection, preservation, documentation and sustainable use of the genetic resources of animals. In particular, the proposed amendment to the breeding law defines definitions relating to the issue of animal genetic resources. It lays down transparent conditions for the inclusion of all participants in the national programme of animal genetic resources, which will also apply to breeding associations and gene banks. It regulates the procedure of the Ministry of Agriculture and the conditions under which it will be possible to amend or repeal the decision to be included in the national programme. Last but not least, it also lays down conditions for the storage of genetic material in gene banks and provides materials from such equipment to third parties. In view of the need to update the National Conservation Programme and the use of plant genetic resources, animals and micro-organisms relevant to nutrition and agriculture, I would also like to ask for the possibility of shortening the discussion of this material in committees for 30 days. Thank you for your attention.
