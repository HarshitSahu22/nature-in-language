ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-08-ps2013-049-03-012-179.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr Vice-President, Members, this Treaty is a standard treaty of this type. Its task is primarily to define in which state a migrant worker will pay social and health insurance, and it is actually similar to agreements to avoid double taxation. From the point of view of the case, the area includes sickness, health and pension benefits, accidents at work and occupational diseases and unemployment benefits. The Senate of the Parliament of the Czech Republic has already made a positive statement on the ratification of this Treaty. In accordance with the recommendation of the Foreign Committee, I would like to request similar consent from you. Thank you.
