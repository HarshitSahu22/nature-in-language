ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-09-ps2013-053-08-009-194.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Good noon, ladies and gentlemen. I would like to ask you to support my amendment, which concerns particularly vulnerable victims of crimes such as children or seniors. The point is that these victims are currently being treated by law enforcement authorities at all levels of special regime procedures, extremely gentle, so as not to cause secondary victimization, actually secondary victim damage, for example by being interrogated in a specially modified room, recording devices are used so that they do not have to report the same facts repeatedly. They are entitled to many similar regime measures, but unfortunately some of them have to ask for them to be fulfilled. This is, for example, very important information that is provided to them upon request, such as that the accused escaped from custody, or his treatment regime was changed from constitutional to outpatients, or he was released from detention, etc. However, these victims or their legal representatives often do not know that they can ask for such information at all. My amendment aims to ensure that all the rights of the victim are fulfilled, regardless of whether the victim or her representative requests them. So I will ask you to support this amendment. Thank you for your attention.
