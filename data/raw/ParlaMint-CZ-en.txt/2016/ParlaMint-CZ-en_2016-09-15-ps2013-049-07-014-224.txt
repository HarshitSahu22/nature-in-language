ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-15-ps2013-049-07-014-224.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the material presented informs of the operation of the deployable coupling module in NATO Joint Enterprise in the Republic of Kosovo. The operation of the Army of the Czech Republic in the mission was approved by Parliament in 2014 under the mandate for foreign operations in 2015 and 2016, with government and Parliament being informed about it in case of a specific commitment. The main task of the operation is to contribute to the creation and maintenance of a safe environment, to support the ongoing peace process and democratic developments in the country, to support the UN mission and the European Union mission in the field. The Czech Republic was involved by a group of three members between 28 August 2015 and 22 December of the same year, with the fact that it completed the operational task from 1 September to 20 December 2015. Their task was to participate in ensuring the merger of units both in the area of operation, as well as between the headquarters of the operation and the superiors of the alliance headquarters. In addition to the members of the DCM, he has worked in Kosovo in the mission headquarters of around 10 soldiers of our army. The funding of DCM members in the Republic of Kosovo is covered by the budget of the Ministry of Defence chapter. The total cost of this module was CZK 422 612. Thank you for your attention.
