ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-06-ps2013-053-05-003-015.u2	Madam President, thank you for the floor. Ladies and gentlemen, ladies and gentlemen, once again, this is essentially a transposition standard, so I would just very briefly put forward a proposal for an amendment to the transplant law. As mentioned here in the first reading, this amendment is closely linked to the current government proposal for an amendment on the Human Tissues and Cells Act, House Press 810, which was just discussed. The main objective of the proposal for an amendment to the transplant law is the transposition of Commission Directive EU 2015/566 as regards procedures for verifying equivalent quality and safety standards for imported tissues and cells, and it is essentially a technical modification. Other partial objectives of the proposed law include some corrections to the concept following the new Civil Code and the elimination of some legislative technical errors in the existing wording of the Act. Finally, I would just like to mention that the Committee on Health, which has discussed the proposal, adopted a resolution on it which recommends to the Chamber of Deputies to agree with this government bill that the committee has not adopted any amendments to it. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, Members, I believe you will support this bill. Thank you.
