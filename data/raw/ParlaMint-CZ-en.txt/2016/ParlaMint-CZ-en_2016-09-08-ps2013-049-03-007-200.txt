ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-08-ps2013-049-03-007-200.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the material presented by the Ministry of Finance is a proposal for ratification of the Protocol amending the Agreement on the establishment and operation of the International Economic Cooperation Bank and the Statute of the International Economic Cooperation Bank, which was negotiated in Warsaw on 25 November 2014. The need for changes in the bank's founding documents came from the fact that the latest official changes to the founding documents took place in 1990 and their wording did not correspond to the current state of play. The International Bank of Economic Cooperation has been operating since 1963 and the then Czechoslovakia has been one of the founding members. In agreement on the establishment and activities of MBHS, the Czech Republic succededed. At present, the share of the Czech Republic in the paid-up capital is EUR 24 million, which makes the Czech Republic the second largest shareholder of the bank. MBHS is currently undergoing transformation, a new strategy has been adopted focusing on new products and services such as commercial financing, support for joint undertakings of Member States or clearing. At the turn of 2012 and 2013, there were changes within the bank's membership base. A contract was signed with Cuba on the mutual settlement of relations, so as of 20 February 2013 she ceased to be a member of MBHS. The effort to settle with Hungary was also renewed, which ended its membership in 1993. At the meeting of the Bank Board on 5 June 2013 in Bratislava, it was decided to reflect the change in the structure of the Member States in the founding documents of MBHS, not only in the list of Member States but also in the structure of capital. Other changes are technical in nature and are intended to ensure that the founding documents are fully in line with the current situation in the bank. The negotiation of the protocol will not have a direct impact on the state budget and the content of the protocol is in line with constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic. As this is an amendment to the international presidential agreement, I am now asking you to give your consent to the ratification of the protocol. Thank you for your attention.
