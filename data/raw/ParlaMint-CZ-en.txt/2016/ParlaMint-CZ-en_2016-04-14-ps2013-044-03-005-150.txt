I hardly bear that I couldn't bear the responsibility of the Minister of Transport to defend his proposal, but we'll leave it to him next time. Let me present at this moment the Government's proposal to approve the ratification of the amendments to Annexes I and II to the Convention on the Protection of Migratory Species of Wild Animals. The basic objective of this Convention is to protect migratory species of animals, not only birds but also mammals, fish and invertebrates in the entire area of their spread, i.e. in nesting places, travels and winters. An integral part of the text of the Convention are two Annexes which include a list of animals covered by this Convention. The last meeting of the Conference of the Parties to the Convention, as has already been said here, took place in Quito, Ecuador, in November 2014 and adopted an amendment to these Annexes. The amendments to Annexes I and II concern the inclusion of new species in the list and adaptation of taxonomy to the current trend. They are an important response to a change in conditions and allow the commitments in force to be adapted to new realities. The changes do not constitute further claims for specific protection measures in the Czech Republic and are in line with the constitutional order and legal order of the Czech Republic, with the obligations arising from the membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union and with the obligations assumed under other applicable international agreements to protect nature, by which the Czech Republic is bound. At the same time as the proposal to adopt amendments to Annexes I and II, I would also like to submit a proposal for additional ratification of the Convention. The Czech Republic acceded to the Convention as an international treaty of a government category with effect from 1 May 1994, the Gestor is the Ministry of the Environment. The accession of the Czech Republic occurred before the amendment of the Constitution of the Czech Republic and therefore before a clearer definition of criteria for categories of international treaties. According to the criteria currently in force, the Convention falls within the category of presidential treaties, which is Article 49 (b). (e) The Constitution and, in order to remedy this non-compliance, the Convention, together with the amendments adopted in Annexes I and II, is now submitted for additional ratification. So, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to refer this government proposal to further discuss it. Thank you for your attention.
