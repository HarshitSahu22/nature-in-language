ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-08-ps2013-049-03-018-187.u2	Thank you, Mr President. I'll be brief, too. Again, this is a second reading. Just let me briefly remind the Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic will give its consent to ratify the amendment of Annexes A and C to the Stockholm Convention on persistent organic pollutants. The seventh meeting of 15 May last year in Geneva included new substances in Annex A and C to the Convention. It is the inclusion of hexachlorobutadiene in Annex A and the inclusion of pentachlorophenol and its salts and esters in Annex A as well as the inclusion of polychlorinated naphthalenes with specific exceptions for the production of these chemicals in Annex A and also in Annex C. Newly classified substances seriously harm human health and the environment. They are very difficult to demolish and are subject to so-called long-distance transmission. So these are dangerous substances, and that's why they're also proposed in these annexes, in these lists, to reintegrate. Thank you.
