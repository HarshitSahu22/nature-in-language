[[There's a lot of noise in the room.]] As has already been said in the first reading, the aim of the bill is to ensure the transposition of Union Directives 2015/565 and 2015/566 governing procedures for verifying equivalent quality and safety standards for imported tissues and cells. To this end, the procedures necessary to establish uniform rules within the EU for the import of tissues and cells from third countries are added in order to ensure a high level of protection of human health in the Union. This is possible by establishing a procedure for verifying equivalent quality and safety standards for imports of tissues and cells, establishing a programme of approval and inspections reflecting the verification process established for tissue and cell related activities carried out in the Union. Another objective is the introduction of a single European code that will facilitate the traceability of human tissues and cells from donor to recipient and vice versa. This transposition amendment is closely linked to the Government's proposal for an amendment to the transplant law, which is also primarily a transposition regulation and is included in the proceedings as the House Press 811. The deadline for implementation for both directives was set at 29. 10. 2016. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, ladies and gentlemen, I believe that, given that the proposed legislation should bring about greater security for the provision of health services in the use of tissues and cells, I would be happy if you would support the bill.
