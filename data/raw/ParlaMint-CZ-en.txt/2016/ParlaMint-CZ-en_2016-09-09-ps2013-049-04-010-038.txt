Members of Jaroslav Foldyna, jeroný Tejc, Michal Hašek and others for the issue of the Act amending Act No. 428/2012 Coll., on property settlement with churches and religious societies and on the amendment of certain laws (Act on property settlement with churches and religious societies), as amended by the Constitutional Court ruling, published under No. 177/2013 Sb. House print 144/ - second reading We dealt with this House press on 5 December 2014 at the 23rd meeting of the Chamber of Deputies, when we interrupted the general debate. It was a pause for the TOP 09 and ODS MPs. On 20 June 2014, at the 10th meeting of the Chamber of Deputies, the press was ordered to discuss the Constitutional Legal Committee. The transitional provision of the amendment to our Rules of Procedure states that, if it is not clear to the Chamber of Deputies before the amendment is effective which committee is a guarantee, the Guarantee Committee shall designate the Chamber of Deputies. In the present case, the draft law was directed only to the Constitutional Legal Committee as a single committee and it is therefore clear that this committee is a guarantee committee. Therefore, we do not need to vote on the designation of the guarantee committee. I would remind you that the committee's resolution was delivered as House press 144/2. Before I ask my colleague Foldyna and Vondráček to take the floor at the newsstand, I am registering the priority right of KDU-ČSL Chairman Jan Mihola. Please, Mr. Chairman, you have the floor.
