ParlaMint-CZ_2016-04-19-ps2013-044-05-009-023.u2	Thanks for the word. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly present the draft customs law when it comes to its second reading. The proposed law adapts the Czech rule of law to the European Union Customs Code, which will redefine customs issues at European level as of 1 May 2016. The supervision of international trade in the European Union and the management of customs duties that are the income of the European Union's budget will, like so far, be governed primarily by the directly applicable European legislation. The proposed customs law only regulates the partial issues which have been left to national legislation, particularly in the procedural and competence area. At the same time, some practical problems from the customs area are also being addressed and some legislative uncertainties and duplications are being removed. In some areas, the administrative burden of importers and other business entities will also be reduced. In conclusion, my introductory words are that it will be proposed to shorten the time limit for the initiation of the discussion of the bill in the third reading to seven days. Thank you for your attention.
