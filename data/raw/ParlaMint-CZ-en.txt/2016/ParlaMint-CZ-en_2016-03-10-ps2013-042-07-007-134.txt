ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-10-ps2013-042-07-007-134.u3	Thank you, Madam Vice-President, Members. This material contains three documents of the International Labour Organisation - Protocol to Convention No. 29 on forced labour, accompanying recommendations on this protocol and at the same time amendments to the Convention on Sea Labour, which the Czech Republic has not yet ratified. The first reading took place here in November last year, and at that time I said that in this case it was actually a formal fulfillment of the government's international commitment to inform Parliament of the adoption of every convention, every protocol and recommendation of this organisation, that is, the International Labour Organisation. The protocol on forced labour focuses on the prevention and protection of victims from the point of view of both their rights in criminal proceedings and the rights to social rehabilitation and assistance, and stresses the need for international cooperation in the fight against trafficking in human beings, which I think is an up-to-date and useful thing. I would also like to say that since the House's presentation of the material, this proposal has already been discussed by the Senate, which agreed to ratify last November. Thank you for your attention.
