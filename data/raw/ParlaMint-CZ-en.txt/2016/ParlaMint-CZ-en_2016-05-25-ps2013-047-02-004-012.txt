Please, Minister, take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-25-ps2013-047-02-004-012.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, let me mention the discussion of the Government's proposal for a law on biocidal products and active substances and the amendment of certain related laws within the framework of its second reading. Let me start by reminding you what biocidal products are. These are usually chemical mixtures designed to destroy, reduce growth or repell harmful organisms in order to protect human health. These are disinfectants, preservatives, rodent control products, etc. So now very briefly to the content. The main objective of the draft law presented is the implementation of Regulation No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products, which regulates a significant part of the obligations in the field of making biocidal products available on the market. As a result of the adoption of a directly applicable Union regulation, it is necessary to remove duplications and deviations in the Czech legal order and to establish only such legislation that will allow its smooth application and linking to national legislation. It is also necessary to repeal the existing Biocide Act and, in the new law, to lay down, in particular, the status of state authorities, the powers of administrative authorities in the exercise of state administration and the penalties for infringements of the obligations laid down by regulations and biocides. Finally, I would like to mention that the Committee on Health, which has discussed the proposal, adopted a resolution to which the Chamber of Deputies recommends agreeing to this Government's bill, with the fact that the committee has not adopted any amendments to it. [[15.00 hours]] I agree with Mrs Marková and Mrs Jan Volný's amendments. In the first case, it is a technical regulation where the language requirements of the applications submitted and the coherence with the administrative rules are adapted. For the second amendment, terminology relating to the customs procedure is amended following the adoption of the new Union Customs Code. Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, I believe that you will support this bill during today's discussion, as it is basically a technical regulation that will contribute to the compliance of the Czech rule of law with the current EU legislation in this area. Thank you for your attention.
