ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-10-ps2013-042-07-011-138.u2	Madam President, honourable Members, the purpose of the Treaty between the Czech Republic, the Republic of Austria and the Slovak Republic on a three-state border point Dyje - Moravia is an immobile anchoring point of contact between the national borders of these three states. The three-state border point is located in the bed of the Moravia River, in the area surrounding the Dyje River. At present, this point is moving. State border with the Republic of Austria on the river Dyji is movable, the center line of the Dyje stream is in the section concerned. Since 1 April 2011, the Slovak Republic's national border has been immobile for a whole length and only with the exception of the section adjacent to this three-state point. It is approximately 60 meters that run through the center of the Moravia stream. The concept of moving state borders at the border of water flows is currently outdone, the Czech Republic is interested in gradually adapting contracts on common state borders with neighbouring states so that the national borders are not moving over the entire length of flows. The anchoring of the point of contact of the State border with Austria and Slovakia is a condition for the implementation of this intention. The contract is based on the current position of the tristate border point, so there will be no change of state border. Thank you for your attention.
