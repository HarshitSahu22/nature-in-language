ParlaMint-CZ_2016-05-31-ps2013-047-05-008-021.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the draft law on the Office for Access to Transport Infrastructure is linked to the proposal for an amendment to the Railway Act, and it also implements the law of the European Union, in particular the Directive on the creation of a single European railway area. This proposal was also presented and discussed in detail in the Economic Committee as well as at the seminar in the Sub-Committee on Transport. Allow me only a brief introduction here. This is the size of a small proposal, the subject of which is the establishment of a new central government body, the definition of its basic competence and organisation. The specific and detailed definition of the Authority's scope and powers is then found in the relevant transport laws, in particular the Railway Act. This new office should, in particular, exercise the scope of the so-called a regulatory body under the Directive on the creation of a single European railway area. In particular, the Directive entrusts this body with the decision-making of disputes between railway infrastructure operator and service facilities and carriers when accessing and using transport infrastructure, control powers as well as the possibility of imposing sanctions. In relation to the position and organisation of the regulatory body, the directive places particular emphasis on the wide-ranging independence of the regulatory entity on business entities and on other authorities and places certain requirements on the persons in charge of that body. In view of these requirements, we propose to establish a new office as an independent central administrative body headed by the President appointed by the Government. The President of the proposal then lays down certain requirements for his appointment, in particular education and practice, establishing the incompatibility of functions and limiting the possibility of his appeal. In addition to this scope in the field of rail transport, the Ministry of Transport proposes to extend the scope of the Authority to sub-areas within the framework of air transport - the price for the use of airports and roads, the introduction of a European electronic myth service. This is a step that is in line with the European directive, and the scope of the new office would thus combine all the type-similar decision-making powers that existing traffic laws contain. However, it should be stressed that, even so, the vast majority of the Authority's activities will focus on railway and rail transport operations. Both areas are small in size. When discussing the Economic Committee, amendments were also proposed in the form of an office, where, among other things, it was proposed to narrow its scope to rail transport only, but these Ministry of Transport did not support and were not adopted. The concept of the new office, its powers and its powers was also explained in detail at the seminar at the Transport Sub-Committee last week. Ladies and gentlemen, I am aware of the controversy of the proposal to establish a new office. However, the Ministry considered different approaches for the transfer of the regulatory body to national law and also made a comparison of selected foreign adjustments. This is therefore not a rash or unthinkable decision. Thank you.
