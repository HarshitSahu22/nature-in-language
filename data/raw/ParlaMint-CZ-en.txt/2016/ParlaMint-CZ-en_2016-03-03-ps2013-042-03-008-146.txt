ParlaMint-CZ_2016-03-03-ps2013-042-03-008-146.u2	Thank you. I will also be very brief. Mr President, colleagues, the Czech Republic has been a party to the Stockholm Convention on persistent organic pollutants since 17 May 2004. The seventh meeting of the Conference of the Parties to the Stockholm Convention decided on 15 May 2015 to include new substances in Annexes A and C to the Convention. This is the inclusion of hexachlorobutadiene in Annex A and the inclusion of pentachlorophenol and its salts, esters with specific exceptions for the production and use of pentachlorophenol for masts and transverse beams in Annex A and the inclusion of polychlorinated naphthalenes with specific exceptions for the production of these chemicals as intermediate products in the manufacture of polychlorinated naphthalenes. Following the entry into force of the amendments to Annexes A and C, the Convention will regulate the handling of a total of 26 chemicals. Newly classified substances seriously harm human health and the environment, they are very difficult to degrade and also decompose in the environment. These are substances that are subject to the so-called long-distance transmission, as they are also detected in areas such as the Arctic and Antarctica, where they have never been produced or used. In order to achieve effective protection against them, action must be taken at international level. The adoption of amendments to Annexes A and C will not require changes to the legislation in force in the Czech Republic, nor will it have an impact on the state budget, public administration or the business sphere beyond what the legislation of the European Union and the Czech Republic provides. The Stockholm Convention has the character of a presidential treaty with regard to national law. The adoption of amendments to the Convention, including an annex which forms an integral part of it, shall be subject to ratification by the President of the Republic for which he is referred to in Article 49 (b). (e) Constitutional Act 1/1993 Coll. The consent of the Parliament of the Czech Republic is required. Thank you for your attention.
