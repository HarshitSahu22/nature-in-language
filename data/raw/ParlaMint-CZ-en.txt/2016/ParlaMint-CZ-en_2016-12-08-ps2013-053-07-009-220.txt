ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-08-ps2013-053-07-009-220.u1	220. Information on deployment of forces and resources of the Ministry of Defence Ministry in foreign operations in 2015 /German press 835/ Mr. Defense Minister again, please.
ParlaMint-CZ_2016-12-08-ps2013-053-07-009-220.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the material informs on the political, military and financial aspects of the activities of Czech troops in a number of missions - Afghanistan, Kosovo, Bosnia and Herzegovina, the Sinai Peninsula, the Golan Heights, the United Kingdom, Iceland, the Malian Republic, Turkey and Italy. The Czech Republic has made a significant commitment last year, as you all probably know, especially in Afghanistan in the Resolute Support mission. Next, I'd name another one in at least a few words. In the Balkans, we continued to operate as part of the Joint Enterprise Alliance Operation Headquarters in Kosovo and the European Union Altea Mission in Bosnia and Herzegovina. Of course, we continued to work on the Sinai Peninsula in 2015, which, I must say, is exceptionally acknowledged, even in the anti-piracy, let's say, such as Atalanta. Next thing we know, this has already been mentioned, they worked in Air Policy over Iceland. We have three soldiers at the EU Sophia Mission Headquarters in Italy. And we also have a number of observers on the U.N. missions, a total of ten. The Czech Republic has prepared forces and capabilities in its territory for possible activation of NATO and UN emergency forces. For deployment in NATO's rapid response forces, 1,500 people were earmarked in 2015 and part of them were also involved in the emerging forces of very high alert - VJTF. In the framework of the UN Emergency System, there were then 50 persons. But the deployment of these units did not occur. In 2015 we spent a total of 1.12 billion on the involvement of our forces and resources in foreign operations. CZK and this amount was fully paid from the budget chapter of the Ministry of Defence. In conclusion, I would like to add a substantial detail that there was no injury or death of Czech soldiers during the performance of tasks in foreign operations in 2015. Thank you.
