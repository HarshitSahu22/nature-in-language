However, allow me now to draw your attention briefly, albeit on behalf of the Minister of Transport, to the matter of a government proposal concerning the approval of the ratification of the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Kingdom of Bahrain on Air Transport. The relations between the Czech Republic and the Kingdom of Bahrain in the field of civil aviation are not yet regulated by a valid international agreement. The attempts to adapt these relations date back to 2001, when there was even a signing of a bilateral aviation agreement, which, for formal reasons, never entered into force on the Bahraini side. In order to overcome legal uncertainty in this area, an expert meeting took place in 2014, resulting in the mutually acceptable text of the new aviation agreement. The Czech Government agreed to the agreement by its resolution in June 2014. The appropriate deadline for signing was found in January this year. The text of the Agreement corresponds to current developments in civil aviation. It is in accordance with constitutional order and other parts of the Czech legal order, including obligations arising from membership of the Czech Republic in the EU. The agreement does not require any changes in the Czech legal order and will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. The agreement offers air carriers of both parties sufficient flexibility in deciding on operational matters. I would therefore like to ask you for the referral of the material for discussion in the relevant committees. Thank you for your attention.
