ParlaMint-CZ_2016-09-07-ps2013-049-02-007-161.u6	Hello, Madam President, honourable Members. Allow me a brief summary prepared in cooperation with the Ministry of Defence. The proposed amendment to the Income Tax Act addresses, for both professional soldiers and members of the security forces, a fundamental matter that has been strongly advocated for several years, once again exempting the most important social security provided by the State for their country's service, and that is the expropriation. The duties shall be in the form of compensation of a social character for former members of the armed forces and security forces for the State's services carried out under difficult conditions and with personal constraints on the nature of the service performed. In the case of professional soldiers, these are in particular risks and restrictions related to the psychological and physical demands of the military service, with the potential risk of life in preparation for combat activity and performing services in various places according to the needs of the military, regardless of family life and the limitations of certain economic and political rights. Since 2011, the State has subsequently taken back, as already mentioned, a 15% tax, from some of the debts granted by the administrative decision. This procedure was strongly affected not only by former soldiers who received benefits, at the Ministry of Defence it is for your idea a total of 16,500 former soldiers, but also by demoting all existing soldiers from the profession, as they logically view the matter by reducing the level of security guaranteed by the state when they joined the service. This negative impact is also mentioned by those interested in joining the army, as the tax measure in question does not concern only the pension obligations, but also the service contribution to housing, for example, which is received by almost all professional soldiers. It also corrects the proposal put forward. Ladies and gentlemen, professional soldiers and members of the security forces need clear support and a signal that the state appreciates their services and supports their security at the moment when we are in a completely different security environment than in 2011. By approving this proposal, we will all make it clear to them that this is indeed the case. Thank you for your attention.
