Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the amendment to the Act on International Cooperation in Tax Administration, which brings another instrument in the fight against tax evasion in the field of direct taxes. The draft law translates into the Czech legal order another amendment to the European directive, which regulates the cooperation of tax administrations of the Member States so-called. DAC3 Directive on Administrative Cooperation. On the basis of this, an automatic exchange of so-called 'European Union' will be introduced between the Member States of the European Union. tax opinions with a cross-border element. [[14.50 hours]] In other words, the Member States of the European Union start exchanging information on national decisions at regular six months intervals. Rulings, which are issued to multinational companies in particular in the field of transfer prices. In accordance with the Directive, anonymous information shall also be provided to the European Commission and the Ministry of Finance to a limited extent. Since 2017, the exchange will take place twice a year and will be secured by a designated contact body for the Czech Republic, which will be the General Financial Directorate. Information on opinions issued in the past shall also be exchanged on a one-off basis between 1 January 2012 and 31 December 2016. Opinions issued from 1 January 2012 and 31 December 2013 shall be exchanged only if they have been effective on 1 January 2014. The aim of introducing this type of automatic exchange is to increase transparency and thus address the problems associated with aggressive tax planning and international tax evasion, particularly following the so-called 'executive tax evasion'. The LuxLeaks case came out in November 2014. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed from 1 January 2017 with regard to the specified transposition periods. In view of the length of the legislative process, the Government has therefore submitted to the Chamber of Deputies a draft law proposing consent to the law at first reading. In the event that the draft law is not agreed at first reading, the deadline for discussing this draft law in committees will need to be shortened by more than 30 days, otherwise it will not be possible to comply with the transposition period. Thank you for your attention.
