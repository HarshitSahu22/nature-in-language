Allow me to present a government proposal to agree to ratify amendments to Annexes I and II to the Convention on the Protection of Migratory Species of Wild Animals. The basic objective of the Convention is to protect migratory species of animals, not only birds, but mammals, fish and invertebrates in the entire area of their spread, that is on nesting sites, travel routes and winter grounds. An integral part of the text of the Convention are two Annexes which include a list of animal species to which the Convention applies. The amendments to Annexes I and II concern the inclusion of new animals in the list and the adaptation of taxonomy to the current trend. They are an important response to a change in conditions and allow the commitments in force to be adapted to new realities. The changes do not constitute further claims for specific protection measures in the Czech Republic and comply with the constitutional order and legal order of the Czech Republic. At the same time as the proposal to adopt amendments to Annexes I and II, I also allow myself to submit a proposal for additional ratification of the Convention. The Czech Republic acceded to the Convention as an international treaty of the government category. The Gestor is therefore the Ministry of the Environment. According to the criteria currently in force, the Convention falls within the category of presidential treaties and the Convention is now submitted for additional ratification to remedy this non-compliance, together with the amendments adopted in Annex I and Annex II.
