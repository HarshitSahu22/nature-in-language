ParlaMint-CZ_2023-01-13-ps2021-050-04-008-011.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, on behalf of the Government, I present to you with the justification of the bill amending the law on the verification of firearms and ammunition. It is a legislative technical amendment to the Act, which aims primarily to respond to the development of technical terminology used in the field of weapons and ammunition. The proposed bill takes into account, among other things, the new regulation of the Firearms and Firearms Act. Furthermore, the amendment includes in the subject matter an activity consisting of checking identification marks. In accordance with the above, the amendment to the Firearms and Ammunition Verification Act provides for the activities of the Czech Office for the Testing of Weapons and Ammunition, consisting of the classification of explosive substances and objects under the ADR Agreement on the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road, where the Czech Office for the Testing of Weapons and Ammunition has carried out this activity since 2010 and on the basis of repeated mandates issued by the Ministry of Transport. Finally, ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to give your consent to the bill. Thank you.

ParlaMint-CZ_2023-01-13-ps2021-050-04-008-011.u4	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. Members of Parliament, honourable Members, the draft law amending Act No. 156/2000 Coll., on the verification of firearms and ammunition, as amended, was delivered to Members as press number 300 on 12. 9. 2022. The Organizing Committee recommended the discussion of the draft law on 29. 9. 2022 by resolution 81 and proposed by the Economic Committee as a guarantee. The reason for submitting this bill is to respond to changes in the field of weapons and ammunition with regard to the transposition of European legislation into the arms law and also to the correction of legislative technical defects that are now in the law. This law regulates the rights and obligations of the controlled person, i.e. manufacturers, importers, exporters, distributors and repairers of firearms, accessories to arms and ammunition, as well as holders of arms certificates in their verification. The basic reason for proposing an amendment to the existing form of Act No. 156/2000 Coll. is a legislative development in the field of weapons and ammunition. The issue of verification is directly related to this area and it is therefore necessary to respond adequately to its changes. Furthermore, the draft law regulates the performance of the state administration in the control activity in the verification of firearms, accessories and ammunition and in the control of the identification data of firearms or ammunition, the provision of tasks arising from the international contract, which the Czech Republic is bound by. This amendment defines the scope of the Czech Office for the Testing of Weapons and Ammunitions and Activities, which are already carried out by them by virtue of the authorisation arising mainly from other legislation, such as the classification of dangerous substances and objects or the control of the depreciation of weapons. Last but not least, the amendment regulates existing terms and regulates written errors in the text of the law. Thank you for your attention.
