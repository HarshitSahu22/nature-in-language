Where a budgetary requirement in the field of defence financing arises with another administrator of the State Budget Chapter, that administrator shall submit to the Ministry of Defence his request for approval no later than 36 months before the beginning of the financial year in which the required defence expenditure is to be included in the draft State Budget Act. If there is an urgent requirement that could not be foreseen, the request may be submitted to the Ministry of Defence at a later date, but not later than 12 months before the beginning of the year in which the required defence expenditure is to be included in the draft State Budget Act. The Ministry of Defence will then assess the request made by another administrator of the state budget chapter and, if it concludes that it fulfils the financing conditions laid down by the Act at the same time and according to the priorities set, the requirement corresponds to the State's defence objectives, it will include it in State defence expenditure. However, the Act does not regulate the procedure in case the Ministry of Defence considers the request of another Chapter Administrator to conclude that it does not meet the conditions described above. This entrusts the Ministry of Defence with authority to decide on the requirements of other chapter administrators. The logical consequence of the non-acceptance of a request by the Ministry of Defence would be to refer the matter to the Government for a decision as joint superior body and, at the same time, to the executive authority. This would prevent the Ministry of Defence or de facto any Minister of Defence from deciding itself on the appropriateness, effectiveness and priority of the budgetary requirements applied, without there being any possibility of redress, including an incorrect assessment. Here it cannot be called into question that it is the Ministry of Defence, which will usually have the best and most complete information on the fulfilment of the statutory criteria in relation to the financing of State defence. However, it is not possible to rule out a situation where it will be a different resort, which will apply a legitimate requirement in this area from the point of view of its competence, such as the Ministry of the Interior or the Ministry of Transport. For these reasons, it is proposed that the Ministry of Defence be obliged to submit a budgetary request to another administrator of the chapter for a decision to the Government and if it does not comply with it itself. In this context, although I have said, the Defence Committee has given a disagreeable opinion on this amendment of mine, but I still ask you to reconsider your position, because it is logical that the ministries are equal, and only who can decide is the government itself.
