Proposal for the election of members of the Radio and Television Council The third election yesterday, the election of members of the Radio and Television Council. Number of ballots issued 179, number of valid and invalid ballots surrendered also 179, quota for the election again 90 votes. For the three candidates proposed, the following votes were cast: 94 votes were cast for the candidate Petr Buchta, 70 votes were cast for the candidate Vladimír Pikora, 91 votes were cast for the candidate Marek Poledníček. In the first round of the election were elected candidates Petr Buchta and Marek Poledníček.
