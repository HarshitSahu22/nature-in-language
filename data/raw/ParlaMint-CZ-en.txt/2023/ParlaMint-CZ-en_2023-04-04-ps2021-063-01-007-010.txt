The reason for submitting this amendment is that the current exoneration legislation ignores the organisational elements of the State against which the Ministry of Defence or the Ministry of the Interior or the Ministry of Justice performs the establishment functions and whose security is necessary in the interests of the security of the Czech Republic. These are organisational units of the State which are not established or established by the Ministry of Defence or the Ministry of the Interior or by the Ministry of Justice, but are directly established by law, while the rights and obligations to them are exercised by the above-mentioned relevant ministries. The competent Ministry is therefore in the same or similar position as the organisational units of the State directly established or established by the competent Ministry. The basis of the draft legislative amendment is to settle the situation and eliminate the unjustified differences between the organisational units of the State which are established or established by the competent ministry and the organisational units of the State against which the competent ministry performs its constituent functions. The organizational components of the state, which are established by law and in respect of which the aforementioned ministries perform establishing functions, are, according to current legislation, unsystemically excluded from the list of organisations whose security is necessary in the interests of security of the Czech Republic, which is a lack that should be corrected.
