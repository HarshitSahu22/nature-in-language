Good evening, Madam Vice-President, fellow Members, I would like to make this proposal very brief in my second reading, House Press 377, and it is a draft law on extraordinary remission of social security premiums and a contribution to state employment policy and execution costs. As the proposal itself clearly states, the aim of the draft law is to allow, through the institution of forgiveness and the termination of the penalty on social security premiums and the contribution to state employment policy, as well as execution costs under the tax code associated with the enforcement of insurance premiums managed by the district social security administrations. The reasons are quite clear, we follow here the principles of the gracious summer I and II, which were realized here during last year and previous year. The point is that most of the Czech Social Security Administration's claims are recovered under tax enforcement and only a fraction of the claims are recovered by judicial bailouts. For the record, in 2021, it was only 0.5 percent of the debt. Thus, much of the debtors whose liabilities are enforced publicly by legal entities in the framework of administrative tax enforcement will not be able to use the procedure under the so-called Grace Summer II to resolve their claims.

ParlaMint-CZ_2023-05-02-ps2021-063-07-001-151.u4	Thank you. The content of the bill is the legal conditions under which penalty payments and execution costs will be extinguished by means of extraordinary forgiveness. Forgiveness is addressed to insurance payers and former insurance payers who are not currently insurance payers, but who continue to owe periodic penalty payments or execution costs. (a) the unpaid periodic penalty payment resulting from the premiums due registered by the district social security administration as at 30. 9. 2022; (b) unpaid periodic penalty payments registered by the district social security administration on 30. 9. 2022, if the premium has been paid by that date at the latest; (c) unpaid execution costs which were determined by an execution order issued until 30. 9. 2022. If the debtor decides to take advantage of the procedure under this Act, he must notify the relevant district administration of this fact by electronic application of the ePortal of the Czech Social Security Administration in the relevant period from 1 July 2023 to 30 November 2023 in the relevant period and pay the premiums due. If the debtor pays the premiums due before the beginning of the relevant period or if it has as of 30. 9. 2022 only due penalty payments or execution costs are registered and, if they are paid on that date, the penalty payments and execution costs shall cease to exist without further payment. The draft law also allows payment of the premiums due in instalments. The application of this Act is proposed on 1 July 2023. As regards the amendments, the amendment was prepared for the Ministry of Social Security according to the requirements of the Czech Social Security Administration in addition to the procedure for reimbursement of the salary or the salary for work leave related to events for children and youth under Section 203a of the Labour Code, i.e. the addition of new parts to the bill, namely the amendment of Act No. 582/1991 Coll., on the Organisation and Implementation of Social Security and Labour Code. Furthermore, the amendment prepared by the Ministry of Finance in cooperation with the MPSV. The content is an adjustment according to which the exceptional remission of periodic penalty payments and execution costs, using the payment scheme, will also apply to debtors whose debt is already recovered through tax execution, and an adjustment consisting of the explicit exclusion of the law on insurance premiums, periodic penalty payments and execution costs recovered through judicial execution. The Sub-Committee on Consumer Protection announced another amendment to extend forgiveness to health insurance debts as well.
