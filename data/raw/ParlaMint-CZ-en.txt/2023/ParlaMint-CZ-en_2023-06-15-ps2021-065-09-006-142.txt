ParlaMint-CZ_2023-06-15-ps2021-065-09-006-142.u4	Thank you, Mr President. Air ambulance is close to me, I spent more than 25 years of my life there. So the rapporteur's statement: a new addition to the draft law is introduced to the provision relating to the financing of the air rescue service. The essential and, in essence, the only reason for the proposed change is that the financing of aircraft operations for medical rescue services inextricably involves the costs of ensuring the operation and use of the single information system in the air rescue service. However, these costs are currently not explicitly specified in the law, which makes it practically impossible for the Ministry of Health to pay them to operators. The Ministry of Health cannot order the regional health rescue services providers to pay for the system and budgets of the regions for the medical rescue service. The proposed legislative regulation shall have a minimum scope and shall only monitor the facilitation of the operation of all components of the medical rescue service. The current amendment to the Act is intended to enable the Ministry of Health to pay for the costs of operating the Single Information System in the Air Rescue Service, the essence of which is the technological interconnection of information related to both the exit of the air exit group and the technical implementation of the flight and its safety. The financial impact of the proposed regulation is only on the state budget in the health chapter and amounts to CZK 5,250,000 per year. The current total cost of operating helicopters for air rescue services then amounts to CZK 482 million per year, so it is a total marginal cost. The explanatory memorandum states that it is assumed that this amount for the operation of the single information system would be paid to air rescue helicopter operators on the basis of the signature of the amendment to the contracts already concluded, after the amendment proposed by the Health Rescue Service Act has become effective. That amount should be increased to cover the costs of operating air rescue helicopters. All financial claims resulting from the draft Health Rescue Service Act shall be covered by the appropriations provided for in the relevant chapter currently approved by the State Budget Act and the medium-term outlook. Thank you.
