The State Enterprise Act is based on the strict principle of resortism, i.e. each department establishes state enterprises in its sphere of competence and only and only in its sphere of competence. Although it is not excluded for a public undertaking to carry out ancillary activities in other fields, it is gradually shown that such strict and rigid arrangements are not appropriate. Therefore, this amendment proposes that several ministries should be able to perform the tasks of the founder. The specific arrangement of their mutual relations will be left to the contract. This will achieve high flexibility. In other words, the petitioner believes that it is not appropriate to impose on ministries a model of management which, of the nature of the matter, will not be appropriate in all conceivable situations. It is more appropriate to set the steering model always according to the needs of the current situation. However, the treaty will always have to be approved by the government, so we would avoid the risk of excessive favour. The government will also be able to unilaterally abolish such an arrangement.
