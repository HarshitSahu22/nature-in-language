ParlaMint-CZ_2023-05-03-ps2021-063-08-001-107.u2	Madam Vice-President, honourable fellow Members of the Government, honourable Members, honourable Members, have a good morning. I submit a bill amending Act No. 123/1998 Coll., on the right to environmental information. The main objective of this proposal is to bring national legislation into line with European legislation, namely by regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council, which is the essence of unification of reporting obligations which are carried out with the use of spatial data and the decision of the European Union which is implemented in the INSPIRE Directive. The Gestor of this implementation is the Ministry of Environment. The amendment introduces the concept of a national data set, which means a data set that meets one of the INSPIRE themes. The list of these topics is now being moved from the implementing legislation to the annex to the Act. Access to the Geoportal National Data Set, an information system managed by the Ministry of Environment, is provided by the spatial data theme coordinator. The coordinators of these spatial data are identified by mutual agreement in the Coordination Committee for INSPIRE, which is an advisory body of the Ministry of Environment. I would like to start by saying, even if I say it at the table, that the Ministry agrees with the proposed amendments as discussed by the guarantee committee. Thanks.
