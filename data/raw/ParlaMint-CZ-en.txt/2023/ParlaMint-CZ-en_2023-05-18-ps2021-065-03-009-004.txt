ParlaMint-CZ_2023-05-18-ps2021-065-03-009-004.u2	Thank you for your word. Ladies and gentlemen, Mr President, the proposal for a new law on public auctions enters second reading. So let me briefly remind you of its main objectives and summarize the current course of its discussions. The bill replaces the existing, almost a quarter-century old law on public auctions, which is, let's face it, largely outdated, and at the time of its creation it only envisaged the implementation of classical auctions. However, the current trend is electronic auctions, which is what the bill reflects. There is also a simplification and some liberalisation of the auction process itself, particularly in the case of auctions now referred to as voluntary auctions, which will significantly reduce the administrative burden and costs associated with the implementation of the public auction. The main objectives of the Act on Public Auctions are above all, and I will now only be in the points: harmonisation of the Act on Public Auctions with the Civil Code, more precise regulation of electronic auctions in the law itself, simplification of the auction process, as I have mentioned, strengthening the enforcement of statutory obligations and electronicisation, publishing information on public auctions and auctions using the new information system Central register of public auctions. At first reading, the bill received support across the political parties, which we very much appreciate. Subsequently, the proposal was discussed at the Constitutional and Legal Committee and within several stages of the negotiations, Mrs Ožanová and I discussed some topics, including in cooperation with the Ministry of Justice. The Constitutional Legal Committee then unanimously recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the draft law on public auctions. So now there is a second reading ahead of us, and I believe that support for this proposal will continue. ParlaMint-CZ_2023-05-18-ps2021-065-03-009-004.u6	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the main objective of the proposal is, above all, to align the legislation of public auctions in this law with the general regulation contained in the Civil Code. Among the other objectives of this proposal is a more precise regulation of electronic auctions, strengthening the legal certainty of the entities concerned in the context of the auction process and the enforcement of statutory obligations, making the auction process more effective and eliminating unnecessary administrative burdens. The draft law will therefore be designed in such a way that it is possible to impose a penalty in the form of a cash penalty in the form of an income penalty for infringements of the essential obligations laid down by law, and the income from these fines is considered to be up to 1 million per year in the Treasury. For auctioneers, the proposed legislation will in particular mean simplifying the auction process, eliminating unnecessary administrative burdens and reducing costs related to the preparation and implementation of the auction. The elimination of non-compliance with the Civil Code and the Act governing the public auction will contribute to the higher legal certainty of the consumer, i.e. the auction participant or the person whose rights are affected by the auction. The SPD generally supports reducing the administrative burden and increasing the legal certainty of entrepreneurs and citizens, so this proposal as a whole makes sense and we support the idea. The technical parameters that we discussed at the constitutional-legal committee meeting will be dealt with in the amendments, so we will see what specific amendments will be read, and then, of course, we will support them, too. Thank you for your attention.
