ParlaMint-CZ_2023-04-20-ps2021-063-05-006-097.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, these are another of the many bilateral air agreements that the Czech Republic currently has agreed with the various countries of the world of 95 in total. The relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Cape Verde in the field of civil aviation have not yet been actually regulated by any bilateral international treaty. From the point of view of the Czech air carriers, this market has been used for a long time in the field of irregular air transport with the potential to establish regular air links or in combination with nearby destinations. The Air Agreement provides for standard conditions for the performance of scheduled commercial air transport between Contracting Parties, such as the scope of transport rights, the issuance of authorisations to air carriers of the Contracting Parties, commercial cooperation between air carriers, but also for operational safety, aviation security and so on. The negotiation of the agreement was, in addition to the market's potential for regular operation, a priority, taking into account the other party's earlier observations, that an air agreement is a prerequisite for such operation, so I hereby allow myself to ask the Chamber of Deputies for the referral of material for discussion in the House's Foreign Committee. Thank you for your attention.
