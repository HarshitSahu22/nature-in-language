It concerns the provision of so-called key information to investors in the collective investment fund. This amendment contains only two amendment points. It introduces a rule that the obligation to provide a key investor with a key information document can also be fulfilled by providing them with this information pursuant to the European Regulation on investment products. This means a small administrative simplification of the fact that the same information may not be transmitted to investors in duplicate.
