Madam Vice-President, Members of the Government, Colleagues, Colleagues, I am here to present a proposal for an amendment to Act No. 123/1998 Coll., on the right to environmental information. At first reading, this amendment is based on making a balance between the changes that have occurred in the area of European law and at our national level. This means the goal is defined primarily by adjusting some things, such as, say, terms and specific terminology in this law, such as a national data set. The national data set is a data set that meets one of the INSPIRE themes. The list of spatial data themes is newly moved from implementing legislation to the Annex to the Act. This national data set also consists of data of one or more mandatory entities. The dates of one mandatory subject are, for example, a cadastral map, the source of which for the Czech Republic is the Czech office of survey and cadastral. Data of more than one mandatory entity are typically territorial plans made up at local level by a large number of municipalities, coordinated at national level by the Ministry of Regional Development. We also regulate the area of access to the national data set on the geoportal, information system managed by the Ministry of Environment. It therefore ensures the so-called spatial data theme coordinator. It provides either for topics where it is the only provider, typically the data from the Cadastral of the Czech Office of Landscape and Cadastral, or in cooperation with other mandatory entities in the data where the cooperation of several providers is necessary. The spatial data coordinators shall be identified by mutual agreement in the Coordination Committee for INSPIRE, the advisory body of the Minister for the Environment. The mutual agreement between coordinators has been working for several years, i.e. the current amendment is the agreement that, let's say, informally anchors the agreement. So these are the fundamental changes that this amendment regulates, or, of course, they are further developed in the content of the explanatory report, so at least so much for the introduction. Thank you for your attention.

ParlaMint-CZ_2023-01-11-ps2021-050-02-004-006.u6	Thank you for the floor, Madam Vice-President. Dear colleagues, the Minister has stated everything that is important. I'll fill in a brief summary here. The proposal for an amendment to the Act on the Law on Environmental Information has been developed because of the amendment of regulations at Union level, namely the amendment of regulations and implementing regulations relating to Directive 2007/2/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council, establishing an infrastructure for spatial information on the European Community, hereinafter referred to as the INSPIRE Directive. On the basis of this amendment, the law and its subsequent decree should be brought into line. It is also necessary to include in the law what has been working at national level for ten years under agreements within the coordination committee for INSPIRE, the so-called KOVIN. Following the directive, a coordination structure is created and new obligations are created for certain obliged entities. It is therefore necessary to adjust this area directly in the law so that the obligations laid down are sufficiently specific and that responsible bodies are identified. This will increase the effectiveness of legislation and its enforcement. Furthermore, Union legislation complements the implementing regulation for the INSPIRE Directive, which has been established gradually since 2008, and lays down obligations for metadata, data sets, network services and data sharing. These regulations are fully directly applicable and the obligations resulting from them further specified what the Environmental Information Law provided for in the framework in 2009 and therefore the implementation of the obligations has been carried out for several years without the need for amendment precisely according to the regulation and the follow-up technical guidelines. Responsibilities among KOVIN members were divided by the INSPIRE implementation strategy. Mandatory entities, i.e. individual KOVIN members, have divided between themselves the coordination and control of the implementation of individual topics. The proposed law is in accordance with the constitutional order of the Czech Republic and is based on the principles set out in the Constitution of the Czech Republic and the Charter of Fundamental Rights and Freedoms. Similarly, the proposed legislation complies with the obligations of the Czech Republic arising from international agreements, in particular fully corresponds with the UN Economic Commission for Europe Convention on Access to Information, Public Participation in Decision-making and Access to Legal Protection in Environmental Affairs. In general, this amendment strengthens environmental protection in terms of data collection and analysis. Therefore, I recommend that this law be moved to further reading for myself. Thank you for your attention.
