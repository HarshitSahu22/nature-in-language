As the Prime Minister said, the proposal is based on the need to fulfil obligations arising from membership of the European Union. It strengthens the position of competition authorities so that they can enforce rules more effectively and is also about ensuring the proper functioning of the internal market. I would also like to emphasise once again what has already been said here that the European Commission is already infraringement proceedings against the Czech Republic, as the Czech Republic is obliged to implement the relevant directive into the Czech rule of law by 4. 2. 2021 has not been complied with. This is what follows the already mentioned introduction of the law into practice, when it is proposed that the effectiveness of this law should be on the 15th day following the date of its publication, precisely in order to fulfil our obligation as soon as possible. I will not focus on what is involved in implementation. I just want to repeat things that do not concern purely implementation. It is a question of settlement, the possibility of public authorities to propose commitments in favour of maintaining effective competition, the possibility of protecting the identity of the petitioner, joint and several liability for payment of the fine imposed, if more than one person making up one competitor commits the same offence, disclosure of the information contained in the file to law enforcement authorities, the treatment of offences and the extension of the Leniency Programme to vertical agreements. At the same time, however, this performance has a direct link to funds allocated from public budgets. Compared to the current situation, it appears highly desirable to specify the performance of the public resources financed, which will actually be subject to a ban. The proposed regulation aims to ensure that the punishment in the form of a ban on performance effectively affects a wider set of transactions than are covered by public budgets, but contains an exception reflecting situations in which an important public interest is given in order not to apply the imposed ban. It seems very logical to extend the prohibition on the exercise of another paid function or any other gainful activity, except for the management of own property and the activities of scientific, pedagogical, literary, publicist and artistic activities, set now only for the President and also for Vice-Presidents. This is really very logical for me, because the Vice-Presidents represent the President, so the same rights and obligations should be in place. Colleagues, I only mention this because this is an important change, and I think we should remind ourselves of it before the vote itself. The new regulation allows the Authority to use records for the purposes of taking evidence into account the most important anticompetitive practices, i.e. the records of telecommunications operations, the data on telecommunications traffic and the records taken in the monitoring of persons and objects already provided by the criminal proceedings authority. The original transitional provision shall be supplemented by a rule on the possibility of using the recorded records in such a way that, in proceedings initiated after the entry into force of this Act, the records which have been issued in criminal proceedings may be used only after the date of application of this Act.
