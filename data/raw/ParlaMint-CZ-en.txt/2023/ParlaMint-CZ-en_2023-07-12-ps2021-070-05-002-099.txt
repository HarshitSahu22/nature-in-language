ParlaMint-CZ_2023-07-12-ps2021-070-05-002-099.u2	Madam Vice-President, Prime Minister, fellow Members, honourable Government, allow me to present the Government's amendment to Act No. 219/2003 Coll., on the putting into circulation of seeds and planting plants. The main objective - this is a minor amendment - is, firstly, adaptation to the Commission Delegated Regulation. It is the production and marketing of plant propagating material from organic heterogeneous material of certain families or species, i.e. seeds or seedlings of one species, which have common characteristics while preserving genetic diversity. Furthermore, the amendment introduces the possibility of voluntary registration of varieties of certain minor species which will be added to the second list. Furthermore, we have an adaptation to a regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council extending the duration of Community plant variety rights for varieties of certain species and groups of species of flower bulbs, small fruit trees and ornamental trees by five years. That is where the duration of the plant variety rights is extended. Further clarification and addition of certain provisions of the applicable law resulting from application practice and electronicisation, including publication of relevant forms on the website of the Central Audit and Testing Institute of Agriculture. We also regulate the tariff of the Act on Administrative Charges. And that's basically it. To sum up, this is a combination of the implementation of European legislation and then some minor changes that have been consulted with the Ministry of Agriculture. The draft law was discussed by the House's agricultural committee on 24 March, recommended that the House adopt the draft law as amended by the amendments adopted. These relate to the breeding law and the Act on Fertilizers and have been drawn up in cooperation with our Ministry of Agriculture. They are in line with the concept of the petitioner and will contribute to the improvement of the government bill. That's why I agree with them as a minister here at third reading. During the second reading, Mr Karel Smetana and others' amendments were then applied. These relate to the Law on the circulation of seed and seed and, in particular, to the use of mobile seed cleaners. This is where the conditions between stationary and mobile cleaning devices are levelled. It's basically a technique, but we're leveling out the condition that isn't the same today and fair for mobile and stationary cleaning devices. Once again, this amendment was agreed by the Ministry of Agriculture and we participated in its drafting. Ladies and gentlemen, this is a small, basically technical and implementation amendment, and I would like to ask you as Minister of Agriculture, although I do not submit this proposal to you until... or I only justify it at third reading, support and approval, as amended by all amendments. Thank you.
