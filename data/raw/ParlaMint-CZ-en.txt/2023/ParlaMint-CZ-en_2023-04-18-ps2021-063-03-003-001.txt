ParlaMint-CZ_2023-04-18-ps2021-063-03-003-001.u2	Madam President, honourable Members, outside the legislative work plan of the Government, the Ministry of the Environment has drawn up a draft law amending the law on the right to information on the environment. The main objective of the proposal is to bring national legislation into line with European legislation, namely with the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council, which is to unify reporting obligations which are implemented using spatial data and the implementation of EU decisions implementing the INSPIRE Directive. The Gestor for the implementation of the directive in the Czech Republic is the Ministry of Environment, which cooperates with a number of entities during implementation. The amendment introduces the concept of a national data set, which means a data set that meets one of the INSPIRE themes. The list of spatial data themes is newly moved from implementing legislation to the Annex to the Act. The national data set consists of the data of one or more mandatory entities. The dates of one mandatory subject are, for example, a cadastral map, the source of which for the Czech Republic is the Czech office of survey and cadastral. Data from more than one mandatory entity are typically territorial plans made up at local level by a large number of municipalities and coordinated at national level by the Ministry of Regional Development. Access to the Geoportal National Data Set in the information system managed by the Ministry of Environment ensures the coordinator of the spatial data theme. The spatial data coordinators shall be identified by mutual agreement in the Coordination Committee for INSPIRE, which shall be an advisory body of the Minister for the Environment. The mutual agreement between coordinators has actually been in place for several years, and the current amendment merely lays the agreement on a legislative basis. The Ministry of the Environment agrees with all amendments approved by the Committee on the Environment. Thank you for your attention.

[[16.00 hours]] The second amendment regulates the grounds for refusing an environmental information request under current caselaw, i.e. if the disclosure of this information would have a negative effect on the course of justice, the ability of any person to obtain a fair trial and the ability of the public authority to conduct criminal or disciplinary investigations. The third amendment replaces the report on the environmental status of individual regions with a summary report on the environment in the regions of the Czech Republic. The last amendment is of a legislative technical nature, responds to the laws already adopted and at the same time changes the effectiveness of the law to the fifteenth day following its publication.
