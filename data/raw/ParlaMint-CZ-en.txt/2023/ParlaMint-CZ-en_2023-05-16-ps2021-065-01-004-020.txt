ParlaMint-CZ_2023-05-16-ps2021-065-01-004-020.u2	Thank you. Mr Vice-President, fellow Members, allow me to briefly mention this House print number 423, i.e. the amendment to the Labour Code, which is presented here as the press at first reading. The primary reason for submitting this bill is to ensure, in particular, the transposition of European Union law, namely, two directives - the directive on transparent and predictable conditions and the directive on the balance between the work and private life of parents and carers, the directive on certain aspects of the regulation of working time. The issue must be dealt with urgently, since both of these directives were to be transposed by August 2022 at the latest. The so-called non-notification procedure is already under way against the Czech Republic and sanctions are in real danger if we do not complete this transposition. The European Commission is monitoring this legislative process. The draft law will change both the general legislation contained in the Labour Code and the subsequent changes in the specific legislation governing the legal relations of dependent activities, such as civil servants in service, members of security forces or professional soldiers. The most important proposed changes for the purposes of the transposition of EU law are the amendment of the legislation on non-employment agreements, the so-called DPP and DPČ, the obligation to schedule working hours, to observe continuous rests, to provide breaks for food and rest, to lay down the right to leave, the possibility to ask, in defined cases, for reasons of dismissal and others, to extend the employer's information obligation when creating or amending employment relations and to tighten up the deadlines for their fulfilment, to establish the employer's obligation in writing to justify any refusal of the application of certain employees - caring for workers, pregnant workers - to carry out work at a distance. Furthermore, important changes beyond the transposition of EU law are the regulation of service of labour documents with regard to progressing digitization and the needs of practice, further the adjustment of conditions for distance work, so-called home working, including the possibility of flat-rate compensation for all costs incurred in carrying out distance work, the possibility of pre-negotiating with the employee that the reimbursement of costs or part of the work does not belong to him, the regulation of distance work under emergency situations following crisis laws, the issue of compensation for service accidents and occupational diseases in accordance with the requirements of the Ministry of Defence. It is proposed for divided efficiency - the first day of the calendar month following the date of the law. New adjustment of leave for employees with DPP and DPČ - the first day of the calendar year following the date of publication of the law, i.e. probably 1 January 2024. On the submitted government bill, there is a consensus between the social partners, including the Chamber of Commerce, with whom this proposal has been intensively consulted throughout its preparation, and the social partners have worked closely on its preparation in order to meet the needs of practice to the maximum extent and to be balanced both from the point of view of employers and from the point of view of employees. Thank you for your attention, and I, Mr President, will have to apologize, because at 9 o'clock I have other urgent work obligations, and I will ask, with permission, to continue with the government for this press for me, Prime Minister Ivan Bartoš. Thank you for understanding.
