ParlaMint-CZ_2023-06-21-ps2021-069-02-002-002.u2	Good afternoon, Mr Vice-President, colleagues, colleagues, the public. I dare to submit an amendment to the Employment Act here. It's Law No. 435/2004 Coll., House printing 450 at first reading. The primary reason for the amendment of this law is to make changes in the field of agency employment and also to respond to changes in the labour market. We are also responding to the area of, say, the so-called anti-bureaucratic package, which was approved by the government at the end of August 2022. The most important changes lie in these areas - and this is therefore a specification of cases of infringements of the Employment Act leading both obligatoryly and optionally to withdrawal of employment mediation permits and to complement the new reason for not providing synergies to the labour inspection authorities during inspection. Furthermore, clarification of the rules governing the costs of the proceedings, namely the extension of the scope of administrative proceedings for which the imposition of costs is required, on the grounds of the employment agency. Furthermore, the extension of information communicated by the employment agencies to the Directorate-General of the Office of Labour of the Czech Republic on a regular basis in the context of compliance with the notification requirement pursuant to § 59 para. 2 of the Employment Act. Further refinement of the legislation in the field of obtaining professional practice, increasing bail, further unification and refinement of the terminology of anchoring the end of the period when repeated authorisation for employment mediation may be requested, as well as measures for the protection of agency staff consisting of the prohibition that the employee of the employment agency should not be temporarily assigned to work with the user for more than three years. The proposed changes partly affect the law of the European Union, namely the Directive on Agency Employment and the Directive on the conditions of entry and residence of third-country nationals on the basis of intra-corporate transfer. The proposed effectiveness is 1 January 2024. I, of course, also honestly announce one thing here that I also perceive the initiatives that come from practice, from the Confederation of Industry and Transport, the Chamber of Commerce and some other organisations that have yet to come up with suggestions that are certainly relevant for me, which I will discuss, I will be very happy to conduct the debate with representatives of individual coalition clubs who are expertly involved in this issue, so that we can make some adjustments, some refinements and tightening up in the second and third reading.
