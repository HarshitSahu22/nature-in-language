It's an honor to present it to you on behalf. The purpose of this amendment, House Press 437, is to enable the State, if there is an urgent need, to increase the share capital of state enterprises from its own resources in order to ensure their financial stability and further operation. State enterprises currently exist in the Czech Republic about sixty. According to the legislation in force, the State can only increase the share capital of state-owned enterprises by the property acquired by the company in the course of carrying out its business. This provision does not apply to public limited liability companies. The update of this amendment, this new legislation, results from the need of the State to respond to the current difficult economic and security situation. The State needs to have at its disposal instruments to capitalise on state-owned enterprises providing the security interests of the State or important public needs. Such a public need is, for example, the service of the Czech Post, whose situation has become an impulse for this amendment of the Act on State Enterprise. It is evident that the state needs the Czech Post to become the primary contact point between the state and citizens who will not be able or willing to switch with the state to digital communication in a far wider range of activities than the state is demanding of it today. As has already been stated, for time reasons, the Chamber of Deputies is proposed that the law should give its consent at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
