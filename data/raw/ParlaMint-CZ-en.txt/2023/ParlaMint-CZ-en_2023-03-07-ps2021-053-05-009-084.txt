ParlaMint-CZ_2023-03-07-ps2021-053-05-009-084.u4	Thank you, Madam President. Ladies and gentlemen, I will be very brief. As the appellant has already said, the Line Act is speeding up procedures for the deployment and authorisation of transport, water and energy infrastructure structures and, for example, enables the issuing of conditional administrative decisions. The substantive content of the amendment to the Act is that the possibilities of this Act should also apply to the construction of a hot water supply from the Dukovany nuclear power plant to Brno, so that this construction becomes a listed priority building in the annex to this Act, and the introduction of this directly construction should help to speed up the process and property preparation of the entire project. This project is not only important for ensuring the supply of heat for the Brno agglomeration and for reducing the dependence of the Brno heating industry on gas supplies, but, by its overall importance, it will contribute to the future increase in energy security and self-sufficiency and to increasing the efficient use of available resources in our country. The Government discussed this proposal at its meeting on 21 September 2022 and agreed with the proposal. Let me conclude by saying a few words about effectiveness, because the appellants originally proposed effectiveness from 1 January 2023. That deadline has already been completed. However, if we today approve this law according to the proposed § 90, that means first reading, it will automatically occur under Act No. 309/1999 Coll. the entry into force of the Act on the date of its publication in the Collection of Laws and, if this is the case, the Act will be published in the Collection of Laws by 30 June, which we assume will take effect on 1 July of this calendar year. Thank you for your attention.
