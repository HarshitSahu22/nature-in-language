Let me come forward and justify the amendment, which is registered under number 2475. It is Mr Metnar, Mr Bělica, Mr Wenzl, Mr Ratiborský and Mr Janulík, to whom I will sign up in a detailed debate. According to the proposed Section 3 of the Defence Finance Act, the Ministry of Defence proposes the amount of State defence expenses and their use in the processing of the draft State Budget Act. Where a budgetary requirement in the field of defence financing arises with another administrator of the State Budget Chapter, that administrator shall submit to the Ministry of Defence his request for approval no later than 36 months before the beginning of the financial year in which the required defence expenditure is to be included in the draft State Budget Act. If it is an urgent requirement that could not be foreseen, the request may be submitted to the Ministry of Defence at a later date, but not later than 12 months before the beginning of the year in which the required defence expenditure is to be included in the draft State Budget Act. The Ministry of Defence will then assess the request made by another administrator of the state budget chapter and, if it concludes that it fulfils the financing conditions laid down by the law at the same time and according to the specified priorities, the requirement corresponds to the State's defence objectives, it will include them in the State's defence expenditure. However, the law does not regulate the procedure in the event that: The Ministry of Defence will assess the request of another Chapter Administrator with the conclusion that it does not meet the conditions described above. This entrusts the Ministry of Defence with authority to decide on the requirements of other chapter administrators, even though they are different to each other. The logical consequence does not accept a request from the Ministry of Defence, when the submission of a case to the Government for a decision as joint superior body and at the same time as the supreme authority was executive. This would prevent the Ministry of Defence from deciding itself on the appropriateness, effectiveness and priority of the budgetary requirements applied, without the possibility of remedying a substantively incorrect assessment. It cannot be called into question that it is the Ministry of Defence, which will usually have the best and most complete information on the fulfilment of the statutory criteria in relation to funding and to ensuring the defence of the State. However, it is not possible to rule out a situation where it will be a different resort, which will apply legitimate requirements in this area from the point of view of its competence, such as the Ministry of the Interior or the Ministry of Transport. For these reasons, it is proposed that the Ministry of Defence should be obliged to submit a budgetary request to another administrator of the chapter for a decision to the Government if it does not comply with it itself. Government bill on the financing of defence and on the amendment of Act No. 218/2000, on budgetary rules and amending certain related laws, as amended, is therefore amended as follows: The following sentence is added: "Otherwise, the Ministry of Defence shall submit a request pursuant to paragraph 3. This Decision shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union. Thank you for your attention.
