ParlaMint-CZ_2023-04-18-ps2021-063-03-002-007.u2	Madam President, honourable Members, allow me to repeat the Government's bill repealing the Act on Agricultural Sheets and Agricultural Public Warehouses. First of all, the objective of the bill is to abolish the legislation no longer applied, namely Act No. 307/2000 Coll., on agricultural storage sheets and agricultural public warehouses, which has been applied in practice for about three years. In addition to the repeal of that law and its amendment, related amendments are proposed in Act No. 229/1992 Coll., on Commodity Exchanges, and in Act No. 634/2004 Coll., on administrative fees consisting in the abolition of part of the provisions of these laws in which agricultural stock certificates and agricultural public warehouses are referred. It is also proposed to repeal Implementing Decree No 403/2000 Coll., determining the types of agricultural goods on which agricultural stock certificates may be issued and the conditions for the operation of agricultural public warehouses. The draft law was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 19th meeting on 1 March 2023 and recommended by its resolution to the Chamber of Deputies to approve the Government's draft law in the submitted version. Thank you for your attention.
