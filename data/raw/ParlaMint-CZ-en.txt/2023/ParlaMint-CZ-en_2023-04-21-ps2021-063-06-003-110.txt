ParlaMint-CZ_2023-04-21-ps2021-063-06-003-110.u2	Thank you. Mr President, honourable Members, we are once again meeting here in a very short time on the occasion of a proposal for an amendment to the Act on Mining Activities, Explosives and the State Mining Administration, which was prepared by the Czech Mining Authority. As has already been presented in the first and second reading of this proposal, its main objective is to improve existing legislation, eliminate its shortcomings identified in practice, reduce the administrative burden on entrepreneurs and save the funds spent from the state budget on the operation of the State Mining Administration. To this end, it is proposed, firstly, to remove the obligation of entrepreneurs to report some identical data to both the Customs Administration and the Czech Mining Authority, secondly, to abolish the District Mining Authority for the territories of the Liberec and Vysočina regions and to transfer it to the District Mining Authority for the territories of the Královéhradecký and Pardubický Region, thirdly, to amend the method of verifying the integrity and health fitness of persons dealing with explosives, fourthly, to establish an institute of the so-called silent consent of the authorities concerned and intelligence services in the procedure for the authorisation of export, import and transit of explosives, fifthly, to register the obligation of the relevant district Mining Authority to inform the Police department of the extent of which they are to carry out the tearing work, which ensures a flexible exchange of information between the Mining Authority and the Police of the Czech Republic. The proposed law was discussed at a meeting of the Economic Committee which recommended that the Chamber of Deputies discuss the proposal and approve it in the version of the submitted Government Bill. On 18 April 2023, there was a second reading of this bill, with no amendments or other proposals being applied to it. Madam, honourable Members, I hereby ask you to support this proposal and to refer it to the Senate. Thank you for your attention.

Dear members of the government, honored colleagues, in brief I would sum up the whole procedure for the adoption or discussion of a government bill amending Act No. 61/1988 Coll., on Mining Activities, Explosives and the State Mining Report, as amended. The bill was sent to Members as press 297 on 9 September 2022. The Guarantee Economic Committee discussed the bill and issued on 17 March 2023 a resolution delivered to Members as press 297/2 with a recommendation to approve the proposal. The bill went through second reading, both in general and in a detailed debate on 18 April 2023 at the 63rd meeting of the Chamber of Deputies, and no amendments were tabled on the bill. In a detailed debate, a proposal was adopted at that meeting that the guarantee committee should not deal with the proposal as Resolution 590. The guarantee committee did not discuss the proposal further. Since there are no amendments, the vote on the law as a whole can be taken. Thank you for your attention.
