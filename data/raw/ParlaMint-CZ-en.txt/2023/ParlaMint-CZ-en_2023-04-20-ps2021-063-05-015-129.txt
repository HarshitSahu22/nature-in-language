Annual report of the Czech Radio Council on the activities of the Czech Radio in 2021 /German press 190/ This House press has discussed the election committee, whose resolution has been delivered to you as House press 190/1, and I ask that the rapporteur of the electoral committee, Mr Lubomír Brož, take the floor and inform us of the committee's deliberations and make a motion for a resolution.
ParlaMint-CZ_2023-04-20-ps2021-063-05-015-129.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the House Press 190, as has already been said, contains an annual report on the activities of the Czech Radio in 2021. Very briefly, when I pull the most important things out of this report: 2021 was the sixth, and therefore the last year of the six-year mandate of the Director-General of Czech Radio, Mr Mgr René Zavoral, however, was re-elected and started another six-year term. The Czech Radio Council worked in accordance with what the Czech Radio Act imposed on it, so the Czech Radio mainly made sure that the Czech Radio performed the function of public media in accordance with the Czech Radio Act and the Czech Radio Code. A great deal of attention was also paid to suggestions and complaints from the listeners, the board focused on the continuous assessment of individual sending stations of the Czech Radio. Two reports were submitted in due time and thus the annual report of the Council on the activities of the Czech Radio for 2020 as well as the report on the management of the Czech Radio for 2020. Interesting news coverage of the Czech Radio: in 2021 the station prepared 15 000 news, news and publicist posts every month. [[unintelligible]] The contributions were shared between individual stations and one of the main topics, as the Czech Radio reported during 2021 was, of course, a worldwide pandemic of Covid-19 disease. Other major topics were parliamentary elections, the arrival of the new President of the United States, social issues, health care and similar. I will now introduce you to the electoral committee's resolution of 6 October 2022 on the House Press: "The Election Committee of the Chamber of Deputies after hearing the opening words of the President of the Council of the Czech Radio . Miroslav Dittrich, Mr Lubomír Brož's report and after the debate I. recommends to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to adopt the following resolution: The Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic approves the annual report by the Czech Radio Council on the activities of the Czech Radio in 2021 according to the Chamber of Press 190; II. Instructs the President of the Committee to present this resolution to the President of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. III. empowers the Committee rapporteur to report at the meeting of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic on the annual report by the Czech Radio Council on the activities of the Czech Radio in 2021 by the Election Committee."
