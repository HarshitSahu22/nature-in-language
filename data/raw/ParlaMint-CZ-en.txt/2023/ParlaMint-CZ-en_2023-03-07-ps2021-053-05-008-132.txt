ParlaMint-CZ_2023-03-07-ps2021-053-05-008-132.u14	Thank you for the floor, Madam Vice-President. I am applying to the amendment, House document 2377 and House document 2378. Since I did not speak in the general debate, allow me to make a brief statement. The House document 2377, the amendment addresses possible complications across the energy sector and aims to maintain uniform conditions for technical infrastructure throughout the Czech Republic and thus not allow differences in the case of selected municipalities, which can determine their own construction regulations. The rules for placing technical infrastructure have their own rules, based on international and national technical standards and sectoral regulations. These solve, among other things, the conditions for placing. Any differences can cause significant complications for designing technical infrastructure in selected municipalities. And allow, in short, the reasoning of House document 2378. The exchange of lines and networks of technical infrastructure can only be considered small buildings according to the construction law if very strict conditions are met. Within the framework of the amendment, it is proposed to extend the terms and conditions concerning the possibility of a contract for the future, the establishment of a material burden. At the moment, it is necessary to have a material burden in place in order to meet the conditions. Although ensuring a material burden is a priority for operators, their practice so far clearly shows that it is not always an acceptable or appropriate path. This is due in particular to the fact that the builder does not always know the exact location of the construction at the time of construction, but it is necessary to know the exact location of the construction for the establishment of the material burden. This does not apply in the case of a future contract, while at the same time it provides for the possibility of demanding the establishment of a material burden in court if there were complications on the part of the owner. The proposal is thus essentially a legislative technical regulation which will allow the exchange of technical infrastructure to be regarded as a minor construction, in most cases known from existing practice, and will enable the technical infrastructure operators to fulfil the public interest while providing sufficient solutions to the private relations between the operator and the owner of the immovable property concerned, i.e. to protect the ownership in question. Thank you.
