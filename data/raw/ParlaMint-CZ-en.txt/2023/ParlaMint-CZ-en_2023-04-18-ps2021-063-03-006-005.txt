ParlaMint-CZ_2023-04-18-ps2021-063-03-006-005.u2	Thank you, Madam President. Once again, ladies and gentlemen, let me mention the Government's bill amending Act No. 156/2000 Coll., on the verification of firearms and ammunition. As has already been said at first reading and subsequently during negotiations in the Economic Committee, this is a legislative technical amendment to the Act, which aims primarily to respond to the development of the technical terminology used in the field of weapons and ammunition. The envisaged bill also takes into account the new regulation of the Firearms and Firearms Act. The amendment also includes the subject matter of the modification of the activity consisting of checking the identification marks. In accordance with the above, the amendment to the Firearms and Ammunition Verification Act provides for the activities of the Czech Office for the Testing of Weapons and Ammunition consisting of the classification of explosive substances and objects under the ADR Agreement on the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road, when the Czech Office for the Testing of Weapons and Ammunition has carried out this activity since 2010 on the basis of repeated mandates issued by the Ministry of Transport. The submitted government proposal was dealt with at its 25th meeting by the Economic Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic. At this meeting, one amendment was made to correct a legislative technical error consisting of a dual penalty for offences. I agree with the resolution of the Committee on Economic Affairs number 134 and therefore, honourable Members, I would like to ask you to support the bill. Thank you.

As has already been justified here by the Minister, the amendment is of a strictly legislative technical nature and only adapts the text of the law to the terminology of the relevant field of expertise. The proposed amendments to the Firearms and Ammunition Verification Act also respond to the recent amendment of the Firearms and Firearms Act, the Firearms Act, through which the transposition of Directives 2017/853 of the European Parliament and of the Council has been ensured.
