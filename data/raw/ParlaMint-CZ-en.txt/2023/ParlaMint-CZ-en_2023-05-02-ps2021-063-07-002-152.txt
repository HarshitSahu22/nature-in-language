ParlaMint-CZ_2023-05-02-ps2021-063-07-002-152.u2	Thank you for the floor. Madam Vice-President, ladies and gentlemen, very briefly because I introduced the law at first reading. The aim of the proposal is the extraordinary disappearance of the designated accessories of tax forgiveness for tax debts, primarily within the remit of the Ministry of Finance, as was the case for the events of the gracious summer I and II in 2021 and 2022. Another aim is to eliminate negative payments, often in the order of CZK units, and thus to one-timely relieve debtors and tax administrators of the need to incur unnecessary costs for their registration and administration. The proposed bill concerns outstanding payments on cash transactions administered in particular by the Financial and Customs Administration of the Czech Republic or entirely marginally by the Ministry of Finance. The specific category is the area of so-called judicial claims, or tax cash transactions administered by courts or the Prison Service of the Czech Republic. Furthermore, the law contains an authorisation for municipalities and regions to issue a resolution of the council, which can also apply the proposed law to certain financial transactions which are administered by municipalities and regions. [[Noise in the courtroom.]] In the case of the present proposal, it is a separate law of one-off character with the subsidiary procedure of the tax system. The proposal includes two relatively separate measures. First of all, it is an extraordinary loss of designated tax accessories by forgiveness. It will be intended only for natural persons, whether or not it is an entrepreneur. The basic condition shall be the payment of the security to which the accessories are attached. The proposed forgiveness will concern interest, periodic penalty payments, penalties for late claims and costs of proceedings, including execution costs. In order to avoid speculative behaviour of debtors, the possibility of forgiveness will concern only the accessories of those securities that arose by the relevant date, namely 30 September last year. The payment of the principal itself will have to take place no later than the end of the so-called decisive period lasting from 1 July to 30 November 2023. As an alternative, the bill allows the distribution of the principal payment in instalments within one year. The second area is the extraordinary disappearance of the tax and tax-related back-payments, which will concern all tax entities, both natural and legal persons. As backpackers, the underpayments of less than 200 crowns will disappear, without distinction to their kind. In the case of real estate tax it will be 30 crowns. The object of the demise will be only negative payments incurred by the decisive day, as in the case of one, 30 September last year. The acquisition of the effectiveness of the draft law is foreseen on 1 July 2023. In the course of the discussion to date, three topics were presented for the amendment of the draft law on which political agreement can be assumed and with which the Ministry of Finance has agreed, including the preparation of the text of the relevant amendments. So I'm just going to sum them up briefly, there's three of them. In advance, I advise that I will give a favourable opinion on all three of them at the third reading. The first is the frequency of instalments from quarterly to monthly, second the acceptance of the repayment scheme also for persons with tax execution running and third the extension of the deadline for municipal councils and regions from 30 June to 30 September so that they can convene a council if they are interested in using this institute and approve the relevant resolution of the municipal council, city or region. Thank you for your attention.
