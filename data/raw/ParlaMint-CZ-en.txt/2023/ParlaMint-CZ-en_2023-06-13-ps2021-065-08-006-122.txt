Mr Vice-President, ladies and gentlemen, once again, I would like to make a few comments, this time on the management of Czech Television in 2021. The basic financial resources of Czech Television are mainly television fees, which we all know very well, so-called licence fees if you want, and they reached CZK 5.7 billion in 2021 and their amount was almost 47 million more than the plan, and CZK 64 million more than the year 2020. [[18.00 hours]] The height of this television fee, i.e. the main source of funding for Czech Television, is constant over the long term. This is the fact that this Annual Report on Czech Television's Management in 2021 repeatedly points out precisely in the context of reducing the real value of this fee and that is an obvious and visible fact. Unfortunately, I lack an analysis of changes in the price of inputs needed for the production of television programmes, for example. There is a presumption here, and I think it is quite reasonable that the price of broadcasting technology is gradually decreasing, because the autonomous decision of management of Czech Television increases the number of programmes of the public service broadcasters, but also increases the finished production of programmes. After all, we see this with private broadcasters and the number of programs they provide. According to the annual report, for example, production increased from 15,500 hours of finished production of programmes in 2008 by 4,000 hours to 19 300 hours in 2021. From this point of view, it seems necessary to reconsider whether the price of production has been shifted as a result of technological progress. I am particularly positive about the efforts made by Czech Television, and I have already mentioned this in the discussion of the report on Czech Radio, namely that in 2021 she tried to obtain new taxpayers who did not comply with this legal obligation to sign up for the television fee. The multiple increases sent from 42 000 in 2020 to 210 000 in 2021 were positively reflected in Czech Television's management. The success of the subscription to the television fee has reached 25% of the growth, i.e. by a quarter, and this is a total of over 51 000 new subscribers. It is inevitable to continue this activity and it is also necessary to start a debate on the non-acceptation of the problem of a stowaway on this public service. I think and hope that this too will be one of the main agendas of the new CEO on Czech Television. According to published data, 4.5 million farm households are in total in the Czech Republic by the end of 2021, with 3.3 million households registered for taxpayers' records for the same period. There is therefore no doubt obvious non-compliance with applicable legislation in this area. And here it is good to note that Czech Television has tools to educate Czech citizens on this issue, which should be used effectively and thus receive additional revenue in its budget. I also have to stop at personnel issues. The number of employees in connection with the production of Czech TV shows is fixed in the long term. The electronicisation and digitization we hear about everywhere is not reflected in the declining number of employees on Czech television. I think this is definitely worth taking into account for the future, because we are constantly hearing about the slimming of the state administration, and I believe that precisely as a result of digitization, there should also be a slimming of the number of states on Czech television. The average monthly wage on Czech Television in 2021 was finely above the monthly wage level in Prague and almost 9,000 above the average wage in the Czech Republic. I only mention it here because, in my opinion, it would be extremely interesting to know the distribution of these wages, and rather, for example, how many employees have a salary of over CZK 100,000 a month. From this point of view, it would be good to analyse the fees of authors and performers, or in this area there is no inefficient use of financial resources on Czech television. I may have been partially critical of Czech television in my performances today, or I have tried to name some problems, but the reason is quite clear why I do so because I am a big fan of Czech television and I am interested in promoting a high-quality public service media in the Czech Republic. Thank you for your attention.
