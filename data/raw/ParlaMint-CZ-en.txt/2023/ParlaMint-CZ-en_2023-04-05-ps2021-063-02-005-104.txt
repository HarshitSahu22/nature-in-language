ParlaMint-CZ_2023-04-05-ps2021-063-02-005-104.u2	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, even the previous material concerned the protection of public health, but in this case it is directly an amendment to the law, which therefore changes the protection of public health and the following related laws. The main objective of this proposal is the transposition of the Directive of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2020 and it is to ensure the protection of the quality of water intended for human consumption within the Czech rule of law. The adoption of the draft law will ensure an increase in the safety of drinking water supplied as the assessment and follow-up of risks throughout the chain enables the focus to be on the relevant risks and the adoption of appropriate measures to protect public health effectively. The proposal includes an accompanying amendment to the Water and Sewer Act, which regulates the rules on access to water infrastructure and the area of information on supply of drinking water to customers. This part of the law was prepared by the Ministry of Agriculture. The application contains the revocation of the authorisation for the issue of Decree No. 84/2001 Sb., on hygiene requirements for toys and products for children under the age of three, due to the elimination of duplication, because the issue is regulated by another legislation, i.e. by another decree. Finally, the proposal brings the Czech rule of law into line with the EU regulation, in the event of its non-acceptance, the Czech Republic will not fulfil its obligations arising from EU membership. Unfortunately, I have to add here that the Czech Republic should have done this before January of this year, and at this time the European Commission has initiated infringement proceedings against the Czech Republic, so it is appropriate to approve it as quickly as possible and to remedy the delay that has unfortunately occurred. Amendments were read on the bill, the preparation of which the Ministry of Health was involved. In the event of the abolition of health certificates, this is an amendment prepared for an earlier unnegotiated amendment to the Act on the Protection of Public Health. The exception is Mr Smetana's amendment, implementing the accompanying amendment to the Food Act, the transposition of the directive, which extends the ban on the content of characteristic flavours and flavourings as well as on heated tobacco products, while at the same time removing exceptions for these products from the obligation to bear certain warnings for consumers. The Ministry of Health also agrees with this amendment. The proposal was discussed by the Guarantee Committee, which agreed with the Members' proposals and the proposal as a whole.

The amendment is essentially about two areas. The first is the transposition of the European Water Quality Directive. The aim is to increase the safety of water supply. The current control system is very old, it dates back to 1998, and is focused on checking the quality of water on the tap at the consumer. Currently such a system is not sufficient because when unsatisfactory quality is found, it is not possible to protect the customer any further. The newly adopted Water Quality Directive of 2020 extends the adaptation to new indicators based on new research in the field of chemicals, their effects on human health, laboratory methods and so on. The second part repeals the empowering provisions of the Public Health Act and its implementation on the health requirements for toys and products for children under the age of three, so that these provisions are abolished due to duplication. And now I would still like to look at the amendments. The important amendment is the amendment by Martina Ochodnická with a proposal to abolish food cards, which makes sense, of course, because reality is that these food cards are basically issued once in a lifetime, and of course the state of health of each of us is changing dynamically, so what makes it possible, in a way, to work with food on the basis of that food card at one time, does not have to accommodate at all with a very short distance, so basically the proposal to abolish this excessive bureaucracy is absolutely appropriate. Furthermore, there is an amendment by my colleague Michaela Šebelová, where it is proposed that persons operating priority areas with regard to the presence of a lead indicator in drinking water, such as schools, health facilities, places where children's groups, catering services, social services are operated, should not be obliged to supplement the operating rules with part of the assessment and management of the risks of internal water supply and connections if the risk of lead being present in these facilities is low, so there is basically no need to take further corrective action. So, once again, I consider this proposal to be meaningful. Then there is an amendment that concerns new recycling technologies, which is a very important matter, and which, once again, transposes the European Union's regulation. It is proposed to extend the competence of county health stations to assess new recycling technologies producing materials and articles from recycled plastics. The aim is to ensure a high level of protection of human health while increasing recycled content in food packaging. Basically, this concerns a very important area. Everyone, even non-medical people, has certainly noticed in the media that the environment and our body are basically becoming an infiltrate, we are infiltrated by microplastics.
