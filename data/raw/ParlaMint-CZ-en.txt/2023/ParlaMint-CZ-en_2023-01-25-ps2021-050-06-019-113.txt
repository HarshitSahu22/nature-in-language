The annual report on the activities of Czech Television for 2018 as House Press 121 was circulated to all Members on 19 November 2022. The Election Committee discussed it on February 3, 2022. In 2018, Czech Television fulfilled the obligations laid down in Law No. 483/1991 Coll., on Czech Television. It thus fulfils the principles of the fulfilment of the public service in the field of television broadcasting. Maybe I'd mention some news on the air in 2018. They were premieres in connection with the so-called eight-year anniversary, presidential elections or Olympic games. Start of project reform portal iSending, creation of new websites - Sport CT, Dečko, ArtZón. A new form of HBBTV's main signpost was created. I would also like to mention that the Prime Minister's share remained stable in 2018 compared to the previous year in the summary of all programmes. Also, compared to previous years, the proportion of programmes that are broadcast in the original version with subtitles or in dual language mode has increased. In addition, in terms of ratings, Czech TV as a whole reached 30 percent in the 15+ group in 2018 and became the most sought-after Czech broadcaster in the audience. I would now read the motion for a resolution: "The resolution of the Election Committee of the 4th meeting of 3 February 2022 to the Annual Report on the Activity of Czech Television in 2018 as the House Press 121. Instructs the President of the Committee to present this resolution to the President of the Chamber of Deputies of Parliament; III.
