ParlaMint-CZ_2023-05-30-ps2021-065-04-003-006.u8	Thank you for the floor. Madam President, Members of the Government, colleagues, I would like to present an amendment to which I will later sign up. The amendment aims to add measures to the law for the use of mobile seed cleaners. Measures are necessary in order to enable the central control and testing institute of the agricultural holding to carry out its activities under the compulsory recognition procedure and control of the putting into circulation of seeds, even if these mobile devices are used. Another intention of this amendment is to ensure that traceability has been observed in the production of seed and crops from the beginning, from the initial material to the resulting seed or seed introduced into circulation, thereby guaranteeing the identity of the material contained in each seed or seed packaging, in particular as regards the authenticity of the variety for which the buyer pays a high price of seed, but also as regards the level of seed multiplication, that is to say its category. The need for this measure arises on the basis of experience gained from the practice encountered by the ÚKZÚZ during checks on the production and circulation of seeds and seeds in certain cases.
