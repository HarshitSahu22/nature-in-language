The Government of the Czech Republic agreed to negotiate this document by Resolution 653 of 27 July 2022. As the Minister said, the possible double taxation is undesirable, as it reduces the income generated by the natural and legal persons of one State from another State. Since the international double taxation of income and property is due to a conflict of tax laws of two sovereign states, such taxation can only be effectively ruled out by measures taken by those States which are mutually coordinated by the international tax treaty. The negotiation of this Treaty will increase the legal certainty of potential investors in both states. Its closure and implementation will not have a direct impact on the state budget. I recall that the Organizing Committee recommended the discussion of this international treaty on 11 January this year by resolution 117 and proposed it for discussion to the Foreign Committee.
