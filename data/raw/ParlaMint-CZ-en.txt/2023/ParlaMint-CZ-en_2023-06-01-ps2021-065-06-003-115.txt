Proposal for the appointment of a member of the Supervisory Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic The second election yesterday was to appoint a member of the Supervisory Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic. Number of ballots issued 179, number of valid and invalid ballots surrendered also 179, quota for the selection of 90. 162 votes cast for candidate František Hrdlička. In the first round of the election he was elected member of the Audit Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic candidate František Hrdlička.
