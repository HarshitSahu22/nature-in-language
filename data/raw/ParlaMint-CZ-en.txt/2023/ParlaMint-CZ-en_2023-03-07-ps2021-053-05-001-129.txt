Following a regulation directly applicable to the draft law, the central liaison office, which lays down certain obligations and powers of the supervisory authorities, unless provided for by general procedural rules, shall define a set of measures which may be imposed by the supervisory authorities, and shall determine penalties for non-compliance with the obligations laid down by this law and by a directly applicable regulation. The aim is for all supervisory authorities to have at their disposal the same minimum set of supervisory powers as well as a set of measures which may be imposed in the event of a finding of non-compliance of products with the legislation. In addition, the law aims to ensure cooperation between the supervisory authorities and the supervisory authorities with the customs authorities, to ensure the exchange of supervisory information and to try to harmonise as far as possible the evaluation of the same or similar situations in the control of the different supervisory authorities and to inform the public about both market surveillance and control findings of the supervisory authorities. Last but not least, the aim is to strengthen cooperation with other supervisory authorities in the Member States of the European Union. Market surveillance for products falling within the harmonised sphere will be carried out by the existing supervisory authorities within the scope of the competences granted to them by legal regulation. The draft law will impose certain obligations on supervisory authorities where the need for certain costs can be assumed. This is in particular the duty of the supervisory authorities to use the communication and information system. In this case, certain initial costs can be assumed...
