ParlaMint-CZ_2023-04-20-ps2021-063-05-007-098.u4	Thank you, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Government has agreed to negotiate an agreement between the Czech Republic and Rwanda on air transport by its resolution of 30 March 2022, No 249. The Agreement was signed in the context of the ICAN 2022 international action in Abuji on 5 December 2022. The implementation of the agreement will be ensured mainly by the Ministry of Transport and the Civil Aviation Office, which will cooperate if necessary with other participating ministries, this is the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Finance, Interior, Labour and Social Affairs, Industry and Trade and the Czech National Bank. The negotiation and subsequent implementation of the Agreement will not affect the expenditure of the State budget, with the exception of the usual budget appropriations of the various ministries and of the Civil Aviation Authority designated in general for administrative and administrative expenditure. In conclusion, I would perhaps just say that the African continent has not yet shown an overall good level of air connectivity, but there is a great potential, and some air carriers have been trying to improve the availability level of some places significantly in recent years. These include the ambitious RwandanAir, which started flights to Europe a few years ago. In this context, the agreement opens up possibilities for expanding the network of lines through Czech, Rwandan and other air carriers. I hope this will improve the transport service of this Central African region, which is interesting not only from the point of view of tourism. Thank you.
