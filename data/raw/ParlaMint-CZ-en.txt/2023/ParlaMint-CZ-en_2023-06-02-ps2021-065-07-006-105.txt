ParlaMint-CZ_2023-06-02-ps2021-065-07-006-105.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, honourable Members, I think that this law will once again be in conflict. In principle, this law is intended to replace the existing law on public auctions, which we already have here from 2000. The fundamental change is probably conceptual, because after the Civil Code has been adopted, the concept is a little different, because according to the existing Act on public auctions ownership of the subject matter of the auction is acquired by means of an extension, however, the Civil Code is based on a slightly different concept that public auction is basically just a way of concluding a contract, i.e. a purchase contract in this case, so these conclusions are reflected in this. Of course, the old law on public auctions was made at a time when digitalisation and electronic auctions had not gone too far. In principle, new legislation also responds to this, and it has been processed by the previous government. She processed it by trying to amend the existing law in some way. The Government's Legislative Council then pointed out that it is better to make a new one. This was indeed planned by the previous government, but it was not enough to pass the law through the Chamber of Deputies, which is why the law appears here repeatedly. He's reviewed the Government's Legislative Council, so the text you're getting today is really quite alienated by repeated discussions on the Government's Legislative Council, and I think there are no conflict points. That is why I suggest you finally approve it.
