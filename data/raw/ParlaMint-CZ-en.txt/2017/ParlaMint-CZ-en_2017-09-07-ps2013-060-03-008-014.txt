ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-07-ps2013-060-03-008-014.u2	Thanks again for the word. Mr President, colleagues, let me just briefly remind you that the aim of the amendment to the Montreal Protocol is to limit the use of so-called partially fluorinated hydrocarbons, HCFs, which are used as replacements for ozone-depleting substances. However, these partially fluorinated hydrocarbons also act as greenhouse gases with a strong effect and therefore the countries around the world agreed that their use needs to be regulated. The amendment to the Protocol obliges not only developed countries but also developing countries to comply with the schedule of restrictions on these substances, taking into account the different conditions of the groups of those countries. At the same time, I would like to propose an additional ratification of the Vienna Convention for the Protection of the Ozone Layer and the Montreal Protocol, which were originally approved as government treaties. The reason for this is only formal reconciliation of the process of negotiating this Convention and this Protocol with the provisions in force of the Constitution of the Czech Republic, which currently requires the approval of the Parliament of the Czech Republic for such international treaties and subsequent ratification by the President of the Republic. I will just point out and point out that due to existing legal protection within the European Union, the adoption of a change to the protocol does not require a further amendment to the legislation of the Czech Republic in this area and will thus have no impact on the state budget, public administration or business sphere. Thank you.
