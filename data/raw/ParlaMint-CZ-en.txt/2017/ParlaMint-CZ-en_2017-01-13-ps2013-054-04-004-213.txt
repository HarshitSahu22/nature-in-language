Given that the proposal has been discussed several times in the Chamber of Deputies, I would just reiterate that the aim of ensuring the transposition of Union Directives 2015/565 and 2015/566, which govern procedures for verifying equivalent quality and safety standards for imported tissues and cells. To this end, the procedures necessary to establish uniform rules within the EU for the import of tissues and cells from third countries are added in order to ensure a high level of protection of human health in the Union. This is possible through the introduction of a procedure for verifying equivalent quality and safety standards for imports of tissues and cells, the establishment of a programme for the approval of inspections reflecting the verification process established for tissue and cell activity carried out in the Union. Another objective is the introduction of a single European code that will facilitate the traceability of human tissues and cells from donor to recipient and vice versa. Since the deadline for the implementation of both directives expired on 29 October, it is necessary for the proposal to be adopted as soon as possible. As part of the second reading, only one amendment containing a few minor corrections of a legislative technical nature, which the Ministry of Health supports, was loaded.
