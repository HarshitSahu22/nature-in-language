ParlaMint-CZ_2017-03-14-ps2013-055-09-011-296.u2	Thank you. vážený Vice-President, Members, this amendment was drafted by experts from the National Security Office, in cooperation with both the professional public and the relevant ministries. This amendment is submitted in order to ensure the transposition of Directive of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 July 2016 on measures to ensure a high common level of security of networks and information systems in the Union. The directive aims to increase and harmonise the standard of security of networks and information systems in European countries, on which the functioning of our society and the economy is largely dependent today. At the same time, the bill reflects comments from the practical application of today's effective cybersecurity law. These comments were formulated by representatives of the professional public with whom the NBU discussed the amendment. The bill also includes an amendment to the Act on Free Access to Information, which exempts the provision of information in the field of cyber security and moves to the Act on Cyber Security. In accordance with EU law, this European directive must be incorporated into the Czech rule of law within a specified transposition period. The Czech Republic needs to introduce these measures by 9 May 2018, so we propose the effectiveness of the law from the first day of the second calendar month after its publication in the Collection of Laws. Thank you.
