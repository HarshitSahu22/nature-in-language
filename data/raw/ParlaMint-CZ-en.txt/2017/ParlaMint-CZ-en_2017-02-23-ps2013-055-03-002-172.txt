ParlaMint-CZ_2017-02-23-ps2013-055-03-002-172.u2	Thank you. Hello again, ladies and gentlemen, Mr President, honourable Members of the Government. On behalf of a group of Members, I submit a proposal for a brief amendment to Act No.134/2016 Coll., on Public Procurement. A really brief amendment to this law consists in supplementing § 48 para. 10 where the words "or State' are added at the end of the text. Why is this addition needed? On 1 October 2016, the new Act No. 134/2016 Coll., on Public Procurement, came into force. This law provided, inter alia, for the exclusion of a tenderer who is a public limited liability company or has a legal form similar to a public limited liability company and does not issue exclusively book-entry shares. At the same time, the contracting authority's obligation to verify for the selected supplier the fulfilment of the grounds for exclusion was newly laid down, provided that the information kept in the commercial register shows that the exclusion is justified, the contracting authority shall exclude the contracting authority from the procurement procedure - it must exclude. There is a derogation from the above obligation in the Public Procurement Act for a tenderer who is a public limited liability company whose shares in the aggregate nominal value of 100 per cent of the capital are owned by the municipality under the Municipalities or Region Act. With this amendment, we would also allow an exemption for public limited liability companies, which are 100% owned by the State. So unfortunately, in that law, the state ownership was forgotten. The obligation to have book-booked shares specifically fell on the company České dráhy, a joint-stock company. This company cannot fulfil the condition to have exclusively book shares, since according to § 5 para. 1 Act No.77/2002 Coll., on the joint stock company of České dráhy, the state organization of the Railway Infrastructure Administration and on the amendment of Act No.266/1994 Coll., on Railways, as amended, are its shares listed in the name with limited transferability only with the consent of the government. This is the exact citation of the Czech Railways Act. If we maintain the current state of affairs, ČD could not participate in competitions to ensure transport services organised by the regions and the Ministry of Transport, or the contracting authority should exclude it from these competitions, which would be, as you will see, quite absurd. This change would also help other equity companies with 100% State participation if they had not yet changed the form of the shares. I ask you to support this proposal and to support this technical amendment under Section 90 of our Rules of Procedure. Thank you for your attention.
