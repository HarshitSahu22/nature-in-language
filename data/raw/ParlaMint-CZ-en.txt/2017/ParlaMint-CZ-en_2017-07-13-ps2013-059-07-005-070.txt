ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-13-ps2013-059-07-005-070.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, I present to you a proposal to give your consent to the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic on cooperation in the mutual protection of the airspace signed in Brussels on 15 February of this year. The protection of the airspace of European NATO Member States is ensured by the NATO Air Defence System, which is one of the pillars of the Alliance's collective defence, the full name of this precision system is the Integrated NATO Air and Missile Defence System. Member States shall, in particular, provide fighter aircraft under the command of the Commander-in-Chief of the Allied Forces in Europe. The system has two major limitations. First, the system is intended solely for defence against military threats and therefore does not provide defense against civilian aircraft used for terrorist attack, that is called. Renegade. Second, the air force of small Member States has often been reduced to a minimum, which can lead to short-term or long-term failures of the ability to provide air defense by its own forces. [[11.40 hours]] The risks arising from the limitation of this system can be reduced by effective bilateral cooperation between neighbouring States, which has led to the preparation and signing of the contract submitted. Thus, the submitted agreement sets out the basic framework for cooperation between the Czech Republic and Slovakia in cases that cannot be addressed within NATO air defense system and also in the event of failures of its own capabilities. On the basis of this Treaty, each Party may also request assistance from the other Party in protecting its airspace. The requested Party may provide such assistance where it has the necessary powers, therefore, there is no obligation to provide assistance. Furthermore, the Treaty lays down the basic conditions for the provision of such assistance, command and management, settlement of damages, criminal jurisdiction and others. Details of the need for assistance in the protection of airspace and of the compensation of costs associated with it will be negotiated at the level of the ministries of defence of both states in implementing documents. So many introductions and thank you for your attention.
