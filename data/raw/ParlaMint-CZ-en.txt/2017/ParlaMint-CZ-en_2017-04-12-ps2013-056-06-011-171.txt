ParlaMint-CZ_2017-04-12-ps2013-056-06-011-171.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Act No. 115/2000 Coll., on compensation for damage caused by selected protected animals, provides for the possibility of paying compensation for damage caused by these animals for clearly defined species. However, the compensation is linked to the introduction of a particular animal between specially protected species in the implementing decree for Act No. 114/1992 Coll., on the conservation of nature and landscape. The amendment to this decree has taken place since 1. 4. 2013 to exempt a major cormorant from specially protected species and to compensate for the damage caused by this animal on fish, although it still counts on this Damage Compensation Act, where the cormorant remains among the selected specially protected animals. This led to a situation where the law, namely the decree, was effectively amended. The proposed bill complies with Act No. 115/2000 Coll., on compensation for damage caused by selected protected animals, and Decree No. 395/1992 Coll., implementing certain provisions of the Nature and Landscape Conservation Act, while creating an area for stabilisation of the conditions of pondmakers and users of fishing grounds. The proposed solution is that from the list of selected specially protected species listed in Section 3 of Act No. 115/2000 Coll. a major cormorant will be removed and a transitional period will be introduced for this species for three years after the amendment of the law in which the damage caused by this animal will be paid. For this three-year period, the percentage key is proposed as follows: in the first and second years 100 per cent of the damage is proposed and in the third, this percentage falls to 80 per cent. The compensation will be paid to the extent specified in Act No. 115/2000 Coll. The proposal submitted is in accordance with the agreement concluded between the representatives of the Ministry of the Environment and the Ministry of Agriculture regarding the resolution of the current situation of damage to fish caused by the cormoranda. The transitional three-year period will allow for the start of processes that will gradually reduce the predatory pressure of a major cormorant on ponds and fish communities in fishing areas. If compensation for damage caused by the cormorane were not re-established, there is a real collapse of some production entities, which simply cannot bear such enormous damage, and would have an impact not only on fish production in the Czech Republic, but it would also have an impact with interference with reduced care for ponds and restrictions on the performance of their non-production functions, such as accumulation and water retention in the countryside. Due to the urgency of time and substance, where the current situation has a significant negative impact on the economic operators concerned, to which the cormorant causes extensive damage every year, and also because it is only a technical amendment, we propose that the proposal be adopted by the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic in accordance with Section 90 para. 2 of the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies, as amended, already in the first reading. Thank you for your attention.
