ParlaMint-CZ_2017-05-24-ps2013-057-06-019-192.u2	Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce you to Act No. 586/1992 Coll., on income taxes relating to voluntary donors of blood, bone marrow and haematopoietic cells. According to the legislation in force, the value of one blood collection or of its components of a donor who has not received the financial reimbursement of expenditure related to blood collection or its components under the Act governing specific health services, with the exception of the reimbursement of proven travel costs associated with the collection, is valued at CZK 2,000. This is not sufficient in the current circumstances. The tax advantage of CZK 2,000 was fixed in 1996, i.e. 20 years ago. At that time, the average gross wage was around CZK 10,000. It is clear that, in the current economic situation, the amount of this amount can no longer be motivated, so we propose an increase to CZK 3,000. Please note that voluntary blood donors - men can give blood five times, women four times a year. In this context, I would also like to draw attention to the fact that you all know from the media that the voluntary number of blood donors is not sufficient and is about 270 000 people. The recommended number of voluntary donors in the population is between four and five percent. It's not the only change we're proposing. The amendment introduces CZK 20,000 for the collection of haematopoietic cells and for the donor to deduct from the tax base. To sum up my speech, the aim of our proposal was to help people who donate the most precious, blood or bone marrow to others, thereby giving life. In view of the fact that this is a supraparty proposal, in view of its non-controversity and simplicity, it is proposed that the Chamber of Deputies should give its consent to the bill in the first reading in the regime under § 90 para. 2 of the Act on the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies. And with this, my fellow Members, I beg you to support our amendment. Thank you.
