ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-29-ps2013-059-03-007-066.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, Members of Parliament, the Czech Republic and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia do not yet have an international treaty in the field of civil aviation. The negotiations on the text of the agreement were concluded in 2011, but the other party announced the readiness to sign only at the end of last year. Consequently, the overall agreement was signed on 7 December 2016. The Agreement contains an adjustment to all important areas of air transport, such as the determination of the extent of traffic rights and other necessary conditions for the operation of commercial air transport between Contracting Parties. The text of the Agreement corresponds to current developments in civil aviation. As in the previous case, the agreement does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. The Air Agreement should contribute to the current development of scheduled air transport between the two States. Ladies and gentlemen of the House, here too, I would like to ask for a referral of the material to be discussed by the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
