ParlaMint-CZ_2017-01-20-ps2013-054-08-002-012.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, as I have already emphasised before the presentation of the bill amending Act No. 311/2006 Sb., on fuel and petrol stations here in the conference hall of the PSP of the Czech Republic at first reading in October last year, the main reason for submitting the proposed regulation is the transposition of Directive 94/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the introduction of infrastructure for alternative fuels into the Czech legal order. This is a partial transposition. In particular, certain new terms and obligations affecting the fuel sector, including electromobility, are being transposed into the Fuel Act. This draft law was drawn up in the framework of the implementation of the legislative work plan for 2016. The new draft law contains the concept of electricity in road transport, which has not yet been legally regulated, including charging stations for electric vehicles, and the different types of such stations. Furthermore, the draft law lays down the relevant obligations for charging station operators arising from Directive No. 94/2014/EU and implement, in accordance with the Directive and the National Action Plan Net mobility, the relevant registration of charging stations, since other service stations of alternative fuel infrastructure, where compressed natural gas, liquefied natural gas and liquefied petroleum gas are sold, for example, are already registered and published by the MIT. In addition to the obligations which are the implementation of part of the directive, the bill regulates and specifies some existing provisions with which there are problems in current practice. Few transposition changes to the law include a change in the process of authorisation, establishment and operation of service stations. According to the government amendment, all filling stations will be treated equally and their authorisation will be dealt with under standard procedures under the construction law by the relevant construction offices. The state administration has found a consensus on this solution across all the departments involved. [[There's a lot of noise in the room.]] It is also necessary to mention the change of the deadline until which service station operators are required to maintain motor gasoline containing up to 5% of the bio-component at least half of their sales network. It is reduced by one year and an agreement has also been reached for this change. The draft law was allocated at first reading to the Committee on Economic Affairs. He dealt with it at his meetings on 20 September, then on 16 November 2016.
