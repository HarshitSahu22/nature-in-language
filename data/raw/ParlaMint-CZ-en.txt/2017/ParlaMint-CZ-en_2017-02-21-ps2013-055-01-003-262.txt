ParlaMint-CZ_2017-02-21-ps2013-055-01-003-262.u2	Mr President, Members, press 984, i.e. Government proposal of the amendment to the Act on Cyber Security, and the second press, number 990, is a government proposal amending the Act on the residence of foreigners in the Czech Republic. Both proposals have already gone through the first reading in the Chamber of Deputies on 11 January 2017 and the deadline for committee discussion ends on 13 March 2017. So today is day 41 of this deadline. In the meantime, however, the two bills were discussed by all the committees to which these laws were ordered, i.e. in the context of the Cyber Security Act, the Guarantee Committee on Security and the Committee on Economic and Defence and the Act on Alien Residence, the Guarantee Committee on Security. Given that the laws have already been discussed by all the committees to which they have been ordered, and we could discuss them, these laws that are quite important for the security of this state, further at second reading, I propose that the deadline for committee discussions in these Houses of Press be shortened by 19 days. So much to justify the proposal.
