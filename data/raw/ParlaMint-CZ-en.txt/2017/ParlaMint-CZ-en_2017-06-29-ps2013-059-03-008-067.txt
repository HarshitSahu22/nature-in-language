Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Agreement between the Czech Republic and the Dominican Republic on Air Services /Household Press 1036/ - first reading I ask that the opening words also be given by the Minister for Regional Development Karla Šlechtová. Please, Madam Secretary.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-29-ps2013-059-03-008-067.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, mutual relations between the Czech Republic and the Dominican Republic are not currently regulated by any bilateral international treaty either. Interest in the negotiation of the air agreement was expressed by the Czech party in connection with the growing activities of Czech air carriers on the Dominican transport market. The draft text of the Air Agreement was finalised in the framework of the expert meeting at the end of 2015 and the agreement was signed on 7 December 2016. The agreement as well as the previous agreements provide for a legal basis for the operation of air transport services by air carriers of both States. The text of the agreement is of a liberal nature and contains all the usual provisions of the air agreements. The agreement thus creates room for the possible development of direct scheduled air links in this area. The agreement does not require changes in any legal order and its implementation will not have an impact on the expenditure of the State budget. Ladies and gentlemen, here too, I would like to ask you to refer the material to the Foreign Committee. Thank you.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-29-ps2013-059-03-008-067.u3	Thank you. I now ask that the rapporteur take the floor for first reading, Mr Pavel Šrámek. Please, Mr. Ambassador, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-29-ps2013-059-03-008-067.u4	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. The Minister has described the agreement very well here. This agreement is not about transport, it is about transport services. For the sake of interest, the agreement allows for the identification of any number of airlines by each air service party in which the rights set out in this Agreement may be exercised. This is the right to fly across the territory of the other Party, the right to land in the territory of the other Party for non-commercial purposes, the right to land and load in the territory of the other Party in the places set out in the Annex to this Agreement. I, of course, also recommend a discussion to the Foreign Committee of the House. Thank you.
