As another reason for rejecting this amendment, I could mention the possible overdrawn, virtually. dismissed in the context of determining jurisdiction of the courts. Once again, I understand the declared intention of the petitioner, who was present here at the speaker's stand and at the hearing of the constitutional law committee, but the proposal brings many complications, and I am more concerned that it is not intended to prevent the actual possibility of carrying out the criminal acts in question.
