ParlaMint-CZ_2017-05-23-ps2013-057-05-003-025.u2	Thank you. Mr President, dear colleagues, let me further mention the Government's bill amending the legislation on plant health care. 326/2004 Coll. The proposal concerns plant protection products. The use of plant protection products is an essential factor affecting both the quality and quantity of plant production. The burning problem of the market for plant protection products is the placing on the market of illegal products. The main objective of the proposal is to reduce the presence of illegal plant protection products on the domestic market, thereby contributing to increased environmental protection and the food chain. This objective is implemented in particular by the following measures: strengthening control over the distribution chain of plant protection products and auxiliary devices, through the compulsory registration of distributors of products for professional use, or by limiting the changing of preparations, by specifying the treatment of plant protection products and by increasing penalties for the most serious offences in the plant health sector. Another large range of proposed changes is linked to the issue of sustainable use of pesticides. Following the conclusions of the National Plan of Action to reduce the use of pesticides, changes to reduce the consumption of pesticides were incorporated in the proposal to specify the regulation of aviation applications, competence for the management of plant protection products, restrictions on the use of products in certain areas, integrated plant protection legislation and the reduction of risks associated with the use of pesticides for human health, animals, plants and the environment. [[15.40 hours]] The Government Bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 41st meeting on 19 April 2017 and recommended by its resolution to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to approve the Government Bill as amended by the amendments adopted. The amendments adopted are in line with the concept of the petitioner. They will contribute to improving the government bill, and I therefore agree with them. Thank you for your attention.
