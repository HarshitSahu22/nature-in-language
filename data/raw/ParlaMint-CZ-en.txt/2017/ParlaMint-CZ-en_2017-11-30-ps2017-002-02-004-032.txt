Request for consent Chamber of Deputies to prosecute Mr Bohuslav Svoboda Once again, I will ask the President of the mandate and immunity committee, Mr Stanislav Grospič, to inform us about the committee's deliberations and to present a motion for a resolution.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-11-30-ps2017-002-02-004-032.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Madam, honourable Members and Members of the Government, on 24 November 2017, the President of the Chamber of Deputies received a request from the Municipal Court in Prague for the consent of the Chamber of Deputies to prosecute Mr Bohuslav Svoboda in criminal matters brought before the requesting court. The mandate and immunity committee has included the discussion of this application for the 3rd meeting of the Committee, which was launched on 28 November of this year. Order No 16 the third meeting of the mandate and immunity committee was suspended until 30 November 2017 and the discussion of the suspended item of the agenda of the 3rd meeting of the committee will begin after the end of today's meeting of the Chamber of Deputies. For this reason, the mandate and immune committee of the Chamber of Deputies is submitting a proposal to interrupt item 32 of the agenda of the 2nd meeting of the Chamber of Deputies until this request is discussed by the Municipal Court in Prague by the mandate and immune committee. Thank you.
