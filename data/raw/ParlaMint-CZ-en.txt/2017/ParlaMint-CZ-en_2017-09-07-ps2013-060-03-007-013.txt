Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Protocol amending the European Convention on Landscape adopted in Strasbourg on 15 June 2016 /German press 967/ - second reading I will ask the Vice-President of the Government and the Minister of the Environment Richard Brabec to present his opening words. Please, Vice-President of the Government.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-07-ps2013-060-03-007-013.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Colleagues, colleagues, I will once again be very brief and just to remind you that the aim of the European Convention on the Landscape and, at the same time, the protocol by which this European Convention on Landscape is changing is to promote protection, care and planning in the landscape and to organise international cooperation in this area. The Convention is based on the common interest of European countries in the pursuit of the sustainability of landscape development and the Protocol is the result of the long-term intention of the Steering Committee on Culture, Heritage and Landscape of the Council of Europe to open the European Convention on Landscape to non-European countries, as well as to provide it with a global treaty on landscape issues. Thank you.
