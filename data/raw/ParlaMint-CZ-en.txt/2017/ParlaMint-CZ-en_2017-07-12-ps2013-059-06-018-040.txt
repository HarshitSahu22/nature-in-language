ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-12-ps2013-059-06-018-040.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, first of all, let me thank you for putting this amendment on the table. So let me briefly introduce to you the proposal for an amendment to the Act on Social-Legal Protection for Children. What is involved in this amendment? This amendment concerns better setting up the financing of so-called facilities for children requiring immediate assistance, the so-called ZDVOP. What are these devices? These facilities, which work for children requiring immediate assistance, are an important part of the care system for vulnerable children, and they are a device that must be ready to take over the short-term care of the child at any hour, or even more children, in a situation where their own parents cannot take care of them. This means, especially children abused and abused belong here. Children are then staying in these facilities until their situation is resolved permanently by their return to the family, or by their transfer to foster care or adoption. It's actually a crisis facility that needs to be available all the time. It is therefore essential for it to function in a stable and predictable manner. However, the current form of financing does not allow for longer-term operation. What does the current funding of these facilities look like? These facilities receive an allowance of CZK 22,800 per month and are not systematically financed by the State in any other way. According to these facilities, the cost of a vacant bed, i.e. securing staff, energy and other expenses, is between CZK 18,000 and CZK 26,000 per month. And there is one big problem, and that is that these facilities are not paid off by the days when children are on so-called long-term passes, that is, they are away from this facility, for example, in the family, or in children's camps, relatives, and so on. I mean, at a time when these devices are fulfilling the most appropriate function, that is, working with the family and bailing out the family, these devices are getting nothing. According to many directors and directors, the current state of funding is completely unsustainable. Before I prepared the proposal for this amendment, I sent a structured questionnaire to 46 facilities that require immediate assistance, and I received 27 answers. That means I got a total overview of the 3/4 device. The questions focused on the ability of these establishments to finance themselves, or to find out how sufficient is the contribution they receive from the State. It turns out that the current legislative regulation is causing difficulties for all the equipment that has been asked. For example, representatives of the children's centre in Dvoře Králové - let me quote them - said: "If an organisation has only these facilities, funding is unsustainable, as the state contribution will not cover all expenses. [[The chairman rings the bell to calm the hall]] Our ZDVOP was funded last year by one million Czech crowns from the operating subsidy for medical facilities." Almost all responses from this empirical probe criticized the reduction of the state contribution if the child is on so-called long-term passes, which I have already mentioned. Under the current legislation, the State contribution covers around 48 to 75 per cent of the costs of each installation. It is owed to say that these devices operate under different entities, both regions and municipalities and various non-profit organizations such as charity or the Fund of Endangered Children. So what does the proposed amendment look like? The amendment changes the method of financing in the following way: The State contribution is divided into two components - fixed and variable. The fixed amount of the allowance is CZK 20,000 and variable CZK 4,500 to CZK 6,350 if the child is physically present in the facility. Basically, there is a form of care in foster families called short-term, or temporary foster parents, when these foster parents also receive CZK 20,000 by being only registered without having a child. And then, if they accept the child, they receive an additional amount for the needs of the child. The resemblance is here because the kind of care is basically similar to those facilities for children requiring immediate assistance. If we looked at the increase in finance from state resources, it is considered that the immediate impact on the state budget could be CZK 107 million. However, this increase will be offset by the fact that the operation of these ZDVOPs will no longer have to be financed from other sources, i.e. essentially from public sources. In summary, this is expected to have no negative impact on public budgets by introducing systemic funding. And in an optimal variant, the result would be positive, as there will be savings in the administration of subsidies and other exceptional payments that these facilities must currently be financed.
