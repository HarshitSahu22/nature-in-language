Proposal for the election of a member of the Council of the State Cinematography Fund The result of the election of a member of the Council of the State Cinema Fund was unsuccessful. We had two suggestions from the Ministry of Culture: Tereza Czesana Dvořáková and Petr Vítek. Mrs. Dvořáková won 45 votes, Mr. Vítek 48. The commission required for the election was 72. No one's been elected. It was the second round of a secret election, and that's the end of that choice.
