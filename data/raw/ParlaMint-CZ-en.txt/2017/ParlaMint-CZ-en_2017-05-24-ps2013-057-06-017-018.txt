ParlaMint-CZ_2017-05-24-ps2013-057-06-017-018.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, first of all, let me briefly introduce you to the main points of the Government's bill amending the Czech Trade Inspection Act and the Consumer Protection Act. The main objective of this law is to establish effective control instruments corresponding to new commercial practices on the market. The newly proposed measures include, in particular, the power to request information from entities in the domino chain on the identity of e-shop operators in whose activities there is an infringement. It is also an authorisation to access the enclosed spaces in which they are located, for example, in connection with the exercise of control. dangerous products, and the implementation of certain types of controls under changed identity. Last but not least, the government bill regulates in more detail the conditions for carrying out control purchases and also contains very important changes following other legislation already adopted, e.g. the law on liability for offences and proceedings concerning them. The draft law was discussed on 19 April by the Economic Committee, which adopted two amendments to it. These proposals related to the incorporation of the exemption from the Act on Liability and Procedure for the Czech Trade Inspection in relation to the imposition of sanctions by order on-the-spot and extension of the deadline for the entry into force of the Act in question. I agree with these amendments. Madam, honourable Members, I hereby ask you to move the law to the third reading. Thank you.
