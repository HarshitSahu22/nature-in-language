ParlaMint-CZ_2017-04-28-ps2013-056-10-004-065.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly present to you the Government Bill on Payment. The new Payment Act is the transposition of a new European Payment Services Directive, whose transposition period expires on 13 January 2018, which brings a number of changes compared to the current regulatory state. The law newly regulates modern payment methods through mobile and internet applications. These methods of payment are an alternative to card payment and, compared to a classic bank transfer, they give a higher guarantee that the payment will actually be made. The trader may therefore dispatch goods or provide a service at the time the payment order is entered. Furthermore, consumers will be able to centrally manage their payment account data via the Internet, which is intended to allow a greater overview of family finances. Higher standards of payment security are proposed through the so-called strong user verification. A strong verification shall always cover at least two of the three security features, which are knowledge of a specific data, possession of a specific item or biometric data of the user. [[11.30 hours]] Consumer rights are also strengthened in case of loss, theft or misuse of payment cards. Consumers will not newly have to pay a charge for payment by card, which has been charged for certain filling stations or for internet ticket sales. In other areas of the bill, it maintains the existing rules to a large extent, which is intended to contribute to the stability of the legal environment. Ladies and gentlemen, in the light of my arguments, I ask you to support this bill. Thank you for your attention.
