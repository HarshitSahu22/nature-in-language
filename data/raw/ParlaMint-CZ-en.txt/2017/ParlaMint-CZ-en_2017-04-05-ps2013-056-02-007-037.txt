Government bill amending Act No. 114/1995 Coll., on Inland Navigation, as amended, and Act No. 262/2006 Coll., Labor Code, as amended /German press 910/ - first reading Under the authority of the Government, Dan Ťok, Minister of Transport, will present the proposal. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-04-05-ps2013-056-02-007-037.u2	Mr Vice-President, ladies and gentlemen, the subject of the discussion is a government bill which transposes the Council of the European Union Directive of 19 December 2014 implementing the European Agreement on the adaptation of certain aspects of working time in the inland waterway transport sector, concluded by the European Inland Navigation Union of the EBU, the European Organisation of Ship Captains of the ESO and the European Transport Workers' Federation. The main objective of the proposed legislation is the safety and health protection of workers in the inland waterway transport sector, the corresponding response to their specific working and living conditions and the implementation of the specific rules contained in the European Directive for these employees. The proposal, in accordance with Union law, provides for the allocation of working time and rest periods for workers in inland waterway transport, the content of the working time register and the relationship of the proposed legislation in the Labour Code. In addition to the transposition of the European Directive, a minor modification and addition of some of the non-compliant provisions of the applicable Inland Navigation Act, the current wording of which causes problems in application practice, is included in the submitted government proposal for the Inland Navigation Act. The draft law was submitted to the Chamber of Deputies in mid-September 2016, with the transposition period of the EU directive in the meantime. At the beginning of this year, the European Commission drew the attention of the Member States to its intention to significantly more strictly and more quickly prosecute delays in the transposition of directives into national laws. I consider the speedy discussion of the proposed law to be crucial in view of these facts, and therefore I would like to ask you to support this bill and also to shorten the time limit for the committees to 30 days. Thank you.
