Proposal for a member of the Supervisory Board of the Wine Fund As far as the Wine Fund Supervisory Board is concerned, two nominations were submitted: Jan Husák for TOP 09 and Roman Sklenák for ČSSD. Jan Husák won 28 votes and Mr Roman Sklenák won 86 votes and became a member of the Wine Fund Supervisory Board. I congratulate him and my colleagues and I look forward to quality Czech and Moravian wine.
