ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-13-ps2013-060-06-007-051.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. I therefore submit information on the operation of the deployable connecting module in NATO Joint Enterprise operations in Kosovo and Resolute Support in Afghanistan, which was taken into account by the Government of the Czech Republic on October 12, 2016. A total of 56 soldiers of the Army of the Czech Republic are assigned to work with the mobile modular communication and information system. Individual soldiers then serve according to the needs of the Alliance in specific international operations. In Operation Joint Enterprise in Kosovo, the Czech Republic was represented by a group of three members who participated in securing the connection and functionality of information systems at the headquarters of the operation. This team worked in Kosovo from 15 August 2016 until 22 December of the same year. As far as Resolute Support is concerned, another group of nine people worked in Afghanistan. Involved in the administration of the command and control system at the operational center of headquarters at the International Airport in Kabul. They supervised the continuous operation of the command and control system, planned and carried out its maintenance and participated in solving user requirements. This team worked in Afghanistan from 27 July 2016 to January this year. The financial costs of their activities were secured from the budget chapter of the Ministry of Defence, estimated at 1,658 000 in Kosovo and 11 370 000 in Afghanistan. Thank you for your attention.
