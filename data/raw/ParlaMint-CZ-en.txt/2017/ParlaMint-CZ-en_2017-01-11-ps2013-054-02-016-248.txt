Proposal for appointment of members of the Supervisory Board of the Grant Agency As regards the election of two members of the Board of Auditors of the Grant Agency, again 170 tickets, kvorum 86. Josef Průša received 32 votes, Jan Roda 117 and Šerý Šerý 87. This choice was therefore successful and in the first round we elected two candidates. Jan Roda and Šerý Šerý become members of the Audit Board of the Grant Agency.
