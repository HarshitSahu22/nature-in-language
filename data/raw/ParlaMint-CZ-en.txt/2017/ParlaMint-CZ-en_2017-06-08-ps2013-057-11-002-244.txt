ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-08-ps2013-057-11-002-244.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, allow me to present the Government's bill to the Parliament of the Czech Republic for agreeing to ratify the amendment of Appendix 1 to the International Convention against Doping in Sport. This is House printing 1093, first reading. The International Convention against Doping in Sport was adopted at the UNESCO Conference in 2005. It is a multilateral treaty that entered into force for us in 2007. The aim is to take account of new circumstances in the fight against doping, also to strengthen national anti-doping organisations and to establish a contractual framework for the joint fight of government and non-governmental organizations against doping. The annexes to prohibited substances or international standard for therapeutic exemptions are integral components. The Convention belongs to the category of contracts whose acceptance or non-acceptance of their amendments is subject to the approval of the Parliament of the Czech Republic and ratification by the President of the Republic. The annexes shall form an integral part. It is therefore necessary to apply this procedure from the point of view of their changes. [[11.20 hours]] In the context of the development of doping substances and methods in the field of sport, Annex 1 to the Convention is updated annually. This is also the subject of the proposal that I submit. The amendments to the Annex shall update and supplement the Annex on newly classified substances and doping-effect methods. In particular, a nominal list of substances in a group of steroids has been published and some quantitative limits have been clarified. These changes will help to clarify some of the contested provisions or to interpret anti-doping rules. The material presented has no impact on the state budget or public budgets and, given that it is an annual update of the Convention, which is basically technical in nature, I would like to propose a reduction of the time limit for committee discussions to 30 days. Thank you for your attention and support at first reading.

ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-08-ps2013-057-11-002-244.u6	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me begin with a brief recapitulation and a final request for support. The Convention itself was drawn up in agreement with the participants of the Global Doping Conference held in Copenhagen in 2003. The Convention was adopted at the 33rd session of the General Conference of UNESCO in Paris on 19 October 2005. It entered into force on 1 February 2007 following the ratification of 30 countries in the world. It is the first legally binding document of this content focus with global application. The Council of Europe Anti-Doping Convention with the Additional Protocol which entered into force in the Czech Republic after ratification on 1 May 2005 still applies to the European region. The aim of the Convention is to unite the fight against doping worldwide, not only at the level of athletes organized, but also in the environment of recreational sport and its background, which represents the trade in doping substances and to doping abused complementary nutrition. The Convention also accentuates the support of educational and educational programmes for school youth and research using international cooperation. For the Czech Republic it entered into force in accordance with the provisions of paragraph 2 of the same Article on 1 June 2007. Annex 1 List of prohibited substances and methods and Annex 2 International Standard for therapeutic derogations are integral parts of the Convention. The World Anti-Doping Agency, in accordance with Article 34 of the Convention, amended Annex 1 to the List of Forbidden Substances and Doping Methods for 2017. We approve of this change today. Amendments to Annex 1 shall update and supplement Annex 1 with newly classified substances and methods with doping effect. In particular, a nominal list of substances in group S3, beta-2 agonists and some quantitative limits have been published. These changes will contribute to clarification of some of the contested provisions or interpretation of the rules on combating doping. Statistics on the development and increase of doping not only in top sports are warning and it is not possible to resign or accept the view that doping belongs to sport. Therefore, allow me to ask for support and speedy discussion of this amendment. Thank you.
