ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-11-ps2013-059-05-006-171.u4	Thank you again, Mr President. Dear colleagues, together with the Minister of Foreign Affairs, I submit a proposal for approval on the operation of forces and resources of the Ministry of Defence in Lithuania, Estonia, Latvia and Poland in the framework of the Enhanced Advanced NATO presence, which was approved by the Government of the Czech Republic on 10 May of this year by resolution 351. The object of the material is the operation of forces and resources of the Ministry of Defence in the Baltic States and Poland in the number of up to 290 people, between 1 January 2018 and 31 January 2019. The mission will be carried out on the basis of the conclusion of the NATO Summit in Warsaw in July 2016 and in accordance with Article 43 (2) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. Article 4 (1) (a) (a) the Constitution of the Czech Republic. The concept of the strengthened forward presence of NATO, the so-called Enhanced Forward Presence, lies in the permanent rotating presence of alliance forces in the countries on the east wing of NATO in order to strengthen collective defence capabilities and demonstrate commitment to defend the alliance's members. The Czech contribution to the EFP consists of a mechanized company of up to 250 people, which will be part of a fighting group in Lithuania, and a mortar platoon of up to 40 people, which will operate in the formation of the Canadian group in Latvia. In Lithuania, soldiers from Germany, Belgium, the Netherlands, Norway, France, Croatia and Luxembourg will work together with us, in Latvia, members of the armies of Canada, Poland, Italy, Spain, Slovakia and Albania. As far as Estonia is concerned, alongside the leading country of Great Britain, French and Danish soldiers will operate in the country. In Poland, British, Romanian and Croatian troops will be part of the American fighting group. The funds for both missions are estimated at 362 miles. CZK and will be drawn from the budget chapter of the Ministry of Defence. Finally, let me stress that the proposed action of the forces and resources of the Ministry of Defence in the Baltic States and Poland is a concrete contribution of the Czech Republic to strengthening allies on the eastern wing of the Alliance. It will be a strong political and practical expression of solidarity and at the same time a willingness to fulfil a collective defence commitment, a principle on which our own security is based. Thank you for your attention.
