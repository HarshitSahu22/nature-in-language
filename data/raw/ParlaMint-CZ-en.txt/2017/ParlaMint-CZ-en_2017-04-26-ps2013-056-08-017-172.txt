ParlaMint-CZ_2017-04-26-ps2013-056-08-017-172.u6	Thank you for the floor. Vice-President, fellow Members, I would like to present to you the bill amending Act No. 39/1999 Coll., on the Ombudsman, as amended. The only and essential objective of the proposal is to entrust the guardian with monitoring the implementation of the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities. Directly from this Convention, the obligation of the Contracting Parties to entrust an authority with independent monitoring of its implementation and implementation of the rights of persons with disabilities. Based on the situation in the Czech Republic, it has been assessed that the best way to fulfil this contractual obligation of the Czech Republic is to entrust the Ombudsman with this activity, which is an independent, sufficiently professional body capable of performing this activity in a quality manner. This solution is also the most advantageous in terms of financial costs, since there is no other similar body in the Czech Republic, and it would therefore have to be re-established. The content of the proposal is therefore to add new powers of the protector to the law and its subsequent more detailed regulation. The Guardian will generally support the exercise of the rights of disabled people and will also propose measures aimed at protecting them, examine the situation of disabled people in the Czech Republic, publish reports and make recommendations on the fulfilment of the rights of disabled people and ensure the exchange of available information with foreign and international entities. All these activities are already being done by the protector in other areas of his activity, and therefore he has a lot of experience with them, which will ensure that monitoring of the implementation of the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities is carried out in a really good way. A specific complement to the Ombudsman's work will then be the Advisory Board of Persons with Disabilities and persons dedicated to their rights, which will ensure the participation of disabled persons themselves in the pursuit of their rights. This aspect also stems directly from the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities. It must be said that the submitted House press is following up on the previous government amendment to the Act on the Ombudsman. We had this novel here for two, two and a half years. A sufficient political consensus has not been found on this amendment, and the government has therefore withdrawn, on my proposal, the previous major amendment to the Act on the Ombudsman. As you know, it contained, for example, the possibility that the Ombudsman would give a so-called anti-discrimination lawsuit or that he would be able to give the Constitutional Court a request to abolish the law or part of it. This proposal has therefore been withdrawn and replaced by the House Press, which I present here, which only really extends the powers of the protector to monitor and implement the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities. On the contrary, a political consensus was found over this step, which confirmed a total of 147 Members and Members who supported this proposal in the third reading of the last press on 29 June 2016 by 147 votes, as I said. The House's press is therefore factually identical to the proposal already approved by the House. [[19.00 hours]] In addition, it must be concluded that the Czech Republic ratified the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities in 2009 and, unfortunately, its commitment to setting up an independent monitoring body has not yet been fulfilled. For these reasons, I propose to the Chamber of Deputies to agree with the amendment at first reading, so that it can enter into force as soon as possible. Thank you so much for your attention.
