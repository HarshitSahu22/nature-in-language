Proposal for the establishment of a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for monitoring the use of wiretapping and recording of telecommunications traffic, the use of monitoring of persons and goods and the cancellation of electronic communications traffic and the manner in which it is set up or elected Here we proceed according to Section 98 of Law No. 273/2008 Coll., on the Police of the Czech Republic, with the House of Deputies setting up a monitoring body to check the use of wiretaps and records of telecommunications traffic, the use of tracking persons and things and the disruption of electronic communications operations, consisting of Members designated by the Chamber of Deputies. The number of members is not determined by law, so the number of members needs to be determined. Proposals should be made in a detailed debate. In the seventh parliamentary term, the number was set at 9. Pursuant to Section 47 of our Rules of Procedure, I propose that this supervisory body should have the status of a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies, and in accordance with Section 115 (1) of the Rules of Procedure. I propose that this Commission be established on the basis of the principle of proportional representation. I therefore submit this motion for a resolution: The Chamber of Deputies sets up a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for monitoring the use of wiretaps and records of telecommunications traffic, the use of tracking people and things and the cancellation of electronic communications, which consists of Members - and I will leave it to the debate this time in a detailed debate - and states that the Commission is setting itself according to the principle of proportional representation.
