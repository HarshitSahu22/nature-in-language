Once again, I represent the Minister of Industry and Trade Jiří Havlíček and allow me to justify the decision of the Government of the Czech Republic to withdraw from the Charter of the International Energy Forum. The Czech Republic acceded to this organisation in 2011, when the International Energy Forum Charter was signed at the extraordinary ministerial meeting of the International Energy Forum. Membership The Czech Republic expected, above all, better access to information on the international energy field. Unfortunately, these expectations have not been met. The world geopolitical situation has undergone a significant transformation over the last decade, and the International Energy Forum has unfortunately no longer played a major role as an intermediary of the key political dialogue between OECD countries and OPEC, which is taking place now in other forums, in particular the International Energy Agency. Another argument for the Czech Republic's withdrawal from the International Energy Forum is payment of a member contribution, which is almost 600 thousand. crowns a year. And if we add to this the fact that the organization is located in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and the costs of sending a representative of the Czech Republic to its meetings are therefore very high, we have come to the conclusion that the contribution of the Czech Republic is not sufficiently assessed and balanced by the benefits of membership. This Decision is also supported by the fact that a number of information and publications from this organisation are freely available on their website. For these reasons, the Ministry of Industry and Trade submitted a proposal to the Government in February of this year to withdraw from this organisation and this proposal was supported by the Government. The next step towards ending this process, i.e. withdrawal from this organisation is, of course, the agreement of both chambers of our Parliament, and this proposal has already been discussed in the Committee on Economics, Agriculture and Transport of the Senate, and this committee has given its consent to the proposal. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I believe that I have convinced you that the membership of the Czech Republic in the International Energy Forum is no longer a priority for the Czech Republic, and that you will agree with the proposal to withdraw from this organisation. And thank you for your attention.
