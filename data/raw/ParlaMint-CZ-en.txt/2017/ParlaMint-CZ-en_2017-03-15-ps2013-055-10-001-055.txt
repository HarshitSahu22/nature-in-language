It is proposed to introduce a process for registering varieties with an officially recognised description which constitutes a simpler process than the standard registration of varieties under the applicable Law on the circulation of seed and seed. This method of registration is less demanding for applicants for registration of these varieties both in terms of compliance with the conditions of registration and in terms of financial complexity. A common certification scheme with a uniform approach to the maintenance and testing of fruit family propagating material and species is being introduced. New provisions concerning the labelling and formalities of accompanying documents, the closure of packaging, propagating material and fruit crops are also an integral part of the transposition. The proposal also responds to the unsatisfactory market situation when seed garlic is put into circulation.
