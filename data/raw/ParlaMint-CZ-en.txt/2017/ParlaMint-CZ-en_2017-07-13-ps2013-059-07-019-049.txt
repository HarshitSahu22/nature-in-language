Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and Turkmenistan on the prevention of double taxation and the prevention of tax evasion in the field of income and property taxes signed in Ashchabad on 18 March 2016 /German press 805/ - second reading I will ask the Minister of Finance, Ivan Pilný, once again, to state this point.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-13-ps2013-059-07-019-049.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, you are presented with a contractual document relating to international taxation. In the present case, it is a double taxation treaty with Turkmenistan. This Treaty was signed on 18 March 2016 in Ashchabad on the occasion of a visit by the President of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to Turkmenistan. A standard comprehensive bilateral contract has been negotiated, which regulates taxation and, at the same time, the exclusion of international double taxation of all types of income and property and which will certainly help to further develop mutual economic activities. The contract ensures an objective distribution of the right to collect taxes on income and property between the two states and uses as the main method of exclusion of double taxation on income and property of Czech tax residents a method of simple credit of foreign tax. As a standard, the Treaty provides for basic standards of cooperation between the competent authorities of both countries, as well as the principle of non-discrimination, the informal resolution of disputes arising from its implementation, and ensures the relevant exchange of information between the competent authorities of the two States, which applies to taxes of all kinds and naming. The conclusion of the contract will not have a direct impact on the state budget of the Czech Republic. [[13.00 hours]] The overall contribution resulting from its closure will depend on the pace of development of the various forms of economic cooperation. For your information, in August last year, the Senate of Parliament of the Czech Republic agreed to ratify this Treaty. At the same time, I inform you that the Turkmen Party has already ratified the treaty. On the basis of what has already been said, I therefore propose that the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic agree to ratify this international treaty. Thank you for the floor.
