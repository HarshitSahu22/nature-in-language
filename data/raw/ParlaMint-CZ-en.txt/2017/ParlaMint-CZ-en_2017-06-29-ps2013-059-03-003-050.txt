Article 4 (1) (a) (b) the United Nations Convention on Contracts, the International Purchase of Goods and the Declaration on the related Additional Protocol on limitation. The UN Convention on Contracts, the international purchase of goods is one of the most widely used international treaties, because it regulates the purchase contract as the most common contractual form of economic cooperation, thus constituting a global standard also in the national regulation of the international purchase contract. In the case of the purchase of goods between the Czech person and its foreign partner, which has a place of business in the Contracting State of the Convention, it is clear that this Convention will apply to the purchase contract. The original reservation applied by Czechoslovakia in 1991 narrowed the application of the Convention or prevented it from being used in situations where one of the contractual partners is not an entity of the State that signed the Convention, but is referred to by international private law by reference to the law of the other State which adopted the Convention. The consequences of reservations can be pointed out, for example, in the purchase contract between Czech and Iranian entrepreneurs, because Iran did not sign the treaty. When the Czech law applies to their relationship, the purchase of goods under the Convention would be subject to the modification of this Convention, but according to the existing reservations Czech law will apply, but without the Convention. The application of this reservation was led by concern that the national regulation of Czechoslovakia should not be replaced by a relatively little known convention at the time of the creation. However, the current Czech legislation is already based on the Convention itself, so there are no reasons to insist further. The withdrawal of reservations is in line with the worldwide trend of the withdrawal of reservations to this Convention in order to simplify its application and thus contribute to the further extension of the unified law which governs the purchase contract. The withdrawal of reservations and declarations is a technical step, which is also in line with the continuous efforts of the Czech Republic to aim to remove obstacles and approximate legislation in the field of international trade, which leads to a decrease in transaction costs for entrepreneurs and also to simplify of course the legal environment. The withdrawal of reservations shall not entail any additional costs or additional costs. The motion for appeal of reservations was approved by the Senate of Parliament of the Czech Republic by resolution number 519 at the 27th meeting which took place on 24 August 2016. The Foreign Committee of the Chamber of Deputies discussed this international treaty on 6 October 2016 and issued a resolution recommending its approval. Thank you.
