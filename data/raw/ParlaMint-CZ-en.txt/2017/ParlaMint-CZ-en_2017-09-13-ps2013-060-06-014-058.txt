ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-13-ps2013-060-06-014-058.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Well, that's a big commitment, and I'm gonna do everything I can to make it work with dignity. Ladies and gentlemen, the material informs on the political, military and financial aspects of the activities of Czech troops and soldiers in 2016 in Afghanistan, Kosovo, Bosnia and Herzegovina, the Sinai Peninsula, the Golan Heights, the United Kingdom, Iceland, the Malian Republic, Iraq and Italy. The material also includes information on the implementation of rapid impact projects, which were implemented only in Afghanistan in 2016, and information on the status of the Czech Republic's involvement in UN missions in 2016 and on the opportunities and opportunities for the Czech Republic to operate in these missions on the basis of the Security Council resolution of 9 June 2015. As in previous years, last year also remained a priority mission for the Czech Republic called Resolute Support in Afghanistan. In addition to the tasks carried out under the alliance mission, members of the special forces of the Czech Republic also provided the protection of the ambassador and the Czech Representation Office in Kabul. In 2016, it was successful, even for only one year, but to return to the United Nations military unit after almost 18 years, namely a decommissioning of 25 special forces, which worked as part of the Dutch contingent of special forces in MINUSMA mission in Mali. In UN missions in Afghanistan, Kosovo, the Democratic Republic of the Congo and the Central African Republic, there were also nine military observers from the Czech Republic. The Czech Republic had prepared forces and means for the possible activation of NATO, EU and UN emergency forces in its territory. 816 people were earmarked for deployment in NATO's rapid response operations, including very high alert forces. As part of the European Union's fighting groups, we kept 825 soldiers on alert in the first half and 244 in the second half. Then 40 persons were assigned to the UN Emergency System. In 2016, we spent a total of CZK 1.5 billion on the involvement of our forces and resources in foreign operations. These funds were financed from the budget chapter of the Ministry of Defence. In conclusion, I would like to state and express the conviction that working in foreign operations, in addition to defending the interests of the Czech Republic and its allies, has also made a significant contribution to maintaining and restoring international peace and security. At the same time, it has contributed to addressing the current problems of increasing migration flows and the fight against terrorism. Thank you for your attention and thank you overall for your cooperation.
