Proposal for the election of members of the Czech Television Council As far as the Czech Television Council is concerned, we were more successful there, as well as 132 votes. In the second round we chose from two candidates. René Kühn received 70 votes and Jiří Závozda won 20 votes. René Kühn was therefore elected, and I can also say that the Czech Television Council is complete at this time, i.e. 15 members.
