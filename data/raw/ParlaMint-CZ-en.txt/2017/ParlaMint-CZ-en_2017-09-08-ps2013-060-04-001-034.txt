ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-08-ps2013-060-04-001-034.u4	Let's do it again. Thank you for your peace, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to present to you in the third reading a draft law amending Act No. 198/2009 Coll., on equal treatment and legal means of protection against discrimination and on the amendment of certain laws, in short it is an anti-discrimination law, as amended, and other related laws. The aim of the proposal is to implement Directive No 1 of the European Parliament and of the Council. 2014/54/EU on measures to facilitate the exercise of rights granted to EU workers in connection with their free movement to ensure equal access to employment, social and tax advantages, housing and equal access to education by children as citizens of the Czech Republic. Similarly, citizens of the Czech Republic are to have equal access to these goods if they reside in another EU country. The directive is transposed into Czech law by the amendment of anti-discrimination law, procedural rules and the Act on the Ombudsman. According to the Anti-discrimination Act, EU workers and their family members are to be protected from discrimination on grounds of nationality in the Czech Republic as well as other victims of discrimination. Changes in the Civil Code and the Administrative Rules of Procedure are intended to enable specialised NGOs to provide assistance to EU staff in judicial proceedings. The Ombudsman will be an institution in support of the free movement of EU workers and will monitor how their rights are implemented and will further recommend possible steps to improve and advise them in their lives in the Czech Republic. Citizens of the Czech Republic will be able to use the services of the same institutions in other EU countries. First of all, I would like to thank the entire House for the swift and helpful discussion of this proposal, as the implementation of this directive should have been carried out in May last year and the Czech Republic is threatened with sanctions by the EU Court of Justice due to the delay. In conclusion. Several amendments have been tabled to the proposal. I myself can support the proposals of the Committee on Petitions under points A1 and A2 and the proposal of the Constitutional Legal Committee under point B3, which are more or less legislatively technical and respond to the development of an amendment to the Act on the Ombudsman, which has not been approved and realistically shifts the effectiveness of the proposal to 1. 1. 2018. I therefore recommend these proposals to be adopted. Unfortunately, however, I cannot support the other two proposals of the Constitutional Committee under points B1 and B2 because they change the Ombudsman's proposed competence contrary to the directive and could therefore lead to further problems with its implementation. Therefore, I do not recommend these proposals for adoption, and I will, of course, express myself during the vote on these amendments. The aim of the proposal is therefore to ensure that all EU citizens, including citizens of the Czech Republic, are able to use their freedom to move and reside in EU countries without obstacles. A high-quality uniform level of protection in all countries, including the Czech Republic, will benefit all EU citizens, including our citizens, while working and living abroad. Thank you for your attention and please approve this proposal. Thank you.
