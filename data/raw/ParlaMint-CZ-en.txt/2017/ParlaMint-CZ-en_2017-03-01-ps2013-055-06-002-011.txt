First of all, the amendment concerns the adaptation of the housing allowance. Based on the practice of paying housing contributions, it has been established that in some cases this benefit may be overused, on the grounds that the owners and landlords of the apartments use the possibility to determine the total cost of using the flat-rate payment provided for in Act No. 67/2013 Coll., which regulates certain questions relating to the provision of performance related to the use of flats and non-residential premises in a house with apartments, without the subsequent specification of the individual items of housing costs for which payment is paid. This may, on the one hand, include items which are not basic transactions related to the use of flats under Act No. 67/2013 Sb., or it may occur that items are not settled, which are paid in advance and are billable. It is therefore proposed to add an exhaustive list of services to be taken into account for the entitlement and the amount of the housing allowance in the same provision and, at the same time, to the condition that, for the entitlement and the amount of the housing allowance, the cost of the housing allowance must be broken down by item and the cost of the advance payment must be settled in the regular settlement period. The settlement period is proposed to be set for a maximum period of 12 months in accordance with the provisions of Section 2 of Act No. 67/2013 Sb. The other amendment concerns the adaptation of the addition to the child, as the current adjustment is inadequately responding to the current financial situation of the entitled person and persons jointly assessed. According to the current legislation, for the purposes of entitlement to child allowance, the applicable family income shall be assessed for the calendar year preceding the calendar year in which the beginning of the period from 1 October to 30 September of the following calendar year falls. For the reasons set out above, it is therefore proposed to base itself on income for the period of the calendar quarter preceding the calendar quarter for which entitlement to payment of the levy is proved and, where appropriate, to apply the entitlement to the benefit, as is currently the case for the housing allowance. This will result in a faster response to a change in the amount of income in the family and greater targeting of social assistance by the State, both in cases where a new entitlement to child allowance is incurred in reducing family income, such as loss of income from one parent or receiving a parental allowance, as well as in cases where the entitlement to benefit ceases. It is also proposed, in the context of a reduction in administrative complexity, to unify the conditions of the applicable income for the last quarter of the calendar year and the conditions for the duration of the child's carelessness.
