ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-07-ps2013-060-03-009-015.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, fellow Members, you will not rest a moment from me, because I will now reincarnate to Mr Dan Took for a moment and lecture you on traffic points. The first is, as has already been said, a government proposal concerning the approval of the ratification of the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Seychelles on Air Transport. Let me just remind you that the relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Seychelles in the field of civil aviation are not yet regulated by a valid international agreement. On the basis of the interest of both parties, an expert delegation meeting was held in December 2012, resulting in a mutually acceptable text of the Agreement. The Government of the Czech Republic agreed to the agreement by its resolution in September 2013, but the agreement was not signed until June 2016. The text of the agreement corresponds to current developments in the field of civil aviation, is consistent with the constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, including obligations arising from membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. The agreement does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not affect state budget expenditure. The agreement represents one way to further develop relations between the two countries. The air carriers of both parties offer a wide range of possibilities for commercial use and a sufficient degree of flexibility in making decisions on operational matters. The agreement was discussed in the Foreign Committee, which recommends that the agreement be ratified. I would like to ask you to give my consent to the ratification of the agreement. Thank you.
