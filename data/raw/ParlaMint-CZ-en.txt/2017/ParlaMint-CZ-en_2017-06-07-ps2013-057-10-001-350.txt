ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-07-ps2013-057-10-001-350.u2	Thank you. Ladies and gentlemen, I come before you on the occasion of the extraordinary anniversary of a major historic event that has foreshadowed the emergence of our modern state and which deserves recognition and a reminder. In the first days of July this year, just 100 years have passed since the successful combat performance of Czech and Slovak legionaries in the Battle of Zborov. The victory of several thousand Čech and Slovák against central powers brought a fundamental breakthrough in the international perception of the desire of both nations for self-determination. With Zborov we have achieved recognition for the matter of future autonomy. The legionnaires' victory encouraged the formation of other military forces abroad, which proved by their commitment that Čech and Slovák were ready to assert their freedom with weapons in hand, with lives and sacrifices. The Memorial of the Battle of Zborov was rightly commemorated as the second most important feast of the Republic throughout the first Republic. Zborovská tradition created one of the cornerstones of the ideas of independent Czechoslovakia and stood at the birth of the fighting tradition of his army as a state attribute. Zborovské heroism was a model of courage and strength and its victims a commitment to defending freedom. The significance of the commemoration of the Zborov battle was also manifested by the establishment of the grave of an unknown private whose remains were collected from the warfield. On the tenth anniversary of the battle in 1927 a grand national pilgrimage to Zborov was organized and in 1937 celebrations of the 20th anniversary of the battle became a massive demonstration of commitment to defending against expanding Nazi Germany. On this occasion, the founder of the state Tomáš Garrigue Masaryk appeared at Strahov Stadium on 4th July. Of course, Parliament was also at the centre of the Zborov celebrations. Just 90 years ago, in 1927, our predecessors made a clear declaration to the Zborovsky idea of giving thanks to the presidency of both chambers of the National Assembly. The corporeal health of the fallen has been inserted into the mound on the battlefield and its copy still belongs to the jewels of the archives of the Chamber of Deputies. In it, the Czechoslovak Parliament acknowledged legionnaires who "issued their first unwritten law on the unification and autonomy of Czechoslovakia with their blood. " In 2018, our company will remember 100 years from the restoration of independence. Let us remember that it would not be on October 28, 1918 without the determination and sacrifice of the legion warriors. Our independence is not only associated with the names of Masaryk, Beneš and Štefánik, but also with thousands of soldiers who bought it out by fighting. They therefore deserve to be reminded and recognized on an unrepeatable 100 - year anniversary, as well as our country deserves to have a pattern in their reference on which they can build and to which they can report. Therefore, with a solemn feeling in the detailed debate, I will present to the Chamber of Deputies a motion for a resolution, which is intended to claim victory at Zborov as a famous milestone in our history, which we can be proud of, and recognise its importance for the existence of our state. Thank you.
