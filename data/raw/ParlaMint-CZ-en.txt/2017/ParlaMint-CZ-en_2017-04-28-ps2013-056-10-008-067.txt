ParlaMint-CZ_2017-04-28-ps2013-056-10-008-067.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the Government's bill amending the Act on Banks and the Act on Savings and Credit Cooperatives. The proposal brings, firstly, changes in technical nature, both by specifying the wording of certain provisions in order to facilitate the interpretation of the law as well as in the form of terminology in order to harmonise with other sectoral laws on the financial market. At the same time, some substantive adjustments are made, and I will mention the two most important ones. One. The proposal introduces a system for sharing information on cyber attacks between banks. This is a preventive tool in cyber security. This new system will be operated by the Czech National Bank, the costs of its creation and operation will be borne by its participants, i.e. banks. The initiative to set up this system came from a discussion between banks and the CNB and is supported by all participants. I consider it important to stress that the proposal does not interfere with the area governed by the Cyber Security Act. The proposal strengthens the protection of bank clients in cases where there are transformations or any available to the bank's business establishment or parts thereof. In particular, it will ensure that relevant information is provided to clients, and then it will give some of them the opportunity to withdraw their deposit immediately without penalty. Given that these are relatively significant changes in terms of ensuring the security of the information and communication systems of entities in the banking sector and consumer protection, I consider it desirable that the bill be approved by the Chamber of Deputies by the end of this parliamentary term. For this reason, I consider it appropriate that the time limit for the examination of the draft law in committees be reduced to 30 days. Mr President, fellow Members, I ask you to give your consent to the bill and thank you for your attention.
