Proposal for the establishment of a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the activities of the Financial Analytical Office and the manner in which it is established or elected According to § 31b para. 1 and 2 of Act No. 253/2008 Coll., as amended by Act No. 166/2015 Coll., as amended, on certain measures against the legalisation of proceeds from crime and terrorist financing, the Chamber of Deputies sets up a special supervisory body for the control of the activities of the Financial Analytical Office consisting of seven Members. Pursuant to Article 47 of the Rules of Procedure, I propose that this supervisory body be appointed as a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies. In accordance with Article 115 (2) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, In addition, I propose that this Commission be established in accordance with the principle of proportional representation. Just a note. During the seventh parliamentary term, the Commission was established on the basis of the principle of proportional representation, which also applies to the following committees. I therefore submit this motion for a resolution: First, the Chamber of Deputies establishes a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the activities of the Financial Analytical Office consisting of seven Members; Second, it provides that the Commission is to establish itself on the basis of the principle of proportional representation.
