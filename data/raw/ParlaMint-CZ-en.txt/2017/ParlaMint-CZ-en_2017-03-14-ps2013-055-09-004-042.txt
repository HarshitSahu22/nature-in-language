ParlaMint-CZ_2017-03-14-ps2013-055-09-004-042.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly mention the amendment to the Act on International Cooperation in Tax Administration. In the field of the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development, OECD, an intergovernmental cooperation project is being implemented to prevent the erosion of the tax base and to shift profits, so-called. the BEPS project. The aim of the project is to effectively prevent aggressive tax planning and artificially motivated transfer of profits. The success of the BEPS project is the creation of an instrument which imposes on multinational groups of enterprises with consolidated annual revenues of more than 750 million. EUR obligation to prepare reports by country, so-called Country-by-Country-Reports. In these reports, they shall, inter alia, report their income and tax obligations in individual tax jurisdictions. These reports will automatically be exchanged between the States in which the group operates. The Czech Republic signed a multilateral agreement with the competent authorities on the exchange of reports by country in January 2016. The OECD project was followed up by the European Union, which transmitted this part of it to the Directive on administrative cooperation in the field of taxation. This directive is transposed by a draft law. The draft law submitted does not go beyond the requirements laid down in the Directive and set out in the January 2016 Agreement. The proposed legislation will strengthen the mechanisms for information exchange and will help to increase transparency in the area of taxation. Tax administrations need comprehensive and relevant information on groups of multinationals in terms of their structure, the policy of determined transfer prices and their internal transactions. In the Czech Republic, the obligation itself to compile a report is estimated to affect about four to eleven of the highest parent entities of multinational groups of companies. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed from 5 June 2017 with regard to the specified transposition period. In view of the length of the legislative process, the Government has therefore submitted to the Chamber of Deputies a draft law proposing consent to the law at first reading. In the event that the bill is not given consent at first reading, the deadline for discussing this draft law in committees will need to be shortened by more than 30 days in order to meet the transposition deadline. Thank you for your attention.
