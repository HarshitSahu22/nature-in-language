ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-12-ps2013-059-06-019-039.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the draft law I propose has the character of legislative technical regulation within the scope of price control. In particular, it concerns the abolition of the State Energy Inspection Authority's authorisation to carry out price control in the field of renewable energy sources. Since 1 January 2016, supervision of compliance with the Act on Supported Energy Resources has been completely entrusted to the ERU, but the Act on the Authoritys of the Czech Republic has not been amended at the same time in the field of prices, where competence for the SEI remains. As a result, no price controls were carried out in 2016 or this year in the field of the promotion of renewable resources, neither by the ERO, nor by the SEI, although the ERO is generally a control body for the energy sector. I consider this to be very alarming and unacceptable given the volume of annual support in this area of CZK 44 billion. The rapid provision of these price controls is therefore a very urgent task. In this context, I would also like to say that the Economic Committee has adopted a resolution for my leadership on speeding up the control of overcompensation in this area, because it is obviously coming to us, and the European Union is not imposing this measure on us until 2019. So far, very little has been done. And once again, I highlight and note that we spend CZK 44 billion in this area.
