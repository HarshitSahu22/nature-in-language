ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-13-ps2013-060-06-013-057.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the subject of this material is a joint military exercise of airborne units of the Army of the Czech Republic and the United States of America in the territory of the Czech Republic under the name Saber Junction. This exercise was organised on the basis of the need for joint training of airborne units. 177 soldiers of the 43rd Airborne Battalion Chrudim and 250 soldiers of the 173rd Airborne Brigade of the USA took part in the exercise. The Saber Junction exercise followed the previous successful cooperation, which dates back to 2014. The aim of this cooperation is to develop the capacity to increase the interoperability of both airborne units for their possible joint deployment in international operations. I ask you, fellow Members, to take note of this material, and thank you for your attention.
