ParlaMint-CZ_2017-01-13-ps2013-054-04-007-207.u2	Mr President, colleagues, I am sure there is no need for me to repeat what has already been said about the content of this amendment to the copyright law. The discussion at second reading was very complicated, which shows the complexity of the material in question. In the course of repeated committee discussions, a large number of amendments were drawn up, which, on the one hand, respond to the jumping increase in OSA's tariffs, but many of them also propose amendments to the provisions of the law which are not the subject of the amendment. I would very much like, in this situation, for the aim of presenting the amendment to the Government, i.e. the transposition of the directive, which brings about a fundamental increase in the transparency of collective administrators. I stand by the fact that the government's proposal is well processed and there is no reason to reject it. We have a complicated voting procedure ahead of us. However, I believe that together we are able to go through it and approve the bill. Thank you for your attention.
