ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-28-ps2013-059-02-001-191.u6	Thank you. The proposed draft law amending Act No. 457/2016 Coll., on the State Budget of the Czech Republic for 2017, is closely linked to the amendment of the Act on Cyber Security. It has been discussed and approved by the Chamber of Deputies under press number 984, approved by the Senate and signed by the President of the Republic. Among other things, it separates the section dealing with cyber and information security from the National Security Office and establishes it as a new central administration. It is very likely that it will be declared in the Collection of Laws in July and will take effect on 1 August. The draft of a purely technical amendment to the Czech State Budget Act for 2017 includes a new chapter in the State Budget Act 378 of the National Cyber and Information Security Office, together with its binding indicators of zero, into which the budget measures will gradually be transferred from the National Security Office's budget. The National Security Office's budget for 2017 is based on the State Budget Act for 2017 and is approved in the amount of CZK 490 134 115. The Ministry of Finance assumes the transfer of funds from the chapter of the National Security Office according to the actual drawing of expenditure to the deadline of the deletion by an estimated amount of CZK 214 million. The amendment to the State Budget Act should take effect at the same time as the amendment to the Cyber Security Act. Thank you for your attention.
