ParlaMint-CZ_2017-01-20-ps2013-054-08-003-062.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce the Government's amendment to Act No. 165/2012 Coll., on Supported Energy Sources. The reason for the submission of this proposal is the need to resolve the promotion of electricity produced by small hydropower plants which were renovated between 2 October 2013 and 31 December 2015 as these power plants are considered to have been put into operation on 1 January 2016. On the basis of the transitional provision of Law No. 131/2015 Sb. the electricity producers from small hydro power plants reconstructed or upgraded during that period did not receive aid from 1 January 2016, although they were paid until 31 December 2015 and there is a notification decision by the European Commission on the compliance of this aid with EC rules. The proposed amendment to the Act on Supported Energy Resources restores the situation to a state which corresponds to the reality, and logically returns the electricity producers' entitlement to support from the date on which the reconstruction or modernisation of a small hydropower plant was actually completed. The current situation, which occurred after the first January 2016, and the fact that electricity from reconstructed small hydro power plants has not been supported for 9 months, has a significant negative impact on the management of operators operating small hydro power plants. In particular, it has a negative impact on the state-owned river basin undertakings, which have been forced to compensate for the deterioration of the operating result by increasing water prices. Therefore, this problem needs to be addressed as quickly as possible. The Government of the Czech Republic recommends that the proposal be adopted at first reading, in accordance with § 90 para. 2, Rules of Procedure of the Chamber of Deputies. Ladies and gentlemen, on the basis of the above, I would like to ask you for the support and approval of the amendment to this law at first reading. Thank you for your attention.
