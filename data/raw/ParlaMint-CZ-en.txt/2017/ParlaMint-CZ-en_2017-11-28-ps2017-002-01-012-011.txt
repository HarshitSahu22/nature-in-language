Proposal for the establishment of a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the activities of Military Intelligence and the manner in which it is established or elected Pursuant to Section 21 of Act No. 289/2005 Coll., on Military Intelligence, the Chamber of Deputies establishes a control body for the control of the activities of Military Intelligence consisting of seven Members. [[18.40 hours]] Pursuant to Article 47 of the Rules of Procedure, I propose that this supervisory body be appointed as a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies. In accordance with Article 115 (2) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, In addition, I propose that this Commission be established in accordance with the principle of proportional representation. I therefore submit this motion for a resolution: First, the Chamber of Deputies establishes a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the activities of Military Intelligence, consisting of 7 Members; Second, it provides that the Commission is to establish itself on the basis of the principle of proportional representation.
