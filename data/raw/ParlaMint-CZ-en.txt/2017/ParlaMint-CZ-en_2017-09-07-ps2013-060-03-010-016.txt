I'm just going to give you a brief introduction. The relations between the Czech Republic and the Republic of Tajikistan in the field of civil aviation are currently not regulated. On the basis of the proposal of the Tajik Party, expert negotiations have been carried out on a correspondence basis since 2012, resulting in mutually acceptable text of the agreement. The Government of the Czech Republic agreed to the agreement by its resolution in October 2016, subsequently the agreement was on 1. 12. 2016 signed. The text of the agreement corresponds to current developments in the field of civil aviation, is consistent with the constitutional order and other parts of the legal order of the Czech Republic, including obligations arising from membership of the Czech Republic in the European Union. As in the previous case, the agreement does not require changes in the Czech legal order and will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. I would therefore like to ask you, fellow Members, to give my consent to ratify this agreement. Thank you.
