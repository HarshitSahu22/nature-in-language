The vast majority of changes are therefore of a technical nature. ParlaMint-CZ_2017-04-28-ps2013-056-10-006-044.u4	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, the bill amending Act No. 374/2015 Coll., on recovery procedures and resolution of the financial market, hereinafter referred to as ZOPRK, as amended by Act No. - will be listed in the Collection - 2017 and other related laws, specify and complement some of the already implemented provisions of the 2014 59/EU Directive establishing a framework for recovery procedures and resolution of the crisis of credit institutions and investment firms, hereinafter referred to as BRRD. BRRD is a legal act of secondary European Union law laying down a framework for the recovery and resolution of credit institutions, such as banks and savings and credit cooperatives, certain investment firms, that is securities traders and certain other persons, if they are members of the same group as a credit institution or investment firm. The main objective of the BRRD is to ensure harmonisation of the resolution of banks' failures, credit unions and securities traders and other defined persons, thereby, inter alia, fulfilling the recommendations of the Financial Stability Council requiring the creation of instruments to ensure that the costs of the institution's failure are borne mainly by its associates and creditors and not by taxpayers. For this purpose, the BRRD also introduces preventive procedures and institutes that relate to the time before the crisis itself. The transposition of the directive BRRD into the Czech legal system took place by the adoption of ZOPRK and Act No. 375/2015 Coll., on the amendment of laws in connection with the adoption of the Act on recovery procedures and resolution of the crisis in the financial market with effect from 1 January 2016. Following the entry into force of both laws, it became necessary to adjust some of the transferred instruments in order to better match the objectives set out in the BRRD, thus extending, inter alia, the framework for amending the conditions of the mandatory person's resolution obligations in the financial market by the power of the Czech National Bank to postpone the maturity of the write-off capital instruments and/or the write-off obligations of the obliged entity. I recommend also shortening the time limits by 30 days to 30 days.
