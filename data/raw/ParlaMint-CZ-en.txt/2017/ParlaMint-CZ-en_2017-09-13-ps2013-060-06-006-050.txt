ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-13-ps2013-060-06-006-050.u2	Thank you very much, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, we have a collection of information and reports of this type. I believe that together we will manage to go through this unharmedly. Information on the participation of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in military exercises outside the Czech Republic and the participation of the armed forces of other states in military exercises in the Czech Republic during the period January to June 2016 is therefore subject to this point. During that period, military exercises were carried out to the extent approved by the Government of the Czech Republic. During this period, troops of the armed forces of the Czech Republic participated in 48 military exercises with foreign partners outside the territory and 21 exercises with foreign partners in the territory of the Czech Republic. The total financial amount needed to carry out these exercises was 69,2 million. Kč. In three cases, training of armed forces of foreign states took place in the Czech Republic without direct participation of the Czech Republic. The main tasks of the armed forces of the Czech Republic in the field of exercises with foreign partners in the first half of 2016 were preparation of units for deployment in operations, preparation and maintenance of the capabilities of dedicated forces and means of action in the European Union's emergency forces and preparation of chemical troops for carrying out tasks under the multinational battalion of protection against weapons of mass destruction. So much basic overview. Detailed information for the period January to June 2016 is given in the Annex to the presented material. Thank you for your attention.
