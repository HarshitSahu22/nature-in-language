Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Vienna Convention for the Protection of the Ozone Layer, agreed in Vienna on 22 March 1985, the Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer, agreed in Montreal on 16 September 1987, as amended, and the amendment to the Montreal Protocol, adopted in Kigali on 15 October 2016 /German press 1048/ - first reading Prime Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-13-ps2013-059-07-004-069.u2	Thank you for your word. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, as has already been said, the 28th Meeting of the Parties in Kigali took place last October. The Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer. The main objective was to adopt a change to the Montreal Protocol and this was also the result of this meeting. The aim was therefore to limit the use of partially fluorinated hydrocarbons used as substitutes for ozone-depleting substances. However, these substances act as greenhouse gases with a strong effect, and that is why the countries around the world agreed that their use needs to be regulated. The amendment to the Protocol obliges not only developed countries but also developing countries to comply with the schedule of restrictions on these substances, taking into account the different conditions of the different groups of countries. The reason for ratification of the amendment of the protocol by the Czech Republic is therefore a contribution to climate protection and thus to the protection of the overall environment at home and in the world. The Montreal Protocol, and this is important to emphasise, addresses environmental issues that fall within the shared competence of the European Union and its Member States. The European Union has already adopted legislation where the issue of reducing the consumption of substances is covered by the regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on fluorinated greenhouse gases, which has been effective since 1 January 2015 and applies directly in the Member States of the European Union. With regard to this Regulation, an amendment to Act No. 73/2012 Coll., on substances that damage the ozone layer and on fluorinated greenhouse gases, and this amendment has already been approved and announced by Parliament of the Czech Republic in the Collection of Laws on 29 March this year. In view of the above legal certainty, the adoption of the amendment to the Protocol does not require further amendments to the legislation of the Czech Republic in the said field at present. The amendment to the Protocol will also have no impact on the state budget, public administration or the business sphere beyond what European Union legislation provides. To conclude, at the same time as the proposal to adopt the amendment to the Montreal Protocol, I would like to submit a proposal for further ratification of the Vienna Convention for the Protection of the Ozone Layer and the Montreal Protocol, which were originally approved as government contracts. The reason for this is only formal reconciliation of the process of negotiating this Convention and this Protocol with the provisions in force of the Constitution of the Czech Republic, which currently requires the approval of such international treaties by the Parliament of the Czech Republic and subsequent ratification by the President of the Republic. This additional ratification of the Vienna Convention and the Montreal Protocol will not affect the commitments of the Czech Republic which are already under the two international treaties. Thank you for your attention.
