ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-07-ps2013-060-03-024-031.u2	Hello. Thank you for the floor, Mr President. I'll give you the material briefly. This material was in the Chamber of Deputies at first reading 13. 7th of this year. It is therefore in agreement with the ratification of the Treaty between the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic on cooperation in mutual protection of airspace, which was signed on 15 February of this year. Only in all brief. The protection of the airspace of European NATO Member States is ensured by NATO air defense system and it has two but essential constraints. It is exclusively intended to defend against military threats and therefore does not provide defence against civilian aircraft used for terrorist attack. And then we must also take into account that the air force of small Member States has often been reduced to a minimum, which can lead to short-term or long-term failures of the ability to provide this air defense with its own forces. This risk can be reduced precisely by the effective bilateral cooperation of neighbouring states and this has led to the preparation and signing of the submitted treaty, which has already been expressed positively by the Senate as well as the Foreign Committee of the Chamber of Deputies. It's a presidential contract. Thank you for your attention.
