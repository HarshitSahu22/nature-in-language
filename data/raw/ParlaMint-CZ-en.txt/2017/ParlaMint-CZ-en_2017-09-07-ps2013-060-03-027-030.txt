ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-07-ps2013-060-03-027-030.u2	Thank you. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, allow me today to justify the decision of the Government of the Czech Republic to withdraw from the Charter of the International Energy Forum. The Czech Republic acceded to this organisation in 2011, when this Charter was signed at the extraordinary Ministerial Meeting of the International Energy Forum. First of all, the Czech Republic expected better access to information on the international energy field. However, these expectations have not been met. The world geopolitical situation has undergone a significant transformation over the last decade, and the International Energy Forum is no longer fulfilling the main role of an intermediary of major political dialogue between OECD and OPEC states, which takes place in other forums, particularly within the International Energy Agency. In addition, the information provided by the International Energy Forum is freely available on its website. Another argument for leaving this forum is the payment of a member contribution of almost 600 thousand. crowns a year. Adding to this the fact that the organization is based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and the costs of sending a representative of the Czech Republic to its meetings are therefore high, we have concluded that the contribution of the Czech Republic is not sufficiently assessed and balanced by the benefits of such membership. For these reasons, in February of this year, we submitted a proposal to the government to withdraw from this organization, which was also approved by the government. The next step is the approval of the two chambers of Parliament. In the Chamber of Deputies, the proposal has already been discussed at first reading and forwarded to the Foreign Committee, with a positive response to it in principle. In mid-August, the proposal was also discussed in the Senate plenary session, where 57 senators voted in favour of its adoption and the proposal was supported. Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I believe that you will accept these arguments and support the proposal to withdraw from this organisation. Thank you for your attention.
