ParlaMint-CZ_2017-05-24-ps2013-057-06-016-017.u1	17. Government bill amending Act No. 90/2016 Coll., on conformity assessment of specified products when making them available on the market /house print 989/ - second reading I ask the Minister of Industry and Trade to give us this press.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-05-24-ps2013-057-06-016-017.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Government's bill amending the law on conformity assessment of specified products when making them available on the market has been drawn up in order to adapt Czech law to the EU regulation on ropeways, personal protective equipment and appliances for gaseous fuels. A major part of these EU regulations is directly applicable. Nevertheless, Member States are obliged by their own procedures to ensure the performance of the public administration in the area. In our legal order, it is necessary to ensure market surveillance by our own procedures and to determine the language in which instructions, safety information for users of products and declarations of conformity are to be provided. The aim of the proposed law is to improve the regulation of the cable car market, personal protective equipment and appliances of gaseous fuels in order to ensure health protection, property protection, the safety of persons and other legitimate interests. The proposed amendment to the Act also provides technical clarification of some of the current provisions of the Act. The draft law was discussed in the Economic Committee, which recommended that the Chamber of Deputies discuss and approve this House's press as amended by the amendments adopted. Thank you for your attention.

ParlaMint-CZ_2017-05-24-ps2013-057-06-016-017.u4	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, gentlemen of the Ministers, what this House press is about, the Minister said very nicely. So I will just add that this main bill passed the first reading on 5 February 2016 at the 54th meeting of the Chamber of Deputies. The Economic Committee has interrupted the discussion of this proposal with the view that amendments may be applied before 1 March. The amendments tabled to the Economic Committee concern three headings. Firstly, the legislation on ensuring standardisation activities is supplemented by the possibility that the Office for Technical Standardization, Metrology and State Testing, as a national standardisation body, will be able to set up a contribution organisation to carry out private activities related to the creation, issuance and distribution of technical standards. The second amendment related to the addition of some of the provisions of this law on requirements arising from EU law which arose during the application practice of the law. And the third point was to shift the law's effectiveness to a closer date, that's on September 1, 2017. The Economic Committee discussed these amendments and recommended to the Chamber of Deputies that the House press be discussed and approved as amended by these amendments. Thank you.
