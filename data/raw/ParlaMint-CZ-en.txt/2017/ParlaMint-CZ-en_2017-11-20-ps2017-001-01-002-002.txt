Promise by Members The actual act of the promise will take place as follows: Mr Vojtěch Filip, as the oldest Vice-President of the Chamber of Deputies of the seventh parliamentary term, will deliver the prescribed promise by the Constitution and will be the first to put it in my hands. Then Mr. Vojtěch Filip will read your names in alphabetical order and you will approach me gradually. You will make a promise by saying the word "I promise" and by hand. You'll confirm your promise by signing it. Signing sheets are placed on the newsstand table. Please note that according to Section 6 of the letter. (a) our Rules of Procedure shall cease to exist by refusing a Member's promise or by making a pledge, subject to the mandate of the Member. I ask Mr Vojtěch Filip to approach the speaker's desk and take the floor, and I beg you all to rise.
