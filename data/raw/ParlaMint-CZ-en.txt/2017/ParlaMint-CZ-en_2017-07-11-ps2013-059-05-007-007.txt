Ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly remind you of the main changes brought about by the Government's bill on payment service at the start of the second reading. The new Payment Act is the transposition of a new European Payment Services Directive, whose transposition period expires on 13 January 2018, which brings a number of changes compared to the current regulatory state. The law introduces regulation of modern payment methods through mobile and internet applications. These methods are an alternative to card payment and, compared to a classic bank transfer, they give a higher guarantee that the payment will actually be made. The trader may therefore dispatch goods or provide a service at the time the payment order is entered. [[Strong noise in the whole hall.]] Furthermore, consumers will be able to centrally manage their payment account data via the Internet, which is intended to allow a greater overview of family finances. Higher standards of security payments are proposed through so-called strong user verification.
