ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-13-ps2013-060-06-011-055.u2	Thank you again for your word. I present the material which the Government of the Czech Republic has taken note of in its resolution No 139 of 20 February this year. The information provides a comprehensive overview of landings, flights and transits within 48 hours in the Czech Republic of other armed forces through the territory of the Czech Republic, for the second half of 2016. The material also includes a comparison of individual numbers of flights of foreign military aircraft over our territory over the last five years. Land and air transport of other armed forces was carried out during that period, mainly because of the participation of these forces in military exercises, trainings, negotiations and the security of military operations. In order to move and exercise on the territory of the Czech Republic, a total of 286 land and 274 air transports were carried out. Transit transits and crossings through our territory were carried out in total 4 319 in the given period, of which 67 transits and 4 252 air crossings. Thank you for your attention.
