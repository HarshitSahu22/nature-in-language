ParlaMint-CZ_2017-11-30-ps2017-002-02-003-031.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. Ladies and gentlemen, honourable Members of the Government, on 21 November 2017, a request from the Regional Police Directorate of the capital city of Prague, the Criminal Police and Investigation Service, the Department of Economic Crime, was delivered to the Office of the Chamber of Deputies for the prosecution of Mr Andrej Babiš and Mr Jaroslav Faltýnek. On 23 November this year, this request was sent to the President of the Chamber of Deputies immediately after his election and subsequently forwarded to the mandate and immune committee for discussion and reporting to the House. At its 3rd meeting held on 28 November 2017, the mandate and the Immune Committee also suspended the consideration of the request by its Resolution No. 15 and requested additional materials necessary for the Committee's decision. For this reason, the discussion of the police's request by the committee has not been completed and the mandate and immunity committee shall submit to the Chamber of Deputies a proposal to interrupt item 31 of the agenda of the 2nd meeting of the Chamber of Deputies until the time of the hearing of the request by the committee.
