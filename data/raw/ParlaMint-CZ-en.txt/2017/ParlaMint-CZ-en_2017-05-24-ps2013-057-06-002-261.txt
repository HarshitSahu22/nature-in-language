Dear colleagues, allow me briefly to recall in the third reading the basic content of the amendment to the Law on Advocates, which was submitted by Members across the political spectrum. The proposal contains, say, two basic circles of problems. It specifies and regulates the law exam itself and, at the same time, some of the conditions for the training of associates by lawyers are specified. The disciplinary liability of lawyers is also strengthened, where the Czech Bar Association's penalty, control and disciplinary instruments are added directly to the law to confiscate the lawyer's signature book if he commits error, violates the law or the law of the state, and the Czech Bar Association can suspend the law. The guarantee committee has discussed this proposal. I support the amendments adopted by the Constitutional Committee. I'll mention the ones that matter. These are amendments which lead to some mitigation of the original version, which concerns the number of associates a lawyer can train, including the length of his practice when he can train. There is also an important guarantee of the time limit for the invitation to the bar exam and, at the same time, the law, on the basis of the amendment of the constitutional law committee, would therefore impose a fee for the bar exam, which means that it would set its maximum amount. Some amendments have also been aimed at this, so I would just like to point out that the committee has already adopted this. The Constitutional Committee also agreed on the need to regulate the unauthorised provision of legal services, which we often see. The current regulation, where on the one hand we have a criminal penalty and, on the other hand, the possibility of affecting this unfair conduct by way of trade law, shows that it is not functional. Therefore, a new administrative offense is introduced to the unauthorised provision of legal services, as is the case in some European countries. And one last thing. Here I would like to mention some helpfulness to the petitioner because, unfortunately, the Ministry of Justice and the Government did not present the necessary law on free legal aid in the Chamber of Deputies in time, so we offered the opportunity to adjust the free legal aid in this proposal. It was also based on negotiations with the Czech Bar Association, which will provide it, but also with the Ministry of Justice, of course. We have come to a compromise option for certain types of proceedings, those which are due to the behaviour of a citizen with state authority, that is to say, in particular, to the Supreme Administrative Court or to the Constitutional Court.
