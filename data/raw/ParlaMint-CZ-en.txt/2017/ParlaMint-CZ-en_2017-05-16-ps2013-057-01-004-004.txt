In the first reading, I mentioned the fact that around 1,100 thousand live in the Czech Republic. population with disabilities and from this affected about 900 thousand. persons fall within the scope of this law. In particular, in view of the availability of compensatory aids, this law is very important for these persons. Taking into account the fact that the health disability affects the entire family of the disabled to a lesser or greater extent, the said law affects the life of almost one third of the population of the Czech Republic. Thus, the pursuit of its cultivation and the correction of some negative interferences from the past is clearly very socially necessary. The amendment to the Act is to improve the access of disabled people to the necessary compensatory aids, which are not covered by public disability and which are very important for disabled people for their normal life and especially for their integration back into work and social life. It was also said that the approach to compensatory aids deteriorated significantly after legislative changes between 2010 and 2012, as well as the changes in the legal standards at that time. There is certainly no need to stress that promoting the inclusion of disabled people and improving their lives is a standard task for the electoral programmes of most parliamentary and non-voting parties. It is therefore up to us whether, at a time when the economy is going well in the Czech Republic, we can, with reasonable demands on the state budget, improve the approach of disabled people to compensatory aids. It is up to us to prove that we care about improving the quality of the life of the disabled and to be able to integrate as best as possible into society. The proposed amendment to the law opens the way to this goal, although it is a compromise and far from addressing all the circuits that deserved it. I presented the specific proposed changes in detail in the first reading, there is no need to go back to this at the moment. I tabled an amendment to the Committee on Guarantees, which resulted from many weeks and relatively difficult discussions and consultations with the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs in order to find solutions that will be acceptable in terms of budgetary implications, but also in terms of practical feasibility. This amendment must be seen as a compromise solution for most of the headings of proposed amendments, be it for example. the mobility allowance, the contribution to the acquisition of a motor vehicle or the limit which distinguishes the arrangements for determining the amount of contributions to specific aids. The guarantee committee accepted this amendment, choosing from a variant solution in one parameter. In particular, this is an increase in the mobility allowance, where the Committee supported a medium variant of the increase in this contribution, i.e. an increase from 400 crowns today per month to 550 crowns per month. Today, in a detailed debate, I will read another amendment which responds to the Committee on Social Policy, the recommended wording of the law within the meaning of the Committee adopted by the amendments.
