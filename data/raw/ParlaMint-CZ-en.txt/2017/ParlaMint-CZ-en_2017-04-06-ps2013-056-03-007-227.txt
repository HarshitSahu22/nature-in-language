ParlaMint-CZ_2017-04-06-ps2013-056-03-007-227.u2	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, this is now a cooperation agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Cuba. It's the first reading of this treaty. This agreement should, in turn, create a framework and a platform for closer cooperation and dialogue between Cuba and the Member States of the European Union. I suppose you can imagine the areas covered by this contract because it's similar to those of the other contracts. It is also an effort to have some cooperation to modernise the Cuban economy, including trade cooperation, but also cooperation in the political area. There are standard provisions concerning the value base for which the agreement should be implemented. From a formal point of view, this is a mixed agreement, which means that validity is conditional upon the approval of the European Parliament. The signature took place on 12 December 2016 during the meeting of Foreign Ministers in Brussels. I said it was a mixed contract. This means that even here it is so that the selected parts, which are able to implement the European Union, are already being implemented and ratification should then, if it takes place, allow the rest to be implemented. As far as the relationship between the Czech Republic and Cuba is concerned, I think that the positives currently prevail there. I would like to pick up, above all, the fact that we have an ambassador in Cuba today, which is something that hasn't been for a long time. I think it's definitely a very positive step forward. In addition to some moments when there are still some exchanges of opinion, I think that overall there has been an increase in the depth of relations between the Czech Republic and Cuba. I don't want to comment on the whole recent development in Cuba right now. Definitely from the European Union, too, is an effort to create room for cooperation with Cuba to deepen. I can't think of a better word. That's what I think this contract should bring. I would recommend this treaty for your deliberations on committees, and I would like it if we could vote first reading here.
