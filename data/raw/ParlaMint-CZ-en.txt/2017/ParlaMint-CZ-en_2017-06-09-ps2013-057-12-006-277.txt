Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, allow me to briefly recapitulate the Government's bill amending the law on conformity assessment of specified products when making them available on the market, which we are presenting to ensure proper adaptation of European Union legislation. These are three new regulations of the European Parliament and of the Council in the field of cable lanes, personal protective equipment and appliances for gaseous fuels. A significant part of the provisions in these Regulations are applicable directly. In our legal order, it is still necessary to ensure market surveillance by our own procedures and to determine the language in which instructions, safety information for users of products and declarations of conformity are to be provided. The proposed amendment to the Act on Conformity Assessment is also a technical specification of some of the current provisions of the Act. I agree with the amendments applied, and I would like to conclude by asking you to support this bill at third reading. Thank you for your attention.
