Proposal for the election of a member of the Czech Radio Council As regards the election of one member of the Czech Radio Council, 170 ballots were also submitted here. Therefore, the Kvorum was 86, it concerned all the elections, the kvorum necessary for the election was 86. In this election Miroslav Dittrich received 45 votes, Božena Jirků 63 votes and Šimon Ryšavý 18 votes. So no one was elected in the first round. Two candidates with the highest number, Miroslav Dittrich and Božena Jirků, advance to the second round. We'll choose from them. I'm assuming at this point that we would have done the second round in a week on Wednesday.
