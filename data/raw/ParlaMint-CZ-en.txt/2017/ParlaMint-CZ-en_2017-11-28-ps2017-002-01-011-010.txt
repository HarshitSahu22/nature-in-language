Proposal for the establishment of a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the activities of the National Security Office and the manner in which it is established or elected Pursuant to Section 145 of Act No. 412/2005 Coll., on the Protection of Classified Information and Security Eligibility, the Chamber of Deputies establishes a monitoring body for the control of the activities of the National Security Office consisting of seven Members. Pursuant to Article 47 of the Rules of Procedure, I propose that this supervisory body be appointed as a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies. In accordance with Article 115 (2) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, In addition, I propose that this Commission be set up in accordance with the principle of proportional representation. I therefore submit this motion for a resolution: First, the Chamber of Deputies establishes a permanent commission of the Chamber of Deputies for the control of the activities of the National Security Office comprising seven Members; Second, it provides that the Commission is to establish itself on the basis of the principle of proportional representation.
