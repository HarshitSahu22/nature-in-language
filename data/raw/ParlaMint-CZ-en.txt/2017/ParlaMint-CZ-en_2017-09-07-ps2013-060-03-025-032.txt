ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-07-ps2013-060-03-025-032.u2	Thank you for the floor. Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, I submit amendments to Annex I to the International Convention against Doping in Sport to give my consent to ratification. The International Convention against Doping in Sport was adopted at the 33rd session of the General Conference of UNESCO in Paris in 2005 and is a multilateral treaty which entered into force for the Czech Republic in 2007. The purpose of this Convention is to take into account new circumstances in the fight against doping, to strengthen the activities of national anti-doping organisations and to create a worldwide strong framework for the joint fight against government, as well as non-governmental organisations against doping. Annex I is an integral part of this Convention, i.e. List of prohibited substances and methods and Annex II, i.e. International standard for therapeutic exceptions. The Convention belongs to the category of treaties where the adoption of their amendments is subject to the approval of the Parliament of the Czech Republic and ratification by the President of the Czech Republic. Annex I is an integral part of this Convention and the adoption of its amendments requires the same constitutional procedure to be followed. In the context of the development of doping substances and methods in the field of sport, Annex I to the Convention is updated annually. Amendments to the Annex I update and supplement the Annex on newly classified substances and methods with doping effect. In particular, a nominal list of substances in a group of steroids has been published and some quantitative limits and further minor adjustments have been made. Amendments to the Annex I will contribute to clarifying some of the contested provisions or interpreting rules in the field of anti-doping. The material submitted has no impact on the state budget, public budgets, and neither does gender equality. Thank you for your attention.
