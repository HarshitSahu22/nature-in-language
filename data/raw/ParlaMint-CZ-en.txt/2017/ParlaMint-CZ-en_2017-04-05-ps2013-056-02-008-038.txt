ParlaMint-CZ_2017-04-05-ps2013-056-02-008-038.u2	Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, the bill tabled contains changes in three areas of the Road Transport Act in particular. Firstly, it is a modification of the licensing procedure in scheduled passenger bus transport, in particular the removal of obsolete modifications by means of a new one, which responds better to the various modes of transport operated and, where possible, simplifies the licensing procedure. Secondly, these are adjustments in the area of taxi service operation. In particular, the proposal extends the authorisation of municipalities to issue specific requirements for the operation of taxi services on their territory, which is intended to allow adequate adjustment to the situation of the city. In particular, Prague and other cities face problems in the area of taxi services that are not relevant to other municipalities, but which should be able to be addressed. Furthermore, the proposal allows more efficient administrative punishment of major infringements of legal obligations and black operation of taxi service. Thirdly, the proposal contains a set of partial formal adjustments to administrative punishment in the Road Transport Act following the adoption of a new Act on Liability for and Procedure for Misdemeanors.
