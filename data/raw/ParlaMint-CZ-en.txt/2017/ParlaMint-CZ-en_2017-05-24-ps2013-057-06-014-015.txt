EH and DSI, and for this activity, the amendment opens up the possibility of creating a national contact centre for electronic health, which needs to deepen and further develop this process. It also empowers the rapporteur of the Committee of Mr Jiří Běhounek to present the opinion of the Committee to the meeting of the Chamber of Deputies, instructs the President of the Committee to present this resolution to the President of the Chamber of Deputies of Parliament, and empowers the rapporteur to make appropriate legislative technical amendments in cooperation with the Legislative Department of the Chamber of Deputies.
