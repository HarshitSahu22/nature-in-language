ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-07-ps2013-060-03-011-017.u2	Thank you. We're moving to the Dominican Republic. I wasn't there either, and maybe now that we have a deal, I'll check it out. Mutual relations between the Czech Republic and the Dominican Republic are not yet regulated by any bilateral international treaty. In this case, the Czech party expressed interest in the negotiation of the air agreement in connection with the growing activities of Czech air carriers on the Dominican transport market. The draft text of the Air Agreement was finalised in the framework of the expert meeting at the end of 2015 and the agreement was signed on 7 December 2017. The Agreement sets out the legal basis for the operation of air transport services by air carriers of both States. The text of the agreement is of a liberal nature and contains all the usual provisions of the air agreements. The agreement thus creates room for the possible development of direct scheduled air links in this area. The agreement does not require changes in our legal order and its implementation will not have an impact on state budget expenditure. Ladies and gentlemen, I would like to ask you to give my consent to the ratification of the agreement. Thank you.
