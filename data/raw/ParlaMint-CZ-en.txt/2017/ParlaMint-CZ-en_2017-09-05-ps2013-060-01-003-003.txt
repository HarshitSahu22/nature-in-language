ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-05-ps2013-060-01-003-003.u8	Mr President of the meeting, honourable representatives of the Government, I have the task of presenting the bill as adopted in the Senate text, so allow me to take up this task. The proposal for an amendment to the Environmental Impact Assessment Act is a transposition amendment to the revised directive of the European Parliament and the petitioner, the Ministry of the Environment, has also made a number of fundamental modifications to the current text in order to simplify and shorten the whole process of assessing the environmental effects of projects. At the same time as the proposed separate amendment to the EIA Act, another amendment was discussed in the House with the same objective as the accompanying part of the press concerning changes to the construction law. Unfortunately, many times the declared intention to achieve a mutually consistent final valid version of the EIA from the two amendments could not be achieved. Reason for the renewal of the amendment The Chamber of Deputies is a discrepancy between the outcome of the two simultaneous amendments to the EIA Act, which would cause application problems in practice and would be incomprehensible in some parts. For both amendments, amendments to the same provisions have been proposed in some cases, but in another version, or where appropriate, the marking has been shifted and therefore the amendment has been completely incomprehensible. The amendments to the EIA Act thus tabled immediately after each other do not indicate a well-thought-out strategy for the implementation of additional amendments to its text and their high-quality legislative representation. The Senate's amendments correct these errors. I think that the Senate's proposals are completely non-conflict, uncontroversial, and I therefore ask the Chamber of Deputies to agree to the amendment to the Act, as returned to the Senate, as rapporteur of the Senate.
