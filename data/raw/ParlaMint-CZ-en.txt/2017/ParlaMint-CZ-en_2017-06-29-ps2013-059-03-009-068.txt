Government proposal by which the Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratification of the Agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Indonesia on Air Transport /German press 1037/ - first reading I also ask for the opening words of the Minister for Regional Development. Please, Madam Secretary, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-29-ps2013-059-03-009-068.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the relationship between the Czech Republic and Indonesia in the field of civil aviation so far is governed by the agreement between the Government of the Czech Republic and the Government of the Republic of Indonesia of 1972, including the subsequent amendment of the Annex. This agreement is restrictive and does not correspond to current developments in the field of air transport. Interest in negotiating a new air agreement was expressed by the Czech party in connection with the new activities of Czech air carriers on the Czech-Indian market, which are not covered by the existing agreement. The draft text of the new aviation agreement was finalised at the beginning of last year and the agreement was signed on 20 January 2017 in Jakarta. The new agreement has a liberal basis for operating air transport services by air carriers of both states. The contradiction of the original agreement with European Union law will also be removed. The negotiation of this agreement does not require a change in our legal order and its implementation will not have an impact on the expenditure of the state budget. The Agreement will create new opportunities for the possible development of direct and indirect scheduled air links between the two Parties. Ladies and gentlemen of the House of Deputies, I would also like to ask you to transfer this material to the Foreign Committee of the Chamber of Deputies. Thank you.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-29-ps2013-059-03-009-068.u3	Thank you. Please take the floor of the rapporteur for first reading, Mr Pavel Šrámek. Please, Mr. Ambassador, you have the floor.

I would just briefly add that the agreement includes important areas of operational, security and civil aviation security. It also allows airlines to establish offices and offer and sell air services within the territory of the other Contracting Party. The Annex to the Agreement contains the definition of lines between places in the territory of the Czech Republic and places in the territory of Indonesia with the possibility of operating through intermediate places or to places beyond the territory of the other Contracting Party's States.
