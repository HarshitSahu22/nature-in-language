These amendments were the result of a series of rather complicated discussions and discussions with representatives of the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs in order to find solutions that would be acceptable in terms of budgetary implications, but also in terms of practical feasibility. I confess that I am sorry to retreat from some of the originally proposed changes.
