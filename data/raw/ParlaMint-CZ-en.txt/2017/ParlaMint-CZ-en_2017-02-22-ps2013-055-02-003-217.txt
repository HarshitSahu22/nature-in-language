ParlaMint-CZ_2017-02-22-ps2013-055-02-003-217.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, first of all, I would like to thank both the President of the Guarantee Economic Committee and the rapporteur of this press and, last but not least, the plenary of the Chamber of Deputies for the preparation and progress of the second reading of the draft amendment to the Act amending the Act on Fuels and Gas Stations. I would remind you that the submitted government amendment is the main carrier of the Czech Republic's transposition obligation under the directive of the European Parliament and of the Council on the implementation of infrastructure for alternative fuels. The proposed bill introduces the necessary definitions and measures relating to the creation of infrastructure for alternative fuels, which are not yet sufficiently enshrined in national legislation. At the same time, it gives consumers the benefits of alternative fuels. In addition to the obligations which are the implementation of the part of Directive 2014/94/EU, the proposed draft law regulates and specifies certain existing provisions. The draft law was discussed by the Economic Committee first on 20 September 2016 and then after the deadline for amendments on 16 November last year. Several amendments were applied to the draft law at the time, and the Ministry, as the legislator, fully agreed with the opinion of the Economic Committee issued on 21 November last year and with the content of these amendments, which the Committee recommended to the Chamber of Deputies to adopt. During the second reading of 20 January 2017, further amendments were made, which were discussed on 25 January by the Guarantee Economic Committee and, at the same time, Resolution 346 was issued with a recommendation from the Committee for further voting. I agree with the position of the guarantee economic committee and therefore allow me to ask you to support the amendments recommended by this committee. Thank you for your attention.
