Mr Vice-President, honourable Members, allow me to give this third reading of the bill amending the law on cyber security, as well as the law on free access to information. This bill prepared by the National Security Authority, which has been submitted by the Government, transposes the directive on measures to ensure a high common level of network security and information system throughout the EU. On the basis of the Government's decision, the Government approved during the discussion of this proposal material in which it changed as Director of the National Security Office. Part of this material was also a proposal for the division of the National Security Office, particularly by separating cyber security issues with the current emergence of a new central administrative office - the National Cyber and Information Security Office based in Brno, which would be responsible for the security of information systems as well as cryptographic protection in the handling of classified information. The provision of professional activities of the new office will be addressed by the transfer of relevant personnel from the National Security Office, which will be linked to the deliberation of adequate funds for wages and related expenditure. The Security Committee adopted by its resolution an amendment which will allow the creation of this new office, which is responsible for cyber security. Ladies and gentlemen, let me thank you for having managed to achieve a broad political consensus on this proposal in the House of Deputies. The fact that a broad political consensus has been achieved also reflects the low number of amendments tabled, which we will vote on against this House press at the third reading. [[9.20 hours]] I am also glad that we can actually proceed very quickly from the point of view of the creation of a new cybersecurity office, so that this office can be established as far as possible during this year. I consider cyber security issues to be very urgent. I think we all have a duty to do our utmost to ensure that our countries and its citizens, its companies, the state administration, self-government are protected as much as possible against potential cyber attacks, and I believe that this amendment will make a significant contribution to this. So, once again, I thank the representatives of all the Members' clubs in this Chamber of Deputies for supporting the proposal.
