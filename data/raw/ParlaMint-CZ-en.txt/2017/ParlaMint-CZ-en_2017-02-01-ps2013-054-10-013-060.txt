ParlaMint-CZ_2017-02-01-ps2013-054-10-013-060.u2	Mr President, honourable Members, on 21 April 2018, three new regulations of the European Parliament and of the Council in the field of cable cars, personal protective equipment and appliances of gaseous fuels will come into force. Those regulations are created according to the principles of the so-called a new legislative framework, which aims to improve the functioning of the EU internal market, with an emphasis on ensuring the safety of citizens and restricting products on the market which do not comply with the requirements laid down. The aim of the legislation is in particular to improve regulation in the field of cableways, personal protective equipment and gas fuel appliances in order to ensure effective protection of the health and safety of persons, the protection of property and other legitimate interests. The legal framework for the implementation of EU regulations, which are created on the basis of the new legislative framework, is Act No. 90/2016 Coll., on conformity assessment of specified products when making them available on the market. A major part of all three new regulations will be applicable directly as directly applicable regulations. However, Member States are required to ensure market surveillance by their own procedures. For this reason, it is necessary to adapt the legal order of the Czech Republic to ensure effective application of the regulation by means of Act No. 90/2016 Coll., on conformity assessment of specified products when making them available on the market. Finally, allow me to put forward a proposal to shorten the drafting of the bill in committees by 30 days. The reason for this proposal is that it is a technical amendment to a law that only adapts directly applicable EU regulations. Dear Members, honourable Members, in conclusion, I would like to ask you to support this bill both in today's negotiations and in the following procedures in the committees of the Chamber of Deputies and subsequently in the second and third reading. Thank you for your attention.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-02-01-ps2013-054-10-013-060.u3	Thank you, Minister. I ask that the rapporteur take the floor for first reading, Mr Petr Kudela.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-02-01-ps2013-054-10-013-060.u4	Good evening, Mr President, fellow ministers, ladies and gentlemen. We are discussing the amendment to Act No. 90/2016 Coll., on conformity assessment of specified products when making them available on the market. The material is submitted for the implementation of the adaptation of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council. 424, 425 and 426 of 2016. The proposal adapts these directly applicable regulations of the European Union to our law. In particular, these are requirements for lifts, personal protective equipment and appliances for gaseous fuels, which are laid down in Council Directives of the European Economic Community and Directive of the European Parliament on appliances for gaseous fuels, and similar Directive on cable lines for the transport of persons. These directives were transposed into the legal order of the Czech Republic by government regulations laying down technical requirements for personal protective equipment, cable cars and appliances. The abovementioned Directives are now replaced by directly applicable European Union regulations. These should be applicable from 21 April 2018. The submitted amendment also contains provisions concerning the administrative body in the Czech Republic. The amendment also contains a technical specification of some of the provisions of this Act. The material went through inter-ministerial commentary proceedings and was discussed without contradictions. I recommend moving this bill to second reading. I think that the proposed economic committee for discussion is a good and sufficient form of discussion in the committees. I will come forward in the debate, and because this is a rather simple standard, I will propose a reduction in the time limit for committee discussions to 30 days. Thank you for your attention.
