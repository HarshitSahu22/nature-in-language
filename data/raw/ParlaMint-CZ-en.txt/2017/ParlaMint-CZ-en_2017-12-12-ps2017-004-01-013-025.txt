ParlaMint-CZ_2017-12-12-ps2017-004-01-013-025.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the Audit Board of the Grant Agency of the Czech Republic pursuant to Act No. 130/202 Coll., on the promotion of research, experimental development and innovation, controls the distribution of funds by the Czech Grant Agency. At the end of the period for which the report was submitted, i.e. from October 2016 to September 2017, the Board of Auditors worked almost in full staffing, at the number of nine members of the legally foreseen ten. It carried out a legal check on the legitimacy and regularity of the distribution of funds by the Grant Agency and the management of state property, discussed complaints about the provider's progress in evaluating the grant projects and submitted opinions to the Bureau of the Grant Agency. The decisive activity of the Grant Agency was to provide special-purpose support for the solution of grant projects in the reference period. The Board of Auditors notes in the report that the whole process of selection of grants and their evaluation took place in a standard manner and that the recommendations of expert panels and expert commissions were always respected over the past period. The members of the Supervisory Board physically carried out 68 inspections at meetings of expert panels and expert committees. During the reference period, I reiterate October 2017 to September 2017, a total of 106 grant projects were audited by 15 institutions. All applications recommended during the evaluation period were settled by the Board of Auditors, which was reflected in the draft remuneration for the activities of the members of the Board of Auditors of the Grant Agency, with a limit of CZK 540 000 not exceeded.
