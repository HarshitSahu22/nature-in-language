Government bill amending Act No. 256/2004 Coll., on Capital Market Business, as amended, and other related laws /house printing 869/ - second reading Under the authority of the Government, the draft bill will be presented by the Deputy Prime Minister and the Minister of Finance, Mr Andrej Babiš. I ask the Minister to take the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-03-14-ps2013-055-09-002-005.u2	Thank you for the floor. Hello, Mr President, ladies and gentlemen. Allow me to present to you the proposed bill amending the Capital Market Business Act and other related laws. The draft law implements several European capital market legislation, in particular the Financial Instrument Markets Directive, the Directive on criminal sanctions for market abuse, the regulation on markets in financial instruments and the regulation on market abuse. Since the rules under the Market Abuse Regulation are to apply before the rules under the Financial Instruments Market Directive, the draft law presented has a divided effect. This amendment continues to unify the regulation of distribution on the financial market, the proposed amendment being similar to the recently adopted Consumer Credit Act. We consider it important that similar issues should be regulated as far as possible in the laws governing the various sectors of the financial market. These are in particular questions relating to qualification, economic and other requirements for the provision of investment services. The proposed bill concerns, in particular, the Capital Market Business Act, but it also amends the relevant regulations, in particular the Capital Market Supervisory Act, the Administrative Charges Act, the Bank Act, the Criminal Code and the Criminal Code. The draft law was continuously consulted with the professional public from the private and academic sphere and with representatives of the Czech National Bank. Thank you for your attention.
