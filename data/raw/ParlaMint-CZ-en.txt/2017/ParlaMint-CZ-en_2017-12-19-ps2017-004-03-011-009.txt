The subsidy programmes follow up on the already proven programmes established by previous governments. The programmes were analysed and found to be effective. Animal production is preferred in the submitted proposal for the amount of allocated resources. The spectrum of other subsidy programmes remains the same as in 2017 only two changes occur. The current programme 2d will not be launched, the purchase of breeding animals due to budgetary possibilities and the permanent low interest of the applicants. Furthermore, it proved necessary to add a new subsidy programme to support municipalities to ensure the cost of care for animals placed in substitute care. This is a dedicated investment grant for municipalities with extended scope, which are forced to deal with cases of prolonged care for abused animals. This subsidy is intended to allow compensation in the form of advances for the period during which the animals are collected in the care of the municipality, which must cover all related costs. This is a solution that will help to address existing problems in the care of abused animals without major consequences for municipal budgets and thus their willingness to participate in this care until the amendment to the Act for the Protection of Animals against Abuse is being prepared.
