I would therefore just like to point out that we would then propose to vote jointly on A1 through A8, amendments. And let me just introduce what is going on in these amendments. It is de facto one single amendment, which is reflected both in the Act on Municipalities, the Regional Law and the Act on the Capital City of Prague. The purpose of this amendment is to make sure that members of the council are covered by the Labour Code in the event of liability for damage caused to a local authority in connection with the performance of the duties of a member of the council. According to the interpretation of the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs, these relations are already subject to the Labour Code regime. This interpretation is based on the Ministry of the Interior. In this context, however, it should be noted that there are opposite legal opinions, which are also supported by the decision-making activities of the courts. As I said before, the amendment is proposed in the law on municipalities, in the law on regions and in the law on the capital city of Prague. This is de facto the content of all the amendments. And then the ones that were still there, they were just legislatively technical, there are no others. Thank you.
