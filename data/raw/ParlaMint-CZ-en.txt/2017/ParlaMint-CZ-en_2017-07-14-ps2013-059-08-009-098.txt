ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-14-ps2013-059-08-009-098.u2	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me only briefly introduce you to the content of the amendment to the Trade Business Act, which we are currently discussing at third reading. The amendment removes the entrepreneur's obligation to notify the trade office of the identification details of members of the statutory body, legal entity and head of the branching plant of a foreign person registered in a commercial or other public register. The draft amendment also meets the requirements of the business and non-business public and introduces the provision of information from the trade register in the form of mass files, so-called. compilations, with current, publicly available data of entrepreneurs. At the same time, it improves the regulation of the trade law following application practice, when it is regulated e.g. authorisation to issue market orders to ensure the functioning of central registration points or to define certain terms following the wording of other legislation. The Economic Committee, as a guarantee committee, recommended the draft law for discussion by the final third reading and its approval. The Economic Committee has applied amendments to the proposal which have a positive impact on the proposed amendment, as they specify the rules for issuing business registers. I would therefore like to ask you, honourable Members, to support this proposal and its approval at third reading. Thank you.
