ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-07-ps2013-060-03-004-009.u2	Mr Vice-President, Members, the purpose of this present protocol is, above all, to remove contradictions in the interpretation of the Czech-Japanese Treaty on Social Security in the field of posting workers. The Czech party here is based on a narrower interpretation of the posting of workers, which is also used in EU law, where the worker has to maintain an immediate relationship with the sending employer during the posting to another country, but Japanese workers who are sent here by the Japanese company often conclude contracts with the Czech company where he operates, and this has led to a break in direct relation to their Japanese employer and breach of broadcasting conditions. And since we could not agree on an expert level on the interpretation, it became clear in the end directly in the article. 7 contracts governing the broadcasting. According to the new regulation, the negotiation of a contract of employment with a company in the Czech Republic will not prevent it from broadcasting on condition that the posted worker continues to remain in employment relations with his Japanese company. This regulation is not unique, it is not exclusively with Japan, it is already included in social security contracts e.g. with the United States of America or Australia. These proposed modifications aim to contribute to increasing legal certainty in the application of the contract and to improving the investment environment for Japanese companies, because it is important to stress that these are among the most important foreign investors in the Czech Republic in the long term. On the Japanese side, the protocol is ready for entry into force and on our side the Senate has already approved it. I would like to ask the Chamber of Deputies to agree to this ratification, in accordance with the Foreign Committee's opinion. Thank you.
