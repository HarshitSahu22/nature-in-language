ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-29-ps2013-059-03-013-082.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Members of Parliament, it may seem strange to you that we are proposing here to agree to ratify the International Labour Organisation Convention on Work at Sea. However, this Convention replaces about 70 existing maritime conventions and recommendations adopted by the International Labour Organisation since 1920 and includes several conventions, which also bind the Czech Republic, because although this sector is of course less significant for the Czech Republic, there is still a small group of Czech citizens who work as members of naval ship crews. So it's actually for them, too. The convention covers, as always, a wide range of issues, including minimum requirements for sailors when working on a ship, conditions of employment, accommodation, leisure, meals, health care and social security. So he's actually modernising the entire employment regime of sailors. And the ratification is simply desirable so that these people are actually protected, and at the same time responds to the relevant European directives. Otherwise, the various requirements of the Convention have already been reflected in national legislation within the framework of the amendments already approved, so there are no additional implications for the state budget with this consent. Thank you.
