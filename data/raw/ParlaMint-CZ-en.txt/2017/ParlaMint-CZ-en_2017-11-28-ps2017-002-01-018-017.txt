ParlaMint-CZ_2017-11-28-ps2017-002-01-018-017.u2	Thank you, Mr. Vice-President. The honourable Members, honourable Members, honourable Members of the Government, the mandate and immunity committee discussed at its 3rd meeting of today's meeting the request of the Ministry of Finance to communicate the number of valid votes cast for each political party, political movement or coalition in elections held on 20 to 21 October 2017 and adopted Resolution No 12, which was distributed to you, on which it proposes to adopt the following resolution: On the proposal to pay electoral costs to political parties, political movements and coalitions for elections to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic in 2017. First, the Chamber of Deputies communicates to the Ministry of Finance pursuant to § 85 of Law No. 247/1995 Coll., on elections to the Parliament of the Czech Republic, as amended, the number of valid votes cast for individual political parties and political movements held in elections to the Chamber of Deputies in 2017 and the number of parliamentary mandates by political parties and political movements. The results are set out in the Annex to the resolution. Secondly, it recommends that the Ministry of Finance should pay the electoral contribution to political parties and political movements under the provisions of Section 85 of the Act cited. The resolution shall be accompanied by two annexes on these mandates and the number of votes. Thank you for your attention.
