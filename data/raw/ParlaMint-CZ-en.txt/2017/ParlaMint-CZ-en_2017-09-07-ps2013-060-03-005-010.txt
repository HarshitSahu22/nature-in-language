ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-07-ps2013-060-03-005-010.u2	Hello, Mr Vice-President, Members. This convention may seem at first glance not to concern the Czech Republic, however, because there is a group of Czech citizens who are still working as members of naval ship crews, and we cannot completely leave them out, even though the group is of course not large. But on the other hand, it's a few dozen people. [[11.20 hours]] This proposed Convention unites and updates a set of international labour standards in order to respond to changing conditions in a globalising economy and lays down minimum requirements for seafarers working on a ship. It contains basic rules for employment conditions, working hours and rest, accommodation, meals, health protection and medical care and, last but not least, social security. It should also be noted that the Convention also affected relevant legislation at European level. Otherwise, for the sake of interest, the Czech Republic is now bound by two conventions of the International Labour Organisation of 1987, the Convention on Social Welfare for Seafarers and the Convention on the Protection of Health and Medical Care for Seafarers, and when this newer Convention is adopted, these two will actually be automatically expelled. Thank you for your attention.
