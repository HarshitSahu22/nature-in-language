ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-05-ps2013-060-01-007-007.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Dear colleagues, let me introduce a government bill amending Act No. 289/1995 Coll., on forests and on amendment and addition of some laws /Forest Act/, as amended. The proposed bill has one objective, namely to anchor the explicit inalienability of state forests in the forest law. The reason for submitting this proposal is, inter alia, an obligation arising from the coalition contract. [[17.30 hours]] The current legislation on the management of state forest land regulates too strictly when it requires the prior approval of the Ministry of Agriculture. However, the ban on the disposal of state forest land is not yet contained in the legal order. The content of the proposal is therefore an explicit ban on the destruction of state forests. However, the inalienability of state forests cannot be exceptional and the law must allow state forests to continue to be disposed of in justified cases, in particular for reasons that ensure efficient management. The draft law therefore defines exemptions from the ban on the destruction of state forests. These are land exchanges and sales of co-ownership shares of the State, in particular because of the consolidation of land, the sale of so-called isolated forest land, the issue of a case under restitution legislation and cases which will be justified by the public interest. Only in these cases will it be possible to further destroy the state forests, but only with the prior consent of the Ministry of Agriculture.
