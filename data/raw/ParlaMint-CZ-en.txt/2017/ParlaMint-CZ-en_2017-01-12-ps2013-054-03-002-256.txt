Members shall sign up for oral questioning in writing with the President of the House, either in paper form or through the electronic information system of the Chamber of Deputies. 2. Members shall be admitted to oral questioning by means of an electronic information system. The Chamber of Deputies shall place its application in this system electronically in the designated section. 3. Application made through an electronic information system The Chamber of Deputies must contain the following information: whether the interpelation is addressed to the Prime Minister or another member of the Government, the subject of the interpelation submitted, and if the Member submits more interpellation, it shall indicate their order. 4. Applications contained in the electronic information system The Chambers of Deputies are not public, the application for oral questioning may be adjusted or withdrawn by the end of the deadline for their submission. 5. At the end of the period for the submission of oral interviews, all applications submitted through the electronic information system of the Chamber of Deputies shall be merged with applications submitted in paper form and the order of Members to be entered shall be determined by lot in accordance with Section 111 (2) of the Rules of Procedure. 2 and 3 of Act No 90/1995 Coll.
