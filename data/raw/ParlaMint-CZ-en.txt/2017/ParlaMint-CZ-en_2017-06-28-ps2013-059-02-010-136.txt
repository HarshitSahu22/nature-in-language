Proposal for the election of members of the Czech Radio Council In the first election of two members of the Czech Radio Council we were not successful, no one was elected. Czendlik Zbigniew won 59 votes, Jan Stanzel 62 votes, Michal Stehlík 44 and Ivan tesař 9 votes. I'm saying that's the end of the election. There are two vacant seats in the Czech Radio Council, and if the current House is still willing to repeat the election, it must go through the whole process, which means calling on the President of the House on the Internet and then narrowing nominations through the media committee.
