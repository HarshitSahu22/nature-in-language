ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-07-ps2013-060-03-014-020.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, I will now submit about eight agreements which concern mostly the Caribbean islands, albeit not entirely. The essence of these contracts is that they have been negotiated in order to allow savings income in the form of interest payments paid in one State to their beneficial owners, natural persons residing for tax purposes in the other Contracting State, to be subject to effective taxation in that other State. Meanwhile, the European Union has adopted a common standard in its legislation, which was created within the OECD framework for notification and automatic exchange of information on financial accounts, so that these bilateral agreements, which the Czech Republic has concluded, need to be abolished. The first such agreement is an agreement between the Czech Republic and the Kingdom of the Netherlands with regard to the Netherlands Antilles. Since education on the Caribbean islands and their geographical location has already been carried out here in the House - the senators were not interested in it - I suppose the eight statements could take place quite quickly. Thank you.
