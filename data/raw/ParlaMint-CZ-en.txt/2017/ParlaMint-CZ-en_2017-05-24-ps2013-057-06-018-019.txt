Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, let me briefly introduce you to the content of the amendment to the law aimed at continuing to reduce the administrative burden on entrepreneurs and improving the business environment in our country. The amendment removes the entrepreneur's obligation to notify the trade office of the identification details of the members of the statutory body of the legal person and of the head of the divisional plant of the foreign person entered in the commercial or other public register. The draft amendment also meets the requirements of the business and non-business public and introduces the provision of information from the trade register in the form of mass files, so-called. set up. The draft law was discussed in the Economic Committee, which adopted a resolution in which the Chamber of Deputies recommends that the draft law be discussed and approved as amended by the amendments. The Ministry of Industry and Trade agrees with these proposals. Thank you for your attention.
