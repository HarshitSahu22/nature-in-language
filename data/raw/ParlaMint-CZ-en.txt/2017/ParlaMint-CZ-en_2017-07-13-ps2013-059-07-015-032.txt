ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-13-ps2013-059-07-015-032.u2	Mr President, honourable members of the government, dear colleagues, dear colleagues, allow me to briefly reiterate the reasons for the amendment of Act No. 268/2014 Coll., on medical devices and on the amendment of Act No. 634/2004 Coll., on administrative fees, as amended. As I said at first reading, the proposed amendment is intended to amend the problematic provisions on services and instructions for medical devices approved by this Chamber of Deputies during the 2014 Act. These unfortunate provisions oblige health care establishments to carry out servicing, safety technical checks and instructions for working with medical devices only through manufacturers and distributors, which means increased costs and complex logistics for health care establishments. I therefore consider it our duty to adjust the problematic points of the applicable version of the law, thereby helping medical devices save money, which must now be spent unnecessarily to ensure the service of medical devices and briefings directly with manufacturers and suppliers. At the same time, I would like to point out that this concerns not only hospitals but outpatients. Today, most clinical ambulances use at least a minor instrumental technique, but they are also disciplines that are actually dependent in outpatient care of instrumental technology, e.g. Eye medicine. If we approve today's regulation of Act No. 268/2014 Coll., we will save money and unnecessary worries to doctors in private practice. The Committee on Health has discussed the amendment and recommended its adoption, and I would also like to ask you to do the same at the final vote at the third reading, which I firmly believe will take place at the beginning of September. Thank you for your attention.

ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-13-ps2013-059-07-015-032.u8	Thank you. Ladies and gentlemen, I have worked for quite a long time on the safety and technical controls of devices in health care, so I have also contacted colleagues from hospitals, rescue services, and I therefore also submit this amendment, which is recorded under number 6630. And the point is that this amendment regulates the implementation of repairs and run-ins, or safety technical checks, for all medical devices at risk class one. That's the lowest one that can't hurt a patient. Class 1 medical devices are those which represent the lowest possible risk. This proposal enables health care establishments to carry out this activity by their own biomedical techniques or persons with the necessary expertise and experience. With this regulation of the legal standard, there would be considerable financial savings in all health establishments throughout the Czech Republic. The financial savings will be created not only in the cost of the hourly rate, but also in the savings for the expensive travel costs of external suppliers. There will also be an effective use of our own biomedical technicians, engineers. The waiting times for repairs and safety technical checks will be significantly reduced and the whole service process will be more flexible. For other medical devices of higher risk classes 2A, 2B and 3, the legal standard remains unchanged. I know that in universities, both at Liberec Technical University and here in Kladno, there is already a major field of biomedical technician and engineer, and I think these professions are absolutely perfect for this activity. Thank you very much.
