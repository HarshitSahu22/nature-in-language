ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-11-ps2013-059-05-009-009.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Dear colleagues and colleagues, as was said at first reading, according to the opinion of the experts on the Administrative Code, the granting of subsidies and return financial assistance from the State budget is a public authority exercise and is therefore subject to the administrative rules. The Supreme Administrative Court came to the same conclusion, despite the fact that the budgetary rules still contain legislation that precludes the application of the Administrative Code and excludes judicial review. On the basis of the above, a draft amendment to the budgetary rules has been drawn up. The aim of this proposal is to adjust the relationship of the Administrative Code to the provision of subsidies and return financial assistance from the state budget. Let me briefly remind you of its content. In accordance with the proposal, the Administrative Code shall apply to the process of granting subsidies and return financial assistance from the State budget, with certain issues being adjusted differently, such as participation, and the application of certain provisions of the Administrative Code is excluded. The process of granting subsidies and return financial assistance from the state budget is designed as a one-stage, no appeal or degradation will be possible. The renewal of the procedure is also excluded and the application of the review procedure is also restricted. An applicant for a grant or a return financial assistance will be able to bring an administrative action against the decision. In the event that current legislation is maintained, there is a risk that unsuccessful applicants for a grant or refundable financial assistance from the state budget will bring actions to court, thereby increasing the pressure on the use of the administrative order in providing such subsidies and the recovery of financial assistance in its entirety. This means that this process would be covered by all the remedies provided for in the administrative order - appeals, breakdowns, retrials, review procedures. Such a situation could lead not only to an increase in the administrative complexity of the process, but to a collapse in the provision of these subsidies, including subsidies co-financed by the European Union budget. The proposed amendment also includes an amendment to the Regional Development Support Act, which allows documents relating to subsidies co-financed by the European Union budget through the MS14+ system, and an amendment to the Research Support Act, experimental development and innovation, which excludes the application of the procedure proposed in the amendment to the budgetary rules in order to maintain the contractual principle of granting grants in the field of science and research. Only point 17 of Article 1 of the amendment to the budgetary rules is not linked to subsidies and return financial assistance, which, on the basis of the findings of the Supreme Audit Office, amends the establishment of budgetary rules governing the government's budgetary reserve. The effectiveness of the Act is proposed on 1 January 2018. Thank you for your attention.
