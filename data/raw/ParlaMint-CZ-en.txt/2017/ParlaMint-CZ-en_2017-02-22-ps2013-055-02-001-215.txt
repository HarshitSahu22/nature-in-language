The proposed proposal envisages the introduction of so-called pharmaceutical-cost groups, in English it is Pharmacy-based Cost Groups, in the health sector we call it PCG, which will allow the distribution of insurance premiums to take account of higher health insurance costs associated with higher cost of chronic patients.
