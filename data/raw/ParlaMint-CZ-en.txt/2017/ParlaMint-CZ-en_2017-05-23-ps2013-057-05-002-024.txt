ParlaMint-CZ_2017-05-23-ps2013-057-05-002-024.u2	Thank you. Mr President, dear colleagues, dear colleagues, allow me to introduce a government bill amending Act No No. 219/2003 Coll., on the circulation of seeds and planting plants and on the amendment of certain laws, as amended, on administrative fees. It is proposed to introduce a process for registering varieties with an officially recognised description which constitutes a simpler process than the standard registration of varieties under the applicable Law on the circulation of seed and seed. The amendment relates to the implementation of Council Directive 2008/90/EC of 29 September 2008 on the marketing of fruit plant propagating material and fruit plants intended for fruit production. This method of registration is less demanding for applicants for registration of these varieties both in terms of compliance with the conditions of registration and in terms of financial complexity. A common certification scheme with a uniform approach to the maintenance and testing of fruit family propagating material and species is being introduced. New provisions concerning the labelling and formalities of accompanying documents, the closure of packaging, propagating material and fruit crops are also an integral part of the transposition. The proposal also responds to the unsatisfactory market situation when seed garlic is put into circulation. On the part of sellers, there is an undesirable substitution of seed garlic subject to the provisions of the Law on the circulation of seeds and seed for potable garlic. The rules for the putting into circulation of seed garlic are thus set so that the control and supervisory authority, which is the central control and testing institution of the agricultural holding, can effectively control the process and impose sanctions in the event of infringement. The proposed entry into force shall be linked to the obligations arising for Member States from the implementation of Commission Directives 2014/96/EU, 20149.7/EU and 2014/98/EU. The Government Bill was discussed by the Agricultural Committee of the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic at its 41st meeting on 19 April 2017 and recommended by its resolution to the Chamber of Deputies of the Czech Republic to approve the Government Bill as amended by the amendments adopted. The amendments adopted are in line with the concept of the petitioner, they will contribute to improving the government's bill, so I agree with them. Thank you for your attention.
