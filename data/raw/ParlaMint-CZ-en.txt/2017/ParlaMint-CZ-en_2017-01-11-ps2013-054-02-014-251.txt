Proposal for the election of a member of the Ethical Commission of the Czech Republic for the Award of Resistance and Resistance to Communism We voted for the Ethical Commission of the Czech Republic for the awards of Resistance and Resistance to Communism from two proposed candidates. Stanislav devátý received 41 votes, Jiří Rajlich 89, so Jiří Rajlich was elected in this election.
