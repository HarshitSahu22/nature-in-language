Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to ratifying the Agreement between the Czech Republic and Belize on the exchange of information in tax matters signed in Mexico on 12 February 2016 /German press 788/ - second reading I beg the opening words of the Minister of Finance, Ivan Pilný.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-07-13-ps2013-059-07-017-046.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr President. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to present a government proposal by which the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic is presented to express its agreement to ratify the Agreement between the Czech Republic and Belize on the exchange of information on tax matters. The negotiations with jurisdiction with the preferential tax regime have resulted in fourteen final drafts of the text of this type of international treaty, which subsequently undergoes a legislative process leading to ratification by the President. Twelve agreements have already entered into force, which are announced in the Collection of International Treaties. By concluding the agreement, the Czech Republic will be able to request information about Czech residents. These will be used in the correct assessment of taxes, possibly. to investigate or prosecute tax offences. However, given the legal constraints imposed by the domestic law, the information provided may be used in criminal proceedings only as operational information. The approval of an agreement on the basis of which tax relevant information can be exchanged with jurisdictions considered until recently as tax havens should lead to an effective reduction in tax evasion. Agreements of this type act as a preventive measure to strengthen the payment morale of Czech taxpayers. I therefore recommend agreeing to ratify this agreement. Thank you for the floor.
