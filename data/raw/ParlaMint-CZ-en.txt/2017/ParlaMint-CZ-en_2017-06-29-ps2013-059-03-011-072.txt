Government proposal by which Parliament of the Czech Republic is to give its consent to the ratification of the Protocol of Accession to the Trade Agreement between the European Union and its Member States, of the one part, and Colombia and Peru, of the other part, with a view to the accession of Ecuador /the House Press 1102/ - first reading The submitted proposal will be made by Minister Jan Chvojka on behalf of the Government. Please, Minister, you have the floor.
ParlaMint-CZ_2017-06-29-ps2013-059-03-011-072.u2	Thank you for the floor, Mr Vice-President. Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce to you the protocol which connects Ecuador to an existing trade agreement between the European Union and Colombia and Peru, from which our companies have benefited for four years. The Protocol with Ecuador shall be applied on a provisional basis as from the beginning of this year after it has been approved by the European Parliament and ratified by Ecuador itself. I believe that the protocol will strengthen cooperation with the Andean Community region. At present, uncertainty is important for us to achieve positive results, and I consider the conclusion of a protocol with Ecuador to be one of them. The conclusion of the Protocol opens up new possibilities for both trade in goods and services, as well as investment or public procurement. The business environment will be more transparent and provide or should provide higher legal certainty for Czech companies, which is of course very important. Various fields will benefit from better access to the Ecuadorian market, whether it is the traditional automotive industry or engineering, as well as the glass or security industry. In the field of services, there is potential especially in information technologies, construction, engineering and architectural services. As an export-oriented country, the Czech Republic supports strengthening economic relations with partners and outside the European Union and welcomes the ambitious agenda in the area of the common commercial policy to contribute to our competitiveness. Therefore, I would like to ask you to recommend to the Chamber of Deputies of the Parliament of the Czech Republic to give consent to the ratification of the said Protocol. Thank you for your attention.
