ParlaMint-CZ_2017-09-13-ps2013-060-06-009-053.u2	Thank you, Mr President. Dear Members, Material Government decision on flights and transits of armed forces of other states through the territory of the Czech Republic in 2017, with which the Government of the Czech Republic agreed by its resolution of 24 October 2016. The subject of the material is basic information about the standard approval process during flights and transits of the armed forces of other states through our territory. By its resolution, the Government authorised the crossings and transits of the armed forces of the European Union States which ratified the Treaty of European Defence Agency. In addition, it has authorised flights and transits to the armed forces of the Member States of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and States participating in the Partnership for Peace programme and, last but not least, the countries with which the Czech Republic maintains partnership relations in the calendar year 2017. It will also be possible to allow the crossing for the armed forces of others, in the material of unnamed states, but only provided the consent of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The Czech Republic will be seen as a reliable and trustworthy partner in this area with our allies by the approved procedure. Thank you for your attention.
