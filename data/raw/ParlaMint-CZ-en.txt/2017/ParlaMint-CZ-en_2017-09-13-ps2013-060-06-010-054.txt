I'm presenting material that's been called, so I'm not going to repeat it. What does the material contain? It contains an overview of planned military exercises with foreign partners this year, it is 113 exercises of armed forces of the Czech Republic outside the territory and 70 exercises of armed forces of other states in the Czech Republic. On the basis of political-military priorities, the training plan is effectively drawn up in four areas. They are: preparation for deployment in operations, emergency forces, bilateral and regional cooperation and capacity building. The priority goal of the exercise with foreign partners in 2017 is to examine and increase the level of readiness of the armed forces of the Czech Republic to defend it in cooperation with allies. The exercise also serves as a preparation for the joint operation of troops in operations and emergency forces of international organizations. The expected costs of securing the participation of the Army of the Czech Republic in these international exercises are about 133 million crowns. The material was approved by the Government of the Czech Republic on 19 December 2016. I would therefore like to ask you to take note of this material, colleagues and colleagues. Thank you.
