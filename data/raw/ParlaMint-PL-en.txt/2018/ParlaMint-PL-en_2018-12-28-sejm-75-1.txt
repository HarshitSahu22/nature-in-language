The main element is the scheme for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading established within the European Union by the October 2003 Directive. It imposes an obligation to purchase CO2 allowances through combustion plants, all installations. The price of emission allowances in 2017 oscillated at EUR 5 and EUR 6 per tonne. In 2018, the price of allowances increased rapidly and exceeded EUR 25 per tonne in September 2018, i.e. a 400 per cent increase in the price of allowances. This price fell for several weeks, and it again exceeded 25 euros before Christmas. The increase from EUR 5 to EUR 25 results from an increase in the cost of energy generation of about PLN 80/MWh. In most European Union countries the price of electricity for consumers has increased in recent years, while in Poland this price has decreased. According to the European Commission in the first half of 2018, the price of electricity for electricity consumers in Poland was one of the lowest in Europe. There are countries, for example. In the draft law we decided to propose a programme consisting of four interventions that will stabilise electricity prices in 2019 and to keep a promise that our citizens do not have to fear an increase in electricity bills, because such an increase thanks to the law that we are proceeding today will not be. We are therefore dealing with a draft law of the highest social and economic importance. Now I would like to discuss these proposed four interventions aimed at maintaining the electricity price in 2019 at the 2018 level. The first intervention to stabilise electricity prices in 2019 is the reduction of excise duty on energy from 20 PLN/MWh to 5 PLN/MWh. The consumer's electricity accounts will be reduced by PLN 1850 million in 2019. The second action is to reduce the transitional fee for all electricity consumers by 95%. Thanks to this change electricity bills in Poland will decrease by PLN 2240 million. The third amendment introduced by the Act is the direct reimbursement of lost income to trading companies. These measures will be addressed to the national green investment scheme. In the proposed draft law, the reference date in which electricity prices for consumers were not yet on the growth path is 30 June 2018.
