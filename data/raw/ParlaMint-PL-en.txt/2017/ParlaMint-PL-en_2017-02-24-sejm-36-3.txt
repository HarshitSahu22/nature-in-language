According to the case-law and the instructions of the Ministry of Finance, the current provisions should be interpreted in such a way that the calculation of the amount of the tax should be carried out by determining the amount of the tax liability on the whole property and then deduction from that amount for the co-owners exempt from tax. However, as is often the case in life, theoretical proposals differ greatly from the practice of tax authorities. The proposed draft calls for a statutory rule whereby the principles of joint and several liability for a tax liability in respect of agricultural tax or property tax will not apply where one or more co-owners are exempt from payment of one or more of those taxes. In such a situation, the tax obligation will be jointly and severally imposed on joint owners not exempt from the obligation to pay the tax, and the amount of that obligation will correspond to the total share of those joint owners in the property right.
