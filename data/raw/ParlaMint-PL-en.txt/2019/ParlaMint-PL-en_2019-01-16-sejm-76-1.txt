The designer has decided that this project should regulate the groups of foreigners covered by the mandatory implementation of Directive 2016/801, i.e. students, researchers, trainees and volunteers participating in the European Voluntary Service programme. The proposed amendments provide for the introduction of provisions that will govern the approval authority of host bodies within the meaning of Directive 2016/801. The implementation of this Directive requires the addition of new objectives for which visas are issued, i.e. taking an internship and participating in the European Voluntary Service programme, as well as amending the provisions governing the granting of temporary residence permits for the purpose of studying, for the purpose of conducting research, for family members of citizens of the Republic of Poland or foreigners and temporary residence permits due to other circumstances. The proposed changes are also designed to organise regulations on the use by foreigners of different types of mobility in our country, for which it is not always necessary to obtain a temporary residence permit. It is worth noting that new information obligations are imposed on the Head of the Office for Foreigners with regard to the Chief Border Guard and the Head of the Internal Security Agency, as well as on other Member States related to the use of different types of mobility in Poland. The Head of the Office for Foreigners will be equipped, on the basis of this regulation today, with a new competence to issue decisions against these forms of mobility. High Izbo! The draft law also provides for legal solutions aimed at eliminating the so-called. Tourist tourism, which has been recognised by our government, by the authorities of the state in 2017.
