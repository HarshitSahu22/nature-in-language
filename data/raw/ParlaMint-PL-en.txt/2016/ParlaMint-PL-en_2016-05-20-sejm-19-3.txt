May 12th this year We celebrated the 68th anniversary of the death of Stanisław Kasznica, ps. Stanisław Wąsacz, Wąsowski, Przepona, Sługa, Maszkowski, Borowski, Stanisław Piotrowski, Polish military commander, lieutenant-in-command of the Polish Army, lieutenant colonel and last chief of the National Armed Forces. Stanisław Kasznica was born on July 25, 1908 in Lviv. He was the son of Stanisław Wincenty Antoni Kasznica, professor, twice rector of the University of Poznań and Senator of the Second Polish Republic. At the age of 11, Kasznica moved with his family to Warsaw, where he started his studies at Wojciech Górski's Junior High School. In 1920 he moved to Poznań, where he continued his studies at the Junior High School. Charles Marcinkowski. At the age of 19, he graduated from the University of Poznań and graduated from law school. In the meantime, he served as a military officer and graduated from the Artillery Sick School. As a master of law, he took legal counsel in Gniezno, and before the outbreak of the war he took the bar exam. Kasznica started his political activity in the Greater Poland Camp. From 1934 he worked in the National and Radical Camp. As a lieutenant, he took part in the September campaign. He served as a reconnaissance officer and commander of the I Platoon 2 battery in 7. Horse Artillery Division. In General Tadeusz Kutraby's army, he fought against Bzura and defended the capital. He was awarded the Silver Cross of the Order of the Virtuti Military War for these wars. After the defeat of the war, he acted in conspiracy, becoming one of the leading activists of the so-called. The Leaning Groups and co-founder of the Lizard Union. In July 1943, he was a member of the Provisional Bureau of the National Political Council and at the same time served as Head of the General Administration of the Civil Service of the Nation. From August 1944 he was a member of the Political Council of the NSZ, head of the organizational branch of the NSZ. In September he became the commander of the VII Częstochowa NSZ district. As the commander of the Western Land Province, he created three conspiracy organizations: the Polish Army (of an intelligence character), the Independent Poland Generation and the Academic Legion. In 1945. Kashnica became a p.o. Chief of NSZ. He also founded the Underground Army, which ran propaganda campaigns and gathered information. In February 1947. The casket was arrested by UB and then tortured. He was sentenced to four death sentences, four prison sentences and other additional punishments, among others. confiscation of property and loss of civil rights. The sentence was executed by shooting. The date of execution has not been notified to the family. She wasn't given the body of the deceased either. The casket was buried in a Wehrmacht uniform in an unmarked place, in a mass grave. The verdict was annulled in 1992 10 years later, Kasznica's remains were found and identified. On 27 September 2015, lieutenant colonel Stanisław Kasznica was solemnly buried in the Pantheon - Mausoleum of Soldiers of the Cursed-Nieczłomnych on the Link at the Military Powązki Military in Warsaw. Kasznica was posthumously decorated with the Great Order of the Rebirth of Poland and the Cross of the National Armed Action. Hello and praise the heroes! Thank you.
