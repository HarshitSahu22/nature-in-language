According to the Constitution, the Senate is one of the two chambers of the Polish Parliament, which forms the National Assembly together with the Sejm. In this role the Senate is called to shape our country as a democratic rule of law, guaranteeing freedom and civil rights, rooted in the Christian heritage of the nation and in human values. The Senate has the right not only to participate fully in the formation of ordinary legislation, but also to initiate a constitutional amendment, to ensure that laws are compatible with the Constitution, to ratify international agreements, to consent to a national referendum and to cast important state offices and to deal with petitions submitted directly by citizens. The Senate shall ensure the rule of law in the State by examining information submitted annually by the Presidents of the Supreme Judicial Authorities and by the Heads of Security Authorities and Civil Rights Guard Authorities.
