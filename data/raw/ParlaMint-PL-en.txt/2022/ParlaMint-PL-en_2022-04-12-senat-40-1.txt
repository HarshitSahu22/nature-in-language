This law provides for the possibility of sharing the funds spent under the civil budget into pools covering all the municipalities and parts thereof in the form of support units or groups of support units. This is important to facilitate especially in those municipalities where support units are not established in the whole area. In addition, flexibility consists in the possibility of creating an additional criterion, namely the category of project quotas, and the allocation of the amount allocated to the civil budget for such quota pools, which may concern all or part of the commune area. This is a request often made by local government units, made by the Parliamentary Committee on Petitions in response to an individual petition.
