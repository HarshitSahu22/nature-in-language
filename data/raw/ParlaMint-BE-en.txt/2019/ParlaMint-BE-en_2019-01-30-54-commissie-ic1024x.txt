ParlaMint-BE_2019-01-30-54-commissie-ic1024x.u3	Mr Buysrogge, we have already had the opportunity to debate this point in plenary, but I will explain it to you again. Derogations from the rule of the three twelfths are requested in accordance with Article 57 of the Law of 22 May 2003 for unavoidable exceptions. These are expenditures that are absolutely necessary to meet legal or contractual obligations over a longer period. The derogations relate, for example, to the payments of 100% of certain legal or contractual obligations of the Foreign Office or to the entry of the total credit provisions in the 2019 budget for the payment to Bpost for the implementation of the management contract. There is indeed an exception for the payment of the civil list. As stated in your question, the amount and method of payment are laid down in Article 2 of the Law of 27 November 2013 establishing the civil list for the duration of the Government of King Filip. Article 2 provides that the civil list shall be paid by trimester and before expiry of the period in accordance with the provisions of the law. This means, in concrete terms, that the payment of the second trimester will be authorised in March. It is therefore necessary to register credit for six months in order to make this possible. This was also the case in the 2008 Finance Act and in the 2011 Finance Act. In reply to your last question, I can inform you that the payment of the second trimester will be made at the end of March 2019 in accordance with the law of 27 November 2013, which means that no payment will be made early, as you state. Finally, it is true that only 25% of the appropriations were entered in the 2013 Finance Act. However, the circumstances are quite different. The draft law on the general expenditure budget for 2013 was distributed in the Chamber on 26 November 2012 and was adopted at the plenary session of 7 February 2013. As a result, the Finance Act lapsed and, as provided for in Article 2 of the Law of 27 November 2013, the disbursement could also take place correctly in the month of March 2013 before the second trimester.
