ParlaMint-BE_2019-03-19-54-commissie-ic1058x.u35	Mr. Chair, these processes must be established at the level of each integrated police entity. In particular, it is the responsibility of the various managers and units responsible for human resources management of these entities to draw up the training plan for police officers in accordance with the needs encountered or foreseeable. The training needs and requests of the entities are relayed to the police school concerned in the light of the Federal Training Plan. The purpose of the programme is to communicate to all schools and police entities the subjects and areas to be given special attention in the development of their plans and the provision of training. At this level, the National Police Academy (ANPA) will give priority to meeting the training needs of members of the Federal Police, while those of local police officers are mainly satisfied by the various accredited police schools located at the provincial level. Each entity, and in particular its human resources management unit, must determine an appropriate system of evaluation of the follow-up of police training. The diversity of police missions, tasks and profiles is far too large for me to indicate, in general, a particular frequency of follow-up to ongoing training. However, it should be stressed that the status of police officer, including the code of ethics, explicitly mentions the law, but also the obligation to ensure that everyone is trained on a regular basis in order to maintain and develop their knowledge and skills in the professional fields in which they are responsible. It may also be noted that the status of police officer provides for a minimum follow-up obligation of 48 hours of continuous or assimilated training for a period of six years. If this minimum is not reached, the police officers concerned are not eligible for promotion as part of their baraemic career. Finally, it is up to the functional superiors responsible for the evaluation of their employees to integrate, where appropriate, the follow-up of certain trainings within the framework of the skills profile or objectives to be achieved.
