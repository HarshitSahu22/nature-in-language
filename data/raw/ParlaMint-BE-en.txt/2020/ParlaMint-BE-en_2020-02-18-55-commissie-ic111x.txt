ParlaMint-BE_2020-02-18-55-commissie-ic111x.u7	Madam President, ladies and gentlemen, my administration is based, among other things, on the information it obtains from various bodies concerned. These are both data based on formal notes as well as data from own databases and/or estimates which are explained orally. The estimates of tax revenue come from the study department of the FPS Finance. In the context of the end-of-year estimate, these are the figures from the note to the Statistical Committee at the end of January 2020. The estimation of new tax revenues is based on the information from FEDCOM, the federal accounting system. The estimates of primary expenditure are also made with the data from the FEDCOM system. The budgetary situation of the public institutions is monitored by my administration via e-BMC. This is an application of the FOD BOSA to collect budgetary and accounting data from the administrative services with accounting autonomy and the administrative public institutions. The estimates relating to the system of employees are based on the data of the Finance and Budget Committee of the RSZ Global Administration of 28 January 2020. The estimates relating to the scheme of selfemployed persons are based on the data compiled by the RSVZ, the Federal Pension Service and the RIZIV in respect of the FOD BOSA. The estimates for health care, which are ultimately balanced, were made in consultation with the Social Security Office. In order to determine the balance of the Communities and the Regions' claims, a survey was carried out at the end of January in each sub-region. It was agreed with the sub-areas that the end-of-year note of my administration only includes totals for the whole of the Communities and Regions, not the estimates of all sub-groups separately. For example, they have the guarantee that they can communicate about their final end-of-year results in due time. For the estimates of the local government balance of claims, figures were taken from the autumn forecasts of December. Then I come to your second question. I am prepared to pass on the note on the end of the year estimate to the members of the committee. With regard to any other available notes and tables on which my administration has based, it is for the Minister concerned to determine whether this information can or may be disclosed. In any case, I would like to stress that the data used for the end-of-year estimate are always estimates that will and may evolve in the coming months. Regarding your third question, the comparison of entity 1's balance of claims between 2018 and 2019 gives a distorted picture. This is due to two factors. For example, in 2018, the calculation of the period 2015-2017 was made following the final determinations of the Autonomy Factors. In view of the systematic overpayment of the Regions during this period, this amount was fully settled in ESA terms in 2018. As a result, the federal government had to spend EUR 1.6 billion less in that year, while the Communities and the Regions had less revenue for the same amount. This partly explains why the deficit at federal government level is increasing so strongly between 2018 and 2019 while the deficit at Community and regional level is falling so sharply. At entity 1 level, there is also a distortion of income due to the increase in tax increase rates in case of insufficient advance payments in both 2017 and 2018. As a result, accelerated revenue was collected through advance payments in these years, which would lead to a decrease in revenue from cashiers in subsequent years. For example, it was assumed that tax revenues in 2018 were around €2.1 billion above the structural level. There was also an expanding effect in 2019. For the time being, the impact is estimated at €0.4 billion above the structural level. As regards your fourth question, as I have just indicated, as regards entity 2, the improvement between 2018 and 2019 is due inter alia to the fact that in 2018 the settlement was made for the period 2015 to 2017 following the final determination of the autonomy factor. As a result, the Communities and the Regions received EUR 1.6 billion less on a one-off basis. As for your final question, since the September 2018 Monitoring Committee meeting, the projections for 2020 have not been updated. That is why, following the publication of the economic budget on 6 February 2020, I instructed the Monitoring Committee to update the budgetary situation. That report is expected in mid-March.
