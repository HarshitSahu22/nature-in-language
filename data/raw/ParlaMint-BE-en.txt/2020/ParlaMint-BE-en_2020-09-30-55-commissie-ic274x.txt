ParlaMint-BE_2020-09-30-55-commissie-ic274x.u6	Madam President, Mr Roggeman, the criteria used by Fedasil mainly relate to the size of the building, because this is linked to the desired minimum reception capacity, the nature and state of the building and the time limit for it to be available. The Government of the Buildings can offer sites from its own real estate portfolio, but can also assist in the prospecting of other sites or the execution of furnishings. A recent internal analysis resulted in a list of four sites that Fedasil prevented from receiving for asylum seekers. These are two sites in Flanders and two in Wallonia. The investigation has not yet been completed, which means that timing cannot yet be established. The arrival of such a centre is sometimes accompanied by local questions and tensions. Fedasil deals with this carefully and only communicates locations after they have been thoroughly investigated and discussed with the local government. Only the centre in Mouskroen is owned by an external partner, in co-management with the Regie der Bouwen. There is no specific budget for the establishment and maintenance of reception facilities. The necessary expenditure on works shall be included in the investment programme or the multi-annual investment plan through the usual procedures and shall be paid with the investment and maintenance of the Regie. This cost is not passed on to Fedasil, no more than to the other customers of the Regie Buildings. As with any federal customer, it is up to Fedasil to determine its needs, to have them validated by the Treasury Inspectorate and then to turn to the Government of the Buildings. The relationship between the Regie and its customers is laid down in an administrative regulation which is binding on both parties. The Regie is working on a new guide to customer relations which must replace the administrative regulations and which is based on an integral housing, whereby the Regie takes away all practical concerns about housing. As soon as a building becomes vacant, an evaluation takes place. Empty buildings are usually placed under vacant protection pending their sale or re-use. As long as a building is not in a start-up sales procedure, it remains available if a customer asks.
