ParlaMint-BE_2022-07-13-voorlopig-55-commissie-ic861x.u10	Thank you for your question, Mr Roggeman. The re-destination of the former prison of Dendermonde has already been discussed, but no decision has yet been taken. No major investments are planned in the short term on the site of the old prison of Dendermonde. In the medium term, the Government of the Buildings and the FPS Justice will jointly examine the site's destination, taking into account the global vision and the various projects and existing problems of detention homes, overpopulation and detention in humane conditions. The Council of Ministers approved the proposal to keep the institution in Dendermonde open for longer in line with the needs and progress of the other projects. I remind you that Dendermonde's old prison is a protected building. It is located in a zone of public utility, the so-called blue zone, which limits the possibilities compared to a residential zone. The building is designed in the form of a star. A large part of the useful area is dedicated to corridors, the circulation zone, and to cells of about 10 square meters, which are too small for alternative solutions. The cells could be combined, but this would involve a significant investment. Finally, the 1875 monument dates back to 1875 and requires heavy investment in order to maintain it sustainably.
