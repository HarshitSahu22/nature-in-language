DECISION adopting procedural rules on the terms and conditions for the offer of candidates, presentation and public disclosure of documents, hearing of candidates and election of members for the judges' and prosecutors' college of the Supreme Judicial Council of the National Assembly quota The National Assembly on the basis of art. 86, para. 1 in relation to art. 130, para. 3 of the Constitution of the Republic of Bulgaria and art. 93, para. 7 of the Rules of Procedure of the National Assembly HAS DECIDED It shall adopt procedural rules on the terms and conditions for the offer of candidates, presentation and public disclosure of documents, hearing of candidates and election of members for the judges' and prosecutors' college of the Supreme Judicial Council of the National Assembly quota. I. Proposal of candidates and submission of candidates' documents for the judges' and prosecutors' college of the Supreme Judicial Council 1. The proposals for candidates for members of the Supreme Judicial Council of the National Assembly's quota shall be made by MPs by colleges, of which six members for the judges, and five for the prosecutors. The proposals shall be submitted in writing through the National Assembly's case-law to the Committee on Legal Affairs within 21 days of the adoption of these rules by the National Assembly. 2. The proposals shall contain written reasons justifying the high professional and moral qualities of the applicant concerned, including expressed opinions from professional, academic and other organisations. The following documents shall apply to the proposals: - written consent of the applicant according to the template - detailed CV; - a copy of documents for completed higher education in the specialty of law; documents related to the requirements for incompatibility; as well as documents for legal internship and career development of the applicant; - a statement according to template "Annex 2" to the decision that the circumstances under art. 18, para. 1, t. 9, 10 and 11 of the Law on the Judiciary; - documents certifying that the applicant meets the requirements for internship under art. 17, para. 1 of the Law on the Judiciary; - evidence of criminality; - Certificates from the National Investigation Service for the existence/lack of data for criminal proceedings initiated and pending. 3. Within 14 days of the publication of the applications, each candidate shall submit to the Committee on Legal Affairs: - written concept of his work as a member of the Supreme Judicial Council on paper and electronic media; - a statement of its property status and the origin of the funds for the acquisition of its property, as well as a statement of the existence of private interest within the meaning of art. 2 of the Law on the Prevention and Establishment of Conflict of Interests according to a model proposed by the Commission and by the plenary of the Supreme Judicial Council and established by the Minister of Justice. II. Public disclosure of documents 1. The proposals, together with the documents referred to in Section I, shall be published within three working days of receipt of a special section on the website of the National Assembly. The name and the reasons of the Member of the National Assembly who proposed the candidate shall be published. 2. The publication of the proposals and documents shall be carried out in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act and the Classified Information Protection Act. 3. Non-profit legal entities registered for public use, higher schools and scientific organisations may submit opinions to the Committee on Legal Affairs on the applicant, including issues to ask him. Opinions and questions shall be sent by post to the following address: gr. Sofia, Pl. "Knyaz Alexander I " No 1, Commission on Legal Affairs, or by e-mail: kpv1@parliament.bg no later than seven days before the hearing of the candidates. Anonymous opinions and alerts shall not be considered. 4. The opinions and questions shall be published within three days of their presentation to the special section on the website of the National Assembly. No specific data constituting classified information shall be published, as well as the facts of the intimate life of persons. III. Verification of admission of applicants to a hearing 1. The Committee on Legal Affairs at a separate hearing prior to the hearing shall verify whether all the necessary documents have been submitted and whether the proposed candidates meet the requirements for taking up the position of a member of the Supreme Judicial Council of the National Assembly quota. 2. Where the Committee on Legal Affairs finds that the applicant has not submitted all the necessary documents, it shall inform him that it may submit them within 5 days of notification. 3. No applicants who have not submitted the documents under Section I or who do not meet the requirements of art. 17, para. 1 of the Law on the Judiciary. 4. Following the verification, the Committee on Legal Affairs shall draw up a list of candidates for the judge's and prosecutor's colleges admitted to a hearing. The list shall be drawn up in alphabetical order on the names of the candidates for the judge's and prosecutor's college and shall be published on the special section on the website of the National Assembly. IV. Hearing of candidates admitted for members for the judges' and prosecutor's college of the Supreme Judicial Council of the National Assembly quota. 1. The hearing shall be held at an open meeting of the Committee on Legal Affairs, which shall be broadcast in real time through the website of the National Assembly. 2. The Committee on Legal Affairs shall only hear the candidates admitted on their order on the list of judges and prosecutors' colleges. 3. An importer of the proposal shall present the applicant at an exhibition within 2 minutes. 4. The applicant is given the opportunity to supplement data from his professional biography and present his concept of work as a member of the Supreme Judicial Council in an exhibition within 10 minutes for each candidate. 5. Members of the National Assembly may ask questions to the applicant, including those of the opinions received from non-profit legal entities registered for public use activities, universities and scientific organisations. The time for questioning is up to 2 minutes. 6. Each candidate shall be given the right to answer after asking a question to him. The answer time for each question is up to 2 minutes. 7. The Committee on Legal Affairs shall submit to the National Assembly a report from the hearing to which a list of candidates shall be attached, alphabetically arranged for their own names, for the Judge and Prosecutor's College involved in the hearing. The report shall also be accompanied by drafts of a decision to elect a member of the Supreme Judicial Council separately for the judge's and prosecutor's college. V. Election of members for the judges' and prosecutor's college of the Supreme Judicial Council of the National Assembly's quota 1. The sitting is broadcast in real time through the website of the National Assembly. By decision of the National Assembly, the candidates admitted may be present at the meeting. 2. The Committee on Legal Affairs shall submit a report to the hearing held. 3. Applicants shall be submitted by an importer of the proposal on their order as defined in the list of candidates for the judge's and prosecutor's college prepared in alphabetical order on their own names in an exhibition within 2 minutes. 4. Discussions shall be held on the submitted applications under the Rules of Procedure for the Organization and Activity of the National Assembly. 5. The vote shall be taken separately for each candidate on the order defined in the candidate list for the judge's and prosecutor's college. 6. The candidate has been elected, who has received the most votes, but not less than two thirds of the votes of the Members of the National Assembly. 7. In the event that more than six candidates from the College of Judges, respectively five candidates from the Prosecution College shall receive the required number of votes according to the requirements of art. 130, para. 3 of the Constitution of the Republic of Bulgaria and of art. 19b of the Law on the Judiciary, among the candidates who received the required but least equal number of votes, a runoff is held and the one who received the most votes is considered elected, but not less than two thirds of the total number of MPs. The decision was adopted by the 44th National Assembly of... 2017 and is stamped with the official seal of the National Assembly. President of the National Assembly.. Dimitar Glauchev.
