No NS-10-147 from 05.05.2017 the CEC has received an application from the candidate for MP Gergana Stefanova Krasteva, saying that it does not wish to be declared MP in the 44th National Assembly from the list of GERB party in the twenty-nineth election district of Haskovo. In accordance with art. 255, para. 1, t. 3 of the Election Code, the consent of the applicant to be registered is an absolute legal prerequisite for its registration by the proposed party, coalition or initiative committee, and after the election has been produced for its appointment as elected MP. Where a candidate for a Member of the National Assembly submits an application to the Central Election Commission that he does not wish to be declared a Member of the National Assembly, it should be interpreted that, in substance, there is a withdrawal of the consent of the applicant given through the application under art. 255, para. 1, t. 3 of the Election Code. The statement addressed to the Central Election Commission containing the unwillingness of the applicant to be declared a Member of the National Assembly prevents the Central Election Commission from doing so due to the lack of the legal prerequisite expressing its consent to registration, respectively its declaration as a Member of the National Assembly. There is essentially a statement of refusal by the applicant to be declared elected, which binds the Central Election Commission and makes it impossible to take a decision under art. 302, para. 1 of the Election Code against this candidate. It follows that on the basis of the application submitted by the applicant for MP, Gergana Stefanova Krasteva should be removed from the list of GERB party due to withdrawal of the consent to registration as an absolute legal prerequisite for its electiveness. On the basis of art. 57, para. 1, t. 1 in relation to art. 255, para. 1, t. Art. 3 and Art. 302, para. 1 of the Election Code Central Election Commission HAS DECIDED He deleted from the list of the party GERB the candidate for MP Gergana Stefanova Krasteva (with the corresponding NGN) from the twenty-nineth electoral district of Haskovo, in the elections for MPs produced on 26 March 2017.
