The bill proposes a comprehensive regulation of the requirements for outsourcing, not allowing this in relation to the management of the investment of pension funds and the carrying out of key functions, given their fundamental importance for the company's and the funds' activities and the high requirements for them. In addition, in accordance with the requirements of the Directive, changes in the regime of investment of funds under professional schemes have been proposed. Basic requirements are introduced to take into account the long-term interests of insured persons and pensioners, as well as the possibility of taking into account environmental, social and management factors in investing. In addition, it provides for the possibility of investing funds in shares and bonds traded in a multilateral trading system and an organised trading system, with a quantitative limit on exposure to instruments that are only accepted at those trading venues in order to manage liquidity risk. The Bill regulates the transfer of a professional scheme or part of it to one another from an additional voluntary pension fund under professional schemes, as well as from and to foreign institutions for occupational retirement provision. The transfer of a professional scheme to a part of it shall be carried out after prior approval has been granted by the competent supervisory authority in accordance with the provisions of the Directive and in order to protect the rights and interests of insured persons and pensioners. Changes in terms of uniformity of the service regime by institutions for occupational retirement provision in the country and other Member States of the European Union have also been proposed. The supervisory review process is regulated in accordance with the requirements of the Directive. At the legal level, the basic requirement for carrying out risk-based supervision is laid down and the carrying out of stress tests is introduced which contribute to the optimal distribution of supervisory resources and timely identification of potential problems in the supplementary pension insurance sector. Compliance with the requirements laid down by the Bill will be controlled by the Financial Supervision Commission, whose powers have been developed with it. The system of control activities shall be precised. It is proposed that the order and manner of carrying out ex ante controls on legality be determined by the heads of organisations on the basis of risk assessment and cost-benefit analysis. A definition of pre-control of legality shall be introduced which includes possible entities carrying out this type of control. It is proposed to carry out follow-up implementation assessments as compulsory control activities aimed at detecting possible errors and irregularities of a completed process, contract or business operation. It is envisaged that the heads of organisations will ensure the implementation and implementation of ongoing control procedures over the implementation of financial commitments and concluded contracts, which are important for their proper implementation, especially in the case of commitments with a longer duration of implementation. The monitoring of financial management and control is provided for in a timely manner to provide information on identified weaknesses and deficiencies to the persons responsible for taking corrective action. It is proposed to extend the scope of public sector organisations where systematic monitoring is carried out by the Minister of Finance on the application of the Law and the acts issued on the basis of it. The Final Provisions of the Draft Law amending and supplementing the Financial Management and Control Act propose amendments to the Internal Audit Act in the public sector: The provision for the establishment of audit committees in the organisations in which an internal audit unit is established shall be clarified, supplementing that it does not apply to undertakings of public interest within the meaning of the Accounting Act in order to avoid collision with the relevant provisions of the Independent Financial Audit Act. The functions of the Audit Committee, which should be examined and adopted by decision and the status report of the financial management and control systems, shall be completed. The scope of public sector organisations, where systematic monitoring by the Minister of Finance on the implementation of the Public Sector Internal Audit Act and the acts issued on its basis, shall be extended. The proposed draft aims at optimising internal control and increasing efficiency in spending public funds in public sector organisations in line with good practices in this area of other Member States, and there is no need to provide additional financial and other funds.
