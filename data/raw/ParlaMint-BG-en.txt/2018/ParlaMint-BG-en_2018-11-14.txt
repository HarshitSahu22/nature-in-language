ParlaMint-BG_2018-11-14.u14	Madam President, honourable Members! The proposed texts aim at introducing a new administrative approach to environmental procedures. The possibility of a united procedure for an investment proposal subject to a mandatory environmental impact assessment is provided for a complex permit or safety report for undertakings with high risk potential. At present, each of the procedures takes approximately six months, and with discussions and coordinations during the three procedures can exceed two years, which is sometimes, of course, detrimental to investment initiatives. If the current change is adopted and a united procedure is proposed, the deadline will be significantly reduced by two to three times. The public discussion will be just one thing, the number of required documents will be reduced markedly, the process of interaction between administrations will be optimized, and the documents will be required by official means. My fellow Members, I will allow myself to make a few announcements in advance of the upcoming texts, so that I do not take a speech later, as all these texts are bound by reducing the administrative burden. I would also like to say that optimizing the process of public access to information on investment proposals is also envisaged and the competent authority announces the proposals on its website and informs the mayor in writing of the municipality concerned, who in turn announces the investment proposal on its website or at a publicly available place. At present, this is done by contracting entities and is a huge administrative and resource burden. The time limits for the decision of the administrative authority in the issuing and updating of complex permits shall also be reduced as well as the time limit for determining the applicable procedure in the case of planned changes in the operation of installations with a complex permit issued. A specific procedure for complex permits from the Ministry of Environment and Water to the Executive Environment Agency is envisaged. At present, the competent authority to issue, update and revoke complex permits shall be the Executive Director of the Executive Agency and, in the case of planned change by operators, the Minister of Environment shall provide guidance on the type of procedures, the issue of complex authorisation or updating. The principle of one-stop shop service and the introduction of a competent authority with regard to the procedures under the complex permit shall be introduced by transferring powers from the Ministry of Environment to the Executive Agency. Full compliance of the provisions with the Environmental Protection Act is achieved with Directive 2010/75/EC on industrial emissions in implementation of European Commission recommendations. In conclusion, I would say that the measures listed are in response to the government's programme to reduce administrative burdens and a major step towards simplification of procedures. The business climate will improve and better conditions will be created for investors for both external and local. Thank you for your attention. [[Applause from GERB.]]
