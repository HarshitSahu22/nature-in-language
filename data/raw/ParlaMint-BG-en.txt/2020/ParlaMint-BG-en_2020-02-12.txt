The other three shall be a service for transactions for the award of production or reproduction of copyrights on optical discs and other media; for transactions for the acquisition of reproduction and distribution rights for audiovisual works; for each import and export of matrices, optical discs and other media containing copyrights. These modes also include additional administrative services for recording changes in the register, disinfecting certificates, as well as collecting and storing control specimens from the manufactured optical discs. In the years with active market in optical media, the Law on the Administrative Regulation of Production and Trade in Optical Discs, Matrixs and other media played a positive role. However, at the moment, the purpose of the regimes and the objectives with which they are created are obsolete and create unnecessary administrative burdens and their maintenance unduly diverts administrative capacity. The activity statistics under the Law in the period 2014 - 2018 show a total constant decline on all indicators of administrative activities, which also reflects the state and trends not only of the Bulgarian but also the world market in demand for optical discs. This situation justifies the withdrawal of the regulatory regimes administered by the Ministry of Culture and requires a change in the current Law. The withdrawal of the regime by the Ministry of Culture was discussed at a meeting of the Council for Intellectual Property Protection on 25 June 2019 and the idea received the full support of the members of the Council, the Bulgarian Association of Music Producers and the representatives of the two legal entities licensed by the Ministry of Economy for the production of optical discs. With regard to the documents collected and created in the Ministry of Culture in accordance with the opinion of the State Agency "Archives" the draft proposes to keep them in the Ministry within 5 years of the entry into force of the Law. No changes are envisaged in the regimes applied by the Ministry of Economy under the order of Chapter 3 of the Law. The bill amending the Law on administrative regulation of production and trade in optical discs, matrices and other media does not lead to an impact on the state budget and does not give rise to the need to approve additional costs, transfers and/or other payments under the budget of the Ministry of Culture. No European legislation is introduced and no rules with which it has been introduced are amended.
