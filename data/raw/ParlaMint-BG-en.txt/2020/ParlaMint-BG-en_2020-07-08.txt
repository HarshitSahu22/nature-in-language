DECISION adopting Rules of Procedure on the terms and conditions for proposing a candidate for deputy ombudsman, the public disclosure of documents, the presentation and hearing of the candidate for deputy ombudsman in the Committee on Religions and Human Rights, and the procedure for election by the National Assembly The National Assembly on the basis of art. 86, para. 1 of the Constitution of the Republic of Bulgaria in relation to art. 11, para. 1 of the Law on the Ombudsman and Article 1 of the Law on the Ombudsman. 93, para. 7 of the Rules of Procedure of the National Assembly HAS DECIDED It shall adopt Rules of Procedure on the terms and conditions for proposing a candidate for deputy ombudsman, the public disclosure of documents, the presentation and hearing of the candidate for deputy ombudsman in the Committee on Religions and Human Rights, as well as the procedure for election by the National Assembly. I. Offering a candidate for deputy ombudsman and submitting his documents 1. The proposal for a candidate for deputy ombudsman shall be made by the Ombudsman on the basis of art. 11, para. 1 of the Law on the Ombudsman. The proposal shall be submitted in writing to the Committee on Religions and Human Rights through the President of the National Assembly. 2. The proposal contains motives to justify the applicant's high moral qualities and human rights experience. The proposal shall be accompanied by: (a) written consent of the applicant according to the template of Annex 1 to the decision; (b) a detailed CV; (c) certified by the applicant a copy of a diploma for completed higher education; (d) a statement in accordance with the template "Annex 2" to the decision that the applicant is eligible for the election of a Member of the National Assembly under art. 65, para. 1 of the Constitution of the Republic of Bulgaria; (e) documents demonstrating that the applicant has experience in the field of human rights; (f) written consent of the applicant under art. 27, para. 2 of the Law on Access and Disclosure of Documents and for the declaration of belonging of Bulgarian citizens to the State Security and the intelligence services of the Bulgarian People's Army according to a model, in two copies of Annex 3 to the Decision. 3. The circumstances of art. 15, para. 1, t. 3 of the Law on the Ombudsman is established by the administration of the National Assembly. II. Public disclosure of documents 1. The proposal together with the documents attached to it shall be published on the specialized thematic website of the National Assembly no later than 7 days before the hearing. The publication of the proposal and documents shall be carried out subject to the Personal Data Protection Act. 2. Non-profit legal entities registered for carrying out a public utility activity no later than three days before the hearing of the applicant may submit opinions on the applicant to the Committee on Religions and Human Rights, including questions to be put to the applicant. 3. The mass media may send questions to the Committee on Religions and Human Rights to the candidate to be referred to him. 4. Anonymous opinions and alerts shall not be considered. 5. The opinions and questions are sent by post to the city of Sofia, Bulgaria. The opinions and questions are published on the specialized thematic website on the website of the National Assembly, subject to the Personal Data Protection Act and the Classified Information Protection Act. III. Perform a preliminary check under the Law on Access and Disclosure of Documents and for the declaration of belonging of Bulgarian citizens to State Security and the intelligence services of the Bulgarian People's Army 1. The applicant shall be checked for belonging to the bodies referred to in Article 1. 1 of the Law on Access and Disclosure of Documents and the declaration of belonging of Bulgarian citizens to State Security and the intelligence services of the Bulgarian People's Army. The request for prior verification under art. 27, para. 1, t. 2 of the Law on Access and Disclosure of Documents and the declaration of belonging of Bulgarian citizens to State Security and the intelligence services of the Bulgarian People's Army are made by the President of the Commission on Religions and Human Rights. 2. The results of the verification shall be published on the specialized thematic site on the website of the National Assembly. IV. Hearing of the candidate for deputy ombudsman 1. Before hearing the proposed candidate, the Committee on Religions and Human Rights shall verify the documents submitted and whether the applicant complies with the requirements for taking up the post. 2. The hearing is held in an open meeting of the Committee on Religions and Human Rights, which is broadcast in real time on the Internet via the website of the National Assembly. 3. The candidate shall be represented by the Ombudsman at an exhibition within 5 minutes. The presentation also includes information on motivation, public reputation, moral qualities and public support for the candidate. 4. The applicant is given the opportunity to supplement data from his professional biography and present his concept of work as a deputy ombudsman in an exhibition within 7 minutes. 5. The President of the Committee on Religions and Human Rights shall present in summary the opinions and questions received under Section II, i.e. 2 and 3 up to 2 minutes each. 6. Members of the National Assembly shall address their questions to the applicant within 2 minutes of a representative of the National Assembly. 7. A full stenographic protocol is drawn up from the hearing, which is published on the specialized thematic website on the website of the National Assembly. 9. The Committee on Religions and Human Rights shall draw up within the meaning of art. 93, para. 4 of the Rules of Procedure of the National Assembly a report from the hearing of the candidate for Deputy Ombudsman and submit it to the National Assembly. The report shall also be accompanied by a draft decision on the election of the candidate for deputy ombudsman. V. Election of Deputy Ombudsman by the National Assembly 1. The sitting is broadcast in real time on the Internet via the website of the National Assembly. The proposed candidate is present at the meeting with a decision of the National Assembly. 2. The National Assembly shall hear the submitted report of the Committee on Religions and Human Rights in summary. 3. The candidate for deputy ombudsman shall be represented by the Ombudsman within 5 minutes. 4. Discussions shall be held on the application under the Rules of Procedure for the Organization and Activity of the National Assembly. 5. The vote is evident through the computerised voting system. 6. The candidate shall be deemed elected if he has received more than half of the votes of the present Members of the National Assembly. 7. If the candidate for deputy ombudsman does not receive the necessary votes, the National Assembly shall adopt a decision to open a new selection procedure. VI.
