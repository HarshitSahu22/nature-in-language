Granting the status of a stateless person in Republic of Bulgaria Art. 21b. (1) The status of a stateless person may be granted to a foreigner who is not regarded as a citizen of any country in accordance with its legislation. (2) The procedure for granting the status of a stateless person shall be laid down by this law and by its implementing rules. Art. 21c. (1) The proceedings for granting the status of a stateless person shall begin with a written application submitted in person to the Directorate of Migration or to the sector/group of "Migration" at the Sofia Directorate of Interior and the Regional Directorates of the Ministry of Interior. (2) The application of a minor shall be submitted by his parents or guardians. The application shall be submitted only by one parent when the other parent is deprived of parental rights. (3) The application of a minor shall be submitted in the presence of his parents or trustees who express their consent by signing the application. The consent can only be expressed by one parent when the other one is deprived of parental rights. (4) In the absence of a parent, guardian or guardian, the application shall be submitted in accordance with the rules of application of the law. (5) The application of unaccompanied minors may also be submitted by a representative of a non-governmental organisation acting to protect the rights of vulnerable groups or by another person designated as his representative under a law. (6) When submitting the application, the applicant shall be informed of his rights and obligations relating to the proceedings under this Chapter and of the consequences of non-compliance with his obligations. (7) In the course of the proceedings, the applicant shall be obliged to cooperate by stating his or her situation in good faith and providing any evidence relevant to the examination of his or her application at his or her disposal or for which he or she has reason to believe that he or she is present.
