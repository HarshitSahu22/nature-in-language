The draft Agreement for the provision of consulting services between the Ministry of Environment and Water of the Republic of Bulgaria and the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development was approved by Protocol No 39 of a meeting of the Council of Ministers on 20 September 2016. The Minister of Environment and Water is authorised to negotiate and sign it on condition of subsequent ratification. The Agreement was signed on 28 September 2016. The Republic of Bulgaria, as a party to the Kyoto Protocol, is obliged to develop a National adaptation strategy which, as a key action plan against the impact of climate change, must respond to our country's vulnerability to the effects of climate change. The obligation to draw up such a strategy also arises from the Climate Change Restriction Act. The first stage of the preparation of the National adaptation strategy was completed in 2014 with the development of a framework document "Analysis and assessment of the risk and vulnerability of the sectors in the Bulgarian economy of climate change." The second phase of the strategy must build on the data collected and evaluated and through macroeconomic analysis and formulation of concrete adaptation measures to shape strategic actions to reduce our country's vulnerability to the effects of the changing climate. The strategy will define specific measures by sector, the deadline for their implementation, the necessary resources and the responsible institutions. The development of the National Strategy and Action Plan is provided for in the budget of Priority Axis 2 The Agreement was concluded in implementation of the Memorandum of Understanding between the Government of the Republic of Bulgaria and the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development for Partnership and Using the European Structural and Investment Funds for 2014-2020. The Agreement provides for the following main implementation activities by the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development: Macroeconomic analysis of the effects of climate change with an assessment of the social and economic consequences, as well as specific adaptation measures in line with gross domestic product, income, sectoral production and level of employment; Sectoral assessments of agriculture, forestry, biodiversity and ecosystems, water sector, energy, transport, infrastructure and construction, urban environment, human health and tourism. This analysis must identify the most important risks of climate change and the development prospects of each sector by 2030; develop a communication plan with stakeholders, including local authorities and the public. The coordination on climate change issues will be carried out by the National Council of Experts on Climate Change and the Coordination Council on Climate Change at the Ministry of Environment and Water. The final draft of the National adaptation strategy is planned to be drawn up by the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development in November 2017. The environmental assessment and compatibility assessment procedures for protected areas will be carried out after the preparation of the project, respectively under the Environmental Protection Act and the Biodiversity Act. On the basis of art. 85, para. 1, t. 4, 5 and 7 of the Constitution of the Republic of Bulgaria, by a decision of the Council of Ministers it is proposed that the agreement be ratified by a law of the National Assembly. After the opening of the debates, the people's representative Borislav Velikov took the attitude about the terminology used, the people's representative Stanislav Ivanov and the people's representative Ivan Stankov on the adaptation strategy received responses from Mrs. Nikolova. On the basis of the discussion and opinions held, the Committee on Environment and Water, with 14 votes in favour, without the opposition and the abstentions, proposed to the National Assembly to adopt, on the first vote, a Draft for ratification of the Agreement on Consulting Services between the Ministry of Environment and Water of the Republic of Bulgaria and the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (Project No P160511), No 602-02-42, submitted by the Council of Ministers on 21 November 2016.
