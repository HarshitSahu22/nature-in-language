Food banking Authorisation for a food bank operator Art. 37 meters. (1) Food banking under this law may only be carried out by persons authorised as a food bank operator. (3) Food banking activities shall be: 1. gratuitous receipt of food from producers and food traders; 2. storage, storage and/or cutting and packaging of donated foods; 3. free of charge provision of donated food to needy persons subject to the requirements for the control and safety of food under this law. (4) The list of foods subject to food banking shall be validated by order of the Minister of Agriculture and Food, in consultation with the Minister of Finance, on a reasoned proposal from the Executive Director of the Bulgarian Food Safety Agency. (5) The list under para. 4 contains information on the time limits from and up to which food may be donated before the expiry date or durability of the food concerned. (6) The operators of food banks authorised under this Law shall ensure and control compliance with the regulatory requirements for food obtained free of charge. (7) The Bulgarian Food Safety Agency shall control food bank operators with regard to the safety of food received and donated. (8) The Minister of Labour and Social Policy shall establish by order a list of groups of persons in need and of persons providing social services to whom food bank operators may provide donated food. The order and list shall be published on the website of the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy. (9) Foods provided free of charge to a food bank operator shall be marked by producers or food vendors marked with a gift shall not be marketed. The authorisation of a food bank operator may be granted to a person who meets both of the following conditions: 1. is a non-profit legal entity designated for carrying out a socially useful activity and entered in the Central Register with the Ministry of Justice under art. 45, para. 1 of the Non-profit Legal Entities Act; 2. there are no public obligations to be enforced; 3. has its own or leased premises and/or areas in establishments for carrying out food banking activities which meet the hygiene and/or animal health requirements laid down by a regulatory act and are registered under art. 12; 4.
