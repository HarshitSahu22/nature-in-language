DECISION adopting Rules of Procedure on the terms and conditions for the offer of candidates, presentation and public disclosure of documents, hearing of candidates and election of the President of the Commission for Countering Corruption and for the withdrawal of illegally acquired property. The National Assembly on the basis of art. 86, para. 1 of the Constitution of the Republic of Bulgaria, art. 89, para. 8 sentence 2 of the Rules on Organization and Activity of the National Assembly in relation to art. 11, para. Art. 5 and art. 9 of the Law on Countering Corruption and Acquirement of Illegally Acquired Property HAS DECIDED It shall adopt procedural rules on the terms and conditions for the offer of candidates, presentation and public disclosure of documents, hearing of candidates and election of the President of the Commission for Countering Corruption and for the withdrawal of illegally acquired property. I. Offering candidates and submitting documents to candidates for President of the Commission for Countering Corruption and for the withdrawal of illegally acquired property. 1. Proposals for candidates for President of the Commission for countering corruption and the withdrawal of illegally acquired property shall be made by Members of the National Assembly. The proposals shall be submitted in writing through the National Assembly's case-law to the Committee on Prevention and Counteraction of Corruption within 14 days of the adoption of these rules by the National Assembly. 2. The proposals shall contain detailed written reasons justifying the high professional and moral qualities of the applicants. The following documents shall apply to the proposals: - the written consent of the applicant to be proposed for the position in the template... Annex 1 to the decision; - written consent of the applicant under art. 27, para. 2 of the Act for Access and Disclosure of Documents and for the declaration of belonging of Bulgarian citizens to the State Security and the intelligence services of the Bulgarian People's Army according to the model Annex No 2 to the Decision; - detailed professional CV; - a copy of documents for completed higher legal education, as well as a certificate of legal capacity; - evidence of criminality; - documents certifying that the applicant meets the requirements for internship under art. 8, para. 2 of the Law on Countering Corruption and the Withdrawal of Illegally Acquired Property; 3. Within 7 days of the publication of the applications, each candidate shall submit to the Committee on Prevention and Counteraction of Corruption: - a written concept of his work as Chairman of the Commission for Countering Corruption and for the withdrawal of illegally acquired property in paper and electronic media; - declaration of incompatibility according to template - a statement of assets and interests on a template approved by the Commission (the Commission for Countering Corruption and for the withdrawal of illegally acquired property) - Annex No 4. II. Public disclosure of documents. 1. The proposals, annexes to them and the name and reasons of the applicant shall be published within two working days of receipt of them on the website of the National Assembly and the documents referred to in Section I, item I. Within three working days of the expiry of the deadline for their submission. 2. The publication of the proposals and documents shall be carried out in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act and the Classified Information Protection Act. 3. Non-profit legal entities registered for public use, higher schools and scientific organisations may present to the Commission for Prevention and Counteraction of Corruption opinions on the candidate, including issues to be put to him. Opinions and questions shall be sent by post to: Sofia, Piazza Knyaz Aleksandar Isandar No 1, the Commission on Prevention and Counteraction of Corruption, or by e-mail: 47_kppk@parliament.bg, no later than seven days before the hearing of the candidates. Anonymous opinions and alerts shall not be considered. 4. Opinions and questions shall be published within three days of their receipt on the website of the National Assembly, subject to the requirements of the Personal Data Protection Act. III. Perform a preliminary check under the Law on Access and Disclosure of Documents and for the declaration of belonging of Bulgarian citizens to State Security and the intelligence services of the Bulgarian People's Army. 1. Candidates shall be checked for belonging to the bodies referred to in art. 1 of the Law on Access and Disclosure of Documents and the declaration of belonging of Bulgarian citizens to State Security and the intelligence services of the Bulgarian People's Army. The request for prior verification under art. 27, para. 1, t. 2 of the Law on Access and Disclosure of Documents and the declaration of belonging of Bulgarian citizens to State Security and the intelligence services of the Bulgarian People's Army are made by the President of the Commission on Prevention and Counteraction of Corruption. IV. Hearing of the candidates admitted for President of the Commission for Countering Corruption and for the withdrawal of illegally acquired property. 1. The hearing shall be held at an open meeting of the Commission for Prevention and Counteraction of Corruption no later than 7 days before the election. The hearing is broadcast in real time through the website of the National Assembly. 2. An importer of the proposal shall present the applicant at an exhibition within 2 minutes. 3. The applicant is given the opportunity to supplement data from his professional biography and present his concept of work as Chairman of the Commission for Countering Corruption and the withdrawal of illegally acquired property in an exhibition within 15 minutes for each candidate. 4. Members of the National Assembly, members of the Commission, may ask questions to the applicant, including those of the opinions received from non-profit legal entities registered for public use activities, universities and scientific organisations. The time for questioning is up to 2 minutes. 5. Each candidate shall be given the right to answer after asking a question to him. The answer time for each question is up to 2 minutes. The Member of the National Assembly shall be entitled to a reply, as is the right of the Member of the National Assembly. 6. A full stenographic protocol shall be drawn up from the hearing, which shall be published on the website of the National Assembly. 7. The Committee on Prevention and Counteraction of Corruption shall draw up a detailed and motivated report on the professional and moral qualities of the candidates offering the candidates for discussion and voting in the National Assembly. The report shall contain an opinion on the submission of the applicant drawn up following its hearing to the Commission and a conclusion on: 1. the minimum legal requirements for taking up the post; 2. the existence of data which question the moral qualities of the applicant, his qualifications, experience and professional qualities; 3. the specific preparation, qualities and motivation for the respective position. IV. Election of the President of the Commission for Countering Corruption and for the withdrawal of illegally acquired property. 1. The sitting is broadcast in real time through the website of the National Assembly. By decision of the National Assembly, the candidates admitted may be present at the meeting. 2. The Committee on Prevention and Counteraction of Corruption shall present a report on the hearing held. 3. Candidates shall be submitted by an importer of the proposal on their order as defined in the list of candidates for President of the Commission for Countering Corruption and for the withdrawal of illegally acquired property drawn up in alphabetical order of their own names in an exhibition within 2 minutes. 4. Discussions shall be held on the submitted applications under the Rules of Procedure for the Organization and Activity of the National Assembly. 5. The vote shall be taken separately for each candidate on the order defined in the candidate list. 6. The candidate who received more than half of the votes of the present Members of the National Assembly shall be considered elected. If several candidates have received more than half of the votes of the present Members of the National Assembly, the candidate who received the most votes is elected. 7. Where none of the candidates receives the necessary votes, a re-vote shall be held in which the two candidates who have received the most votes for................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................ 8. If none of the candidates receives the necessary votes in the second vote, the National Assembly shall adopt a decision to open a new selection procedure. The decision was adopted by the Forty-Seventh National Assembly and was sealed with the official seal of the National Assembly.
