ParlaMint-NO_2005-11-22.ana.ud64e84	The budget for 2005 will be examined by the European Parliament today in the area of the Ministry of Fisheries and Coastal Affairs. The Committee on Transport examined a document no. 8-proposal to initiate immediate action to remove 65-70 tonnes of mercury from the German submarine "U-864", which was lowered by a British submarine in the Norwegian territorial sea west of Fedje on 9 February 1945. The wreck was found by the Marine Defense in 2003, and in St.meld. No 14 for 2004–2005, On the safe side — maritime safety and oil protection, it was informed that the wreck could represent a risk of point emissions of 65–70 tonnes of mercury. The Norwegian Institute of Water Research, on behalf of the Coastal Directorate, has carried out an assessment of the potential risk to the environment of the mercury contained in the wreck. In the light of the report and the consultations with, inter alia: The Marine Research Institute, the Directorate of Fisheries and the NIVA have been shown to have already leaked out some of the mercury contained in the submarine, but it is not yet possible to say how much. In view of this, as well as the risk of further emissions, the Government has chosen to take concrete action immediately to eliminate the risk of contamination in the event of the wreckage. The coastal works have therefore launched a project to identify ways to eliminate the risk of pollution. The Committee points out that the Government will be asked to come back and properly inform the Storting about the progress of the case. Otherwise, the Committee has no comments and refers to the documents relating to the matter.
