ParlaMint-NO_2005-02-18-lower.ana.ud1944e42	An overall committee is at the back of the law on the amendment to the Law of 9 June 1978 No. 50 on cultural memories. This proposal is a follow-up to the Norwegian ratification of the 1970 UNESCO Convention on Measures to Ban and Prevent the Illicit Import and Export of Cultural Objects and the Transfer of Ownership of Cultural Objects. The Contracting States undertake to prohibit the export and import of special cultural objects, to regulate the search for and return of such objects, to establish national professional bodies in the field and to participate in international measures for the protection of cultural heritage threatened by plundering and to restrict trade in such objects. Responsibility is mainly linked to three categories: the registration and control of articles, the prohibition of exports and imports, and the repatriation of such articles. In an age of increasing illicit trade in cultural objects, it is important that we in this way establish rules that protect the physical cultural heritage.
