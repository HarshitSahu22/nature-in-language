ParlaMint-NO_2019-09-30.ana.ud340e22	From the Prime Minister's office there is the following letter to Stortinget, dated 30 August 2019: ‘Change of Ministerial designation in the Ministry of Industry and Fisheries The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Fisheries is currently governed by two government councils. The Council of State governing the fisheries matters of the Department is today referred to as the Minister for Fisheries. ParlaMint-NO_2019-09-30.ana.ud340e92	We've come to the end of the 163rd big thing, the second big thing in the current parliamentary term. The President would like to say a few words about the business of the session that is now behind us. There has been a high level of activity throughout the session, and the questioning institute has been frequently used. At this stage, 2 353 written questions have been submitted by the representatives. The members of the government have answered 261 questions to regular Question Time, 147 main questions in oral Question Time and 59 interviews. The Committees held a total of 105 consultations and 426 positions were put in place during the 2018-2019 session – a figure approximately at the level of previous sessions. In the 2018-2019 session it was promoted to a total of 186 representatives. It was less than the previous session, but still the second highest number of representatives it has been in a session. In January 2019, the Christian People's Party entered into government cooperation with the Right, the Advance Party and the Left. The government got a majority behind it in Stortinget. The transition from minority government to majority rule naturally affects parliamentary everyday life. It also clearly shows the statistics for requests decisions: In the 2017-2018 session, 429 requests for government were adopted. In the current session, the number is down to 91. The majority examines a number of legislative proposals in the 2018–2019 session. Among the most debated were the proposals for the amendment of the abortion law and the ban on the keeping of fur animals, both of which were discussed on Thursday 13 June. In total, Stortingen has dealt with 148 propositions and 27 communications from the government this session. There have been 13 presentations by the members of the government. Finally, the President would like to thank everyone who has been involved in Storting's activities in the year that went on. I hereby declare the negotiations of the 163rd meeting adjourned and at the same time ask the representatives to sign the minutes submitted.
