ParlaMint-NO_2020-09-30.ana.ud380e580	We've come to the end of the 164th big thing, the third big thing in the current parliamentary term. The President would like to say a few words about the business of the session that is now behind us. The session we end today has been a session completely out of the ordinary. As of March, the Stortinget’s work on both cases and work forms was strongly influenced by the covid-19 virus, but with stringent infectious measures, the Stortinget continued to take decisions even when large parts of the country closed down. The Korona Act was unanimously adopted by the Stortinget on 21 March. The size was behind a law that clearly defined the framework for cooperation between the national Parliament and the government in the exceptional situation. From the law came into force on 27 March, and until it was lifted on 27 May, the government notified the Stortinget 32 regulations in compliance with the law, five of which were stopped in Stortinget. In the spring, a number of measures were adopted to address the economic impact of the virus outbreak. Things related to the virus outbreak have naturally affected enough Storting's work in the months before the summer, but the session we are now laying behind has also included a lot of activity in Stortinget in other fields. When the session began, the country had a majority government, but in January the Progress Party went out of government cooperation with the Right, Left and Christian People's Party, and we got a minority government again. This, of course, has affected Parliament's everyday life. The number of request decisions in this session was 233. This is a significant increase from the previous session, when the number was 91. At this stage, 2795 written questions have been submitted by the representatives. The members of the government have answered 206 questions to regular Question Time, 116 main questions in oral Question Time and 37 interviews. The Committees held 101 hearings, 24 of which were held digitally. Furthermore, 399 settings were made in the session, a number that is almost on the level of the previous sessions. In the 2019-2020 session it was promoted to a total of 154 representatives. This is a number that is also not so far behind the number from the last sessions. During the session, the size has examined a number of legislative proposals. Key issues that can be mentioned were changes in the Biotechnology Act and changes in the Creeding Society Act. On 7 January of this year, the Constitution was also amended, when Section 63 on the Ombudsman obligation was amended, and on 14 May, four further constitutional amendments reached the necessary majority. In total, the Storting has dealt with 150 propositions and 29 communications from the government this session. In addition, 15 statements have been made by the members of the government. Finally, the President would like to thank everyone who has been involved in Storting's activities in the year that went on. I hereby declare the negotiations in the 164th Grand Prix for the conclusion, and at the same time ask that the representatives, one by one – with a good distance between themselves – sign the submitted protocol. There are distributed pens in all the places, so that the representatives can use each of their pens. The minutes will be held until noon. 15, so that it is possible to come back when the traffic is smaller.
