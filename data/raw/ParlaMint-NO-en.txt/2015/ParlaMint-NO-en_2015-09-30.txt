ParlaMint-NO_2015-09-30.ana.ud1714e393	Now we're going to end the 159th big thing, the second big thing in the current parliamentary term. The President will take the opportunity to link some comments to the business of the session that is now behind us. The majority had 392 cases for treatment in the 2014–2015 session. The President will briefly mention some of the most central: On 14 October 2014, Stortinget adopted the legislative amendments necessary to introduce the general protection obligation in Norway covering both sexes. The near-police reform was discussed and adopted on 10 June this year. On 19 June this year, the Stortinget adopted the draft representative for increased efforts in connection with the humanitarian crisis in Syria and its neighbourhoods. The representative's proposal was based on what was generally referred to as the Syria agreement. In addition, Stortinget has discussed major reforms in the transport sector and addressed the key issue of municipal reform. The majority of cases have been dealt with by the Storting during this session, but it was only the proposal on the right to apply to the courts that had the necessary constitutional majority. During this session, the majority held 95 meetings. The government has promoted 196 cases for Stortinget, divided by 66 deposit proposals, 85 billing proposals, and 7 combined propositions and 38 large-scale notifications. In addition, 10 presentations have been made. The representatives of Storting have promoted 147 proposals for representatives. We also see a clear tendency for the number of request decisions to increase with minority governments. The majority made a total of 192 such decisions in the current session. In the 2013–2014 session, the figure was 79. The number of questions to the ordinary Questionnaire has been 201, while there have been 156 main questions in the oral Questionnaire. In addition, the government has responded to 76 interpellations. Written questions to the government are at present 1,484. That's about 200 more than in the previous session. The size has also held two own-initiative debates. The new form of debate was introduced for the first time on 7 April 2015. In the first place, the Labour and Social Committee, which took the initiative in a debate on the impact of the reform. At the initiative of the Family and Culture Committee, a debate was held on 26 May on the conditions of freedom of the press in Norway. Finally, the President will thank all those who have been involved in Stortingen's activities this year for the effort, and I hereby declare the negotiations in the 159th big deal for the end.
