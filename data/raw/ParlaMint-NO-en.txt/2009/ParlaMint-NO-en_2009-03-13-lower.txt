ParlaMint-NO_2009-03-13-lower.ana.ud1740e49	It is a unanimous committee which today presents its position on a case concerning the implementation of the Building Energy Directive. This means extending the scope of the Energy Act and introducing energy labelling schemes for buildings and dwellings as well as energy assessment of boilers and air-conditioning systems. The point of implementing the Directive is that energy labelling should ensure building owners, tenants and other operators in the property market basic information about the energy status of the building. The entire committee stresses that in Norway today we use something over 80 TWh in total building mass and that there is a significant savings potential. The implementation of the Directive will provide us with more knowledge of the energy state of the building mass. The buildings will have an energy certificate, which naturally will be graduated. Professional buildings over 1000 m2 and smaller professional buildings and dwellings sold or rented out are covered by the Directive. Finally, I would just like to stress that the consultation round has given positive feedback across almost the whole line. Many have stressed the need to make the implementation of the Directive unbureaucratic and simple. It is a co-ordinated committee that underlines that this will increase the energy awareness of all housing owners – at least it should contribute to this – and that this will provide a significant energy saving potential as well as a win-win situation for both housing owners, tenants, energy consumption and, in particular, the overall environment.
