ParlaMint-NO_2006-12-07-lower.ana.ud287e37	The proposition we are about to deal with now is about a change in the Transparency Act § 3a. This paragraph has not been in force since its adoption in 2001 because it has proved impracticable. The proposal that we are going to consider provides an opportunity for all those who have been given access to the records and records of the surveillance police to come up with their version of the case. This change will strengthen privacy and legal certainty for individuals – this is because it is no longer necessary to prove the accuracy of the information contained in the file, but instead it will be possible to provide additional information in the file. In this context, it is important to point out that the supplementary information shall not be subject to any control. It is also proposed to ensure that all those subject to this new law are informed of the new changes and of the possibilities available to each person to supplement the dossiers. We also believe that this approach has a political-historical value that ensures the history of the future in a better way, by getting additional notes into the case papers instead of removing them from the archive. This was also widely supported in the consultation round, among others. from the Organisation against political surveillance. This law is intended to operate until mid-2007 when the work of the Transparency Committee is completed. It has been unanimous throughout the committee process. The Judicial Committee therefore recommends that Odelstinget support the proposal submitted.
