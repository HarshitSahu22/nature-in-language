ParlaMint-NO_2006-10-02.ana.ud1206e53	Honourable co-representatives! It was with great sorrow that we received the message that our good colleague and friend, Vice President of the Lagtinget and representative of West Agder Jon Lilletun had lost the fight against cancer. He slept quietly in his home with his closest friends around him on August 21, 60 years old. Jon Lilletun was born in Vossestrand on 23 October 1945. His parents belonged to Pentecost in the village, and Jon got many and strong impressions from here. He was rich in knowledge even though formal education was limited to secondary and commercial schools. In his professional life, he was among others merchant and municipal youth secretary, cultural consultant and action consultant. Jon Lilletun was elected to Stortinget in 1989 and started in 2001 during his fifth period. Before this he represented the Christian People's Party in various municipal and other public positions, he was active in the party's youth organization and was Vice-Chairman of the Christian People's Party for 12 years, from 1987 to 1999. From 1983 to 1987 he was a member of the National Council for Children and Youth. At Stortinget, Jon Lilletun has been an active and committed member of both the Municipal and Environmental Protection Committee, the Church and Education Committee, the Business Committee and the Foreign Affairs Committee. From 1993 to 1997, he was head of the Church, Education and Research Committee. He has been a delegate to the United Nations General Assembly and a member of the Parliament's delegation to the NATO Parliamentary Assembly and the Delegation for Relations with the European Parliament. In the last year, Jon Lilletun has been Vice President of the Lamentations. When the central government was formed in 1997, Jon Lilletun was the natural choice for the post as Minister of Church, Education and Research. Such a large department needed a knowledgeable, committed and liberal boss. When the Cooperation Government was formed in 2001, Jon Lilletun received the important key office as parliamentary leader of the Christian People's Party and was thus an important person to solve the government's affairs in the Stortinget. Jon Lilletun pursued politics through unselfish efforts throughout his adult life. The tracks stand there as things he fought for and won, such as the Research Fund and the Faith Training Reform, or as difficult matters he wisely contributed to the solution of, like the CRL subject. He had an involvement in the whole spectrum of political issues, from cultural and environmental issues, abstinence and voluntary activity to the conditions of business, in particular small and medium-sized enterprises and primary industries. Children and young people were always close to his heart. The tracks are also among people whose cause he spoke – those who are persecuted for their faith, the poor, drug addicts of the world, the unborn, the oppressed people of Kashmir or the victims of holocaust and antisemitism. There was never any doubt that he was standing on their side that needed it the most. He was a man who cared for others. Jon was a political gardener. He sowed and planted his ideas and values generously. Curious and innovative – occupied by people's everyday lives. He made sure that the ideas got nourishment, light and warmth with their good follow-up and their many encouraging words. That is why he was also reaped in the form of political results. Jon Lilletun was a committed and knowledgeable person and a pure and raw person. Through his life and his whole manner of being, he also communicated the core of the Christian faith, which was an integral part of his entire person. He fought markedly for his values and for new generations to have a share in them. He wanted respect for human dignity to shape society. But above all, he let it affect his own dealings with people. He had a vision of a warmer society with more care and closeness, and we could see the vision realized in his own care and love for those who came in his way. Jon Lilletun was a populace of which it glowed on the pulpit, and which could often come with good and striking spontaneous lines in debates. He was solution-oriented, and through his contagious laughter and a witty line he could solve the most locked situations. He never used humor to hit anyone, just to highlight something. This says something about how Jon Lilletun was in every context, and that's the best nobility a human can get. Jon Lilletun had a serious cancer diagnosis a year and a half ago. But we had all hoped that he would get well again and get to live and enjoy us for many more years. He was a person in whom it was easy to be happy, and who was easily happy in others. With him is a distinct Christian People's Party politician gone. He will be deeply missed by the colleagues from all parties in Stortinget. In reverence and gratitude, we bring peace to Jon Lilletun’s good and happy memory. The representatives heard the President's commemorative speech.
