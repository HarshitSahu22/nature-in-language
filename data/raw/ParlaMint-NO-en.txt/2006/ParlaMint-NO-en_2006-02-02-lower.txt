No 26 The defence will now have the opportunity to contact women in the same way as they contact the male population when calling for a voluntary session for women. The fact that the Storting is today in favour of changing the protective duty law so that this is now possible is a step in the right direction for an equal society. The size has previously been the reason why women can be called to a voluntary session, but the way to reach them has not been clarified until now. This has led to the defence not reaching out to the individual women's year-old coals. The aim of the working party is to achieve 15 pst. Women's share in Defence by 2008, which we are proposing with this amendment to reach. As rapporteur of the case, I am pleased that all parties agree to introduce a voluntary session for women. The changes that will now be made mean that the Swedish Civil Protection Agency can use the same sources to reach the women concerned as men, and will also be a suitable tool for recruiting a larger proportion of women for service in the Defence than so far. Several women in Defense will help maintain the legitimacy of the duty of protection in the people and strengthen the links between women and defence. In addition, the defence will be more a reflection of Norwegian society. At the same time, an increased proportion of women among the protection and command masses will enrich the organisation by influencing the structures, methods, training and attitudes of women in defence. The Workers' Party further assumes that the Defence will practice a gender equality policy line in which all women in the same year's age as all men who are called to the session are normally called. The first-time service usually lasts for 12 months and starts with basic military skills training. This training focuses on improving survival and using personal weapons in a conflict situation. As a soldier, there are many disciplines in the Army, the Maritime Defense, the Air Force or the Homeland Protection Department where you can serve. It is also important to bear in mind that women today have the same terms of service and advancement as men if they want to choose master training. In theory, there are no positions women cannot have access to. The defense has several female pilots and many female officers, at all ages and at different degrees. Norway is one of the few countries in the Western world that allows women in all forms of combative service. International operations are one example. We have achieved this, among others. because the Workers' Party has worked for full equality in society and in Defence, and we will continue to do so when, after today's decision, there will probably be even more women to find in Defence.
