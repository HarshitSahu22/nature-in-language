ParlaMint-NO_2002-02-05-lower.ana.ud1249e40	I will be brief as there is broad consensus in the Committee on the proposed legislative changes. The proposal – or rather the propositions – proposes amendments to the pollution law and to the municipal water and sewerage taxes law, as part of the Government's programme for the renewal of state legislation aimed at the municipal sector. The aim of the changes is to give municipalities and county municipalities more space for action. According to the Ministry, legislation which imposes unnecessary obligations on the production of services, the organisation and administration of municipalities and counties must be abolished or amended. This will contribute to more service production and less administration, as unnecessary rules governing the retail sector are removed. The majority of the Committee – all outside SV – subscribe to these objectives and look forward to other changes in the same direction. In addition, a unanimous committee supports the Ministry’s positive view that businesses and other organisations themselves can contribute to making their own business or organisation more environmentally friendly through EMAS certification. This can contribute to increased involvement and positive attitude to environmental issues within each organisation. Finally, I would like to stress that the proposed legislative changes are supported by a unanimous committee, and would recommend the setting.
