ParlaMint-NO_2002-11-12-lower.ana.ud2024e86	It is a unanimous municipal committee that supports the proposed adjustment in the law on intercommunal companies which makes it clear that the statutory audit of the accounts of intercommunal companies does not apply. There was an unintended consequence of the amendment to the Accounting Act on 16 June 2000 which created the need to reverse this. A unanimous committee also supports the selection of auditors by intercommunal companies. This may be a registered or state-authorised auditor, the audit of one of the participating municipalities or counties or a district audit. This proposal is not intended to change the practice of audit of inter-municipal companies which draw up annual accounts in accordance with the Accounting Act in accordance with Chapter 5 of the Accounting Act on the auditor’s tasks in auditing annual accounts and standards of sound auditing. In the Odelstingsproposition, the Ministry of Local and Regional Affairs suggests that this practice forms part of good municipal audit practice. For inter-municipal companies that prepare annual accounts in accordance with municipal accounting principles, the standards of sound auditing are used, with the exceptions provided for in municipal audits. The Ministry of Local and Regional Affairs has announced a legislative proposal on systems for local internal control and self-control, in which it will be proposed to open up competition. It is worth mentioning that the members of the Municipal Committee of the Progressive Party, the Christian People's Party and the Right in a note wish to welcome these changes. Furthermore, an overall committee has no comments and recommends that Odelstinget adopt the proposed legislative amendment.
