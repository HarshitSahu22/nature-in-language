The asylum agreement was adopted in Stortinget on 3 December 2015, in connection with the financial debate. On 16 December 2015, as a follow-up to the asylum agreement, it also agreed on measures for the integration of asylum seekers, the integration agreement. This agreement was adopted on 12 January 2016. In this session, the European Parliament has examined 27 draft constitutions and two of the proposals received the necessary constitutional majority. On 31 March 2016, the Stortinget adopted a new provision in the Constitution § 49, on local self-government/local democracy. The majority also decided on 24 May 2016 to ratify Norway’s Bank as its central bank, in a new section 33. A number of legislative proposals were examined during the session. A new law on the modification of legal gender was adopted, and the Nord-Trøndelag counties and the South-Trøndelag counties were merged into the Trøndelag counties, applicable from 2018. In 2016, the Storting decided to consent to the Norwegian link to the EU financial supervision. The decision was taken in accordance with the Constitution § 115, which requires a three-quarters majority. The representatives of Storting have promoted 129 proposals for representatives.
