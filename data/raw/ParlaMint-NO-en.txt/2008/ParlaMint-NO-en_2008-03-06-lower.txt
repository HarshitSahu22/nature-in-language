ParlaMint-NO_2008-03-06-lower.ana.ud931e34	In the case of Ot.prp. No 21 for 2007-2008, the Government receives full support from an overall committee. The proposal includes proposals for amendment of the Law of 20 March 1998 on Preventive Security, Object Safety. The Committee has noted that the aim of the proposal is to strengthen the work of preventive safety measures to protect vulnerable objects from hazardous activities. The administrative implications of the legislative proposal are essentially due to the assumption that each sectoral ministry is responsible for the designation and classification of displayable objects. Today the responsibility for the designation is assigned to the appropriate owner of the display object. The proposal thus provides for the centralisation of this part of the preventive safety work. The proposition is based mainly on the report ‘Prevention of objects against acts of terrorism and sabotage’, presented by an inter-ministerial working group. This is a big and important thing. It is good that there is full political agreement on how this should be implemented and on how the law should be.
