The President-in-Office shall refer to the second and third paragraphs of Section 6 of the Rules of Procedure, which shall read as follows: ‘In the first election of the President of the Grand Duchy and Vice-Presidents during the term of the election, the votes shall be in the case of non-signatured banknotes. In the case of subsequent elections in the same parliamentary term, the votes under point (a) of the first paragraph of Section 60 shall take place unless the conditions for voting in currency without signature in point (d) of the first paragraph of Section 60 are fulfilled. The election of presidents and secretaries requires a normal majority, i.e. over half of the votes cast. If none of the candidates reaches such a majority in the first election or in the case of free re-election, the two candidates with the highest number of votes shall be re-elected. Paragraph 61, second paragraph.’ The President of the Grand Duchy must first be elected. The choice is therefore made by written voting.
