ParlaMint-NO_2001-10-17.ana.ud1861e70	After the government decided to leave in March 2000, the Workers' Party, as the largest group in Stortinget, was asked to form a government. The elections on 10 September 2001 did not give a majority for any of the declared government options. After the election, the Right Party, Christian People's Party and Left, with the invitation of the Advance Party, decided that they would conduct negotiations on the formation of a tripartite government. In the light of these negotiations and further clarifications between the parties concerned, the Party has indicated that the Party will support the formation of such a Government. After this, it is clear that a majority in the Stortinget wants the formation of a new government. I would therefore like to present today, in the Council of State, an application for a waiver to the Government.
