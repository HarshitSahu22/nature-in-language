The Management Board shall decide on the use of funds for market regulation, information and professional action. In a serious effort to achieve the goal of that 10 pst. Norwegian agriculture shall be organic in 10 years' time, experience and important concerns for organic farming must be part of the basis for the measures in question. And at the same time, a representative of organic farming will provide agriculture with a continued majority in the Council of Turnover. Marketing and logistics have proved to be a major problem for organic food in the start-up phase. Agricultural cooperation and food chains have not adapted their competence and structure, and they are also unfortunately lacking motivation for the success of organic products. Too much of these goods do not come to consumers as eco-products. As a result, consumers lose their diversity and agriculture loses its added value, its motivation for development and its competitiveness. We believe that there is a costly waste of opportunities. Experience from other countries and from different market projects in this country shows that the efficient marketing and distribution of organic products in a development phase must take place through small and specialised distribution channels, where the eco-friendly seas are more directly linked to marketing and sales of goods. That is the background to the proposal, and I would therefore like it to be included in the vote on the matter.

The bill on this investigation commission follows the same model as the law on the investigation commission on allegations of illegal surveillance of Norwegian citizens. The law authorizes the presence and the obligation to testify to the Commission. Explanation is given for closed doors and the Commission may decide that its identity shall not be disclosed. The Commission may also require the holder of a written certificate to submit it to the Commission. There are different views in the Committee and the Storting on setting up this Review Commission.
