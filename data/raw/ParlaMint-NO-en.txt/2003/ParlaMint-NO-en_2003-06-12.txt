ParlaMint-NO_2003-06-12.ana.ud1823e79	Honourable co-representatives! Former Major Lars T. Platou is dead, almost 83 years old. Lars Tokstad Platou was born on 2 July 1920 in Stavanger. He graduated from Artium in 1939 and became an electrical engineer from NTH in 1946. Lars T. Platou took an active part in the campaign in April 1940 and was wounded during the strike at Tangen. Already in 1941 he became a farmer at the genealogy farm of Tokstad in Veldre and was responsible for the operation until 1982. Over many years he also managed the family business Elektrobyg A/S in Hamar and had many assignments as an electrotechnical consultant. Lars T. Platou engaged early in political work and became chairman of Hedmark Right in 1950 and thus also a member of Højre's National Government. He became a member of Ringsaker Municipal Council in 1952 and was a member of the Presidency in the period 1960-1967. It is impossible to go into the details of the long line of Lars T. Platous trustees in local government and care, but the list ranges from the councils via land government to county councils and local party organisations. During the 1950s, he also held a number of important positions in the Norwegian Confederation of Bonds and was among others. for a few years the chairman of the Hedemark circuit of the Bond Team. Already in 1953, Lars T. Platou became alternate from Hedmark for Høyre's Alv Kjøs. He was also an alternate in the period 1958-61 and was elected as a representative in the election in 1965. Lars T. Platou was re-elected in 1969 and 1973 and quickly became one of the Right's most central politicians – both inside and outside the Stortinget. In Stortinget he was a first-time member of the Municipal Committee and then of the Financial Committee. From 1969 to 1973 he was chairman of the financial committee and then vice-chairman of the same committee from 1973 to 1977. Lars T. Platou was an active staging representative, who engaged in numerous big and small things. It should only be mentioned that in the spring of 1969 he was given a primary responsibility as the rapporteur to unload the large tax reform of the Borten government – the VAT reform – through the Stortinget. Lars T. Platou was also deeply concerned about providing the best possible conditions for businesses and value creation in society. Even he was both before, during and after his time at Stortinget engaged in a wide range of businesses. He had leadership positions, among others. in the following companies: A/S Vinmonopolet, where he was the Chairman of the Board of Directors, Raufoss Ammunition factories, where he was the Chairman of the Board of Directors, and the Bank of Credit, where he was the Chairman of the Board of Directors until 1990. It was also typical of Lars T. Platou that at a time when it looked more than usual heavy, he engaged himself strongly in the work to ensure that «Skibladner» would remain Mjøsas White Svane. Lars T. Platou was awarded the King's medal in gold in 1982. In his necrologist, Højres leader, Jan Petersen, says that «Lars T. Platou ruvet, both as a person and a politician». It is not difficult to agree with such a characteristic, although in this context we focus on the years on Stortinget. He was an important and influential representative of big business, not primarily because of the formal positions he subsequently received both in his own party group and in the committee work, but perhaps equally because of the ability of trusting and committed cooperation across all party boundaries and other dividing lines in the Stortinget. Lars T. Platou was always prepared to listen to the views of others, but did not hesitate to express his own views when necessary. And Lars T. Platou was worth listening to. Lars T. Platou was in all his ways a very comfortable person. It got everyone who had the pleasure of cooperating with him for twelve years on the Stortinget, experience. We remember Lars T. Platou with deep respect and shine peace over his memory. The representatives heard the President's commemorative speech.
