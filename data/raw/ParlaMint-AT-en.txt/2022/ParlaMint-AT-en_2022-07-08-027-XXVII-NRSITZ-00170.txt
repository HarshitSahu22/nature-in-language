ParlaMint-AT_2022-07-08-027-XXVII-NRSITZ-00170_d8e1428	Ladies and gentlemen, I can contact all of you very briefly, especially our employees. I want to do this with a thank you and a request. I would like to thank the fact that it is possible, before the meeting, after the meeting and also, as we see it, to find the discussion among the parties, beyond the parties, and to laugh together, to rejoice and to discuss everyday problems as well. I think this is very necessary to maintain a basis for discussion. Thank you for your willingness to stand up for your conviction. But I have a request: Remember, there is only one concept of the politician, whether he belongs to a government group or an opposition group! The concept of the politician, we know, is, of course, for many reasons – since everyone will have his own view – not the best, but we can all, especially now in holiday times, make a contribution to raising it again in the public. It is, I believe, a very great task for each of us to always live up to the image in public. I can expressly thank all of you, your families, your partners, your friends who support you so that you can do your job as you did. Do this to them, too! I would like to thank my colleagues in the Bureau, President Bures and President Hofer for their presidency. This is not always easy, but we have done it in good common tradition.
