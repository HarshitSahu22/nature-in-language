First of all, I would like to thank all the Members of the House who have contributed tremendously to the fact that there has been a lively political debate this year. I think that, above all, the presence quo will be a very good witness to the House. I know that the political debate has often been very, very intense and emotional, and I know – and we all know – that sometimes lines have been crossed. But I assume – and I am sure of that – that it is in the interests of all of us and of every group that we work together on the dignity of the House, that we are working together for the interests of our citizens. I know that there is criticism, criticism of me, of the Presidency there and there, and I also know how to deal with her and, as with all other ways, I will be able to continue to deal intensively with the requirements of this office. However, I think that only in common – even in the reflection of how we meet each other – can we always help to live up to the claim that we should not miss respect for the dissident, the dissident, or the person who is appealing to another view of the world. There were high-ranking debates, with high-intellectual conflicts, there were some that were conducted polemically, or some that might not have been conducted with this commitment. I am particularly grateful to my two colleagues for their participation in the Presidency. One of them, Madam President Bures, is unable to perform this function at present, and we are sending her once again the best wishes for her recovery to the hospital. In cooperation with Madam President Kitzmüller, we have helped each other to best communicate the common position of the House to the public – via television, as well as to the numerous guests at the gallery. I hope that in autumn we can strengthen again and also start a little recovery through the holidays, that we are able to get together where it is appropriate to find a compromise. There are always possibilities for this, and let us be aware of this: The relationship we maintain here is also responsible for how people's relationship appears outside.
