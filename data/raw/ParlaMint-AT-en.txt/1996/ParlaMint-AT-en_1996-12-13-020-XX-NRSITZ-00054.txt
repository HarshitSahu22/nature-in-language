Address by the President
ParlaMint-AT_1996-12-13-020-XX-NRSITZ-00054_d7e635	Ladies and gentlemen! We were just in a very emotional situation. I would therefore simply like to express the hope that it is a common desire of the House to express a very warm thanks to our employees for their work in the past year. [[General condolences.]] In spite of all the difficulties, I believe that cooperation in the Bureau with the Second President and the Third President was a comrade and correct. Together, we have tried – everyone according to their strengths – to master an increasingly difficult task. I would also like to thank the President-in-Office of the Council for all the political differences in their views on cooperation at the Presidential Conference and, on the other hand, the resolution of many difficult issues which we have dealt with together in most cases. I do not want to look back in any way now, but on this occasion I would like to say that we have done a great deal in recent years, especially last year, to help in other democracies to build parliamentaryism. We are sending experts from Parliament's Directorate to Albania. We will hold a seminar in January with Caucasian parliamentarians to support and facilitate their work. We have also received recognition for the introduction of new information methods and for the presentation or presence of the Austrian Parliament on the Internet. Ladies and gentlemen! I would also like to tell you that I visited the President-in-Office this morning and that his recovery process is going very well. I suggest that from this point of view we send him warm greetings and wishes for his complete restoration. [[General condolences.]] Traditionally, we also wish all our fellow citizens in Austria the very best before the turn of the year. We hope for a peaceful year in 1997. [[General condolences.]] The next meeting of the National Council will be held on 22 October. ==References== It shall be convened in writing. This meeting is closed.
