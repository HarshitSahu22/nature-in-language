[[Those present rise from their places.]] [[10:03:11]] Ceremony on the occasion of the death of the head of the Carinthian Dr. Jörg Haider
ParlaMint-AT_2008-10-20-023-XXIII-NRSITZ-00073_d7e525	On the night of the 10th to the 11th. In October 2008, a traffic accident led to the head of the state of Carinthia Dr. Jörg Haider torn from the middle of life. Dr. Jörg Haider was first elected to the National Council in 1979 and belonged to the National Council – with interruptions – until 1999. There were a total of around 14 years in which Dr. Jörg Haider worked as a representative here in this House. During this time, Dr. Haider had various functions, and he was a member of the Foreign Policy Committee and the Constitutional Committee. From 1986 to 1989 and from 1992 to 1999, Dr. Jörg Haider led the Freedom Party as a clubman. In the Austrian Convention, Dr. Jörg Haider worked in the “State Institutions” Committee; he actively participated in the fundamental discussion and the resulting draft reforms. Ladies and gentlemen! Political differences and competing values are not eliminated by death, but they are decisively relativized. We recognize and appreciate the great political achievements of Dr. Jörg Haider, who has contributed to Austrian politics over the last decades. His death should remind us that, at the height of the political debate, respect for man must be safeguarded. Our compassion is in this hour of his family, his family, especially his sister, who is among us. I ask you to remember this minute of the deceased.
