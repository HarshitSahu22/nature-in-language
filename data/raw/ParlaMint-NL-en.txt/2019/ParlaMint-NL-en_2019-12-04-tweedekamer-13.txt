ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-13.u6	We have arrived at the continuation of the VAO E-health/Smart Care. The Minister for Medical Care has the floor again.
ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-13.u7	Mr. Chairman. In the first motion, Mrs van den Berg's motion in part No. 204, the government is invited to investigate how patients and clients can be tempted to use e-health applications. I think it is very important that patients and clients are convinced of the added value of e-health. Several actions are already under way in this area. I recall Care of Nu, in which we inform about all kinds of opportunities around e-health with meetings in the country and digital citizens. I recall the e-health week, where healthcare institutions across the country open their doors to make people experience what e-health means. And so there are other initiatives. Think also of the attention we give to the personal health environment. But in addition to what is already being done, I would like to explore what other possibilities there are left to enthuse the patient for e-health. If I may explain the motion in this way, I would be happy to leave it to the Chamber. For the motion by Mrs Van den Berg and Mr Veldman on item No. 205 is similar. It invites the government to consult with the healthcare sector in order to establish a training plan informing healthcare professionals about e-health applications. A great deal is already happening in this regard too. In that process Care of Nu is not only looked at informing healthcare professionals, but also at training healthcare professionals. This involves, for example, training in digital skills and developing self-tests. There are more examples to give. But I would like to continue with the field of care and education on what else we can achieve to give more attention to e-health education. If I may explain it that way, I shall also leave this motion to the House to judge.
ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-13.u8	We are particularly concerned about reaching larger groups. We see positive initiatives, many different things, from the Minister. But how can you really scale it up in a sector where 1.2 million people work? I would like a reply from the Minister.
ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-13.u9	I couldn't agree more. We also shared this in the AO. It is not about the small upper layer, as I believe we mentioned it in the AA. It concerns the large group of people who know that there are all kinds of e-health in development: to teach them how to use it, to invite them to use it, to prepare them well, to organise their training. I believe that this is very important, together with the author of the motion. With this explanation, I would like to leave the verdict on this motion to the Chamber. Mr Veldman's motion and Mrs Bergkamp's motion for a resolution 206 calls on the government to ensure that if eHealth applications prove to be effective, they become part of the healthcare guidelines as soon as possible. I strongly support speeding up proven effective innovations. If an innovation is proven to be effective, it should simply become part of the directive or the quality standard. Of course, it is up to the profession, insurers and patients to determine what is in that directive. If that is not self-evident, then the Healthcare Institute can appeal to parties. The Healthcare Institute will also explicitly include this issue in its review framework from 1 July. I will also appeal to the professional associations to play their role in accelerating e-health. I expect that I will be able to promote the faster inclusion of e-health in directives. With this explanation I show that I agree with the operative part of the motion. With this explanation, I would also like to leave the vote on the motion to the Chamber. Mr. Chairman. The last motion, Mrs Agema's motion to item No. 207, calls on the government to promote e-health by making it financially more attractive in the dbc-systematic. I don't recommend that motion. Free prices apply in hospitals. Hospitals can already come to the insurers with proposals on e-health. So the insurers can have the conversation and make arrangements with a hospital. I see this as something that lies with the parties and not as something that should be requested from the government. That's why I advise against this motion.
ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-13.u10	Thank you. This brings us to the end of this VAO on e-health.
