ParlaMint-NL_2019-09-11-tweedekamer-4.u13	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. First of all, I should like to comment on the motion on part No. 440 with two requests to the government. This is about an assessment of the methodology. We already have this assessment of the way in which hormone-distorting substances are handled in Europe. The question that actually arises in several motions is whether we should also make a Dutch policy in addition to European policy. I am not really in favour of this, because I would prefer that we first use our expertise on this issue for Europe and then, in some cases, we look at what extra is needed from the Netherlands. That is also what I wrote in my plan of action in the letter received by your Chamber on 1 April. For example, I think it is a Dutch task to raise awareness about what hormone disruptors are all about. For example on the website waarzitwatin.nl we have collected information about this, but also the brochure Zwanger! contains information on this. In short and well, with regard to the motion in part No. 440 I believe that in Europe there is a method of assessing substances. Depending on the results of the assessment, a substance shall be placed on the list of substances of high concern and, where appropriate, restrictions on its use to reduce exposure. This list is not a static list but is updated. That's why I don't see any added value in a Dutch inventory. For that reason, I advise against the motion in part No. 440.
