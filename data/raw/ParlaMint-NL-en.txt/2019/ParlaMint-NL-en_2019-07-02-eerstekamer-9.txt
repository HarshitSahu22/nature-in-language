ParlaMint-NL_2019-07-02-eerstekamer-9.u1	The next item is the procedure for the election of the two Vice-Presidents. I propose, after hearing the College of Seniors, that the nomination for these posts may take place in writing after this public meeting until 12 noon on Friday 5 July 2019. Candidates must clearly state whether they are candidates for either the first or the second Vice-Presidentship or both. The latter possible in the event of no election as First Vice-President. The Registrar will send the candidate letters to the members digitally the same day. In the plenary session of 9 July 2019, candidates for the first Vice-President shall be given a maximum of five minutes in alphabetical order to explain their candidacy. Following that, the Chamber may ask questions to the candidates. Finally, the vote will be taken in accordance with Rule 113 and further to our Rules of Procedure. The same procedure will then be followed for the second Vice-President. Does any of the Members still wish to speak on this matter? That is not the case.
