ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-17-eerstekamer-19.u1	The next item is the appointment of a confidential representative for the members of the Upper House. Article 12 of the Code of Conduct on Integrity requires the Chamber, on a proposal from the Household Commission, to appoint an independent confidential representative to advise members and political groups on the interpretation of the Code of Conduct and on the desired action in concrete situations. The Committee now proposes that Mrs Carla Verwoerd be appointed to this post. I have found that there is no need for a written vote on this appointment. I therefore put to the House the question of whether it can accept the proposal to appoint Mrs Verwoerd. That's the case. Mrs Carla Verwoerd has thus been appointed a confidential representative for the Upper House members. On behalf of all of you, I would like to welcome Mrs Verwoerd. I'll suspend the sitting for a few moments so you can meet her at the Hall.
