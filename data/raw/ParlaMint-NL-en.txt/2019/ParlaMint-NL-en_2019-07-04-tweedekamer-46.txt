ParlaMint-NL_2019-07-04-tweedekamer-46.u6	Mr. Chairman. We have had a good debate on a subject that is of enormous importance for people struggling with their gender registration. I thought it was a good and dignified debate, in which we said: we want to take advantage of the opportunity and the space available to make more possible even at a younger age. A number of motions say that we want to go further. In the motion in part No. 136 says Mrs Bergkamp: do not assume 16 years of age, but 14 years of age. Of course, it's not a law of Meden and Persians where you lay the line. We have also really looked at a number of interests that you want to weigh against each other, such as the importance of young people, and to some extent the importance of care. Experts also say on this point: start from 16 years, because in puberty the experienced sex can still be fluid. In addition, other highly personal choices which have a certain depth, such as medical procedures or euthanasia declarations, also set the limit at 16 years. A combination of these considerations makes me say: keep that line on. That is why I would advise against this motion. The same applies to the motion in part No. 137. Again, there is a balancing of interests. It will not happen so terribly often that after a modification of his gender registration someone does so a second time. But if you do it a second time, you can also do it on the low-threshold route of the civil servant. But if you would like to change it again afterwards, that is a moment in which extra care and alertness play a role. Hence my preference to stick to the route by means of a judicial test. Mr. Chairman. Finally, the motion in part No. 138, by Mrs Buitenweg. That motion is about the usefulness and necessity of gender registration. I have, of course, already dealt with this in the letter. A number of years ago, a detailed investigation was carried out by the WODC, under the title M/V and beyond. At the same time, there is a motion by Mrs Den Boer and Mrs Bergkamp asking for a study on the transfer of the BRP and the ID card. If I may interpret the motion in such a way as to include the position of the birth certificate, I would be able to do so and then I could take over this motion.
