The motion-Palland/Aartsen in part No. 16 deals with the professionalisation of complaints handling at local level and asks to report on them. Part of my measures to improve legal protection for entrepreneurs in a call for tenders is the professionalisation of complaints at local level and also to be accountable to local councils, for example. In addition, the motion calls for annual monitoring of the professionalisation of complaint handling. I am thinking of a quantitative survey with samples from the government, and of course we should also look at what this professionalization looks like, because otherwise you don't know anything. That's where I'm gonna give the judge room.
