ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-10.u8	The next item is the continuation of the report on written consultation Risk settlement model 2020. The Minister for Medical Care has the floor.
ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-10.u9	Mr. Chairman. The motion in part No. 1035 invites the government to investigate, in addition to research into the risk settlement, in order to gain more insight into cross-border transactions and connections of health insurance companies active in the Dutch health insurance market. Equalis' research is not about the desirability or undesirableness of money flows abroad. The research is about whether risk settlement can be even better. That is exactly what we are constantly working on in the Netherlands. The motion expresses the opinion that money that runs through the risk settlement should remain available for Dutch care. I agree, of course, that the risk settlement is at the service of the Dutch insured, but I do not agree with the statement that every euro must remain in Dutch hands. The point is that the money leads to quality and affordable care. A foreign party may or may not be better able to do so, for example a foreign reinsurance company which reinsures the risks of a Dutch health insurance company. That is a practical example. So I advise against this motion.
ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-10.u10	Before there's any confusion between the Minister and the CDA, the following. We are not concerned that foreign players should not be allowed to enter the Dutch market. We are concerned, for example, that they should not offer a cheap student policy, benefit from the risk settlement and take the profits with them, in the name of a country in the south of Europe. That is what we want to prevent. So it is not our concern that foreign players should not be allowed to enter the market. We want the profits that may be made in the Netherlands to remain for the benefit of care.
ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-10.u11	But I'm looking at the operative part of the motion. This is about getting more insight into cross-border transactions. So I don't think that's very interesting because I think that can be very good for Dutch insured people. The motion in part No. 1036 calls on the government to set up an independent committee, which, together with the relevant parties such as Health Insurance Agency Netherlands and individual health insurance companies, will investigate the optimisation of the risk settlement model and come up with concrete recommendations and inform the Court of Justice of this before 1 October 2020. I also advise against this motion, because I already organise an annual evaluation once the settlement year is fully completed. This is the so-called Risk Settlement Monitor, which is carried out by an independent investigation agency. Right now, that's PwC. This research is accompanied by experts from insurers and researchers. I would also like to point out that the risk settlement model, Mrs Van den Berg and I have often talked about this through a joint process, involving both the health insurers and the individual insurers, as well as the researchers and the healthcare institute. This also applies to the studies on improvements, as well as to the accounting and decision-making of the model for any subsequent year.
ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-10.u12	I have already indicated that the CDA attaches great importance to solidarity in the system. The system is now cracking on all sides and it simply pays to avoid chronically ill. That is why we believe that once again an integral review of the risk settlement is necessary. That is the reason for this motion. I would therefore ask the Minister to look at that again.
ParlaMint-NL_2019-12-04-tweedekamer-10.u13	I am sticking to my view, because I think we are at a different stage. We have been building the risk settlement model for many years. I would also like to stress that I believe that such a model is never finished. I do not think it is a good idea to review it all, organise it in a different way and set up an independent committee.
