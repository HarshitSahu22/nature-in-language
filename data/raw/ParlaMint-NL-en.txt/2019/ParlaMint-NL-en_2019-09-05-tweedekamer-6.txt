ParlaMint-NL_2019-09-05-tweedekamer-6.u5	Mr. Chairman. This is indeed a discussion that has already been held here in the past, including at the start of the experiment. At that time, an experimental decision was made by means of an AMvB. At the time, it was very consciously chosen to allow only schools with a demonstrably good quality of education. The excellent schools and the good schools can demonstrate this actively on the basis of a self-evaluation. That was a condition that was included in the experimental decision at the time. The discussion on this can always be repeated, but I would just like to point out what has been the start of the experimental decision, as it is now in force through the AMvB passed by the Chamber. We're halfway through that period now. The final phase has entered. The authorisation for the latter phase has now been closed. New schools have been admitted. That's why the moment you're experimenting, there's got to be enough time to get results. You can't do that in six months or in a year. It takes at least two years for that. That's why the last schools could still start. In two years time, the experimental period will end, as arranged in the AMvB. On that basis, we can look again at how we move forward. If, in this final phase, while the last authorisation has already been closed, we are going to allow others, then we are going to get stuck with the time period. So I'm going to have to make a new AMvB. I've been working on that for at least six months, or even more, I think. Then we are a lot further into the period that is now going on. So just as practical: apart from the substantive discussion on whether these schools should be admitted, your motion is simply not possible at the moment and I must advise against it. But again: new round, new opportunities. Once the experimental period is completed, the discussion will undoubtedly take place again. If you are still in the Chamber, you can do it yourself, otherwise others can do it. This will undoubtedly also be the subject of a debate. But at the moment it just can't, very practically driven. So I'm deceiving the motion, Madam President.
