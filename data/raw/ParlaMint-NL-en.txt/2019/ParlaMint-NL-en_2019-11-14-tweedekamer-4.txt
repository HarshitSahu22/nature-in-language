ParlaMint-NL_2019-11-14-tweedekamer-4.u6	I give the floor to the Minister.
ParlaMint-NL_2019-11-14-tweedekamer-4.u7	Thank you. Mr Wassenberg's first motion concerns cod and by-catch. The by-catch of Dutch cod is a very small part. The problem with cod is much greater in other areas. I also think that we should take the measures where they really have effect. This is mainly in the north of the North Sea. That's where the Netherlands doesn't fish very much. So I would like the focus in the negotiations to be on where the problem is and therefore I advise against this motion. The second question concerns the Maritime Fund and the grants. The aim of the subsidies is precisely to make the fleet more sustainable. So when we do not use these subsidies, you do not get the environmental impact that we want to share. The new fund is now called the EMVAF instead of EMFZV. I always have to get used to the abbreviations, because they are always complicated. That fund prohibits the expansion of capacity. The funds are aimed at innovation in the sector, for example to improve selective fishing. They also strengthen the social-local fishing communities and promote the sustainability of maritime culture. Then it seems to me right that we should want that and not oppose it. So I advise against this motion. Then there is Mr Wassenberg's question about whaling. The negotiations are taking place between the European Commission and Norway, not between separate Member States and Norway. I also believe that it is up to the Commission to transfer these issues in those negotiations. Of course, we're against whaling. We shall bring that up in the appropriate committee. But I am not going to take an active action now that the Commission must bring this into the negotiations.
