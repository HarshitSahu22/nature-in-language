[[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[noting that there is no understanding of the spending of healthcare institutions on ICT, real estate and capital charges, overhead, accountants, external advice and consultancy;]] [[considers that the government and the Chamber should have an insight into this in order to carry out their steering or controlling role properly;]] [[calls on the government, the Dutch Healthcare Authority, to carry out an analysis of the expenditure of healthcare institutions on ICT, real estate and capital costs, overhead, accountants, external advice and consultancy,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion has been tabled by Mr Leijten. Mrs Leijten does not think so, but I would advise against this motion.
