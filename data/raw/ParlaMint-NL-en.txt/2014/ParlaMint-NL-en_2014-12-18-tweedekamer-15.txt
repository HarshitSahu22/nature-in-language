New data have been submitted to the Ctgb for this purpose. After an initial moratorium, this has ultimately led to the safe use of metam sodium, if additional, very strict conditions are met. Mrs Ouwehand asked if I would keep the Chamber informed of developments in the file. I admit that, because that seems logical to me. It is said in the Chamber that there are citizens who are concerned about the medicine, how it is used and what it may mean to themselves. I understand.
