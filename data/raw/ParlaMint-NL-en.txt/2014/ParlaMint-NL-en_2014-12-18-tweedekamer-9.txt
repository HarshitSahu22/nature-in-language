ParlaMint-NL_2014-12-18-tweedekamer-9.u10	Mr. Chairman. Three motions have been tabled and three questions put. In view of the wording, I shall respond a little more comprehensively to the first motion, the Helmut motion in part No. 179. I would be prepared to leave the verdict on the motion to the Chamber if it were to call on me to inform the Chamber how the ILT has ensured that the various roles are filled in sufficiently independently of each other. With the Chamber, I believe that they should always be able to pass the test of criticism. I can include the follow-up actions following Horvat's audit, on which I informed the Chamber on 30 January this year. That report already looked at the way in which the ILT authorisation process was organised. The European Railway Agency also carried out an extensive audit of ILT licensing, certification and enforcement in 2013. I can also include the follow-up of this in the information given to the Chamber, because I find another audit unnecessary. Furthermore, with the coming entry into force of the technical pillar of the fourth rail package, the ERA will be responsible instead of the ILT for the licensing of internationally operating companies and equipment. If I see it as formulated, I would advise against accepting this motion. Having said that, if I may read the motion with these frameworks, I could leave it to the Chamber.
