Arms exports contribute to our country's economy, not only through trade, but above all through innovation. The Dutch defence and security-related industry has more than 14,000 jobs in the Netherlands with more than 450 companies involved. This turnover is mainly directed towards exports. The growth of the Netherlands comes from these exports. In addition to arms exports, the title of the AO, there are also many dual-use goods. In this area, in particular, many entrepreneurs suffer from long waiting times. In times when entrepreneurs have to work hard for their turnover, it is therefore incomprehensible that an entrepreneur sometimes has to wait 13 weeks for a licence to export. From a number of sectors of large and small individual companies I get distress signals about applications for licences that remain for months. It is therefore a question of licences which could in principle have been granted simply because there is nothing wrong with the products. Due to capacity problems, applications remain long, which results in letters or credits being issued, so that the export transaction can ultimately not continue. Six months ago, I gave the Minister a list of 40 applications which remained in the Netherlands for more than six months but were completed in Germany within three weeks. I believe that this is no longer possible and that the government must deliver on this point. In the first term, the Minister said that she knows the urgency and that she will make an effort to reduce the length of time by making more manpower available, but I would like to tighten this up a little in the second period. It is important to act carefully when issuing a licence. The waiting period seems to be mainly due to a shortage of administrative capacity. This will have to be done in the very short term, because competition from other countries will have to deal with shorter waiting times, which will give trading partners greater certainty to entrepreneurs from those countries. The Minister has indicated that he is seeking a solution, but I would like to know from the Minister at what time that solution is coming, whether that solution is structural and whether she can give an overview of the reduced waiting time for entrepreneurs for the next AO Arms Exports. I'd like to get a response from the Minister.
