ParlaMint-NL_2014-04-16-tweedekamer-2.u4	Mr. Chairman. I will respond to the motions that have been tabled. Mrs Agema asks in her motion for a resolution for item No. 98 to introduce a three-fold higher sentence. We discussed this in the debate. I indicated that I spoke about this in an earlier consultation with the Minister of Security and Justice and that he informed the Chamber that he did not consider it necessary to increase the maximum sentence. Even now, the victim's vulnerability, including that of an older person, is a circumstance which the Public Prosecutor and the judge can consider, within the maximum penalty limit, in the case of the offence of "abuse" or "heavy abuse." Moreover, where there is abuse, Article 304 of the Criminal Code allows for a third increase in prison time if the guilty party has committed the crime against, among others, his mother or father. That is why I would advise against this motion.
ParlaMint-NL_2014-04-16-tweedekamer-2.u5	So that was exactly not my point. Of course it is true that this can be taken into account, but it is only when a sentence of three times higher than that is applied that the Public Prosecutor's Office counts for a sentence that is no longer a community service, but a prison sentence. I believe that someone who is so incredibly cowardly that he not only robs someone on the street, but also elects someone who, for example, walks with a rollator, so someone who is doing so seriously deserves a prison sentence and not community service.
ParlaMint-NL_2014-04-16-tweedekamer-2.u6	I think Mrs Agema and I agree on the rejection of the abuse of the elderly. I think we are in complete agreement on that. Something else is what punishment goes with it. I believe that it is a matter for the Minister of Security and Justice to consider how this takes place within the overall criminal justice framework. He stated in that statement that there is no need to increase the level of punishment in order to impose heavier penalties. That is why I would like to discourage the motion. Mrs Baay's motion on item No. 99 calls for changes to the naming of the mandatory code of domestic violence and child abuse law. That law was passed so we can't just change its name. A new law would then have to be adopted. I would like to confirm here that I have agreed in general consultation to look at the naming of the Advisory and Contact Point on Domestic Violence and Child Abuse (AMHK), because, as Mrs Baay mentioned earlier in the debate, this current naming may not make sufficiently clear that elderly abuse can also be reported to that AMHK. I would therefore like to give her an undertaking to look at that naming and I would advise against the motion on the naming of the law, because we have just passed that law and I cannot change its name without making a legal proposal.
ParlaMint-NL_2014-04-16-tweedekamer-2.u7	I would just like to say, as President, that if you wish, you could submit a legislative amendment to that effect.
ParlaMint-NL_2014-04-16-tweedekamer-2.u8	Sure, but given the fact that we passed the law together, including the naming, I don't intend to propose a change of law. However, I would like to take Mrs Baay's point with me. She has a point where she asks to clarify the naming of the AMHK to the citizens, in the sense that they can also go there for reports of elderly abuse.
ParlaMint-NL_2014-04-16-tweedekamer-2.u9	Of course, it is important that a law is also applicable to the target group concerned. If there is any doubt or confusion about this, it must be removed. I will take both suggestions with me, but I maintain my request to you to see whether this change of law cannot be enforced in name.
ParlaMint-NL_2014-04-16-tweedekamer-2.u10	Once again, I would have to table a legislative proposal to change the naming. It all takes time and procedures again. I admit that I will make clear, both in the naming of the AMHK and in the information on the application of the law on the reporting code, which forms of abuse can be reported, with particular attention being paid to the fact that elderly abuse is also included in the reporting code. I want to make those two commitments. For that reason, I would like to continue to discourage the motion.
ParlaMint-NL_2014-04-16-tweedekamer-2.u11	Can you also put a deadline on it?
ParlaMint-NL_2014-04-16-tweedekamer-2.u12	We will be able to do this immediately in the information which is already taking place on the law on the reporting code.
