ParlaMint-NL_2016-07-07-tweedekamer-51.u8	Mr. Chairman. I want the motion Rog c.s. in piece No. 8 for three reasons. Firstly, next year is the third and final pilot year of the diagnostic interim test. That is exactly the year where schools and students are most helpful. Secondly, without that last year, the dtt is not finished and there is no instrument that we can easily transfer to the market. Thirdly, the DTT is currently being used in some 200 schools which participate voluntarily and themselves indicate that it has much to gain from it. In short: dissuade. The same applies to the motion in part No. 9. Mr Grashoff has already indicated that it is somewhat less far-reaching. However, the motion assumes that in a final year we can now completely change the essence of that test. That is simply not feasible. We can finish the dtt as it is now. We can make them work properly, so that schools and market players will benefit in the future. We cannot convert the dtt into something that is actually there when you look at, for example, the keys that are in the student tracking systems. This is based more on an annual basis while this test really takes place halfway through the school career, at the end of the school curriculum, to see if students are well on their way to the final exam. I am very well equipped with the Ypma/Straus motion on part No. 10, because with that motion it is made possible to complete the entire pilot and to arrive at a diagnostic intermediate test which can then also be transferred. It is then left to the schools to make their own choices when it comes to how best to invest in a feedback culture. I'd be happy to take over that motion.
