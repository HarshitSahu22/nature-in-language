ParlaMint-NL_2016-09-07-tweedekamer-4.u2	Mr. Chairman. The Committee for the Examination of the Letters of Faith has examined the documents relating to Mrs A.A. Asante in Badhoevedorp and Mr D.A. Van der Ree in Amsterdam. The committee concluded that Mrs A.A. Asante in Badhoevedorp and Mr D.A. Mr van der Ree in Amsterdam was rightly appointed as a member of the Second Chamber of the States-General. The committee therefore proposes that they be admitted as members of the Chamber. To this end, they shall first serve the oath, as prescribed by the Act of Prosecution of Ministers and Members of States-General of 27 February 1992, Official Journal No. 120, to be done. Finally, the Committee asks you to propose to the Chamber that the full report be included in the Acts.
