I should like to comment on the motion for a resolution before the Chamber. Then the motion to part No. 83 by Mrs Leijten and Mrs Keijzer. This concerns the intention to place the funding of social care in the 390 municipalities by 2020. Following the report of the Dannenberg Commission, extensive consultations were held with VNG and Home Affairs. In doing so, it has been decided to express this intention for 2020 precisely to take sufficient time to find out everything and to ensure that this can be mapped out. It is not just a question of what it means for municipalities. The Federation Opvang also welcomes the movement, but says that the municipalities must be ready for it. That means that we have to take our time. In addition, we must take sufficient time to come up with a financing model. We must also consider whether it can be introduced immediately or whether a transitional period is necessary. That is why I would like to stick to my intention, noting that we have enough time until 2020. That's why I'm dissuading the motion.
