ParlaMint-NL_2016-03-29-eerstekamer-3.u1	I ask the Members to stand up. At the beginning of this sitting, we are considering the horrible events that took place in Brussels last week. We do so in the presence of the Belgian Ambassador, Mr Chris Hoornaert. Your Excellency, I'd like to welcome you. 22 March became an ink black and heavy day for our neighbour, Belgium. The next few days, when the full extent of the terrible attacks penetrated, were perhaps even heavier. There are no words for describing the destruction of lives and grief. This attack wasn't on its own. Last Sunday Lahore was also very badly hit. The world has been alarmed by acts of terror decades ago. In some places they hardly got the chance to recover, or they were hit again. The purpose of those who commit these acts of terror is to sow fear and destruction. It is up to us to ensure that we keep our heads down in the midst of that fear and in spite of that fear. We must ensure that fear destroys freedom, respect and tolerance in our society. The key lies not in raising walls, but in building bridges, in searching for connection. Only through cooperation can we defend the core values of our democratic rule of law. Today we show respect for all those who have been affected by these acts of terror, and also for all those who make every effort to restore order, peace and security and prevent another attack. I ask you all to observe a moment of silence.
