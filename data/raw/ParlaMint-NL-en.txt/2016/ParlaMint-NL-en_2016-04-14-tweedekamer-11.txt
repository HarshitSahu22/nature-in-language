ParlaMint-NL_2016-04-14-tweedekamer-11.u3	Mr. Chairman. Following Mrs Straus' motion, I can say that there is already a monitor on the approach of illiteracy and low literacy in the framework of the action programme Tel along with Language. That monitor charts the range and effect of different programs to prevent and combat low literacy and language disadvantages. For example, these are programmes that promote reading and increase reading pleasure, such as BookStart and the School Library, and the approach to low literacy among adults through, among other things, the activities of the Read & Writing Foundation. This monitor gives as complete a picture as possible of the scope and results of the action programme. That programme started in January 2016, an interim monitor will be published in 2017 and a final picture in 2018. So far to the monitor that's coming anyway. Ms Straus asked for a composite monitor to be set up on the basis of different sources. So this monitor is already coming. I could understand the motion in such a way that I add what is required in it to the results of the three-yearly PISA research and other existing sources of information, so that an even more complete picture can be created and more attention paid to the level of language and, if necessary, to the low-literacy of fundamental education. If I may understand the motion in this way, I consider her to be a supporter of policy and I am happy to take her over.
ParlaMint-NL_2016-04-14-tweedekamer-11.u4	We are concerned that the Minister's action programme should focus on the period before children go to school and the period after children or young adults are already out of school. We'd like to make the connection to the school period. During a very large part of this, attention is paid to combating or at least preventing and eliminating language disadvantages. In this sense, I can follow the Minister when she says she wants to add this subject to the existing monitor with the existing sources of information, but they are all very fragmented now. I would very much like to be able to follow it this way and not only during the pre-school period and the period after that, but also in the period between.
