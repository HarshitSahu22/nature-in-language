ParlaMint-NL_2016-03-31-tweedekamer-5.u18	Mr. Chairman. In the motion in part No. 283 calls on the government to treat cross-over training in the same way as regular new training. I advise against this motion. Let me say first of all that the condition for practical placements in cross-overs is not much different in principle than in the case of regular training courses. For all qualifications, there must be sufficient places of practice. We talk about that a lot. We discussed this with each other yesterday in another context. In my view, there is a broad shared desire to focus on the development of professional practice, i.e. the number of places of practice. I think that's something that everyone sees as very important. However, there is a difference between new training courses and existing training courses, because with regular qualifications there are already several recognised learning companies from which a student can choose. The experiment with cross-overs involves new training courses, for which no existing practice is yet available. It is therefore important that it is clear in advance that these practical places will be there, because if they are not there, a student cannot complete the training. It is precisely students who benefit from innovative training that is well suited to the latest developments in the labour market. I would very much like to encourage the involvement of companies. I understand that flexibility is important. It is therefore a good balance between all these interests, but the formulation of this condition also offers scope and should not be a barrier. So I expect companies to show commitment and to indicate their willingness to offer practical places so that students can go through these courses properly. In doing so, we can strike a good balance between encouraging the involvement of companies and safeguarding the interests of students. However, as it has been said in this motion, I think it is a step too far, because this does not take into account the additional challenges for new training and the learning firms that we need to find.
