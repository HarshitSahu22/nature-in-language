This is also happening for mental health care in 2012. Because there have been a lot of changes between 2012 and now for mental health care institutions, this income relief is like a sword of Damocles over some institutions. Because we are concerned about continuity of care, we are tabling the next motion. [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[noting that the final 2012 revenue statement for curative mental health care (ggz) leads to changing budgets after four years;]] [[having regard to the opinion of the Committee on Budgetary Control,]] [[Whereas it is not possible to rule out the risk of continuity of care in advance;]] [[considers it undesirable that a past account jeopardises the continuity of care and/or employment of people;]] [[calls on the Government to ensure that the 2012 revised revenue statement is not so detrimental as to jeopardise the continuity of care for patients and the employment of employees,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion has been tabled by Mr Leijten. ParlaMint-NL_2016-04-21-tweedekamer-8.u3	Mr. Chairman. I was expecting this motion, so I can answer it already. There is no objective basis for avoiding two ggz institutions for which the revised revenue statement is negative, while the revised revenue statement is carried out for all other ggz institutions. If we did that, we would not be able to support it. The risk of objection proceedings and long-term uncertainty would then increase. Therefore, I advise against the motion.
