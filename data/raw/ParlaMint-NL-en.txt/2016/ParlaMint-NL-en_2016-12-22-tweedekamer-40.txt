ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-22-tweedekamer-40.u1	I would like to welcome the Minister for the Interior and Kingdom Relations. I shall be the first to give the floor to Mr Veldman on behalf of the VVD Group.
ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-22-tweedekamer-40.u2	Mr. Chairman. As of 1 January 2015, in the field of youth care, employment and income, and social support, we have decentralised a number of responsibilities to municipalities. Over the past two years, a Transition Committee on Social Domain has been working to everyone's satisfaction. The transition phase is now over. We are now at the beginning of the transformation phase. For that we do not have a committee that looks with municipalities and provinces how it works together. According to the VVD that would be a good idea. That's why I'm filing the next motion. [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[Whereas, as of 1 January 2015, the tasks in the field of youth care, employment and income and social support have been decentralised from the Empire to municipalities;]] [[Whereas the Social Domain Transition Committee, which has now been dissolved, has played an important supporting and encouraging role in these decentralisations towards the Empire and the municipalities;]] [[Whereas, now that the first stage of decentralisation has been completed, the Empire and the municipalities still need to take the necessary measures to further shape the change in the social domain;]] [[calls on the Government to appoint an independent third party to follow up the three decentralisations with the task of keeping an eye on the pulse of the social transformation, to discuss its findings with municipalities and to report to the Cabinet on this;]] [[invites the Government to send these reports to the Chamber,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion was tabled by Mr Veldman and Mr Fokke. It seems to me that their submission is sufficiently supported.]] [[She's getting No. kst-34477-12]]
ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-22-tweedekamer-40.u3	Mr. Chairman. [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[Whereas the credo "one family, one plan, one director" was the starting point for the decentralisation of youth care;]] [[noting that this director's function is often filled out by a youth support professional who is not part of the circle of intimacy of the child and/or his or her family;]] [[Whereas it is primarily in the best interests of the child when this role of director is taken by someone who is (already) part of the child's network;]] [[noting that the Transition Committee on Social Domain expressed similar criticism in its final report on Social Domain;]] [[noting that in its response to this report, the Cabinet underlined the principle of "going out of its own direction," but subsequently did not add any concrete objectives;]] [[calls on the Government to draw up concrete plans and measures to further stimulate and secure the "own direction" of people in the field of youth assistance and to inform the Court of Justice as soon as possible,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion has been proposed by Mr Klein. ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-22-tweedekamer-40.u4	I can see that the Minister is quite ready for the answer. Go ahead.
ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-22-tweedekamer-40.u5	Mr. Chairman. As regards the motion in part No. 12: As we discussed in general consultation with each other, it is my intention to start an intergovernmental programme together with the municipalities as a follow-up to the Transition Committee on Social Domain. But I can well imagine that the Chamber says more specifically that she liked that committee and that, as in the operative part, she wants to designate a third party to fill in this task. I'll leave that judgment to the Chamber. If this motion is adopted, I will decorate it with the congregations along the lines as described in the motion. The operative part of Mr Klein's motion means that the Empire must draw up programmes to fill in the precise details of the direction of youth care. I advise against this motion because we have just handed over this task to local government. I also agree with the consideration that the transformation, i.e. offering comprehensive policies on the ground, is not yet complete. That has yet to be done. We will work on this together with the municipalities. We have just discussed a motion which refers to a third party who thinks about it. I do not recommend that the Chamber fill this out here, because that is contrary to the idea of decentralisation. I therefore advise against this motion.
ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-22-tweedekamer-40.u6	Thank you very much.
