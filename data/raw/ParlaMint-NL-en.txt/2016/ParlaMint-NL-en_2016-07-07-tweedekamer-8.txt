ParlaMint-NL_2016-07-07-tweedekamer-8.u1	Before proceeding with the plenary agenda, we are considering the death of the two Dutch soldiers on the UN mission MINUSMA in Mali. I would ask everyone in the Chamber and in the public gallery to stand as far as possible. Less than two weeks ago, the king here in the Lower House revealed the List of fallen during wars and missions since World War II. It is a monument of the States-General with the names of over 6,400 Dutch soldiers who have participated in wars and missions to build a more stable, safer and freer world and who have never returned home. Yesterday, in the evening of 6 July 2016, we received the sad news that we have to add two names to that list of fallen. In a tragic accident during a mortar shooting exercise in Mali, two Dutch soldiers died: First Class Sergeant Henry Hoving, 29 years old, and Corporal Kevin Roggeveld, only 24 years old. Both soldiers were part of the mortar group of the C Company of the 13 infantry battalion of the Airmobile Brigade in Assen. During the accident in Kidal, a place in northern Mali, a third military was seriously injured. On behalf of the House of Representatives, I wish their families, friends and colleagues a great deal of strength in this dark and difficult time. We have the greatest respect for Henry, Kevin and the wounded military. They are soldiers who, under very difficult and difficult circumstances, wanted to do the right thing and make the difference. That requires commitment and courage. I hope that science will offer some comfort to their next of kin somewhere. In our minds and in our hearts, we are with them. Together with the chairman of the Permanent Defence Commission, I am sending a letter on behalf of all of us to the next of kin to express our condolences. I would ask you to observe a moment of silence.
ParlaMint-NL_2016-07-07-tweedekamer-8.u2	Thank you very much.
