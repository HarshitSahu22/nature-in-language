ParlaMint-NL_2016-03-31-tweedekamer-8.u3	Mr. Chairman. Mrs Vos once again expressed her concern about the postal market situation, as she did during the general discussion we had on the postal market. Other spokesmen also shared their concerns about the situation in the postal market. In the Netherlands we have a mail market with shrinking volumes. Last year the shrinkage was 8%, and this year we expect another 8% contraction. At the same time, we have strong competition in that market. This means that profit margins are very under pressure and costs increase. That situation is currently, in the short term, good for the customers, but it is doubtful whether this will continue to be the case in the long term. There are signs that give us the opportunity to look into the situation in the postal market and to see whether the conditions for good, healthy development are fulfilled in the future, or whether measures are needed. Mrs Vos said this together with Mr Bosma in a motion asking for two investigations and for the search for alignment with the ACM. To start with the latter: the ACM has its own powers, is independent and makes its own decisions. Nevertheless, it is wise to seek coordination if the ACM conducts research or if we as the Ministry of Economic Affairs have investigations done. I welcome the inquiries requested in the motion. I am already preparing for this, because I have reached the same conclusion as the authors of the motion. That's why I'm taking over the motion. As soon as possible, I will work on the implementation of the motion.
