ParlaMint-NL_2016-03-22-tweedekamer-2.u1	This morning we were alarmed by reports of a series of attacks at the airport and at various metro stations. In addition, people were killed and injured. How much exactly is still unclear. The panic and chaos are great. The closer it gets, the more unsafe it feels. In an open and free society like ours, we will never give way to this kind of violence. Here in the Chamber, our thoughts are with all those people in Brussels and in the rest of Belgium who are involved in these terrible and cowardly attacks; with the victims and their families. We hope that there will soon be more clarity and will commemorate these shocking attacks at a later date.
