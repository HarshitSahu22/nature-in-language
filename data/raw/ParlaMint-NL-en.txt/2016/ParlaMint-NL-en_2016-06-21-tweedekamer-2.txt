ParlaMint-NL_2016-06-21-tweedekamer-2.u1	Before we begin oral Question Time, we are considering the murder of Jo Cox. I would like to welcome the United Kingdom Ambassador and our Prime Minister. I would ask everyone in the Chamber to stand as far as possible. Last Thursday, 16 June, together with the rest of the world, we were shocked by the cowardly and pointless attack on British MEP Jo Cox. She was so young. Tomorrow she would have been 42 years old. She was a wife, mother of two children, a woman in the thriving of her life and career, full of ideas and ideals. We're currently investigating whether the suspect had a political motive. But whatever the background to this cowardly act, hatred and violence will never win over the debate, an open dialogue, the freedom to say yes or no. These are the core values of our parliamentary democracy. In the Netherlands, we have experienced at close range what it feels like to have these core values, the right to say what you think, to be under fire and what this does to society. On behalf of the House of Representatives, I wished the United Kingdom much strength, peace and solidarity at a time of great political contradictions and uncertainty. A lot of people are gonna get their lives back together. That does not apply to Jo Cox's family, family, friends and colleagues. They will have to learn to live with the loss of a special woman who wanted to realize her ideas and ideals with words and arguments. Our thoughts are with them. I would ask you to observe a moment of silence.
