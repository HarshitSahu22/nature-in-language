[[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[Whereas there is still much room for improvement in the cooperation between childcare, early and pre-school education and schools;]] [[noting that this is often caused by differences in regulation, financing and supervision;]] [[noting that there are already many initiatives in municipalities to achieve a broad provision in which reception, development and education come together, but these initiatives are often still faced with regulatory problems;]] [[calls on the government to take further steps in this cabinet period to give greater scope to the development of these wide-ranging provisions,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion has been proposed by Mr Van Meenen. ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-14-tweedekamer-3.u3	Mr. Chairman. I don't have a motion, but I think it's important to say a few things. We have had a major debate on the deficit policy, the indicator and the measure of the distribution of resources and the way children are achieved in larger, medium-sized and smaller municipalities. We have also done this because investing in young children for all groups is important in order to prevent delays and to provide them with a flying start in education, but also in order to prevent division later. It is also important to be able to make good use of all talents in the labour market later on. The debates over the last year and a half have therefore been intense, but very relevant. I was extremely pleased with the letter we received yesterday, which shows the state of play of the new indicator and the new measure. For a majority in the Chamber, actually for all political groups, it was the question of whether the current measure would be good enough for all children. The letter is encouraging, because it seems that there really is a much better indicator that will enable us to reach all the children in all the municipalities much better. I look forward to the debate and I hope that in the debates we can reach a joint decision on a good measure by which we can really reach all the children in all the municipalities. I also asked for the floor to thank the Secretary of State for the proper implementation of the motion, as far as we can see now. I appreciate this very much, because it is very important for the future of children, but also for policy. My group attaches great importance to this.
ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-14-tweedekamer-3.u4	The Secretary of State can answer directly.
ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-14-tweedekamer-3.u5	Mr. Chairman. It was a good debate on an important issue. Mrs Yücel has already said that we will probably go into more detail about the indicator in the near future. I believe that the CBS has done a good job in achieving a better prediction of backlogs. It is a double blow, because schools will soon be able to 'care' about the many paperwork they are dealing with today. That is the subject of a later debate. We have now discussed precisely what is preventing cooperation between childcare and schools. I think we all have a desire to make these two work together more. I simply wonder whether Mr Van Meenen's motion adds anything to that, because his motion is similar to Mrs Yücel's previous motions and the cabinet has succeeded in following them. At the moment, a task force is looking at what can still be simplified. I do not recommend Mr Van Meenen's motion on item No. 349 because it is unnecessary.
