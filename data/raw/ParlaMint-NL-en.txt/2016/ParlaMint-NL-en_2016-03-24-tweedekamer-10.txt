ParlaMint-NL_2016-03-24-tweedekamer-10.u6	Mr. Chairman. The first motion, the Voortman motion in part No. 291, deals with risk profiles. The GGD is a supervisor on behalf of the municipality and the college of B and W designates the GGD as a supervisor. In doing so, the GGD can use a risk profile as an indication of which organisations one wants to look at. A municipality may then decide as the final responsible to disclose such profiles. There is no legal basis for me to require municipalities to publish or provide risk profiles for any national database. Moreover, risk profiles are not uniform or already mandatory in all respects, so publication will not lead to comparable insights. In short, I would advise against the motion in part No. 291.
