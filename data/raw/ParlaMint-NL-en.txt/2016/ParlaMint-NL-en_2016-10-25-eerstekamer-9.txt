ParlaMint-NL_2016-10-25-eerstekamer-9.u1	I have already welcomed the Minister for Health, Welfare and Sport. I would remind the House that on 11 October last, Mrs Teunissen held her motion under letter S, on access to medical records, also to be possible on a decentralised basis through medical consents and authorisations, and asked for an amended motion to be tabled in a short fourth term. I give the floor to Mrs Teunissen.
ParlaMint-NL_2016-10-25-eerstekamer-9.u2	Mr. Chairman. Thank you very much for allowing me to table an amended motion in the fourth term. I will keep my explanations as brief as possible. When the Minister first indicated that she had no difficulty with the first part of the request in my motion and on the second part indicated that he did not want to actively promote one system because of the neutrality of the government, I did not even have to consult myself, but immediately after the debate my phone was glowing with calls from specialists from the field of privacy and care. They asked me to do everything in my power to ensure that the motion has the broadest possible support in this house, because they believe that the motion can be an important anchor for the patient's privacy, and to prevent privacy protection from becoming the enemy of good care. I therefore, in consultation with the co-subscribers in the motion with the letter S, deleted the last part of the operative part. I have also extended the term "doctor" to the broader term "care provider."
ParlaMint-NL_2016-10-25-eerstekamer-9.u3	The movement-Teunissen c.s. (' point ') has been amended to read as follows: [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[noting that progress has been made in recent years in the development of central systems for the exchange of medical data;]] [[noting the need for healthcare providers to be able to provide improved access to, or access to, patient data to other healthcare providers for responsible care;]] [[whereas both doctors and patients should be able to rely on their ability to achieve this access as privacy-protected as possible;]] [[Whereas the centralised processing of sensitive medical personal data and privacy protection may be at odds;]] [[calls on the Government to ensure that access to the medical file is not only centralised, but also decentralised through authorisations and authorisations established by the healthcare provider,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This amended motion was signed by Mr Teunissen, Mr Wezel, Mr Don, Mr Ganzevoort, Mr Bredenoord, Mr Koffeman and Mr Gerkens.]] [[She's getting No. kst-33509-T]]
ParlaMint-NL_2016-10-25-eerstekamer-9.u4	Mr. Chairman. When I see the consideration before the request, that centralised processing of sensitive medical personal data and privacy protection may be at odds, I note that this is the case for all systems. It depends on how you've managed the security and privacy of a system. I have often debated electronic data exchange in this Chamber and I have been explicitly instructed by the First Chamber to organise it in such a way that it is system-neutral. We give room to all systems and so the law does not regulate that one system goes above another system. We leave that to the field. If it is noted that progress has mainly been made in the development of central systems, it is because the field of other systems seems to be less organized. It is up to the healthcare provider to determine how the exchange and registration is carried out, as long as the system complies with the required safety requirements. Initiatives from the field are made possible as long as they meet the requirements of the present legislative proposal. In view of this, I can only conclude that the motion is superfluous, because what is requested here can already be done.
