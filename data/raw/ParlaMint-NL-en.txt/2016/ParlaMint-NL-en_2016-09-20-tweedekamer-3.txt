ParlaMint-NL_2016-09-20-tweedekamer-3.u1	I propose that, as committees referred to in Rule 32 (2) and 37 (1) of the Rules of Procedure: the Intelligence and Security Services Commission; the Committee on Petitions and Citizens' Initiatives; the Committee for the Examination of the Letters of Faith. I propose that the Millions Note, the Autumn Note and the Spring Note be put in the hands of the Permanent Finance Committee. I also propose that the proposals of budgetary laws and the draft final laws, together with the reports accompanying the accounts of the Court of Auditors and the supplementary budget proposals, be put in the hands of the relevant standing committees. I suggest: to place the budget chapters I, IIA, IIB and IIIA to C and the budgets of the Municipal Fund and the Province Fund in the hands of the Permanent Committee for the Interior; to place the budgets of the Infrastructure Fund and the Delta Fund in the hands of the permanent Commission for Infrastructure and the Environment.
