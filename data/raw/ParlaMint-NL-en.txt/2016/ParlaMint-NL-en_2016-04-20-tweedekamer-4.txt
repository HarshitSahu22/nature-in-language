There is good consultation with the so-called Security Council. As part of my system of responsibility, I am talking to them about this. I therefore advise against this motion.

ParlaMint-NL_2016-04-20-tweedekamer-4.u16	The motion in part No. Mrs Tellegen's 31 points out a problem on which I have been very intensively consulted in recent months with the security regions and the Security Council. I would like to outline the situation very briefly, because we have indeed not discussed it in general consultation. It has been agreed that the security regions will assume responsibility for the rescue capacity on their own waters. This also fits in with the system we agreed on. A transitional period has been agreed for this; I say, in my mind, that this is a five-year period, but perhaps it is a little shorter. During that period, my department would finance the National Rescue Fleet. The implementation is in the hands of the Dutch Rescue Brigade, supplemented by the KNRM in a number of areas. The scheme ended in 2015. At that time, of course, we said that it was time that this responsibility was taken over by the security regions on 1 January. That turned out to be very complicated at the time, because there was still a lot of consultation to be done. Then I said that in order to meet that process, I wanted to extend my funding for another year, but that it really ended by the end of 2016. Then the security regions really have to take over that responsibility in accordance with the agreement. The Dutch Rescue Brigade is now very concerned about this, because there are no agreements with the security regions. I understand the concerns of the Rescue Brigade. I note that it is ultimately up to the security regions themselves to decide how and through whom they will take care of the National Rescue Fleet, but that does not mean that there must be clarity, including for the Rescue Brigade. For this reason, I would like to leave the judgment on this motion to the Chamber.
