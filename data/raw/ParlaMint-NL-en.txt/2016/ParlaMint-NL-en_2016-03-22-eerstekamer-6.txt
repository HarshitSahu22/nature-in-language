ParlaMint-NL_2016-03-22-eerstekamer-6.u1	Mr Van Apeldoorn made a request at the meeting of 1 March last for a brief reopening of the General Financial Reviews, as he wished to table a motion previously held in amended form. This motion on the fight against child poverty in the Netherlands was originally tabled during the General Financial Reviews of 17 November and was subsequently discussed during an oral consultation with the Secretary of State for Social Affairs and Employment. I welcome the Secretary of State.
ParlaMint-NL_2016-03-22-eerstekamer-6.u2	Mr. Chairman. During the General Financial Reviews, I tabled a motion on behalf of my group on 17 November last on child poverty in the Netherlands. The motion noted that the number of poor children in the Netherlands, despite the efforts of the cabinet, continued to increase so many years after the start of the crisis. This is a finding confirmed by a report by the CBS last December. The motion called on the government to investigate how we could achieve a more effective policy to reduce child poverty. The motion has been held in order to allow further consultation with the Secretary of State. That oral consultation took place the day before the ceremony. Following this positive discussion, other groups have also been consulted. There's been an informal meeting with the Secretary of State. There was also a discussion with the President of the Economic and Social Council, after the idea was born to have the desired research carried out by the Council. On that basis, I would now like to table the slightly amended motion in the third term.
