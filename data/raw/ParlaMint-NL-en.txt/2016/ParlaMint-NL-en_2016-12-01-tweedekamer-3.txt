ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-01-tweedekamer-3.u3	Mr. Chairman. I think Mr Bosma speaks well of the term Wi-Fi by doing it in English, if only because the title of the proposal is Wifi4EU. However, I'm used to speaking Wi-Fi on his Dutch and I'm just going to keep doing that. Mr Bosma pointed out that there were different techniques for mobile communication: 4G, 5G and Wi-Fi. The European Union wants to subsidise wifi. It wants to make available an amount to encourage its use. The question is whether you should do that. It is important that the European Union's resources are used effectively for necessary things. In Europe, there is still a debate to be held on whether we should do this, or whether it has added value. I see Mr Bosma's and Mrs Mulder's motion, in which this is approached critically, in support of my policy. I'd like to leave that to the Chamber. In his second motion, Mr Bosma stated that he was concerned about the shift of powers from the minister to the ACM, the Dutch supervisor, but also to Europe. BEREC and the European Commission are getting things to say about this, while we in the Netherlands have been talking about it so far. If I am competent for anything, that means that I have to answer to the Chamber, and that the Chamber can influence that process. We need to be critical of getting that out of there and laying it down with supervisors or bodies in that sphere. In doing so, you withdraw it from democratic control and it ends up in a bureaucratic environment. As far as spectrum policy is concerned, there is Mr Bosma's motion, particularly in relation to the issue of the European Union, I think it is very useful to strengthen cooperation between the countries of the European Union and between the supervisors in those countries. I see opportunities to move forward in the harmonisation of technical requirements for the use of frequency space. It is very useful to formulate common goals and principles, to exchange auction designs and to increase transparency in intended auctions, but I am not in favour of transferring them now to BEREC, the umbrella of supervisors in the European Union, and the European Commission, so that they can also have a right of veto in decisions taken by ACM in the area of access regulation, for example. Mr Bosma is not in favour of this either. He calls on the Chamber to give a ruling on this, and I would like to leave it to the Chamber. I believe that this supports me in further action in the European context on this issue.
ParlaMint-NL_2016-12-01-tweedekamer-3.u4	I propose that we vote on the motions today. I thank the Minister and suspend the sitting for a few minutes.
