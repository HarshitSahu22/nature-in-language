ParlaMint-NL_2022-06-07-tweedekamer-37.u7	Thank you, Mr President. Thank you to Mr Nijboer for both motions, if only to underline how important it is that we should indeed say goodbye to those lead pipes. There is progress on this subject, but it is not fast enough. That was also the reason for me to announce a ban on rental homes and child locations in the Building Decree and to include that lead pipes should no longer be used in rental homes and child locations. For these groups, therefore, I welcome a motion tabled by Mr Nijboer, Mr Beckerman and Mr Bromet. That's the same trinity, by the way. There is therefore an extraordinary consistency in left-wing cooperation. That's encouraging. In my view, this is a very important response to the previous motion. The motion in part No. 568 seems to go even further, because it is not so much about child locations and rentals, but about a total ban. That is what I argued in the letter I sent to the Chamber that I think that goes a long way. The Health Council's advice is really aimed at protecting vulnerable groups. These are tenants who themselves have no influence on how the pipes in their home are structured and what they are made of. These are children in child locations, for example. Children are especially vulnerable to lead pipes. A general ban would see the ownership situation. I think that's a long way to go. I don't think that's necessary either. The Association Eigen Huis also has no indication that it would be a major problem, so they are not for that either. For this reason, I would like to advise against the motion, with which I do not want to say that I do not share the issue and that I would not share the commitment of Mr Nijboer, Mrs Bromet and Mrs Beckerman. Because I do share it, albeit for rental locations and for child locations. The motion in part No. 569 is about the free segment. People in the free segment should also have the opportunity to go to the Rent Committee. I think a number of important developments are going to take place there. If we make it part of the Building Decision in relation to rental locations, then the corridor is open to either the municipality, the municipal building and housing control, or the Rent Commission, especially since we are going to regulate a large part of the middle rent, which falls most of the rentals in this segment. So they will probably be able to go to the Rent Commission in 2024, as soon as the law enters into force. In short, I think that this already provides for protection, including along the way of the Rent Committee, although protection through the Building Decision is mainly a protection through municipal building and housing control. I do not think that this motion is necessary. I therefore advise against this motion.
