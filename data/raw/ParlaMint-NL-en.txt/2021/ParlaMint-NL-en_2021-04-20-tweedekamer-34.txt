ParlaMint-NL_2021-04-20-tweedekamer-34.u3	Mr. Chairman. Let me say that I think it is very important that Dutch citizens and governments have a say in the decision-making process in the neighbouring countries. This applies both to Belgium, where we have discussed this a number of times, and to Germany. There is currently no concrete intention in Germany to store radioactive waste in salt domes in Eemsland. What is at issue is that the German government has started a process for the location choice for a final disposal. The Espoo Convention obliges Germany to involve the Netherlands in the search process in the EIA procedures. Participation is possible via the route to be indicated by the German authorities in due course. The Ministry has regular contact with the German authorities concerned and will participate in the participation procedures. So we're in constant consultation. But because I really want to emphasise how important I think it is that we not only provide theoretical opportunities for participation, but that we also ensure that people are really well informed in due course, I give this motion judgment Chamber.
