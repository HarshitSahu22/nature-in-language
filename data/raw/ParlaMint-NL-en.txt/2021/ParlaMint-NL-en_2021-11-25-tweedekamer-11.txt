And two, given his main function and secondary functions, someone can perform this assignment independently. There is no doubt about Mr De Grave's experience and expertise, and since he has been in a very neutral position as a member of the Council of State for three years and does not fulfil any additional functions conflicting with this investigation, we can assume that he will fill this function independently. And if we were to use the principle that someone who has held political positions in the past is no longer neutral and independent, then we would question the integrity of people in advance and thus damage our political culture. Then no former politician can conduct an investigation or perform a function for the Council of State, the General Court of Auditors or any other neutral body. Please, let's not go down this sliding bowl together. This is damaging our system and our political culture, which are calling for reinforcement at this time. That's the culture that we determine with each other here, in this Chamber. I assume that the integrity of every former politician who subsequently performs a neutral function is beyond any doubt, whether it is a PvdAer, a SP'er, a PVVer, a CDAer or a VVDer, or anyone of any political colour.
