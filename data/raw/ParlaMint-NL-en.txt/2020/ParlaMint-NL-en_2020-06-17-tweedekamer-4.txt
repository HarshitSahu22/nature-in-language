I propose the VSO CBR and medical examinations of driving fitness (29398, No. 809), with the first speaker, Mrs Postma, on behalf of the CDA, to be added to the agenda of the Chamber. I propose to add the following legislative proposals to the Chamber's agenda: Amendment of the budget statement of the Ministry of Finance (IXB) for the year 2020 (Third incidental supplementary budget on the employment and economic emergency package 2.0 and COVID-19 crisis measure SURE) (35466); Amendment of the budget statement of the Ministry of Social Affairs and Employment (XV) for the year 2020 (Third incidental supplementary budget on the employment and economic emergency package 2.0) (35473); Rules on secondary education (Law on secondary education 20xx) (35297); Amendment of the Participation Act and some other laws related to improving the labour cost subsidy scheme and some other changes (implementation and broad offensive) (35394). I propose to vote on Tuesday, 23 June next, on the motions that have been tabled in connection with the submission of notes on the Netherlands and on the global approach of COOVID-19 and on Care. At the request of the applicant, I propose that the debate on research by the LAKS on school exams that have not been completed should be included in the agenda review programme. I propose that the debate on the Spring Note be added to the Chamber's agenda, with maximum speaking time of: 10 minutes for VVD, PVV, CDA and D66; seven minutes for GroenLinks, SP and PvdA; five minutes for Christian Union, Animal Party, 50PLUS, SGP, DENK and Forum for Democracy; two and a half minutes for the Krol/Van Kooten-Arissen Group and the Van Haga member. At the request of the applicants, I propose that the following motions be renewed: 33037-317; 35000-XIV-23; 25087-199.
