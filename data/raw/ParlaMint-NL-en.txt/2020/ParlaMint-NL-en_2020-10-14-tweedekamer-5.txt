ParlaMint-NL_2020-10-14-tweedekamer-5.u1	We now come to a brief settlement of the matter. I propose that, in the coming period, debates on the development of the coronavirus should be placed on the agenda of the Chamber, that these debates should be brought to an end after the announcement of new measures in this area, and that the group size should be used when determining the order of speakers. I propose to vote on Tuesday 27 October on: the legislative proposal Adoption of the Cooperation Agreement on Partnership and Development between the European Union and its Member States, of the one part, and the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan, of the other part, established in Munich on 18 February 2017 (Trb. 2017, 45) (35445); the motions tabled in the note-to-note consultation on road safety. Since the time limits for the following documents have expired, I propose that these documents be adopted for notification: 35300-XIII-107; 3019-619; 30872-249; 30872-248; 35570-XVI-8; 33977-26; 35434-11. At the request of a number of Members, I propose that the following motions be renewed: 21501-07-1633; 35468-30; 35309-7; 35300-XII-41; 35000-VII-30; 31532-244; 31532-243.
