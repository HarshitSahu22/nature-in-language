ParlaMint-NL_2020-05-27-tweedekamer-4.u1	We now have a brief arrangement of work. I propose to vote on Tuesday 2 June on three legislative proposals for changes to the Constitution: the legislative proposal Declaration that there is reason to consider a proposal for a change in the Constitution aimed at repealing additional articles that have been drafted (35417); the legislative proposal Declaration that there is reason to consider a proposal to change the Constitution to include a provision on a electoral college elected by non-residents for the election of the First Chamber of the States General (35418); the legislative proposal Declaration that there is reason to consider a proposal for a change in the Constitution of the provision on changes in the Constitution (revised Constitutional revision procedure) (35419). I also propose to vote on Tuesday 2 June on: Mr Segers and Mr Marijnissen's own-initiative paper on an Action Plan on labour migration submitted the motions to the memo. (35409, No. 10) would have liked to have voted in favour of this motion.
