ParlaMint-NL_2020-02-11-tweedekamer-7.u1	I now give the floor to Mrs Leijten to report on behalf of the Committee on the Verification of Credentials and Immunities. I call Mrs Leijten.
ParlaMint-NL_2020-02-11-tweedekamer-7.u2	Mr. Chairman. There are three seats on behalf of the Netherlands in the European Parliament. The Committee for the Examination of the Letters of Faith therefore examined the documents relating to Mr M.J.R.L. the Count in Landgraaf, Mr B. Groothuis in Voorburg and Mrs D. Rookmaker in Hattem. The committee concluded that they were rightly appointed members of the European Parliament in three additional seats. The committee finds that they can be admitted to the European Parliament on the basis of national provisions. The committee proposes that this be communicated to the President of the European Parliament and to those appointed. Finally, the Committee proposes that the full report be included in the Acts.
ParlaMint-NL_2020-02-11-tweedekamer-7.u3	I would like to thank the committee for its report and propose that it be decided accordingly.
ParlaMint-NL_2020-02-11-tweedekamer-7.u4	Thank you very much, Mrs Leijten.
