ParlaMint-NL_2020-11-18-tweedekamer-3.u3	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. The concerns expressed in this motion are understandable. But there are two nuances to that. There are European countries with weaker competitiveness. If they are going to do better because of investments and reforms, it is of course also good for the Netherlands. The RRF plans and the resulting money will also have to comply with State aid frameworks. So that's the first comment. The cabinet has previously agreed to send all RRPs, i.e. the plans under that Recovery and Resilience Facility, to the Chamber with a cabinet juice valuation as soon as the European Commission has looked at these plans. This appreciation can therefore include the element of a level playing field. An overall analysis of all Member States' plans is only possible once the European Commission has approved these plans. The proposal is therefore that the Cabinet should share with the Chamber the Commission's overview of the RRF and the Member States' plans, with a brief cabinet response. How often and in what way is reported by the Member States is still part of the negotiations. We now assume that's annual. We will then be able to inform you every year, but that will depend on what the outcome of these negotiations will ultimately be in Europe. With this remark, I shall leave this motion to the House.
