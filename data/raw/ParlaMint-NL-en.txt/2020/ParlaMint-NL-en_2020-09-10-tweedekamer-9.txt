ParlaMint-NL_2020-09-10-tweedekamer-9.u1	As those who follow the debate in the Chamber via the livestream have seen, I have just had a moment with the spokesmen. We would start with the debate on the corona spread in relation to international passenger traffic. But we have noted that elsewhere in the Chamber, in a committee meeting, a debate is taking place, called a 'general consultation' which, in our terms, follows the fire that raged in the refugee camp at Lesbos. Yesterday, the Chamber decided that there should be a general debate on this report this evening, a plenary debate, including the votes. That would make the evening a bit chaotic if we started this debate. In addition, the vote on this matter could not take place until 22 September. We agreed that this debate should be held in plenary during that week and that we should also vote on it in that week. In terms of urgency, I do not want to change the subject, but when it comes to the decisions and the votes, that does not mean that much time is wasted. For those who had expected us to be able to deal with this issue now, I regret that it cannot be done, but there are also other priorities this evening that we will be discussing in plenary. That will happen later in the evening. I shall now suspend until that moment and thank my fellow Members for their constructive attitude.
