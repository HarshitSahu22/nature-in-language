ParlaMint-NL_2020-09-02-tweedekamer-5.u1	We now come to the order of business. At the request of the Permanent Committee on the Interior, I propose to add to the Chamber's agenda a debate on the parliamentary investigation report "Update required - Towards greater parliamentary grip on digitisation" (35229, no. 9). I also propose to add to the agenda the proposal for a general revision of the Rules of Procedure (35322). I also propose to vote on Tuesday 8 September on the legislative proposal Amendment of the EU Energy Efficiency Directives Implementation Act in order to provide a basis for the energy audit (35435) and on the Sneller/Kuiks motion (35470-I, No. 8). At the request of the applicants, I propose that the following 30 Members' debates be removed from the agenda: on the notice that the Netherlands does not have a plan B in the event of a further rise in sea level; on the message that passengers in public transport pay too much due to differences in tariff systems; about the news that only English will be spoken at the University of Twente from 1 January 2020. At the request of a number of Members, I propose that the following motions be repeated: 337 and 30015, No. 67.
