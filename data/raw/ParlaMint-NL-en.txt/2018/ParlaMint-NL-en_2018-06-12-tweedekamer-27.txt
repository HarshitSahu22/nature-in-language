ParlaMint-NL_2018-06-12-tweedekamer-27.u20	Mr President, thank you very much. All three motions tabled by Mrs Bergkamp concern the investigation into the effects of medicinal cannabis. When one of the predecessors, Mrs Els Borst, started the Agency for Medicinal Cannabis in 2003, the main reason was that cannabis of pharmaceutical quality became available. Clinical studies could then be performed with this medicinal cannabis. Partly because of this a cannabis medicine could be developed. The Agency for Medicinal Cannabis, BMC, was given the task of facilitating the investigation and not to carry it out on its own. This facilitating role has therefore been invested at BMC from the outset. Mr. Chairman. That was in 2003. It's all been a long time, taking on that research role by BMC. This is due, among other things, to the fact that developing a placebo, so that you can do good research into the effects of the medicinal cannabis on one side and a kind of control placebo on the other, has long been delayed. Also in the past, I was told, there was not much interest in pharmaceutical research. Things have changed lately. I understood that from the round table that BMC held in which, among other things, the LUMC participated, but also pharmacists participated. The urge to do that research has grown. I say this in advance of the appreciation I would like to express on the three motions tabled by Mrs Bergkamp. I'll get to the other two motions. Mr. Chairman. The operative part of the first Bergkamp motion reads: "calls on the government to have a short-term study of the effects of medicinal cannabis on patients with specific indications." The request is also to inform the Court. It is possible that Mrs Bergkamp is referring here, for example, to the research that is already going on into medicinal cannabis in the situation of a specific disease, namely the soft tissue rheumatism. I expect the first results of this study to be known after the summer. I just mentioned the round table talks with the field. That's when we looked into the possibility of more research. That depends on finding suitable indications for research. I'm going to ask interested parties to submit a research proposal to the Agency for Medicinal Cannabis. I would then like to inform you of this during the year. That means I'll leave the verdict on this motion to the Chamber.
