ParlaMint-NL_2018-11-28-tweedekamer-13.u4	Thank you, Madam President. Before I give the verdict on the motion, I would like to make a few points. It is an interesting motion, of course also with what, if I may put it that way, tendentious words, for example plofkip, and a few assumptions. The trade policy of the Netherlands is, of course, much wider than just imports and exports of animal products. This is important, but trade is much wider. It concerns trade in investment, technology, knowledge and the building of growth markets. So it's much wider. That is the first big comment. The second comment is the specific point that trade and animal welfare are used a little over-the-counter or mixed. Animal welfare is a standard element in all trade questions negotiated by the EU. That's two. The third is that any outcome is not necessarily the outcome of negotiations. These are very important comments to make to the Chamber. The last or second paragraph calls on the government to review its policy on agriculture in the light of the objectives set in the agricultural vision. The cabinet, of course, always focuses on policy coherence between the different ministries. Trade is, of course, also a much wider area. Keeping everything under review, I'd say that's what we do all the time. This has also been done in the drafting of the agricultural vision by Minister Schouten van Landbouw. In my view, it is not clear what is meant by 'testing' and what obligations may be derived from it. Having said this, I believe that the message of coherence and animal welfare is important. Having said that, I leave the motion for a resolution to the Chamber, but with all these reservations, comments which I consider to be very important.
