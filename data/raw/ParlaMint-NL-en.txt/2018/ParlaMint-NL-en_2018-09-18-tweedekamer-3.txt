ParlaMint-NL_2018-09-18-tweedekamer-3.u1	As previously decided, the Chamber will start tomorrow with the General Political Reviews. I propose to designate as committees referred to in Rule 32 (2) and 37 (1) of the Rules of Procedure: the Intelligence and Security Services Commission; the Working Method Committee; the Committee on Petitions and Citizens' Initiatives; the Committee for the Examination of the Letters of Faith. I propose that the Millions Note, the Autumn Note and the Spring Note be put in the hands of the Permanent Finance Committee. I also propose that the proposals of budgetary laws and the draft final laws containing the reports on the accounts of the Court of Auditors and the supplementary budget proposals on the spring note and the autumn note should be put in the hands of the relevant standing committees. I suggest that: the budget chapters I, IIA, IIB and IIIA to C and the budgets of the Municipal Fund and the Province Fund are placed in the hands of the Permanent Committee for the Interior; the budget of the Infrastructure Fund and the Delta Fund are held by the permanent Commission for Infrastructure and Water Management; the Animal Health Fund budget is held by the Permanent Committee for Agriculture, Nature and Food Quality.
ParlaMint-NL_2018-09-18-tweedekamer-3.u2	This brings us to the end of this sitting. I shall close the sitting and thank everyone in the Chamber and in the public gallery.
