The income-dependent childcare allowance will be increased. In this way, almost all parents advance and work becomes more rewarding. This also further stimulates the combination of work and care. It has also been taken into account that in the past the cuts have been particularly reduced in the case of middle and higher incomes. And it also follows the WRR's advice to make childcare cheaper for middle income. In addition, parents with a minimum income will be reimbursed 96% of the costs. That's 94% now. At the moment I have no explanation for the differences in the figures from Buitenhek's research and SEO's research. In coordination with field parties, it has been agreed that SEO will conduct an additional study to try to explain the figures and the differences in them.
