ParlaMint-NL_2018-05-24-tweedekamer-3.u2	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. The Minister indicated that he was in favour of the local deviation from the national energy performance coefficient standard. In our view, the Crisis and Recovery Act is designed to stimulate construction production and has not been created to impose even stricter sustainability requirements for construction by local authorities. Hence the next motion. [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[whereas the Netherlands is in favour of a strong construction plan;]] [[Considering that in the seventeenth tranche of the Crisis and Recovery Act, six municipalities may, as an experiment, deviate from the national energy performance coefficient referred to in the Building Decision 2012, i.e. the EPC standards;]] [[noting that the current national standard implements the realisation of sustainable housing;]] [[noting that stricter and arbitrary local sustainability requirements lead to delays and uncertainty for the construction sector;]] [[considers that stricter and arbitrary EPC standards do not contribute to the rapid realisation of more housing;]] [[calls on the Government to delete Article 6p Decision implementing Crisis and Recovery Act (seventh tranche),]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion has been proposed by the Member Laan-Selschap. ParlaMint-NL_2018-05-24-tweedekamer-3.u3	Mr. Chairman. Perhaps a little contrary to the previous speaker, we think it is very good that municipalities should take the initiative to ensure that the new building is more energy efficient. This is a good step in the area of sustainability. That is why we have the next motion. [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[noting that the Crisis and Recovery Act allows municipalities to enforce better energy performance;]] [[Whereas it is much more cost-effective to improve energy performance in new buildings than in existing ones;]] [[Whereas, therefore, it is better to build a building as energy efficient as possible directly, rather than waiting;]] [[Whereas it is important that municipalities, in addition to national regulations, have the space to impose additional requirements themselves;]] [[calls on the Government to extend the possibilities offered by the Crisis and Recovery Act to municipalities to enforce better energy performance, possibly in cooperation with the UNG,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion has been proposed by Mr Van Eijs.
