I come to the Paternotte/Weverling motion in part No. 430, which calls on the government to modify spectrum policy by the end of the year so that the 5Groningen project can be used to experiment with mobile communications on the 3.5 GHz band. I advise against this motion. When making the 3.5 GHz band available, the use by the intelligence services for the collection of information in the context of national security is important. Currently, there is a limitation of the roll-out of 5G networks above the Amsterdam-Zwolle line to prevent interference with satellite communications interception. This limitation must also be taken into account when allowing experiments. Measurements carried out by the Telecom Agency have shown that, under all circumstances, unacceptable interference occurs on satellite interception. It is therefore irresponsible to call this motion irresponsible, and I therefore advise against it.
