ParlaMint-NL_2018-02-15-tweedekamer-10.u5	Thank you, Mr President. The European common agricultural policy is a major distortion of sustainable food production at taxpayers' expense. Production increase may be the only goal, and that might have been good to defend shortly after the Second World War, but certainly not anymore in 2018. However, this has been confirmed by the European constitution which the people did not wish for, but nevertheless came. This, together with the dictatorship of the free market which puts Europe above fragile values, means that any attempt to make the common agricultural policy a little more sustainable is doomed to failure. However, we are on the eve of a new review. GroenLinks has tabled a number of motions. The Party for Animals wants a much more radical shift, and yet we support the motions on pieces Nos. 1084 and 1085, tabled by Mr Grashoff, because I know that they also know that it needs to be more sustainable at GroenLinks. The first, in part No. 1083, unfortunately, we cannot support it. The second explanation of vote concerns the motion tabled by Mr De Groot.
ParlaMint-NL_2018-02-15-tweedekamer-10.u6	It must be a bit quiet in the room, otherwise we cannot follow Mrs Ouwehand.
ParlaMint-NL_2018-02-15-tweedekamer-10.u7	Mr. Chairman. D66 is concerned about proteins, and says it needs to be more sustainable. That is not new in itself, we all think. Europe is working on a protein strategy and D66 says it needs to be broader. What is striking in Mr De Groot's motion is that D66 wants to broaden this protein strategy by combating food waste, biomass from the land, insect farming, but that D66 does not dare to mention the most important: it must be extended to our consumption of meat and dairy products. Since D66 does not mention it in the motion, we cannot support her.
ParlaMint-NL_2018-02-15-tweedekamer-10.u8	Thank you. We shall now proceed to the votes.
