ParlaMint-NL_2018-11-01-tweedekamer-9.u7	Mr. Chairman. In the first motion, in part No. 532, the government is requested to stop allowing chickens to be kept in the highest occupancy of 42 kg/m. This is European regulation. It indicates that this is the maximum and that this can be done. We have indicated that we will follow these rules, so I advise against this motion. The second motion, in part No. 533, concerns registration of foot sole lesions in all holders of chickens, ducks and turkeys. At present, this is maintained when there is the highest occupation. Additional requirements may be imposed at the highest occupancy. The Netherlands has done so with regard to foot sole lesions. Mrs Ouwehand is now asking for this to be made available everywhere. Then you will have to deal with this through national legislation, because it cannot be done on the basis of European legislation. You'll end up back in the national cups. The Netherlands has chosen not to, and I choose not to. So I advise against this motion.
