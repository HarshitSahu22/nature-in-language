ParlaMint-NL_2018-12-20-tweedekamer-32.u1	The next item is the VSO Recognition of the willingness of employers' trade associations to grant a one-off financial compensation to victims of the OPS occupational disease (29544, No. 870). I welcome the Secretary of State for Social Affairs and Employment and give the floor to Mr Aartsen on behalf of the VVD.
ParlaMint-NL_2018-12-20-tweedekamer-32.u2	Thank you, Mr President. It is a pity to read in last Monday's letter that, after having talked about it for so long, employers did not come out with each other to find a good solution for this group of people, people who have worked hard for many years, also got sick because of that work and then could not tell stories on their former employers. Over the last few days, a number of colleagues and I have worked hard to find a political solution for the group of 350 victims of OPS, also known as the 'painting disease'. It worked. I must say that it really has become a dragon of a motion, but it was necessary to avoid the precedent of this motion, which was feared by the Secretary of State. [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[noting that no workable solution has been found by the explorers for a one-off financial compensation for the victims of the OPS occupational disease, i.e. painter's disease, whose employers are no longer to be found;]] [[Whereas the consequences of adverse working conditions are the responsibility and liability of employers and not of the public authorities;]] [[Whereas the situation of the group of approximately 350 OPS victims is very exceptional due to the prolonged latency and the fact that there are no real possibilities for them to hold their former employers or their successors liable;]] [[Whereas frequent high exposures to volatile solvents have not been present for some time;]] [[Whereas there is a very serious physical injury to OPS victims due to nervous system damage, which usually results in complete incapacity for work;]] [[Whereas OPS has a specific one-to-one relationship between the harmful substances released during work and the occupational disease OPS;]] [[Whereas the group of OPS victims is very limited and well defined and the diagnosis has been established by one of the two multidisciplinary Solvent Teams by means of a CTE diagnosis;]] [[Whereas, in this very exceptional case, a form of financial assistance is appropriate in the light of the above considerations;]] [[Calls on the Government to set up a one-off coulance regime for this very exceptional group and situation, which should not be subject to precedent, for the identified OPS victims group and to provide them with the compensation provided for, in addition to the condition that all victims refrain from future legal action against the government, which, wherever possible, takes over the claim of the victims to be held liable and that the employer's liability is maintained;]] [[Calls on the Government to cover the form of the one-off coulance financial contribution from non-legally compulsory expenditure in the 2019 SZW budget;]] [[expresses its opinion that it does not consider it desirable to set a precedent for this one-off coulance scheme for this very exceptional group,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion was proposed by members Aartsen, Van Weyenberg, Gijs van Dijk, Bruins, Renkema, Van Kent, Pieter Heerma and Stoffer.]] [[She's getting No. kst-29544-871]]
ParlaMint-NL_2018-12-20-tweedekamer-32.u3	Thank you, Mr President. Like the Chamber, I am concerned about the harrowing situation of the victims of this nasty painter's disease. I understand your call to meet these people financially out of coulance. In the in-depth investigation by the explorers of a concession, they found that, unfortunately, there is no basis for a financial concession. I find this extremely disappointing, especially for the victims, who have been in uncertainty for a long time. Employers are responsible for safe and healthy working conditions. That is also the reason why, as Secretary of State, I cannot help but discourage this motion, which I find perfectly understandable.
ParlaMint-NL_2018-12-20-tweedekamer-32.u4	Thank you. This brings us to the end of this VAO.
ParlaMint-NL_2018-12-20-tweedekamer-32.u5	On the motions that have been tabled, no, it was only one motion, we shall be voting this evening. I thank the Secretary of State.
