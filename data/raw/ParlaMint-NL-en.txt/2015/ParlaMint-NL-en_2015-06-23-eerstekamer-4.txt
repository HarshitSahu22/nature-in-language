ParlaMint-NL_2015-06-23-eerstekamer-4.u1	No individual profile shall apply to the Vice-Presidents. The profile for the President is also relevant to the Vice-Presidents, because they must be able to replace the President in his duties. I propose to the Chamber that the nomination after this public meeting be made in writing by letter to the First Chamber, which must be submitted to the Registry by Friday 26 June 2015 at 12 noon. Candidates shall indicate whether they wish to be candidates for the first Vice-Presidentship or the second Vice-Presidentship or not. for both, with the exception that in the case of the election of the first Vice-President, the candidacy for the second Vice-President shall lapse. The Registrar shall send the letters of application received in a copy to the members of the First Chamber. In the plenary session of 30 June 2015, candidates will be given a maximum of five minutes in alphabetical order to explain their candidacy. Following that, the Chamber may ask questions to the candidates. Finally, the written vote will follow in accordance with Rule 113 et seq. of the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber. Does any of the Members still wish to speak on this matter? That is not the case.
