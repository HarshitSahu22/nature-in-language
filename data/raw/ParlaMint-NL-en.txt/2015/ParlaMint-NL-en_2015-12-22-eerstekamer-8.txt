ParlaMint-NL_2015-12-22-eerstekamer-8.u1	Madam President. At the end of last week's debate, after the Minister's reluctant reaction to the motion I tabled, I indicated that I would consider amending it. My group believes that the text before us, which has been amended in particular in the operative part, can and will respond to the problems which the Minister had with the motion. The thrust of the motion remains the same, i.e. the broadest possible support in this Chamber for the Minister to bring the budget of Security and Justice to a level that will substantially reduce the pressure on the rule of law. I hope, of course, that the motion will also have broad support in this Chamber.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-12-22-eerstekamer-8.u2	The English motion c.s. (' point ') has been amended to read as follows: [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[Considering that the budget of the Ministry of Security and Justice for 2016 after the latest letter of amendment has fewer financial risks and budget tensions;]] [[Considering that signals have been issued from the supply chain partners that additional resources are still needed for the functioning at an appropriate level of justice, the Public Prosecutor's Office and the police for 2016;]] [[noting that investigations are now being carried out from the Ministry and price negotiations are taking place with a number of chain partners on whether and to what extent these are being conducted;]] [[Considers that the above-mentioned budget should provide a solid basis for ensuring the rule of law in general and the quality of the criminal justice chain in particular;]] [[urges the Government, in the forthcoming Spring Note on the basis of the results of the ongoing investigations and negotiations, to arrive at a balanced budget which minimises the risks of possible shortages and inadequate performance in the case-law, the Public Prosecutor's Office and the police,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This amended motion has been signed by members English, Backer, Bredenoord, Stienen, Rinnooy Kan, Ruers, Strik, Bikker and Diederik van Dijk.]] [[She's getting No. kst-34300-VI-O]]
ParlaMint-NL_2015-12-22-eerstekamer-8.u3	Madam President. The cabinet always appreciates the support of Mr Engels. His amended motion is in line with three motions adopted in the Lower House and which also refer to ongoing investigations into the adequacy of the multiannual budgets of the Public Prosecutor's Office, the judiciary and the police. The Cabinet also believes that the budget of Security and Justice should provide a solid basis for ensuring the rule of law and the quality of the criminal justice chain. In the next Spring paper in the spring of 2016, the cabinet will report on the results of various investigations and price negotiations, as mentioned by Mr.English, and will indicate the budgetary consequences. In doing so, we are committed to reducing the risks of possible shortages as much as possible. We cannot, of course, give a 100% guarantee on this point, because the policy area of Security and Justice is very multifaceted and wide and incidents can never be completely ruled out. The direction of the amended motion and that of my colleague, the Minister for Security and Justice, are the same. That is why my colleague, the Minister for Security and Justice, regards the motion as a support and leaves the vote on the motion to the House.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-12-22-eerstekamer-8.u4	Thank you.
