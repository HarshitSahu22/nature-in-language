ParlaMint-NL_2015-07-02-tweedekamer-32.u7	Mr. Chairman. In response to Mrs Gesthuizen's motion c.s. in piece No. 386 I should like to state what I have already said in general consultation this morning. The proposal made to the cabinet by the Secretary of State of OCW, the Minister of Finance and myself, which led to the cabinet position, means that there will be an auction for commercial radio frequencies, whereby instead of the government the market will be soberly determining the value of those frequencies. With that auction, we're offering 10 years of security to the commercial radio market. Later, in 2017, after fourteen years, we will give newcomers opportunities again. The restrictive conditions imposed in 2003 are removed. We're going to increase the indoor coverage further. The outdoor coverage of digital radio is now at 95%, and we are also bringing up the indoor coverage, which is now between 40 and 60%. And we will assign additional frequencies to digital radio. With that package, commercial radio could take a big step forward. What's in the motion is wrong about this. That's why I advise against the Gasthuizen c.s. motion. In the Klein motion on part No. 386 states that regional public service broadcasting should receive further financial compensation. This subject was not discussed in general consultation this morning. It wasn't on the agenda and it hasn't been on the agenda. The subject is covered by the Secretary of State of OCW, but has now been raised here, so I would like to respond. The agreements made apply until 2017. There is no reason to grant further subsidies now. That's why I advise against this motion. In his motion in part No. 387 calls on Mr Verhoeven to ensure that the market authority interprets exceptions as strictly as possible. That's not the relationship we have with the ACM. The ACM is an independent supervisor, which we do not give orders. That's why I advise against this motion.
