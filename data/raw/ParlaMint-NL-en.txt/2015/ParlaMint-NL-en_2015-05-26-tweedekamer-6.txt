ParlaMint-NL_2015-05-26-tweedekamer-6.u1	We are briefly holding a first part of the order of business because Mr Omtzigt has a request for this evening's debate. I call Mr Omtzigt.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-05-26-tweedekamer-6.u2	Mr. Chairman. Thank you for giving me the opportunity to make the request now. I would like to receive a letter on the final act before 4.30 p.m., before the start of the debate with the Prime Minister on the 2014 financial statements. Chapter 5 (3) shows that EUR 133 million has been transferred to the European Union. As an explanatory statement, the increase in expenditure on the EU contribution is caused by a gross post-tax due to GNI revisions. We thought we'd already paid for all that. In October, 1,057 million, i.e. more than 1 billion, were transferred to Brussels. The CDA Group would like to know what new calculation it is based on, when it is published, when it has been paid and why the Chamber has not heard it before. My group would also like to see all correspondence between the Dutch Government and the European Commission on this and the previous post-tax as soon as possible. We don't have to do that tonight, but at least we need to be able to check what happened.
