ParlaMint-NL_2015-10-14-tweedekamer-6.u1	I propose to authorise the holding of legislative consultations today on the amendment of the General Law on the recognition of EC professional qualifications in connection with the implementation of the Directive on the recognition of professional qualifications and the IMI Regulation (34272). I would like to explain to you that this bill is on the plenary agenda this evening and that all the spokesmen have agreed to deal with the legislative proposal instead in a legislative consultation.
