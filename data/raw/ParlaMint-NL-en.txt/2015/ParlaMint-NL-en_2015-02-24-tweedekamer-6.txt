ParlaMint-NL_2015-02-24-tweedekamer-6.u1	Before we begin work, I would ask you all to stand in the gallery and in the public gallery, as far as you can. On 14 February, two horrible attacks took place in the Danish capital Copenhagen: one on a cultural centre where a debate on freedom of expression took place at the time, and the other on a synagogue where a bat mitzvah was celebrated. Two people were killed. A whole people got hit. It is only a few weeks since we were here in the Chamber considering the events in Paris. I repeat what I said today: in our Western democracy, whether in France, Denmark or the Netherlands, you should not be afraid to express your opinion or go to a prayer house. That is the core of an open and free society. Freedom of expression and freedom of religion are fundamental values, which are enshrined in our Constitution and which we will always defend. Our thoughts are with the victims, with the families of the attacks and with the entire Danish population. We wish them much strength and wisdom to move forward after these terrible events. I have now sent a message to the Danish Folketing on behalf of the House of Representatives. I ask you all to sit down again.
