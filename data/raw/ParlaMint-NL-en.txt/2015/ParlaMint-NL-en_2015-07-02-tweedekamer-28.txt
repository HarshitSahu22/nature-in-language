ParlaMint-NL_2015-07-02-tweedekamer-28.u3	Mr. Chairman. Negotiations for a new collective agreement have stopped since April. That's extremely unfortunate. A new collective agreement is an opportunity to align working conditions with the 2015 labour market and to provide hard-working civil servants with security. Everyone would like the parties to return to the table soon, but it is not appropriate for the Chamber to interfere in collective bargaining. Our constitutional task is legislation and control. You cannot exercise control if you have first made yourself responsible for the outcome of the negotiations. This applies to the content, that is to say timing, that is to say all aspects of a negotiation process. Also in a practical sense, the VVD does not want to get in the way of the Minister. That applies to all these aspects. No matter how much the VVD attaches to a rapid outcome, it will not therefore support motions on this point. The VVD wishes the parties wisdom and success and likes to see the results come along in due time.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-07-02-tweedekamer-28.u4	Mr. Chairman. With Mr Slob and the other authors of the motion, I share the ambition to achieve a good collective agreement for the state officials as soon as possible, indeed, and I realise that very well, because they are long overdue and because, as an employer, I also think it is important that we conclude a new collective agreement. It will be clear that we have a desire on both sides and that we will eventually have to agree on this in order to reach a collective agreement, but I would like to return to the table as soon as possible. Mr Slob pointed out in his speech today and also yesterday that he was struggling a bit. On the one hand, he is concerned about the collective agreement process. On the other hand, there is the role of the Chamber: not at the table, not negotiating, but concerned. I think he's gonna let that balancing act go down in the text of his motion. Without being able to promise that I will suddenly have a lot of money, I do have the ambition to quickly sit down at the table. I would also be happy to inform the Chamber about this. On balance, I will leave this motion to the Chamber.
