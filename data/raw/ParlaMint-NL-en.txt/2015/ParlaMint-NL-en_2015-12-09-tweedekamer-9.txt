ParlaMint-NL_2015-12-09-tweedekamer-9.u1	Before we begin the arrangement of work, we first jointly reflect on the death of a Dutch soldier who was on mission in Mali. I call on all those present in the Chamber and in the public gallery to stand as far as possible. On 29 November 2015, Lieutenant-Colonel Wolter van Thiel died unexpectedly at the age of 44. At that time he was working in Mali at the headquarters of the MINUSMA mission. In doing so, he made an important contribution to peace, security, stability and a better future for the people there. Wolter has worked as an air liaison officer in the Maritime Battle Staff of the Netherlands Maritime Force since June 2015. Before that, he worked as a kite at the 300 Squadron, first on Soesterberg and later on in Gilze-Rijen. His colleagues remember him as a passionate social colleague, a real enjoyer of life. We wish his wife, his daughter, his son and other family, friends and colleagues much strength in this difficult time. A letter with similar words has been sent to the next of kin by the chairman of the Permanent Defence Commission and myself on your behalf.
