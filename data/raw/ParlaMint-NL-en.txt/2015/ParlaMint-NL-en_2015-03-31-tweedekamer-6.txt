ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-31-tweedekamer-6.u1	I give the floor to Mrs Neppérus to report on behalf of the Committee on the Verification of Credentials and Immunities.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-31-tweedekamer-6.u2	The Committee for the Examination of the Letters of Faith has examined the documents relating to Mrs C.N.A. Nijkerken-de Haan in Schinveld and Mr L.A. The Lange in Noordwijk. The committee has unanimously concluded that Mrs C.N.A. Nijkerken-de Haan in Schinveld and Mr L.A. De Lange in Noordwijk was rightly appointed as a member of the Second Chamber of the States-General. The committee therefore proposes that they be admitted as members of the Chamber. To this end, they shall first serve the declarations and promises required by the Act of Prosecution of Ministers and Members of States-General of 27 February 1992, Official Journal No. 120, to be done. Finally, the Committee asks you to propose to the Chamber that the full report be included in the Acts.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-31-tweedekamer-6.u3	I would like to thank the committee for its report and propose that it be decided accordingly.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-31-tweedekamer-6.u4	I would ask Members and others present in the Chamber and in the public gallery, as far as possible. Mrs Nijkerken-de Haan and Mr De Lange are present in the building of the Chamber to make the required statements and promises. I ask the Registrar to lead them in.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-31-tweedekamer-6.u5	I wish you both the best of luck with the membership of this Chamber. I congratulate you very much. I would ask you to sign the attendance list and take a seat in our midst. After the suspension, there will be an opportunity to meet the new colleagues and wish them luck. We stand still, because we are going to do another swearing-in and a very special one.
