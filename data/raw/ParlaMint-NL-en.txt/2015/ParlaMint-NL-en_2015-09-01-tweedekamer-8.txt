ParlaMint-NL_2015-09-01-tweedekamer-8.u1	I give the floor to Mrs Neppérus to report on behalf of the Committee on the Verification of Credentials and Immunities.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-09-01-tweedekamer-8.u2	Mr. Chairman. The Committee for the Examination of the Letters of Faith has examined the documents relating to Mr A.J. Zijlstra at Ridderkerk. The committee has unanimously concluded that it has rightly been declared a Member of the European Parliament. The committee finds that it may be admitted to a Member of the European Parliament on the basis of national provisions. The committee proposes to inform the President of the European Parliament and the person appointed. Finally, the Committee proposes that the full report be included in the Acts.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-09-01-tweedekamer-8.u3	I would like to thank the committee for its report and propose that it be decided accordingly.
