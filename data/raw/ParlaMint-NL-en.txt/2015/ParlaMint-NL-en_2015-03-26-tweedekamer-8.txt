ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-26-tweedekamer-8.u1	I give the floor to Mrs Neppérus to report on behalf of the Committee on the Verification of Credentials and Immunities.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-26-tweedekamer-8.u2	Mr. Chairman. The Committee for the Examination of the Letters of Faith has examined the documents relating to Mr F. Teeven in Amsterdam. The Committee unanimously concluded that Mr F. Teeven was rightly appointed a member of the Second Chamber of the States General in Amsterdam. The committee therefore proposes that he be admitted as a member of the Chamber. To this end, he shall first serve the declarations and promises required by the Act of Prosecution of Ministers and Members of States-General of 27 February 1992, Official Journal No. 120, to be done. Finally, the Committee asks you to propose to the Chamber that the full report be included in the Acts.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-26-tweedekamer-8.u3	I thank the committee for the report and propose that it be decided accordingly.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-26-tweedekamer-8.u4	I would ask Members and others present in the Chamber and in the public gallery, as far as possible. Mr Teeven is present in the building of the Chamber to make the required statements and promises. I ask the Registrar to lead him in.
ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-26-tweedekamer-8.u5	May I warmly congratulate you on your appointment to the House of Representatives? I would ask you to sign the attendance list and sit in our midst. Mr Teeven has informed me that he has been installed for the fifth time today as a member of the Chamber and that for that reason he does not need a separate suspension for congratulations, but that you have all been cordially invited to intercept him in the corridors or elsewhere to congratulate him personally.
