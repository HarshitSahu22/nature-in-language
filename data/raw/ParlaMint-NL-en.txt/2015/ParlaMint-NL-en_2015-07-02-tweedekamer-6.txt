In the motion in part No. 474 we are invited to take 2013 as a starting point in setting the 2016 budget. We have discussed and negotiated extensively with the VNG on the May Circular 2015. If we get signals from municipalities or the VNG that the waiting lists are on the verge of increasing, that will of course be the subject of discussion. Then we'll put the horse back behind the wagon. That's why I don't recommend the motion. In the motion in part No. 477 the government is invited to consult with the VNG in order to provide a tailor-made solution for young people with ggz problems living across borders. So everyone can get used to the new situation that they have to get care in their place of residence.
