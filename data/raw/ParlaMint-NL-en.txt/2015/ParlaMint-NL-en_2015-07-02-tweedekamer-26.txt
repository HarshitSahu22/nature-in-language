ParlaMint-NL_2015-07-02-tweedekamer-26.u6	Mr. Chairman. I'll start with the Knops motion in part No. 154. I consider this motion to be an exception to what I said about it earlier during the debate and what was also reported in the letter of the Chamber in response to the Van der Staaij motion. I myself indicated that the increase in the cost of defence equipment is a risk to the operation of defence and that exchange rate fluctuations distort a balanced planning process, sustainable management and the budget. In other words, I read that motion as a derogation from what I said and what was also brought up in the debate by Mr Vuijk and Mr Eijsink. I'd like to leave the verdict on that motion to the Chamber. I see this motion as a support to further stabilise the operation of defence. I come to the Hachchi motion in part No. 155. We have recently sent a letter to the Chamber with a view to the submarine service and the replacement of the Walrus class in particular. I also informed the Chamber that the A document will follow by the end of the year. At least that's what we're trying to do. I said in the debate a few weeks ago that the decision to replace the Walrus class involves a very careful but also complex consideration. At that time, I also expressed a strong desire not to turn it into a gender issue. The A letter comes before the end of the year. We've just established the need. The A letter will deal with the specifications of the need and the requirements that we impose on it. I have informed the Chamber that two working associations have been established by the CZSK in the case of mixed sailing. The submarine service already employs women in shore support. Keep up the good work. So this motion is far too early. I know Mrs Hachchi as a rather impatient type and it is also good if you want to speed things up, but in this case I say: this motion is coming far too early and I consider her superfluous. Furthermore, I believe that this motion undermines the complexity of replacing the Walrus class. So I really don't want to recommend this motion.
