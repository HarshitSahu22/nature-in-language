This is a good thing for entrepreneurial financing, especially because banks are reluctant. We are concerned about abuse, because there are credit intermediaries who call themselves credit union and they are not. They don't have a common union, they don't have a greenhouse and they don't share in the return and risk. They are often mediators or crowdfunders who are posing as a credit union. How do the initiators intend to prevent abuse of the term "credit unions"? Are they thinking of some kind of certification or label, so that an organisation that issues itself as a credit union is really a credit union? Nor is it entirely clear to me whether credit unions are allowed to act as intermediaries in credit or to combine this with lending from their own funds. Crowdfunders who call themselves credit unions are more mediators. Have any measures been taken against these hybrid constructions? Like the AFM, the PVV wonders whether a credit union can only attract reclaimable funds from its members or that it may also issue financial instruments, the so-called member participations. In the latter case, Chapter 5.1 of the Financial Supervision Act applies with a role of the AFM. Can the initiators make it clear whether the issue of members' participations is permitted? In the reaction of DNB in May 2014, I read that he found €14,000 in supervisory fees on the low side. This has been adjusted to €20,000 in the second letter of amendment. Is this with the approval of DNB or is DNB not consulted in it? The initiators also indicate that much still needs to be regulated in lower legislation, the so-called AMvB. Can they give an overview of what still needs to be arranged per AMvB? And how and when will that be arranged? The Minister of Finance is hiding, in the sense that he says he can't go to Brussels before the AMvB arrives. That is my final point: when will the Minister of Finance go to Brussels with this bill? How long does he think he will need for the enlightened regime under the CRD IV, the so-called Implementing Act? He sees great opportunities for this bill, but the PVV is worried because she thinks that this may take a long time. We don't have that time. How soon can the Minister arrange this? When can this bill come into force? According to the PVV, urgent action is required.
