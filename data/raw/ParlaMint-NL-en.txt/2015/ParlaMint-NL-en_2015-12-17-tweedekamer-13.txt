At the beginning of 2016, the Chamber will be given the concept. I have already said in an earlier debate that we will be busy with each other at the beginning of 2016, because a lot will come to the Chamber. In Mr Wassenberg's motion to item No. 319 calls on the government to adapt the GMO legislation in such a way that an ethical test is also required when showing GMOs. That is already the case, because an ethical test is required for each GMO exhibition. The second part of the request is to prohibit the genetic modification of animals for exhibitions. There is already an explicit ban on genetic manipulation of animals for sport or entertainment purposes. Therefore, if an exhibition is intended for entertainment, the prohibition is already explicit. If an exhibition has a different purpose, then an ethical test is already needed to get permission to manipulate genetics. I therefore consider this motion to be superfluous.
