ParlaMint-NL_2015-02-12-tweedekamer-6.u2	Mr. Chairman. We are on the eve of the vocational training experiment combined learning paths bol-bl. As the rules are now, there is a strict hour standard. My motion calls for some flexibility. That leads to the next motion. [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[noting that in the course of the experiment vocational training combined learning paths bol-bbl it is not possible to deviate from the hourly standard;]] [[Whereas deviation from the hourly standard provides flexibility in the education programme;]] [[Whereas it is possible to map the effects of the experiment combined learning paths bol-bl, even in case of deviation from the hourly standard, provided that the correct measurement is made;]] [[calls on the Government, also in the course of the vocational training experiment, to make it possible to deviate from the hourly standard, provided that, on the basis of the results obtained and the satisfaction of students and their future employers, it can be established that the training is of sufficient quality and that it is guaranteed in the quality management system of the institution and the student or participant council, as laid down in the law,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion has been proposed by Members Van Meenen and Jadnansing. It seems to me that their submission is sufficiently supported.]] [[She's getting No. kst-31524-236]]
ParlaMint-NL_2015-02-12-tweedekamer-6.u3	Mr. Chairman. I'll leave the verdict on this motion to the Chamber. Let me explain. In the experiment I have already tried to shape different variants of vocational training in a combined learning path bol-bbl. In doing so, I have already opted for a great deal of flexibility in training, in the combined learning paths. Education institutions can use flexible minimum hours, guided learning time and professional practice over different years of study. Now I could say that this is enough, because there is already a lot of flexibility. There's something to be said for that. If you want to examine the effects of the experiment as closely as possible, you actually want all other factors to remain constant. That would be a reason not to introduce more flexibility. At the same time, the motion by Mr Van Meenen and Mr Jadnansing is very much in line with my policy of giving more flexibility to institutions with a good educational vision, which also communicate well with their teachers and students. The motion is therefore in line with the hour standard that I have previously made flexible, but subject to conditions. Suppose I may read the motion in such a way that these conditions also apply in the context of this experiment. I think I can read the operative part in this way, because Mr Van Meenen refers to the results achieved, to the satisfaction of students and their future employers, and to the assurance of quality care. In that sense, therefore, the motion is exactly in line with what I have formulated in general. That makes the pilot a little more complicated for us, but that's what I'd like to do. I shall therefore leave this motion to the House.
