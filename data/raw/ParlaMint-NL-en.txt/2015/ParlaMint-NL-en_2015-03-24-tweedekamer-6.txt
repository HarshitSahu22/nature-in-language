ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-24-tweedekamer-6.u1	Before we begin to regulate work, we are considering the death of two Dutch soldiers. I would ask everyone in the Chamber and in the public gallery to stand as far as possible. On Tuesday 17 March we received the sad news that Captain René Zeetsen and First Lieutenant Ernst Mollinger died in Mali in an accident with an Apache helicopter. They were both part of the 301 Squadron of Defense Helicopter Command on Gilze-Rijen Air Base. The accident once again makes us realize how much our responsibility is when we decide to send Dutch soldiers to dangerous areas around the world. We wish all family, friends and colleagues much strength and strength in this difficult time. We hope that they feel strengthened by the knowledge that René and Ernst have given their lives for international peace and security, in this case for the people of Mali. A letter with similar words has been sent to the next of kin by the chairman of the Permanent Defence Commission and me on your behalf. I'd like you to sit down again.
