Deficiencies are generally what we discussed in the debate, a global problem on which we in the Netherlands have very limited influence. The inspection has not so far issued any administrative fines or warnings in the case of deficits. This is because the causes of the deficits are very diverse and cannot be clearly identified in most cases. With the imposition of a fine, the deficit has not been resolved and the patient will have little use for it. I think it is important that the inspection should take place in consultation with the parties concerned in order to resolve a deficit as quickly as possible. This is what the inspection does by allowing accelerated alternative parties to allow medicines to enter the Dutch market. In addition, the inspection is actively involved in the working group on drug shortages, in which field parties work together to combat deficits insofar as a solution can be found in the Dutch situation. I'd like to continue with that. I'm dissuading the motion.
