ParlaMint-NL_2015-11-17-eerstekamer-4.u1	May I ask you all to stand up. At the beginning of this afternoon's sitting, I would like to take a brief look at the horrible events that took place in Paris last Friday evening, and we are also thinking of innocent victims of attacks elsewhere. We do so in the presence of the ambassador of France, His Excellency Monsieur Pic. Monsieur l'Ambassador, je vous souhaite le bienvenu. These barbarous acts of terror are a direct attack on open society and the democratic rule of law as we know it. Our thoughts are primarily with the victims and severely injured, who were innocent and unsuspectingly affected by this brutal violence. Our thoughts are also with the next of kin, who must bear the heavy loss. The quintessence of terror is to sow fear. We must not give way to terror. In the midst of all this grief, we must continue to defend the values that define us. It is our mission to stand firm for the core values of our democratic rule of law and to fight for our long-term and fragile freedom and security. Respect and tolerance characterize our society. From these core values, we can ensure that acts of terror do not scatter and destroy our open, free society. I would ask you all to observe a minute's silence.
