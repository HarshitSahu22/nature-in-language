In the general consultation I noticed that this wish existed, so all leave was withdrawn from the Ministry. The people of the board are now with pizza's and Red Bull trying to come up with a letter in which all this is recorded before Christmas. I shall therefore leave the vote on this motion to the Chamber. The second motion, in part No. 375, it's about the BIT test. There is also a specific request for that test to be completed by March at the latest. It's gonna be tight, but the motion's putting pressure on it. I will direct the House's explicit request to do so at that rate to the people of BIT. I'll leave the verdict on this motion to the Chamber. In her third motion, in part No. 376, asks Mrs De Caluwé to come up with at least one public authentication tool in 2017. That is also the objective of the cabinet. I shall therefore leave the judgment on this motion to the House as well. In the motion by Mr Van Raak, in part No. 377, is asked not to start a pilot with private resources. I understand that request, given the position of the SP in this debate. However, as Mr Van Raak knows, the government has explicitly opted for a mixed public-private system. I therefore reject Mr Van Raak's motion. Finally, I come to Mrs Koşer Kaya's motion, on item No. 378, which is about protecting personal data. This is done by the Personal Data Protection Act. That law falls under the Ministry of V and J. There will be no change to this aspect by any other authentication agent. I would therefore recommend to Mrs Koşer Kaya that this motion be tabled in a debate with my colleague from V and J. Should the motion be debated now, I would advise against it, because it gives the impression that something would change the protection of personal data.
