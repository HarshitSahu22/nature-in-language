ParlaMint-NL_2015-07-01-tweedekamer-3.u10	Mr. Chairman. Mr Van Gerven's motion for a resolution 149 requests that the advantages and disadvantages of the acquisition by a device manufacturer of a clinic or another healthcare provider be investigated. The question of vertical integration has been raised a great many times in this Chamber. On 10 June I wrote a letter to the Chamber about this. The acquisition of an institution by manufacturers is permitted by law. That's how it is in the system. However, conditions and quality requirements applicable to all, governance requirements and the WTZ requirements of the governance structure must be met. The inspection will see to that. It is also necessary to comply with the protection of patient data, on which the personal data protection board monitors, and with the competition rules, on which the ACM supervises when taking over. As far as I'm concerned, the patient's importance is paramount in all of this. This interest is also secured by all these legal requirements. We have supervisors to oversee that. However, I indicated in the debate that I would like to go into more detail with the inspection, the health insurers and the patient organisations, because I want to remain informed about these developments and trends. I'll inform the Chamber about that. I would, however, advise against this motion because it goes against the system. Mr Van Gerven's motion for a resolution 150 states that bladderwashes with bladder wall-reinforcers should be included in the package and reimbursed. This too goes against the system we have agreed to. We have agreed that the Zorginstituut Nederland, as package advisor, will gather experts in the Netherlands. They shall give an opinion on the package on the basis of the state of the art and on the basis of their interest in it. They consult the field intensively and all involved. I do not want to lose that role, and for that reason I would also like to discourage this motion.
