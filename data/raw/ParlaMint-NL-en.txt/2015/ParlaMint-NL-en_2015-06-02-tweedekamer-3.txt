ParlaMint-NL_2015-06-02-tweedekamer-3.u1	Mr. Chairman. It was a year ago last week that a terrible, shocking attack took place at the Jewish Museum in Brussels. It became clear that an attack that could be carried out there by a jihadist returned from Syria, a Syrian, could also be carried out here. In that week, again such reports appeared about the security situation of the Jewish community. The Foundation Bij Leven en Welzijn, the advisory body for the protection of the Jewish community, has issued a report showing an increase in suspicious activities around Jewish institutions and events. I am very concerned about that. What is at stake here is the question of whether the Jewish community can live in freedom and security in the Netherlands. After all, the quality of a civilization can be read from the way it deals with a small minority, such as the Jewish minority. I therefore have a number of questions for the Minister of Security and Justice. With the former Minister of Security and Justice, it took a long time before we arrived where we finally arrived: a reduction in the costs borne by the Jewish community. That took a long time. What is the attitude of this minister? Is he going to continue where we ended up with the previous minister? Does he stand for the security and freedom of the Jewish community? Is he willing to talk to Bij Leven en Welzijn about the security situation? If these disturbing messages come forward, will he immediately engage in the conversation to see to what extent he can remove those concerns? A question has been asked about the need for the use of trained agents, because this question special commitment, special skill. Is the Minister prepared to deploy these agents and have them trained for that purpose? Finally, I have a question about costs. It is a small community of about 40,000 Dutch people, who have to pay a lot of costs themselves. There is a small concession from the Empire against this, sometimes also municipal subsidy. Is the Minister prepared to consider whether this is still feasible and whether it should be reduced to a more portable level?
