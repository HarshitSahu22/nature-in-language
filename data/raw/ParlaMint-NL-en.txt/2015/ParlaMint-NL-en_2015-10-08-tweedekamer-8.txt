In the Jasper van Dijk/Sideerius motion in part No. 257 is asked for extra money for teachers in the social plan. I would point out that the social plan is something of the social partners. It is up to the social partners to formulate a good social plan. A social plan may not be ideal in all respects, but it is better than a cold bankruptcy. That's why I advise against this motion. The point made by Mr Van Dijk and Mrs Siderius in their motion on item No. 258, I think, is right. They argue that the principle of "puppet follower" should apply not only to the schools included in this plan, but that the same principle should be applied when one of the pupils would say, for example, that he goes to school in Zoetermeer or Wassenaar. In the coming years, as part of the top talent policy, extra money will go to schools in their regular lumpsum. All in all, there is enough money and schools should give these pupils an appropriate place in their consideration of appropriate education together with the partnerships. There is no need for further investigation.
