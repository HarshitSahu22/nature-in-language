ParlaMint-NL_2015-12-10-tweedekamer-7.u1	On Tuesday, I propose to vote on the motions tabled at the time of the notation on the State of the European Union and on the initiative paper of Mr Straus "Krimp in secondary education from cramp to opportunity" (34226) and on the Kuzu motion (34300-V, No. 37). I also propose that we vote on the letter from the Permanent Committee for Defence on a proposal to designate a major submarine project (34225, No. 2), on the letters of the Permanent Commission for European Affairs on the European Commission Work Programme for 2016 (22112, No. 2032), on a reservation of treatment for the EU proposal amending the Act concerning the Election of Members of the European Parliament (34361, No. 1) and on the letter from the Bureau on the Foreign Travel Scheme (34183, No. 36). I propose that the following legislative proposals be added to the Chamber's agenda: the legislative proposal Amendment of the Tobacco Act implementing Directive 2014/40/EU on the production, presentation and sale of tobacco and related products (34234); the legislative proposal Rules on the safety and marketing of recreational craft (Act on recreational craft 2016) (34265). I propose that the maximum speaking time for the debate on the Year Note 2015 should be as follows: 12 minutes for VVD and PvdA; 10 minutes for SP, CDA, PVV and D66; seven minutes for ChristenUnion, GroenLinks, SGP and PvdD; 5 minutes for 50PLUS; three and a half minutes for Group Kuzu/Öztürk and Group Bontes/Van Klaveren; two minutes for Houwers, Klein and Van Vliet, and during the debate on the European Summit on 17 and 18 December 2015 maximum speaking time of: 10 minutes for VVD and PvdA; 7 minutes for SP, CDA, PVV and D66; five minutes for ChristenUnion, GroenLinks, SGP and PvdD; Four minutes for 50PLUS; two and a half minutes for Group Kuzu/Öztürk and Group Bontes/Van Klaveren; One and a half minutes for Houwers, Klein and Van Vliet. As for the documents on Nos. 490 and 491 (31288) the deadlines have expired, I propose that these documents be adopted for notification. I also propose that the following work be carried out: I propose to add to the agenda: the VAO Counter-Terrorism Conference, held on 9 December, with the first speaker, Mrs Tellegen, of the VVD.
