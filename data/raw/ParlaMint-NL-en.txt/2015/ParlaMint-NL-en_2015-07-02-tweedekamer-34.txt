ParlaMint-NL_2015-07-02-tweedekamer-34.u1	Mr. Chairman. This morning, there was the intrusive presentation of the reports of the Queen Wilhelmina Fund's Committee for the Netherlands Cancer Control and the Dutch Healthcare Authority on expensive medicines, in particular on expensive cancer medicines. There are signs that cancer patients may not receive the medicines they are entitled to. We also see the rapid increase in the cost of these medicines. A cancer patient put it as follows: can I still trust my doctor, in the sense that he or she gives me the necessary medicines for optimal treatment? If this happens, the core of the healthcare system is at stake, namely the duty of care given by hospitals and health insurance companies. All this led to a strong debate with the Minister this afternoon. It is now presenting proposals on expensive cancer medicines in the autumn, but we are also dealing with the current situation. Next week, the Minister will discuss this issue with health insurers and hospitals. That is why the parties who took part in the debate this afternoon would like to present the following by means of the motion. [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[Whereas there are increasing signs that patients do not in all cases receive the treatment with expensive medicines that are available, thereby compromising the confidence of patients as to whether they are receiving the right treatment;]] [[Considers that healthcare providers and health insurers have a responsibility to come up with good solutions here to ensure compliance with the obligation of care;]] [[Whereas the Minister can play a facilitating role from the point of view of her system responsibility;]] [[calls on the parties involved in the distribution of responsibility to resolve the acute problem of expensive medicines;]] [[urges the government, during the administrative consultation, to introduce this feeling of the House of Representatives and to inform the House of Representatives before 12 July 2015,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion was proposed by members Van Gerven, Bruins Slot, Rutte, Otwin van Dijk, Agema, Pia Dijkstra and Krol.]] [[She's getting No. kst-29477-336]]
