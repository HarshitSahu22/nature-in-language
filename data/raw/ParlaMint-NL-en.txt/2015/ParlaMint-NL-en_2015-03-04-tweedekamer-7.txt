[[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[Whereas, as of April 2014, the Council of State provided that residential ships are subject to authorisation under the General Environmental Law Provisions Act (Wabo);]] [[Whereas repair legislation is in the process of establishing that existing residential ships are automatically granted a Wabo permit and that new and replacement residential ships are subject to the adapted rules of the Construction Decision;]] [[Whereas since the motion for a resolution by Mr Paulus Jansen (32730), No. 12) is committed to amending legislation governing the protection of the legal status of residential ships;]] [[Whereas improving the legal position of houseboat residents with regard to berths is complex and major interests are at stake;]] [[Considers that the amendment of the Decision on noise pollution and the Environmental Activity Decision (admissible taxation of residential ships at sunny industrial sites and establishments) does not provide sufficient relief to the legal situation of residential ships;]] [[requests the Government to amend the Decision on noise pollution (e.g.) explicitly involve in the legislation on the protection of the legal status of residential ships,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion has been proposed by Mr Smaling.]] [[She's getting No. kst-29383-235]]
ParlaMint-NL_2015-03-04-tweedekamer-7.u3	Mr. Chairman. Then we saved Mr. Smaling. He could still file his motion. This VSO is about the permissible noise load of residential ships that are moored in industrial areas, so to speak, in ports. Due to previous decision-making in 2012, residential ships were now classified as sound-sensitive objects. Of course, that meant something. As a result, the industrial companies in the vicinity, for example a shipyard, were confronted with new noise standards. You can try to isolate a houseship, but that's very difficult. That's hard to do. It's different from a house. That is why we are now proposing an increase in the permissible noise levels. There will also be a transitional period for the residential ships lying there. In due course, the Law will introduce noise nuisance into the Environment Act. It is also intended to make the most of existing regulations and standards of government, for example the SWUNG legislation on noise. All in all, this proposal can count on the support of the VVD. If people live in residential ships close to industrial activities, there will always be a certain amount of noise nuisance. That is logical and that is also good, because then we work for the BV Nederland. We therefore support an increase in the number of decibels and a transitional period. That means everyone knows what they're up to. This avoids high costs and all kinds of red tape. The VVD therefore supports this proposal.
