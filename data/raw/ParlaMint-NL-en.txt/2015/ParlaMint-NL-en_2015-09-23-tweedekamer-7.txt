ParlaMint-NL_2015-09-23-tweedekamer-7.u6	Mr. Chairman. In the motion in part No. 163 asks member Kooiman of the SP the government to carry out a broader analysis of the quality and completeness of the source register in order to find out what the quality of data is. This has indeed been discussed extensively in the AA. Human Inference conducts technical research into data quality and coherence between source registers. In conclusion, the data quality of each individual registry is good, but the key data need to be improved. These are therefore data that ensure consistency between registers and not patient-level data. Important step is to add the trade register number to the AGB file. As I have reported in general consultation, that step is now being carried out by Vektis. Therefore, I advise against this motion. In the motion in part No. 164 asks Mrs Klever of the PVV to investigate possible alternatives to the LSP, making a number of suggestions. We found together years ago that the government is not responsible for systems, but that it is responsible for the framework conditions within which data are exchanged. If someone wants to apply certain things and apply them within the framework conditions laid down by the government, then that is fine with us. We have already done research, which I do not wish to repeat. Anyone can start with any system, as long as it meets the conditions. I therefore advise against the motion.
