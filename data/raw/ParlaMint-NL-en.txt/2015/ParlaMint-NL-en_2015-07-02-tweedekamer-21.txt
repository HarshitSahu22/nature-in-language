ParlaMint-NL_2015-07-02-tweedekamer-21.u3	Mr. Chairman. I have almost literally remembered what Mr Rog has said. If you do not hear me literally, Mr President, I dare give an opinion on the Rog c.s motion now. in piece No. 247. You could say there's an interesting situation. The CDA Group was first in favour of the introduction of the calculation test. It then opposed the introduction of the calculation test. Now it seems that the CDA is in favour of introducing the calculation test, but not for next year. I do not quite follow it, but in that sense there is an improvement on the previous input from the CDA Group. Nevertheless, I advise against this motion. We have noticed in recent years that schools also need peace, as little change as possible in the policies and rules on calculation. In 2012 we already postponed it once. On the contrary, if schools are delayed, they will make less work on calculation rather than more. The motivation will fall. This was also noted by the committee which carried out the mid-term review. It is true that the letter contains the preliminary figures for secondary education. That's always so communicated. The Court knows that the final figures will be available at the end of September, as soon as possible after the summer recess. Then we'll be able to analyze them well. If the final figures give reason to reconsider something, it can always be done at that time. This really isn't the time to say that next year shouldn't count.
