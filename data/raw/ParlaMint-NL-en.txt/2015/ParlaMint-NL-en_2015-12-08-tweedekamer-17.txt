ParlaMint-NL_2015-12-08-tweedekamer-17.u6	Mr. Chairman. I would like to thank the committee for the good discussion we have had on the reception of refugee children and the provision of good education. I share what Mrs Ypma has said in her motion, namely that anyone who puts their shoulders on this to do so in the best possible way, whether they are municipalities, school boards or people from my ministry, from V and J or from the COA, deserves a great compliment. After all, it has a lot to do when you suddenly have to stamp new education out of the ground for these numbers of refugees that we are currently dealing with. I can see that this is getting better and better. Two questions have been asked. Mr Grashoff asked about the implementation of the Voordewind/Voortman motion on limiting movement. I'm talking to my colleague from V and J about this. Besides education, there are other reasons why it is good to reduce movement. The breakthrough that we achieved two weeks ago in the administrative agreement together with municipalities and provinces that is precisely aimed at achieving more permanent reception for refugees in municipalities and municipalities will help greatly. It's clearly on our retina. Thanks to Mr Van Meenen for the commitment on pre-financing. I don't think it's gonna play with a lot of school boards, but where that is the case, I'm absolutely prepared to jump in and offer some kind of customization. I will make sure that this is always clear in our communication to schools. Four motions have been tabled. I shall begin with the Grashoff motion in part No. 4. It actually asks: extend the scheme of first primary care from one year to two years. I'd like to discourage this motion, and this is why. It is not new that asylum seekers come to the Netherlands and that we are dealing with refugee children. This arrangement already existed and worked well. In that sense, it is adequate. The challenge we face now is that we are facing far greater numbers. We are trying to make the regulation more precise by applying it once in a while by means of more time limits. A motion was tabled by Mrs Ypma on this subject, which was co-signed by Mr Grashoff. I can go a long way with that, but this is actually the broadening, the raising of the regime and in my view there is no need to do so. I have already indicated in the debate why there is a difference between the po and vo. Moreover, this is a motion which will have major financial consequences in implementation. I don't see any cover. Again, I don't think the motion is necessary and I don't think she's covered either, which is why I'm betraying her. In his motion in part No. 5 Mr Van Meenen asked for the OHBA scheme to be extended or opened up, especially at emergency shelters. By the way, that arrangement is not from my ministry, but from the Ministry of Security and Justice. It was deliberately chosen to limit that scheme to six months. When it comes to a shorter time, for example two months, it is not justified to set up a whole new building. In such a case, it is better to look at alternatives, for example to transport children to nearby schools. I would therefore advise against this motion.
