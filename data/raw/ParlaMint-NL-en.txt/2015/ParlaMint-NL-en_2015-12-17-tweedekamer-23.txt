ParlaMint-NL_2015-12-17-tweedekamer-23.u5	Mr. Chairman. Mrs Siderius's first motion calls on the government to make a step in the way of the reduction of beds. I would like to emphasise that the effort to cut down beds is not accompanied by cuts. I'm not cutting back on the ggz. We still have a lot of beds, especially compared to the rest of Europe. We are still among the top three in Europe. We build the number of beds in the Netherlands because we all think that people should receive care and support in their own environment as much as possible. I don't think it's right to stop the movement to cut off the inpatient ggz. I want to make a commitment to intensifying the care system. What can help me is a clear instrument and clear criteria that allow local parties to work to determine how much and what care is needed. I'd like to make a big effort to do that, but I'd like to advise against this motion. The second motion by Mrs Siderius asks me to ensure that cooperation between large institutions with small healthcare providers is ensured within the framework of the quality statute. It is clear that cooperation is of great importance. Cooperation is also part of the quality status, and that is not yet the case. The parties are still playing around with this and I want to prevent us from investing in a process that is already very complicated and which I really want to see in January in the form of a quality statute, with all kinds of things. Cooperation is important, but let me first wait for the quality status. Then I'll judge it on his merits. So I'd like to discourage this motion.
