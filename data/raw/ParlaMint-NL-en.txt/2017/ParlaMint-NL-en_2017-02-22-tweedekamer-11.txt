ParlaMint-NL_2017-02-22-tweedekamer-11.u1	I call Mrs Schut-Welken on behalf of the VVD Group.
ParlaMint-NL_2017-02-22-tweedekamer-11.u2	Mr. Chairman. In the AA we discussed in detail the Minister's commitment to the negotiations on the revision of the coordination regulation. The Minister replied in three minutes because he wanted to keep his hands free as much as possible for the negotiations. We therefore consider it necessary to send the following message to the Minister. That is what we are doing in the form of a motion. [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[Whereas in many countries of the European Union the cost of living is lower than in the Netherlands;]] [[whereas on 5 December 2013, the Chamber adopted the Heerma motion calling on the Cabinet to adopt an export ban on child benefit in the EU;]] [[Considering that on 3 March 2016 the Chamber adopted the Schut-Welkenn/Herma motion calling on the government to make efforts to implement the country-of-home principle as soon as possible in the export of all child schemes within the EU;]] [[Whereas on 19 February 2016 the European Council concluded that the country of residence principle would apply to the export of child schemes if the United Kingdom had remained in the European Union;]] [[Whereas the negotiations on the revision of the coordination regulation will start this spring;]] [[calls on the Government to pursue the application of the country of residence principle in the export of child schemes within the EU in the context of the revision of the Social Security Systems Coordination Regulation,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion has been proposed by Mr Schut-Welken. It seems to me that their submission is sufficiently supported.]] [[She's getting No. kst-34655-3]]
ParlaMint-NL_2017-02-22-tweedekamer-11.u3	Mr. Chairman. I'd like to dissuade the motion. The motion calls on the cabinet to pursue the application of the country of residence principle within the EU. In general terms, I have outlined to the Chamber what the negotiations are like now. As this is not feasible at the moment, it could worsen the negotiating position of the Netherlands on a number of other dossiers, including one to which Mrs Schut attaches great importance, namely the waiting time for the WW. That is why I do not think it is appropriate to do so. I understand very well that the Chamber has expressed that wish; so there is that wish. It's in the back of my head. If there is a possibility, we will. But I am afraid that this motion could damage the position of the Netherlands in those negotiations. That's why I'm trying to dissuade her. At the same time, these are the last words I speak in the Chamber in this Chamber. Through you, Mr President, I would like to express my thanks for years of extremely constructive cooperation with opposition and coalition. Thank you very much.
ParlaMint-NL_2017-02-22-tweedekamer-11.u4	The motion tabled will be put to the vote tomorrow.
