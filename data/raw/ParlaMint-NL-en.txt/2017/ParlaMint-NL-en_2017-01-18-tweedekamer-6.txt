ParlaMint-NL_2017-01-18-tweedekamer-6.u2	Mr. Chairman. We cannot ignore it: the development of the value of the derivative portfolio still has a potentially substantial impact on the financial position of and risks to housing corporations. In its response, the Minister states that the total liquidity buffer of the joint corporations of 2.3 billion will therefore be large enough for the time being, but that it is likely to increase in 2018 and later years in view of the break-up clause. But with how much and with what risk for the corporation sector is not sufficiently clear. As mentioned earlier, the corporations in question have submitted or are in the process of breaking plans for this purpose, but the multiannual review is not yet clear and therefore the long-term risks are not clear. That's why I'm tabling the next motion. [[Motion]] [[The Chamber,]] [[heard the debate,]] [[Whereas long-term effects on the liquidity buffer of the joint corporations are still not sufficiently understandable;]] [[Whereas it should be clear what financial risk is still incurred by corporations in the long term;]] [[Calls on the Government to report to the Chamber the long-term effects for the liquidity buffer of the joint corporations before 1 July 2017,]] [[and goes to the order of the day.]] [[This motion has been proposed by member Ronnes. It seems to me that their submission is sufficiently supported.]] [[She's getting No. kst-29453-435]]
ParlaMint-NL_2017-01-18-tweedekamer-6.u3	The motion is being copied. I see the Minister can respond immediately.
ParlaMint-NL_2017-01-18-tweedekamer-6.u4	Mr. Chairman. In his motion, Mr Ronnes asked for item No. 435 to report to the Chamber the long-term effects for the liquidity buffer of the joint corporations before 1 July. This means that, on the basis of assumptions about interest rate developments, we make an estimate of the liquidity buffer that would be needed at that time. Of course, I cannot predict the interest rate itself; I can only estimate which buffer would belong to which interest rate. We can provide such an overview. So I'd like to leave the verdict on the motion to the Chamber.
ParlaMint-NL_2017-01-18-tweedekamer-6.u5	We shall vote on the motion tabled next Tuesday.
