ParlaMint-NL_2017-12-20-tweedekamer-7.u7	Thank you, Mr. Chairman. I would like to thank the Chamber for its input, both during the VOC and during the AA on working conditions and enforcement. I'll be happy to provide the motions with a response. I shall begin with the first motion by Mr Van Kent and Mr Van Dijk. That's the motion in part No. 315, about the heavy objects. I take the signals about the risks of lifting heavy packages in sorting centres and in parcel delivery very seriously. Where the inspection detects abuses, it also acts. I think it is important for employers in this sector to examine the risks of physical overload on their employees and to include them in the risk inventory and assessment. However, since this is really about tailor-made work, the solution should not be sought in setting one general standard, but rather in making good agreements between employer and employee on technical aids and the organisation of work, in order to minimise the lifting load. What I have said in general consultation and what I would like to repeat here is that I am prepared to bring the risks of the work to the attention of the sectors concerned and to discuss them, but I would advise against this motion. In the second motion, the motion by Mr Van Kent/Van Dijk on part No. 316, is asked for an extension of the language requirement. Indeed, the inspection also indicates in reports that non-management of the Dutch language in some places leads to dangerous situations. Good language management is therefore important for the safety of the workplace, but it is not an obligation based on the Arbo Act. There are other obligations, however, that must prevent dangerous situations. Good communication is important for security. The employer will have to pay attention to this in the risk assessment and assessment and take appropriate measures. The inclusion of a language requirement is therefore unnecessary. I therefore advise against this motion.
