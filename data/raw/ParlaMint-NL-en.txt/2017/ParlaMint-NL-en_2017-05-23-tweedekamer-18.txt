ParlaMint-NL_2017-05-23-tweedekamer-18.u6	Mr. Chairman. I cannot congratulate Mrs Van den Berg and Mr Weverling on their maiden speech, but I can say that it deserves congratulations if you have really started the actual work in the Chamber, today with an AO and here with a VAO. I think that in the coming years they will be able to enjoy this very honorable function, to represent the people in this house. Four motions have been tabled. In the motion in part No. 401 by Ms Van den Berg and Mr Weverling, the government is asked to consult the Chamber in the event of significant developments on the file of the European e-card, i.e. the service card, with the BNC sheet and the comments of the Chamber during the General Consultation Council on Competitiveness of 23 May 2017. I intend to do what's in that motion. We have the opportunity to inform the Chamber twelve times a year through the annotated agendas and the Competitiveness Council reports. In addition, if there is a new proposal from the European Commission, we will present a BNC sheet within six weeks. If necessary, we will also come to the Chamber with information in the meantime. I think that the request to inform the Chamber of this interesting development once a quarter in this pattern is useful. I'll leave the verdict on this motion to the Chamber. The motion in part No. 402 by Mr Weverling and Mrs Van den Berg on national competition authorities. A directive is on the table to strengthen its powers. In fact, this motion calls for the same thing with regard to this subject. What I just said also applies to this motion. I therefore leave the judgment to the Chamber and will amend it in the regular and, if necessary, in the additional information that will be provided to the Chamber. The motion in part No. 402: Judgment Room. In the motion in part No. 403 asks Mr Weverling to enter into intensive consultations with entrepreneurs from various sectors before the summer to find out what bottlenecks are being experienced in practice. I will organise this through the business organisations, with which I streamline and manage contacts of this kind. I'll do that before the summer. We have already had discussions and we will continue to do so. The sectors covered are those covered by the e-card or service card. What's written here, I can do it this way. I shall leave the judgment on the motion in part No. 403 to the Room. The motion in part No. 404 by Mr Paternotte was also signed by Mr Van Eijs, Mr Van den Berg and Mr Van der Lee. This motion calls on the government to support the European Commission's improvement proposals, including proposals on compliance checks and inspections. In the spirit of that motion, I would like to support the Presidency compromise. That Presidency compromise is in the spirit of the European Commission's proposals and, in our opinion, is also feasible with the 28 countries at the Competitiveness Council on Monday. The explanation I give to the motion is that we support the European Commission by supporting the Presidency's proposal. If that is the intention of this motion, and I am not against it, then I shall leave it to the House to decide. Thank you, Madam President.
