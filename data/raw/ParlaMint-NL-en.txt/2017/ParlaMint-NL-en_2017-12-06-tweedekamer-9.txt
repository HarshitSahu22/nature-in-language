This must be discussed not only with the poultry sector, but also with the European Commission. I would like to emphasise that the discussions with the European Commission are still ongoing. ParlaMint-NL_2017-12-06-tweedekamer-9.u65	The final motion.
ParlaMint-NL_2017-12-06-tweedekamer-9.u66	The last motion, in part No. 246. The idea of equivalent measures is sympathetic. In the case of equal or better environmental results, farmers may, if scientifically proven, take measures that are more appropriate to their business operations. However, the elaboration of the equivalent measures in the Fifth Action Programme has shown that good support is required and that equivalent measures can only be taken under strict conditions, because of the need for proper safeguards, and without increasing the burden of implementation and enforcement. This is reflected in the extent to which equivalent measures are used: significantly less than expected. The draft Sixth Action Programme therefore partly continues the equivalent measures contained in the Fifth Action Programme, but also terminates them after the entry into force of a generic measure instead. On this basis, therefore, I do not see any need to introduce further equivalent measures. That's why I advise against this motion.
