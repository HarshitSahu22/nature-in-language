Mr. Chairman. Tomorrow is the social summit in Gothenburg, where the social pillar is indeed being signed. It is a manifesto to boost social policy in Europe, but on the basis that it remains the responsibility of the national Member States. This has also been explicitly agreed in the social pillar. The convergence that is being discussed is learning from each other by incorporating good examples from other countries into your own policies. We have seen in recent years that there are considerable differences between Member States. Youth unemployment and unemployment in a number of southern European countries were very high. What can you learn from each other and how can you ensure that unemployment is not so high? That is the convergence we are talking about. My opinion on the motion in part No. 463 by Mr De Graaf c.s. is that I should discourage it, because I think that the social pillar is an important step in the right direction, which the Cabinet wishes to support wholeheartedly.
