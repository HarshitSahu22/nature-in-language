ParlaMint-NL_2017-02-21-tweedekamer-36.u8	Mr. Chairman. The motion-Ouwehand/Smaling in part No. 336 on the damages fund, I advise. I have already said in general consultation that there must be agreement on a compensation fund before a crop can be authorised. However, this agreement does not necessarily mean that there will be a compensation fund. There may also be agreement that this should be waived. Consensus is the prerequisite for me. The motion-Ouwehand/Smaling in part No. 337 on freedom of choice, I consider policy support, so I leave it to the Chamber to decide. The motion in part No. Mrs Ouwehand's 338 is a statement which is submitted to the Chamber. It is not a request to the government, so I await the Court's judgment on it. The motion-Old-Hand in part No. 339 I must advise against. Although it explicitly states "involvement in the balancing framework," it is assumed that if a crop is patented, herbicide-resistant or produces a pesticide itself, this is reason to reject a crop. It's not that. This is contrary to the GM Directive, because other European regulations allow patenting and patenting. However, the use of plant protection products is one of the elements in the balancing framework. The authorisation and use of those means are regulated by law, but it is one of the elements in the balancing framework. The Secretary of State for I and M will deal with the motion in section No. 340. I shall now come to Mr Smaling's motion on item No. 341 on co-existence agreements. In fact, that is a policy support, because that issue is in the balancing framework. I'll leave the verdict on this motion to the Chamber. In Mr Smaling's motion to item No. 342 the Government is invited to update the co-existence agreements of the Covenant in consultation with the Parties to the state of science. The current policy is topical, but if there are new insights, then the policy will be adjusted if necessary. I do not see any reason for this at the moment, but it is possible that this will be the case in the future. As far as this motion is concerned, too, I leave it to the Chamber to decide.
