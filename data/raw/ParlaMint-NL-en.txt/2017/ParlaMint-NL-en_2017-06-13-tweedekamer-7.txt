ParlaMint-NL_2017-06-13-tweedekamer-7.u2	Mr. Chairman. The Committee for the Examination of the Letters of Faith has examined the documents relating to Mr A. Elissen in The Hague. The committee has concluded that it has rightly been declared a Member of the European Parliament. The committee finds that it may be admitted to the European Parliament on the basis of national provisions. The committee proposes to inform the President of the European Parliament and the person appointed. The Committee also proposes that the report be included in the Acts.
ParlaMint-NL_2017-06-13-tweedekamer-7.u3	I would like to thank the committee for its report and propose that it be decided accordingly.
