ParlaMint-NL_2017-06-07-tweedekamer-6.u1	I would like to welcome the Minister for Security and Justice. I give the floor to Mr Van Nispen as the first speaker on behalf of the SP Group.
ParlaMint-NL_2017-06-07-tweedekamer-6.u2	Mr. Chairman. We have been talking for several years about the creation of a European Public Prosecutor's Office. For a long time it was the question of whether the European Public Prosecutor would be there, but in the coming days 17 Member States are indeed setting up a European Public Prosecutor's Office and the question is whether the Netherlands will join us. It may sound sympathetic, but who is not in favour of tackling fraud with European money? But it is a bad idea. The SP has always opposed this, because there are already alternatives to combating fraud, such as better cooperation between investigation services and strengthening OLAF's anti-fraud office. In addition, it is in line with the expectation that a once-established European OM will be expanded. Moreover, the European Public Prosecutor's Office is basically a transfer of powers. We are losing control of the use of Dutch detection capacity. So it also violates our principle of opportunity. The Minister asked the House of Representatives in his letter for a final judgment. That's why I'm filing the next motion.
