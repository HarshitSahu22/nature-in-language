ParlaMint-IT_2017-11-27-LEG17-Senato-sed-910.ana.u4	Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the 5th standing Commission is proceeding, I must say with very regular work, in examining the measure submitted to it and the many amendments tabled by the senators. First of all, I should like to express my thanks and recognition to all the members of the 5th Commission, who are working very hard, and in particular to the opposition groups, who have chosen a very responsible and cooperative attitude. We are at a good point in our work: I can say that. Of the three major dossiers - my fellow Members know how our work works - of the amendments that have been pointed out, on which - although we have a regulation different from that of the House which does not exempt us from the need to evaluate all the others - the Commission's attention is in some way concentrated, two have been completed. However, Mr President, we still need a few more hours to complete our work. So I would be here to ask you to be able to use all of tomorrow's day for the work of the Commission and therefore to be able to come to this House, hopefully with the mandate to the rapporteur - I have to say that all the groups are working to make this happen - Wednesday morning.
