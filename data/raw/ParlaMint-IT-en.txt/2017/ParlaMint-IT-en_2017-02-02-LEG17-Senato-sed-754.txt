Here we are in the presence of the obligation, imposed on all territorial bodies, to draw up plans for civil protection and intervention. What if someone doesn't do it? Doesn't anyone intervene? It is therefore necessary to define a replacement power mechanism, because civil protection has needs that go beyond local competences and privileges of local power; it has national needs and must be present throughout the national territory.
