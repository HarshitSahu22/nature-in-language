ParlaMint-IT_2017-05-30-LEG17-Senato-sed-832.ana.u3	The next item is the President's statement on the timetable for proceedings. The Conference of Leaders approved the timetable for the Assembly's work until 29 June. Today's sitting will be taken off immediately after the timetable. This week will continue - where possible with respect to the opinion of the Budget Committee - the examination of the bill on the recognition of the Italian language of signs and the following draft laws will be discussed: collective domains; aggregation of the Municipality of Torre de' Busi to the Province of Bergamo; detachment of the Municipality of Sappada from Veneto and aggregation to Friuli-Venezia Giulia; institution of a parliamentary committee of inquiry into the events that occurred in the community Il Forteto. The timetable also provides for the Parliamentary Commission's report on regional issues on the State-Territorial Autonomies link. Next week the Assembly will not hold a session to allow the work of the Commissions 1a and 5a on the decree-law on financial matters currently on the agenda of the Chamber of Deputies. At the afternoon sitting on Tuesday 13 June, with the possible continuation of the afternoon sitting, the motions for resolutions on the reform of the common agricultural policy and others on agriculture will be discussed. The calendar of the week also includes the draft law on the anti-mafia code, where concluded by the Commission, and the decree-law on financial matters. At the opening of the afternoon sitting on Wednesday 14 June, the call for the election of a member of the Court of Auditors' Board of President will be held. Then the ballot boxes will remain open until 1 p.m. During the afternoon session on Thursday 15 June the examination of the bill on citizenship will be started. Amendments to the measure should be tabled no later than 1 p.m. on the same day. The sessions of Tuesday 20 June will be devoted entirely to the discussion of motions for abbreviated motions on the Council summits and on the proliferation of nuclear weapons. The timetable for the week also includes communications from the President of the Council of Ministers with a view to the European Council on 22 and 23 June. In the week from 27 to 29 June will be discussed the decree-law on the obligation of vaccinations - where presented to the Senate - and the related bill on the discipline of cinema, audiovisual and entertainment, where concluded by the Commission. The draft law on the celebration of the two thousand years since the death of Ovid will also be examined; on the provision of Internet services and - where concluded by the Commission - on the anticipated provisions of treatment. In the afternoon sitting on Tuesday 27 June, the motion on Taiwan's participation in international organisations will be discussed. Finally, requests for information from the Minister for Economic Affairs on the Venetian popular banks and the Minister for Foreign Affairs on the summit on the Western Balkans scheduled in Trieste on 12 July can be made in the Commission as there are no comments, so it remains established.
