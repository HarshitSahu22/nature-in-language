ParlaMint-IT_2017-03-29-LEG17-Senato-sed-796.ana.u5	The next item is the vote on the election of three Secretary Senators. The election will take place through the use of two cards: one, yellow, for the supplementary election of two senators Secretary; the other, pink, for the election of a senator Secretary at the request of the Group Grandi Autonomie e Libertà. Pursuant to Rule 5 (3) and (4) of the Rules of Procedure, each senator will write on his yellow card a single name. The two senators will be elected to obtain the highest number of votes. With equal votes, the oldest will be elected. Pursuant to the transitional provision of the XVII legislature and Article 5, paragraphs 2-c and 4, cited therein, of the Rules of Procedure, each senator will write on the pink card a single name. He will be elected who, being a member of the Group Grandi Autonomie e Libertà, will obtain the highest number of votes. With equal votes, the oldest will be elected. In order to ensure the smoothest conduct of voting operations, a cabin has been set up in front of the Presidency. My fellow senators, immediately before entering the cabin, will receive from the parliamentary assistants the two ballot papers which, after the vote, will be placed in the appropriate ballot boxes at the exit of the cabin. The call will be done in alphabetical order. I therefore declare the votes open and invite the Senator Secretary to proceed to the appeal. (The Senator Secretary Sibilia and, later, the Senator Secretary Saggese make the appeal). (In the course of voting, it takes the Presidency - 18.57 p.m.)
