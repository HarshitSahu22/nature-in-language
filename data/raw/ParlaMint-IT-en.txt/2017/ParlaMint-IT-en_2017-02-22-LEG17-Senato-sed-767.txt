ParlaMint-IT_2017-02-22-LEG17-Senato-sed-767.ana.u7	Mr President, I believe that the debate on this decree-law, which we held in this House yesterday, was important. In particular, several interventions, such as those of Senators Padua, Santini and Gualdani, have highlighted the meaning of the decree-law, but I also appreciated the critical interventions, which however (in principle) have captured some important elements present in the measure. I would like to emphasise the overall meaning of this decree-law. Certainly, as the title of the decree-law states, we are not talking about a measure that introduces a whole set of interventions on the subject of the Mezzogiorno, but of a measure that brings urgent action, with particular reference to certain critical situations, in some areas of the Mezzogiorno. However, as was pointed out in yesterday's debate, these are specific situations which are of great general importance. I am thinking, for example, of Article 1, which deals with the issue of environmental rehabilitation of Ilva and the protection of the health of citizens in the city of Taranto. This is a specific issue, but at the same time of great general significance, because it means addressing the underlying theme of a new phase of industrial development, which is environmentally compatible and which fully protects the health of citizens. Within that article, a further strengthening of the protection of workers during the transition phase, which is opening up through the transfer of the ILVA complex, was introduced with a government amendment. Always reattaching myself to the topic of health protection, I am also thinking of the amendment of parliamentary origin, introduced to the Chamber of Deputies (which allocates 100 million euros to the development of cancer radiotherapy in the regions of the Mezzogiorno and therefore to the strengthening of the health structures of the Mezzogiorno in this direction), which accompanies what already foreseen in the original version of the decree-law with specific reference to the health structures of the city of Taranto, for which the Government has foreseen the allocation of 70 million euros for the acquisition of advanced instruments in the field of diagnostic. Article 2, which deals with the question of purifiers and the acceleration in the implementation of waste water treatment plants, in response to European infringement procedures, also addresses a question which is specific, but which is also of general importance, namely the protection of the environment and in particular the seas of the Mezzogiorno, which are a fundamental resource for the economic development of the Mezzogiorno, starting from the activities of tourism, not to mention, of course, those related to fisheries. Also the article 4 of the decree-law, that introduces the possibility to constitute agencies of administration of the job in the case of ports touched by the crisis of the transhipment - we think in particular to those of Taranto and Gioia Tauro - touches a specific topic, but of general relief: it is the role of Italy and in the specific of the Mezzogiorno in the development of the traffics goods in the Mediterranean, in particular after the opening of the second branch of the Suez Canal. Finally, in Article 7, there is the question of the G7 which, as is clear, concerns a fundamental theme, namely the image of our country on an international level, witnessed precisely by a reality of particular importance, beauty and cultural tradition as is Taormina. Several amendments introduced to the Chamber (I have already mentioned that on the strengthening of oncology radiotherapy structures in the Mezzogiorno) have further expanded and strengthened the establishment of the Decree-Law. I refer, in particular, to three amendments, the first of which concerns the possibility for municipalities to use property confiscated from companies for reasons of organised crime, including for social purposes: I am thinking, for example, of the whole issue of housing. I consider this an important and significant step forward, in the spirit in which we proceed in the use of property confiscated from criminal organizations. The amendment on tax credit has significantly strengthened this instrument, so that companies investing in the Mezzogiorno can make full use of the financial envelope of 600 million per year for four years, since these resources, where fully used, can have a very important leverage effect for more than 1.7 billion investments per year in the Mezzogiorno. In addition, an amendment of parliamentary origin and reworded by the Government provides an address to the Government in the allocation of budgetary resources from the Budget Act for 2018, so that ordinary capital appropriations for investments are equally distributed on national territory so that the additional resources of the Development and Cohesion Fund and the Structural Funds can play their full role in reducing the gap between the Mezzogiorno and the Centre-North. It is therefore a decree-law that puts urgent interventions in the field in specific critical situations, which also has a more general meaning because those same interventions have a more general meaning. The measure under consideration is therefore part of a broader plan of action that with the Renzi Government has seen the approach of the masterplan for the Mezzogiorno and its application through the Pacts for the South with the Regions and the Metropolitan Cities of the Mezzogiorno and that sees the Gentiloni Government Silveri committed to the Mezzogiorno as a priority on its agenda. From this point of view and in response to some interventions, I would like to stress that the funds available for cohesion policies for the Mezzogiorno are very important; in particular, I would add that in the last budget law the Development and Cohesion Fund has been further increased by almost EUR 11 billion, so it has a total budget of EUR 54.8 billion today. Adding to this the European Structural Funds, of which almost half are national co-financing, we are more than 100 billion, to be precise to 124 billion euros for cohesion policies, which partly very large (between 70% and 80%) go to the regions of the Mezzogiorno. Of these 124 billion, 82 are from the national budget and 42 are from the EU budget. These are important resources, the key point is to make full use of them. We have demonstrated, with the recovery in the use of the Structural Funds 2007-2013 (today we are on the absorption plan of those resources), the ability of the administrations, as today reorganized through both the Department for Cohesion Policies of the Presidency of the Council and the Agency for Territorial Cohesion and the task forces that we have built together with the Regions of the Mezzogiorno, to use the funds. Today, therefore, we are able to give full effect to the policies that use that mass of resources I have spoken about, which must be at the service, as we wrote in the masterplan, of the potential that exists in the Mezzogiorno. The task of the institutions, of the Minister for Territorial Cohesion and the Mezzogiorno and of the Government all, the task of regional institutions, metropolitan cities and municipalities must be to build, with these funds, the conditions within which the excellences present in the Mezzogiorno can express themselves fully: entrepreneurial skills, skills, innovative skills, which are already felt today. We saw it yesterday with the ISMEA-Svimez report on agriculture in the Mezzogiorno, but we also saw it with the Svimez report last summer and with the so-called Mezzogiorno check-up by the Confindustria study centre and we also saw it in the Confindustria-union document on the policies of the Mezzogiorno: productive realities of the Mezzogiorno that are projecting on the international markets and that have strong potential for development. Our task is to ensure that they spread throughout the Mezzogiorno and create the possibilities for a strong recovery of growth in the Mezzogiorno. I would like to stress one last point, which was also highlighted in some of yesterday's speeches. We have repeatedly said that without the recovery of the Mezzogiorno there is no recovery of the whole country and I would like to stress that the task of the Minister for Territorial Cohesion and the Mezzogiorno and of the whole Government is to make the southern question a national issue, the development of the Mezzogiorno an integral part of national development and policies for the Mezzogiorno an integral part of our country's economic policy. In other words, the task is to create the conditions of a really cohesive country, from Aosta to Bari, from Trieste to Palermo, so that the possibilities of development are homogeneous throughout the national territory and, indeed, the Mezzogiorno can grow even more so as to reduce the gap from the Centre-North and to ensure that all Italian citizens are fully Italian citizens, with equal rights. The southern issue as a national issue: we started with the Renzi Government, we continue with the Silveri Gentiloni Government. At this point, I would like to thank Mr Tonini, the rapporteur, Mr Lai, for his work in the Commission, both in the majority and in the opposition, for this has created a very collaborative working environment, as we have already seen in the work of the House of Deputies. Here in the Senate we found a very important spirit of cooperation. In particular, we have appreciated the choice made in the Commission of a rapid vote on the amendments, with the withdrawal or transformation of several of them into agendas, both of the majority and of the opposition forces, many of which have been accepted by the Government. I hope that this climate of cooperation will continue here in the Senate House because, bearing in mind that, however, as we know, the time is limited and the decree-law must be converted by 28 February, it would be important for the Government, not putting the vote of confidence, to ensure that we share together, majority and opposition, the membership of the measures contained in this decree-law. I repeat that these, in my opinion, are important in themselves and because they are part of a general plan, to which Parliament has made an important contribution in the course of its work. Thank you for your attention. [[Applause from the PD Group and Senator Lucio Romano.]]
