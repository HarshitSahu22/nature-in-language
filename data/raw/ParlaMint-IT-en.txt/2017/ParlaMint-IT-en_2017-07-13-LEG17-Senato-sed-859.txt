ParlaMint-IT_2017-07-13-LEG17-Senato-sed-859.ana.u6	Madam President, the act of inspection union under discussion concerns the modalities of activation and use of the electronic card for updating and training referred to in Article 1, paragraph 121, of Law No 107 of 2015. It should be recalled, first, that on December 1, 2016, the decree of the President of the Council of Ministers was published in the Official Journal, which replaced the previous decree of the President of the Council of Ministers with which, in a transitional way, the arrangements for the allocation and use of the Electronic Card for the updating and training of the teacher of the role of the educational institutions of each order and grade for the previous school year 2015-2016 were governed. In order to ensure the immediate payment of the nominal amount of 500 euros for the school year 2015-2016, in fact, the said amount was assigned to the teachers of role through collective payment orders pursuant to article 2, paragraph 197, of the financial law for 2010, i.e. through the so-called single cedolino. The use of the single cedolino has been a transitional measure, as reiterated by the same General Accounting of the State, which has specified that the solution adopted for the year 2015 is to be considered temporary and extraordinary and that there is no willingness to continue with the management of NoiPA for the year 2016-2017. With regard to the school year 2016-2017, it is possible, starting from 30 November 2016, through the application of the teacher's charter web, available at the paper address of the teacher. instruction.it, to make purchases with the operators and institutions accredited to sell the goods and services that fall within the categories provided for by the norm. Each teacher, through the application, can directly generate electronic spending vouchers with identification code associated with a purchase of goods or services permitted by Article 1, paragraph 121, of Law No 107 of 2015, on display of the said voucher. With reference, as pointed out by the questioner, to the sums already spent on teacher training from 1 September to 30 November 2016 and related to the school year 2016-2017, with Article 12, paragraph 3, of the decree of the President of the Council of Ministers 28 November 2016, it has been established that the same are recorded by the teacher on the platform (so as to allow the relative reduction from the electronic purse of the corresponding sum) and, through the same application, communicated to the MIUR, who will provide, after reporting the school institutions, to provide special financing to the school institutions of teacher service, which in turn provide the provision to the directly concerned. In fact, according to Note Protocol No 5314 of 10 March 2017 operational instructions were provided to school institutions for the above-mentioned reporting of the amounts spent in the period 1 September 2016 - 30 November 2016 and related to the school year 2016-2017. Since April 19 of the current year, this administration, for the purposes of the management of self-declarations presented by teachers for the aforementioned period, has enabled all educational institutions to operate on the SIDI portal. By notice published on the SIDI portal on 28 June 2017, the deadline for such operations was extended to 15 July. CONSAP will provide the amounts relating to the costs validated and deemed eligible directly to the school institutions, which will reimburse the teachers who submitted the request for reimbursement. In the light of the above, there is no form of discontinuity in the provision of the benefit referred to in paragraph 121 of Law No 107, which rather continues to be guaranteed only in different ways, but all equally effective.

ParlaMint-IT_2017-07-13-LEG17-Senato-sed-859.ana.u11	Madam President, the honourable Member, on the basis of the situation in which some children with disabilities would have been attending the schools of the Municipality of Vigevano, who would have risked being deprived of assistants to the person since 2 March, recalls the need to clarify the competences for the provision of assistance services, as required by the agenda at his first signature approved in the conversion of Decree-Law 243 of 2016 on urgent measures for social and territorial cohesion, with particular reference to critical situations in certain areas of the Mezzogiorno. Firstly, there is an obligation to point out that this Ministry takes great account of the problem raised by the honourable Member. To the various initiatives implemented over time, the Legislative Decree No. 66 of 2017 was added, with which the delegation conferred on the Government by Article 1, paragraphs 180 and 181 (c) of Law No. 107 of 2015, was exercised in order to reorder the matter related to inclusion. The aforementioned legislative decree, in Article 3, identifies and rearranges the benefits and competences for the implementation of school inclusion between State, Regions and local authorities. The main novelty of the text is that, for the first time, as part of the definition of the staff of ATA staff, account will be taken of the presence in each school of pupils with disabilities, as well as of the type of each student. In particular, according to the provisions of paragraph 5 of the aforementioned article 3, the local authorities, in compliance with the responsibilities provided for in article 1, paragraph 85 and following, of the law 7 April 2014, n. 56, provide to ensure, within the limits of the resources available: the allocation of the staff dedicated to educational assistance and assistance for autonomy and personal communication, as provided for in article 13, paragraph 3, of the law n. 104 of 1992; the services for transport for school inclusion as guaranteed by article 8, paragraph 1, letter g), of the law n. 104 of 1992 and exercised according to the division of competences established by article 26 of the same law, as well as by article 139, paragraph 1, letter c), of Legislative Decree n. 112 of 1998; accessibility and usability of the physical spaces of the state educational institutions referred to in article 8, paragraph 1, letter c), of the law n. 104 of 1992 and article 2, paragraph 1, letter b), of the law 11 January 1996, n. 23. Coming now to the specific case pointed out by the honourable Member, concerning the Municipality of Vigevano, refers to the information acquired by the competent regional school office (USR) for Lombardy, which represented to this Ministry that the information set out in the press article on "La Provincia Pavese" of 2 March 2017, even if it corresponds to the truth in the numerical quantification of the distribution of pupils with disabilities resident in the Municipality of Vigevano, to date is exceeded. In fact, with a letter sent on March 3, 2017 to the mayors of the municipalities located in the territory of the province and to the piano offices, signed by the president and the vice president and managing director for education and social cohesion, the provincial administration of Pavia has communicated that the Lombardy Region, with note of 2 March 2017, has expressed the will to ensure the completion of the current school year, providing to transfer to the Province of Pavia the amount due for the period January-July 2017. With the same note, the regional city council member to the economy, the growth and the simplification has reported that the competence and the relative burdens of the services in question will be borne by the Lombardy Region from August 2017. Consequently, to date, the problem reported is resolved: the financing and continuity of the service are guaranteed and, therefore, all children with disabilities resident in Vigevano regularly benefit from the assistance service to the person, who has not suffered any interruption.
