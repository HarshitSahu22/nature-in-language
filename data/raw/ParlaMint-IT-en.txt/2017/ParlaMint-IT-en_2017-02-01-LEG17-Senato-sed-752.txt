ParlaMint-IT_2017-02-01-LEG17-Senato-sed-752.ana.u4	The next item is the vote on the election of a Commissioner of the Authority for Communications Guarantees and a member of the Court of Auditors' Presidency Council. As regards the Commissioner of the Authority for Communications Guarantees, each Senator will receive a pink card indicating only one name. The one who gets the highest number of votes will be proclaimed elected. For the election of a member of the Board of President of the Court of Auditors, each Senator will receive a grey card indicating only one name. The one who will obtain the absolute majority of the votes of the members of the Senate will be proclaimed elected. For voting operations, two ballot boxes have been set up which, once the call has been exhausted, will remain open until 12.30 p.m. Senators will be able to pass under the Presidency by communicating their names to the Secretary Senators and placing the ballot papers in the ballot boxes. I therefore declare the ballots open by secret ballot and invite the Secretary Senators to proceed with the call. (Follows he calls her.) (In the course of voting, they take the Presidency - at 10.20 a.m. - at 11.05 a.m. - at 12.30 a.m.) I warn the Senators who have not yet voted that they will be able to do so until 12.30 a.m., passing under the Presidency and communicating their name to the Secretary Senators. (The ballot boxes remain open.)
