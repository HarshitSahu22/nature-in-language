ParlaMint-IT_2019-05-28-LEG18-Senato-sed-114.ana.u12	FOURTH (M5S). Mr President, at 10:13 a.m. on 21 May Barletta was the epicenter of an earthquake with magnitude 3.7, 30 kilometres deep. Given the energy and depth, the earthquake was felt on a vast area (about 2,000 square kilometers), with resentments up to the fourth and fifth degree of the Mercalli scale, but not generating sensitive damage, but only so much fear. Most likely a dangerous seismogenetic structure has been reactivated (right east-west transcurrent fault of Barletta). Barletta's seismic history doesn't report catastrophic earthquakes, but it's not at all quiet. There are two strong events with local epicenters, with a magnitude of 5.7 and 5.2, as well as important resentments of neighboring earthquakes. It is not my aim to frighten, but I am convinced that knowledge of the risks is fundamental to avoid catastrophes as a result of natural earthquakes or completely natural phenomena. After the earthquake of 21 May, in the awareness of a much greater risk, it is opportune to convert fear in the occasion of reflection on the need to defend ourselves from earthquakes. At the time of the earthquake we can defend ourselves with suitable behavior, but to do this we need an adequate education to the earthquake, frequent civil protection exercises and real-time communications. This earthquake, which took place during school hours and the opening of public offices, can be used as a real exercise, to analyze the emergency operations carried out and to ensure that, in a possible stronger earthquake, everything works best. I therefore urged the Civil Protection Department, which has readily accepted, to promote a special meeting in the prefecture. These events must also be an incentive for preventive government action. On this front, unfortunately much neglected in the past, the Government of change is very active: from financing for reconstruction in areas affected by earthquakes to seismic microzonation, to studies of vulnerability of school building, to the earthquake bonus. So much can still be done to mitigate the great natural risks, beginning with the knowledge of the territory. In this respect, greater coverage of seismic stations at sea, studies of seismogenetic structures and crustal models of speed of propagation of seismic waves and the completion of geological mapping is desirable. [[Applause from the M5S Group.]]
