ParlaMint-IT_2019-09-17-LEG18-Senato-sed-149.ana.u9	BERNINI (FI-BP). Mr President, I would like to give my fellow Members the feedback of the Conference of Leaders that we have just held with President Alberti Casellati and my fellow Leaders. As is clear, the vote on the calendarization that will start next week was unanimous, having found the right convergences both on the emergency measures, and on the measures that had already been scheduled before the closing of the summer session (closed... here has never been the closure, let's say before Augustan events). The only issue on which I would like to draw the attention of all my fellow Members is the well-known judgment that we await from the Constitutional Court on 24 September. The theme is that of assisted suicide, known to all, on which only the Chamber of Deputies has been measured so far, giving a deliberate opinion, a kind of minutes of joint session of Commissions, in which it has been established that it is impossible to legislate on the matter, but on which, precisely because the Chamber of Deputies has held back the draft laws on the matter for a whole year, the Senate could not be pronounced. Forza Italia has asked, through a motion, to be able to commit not only the Government, of course esteemable but nevertheless representative only of a majority of Parliament, but the entire Parliament, to be able to call upon the Constitutional Court; that is, it has asked, in accordance with their respective powers and competences, but on the basis of a principle of loyal collaboration between constitutional bodies, that we members of the Upper Chamber, the Senate of the Republic, to give our opinion on the matter, to measure our different sensibilities while legalizing. Therefore, Mr President, we have asked that our rights as legislators should not be waived and that we should be able to pronounce on what appears to be a legal vacuum in the field of assisted suicide. [[Applause from the FI-BP Group]] The theme was not defined in today's Conference of Leaders, but was postponed until next week, because the unanimous request of all the Leaders was to ask our President to report to the Constitutional Court that there are some draft laws deposited in the Senate and there is the will of the Senate to legislate on the matter, thus giving the Constitutional Court, always respecting the powers and competences of the two constitutional bodies, the possibility to reflect on the point and to consider a deadline, on 24 September, by its authorising nature, as a flexible term. That is the point to which we will return next week. That is the point of our motion on assisted suicide. This is the point that Forza Italia wants to bring to the attention of colleagues, because it is a subject that must involve all of us, it is not a matter that concerns only the minority or the majority. President Conte himself, following a request made by our Senator Binetti, said that the subject is for the attention of the Government, but that the legislative initiative cannot be of the Government, but of Parliament. Then, strong also of this suggestion that President Conte presented to the Chamber no later than last week, we are here to say that, through President Alberti Casellati, we will signal to the Constitutional Court our intention to be present in the legislative path on assisted suicide and, starting next week, we will return the request for calendarization of our motions. [[Applause from the FI-BP Group.]]

ParlaMint-IT_2019-09-17-LEG18-Senato-sed-149.ana.u12	RAUTI (FdI). Mr President, I am speaking on the same subject on which Senator Bernini spoke, who was among other things the only one who requested a debate and a debate at the Conference of Leaders. Brothers of Italy has tabled a motion aimed at launching in the parliamentary seat any initiative of competence aimed at ensuring the examination and approval within a reasonable time of a specific legislation on the end of life, also in order to fill the existing legislative vacuum. I would like to point out that next week we will return to this subject at the Conference of the Leaders, calling for an urgent timetable for the matter and its motions. To contextualize our request, I would like to remind you, as Mr Bernini has already done, that the Constitutional Court's ruling on the legality of Article 580 of the penal code, which punishes the crime of incitement or aid to suicide, is due to take place on 24 September. There will then be a ruling from the Court, while the proceedings initiated in the Chamber in the committees responsible - the II (Justice) and the XII (Social Affairs) - unfortunately ended on 31 July with nothing in fact. Hence the decision of the Brothers of Italy to present a motion to initiate the necessary process in the Senate to regulate the matter, filling the current legislative vacuum. Both in the motion and in the debate at the Conference of Leaders, we have not entered into any way into the substance of the motion, but we have put a question of method and we will go back to doing so because we believe that it is Parliament's job to legislate. President Conte himself, on 10 September last in this House, pointed out that government action on the subject would be inappropriate and that the Court's ruling would, however, cover a segment, an aspect and not the whole and vast subject of euthanasia. As far as this is concerned, we stress the urgent need to regulate the matter. [[Applause from the FdI Group.]]
