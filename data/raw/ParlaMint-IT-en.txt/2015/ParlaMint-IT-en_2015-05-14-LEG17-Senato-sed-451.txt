ParlaMint-IT_2015-05-14-LEG17-Senato-sed-451.ana.u4	Madam President, we have repeatedly called for the answer to this questioning, because I believe there is a need for urgent clarification at a time when tax returns are being deposited. Now, this definition needs clarification, as both the tenants' unions and the CAFs and public housing managers have pointed out several times in recent weeks. In particular, the theme is whether the tenants of housing in public housing (and therefore former IACP or municipalities) are among the tenants of social housing. It is important to have this answer, knowing - and this is the thesis of the questioner - that, also in Decree-Law No. 47 of 2014 and then above all in the law that modified this point, evidently the houses of public housing, especially those with social charges, fall fully among the social housing. So much so that they are also buildings for which it is possible, for the managing bodies do not pay the IMU, because they are recognized as main houses, having a social purpose and, therefore, exempt. It is important, however, to know the opinion of the Government because, in these hours and these days, it is necessary to give clear indications to all the bodies involved in this affair and, above all, to the citizens. Therefore, I ask the Government to respond to the questioning.
ParlaMint-IT_2015-05-14-LEG17-Senato-sed-451.ana.u5	The representative of the Government has the right to respond to this question.
ParlaMint-IT_2015-05-14-LEG17-Senato-sed-451.ana.u6	Madam President, with the document under consideration, the Senator represents that Article 7 of Decree-Law No. 47 of 2014, converted with amendments to Law No. 80 of 2014, which contains urgent measures for the emergency housing for the construction market and the Expo 2015, provides, for the three years 2014-2016, a deduction in favour of the persons who hold contracts for the rental of social housing, as defined by the decree of the Minister of Infrastructure of 22 April 2008, used as main dwellings. In particular, the questioner would be of the opinion that the definition of social housing referred to in the aforementioned ministerial decree includes the housing attributable to the type of intervention and management of public housing by the territorial companies for public housing or the autonomous public housing institutes, however called, and therefore the deduction introduced by Decree-Law No 47 of 2014 in favor of the conductors of these social housing should be considered applicable. In this regard, the questioner draws up the deadline for submitting the tax return and points out that certain revenue offices are experiencing uncertainty about the right of deduction in relation to the definition of social housing itself, which creates confusion and uncertainty in taxpayers and tax operators themselves for the assistance of the tax return. For the reasons given, the interrogating senator asks to know what acts the Ministry of Economy and Finance intends to adopt in order to make more explicit and to make sure the right to deduction for the contributing taxpayers of social housing. In this regard, after hearing the Revenue Agency, the following is mentioned: in the meantime, it must be noted first that Article 7 of Decree-Law No 47 of 2014 provides that for the three years 2014-2016 the holders of social housing leases, as defined by Article 5 of Law No 9 of 8 February 2007, which is used as their main dwelling, owes a total deduction of 900 euros, if the total income does not exceed 15,493,71 euros, to 450 euros, if the total income exceeds 15,493,71 euros but does not exceed 30,977,41 euros. For the definition of social housing, the rule refers to the decree of the Minister of Infrastructure of 22 April 2008, implementing Article 5 of Law No 9 of 8 February 2007 on measures to reduce housing hardship for particular social categories. Article 1 of the Ministerial Decree identifies social housing as the housing unit used for residential use in permanent rental, which serves as a general interest, in safeguarding social cohesion, to reduce the housing hardship of disadvantaged individuals and households, which are unable to access the rental of housing in the free market. The definition referred to in paragraph 2 shall also include accommodation made or recovered by public and private operators, with the use of contributions or public facilities - such as tax exemptions, allocation of areas or buildings, guarantee funds, urban facilities - intended for temporary rental for at least eight years and also for ownership. Article 2 of the decree states that the regions, in consultation with the regional ANCI, define the requirements for access and residence in social housing. The rental fee for the social housing referred to in Article 1, paragraph 2, is defined by the Regions, in consultation with the regional ANCI, in relation to the different economic capacities of those entitled, the composition of the household and the characteristics of the accommodation. The rent of the social housing referred to in Article 1, paragraph 3, may not exceed that resulting from the values resulting from the local agreements signed pursuant to Article 2, paragraph 3, of Law No 431 of 1998, or, if not updated, the value determined pursuant to Article 3, paragraph 114, of Law No 350 of 2003, and may be articulated in relation to the different economic capacity of those entitled, the composition of the household and the characteristics of the accommodation. The application of the cited legislation, which moreover assigns a significant role to the Regions for the definition of the requirements of access and permanence in the social housing, necessarily involves the acquisition of the notice of the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, which adopted the aforementioned decree of 22 April 2008. In this regard, the Agency points out, however, that the identification of social housing also has effect with regard to other provisions of Decree-Law No 47 of 2014, which provide tax relief, as well as with regard to the provisions in the matter of IMU, which provide a favorable treatment for social housing, distinguishing them from those assigned by public housing agencies. In particular, with regard to the IMU, Article 13 of Decree-Law No 201 of 6 December 2011, provides to paragraph 2 that "The municipal tax own does not apply, also: (...) (b) buildings of civil housing intended for social housing as defined by the Decree of the Minister of Infrastructure of 22 April 2008, published in the Official Gazette No 146 of 24 June 2008; Paragraph 10 of the same article provides that the deduction for main dwelling • applies to the housing regularly assigned by the autonomous institutes for housing (ACP) or by public housing agencies, however called, having the same purposes as the IACP, established in implementation of Article 93 of the decree of the President of the Republic 24 July 1977, n. 616 •. In other words, the provisions on IMU seem to show that the fact that a property is assigned by an IACP or a public housing agency does not automatically entail the non-application of the tax, as it is necessary to verify whether the property actually has the characteristics of social housing, as referred to in the aforementioned Decree of 22 April 2008. This approach is reiterated in FAQ IMU/TASI No 15 and No 20, prepared by the Department of Finance, where it is specified, respectively, that the buildings owned by former IACP, which do not have the requirements of social housing, are subject to IMU, and that the IMU exemption applies only when the properties have the requirements and characteristics indicated by the Decree of 22 April 2008. In FAQ No 20 it is also stated that "in the model of IMU Declaration the owner of the social housing must also cross field 15 related to the "Exemption" and bring back, in the space dedicated to the "Annotations," the following sentence: "The property has the characteristics and requirements required by letter b), paragraph 2, of Article 13 of Decree-Law No 201 of 2011" (). Therefore, it constitutes a specific burden of the owner of social housing to certify the existence of the relevant requirements. This place is believed that, contrary to what seems to be deduced from the act of inspection union in question, not all the housing attributable to the type of intervention and management of the public housing - by the territorial companies for the public housing or the autonomous public housing institutes anyway denominated - supplement the premises to be considered social housing and allow the tenant, therefore, to make use of the deduction provided for in Article 7 of Decree-Law No 47 of 2014. The above also notes for the further assessment of the appropriateness of the quantification of the financial coverage envisaged for the deduction in question. Finally, in relation to the request made by the interrogator to the Minister of Economics and Finance, to adopt acts to clarify and make certain the right to deduct for the contributing taxpayers of social housing, without prejudice to any initiatives of the competent Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, it is appropriate to specify that, where the nature of social housing is specified, pursuant to the decree of the Ministry of Infrastructure of 22 April 2008, the owner of the accommodation will not be expressly indicated by the lease, he will have to issue to the tenant an attestation in which it is specified, similar to that provided for in the IMU declaration, that the property possesses the characteristics and requirements provided for in the aforementioned decree of 22 April 2008. This certificate must be provided by the tenant who intends to make use of the deduction to the intermediaries operating the tax assistance.

In this regard, I would point out in advance that the interventions provided for in Legislative Decree No 102 of 2004 for the support of agricultural enterprises affected by exceptional atmospheric adversities can be activated provided that the damage on the gross production that can be sold is more than 30% and exclusively for the adversities and damaged crops not included in the Annual Risk Insurance Plan, whose policies are facilitated by a state contribution up to 80% of the premium expenditure incurred for the weather events that occurred until 2014 and up to 65% for those that occurred in 2015. I would also like to point out that ex ante instruments, such as insurance instruments, have proved to be significantly more effective over time than compensatory measures, in fact guaranteeing more than EUR 7 billion of gross agricultural production. In addition, facilitated insurance has been included as one of the measures analysed by the European Commission to address, from the 2014-2020 programming period onwards, crises affecting the agricultural sector. In fact, targeted actions are being considered to ensure the territorial extension of the measure and to better inform agricultural enterprises about the scope and potential of the insurance instrument. We have had 80% of companies insured in the North of the country, while very few have been the companies insured in the Mezzogiorno. In this context, the draft law related to the public finance maneuver for 2014, Article 12, provides for a delegation to the Government in order to adapt the current legislation to the EU guidelines on State aid to the agricultural and forestry sector and to the new programming of the European Union for the period 2014-2020. I would also add that on 29 April 2015 the Council of Ministers approved Decree-Law No 51 of 2015, which provides, among other things, for the extension of the cases in which access to the National Solidarity Fund is allowed to agricultural enterprises, with particular reference to companies that have suffered damage due to flooding events occurred in 2014 and until the date of entry into force of the aforementioned Decree-Law, as well as infections of organisms harmful to plants. Therefore, agricultural enterprises damaged by flood rains that have not signed insurance policies facilitated to cover the risks can access with the new measures to the interventions in order to favor the resumption of the economic and productive activity. All the regions concerned, including the regions of Puglia and Tuscany, that to date have not sent any formal request for intervention for the events recalled, by way of derogation from the terms established by Legislative Decree no. 102 of 2004, can then deliberate the proposal of declaratory of exceptional weather events within sixty days from the entry into force of the decree-law. Finally, as regards the exclusion from the Stability Pact of the sums committed by the municipalities to deal with the emergency, I would point out that the Government has intervened within the terms described above also because of the financial requirements and that any derogations from the Stability Pact would require appropriate insurance cover. In the National Rural Development Programme this year we have defined a financial contribution for the risk management insurance scheme of EUR 1.6 billion: a sum that is absolutely appropriate and sufficient for a national plan to be distributed evenly across the national territory.
