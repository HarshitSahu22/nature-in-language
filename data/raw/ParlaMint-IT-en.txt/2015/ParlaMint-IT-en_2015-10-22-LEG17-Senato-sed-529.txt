ParlaMint-IT_2015-10-22-LEG17-Senato-sed-529.ana.u205	GIROTTO (M5S). Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I wanted to point out two serious dangers to the environment of the Veneto Region, both in the province of Treviso. In one case it is a storage of underground gases in which it is pumped at very high pressure methane gas. In addition to the concern arising from the high noise situation for 24 hours a day, there is a much more serious concern regarding seismic risk. In fact, we have an assessment by an official who has spent his entire career in bodies such as the CNR, which indicates that these very high pressure pumping could generate induced earthquakes. I ask for a moment of attention as I am talking about earthquakes and not amenities. I have here before me a letter signed by hundreds of Venetian entrepreneurs - so we are talking about concerns not of the usual environmentalists, and neither in this case of triggers - sent to the President of the Region to express their extreme concern for this deposit of methane gas that pumps the gas in the Montello area, then in Treviso. There have already been appeals to the TAR and we urge the President and the Venetian Council to make the matter as clear as possible and to minimise the risks as much as possible. The second concern concerns a rolling project in the river Curogna area (we always talk about the Treviso area). To make this rolling will be opened a new clay quarry, yet another quarry, yet another hole in the most excavated territory of Italy: I remember that Veneto is the territory with the most quarries in Italy. It is, among other things, a territory of beauty such as to be exposed to the Expo in Milan; we are talking about magnificent areas, always on Montello, therefore landscaped exceptional. The project is now being evaluated by regional EIA. In this case too, we invite all Members, senators and the Regional Council to check the situation because the most justified doubt, given the history of the last twenty years, is that, as always, there are large personal interests: hollow means businesses, personal profits, historically means bribes, corruption, bad management of the public thing. So I earn for a few and give for many. We are going to raise awareness on Saturday and other institutional activities, so here too I hope that the presidency of the Veneto Region will give the utmost attention to the issues.
