For the umpteenth time, therefore, and this time unanimously, the political forces present in the regional council have unequivocally reaffirmed the will to oppose the realization of this work, because of the high existing criticalities, both from the seismic and environmental point of view. This position is in addition to the resolutions already adopted in the Regional Council on 18 October 2011 and 14 February 2012 and the resolutions approved by the Environment Commission of the Chamber of Deputies on 26 October 2011: therefore, the issues have been stated several times, but they have always been ignored. I would like to point out that the Government was committed to placing the modification of the pipeline line, excluding the Apennines, in order to avoid the considerable environmental damage and the very high danger to the safety of citizens. Last July 30 I presented at my first signing, together with my Group, the urgent questioning 2-00057 with which I asked the government to know the timing within which it intended to follow up, without further delay and delays. However, the questioning has not yet been scheduled in this House, despite the fact that this issue is very important. And now - I am referring to Act No 40, which arrived last week under joint scrutiny in the 8th and 10th Commissions - it is expected that they will be included in the strategic acts of national importance - the national natural gas transport network and its compression stations and dispatch centres. It does not appear to me that a parliamentary passage has been foreseen before the definitive identification of assets or that a detailed list of plants considered strategic by the Government is attached to the same measure. So I'm going back to asking for the urgent calendarization of the questioning presented.
