ParlaMint-IT_2013-10-01-LEG17-Senato-sed-114.ana.u15	PADUA (PD). Mr President, a brief thought about what happened yesterday in Sampieri, in the municipality of Scicli: you have heard of this terrible event. Once again, we witness the death of men and women fleeing from a situation of war, pain and discomfort. This time it happened right at my house. I was there, on that beautiful beach, which many of you have known and appreciated; beach that, however, gave death to these people, who were thrown into the water - I mean - violently by the boatmen, a few meters from the shore: people who do not know the sea, who cannot swim, and drowned there. Someone was saved by a carabiniere and his other generous colleagues, who immediately dived, but thirteen boys - they talk about boys, eighteen to twenty years - died; thirteen bodies put in line one to the other on the beach, alone, with a community around, yes, who supported and wept them, but without the tears of their parents, who may never know about their children, because it is not said that the bodies will be recognized (now expects recognition and everything that must happen). So I once again ask this House for a strong commitment, Mr President. This is not just any problem: it is a problem that concerns the whole world, our planet and cannot be left, abandoned, to those coasts that are there, in front of the Mediterranean. My city was cozy: people offered help to those who ran away. Of the people who have been thrown into the sea, thirteen have died, one is in very serious condition at the hospital Cannizzaro of Catania, one, fleeing, has been overwhelmed by a pirate car and, not rescued, is in very serious condition, others have hospitalized; but others have fled the fields and have been rescued by the population who tried to give them a drink and to eat: they are people turned upside down. This great gesture of solidarity on the part of a community cannot but be accompanied by a strong, political action by a Parliament and a Government that cannot pretend that situations will resolve afterward. Facts of this kind are happening too often: on this same beach they died twenty-five years ago. It is no longer possible, nor tolerable. Men, women and children were taken to the First Aid and Reception Centre in Pozzallo, but there the situation broke out. There were the Prefecture, the quaestor, the Carabinieri, the police, the Civil Protection: all there to help; but we cannot go forward to rescue, because this is a structured situation: here there is a traffic of people; evidently behind there is a great criminal association that takes advantage of it (and it is really a big profit what comes from this trafficking of human beings). I therefore ask this Parliament - if we are going, as I hope and hope for our country, towards the European semester - to define and place this problem firmly on the table, which is not a problem of Sicily, or of the province of Ragusa, or of Italy, but of our Europe, that if it is to be a mother (and stepmother, sometimes) it must be in any condition. [[Live, prolonged applause. Congratulations.]]

ParlaMint-IT_2013-10-01-LEG17-Senato-sed-114.ana.u18	AIROLA (M5S). Mr President, ladies and gentlemen - I am addressing my colleagues in my region, Piedmont - I have to appeal to you for the umpteenth closure of a historic company in Grugliasco, near Turin, ABIT, which has been producing milk for a very long time and is an excellence: it is now being moved to Lombardy, leaving 97 families at home, and then bringing milk back to Piedmont, covering 300 kilometres. A trade unionist writes to me: While you in Parliament are splitting up, debating, we are here on the ground losing our jobs and staying at home. I don't know about you, but I don't sleep at night anymore because of dozens of reports from a dying country that no longer has the slightest dignity of the work and basic needs of a family. I therefore ask everyone to make an effort to revive employment and try to stop this bleeding of jobs of companies that, once closed, relocated or transferred elsewhere, will no longer return to giving wealth on the territory, will no longer return to being active and give a minimum of subsistence and dignity to Italian families and workers. [[Applause from the M5S Group.]]
