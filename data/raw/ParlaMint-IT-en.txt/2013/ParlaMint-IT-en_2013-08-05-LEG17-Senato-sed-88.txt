ParlaMint-IT_2013-08-05-LEG17-Senato-sed-88.ana.u19	PADUA (PD). Madam President, I would like to point out to the House that there has been a very unfortunate occurrence in my province, Ragusa, and precisely in Scoglitti, as well as in Licata, in the province of Agrigento, where two fishing boats have been kidnapped, which were pushing for fishing - and therefore for the carrying out of their daily activities - towards the island of Malta. The two fishing boats, which were fishing there, were blocked - in a bad way for what has been reported - by the Maltese authorities, who imprisoned the commander and detained the other sailors inside the fishing boats, on the charge of invading, as it were, the Maltese waters. I would like to point out this way of working very incisively, very little careful and respectfully, as reported, towards these people immediately imprisoned: we are talking, among other things, about fishermen who risk their lives every day. The captain has already been sentenced to the payment of a fine of about 20,000 euros, a huge sum, if you consider the serious crisis that also affects the fishing sector: a sum that corresponds, perhaps, to the revenue of a whole year of work. I would therefore like to ask this House for support for a revisiting of the agreements between Malta and our country, being absolutely disproportionate the sea radius of which the island of Malta can enjoy, which is certainly a very small island in front of our country. Secondly, I would also like to point out that the Sicilian fishermen's associations have always behaved seriously, avoiding poaching at sea and respecting the sea, both because it is right to respect the rules and to respect a means that provides them with sustenance. I therefore believe that it is absolutely necessary to denounce these unpleasant episodes that are being told to us. In this regard, the Councillor for the legality of the Municipality of Vittoria has worked hard and has been interested in the Farnesina that is being activated to have the precise position and to defend our fishermen, that we have no doubt they can tell the truth. Above all, however, I would invite - and I will do so in a question that I have put this evening - to the revision of the European Agreements between Malta and Italy, because it is not clear why countries within the European Union itself should not have the same rules. [[Applause from the PD Group.]]
