ParlaMint-IT_2013-06-05-LEG17-Senato-sed-34.ana.u14	SANTANGELO (M5S). Mr President, Mr Senators, ladies and gentlemen, today is World Environment Day. I take this opportunity because, in Sicily, on the contrary, these days are celebrating the days for the environmental disaster. In fact, two very serious facts have happened: one yesterday and another just today. Yesterday, in the petrochemical plant in Gela, an oil spill at the mouth of the river Gela caused damage of serious environmental impact. The other fact of great importance is that today, in Sicily, we are discussing the project of an offshore wind farm, in the coastal belt between Mazara del Vallo and Petrosino. In particular, the installation of 48 air generators with a unit power of 3.6 megawatts, each with a total height of as much as 195 meters, of which about 150 are outside the water, is planned. The area is located about two miles from the Sicilian coast. It is part - and I would like to draw this to the attention of my colleagues - of the Natura 2000 ecological network and is a site of Community importance. Moreover, in July 2011, given the high ecological value of the area, it was even designated as a wetland of international interest under the Ramsar Convention. The corresponding stretch of sea is, in fact, one of the most important in the Mediterranean for migratory flights of birds of the Western Palearctic. Mr President, the 5 Star Movement opposes the project that would have a disastrous environmental impact and would cause serious damage to the ecosystem and underwater archaeology of the area. The plan provides, in fact, anchoring platforms that, among other things, would alter the prairie of posidonia that characterizes the area. The blades - reaching, as I said before, a height of 190 meters - could seriously endanger the presence of the sedentary and migratory bird. It is absurd to talk today about a wind farm just in front of an area already funded for natural conservation and tourism development. It is well understood that reconciling the project for the establishment of a National Park is not simple, but it is desirable to speed up the time for at least the natural reserve to be set up to protect the swamp, in order to protect effectively the stretch of sea and coast adjacent. [[Applause from the M5S Group.]]
