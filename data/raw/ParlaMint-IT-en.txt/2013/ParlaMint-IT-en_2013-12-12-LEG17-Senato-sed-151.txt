ParlaMint-IT_2013-12-12-LEG17-Senato-sed-151.ana.u10	Madam President, I would like to talk about a subject that concerns health and concerns us all: it is smog emergency with PM10 skyrocketing, but the City of Florence is silent. This is the charge of the NO SMOG Network after six consecutive days of overtaking the PM10 index in the switchboards of Florence and other cities of Tuscany. Since last Wednesday PM10 levels have begun to go beyond continuously in the boboli garden units as in Scandicci, where on Friday they reached the peak of 116 micrograms per cubic meter (remember that the threshold is 50 micrograms), and at via Ponte alle Mosse, where 108 micrograms per cubic meter were measured, and it was the 36th day of overtaking, thus exceeding the limit of 35 days provided for by the legislation, which makes that switchboard the black mesh, with the highest pollution values in Tuscany. In the coming days, PM10 bursts are destined to continue, given the stable weather conditions, with cold and high pressure, which are conducive to the concentration and raising of pollutants in the air. Despite the overflows, the Municipality of Florence and the other municipalities of the plain do not take measures, as emphasized by the associations members of the Network (Città Cyclabile, Italy Nostra, Medici per l'ambiente, Medicina Democratica), because continues the deception of the base units that record pollution to a lesser extent (a third of the days of overcoming), but are taken as a reference for the overflows and therefore for the antismog measures. Where are these switchboards? They are in the Boboli Gardens and in the Bassi Avenues and are considered to be the most reliable for the exposure of citizens to pollutants, as if many cars were circulating in the Boboli Gardens. Thus the control unit of via Ponte alle Mosse, in the traffic zone and highly residential, holds the negative record of all the control units of Tuscany, but for the City of Florence its 36 days of overtaking do not count, because they are worth the 13 days of the control unit of Scandicci and the 10 days of that of Viale Bassi. Thus, in these days the Florentines breathe PM10 in full lungs, but the municipality not only does not take action, waiting for the 15-day threshold crossed by the Scandicci control unit, but it is totally uninterested and does not even bother to warn citizens about the risks to their health.
