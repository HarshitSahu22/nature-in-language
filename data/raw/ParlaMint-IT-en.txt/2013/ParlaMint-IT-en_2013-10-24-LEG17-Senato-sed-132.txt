A penultimate consideration concerns a very complex and sensitive issue, on which it may be appropriate to clarify, and I therefore take this opportunity to do so. It was also felt yesterday, in many speeches, that there was a polemical reference to the question of balance of the budget and the Constitution. The Italian Constitution never uses the term balance, and this was the result of a very difficult discussion: President Lanzillotta certainly remembers the discussion on this issue at the time of the construction of the law. Article 81 of the Constitution states exactly as follows: The State shall ensure the balance between the revenue and expenditure of its budget, taking into account the adverse phases and the favourable phases of the economic cycle. All literature knows that the concept of balance is a closed concept within a single financial year. Here, instead, the concept of an adverse cycle and a favourable cycle is deliberately introduced by the legislator, in order to allow parliaments and governments to intervene in political terms that are not merely a simple accounting calculation of the results. This is a difference in the approach of the Italian Constitution, as a result of a conscious choice taken by Parliament. Finally, I take note of the comments made by some senators, and in particular by Senator Carraro, and I will of course report to the Minister for relations with Parliament on the question of relations between the two chambers.
