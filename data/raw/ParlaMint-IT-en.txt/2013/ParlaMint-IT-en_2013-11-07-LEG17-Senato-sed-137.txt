Yesterday, both when President Grasso presided over and when Vice-President Gasparri presided over, our request for a very urgent procedure was considered inadmissible. However, the request to examine an item not included on the agenda, made by the 5 Star Movement Group pursuant to Rule 56, paragraph 4, of the Rules of Procedure cannot, in our opinion, be considered to be excluded due to the previous vote of the Assembly made pursuant to other regulatory provisions (Article 55, paragraph 3). The Rules of Procedure of the Senate provide in fact two distinct and different procedures concerning the planning of the intense activities. The first is the timetable not adopted unanimously by the Conference of Presidents of the Groups: in this case Article 55 (3) provides that the Assembly shall decide on any proposed amendments by a simple majority, with a vote by show of hands. [[Brusio. Call the president back.]] The so-called urgent procedure, pursuant to Article 56, paragraph 4, also gives the possibility to eight senators to request the inclusion of new items on the agenda, not mentioning in any way, either implicit or explicit, the reference to the timetable of proceedings. The formal and substantial difference between the two procedures is clearly deduced from the different procedures that can be followed and applied. In particular, the urgent procedure requires, also in accordance with an opinion of the Council for the Rules of Procedure of 15 February 2000, the prior communication to the President, the authorization of the President himself, the two-thirds majority of those present (and not the simple one necessary for the changes to the calendar) and the vote by electronic procedure with relative notice (and not the vote by show of hands, required for the changes to the calendar). These are, therefore, radically distinct and different procedures which derive their raison d'être from two different regulatory provisions. One of them is in an article under the heading 'Calendar of work', the other in an article whose heading is not by chance the agenda for the sitting. The stated foreclosure of the urgent procedure set out in an evaluation by the Presidency on the basis of previous approval of the timetable is not supported, in our view, by a literal interpretation of the Rules of Procedure of the Senate. Moreover, during the sitting of 2 October 2001, the procedure provided for in Rule 56 (4) for the examination of matters not on the agenda was deemed to be inapplicable, because the Assembly approved the timetable for the proceedings at the same sitting. In today's and yesterday's cases, requests and procedures are completely distinct, not connectable or connected, because they relate to two different articles of the Rules of Procedure, which regulate different times, with different procedures, different majorities and different voting methods. Today there is a different President, and we hope that the interpretation will be different. It is for the reasons set out above that, pursuant to Rule 56 (4) of the Rules of Procedure, we once again ask that the House now decide on the inclusion on the agenda of today's sitting of the document of the Junta of Parliamentary Elections and Immunities concerning the forfeiture of Senator Berlusconi, which, even in the light of everything that is happening, seems to us increasingly distant. So let's schedule it today, Madam President, and do this country a favor. [[Applause from the M5S Group.]]
