Mr President, I would like to draw, through you, the attention of the Minister of Health to Legislative Decree No 27 of 2 February 2021, which was published in the Official Journal. This measure, in Article 18, repeals a whole range of rules relating to food quality control. Among these, the 1962 Law 283 is also repealed, which, in Articles 5 and 6, fined with violations of manslaughter, but not arson, therefore not treated as a crime. Well, repealing that prediction leaves a gap open so that we could run the risk that produced in part in a poor state of conservation, with microbial charges exceeding the limits, adulterated or counterfeit, artificially colored - and a whole series of other cases threatening human health - will no longer receive any fine because between the serious crime and nothing has been abolished all the case of intermediate manslaughter behaviors. I ask the Ministry of Health to check this situation promptly and, if necessary, use one of the decrees of next issue to reinsert the normative forecast because otherwise, as some press have denounced, the normative vacuum that has been created risks being dangerous to human health.
