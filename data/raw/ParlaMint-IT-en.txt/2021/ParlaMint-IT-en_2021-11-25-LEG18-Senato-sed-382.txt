ParlaMint-IT_2021-11-25-LEG18-Senato-sed-382.ana.u6	TRENTACOSTE (M5S). Mr President, after the government's appeal for the Sicilian regional rule which intended to stabilize 4,571 beneficiaries of social assistance for socially useful activities (ASU), the Musumeci Council would now be planning to release the sums already allocated to those workers. The rule approved by the Sicilian Regional Assembly provided for a stabilisation to fourteen hours per week, increasing by an additional €10 million the financial envelope foreseen for 2021 and allocating 54 million for each of the following two years. Socially useful workers (LSU), established with the approval of Law No 390 of 24 July 1981, are the last precarious introduced into the system of public administration, mainly in Sicily and Campania, between the seventies and nineties. These are disadvantaged persons included in the special list when exhausted, with the aim of being employed in public utility activities. They are entitled to a monthly benefit of 580 euros (well below the minimum poverty threshold set in 780 euros) against employment reaching up to twenty hours per week. For twenty-five years these people have lived in limbo: they do not have a contract of employment, nor the right to leave and illness, thus continuing to be workers without guarantees, even if they are employed in the public administration. Yet the precarious Sicilian ASUs, paid with regional funds, are responsible for relations with the users who consider them civil servants when, for example, they require information or the issue of a certificate. In small fact, some even in financial distress, they account for up to 70 percent of the staff. Almost 300 are also employed by the Sicilian Region in the management of cultural heritage, in museums, sites and archaeological parks, guaranteeing for example the opening of sites (essential services for the visitor that a Region devoted to tourism should have structured in a more effective way). It is the history of the Sicilian precariousness, if we want even more sad than that of vocational training. These realities are united by the fact that they have been client systems used to recruit women and men then kept under check by the politician on duty, precarious for twenty or thirty years and condemned to remain so in the uncertainty of the future, up to the retirement threshold. I believe that after the appeal of the regional law the Government should propose a definitive solution to the Sicilian Region, in order to intervene on a historical context, but still worthy of attention. Illusioned and humiliated beyond all human endurance, it should be recognized the work carried out by those precarious and the competences acquired by them over the years, respecting the constitutional dictation and the laws. It is no longer possible to ignore anything. [[Applause.]]
