ParlaMint-IT_2018-01-09-LEG17-Senato-sed-923.ana.u11	Ladies and gentlemen, as a result of the dissolution of the Chambers, the legislative activity of the Assembly and the Commissions, in accordance with parliamentary practice, will be limited to the examination of due acts, such as the bill of conversion of decrees-laws and urgent acts related to international and Community obligations. Procedures for parliamentary opinions on government acts may also take place in the Commission. The inspection union will take place through written questions. With regard to cognitive investigations and parliamentary investigations, the Commissions will be able to meet for the sole purpose of making explicit the conclusions of the work carried out before the dissolution. Any further activities of external importance, even if provided for in the programmes already approved, are excluded. Finally, the regime of extension of the Senate allows in the various fora the fulfillment of acts relating to the internal corporis of the Assembly.
