In the week from 10 to 14 December, which will mainly be devoted to the work of the Commissions on the draft budget law, the Assembly will be able to meet for the communications of the President of the Council of Ministers with a view to the European Council of 13 and 14 December, as well as for the examination of the draft anti-corruption law - where transmitted by the Chamber of Deputies - and the draft law of ratification of the Faro Convention on Cultural Heritage, where concluded by the Commission.
