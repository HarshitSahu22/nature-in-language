Start on agenda G1.2, relating to areas currently housing emergency housing, which are used under lease. Well, with this agenda we ask the Government to consider the possibility that, within the framework of a next measure, the acquisitions can be established, through the expropriation, of the areas on which the emergency housing structures insist, as well as to evaluate the activation of the relative maintenance burdens that these structures need. This is a very important operation, for a reflection that is necessary from the point of view of urban planning on many areas present in many municipalities of the crater that need a programming. I would also like to mention the similar agendas of the G1,300 and G1,301, which relate to the installment of the heavy paycheck. The current decree-law provides for the extension of the period beyond which tax payments and social security contributions must be returned to January 2019 and places in 60 instalments the refund of such contributions. We believe, in analogy to what was done for the earthquake in L'Aquila in 2009, that the Government may think of extending the installment of both the tax payments to 120 installments - and this is foreseen on the agenda G1,300 - and the social security and insurance contributions also in 120 installments instead of 60, as provided for in the agenda G1,301. I remind my colleagues in the Assembly that, through successive measures concerning the earthquake of Central Italy, the installment has gone from a first hypothesis of 9 installments to 24 and, with the current decree-law in the conversion, to 60 installments. Well, we believe that, in order to lighten the burden of double taxation for private citizens, it would be appropriate to think, in the next budget law, of a installment of 120 installments. With the agenda G1.306, the Government is asked to consider the possibility of extending for another two years beyond the current limit, set for 31 December 2018 (i.e. at the end of 2020), the temporary recruitment of the personnel currently engaged in the four special offices of reconstruction, associated with the four regions affected by the earthquake, and of the staff employed, always for a fixed time, in the municipalities. I then outline the very important agenda G1.307, which highlights a need that comes from the municipalities: the municipalities of the crater are increasingly engaged in having to face a series of legal costs that arise from administrative or criminal procedures deriving from the management of the post-sysm emergency. Many mayors are obliged to challenge orders for the requisition of land areas for the realization of SAEs or public service structures, verification and revocation of the contribution for self-establishment (CAS), assignment and revocation of the contribution for self-establishment, assignment and revocation of SAEs. With this agenda we ask the Government to be able to take initiatives to create a specific fund for the crater municipalities to support legal expenses - there are many and will be more and more - to deal with such disputes. I shall conclude by setting out jointly the agendas for G1,311 and G1,312, which essentially concern the urban free zone, which established the right to a number of exemptions by companies. The problem is that these exemptions, which I am not here to list, are established on the basis of a decrease in the turnover that companies have recorded in the last period of 2016 compared to the same period of 2015. Basically it is said that, if you compare these two periods and you find that the company has had a decrease in turnover of 25%, it is entitled to a series of exemptions. Too bad that many companies hit heavily by the earthquake, also thanks to the solidarity put in place by many citizens in the weeks following the earthquake, held a high turnover, which then in the first months of 2017 went down. With these two agendas we ask the Government to review the percentage of reduction in turnover - we have assumed 15%, but it is a negotiable percentage - and to review the time frame that is the subject of comparison of the turnovers of companies that have been damaged from the territory. There are other agendas presented by the Lega - Salvini Premier Group, which will then be presented by colleagues. [[Applause from the L-SP and M5S groups.]]

I am thinking, for example, of an intensification of relations with Libya and Niger, with which we have already significantly reduced departures this year. We have a real duty to prevent people's lives and safety from being put at risk, a risk that begins long before migrants start crossing by sea and reach European land borders. European solidarity must already be manifested at this stage, in order to effectively protect both the rights of migrants and, at the same time, the security of our citizens. Second objective: to create centres of international protection in the countries of transit, to offer legal assistance and advice to migrants themselves, so as to manage asylum requests early and quickly and to organize the voluntary returns assisted to the countries of origin. Fourth objective, which I believe to be crucial: we must overcome the Dublin regulation, which must not be reformed, but must be overcome because it is completely inadequate to deal with the problem of migratory flows. This must also be said in the light of statistical data: about 7% are entitled to refugee status.
