Mr President, I do not want to abuse the patience of the very kind friend undersecretary Pizzetti, but two weeks ago I asked for an answer to Question No 3-02831 concerning the intention of Poste Italiane to proceed on June 13th (i.e. in five days) to start the delivery activity every other day of all correspondence in the difficult or difficult to reach areas. The delivery of mail every other day is in operation in other countries of the world, even advanced, also in some territories of the United States of America. But one thing must be clear: that the legislation of everything that means notifications and everything about citizens' rights must be adequate. Since the Post Office carries out a universal service, if the delivery takes place every other day, that is for the whole of Italy, adapting the relative regulations for all that concerning the service of notifications and anything else. What is unacceptable is that in Italy there are citizens who have a different treatment than other citizens for a service that is universal. If the mailing address is to be every other day, it is for everyone or it is not for anyone. Not because a municipality is more difficult to reach, the mail must be delivered one day yes and one day no; provided that then such alternation is guaranteed, considering that in some cases the mail comes directly after fifteen days, already now, without the service to alternate days. I therefore ask the Undersecretary Pizzetti to be interested again, since he did it last time with extreme kindness but we did not get the result, and to have an answer from the Government to know if it is true that in Italy it is decided for an activity of Italian Posts (which should start in five days), company that is concessionary and that it must carry out on behalf of the State an essential and important service, that it previews that there are citizens of series A and citizens of series B.
