ParlaMint-IT_2020-01-14-LEG18-Senato-sed-180.ana.u12	CASTELLONE (M5S). Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, today I am taking part in a drama, which is the death of Rosa, a 19-year-old girl from Campania, who felt sick on 4 January and was rescued by an ambulance of 118, who arrived there only after an hour, as the family members have said, or after only twenty-three minutes, as declared by the Director-General of ASL Napoli 3, far too late to save her life. Beyond the timing, which will be established, and the type of sickness that struck the young woman and therefore the possibility of being saved or not with a timely intervention, we are faced with a fact that is no longer negligible: the times of the rescue in cases classified as code red and fixed by the legislator in a few minutes are punctually disregarded in some Regions, including Campania. In serious cases like this, this means certain death, it means a broken life, it means a family torn apart by a loss that will never find comfort and that cannot and must not be news among many. In addition to the timing, there is a series of other data to be analyzed in this matter, concerning the lack of staff and the lack of organization of the territorial emergency, which constitute a dangerous breach in our life-saving system. The death of Rosa and the seizure of an ambulance and her staff by a group of stumps, the continuous aggressions to those who work in the rescue report in a powerful way to our attention the topic of the very serious criticalities of the system 118, in particular in some Regions, where an evil regional policy has stimulated the demedicalization and entrusted the life-saving aid to cooperatives that work with staff constituted almost entirely by volunteers exploited with massacred shifts for a few euros per day, without any legal and working recognition. In October we filed a motion to commit this Government to implement a legislative reform of the organizational model of 118, with the aim of recovering precious minutes and saving lives. The appeal I make to you today, in this House, is to share with us the will and responsibility to finally provide answers to our citizens and to schedule this motion in this House shortly. [[Applause from the M5S Group.]]

ParlaMint-IT_2020-01-14-LEG18-Senato-sed-180.ana.u15	LEONE (M5S). Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I am taking the floor today because I cannot help but denounce a disturbing phenomenon that is taking place in the territory of which I am also a spokesperson, the hinterland of Palermo. I am referring to acts of serial group violence conducted by a baby gang, which are consumed openly and in public places. And while it is true that bullying today has become a widespread phenomenon in all societies, it takes on a particular connotation from my side, which would be a serious mistake to underestimate. We are a Region with a special Statute and we are special in this too, unfortunately. For some time, in fact, acts of bullying have taken place that disturb the serenity and quiet living of children and often harm their physical safety. From us bullying is not only a manifestation of the discomfort of the young man in boredom, who seeks a diversion on Saturday evening and finds him, precisely, beating the ill-capitulated. From us it becomes an unequivocal way to reaffirm the control of a territory, of the things that are found there and even of the people who inhabit it. And so it happens that in the heart of the country, during the first evening of any Saturday, a young man, watching videos on his cell phone with his sister and a friend, between a chat and a laugh, sees first two young people, then three, then four, to become a ten. In short, a gang of guys, captained by the cramp of a well-known family in the country for his judicial history, begins to beat up a boy for endless minutes and also does it unpunishedly. However, it happens that this time, the Carabinieri denounced it and, interested in the matter, I come to discover that this was not an isolated case, but that the baby gang has done other actions of this kind, which have remained unknown to the competent authorities, which I hope will go to the end of the matter, because - I repeat - what happened is disturbing and puts in the mouth an ancient flavor. At one time, when a country mobster rode the streets of the country along with his pigeons, people withdrew into their homes and those who couldn't get away lowered their heads in respect. Sicily, with its lack of economic development, is becoming involved and sociological phenomena are emerging that we thought were relegated to the past. Moreover, what is happening is likely to frustrate decades of struggle against the Mafia and, therefore, to desecrate the hundreds of victims who have opposed this drift. In Sicily there is a great nostalgia for the mafia and there is no respect for a state that appears to its inhabitants inefficient and far away. The Mafia gives work, security and serenity, while democracy appears, once again, an empty word. And so, while the neglected provincial roadway is making the local economy languish and depreciating the value of the land, causing so many small daily inconveniences to thousands of people who live and work there, sometimes even dying, we also have to see the emergence of phenomena such as what I am denouncing on behalf of my communities. We need the presence of a state in all its declinations, more attentive and careful towards its citizens, more effective in short, otherwise they will take back old practices of control of the territory and the people who inhabit it. [[Applause from the M5S Group.]]
