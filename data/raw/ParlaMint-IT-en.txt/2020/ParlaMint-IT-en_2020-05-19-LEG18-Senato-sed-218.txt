ParlaMint-IT_2020-05-19-LEG18-Senato-sed-218.ana.u11	ALESSANDRINI (L-SP-PSd'Az). Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, the very serious economic and financial situation in which many municipalities live is now known. The more than two months of closure of non-essential economic activities and the restrictions to which those that have been able to continue their exercise have led to dramatic effects, for some irreversible ones, on the national productive fabric. The consequences of a picture of such proportions are reflected not only on the social and economic fabric of the country, but also and I dare say above all on the financial estate of the municipalities, that is, the neighboring agencies, now more than ever called to provide concrete and timely responses to the numerous and increasing requests for assistance and support coming from their territories. The drastic reductions in revenue from the proceeds of tourist-cultural attractors, the lack of revenue related to local residence taxes and taxes, as well as the sharp reduction in revenue deriving from individual demand services and sanctions for violations of the road code, are obviously putting in pain the municipal budgets, thus compromising both the adequacy of the interventions, which this emergency requires, and the provision of essential public services itself, as repeatedly officially reported by the president of the Italian National Association of Municipalities (ANCI). These difficulties are even more decisive, even vital, for those municipalities that already operate under a regime of reduced fiscal manoeuvrability due to the plans of financial rebalancing in state of failure, like the Municipality of Terni. After nineteen years of center-left junta and unscathed management of municipal finances, in March 2018, the extraordinary commissioner resolved its state of failure: 87 million euros of creditors, almost 45 million euros of passive mass and another 20 million to be valued, five years of rates and rates for local taxes and taxes fixed at the maximum allowed and twenty years of the program of repayment of the loan received from the State to pay the debts and the suppliers. These are the numbers that photograph the financial reality in which the new administration is obliged to operate, linked to the strict constraints of the return from the deficit in order to face this economic crisis. Nevertheless, the Municipality has provided for the exemption of economic activities from the waste tax (Tari) and from the tax for the occupation of public spaces and areas (TOSAP) with reference to the period of effective closure, as well as of the rental of municipal buildings. Measures that are not decisive, but that mark the intention of the municipal administration to give concrete support to its community in the absence of real support from the Government. A week has passed since the press conference at which the President of the Council announced the measures contained in the so-called relaunch decree, which has not yet been published. We therefore wait for the text to finally see the light because it is necessary and fundamental for many realities, such as that of Terni, that the Government provide to the municipalities, including those in financial failure, effective and adequate tools to face the very delicate economic and social phase that awaits us in the coming months. If the objective stated by the President of the Council and the Government was not to leave anyone behind, then it is time to prove it. [[Applause.]]
