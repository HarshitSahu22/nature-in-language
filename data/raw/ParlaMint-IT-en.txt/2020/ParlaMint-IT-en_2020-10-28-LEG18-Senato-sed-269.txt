Amendment 20.0.38 calls for two things: firstly, the amendment of the Italian regulation, which is much more rigid than the European one, to adapt it and align it with the directive on the subject, which is much more liberal; secondly, I would like to postpone the date of entry into force of the one-year alert procedure until 1 September 2022, in order to prevent too many companies from being exposed to crisis procedures virtually automatically, especially in the historical period in which many companies are actually experiencing economic difficulties.
