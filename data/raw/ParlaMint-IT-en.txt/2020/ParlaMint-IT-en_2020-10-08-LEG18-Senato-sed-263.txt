ParlaMint-IT_2020-10-08-LEG18-Senato-sed-263.ana.u6	LANNUTTI (M5S). Mr President, I would like to draw the attention of this House and of my fellow Members to yet another leap to the detriment of citizens, who have already been harassed, in a dramatic economic phase like this for families, by the national system for payments PagoPA (public administration), carried out by the Agency for Digital Italy, which would have the duty to make life easier for the consumer, instead of continuing to vex it. At the same time, I would like to raise the question No 4-04167. With this unique system, electronic payments to the public administration (such as taxes, fines, stamps, ASL visits, utilities, school canteens, university network and much more) are not conveyed to a single site, but are distributed between different telematic channels, sites and apps of the many public bodies, municipalities, regions and universities, on which you can pay or ask the agency for a payment notice, i.e. a paper bulletin, to be paid through other channels, such as physical offices, ATMs, websites, bank apps and so on. Digitalizing processes involves the possibility of doing everything online, without leaving the house and without making queues, while in reality citizens still have to distangle themselves, as said, between paper bulletins. For example, when you have to pay Post Office a renewal of license or passport, while previously some payments housed in the bank were free, now we have a jump from 2.50 to 2.85 euros. The Consumer Code and the rules transposing the Payment Services Directive prohibit the application of fees on electronic payments. Those who buy an air ticket online or shop on the supermarket website do not have to pay a fee, as they have paid by credit card or other digital mode and this also applies to payments via POS. The implementation of this payment system for public administrations should also assess the consequences to the detriment of citizens; President, to the detriment of the citizens, who perhaps, certain of the banking house (and here I appeal to everyone, even to the senators), could by mistake ignore the payment notice, becoming so deadly, since there has never been a widespread campaign of information, setting up even an erarial damage. I shall conclude, Mr President, and thank you. Paying what is considered to be a tax in the eyes of citizens is always an injustice, especially in favour of shops and professionals... [[The microphone automatically switches off]] .. the tax act of 30% on costs. Payments and payments to public administrations and public companies must not lead to jumps and charges, as any costs are amortised by personal savings. Thank you, Mr President, and I hope that the Government will reconsider that. At such a dramatic stage, it is not fair to burden the citizens who have already been tried by this pandemic with new leaps. [[Applause.]]
