ParlaMint-IT_2020-05-05-LEG18-Senato-sed-213.ana.u17	DI NICOLA (M5S). Mr President, ladies and gentlemen, I have asked for action to bring to this House the disappointment and despair of the families of the victims of the tragedy in Rigopiano. In this locality of the mountain of Abruzzo - you will remember - on 18 January 2017 a homicidal avalanche, caused more by the impriety of men than by the malice of nature, caused the death of 29 innocent people. Two and a half years have passed and since then the victims' families have still been waiting for the relief and economic aid that the State had promised them. As far as the judicial front is concerned, I would like to protest at the length of the proceedings, which are ultimately caused even by the inability to find a suitable place to run the hearings quickly. I hope that the Minister of Justice will intervene with all the means available to resolve the problem. With regard to the other problem, the economic relief promised by the State to the survivors, I would like to remind you that Article 4-a of Decree-Law No 135 of 2018 allocated resources amounting to EUR 10 million. Not without emphasis to the press, the measure stated that it would then be the Presidency of the Council of Ministers, together with the mayors of the municipalities concerned, that would identify the beneficiary families and the sums owed to each. In implementation of the norm a special technical commission was established and made operational, also constituted by the aforementioned mayors of the municipalities of residence of the victims and those who have suffered serious and serious injuries. The inauguration and start-up meeting of this committee took place on 5 November 2019 at the Department for Administrative Coordination of the Presidency of the Council. By letter dated 21 January 2020, the same committee sent the Council Presidency the formal agreement reached on the distribution of payments. Problem solved? No way. To date, as represented by the Committee for the Victims of Rigopiano, no communication has yet been received from the Presidency. At this point, Mr President, by announcing a question to the President of the Council, I hope that the President and his offices will intervene as soon as possible, indeed immediately, to give the family members of those victims the economic refreshment they are entitled to, in addition in a situation aggravated by all the difficulties of the socio-economic emergency caused by the emergency Covid. [[Applause from the M5S Group.]]
