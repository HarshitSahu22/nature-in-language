This does not mean that there is, inter alia, a reduction in workloads for the competent authorities. In some ways there may be a reduction in work for the judicial authority, but let us remember that these administrative offences will open up any administrative disputes. We believe that this is a very sensitive matter, particularly at the moment, and that it should not therefore be subtly amended by simple amendments to the draft law under consideration. On the other hand, we believe that those relating to the administration of justice, taxation and financial matters should be included among the subjects to be excluded from decriminalisation.
