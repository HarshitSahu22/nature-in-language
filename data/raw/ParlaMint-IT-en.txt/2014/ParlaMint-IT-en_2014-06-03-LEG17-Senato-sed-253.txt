ParlaMint-IT_2014-06-03-LEG17-Senato-sed-253.ana.u15	Barani (GAL). Madam President, the Minister for the Interior, during the last elections for the European Parliament, assigned seats not under the law for the elections themselves, but on the basis of an opinion of the Council of State suggesting that the system for the election of the Chamber of Deputies be applied. I would therefore ask you, Madam President, to ask the Minister for the Interior to come to this House to report on this point. The National Central Election Office at the Court of Cassation is also asked to suspend the proclamation of the elected representatives to the European Parliament until the whole matter has been finally clarified. This Office, precisely because it is composed of members of the Court of Cassation, cannot abdicate to its role of direct hermeneutics of the law by uncritically transposing the contra legem determinations of the Ministry. In summary, it is believed that, by applying European law, there is a redetermination of the seats assigned in the different districts: for the 5 Star Movement they would no longer go to the southern Italian constituency but to that of island Italy; for the League they would no longer go to the central Italian constituency but to that of southern Italy and, of course, for the New Right Center would no longer go to the island Italian constituency, but to that of central Italy. If you will excuse me, I would also like to give you the technical and legislative reasons for my request for them to be annexed to the Minutes of the sitting.
