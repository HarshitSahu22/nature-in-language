It was in fact the next day, on September 13, 1982, that those standards were approved. The death of Pius La Torre was not enough. On September 3, the Prefect, General Dalla Chiesa, who shared those norms with Pio La Torre, also fell. Parliament did not intervene until after that. We have to move to the anti-mafia the day before. I think then of a dedicated session - as we do, for example, with the financial session - where the bodies of organised civil society could be gathered. On this day has a special role the "Centro Pio La Torre" (Centro Pio La Torre), but I also remember the "Fondazione Caponnetto" (Fondazione Caponnetto), "Libera" (Libera) and many associations that have an elaborate and design knowledge about legislation. We could gather the work of the Parliamentary Commission and ask all the Groups, giving a reasonable time, to deposit their legislative proposals in the Justice Commissions of the House and Senate and, then, to call the House to approve those texts that make us make a qualitative leap. This leap in quality would trigger unprecedented enthusiasm in our country. It would also be an unprecedented development factor, because legality and development must proceed together, and also the way to prepare ourselves for other days of memory. In fact, we will have the day of memory dedicated to Falcone and to the massacre of Capaci, to Borsellino and to the massacre of Via D'Amelio, on September 3, that dedicated to General Dalla Chiesa and many other moments of memory equally important. I think, however, that, in addition to remembering with the days of memory, we should move on to commitment. Parliament, with its institutional function, should make such a commitment to allow anti-mafia legislation to make a qualitative leap with the development that groups and civil society already have in their possession. We could finally make our country free from the mobs. [[Applause from the PD Group.]]
