Therefore, 18 million people will be able to have a much simpler situation, with the elimination of about 1 million formal errors, which currently affect these taxpayers and, at the same time, slow down the processes of detection and collection. These are standards which must be implemented and implemented quickly and which will enable us to take a step forward in our system. The measure also contains innovative rules on the tax process: this has been discussed in the various interventions and is one of the sore points of our tax procedure. It is necessary for Italy to make progress in this area, in order to eliminate the very high number of disputes, to try to simplify procedures as much as possible, to give greater certainty to the taxpayer and to allow the greatest possible third party to the decisions of the tax court. The legislation, as I said, also contains a number of procedural rules, which tend to improve the relationship between taxpayer and tax, also introducing a principle relating to what can be presented as preventive advice and technically as a question: to allow the taxpayer to have a preventive relationship with the tax, to be sure that certain acts he intends to carry out are regular or not. This function of the Revenue Agency must be developed: in addition to the function of the audit, a specific preventive advisory function must also be provided for certain interventions. I believe that this is another of the rules contained in this delegation law that will make progress in our country.
