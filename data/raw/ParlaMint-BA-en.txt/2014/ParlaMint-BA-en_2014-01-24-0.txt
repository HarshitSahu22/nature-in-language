ParlaMint-BA_2014-01-24-0.u3865	Ladies and gentlemen, dear colleagues and colleagues, representatives of the Council of Ministers of BiH, the international community, respected media representatives, I warmly welcome you and open the 36th session of the House of Peoples of the Parliamentary Assembly of BiH. Dear delegates, we find that a total of 12 delegates from the Bosniak people, 5 from the Croatian and 5 from the Serbian people attend the session. Delegates Sulejman Tihić, Nermina Kapetanović and Hasan Bećirović are not able to attend the House of Peoples session. Using Article 61. The Rules of Procedure of the People's House must be determined that we do not have the necessary quorum to hold a session of the House of Peoples, and note that the quorum consists of 9 delegates, subject to the presence of at least 3 Bosniaks, 3 Croatian and 3 Serb delegates, as well as the fact that at the beginning of each session and before the vote, the chair determines whether there is a quorum or no quorum and that the House informs it. By using the business obligation, we find that we do not have the necessary quorum to hold the session and at the same time I am informing you that we will define the term of the 36th session of the House of Peoples in the coming days. I also take the opportunity to inform all delegates who received receiving e-mails or e-mails by expressing our position to the College that we will inform the relevant police agencies in BiH, the Intelligence and Security Agency and ask them for an emergency response. The session will continue on the basis of a decision taken by the Kolegijum and I ask for your understanding and you will be timely informed of the continuation of the 36th session of the House of Peoples. Thank you. Session aborted at 11:32 p.m.
