ParlaMint-RS_2019-10-02-0.u61475	Dear ladies and gentlemen of the People's Parliament, we are continuing the work of the First Session of the Second Regular Meeting of the National Assembly of the Republic of Serbia this year. Based on official records of the presence of the MPs of the people, I find that the sittings are attended by 72 MPs. To determine the number of MPs present in the hall, please insert your identification cards into the parliamentary units of the electronic voting system. I find that by applying the electronic voting system, 129 MPs are present in the hall. We move to a decision on the proposals for acts referred to in points 1 and 2 of the agenda. I put to the vote the proposal for a conclusion on the review of the regular annual Report of the Commissioner for the Protection of Equality for 2018, which was submitted by the Committee on Human Minority Rights and Gender Equality on 10 September 2019. I conclude the vote and declare: for - 129 MPs. I find that the National Assembly has adopted a report with a proposal for a conclusion. I am putting to the vote the Proposal for a decision to amend the decision on the election of members of the Commission to investigate the consequences of NATO bombing in 1999 as a health impact on the environment, with a particular reference to the consequences left by the use of a projectile with impoverished uranium, in general. I hereby conclude the vote and declare: for - 129 MPs, one MP did not vote. I find that the National Assembly has adopted the motion for a decision. Since the National Assembly has carried out an examination of the agenda points of this session, the decision on them, in accordance with Article 102. I hereby conclude the First Session of the Second Regular Session of the National Assembly of the Republic of Serbia in this year.
