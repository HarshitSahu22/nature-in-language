ParlaMint-RS_2014-05-15-0.u1384	Dear Members of Parliament, in accordance with Article 90(2) and Article 101(2) and (5). The Rules of Procedure of the National Assembly, I would like to inform you that, due to an emergency caused by an elemental accident, Interior Minister Dr Nebojsa Stefanovic, is unable to attend the National Assembly session today at 10.00 hours, as an authorised representative of the government, when considering the 12th session agenda. A letter sent to us a few minutes ago and I would like to read both to the public and to you as MPs: "I ask that, because of the need for emergency maintenance of the Republic Emergency Staff, which is headed by the Minister of Interior, Dr Nebojsa Stefanovic, who will be held on 15 May 2014, at 11.00 p.m., allow you to move the session of the National Assembly, on 15 May 2014 from 10.00 to 15.00 p.m. We also ask that, for the same reasons, the Committee on Defence and Home Affairs is scheduled to be moved from 9:00 p.m. to 14.00 p.m. on 15 May 2014. For these reasons, the continuation of today's session of the National Assembly is delayed by 15.00 hours, until I set a break in work, and the Committee will be in charge at 1400 hours. Thank you for coming.
