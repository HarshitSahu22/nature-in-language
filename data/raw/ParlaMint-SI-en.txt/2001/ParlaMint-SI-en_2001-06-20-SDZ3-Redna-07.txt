ParlaMint-SI_2001-06-20-SDZ3-Redna-07.ana.u4	There are three reasons for the adoption of a law amending and supplementing the law on the participation of workers in management. The first is Constitutional Court Decision 1302/97 of 15.6.2000, by which the Constitutional Court found that the first paragraph of Article 79 of the Act on the participation of workers in the management of a non-conformity with the Constitution. Article 79, in the view of the Constitutional Court, in a work where it provides for at least half the number of employees in the Supervisory Council, excessively or disproportionately interferes with the right of owners, i.e. shareholders to own property and a free economic initiative. In addition, the fact that the decision is not regulated by law may result in unnecessary problems and disputes or even a blocking of decisions by the parental composition of the Supervisory Board. This non-compliance must be eliminated by the National Assembly no later than one year after the publication of this Decision in the Official Journal. The deadline expired last week on Friday, which is 15.6. The second reason is to link this law with the adopted law on safety and health at work. Coordination is needed with regard to the tasks of the Council of Workers or of the Labour Commissioner for Safety and Health at Work. In particular, the Law on Safety and Health at Work in Article 28 provides for consultation on any measure which may affect safety and health at work, while the Law on the participation of workers in management in Article 95 provides for a different form of labour participation, and this different regime introduces confusion in this area. And the third reason is the requirement of the European Union Directive on the protection of workers' rights in the event of a transfer. Taking into account the requirement of the Directive, it is necessary to maintain the status and function of working representatives in the event of a transposition, unless the conditions for a new appointment are met. A change of employer shall be considered as a transfer, e.g. takeover, sale of the company and so on. The law in force, I must make it clear, that the participation of workers in management is governed only by the protection of the members of the Council of Workers during the management of the function, and taking into account the provisions of the directive, it is necessary to regulate, in addition to the transposition, the protection of these representatives after the end of the term of office. Those are the reasons. The Government supports the amendment adopted by the mother's working body. The other amendments that have been tabled to this law are rejected.
