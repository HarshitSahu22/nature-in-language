On 25 September, I dismissed Prime Minister Stefan Löfven, the Social Democrats, since the newly elected Parliament in the Prime Minister's vote the same day declared that the Prime Minister did not have sufficient support in the Riksdag. According to the form of government, it is up to me to submit proposals to the new Prime Minister for approval today. I have consulted with representatives of each parliamentary party group and consulted with the Vice-Presidents, and I now propose that the Riksdag appoint Ulf Kristersson, the Moderates, as new Prime Minister, who intends to form a government composed of representatives of the Moderates and Christian Democrats.
