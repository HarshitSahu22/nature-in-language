ParlaMint-UA_2010-02-25-m0.u13	Dear National Deputies! Dear compatriots! I just made an oath to the President of Ukraine. How did I feel as I held my hand on the Holy Persupnic Gospel? The responsibility and humility of the people of Ukraine, who, in the course of free will, gave most votes for my candidacy; before the Most High, whose will I take over as Head of the Ukrainian State in such an unforgivable time for her. The election's over. The whole world recognized their democracy. The Ukrainian people said their word. And the international community has confirmed that Ukraine is a free state, where citizens' rights and freedoms are the highest values, and where people's choice cannot be questioned by anyone's ill will. This latter situation is particularly important in view of the young age of our democracy and the growing diseases that are so difficult to avoid in the process of State engagement. Nevertheless, we have endured this responsible examination with dignity. And today the 2010 presidential election page is over. It begins the next period of our new history. The country is in a very difficult situation: the absence of the State budget for the current year, colossal external borrowing debts, poverty, a broken economy, corruption -- a far short list of the problems that make up Ukrainian reality. Nevertheless, I think that the state can not only be saved from a socio-economic collapse, but also quickly lead to an accelerated development. If it wasn't for this confidence and this belief in my own strength, the strength of my team, and the power of the Ukrainian people, I would never have run for presidents. I know what to do and how to do it. The first thing is to re-establish the system of effective government. We must quickly create effective executive power that will immediately address the most affected sectors of the economy and the social sphere. The first priority on this path is to reform the system of power and, above all, the Cabinet of Ministers, turn it into a team of professionals, not political waiters. In this regard, I urge the Supreme Council to support my efforts to create a transparent, effective, responsible system of government capable of working with the President in synchronous mode. I emphasize that I see this special role in reforming the public administration system in Parliament. This is a place for discussion, this is a place for strong opposition, which should control the actions of the government and the President, but above all, this is a place for a strong and stable parliamentary majority. () [[Applause]] If we fail to overcome the existing political differences in the name of saving the state, if we fail to cleanse our hearts of the poison of hatred caused by futility and selfishness, the state will be doomed to continue wandering in political dusks and socio-economic mazes. The mutual rejection of the defeat of some and the victory of others is not only destructive in the interests of the state, but also deeply immoral in that it says in the Holy Scriptures: “Become reconciled with your adversary quickly while you are still on the road with him.” Life has confirmed this simple and obvious truth: People do not like it when they are shown fists, they tend to trust more those who give their hand in peace. That is why I appeal to all the members of the Supreme Council to cooperate in the name of Ukraine. [[Applause]] Such cooperation will be crucial to the immediate reform of power, to the judiciary, to the amendment of the Constitution. Today, the government is governed by a structure designed to achieve the objectives of individual politicians. This is true of both the judiciary and many other important aspects of Ukrainian society. We need to change the state of things. The structure of all branches of government should serve a single purpose: the swift adoption of the laws required by the State and their rapid implementation. At the same time, we need to ensure the legitimate interests of ordinary people, ordinary citizens, middle class representatives, intelligence, business. Effective cooperation between the President, Parliament and the government will clear the way for rapid economic progress. In turn, the solution will allow not only the elimination of chronic poverty but also the unification of the country. People tend to politicize certain issues of spirituality mostly when they are in a state of permanent financial instability. In economically developed nations, the likelihood of internal conflicts arising because of differences in cultural traditions of a region is tens of times lower than in economically lagging countries. Ukraine has to choose the right long-term development strategy, and many of our problems have arisen because instead of moving to a post-industrial society of the 21st century, we went through the initial accumulation of capital, that is to say, to what is called wild capitalism. We will be able to compete successfully in today's world only if we work in the knowledge industry, in modern technology, in stockpiling and in the realization of science. I realize that catching up to the industrialized countries is very difficult, but it is possible. To do this, Ukraine needs an innovation strategy, and this strategy is designed by our team. It envisions development and State support for a small number of priority areas where we can take a decent place in the international distribution of work. Instead of strengthening the state’s role in the economy, the State’s role in creating effective market mechanisms is involved. I'm convinced that the direct influence of the state on the economy, the manual control of it, is the way to nowhere. The prerequisites for restoring confidence in Ukraine by investors and international financial institutions are to ensure internal stability, overcome corruption, establish clear, and, above all, maintain stable relations between the state and business. Providing the first two conditions is going to be a particularly difficult process, but I have enough political will to transform them into reality. As President, I have a clear idea of which foreign policy strategy today is most suited to Ukraine's national interests. As a bridge between East and West, an integral part of Europe and the former Soviet Union, Ukraine will also choose foreign policy that will allow our nation to have the greatest impact on the development of an equal and mutually beneficial relationship with the Russian Federation, the European Union, the United States of America and other states that have an impact on the development of the world situation. In my opinion, the challenges facing the international community dictate the need to unite as widely as possible. Mankind, and Ukraine, among other things, need the European Union in global reading. I mean, the only world as a power capable of guaranteeing the planet a peaceful coexistence of various civilizations -- energy, environmental, food security. We are ready to participate in such processes as the European Non-Block State. I intend to propose this concept of parliament to build the foundations of Ukraine's foreign policy, and I hope that the higher legislative body will support me. When I take office as Head of the Ukrainian State, I want to tell the people of Ukraine that all my promises to him will be fulfilled. We're going to pay off our wages and pensions, which is not what the government has done. Taking into account the disastrous state finances situation, we're going to do it by reducing the costs of the bureaucratic system and starting with ourselves. One of my first orders as President of Ukraine will be a decree to cut spending on the President's Secretariat and other structures that will ensure its operation. The work and outcome in the name of the prosperity of my Fatherland is the credo with which I approach my responsibilities in my new position of responsibility. I am counting on your support, as well as for the support of an international community that wants to see our country strong and stable. And may the Lord help us in this righteous striving.
