The current provisions make it clear that the initial placement of staff is by decision of the President of the Parliament in the light of the needs of the Services and the qualifications of the officials. Furthermore, on the movement of personnel and for the purpose of better planning of the work of the Parliamentary Services, it is laid down that the Directorate-General and the Organisational Units, which are administratively subject to the President of Parliament, submit not every six months as they were before, but in the last quarter of each year at the Human Resources and Training Report of their needs. Article 9 adjusts the salary paid by the doctors of the Parliament with the remuneration of doctors and dentists of the National Health System. Articles 10 and 11 regulate the payment system. It is planned to open a separate account with the name "Volumes of the Greeks" to the Bank of Greece, through which Parliament's payments will be made, which have so far been made via DU. The provision of this provision is also the provision of Article 12, which redefined the procedure for payment of Parliament's budget expenditure. Under Article 13, the existing Legal Service of the Parliament is part of the Scientific Service of the Parliament on the grounds of essential and functionality.
