If the Ministry finds that an applicant does not comply with the conditions laid down in the Circular on Naturalisation, the Ministry shall examine whether it is possible to refer the matter to the Committee of Insights, either in accordance with the rules laid down in the Agreement of Accession or in accordance with established practice. If the Ministry finds that the applicant does not fulfil the conditions and the case cannot be submitted to the Committee of Insights, the applicant is informed that the conditions for becoming a Danish national are not fulfilled and the applicant is refused his application for Danish citizenship. Once the applicant has been refused, the Committee may ask to see a specific case. Thus, an applicant who does not fulfil the conditions for becoming a Danish national is only included in a bill on the notification of citizenship if the Committee of Insights has previously granted a derogation from one or more of the conditions laid down. I think that's a good model.
