ParlaMint-DK_20190912100203	The motion for a resolution was for the first reading on Tuesday, 10 September. It is, of course, a motion for a resolution based on a recommendation from the Committee on the Rules of Procedure, and it is a motion for a resolution tabled by the whole committee. That is why it would have been natural for the proposal to have been referred directly to this second and last reading without having to go back to the Committee on the Rules of Procedure. However, it happened at first reading that the proposal was nevertheless referred to the Committee on the Rules of Procedure, and since there is nothing really new to discuss, we have not found any reason to write a supplementary report on the proposal. Instead of being published, I am now making an oral recommendation on behalf of the committee on the adoption of the proposal. I therefore propose, on behalf of the Committee on the Rules of Procedure, that this motion for a resolution be adopted without amendment.
