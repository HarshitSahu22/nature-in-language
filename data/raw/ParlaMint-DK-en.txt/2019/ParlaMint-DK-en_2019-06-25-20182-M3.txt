ParlaMint-DK_20190625130157	Thank you for that, President. I am taking the floor as Vice-President of the Committee on the Verification of Credentials. The Folketing is today to decide on the approval of the European Parliament elections, which took place on 26 May 2019. Approval can only take place now because we have waited for approval of the elections to the Folketing. That is why the Committee on the Verification of Credentials has only yesterday, 24 June 2019, reviewed the outcome of the elections to the European Parliament and the electoral complaints received here. A total of 36 complaints were received concerning the elections, of which 35 are within the time limit for appeal and one outside. 14 of these complaints are complaints both about the elections to the European Parliament and to the Folketing. We have examined in committee the individual complaints and the comments of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and the Ministry of the Interior on each complaint. In the report and recommendation before us, the committee clearly expresses its conclusion to each of the complaints. During the committee's examination of the complaints, we discussed, among other things, that a number of citizens of one of the other Member States of the European Union have complained that they were not on the electoral register at the polling station and that they were among other things. in their complaint wondered about this, when they remembered that they had voted the last time there were elections. Paragraph 6.2 of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and the Interior's statement on the elections shows that three months before the elections, the Ministry sent a letter to the approx. 183.000 EU citizens living here also in English and with short references in French, German and Polish to draw attention to the need to apply to appear on the electoral list. This is necessary if you were not already on the electoral list at the last European elections. In this context, it should be noted that it is not enough to be on the electoral list for the last municipal elections. The Ministry has examined the cases in which EU citizens have approached and the Ministry's conclusion is that no errors have been detected in the specific cases. Bearing in mind the measures taken by the Ministry to advise these citizens about their opportunities to participate in the EU elections, the Committee considers that this does not give rise to any criticism. We also discussed a number of complaints about postal voting. The complaints concerned, in particular, waiting time at the polling stations, but there were also complaints that in some cases there appears to have been some confusion between the votes in the European Parliament and the parliamentary elections. The Committee notes, therefore, that the closeness of the two elections has led to difficulties, but also that the municipalities concerned have indicated that they regret the inconveniences that have occurred and that the authorities will review and learn from the experience of the two elections in order to avoid similar problems in future elections. An agreed committee recommends that the Folketing approve the election of 14 Members to the European Parliament on 26 May 2019. This means that the Danish Parliament approves the calculations and statements of the elections by the Ministry of Economic Affairs and the Ministry of the Interior and the 14 candidates listed in Annex 1 who elected, including Kira Marie Peter-Hansen-Hansen instead of Karsten Hønge, who has indicated that he does not wish to receive the mandate. On behalf of the committee, I should like to recommend to the House the committee's recommendation. Thank you.
