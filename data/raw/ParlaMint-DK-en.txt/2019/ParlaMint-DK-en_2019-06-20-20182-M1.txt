According to section 35 of the Constitution, the Folketing is to be convened no later than the 12th working day after the election day kl. 12 dinner. And immediately after the election of the President and Vice-Presidents, Parliament must be elected, as it were. It is therefore not the government, but the Folketing itself, which elects the President and the Bureau, just as it is the Folketing itself, which, according to Article 33 of the Constitution, determines the validity of the election of its members. This is because the founding fathers wanted to protect the Folketing from any influence of the government and the formation of the government. Pursuant to Paragraph 36 of the Constitution, section 1 of the Rules of Procedure says what we should do now: In the first session of the Parliament, after a new election, the President of the Parliament shall elect a temporary President to chair the proceedings of the Parliament until the general review of the elections has taken place. And the old-age president is not the oldest but the longest-standing member who also opens the meeting when Parliament meets again at the beginning of the parliamentary year on the first Tuesday of October.
