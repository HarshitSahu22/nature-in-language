September 2017 determined that the responsibility for all tasks, contracts and service agreements relating to basic IT operation of internal data centres, networks, servers and storage, operating of operating systems, standard IT workstation, service desk and user administration, information security tasks relating to upstream and upstream contracts and supply management tasks relating to outsourced IT operations relating to the Danish Agricultural Authority, shall be transferred from the Minister for Environment and Food, as well as the Minister for Fisheries and Gender Equality, respectively, to the Minister for Public Innovation per September 2017.
