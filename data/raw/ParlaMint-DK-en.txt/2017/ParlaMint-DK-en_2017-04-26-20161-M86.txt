ParlaMint-DK_20170426151324	B 71 refers to permanent residence or, in the case of daily residence, to permanent residence. This is a complex area of legislation because it is regulated both by the Planning Act and by the Housing Regulation Act. The Housing Regulation Act provides for the provision of housing and regulates dwellings occupied for the full year's residence. The purpose of the rules is to allow the municipality to maintain the population of full-year residences. Therefore, the rules of the Housing Regulation Act can only be applied once the dwelling has been put into service for a full year's residence. This is clear from section 50 of the Housing Regulation Act. 1, that a dwelling which has been used for permanent residence for the last five years may not, without the consent of the municipality, be used for summer or other temporary use which prevents the dwelling from continuing to be used for permanent residence. When the dwelling is covered by this provision, it refers to the obligation of residence in the dwelling. The obligation is to make sure that you inhabit or rent the dwelling yourself. The obligation to reside arises at the moment when the dwelling is occupied for a full year's residence with a permanent residence. If an owner wishes to use a perennial residence for purposes other than perennial residence, consent must be obtained from the municipality. Thus, in principle, the Housing Regulation Act does not provide for the obligation of residence for a newly constructed dwelling until the dwelling has been used for full-year residence. However, the legislation now allows for new construction of a local plan requiring housing to be used for all-year residences. With the Planning Act, the municipalities can allocate areas to the full-year residence. The Planning Act provides for planning for the use of an area, including full-year residence. The purpose of local plans is to allow municipalities to lay down rules for the development of certain areas, including the extent to which housing in the area is to be used for the full year residence. In the local plan, the municipality may provide that newly built dwellings may only be used for full-year residence and therefore cannot legally be used for other purposes. If the dwelling is used for other purposes, e.g. holiday rental, the owner can be punished with a fine. Enforcement of a local plan's requirement for full-year residence can only be done by ordering the cessation of illegal use. It is therefore not possible to require legal use to be carried out.
