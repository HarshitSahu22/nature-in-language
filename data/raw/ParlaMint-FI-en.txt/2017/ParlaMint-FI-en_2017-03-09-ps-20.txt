The Nordic borders have been gradually opened up for several decades in different areas, largely thanks to the important work of the Nordic Council. The aim of the Nordic border barrier work is to remove obstacles to free movement between the Nordic countries. Border barriers refer to laws and public rules or practices that hamper the mobility of persons or the cross-border activities of companies in the Nordic countries. The opportunities for workers and businesses to move across borders contribute to economic growth and employment both in the Nordic region and in Finland as a whole. Together, the Nordic countries are among the ten largest economies in the world and the five largest in Europe, so the removal of obstacles to mobility is not insignificant. During the Finnish Presidency, Finland wishes to make border barriers and their dismantling a strong political focus. The aim is to make everyday life easier for Nordic citizens in terms of mobility. The Nordic Council parliamentarians have an important role to play in border barrier work, including by exerting pressure on the Nordic governments to remove border barriers.
