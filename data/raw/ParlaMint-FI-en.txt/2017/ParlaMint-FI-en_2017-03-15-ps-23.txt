It is a promise that we do not have an impasse, but that the education system works in such a way that you can update your skills at a later stage and continue that path through vocational education to higher education, too, if you have such willingness and motivation. That is why we currently have a working group to consider how this postgraduate qualification would work in real life, so that more people would be able to move on to further studies after completing their vocational training degree. The big picture of the development of vocational education and training is the need for a vocational education system that serves the needs of different learning needs, different target groups and better responds to the future skills needs of society and working life. On this basis, reform will be built, and I thank Parliament for its cooperation already.
