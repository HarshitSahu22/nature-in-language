ParlaMint-FI_2017-11-15-ps-118.u458	Mr President! In their alternative budget, the Finns have proposed supporting family carers’ ability to function and to cope with them through versatile means. But, of course, financial support is also needed and can be obtained through this legislative initiative if it is adopted by Parliament in due course. It is estimated that there are some 350,000 informal care situations in Finland, of which 60,000 are binding or demanding. As is justified in this bill, from the point of view of the person being treated, informal care is a very humane form of care and its importance is emphasised by the reduction of institutional care, but, admittedly, for a family carer caring for someone close to him, this work is in many cases very demanding and burdensome, both mentally and physically, and reduces the chances of obtaining other incomes. The support received by carers is generally not very large, and the proportion left in hand is often very small in view of the pregnancy of this work. In our view, the aid element should be higher than at present, particularly given the fact that informal care saves a considerable amount of society's money. It would therefore be justified to facilitate the financial situation of carers by means of taxation. Consequently, this bill proposes that the carer’s allowance provided for in the Act on support for informal care should not be regarded as taxable income. We believe that this would be the right way to improve the financial situation of family carers.
