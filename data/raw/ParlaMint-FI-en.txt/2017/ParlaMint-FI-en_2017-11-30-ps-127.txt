On the 78th anniversary of the Winter War, I would like to remind you that the Library of Parliament has done a very valuable job in fostering the spiritual heritage of the veterans by organising an exhibition on the Last Man. That exhibition contained pictures and brief information about the men and women who served in our last wars. Thank you, too, for the many other things that the library has produced for us.
