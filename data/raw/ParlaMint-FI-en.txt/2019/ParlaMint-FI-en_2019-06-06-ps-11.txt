ParlaMint-FI_2019-06-06-ps-11.u1	The second thing in the order of business is the choice of the Prime Minister. By letter dated 6 June 2019, the President of the Republic informed Parliament of the candidate for Prime Minister within the meaning of Article 61(2) of the Finnish Constitution. The letter was brought to the attention of Parliament by distributing it to its representatives. (M1/2019vp)
