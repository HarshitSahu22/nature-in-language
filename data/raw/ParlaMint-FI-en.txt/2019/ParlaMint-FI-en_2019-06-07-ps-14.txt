ParlaMint-FI_2019-06-07-ps-14.u1	The election of the first Vice-President will now be followed by compliance with the provisions of Article 34(2) of the Finnish Constitution and Articles 5 and 6 of the Parliamentary Statute. So the election will be delivered on a full-time basis. Representatives shall bring their ballots to the urn according to roll call, which shall be delivered in alphabetical order. I would like to point out that the name of the candidate must be identified with sufficient precision, including, where appropriate, the first name. I note that the votes cast are not made public.
