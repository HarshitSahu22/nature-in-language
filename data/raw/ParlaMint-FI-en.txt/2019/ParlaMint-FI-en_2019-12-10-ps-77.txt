ParlaMint-FI_2019-12-10-ps-77.u1	The second thing in the order of business is the choice of the Prime Minister. By letter dated 9 December 2019, the President of the Republic informed the President of Parliament that the candidate for Prime Minister, as referred to in Article 61(2) of the Constitution, was Mr Sanna Marin, a Member of Parliament and a Master of Administration. The letter was brought to the attention of Parliament by distributing it to its representatives. (M3/2019vp)
