ParlaMint-FI_2019-12-10-ps-78.u1	The second item in the order of business will be presented for discussion. The previous plenary session presented a statement by the President of the Republic dated 9 December 2019 stating that the candidate for Prime Minister is a Member of Parliament, a Master of Administration Sanna Marin. The adoption of the proposal must be put to the vote. The choice follows the provisions of Article 61(2) of the Finnish Constitution and Article 65(1) of the Rules of Procedure of the Parliament. The candidate has become elected Prime Minister if more than half of the votes cast in the open vote have supported his election. There will be no discussion. The approval of the Prime Minister candidate shall be subject to a machine vote. Member of Parliament, Master of Administration Sanna Marin's election as Prime Minister; who is not in favour of the election, votes no.
