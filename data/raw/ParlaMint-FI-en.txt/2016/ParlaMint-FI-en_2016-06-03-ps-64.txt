ParlaMint-FI_2016-06-03-ps-64.u34	Madam President! With regard to Sitra’s 2015 Annual Activity Report, the Committee on Economic Affairs notes that: Finland faces the necessary structural changes as the economic situation tightens and the organisation and financing of public welfare services faces new challenges. Over the years, the Finnish Fund for Independence has become a pioneer of research on financiers and venture capitalists and a catalyst for societal change. Sitra has facilitated networking and joint projects between domestic and international actors in different sectors and the dissemination of best practices. Sitra has also set the right and appropriate target for translating the identified megatrends, such as the interdependence of technological phenomena and the sustainability crisis, into Finland's competitive advantage. The Committee on Economic and Monetary Affairs and Industrial Policy believes that Sitra, as a self-funded pilot organisation, has been able to recognise and react to changes. The key themes have been renewable capacity, resource wisdom and carbon-neutral society, as well as new working life and a sustainable economy. These themes have been implemented through key regional projects. The Committee on Economic and Monetary Affairs and Industrial Policy considers Sitra's guidelines to be successful influencing international research institutes and think tanks networks and implementing well-being research projects. The Economic Committee also considers that Sitra's role as an independent development organisation is becoming increasingly important in a changing operating environment. The importance of national reference projects and public procurement should therefore be seen in order to make full use of the leverage they allow. The committee also notes that the resourcing of research and experiments that follow its time and the selection of guidelines are difficult tasks, but Sitra has also made consistent and bold decisions in this respect. The Committee on Economic and Monetary Affairs and Industrial Policy believes that climate change and the reduction of natural resources are one of our main problems, which are already affecting both the business environment and the everyday lives of private citizens. The committee agrees with Sitra that Finland could play a bigger role in solving the sustainability crisis. Resource-wise solutions and clean technologies could be an integral part of the foundation of Finland's new economy. The committee also wishes to stress the importance of cooperation with Sitra and the parliamentary committees. The Committee on Economic and Monetary Affairs and Industrial Policy believes that Sitra has been very successful in its 2015 report year. The report by the Committee on Economic and Monetary Affairs and Industrial Policy on this point is unanimous. The Committee on Economic Affairs and Industrial Policy therefore unanimously proposes that Parliament approve the committee's opinion on the Finnish Fund for Independence, Finland's report K 2/2016.
