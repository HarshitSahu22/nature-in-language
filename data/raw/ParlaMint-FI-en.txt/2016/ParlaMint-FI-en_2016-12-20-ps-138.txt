In its report, the Committee deals with the crisis of agricultural profitability and notes that the market situation for agricultural products is difficult in Finland and the EU as a whole. The Natural Resources Centre estimates that entrepreneurial income per farm will fall to EUR 9400 in 2016. According to the forecast, 38% of agricultural enterprises do not have any entrepreneurial income. The committee considers that the government's decisions aimed at facilitating the economic situation of farmers are heading in the right direction and their impact on entrepreneurial income is positive. In the context of the draft budget, it was decided to allocate a total of EUR 50 million to finance agricultural crisis measures. Most of this was introduced in the third amending budget for 2016. According to the Government Programme, a total of EUR 90 million will also be invested in increasing the profitability of agriculture through the Agricultural Development Fund. Of this, EUR 30 million is included in the draft budget under discussion. The Committee notes that the high level of debt, the volatility of agricultural markets and fluctuations in yields have increased the risk of farmers, which has made subsidies a stabilising factor. The Committee also considers it necessary to improve the profitability of agricultural holdings by means of other measures. Farmers need concrete measures to help overcome the current difficult situation, such as comprehensive, objective advice, because there are large differences in profitability between farms, so individual solutions play a key role in the profitability of the farm. The committee will therefore increase the amount of state aid for the development of rural livelihoods by EUR 1.9 million. In order to improve the profitability of agriculture, the Committee continues to see the need for farm organisation and to increase the amount of EUR 400000 in support of real estate supplies. The committee also draws attention to improving the competitiveness of Finnish food production and notes that there are unused market opportunities that should be exploited in pricing. The committee attaches importance to the allocation for the development of the national food chain and stresses that successful export efforts require international visibility. The committee will add EUR 100000 to the Arctic Aromit ry in order to promote exports of natural products and related tourism. The committee also considers it important to increase the knowledge of public food procurement, to highlight the extensive development of the EkoCentria sector and to add EUR 150000 to promote the use of local food. With regard to forestry, the Committee highlights the need to ensure the sustainability of wood production and notes that the problem with the renewed Kemera system is not the adequacy of the funds to be allocated in autumn 2016, but the failure to function. Due to administrative reasons, Kemera support is expected to remain unused by around EUR 15 million in 2016. The committee therefore proposes that the statement be adopted: 'The draft amending budget for 2017 requires the addition of an amount equivalent to that of the 2016 support for ensuring the sustainability of wood production.' In addition, the committee will add EUR 400000 to the Finnish Forest Centre to develop the electronic system for dismantling the Kemera application congestion and processing applications. The committee will also increase the budget of EUR 600000 to the 4H organisation for activities promoting entrepreneurship and youth employment, EUR 250000 to the state aid for village activities, and will emphasise support for regional and local activities, EUR 100000 to manage fisheries, EUR 50000 to the Finnish Union for Professional Fishing to promote commercial fishing and EUR 50000 to be used for the development of alternative research systems to replace animal testing. The total increase made by the committee is EUR 4 million. Finally, I would like to mention a few points which the committee will have to revise year after year. The committee considers the procedure to be reprehensible and considers it important that the government corrects the procedure in the draft budget for 2018.
