The Government proposal proposes to amend the lottery law, among other things, so that only one gambling company has the exclusive right to execute gambling in Finland. This gambling company would be called Veikkaus Oy. The proceeds from the gambling business of Veikkaus Oy should continue to be used for general interest purposes. In future, 53 per cent of the bet will be used to promote sport and sports education, science, art and youth work, 43 per cent to promote health and social well-being, and 4 per cent to promote horse training and horse sports. I think it is particularly important that the money invested in sport should go to greater lengths in youth sports, because that is where our future top athletes come from. In addition to raising money, one of the main objectives of the Finnish gambling policy, if not the most important, must be to combat the personal and social disadvantages associated with gambling. This is easy to forget. Gambling always involves risks for the individual, and as a result of gambling, the adverse effects can also be reflected in the closeness of the player, the marriage mate and wider society as a whole. The disadvantages of gambling cannot be minimized in any way. Gambling can lead a young person’s life to the sidelines from which he has no return. He loses his credit records and has no access to loans from a bank, for example, for many years. I believe that our country has rightly taken the view that the best way to achieve this is through an exclusive system of gambling. In this proposal, the provisions would be specified in such a way that economic, social and health damage must be prevented as well as reduction. Moreover, the obligation to prevent and reduce will also apply to economic disadvantages as a result of the reform. According to this proposal, the measures to reduce the risk of gambling are divided into three categories. There are also studies on this. International impact studies contribute to demonstrating that the best way to prevent harm is to regulate game offerings and that education produces less effective results. In view of the importance of combating gambling risks, it must be understood that a successful prevention of harm is likely to reduce, at least to some extent, the proceeds from gambling. According to the proposal, the new Veikkaus Oy is obliged, as is the case with the law in force, to compensate the State for the costs of monitoring and research on the harm caused by the execution of gambling, as well as the development of their prevention and management. ♪ By the show ♪ Bets have an obligation to open a player-specific account. A player can only have one account. The game can be combined with mechanisms for monitoring your own gaming management and your own gaming consumption. I also think it is important that the account can be closed if there is any indication that the player is playing across his borders, engaging in fraud, or otherwise in the fight against gambling disadvantages. In this context, I think it is a good idea to talk about the fast-start companies that lend money to gambling addicts at the annual interest rate of hundreds of euros. This should be explicitly prohibited by law, because it is a criminal activity comparable to blackmail. The prevention and research of gambling disadvantages and the development of care and the financing of these measures are a key issue for the success of our gambling policy. In my opinion, this also includes, very substantially, a ban on fast-start companies. At the same time, it can be said that primary school education should already include compulsory home and housekeeping hours, which would include, among other things, the disadvantages of gambling addiction.
