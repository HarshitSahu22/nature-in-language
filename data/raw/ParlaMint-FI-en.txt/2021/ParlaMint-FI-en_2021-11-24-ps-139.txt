ParlaMint-FI_2021-11-24-ps-139.u389	Mr President! In the course of this debate, it has become clear — and it is good — that as part of the process of preparing for a comprehensive reform of the Emergency Act within the government and in conjunction with the opposition parties, we will see whether there is any reason to distinguish between this overall reform, which will last for many years, which would be linked to today's theme and which could be prepared not in years but in months, for example — good! It has also become clear, among other things, in the last speech made here by the Minister, that the government is also considering whether this theme should be included in the ordinary laws which need to be reformed in this respect, and that in this respect the preparation is already under way or at least starting — good! The problem is the following quote from the Government’s joint question: ‘It is clear,that the emergency situation which has been raised in the resolution on the suspension of applications for asylum is not possible within the framework of the Constitution As I said in my speech, I am not at all convinced that such an emergency procedure would be temporarily contrary to our international commitments. It is now my wish, through Mr Köntä, to go all the way to the centre, that this preparation, which has begun or is about to start within the government, should be more open-minded to this possibility in the event of an exceptional temporary suspension of the reception of asylum applications and would not rule out this possibility in advance in this drafting of the law. I personally hope that, although this interim answer is, of course, shared by the government, the centre in this respect could represent a broader reflection within the government. I am not at all convinced, for example, that there will not be any international interpretations in the near future that would allow the exceptional emergency procedure on this issue in this case. In other words, I hope, Mr Könttä, that in this matter the centre of the government will see to it that the preparation is more open-minded than what was said in this interim question answer in this respect.
