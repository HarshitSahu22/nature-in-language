ParlaMint-FI_2021-05-25-ps-63.u169	Mr President! The proposal provides for provisions supplementing the EU Regulation on Medical Devices and the EU Regulation on Medical Devices used in extra-human-body studies, i.e. the so-called in vitro diagnostic devices. The aim of the new equipment regulations is to protect patients' health and support innovation. The aim is also to restore the confidence of patients, consumers and health professionals in the safety of equipment. The placing on the market of medical devices is not subject to authorisation by the authorities. However, a medical device may be placed on the market or put into service only if it is properly supplied and installed, maintained and used in accordance with the above regulations. The Regulations provide for the conformity assessment of devices and the related clinical assessment and performance assessment. In addition, the regulations include provisions on clinical trials, traceability and registration of devices, control and incident systems, and cooperation between Member States. According to the study, the regulations are broader in scope than the previous Directives and stricter in terms of, for example, clinical trial procedures, device identifiers and quality management systems. The proposed new law on medical devices regulates the matters for which EU regulations require or allow national provisions. Other legislative proposals in the proposal mainly concern changes to the terms used and other necessary technical amendments. In addition, the Act on Patient Insurance, which is the fourth draft law, proposes an amendment in which the term ‘medically researchable’ does not refer to the subject of research within the meaning of the Law on Medical Research. It is intended that the reference should also cover the subject of investigation in accordance with the equipment regulations, the pharmaceutical research regulations and the additional national laws. Mr President! The committee considers that legislative proposals are necessary and appropriate and supports their adoption with minor technical amendments, mainly. This committee's report was also unanimous.
