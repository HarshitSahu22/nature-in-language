Domestic fish would sell more than we can catch. However, the number of commercial fishermen has decreased in the 21st century. The industry is particularly depressed by the ageing of fishermen. As the Committee on Agriculture and Forestry points out, this proposal avoids the negative consequences of raising the VAT duty limit on commercial fishing and should therefore be supported. The livelihood of a declining number of fishermen must be considered worthwhile so that we will continue to have Finnish, healthy and high-quality fish on the counter. During the time of the interest rate, many have found fishing as a hobby. We should promote that for someone a hobby can also become a business or part-time or full-time job.
