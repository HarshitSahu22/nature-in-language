ParlaMint-FI_2021-04-09-ps-39.u2	Madam President! I wanted to experience a plenary session with only one representative here on the floor. The Committee on Constitutional Affairs considers the remote meetings of the committees for reasons of corona to be essential in order to ensure the functioning of Parliament. In the committee, we considered it important to ensure security links and to ensure that the committee's discussions at a distance could be held in a confidential manner and that no information on matters or documents would be available to third parties before they became public. Security must be fully secured. We also stressed the need for the meeting to ensure that the right of everyone to participate is genuinely realised. The Committee on Constitutional Affairs believes that it is justified that, in addition to urgent and other necessary matters, only non-controversial matters should be taken into consideration at the formal teleconference.
