ParlaMint-FI_2021-03-08-ps-20.u218	Madam President! I think that Mr Al-Taee will make a very important point here. Indeed, these difficulties have become more difficult for people, citizens, especially older people or people with reduced mobility, and now especially for those at risk in the covid-19 period, through previous measures. Among other things, ATM networks and terminals, and so on, are increasingly concentrated near these small municipal centres or agglomerations themselves, so that they cannot be reached. It is therefore of course important that these digital first recognition services exist in order to obtain as many different services as possible online. I would remind you, however, that this legislation, which we were now doing in committee and which is deciding the price regulation of this chain of initial recognition, has nothing to do with these physical services themselves. This legislation does not exclude the possibility of access to and identification of physical services in ways such as those used to be, whether through an identity card or otherwise physically on the spot. They are the problems of the service networks themselves, then. This observation was very important and I fully share it with Mr Al-Tae.
