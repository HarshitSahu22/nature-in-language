ParlaMint-FI_2021-10-07-ps-111.u159	Mr President! This proposal proposes to provide for a law on the recruitment aid trial. Recruitment aid should be paid to an undertaking in the form of a financial contribution to the wage costs of the first employee. Its aim is to lower the company's threshold for hiring an employee. The purpose of the aid is therefore to promote the creation of new employer enterprises and thus to promote business growth and employment. Recruitment support would be carried out as a controlled trial based on a trial design to determine whether the aid has any of the above-mentioned effects. The target group of the experiment would include sole entrepreneurs and other enterprises which, during the previous 12 months, have had no external paid labour force and whose turnover in 2021 is at least EUR 15000 but not more than EUR 1 million. An experimental and control group would be selected from the target group by random sampling. In the first phase, 3500 companies would be selected in the pilot group for which the possibility of aid would be offered. If less than 900 of these companies were to make use of the possibility of aid by the deadline set, a second phase of the experiment would be carried out in which the possibility of aid would be offered to a new pilot group. The size of the Phase II pilot group would be defined on the basis of the information obtained from the first phase, with the aim of achieving the 900 eligible companies. This is an estimate made by researchers of the number at which statistically significant results can be expected from the experiment. The amount of recruitment aid to enterprises would be EUR 10000, but 50% of the wages paid by the company would be paid for a maximum period of 12 months. This 12-month support period would start from the start of the first employee’s employment relationship. However, the aid would not only be linked to the salary of the first employee but could also cover the company’s salary costs up to the maximum amount of the aid. The proposal also proposes a temporary amendment of the Public Employment and Business Services Act, the Incomes Information System Act and the Criminal Records Act. The proposed changes would concern the right to information related to the experiment. The proposal relates to the proposal for a budget of the State for 2022 and is intended to be dealt with in connection with it. The Government's draft budget for 2022 provides for an allocation of EUR 11.65 million for recruitment support and EUR 450000 for the KEHA Centre for the execution of payment tasks. In addition, EUR 900000 has already been earmarked in the 2021 budget for the IT and human resources costs of the experiment. The Ministry of Employment and the Economy will carry out an evaluation of the experiment. The evaluation would take place after the experiment, i.e. in the spring of 2024 at the earliest, when all recruitment grants have been paid. The laws are intended to enter into force on 1 March 2002. The Act on the Recruitment Support Experiment would mainly be in force until 31.12.2023.
