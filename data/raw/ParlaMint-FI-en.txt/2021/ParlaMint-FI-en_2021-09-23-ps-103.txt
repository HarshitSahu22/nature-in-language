The aim of this Act is to amend the Student Support Act so that a student of vocational education completing an apprenticeship as part of his or her studies would be equal to a student of higher education who is studying as part of a traineeship. The law on vocational education and training provides for vocational qualifications falling within the administrative domain of the Ministry of Education and Culture, the training needed to acquire professional skills, and the demonstration and verification of professional competence. Apprenticeship training was reformed in the Act. Apprenticeship training is, in accordance with Article 70 of the Act on Basic Vocational Training, mainly organised in the workplace on the basis of a written fixed-term contract between a student aged 15 and over or a written contract between a student and an employer who is in a public service relationship or a contract of employment comparable to a service relationship. Apprenticeships may be awarded if the employer and the organiser of the training have agreed on the organisation of apprenticeship training. Mr President! The general conditions for the granting of student aid are the approval of the educational institution, the full-time pursuit of studies and progress in studies, and the need for financial support. According to Article 5a(3) of the Student Support Act, vocational studies are main activities, on average at least three weeks When the scope of studies is not measured in credits or competence points, an average of at least 25 weekly hours of participation in education or training programmes or training courses is required. The employer decides for himself whether to pay the student a salary during the period of knowledge-based studies. The student is entitled to financial benefits if the employer does not pay wages during the period of knowledge-based education. During the apprenticeship period of vocational qualifications, the student can apply for daily allowance from the organiser of the education for days when the education is local education in the school environment of the organiser instead of the job.
