ParlaMint-FI_2021-03-11-ps-21.u189	Mr President! In our society based on trust, transparency and democracy, there should be no room for terrorist action or its financing. According to the motion, the financing of an individual terrorist is also punishable as a new crime. This amendment of the law is necessary and is also supported by the assessment of Finland’s penal provisions by the international institutions. The focus of the fight against terrorism is on prevention and prevention in Finland, where funding plays an important role. Money donations are also sent from Finland to conflict areas, which should be monitored. Even small sums of money in conflict areas are capable of equipping and supporting terrorist groups in important ways. Terrorism offences are fundamental functions of society and very serious crimes that endanger people's lives, health and safety. The laws relating to terrorist offences must be fully up-to-date. The Centre will support the Government in bringing new legislation to Parliament. We attach the utmost importance to making all terrorist activities more punishable than is currently the case, as the experts have suggested. Sooner or later, there will be a need for a comprehensive reform of the law on terrorism. We also believe that, in addition to international terrorist groups, we must evaluate the behaviour of domestic extremists.
