ParlaMint-FI_2015-06-04-ps-15.u8	A point of order 6 will be presented for the first reading. This is based on the report by the Committee on Culture, Public Health and Consumer Policy on the SiVM 1/2015 vp. The content of the draft law is now being decided.
ParlaMint-FI_2015-06-04-ps-15.u9	Madam President! We in the Committee on Culture and Education have already worked hard in this process, even in the course of the government negotiations. It is a very technical matter, a minor correction of the High School Law which we had before the Committee on Education. The current law on upper secondary school assumes that the application procedure is regulated separately and this has led to the drafting of the act not allowing the regulation to be adopted. The Government has therefore submitted a proposal 3/2015 proposing, according to the wording, that the application procedure in the Upper Secondary Secondary Education Act be applied for.
ParlaMint-FI_2015-06-04-ps-15.u10	The 7th item in the order of business will be presented to the first reading. This is based on the report by the Committee on Economic and Monetary Affairs and Industrial Policy on TaVM 1/2015 vp. The content of the legislative proposals is now being decided.
ParlaMint-FI_2015-06-04-ps-15.u11	Madam President! The Committee on Economic and Monetary Affairs and Industrial Policy supports the adoption of legislative proposals without amendment. The aim of the proposal is to put into effect the specifications for extensive regulation of the resolution of credit institutions and investment firms. The proposed amendments relate to the implementation of the DGS Directive and the clarification of the scope of the resolution directive. The proposed changes are mainly required by the new EU regulation and will enter into force by 3 July 2015. The committee considers the proposed amendments to be justified and appropriate.
