ParlaMint-FI_2015-06-26-ps-26.u6	Madam Speaker! This proposed amendment to Article 30 of the Åland Self-Government Act means that the Government of the Åland Islands would also carry out tasks which, under the legislation of the Kingdom of Åland on the control of communicable diseases in humans and domestic animals, belong to municipalities. This is a constitutional amendment in itself, but a technical amendment which is necessary. The municipalities in Åland no longer have municipal doctors or municipal doctors’ posts, and therefore in Åland the health and veterinary authorities of the province should be allowed to take care of these communicable tasks under national law. The proposal would not entail additional costs for the State or the Province of Åland, nor would it have any other administrative implications. According to the Åland Act of Self-Government, the law can only be amended by unanimous decisions of the parliament and the provincial days. In Parliament, the decision must be taken in the order in which the Constitution is laid down. On 10 December 2014, Parliament has adopted a draft law to be submitted to rest in the first parliamentary elections following the elections, and the Åland Regional Days have announced that they will accept the proposal.
