ParlaMint-FI_2015-05-28-ps-10.u1	The second item in the order of business will be presented for discussion. The previous plenary session presented a statement dated this day by the President of the Republic stating that the candidate for Prime Minister is a representative of Parliament, a diploma engineer Juha Sipilä. It is now up to Parliament to decide whether or not to accept the candidate. The choice follows the provisions of Article 61(2) of the Finnish Constitution and Article 65(1) of the Rules of Procedure of the Parliament. The candidate has become elected Prime Minister if more than half of the votes cast in the open vote have supported his election. There will be no discussion. The approval of the Prime Minister candidate shall be subject to a machine vote. Who is in favour of the proposal to elect a MP, a diploma engineer, Juha Sipilä, as Prime Minister, to vote 'yes'; who is not in favour of it, to vote 'no'.
ParlaMint-FI_2015-05-28-ps-10.u2	The Parliament should decide to bring the result of the election to the attention of the President of the Republic.
ParlaMint-FI_2015-05-28-ps-10.u3	Ladies and gentlemen of Parliament! Thank you very much for your confidence in me. After I have been elected as Prime Minister in the recent vote, Parliament will verify that my role as President of Parliament will cease from the moment when the President of the Republic appoints me as Prime Minister. It is announced that the election of the President will take place at the first plenary session following the appointment of the Prime Minister.
