Kari Uotila vas Li Andersson's opposition: "The government is not in a position to improve Finland's economic and employment situation by means of its budget proposal or other measures presented by it. Therefore, the government does not enjoy the confidence of Parliament." With the support of Joakim Strand r Anders Adlercreutz r: "The Sipilä Government is proposing massive cuts in education, despite the election promises of the government parties. Education cuts represent a real threat to the quality of education and research, to equality in education and to the competitiveness of our country. The government also cuts huge amounts of development aid. These cuts fall to the shameful 0.35% of GDP of development aid and are directed directly at the most vulnerable people. Development aid cuts also endanger Finland's reputation as a responsible and reliable international player. As a result, Parliament states that the government does not enjoy the confidence of Parliament."
