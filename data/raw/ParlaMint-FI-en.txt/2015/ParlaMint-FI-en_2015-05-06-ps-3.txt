In accordance with Parliament's Rules of Procedure, the Council of Presidents, which is responsible for making proposals for the arrangement of sittings between parliamentary groups in the Chamber, proposes that Parliament should decide that representatives should sit in the Chamber by parliamentary groups, with a Swedish parliamentary group on the extreme right from the seat of the President, and from then on other parliamentary groups in the following order: a parliamentary assembly of the Parliament of the Coalition Party, a Christian Democratic Parliament Group, a parliamentary group of the Centre Party, a parliamentary group of Finns, a Green Parliamentary Group, a Social Democratic Parliament Group and, on the extreme left, a parliamentary group of the Left, each group within the limits laid down by the Council of Presidents.
