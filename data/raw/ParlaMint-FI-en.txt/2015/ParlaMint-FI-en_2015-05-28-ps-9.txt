ParlaMint-FI_2015-05-28-ps-9.u1	The second thing in the order of business is the choice of the Prime Minister. By letter of 28 May 2015, the President of the Republic informed the Parliament of the candidate for Prime Minister within the meaning of Article 61(2) of the Constitution of Finland. The letter was brought to the attention of Parliament by distributing it to its representatives. Pursuant to Article 65(1) of the Rules of Procedure, the matter must be put on the table at one of the following plenary sessions. The Council of Presidents proposes that the matter be put on the table for the second plenary session to be held today.
