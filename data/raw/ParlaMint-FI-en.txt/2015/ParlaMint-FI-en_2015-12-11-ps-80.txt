ParlaMint-FI_2015-12-11-ps-80.u32	The Council of Presidents proposes that the procedure for the sole reading of the draft budget be as follows: the representative who intends to propose amendments to the report of the Committee on Finance must submit his amendment to the central assembly. This must be done even if the amendments are included in the objections. I would ask the representatives in their amendment to mention the budget initiative or the number of objections on which the amendment may be based. Amendments to sections 21 to 31 must be submitted to the central office by Monday 14 December at the latest. 2 p.m. and other amendments by Tuesday 15 December at the latest. 12.00 p.m. The proposals will be put together as hate to be distributed to the representatives. The proposals shall be numbered in each section and title, as well as in the general provisions of the detailed explanatory memorandum and in the general statement of reasons, starting with number 1. The debate will begin with a general debate on the entire draft budget. The detailed examination first presents the expenditure, then the revenue, then the general provisions of the detailed explanatory memorandum and the general arguments and finally the proposal for the application of the budget. A general discussion is first allowed for each section, followed by proposals for the section in numerical order. Since the proposals put forward are included in the Minutes, simply referring to the number of the proposal is sufficient. A statement of support shall be made immediately after the intervention of the proposer. Once the proposals have been made, they will be put to the vote. The figures and other items in the section for which no amendments have been proposed shall be deemed to have been adopted in accordance with the report of the Committee on Finance. Titles of revenue, general provisions of detailed explanatory notes and general arguments shall not be allowed to be the subject of a general discussion. Otherwise, the procedure is the same as when dealing with the main categories.
