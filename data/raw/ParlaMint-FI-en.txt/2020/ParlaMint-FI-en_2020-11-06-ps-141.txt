ParlaMint-FI_2020-11-06-ps-141.u1	The second item in the order of business will be presented for discussion. This is based on the report by the Committee on Legal Affairs and Citizens' Rights on LaVM 6/2020 vp. The debate was closed at the plenary session on 4 November 2020. In the debate, Antero Laukkanen, supported by Sari Tanus, has tabled a proposal under protest 1 and Sebastian Tynkkynen, supported by Mari Rantanen, has tabled a proposal under protest 2.
ParlaMint-FI_2020-11-06-ps-141.u2	Voting shall take the form of a machine vote in three voting groups. The first vote starts at 1 p.m. and ends at 1.20 p.m. The final group of the first vote will remain in the Chamber to vote as the first group of the second vote. The second vote starts at 1.20 p.m. and ends at 1.40 p.m. The representatives present at the beginning of the sitting shall vote first. Other representatives are invited to come to the Chamber through the back door through the State Hall. After voting in the Chamber, I would ask the representatives to leave the House's side doors and avoid meeting in the vicinity of the doors and in the round corridor. The progress of the vote can also be followed by an online transmission. I would also ask you to take account of the security gap during the vote and use face masks. Mr Tynkkysen's proposal is put to the vote against Mr Laukkanen's proposal and then against the successful report.
