In view of the urgency of the matter, the Council of Presidents proposes that the report be put on the table for the next plenary session, to be held today on 25 May 2020. The Council of Presidents proposes that the following procedure be followed for the sole examination of the third draft amending budget: The debate will begin today at the plenary session following this sitting on Monday 25 May 2020 with a general debate which will allow for a maximum of 5 minutes of pre-reserved speeches. Other interventions shall not exceed 3 minutes. In addition, the President may, in the order in which he is considering, issue replies. When there is a wish to hold a debate after the presentation of the draft amending budget report, it will be held today, exceptionally, without prior notice, after the half-hour debate. The amendments must be submitted to the Central Assembly no later than 2.30 p.m. on Monday 25 May 2020. The relevant votes will take place today on Monday, 25 May 2020. To the extent that no amendments to the third amending budget have been proposed, it shall be deemed to have been adopted in accordance with the report of the Committee on Finance. The procedure was adopted.
