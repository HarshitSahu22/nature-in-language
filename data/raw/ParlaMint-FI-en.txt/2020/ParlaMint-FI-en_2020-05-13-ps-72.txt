ParlaMint-FI_2020-05-13-ps-72.u2	Mr President! Ladies and gentlemen of Parliament! The present Government proposal proposes a temporary amendment to the Aliens Act. The amendment would extend the right of asylum seekers to work. The changes would concern the start and end of the right to work for the asylum seeker. Firstly, the limitation period for starting work would be abolished for applicants for international protection who, at the time of entry into force of the law, have an international protection case pending either before the Finnish Immigration Service or the Administrative Court, but not yet the right to work, because the application has not passed the 3 or 6-month period of validity laid down in the current regulation. These asylum seekers would have the right to work as soon as the law came into force. The amendment would concern both the first applications and the renewal applications. Secondly, the duration of the right to work would be extended so that the termination of the right to work would not be linked to the enforceability of the decision on the removal of a foreigner. The right to work would be tied to receiving reception services. According to the proposal, the right to work would continue despite an enforceable removal decision for as long as a foreigner is provided with reception services provided by the Reception Act. This amendment would apply both to asylum seekers who have been removed as described above and to applicants who, at the time of entry into force of the law, had the right to work at the end of the qualifying period. The changes concern work in agriculture, forestry, horticulture and fisheries. In the case of other tasks, the existing qualifying periods would continue to be respected. In the case of other tasks, the right to work would also end, in accordance with the existing regulation, with the enforceability of the removal decision. The aim of the proposal is to increase the supply of labour, in particular in those jobs which are essential for security of supply or for the functioning of the labour market. The proposed amendments aim to contribute to the achievement of the security of supply targets and to reduce the economic damage caused by the restriction of border traffic by increasing labour supply in critical sectors of primary production where the lack of foreign labour due to exceptional circumstances causes significant disadvantages. The law proposed in this proposal is intended to enter into force as soon as possible and would be in force until the end of October this year. The proposal is closely linked to the exceptional circumstances of the corona epidemic, in particular the limitation of border traffic and the resulting problems of access to seasonal labour. Due to exceptional circumstances, the entry of foreign labour has decreased significantly and in recent years foreign seasonal labour has been an important source of labour in many primary production sectors. The challenge now is to have sufficient labour in agriculture, forestry, horticulture and fisheries sectors, especially those in need of seasonal workers.
