ParlaMint-FI_2018-05-08-ps-47.u2	Mr President! When assessing the government's employment policy, it is good to go back to the beginning of May 2015, when the current government agreed on its programme. As you will recall, the economic and employment situation in our country was disastrous at that time. As a result of several years of non-existent or negative economic growth during the previous government term, employment had fallen by some 40,000 persons and public finances in decline. The options were clearly higher employment or additional cuts in billions and tax increases. We made a choice of values and chose to pursue a high level of employment. The central objective of the Government Programme was to increase the number of employed persons by 110,000 persons and increase the employment rate to 72%. This was done primarily because without high employment we cannot finance our current welfare society in a situation where our population is ageing at a rapid pace. The next government is also faced with the same choice of values, because the employment rate of 72% is not enough, but the target must be 75 and even above it. This is a good thing to remember when billions of election promises are made. This choice is consistently reflected both through the Government Programme and in the subsequent employment packages. Last time this consistency was recognised by STTK’s Chief Economist Ralf Sund in an interview with Suomenmaa, although he did not agree with all the individual decisions.
