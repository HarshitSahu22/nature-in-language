More specifically, I think it would be good if, as of January 1, 2018, online platforms were required to automatically report the income of their users when they are individuals. In the current state of the law, this obligation must come into force on January 1, 2019, but I think it is technically possible to advance this one-year date, since since 2016 the platforms must send each taxpayer a statement of the revenues collected during the tax year. They should therefore be able to send the same information to the tax administration.
ParlaMint-FR_2017-10-20-O1019.u557	What is the opinion of the committee?
ParlaMint-FR_2017-10-20-O1019.u558	This amendment will not necessarily have the same fate as Mrs El Haïry, if only because it was not adopted by the Finance Committee. The date of entry into force of this scheme on 1 January 2019 had been set in view of the difficulties that would result from its application. The identification of the persons concerned, in particular, is hampered by the use of pseudonyms by the users of these platforms. Moving ahead with this one-year date will not solve these difficulties. I therefore maintain the committee's unfavorable opinion.
