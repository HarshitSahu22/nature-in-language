In order to do so, an exceptional, conditional reduction for the determination of the net taxable surplus value of both income tax and social tax shall be introduced for the divestitures committed from 1 January 2018 to 31 December 2020 of building land or built real property intended for demolition for the purpose of building housing. This exceptional reduction is conditional on the assignee's undertaking to build premises for the dwelling, subject to the density of the housing supply, within four years of the date of acquisition. The rate of the allowance, fixed at 70 per cent, may be increased to 85 per cent when the transferee also undertakes to carry out and complete mostly social or intermediate housing. Furthermore, in order to encourage the construction of social housing throughout the national territory, the existing exemptions for direct or indirect transfers to social housing bodies are also extended until 31 December 2020. Finally, since the divestitures of the right of elevation contribute to the densification objective pursued by the Government, their exemption for real estate gains is also extended for a period of three years, until 31 December 2020. The cost of this major effort by the Government in support of the supply shock is estimated at €170 million in 2018, including €60 million in income tax and €110 million in social contributions. It would be increased to €180 million in 2019 and 2020.
