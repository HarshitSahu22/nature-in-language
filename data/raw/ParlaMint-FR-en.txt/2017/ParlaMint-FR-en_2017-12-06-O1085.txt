ParlaMint-FR_2017-12-06-O1085.u683	This amendment is intended to provide a legal basis for the exemption applicable to real estate capital gains resulting from divestitures made in connection with the exercise of a right of abandonment. Currently, the General Tax Code provides for an exemption from capital gains where the debtor is affected by an expropriation procedure decided by the public authority. The General Tax Code provides that this exemption from real estate capital gains is conditional since the obligor must re-employ expropriation compensation in the acquisition or reconstruction of a new building within 12 months. The amendment is simply intended to extend this exemption scheme on condition of reinvestment to the abandonment procedures provided for in the Urban Planning Code, since this code provides that the owner of a real estate may abandon its property to the public authority, in practice forcing it to buy it when it is affected by several procedures: when the local urban plan – the PLU – reserves the real estate for public works or social housing, when the property is integrated into a concerted development area – ZAC –, when the authority issuing the building permit opposes a stay of proceedings and when the property is integrated into a loan. In all these cases, it is planned to amend the General Tax Code in order to extend the scheme of exemptions under conditions of re-employment provided for expropriations. The cost of the scheme is estimated at around EUR 15 million per year.
