ParlaMint-FR_2017-11-17-O1062.u56	Amendment No 1662 concerns intermediate housing. The Law for the Economic Development of Overseas Countries of 2009, known as "LODEOM", has opened up the challenge of building so-called "intermediate rent" housing. Since July 1, 2014, this provision has taken the form of a tax credit. These intermediate housing units target middle-class households, with modest incomes but exceeding the social minimums and resource ceilings of conventional social housing. It is therefore an offer that creates real possibilities for residential routes, by offering rent levels capped by decree, between those in the social park and those in the private park. In the overseas regions, intermediate rent housing is subject to annual quotas, depending on the production of social housing in the previous year. There is no waiting list or allocation commission for these dwellings, which are typically marketed in accordance with the criteria of resource ceilings. Operators, as prudent investors, have every interest in renting these homes as quickly as possible. The aim here is to take account of ultramarine specificities and to ensure consistency in the time taken to rent social housing. Amendments Nos 1661, 1663 and 1660 are also intended to better align the tax credit scheme with the characteristics of social housing production overseas. Indeed, the legal framework applied so far is the result of the development of challenges for productive investment in the industrial sector. However, after several years of practice, the gap appears clearly and places a permanent risk on the financing of housing operations. In this way, all operators deplore the six-month deadline, which is set for the commissioning of productive equipment, which is too short in the case of social housing. Amendment No 1660 seeks to extend the lease period from six to twelve months. This extension is all the more necessary since it often happens that dwellings, although finished, cannot be offered for rent due to the lack of connection to the electricity grid or to the water network, regardless of the will of the social housing organisation. I would also like to draw your attention to the fact that these deadlines are all the more important as they must be respected by all the programmes; Failing this, it is a whole process of producing proofs that is more or less lengthy, with the latent risk of calling into question the financing granted under the tax credits. This amendment would not generate any additional tax expenditure. In order to avoid any semantic ambiguity, I am prepared to delete the second preambular paragraph, where it is proposed to replace the words "given in" with the words "proposed to". Amendment No 1661 concerns rental sales. It tends to extend the period of housing marketing from six to 18 months to take account of the requirements of sale. The rental-sales scheme, which allows the acquisition of social housing by its occupants, comprises a rental phase of a minimum duration of one year and a purchase phase materialised by the signing of a reservation contract, equivalent to a sales compromise, with the various steps linked to it: validation by a notary, search for a loan from a banking agency, obligation to waive the right of pre-emption of the municipalities. Not all of these deadlines are currently taken into account. Amendment No 1663 is a fallback amendment designed to take into account not the accession lease, as at present, but the preliminary reservation contract, which must be signed within 12 months, with the aim of taking into account the incompressible deadlines and the procedures inherent in the rental sale.
