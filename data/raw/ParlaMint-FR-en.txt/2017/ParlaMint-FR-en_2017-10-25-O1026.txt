ParlaMint-FR_2017-10-25-O1026.u666	There are 200 casinos in France, which have been subject since 1996 to the payment of two CSGs. As the only legal entities thus imposed, they are an amazing exception. The base used for the calculation of the first contribution is a fraction of the gross proceeds of games of automatic game machines, which account for 95 % of their turnover. The collection of this tax began in 1996, at an initial rate of 3.4 per cent, which was subsequently raised twice, in 1997 to 7.5 per cent and in 2005 to 9.5 per cent, which is still very high. This amazing exception is of course added to a revenue levy made on games, so that the French casino market is currently the most taxed in Europe! The second sample directly concerns casino players. Indeed, all gains above 1500 euros are subject from the first euro to a 12% levy, while nothing like this exists for a similar gain made with La Française des jeux or the Pari Mutuel Urbain. The increase in the CSG provided for in Article 7 is likely to cause very serious harm to the casino industry. The increase in its rate of 1.7% will cost them directly several tens of millions of euros. This will result in considerable job losses and even outright closures, especially among small casinos that are already struggling to achieve balance and represent about 35% of French casinos. The direct destruction of jobs is expected to amount to about 1,000, mostly in isolated territories, where the smallest establishments are located. This measure will have a second effect: the inevitable decline in customers due to the increase in the rate applied to earnings above 1500 euros. The increase in the levy will only worsen the distortion of competition already benefiting other legal or illegal gambling offers, as well as casinos located in neighbouring countries, competing with border establishments. This initiative therefore plunges this whole sector of activity into a very dangerous spiral. That is why Amendment No 875 proposes to abandon it. Amendment No 877 is a variant of the previous one. It deals only with the CSG on earnings, which it proposes to remove the 1.7% increase in order to avoid the relocation of customers.
