These are intended to exempt from social levies income from French source capital received by non-residents: CSG on real estate income and investment income, social levy and CRDS on the same bases. The scope of income subject to social levies was expanded to include property income and capital gains by the second amending finance law for 2012, at the beginning of the last quinquennium. Three years later, as has been said, the Court of Justice of the European Union delivered a judgment, known as 'de Ruyter', in which it held that persons affiliated with a social security scheme in one Member State of the European Union cannot be subject to social levies intended to finance contributory social security schemes in another Member State. In practical terms, for example, a French resident in the Netherlands and affiliated with the Dutch Social Security cannot be subject, on his income from French sources, to levies from which the proceeds would go to the basic social insurance schemes, since he does not benefit from the benefits paid by these schemes. In the 2016 PLFSS, the Government proposed to the Parliament, which accepted it, the following solution: rather than exempting non-residents from social levies – which would have cost 250 million euros and meant that French residents in the Netherlands and collecting income from their capital in France would no longer have had to pay levies on them, unlike those residing in France – the proceeds of these levies were allocated, still within the social sphere, to non-contributory bodies, including the Old Age Solidarity Fund. These organizations, which are mainly tax-funded, do not provide benefits in return for taxes collected. In this way, they depart from the insurgency logic of the basic plans, according to which the payment of contributions is entitled to benefits. The three amendments contest the merits of this solution, considering that only the exemption of non-residents would allow full compliance with the case law of the Court of Justice of the European Union. The doctrine of the Government and the new majority has not changed, nor has the doctrine of the rapporteur and the commission. We gave an unfavorable opinion.
