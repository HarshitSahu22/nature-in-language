According to magistrate Marie-Christine Le Boursicot, the consequence is major for the child; His birth parents will be able to challenge his adoption at any time, including years later, and the children concerned will find themselves at the heart of painful conflicts. For these two reasons, it is essential to revisit the removal of the collection of parental consent from birth to adoption.
