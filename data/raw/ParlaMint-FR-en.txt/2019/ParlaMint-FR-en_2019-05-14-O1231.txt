ParlaMint-FR_2019-05-14-O1231.u522	The transformation of human resources policies necessarily requires a more strategic focus whose objectives will be more focused on the real issues identified among all the issues addressed in social dialogue forums. I propose that the Single Social Report become the first forum for reflection on the management guidelines that will ultimately determine the multi-year human resources strategy. It is developed annually and will therefore serve as a valuable information and decision support tool for each jurisdiction, community or institution. The development of these management lines in the area of mobility, promotion and valorisation of career paths will be based on the strengths that will be derived from the qualitative and quantitative analysis of overall strategic priorities, or at times specific to each structure, through the study of aggregated statistical data based on the social database. The main issues identified for the architecture of this report include the issues discussed in the social committees, in particular those on the functioning and organisation of services, general conditions of employment, mobility and policies on occupational equality and anti-discrimination. It will also replace the availability and disability reports on each side of the public service. Without duplicating the economic and social database established in the private sector by the Act of 14 June 2013 on job security, the amendment provides for the development of a social database among public employers with both quantitative and qualitative elements, which will provide an opportunity to refine the analysis of the composition and balances of each level of structure. By identifying a clear and broad social landscape for each jurisdiction, community or institution concerned, this social database will first strengthen the identification of short-term strategic issues for each entity and, second, the efficiency of steering management guidelines and longer-term human resources policies, for example by enabling indicators to be developed. Its dissemination will, thirdly, facilitate the transparency of the human resources management expected of all actors in the social dialogue, in real time. This base can be used, under certain conditions, by staff representatives, which can significantly improve the quality and effectiveness of social dialogue. Its implementation will ultimately lead to a better productivity of the management services of the public administration while improving the reliability of data and returns through standardized, automated and centralized digital statistical returns. Thus, the single social report drawn up by administrations, communities and institutions will focus on strategic issues, without excluding the treatment of specific issues for each employer, or limiting the access of trade union organisations to information they may wish to extract from the social database for the preparation of meetings. The scope, content, conditions and modalities for the development of the single social report and the social database by administrations, communities and their institutions in the public service will be specified by decree. It is necessary to specify by regulation the balance between, on the one hand, the means required to deal with the diversity of the nature, situations and sizes of employers and, on the other hand, the objectives of access to information and the harmonisation of management which are aimed at both the single social relationship and the database.
