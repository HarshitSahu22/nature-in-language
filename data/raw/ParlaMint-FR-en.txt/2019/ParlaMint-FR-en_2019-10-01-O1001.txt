ParlaMint-FR_2019-10-01-O1001.u1	In accordance with the first paragraph of article 28 of the Constitution, I declare the 2019-2020 ordinary session open.
ParlaMint-FR_2019-10-01-O1001.u2	The next item is the appointment of the six Vice-Presidents, the three Quaestors and the twelve Secretaries of the National Assembly. Pursuant to Rule 10(3) of the Rules of Procedure, the group presidents met last night. They reached agreement on the distribution of posts and the order in which the candidates were presented. The presidents of the groups La France insoumée and of the Democratic and Republican Left, however, regretted that the Rules of Procedure did not allow all groups to have a position of Vice-President.
ParlaMint-FR_2019-10-01-O1001.u3	That's right!
ParlaMint-FR_2019-10-01-O1001.u4	We take note of that position. In accordance with Rule 10(8) of the Rules of Procedure, I declare, in the order in which they are presented, Vice-Presidents: Ms. Annie Genevard; Mr. Hugues Renson; Mrs. Laetitia Saint-Paul; Mr. Sylvain Waserman; Mr. Marc Le Fur; [[Applause on the benches of the LaREM, LR, MODEM, SOC, UDI-AGIR, LT, GDR and non-attached Members]] Mr. David Habib. I proclaim the Quaestors: Mr. Florian Bachelier; Ms. Laurianne Rossi; [[Applause on the benches of the LaREM, LR, MODEM, SOC, UDI-AGIR, LT, GDR and non-attached Members]] Mr. Eric Ciotti. Finally, I proclaim secretaries: Ms. Bérangère Abba; Ms. Sophie Auconia; Mr. Alexis Corbière; Mrs. Bérangère Couillard; Mr. Yannick Favennec Becot; Ms. Véronique Hammerer; Ms. Laure de La Raudière; Mr. Jean François Mbaye; Ms. Sophie Mette; Mr. Remy Rebeyrotte; Mr. Gabriel Serville; [[Applause on the benches of the LaREM, LR, MODEM, SOC, UDI-AGIR, LT, GDR and non-attached Members]] Mr. Bertrand Sorre. The Bureau of the National Assembly is therefore constituted. Its composition will be notified to the President of the Republic, the Prime Minister, the President of the Senate and published in the Official Gazette.
ParlaMint-FR_2019-10-01-O1001.u5	Next meeting, this afternoon, at 3 p.m.: Tribute to President Jacques Chirac; Questions to the Government. The meeting is adjourned.
