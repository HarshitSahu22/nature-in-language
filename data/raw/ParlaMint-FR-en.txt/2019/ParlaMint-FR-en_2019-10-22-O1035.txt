It establishes a compensation for the euro, on whose behalf the exemptions were offset by budgetary allocations from 1994 to 2006. In 2005, the Organic Law perpetuates this principle and introduces the possibility of non-compensation, which leads all social security financing laws, from this year onwards, to endorse uncompensated measures.
