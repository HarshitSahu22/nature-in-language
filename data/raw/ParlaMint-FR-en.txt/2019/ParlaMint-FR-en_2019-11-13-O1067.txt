ParlaMint-FR_2019-11-13-O1067.u460	The various arrangements provided for in Articles 199a (B) and (C) and 244c (W) of the General Tax Code are designed to introduce tax aid for overseas investment. In particular, these articles make the granting of tax aid conditional on the formality of the deposit of annual accounts with the trade and company register provided for in the law of commercial companies. Such an obligation raises many questions and leads to a high level of legal and fiscal uncertainty, as well as deep financial uncertainty for investors, who may be subject to heavy tax recalls if the operator has failed to comply with this legal obligation several years earlier. In particular, investors do not have the capacity to verify whether the operators have properly deposited their accounts at the court registry within one month of the approval of their annual accounts, over an indefinite period of time. At the same time, the laws in force make the tax aid conditional on the deposit of the accounts on the date of completion of the investment, whereas the legislator has fixed the operative event of the tax aid for the investment to be put into operation of the investment in question. The aim of this amendment is therefore to clarify this condition for the application of the investment tax aid in order to remove all uncertainties and reassure investors of the granting of the tax aid provided for in the legislation in force. For the sake of consistency and accuracy, the proposed amendment makes the tax aid conditional on the deposit of the annual accounts before the date of commissioning, while specifying that these are the annual accounts for the last financial year ended on that date.
