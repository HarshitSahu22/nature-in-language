It would exercise relatively extensive control over these companies in order to ensure that the rates proposed to publishers are reasonable and non-discriminatory. With regard to the latter, the Authority would lay down the rules on their establishment, as well as the conditions for the remuneration of their staff. The bill is also concerned with the digital dissemination of the press. Indeed, given the importance of digital in the dissemination of the press, whether it be search engines, the sharing of press content through social networks, the applications of digital kiosks included in telephone subscriptions or the widgets of our mobile phones, it is necessary to transpose into the digital world the principles of the distribution of the press necessary for democratic debate. These criteria legitimize a particular treatment, which requires an assortment negotiated at interprofessional level. For them, the requirement in terms of coverage is less strong than for the IPG – since it implies an interprofessional agreement between newspaper merchants and publishers – but they are guaranteed a fairly wide dissemination. On the other hand, non-CPPAP titles are voluntary between newspaper merchants – or more widely the broadcasters – and publishers. This allows us to regain management flexibility.
