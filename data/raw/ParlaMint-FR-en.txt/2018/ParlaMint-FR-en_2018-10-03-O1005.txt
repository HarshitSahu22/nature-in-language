If we believe that the future company ADP does not undertake these investments, the State will therefore be able to compel it to do so, which is not the case at present. The State may also set the level of performance to be achieved in terms of public airport service. If we believe that the necessary diligences are not fulfilled, we can therefore impose them. Finally, I repeat as clearly as possible, the State will fix the amount of the fees every five years through the regulatory agreement. Either ADP and the State agree on this amount, or we consider it excessive, in which case it will be the responsibility of the State to fix it: this is the major difference with the privatisation of motorways, for which, by definition, no regular tariff revision, in this case every five years, was foreseen. Under the terms of reference, the State will also have the power of authorisation. To answer the question posed by Eric Woerth during the general discussion, it is the state that will allow – or not – changes in control. If the shareholder wishes, at some point, to change control and put his stake at stake, it will be up to the state to decide: this will avoid the entry of undesirable investors into ADP's capital. It is still the State which will allow investment transactions exceeding a certain threshold of area or amount. It is he who will authorize transfers and creations of security rights. Finally, the third power conferred on it by the terms of reference, the State will have a supervisory power, since a government commissioner will sit on the board of directors. It will give its approval to the operational leader in charge of all functions related to platform operation, security and safety. I stress this because it means that, in addition to the regal missions still carried out by the national police, the gendarmerie and customs at the sites of Paris Airport, the State will have, on all the security arrangements implemented by this company, the possibility of approval and control. In addition to all this, the Court of Auditors guarantees control. These are the key elements of the terms of reference, which I assure you will be forwarded to the national representation as soon as its drafting is completed.
