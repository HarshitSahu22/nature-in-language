In order to consider the care of the most vulnerable in long-term residences, this amendment includes health care beds, nursing beds and places in social hotel homes – RHVS. On the other hand, emergency accommodation centres would be excluded because their places are not permanent. [[Applause on the benches of the LR group]] By adopting this amendment, the House would make a gesture for the municipalities which set up such structures for the accommodation of homeless or extremely vulnerable people.
ParlaMint-FR_2018-06-06-O1256.u775	What is the opinion of the committee?
ParlaMint-FR_2018-06-06-O1256.u776	As you said, Member, the SRU Act already includes accommodation and reintegration centres in its quota, as these structures are closer to housing, unlike emergency accommodation centres and HRVS. The SRU law already includes all social residences and residential housing, adding temporary, non-permanent housing structures would not correspond to demand. We are therefore making a request for withdrawal. Otherwise, an unfavorable opinion.
ParlaMint-FR_2018-06-06-O1256.u777	What is the Government's opinion?
ParlaMint-FR_2018-06-06-O1256.u778	Same opinion, for the same reasons.
ParlaMint-FR_2018-06-06-O1256.u779	Is the floor open to Mrs Martine Wonner?
ParlaMint-FR_2018-06-06-O1256.u780	There is confusion, because I said that the amendment was not intended to include emergency accommodation centres. It aims to add only authorized structures, perennials, that I know well because I took care of it when I was managing the SAMU Social de Paris. [[Applause on the benches of the LR group]] I therefore do not withdraw my amendment.
