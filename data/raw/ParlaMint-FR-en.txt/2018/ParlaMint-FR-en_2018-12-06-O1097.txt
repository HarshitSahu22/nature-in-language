Thus, the close helper who uses it loses part of his salary. He must choose between accompanying the aided person but losing part of his salary, or continuing to work to complete the end of the month but being consumed by the guilt of not being able to accompany a loved one in difficulty. This article is positive. It provides that close care leave will finally be compensated, which would be a major step forward. We will vote on it.
