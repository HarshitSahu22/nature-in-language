Article 17 of the Covenant states: "The parties shall refrain from authorizing activities or adopting standards which have the effect of reducing the overall level of environmental protection guaranteed by the law in force. » This is the principle of non-regression, and that is why it makes sense to include it in the Constitution: it is a constitutional lock that will prevent the legislator tomorrow from adapting laws but from reducing their level of ambition for the protection of the environment. This principle is therefore very important. If the constitutional review is to make progress in the Constitution for ecology, the environment and the climate, it is the principle of non-regression. I will therefore vote for this amendment, even if it amends the Environment Charter; we will return to this in debates on Article 1.
