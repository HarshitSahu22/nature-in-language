ParlaMint-FR_2018-10-01-O1001.u1	[[Applause]] In accordance with the first paragraph of article 28 of the Constitution, I declare the 2018-2019 ordinary session open.
ParlaMint-FR_2018-10-01-O1001.u2	[[Applause on the benches of the LR group and on a few benches of the LaREM and GDR groups. – Mrs Caroline Fiat also applauds]] I received today, from the Minister of State, Minister of the Interior, a communication informing me that yesterday, Sunday, September 30, 2018, Mr.Jean-Luc Poudroux was elected Member of Parliament for the seventh constituency of La Réunion.
ParlaMint-FR_2018-10-01-O1001.u3	The next item is the appointment of the six Vice-Presidents, the three Quaestors and the twelve Secretaries of the National Assembly. The presidents of the groups who met today at twelve o'clock did not reach agreement on the distribution of posts within the Bureau. For the functions of Vice-President, I have before me the nomination of: Ms Carole Bureau-Bonnard; Mr. Hugues Renson; Mr. Sylvain Waserman; Ms. Annie Genevard; Mr. Marc Le Fur; Mr. Maurice Leroy; [[Applause]] Mrs. Sabine Rubin. As the number of candidates exceeds the number of seats to be filled, a vote should be taken. I would remind you that the vote is secret. In the first two ballots, in the order of votes, the candidates obtained an absolute majority of the votes cast. In the third round, the relative majority is sufficient. In the event of an equality of votes, the oldest shall be elected. Handwritten bulletins are valid. However, in order to facilitate the work of our colleagues, printed bulletins are available in the rooms adjacent to the Chamber. These ballots must be placed in the ballot box under wrapping. It will be carried out under the supervision of four incumbent deputy returning officers and two alternate deputy returning officers, whose names I shall draw by lot.
ParlaMint-FR_2018-10-01-O1001.u4	Mr. Francois Jolivet, Mr. Jean-Carles Grelier, Mr. Stéphane Viry and Ms. Trastour-Isnart are appointed full deputy returning officers. Mr. Jean-Louis Thiériot and Mr. Thierry Michels were appointed alternate deputy deputy deputy deputy deputy returning officers. We shall proceed to the first ballot, which will be open for 45 minutes. I open the ballot, which is announced in the National Assembly. The voting will be closed at 15.5 p.m.
