It is by giving control and decision-making power over the whole that we can give meaning to this election and improve participation in the European elections. [[Applause on the banks of the MODEM group and on some benches of the LaREM group]] We are therefore in favour of this text, even though it does not represent the European reform necessary to reinvigorate the European project, as the text aims to do. This will require profound ideas and structural changes. That is, in a few words, what one Member who believes deeply in the European Union wanted to say.
