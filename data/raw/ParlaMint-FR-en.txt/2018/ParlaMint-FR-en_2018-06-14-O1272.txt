That is why the amendment is intended to supplement the first sentence of paragraph 6 with the words "or in the event of the death of a master apprenticeship employer", which would limit these specific cases to a single-person enterprise, since such cases obviously do not arise in the context of companies, since the apprentice then deals with several persons. This year I have seen two cases of this type: it seems to me very important to find a solution to this absence of break-up, because it poses as much a problem for the young person, who no longer has a master of apprenticeships or training, as for the employer's family.
