ParlaMint-FR_2018-10-16-O1019.u670	This amendment concerns the method of indexing the exemption ceiling of the restaurant ticket. For half a century, the restaurant title now enjoyed by more than 4 million employees remains a strong symbol of the social dialogue between employees and employers. This original system, which was endorsed by the French, is also a driving force for the economy and employment thanks to its strong multiplier effect. Twenty-three additional beneficiaries correspond to a job created in the catering sector. By the favourable tax system granted to it, the State is entering a virtuous circle, especially since the additional revenue for it and for social schemes is estimated at some 870 million euros net per year. It must be noted, however, that this social advantage no longer fully plays its role today. In fact, over the last seven years, French employees benefiting from a restaurant title have seen their purchasing power dedicated to catering erode very significantly. While consumer price indices in the food and food industries increased by 7 and 15 per cent, the ceiling on the employer contribution of restaurant titles remained almost stable over this period: more than 2.5 per cent. Thus, in order to maintain the social vocation of the restaurant title while stimulating consumption, the indexation of the ceiling for exemption from the employer's contribution should be reviewed in a way that is more in line with its social mission. This amendment therefore provides for indexation based on that of the social security ceiling, as is the case today in the calculation of the ceiling for the exemption of other benefits for social workers, such as gift certificates.
