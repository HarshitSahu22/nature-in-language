ParlaMint-FR_2021-07-07-E1005.u25	I'm speaking on behalf of Caroline Janvier. The exercise of parental authority, although inherent in parenting, is accompanied by three duties: to protect the child, to maintain him or her and to ensure his or her upbringing. Parents must act in the child's best interests and, as far as their age and maturity permit, involve the child in decisions affecting the child. Where a child is placed, the division of powers between the holders of parental authority and the service or third party to whom the child is entrusted is based on the distinction between so-called customary and so-called non-routine acts. The usual acts, related to the day-to-day care of the child, are the responsibility of the institution or individual to which the child has been entrusted. The non-routine acts, i.e. the most important decisions for the child, which cannot be regarded as ordinary acts, remain the responsibility of the holders of parental authority. In the case of an educational assistance measure, parents therefore continue to exercise all the attributes of parental authority that are not irreconcilable with the measure, except where the child judge, in the child's interest and in exceptional circumstances, allows the child's guardians to perform certain unusual acts. The purpose of article 2, which we are considering, is to make the day-to-day lives of children and their guardians more fluid by giving the child judge greater flexibility to delegate to the child's guardians non-routine acts, particularly when parents are abusively refusing or negligent to their child. It is also in the interest of extending this procedure, which remains exceptional, to cases where the holders of parental authority are prosecuted or sentenced, even in a non-final manner, for crimes or offences committed against the child. This provision will enable the guardian of the child victim to have the power to make decisions to protect him or her as best as possible and to create the most protective environment for him or her. For all these reasons, the La République en Marche group is satisfied with the progress proposed by the Government and will vote in favour of this article as drafted.
