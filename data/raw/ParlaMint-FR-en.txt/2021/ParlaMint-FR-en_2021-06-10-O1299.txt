As a market place, Back Market – call it by name – is an intermediary that connects sellers and buyers, but it does not sell media itself. As this platform is not an importer within the meaning of the IP Code, it is therefore not liable to the SPC. There is no distortion of competition at this level. Another argument I have just heard is that the subjecting of the repackaged media would result in double payment of the remuneration. The purpose of the SPC is to compensate for the damage suffered by rightholders resulting from the making of private copies.
