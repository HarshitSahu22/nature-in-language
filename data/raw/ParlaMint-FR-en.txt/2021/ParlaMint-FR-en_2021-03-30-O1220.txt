For this new remedy, the proposed law provides for a precise procedure, which is organised in three main stages. First step: the filing of an application and the judge's decision on its admissibility. Second stage: where the application is admissible, the judge shall have checks carried out and shall receive the observations of the prison administration; In the light of these facts, the Committee decides on the merits of the application and, where appropriate, informs the prison administration of the conditions of detention which it considers unworthy, by setting a time limit for its termination. The third and final stage is only possible: if the prison administration fails to resolve the problem within the time limit, the judge himself takes a decision to remedy the situation and put an end to the unworthy conditions of detention; It may be, depending on the circumstances, a transfer, release, or adjustment of sentence. It is of course specified that the judge's decision may be appealed either to the President of the Chamber of Investigation or to the President of the Chamber of Enforcement. Senators have added, with great relevance, that this appeal may also relate to the decision on admissibility of the motion. We have clarified the deadlines for each step.
