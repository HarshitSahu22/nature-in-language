Let us hear you well: your measures are indispensable; We support the financing of partial unemployment to the tune of EUR 24 billion, we welcome the extension to many very small companies of carry-overs, state-guaranteed loans and other specific efforts, we support the provision of EUR 20 billion for state-funded strategic enterprises. But, in the course of the debates, speaking seems useless, to paraphrase the singer and poet who left us last night. Even in the face of this major crisis, we do not have the same software or grammar to think about the economy, especially that of tomorrow. No answer, or so vague, concerning the sectors that will benefit from these 20 billion: no environmental conditions for granting aid, no certainty about its social conditions, refusal to charge taxes on capital as well as tax niches harmful to the environment, no revalorisation of wages for caregivers, no compulsory premium for second-line workers, no masks! Even the care of the funeral has not received your consent. It is really too difficult for us to vote for a text inspired by the world before, which insists on saving it or procrastine instead of transforming its matrix. A month ago, we could give you the benefit of the doubt; This morning, at 3:10 p.m., there is no longer any doubt: the group La France insoumise will vote against your project.
