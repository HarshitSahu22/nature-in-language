The purpose of this amendment is therefore to introduce an exceptional contribution on life insurance stocks. It still has a limit: only outstanding amounts exceeding 30000 euros are taken into account – the amount withheld because it is precisely that of the average amount outstanding in life insurance. In addition, 1% of insured persons alone own one quarter of the life insurance outstanding, or EUR 425 billion. Such a measure could still bring some billions back to the state and fund part of the support plan in solidarity.
