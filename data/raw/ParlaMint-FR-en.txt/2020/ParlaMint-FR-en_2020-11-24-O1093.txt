It is thus proposed that the reduction of the contribution due to an undertaking under the safeguard clause – up to 20% – be adjusted, depending on the undertaking's participation in the regulation of expenditure. An amendment adopted by the Senate aims to raise the minimum rate of reduction to 10 per cent, while the Government proposes to set it at 5 per cent. It seems appropriate to leave a sufficiently large magnitude – between 5% and 20% – to the reduction provided for under an undertaking's contribution to the conventional price reduction negotiations, in order to have a clear incentive for manoeuvre: a company actively participating in the negotiations would thus benefit from a much larger consideration than a company with a lower participation.
