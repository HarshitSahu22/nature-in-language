They are particularly aware of the current situation of enterprises. A general measure would not necessarily be bearable given the amounts involved. Nevertheless, the common law operates and offers targeted measures to companies that face special cash flow difficulties.

This proposal is therefore also a measure of tax justice. The Government had said that support for households, especially the most modest ones, was not its priority because it wanted to pursue supply policy and give priority to business support. Reducing VAT on basic necessities is, on the other hand, a measure in favour of purchasing power, which will benefit the most modest, since they devote almost all their income to the purchase of this type of goods.
