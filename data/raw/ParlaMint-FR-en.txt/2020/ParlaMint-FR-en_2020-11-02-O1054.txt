In order to deal with their financial losses, which have also resulted in an estimated gain of over €180 million for social security, the amendment aims to provide them with aid of €16 million. These institutions, which have not had to regret any clusters, have introduced protocols validated by the DGS – Directorate General of Health – which have had exceptional results. I would like to remind you that the thermal establishments are located in small rural communes, where they provide a lot of activity. It is therefore important to maintain them and to accompany them in this difficult time, especially since, for the time being, nothing has been done by the Government.
ParlaMint-FR_2020-11-02-O1054.u333	What is the opinion of the committee?
ParlaMint-FR_2020-11-02-O1054.u334	This amendment proposes to indirectly support the spa sector with a credit of EUR 16 million. It has not been discussed in committee and I am therefore voting in my personal capacity. As real as the question is, the proposed medium is not the right one. The thermal sector suffers harshly from the consequences of the health context, as I can see in my department of Orne, with the decline in the activity of the cures of Bagnoles-de-l'Orne, but the organisation of support for the economic activities affected by the health context falls under the mission "Plan of Recovery" created in this PLF 2021, and not the mission "Health". I have criticized enough in committee the inclusion of appropriations from the traditional budget missions in the "Relaunch Plan" mission so as not to support an amendment that would include in a traditional budget mission an appropriation under the "Relaunch Plan" mission. Unfavorable opinion, therefore, even though I am well aware of the difficulties that thermal establishments are experiencing.
ParlaMint-FR_2020-11-02-O1054.u335	What is the Government's opinion?
ParlaMint-FR_2020-11-02-O1054.u336	I agree with the rapporteur. The PLFSS adopted at first reading created an additional exemption scheme for companies once again affected by the restrictive measures decided this autumn. In addition, during the crisis, health insurance allowed cash advances for the most difficult thermal establishments that asked for them: for example, to be paid on invoices up to 40% of the 2019 monthly revenue estimate for establishments hosting less than 4000 curists per year – roughly what you described – and with a fixed charge rate of at least 40%. To date, 17 institutions have applied for this aid, amounting to EUR 1.9 million.

ParlaMint-FR_2020-11-02-O1054.u339	Mr Eric Coquerel has the floor to support Amendment No. 1941.
ParlaMint-FR_2020-11-02-O1054.u340	The aim of this appeal amendment is to learn from the crisis and the fragility of our health system, particularly in the production of medical products. It aims to create a new programme entitled "Public drug centre", which will be abounded for the first year to the tune of €10 million. We found during the health crisis that we had been very close to breaking up the supply of absolutely essential medicines, especially for resuscitation, such as curare, morphine, propofol or midalozam, as well as for anesthetics and hypnotics, whose demand is growing. Such a structure would therefore solve supply problems, but above all to answer questions related to the cost-effectiveness of these drugs for the commercial and private sector, which do not consider it sufficient for these products to become a priority. Since the public interest and public health are at stake, it is up to us to make up for this lack and not to let the market decide on the supply of medicines.
ParlaMint-FR_2020-11-02-O1054.u341	What is the opinion of the committee?
ParlaMint-FR_2020-11-02-O1054.u342	This amendment seeks to create a public drug centre, with the aim of strengthening the sovereignty and independence of our health care system. It has not been examined by the committee and I am therefore voting in my personal capacity. Sovereignty and independence are words that also belong to my political vocabulary and I am therefore quite sensitive to this amendment, which emphasises real difficulties in the supply of medicines, but if, on principle, I share your concern, the only thing that keeps me from issuing a favourable opinion is that I do not understand well what justifies this figure of 10 million euros: why 5 million, or 50? This is essential because, without a precise financial analysis of the need to meet, we do not know where we are going. This point should therefore be explored; Perhaps we should establish a National Assembly fact-finding mission, which would provide more information on this subject, on the nature of the Government's responses to it and, above all, on the financial aspect of the proposals to be made. Behind the notion of the "public pole of the drug", we can actually put a lot of things. I therefore express a wisdom opinion, but I repeat that the issue raised is interesting and deserves further consideration.
ParlaMint-FR_2020-11-02-O1054.u343	What is the Government's opinion?
ParlaMint-FR_2020-11-02-O1054.u344	With what we are going through and the various tensions we have suffered, we can legitimately ask ourselves this question, without creating a new programme. Actions have been implemented to avoid risks and supply disruptions. At this stage, manufacturers are required to prevent and respond to manufacturing disruptions where appropriate. It is therefore not up to the public authorities to act in the place of companies and to compensate for their shortcomings. Rather, it is about putting them in the face of their responsibilities and asking them to assume them. With regard to the health products you mention, the National Public Health Agency may, at the request of the Minister of Health, establish stocks to meet the needs of the population to cope with these tensions. For all these reasons, unfavorable opinion.
