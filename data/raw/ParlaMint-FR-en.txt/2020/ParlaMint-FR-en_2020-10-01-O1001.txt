ParlaMint-FR_2020-10-01-O1001.u1	In accordance with the first paragraph of article 28 of the Constitution, I declare the 2020-2021 ordinary session open.
ParlaMint-FR_2020-10-01-O1001.u2	On September 27, 2020, Ms.Valérie Boyer, Mr. Stéphane Demilly and Mr. Philippe Folliot were elected senators. Consequently, pursuant to article 137 of the Electoral Code, they cease to belong to the National Assembly as of today. The vacancy of their seats will, however, be noted only after the expiry of the time limit for appeal or, if necessary, after the decision of the Constitutional Council confirming their election.
ParlaMint-FR_2020-10-01-O1001.u3	The next item is the appointment of the six Vice-Presidents, the three Quaestors and the twelve Secretaries of the National Assembly. Pursuant to Rule 10(3) of the Rules of Procedure, the group chairmen met yesterday afternoon. They reached agreement on the distribution of posts and the order in which the candidates were presented. In accordance with Rule 10(8) of the Rules of Procedure, I declare, in the order in which they are presented, Vice-Presidents: Ms. Annie Genevard; Mr. Hugues Renson; Mrs. Laetitia Saint-Paul; Mr. Sylvain Waserman; Mr. Marc Le Fur; [[Applause]] Mr. David Habib. I proclaim the Quaestors: Mr. Florian Bachelier; Ms. Laurianne Rossi; [[Applause]] Mr. Eric Ciotti. Finally, I declare secretaries: Ms. Clementine Autain; Ms. Annie Chapelier; Ms. Frédérique Dumas; Mr. Yannick Favennec Becot; Ms. Véronique Hammerer; Ms. Laure de La Raudière; Mr. Jean François Mbaye; Ms. Sophie Mette; Mr Pierre Morel -à- L'Hussier ; Mr. Remy Rebeyrotte; Mr. Gabriel Serville; [[Applause]] Mr. Bertrand Sorre. The Bureau of the National Assembly is therefore constituted. Its composition will be notified to the President of the Republic, the Prime Minister, the President of the Senate and published in the Official Gazette.
ParlaMint-FR_2020-10-01-O1001.u4	Next meeting, this afternoon, at 3 p.m.: Ratification of three international conventions; Discussion of the draft law extending the transitional regime for the exit from the state of health emergency; Further discussion of the bill to speed up and simplify public action. The meeting is adjourned.
