ParlaMint-ES_2018-07-25-CD180725.u1	Ladies and gentlemen, the sitting is open. Sole item on the agenda. We begin to examine, as the only item on the agenda of this special session, the election of a Member of the Confederal Parliamentary Group of the United Podemos-En Comú Podemos-En Marea, in accordance with the third paragraph of the Resolution of the Presidency of the Congress of Deputies on Official Secrets of 11 May 2004. We shall proceed to the election by ballot, in accordance with Rule 205, in respect of 204 of the Rules of Procedure, and the Member shall be elected by a three-fifth majority, that is to say, 210 votes. Each deputy will write a name on his or her ballot. I remind you that there will be no ballot papers containing more than one name or this one does not correspond to a Member of the Confederal Parliamentary Group of the United Podemos-En Comú Podemos-En Marea. In this vote, five telematic votes have been cast: by dona María Auxiliadora Honorato Chulián, by dona María Isabel Salud Arete, by dona Irene María Montero Gil, by don Pablo Manuel Iglesias Turrión and by don Juan Pedro Yllanes Suárez, who I am introducing in the ballot box. Please close the doors. I ask the Secretary to proceed. The secretary-generals of the Bureau made an appeal to the ladies and gentlemen, who were putting their ballot papers in the ballot box. After the vote, the voting began. "End of scrutiny," he said.
ParlaMint-ES_2018-07-25-CD180725.u2	Ladies and gentlemen, the voting was as follows: 324 votes in favour of Ione Belarra Urteaga, 309 votes in favour, 3 votes against, 12. Having obtained more than three fifths of the votes of the members of the House, Mrs Ione Belarra Urteaga was elected for the purposes of the Presidency Resolution of 11 May 2004. Congratulations, Your Honor. The meeting rose. Thank you so much. It was twelve thirty-five minutes from noon.
