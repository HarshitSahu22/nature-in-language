Ladies and gentlemen, as you will understand, it is a great honour for me to accept the appointment of President of the Congress and I would like to thank on my behalf and on behalf of the entire Bureau for the confidence they have just placed in us to lead and accompany the life of this institution. Mila esker, bihotz bihotzez, many thanks, many thanks, many thanks to everyone. I would like you to know that this is a sincere and unprotocolary thanks to all of you, you have voted for what you voted for, because the essential thing is that we have all, before the result, assumed compliance with the rules, which is what it does to all of us, all of us, participants and colleagues in the same democracy. Today the truly significant act is not the election of this Bureau but the constitution of the XI Legislature of Congress. All parliamentarians have recognized each other as equals and also representatives of the citizens of Spain. We have recognized each one, each one, the power of the representation of the voters and thus this Chamber becomes the representation of the entire Spanish society, of all its citizens whose freely expressed will is contained in this House. Therefore and because this has not always been so, let me remind all those who gave the best of themselves, even their lives, so that this act, which has its protocol part, is above all an act of freedom. I would especially like to remind Clara Campoamor, who fought to open the door of this Chamber to marginalised Spain: to women. I would like to remind those who, during the dictatorship, remained faithful to the values of democracy, those who resisted defending freedom from terrorism, those who preceded us in these seats, dignified the free exercise of noble politics. And that freedom, freedom, is a conquest that must be defended day by day. No power should be imposed on the citizens gathered in this Congress. In our hands, ladies and gentlemen, it is the responsibility to defend the autonomy of politics and the autonomy of the will of the citizen against any pressure external to the democratic representation of Spanish society. [[Applauds]] There are representatives in this House who defend different ideologies and policies, and there are also Members who defend different ways of understanding identity. Well, that's the very essence of free societies. Ideological plurality and the diversity of identities are the blood that feeds a free and democratic society. That is why discrepancy and criticism, but also dialogue and agreement, are the fundamental elements of political life in democracy, and that is what we will have to develop and exercise here: dialogue, debate and seek understanding; dialogue and understanding as the great political values. Because Spain is plural in the ideological and is diverse in the way of understanding identities, but it is above all a common project. Spain is not a map or an identity; it is the citizens' pact for freedom and collective progress. Citizens from every corner of this country had the same dream of freedom that united them all and together we have built the era of greatest progress and freedom in all our history, and together now, as we have done on other important occasions in our recent past, we must know how to manage discrepancies and seek on all occasions as much as possible what unites us than what separates us, because that is the very essence of our country, it is the very essence of democracy. And we must understand very well that this House is the expression of that plurality and that diversity, the diversity and plurality of the whole of Spanish society, and that is why we must be above all guardians and guarantors of that plurality and diversity so that democracy itself is not marginalized. Ladies and gentlemen, we are living in times of great uncertainty. Spanish society must face new problems in this new century: globalisation, which has disrupted all political and economic relations; the consolidation of the European Union, in the face of the temptation to divide Europe again with borders from north to south and from east to west. But we also have old problems that arise with special cruelty, such as the unbearable inequality between those who have a lot and those who have nothing or almost nothing, and it is up to us all and all of us here to implement the political and institutional reforms necessary to face up to these problems and to respond to what the citizens of this country really expect from us. For all this, this Bureau asks them for help, collaboration and commitment, and hopes not to disappoint the trust, their confidence, while meeting the challenge that we have collectively taken on. Thus, in order to put us to it as soon as possible and in accordance with the provisions of article 4 of the Rules of Procedure of the Chamber, I declare the Congress of Deputies constituted, and this will be reported to his majesty the king, the Senate and the Government.
